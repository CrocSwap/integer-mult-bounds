# Attribution and source scope

Prepared for Ali Romman (@aliromman) with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance.
Apache-2.0 applies. The source notices and licenses remain in the retained
packages. This work includes a separately written checker, but no claim of
independent human peer review.

The new contribution is the common dual for mixed provider inventories in
one owner pool. It includes the complete base catalogue, all 25 reduced-cost
certificates, their uniform positive derivatives, and controls that detect
missing records and favorable new profiles. It adds no local factory, owner
witness, finite invoice, or kappa improvement.

- Chafik Boukhalfa (@chafreaky), PR795 and PR890, supplied the signed width
  prices, capacity bonuses, and fixed-family equations. PR795's
  `research/sw4-owner-optimality/PROOF.md` remains a source.
- Jonathan Capalbo's PR942 supplied the U8/T37 owner construction and the all-T
  support argument. PR962, by Joel Pulikkan, independently found the same
  T37 residual profile. This package credits both and uses the merged PR942
  claim as its reference. It does not claim a new all-T U8 support theorem.
- PR995, PR1000 and PR1010 supply concurrent uniform or sharp support
  results for the fixed U8 inventory. PR992 treats the integer penalties
  at other replica counts. These are separate from the mixed-inventory
  result. No code from those pending packages is imported here.
- PR615 and `research/complex-pr615-ceiling/PROOF-CEILING.md` supply an earlier
  all-dispatch ceiling for two providers. Their construction and owner credit
  belongs to Jeremy Kothe, DreamingOfClouds, and the retained predecessors.
  The present catalogue is larger and uses the U8/T37 shared-pool support.
- Jeremy Kothe's X4 work, PR799, and the PR869/870 U8 lineage supply the local
  reference. Their retained notices credit NewBArch, ColdWitness/icekylinx,
  and the earlier factory contributors. Hao Yan (@FanPUYen), PR858, and
  Chafik Boukhalfa, PR890, supply the T16/T21 controller lineage used by PR942.
- Every other local cost vector comes directly from the source file named
  in `CATALOGUE.json`. Equal vectors keep all source records. Their local
  construction, recipes, audits, and owner maps remain the work of the
  contributors named in those source packages.
- The exact `moment.py`, `base_two_moment.py`, and `outer.py` engines are
  imported unchanged from the multicold dependencies. Their retained
  notices include eumemic and the earlier maintainer arithmetic lineage.
  The outer engine credits icekylinx's PR144, Zhihao Chen's PR23, and the
  RaD balanced positional transform (hipotures). The unchanged
  `price_profile_t16.py` credits icekylinx with OpenAI GPT-6 Astra assistance.
  The PR589 bit supplier is unchanged.
- PR987 supplies the U10 profile direction used in a control. The leaver
  controls use the R489 and R480 profiles of PR1004/1005, credited there to
  LeaverSAT, LeaverSAT2, G44Pack, G44Pack3 and the retained local lineage.
  These controls do not admit the new programs or owner maps.

`DEPENDENCIES.json`. The 35 catalogue sources have separate hashes in
`CATALOGUE.json`. Current provider additions are reported separately and
are not silently admitted. All inherited all-size, compiler, physical,
hosting, precision, tape, analytic-transfer and bit H1-H5 obligations remain.
