## The current caller saturates its scalar header-query budget

Both final callers have 34 independent fields. After setting every fresh body variable to one, 32 outputs carry the central involution q and two carry the identity. There are exactly 33 header-query gates: 18 LOCAL gates and 15 thin QUERY gates. Within the same central scalar contract, with q-independent invertible ordinary controls, one header query cannot be removed while retaining these independent targets.

For characteristic different from two, compare the complete words at q=-1 and q=1. Their relative map is conjugate to diag(-I_32,I_2), and each query contributes a conjugate rank-one unipotent I+u v with v u=0. A product of m such factors has M-I=U L V, where L is unit triangular. Rank(M-I)=32 forces m>=32. If m=32, the columns U span the negative eigenspace, so L V U=-2I. This makes the diagonal of V U equal -2, contradicting each v_i u_i=0. Thus m>=33.

For the BIT scalar contract, q=1+ε in F2[ε]/(ε²). After removing the constant ordinary map, the ε coefficient is a rank-32 idempotent Π. Each query contributes one rank-one nilpotent u_i v_i. If 32 queries sufficed, Π=UV with both U,V full rank 32. Since im(U)=im(Π), idempotence gives VU=I, again contradicting its zero diagonal. The formal central involution is essential; specializing only q=1 would lose this information.

This closes header-query deletion in this fixed scalar caller model. It does not cover a different native source, different targets, operator-valued port coupling or a different physical oracle contract.

## Retirement with owned erasure and completed-output feedback

Let P map arbitrary public inputs to the live state at a cut, S map that state to public outputs, and SP=C be the required public endpoint. At one common coframe bank choose a resident row a and column b with ab=1. Suppose d=aP is supported on an equal-target helper group G and Sb=Cc with c supported on G. Let γ=dc be a nonzero legal scalar unit. Require every future zero-before-COPY functional M to satisfy Mb=0. Dirty erasure alone imposes no extra zero-return condition.

Choose an input basis E with E e_g=c/γ and e_g^T E^-1=d, and a cut basis J with Jb=e_r and e_r^T J=a. At the cut, park old r by swapping it with a genuinely fresh arbitrary input y at the same coframe. Before inverse J and the suffix, set the replacement component to

```
(y + (γ-1) * parked) / γ.
```

The parked output is already pure and is never overwritten. The resulting suffix public output is

```
C E x + C c * (y-x_g) / γ.
```

After inverse E, old output g equals y and all other public outputs are unchanged. A final wire swap associates the parked prefix with original input g and the suffix with the new input. Each future COPY monitor remains zero because MP=0 and Mb=0. The feedback is ordinary and occurs at the parked output's own coframe; it uses no Gaussian inverse oracle. This algebraic statement does not on its own assert a physical paying source. Its actual dyadic bases, rank recipes, ownership and ordinary bill remain necessary for any positive application.

## All-size allocation ceiling for this exact complex root

For the admitted root, let the positive child sizes be (a,b,c,d,e,f), F their sum, and let D(n) be the maximum contribution of a binary subtree with n leaves after its native leaf contribution has been removed. Put h(r)=r^(1-k), i(n)=h(8n+1)-h(8n), and let ell be the complete signed native helper/paid moment divided by 104. Each internal node pays one thin query and contributes two independent targets and two paid idles, so

```
D(1)=0,
D(n)=max_{1<=j<n}(D(j)+D(n-j))+2*i(n)-1.
```

The exact full gap per port, including both paid fragments and all thin work, is

```
F*ell + sum D(child)
+ h(8F+1) + h(8(a+b+c)+1) + h(8(d+e+f)+1)
+ i(b+c)+i(c)+i(d+e)+i(d)-2.
```

The formula is bound to the actual frozen invoice at (4,4,1,1,4,4). It has no unearned productive targets. Because i(n) decreases with n, each internal-node contribution is at most A=2*i(2)-1. There are F-6 internal nodes. Jensen's inequality bounds the two three-child aggregate targets, and each of the four remaining increments is at most i(1). Thus the full gap is at most

```
B(F)=F*ell+(F-6)*A+4*i(1)+h(8F+1)+2*h(4F+1)-2.
```

At k=0.0033072446849, exact rational intervals give B(50)<-0.909102643724 and B'(50)<-0.051204976823. B is concave in F, so every F>=50 loses. Outward interval dynamic programming over every ordered binary split and every positive six-child allocation covers F=6,...,49. Its maximum occurs at F=18 and is less than -0.000000000101790666829957164467 per port. The native signed moment and every other term are decreasing in k on [0.0033072446849,0.004], so the exclusion persists beyond the raw ceiling through the retained ordinary BIT stock limit.

This is a negative in the current coordinates, rather than an exclusion of every possible rotated or coupled idle.

There is also a short explanation for the most direct auxiliary-bank retirement at the cut after the left child. Work before the final ordinary lambda normalization and put S=lambda*q^(-1), so S^2=1. Let X,Y be the active inputs, A,B the parent's arbitrary auxiliary inputs, and L,R the independent child body monomials. At that cut the active fields are

```
x=L*(B-S*Y+S*A),
y=L*(Y-S*B).
```

Let J be the ordinary projector onto the auxiliary directions receiving only L at this cut; the others still carry LR. The auxiliary vector is

```
L*(J+R*(I-J))*(X+S*Y-S*A, Y)^T.
```

A nonzero auxiliary input column c=alpha*A+beta*B cannot have a pure head-free L prefix: the active y coefficient forces beta=0 and then active x forces alpha=0. A pure SL prefix forces beta=0 and `(I-J)e1=0`. If J has rank one this means `J=[[1,u],[0,0]]` for an arbitrary legal ordinary u; if J has rank two it is I.

Now take any ordinary cut row w=(p,q,r,s) whose prefix response is supported only on A,B and is SL times an ordinary row d. Its X coefficient forces r=0. Its Y coefficient is `L*(-p*S+q+s*R)` in the rank-one case, or `L*(-p*S+q+s)` when J=I. The even ordinary coefficients and the odd central S coefficient are independent, so p=0. But d_A=p-r=0, while c is supported on A. Hence `d*c=0`: the required independent input/output pairing is impossible. Rank-zero J cannot supply a nonzero pure SL column at all.

This argument covers arbitrary ordinary rotations of the parent's auxiliary idle projection at this completed-child cut, for retiring one of these parent's equal-target auxiliaries through a pure L or SL prefix. It does not cover cuts inside a child, additional paid contacts, different input groups or operator-valued ordinary controls. **No supported next construction action is currently recommended on this internal-fragment branch.** A legal paying prefix/suffix unit pair outside those premises would reopen it.
