# A finite-character replacement for formal body saturation

Jerry Chen / @CubeJerry, with substantial OpenAI assistance, Apache-2.0. This is a further scoped lemma for the preceding header proofs. It addresses a real distinction: a physical body may satisfy D^4=1, whereas differentiation of a formal Laurent identity cannot be justified from that quotient identity alone.

## Hypotheses

Work over a field of characteristic different from two containing a primitive fourth root ζ. Retain the S-header and atomic native query/body assumptions of HEADER-SATURATION.md. For the body currently examined, require two jointly available scalar characters D=1 and D=ζ at S=1, with all other bodies fixed to one and **the same ordinary control matrices** in both characters. Each positive body factor is I+(D−1)P_j, where P_j is an ordinary split projector of rank r_j. Its native query, when present, is adjacent and supported on P_j on both sides.

Let s=sum r_j. The required endpoint in these two characters obeys

    W(1,ζ) W(1,1)^−1 = I+(ζ−1)P_D,

where P_D is the prescribed ordinary final body projector of rank r. This is an explicit character-interface assumption, not a claim that arbitrary physical address controls preserve the chosen character fibers.

## Rank and congruence lemma

Absorb the D=1 ordinary suffixes and put z=ζ−1. The normalized word is a product of factors I+z Q_j, where Q_j is the corresponding conjugate of P_j. Write Q_j=U_j V_j with V_j U_j=I_r_j. Concatenate their columns and rows into U and V. Expanding the product gives

    P_D = (W(1,ζ)W(1,1)^−1−I)/z = U T(z) V,

where T(z) is block-unit-triangular and therefore invertible. Consequently s≥r. If s=r, U and V have full column and row ranks: their image and row spaces equal those of P_D. Every Q_j is then supported on P_D on both sides. The atomic native query has the same suffix conjugation, so its normalized nilpotent has this support too. This is exactly the support conclusion needed by the header/quotient proofs, obtained without differentiating in D.

Taking determinants gives ζ^s=ζ^r. Thus s−r is divisible by four. Combined with s≥r, the only possibilities are s=r or s≥r+4. The argument also applies separately to each independent body of the two-module contract.

## Precise pricing consequence

For a scalar binary parent, hold the native child contracts and productive targets fixed. Assume each additional scalar body application outside those unchanged native stages is charged its complete ordinary whole-body tariff H(b), with b≥1 and H(b)=b^t, 0<t<1. There is no packed-provider discount or new productive credit in this comparison.

At the intended minimal body bill s=r, the earlier header obstruction applies unchanged. Escaping saturation by positive fourth-power cycles requires at least four extra scalar body applications, costing at least 4H(b)≥4. One or two scalar fragments gain strictly less than two before that new charge. They cannot pay for this alternative. The one-fragment and mixed-two-fragment header-price conclusions therefore survive this particular order-four-body relaxation under the stated per-channel tariff. The separate equal-orientation limitation concerning a changed history with an additional *central* query remains; it is not a body-cycle loophole.

This price statement is scalar, or independently replicated per port. It is not a general coupled-width or new-provider theorem. Inverse body calls, new native suppliers/helper targets, character-dependent controls, missing joint characters and changed body exponents require separate accounting.

## Scope control

The one-coordinate word D^5 equals D whenever D^4=1. Its formal derivatives at one are five and one, so the formal differentiation proof would be invalid for this quotient-only identity. Here s=5, r=1: the finite-character argument correctly detects the four extra positive applications. This explicit example explains why the new lemma is needed and why its excess-cost premise must be retained.

`quartic.py` checks exact Gaussian-integer character identities, the degree-five limitation example, and an extra four-step cycle outside the final body space. The universal rank and congruence statements above are mathematical proofs, not consequences of a finite census. No physical supplier admission, framework-assumption discharge or new κ is claimed.
