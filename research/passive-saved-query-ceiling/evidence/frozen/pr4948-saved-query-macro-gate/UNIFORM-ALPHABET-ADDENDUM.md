# Uniform finite alphabet for the large-width argument

This clarification leaves the six-file freeze and its manifest unchanged. Independent reviewer B requested that the finite-alphabet premise be explicit.

All one-column literal address maps in this gate lie in the Clifford group on the **fixed 17-axis ambient space**. Its projective quotient is finite. Scalar root phases are absorbed into the coefficients after tensor lift; they do not change Fourier grade. The finite universe of projective one-column Clifford maps is therefore independent of column width f.

The Gram argument in `PROOF.md` can be applied once to representatives of this whole finite projective universe, rather than merely to the words appearing in a single chosen decoder. For sufficiently large f its tensor-power Gram matrix is positive definite. This gives a uniform threshold even when scalar reconstruction coefficients, selections of allowed controls, or numbers of passive scalar operations depend on f. The source one-column C1/C3 entries and all their allowed left expansions remain in the monomial subset and preserve the grade support proved in the frozen packet.

This does not include f-dependent payload permutations outside the fixed columnwise Clifford alphabet. Such maps are not granted by this gate's scalar-owner/actual finite-code contract. It also does not turn the source's finite coefficients into coefficients raised to tensor powers.
