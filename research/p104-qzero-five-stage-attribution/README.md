# Prove rank-three mask attribution through five active stages

Assumes a full formal ordered matrix identity on independent stage variables with rank-one incidence projectors and squarefree masks. It gives no vector-support attribution, odd-target HOST assignment, or signed tariff. One-shared-stage and smaller-stage cases use the exact predecessor result.

For three-dimensional characteristic-zero quotient words, every event admits mask-only attribution when there are at most five active body stages. The five-stage scan with at least two shared stages has 85 endpoint orbits, 20,232 event orbits and 828 admissible nonpartitions. Saturated projectors rule out 795. Linear/saturation equations exclude 31 of the remaining 33 and rank-one constraints exclude the last two.

Observed validation. Corrected explicit row and column certificates and the full census passed complete cold replay. An independent standard-library checker reproduced all 33 strengthened certificates and rejected the missing-row mutation.
