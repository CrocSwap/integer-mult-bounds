# A polynomial-width compiler for mixed-rank multiplication suppliers

A native multiplication supplier can produce many independent transforms of different ranks and request other ranks as it runs. The complete multiplier needs independent **full** transforms and positive rank-one requests. This package gives a different way to compile one interface into the other. For the selected and pending complex and binary words, the compiler needs at most **`N^16` productive fields** and **`O_source(N^51)` pre-cover instruction records** at final rank `N`. The degree is uniform as a strictly funded price approaches the raw crossing. The proof supplies the finite compiler implication for these four words from their raw primitive contracts. Both binary services start from individual digit swaps.

The algorithm keeps one productive grade of a chronological product. It splits oversized paid requests. Each recursive request works inside the *original* tensor, where it can borrow real spectator coordinates and return them. Every productive owner receives its entire transform through disjoint tiles at the root. This avoids an integer Pascal flow, a completion job per source, and intermediate covered binary weights. A necessary and sufficient interval criterion determines when this particular single-grade construction can succeed. Even a raw surplus at every price can miss that criterion.

The selected program uses at most `N^15` productive fields on each supplier. The pending complex program gives the largest bound, `N^16`. Hence the outer complex productive row divisor at dimension `d` is at most `N^48 max(1,d)^16`, before separately reserved owned banks, compact controls and simultaneous BIT stock. The binary cover still has the full `GL_N` row factor. The algorithm pays for it.

There is a substantial tradeoff. For the pending complex word the old sufficient `log2 N` was **15,171,300,000,000,000,000**. This construction uses **223,634,496,475,974,210,983,125,057,536**. The polynomial guarantees concern the program at its own rank. Comparing those guarantees does not establish a smaller physical machine, faster practical multiplication, or a larger κ. The large words have constructive descriptions and finite dimension witnesses. The complete words have not been emitted or executed at those dimensions.
