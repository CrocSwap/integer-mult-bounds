# Verification boundary

The written proof gives the quantified compiler and physical implication. The finite tests check source data, integer witnesses, executable schedules, and vulnerable transitions in that argument.

| Replay | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| `check_funding.py` | Authentic source bytes, positive directed margins at five raw prices, exact retained types, effective dimensions, constant cuts, width and visit degrees, both complete binary price chains from saving zero, and a negative chord control even with a strict raw surplus at every price. |
| `check_schedule.py` | 36 complete formal-array cases at three positive tensor lifts. Tracks full address permutations of every source field and all coefficients, internal use of spectator axes, a paid owned center, retirement and the exact leaf bill. It also checks dimensions exceeding a platform range-length limit. |
| `check_product.py` | Ten end-to-end cases start at a two-grade raw word. They construct its product, supply 396 discarded whole fields, split a rank-four paid return, expand all 3,140 leaves and verify independent full endpoints. Corrupted ownership, extra public dependence and incompatible serialized type counts are rejected. |
| `check_cover.py` | Two genuine protected rank-four helper frames, 25-dimensional rational-projector geometry modulo 101², ragged tiles, nested right routes and inverses. A second test exercises all 48 rows of GL₂(F₃) and all 81 addresses on two independent roles. Omitting a tile, changing a matcher and inserting an uncompensated early cover alter 5,184, 2,592 and 6,912 labelled cells respectively. The paid slices in the geometry fixture are constructed test slices. |
| `check_cold.py` | 72 fixed-radix layouts and 618,408 independently labelled cells check the elementary forward and inverse digit interchange with complete spectators. |
| `check_callers.py` | Reconstructs the two actual complex invoices, verifies their real source grids and depths and runs the original outer gates. Each passes 54 strict and four functional predicates with minimum margin 10⁻¹⁵. The increased dimension and new depth witnesses are checked separately from the old gates' internal reference calculations. |

All registered commands require only Python 3.12 and the standard library. The source gates contain assertion-based checks and run with assertions enabled. The new optimized-mode replays keep their explicit rejection checks active. The inputs and the historical source exports have exact Git blob and SHA-256 bindings. The compressed pending objects expand byte-for-byte to their pinned files. [Input bindings](ORIGINS.json) · [Replay manifest](VERIFY.json)

Separate adversarial checks compared more than 22,000 exact leaf recurrences and independently enumerated 1,375 small multinomial optima. The complex reviewer tested every one of 220,223 pre-add products and 6,998 ordered cuts in the actual pending native scalar graph with correlated nested input. Omitting a local odd factor failed a division at row 155953. The binary reviewer checked the original nonintegral high-row right route on 7,776 affine carries, and separately obtained a direct binary witness by using the original tail bounds and pigeonholing a grade. These are additional development checks. The registered package uses the five explicit modal types and its own full-volume fixtures.

The original complete native words, operator/adapter implementations, physical movement tariffs, analytic multiplication and final recovery remain source dependencies. This package does not replay unrelated historical suites or the whole native admission from scratch. The large product, full terminal program, prime and binary cover are specified by finite algorithms. Their complete physical instantiations have not been built. There is no end-to-end multiplier benchmark or proof-assistant result. A passing hosted or local replay is a software result. Scientific acceptance is a separate review.
