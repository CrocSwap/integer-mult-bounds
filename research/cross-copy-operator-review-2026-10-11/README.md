This source-closed review accompanies [PR448](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/448) at revision9b966d8188e1cadd00aa834078ee6a8742a9d9b4. Under the inherited complete-stream contract, each physical role carries an arbitrary full address-indexed value array. Splitting two address factors across two physical families therefore needs explicit value embedding, local read/write lowering and spectator restoration. The finite trace, partial-flag, constant-mask and selector witnesses are scoped to their stated contracts. A smaller payload/coset compiler, time-dependent encodings or separately paid value operations remain open. The current signed95 compressed compiler itself remains conditional.
