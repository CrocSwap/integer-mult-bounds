## Deferred #1048: pricing and exactness need correction

The PR claims that, for any fixed exponent a0, the weighted count G(a0)=sum n_r r^(1-a0) orders the certified roots. This is false even for equal rank mass and equal m,W. Take m=8, W=4, A={2:15}, B={1:14,4:4}; both have mass 30 and deficit 2. At a0=1/2, G_A=15 sqrt(2)<22=G_B, but root_A<47/1000<root_B. The [exact rational counterexample](../../research/community-batch1099-audit/check_pr1048_counterexample.py) checks both strict moment inequalities without floating point. Comparing at a certified reference endpoint can answer a specific improvement question; arbitrary fixed-exponent root ordering cannot.

Moreover `StateProf.objective()` and the one/two-move acceptance comparisons use floating-point powers and tolerances. The baseline nomination comparison does not certify nomination completeness after every first move. The interval bisection treats failure to prove strict acceptance as a rejected upper bound rather than separately proving that upper bound's sign. These gaps prevent recording the stated exact neighborhood exhaustion. The long search was stopped after the decisive mathematical finding; a completed exhaustive run is not claimed. The proposal stays open, with its author credited for the research direction. Required repair: scope the objective to the correct certified reference moment including surcharge, certify sign decisions and root brackets, and justify the claimed search coverage. No construction counterexample to the frozen PR589 word has been established by this review.
