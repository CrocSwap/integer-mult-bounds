# FOUR428 owner/native bridge

`build_bridge.py` connects the verified literal owner restriction to the two
actual native FOUR428 words. It independently enumerates the finite frame
subspaces, checks the local frame chronology and recounts the full paid rank
profile. It imports no scalar emitter, transport implementation, optimizer or
COPY accounting routine. The inherited semantic identity helper is used only
to construct the parent-format artifact hashes.

All 285,048 owner roles bind to the exact original helper ID in the native
`helper_origin` list and to that helper's entrance-to-exit width. The 666-call
provider dispatch remains 249 gauge44 and 417 extra. Both native profiles plus
the independently verified idle remainder reproduce the exact restricted
global profile and deficit 23,680.

The new interface changes four internal target levels, at target offsets
23, 34, 50 and 108, to the line spanned by 30. Centers 0, 3, 5 and 6 serve this
line before frame zero, so each has recursive children of ranks 7 and 1.
The other four centers retain their rank-8 child. All twelve segments are
bound literally to both native orientations, with the backward path reversed
within each center. Removing a segment or changing a target level is rejected
in each provider. Native scalar/phase receipts remain separate pinned
dependencies; this finite recount does not replace them.

The 224 source/target entrance and exit frame subspaces, ordered ports and
eight ordered center frame subspaces are identical to the pinned source.
The internal target lift therefore does not change the external data-phase
controller interface. The exact data-phase recipe and source global envelope
are retained by hash. The independently verified owner windows, pending axes
and spectator charts continue to apply to the retained helper widths.

## Parent integration obligations

The parent source remains immutable. The following obligations motivated the
separate runtime overlays and are checked by the final global evidence gate;
the bridge receipt alone does not certify their completion:

1. `new/rebind.py` copies the old owners and construction record and uses its
   old idle profile. The new artifacts here replace that rebind operation.
   Copying only the new local profiles would leave incorrect owner and idle
   counts. Its physical formula also omits the zero buffers.
2. `independent_core.py::local_inventory` explicitly requires empty
   `target_levels` and charges one full rank-8 copy per center. That limitation
   must be replaced by the nested target-level recount above while preserving
   its remaining frame, owner, window, axis and residual checks. The native
   boundary frame identities and ordered center frames must remain checked.
3. `code/bind_providers_portable.py` can bind the newly hashed native programs,
   widths and profiles. Update strict expected native receipt values: both
   providers have 46 rejected native controls and 224 endpoint adapters;
   directed phase edges are 2,900 for gauge44 and 2,899 for extra. The existing
   helper-258 spot check remains width 9 in both providers, as derived from
   the pinned pivot recipe; do not remove it.
4. `code/emit_allcold.py` already dispatches complete native programs and
   sums their explicit child ranks. Its stock/namespace formula must include
   the 76 private buffers, and the emitted template must bind the new internal
   interface record or be checked against this bridge receipt explicitly.
5. `code/audit_allcold.py` already recounts literal `climb`/`center` events
   and checks the unchanged data controller and all owner charts. Its
   independent physical-stock/namespace checks must include the zero arena.
6. `code/invoice_allcold.py` recognizes split center events and zero-buffer
   lifetimes, but its physical identity and local adapter reserve omit the
   explicit zero arena and warp-event charges. The full stock, clearing,
   routing, prefix and finite inequalities must be recomputed from the
   actual selected calls. A favorable recursive moment alone is insufficient.

The later runtime binder may normalize validated generated receipt metadata
according to the pinned `generated_identity.py` policy. It must preserve
