## 1. Declared replacement class

Use the retained canonical binary-subspace chart family in ambient dimension 9.
The complete 76475-instruction source has 1501 independent public fields and
6,279 paid native MOVEs. A visit is one physical owner's maximal residence
between MOVEs or public/INIT/CLEAR boundaries. Join both operand visits of
every ADD/COPY; SCALE is internal to one visit. The 1,517 resulting components
each share a current chart. There are 1,003 internal components.

Replace the chart of each internal component by any other subspace chart,
independently. Preserve every scalar coefficient,source,destination and clock,
the visit/contact structure, external source/target charts, owners and lifetimes.
Any component touching a public source/target,INIT or CLEAR is frozen. Public
MOVEs retain the source grammar's ascending containment; owned MOVEs may use
either nested direction, including a reversal of their old orientation. Equal
endpoint spaces are allowed as an optimistic rank 0 recursive bill.

The public ascending/owned two-direction distinction is authenticated by

`21f46a8340331abb1486b50bd4e04453f1968b0a5950d86ff7780dd368590874`).
It is the explicit retained word grammar, not a conclusion from the observed histogram. A new grammar
allowing public descents,nonnested edges or arbitrary Clifford charts outside

## 2. Necessary complete public corridors

Orient ONLY public MOVE edges from their old smaller to larger component.
Their old dimensions strictly increase, so the graph is a DAG. Every admitted
replacement satisfies C_a' subset C_b' on each such edge.

Set floor(a)=cap(a)=C_a on a frozen vertex. At an internal vertex set
floor(a)=span of floor(b) over incoming public neighbors, with empty span0;
cap(a)=intersection of cap(b) over outgoing public neighbors, with empty
intersection the full ambient space. Compute floors by increasing old
dimension and caps by decreasing old dimension.

Following public paths from frozen lower charts and to frozen upper charts,
or directly inducting along the DAG, proves for EVERY admitted replacement

    floor(a) subset C_a' subset cap(a).

These bounds are necessary, not individually sufficient; all actual edge
constraints are checked again jointly below. Owned edges are deliberately not
used to derive them, because an owned edge may reverse its nesting.

Exactly 96 internal vertices have a nontrivial corridor. Their quotient
dimensions are 91 of dimension1,three of dimension2 and two of dimension3.
Twenty can grow and76 can shrink; these sets are disjoint. The corridors also
cover any otherwise incomparable chart choice, rather than assuming that a
replacement is comparable to its old chart. In this particular source the
disjoint shrink/growth split makes each corridor one-sided.

The two components with internal owned visits are482 and883. Component883 is
fixed by its public corridor. Component482 is permitted to shrink by its public
corridor; its actual owned edges are checked for comparability in BOTH
directions for every candidate. No unproved old-owned-orientation restriction
is imposed.

## 4. Exact increasing-concave conclusion

For any increasing concave tariff f on 0,...,9 with f(0)=0, put
Delta_j=f(j)-f(j-1). Then Delta_1>=...>=Delta_9>=0 and

    f(r)=Delta_9 min(r,9)
         +sum(k=1,...,8)(Delta_k-Delta_(k+1))min(r,k).

All coefficients are nonnegative. Each legal assignment's saving is the same
combination of its nine extreme-tariff savings, all at most 0. Summing the 77
factors proves an exact zero best saving over the ENTIRE declared recharting
class for every increasing concave f. This includes every Gaussian tariff
r^(1-kappa),0<kappa<1, and in particular .0038 and.004.

The actual mixing rank of a quotient is at least the absolute difference of
its chart mixing ranks. This is the triangle inequality obtained from rank
subadditivity of the symplectic B block under composition and inversion. Each
canonical source chart has B rank equal to its dimension, because it is a
positive partial C tensor conjugated by monomial maps. This remains true for
the retained general-GL charts with degenerate binary Gram form. Therefore
actual complete quotient tariffs cannot beat the optimistic dimension bill.
Additional ordinary/phase/compiler services cannot turn its nonpositive saving
into a positive paid saving.

The gate concerns the finite template's rank histogram and retains separate
source positions and their complete-field reservations. The all-column and
whole-job implementation conventions remain inherited assumptions; this finite
invoice check does not itself prove a tensor or column-multiplicity scaling
law. Changed visit fusion or joint source compression is outside its
fixed-position accounting class.

The exact retained 17-call caller and its native helper targets are independently
recounted by the wrapper. The resulting target and paid histograms match the
source's complete `CURRENT-PRICE.json` invoice, with 22,461 productive fields
and 110,519 paid jobs. Targets are unchanged and each native paid saving is
nonpositive, so the complete signed moment cannot improve by these frame
