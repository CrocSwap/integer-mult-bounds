4. Stream completeness: retain the distinction

I confirmed that the retained driver ignores relevant output-function return values:

execute() prints and flushes before its verbose-only return.
-dumpspecs prints before exit(0).
-print-search-dirs performs three unchecked printf calls.

Therefore exit0, EOF, syntactic validity, or two equal dumps cannot establish complete intended emission. 
Exact retained gcc.cc

A concrete future capture protocol could use separate blocking pipes, fair draining into exclusive raw files, overflow detection, and genuine ownership of every writer. It would require writer-domain termination, closure of parent writer copies, retained bytes before interpretation, and failure on uncertain cleanup or read/write/fsync/close errors. This proves complete capture of delivered bytes, under the named OS interfaces. It does not prove successful formatting or emission inside GCC. It is a controller design option, not a silent alteration of the current frozen pilot or original helper.

An independently checkable intended-output certificate would compare raw bytes with a complete serialization derived independently: field population, cardinality, order, values, escaping, and lengths. This is available for predeclared calibration markers. It is not currently available for complete Q1/Q3/Q4 output, because the underlying installed state is not independently closed. Computing the expected digest from that same dump is circular.

Accordingly, keep the stronger interpretation HOLD, but do not make it a prerequisite for a genuinely admitted RAW_UNQUALIFIED experiment. Nor should truncated or merely equal output acquire a completeness label.
