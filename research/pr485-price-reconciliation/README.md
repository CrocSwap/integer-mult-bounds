# Archived PR485 composition: portable aggregate-price reconciliation

This package closes a reproducibility gap in the archived PR485 composition screen. Its five saved variants contain two distinct words, but its discovery and pricing scripts require an unavailable original workspace layout. The new offline checker reconstructs the chain from the archived kernel selection and literal words to the saved aggregate assignments and exact moment brackets. It confirms the historical no-gain comparison. It does not establish a new kappa or physical hosting result.

The historical evidence is in [the original archive](../experiment-log/negative-searches/pr485-composition/README.md). Those files remain unchanged. This is a superseding verification record, not a relabeling of the original experiments as full supplier verification.

## Reproduce

From the repository root, run:

```sh
python3 -I -O -B research/pr485-price-reconciliation/verify.py
python3 -I -O -B research/pr485-price-reconciliation/test_package.py
```

The first command must print:

```text
PASS_PR485_ARCHIVED_AGGREGATE_PRICES variants=5 words=2 reuses=3 replicas=21,84 columns=955
```

The second runs 20 tests. The standard library is sufficient. Assertions may be disabled because admission checks raise explicit exceptions. `--write-receipt /absolute/new/file.json` writes the recomputed receipt and refuses to overwrite a file. Default verification compares every field with [EXPECTED.json](EXPECTED.json).

## What is checked

The five word digests establish exactly two equivalence classes. The promotion logs preserve the original 112 pivots and 104 donors, and their literal entrance frames match the words. Seven promotions raise the entrance-rank total from 112 to 119. Three duplicate variants reuse the promoted word's price.

Fraction-free rational elimination derives every frame space. The local replay checks nested transitions, positive integer operand supports, retained COPY ranks, arbitrary dirty helper restoration, and all 955 Boolean input columns. Empty-source records still incur frame visits. These are abstract local checks, not native physical execution.

With `m=78` and `W=1200*T`, their rank deficit is exactly `408*T`.

For saving `a`, the inherited moment is:

```text
sum_r [n_r*r/(78*W) * (78/r)^a]
  + [32*78*sum_r(n_r)/(10^16*W) * 78^a].
```

The second term retains the inherited bad-class fallback charge. Independent positive-binomial enclosures check both reported endpoints, separated by exactly `10^-24`, without logarithm or exponential routines. Range reduction brings the base into `[1,2]`. For `t=1-1/base`, the positive series for `(1-t)^(-a)` has successive-term ratios below `t` when `0<a<1`. After 128 terms, the remaining tail is at most the next term divided by `1-t`. Every recurrence and product is rounded outward on a `2^-160` grid. Exact positive margins are recorded in EXPECTED.json.

The saved projected values, approximately `0.0012948386542432882` and `0.0012959767024392872`, both remain below the archive's stated comparator `0.001296611982495107`. The receipt records exact rational projections. These are arithmetic projections, not admitted multiplication bounds.

## Verification and limits

Twenty tests cover source-byte corruption, scalar corruption, non-nested frames, invalid capacities and counts, duplicate rows, broken conservation, promotion provenance, and endpoint rejection. An independent Fraction Gauss-Jordan oracle checks all 964 distinct frame descriptions. Exact square- and cube-root comparisons check the new power enclosures. A stage swap preserves total supplies but violates an idle window and is rejected. A scalar-preserving empty frame visit preserves rank mass but increases the paid-call count and is rejected by price comparison. Omitting the fallback term reverses the sign at the archived upper endpoint, demonstrating that the charge is material.

Local validation uses CPython 3.14.6 and 3.9.6, a relocated source-only checkout, and byte-identical receipt regeneration. The selected-record check and its five tests pass separately. A focused Linux workflow is supplied; no hosted CI pass is claimed at submission. The expensive repository-wide suite and the selected supplier's cold replay were not run because no supplier or selected artifact changes.

No discovery search was repeated. The complete bounded domain is the five saved variants, two words, two aggregate assignments, and four reported endpoints. This does not reconstruct every constructor step or validate the original 112-pivot optimality proof. Explicit per-helper expansion, native/global execution, physical charts, prefix and row invoices, outer assembly, and all-size/compiler/analytic contracts remain outside this contribution. Passing aggregate equations is not physical admission or an impossibility theorem.
