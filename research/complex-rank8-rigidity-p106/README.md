# Rank-8 rigidity on every P107 and P106 unit, via PR1560's frame-incidence criterion (no new κ)

**Status: finite verification of a conditional theorem. No κ claim and no construction.** The algebra is
an664's [PR1560](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/1560), open. I read head `5bebfd00`; the
current head `eb9f6f15` changes only §7 controls and §8 attribution. This package supplies the finite premises for
every unit. It continues the rank-8 line of PR1212 (burntout76, head `57099cb5`, open) and the P107 pivots
package (PR1568, `38d074f0`, open), and answers PR1560's open question. ToikY's PR1609 is adjacent but separate:
it keeps one factor root-linear and varies an arbitrary deletion count.

**Theorem.** Let X be a 5-subset or a 6-subset of P112 and L = P112 \ X. Let K be an L × L matrix over a field of
characteristic 0 with rank K ≤ 8 that satisfies PR1212's (E). Then K = D (2 G_L) D⁻¹ for an invertible diagonal
D.

**Corollary.** In the E-graph model, every decoder with exactly eight costly terms on P107 has cst ≥ 59 = 72 − 13,
and on P106 has cst ≥ 58 = 72 − 14. This is PR968's floor 72 − p, reached through PR1212 §8 as in the P108
package.

## Proof

PR1560's Corollary 2 gives the conclusion for a live set L and a pivot frame F ⊂ L whenever four finite
conditions hold, all computed from root incidences alone:

- **(D)** For each pivot k, the seven vectors G_ik G_il (l ≠ k) on I_k are independent, and the 0–1 incidence
  matrix of the live frames against I_k has rank |I_k| − 7.
- **(G)** For each pivot pair, the graph on I_kl is connected. Lines i and j are joined when some live frame
  meets I_kl in exactly {i, j}.
- **(W)** Every pivot pair lies in a block that has one complete live class and a live line of the other class.
- **Nonempty blocks:** each of the 14 blocks has a live non-pivot line.

PR1560 proves the implication in writing, with no SAT or DRUP step. This package verifies the premises on every
unit:

1. **Coverage.** H is the group generated by `verify.py`'s 8 FRAME0-stabiliser generators. `make_units.py`
   enumerates it at order 172032, but the argument does not need that number. H maps P112 to itself and frames
   to frames, so it preserves (E), all four conditions and their conclusion (PR1212 §10 transport). Coverage is three levels deep, and `verify.py` replays every word:
   - every quadruple is equivalent to one of 180 representatives, through 2517 candidates;
   - every 5-subset is equivalent to one of the 1614 P107 units, through 18782 candidates;
   - every 6-subset is equivalent to one of the 19295 P106 units, through 156627 candidates of the form
     P107 unit plus one line.

   The step from one level to the next is the candidate rule of the P108 package: a k-subset contains a
   (k − 1)-subset equivalent to a representative. The frame-contained deletions are tested directly, so
   PR1419's extension lemma is not needed.
2. **Units.** For each unit, `UNITS.json` lists a pivot that is a live frame and passes (D), (G), (W) and
   nonempty blocks. Every check is exact:
   - Ranks are computed modulo the prime 1000003, which gives lower bounds over Q.
   - The matching upper bound comes from the seven kernel vectors. They lie in every kernel by PR1560's identity
     (6), which `verify.py` checks once for all 2025 frames as Σ v vᵀ = 8 I.
   - (D) and (G) use the family of all live frames.

That the units lie in distinct orbits comes from `make_units.py`'s census, whose orbit sizes sum to exactly
C(112,5) and C(112,6). The argument does not need it, because a redundant unit would only add work. ∎

This route uses none of PR1212's Lemmas 5–6, PR1399's Lemma 9, PR1419's Lemma 10, or any DRUP certificate.

## Results and controls

| | units | first live frame passes | most failed frames before a pass | distinct pivots |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| P107 | 1614 | 1567 | 4 | 41 |
| P106 | 19295 | 18596 | 4 | 66 |

Every frame tried before a pivot failed (W) or nonempty blocks: 66 at P107 and 1031 at P106. **Every frame that
passed those two conditions also passed (G) and (D).** The criterion checks them in that order, so the frames that
failed early were never tested for (G) or (D). The verifier records the first-frame failures on the P107 units as
a control.

**PR1560's open question.** Its coverage-failure deletion (3, 13, 14, 22, 23):

- fails (W) at its pivot, as PR1560 reports;
- passes at (2, 5, 15, 16, 30, 41, 109, 116), the first frame that passes in frame order;
- passes at 820 of its 850 live frames, in the scratch search.

PR1560's fixture also passes. Both appear as `control.pr1560`.

**(G) and (D) are not vacuous.** On PR1560's fixture, two frame families each fail (G): the pivot alone, which
is PR1560's control, and the first 250 live frames. Each also fails (D) when (D) is checked by itself
(`control.rank-and-graph`). With all live frames, both pass.

In scratch tests on the fixture, (D) always held whenever (G) held, even for frame families as small as 73
frames. The converse is false: the first 290 live frames pass (D) but fail (G).

## Reproduce

From the repository root, Python 3.10+, standard library only:

```sh
python3 -I -B research/complex-rank8-rigidity-p106/verify.py --level 5
python3 -I -B research/complex-rank8-rigidity-p106/verify.py --level 6
```

- `--level 5` checks the model, generators, triple orbits, all three coverage levels, the controls and the P107
  units. It takes 36 s.
- `--level 6` checks the P106 units in 59 s. The registered replay splits it with `--part 1/2` and `--part 2/2`.

Times are wall-clock on a 10-core Apple-silicon machine, using 76 and 558 CPU-seconds. Units are checked in
parallel, and `--jobs` defaults to the CPU count. Expected final lines:

```text
PASS_COMPLEX_RANK8_RIGIDITY_P106: checks 11; levels 5: every 5-subset and 6-subset of P112 is equivalent to a unit; every unit has a pivot satisfying PR1560 (D), (G), (W) and nonempty blocks
PASS_COMPLEX_RANK8_RIGIDITY_P106: checks 3; levels 6 (P106 part 1/1): every unit has a pivot satisfying PR1560 (D), (G), (W) and nonempty blocks
```

To regenerate `UNITS.json` and `COVER.json.gz`, run the command below. It takes about 4 minutes with 10 processes
and needs numpy. numpy only proposes the grouping and the pivots, using float ranks; `verify.py` checks every
word and pivot exactly.

```sh
python3 -I -B research/complex-rank8-rigidity-p106/make_units.py --out-dir /some/new/dir
```

Line IDs are positions in the sorted list of canonical doubled roots, the same as PR1560's IDs.

## Not covered

- **PR1560's Theorem 1 and Corollary 2** are cited, not re-derived. I read the written proof and found no gap:
  - Section 4: (7), (8), the 4+1 dichotomy, and that w_j = ±1/2 contradicts the common quadratic;
  - the gauge reconstruction of Corollary 2.

  That reading is not an independent human review. The PR is open.
- **Other inherited steps:** PR1212's (E), §8 and §10 transport, and PR968's premise (F6) through the floor.
- **P105 and deeper, and nine or more costly terms.** P105 has 242802 orbits, and their sizes sum to
  C(112,7).
  - `search_p105.py`, an unregistered numpy search with float ranks, found a passing pivot for every orbit.
    The first live frame passed for 230560 of them.
  - That search is evidence, not a proof. At about 25 ms per unit, a stdlib replay would exceed the registered
    1200-second budget.

## Open next questions

1. **P105 (floor 57).**
   - *Why it matters.* It is the bottom of PR968's p ≤ 15 range.
   - *First step.* Done as a search: `search_p105.py` finds a pivot for all 242802 orbits, using float ranks.
     Next, make an exact replay affordable, for example by giving each (D) check only the rows it needs.
   - *Blocker.* Replay cost: about 2.4 × 10⁵ units, plus about 1.65 million coverage words. It needs either a
     dedicated workflow or a structural reason why (D) and (G) hold.
2. **Do (D) and (G) hold for every pivot whenever enough frames survive? Does (G) imply (D)?**
   - *Why it matters.* Across about 2 × 10⁴ unit searches, no frame passed (W) and nonempty blocks and then
     failed them. A lemma would leave only (W) and
     nonempty blocks, which are counting conditions on X, and would remove the per-unit rank computations.
   - *First step.* Find the smallest live sets where (D) or (G) fails. Then test whether (G) implies (D) for
     the incidence matrices; it held in every scratch test.
   - *Blocker.* No candidate lemma yet.
