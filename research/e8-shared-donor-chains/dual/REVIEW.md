# Independent review of overlapping target brackets

The exact chronological target-word check is an appropriate replacement for the
old disjoint-pivot/target sufficient heuristic. An overlapping Y chain is legal
when all of its literal gates use a common actual chart for their source and
target, its chronological rational Y-only product is identity, and the complete
changed raw dirty operator and complete normalized endpoint are independently
verified. Pairwise coefficient sums alone would be insufficient.

## Symbolic endpoint and cleanup

Reviewed overlap-local-admit.py computes its old response from the frozen helper
baseline and the actual entrance-only prefix. It checks all1021 columns of the
complete candidate raw operator against that baseline and separately requires the
same non-Y-target helper cleanup subword. Every Y-read is restricted to a Y-target;
there is no forward Y-to-helper or Y-to-X operation. Cleanup intentionally omits
all original Y-target operations, including the new target-target bracket.

The full old-response/main/cleanup schedules are replayed in both orientations,
first with ordinary dirty initial columns and then with formal helper entrance
operator tokens. These tokens are keyed by both helper identity and frame, so
helpers sharing a chart still have independent initial columns. The schedule
must restore every helper's own token, leave no helper/data off-diagonal endpoint
term, produce exactly the prescribed X/Y addition, and erase all private work.
This is the necessary complete endpoint check; neither Y-only identity nor a
clean-input replay alone would establish it.

Scalar gates are used at common exact frame charts. The emitted frame children
and adapters telescope the normalized scalar program into the physical charts;
no basis adapter is commuted through internal gates. The inverse-adjoint rule is
multiplicative and fixes unitary chart adapters. Consequently the same literal
chart walk and chronological source/target-swapped negative scalar operations
are justified in the backward convention. Existing final frames may differ by
role without invalidating the common-frame internal gate checks.
