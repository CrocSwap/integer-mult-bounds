# Research record and unpriced routes

The priced witness is the 119-gauge response-subspace construction only. Discovery uses native C++ finite searches and the native HiGHS integer solver; floating scores nominate candidates. Exact rational reconstruction, integer inventory checks, Gaussian phases, template readback and strict moment inequalities determine acceptance.

## Productive representation change

Search first screened simultaneous 3-by-3 target bases and multi-helper readout, with no charged gain. It then searched response relations inside first-helper frame intersections rather than only existing registered frames. The finite pool had 555 distinct first frames, 4,341 deduplicated pair intersections/lines and 4,202 nontrivial groups. Across 672,320 weighted-basis trials, 224 groups passed an early positive nomination gate. Disjoint whole-role selection (1,400 bounded greedy restarts) first chose 18 groups/46 gauges. A residual screen with 313,600 trials and 600 further bounded restarts added eight groups/73 gauges. Exact rational reconstruction then produced the frozen 26-space/119-pivot witness. None of those spaces was in the starting frame table.

The useful synthesis crosses linear response kernels, matroid basis selection, phase-sensitive Clifford charts and heterogeneous integer packing. Even/degenerate spaces require two-sided residual normalization; pretending they have the earlier odd-vector orthogonal complement would be invalid. The exact sufficient identity is in frames/PROOF.md.

## Bounded screens with no additional priced gain

- 280,840 simultaneous target triples, including 139,055 legal unit-determinant signed bases: no gain beyond the inherited 25 signed prefixes.
- 529 destination cuts, 27,958 target/frame gates, 2,697 weighted bases and 60 multi-helper representations: no paid readout improvement.
- 1,833 intervals, a 44,415-frame join pool, 345,096 target-only gates and 3,510 weighted bases: no detour-inclusive gain.
- Dead-helper exploration checked 20 sinks and 80 basis variants; only the existing three free sinks were positive. A shared ledger screened 41 nominees/2,789 subsets/134 legal subsets without a new gain. Joint survivor tests used 1,128 bases and 16 zeroable representations without improvement.
- Removing 35 empty retained visits did not change the paid profile.
- Homogeneous odd-stage schedules from 3 through 61 retained nine stages as best in that family. Overlapping nine rank-nine children in width 73 failed after full deficit accounting.

These are bounded negative search results, not impossibility or optimality theorems. Auxiliary target/helper prewhitening is a sufficient algebraic identity, included separately, but no extra priced saving is attributed to it.
