# Verify grade-14 weighted routes on complete cover fields

## Why this review is needed

PR #7837 claims removal of `native_all_columns` for the grade-14 application.
Its binary argument combines different role routes, low/high carries,
mixed-role native programs, ancestor weights and complete inverse returns.
The author's width-one mixed-cut test and separate high-route probes leave
their joint composition as a distinct verification obligation.

This PR reviews that seam at exact head
`a7f99a5f5f919dd3ffb2987f90c1e7d6527feb1e`. It does not accept the complete
four-premise multiplication theorem or alter the selected five-assumption
record.

## What this adds

- A reusable complete-cover matching lemma with the full carry cocycle and
  inverse for every width, including the singleton high domain at width one.
- The exact row-local endpoint criterion: a nonzero off-diagonal block forces
  equal constant right actions on its coupled roles, unless the target
  explicitly includes the displaced donor row. The actual X/Y pair shares
  its matcher; independent productive targets can use different matchers.
- A common-weight correctness refinement: route invariance suffices.
  Ancestor-only weights are one sufficient case. A nonconstant determinant
  weight is checked for the determinant-one test routes; det(g)^2 is invariant
  under actual port maps of determinant -1, but not arbitrary later matchers.
  This supplies no new descriptor, time or full-runtime theorem.
- Independent exact rational product checks of all 111 actual port matchers
  at q=3 and q=5, including their determinant and unit-coefficient conditions.
- A joined independent test on complete GL_2(Z/3) and GL_2(Z/9), plus a stated
  Z/27 subset, with 109,536 source-contract weighted column cases and another
  109,536 nonconstant invariant-weight cases at multiplicities 1, 2 and 4.
- Six failing controls for the two carries, inverse return, composition
  order, non-invariant fresh weights and incompatible routes on a coupled pair.

Only the source row factorizer is imported, as the operation being audited.
The joint check imports no author route evaluator, projector helper or leaf
implementation. The small closure preserves exact source pins and notices.

## Result and limits

No blocking flaw was found in this binary composition seam. The proof makes
the shared pair routes, weight invariance and complete fresh-cylinder return
explicit. Arbitrary child inputs alone would not justify arbitrary routes
around a coupled endpoint.

Allocation, erasure, suspension, layout and physical tariffs remain premises.
The package does not rerun the whole native incidence certificate, materialize
the compiled production word, build a literal dispatcher, audit the K-shear,
or accept the complex and analytic application. A selected #7000 corollary
needs a separate source-bound specialization. The reviewed #7837 source
theorem remains unchanged.
