# An independent audit of the five-call center pilot's cover obstruction

**Corroboration of [discussion 1636](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/discussions/1636), no new κ.** The discussion gives an exact five-call local shear identity and then reports that inherited fresh-window cover routing prevents it from being a supplier. This package independently proves the route's same-port condition from repository sources, reconstructs the 112 sign-line Gram identity and checks the stated rational matrices. Its five-call conclusion has two inputs that are not repository sources. The coefficients are pinned verbatim from [comment 18915216](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/discussions/1636#discussioncomment-18915216) in `DISCUSSION-1636-PIN.json`, with that comment's body hash; there is no candidate package or head. It also assumes the no-return routing premise stated below, which this package does not derive for the five-call word. Only the port frames, the route census and the sign-line geometry are source-bound.

## The inherited route condition

The retained [complex geometry proof](../split-centres-global/proofs/INHERITED-COMPLEX-GEOMETRY.txt) defines a local 9-space and a fresh 8-space `E_s`. Every port `q` has odd weight and a zero coordinate. Its orthogonal `U_q` maps `e_0` to `q`, and columns 1–8 span `q^perp`. The involution `rho_(s,q)` fixes `q`, exchanges `q^perp` with `E_s`, and fixes the other fresh spaces.

Consider a port transition `rho_(s,p) rho_(s,q)`. The first image of `E_s` is `q^perp`. The second map sends a local vector entirely back into `E_s` precisely when it lies in `p^perp`. Thus the product preserves `E_s` iff `q^perp` is contained in `p^perp`. Both are 8-dimensional hyperplanes, so they are equal; their nonzero normal lines over F₂ are equal, hence `p=q`. Conversely `p=q` gives the identity. Other stage factors fix `E_s` pointwise and preserve its complementary local-plus-other-stage space. They therefore cannot erase a nonzero component outside `E_s`. A product with one transition at each fresh stage can return to the original cover class only when every stage transition has the same port at both ends. This last statement depends on the one-transition-per-fresh-stage form implicit in discussion 1636's argument; it is not asserted for routing words that revisit a stage.

The selected source manifest pins the archived 112-port `DATA-PHASES.json`. Our checker independently implements the swap-plus-even-transvection construction of `U_q`, proves its 9×9 orthogonality for each port, constructs `rho` on a 41-bit space with four fresh 8-spaces, checks every `rho` on all 41 basis vectors is involutive, and tests all `4·112² = 50,176` ordered stage/port pairs. Exactly 448 pairs (same port at each of four stages) preserve the fresh space; 49,728 do not. For example stage 1, `(p,q)=(11,19)` sends fresh basis vector 0 to a vector with nonzero local image 11.

## The five-call scalar failure

The retained [Hamming packet proof](../split-centres-global/proofs/PR425-COMPLEX.txt) describes 112 sign lines on 14 weight-four Hamming blocks. Independently construct each block as an affine plane in F₂³ and take its eight sign choices modulo overall sign. Each integer row has four ±1 entries, so its normalized row `r/2` has squared norm one. The exact 8×8 sum of outer products is `R^T R=14I`: each coordinate appears in seven blocks, and mixed signs cancel within each block. Hence `M=I−RR^T` has eigenvalues 1 and −13, and every diagonal entry `M_pp=0`. The checker reconstructs all 112 rows and tests every Gram entry. This depends on the stated Hamming sign-line geometry, not a byte-level recheck of PR370's historical table order.

For this `M` and `K = I − M`, the discussion's chronological word is

`L(M/12), U(−12M/13), L((143M+131K)/144), U(−12M/13), L(M/12)`.

With `L(t)=[[1,0],[t,1]]` and `U(t)=[[1,t],[0,1]]`, direct exact multiplication gives the desired exchange `[[0,−1],[1,0]]` at both eigenvalues. Discussion 1636's obstruction then takes the same-cover/same-port block to be the product of each pulse's own same-port block. That holds when a path that leaves the starting cover class never returns to it before the word ends. **Under that no-return premise**, `M_pp=0` gives `L(131/144)=[[1,0],[131/144,1]]` for the block, which is not the exchange. The premise is not automatic. Discussion 1636 does not say how its five calls map to the four fresh stages. If two transitions use the same stage, an excursion `p→q→p` multiplies the cover class by `rho_(s,q)rho_(s,p)·rho_(s,p)rho_(s,q) = 1` and returns. Such terms, like `(M²)_pp`, then enter the block (see the return-path fixture below). This package therefore corroborates the five-call failure **conditionally**: it holds for one transition per fresh stage or any other routing with multiplicative same-cover projection. Even then it is a failure of that candidate under the inherited routing, not a barrier for a different global geometry, a paid intertwiner, or another word.

There is a slightly broader necessary condition for a **single-pass fresh-stage word** in this same two-stream shear model, provided each port excursion has no route back to the same cover class before the word ends. Under that route condition, the same-cover projection is multiplicative: every `L(cM+dK)` or `U(cM+dK)` contributes `L(d)` or `U(d)`. A pulse with `d=0` contributes the identity; call `d≠0` center-bearing. Zero or one such pulse cannot be the exchange. With two pulses of the same orientation both diagonal entries stay 1; with opposite orientations one diagonal entry stays 1. The exchange has both diagonal entries 0. Thus at least **three center-bearing shears** are necessary, whatever the number of center-free `M` pulses. The bound is sharp for this projected 2×2 problem: chronological `L(1), U(−1), L(1)` gives the exchange. This is the classical fact that a quarter-turn needs three elementary shears; what is new here is only its use as a count of center-bearing pulses under the projection premise. The checker verifies the two-pulse statement symbolically over integer polynomials in two independent coefficients and the three-pulse rational witness. This counts pulses, not recursive rank mass: a proposed supplier must still price a physical realization.

The no-return condition matters. A two-port algebraic control takes `M=[[0,1],[1,0]]`. Although `M_00=0`, the same-port upper-left entry of `U(M)L(M)` is `1+(M²)_00=2`; multiplying the individual same-port projections would give 1. A route that revisits a fresh stage can admit such a return path, so the pulse floor does not apply until its cover-class projection is separately proved multiplicative. This control illustrates the limit; it is not a candidate route or an example with the selected Hamming `M`. Multi-pair gates and changed global routes likewise require a separate argument.

## Reproduce and limits

```sh
python3.12 -I -B research/centre-fivecall-cover-audit/verify.py
python3.12 scripts/research_package.py --package research/centre-fivecall-cover-audit
```

`verify.py` checks source hashes and the selected route manifest, parses the pinned discussion word and requires it to equal the checked coefficients, 112 distinct admissible port labels, orthogonal `U_q`, four-stage involutions, all 50,176 products, the 112-row normalized Gram matrix, the rational shear word, the symbolic two-pulse obstruction, the return-path projection counterexample and `RESULT.json`. Five mutation controls reject a duplicate port, a corrupt `U_q` column, a changed middle coefficient, an off-spectrum substitution and a tampered discussion pin. The checker is standard-library only and does not import the historical route implementation. Its five-call result is conditional on the pinned discussion coefficients (provisional text, body hashes as retrieved 2026-10-14 05:47 EDT), the no-return/multiplicative-projection premise and the inherited route formula. It does not replay 704 local dirty columns, exact complex phases, helper hosting, coefficient precision or any supplier invoice. The selected frontier is unchanged.

## Open next questions

1. **Can a different global geometry implement this local identity?** A first step is an explicit stage route or paid intertwiner with exact cover-class and same-port blocks, followed by a full controller and invoice. The inherited `rho` route fails the necessary block above.
2. **Does the five-call word satisfy the no-return premise?** The `L(131/144)` block needs every cover-leaving path to stay away until the word ends. A first step is the explicit assignment of the five calls to fresh stages, then an exact routed computation of the same-cover block on the 112 ports, including all two-hop returns. The blocker is that discussion 1636 states neither the assignment nor a candidate package.
3. **Can the local pilot be bound to an on-disk candidate?** The discussion gives the rational word and reports a 704-column local replay, but no package or head is public. This package pins the discussion text only. A first step is to pin such a package and recheck its coefficients and port routing before treating this audit as a candidate-specific review.
