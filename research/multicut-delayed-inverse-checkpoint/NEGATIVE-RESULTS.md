# Research findings and scoped negative results

These results distinguish exact incompatibility of fixed witnesses from a paid-cost exclusion at a specified rate. They do not establish global optimality. The standalone verifier checks the selected construction; the earlier screens below concern declared alternative families.

The alternative-family screens and exploratory controls summarized here are
reports of separate bounded research checks. Their test programs and execution
reports are not bundled in this minimal construction package, and verify.py
does not replay them. The displayed case counts and measured operation
inventories report those separate checks. In contrast, the selected hybrid's
compatibility, retiming and final paid ledger are reconstructed by the bundled
verifier from the current witnesses. These research reports do not add new
transformations to the certified construction.

## 1. Fixed-star inverse at the high compensation frame

**Baseline:** PR249 at `96495746c786d6d0339dbb38c7f553d4af3f88ed`, raw word SHA256 `2e0bc2a5c54cf8caf26fa30a1a2949702d842f620b28023ca2ba59140ecbed1a`.

**Rate:** `a = 44431075156777 / 62500000000000000`, with paid weights `r(120/r)^a` and the complete `32·120²/10¹⁶` rank-one fallback allowance per positive child.

**Family:** the 128 declared two-helper closures with equal transported signed response columns; one fixed invertible target star-basis preconditioner; each helper compensated exactly once at a positive entrance frame; nested compensation frames L ⊆ H; literal target inverse executed at H. Producers, read successors and external observations are unchanged. Every allowed integer rank, helper order and target pivot is screened, including optimistic rank choices without a demonstrated nondegenerate realizing subspace.

Of the 128 helper pairs, 121 have zero common allowed entrance dimension. For the other seven, all 2,940 rank/order/pivot cases have strictly positive paid residual under optimistic fractional bank volume. The minimum outward-rational lower bound exceeds 0.00963624. Even after including the baseline's available moment slack, the minimum full-recurrence residual numerator exceeds 0.04818120. Those exact signs exclude improving the certified rate within this family. Extra nonnegative packing, routing or chart charges cannot repair the rejection.

The exclusion requires paying the target paths: omitting their tolls makes all 2,940 cases look favorable. It does not exclude a later inverse at a common K containing H. The selected rank-20 K changes the family by letting satellite targets avoid the intermediate H visit. This is why the delayed-inverse construction can improve the price.

## 2. Direct union of the seven intervals with all 518 PR259 cohorts

**Pinned witness:** PR259 at `a53ec0ca4d59634f16630dd16adf035c9f12595c`, candidate gzip SHA256 `5f6863ad27eb9f2fcb14db10320c00607451c01e12da303def13e48eda7a5d2d`.

Two low helpers are quartet pivots with nonzero entrance frames, and five are written donors. The unchanged intervals require those helpers to remain zero-initial and untouched through their cut. Keeping both fixed witnesses violates that entry contract, so the independent union is inadmissible.

The selected construction removes all five quartets, checks the exact signed helper responses and their zero columns on every retained cohort operand at all four cuts, then inserts the seven intervals. Removing five quartets loses 55 entrance-rank units; the seven intervals add 133, for net 78. Normalization is applied after composition: the final new entrance rank is 1422, divisible by three. Normalizing the partially removed witness first would accidentally discard two additional rank-one cohorts.

All sixteen entrances that PR251 adds beyond PR249 also overlap the pinned PR259 selection, across ten cohort blocks. They are excluded here. This is a conflict between unchanged witnesses, not an impossibility proof for redesigned entrances or a reoptimized cohort selection.

The all-seven/five-removal choice has the best certified price in that declared ideal-stock model. At its certified coarse rate every other subset has moment greater than one.

## 5. Persistent transformed blocks: real reuse, unsuitable immediate insertion

**Evidence status:** exact finite control and source-specific interface screen; no full-supplier replacement or total-runtime benchmark.

The control receives two growing radix-four digit streams, retains each completed two-digit block as a length-four transform over Z/97, and treats the open boundary separately. It checks every required coefficient observation, exact final product, carries, ordinary output order, input preservation and owned-storage cleanup. All 1,585 cases pass: 1,364 exhaustive binary-string pairs of lengths 1–5, 204 deterministic radix-four cases, and 17 maximal-digit carry cases. Contamination, omitted product, missing carry, premature observation and missing cleanup are rejected.

Both comparators retain the two newest transforms within each batch. At n=8, persistent retention reduces forward transforms from 20 to 8; the charged seven-tape data-access subtotal rises from 2,520 to 4,192 bit steps. The exact difference is 1,672 data-access steps minus 96 residue additions and 12 constant multiplications, plus the control-cost difference. Arithmetic primitives and control costs are not declared free or equal. Thus the access subtotal alone proves neither an overall win nor an overall loss. The constant-size blocked schedule also has quadratic block-product count; it is not the published multilevel relaxed-multiplication algorithm.

The inspected PR249 five-stage schedule has successive source values x, x+y, y+z, y+z+w and y+w on four independent bank inputs. Repeated register names do not mean equal operands. Its stage routes occupy different full address windows, and independent dirty helper contents prevent silently adding cached representations without compensation and restoration. The one-shot outer multiplication already transforms each operand once.

**Future use:** a caller that repeatedly consumes unchanged completed blocks, such as a growing-precision or power-series calculation, with a paid multilevel schedule, exact observation boundaries, physical access and cleanup. No such unchanged-operand repetition was found in the inspected supplier contract. Linear relations among stages might still support a different fully paid representation; this screen does not prove algebraic reuse impossible.

## 7. Lift and contraction: useful identities, no free bilinear work

**Evidence status:** algebraic statements under an explicit model and an exhaustive finite coefficient test; physical integration and an improved complete cost bound are absent.

For radix digits a and b, the lifted matrix has entries T_ij=a_i b_j and coefficient observation C(T)_k=Σ_(i+j=k)T_ij. Dense storage can be avoided by immediate accumulation or factored bilinear algorithms. Calling T rank one does not remove the products, transform work or coefficient widths.

For four radix-four digits, all 65,536 operand pairs give identical coefficients under dense contraction, fused accumulation and recursive Karatsuba, and the coefficients evaluate to the exact integer product. The dense method uses 16 scalar products and nine accumulating additions; the factored one uses nine products and 24 additions/subtractions, with wider intermediate values. These are operation/width comparisons, not a tape-runtime ranking.

For a fixed projection P of lifted state, preserving every coefficient map requires CP=C, since elementary outer products span the lifted matrix space. Period-four exponent folding violates this condition on six lifted basis columns and corrupts 62,208 of the 65,536 tested products. Carrying the aliased coefficients cannot reconstruct the lost exponents. Guarded packing recovers the coefficients, but its tested operands grow from eight to twenty bits, so it is not a reduction to a smaller integer multiplication.

At the separate linear supplier boundary, the full arbitrary-input endpoint S is invertible. If a fixed full-state projection must preserve that endpoint and there is no other retained recovery information, SP=S implies P=I. This excludes permanent rank loss under that unchanged contract. Temporary invertible basis changes, dependent copies and revised observation/retirement boundaries remain possible. A nonlinear lift also cannot inherit the linear formal-column proof: xy over F2 vanishes on both input basis vectors but not on their sum.

**Future use:** an explicitly changed coefficient/observation boundary with a paid bilinear factorization and exact recovery. The bill must include transforms, products, widths, physical routes, carries, cleanup and any retained recovery information. No admissible lower-cost replacement of the present supplier has been established.

## 8. A 79-factor address router: a local improvement without exponent gain

**Evidence status:** independently checked exact semantic helper; a proposed finite-tariff specialization is conditional and unadmitted.

The canonical 48-coordinate rational address router has an explicit 79-factor realization: 56 additions and 23 swaps, compared with the pinned Gaussian program's 195 additions, 44 swaps and 27 scalings. It uses no clean extra coordinate. All rational columns, inverse factors and the actual right-multiplication orientation are checked. Denominators introduce no prime exclusions beyond two and three.

The source-bound PR249 adapter preserves the actual one-block permutations and stage windows. Checks cover all 1,760 port adapters, 100,540 owner/address sites, exact rational port cases, local-ring low/high carry semantics, inverse return and unchanged spectators. Missing adapters, carries and reversed factor order are rejected. This establishes a reusable semantic implementation and a smaller operation inventory, without assigning equal machine costs to unlike primitives.

The current finite checker charges the generic high-address envelope `16(m²+1)²` per route. The 266-factor reconstruction is a diagnostic, not that coefficient's multiplier. Replacing it by 79 therefore leaves the emitted finite coefficient and recursive moment unchanged. A proposed dimension-two sparse-affine tariff still needs a compiler proof that includes nonadjacent relocation, descriptors, low-class selection and tape movement.

Even granting that additional tariff on the pinned PR249 profile, its conditional coefficient screen retains the same 56-bit envelope. The term `36/δ²` dominates all three displayed cutoff levels, so those cutoffs remain equal. The full inherited primitive constants are still required. No smaller all-size threshold, runtime percentage or κ improvement follows.

**Future use:** a justified tariff-parametric sparse compiler or a practical implementation study under an explicitly chosen cost model. A local address-factor improvement should not be credited as a recursive-child saving or attributed the entire hypothetical generic-to-sparse reduction.
