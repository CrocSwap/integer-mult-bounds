Independently reported exponent improvements are never added together. Attribution does not imply review or endorsement of this submission.

PR links identify contributions; immutable commits and the packaged source manifests identify the versions actually consumed. A live PR head can advance beyond an imported snapshot. Evidence paths below are relative to this public package.
