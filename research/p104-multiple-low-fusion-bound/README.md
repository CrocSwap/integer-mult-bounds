# Multiple low peers in a fixed compensated fusion

**NO VERIFIED GAIN. Conditional κ change 0%.** On exact
[#7334](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/7334),
the frozen compensated-fusion geometry contains eight sites with
more than one low-chart donor. Seven have no valid dirty readout
even with arbitrary rational low coefficients. The remaining site,
helper 1182, fails the full low-donor exterior span. Its exact
donor and augmented ranks are two and three. Both rational checks
agree on every site. Zero cases remain unresolved and the compatible
paid saving upper is zero.

The edit would fuse one paid public rank pair, retain the existing
closed retime, all other paid itineraries and productive targets,
and allow the full existing low bank. Every site has a singleton
eligible high peer. The exact one-edit optimistic price upper is
about 0.118078219358088 raw before support. See [PROOF.md](PROOF.md).

## Open next questions

A changed legal cut, larger closed block or different ownership
contract could expose a different donor bank. First pin one such
family and bound its full price, including added routing and setup.
Any surviving exact response must still pass signed arbitrary-input
forward and adjoint action, dirty restoration, compiler, precision,
tape, caller and outer checks. One added rank-one paid position on
the complete signed invoice erases the tested one-edit price gain.
