Three active targets with one returned register need nine calls
===============================================================

Theorem and model
-----------------
Work over F_2[G], where G is the additive group of rational 3 by 3
matrices. Let [A] denote its group element. A paid factor is

  D_N([A]) = I + ([A]-1)N,
  N = xy^T,  x,y in F_2^4,  y^T x = 1.

Each paid frame A is nonzero and has rational matrix rank one. For
invertible lower-triangular rational C_1,C_2,C_3, an identity

  product_j D_(N_j)([A_j]) = diag([C_1],[C_2],[C_3],1)

requires at least nine paid factors. Nine suffice. The fourth input
is arbitrary and must be returned exactly. This is a formal identity
in all address variables, not a check on selected input values.

The finite part of this proof has two exact implementations. The
status of the independent proof audit is stated in AUDIT.txt. This
result does not give a positive bound improvement for integer
multiplication. It excludes one proposed small circuit.

1. Reduction to eight calls and relation rank at most three
---------------------------------------------------------
A factor can be split into two: D_N([A/2])^2 = D_N([A]). Thus every
nonempty word with fewer than eight calls can be padded to eight,
with nonzero rank-one frames. Zero calls cannot give an active target.

Use the span-four lemma of the three-register lower-bound proof:
https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/468
The source used here is research/bit-three-register-lower-bound/README.md
at commit 07a78bf335b0b5039a1fecd7cd74a0fe1d1605ea in the author's fork.
Its proof applies to any number of scalar registers and extends to
zero target frames: replace every target-minus-identity rank W by
the number of active targets. Choose an active target for the common
ratio flag argument. Zero targets remain zero under each projection.
The result is that rational paid-frame span at most four needs at
least three calls per active invertible lower-triangular target.

For clarity, the span-four proof has two cases. Three independent
rank-one basis frames can be mapped to diagonal coordinate matrices.
The fourth is uv^T. If u and v have full support, every rank-one frame
is on one of four basis lines. Each invertible target needs at least
three lines, and line projection gives one call per active coefficient.
Otherwise the frame space has upper-triangular diagonal blocks of size
at most two. Rank-one calls have at most one nonzero diagonal block.
The target ratio flag descends to each block. A one-dimensional block
needs W active calls. For a two-dimensional block, project its upper
entry to zero. If U calls survive, the compact product formula gives
r_X+r_Y <= U. The two diagonal projections give W <= r_X+l_i and
W <= r_Y+l_i for i=0,1. Hence 2W <= U+l_0+l_1 <= E_2. The block bounds
sum to 3W. This argument uses the lower-triangular target hypothesis.

An eight-call solution therefore has frame span at least five. Its
rational relation space in Q^8 has dimension at most three.

2. Scalar irreducibility and distance
------------------------------------
One active target needs at least three calls, even with returned
registers: its frame is in the span of the paid rank-one frames.
Two active targets need at least six. At five calls, frame span at
most four is covered above. At span five the frames are independent;
all subset monomials are distinct. Vanishing off-diagonal coefficients
and differentiation at one make every scalar projector diagonal.
The calls then divide between the targets, whose ranks sum to six.

Suppose the projectors preserve a proper binary subspace. The diagonal
target preserves it as well. Its distinct monomial coefficients are
coordinate projections onto groups with equal target frames, including
the zero frame. These projections preserve the subspace. Choose bases
inside each group adapted to that subspace. The target stays diagonal.
Each rank-one idempotent acts nontrivially on exactly one diagonal
block. If both blocks have active targets, their counts are one and
two, so their disjoint call counts sum to at least nine. If all three
active targets are in one block, it has dimension three; PR468 applies
and again requires nine. Thus an eight-call solution is irreducible.

For an allowed returned coordinate a, every nonzero binary row r other
than e_a must see at least three image vectors x_j, and at least three
row vectors y_j. Otherwise choose a rational functional that kills the
at most two frames seen by r and does not kill an active target in
its support. The projected circuit fixes r but the target does not.

3. Finite scalar catalogue
-------------------------
Let L be the saturated kernel of Z^8 -> <A_1,...,A_8>. Its reduction
modulo two has dimension at most three. Augmentation degree one shows
that all off-diagonal coefficient rows ((N_j)_(u,v))_j lie in this
space. The returned diagonal row lies there too. Thus the off-diagonal
patterns of the used projectors span a binary space of dimension at
most three, with the corresponding augmented condition for coordinate a.

List all 120 binary rank-one idempotents. They have 104 distinct nonzero
off-diagonal patterns. Generate every pattern space of dimension one,
two, or three. For each space include all projectors with that pattern,
including the four coordinate projectors. An irreducible pool must have
a strongly connected union graph. This is also sufficient for the full
pool, since its coordinate projectors force invariant subspaces to be
coordinate subspaces. Quotient only by coordinate permutations.

Dimension       1         2          3
All spaces    104     4,964    140,281
Full irreducible pools
                0       634     63,567
Permutation classes
                0        45      2,846

Dimension zero is diagonal and reducible. List every eight-call multiset
in the remaining pools. Require actual pattern rank, the distance and
augmented conditions above, and irreducibility of the used support.
This leaves 103 rank-two families and 4,020 rank-three families.

List every order modulo swaps of commuting factors and word reversal.
Reversal is valid by inversion and negation of all frames. Rank two
gives 27,156 canonical words, all checked by both exact engines. Rank
three gives 3,560,923 words. Here the rank-three derivative space equals
L modulo two. In F_2[(F_2)^8/(L mod 2)] the product must still be diagonal
with one monomial per entry and identity in a permitted returned entry.
Exactly 1,901,600 words pass this necessary check.

4. Complete exact relation search
---------------------------------
Expand each word into at most 256 subset monomials. At a partial
rational relation space R, collect equal monomials in Q^8/R. A nonzero
off-diagonal term must cancel with some other term in the same entry.
Branch over all such partners, adding their original subset-difference
row. Each step raises relation rank. Stop at rank three.

Reject zero paid frames. At a diagonal terminal each entry is a single
monomial: the determinant is a monomial, so each diagonal factor is a
unit in a Laurent polynomial ring. For each allowed returned role, add
its original target subset indicator as a zero relation. Reject if this
needs rank four or kills a paid frame. Also reject if an active target
lies in R plus the span of any two coordinate unit vectors. Its matrix
would then lie in the span of two rank-one frames and have rank at most
two. This obstruction persists under every further relation.

The primitive-integer engine performs exact fraction-free row operations.
The second engine uses F_101. Every state has at most three original
rows with entries in {-1,0,1}. Its RREF entries have numerator and
denominator of absolute value at most five, by minors and Cramer's rule.
Rational reconstruction is unique modulo 101 since a cross difference
is at most 50. Every equality or rank decision uses at most four such
nonunit rows, plus coordinate rows. Expansion along the coordinate
rows and Hadamard's inequality bound each relevant minor by 16. Hence
no required rational rank changes modulo 101. The same bound separates
unequal cached row spaces. Reconstructed primitive-row parities belong
to L modulo two and give a further safe first-order check.

Both engines check every retained word. Rank two has no diagonal
terminal. Rank three has 538, with exactly matching word IDs and one
terminal each. Every terminal is rejected. No physical eight-call
solution remains. The recorded counts and input hashes are checked by
generate.py, and receipt.json records the full independent replay.

5. Upper bound and boundary
---------------------------
For each C_w, use its three rank-one column matrices as paid frames
with scalar projector e_w e_w^T. Their sum is C_w, so three calls give
[C_w] on register w. Nine calls give the target and fix the fourth
register. The lower bound is therefore sharp.

The theorem does not cover clean initialized workspace, more than one
extra scalar register, noncommuting address operations, or a weaker
output contract. It does not rule out a larger-batch improvement.
