Independent audit of the eleven-call H/I/H exclusion

Scope

There are four scalar registers over F2. Each paid call has a nonzero
rational rank-one 3-by-3 address frame A_j and a binary rank-one
idempotent N_j. The required output is diagonal, with four invertible
lower-triangular rational 3-by-3 target frames. There are eleven calls.

The checked words use three complete binary basis layers H/I/H, where
H=I+J_4, with one of the twelve factors omitted. This audit does not
exclude every eleven-call four-register word. It does not establish a
construction, a new kappa, or a general four-register lower bound.

1. Bound on relation rank

The span-four lemma in PR468 states, for any number W of scalar
registers, that address-frame span at most four requires at least 3W
calls for invertible lower-triangular targets in address dimension three.
For W=4 and eleven calls, the frame span therefore has dimension at
least five. The rational kernel of the map

  Q^11 -> Mat_3(Q),   e_j -> A_j

has dimension at most six. The code's MAXREL=6 uses that lemma.
The lower-triangular hypothesis is essential to this reduction.
The audit read that proof in the PR468 worktree at commit 07a78bf.
Its two cases do not use W=3. In the full-support case every paid
frame is on one of four rank-one lines; invertibility requires at least
three nonzero line coefficients per target. In the other case, the
diagonal blocks have sizes at most two. The inherited common rational
flag gives the dimension-two bound 2W; a one-dimensional block needs W.
The assigned calls are disjoint and the block sizes sum to three.
Thus the same written proof gives 3W when W=4.

2. Polynomial reconstruction

Each call is I+(z_j+1)N_j over F2. Since every variable occurs in one
factor, each matrix entry is multilinear. The source forms its exact
polynomial by multiplying a current coefficient matrix on the right by
N_j or I+N_j. The stored matrix uses row-major binary entries.

The independent checker uses a different construction. It evaluates the
eleven-factor dense 4-by-4 product at all 2^11 Boolean points, then uses
Boolean Mobius inversion to recover every polynomial coefficient.
Both omission types agree with the source coefficients, with 36 nonzero
support terms in each case. The two word types thus receive
4096 independently formed dense matrix evaluations.

3. Saturation and the parity prune

Let G be the additive group generated by the actual rational frames.
It is a finitely generated torsion-free abelian group. Let

  K = ker(Z^11 -> G).

Then K is saturated: Z^11/K is free abelian. In particular, reduction
modulo two embeds K/2K into F2^11, with image of dimension rank(K)<=6.
One must use this group G, not an arbitrary denominator-cleared ambient
integer matrix whose reduction modulo two can lose rank.

Apply augmentation degree one to the group-algebra identity. Since the
target is diagonal, for each off-diagonal scalar position (a,b),

  sum_j (N_j)_(a,b) [A_j] = 0 in G/2G.

Thus the binary coefficient vector belongs to the mod-two image of K.
These are exactly the code's twelve first_order vectors.

Every current rational collision relation lies in the actual rational
kernel along a branch that can describe a valid circuit. Clear its
denominators and divide the integer row by its gcd. The resulting
primitive integer row still lies in K. Its parity is also in the same
rank-at-most-six space. If the combined binary rank exceeds six, that
branch cannot contain a valid circuit.

The current RREF rows need not generate the whole saturated lattice.
This causes no soundness problem: their primitive rows are a subset
of K. An incomplete subset can only weaken this prune. The proof does
not identify their integer span with K.

4. Exact quotient and complete branching

Let R be the actual rational frame kernel and L the currently imposed
relation space. Along a compatible branch L is a subspace of R.
Grouping terms by their RREF remainder groups exactly equal images in
Q^11/L. The independent checker confirms this quotient comparison by
integer, fraction-free rank calculations on separate generated rows.

If an off-diagonal entry is still nonzero, choose one surviving support
u in it. In a final valid quotient its contribution must cancel with
another surviving support v from the same scalar entry. Otherwise its
coefficient remains one. Hence u-v belongs to R. The search branches
over every such v. The chosen supports differ modulo L, so this new
relation increases dim(L). Recursion reaches depth at most six.

If a unit coordinate e_j enters L, then A_j=0 in every further quotient.
That branch violates the nonzero paid-frame hypothesis. If dim(L)=6
and an off-diagonal entry remains, no further relation can occur in a
compatible rank-at-most-six kernel. Both stopping rules are sound.

The cached state is canonical rational RREF. Repeated states have the
same future possibilities. Reusing a completed rejection is sound.
There is no cycle: each recursive collision increases relation rank.

5. Diagonal terminals and the rank-two obstruction

For a rank-one idempotent, det(I+(z+1)N)=z. Therefore the determinant
of the product in every torsion-free quotient is a monomial. A
diagonal terminal has diagonal entries whose product is a unit. Each
diagonal entry is consequently a unit in the Laurent polynomial ring
F2[G_L], and each such unit is one monomial. This justifies the code's
assertion that every diagonal has exactly one support.

If target support t belongs to L+span_Q(e_i,e_j), then in every further
physical realization its target frame belongs to span_Q(A_i,A_j).
A sum of two rank-one rational matrices has rank at most two. It cannot
equal an invertible 3-by-3 target. The pair-span rejection is therefore
sound and remains sound after additional relations. Pairs also cover
the zero- or one-frame cases by giving a coefficient value zero.

6. Independent terminal identities

For an omitted outer factor a_1, the surviving terminal relations are

  b_1=b_2=b_3=b_4,
  a_i+c_i=c_1 for i=2,3,4.

All four targets equal b_1+c_1. The checker substitutes these relations
directly into the independently reconstructed polynomial in a free
five-dimensional rational address space. Its four diagonal targets
are exactly that sum of two paid frames.

For an omitted middle factor b_1, the terminal relations are

  a_1=a_2=a_3=a_4,
  c_1=c_2=c_3=c_4.

The first target is a_1+c_1, and the others are a_1+b_i+c_1. The same
direct independent substitution verifies the full matrix identity.
The first target again has rank at most two.

7. Symmetry and limits of coverage

The four projectors in each complete layer are mutually orthogonal,
so their order within that layer does not matter. A simultaneous
permutation of the four scalar coordinates permutes the omitted
coordinate and preserves H=I+J_4. All outer omissions are equivalent
under this symmetry and word reversal/transposition. Middle omissions
are also equivalent. Thus two cases cover one omitted factor from
the twelve-factor H/I/H template.

This argument does not assert that every eleven-call scalar word is
conjugate to this template. It does not silently include templates
with multiple omitted factors or other interleaved orders.

Verification boundary

The exhaustive counts are from the source engine. This audit does not
claim a second exhaustive implementation. It checks the mathematics
of the complete search, reconstructs both full input polynomials by
an independent method, checks quotient/pair-span operations through
independent integer rank calculations, and directly verifies both
terminal identities. See RESULT.json for actual finite check counts.

The source increments stats.nodes before its repeated-state lookup.
Therefore 125529 and 120694 count recursive calls, not necessarily
distinct relation states. The saved output does not report len(seen).
This affects the label on a search count, not the mathematical result.

No correctness bug was found in the scoped search logic. No source or
public branch was changed by this audit. The source result remains
conditional on the stated span-four lemma and model hypotheses.
