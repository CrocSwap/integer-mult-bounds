Exact arithmetic for the eight-call returned-register search
============================================================

The state is a rational subspace of Q^8 with dimension at most three.
Each generating relation is one of these original rows:

1. The difference of two subset indicator vectors. Each entry is -1, 0,
   or 1.
2. At a diagonal terminal, the original subset indicator for the target
   of the returned register. Each entry is 0 or 1.

The second row is added only when the resulting rank is at most three.
The code uses an original monomial representative. It does not use a
reduced row as a new bounded generator.

For r at most three, every r by r minor of these original rows has
absolute value at most floor(r^(r/2)), hence at most five. Cramer's rule
therefore writes each nonzero RREF entry as n/d with |n| at most five
and 1 <= d <= 5. Two such reduced fractions with the same image in
F_101 are equal: their cross difference has absolute value at most
50, which is less than 101. This proves the rational reconstruction
used for primitive-parity pruning.

After reconstruction, the least common multiple of all denominators
is at most 60. Each integer numerator after this multiplication has
absolute value at most 300. Divide their greatest common divisor and
reduce modulo two. This is a primitive integer row of the rational
relation space. It lies in the saturated integer kernel of any actual
frame realization. The off-diagonal derivative rows and the returned
diagonal derivative row lie in that same kernel modulo two. Its binary
dimension is at most three. This proves the parity rejection rule.

Every finite-field rank comparison agrees with the rational comparison.
A state has at most three original bounded generators. Testing a new
collision, a zero paid frame, or a returned target adds at most one
nonunit bounded row. The relevant minors have at most four nonunit
rows. Their absolute values are at most 16 by Hadamard's inequality.
Testing whether an active target lies in a two-frame span also adds
two coordinate unit rows. Expand a determinant along these unit rows;
the remaining minor still has at most four nonunit bounded rows.
Thus no nonzero minor relevant to a decision vanishes modulo 101.

Two cached states have the same rational row space if and only if they
have the same row space over F_101. Each has rank at most three. If their
union has larger rank, at most four rows witness that fact. Those rows
are again original bounded generators, so the same minor bound applies.

The code's GeneralBasis has capacity for six rows: three state rows,
two coordinate unit rows, and one target row. Forward elimination with
pivots in increasing order is sufficient for its rank tests. RREF is
used for cached states.

The F101 source and these bounds passed an independent source audit by
the gaussian_escape agent. A separate integer engine checked the same
binary word files. The two engines found the same 538 rank-three word
IDs with diagonal terminals and the same terminal count for each ID.
Both found no accepted terminal. They also checked all 27156 rank-two
canonical orders and found no diagonal terminal.

Seven additional controls check the formal polynomial search core.
They include acceptance after a required collision and acceptance after
a returned-target relation, as well as targeted rejection cases. These
are polynomial fixtures, not admissible paid-call circuit constructions.
Their separate scope is stated in dirty_f101_controls.py.
