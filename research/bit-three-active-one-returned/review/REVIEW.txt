Independent agent review: three active targets and one returned role
==================================================================

Review scope
------------
I reviewed the package bit-three-active-one-returned. This review is by a
separate OpenAI Codex agent. It is not human peer review or proof-assistant
verification. I read PR-DESCRIPTION-STANDARD.md before this report.

The exact claim is a nine-call minimum in F_2[M_3(Q)] for three invertible
lower-triangular rational 3 by 3 targets and one arbitrary scalar role that
must return unchanged. The paid scalar projectors are binary rank-one
idempotents on four roles. Paid address frames are nonzero and have rational
rank one. The targets may differ. No numerator, denominator, alphabet, or
address-lattice parity bound is imposed.

This is a scoped obstruction. It supplies no new supplier, physical circuit
price, multiplication exponent, or kappa improvement. It does not cover clean
workspace, more returned roles, arbitrary nontriangular target triples, or
noncommuting address operations.

Proof review
------------
1. Padding a shorter nonempty word by splitting A into A/2,A/2 is valid.
   The two factors have the same projector, and idempotence gives the
   original factor. All frames remain nonzero and rank one.
2. I read the span-four argument in the pinned PR468 proof. Its use of
   target-minus-identity rank extends to the active-target count when a
   returned target has frame zero. The common-ratio flag argument uses an
   active invertible target. The zero frame remains zero in every block.
   Thus eight calls require frame span at least five and relation rank at
   most three. This step retains the lower-triangular hypothesis.
3. The invariant-subspace reduction handles equal active target characters.
   Distinct formal coefficients force invariance under each coordinate
   projection onto an equal-character space. A basis chosen within each
   such space leaves the target diagonal. A rank-one idempotent in block
   upper-triangular form has one nonzero diagonal block. The disjoint call
   bounds are 3+6, or the retained three-register nine-call theorem.
4. The visible-distance bound is over Q. A functional can kill at most two
   seen rank-one frames without killing an invertible target selected by the
   row. This does not reduce rational matrix entries modulo two. The code
   tests both rows and columns and the union of all returned roles.
5. The finitely generated group H of paid frames is free abelian. The kernel
   of Z^8 onto H is saturated. Reduction modulo two is exact because H is
   torsion-free. This justifies the derivative-space bound and the rank-three
   finite quotient. It does not use augmentation of the divisible ambient
   rational group.
6. The catalogue includes every binary rank-one idempotent in each pattern
   space and all coordinate projectors. Their inclusion makes strong
   connectivity equivalent to irreducibility of the full pool. Each used
   support then gets its own invariant-subspace check. Coordinate
   permutations preserve the domain because all returned roles are tested.
7. For two distinct rank-one idempotents, commutation is equivalent to both
   cross contractions being zero. The normal forms use only these swaps.
   Word reversal follows by inversion and the additive-address sign map;
   it does not require symmetric projectors.
8. At a nonzero off-diagonal coefficient, every valid extension must merge
   the chosen surviving exponent with another surviving exponent in the
   same entry. Branching on every partner is complete. Already-cancelled
   terms stay cancelled after further quotienting. Each new branch raises
   rational relation rank, so depth three is enough.
9. A diagonal terminal has monomial entries because its determinant is a
   monomial unit in a Laurent polynomial ring. Adding the exact returned
   exponent as a relation is required; a binary parity test alone would
   not suffice. A target in the relation space plus two coordinate units
   lies in the rational span of two paid rank-one frames. This rejection
   remains valid after every further relation, so terminal pruning is safe.
10. I checked the exact-arithmetic bounds. Every state has at most three
    original cube rows; membership tests use at most four bounded nonunit
    rows after coordinate rows are removed from minors. Their determinants
    have absolute value at most 16, so reduction modulo 101 preserves rank.
    The reconstructed RREF fractions have numerator and denominator at most
    five in absolute value, with positive denominator. Cross differences
    are at most 50, so their residues are distinct. Primitive row parities
    are elements of the saturated relation lattice. The stated primitive
    integer intermediate bounds are below signed 64-bit limits.
11. The nine-call upper bound uses each target's three nonzero column
    matrices. Coordinate scalar projectors give the complete diagonal
    endpoint and leave every column of the fourth input unchanged.

No material finding remains in this scope. The result is suitable for
publication with the stated limits. No mathematical defect was found. The three-register finite
calculation inside PR468 is a retained dependency. I read its statement and
used written lemmas; I did not rerun that predecessor's full finite search.
