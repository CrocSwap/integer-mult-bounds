# Exact scope of the commuting-swap census

This is a finite statement about the pinned round-three word, not a theorem
excluding other native words or a new multiplication exponent. The statement
does not require a numerical comparison of powers.

The source's forward word has 9,829 adjacent persistent ADD pairs with exactly
one common role and no source/target dependency. They commute because the
corresponding elementary off-diagonal matrices have both cross-products zero.
The same condition holds for their inverse transposes. Of those pairs, 98
touch a late native warp on a role's next or previous contact. The other 9,731
belong to the fixed-warp domain of Matarener's prior bounded search and are
outside this new census.

For each of the 98 pairs, swap the two ADDs. Release only their two contact
frames and all incident selected late native-map contracts. Keep every other
scalar contact, all main and helper endpoints, every prelude line contract,
all other warp occurrences and the eight owned-center jobs fixed. The selected
late occurrence of each operator is positive. Its other nontrivial occurrences,
if any, prescribe just one shared line map. Dimension-zero and full-dimension
occurrences impose no additional subspace constraint. The census checks these
facts from the actual source instead of assuming a different map for each use.

Write U for a contact's binary subspace. A standard monotone bridge U -> V is
allowed when U is contained in V and costs one child of rank dim V - dim U
when that difference is positive. A scalar gate has the same contact on both
roles. The chronological per-role contact graph therefore specifies all
ordinary frame bridges, including zero-rank edges that could become paid.

Across a released native warp, let A be the preceding contact and B the next
contact. An arbitrary invertible binary G can have G(A) contained in B exactly
when dim A <= dim B. When a separate shared contract G(a)=b is retained, one
also needs

* a in A implies b in B;
* a outside A requires either dim A < dim B or b outside B.

These conditions are necessary by injectivity. They are sufficient: first map
the prescribed line, extend a basis inside A and inside B, then extend to
ambient bases. This is the simultaneous line/subspace transport used in Cao
Yifan's source. Releasing an incident map does not release its prelude contract.

The graph charges a single consolidated bridge of rank dim B - dim A across
each released warp. This is an optimistic bound for every monotone placement
of bridges before and after that warp. If those bridges have ranks r and s,
then their total rank is dim B - dim A and

    (r+s)^t <= r^t+s^t,   0 < t < 1.

The abstract single bridge can also be realized by choosing its entry at A,
extending G as above, and performing the bridge after G. The source graph's
complete nonzero histogram is independently recounted and agrees with its
actual climbs: `{1:2201,2:412,3:510,4:127,5:40}`. The eight rank-eight center
jobs are added separately. In particular, the graph has not silently improved
the baseline by deleting a source bridge.

All normal edges to fixed neighbors give lower and upper bounds for each of
the two variable frames. Edges between the two variable contacts are checked
after both choices are made. Free-warp edges to fixed contacts impose the
dimension/incidence tests above. Every interval of binary subspaces between
L and U corresponds bijectively to the subspaces of U/L. Its quotient dimension
is at most five in every actual case. Thus the declared fallback to a finite
frame catalogue is never used in this result. The retained RREF enumerator is
checked against the Gaussian-binomial subspace counts 1, 2, 5, 16, 67 and 374
in dimensions zero through five.

The exact census has 1,077 frame pairs. After ordinary containment and shared
map constraints, 74 are feasible, one for each feasible swap; the other 24
swaps are infeasible in this two-contact model. For every feasible assignment,
the multiset of positive incident bridge ranks equals the original multiset,
including multiplicities. All edges away from the pair are unchanged. The
whole paid profile is therefore exactly unchanged for every feasible swap.

The 632 productive endpoints, including all 408 arbitrary independent helper
jobs, are fixed. Consequently no rank price, recursive moment, completion or
outer transfer can extract a kappa gain from this bounded change. If the
physical realization carries additional charges, those cannot create a gain
missing from the optimistic bill. No new physical realization is admitted by
this negative result.

The result does not cover propagation into a third scalar contact, larger
reordered blocks, changed prelude directions, nonmonotone or symmetric phase
frames, paid nonmonomial warp realizations, helper boundary changes, new owned
center recipes, or a different controller topology. It is not an exact bound
on all commuting-window searches with arbitrary native-map changes.

The discovery script's floating prices and timing fields are not used by the
exact verifier. `RESULT.json` is regenerated using containment, dimensions,
integer histograms and exact small matrix products. Native all-column,
compiler, precision/tape and analytic assumptions of any eventual improved
multiplier remain separate.
