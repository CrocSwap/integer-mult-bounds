# Open next questions

## Global outward-slack infimum at error one

For global injective integer-output maps rounding the ideal #6509 Gaussian
pair with uniform Euclidean error at most one, what is the infimum of the
outward norm slack, and is a global map attaining it possible?

The current necessary lower bound is \(\sqrt{78}-\sqrt{73}\), from the
radius-nine Hall obstruction. The inherited #6509 map gives upper bound one.
The original R5 proof remains a smaller, compact obstruction. The four later
finite upper certificates do not supply a global construction.

A useful next step is a global matching construction, a uniform Hall proof,
or a stronger necessary bound under the same hypotheses. A map must retain
injectivity and the error bound on the entire lattice; pointwise selection,
finite success and a limit guessed from small radii are insufficient.

## Finite domains and interfaces

The exact radius-8 optimum and larger radii beyond11 were not determined.
Radius10 follows by inclusion from the equal radius9/radius11 values, without
a separate computation. The bounded sweep does not prove a formula for all
radii. Other error caps and operators, an efficient global selector, actual
physical caller admission, and selected multiplication precision recovery
remain outside this package's conclusions. No selected kappa changes.
