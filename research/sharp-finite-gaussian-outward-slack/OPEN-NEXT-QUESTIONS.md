# Open next questions

## Global outward-slack infimum at error one

**Question:** For global injective integer-output maps rounding the actual
#6509 ideal Gaussian pair with uniform Euclidean error at most one, what is
the infimum of the outward norm slack, and is it attained by a global map?

**Current bound:** This package proves the necessary lower bound
\(\sqrt{21}-\sqrt{19}\) from the transferred radius-five Hall obstruction.
The inherited #6509 map and triangle inequality give the upper bound one.

**First step:** Prove a global extension theorem for the finite matching, or
derive a stronger necessary bound under the same global hypotheses. Either
direction must retain injectivity and the uniform error bound on the entire
integer lattice.

**Blocked by:** The radius-five matching is finite. Its existence does not
extend it to a global injective map, and the Hall witness proves necessity
only below its finite threshold.

## Other scopes

No result here treats other error caps, other operators, other grid scales,
physical implementation costs, or the selected \(\kappa\). These require
separate source-bound statements and are intentionally outside this package.
