# Open questions after the bounded sweep

- Whether the finite optima at (R=6,7,9,11) reflect any theorem for other
  radii remains open. No other radius was evaluated.
- The β-a family is only probed at (R=7,9,11), and the inherited
  β₂ threshold is only probed at (R=6). These checks neither prove nor
  refute a general formula.
- A quotient flow proves existence of an integral finite injection through
  bipartite matching polytope integrality; this package does not provide a
  closed-form assignment rule.
- This bounded numerical result alone does not merit a separate PR. The
  reusable orbit quotient, exact capacity accounting, flow certificates,
  and Hall-cut receipts may be reviewed for integration into the existing
  finite-rounding branch.
