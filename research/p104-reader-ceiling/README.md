# P104: per-reader endpoint charge, frontier-word ceiling kappa <= .0115428 (Lean, conditional, no kappa claim)

Authors: DreamingOfClouds with Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), via Claude Code. **No kappa claim.** Everything here is a Lean statement with named hypotheses; the reader hypotheses are open (paper statements, computed on five words but not proved in Lean). It builds on #7678 (the tree ceiling `ceiling_tree_GD`, t0 = .0116750) and through it on #7668 (`ceiling_native_J`, t0 = .0118103): the modules of #7678 that the new files import (`CTreeCore`, `CongestionTreeCore`, `CongestionTreeW`, `CongestionTreePiece`) and the 46 modules of #7668 are included unchanged. ## What the new charge is
The tree bound of #7678 charges each source's moves; the reader side (IN) of the same master problem charges each of the 104 readers. A register's excess equals the sum of its move costs `c2` plus a residual `rho`, and a register that is TIED to a reader (a Z-class register, born at dim 0 and ending at dim 8, or a V-class register born at dim 1 and ending at 9, with a stop at a dim in [2, 7]) has `rho >= delta = beta(7) - beta(8) = 7^s + 2^s - 8^s - 1` (`tied_rho`, proved, from the concavity of x^s). Distinct readers tied to distinct registers therefore each add `delta` to the budget, additively (`sum_c2_pay`, proved). The readers that are not tied and not fully transported are absorbed by an allowance: at most 5 mixed registers, at most 10 owned last movers, and `omega` (the net G'-token rank that owned rows absorb), each costing one `delta` (`tree_lower_reader`, proved; `lineOKR`).

## What Lean proves
`resid_V`, `resid_Z` (the register identity, inequality form), `beta_ge` and `tied_rho` (a tied register has `rho >= delta`), `sum_c2_pay` (payer summation), `tree_lower_reader` (the Lagrange line bound with the per-reader allowance `dI <= SC delta` for tied readers and the allowance `NDL * dI` subtracted for the untied), `reader_regs_lower` and `reader_piece` (a piece lemma in the shape of `tree_piece`: the `delta |Q|` term cancels, so `|Q|` is never bounded below), soundness of two 230-row DP tables per variant, the A-class and the I-class one (six in all, `CTree{rda,rdi,rfa,rfi,rea,rei}`, one `decide +kernel` per row), the per-piece numeric certificates (weights, delta enclosure `hdel*`, line, sigma, point, unit) and the sign `0 <= corrC cstF s` given `hcstLo` (`corr_nonneg*`). All theorems print only propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound; no `sorry`, no `native_decide` (`CHECK.txt`). One draft statement was false and was caught by Lean: the first draft allowed a negative `gB`, which is unsound for readers with no cells (caught by the `ℕ` typing); the final statements use `gamma = max(0, ...)`.

Reading of the new hypotheses (status):
1. `T` (IN side): `T.S` = readers, `T.J` = (reader, source) cells, `T.R` = sources of non-transported cells; the IN DP table is the reflection of the OUT table (paper: the root value is equal at every k, c2 is reflection symmetric) and the reflection is folded into `T.dA`, `T.dB`. The restricted-edge set of the 48 readers is the I-class set (tables `rdi`, `rfi`, `rei`; 18 of 230 rows differ from the OUT table, with the same root value; the table that Lean checks is the IN one). Open (paper); the IN-side trees were not extracted from any word. The OUT-side counterparts were (`NATIVES.md`).
2. `Q, Uo, hUo, hQ, hcase`: every reader is tied (`Q`), or all-transported (`x = k`, needing only `lam <= capI`), or in `Uo`. This is the dichotomy: cells on dim-1 moves carry at most one cell each at cost at least `min(c18, capv)`, else a register dedicated to the reader pays. Open (paper). Numerics: on the natives the dim-1 branch never binds (its cost is at least 3.2 times the reader's tree cost).
3. `ω`, `hω`, `hUc : Uo.card <= 15 + ω`: `|Uo| <= 5` mixed `+ 10` owned last movers `+ ω`. **`ω <= 10` is proved on paper (a rank-count argument whose premises are: no owned register rises from dim <= 7 into V, and every owned lifetime ends in a CLEAR) but is a named hypothesis in Lean**; the CLEAR certificates are not wired into this package. `ω = 0` on all five words (`NATIVES.md`), which is the `ω <= 0` theorem's hypothesis; the headline `ω <= 10` is the variant whose bound is proved on paper for the class. `ω <= 16` is kept as the wide variant (the owned token rank if none of it were redundant).
4. `π, hπR, hπinj, hTied`: each tied reader has a distinct public register `π t` with `Tied` (as defined in `PROVED.md`). `tied_rho` is proved, so `rho >= delta` is not a hypothesis; the existence and uniqueness of the tied register is. Paper premises: the cell paths of #7678's tree, at most 5 mixed registers, the owned-surplus accounting of #7668 (`Omega ks mr s = dprice ks s + mr - (6 * 9^s + 10)`, appendix of `PROVED.md`), mains X, X2 born at dim 1 (audited as a restriction on the class, not a class fact; it holds on all five words), and the lineage step below. Uniqueness uses "every register visits at most one dim-8 chart".
5. `hcstLo : 0 <= cstF t` on `[a0, 1/10]`: makes the sign `0 <= corrC cstF s` (needed for `hP2`) a Lean fact. `cstF` is a free function in the Lean text with upper brackets only; the lower bracket follows on paper from `cst(t) = max c2(p,q)/(q-p) >= c2(7,8) > 0`. `hcstMonoT`, `hcst0`, `hcst1` (and `hcst2` in `ceiling_reader_GD`) are the monotonicity and the outward-rounded brackets on the new window.

## Caveats, stated plainly
- The F2 (lineage) lemma as first posed ("some register in S_t pays") is FALSE: a five-register token-swap counterexample (a payer outside S_t, none inside) exists, and `TL-a`'s clause "only public registers hold e_g" is false on the natives (15 to 28 owned registers hold G' tokens, depending on the word). Neither is used. The statements use the count form F2* (payers `>= N_high - 15 - omega`), proved on paper for the class under the premises of item 3; Lean uses only the existence of a tied register per tied reader (item 4) and the count bound on `Uo` (item 3).
- The paper figure with 5 allowances (.0115086) is not formalised; an audit showed owned moves can end at dim-8 charts without a public payer, hence NDL = 15 (5 mixed + 10 owned last movers), and, for `omega`, up to 10 more.
- Digits: the Lean-certified digits are weaker than the paper digits by 1.1e-6 to 1.2e-6 (above); a different rounding of t is not claimed. The window of each piece is `[a0, tJ0]`, `tJ0 = .0118103`, and the conclusion is for `t` in `[t0, 1]`.
- The tree premises of #7678 (`Tr` out-trees, `hcap`, `hnd`, `hP1`, `hP2`, `hcell`) were re-checked here on five words (OUT side, both primes; the word side of `hP1` also at the lower end of the new window; `NATIVES.md`): they are not proved in Lean and the IN-side analogues are open.
- Word-level facts (`NATIVES.md`) are computed by scripts that are not part of the package: five words, one port set of 104 odd-weight vectors (close to a single data point for port-set-dependent facts); `hmix = 5` is tight. Nothing in Lean ties a word to the hypotheses.
- The docstrings of the three final modules stated a wrong digit for `t0` in the working copy; they were corrected in this package (comments only; the numeric definitions are those compiled).
