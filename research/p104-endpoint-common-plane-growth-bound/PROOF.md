# Common-plane growth with productive endpoints

Work in the nine-dimensional binary chart space. Cocharted ADD/COPY contacts connect chart segments into groups. Every member of an eligible group belongs to a public productive helper. Main and owned frames, scalar chronology, and the signed caller stay fixed. An eligible group may keep chart A or change it to A+P, where P is one common two-dimensional subspace. Initial and terminal helper charts may change, subject to their productive edge staying strictly nested.

On each old nested edge A⊂B of gap d, write a=dim(A+P)−dim(A) and b=dim(B+P)−dim(B). Then a≥b. If b>0, selecting the lower group forces selecting the upper group, and old, upper-only and both-grow gaps are d, d+b and d+b−a. If b=0, lower growth has gap d−a and the upper chart is unchanged. A zero gap costs zero on a paid edge, and a zero productive gap is forbidden. An equal paid edge with b>0 still forces the lower-to-upper implication and has zero both-grow saving.

The gain on a paid edge is H(old)−H(new) and on a productive target edge it is H(new)−H(old), where H(r)=r^(1−κ) and H(0)=0. On allowed states this objective is affine in the two selection bits, so the lead implements maximum closure. The independent evaluator recovers groups from the literal tape in reverse and prices each edge with direct cut capacities. On a paid edge, offer the both-grow shortening reward at the lower vertex and charge upper-only growth on an upper-to-lower arc. On a productive edge, offer the upper-only reward at the upper vertex, cancel that reward by charging it at the lower vertex, and charge any productive shortening at the lower vertex. Required nesting and positive productive rank are hard implications. Fixed vertices reduce these arcs to terminal capacities where applicable.

The directed prices enclose all nine increments H(r)−H(r−1) at the same pinned comparator κ. First allow independent directed endpoints for each signed edge contribution. For an unresolved nonpositive case use one common increment vector for every edge. Doubling the increment box gives center c and one-norm radius R equal to the sum of the original interval widths. The doubled box lies in the convex hull of the 18 exact points c±R e_i. For a fixed plane and legal selection the objective is linear in those increments. Its maximum over selections is convex and is bounded above throughout the hull by the largest exact upper at a vertex. A zero optimum includes the option to leave all groups unchanged.

The candidate ranking uses the sum of available positive isolated paid shortening and productive growth, ignoring dependencies only for ranking. The replay pins the 1,014 planes from the predecessor's four-direction sample, recomputes their entire ordering, and solves only the frozen first 256. The objective grants no extra realization expense. The result applies to the fixed common plane and allowed group selections only.
