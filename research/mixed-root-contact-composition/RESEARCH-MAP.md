## Other research retained as bounded evidence

The [original search note](../mixed-projector-query-root/SEARCH.md)
records the finite basis catalogue, child-size discovery, structured
larger roots, braid moves, coupled copies and internal-query tests.
The original scripts and logs remain in the local work directories.
They are not required to accept the exact positive result.

The later local searches also covered common advancing stages, period
two and three stage hypotheses, nested ten-field bases, coincident
endpoint projectors, helper-reflection interfaces and a fixed triangle
query tuple. No better complete caller emerged. An early period-three
candidate used a stage-dependent basis and was rejected. The original
fixed-parameter fifth-stage test was replaced by the all-parameter
classification; its narrower scope is not used as a universal proof.

The old seven-mask integer solver timed out with positive upper bounds.
Its retained receipt is now discovery history. The new exact recurrence
and tail proof supply the exclusion. Similarly, the direct border
correction was first tested only through child size 64; the new exact
bound covers all positive sizes and shapes. The fixed triangle tuple
check does not exclude all triangles.

These bounded searches are not converted into global impossibility
claims. The useful identities and all-size bounds are now in registered
replays; raw exploratory logs are kept separate from acceptance evidence.
