# Exact cancellation in consistent frame charts

Let C_F be an invertible chart for each binary frame F, with C_0=I. A
normalized value z at F is physically C_F z. Assume the inherited child
lemma realizes C_G C_F^{-1} at cost dim(G)-dim(F) for F subset G, or the
corresponding charged descent. This package verifies the needed binary
inclusions; concrete chart adapters and their compiler are an open obligation.
No commutation of charts is used below.

For centre k, Q_k is its rank-eight gather hyperplane and E_k is its
singleton survivor. The pinned geometry verifies E_k subset Q_k. A query
parent contains C_Q q. Copy it privately and apply C_E C_Q^{-1}, paying
rank seven. This gives C_E q without changing the parent.

For carrier i assigned to centre k, let h_i be its arbitrary normalized
input at frame zero. Before any target moves, subtract D[t,k] h_i from each
target assigned to that carrier. This costs scalar additions at common
frame zero. Advance the carrier to E_k (rank one), add the private query,
then clear the private copy. Its normalized contents are now h_i+q_k.

For a target read at frame F, the pinned schedule guarantees E_k subset F,
and both the target and assigned carrier are at F. Add D[t,k] times the
carrier. The h_i part cancels its earlier precompensation: every subsequent
common-chart scalar addition and target transport is linear. Thus the net
normalized contribution is exactly D[t,k] q_k. This holds independently for
every carrier and target, including arbitrary nonzero dirty inputs.

Complete the query parent and carrier to the same full frame V. Their
physical query components are both C_V q_k, independent of transport path,
since adjacent chart inverses telescope. Subtract the parent from the
carrier. Its result is C_V h_i, precisely its prescribed residual transport.
The query parent remains C_V q_k. Every private copy is explicitly erased.

of rank one, and 104 target paths of rank eight. Their sum is 1128. The

The complete displayed boundary profile therefore has rank mass 1184.
This identity does not prove global hosting, full-word feasibility, or a
new exponent saving. In particular, labelling the carrier ranks coverage
is justified only after a future owner assignment absorbs those lifetimes.
