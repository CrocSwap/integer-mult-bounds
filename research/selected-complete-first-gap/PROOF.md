## 4. Uniform signed half-copy on all components

At a component start, source and target heads are on their first MSB,
and W is on its first unary digit. Let the original source bits be
`b0 b1 ... b_(w-1)`, b0 the sign. Finite control initially remembers b0.
For each of the w positions it reads the current source bit b_j, writes
the remembered bit into that source-work cell, remembers b_j, and advances
source, target and W by one. The target bit is only checked for binary
membership and is never changed.

After j>=1 iterations the source prefix is
`b0 b0 b1 ... b_(j-2)` and control remembers b_(j-1); the unvisited suffix
is unchanged. This follows by direct induction from the two remembered-bit
states. Encountering W's blank immediately after its w-th digit proves
that the remembered bit is the original least significant bit. Require
that bit to be0. The resulting full word is

`b0 b0 b1 ... b_(w-2)`.

For a nonnegative input this is its exact binary quotient by2. For a
negative input with unsigned encoding u and signed value q=u-2^w, the
new encoding is `2^(w-1)+floor(u/2)`, whose signed value is
`floor(u/2)-2^(w-1)=q/2` when q is even. This includes q=`-2^(w-1)`.
The quotient lies inside the signed interval, so no widening is needed.

The W head is physically rewound to its permanent origin and then moved
to its first digit after every completed component. Source and target
heads already point at the next component; no random component access or
free reset occurs. The finite traversal and complete W reset cost at most
4w+8 transitions per component. Because there are V/w components, this
part costs at most8V for w>=2. This scan automatically includes all f
columns, all P/S spectators, all r coefficients and both components of
each Gaussian value. It does not assume separable input data.

At simultaneous final source/target delimiters, clear every W digit,
rewind W, then return both operand heads and L/B to their origins. The
remaining cleanup is bounded by8(F+2), including both full operand returns.
Every private body is blank, headers are unchanged, and only source work3's
DATA contains B/2. This source is a working duplicate: the original public
449 has never been written by the half-copy.

Combining these explicit bounds gives the convenient inherited-size bound

`T_half <=4096(F+1)`.

The arithmetic and cleanup constants are fixed before w varies. Two operand
windows, two at-most-w guides and one at-most-E counter give the conservative
historical increment `Delta K_half <=8(F+2)`. Entry history may already be
larger; erasing guides never refunds visited cells.

## 7. Failure boundary and verification limits

The five-event prefix is not a transaction. An odd half-source or overflow
in28038 occurs before its public scatter, preserving all public contents
while the earlier INIT remains committed. A failing MOVE28039 preserves its
public donor, but the earlier212 update remains committed. A failure in28040
leaves both earlier ADD and MOVE committed. In all cases, private cleanup,
head returns and reuse after rejection are not promised. The successful
theorem requires all stated numerical conditions.

symbols, fixed writes, unit moves and finite transitions. Its45-tape CPU
inherits the original instruction compiler; the run method's AST is checked
identical to C4, with only its enclosing fixed tape inventory changed.
Unbounded integer arithmetic appears solely in initial fixture construction
and post-run numerical oracles. The complete positive case enters once at
INIT and runs to HALT with no intermediate host packet writes.

Every public and owned result is checked. A returning skipped28038 mutation
still produces the correct1349 and1526, but fails both affected public

insufficient. The direct source oracle uses the closed XOR137/sign9 formula,
not factor execution. Scalar expectations use ordinary integer A+B/2 and

Five complete half-packet cases cover widths2,3,6,17,129, including all even
small-width values and a2049-bit cutoff value. All headers, target/source
