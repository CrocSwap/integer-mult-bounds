# Conditional κ = 0.00351406 (+1.652% over #6889): one native word serves two root chains

**Result.** The exact-kernel crossing of the complete signed invoice is **0.00351406186742169**, which is **+1.6523% over #6889** (0.00345694177515779; exactly 1.65233%).
The claim for the outer statement is **κ = 0.00351406** with tested price 0.00351406007 (grid 10⁻¹¹), both strictly below the raw crossing, which lies in (0.003514061867421, 0.003514061867422).
The package builds on #6889: it keeps the #6889 native word's structure (the hosted native of #6883 with its shared cover, the deletions, the joint merges and the direct reads), its owned-ascent declaration and its BIT line, and changes what the native serves: one native word now delivers **two** copies of the #6833 chain root at once, on separate main registers.

**Conditional statement.** The result holds under the five framework assumptions of #6833; under assumption A6 (owned ascent) as declared in #6883; under the BIT saving of the open #6726 (below, necessary for this claim); and under the new forms declared below (a reducible root, one native contact serving two chains, same-label second mains).

## What the construction is
* **Root.** Two #6833 chain roots on orthogonal coordinates (n = 2×20 = 40, N = 42 units, 18 stages, 6 thin units), one stage variable per stage, units interleaved (chain A, chain B). `root_check.py` regenerates the chain root from its closed form and requires **all 21 units of each chain to equal the file**, the cross Gram between the chains to vanish, each chain's Gram to be sgn(i−j), and then re-proves the SPEC-level identities for the whole root in exact Fractions: isotropy, product −I, stage order, units inside masks, masks commuting with the prefix before and after each stage, the symbolic whole-job identity M(D) = −diag(∏D^ε) with each of the 18 stage variables once and affine, full mask credit, dyadic entries.
* **A wrong root fails, directly.** The composition replay reads the root only through its masks, so a wrong X or Y entry does not fail it. The block identities do, and `check.py` asserts it: a wrong X entry, a wrong Y entry in chain B, and two swapped unit rows each fail `root_check.py`; a wrong mask bit fails the caller replay and the mask comparison.
* **Caller.** The #6833 caller twice, on roles 0–19 and 20–39, with one HOST2 event per stage: the shared native delivers `Y += Q⁻¹X` on the first plane and `X2 += α₂ Q Y2` (α₂ = −1) on the second, times the stage variable D on all four registers. The second chain's LAMBDA pair is flipped. 124 LAMBDA events (62 per chain), 32 IDLE_ONE, 6 thin queries.
* **Native word.** Endpoint X = x, Y = x + y, X2 = x2 − y2, Y2 = y2, helpers identity: 38,334 events, 1375 public registers (416 mains, 4 per port, and 959 helpers), 6440 MOVEs; the first-plane mains and the second-plane mains pay 917 MOVEs each, no move is shared or fused.
* **Invoice.** The root part is exactly twice the #6833 chain root part (`check.py` asserts it): there is no root discount, and nothing is fused. The native part is 18 calls of the helper and paid histograms of this word. S = 104·34 − 36·56 = 1520, net of 56 per call.

## Why this is not row 19 (stated plainly)
Row 19 reads "many registers, same frame transition, same time: one job per register": it forbids fusing the jobs of two registers. No two registers' jobs are fused here: every register pays its own moves (first and second mains 917 each), and the root has no discount. What is shared is a helper, and a shared helper is **one field, one job, one credit**: FP section 1 (`research/kappa-rebuild/architecture/FINITE-PRECOMPILATION.md:32-35`) requires "At every integer column count c, the unit computes exactly its independent rank k*c output jobs ... All borrowed data return to the declared endpoint, and owned storage is explicitly initialized and erased"; FP section 3 matches job copies by the Pascal bijection and its edges "carry complete array jobs"; FP section 6 (lines 258-268) asks for "field-identity and chronological requirements; statistical independence of current values is not required"; the review of #4416 allows owners that "may be correlated and may include owned centers; arbitrary-input correctness permits that, but does not permit aliasing two physical slots". Whole-job matching constrains the fields, not who reads them. This is the accounting the line already uses for ports: in the #6889 word 844 of the 1153 helpers already read or write mains of 9 or more ports (`cover_check.py` recounts it).
The planes meet inside the core only through helpers and owned registers; the 174 events that couple a first-plane main with a second-plane main directly are all in the two gate blocks (at frames Z and V). Doubling with no sharing is an exact tie; the gain is entirely from the helpers read by both chains.

## The family lines this root does not meet, and why they do not apply
The predecessor family checks have three lines that this root fails: `hosted t not 0, not -1` (here t = α·β = 0 at every stage: two separate planes per stage, no coupled plane), `hosted alpha,beta dyadic units` (α = β = 0), and `Gram digraph strongly connected` (added by the #6883 `root_check.py` itself, "Added here", not a SPEC line; the Gram digraph has two components). T4′ needs only 1 + t ≠ 0, and t = 0 satisfies it; the condition t ≠ 0 came from the definition of the two-way plane host. What the outer theorem requires is on the unit identity and the whole-job credit: Theorem 1G pays "rounds j, in word order, of ranks r_j >= 1" with hypothesis (S) about ranks only (`research/lever-mixed-targets-2979/PROOF.md:14-16,25`), and FP requires the all-column word above. `root_check.py` re-proves the SPEC-level identities for this root; none of the three lines is used by Theorem 1G or FP.

## LAMBDA is a review question
LAMBDA at width f is the single elementary gate C on (q,…,q), not a lift of the width-one LAMBDA (they agree only at f = 1). The caller identity holds under both readings, so the algebra does not decide it; the price does. If each LAMBDA had to be paid as a rank-1 job per port, S = 1520 − 124·104 = **−11,376 < 0** and no crossing exists (the #6889 caller: 1520 − 82·104 = −7,008). The κ rests on pricing LAMBDA as ordinary work, as in every caller of this line from #6726 on, including #6833 (62 events per chain here). Declared as a convention, not admitted on main.

## Logarithm power
The smallest admissible value at this price is **2133**; **2134 is written** and used. 2100 fails here: the complement-capacity line (η > (63/64)^logp at maximum target rank 145) binds at 2100 for a tested price of about 0.0034607, below this claim. The power is a free parameter of the compiler theorem (`research/higher-moment-strictification/vendor/LOG-MAJORANT.md:110-122`; stated with an integer q and 1/q in the bound, its literal conditions fail for the real exponent used, and the code uses the same argument with a real p, which a reviewer of #6884 re-walked and I did not). The interval engines are the retained rational ones (no Decimal) at the tested price: α = 0.9999999975, γ = 0.9480, δ = 0.8116 (< 31/32), the composite bound 1/(1+power·reserve) + 4·decay^power < 1 holds, the 54 strict and 4 functional outer inequalities are positive (their smallest value is the chosen parameter r0 = gap/10, not a measured margin). The input-lift saving lemma of #6833 has its cap moved to the pin and passes.
