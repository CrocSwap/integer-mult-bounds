# A quotient-parity argument excludes the P112 profile `4,5^9`

Let K have characteristic zero. For the pinned 112 live E8 root lines,
write C={c_h : c_h(t)=<r_t,h>, h in K^8}. A piece q has weight equal to the
binary rank of the labels on its support. Ten independent pieces spanning
T with dim(T intersect C)>=5 cannot have weight bounds `4,5^9`.

This theorem closes that explicit piece configuration. Its use in the
TWOSIDE decoder reduction inherits the other hypotheses of that reduction.
It does not establish full C5, the decoder floor, a supplier or a new kappa.

## Exact geometric dependencies

The proof uses these facts about the pinned P112 set:

1. Weight at most four gives support size at most eight; weight at most
   five gives support size at most twelve.
2. A nonzero codeword has support at least 53. If its support is below 70,
   its direction is a scalar multiple of an E8 root.
3. Evaluation h -> c_h is injective, and a plane contains at most three
   root lines. If three distinct root lines lie in a plane generated by
   two roots, the third has coefficient ratio +1 or -1 in those roots.

Fact 1 in dimension four follows from the leading-one binary label bit:
a d-flat meets that affine hyperplane in at most 2^(d-1) points. The
five-flat bound is a complete enumeration of all 3,309,747 annihilator
spaces, in unique reverse row-echelon form, with maximum twelve.

For fact 2, the standard Weyl-chamber theorem moves a real direction to a
closed chamber. Its perpendicular roots are generated by the simple roots
on which it vanishes. Every parabolic on at most six simple roots has at
most 36 root lines. The eight seven-node parabolics have line counts
42,28,22,14,16,23,37,63. Only the final one can contain at least 43
perpendicular live lines, which a support below 70 requires. Its normal
is a root direction. Root live supports are 53 or 56. This also rules out
nonzero support below 53.

For K-valued directions, their perpendicular rational roots span dimension
at most seven. If that span had dimension at most six, a generic real
normal to it would have exactly those perpendicular roots. The preceding
parabolic bound would allow at most 36 lines, contradicting 43. Thus at
support below 70 the perpendicular span has dimension seven, and its
rational one-dimensional normal supplies the same root classification.

The live-root Gram sum is 112 times the identity in the doubled coordinates
used by the verifier, giving injectivity over K. All 7,140 pairs of E8
root lines have only two or three root lines in their rational plane, and
every third is the A2 sum or difference. These finite facts are replayed.
A rational root in the K-span of two independent rational roots has
rational coefficients, by an invertible rational coordinate minor. The
same plane inventory and coefficient ratios therefore hold over K.

These arguments and the retained geometry code follow PR3164/PR3169.
The Weyl-chamber theorem itself is a mathematical dependency, not a
program-proved theorem. SOURCES.json pins the exact predecessor bytes.

## Six points in a four-flat would force six roots in a plane

Set D=T intersect C and Q=T/D. Let v_i be the ten piece images in Q.
Every four are independent. Otherwise a nonzero linear combination of
at most four independent pieces would be a codeword supported on at
most 4*12=48<53 coordinates. The quotient has dimension at most five.

Suppose a four-dimensional subspace of Q contained six v_i. Their original
pieces span a six-dimensional space E. Their quotient rank is exactly
four, so E intersect D has dimension two. Delete each of the six pieces
in turn. The remaining five have quotient rank four, hence have a
one-dimensional kernel L_i in E intersect D.

The six L_i are distinct. If L_i=L_j, their common nonzero vector is
supported on at most the four pieces other than i and j, contradicting
four-image independence. Each L_i gives a nonzero codeword supported on
at most 5*12=60<70 coordinates, hence a root direction. Evaluation
injectivity maps the two-dimensional kernel back to a two-dimensional
direction space containing six distinct root lines. Fact 3 forbids this.

Consequently every four-flat contains at most five piece images. Also
dim Q=5: the ten images span Q, and dimension at most four would place
six of them in one four-flat.

## An odd number of remaining points supplies a good four-set

Fix any three pieces B including the piece assigned the weight-four bound.
Their images are independent. In the two-dimensional space Q/span(v_B), the other seven
images are nonzero. Each projective proportionality class has size at
most two: three such images together with B would give six images in
one four-flat.

Seven is odd, so at least one class has size one. Adjoin its piece x to B.
The resulting four images are independent, and their span contains none
of the six remaining piece images. The four selected supports have union
S with s=|S|<=8+3*12=44. This proves the independence step that the
earlier support-only estimate 44+12=56 could not establish.

The finite matching control enumerates all 232 partitions of seven
vertices into singletons and pairs. It checks the parity consequence,
not every possible quotient configuration. The preceding linear algebra
is the universal proof that this control applies.

## The six remaining pieces form an affine root A5 simplex

Write the remaining pieces q_0,...,q_5. Modulo the selected four images,
each has a nonzero class in one dimension. Rescale them to have equal
classes. Supports do not change. For i=1,...,5 there is a unique selected
piece combination a_i such that c_i=q_i-q_0-a_i belongs to D. Set c_0=0.

The five c_i are independent: their coefficients on q_1,...,q_5 in the
original ten-piece basis are distinct unit vectors. Every difference
c_i-c_j is nonzero and has support in S union supp(q_i) union supp(q_j),
of size at most 44+12+12=68<70. Thus c_i=c_{h_i} for independent h_i,
and all differences among 0,h_1,...,h_5 are scalar root directions.

Normalize roots to norm squared two. Write h_i=b_i alpha_i. Each
h_i-h_j is a third root line in the plane of alpha_i and alpha_j, so
b_i/b_j is +1 or -1. Absorb signs into the roots. All b_i then equal one
nonzero scalar b, and alpha_i-alpha_j is a root, so <alpha_i,alpha_j>=1.
This is an affine root A5 simplex.

At a live coordinate r, its six integer endpoint levels have pairwise
differences in {-2,-1,0,1,2}. If a difference has absolute value two,
Cauchy-Schwarz makes r the corresponding edge root. The multiplicities
are then 1,1,4, giving nine unequal endpoint pairs. Otherwise there are
at most two consecutive levels, giving at most 3*3=9 unequal pairs.
The common field scalar changes no zeros. Each coordinate therefore
belongs to at most nine of the fifteen edge supports.

Outside S, the edge values are q_i(t)-q_j(t). If k of the six endpoints
are nonzero, at most k(6-k)+k(k-1)/2 <=5k pairs differ. Thus, writing
U=sum_i |supp(q_i)|<=72 and summing edge supports, we obtain

```
15*53 <= 9s+5U <= 9*44+5*72 = 756 < 795 = 15*53.
```

The contradiction has margin 39. The stronger coordinate-budget inequality
of PR3169 is unnecessary.

## Binding and limits

The literal profile `4,5^9` passes the original TWOSIDE `(5,5)` BK/BK7
weight filters in DreamingOfClouds's PR3154. Here the five retained prefix
support caps at levels one through five are 68,80,92,104,116, meeting
the relevant thresholds. Its total sum(w-2)=29 is below 30. The five
smallest support caps sum to 56, so the original support-certified
quotient-basis test does not apply. PR3169 explicitly leaves this profile
as an open next question. We close it by replacing that test with the
six-circuit and odd-class arguments above. No claim about the total number
of currently remaining beta shapes is made.

The proof is over every characteristic-zero field. Exact finite geometry
and meaningful failure controls support it; no proof-kernel verification
or independent human review is claimed. The selected frontier is unchanged.
