# A ternary rejection beyond any bounded dyadic counting screen

Let H be the additive subgroup of M_d(Q) generated by finitely many paid
frames A_j. Work in F2[H]. A paid factor on R scalar roles is

```text
P_j = I + ([A_j] - 1) N_j,
```

where N_j is a binary rank-one idempotent. The desired full formal endpoint is
`diag([C_1], ..., [C_W], 1, ..., 1)`, with every additional role restored for
arbitrary initial dirt. Targets must belong to H. Scalar basis changes may be
absorbed into the N_j when the residual scalar map is identity, as augmentation
requires. Free address maps between factors and clean-only workspace contracts
are outside this statement.

## Necessary ternary cut

For any additive character lambda from H to Z/3, substitute

```text
[A] -> omega^lambda(A),      F4 = F2[omega]/(omega^2 + omega + 1).
```

This is a unital algebra homomorphism because omega has multiplicative order
three. A projected factor is identity when lambda(A_j)=0 and differs from
identity by rank one otherwise. For the surviving factors Q_1,...,Q_k,

```text
Q_1 ... Q_k - I = sum_j Q_1 ... Q_(j-1) (Q_j - I).
```

Each summand has rank at most one over F4. The endpoint minus identity has
rank exactly the number of target occurrences with lambda(C_i) nonzero.
Consequently every successful word satisfies

```text
#{j : lambda(A_j) != 0} >= #{i : lambda(C_i) != 0}.
```

This proof does not depend on call order, R, or the choice of the scalar
idempotents. Extra restored dirty roles add zero diagonal entries to the
endpoint minus identity. In fact, the rank argument also applies to arbitrary
rank-one N_j; idempotence is needed here only for the separately checked
determinant condition `sum A_j = sum C_i`.

The homomorphism is defined on the actual generated H. It is not a nontrivial
character of the divisible ambient group M_d(Q). Entrywise reduction modulo
three is valid for the integer example below; arbitrary rational denominators
would require an actual character of their generated lattice.

This is the retained projection and telescoping rank method, specialized to
an odd-order character. No novelty of that method or of finite cyclic quotients
is claimed. The new result is the separating family below and its replay.

## Exact separating family

For any integer s>=1, set A_1=4^s E_11 and A_(j+1)=3 v_j v_j^T for

```text
(-3,3,3), (5,5,1), (3,-1,2), (-3,-4,4),
(3,-2,3), (-1,-5,-3), (5,5,5).
```

Set `C_1=A_1+A_2+A_3`, `C_2=A_1+A_4+A_5`, and
`C_3=-A_1+A_6+A_7+A_8`. Writing a=4^s, their determinants are
`1296a`, `144a` and `67500-10494a`. The last could vanish only at
`a=3750/583`, which is not an integer. All targets are invertible, each
paid frame has rational rank one, and the target sum equals the paid sum.
The committed example uses s=1 and determinants 5184, 576 and 25524.

All paid frames are symmetric. Every one of their 28 six-frame subsets at
s=1 is rationally independent, as checked by exact elimination and separately
by the Leibniz determinant formula on the six symmetric coordinates. Scaling
the first frame by 4^(s-1) preserves independence. Thus they span the
six-dimensional symmetric subspace. Any rational functional nonzero
on that subspace kills at most five paid frames, retaining at least three.
There are only three targets. A functional zero on the subspace kills every
paid frame and target. Therefore all rational projection/rank cuts pass.

LATTICE.json supplies six independent integer basis rows B, integer
coefficients expressing B in the paid frames, and integer coordinates
expressing all paid frames and targets in B. Checking both directions proves
that B generates exactly H, with no rational saturation. Hence every binary
character is obtained exactly once by the 64 assignments to B modulo two.
Their paid-minus-target margins have histogram

```text
margin 0: 21; margin 2: 25; margin 4: 15; margin 6: 3.
```

All generated-lattice parity cuts therefore pass. This compares to the
first-order augmentation cuts of pending PR2938, without importing its code
or assuming its checker correct.

There is a stronger uniform dyadic comparison. The seven coefficients

```text
(-523126, -462683, 3941456, 395441, -2251832, 729350, -128789)
```

express 3E_11 in A_2,...,A_8. This identity is checked directly in
LATTICE.json. Since 4^s is 1 modulo three,

```text
E_11 = A_1 - ((4^s - 1)/3) (3E_11).
```

Consequently H contains E_11 and is the same group for every s. For every
character lambda:H -> Z/(2^e), with 1<=e<=2s, lambda(A_1)=0. Each target's
image is then the sum of its own disjoint group of the remaining paid labels.
A nonzero target image requires at least one nonzero paid image in that
group. Therefore

```text
#{j : lambda(A_j) != 0} >= #{i : lambda(C_i) != 0}
```

for every such character. These are the nonzero-label counting cuts associated
with the retained cyclic-root barrier over F2[C_(2^e)]. Given any finite
maximum dyadic order, choosing s large enough passes all these cuts through
that order. For s=1, the replay explicitly checks all 4,096 characters into
Z/4 as well as all 64 into Z/2.

In fact, the covered quotient has an explicit full word. Assign the eight
paid factors the diagonal scalar projectors with role indices
`(1,1,1,2,2,3,3,3)`. The resulting three formal exponent sums differ from
the desired targets by `(0,-A_1,+A_1)`. Because A_1 belongs to 4^s H, these
differences vanish in H/(4^s H). The same fixed commuting eight-factor word
therefore realizes the full endpoint over F2[H/(4^s H)], and after every
covered dyadic character. Additional scalar roles are unchanged. This is a
formal quotient identity, not a native-address implementation. Its full-H
endpoint is wrong, as the ternary witness also detects. The replay checks
these exact exponent differences for s=1,...,16; the displayed identity
proves the quotient claim for all s.

Now use `lambda(X)=X_11 modulo three`. The paid residues are
`(1,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)` and target residues are `(1,1,2)`. Only one paid factor
survives but all three targets survive. The necessary ternary inequality fails
by two, since 4^s is 1 modulo three. This excludes every word for each
multiset and endpoint in the family, even
with any finite number of restored dirty scalar roles.

The replay also enumerates all 729 characters H -> Z/3. Exactly two violate,
both scalar multiples of the displayed character. It independently confirms
the character-margin histograms by building their image codes through vector
addition. Exhausting these finite character sets corroborates the witness and
comparison; the written rank argument establishes the all-order, all-role
exclusion.

## Boundary of the result

Passing rational, determinant and any prescribed finite range of dyadic
nonzero-label counting screens is insufficient before scalar-word search.
The family supplies exact rejections beyond that specific conjunction. It
does not prove independence from every other screen or classify arbitrary
mixed targets. No uniform native
implementation, hosting, precision, all-size compilation, completed supplier
moment, or multiplication exponent is obtained.
