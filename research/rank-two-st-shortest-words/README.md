# All shortest normalized words for one rank-two st endpoint

Over any field, a rank-two endpoint I+([st]-[e])E in the full formal
S4 group algebra has exactly two families of shortest normalized paid words:

    D_s(P) D_s(E-P) D_t(Q) D_t(E-Q),   arbitrary plane projectors P,Q;
    D_s(P) D_t(P) D_s(E-P) D_t(E-P),   arbitrary plane projector P.

Here s=(12), t=(23), u=(34) are the only paid labels,
D_r(N)=I+([r]-[e])N, N is a rank-one idempotent, E is a rank-two
idempotent, and every dirty spectator is restored at the full formal endpoint.
Products act on columns, rightmost first. Projectors satisfy EP=PE=P;
similarly for Q. The first family permits noncommuting P,Q. No u call occurs
in a shortest word. In fixed coordinates over F_q there are m^2+m words,
where m=q(q+1); no conjugacy or commuting-word quotient is taken.

The minimum four calls, general tight spectator elimination and rank-one
rigidity are predecessor results. The addition is the complete equality
classification inside the rank-two active plane for this one endpoint.
[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) gives the all-field argument: the second tight peel has
only two possible image/kernel pairs. The universal statement rests on that
proof, not finite extrapolation. This answers an explicitly released next
question of Nick Petalas PR6449. See [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md) for exact credit.

The two independent bounded engines enumerate every ordered four-factor word
over F2, F3 and F5, using respectively a full formal 2+2 join and a faithful
left-regular-action 3+1 join. They retain all matching word lists and compare
them with the two separately generated families. [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md)
states actual coverage, controls, commands, environment and remaining limits.
No predecessor scientific checker or finite census is executed.
