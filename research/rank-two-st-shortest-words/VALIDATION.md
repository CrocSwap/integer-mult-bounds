## Frozen bounded domain

s,t,u and every rank-one idempotent2x2 over F2/F3/F5 is included. Written
factor order and row-major projector coefficients are explicit in the receipts.
Projectors are sorted by base-q coefficient code, with the first coefficient
least significant; gate ID is label_index*m+projector_index (s,t,u=0,1,2).
Actual matching ordered four-gate lists are retained, not only their counts.

The primary enumerates scalar matrices directly, tests rank-one idempotence,
and multiplies sparse full formal group-algebra coefficients. A complete 2+2
join retains every ordered factor-pair collision. For each G3,G4 it joins
G1G2=T G4G3, since all paid factors are involutions. It then re-expands
every actual four-factor hit in the full formal algebra. The two conjectured
families are separately constructed and compared as complete sets, without
pruning the join by them. The first F2 falsification passed before F3/F5.
The full candidate count describes coverage by the exact join; it is not a
claim that each of 67,394,592 full words was individually iterated.

The independent oracle enumerates normalized image lines and functionals
v(u)=1 instead of screening scalar matrices. It interprets calls by their
faithful left-regular action on both scalar input columns at the identity.
All 24 address blocks are retained; those columns determine every formal
coefficient. A complete 3+1 join streams every ordered triple and looks up
G3G2G1 T among single-call states. It imports no primary or author program
and reads no primary results before sealing its first independent receipt.
Common encoding is only a naming convention, not shared multiplication code.

Both blind first receipts agree on all1,128 actual ordered hit lists,
projector matrices, gate/candidate counts, word hashes and label histograms.

The separate written proof in PROOF.md, including arbitrary spectators and

## Fixtures and controls

The primary checks all72 faithful regular basis columns of two rational
three-register fixtures: a noncommuting block-family pair and an oblique
interleaved pair, each with one arbitrary dirty spectator. Its P is
[[1/3,1/3],[2/3,2/3]] and Q=diag(1,0), so PQ!=QP. These tests use a
diagonal rank-two E with noncoordinate rank-one couplings; arbitrary oblique
E is covered by the written proof, not an additional executed gauge fixture.

Ten primary controls test an actually omitted join halfword, broken
complementarity, reversed written order, a missing factor, boolean or
unsupported field metadata, duplicate/nonfinite JSON, source corruption,
and a nonzero formal group-norm matrix whose ordered-pair image vanishes
over F2. The last is a representation-faithfulness warning example, not a
claimed extra legal four-factor word. The checker never uses that quotient
as its endpoint oracle.

The oracle separately checks a rational noncommuting pair P=diag(1,0),
Q=[[1/2,1],[1/4,1/2]] on all48 active regular columns and all72 columns
with one dirty spectator. Its fixtures differ from the primary's, so their
column hashes are not claimed to agree. Four actual incorrect words
(reversal, omitted last call, duplicate instead of complement, substituted u)
produce explicit nonzero formal coefficient witnesses over F3. A separate
signed group-algebra element has zero natural permutation image; this is
again an algebra-element toy, not a claimed legal-word fault. Oracle strict
source/JSON/typing checks and full receipt comparison remain separate.

Both default modes require a retained RESULT.json or ORACLE.json and exact
recursive type/value equality; booleans cannot stand in for integers. A
missing receipt is a failure. Source text bytes and SOURCE-PINS.json are
authenticated before scientific work. External isolated-copy corruptions,
commit/source/mode checks, official export integrity and read-only Git-free
empty-PATH replays are separate packaging verification, recorded in the
handoff rather than silently equated with mathematical acceptance.
