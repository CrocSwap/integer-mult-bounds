## Limits and credit

The five framework assumptions remain: native-all-columns, owned erasure,
compiler, precision/tape and analytic recovery. The more specific inherited
contracts include exact child returns, odd scalars in Z[1/2,1/3,1/5], owned
ascent A6, per-port/reducible roots, one-field idle and ordinary LAMBDA pricing.
The prefix grid is S=1125. Native/common-context height and envelope checks
are retained in `FULL-RESULT.json`; they do not admit a rounded physical
provider or independently rerun every guard of #6985. No complete fixed-tape
multiplier, external human review or selected-certificate update is claimed.

Finite deterministic cut screens and alternate-order checks found no further
witness in their examined domains. They are not impossibility results; only
the two nominated directions are used in the proof. No generalized owned
scratch split, speculative mirror flip or unpaid boundary correction is used.
