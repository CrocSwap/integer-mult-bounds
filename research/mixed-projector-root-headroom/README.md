# How far the minimum-idle mixed root can go

The complete conditional result is **kappa 0.00328551**. This companion
proves that its ceiling **0.00328554** covers every six-field, seven-query
root with four full stages and at most two extra whole idle jobs, with
arbitrary scalar bases and all positive binary child sizes and shapes.
The native source stays fixed at PR6353. This is a new structural result,
not a further kappa increment.

The [final PR6372 composition](../mixed-root-contact-composition/README.md)
raises the complete claim to 0.00328584 and recalculates the same family's
ceiling at 0.00328587. The classification itself does not depend on the
native source profile. This package retains the first source-bound price
example in its exact receipt.

The proof also shows that at most two full projectors can have rank two
in any shortest-query root on an even number of fields at least four.
On six fields, five, six and seven full stages need at least four, six
and eight extra idle jobs, respectively. The all-width normalization
argument keeps scalar coefficients outside tensor powers.

Read [the proof](PROOF.md) before interpreting the finite replay. Its
free endpoint parameter is retained. The source ceiling applies only
after the output pattern is classified. The separate
[complete composition](../mixed-root-source-composition/README.md)
checks the native source, full caller, price, compiler and outer ledger.

```sh
python3 -I -O -B research/mixed-projector-root-headroom/verify.py
```

The three live inputs are pinned and declared in `SOURCES.json` and
`VERIFY.json`. They are reachable ancestors of this contribution.
`RESULT.json` records the exact structural checks. The complete price
ceiling is recomputed by the separately registered composition package.
This replay authenticates that receipt and proves the larger domain to
which it applies. It does not rerun the native word or price engine.
A separate reverse-functional engine checks all 1,156 formal caller
entries through four exact central characters and rejects seven omitted
operation or off-diagonal controls. It uses no source replay code.

The result remains author-checked and conditional. It does not prove a
global optimum, a practical speedup, a materialized uniform algorithm,
the five framework assumptions or independent scientific acceptance.
The selected frontier stays unchanged.

## Search beyond the proved class

The new exact restrictions were used to screen the next cost level.
An abstract five-full-stage model with ranks 2,2,3,3,4 was tested after
the two rank-three H lines were required to be distinct. Seven support
patterns survived the necessary tests. This search used the fixed PR6353
profile. Floating integer programs used
positive child sizes and total size below 200, with 20 seconds per model.
Every returned integer candidate lost at 0.00328554. All seven runs
reached their time limits with positive upper bounds. They do not exclude
better candidates. No native construction follows from the relaxation.
`SEARCH.json` retains the solver receipts. The optional
`search_next_cost.py --output /tmp/mixed-root-next-cost.json` reproduces
the model with NumPy 2.5.4 and SciPy 1.18.1. Time-limited solver output
need not be identical. These dependencies are not used by exact replay.

The wider bounded searches in the
[source search note](../mixed-projector-query-root/SEARCH.md) also remain
bounded evidence. The proof here does not convert their failures into
a global ceiling. No new positive candidate was withheld from the claim.
