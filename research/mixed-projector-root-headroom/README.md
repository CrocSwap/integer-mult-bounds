# How far the minimum-idle mixed root can go

With the original PR6353 source, the complete conditional result is
**kappa 0.00328551**. This companion
proves that its ceiling **0.00328554** covers every six-field, seven-query
root in the following class: all six outputs contain the central
involution, four independent forward stages act at distinct sites,
and at most two extra whole idle jobs are paid. Scalar bases are arbitrary
but independent of the central and stage variables. All positive binary
child sizes and shapes are permitted.
The native source stays fixed at PR6353. This is a new structural result,
not a further kappa increment.

The [final source composition](../mixed-root-contact-composition/README.md)
refines the complete claim to 0.00328621476572836015146308 with the
same invoice. Its [exact raw optimum](../mixed-root-contact-composition/OPTIMALITY.md)
is less than 2e-26 above that claim. The former exclusion bound
0.00328623 is replaced by the exact raw limit. The classification itself does not depend on the
native source profile. This package retains the first source-bound price
example in its exact receipt.

The proof also shows that at most two full projectors can have rank two
in any shortest-query root on an even number of fields at least four.
On six fields, five, six and seven full stages need at least four, six
and eight extra idle jobs, respectively. The all-width normalization
argument keeps scalar coefficients outside tensor powers.

Read [the proof](PROOF.md) before interpreting the finite replay. Its
free endpoint parameter is retained. The source ceiling applies only
after the output pattern is classified. The separate
[complete composition](../mixed-root-source-composition/README.md)
checks the native source, full caller, price, compiler and outer ledger.

## Replay

Use Python 3.12 and its standard library:

```sh
python3 -I -B research/mixed-projector-root-headroom/verify.py
python3 -I -O -B research/mixed-projector-root-headroom/verify.py
```

The live inputs are pinned and declared in `SOURCES.json` and
`VERIFY.json`. They are reachable ancestors of this contribution.
`RESULT.json` records the exact structural checks. The complete price
ceiling is recomputed by the separately registered composition package.
This replay authenticates that receipt and proves the larger domain to
which it applies. It does not rerun the native word. The extension prices are recomputed
with the pinned rational interval engine and final native invoice.
A separate reverse-functional engine checks all 1,156 formal caller
entries through four exact central characters and rejects seven omitted
operation or off-diagonal controls. It uses no source replay code.

The result remains author-checked and conditional. It does not prove a
global optimum, a practical speedup, a materialized uniform algorithm,
the five framework assumptions or independent scientific acceptance.
The selected frontier stays unchanged.

## Search beyond the proved class

The [extension proof](EXTENSIONS.md) adds two results. A shortest-query
root with extra outputs that omit the central transform reduces to its
active fields; their additional productive price cannot exceed their paid
idles. This result requires exactly r+1 queries on r active fields. It
does not cover seven queries on four active fields. Also, every rank-four
fifth-stage extension that retains the two
rank-two endpoints and two rank-three stages loses for all positive binary
child sizes and shapes. All seven possible masks are covered. The proof
uses integer dynamic programming below total 100 and a concave bound for
the unbounded tail. It is replayed for both the old PR6353 profile and the
final PR6375 profile.

This exact result supersedes the earlier seven time-limited integer runs.
Their original SEARCH.json is preserved as discovery history, not as the
proof. The optional search_next_cost.py still reproduces that old model;
its NumPy and SciPy dependencies are not needed by registered replay.

The [boundary module proof](BOUNDARY-MODULES.md) records the exact
three-field helper-consuming module and five-call reflection border.
Their replays need only the standard library. Both the helper module and
the border with its two direct corrections lose for every binary child
size and shape. The uncorrected border has a favorable formal limit
price, but two nonzero endpoint errors remain. Its precision and recovery
obligations are explicit. It is not another kappa improvement.

The wider bounded searches in the
[source search note](../mixed-projector-query-root/SEARCH.md) also remain
bounded evidence. The proof here does not convert their failures into
a global ceiling. No new positive candidate was withheld from the claim.
