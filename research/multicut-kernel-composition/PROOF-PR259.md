## New sufficient mechanism

The earlier construction only paired equal dirty-response columns. We now treat the actual target-response matrix as a linear code: its short dependencies provide several simultaneous cancellation coordinates. Choose distinct pivot helpers and donor helpers, with no pivot also a donor. At the chosen cut, each pivot's target-response column is the sum of its donors' columns over F2. Choose an explicitly nondegenerate common entrance frame contained in the first required frames of every participant.

Delete that pivot's initial target compensation reads, raise its initial frame to the common entrance, and execute donor += pivot for every donor in its relation. After the retained word reaches the common full frame, execute the inverse shears. All shears form Q=I+N with N^2=0; therefore Q^-1=I-N. Full arbitrary dirty inputs are restored. Donors may be shared when their required entrance frames form a nested chain, executed in increasing dimension. Shared donors pay their frame lift once, instead of independently in every relation.

The invertibility of the target-only prefix map handles the actual target-to-target setup operations. The original helper word followed by Q^-1 restores the original arbitrary dirt. Gates at multiple cuts are admitted only with the corresponding untouched-prefix and response conditions at each actual cut. The actual combined whole word is independently checked on every input column in both directions, so no inference from separate local prices replaces composition.
