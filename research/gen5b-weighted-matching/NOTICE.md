# Attribution and scope

Prepared for George Lydakis with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance, including
Ody's matching research and coordinated independent integration and review.
New contributions are Apache-2.0; no exclusive priority is claimed.

This package contributes donor-rank-weighted compensated matching after producer
endpoint descent, its scoped fixed-graph proof, the regenerated word and literal
composition with PR299 and PR300, a reproducible matching producer, and portable
verification. The second constructed-frame descent and rebind_descent2 ledger are
Rohan Arun's PR300 contribution (inherited from PR291), prepared with Anthropic
Claude assistance. NOTICE-PR300.md preserves that full notice unchanged. Our
contribution here is binding and verifying those 89 retimings on the weighted word,
with freshly derived restoration/sink selections and the complete resulting bill.
Compensated reuse itself, the gen5b modules, exact checkers, retiming, kernels,
early restoration, terminal sinks, complex supplier and pricing mechanisms are
inherited. Chafik Boukhalfa's full PR299 notice is preserved unchanged in
NOTICE-PR299.md, including credits to DreamingOfClouds, Rohan Arun, Eumemic,
Dugongue, utcorvusvolat-dotcom, Henry Grant, Jacob Sussman, Evan McKinney and the
full source chain, with their original assistance disclosures and licenses.

The all-size hypotheses remain conditional. Neither a complete formal proof of
integer multiplication nor global optimality is claimed.
