## Written proof review

I find the all-n argument sound within its stated hypotheses. It is a written mathematical proof, not a proof-assistant theorem. The following is my derivation of the crucial implications rather than reliance on a finite sample of widths.

1. **The allowed phase is discrete.** Conjugation on Paulis has finite image. Its kernel commutes with all X/Z generators and therefore is scalar. For a dyadic Gaussian scalar `(a+bi)/2^k` with norm one, unique factorization forces a unit times `(1+i)^(2k)` in the numerator, leaving one of `1,i,-1,-i`. This proves the normalizer is finite and disallows `(3+4i)/5`. The argument does not discard an allowed global phase.
2. **The trace alphabet is sufficient, including nonmonomial Cliffords.** On the Hermitian Pauli basis, conjugation is a signed permutation. On `ker(S-I)` its signs form a character because the same Pauli multiplication cocycle cancels on both sides. Thus `|tr U|^2` is zero or a power of two. A finite-order dyadic Gaussian matrix has trace in both the algebraic integers and Q(i), hence in Z[i]. Gaussian factorization gives `tr U = i^k(1+i)^d`, `0<=d<=2n`, or zero. For a normalized trace `z=tr U/2^n`, put `j=2n-d`: `z^4=(-1)^j4^(-j)`. The j=0 values are precisely the four scalar values. The filter in PROOF.md kills zero, the nonidentity scalar values, and every j>=1 shell, while taking value one at z=1. For a unitary, trace D forces the exact identity, so it selects equality of full phase-bearing operators.
3. **Grouping exact matrices is essential.** Expand each endpoint entry as `sum c_g g^tensor f` with coefficients independent of f, and first add the coefficients of equal exact matrices. Expanding Hilbert-Schmidt norms gives `E_f=sum conjugate(c_g)c_h z_gh^f`. The filter identity then yields `sum b_f E_f=sum |c_g|^2`. This is valid for arbitrary complex coefficients in the theorem, even though the executable uses Q(i). If all tested energies are zero, every grouped coefficient vanishes. No rational-nullspace argument or floating-point rank test is being silently substituted. Merely projectively equal matrices must remain distinct because their phases are raised to f.
4. **The finite maximum 8n+4 follows for every n.** The filter has zero constant coefficient and degree 8n+4. Thus zero at all widths 1 through 8n+4 implies zero grouped coefficients and hence zero at every positive width. This holds separately for every input/output role and needs independent arbitrary inputs. It does not justify testing only a clean subspace.
5. **The eight selected widths follow for every n>=2, not just tested n.** The length-eight code has 16 words, is self-dual, and every weight is divisible by four. The vector obtained by selecting a codeword independently on every axis is fixed by S, CNOT, native H, and iI. For native H, `((1+i)/2)^8=1/16`, and the code Fourier sum contributes exactly 16; the absolute phase is essential. These generators produce the whole dyadic normalizer: reduce the image of Z1 to Z1; while preserving it, remove the other-axis X/Z components of the image of X1 with CNOT/CZ, then remove its Z1 component and sign. Commutation with both X1 and Z1 leaves `I tensor V`. Iterate, leaving only a dyadic scalar unit. Native H has the ordinary H Pauli action, with its scalar absorbed in that final allowed unit. Consequently every g fixes the eighth-copy code vector. The nonzero injection `v -> v tensor tau` intertwines the f and f+8 residuals. Zero at f+8 therefore implies zero at f. Every width 1..8n+4 ascends into `[8n-3,8n+4]` in steps of eight. Combining with item 4 proves the claim. This descent does not preserve the same stability constant on the eight selected widths.
6. **The stability improvement has an all-n argument.** The four coefficient residue classes of `q_n` are disjoint, so its coefficient L1 norm equals that of P_(2n). The elementary-symmetric-product formula has positive real product factors even for r=-1/4, giving descending signs ++--. When the roots -a,b are added, the sign pattern gives the displayed recurrence for L_new-L. The first odd-index coefficient gives `U_odd/L >= (3/16)/(3/2)=1/8`; meanwhile `ab/(a-b)<=1/192`. Hence the L1 norms strictly decrease for n>=1. At n=1 the norm is 77/75, and at n=0 it is one. Because q_n(1)=1, positive coefficient mass is at most 76/75. Removing negative multiples of nonnegative E_f gives `max E_f >= (75/76) sum |c_g|^2`, for the full range 1..8n+4. Finite polynomial checks in the oracle corroborate this derivation; they do not replace induction.
7. **The n=0 and n=1 exceptions are correctly separated.** C0 consists of the four scalar units; the four positive residues 1,2,3,4 give the Fourier separation. In one axis, exact closure of C and S has 96 matrices; there are at most 24 signed anticommuting Pauli-image pairs and four scalar lifts, so this is the full group. A nonsingular sum of Gram matrices for widths 5..8 implies zero joint kernel over characteristic zero. Modular nonsingularity is legitimate because the entries lie in Z[i,1/2] and odd-prime reduction maps i to a square root of -1. The Reynolds average is an orthogonal projection; traces zero at widths 1..7 imply the zero operator, while rank 15 at width 8 proves a counterexample to every test whose maximum is <=7. Scalar Fourier residuals force at least one width in each residue modulo four, establishing minimum count four. Neither argument proves sharpness for n>=2.

## Compact implementation and trust boundary

`compact.py:80-100` expands literal signed words, interprets gate order by reversing the stored prepend order, and merges full matrices only when their normalized exact Hilbert-Schmidt inner product is one. For unitaries, `||g-h||_HS^2=2D-2Re tr(g*h)` makes that equality criterion exact. The gate grammar contains unitary gates with dyadic entries, so the required hypothesis is maintained by construction.

`compact.certify` sets its positive all-width result from the absence of grouped residual coefficients. A positive result is therefore trusted execution of the word expander, quadratic-sum trace implementation, exact arithmetic, and merge loop. It does **not** emit a separate small independently replayable proof of positive equality. This is a substantiation boundary, not an unsound implication in the stated algorithm.

The existing `check_witness.py` is explicitly a **negative-only** literal witness checker (`:123-136`). It recomputes a single nonzero discrepancy, which suffices to disprove all-width equality without trusting the filter or Gram energies. It does not validate a positive equality receipt or every displayed energy. Moreover, the compact producer imports `word_endpoint` and `add_term` from that checker, so the word expansion is shared in this compact path. The original dense producer and the literal checker have separate compilers; that stronger separation should not be read as applying to every compact component.

There is also an interface distinction: compact emits schema `clifford-tensor-gram/native-v1` with gate circuits; `check_witness.check` accepts `clifford-tensor-gram/v1` with explicit base matrices. A compact JSON receipt is not directly accepted by the existing checker. The author's advance verifier converts through its dense test problem and invokes the discrepancy evaluator. The independent audit does the conversion from separately constructed exact matrices and explicitly rebinds schema/digest. This conversion is part of the audit, not a hidden claim that the CLI already accepts native receipts.

No flaw follows from a checker ignoring a forged energy field while confirming a real nonzero literal entry: that field is outside this negative checker's acceptance statement. A malformed witness value/digest/width, or a purported positive receipt, must be rejected; the bounded tests exercise those distinctions.

## Independent oracle and bounded evidence

`independent_audit.py` owns its arithmetic (pairs of stdlib Fractions), gate matrices, matrix multiplication/adjoint, literal gate composition, tensor products, direct role-block interpreter, exact matrix-key endpoint expansion, one-axis group closure, modular rank elimination, and filter construction. It imports submitted quadratic/compact/checker code only as the device under test. It does not import the submitted arithmetic, compiler, kernel, traces, or path engine as ground truth. No source module is edited.

The discriminating cases include: phase inside a tensor versus an outer scalar; C inverse and q=0; query sign; a self query requiring snapshot semantics; noncommuting gate order; loss of an arbitrary dirty role; role swap and rational scaling; and exact coefficient grouping. Direct small tensor matrices bind the oracle's expansion on widths <=3 (n=2 restricted to width<=2). Witnesses at larger selected widths are evaluated by products of independently constructed base entries, without allocating tensor matrices. The independent 96-matrix one-axis closure is also used for a fresh modular Gram rank and Reynolds ranks; it does not read the submitted inverse as an oracle.

`RESULT.json` records successful execution only after the root resource guard runs the frozen checker. This scope is finite implementation evidence, not exhaustive correctness of arbitrary circuits or arbitrary n. The all-n conclusion comes from the written proof above.

## Complexity and admissibility boundaries

The compact quadratic representation and bitwise conditional norm splitting avoid arrays indexed by all 2^n base states or 2^(nf) tensor states. This is compatible with polynomial arithmetic cost in expanded support and literal path size. The support can grow exponentially in register-program length, and every pair of surviving terms is compared. Integer/Fraction operation counts are not bit complexity bounds; they still depend on operand sizes. The selected provider support measurements are case-specific and cannot establish a general support bound. The small dense matrices in this independent oracle intentionally do not claim the compact implementation's scaling.

No statement here proves physical tape layout, precision/rounding, stock, analytic recovery, a new complete supplier, or a smaller complete kappa. The five framework assumptions remain. I have not independently replayed all 7,000 recipe classes, the two complete selected-provider programs, or the 30,328 fusion obstructions in this assignment.
