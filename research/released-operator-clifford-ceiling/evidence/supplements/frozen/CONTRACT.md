## Explicit paid perturbation that escapes the FREE obstruction

This is a concrete changed test class, not a successful complete decoder.
Choose distinct owner indices a,b and i,j with A[i,a] A[b,j] nonzero. Define

    v = G_(1,b)^-1 q_j,
    w = G_(3,a)^-1 q_i.

Because A has zero diagonal, v has a nonzero U component and w a nonzero
W component. They are nonzero and independent. Let C_v and C_w be the
rank-one Gaussians along the Pauli-X translation directions v,w, with the
source's exact phase convention. Their product H=C_w C_v is one rank-two
Gaussian under a fixed invertible binary address permutation carrying two
coordinate directions to v,w. No extra address axis is introduced.

For a fixed rational coefficient c, set

    S = I + c E_(a,b) tensor H.

Its inverse is I-c E_(a,b) tensor H since a!=b. Both have a literal query
implementation: copy the source field b to a new owned zero field, apply
the rank-two Gaussian with its monomial pre/post address maps, add c times
the result to field a, and clear the owned temporary after its last read.
The source field is retained, and no arbitrary dirty helper is borrowed.
The inverse uses the same H query with the opposite coefficient; it does
not require H^-1. Each use costs one H2, one COPY, one scaled ADD, one
CLEAR, and the fixed monomial address maps, all at their actual precision.

All C directions here are Pauli-X Gaussian directions. They commute with
Q and D3. Therefore S, Q^-1 S Q and D3 S D3^-1 are the same paid operator.
For a product t0=S_m ... S_1, inverse execution reverses the order and
negates each coefficient. The all-width lift preserves each product and
its phase; the owner sums are never tensor-powered.

This changes the obstruction's premise in a visible way. At the selected
endpoint block (i,j), H sends the rank-one chart L(v) to L(w), so the
corresponding term of Q^-1 C3 t0 C1 Q^-1 is monomial. With one S, this new
monomial coefficient is nonzero and cannot be contributed by the baseline
t0=I. This is a real necessary-condition change, not a parity-only claim.

It does not show that the remaining nonmonomial terms vanish, that E has
an explicit FREE inverse, or that a short product suffices. A single local
entry becoming monomial earns no productive target or kappa credit.

use one explicit (a,b,i,j), exact v,w and phase-correct literal H word to

released standalone fresh-query lower bound does not settle such a changed
information boundary, but it does not provide the observation either.
