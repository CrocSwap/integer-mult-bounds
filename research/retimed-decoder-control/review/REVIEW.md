## Actual charts and axes

The binary receiver installs external Jobs with tuple(range(N)); its zero
operation preserves the exemplar's integer axes. The original native source
has exactly three paid branches: MOVE difference, QUERY head and IDLE_ONE empty
basis. Private matcher/restoration words are not paid Job bases.

The independent census follows the literal graph and caller: 18,906 targets and
104,904 paid occurrences, consisting of 100,908 MOVE, 444 QUERY and 3,552 IDLE_ONE,
with 2,481 distinct requests. Each difference word is evaluated densely, then
its projector is checked against independently computed original projectors.
Each joint word is checked against its exact original frame. This validates
right-action chronology in addition to source hashes.

The common dictionary has 595 words, maximum length 61, and five-bit signed
numerator and denominator bounds. Its 215,644 bytes have SHA256
`734035814cb639382a12a81e7f408b222f949cd69ad28a28544aeb5d0490d3f9`, below 2^21
bits. Different column multipliers (1,3,7) check the original operation-major,
lane-minor expansion and ordered masks.

An Axis is (basis,column). Erasing the basis, sorting the mask, or collapsing
typed empty bases into integer axes changes the interface value. The codec
preserves all these distinctions. Selections are ordered subsequences, so no
new basis is introduced. `job_frame` accepts value-equal bases; reconstruction
does not need original tuple identity. A first-token convention handles
multiple token descriptions of the same exact word without changing a value.

## Decoder, delayed loans and callback continuation

The independent calendar creates appointments from each birth and sorts them;
original Plan.events instead loops over ticks, bands and paid indices. Equality
of their full records checks delayed loans, closes and exact event counts.

A separate serialized interpreter is compared to original Plan.decode by full
ordered service traces. Fixtures cover zero creation, successful and failed
external-index guards, nontrivial ordered typed bases, selected loans, child
return, owner reuse and closing resumes. These are supplied calendars and
abstract numerical services, not production mass calculations.

Every event boundary roundtrips the entire decoder state. Every leaf callback
roundtrips the **full paid Job** and five continuation controls before selecting
the one-axis argument. Retaining only the leaf argument loses later axes and
the selected prefix; a negative control exhibits this loss.

The inherited inclusive count is appropriately conservative: live<C=2N^83,
zero entries<=PC and pending<=qC. A pending record belongs to a paid resume of
a still-live origin, so charging all q records per live origin suffices. Owner
counts and descriptor counts differ; no simultaneous-maxima assumption is used.

## Event tape, widths and universal allowance

The whole immutable event tape is necessary: otherwise the original generator's
pending map and counters remain uncharged. There are TS opens, qTS paid records
and TS closes, where T=N^82 and S<2N^6. Their maximum scalar counts are 3+4P,
five and two. Thus K_events<=w+TS(5+4P+5q)w<BNh, with w=128h. The source resets
serials on each pass and depends only on the fixed Plan, so the tape is reusable
over groups and rounds. Its kinds and lengths are encoded; no free separators
or arbitrary external table are required.

Dynamic scalars fit: owners<2N^92, context identifiers<2N^89, paid serials<N^90,
source/trial row indices<3N^92 and image/tape offsets<N^96. Negative births are
filtered before emission. Source base indices follow allocated intervals, and
the round guard rejects a cycle reaching an interval end. These estimates fit
128h bits with substantial room.

Here is an independent uniform proof of the budget. Put P=18906, q=104904,
J=4w+1+Nh<2Nh and A=5+4P+5q. Divide by BNh=N^93 h. Sufficient ratios are

    state: 2 +640N^-93 +(768+256q)N^-10 +4(P+q)N^-9,
    tape:  128N^-93 +256A N^-5,
    transfer: (4P+16+256)N^-92 +128(4P+20)N^-93,
    dictionary: 2^21 N^-93.

Every nonconstant term is positive times a negative power of N. Exact rational
evaluation at N0=2^30 proves state<3, tape<1 and twice the state plus all other
terms<8. Every production N>=2^3000 exceeds N0; monotonicity proves these bounds
for all production heights. The review accepts the author's looser 32, whose
slack also pays the fixed final-byte padding. This argument does not extrapolate
from sampled heights.

The single expanded Job bridge fits 256Nh: at most 61N operations each need a
finite opcode, two h-bit coordinates and two five-bit rational fields, plus
mask and header. At h>=3000 there is ample room. Two partial P-entry lists are
separately charged; possible aliases do not reduce the estimate.

Finally W/B>=9^N, h<=N and 4N^2<9^N for N>=2. The last inequality is strict at
two and propagates since 9N^2>(N+1)^2. Hence 4BNh<W and the published strict
bit/byte comparison follows. The author writes a weak intermediate chain, but
its strict conclusion has this valid immediate strengthening.

## Native boundary and warm histories

A service slot names the resident native body; it does not serialize it. The
original native source retains registers, productive entries, restoration words
and expanded bases. The exclusion is essential: different arbitrary private
states can have the same slot and identical interface trace. A negative control
makes this distinction explicit.

The author confirmed the value-level boundary: decoder partial lists are
tokenized records; expanded copies retained by native services receive separate
charges. One currently transferred expanded Job is paid by the bridge. Literal
Python unpacking is a correctness emulator, not a bounded-memory interpreter.
In particular there is no claim that all expanded P port Jobs are paid by the
single-Job bridge; retained copies belong to the still-open service obligation.

For fixed installed roles, same-stage ancestor volume sums to less than
W(1+B^-1+...)<2W. The inherited stage entrance bounds give
2 sum_(k=1)^7 2^k T_ternary=508 T_ternary<4572X. These are coordinatewise role
allowances; erasure does not shrink historical endpoints. Different public
shapes use X_max, and initial reservations require the stated per-role maximum.
The conclusion retains all excluded native, setup and header obligations.

## Reproduction and controls

    python3 -B work/review-storage/review.py --output work/review-storage/RESULT.json
    python3 -O -B work/review-storage/review.py --output work/review-storage/RESULT-OPT.json

The checker also accepts `--package /path/to/decoder-control`; its default finds
the parent package when installed in that package's `review/` directory.
The final fixtures are (q,ticks,replicas)=(2,1,1),(3,5,2),(4,6,1),(5,3,2),
each at three group/round choices. The six-tick case retains overlapping
origins, all delayed-loan branches and tails/closing resumes after the birth
window. Extending it to 22 ticks exercises no additional source branch and was
removed to keep complete-image replay bounded. Exact whole-image semantics and
the full paid-label/chart census are unchanged.

The checker uses explicit conditions that survive optimization. Receipts record
exact case counts and trace hashes. Negative controls cover invalid owners and
axes, mixed kinds, unsupported bases, truncation, nonzero padding, mask sorting,
basis erasure, typed identity collapse, omitted zeros/pending, lost full-Job
continuation, unpaid event fields and arbitrary unrepresented native state.

Remaining obligations include native restoration/register state, other receiver
controls, setup, physical headers, accesses and movement. No canonical assumption
or selected exponent changes as a result of this review.
