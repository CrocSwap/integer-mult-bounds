## General cost argument and credit

Cinna Davis's [PR4696](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/4696),
head `def829364e8e53a74eac7997b49b5366afd5bc29`, supplies the stronger written
rank-debt argument for arbitrary paid symmetric-form changes on the retained
fixed-subspace graph. We independently replay its source checks and audit the
helper paths. The narrower one-edge classification does not replace or claim
priority over that result.

The new `phase-debt-audit/RankDebt.lean` formalizes the general finite-list
analytic inequality. When a helper's extra rank is bounded by its own added
paid ranks and every old paid block rank is at most its original helper rank,
concavity bounds helper reward by paid cost. The source-specific path facts,
rank grammar and complete physical semantics remain separate premises.

[PR4690](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/4690), head
`ddb7ead76479b1e281b485a78b65ce21cc7de8d7`, supplies related charged non-nested,
symmetric-phase and common-Pauli search models. Overlapping ambient-form
sweeps were stopped after this work was inspected. Our active masks are
chart-dependent choices, and odd masks change the actual Fourier rowspace;
that escapes the fixed-subspace debt premise but has not yielded a gain.

[PR4700](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/4700), head
`34434d4ea5d9edf58529eaf79b543cd6c58f3e41`, reports a 64-rank center floor
for its stated global monotone retiming model. That separate result is
credited, not independently re-proved here. It does not claim a bound on
every changed protocol or the optimal kappa.

## Lean and implementation boundary

Eight fresh modules in the round-seven packages expose **29 standard-axiom
reports**: two independent preprocessing modules (seven reports), grouped
phase/route/palette algebra (fifteen), the Pauli hole (two), and the general
finite-list debt inequality (five). Exact source, log, object, compiler,
dependency and input provenance is recorded by their individual receipts.
The artifacts use Lean 4.21.0 and pinned mathlib, with only `propext`,
`Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` in exported axiom reports.

These scoped proofs do not formalize the Python native amplitude evaluator,
all-size physical execution or the complete integer-multiplication compiler.
Finite controls and exact invoices are distinguished from kernel proofs;
solver optimality is distinguished from an exact mathematical exclusion.

`check.py` verifies the frozen source/receipt inventory, unchanged baseline
pins and the actual 29 exported axiom reports. Use `--objects` to check the
local compiled-object hashes as well. It does not rerun Lean; each package's
README gives its separate fresh replay commands. Do not regenerate frozen
receipts during an integrity check.

The grouped route retains same-offset, disjoint K-bit windows. The saved
overlap counterexample is explicitly outside that contract and is rejected.
No tensor of f phase gates is replaced by one uncharged global phase. The
actual canonical K phases, true adjoints and all newly paid warps remain in
the invoice.

## Next construction contract

A useful next attempt changes the owner protocol, fuses an actual shared
operation across a scalar contact, changes the main/query type or changes
the center caller. First emit a complete arbitrary-input operator with all
owner returns and phase/normalization bills. A new recursive type needs an
all-f producer/consumer identity and a paid recurrence before any exponent
can be credited. Larger fixed-graph phase sweeps alone do not evade the
rank-debt argument. No improvement is assumed to exist.
