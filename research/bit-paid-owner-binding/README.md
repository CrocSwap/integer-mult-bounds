## Actual source and finite receipt

The native PR589 record stream has 27,234 records. The source's frame-difference interpretation of a COPY is retained; this audit does not re-prove or reprice that primitive.

`OWNER-JOBS.json.gz` emits every raw paid job as `[rank, physical_field, controller_event, local_record_or_port, kind]`, plus the productive field ranks. Native main roles use the source's side reversal on backward calls, and helpers belong to their actual full-call stage. The query-only source contract issues its rank-one call on private Z. IDLE jobs use each actual main owner and the surviving thick-stage rank. Scalar ADD and SWAP events act in place on those fields. The complete actual paid-rank histogram agrees with the unchanged source invoice:

- **11,454 productive fields:** 3,552 main fields and 7,902 independent helper fields; one additional private owned field is excluded from productive credit.
- **57,555 chronological paid positions:** exact order and native source references are preserved, including COPY, thin-query and IDLE calls.
- **18 local COPY lifetime records:** nine forward and nine reverse templates, reused sequentially at their actual full-call sites; every source stays immutable, every buffer is zero on entry, and every clear follows the last actual buffer read.

The compressed payload is compared after decompression against the regenerated complete list; its SHA256 binds canonical JSON, not a platform-dependent gzip container. The ordinary replay modifies no files.

## Source-bound product implication

Let `o(ell)` be the emitted raw owner's field at paid position `ell`, with rank `r_ell`; let actual productive role `j` have target rank `h_j`. The retained product algorithm instantiates one complete U copy for each productive V role `j`, at column count `h_j`. At common chronological position `ell`, it suspends those copies and invokes V at column count `r_ell` on their fields `(U-copy j, o(ell))`.

For different `j`, those fields belong to disjoint U copies even when `o(ell)` is the reused private center. Thus the input-role map is injective. The requested rank and supplied target rank are both `r_ell*h_j`. Whole arbitrary-input job correctness applies to correlated COPY values too; it does not require their statistical independence. All complete spectators and priced input/output adapters remain part of the inherited whole-job contract. At multiplicity `f>0`, tensoring each selected coordinate with `I_f` multiplies both ranks by `f` and leaves this field-identity argument unchanged.

The child V copy has its own private center, disjoint from every suspended U center. Its completed job returns and all role routes/adapters are undone before a U copy resumes. The actual local native COPY templates then read and clear their own centers in source order. This supplies exactly the native chronology premises missing from the predecessor's BIT-shaped examples. It does not replace Mark/Matarener's generic live-role induction with a new allocator.

The raw product has `11,454^2 = 131,194,116` productive arrays and `57,555^2 = 3,312,578,025` paid positions. None of this Cartesian word is materialized. A bounded source-derived witness takes two actual V roles of unequal target ranks at the first actual U COPY-job position, and calls the predecessor's unchanged `Compiler.push/pop` primitives on their named native field identities. Its recorded nested stack is a logical interface check of the retained product schedule, **not an implementation of physical tape allocation**. Separate controls reject collapsing the two U-copy fields, substituting an unscaled partial job, and clearing a parent while its child remains live. A mutation of the actual native scalar trace clears the buffer before its last ADD and fails exact cleanup, with the lost input polynomial recorded.

Completion is performed after the whole product has returned, on unused axes of the same owner; splitting creates sequential paid rounds on that owner. Neither transformation merges arrays or clears a suspended center. The existing whole-job normalization, completion/splitting proof and I1–I6 compiler result therefore apply to this bound raw chronology under their original hypotheses. The raw controller itself has overlapping target/paid ranks; it is not passed directly to the separated lockstep rule constructor. Strictification remains the selected source's prerequisite, and no assertion is removed to bypass it.

## Replay and limits

CPython 3.12, standard library only. All retained replay bytes and their exact original paths/hashes are in `SOURCES.json`; direct replay needs no Git, network, external packages, native matrix search or large archive. The single raw list is under 100,000 jobs.

```sh
python3.12 -I -W error -B research/bit-paid-owner-binding/verify.py
python3.12 -I -W error -O -B research/bit-paid-owner-binding/verify.py
python3.12 -B scripts/research_package.py --package research/bit-paid-owner-binding --check-only
```

Expected marker: `PASS_BIT_PAID_OWNER_BINDING`. `--write-artifacts` explicitly regenerates the deterministic receipt and compressed canonical raw-owner payload. The source inventory/lifetime run took 0.20 seconds on CPython 3.12.14/macOS arm64; no full existing audit suite, `v=2` Cartesian word, 1024-fold word, Lean, Docker, production build, selected cold replay or physical tape program was run.

The observable contract is the actual raw native paid-owner order, complete-job rank matching and COPY lifetime at its source record boundary. A wrong owner, partial target substitution or premature clear is a credible regression. The predecessor's independent executor tests random BIT-shaped chronology and complex p2, but explicitly leaves actual BIT binding open; this package owns that source-to-interface link. It reuses the existing stack primitive and adds no production exports, allocator, compiler flag or native implementation.
