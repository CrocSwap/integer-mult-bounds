All independent native helper jobs retired by a replacement are debited.

The first three calls offer gross +55.00236355614 raw with four new Type A queries per port. Their exact opposite-sign difference requires at least six. The complete census gives a strictly negative optimistic raw price for every window, uniformly from the incumbent crossing through the retained BIT cap. The largest upper is below −43.31626802209 raw. Both exact engines agree, no window remains unresolved, and the verified κ change is zero. [Proof and price](PROOF.md).
