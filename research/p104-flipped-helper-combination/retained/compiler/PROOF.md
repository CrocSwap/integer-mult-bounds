## 1. The theorem being instantiated

with arbitrary independent productive arrays, a chronological list of paid

initialized/erased owned fields. Its §6 expressly permits a paid owner to
contain a correlated value or to be an owned center. The parent waits for the
entire child and its adapters before continuing. It does not identify a frame

`SPECIALIZATION.md` §§1–4 supplies the permitted class: fixed finite

phases, positive Gaussian children, and the retained owner/restoration

positive implementation theorem for a fixed catalogue under retained
physical hypotheses, not permission for an arbitrary RAM permutation.

Here is the precise conclusion proved below. Let the source word's signed

two-chain origin and repeated frame types impose no additional compiler

## 4. Product construction with an independent ambient cap

The retained product note specializes to units whose ambient rank equals
their maximum productive rank. The following slightly more general statement
is needed here and follows constructively from its algorithm.

Let U,V have ambient caps A_U,A_V, containing every target and paid selected
axis set. At paid position l of U, instantiate U for each productive role j
of V at column count h_j. That instance uses ambient `A_U*h_j<=A_U*A_V`.
Suspend all such copies at the common position l, and invoke V at column
count r_l on their distinct actual owners. It uses ambient
`A_V*r_l<=A_U*A_V`. Whole-job adapters place the requested rank `r_l*h_j`
jobs on the proper coordinate sets; all unused coordinates remain complete
spectators. V and its adapters finish before any parent resumes.

Thus the physical product fits ambient cap `A_U*A_V`, with targets `k_i*h_j`
and paid ranks `r_l*s_a`. The maximum target need not equal the ambient cap.
The moments still multiply, every productive field has its distinct pair
label `(i,j)`, and all owned banks have separate occurrence tags. No equality
`A_U=max k_i` or `A_V=max h_j` was used in this construction. Only the
displayed containment inequalities were needed.

For v raw copies here, the correct conservative ambient is **153^v**, while
the maximum target is **145^v**. The source's primitive coordinates really
use9+18×8=153 axes. Rank145 is therefore not, by itself, a certificate of
145-axis working geometry. A smaller geometry might exist, but it would
require its own full adapter and internal-support construction.

## 6. Ambient-cap version of the exact finite flow

Let a separated mixed unit have targets k with multiplicities W_k, paid
ranks r_j, and a fixed integer ambient cap A at least every target and paid
rank. Assume its whole-job and owner contracts as above. In the exact flow
of `FINITE-PRECOMPILATION.md` §§3–5, replace the truncation by

\[
 A\rho_s c(a)\le N,
 \qquad c(a)=\prod_k k^{a_k},\qquad
 u(a)={|a|!\over\prod a_k!}\prod_k W_k^{a_k}.
\]

Take G to be the greatest |a| with `A*c(a)<=N`; use the ordinary sequential
program for N<A. At class s of depth d, keep the usual units |a|=G−d
satisfying this truncation. The Pascal identity for u is unchanged.

**Every parent demand still matches.** A paid rank r_j from parent index a
is matched to child target k at `b=a-e_k`. Its child ambient is
`A*rho_s*r_j*c(b)=(r_j/k)A*rho_s*c(a)<=N`, since `r_j<k`.
Therefore no demanded child unit is removed by the larger ambient cap.

**The unmatched jobs are legitimate top sources.** For a deeper child unit
whose corresponding parent is absent, `A*rho_parent*c(b)*k>N`.
Its source rank is `s0=k*rho_child*c(b)>r_j*N/A>=N/A`.
It is at most N because `k<=A` and the child unit itself satisfies the
ambient truncation. Root-class sources also exceed N/A: otherwise adding
one target factor would contradict the maximality of G. Hence
`N/A<s0<=N`, exactly the interval needed for paid source complements.

**All exits shrink.** Since `k_min^G<=N/A`, depth-G paid exits obey

\[
 e\le r_{max}^{G+1}
 \le r_{max}(N/A)^\theta,
 \qquad \theta={\log r_{max}\over\log k_{min}}<1.
\]

The same depth/round order is finite and acyclic. The product or lcm of
the smaller-program widths clears every source-complement and exit-batch
denominator; all edges remain whole jobs on actual arrays. The larger cap
has not introduced fractional arrays or copied productive inputs.

Cancellation of matched internal jobs gives the same t-superpotential
inequality. For source fractions `y=s0/N>1/A`, concavity gives

\[
 1-(1-y)^t\ge ty\ge \eta y^t,
 \qquad\eta=tA^{t-1}.
\]

Thus every step of the retained leaf-count induction holds with the ambient
cap A. The logarithmic majorant from `STRICTIFICATION.md` §4 becomes

\[
 L(N)\le N^t(1+\log_2N)^p
\]

provided `theta<theta_bar=a/b<1`,
`2^(b-a) r_max^b<=A^a`, `theta_bar^p<eta`, and `A^(1-t)<2^p`.
This proof does not require a productive target of rank A. It applies to
any source satisfying its stated geometry and complete-job hypotheses,
including another supplier category with different A, k_min and r_max.
No such other category is admitted solely by this lemma.

## 8. Transfer to verified boundary-preserving rewrites

The instantiation is deliberately parametric. A changed native may be used
when it proves the same four-main endpoint and complete autonomous helper
endpoints on all fresh inputs, legal current-chart/owner chronology, a fully
recounted ordered paid list and finite charged scalar/adapter work. Helpers
may be split only with their separately proved arbitrary-input cut contract.
No old field count or scalar envelope is transferred automatically.

A caller constant-block replacement may be used when it proves the complete
integer matrix at every block boundary, keeps every nonconstant event and
its owner/phase/geometry metadata, and prices its new intermediate growth.
Then unchanged events see identical actual full input arrays, so their
physical and whole-job proofs transfer. The raw ambient stays153 when no
address coordinate is added. Target and paid profiles are always recounted.

These conditions explain how the separately reviewed46-split native and
compressed caller use this lemma. They do not recertify a later scientific
payload by its name, nominal histogram, or lack of a new instruction type.
Their updated moments, complete budgets and outer inequalities remain
separate exact-source receipts. The generic p=2300 geometry bound covers
their tested saving while it remains at most0.0036.
