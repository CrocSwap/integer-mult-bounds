# Bounded combination investigation

The donor assigned the exact question: can #6917 and #6916 combine for a
larger improvement? Both heads and canonical main were read and pinned.
The latest open-PR list through #6917 and the source discussion were checked;
no claim of exhaustive novelty follows from that overlap check.

## Why a fresh construction was necessary

#6916's source has 416 mains and a homogeneous two-chain caller. #6917 has
400 mains, a mixed 96/8-port caller and altered helper equations. Its eight
mirror flips, valves and deletions change the prefix and suffix operators.
The old 68 witnesses cannot be accepted merely because both sources have
rank-nine helpers. The mixed endpoint and port binding were first built
into the forward, reverse and pricing checks.

The original #6917 primary checker passed before new cuts were searched.
Separate formal reverse checks also passed for its complete native and
78-role caller, including nine corruption controls. Its freshly recounted
ordinary total is 989,502,552 after adding the entire LAMBDA translation
debit; the reviewed source figure without that extra debit is 976,942,552.

## Screen and bounded pass

The dyadic-unit screen found 2,222 candidate variants in 156,228 paired tests.
It examined 39,939 prefix systems with 19,518 snapshots. Candidates were
ordered by their predicted complete moment gain at saving 0.00357027.
Only single public MOVE retimings at an original endpoint were allowed.
Each accepted candidate was reconstructed, replayed exactly, and required
to retain the original paid operator histogram and full ordinary allowance.

The pass stopped at its 900-second limit: 1,885 template attempts, 58 accepted
cuts and 1,827 source-boundary rejections. It did not exhaust the 1,906
unchanged-paid templates. Since the frozen result exceeded both source PRs,
the stop rule required moving to verification rather than another search
round. The time-dependent accepted prefix is not a portable optimality
certificate. `WITNESSES.json` is a deterministic, hash-bound record of the
chosen result, and `check.py` reconstructs it without trusting the search.

The construction's 58 splits replace 58 rank-nine helpers with complementary
positive ranks, preserving rank mass and every paid native operator. The
complete crossing rises from about 0.003530104424 to 0.003579065166. The
compatible type-A caller compression independently lowers ordinary cost;
it does not change this crossing.

No further-candidate, all-frame, all-basis, universal barrier, or optimum
claim is made. Finite search success is used only to supply witnesses for
the separate exact checks and the conditional proof.
