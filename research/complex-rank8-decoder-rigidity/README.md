# Rank-8 complex decoders are gauge-rigid: the E-graph-only eight-term copy-cost floor (no new κ)

**Status: research note and scoped structural result (a proof). No κ claim, no new supplier.** The selected
record (κ = 0.001672660197076918 from PR1094 at the base commit) and every existing file are
unchanged; this package only adds `research/complex-rank8-decoder-rigidity/`. The complex supplier's decoder
constant discussed below (cst = 64 on P112) is the same in the PR942 lineage on which the work started and
in the current selection.

## What this establishes

The selected complex supplier is the binding side of the current κ. Its copied-information cost is the
decoder constant `cst = 64` on the P112 unit (112 E8 root lines, labels in F2^9), which enters the T37
deficit `37·(8·112 − 9·64) = 11840`; one cst unit is worth about 2.8% of the complex saving b.

PR717 (DreamingOfClouds, open) set up the exact copy-cost model for this decoder. In its model (row D1(a))
a term whose row and column supports are label-orthogonal costs nothing, every odd root pair is
label-orthogonal, so exactness is only required on the *E-graph* (the diagonal and the orthogonal pairs);
the odd entries of the target `2G` are free. PR717 then showed (D1(b)) that **with exactly 8 costly terms**
the cost is at least 64, but its support step (that the costly supports are hyperplane-flat complements,
the W(E8)-orbits of the fundamental weights) was recorded as ARGUED: it rested on the tangent-space
rigidity at the coordinate decoder (D1(d)) plus an orbit census, with *global* rigidity only sampled (10 of
12 random starts, RECORDED). PR968 (merged) proved the corresponding floor rigorously, but only for decoders
exact on **all** live pairs, where the normal form `D = RA, T = RB` follows at once from `rank 2G = 8`; it
lists E-graph-only exactness as not covered. The gap between the two models is exactly the question
whether E-graph-only exactness admits rank-8 decoders outside that normal form.

This package closes that gap:

**Theorem 1 (rigidity).** On P112 (and on the unpunctured P120), every real matrix K of rank ≤ 8 with
`K_ii = 4` and `K_ij = 0` on orthogonal root pairs equals `D (2G) D^{-1}` for a diagonal matrix D with
nonzero entries. The rank-8 solutions of the E-graph system form a single orbit of the diagonal torus, and
no odd entry of such a K vanishes. "P112" means the 120 lines minus *any* frame: W(E8) acts transitively on
the 2025 frames (checked) and the E-graph system is W(E8)-invariant, so the statement covers PR717's
FRAME0 `{e_2a ± e_2a+1}` and the coordinate frame `{2e_i}` of the Hamming description used by the shipped
word alike.

**Corollary 2 (eight costly terms).** In PR717's E-graph-only model, every decoder with exactly eight costly
terms has PR968's normal-form supports `C(a_k)`, `C(b_k)` for a dual basis `A Bᵀ = 2I`; `(RA, RB)` is then
literally an all-pairs-exact decoder with the same supports and costs, so PR968's Theorem 4 gives
`cst ≥ 64` on P112 and `cst ≥ 72` on P120. This reaches the conclusion of PR717 D1(b) by a proved route
(the supports of a general dual basis are *not* hyperplane-flat complements, so PR968's Lemma 1 is what
carries the floor), and extends PR968's floor to E-graph exactness at p = 0 and for any frame puncture.

**Beyond one frame (PROOF.md §10).** The stabiliser of FRAME0 (generated by single and double root
reflections) is transitive on the 112 lines and has three orbits on the 1260 frames inside P112 (sizes
28/336/896 — PR968's inadmissible and admissible frames). On one representative per orbit and on P111, the
verifier re-establishes whichever finite inputs of the proof hold: **Theorem 1 transfers verbatim to the 336
P104 units of type (2,2,2,2)** (all classes complete, zero exclusion, witnesses), giving `cst ≥ 56` there for
eight costly terms; zero exclusion (Lemma 4) holds on P111 and on *all* 1260 P104 units, with the type-(4,4)
case needing the full set of necessary conditions; for the 896 admissible units (PR717's P104 among them),
the 28 type-(4,4) units and P111 the missing input is named exactly (incomplete classes, resp. two blocks
without a live class), and rigidity there rests on numerical evidence only.

What this does **not** do: it says nothing about decoders with nine or more costly terms (PR717's open
mixed-β problem; in the E-graph model its locus is the rank ≥ 9 solutions of the same system together with
the ≥ 9-term factorisations of the rank-8 solutions), about rigidity on P111 and on the other 924 P104 units
(numerical evidence only), or about any physical word. It changes no κ.

From the repository root, Python 3.10+, standard library only, about 10 s of CPU (up to ~30 s wall) per unit:

```sh
python3 -I -B research/complex-rank8-decoder-rigidity/verify.py               # P112
python3 -I -B research/complex-rank8-decoder-rigidity/verify.py --live P120   # unpunctured control
```

`--out /new/file.json` writes the complete certificate (log, pivot, CNF statistics and digests, proof hashes,
sampled costs). `--dimacs FILE` dumps the zero-pattern CNF (with the "some entry vanishes" clause) so that
any SAT solver can regenerate the refutation; `make_proof.py` does this with python-sat (not needed to verify;
`--families two,diag` produces the reduced proofs).

## Proof in one paragraph

Pivot on a live frame F chosen so that the punctured lines form whole classes (28 of the 1260 live frames of
P112 qualify): `K[F,F] = 4I₈`, so `K = ¼ K[:,F] K[F,:]` and the problem becomes bilinear in the rows
`a_i = K[i,F]` and columns `b_j = K[F,j]`. Each non-pivot line is odd to exactly four pivot lines; the
14 four-sets form the Steiner system S(3,4,8), each carrying two classes of four mutually orthogonal lines
(a D4 with the pivot lines). Two orthogonal lines in different blocks share exactly two pivot coordinates,
so their equation has two terms: `a_ik b_jk + a_ik' b_jk' = 0`. A propositional argument (shipped as a
checked refutation, needing only the two-term and diagonal equations) shows no entry of any a_i or b_i on
its block can vanish. Then two rows of a class that agree on a sign product are forced parallel on that
coordinate pair by any live orthogonal witness, the twelve parallelism relations leave exactly two solution
families, one of which has a rank-1 class matrix and is excluded, and the other is `A_C = diag(δ) H_C diag(γ)`.
The two-term equations between blocks make the column scalings γ agree across blocks up to a scalar, which
the 3-design turns into a global potential g on F. Everything then reads `K_if = 2G_if d_i/d_f`, and
`K = ¼ K[:,F]K[F,:] = D(2G)D^{-1}`.

## Numerical probes (evidence, not proof)

`probe_numeric.py` (numpy, optional) runs Levenberg–Marquardt from random starts on the E-graph system in
rank 8 and tests each converged solution for diagonal gauge equivalence to 2G. Recorded runs (seed, trials):

| unit | tangent nullity at 2G | random starts | gauge-equivalent | stalled (no solution) | non-gauge solutions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P120 | 183 = 64 + 120 − 1 | 8 (seed 1) | 8 | 0 | 0 |
| P112 | 175 = 64 + 112 − 1 | 6 (seed 7) | 5 | 1 | 0 |
| P104, PR717/PR968 unit (type (1,…,1)) | 167 = 64 + 104 − 1 | 6 (seed 11) | 5 | 1 | 0 |
| P104, type (4,4) representative | 167 = 64 + 104 − 1 | 8 (seed 5) | 8 | 0 | 0 |

The tangent nullity equals the gauge dimension `dim GL₈ + (number of lines) − 1` in all cases, as PR717
D1(d) found for P112. Trial counts are from single recorded runs. For the type-(1,…,1) and type-(4,4) P104
units these lines are the only evidence of rigidity; Theorem 1 is claimed on P104 only for type (2,2,2,2).

## Scope and inherited obligations

- **Covered.** Real matrices of rank ≤ 8 satisfying the E-graph system on P112 (any frame puncture), P120
  and the 336 P104 units of type (2,2,2,2); the class of decoders with exactly eight costly terms in PR717's
  D1(a) model on these units. Zero exclusion (Lemma 4) on P111 and on all 1260 P104 units.
- **Not covered.** n ≥ 9 costly terms (PR717's mixed-β open problem is open as before; with a rank-8 sum
  Theorem 1 still describes K but no support statement follows, and for rank ≥ 9 the pivot reduction fails);
  rigidity on P111, on the 28 type-(4,4) P104 units and on the 896 admissible P104 units (PROOF.md §10 names
  the missing input for each; numerical evidence only); punctures of more than two frames or of other shapes; the corollary over complex coefficients (Theorem 1
  itself is algebraic and holds over any field of characteristic zero; Corollary 2 over ℂ would need PR968's
  theorem over ℂ, PR1077, which is not invoked); any realization question.
- **Inherited conditions.** Corollary 2 inherits PR968's one cited, not recomputed, premise — the
  classification of E8 hyperplane flats into eight W(E8)-types (PR968 (F6)) — and PR968's written argument,
  which has had no independent human review. All of the selected record's assumptions are untouched: all-size
  compiler, common-label and mutable-source realization, whole-call hosting, precision/tape, analytic
  transfer, the bit interface. Nothing here is a formal proof of the multiplication theorem. The written argument has had
  two rounds of AI-assisted adversarial review, and a reviewing agent re-implemented its finite facts from
  scratch (not shipped); it has had no independent human review.
