# Review and verification disposition

This package was prepared for @CubeJerry with substantial OpenAI
assistance. Two separately assigned source-aware AI reviews challenged
different obligations before integration. They are not independent human
reviews or formal proof-kernel acceptance.

## Numerical and original-source review

`allocation_review.py` and [ALLOCATION-REVIEW.md](ALLOCATION-REVIEW.md)
independently derive the effective upper matrix from stored reciprocal
pages, check the two distinct perturbation admissions, and verify the
actual completed-equation residual and carrier bounds. Their exact
Gauss-Jordan oracle does not call `pages.lu`, `pages.exact_solve` or
`pages.rounded_oracle` for its matrix-inverse expectation. The fixed
27-case domain and the actual Reference Schur case are registered in
both normal and optimized Python.

The complete physical source fixture also compares every returned bit
with the page schedule and the separately written rational rounded
equations. Its original-Schur inverse is checked by all matrix-product
identity entries. The final error inequality is exact rational
arithmetic; decimal values are summaries only. Physical wrap and unfold
controls fail the original-matrix expectation by more than its allowance.

## Physical layout and ownership review

`independent_physical.py` and
[TRANSPORT-REVIEW.md](TRANSPORT-REVIEW.md) were authored separately from
the folded implementation. Their expectation is the original-order
equation d_i*y_i=x_i for exact nonuniform diagonal systems. It does not
reuse the author's fold or numerical stage oracle to decide the answer.
The review covers singleton/odd/even sizes, multiple RHSs, smaller reuse,
borrowed preservation, inactive descendants and full retained erasure.

Three source issues were corrected before the final package replay:

* Origin admission initially consulted a backend head integer; it now
  performs paid local delimiter operations.
* The owned-role inventory initially omitted inactive word descendants;
  they are now included in both alias rejection and erasure inspection.
* The parent factory scope remained installed across the child call;
  it is now suspended, retaining parent leases while child allocations
  use child roles. The observed parent peak fell from 27 to 15.

No numerical result is inferred merely from those changes. The complete
current source is replayed, including aliases to an inactive twiddle role
as either payload or Count descriptor, pending-output rejection with a
head on blank, and failure after a preceding successful RHS.

The physical reviewer subsequently assisted in packaging the final
source fixture and documents. The earlier independently authored
expectation and controls are retained with that provenance; this is not
a claim that every final packaging edit was independently authored.
The lead conducts final source/export review separately.

## What the receipts establish

`RESULT.json` is the actual four-RHS Reference execution receipt. It
records exact output words, original-source identity, numerical bounds,
primitive actions, product calls and retained cells. Independent checks
print their own source hashes and generated receipts. Finite arithmetic
fixtures use the inherited word engine's base branch; the FFT branch's
correctness and asymptotic cost remain explicitly inherited propositions.

Registered replay authenticates ten exact live source files/archives.
The committed export additionally checks package/input byte integrity and
is replayed without Git from an unrelated working directory. Metadata and
commit checks establish reproducibility boundaries, not theorem approval.
The optional full-history ancestry check is not claimed for the shallow
canonical checkout.

The universal conclusion rests on PROOF.md, the separately reviewed
allocation, the literal control invariants and exact inherited sources.
It is one boundary-solve implication. No complete periodic inverse,
selected-assumption removal, new κ or full multiplication theorem is
claimed.
