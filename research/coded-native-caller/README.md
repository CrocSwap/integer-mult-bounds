# A physical central-query interface for the center-free coded producer

The new result is an exact, phase-correct common-query interface for the changed native producer of [PR4929](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/4929). It includes 896 compiled monomial wrappers, the corrected backward convention, a costed eight-mode owner recoding, and an emitted exact rational recipe for the entire p16 coded caller. This is a partial result toward turning 144 center-call deletions into a stronger recursive multiplication bound. **No complete profitable caller or improved κ is established.**

For every positive fiber width, the forward main interface is `diag(D,D) L(C Q^-1)` and the backward interface is `diag(D,D) U(-Q C)`, where `C=K^-1(A tensor I)K`, `C^T=C`, and `[C,DQ]=0`. The signed-permutation normalizers `K_i` preserve the full Gaussian including absolute phase. The corrected backward convention conjugates X by the monomial `Q^2`; copying the old Y-only convention fails. The 408 independent helper jobs and paid profile `{1:2201,2:412,3:510,4:127,5:40}` are inherited unchanged from the pinned producer.

[PHYSICAL.md](PHYSICAL.md) gives the all-width proof, literal wrapper words, independent-input and owned-storage contracts, and local bill. Each recoding uses eight owned temporary fields, 896 scalar ADDs, 224 monomial field maps and 16 CLEARs. Inverse recoding uses coefficient `-1/52`: its exact rational identity is checked, while native fixed-denominator rounding, full precision and tape costs remain caller obligations. Nonrecursive operations are charged work, not zero-cost instructions.

[GAUGE.md](GAUGE.md) derives the entire coded caller from exact noncommuting local transport and already-paid IDLE identities. All 3,584 main inputs and 7,344 helper inputs remain independent. The 16 positive-query main banks have outputs `B_i D_i Q`, and the 16 negative-query banks have outputs `Q^-1 B_i D_i`; each bank contains 112 owners. Postdecoding the positive banks is explicitly priced. The negative banks remain coded jobs, so their old Gaussian rank rewards are not admitted. The recipe removes exactly 144 rank-eight children but does not close the recursive supplier contract. Its Q=1 noncommuting core replay is an independent binding of Dugongue's existing `frame-aware-native/noncommuting-controller` result, crediting Cinna Davis #4470 and Ryanson Jonathan #4516; it is not claimed as a new core theorem.

[TERMINAL.md](TERMINAL.md) proves a useful scope gate: every one of the 5,824 nonzero blocks of a one-stage terminal `Q^-1 C Q` has exactly `4^f` support per row. Therefore it cannot be silently erased by a fixed finite monomial/scalar word uniformly in fiber width. This applies also to one `C^-1`; it does not exclude cancellation in products of codes, a paid decoder, or a different caller.
