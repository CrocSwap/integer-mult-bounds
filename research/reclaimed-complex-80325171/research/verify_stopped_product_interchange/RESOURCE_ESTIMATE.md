# Bounded independent controls

These are original standard-library mathematical controls, not upstream programs.
A single fixed dimension-four family contains two actual exterior projectors and
three coordinate projectors. One deterministic common rational basis is chosen
from at most 200 small candidates. Full rational matrices are at most 16 by 16;
modular specializations are at most 24 by 24, with three fixed atom widths.
No producer, graph, parameter sweep, installation, Lean, or Covering replay.
Expected runtime below 10 seconds and memory below 64 MiB. Hard caps: 60 CPU
seconds, 256 MiB virtual address space, 75 seconds wall time. Rational count and
moment checks use the already cached certificate data, without importing code.
