## 1. The completed ordinary operator is the interchangeable bit boundary

The reviewed104 primitive returns ordinary equal-chunk interchange at every
integer width, preserving the original numerical order, complete spectators,
inactive digits and row interface. Internally, its coarse child is
`F_n(H,D)=(C_n D,C_n H)` over `(Z/q^w Z)^n`. Both F calls in the three-shear
ordinary wrapper are *whole completed top-level calls*. They are not inserted
at every recursive child; no recursive factor two is introduced.

The original earlier physical chunk is named the head in either invocation
orientation. All frames within one invocation share the same opposite-bank
convention, and both complementary/copied-center directions have the reviewed
factorization. Atom-reversed cross-shear controls are nevertheless physically
earlier than their targets. The source explicitly pays O(Vn) adapter passes,
including descriptor work, on fixed tapes. There is no newly assumed free
reversal, gather, dense basis application or per-child orientation conversion.

The outer binary-to-fixed-q numerical padding is removed only after ordinary
interchange is complete. The old ordinary atom leaves may use their own binary
padding and their own internal address prime; each completed leaf preserves
the complete spectators. The <4 leaf padding factor is a local fixed toll,
not a padding multiplier propagated down the coarse child list. Standalone
high-digit row borrowing is likewise used only around the completed ordinary
endpoint. These facts are the precise input contract of the retained router
and the h24 binary basis/address adapters; their proofs do not need104's
internal F operator as a public primitive.

## 4. All three row stocks coexist, then are reused

Independent exact power checks give the least halving degrees

    coarse: 529/506, degree16, W143617474 has28 bits;
    old ordinary: 575/529, degree9, W188181929 has28 bits;
    complex: 576/552, degree17, W170157680 has28 bits.

For a conservative global width bound e, reserve
`Q=W_c^D_c W_b^D_b W_old^D_old`, with each D bounded by its halving degree
times ceil(log2 max(2,e)). At complex depth j and coarse depth k, the remaining
rows retain divisibility by
`W_c^(D_c-j) W_b^(D_b-k) W_old^D_old` before an old leaf starts. Its own old
stock is consumed only during that leaf and is restored on return. Coarse
adapters return before the next complex split. The first completed F call
restores rows before the second outer F call. Sequential copied streams also
return the same row field. None of these sequential uses adds an independent
live row coordinate or another factor to Q.

The original leading selected chunks are transformed once with elementary C
kernels, remain complete and physically preceding, and supply R>=Q rows.
The single padding to Q ceil(R/Q)<2R appends complete zero rows. All-role
completed returns bring those appended rows back to zero before removal.
The prefix is not re-transformed inside a descendant or gathered from a suffix
for free. If too few selected axes exist, the retained elementary fallback
completes that call. Fixed numerical range conversions are distinct from this
single row-stock padding.

Thus K=28(16+9+17)=1176 and
`4000-(51/25)1176=40024/25>0`. Under retained eventual e<=Cp, p>=C and
log2p>=25, p^4000 supplies the product stock including fixed constants.
The unchanged sufficient suffix condition is
`b_in^(1-epsilon)>16000(log2 b_in+8)`. Complete-root spectator ranges pay
polynomial descriptor work and the depth-first scheduler never scans parked
ancestors. Additional finite role streams are not additional physical tapes.

## 5. Changing beta does not reintroduce an old precision obligation

The PR23 linear semantic-guard proof explicitly holds for *any fixed stopping
exponent* (`semantic-bulk-17-note.tex`, lines144-167). It does not use the older
nonlinear path guard. Candidate beta=10^-6 is positive and fixed, as are the
new scalar constants. Stopped leaves have exponent
`sigma+beta(1-sigma)`; since tau>sigma, paid internal bit work has exponent
tau and compact reservations have exponent max(1-c,0). Direct exact arithmetic
gives

    (1-beta)*a_c-a_b = 16175583229/2500000000000000 > 0.

Using a smaller positive beta can postpone the eventual comparison between
the O(log p) prefix and d^beta, and enlarged C0 can postpone O(p) record and
other constant-domination thresholds. It changes no asymptotic row degree,
fixed-tape scheduling rule, source/target routing contract or dyadic grid
requirement. The author proof preserves these eventual dependencies rather
than asserting practical-size guarantees. No additional interface assumption
is needed beyond the explicitly retained ones.
