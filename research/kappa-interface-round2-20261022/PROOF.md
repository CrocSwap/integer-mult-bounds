## The changed degree of freedom

The PR4470 supplier proves its logical endpoint identity in the free group
algebra of 72 independently numbered invertible warp operators. Its two
literal evaluators retain all 632 arbitrary persistent inputs and the owned
center input. They require no equality between distinct warp generators.
Consequently a generator can be replaced consistently, at every occurrence,
by another invertible operator, with negative occurrences bound to its actual
inverse. Substitution preserves the entire endpoint identity, including the
autonomous dirty-helper outputs. In the backward orientation the substituted
physical operators and frame transports use the retained conjugate convention.

This permits changing the binary map G itself. It goes beyond moving a warp
with G held fixed. For 60 operators the only nontrivial frame occurrence is
one forward B warp; other occurrences have zero or full frames. If U and V
have equal dimension, extending bases constructs an invertible G with GU=V.
The remaining 12 operators are kept fixed by this search. All occurrences of
each changed operator use the same new G and exact phase convention.

For a frame U in F2^9 put

    L_U = {(x,x+p): x in U, p in U-perp}.

The native symplectic action is

    S_G(x,z) = (Gx, Gx + G^(-T)(x+z)).

Thus S_G(L_U)=L_(GU). Exact Clifford lifts show the physical warp
T_(GU) K_G T_U^-1 is monomial. This is the transported-frame lemma already
proved in `../transported-warp-frames/PROOF.md`; it alone does not determine
the absolute phase. The new certificate explicitly binds every changed G to
its native K_G, checks all 512 columns by independent integer formulas, and
lowers every used physical warp including its absolute phase.

The forward/backward formal prefix L1 bounds remain 457/169, with maximum

This improvement is strict for every moment exponent 0<t<1. Define

    g(t) = 3 + 4^t - 2*2^t - 3^t.

We have g(1)=0 and

    g'(t)/3^t = 2[(4/3)^t - (2/3)^t] log(2) - log(3).

The bracket is increasing in t, so on [0,1] the right side is at most
(4/3)log(2)-log(3), which is strictly negative because 16<27. Hence g(t)>0
for 0<t<1. The local paid-moment reduction is 12g(t), and the complete
controller's reduction is 216g(t). This is a changed invoice, not additional
digits of the old profile's root.
