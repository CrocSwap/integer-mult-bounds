# Rank-8 rigidity on every P109 unit

Notation, (E), equations (2), (E0), (E2), and Lemmas 2–8 are those of PR1212
(`research/complex-rank8-decoder-rigidity/PROOF.md` at head `57099cb5`). Lemma 9 and the "Step 1" argument are
those of PR1399 (`research/complex-rank8-rigidity-p104-admissible/PROOF.md` at head `4e1ba3a1`). Lemma 10 and the
coverage rule are those of PR1419 (`research/complex-rank8-rigidity-extension/PROOF.md` at head `81b8997b`).
The per-unit argument is PR1425's (`research/complex-rank8-rigidity-p110-odd/PROOF.md` at head `4b707c22`),
applied to 19 new units. Finite facts marked **[X]** are recomputed by this package's `verify.py` (check id `X`;
`u` is one of `u01`, …, `u19`).

**Theorem.** Let X be a 3-subset of P112 and L = P112 \ X. Let K be an L × L matrix over a field of
characteristic 0 with rank K ≤ 8 that satisfies (E). Then K = D (2 G_L) D⁻¹ for an invertible diagonal D.

**Corollary.** In the E-graph model, every decoder with exactly eight costly terms on a P109 unit has
cst ≥ 61 = 72 − 11. This is PR968's Theorem 4(b) with p = 11, reached through PR1212 §8 verbatim.

## Reduction to 19 units

Let H be the group generated by `verify.py`'s 8 generators, each a reflection or a product of two reflections
fixing FRAME0 **[model.generators]**. Its orbits split the 227920 triples of P112 into 24 classes, and whether
a triple lies in a frame inside P112 is constant on each class **[census.triples]**:

- **5 orbits, 29232 triples, inside a P112 frame.** PR1419's coverage rule applies, so these units are rigid by
  PR1419's Theorem, which cites the P104 units of PR1212, PR1389 and PR1399.
- **19 orbits, 198688 triples, not inside one.** 18 of them contain an odd pair; one, of 1792 triples, is
  pairwise orthogonal **[census.open-orbits]**.

Using a subgroup is sound. The orbits can only be finer than those of the full stabiliser, and PR1212 §10 transport
needs only some Weyl element carrying one unit onto another. The 24 orbits match the full stabiliser's count,
which an independent implementation found as well (see README). `UNITS.json` lists one representative per open
orbit, the first triple of each in line order **[units.cover]**, with its pinned pivot frame F inside L.

## Per-unit argument

For each representative, the following facts are checked **[u.rigid]**:

- PR1212 Lemma 2's D4 facts relative to F;
- PR1212's (I3) on every complete class, with live witnesses;
- PR1212's (I4): all 14 blocks carry a complete class, and complete classes glue through every shared pivot pair;
- PR1399's Lemma 9 connectivity for every non-pivot live line outside a complete class, with the sign identity
  for every witness;
- **(I2) zero exclusion**: the zero-pattern CNF of PR1425 (families *two*, *four*, *diag*, *trans*), with the
  clause "some pattern variable is true", is refuted by the DRUP proof in `PROOFS.json.gz`. That proof is
  replayed by `verify.py`'s own two-watched-literal RUP checker and fails without that clause;
- exact rational cross-checks of Lemma 9's rank-3 systems and of the §7 assembly on all 109 live lines;
- the gauge pattern satisfies the CNF.

Between 18 and 24 classes are complete, depending on the unit, and Lemma 9 carries the remaining 5–29 lines.

The proof is then PR1425's verbatim:

- **Step 1** (PR1399): Lemma 5 on each complete class and Lemma 6 on the set of complete classes give the gauge form
  a_jf = d′_j ε_jf g_f and b_jf = 4 (d′_j g_f)⁻¹ ε_jf on those lines.
- **Step 2:** Lemma 9 extends that form to every other non-pivot live line.
- **Assembly:** PR1212 §7 gives K = D (2G) D⁻¹.

Transport carries the result to every triple in the orbit. ∎
