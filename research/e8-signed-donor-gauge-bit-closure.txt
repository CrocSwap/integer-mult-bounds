PR396 inherited bit source closure and relative integration

Run with Python 3.11+ and SymPy 1.14.0:
  python -B research/e8-signed-donor-gauge-bit-closure/verify_closure.py --out /fresh/output

The package includes all 158 PR370 source files named by PR386 PINS.json,
PR386 source and the retained record/receipt chain. It reconstructs compressed
bit geometry, the global word, its independent audit and finite price, then
checks the six-round PR396 assembly against its retained complex receipt.
Every input is manifest-bound; outputs go to a new directory. Source hardlinks
are immutable. At least 25 MB free output headroom is required.

The local PR370 native receipts and the unchanged PR377 complex receipts are
explicitly reused, not rerun. The original historical verification commands
remain available in the vendored sources. This is an offline source closure
and fresh integration audit, not a fresh full historical supplier replay or
an all-source mathematical proof. All conditional interfaces and draft status
remain. No source pins are waived to address the unrelated joint CI failure.

The recorded initial packaging failure concerned two extra retained receipts
in the source directory; they were moved outside that exact source manifest.
The assertion was retained and the corrected fresh run passed. Original
source licenses and notices are included without alteration.
