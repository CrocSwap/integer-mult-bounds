# Matching-only transfer and exact physical cost

Let the complete producer and frame family be exactly PR108's. All scalar
identities, center decoding by 21, mixed complex/integer interfaces and paid
center copies are therefore inherited without change. The graph is regenerated
from PR108's source; no endpoint, early/late gate or restoration is merged.

A selected link has a donor addition x, one of its operand values v, and a
specific later use of v in an addition or output t. Require that x and the use
are unique in their respective matching parts, that their frames satisfy
F(v) ⊆ F(x) ⊆ F(t), and that (rank F(x), x) precedes the target in the inherited
strict rank/order relation. Those are exactly the hypotheses of the inherited
legal-carrier transfer used by PR104 and PR108. Thus the same simultaneous-basis
and dirty-register transfer argument applies to each selected matching. We do
not introduce a new interface lemma or claim an independent all-size proof.

In ambient dimension h, write ru, rv and rt for the three frame ranks. The local
transfer replaces the paid calls of widths h−ru, rv and rt−rv by one of width
rt−ru. Its signed histogram contribution is

    −[h−ru] −[rv] −[rt−rv] +[rt−ru].

The rank-weighted change is exactly −h. Zero-width calls are retained in the
finite ledger but contribute no recursive cost. The audit starts with every
ordinary addition, every use, all outputs and every retained center. It applies
this change once per distinct donor/use pair, verifies nonnegative final counts,
and checks total rank h(c+q−matched)+2·loss. No rank-one or retained-center
correction is omitted. The two-stage copied-center correction and complete
children are then assembled by the unchanged stopped-product profile routine.

The independent Python audit constructs frames from the binary file itself:
shared-pair triple indicator vectors, coordinate spans, or the prescribed
center-normal complement. Gaussian elimination over GF(2) checks rank,
nondegenerate Gram matrices and each inclusion. It does not consume the search
weights, exported candidate edges or their reported ranks.

The selected physical profile has the same 15,422 matches and 43,270 roles as
PR108. Its different rank distribution contracts at b=7997468953/10^14, whereas
PR108's complete profile does not. With the inherited beta=10^-6, bit parameter
min(803380799/10^13,(1−beta)b−10^-10), and all three paid row stocks, the exact
assembly contracts for κ=39980858679/500000000000000. All 47 constraints and
seven margins hold. The next 10^-14 complex grid point and next 10^-17 κ point
are rejected. These are finite grid witnesses, not a continuous optimum claim.

The search used floating-point assignment only to propose the explicit matching.
Neither weighted optimality nor a global minimum over graphs or dimensions is
needed by this conditional construction. The unchanged general proof dependencies
are those listed in PR104's stopped-product notes and PR108's README.
