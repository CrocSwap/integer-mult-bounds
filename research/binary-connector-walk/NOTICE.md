PR889 by @shhxera (head e2021d1c9e06948528cbc0a8fd7079b671da981d)
provides the direct predecessor: the common-right-factor dirty fanout contract,
its star-query lower bound, explicit child pricing and the distinction between
restoration and exact linearity. Its README and NOTICE are retained unmodified
with source pins. No predecessor executable is imported by this package.

Chafik Boukhalfa (@chafreaky), PR628 at
0768f97d06c623b8211e651543c66135796b62bf, supplies the dirty product commutator
and literal-inverse convention cited by PR889. PR822 by @shhxera explores dirty
sibling connector reuse in one-output trees; this contribution instead changes
the multi-input/multi-output query schedule. The comparison does not claim an
optimum among PR822's or the earlier higher-arity echo architectures.
PR896's nonlinear restoration formalization is related context, not a proof
of the linear product identity used here. PR522's coordinate-sector construction
has a distinct physical supplier contract and is not imported.
Concurrent PR929 at 85a1d11adbbd7610d269cfeb3e477cf7d457f2eb groups cyclic-inverse
factors in a single-output dirty commutator, including rounded complex costs.
Its source is not used: this package is an exact characteristic-two, multiple-
source/output scheduling result and does not combine the two savings.

The commutator identity and finite-state scheduling methods are classical;
no literature-priority claim is made. The new evidence is the complete
three-by-three binary schedule table, its independently checked support-bound
optimality, the concrete 12-query cycle, and the paid recursive lowering.
