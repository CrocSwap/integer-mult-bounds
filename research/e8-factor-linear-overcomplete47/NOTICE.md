# Credit and scope

Prepared for @woklerx with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance in research,
proof, implementation, verification and writing. Apache-2.0. Independent
algorithmic checks and a second AI proof review are not human peer review.

Direct predecessors are themilkman216's PR968 real eight-term decoder floor
and woklerx's PR1077 complex-coefficient extension. Their proofs, exact source
and finite verification routines are pinned and used unchanged. DreamingOfClouds'
PR717 defines the B14 decoder model and exhibited the punctured examples;
Joel Pulikkan/GamingPuzzled's PR620, PR595 format geometry and icekylinx's P112
lineage retain their original credit. The real E8 hyperplane classification
is inherited rather than newly proved here.

Mister-Meeseeks' PR765 covers binary, unpunctured tensor-code factors and is
distinct from the complex punctured model here. Josie Elliston's PR1071
independently corroborates the eight-term geometry and studies physical center
delivery; this package proves no delivery construction. Neither existing
result is contradicted. The new contribution is the all-term factor-linear
extension, its exceptional nine-term proof and exact scope countercontrol.

Current selected supplier credit stays with Rohan Arun, Jeremy Kothe and the
complete retained slot/T16/bit lineage. Its all-size, compiler, common-label,
hosting, precision/tape and analytic obligations are untouched.
