# Three rank-five pieces after a common cut of 46

Use the retained E8/P112 model and its nine-bit labels. Over any
characteristic-zero field K, let C consist of the 112 evaluations
`c_h(t) = r_t dot h`, for `h in K^8`. For a common set S of live
coordinates, let pi delete those coordinates. The binary support rank of
a piece is the F2 rank of its labels at nonzero coordinates.

**Theorem.** If `|S| <= 46` and each of three pieces q_i has binary
support rank at most five outside S, then

```
dim_K(pi(C) intersect span_K(pi(q_1), pi(q_2), pi(q_3))) <= 1.
```

Coefficients are arbitrary and pieces may be dependent. This is a sufficient
threshold, with no sharpness or actual-caller claim.

## Inherited premises

Nick Petalas's pending PR3249, head
`22c7bf316d55e59f381cc9ba833afccc7702fd58`, establishes global rank-five
support capacity 12 and the A2 root-plane union bound 78. PR3257, head
`1499da8f88c4d0293fe3ae443d0c7fec80f0e2d7`, proves the A2 joint bound
`m_i + l_i <= 21`, where m_i counts a piece's incidences in the live
code union and l_i its singleton sites. Its ten-singleton full-E8 closure
check is the step beyond the elementary `12+10` bound. The proof and
receipt from each head are retained verbatim in vendor/, authenticated by
SHA256 and Git blob digest. They are pending mathematical dependencies,
not selected scientific results. These older global/A2 censuses are not
rerun here.

The standard real Weyl-chamber theorem is also used. If a subspace is
spanned by roots, a generic vector in its orthogonal complement has
exactly the roots of that subspace as its perpendicular roots. After a
Weyl transformation these form a parabolic subsystem, generated by the
vanishing simple roots. The executable checks the relevant finite face
counts and normal planes. It does not prove the chamber theorem.

## The missing normalization branch

Suppose the intersection has dimension at least two. Choose h,k lifting
two independent restricted codewords and let U be their full live support
union. Outside S their supports are contained in the three residual piece
supports. Capacity 12 gives

```
|U| <= 46 + 3*12 = 82.
```

At least 30 live roots are perpendicular to both directions. Their rational
span has rank at most six: a rational matrix with two independent kernel
vectors over K has the same rank over Q. Five-node parabolic subsystems
have at most 20 root lines. Among all 28 six-node faces, only two have at
least 30 lines: D6 has 30, and E6 has 36. Their normal planes contain,
respectively, two orthogonal root lines and three A2 root lines. This also
forces rank six, so h,k span the scalar extension of that rational normal
plane. This argument applies over K without giving it a real ordering.

In the E6/A2 branch, `N = |U minus S| >= 78-46 = 32`. A coordinate of U
outside S with exactly one nonzero piece must lie in the projective
evaluation fiber of that piece's fixed coefficient column. Nonsingleton coordinates have at
least two incidences. The inherited joint bound gives

```
64 <= 2*N <= sum_i(m_i+l_i) <= 3*21 = 63,
```

a contradiction. Cancellations and support outside U cannot increase the
available incidence budget.

## D6 makes every residual piece a singleton piece

The other normal plane is spanned by orthogonal E8 roots a,b. The full
120-root evaluation configuration has zero fiber size 30 and four
nonzero projective fibers of sizes 33,33,12,12. Its full union is 90, so
removing eight frame roots leaves at least 82 live roots in U. Consequently
all bounds above are equalities:

```
|U| = 82, |S intersect U| = |S| = 46,
|U minus S| = 36 = sum_i |supp(pi(q_i))|.
```

Each residual support has size 12, the three supports are pairwise disjoint,
and they lie entirely inside U. Thus every residual site is a singleton.
The 2-by-3 coefficient matrix expressing the chosen codewords is fixed.
Each piece therefore lies in one projective fiber of the normal plane.

The replay enumerates every five-dimensional binary subspace in each
canonical fiber span. The two 12-root fibers have rank-five capacity 8;
they cannot contain a piece of size 12. Only the two axis fibers remain.
Three pieces in two axis fibers force two disjoint twelve-element supports
in the same axis fiber.

## The removed frame forbids that disjoint pair

The frame cannot contain a zero-fiber root, since all 30 perpendicular
roots must be live. The exact census of all 3,780 orthogonal root pairs
checks all 448 minimum-union planes. Each removes four frame roots from
each axis fiber and none from the two smaller fibers. The four roots in
each puncture are mutually orthogonal, as all frame roots are.

To make this check independent of any choice of conjugated frame,
`verify.py` checks every mutually orthogonal four-root subset of each
canonical full axis fiber. Each 33-root fiber spans eight binary dimensions.
All 97,155 five-spaces are covered once by their three-dimensional
annihilators in unique reverse row-echelon form. There are exactly 120
distinct twelve-root intersections. Every rank-at-most-five twelve-root
subset must occur: extend its span to a five-space, then use the checked
capacity 12.

There are exactly 120 orthogonal four-root punctures per full axis.
For every puncture, exactly twelve of the maximum supports survive.
Their 66 unordered intersections have sizes

```
2 on 12 pairs, 4 on 24 pairs, 8 on 30 pairs.
```

In particular no two are disjoint. Before puncturing, the same full axis
has 240 disjoint maximum pairs. Thus this argument uses the actual frame,
not a false assertion about the full E8 fiber.

The eight simple reflections generate one orbit of all 3,780 orthogonal
root pairs, as checked by a bounded orbit traversal. On the nine-bit
labels each reflection is an invertible linear map: the leading bit stays
fixed and one simple-coordinate bit changes by a linear parity. All 960
root/reflection label identities are checked. The canonical full-fiber
capacities and orthogonal-puncture statement therefore transport to every
normal plane and its transformed frame. This contradicts the disjoint pair
forced above and proves the theorem.

`crosscheck.py` independently enumerates all 474,672 five-label subsets of
the two full axis fibers with a separate elimination routine. It recovers
the exact same 240 maximum-support masks, including their hashes and the
unpunctured disjoint pairs. This is an independent enumeration method,
not a separate proof-kernel or independent human review.

## Limits and next boundary

At 47 the A2 count is only `2*(78-47)=62 <= 63`, and the raw support bound
leaves only 29 guaranteed perpendicular roots. Neither argument above
claims that boundary. No global sharpness, literal TWOSIDE application,
full C5, decoder floor, physical implementation, supplier, all-size
multiplication theorem or improved kappa follows here.
