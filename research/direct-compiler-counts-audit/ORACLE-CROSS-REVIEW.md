Parent-only terms are exits, child-only terms are sources. The complete Pascal sum justifies these signed residuals and would produce the same integer min matching per full b. Product collisions of different tuples add their correct weights. Paid support words retain all occurrence multiplicities; terminal outputs include every paid rank. The flow recipe is cached by floor(n/m), which is valid for sources/exits/units/columns/height.
