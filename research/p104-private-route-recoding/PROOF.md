## A simultaneous charge for two kinds of information

Contract the free equal-span classes. Represent each paid message by a unit-capacity directed edge, including parallel edges, and attach a super-source to 11, 58 and 278 with arbitrarily large capacity. On the plane the destination side of any separating cut has zero frozen observation. Under the fixed linear message rules, every observation on the destination side is a linear function of that restriction. Induction over time bounds its row space by the span of the incoming cut packets. Reverse messages and internal operations cannot enlarge this span. If its rank is below $2L$, two plane inputs give identical observations and different required outputs, which defeats even a nonlinear final readout. Exact deterministic recovery therefore forces cut capacity at least $2L$. By integral max-flow/min-cut the static graph contains $2L$ edge-disjoint directed paths from the three source charts to receiver 127. Discard cycles. Ignoring the times only enlarges this set of paths. Independently the second restriction forces at least $L$ messages to exit $D$: outside it the frozen observations vanish and the receiver recovers all $L$ scales.

Define $\delta=p(2)+p(5)-p(7)>0$. On a source path $P$, write $g$ for total grade and $n_D$ for the number of exits from $D$. We claim
$$
\operatorname{cost}(P)\ \ge\ p(7)+n_D\delta.
$$

* Since rank starts at most two and ends at nine, $g\ge7$. If $g=7$, the path starts at 278 and strictly ascends within the original span interval. When the path avoids 288 it has no exit from $D$, and monotone concavity bounds its price below by $p(7)$. If it passes through 288 it exits exactly once. Its portions before and after 288 have grades two and five. Concavity bounds their combined price below by $p(2)+p(5)$.
* If $g\ge8$, put $c=p(9)/9$. Every positive edge of grade $d\le9$ costs at least $cd$. There are at most $g$ edges, hence $n_D\le g$. The directed price check gives $8(c-\delta)>p(7)$. Thus this path's price exceeds $p(7)+n_D\delta$.
* Each edge unused by the extracted paths costs at least $p(1)>\delta$, and can pay for its own exit from $D$.

Adding over the paths and the unused edges gives $2Lp(7)+L\delta$ as required. Strict concavity and the strict price inequalities classify equality. Each path avoiding 288 must be one direct grade-seven jump. Every path through 288 must consist of exactly the two original private legs. There must be exactly $L$ such paths and every paid edge must lie on one extracted path. The unique optimal positive-edge multiset up to equal-span sharing consists of $L$ copies of each of $278\to127$, $278\to288$ and $288\to127$. This classifies topologies and leaves freedom in the codewords, basis and times. [Directed enclosures](check_prices.py)

## Attainment and private payload recoding

Let $h$ be minus the original $A$ payload if $A$ was retired, or the original $B$ payload if $B$ was retired. Relative to the original outgoing functions at 278 the merger shifts $A$ by $h$ and $B$ by $-h$, interpreting a retired value as zero. With the old private path restored unchanged, the resulting shifts are $h/2$ and $-h/2$ at the two receiver outputs in the order 155442, 155490. These are the only changed terminal functions. Send $h$ on $278\to127$ and subtract the shifts at the two readers. All three replacement sends use local stock in time.

For *every* rational $t$, the private ingress may instead carry $v_C+t h$. Keep the original side information $s=v_E-v_C$ at 288. Its outgoing packet then carries $v_E+t h$. Subtract $(t+1/2)h$ and $(t-1/2)h$ at the respective terminal readers. At $t=\pm1/2$ one affected reader needs no correction from the direct edge. The two independent plane coordinates still force the same bill. Across independent copies choose any rational $L\times L$ matrix $T$ in place of the scalar $t$, vectorizing $h,v_C,v_E$. Recover with corrections $Th\pm h/2$ after delivery of the $L$ direct packets. The word proof uses only the scalar template repeated on separate native calls; joint mixing assumes the model's stated access to all copies at a common chart. Headroom in a particular correlated caller would require another argument.

This construction extends to **any** ordered pair of strictly increasing chart chains from 278 to 127 with 288 on the designated private chain. At the retired exterior position forward $h$ along the other chain. At the old $C$ position forward $v_C+t h$ along the private chain to 288. At the old $E$ position add the side form and forward to 127. These insertion positions have the same available local inputs as in the original word. Fresh slots can relay consecutively since intermediate payloads are identical copies. Both affected terminals are later, and all earlier native reads retain their correct values. The verifier builds eight chronological programs spanning both mergers, $t=0,\pm1/2,11/7$, representative maximal detours and the direct optimum. It checks every terminal on common formal unchanged wires and independently pulls the two sensitive terminal rows back to all public coordinates.
