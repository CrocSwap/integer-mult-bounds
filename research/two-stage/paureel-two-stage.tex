\subsection{Two tensor stages and a charged endpoint correction}
\label{sec:two-stage}
This is Aurel Prosz's (Paureel) conditional research construction, developed
with ChatGPT and independently reconstructed with Codex. The cloud archive
was unavailable. The reconstruction uses the bundled paired-bit generator,
whose role count differs from the conversational count. Its slightly weaker
bit exponent still supports the same final multiplication witness.

Replace the three-factor ambient label space by $\mathcal F=F^{\otimes2}$.
Put $v=\binom h3$, $N=v^2$, $m=h^2$, $u_a=t_{a_1}\otimes t_{a_2}$ and
$U_a=\langle u_a\rangle$. Keep the original terminal labels: $U_a$ to
$\mathcal F$ on $X_a$, zero to $U_a^\perp$ on $Y_a$, and zero to
$\mathcal F$ on every auxiliary. Perform only the forward first stage
and inverse second stage. Their exact scalar map is
\[
 (x,y)\longmapsto(-y,x+y),
\]
with all arbitrary auxiliary inputs restored. This is not a bank exchange;
the explicitly charged endpoint correction below completes the array map.

\paragraph{Two-factor labels, including auxiliary endpoints.}
At stage $j=1,2$ put
$A=F^{\otimes(j-1)}$, $P=\langle t_{a_1}\otimes\cdots\otimes
t_{a_{j-1}}\rangle$, $B=P^\perp_A$, and
$Q=\langle t_{a_{j+1}}\otimes\cdots\otimes t_{a_2}\rangle$.
Empty products are the one-dimensional ground field. The data boundaries are
\[
\begin{array}{c|cc}
&\text{incoming}&\text{outgoing}\\\hline
X&A\otimes\langle t\rangle\otimes Q&A\otimes F\otimes Q\\
Y&B\otimes\langle t\rangle\otimes Q&
 ((B\otimes F)\perp(P\otimes t^\perp))\otimes Q.
\end{array}
\]
For the second stage, $A'=A\otimes F$, $P'=P\otimes\langle t\rangle$
and $B'=(P')^\perp$, so the first outgoing labels are exactly the second
incoming labels. The second outgoing labels are $\mathcal F,U_a^\perp$.

Within an invocation suppress $Q$ and use
$D_0=B\otimes F$, $D_1=A\otimes F$,
$D_U=(B\otimes F)\perp(P\otimes U)$.
Use the already proved forward source-span and reverse complement graph
frames. Early mixer, side injection and center incidences use $D_0$;
source copies, middle mixers and side injections use their respective
line, source span and target-line complement frames. Cleanup uses $D_1$.
In the reverse invocation use the retained reverse order and complements.
Grow each initially zero scratch to its first incidence label, and enlarge
its final label $D_1\otimes Q$ to $\mathcal F$. These are monotone endpoint
edges, with no extra loss. Every scratch therefore receives the full frame,
rather than just the exposed stage subspace. Common-frame compatibility
holds at every scalar gate. The only decreasing edges remain the central
returns, of dimension $h$ each.

\paragraph{The independently generated finite circuits.}
For bits use $h=32$, the rational form $I-J/9$, the paired exclusion circuit
on 31 points and its cross-common-point sharing compiler. Every source span
has a common point and is positive definite by the retained sum-of-squares
argument; its orthogonal complement is nondegenerate. All compiled gates
and both span/complement inclusion schedules are checked explicitly.
The actual bundled graph has 108277 additions and 14880 outputs, hence
$R_b=123157$ side roles. We do not claim reproduction of the conversation's
119584-role graph or its larger bit saving.

For complex values use $h=34$ and the orthogonal-star construction with two
parts of size 17. The proof of the fixed-pair orthonormality, exact side
coefficients, reversible compilation and canonical-vector repair above
holds with $h$ substituted for 25. Enumeration gives 272 groups, 768202
additions, 1611056 outputs and $R_c=2379258$ roles. These counts include all
11968 alternating-output repairs. Every nonzero source-span complement and
output residual has nonzero canonical vector, as checked on the generated
graph after repair. In particular at even $h$, the full star has canonical
vector equal to the sum of coordinate units outside its pair, distinct from
$\mathbf1$; any proper subset differs too. The retained common-component,
center and terminal residual proofs use a coordinate outside a tensor
indicator support, here of size at most $3^2<34^2$. They therefore still
provide a norm-one vector. The binary nonalternating-space argument gives
an orthonormal basis for every nonzero residual. The full matrix audit
checks all 35808256 coefficients, and the scratch schedules trace 37793456
incidences over both orientations.

There are $2v$ invocations with distinct auxiliaries, $c_0=h$ centers for
bits and $c_0=h+1$ for complex values. Thus
\[
 W=2v^2+2v(R+c_0),\qquad L=2vhc_0.
\]
The source and sink dimension sums are $N,Wm-N$. Edges cost
$s_{\rm edge}=Wm-2N+2L$. Both endpoint constructions use exactly one
rank-one child on a copy per data pair. Their total charged budget is
\[
 s=Wm-N+2L,\qquad Wm-s=v(v-4hc_0),\qquad
 \eta=\frac{v-4hc_0}{2h^2(v+R+c_0)}.
\]
The copy adds sequential work and temporary tape storage; its recursive
call retains precisely the original role volume $V/W$. No logical-volume
discount for duplicated storage is assumed.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:two-stage-bit-interface}
The two-stage bit construction, direct partial-swap transfer and its charged
copy correction give the retained power-width and arbitrary-width interchange
contracts with $m_b=1024$, $W_b=1271238080$, $s_b=1301743508480$ and
$\tau=1-47/10^8$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:two-stage-complex-interface}
The two-stage complex construction and diagonal endpoint correction give the
retained phase interface with $m_c=1156$, $W_c=28546995136$,
$s_c=33000319052800$, and $\sigma=1-3147/10^{11}$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The preceding scalar and frame arguments, the direct-swap line lemma and
the phase correction below establish the array contracts. Positive Taylor
sums certify $\log1024<6.932$ and $\log1156<7.05273$. The generated exact
counts give $\eta_b>(47/10^8)6.932$ and
$\eta_c>(3147/10^{11})7.05273$. Since $e^{-x}>1-x$, both strict
branching inequalities $s/W<m^{1-a}$ follow. The complex guard includes
the correction copies, sums and phase wrappers in the explicit scalar-node
allowance and verifies $2\le s_c<m_c^5$. The retained compact-control
reservation, repair, stopping-depth and assembly proofs apply with these
separate replacement arities and corrected budgets.
\end{proof}
