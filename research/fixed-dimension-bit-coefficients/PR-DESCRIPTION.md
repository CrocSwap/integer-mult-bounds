explicitly N=64. The existing #2835/#6896 capacity theorems also pin that old

## Exact contribution

continuation tapes on each closed reentry.

retired permanently. No stale-handle generation service is claimed.

pending demand of at most `16N²+128` residue and `128` index Words over **all**
entry residents before the first capacity guard, and explicit full-prefix and

size; absorption by `q^(ceil(e^.004)-1)` is conditional on a proved `root_size≤e`
coupling and the actual number of calls per fibre.

## Limitations and κ status

**No new κ.** All five foundational assumptions and the selected bound remain
conditional. The actual `.0034` catalogue/label generator and root map,
literal finite-table join, arbitrary changing-R/I physical bank and complete
caller stock/history composition remain open. Entry allocated history and
all live residents are charged explicitly. This theorem does not turn one
fixed arena's slot bound into a varying-precision physical bank or erase
other retained ancestor/producer/payload owners from the full source bill.

The package is a narrowly scoped compiler/ownership enabling lemma. It claims
no formal-kernel or independent human proof, selected cold replay, complete
multiplier implementation, or unconditional asymptotic improvement.
