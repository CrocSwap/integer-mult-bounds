# The shell exponent is sharp for every finite alphabet

This contribution was produced by Nick Petalas. Apache-2.0.

## Statement

Let A be a finite set of positive integers, let M >= 2 be an integer,
and let Gamma be the multiplicative monoid generated by A union {M},
including 1. Write v(g) for the vector of prime valuations of g, using
the finite prime support of A union {M}. Put

    r = dim_Q span{v(g): g in A union {M}},
    J_l = |Gamma intersect [M^l, M^(l+1))|,  l = 0,1,2,... .

Choose g_1,...,g_d from A such that v(M),v(g_1),...,v(g_d)
are independent and span that space, where d=r-1. Such a selection exists
because v(M) is nonzero and the original generators span the space.
When d>0 put H=max_i ceil(log_M g_i); when d=0 put H=1.
Every selected g_i is greater than one, so H>=1. Then

    J_l >= binom(floor(l/H)+d, d)                    (1)

for every l>=0. Together with JoyboyBrian's cited upper theorem in
PR6086, J_l <= K(l+1)^d, this proves

    J_l = Theta((l+1)^(r-1))                       (2)

for every fixed A and M, including dependent alphabets and rank one.
Constants may depend on A and M. This is a scoped counting theorem,
not a lower bound on compiler widths.

## Constructive lower bound

For each tuple b=(b_1,...,b_d) of nonnegative integers with
sum_i b_i <= floor(l/H), put G_b=product_i g_i^b_i. Since
g_i <= M^H, we have 1<=G_b<=M^l. Let a_b be the least nonnegative
integer with M^a_b G_b >= M^l. It exists with a_b<=l. Minimality gives

    M^l <= M^a_b G_b < M^(l+1).

For a_b=0 the upper inequality follows from G_b<=M^l<M^(l+1).
For a_b>0 it follows by multiplying the preceding strict inequality
M^(a_b-1)G_b<M^l by M. Thus each tuple gives a member of the required
half-open shell, using only the original generators.

If two images agree, unique prime factorization gives

    (a_b-a_c)v(M) + sum_i (b_i-c_i)v(g_i) = 0.

Independence forces b=c and a_b=a_c. The map is injective, even when
other generators of A satisfy multiplicative relations. Stars and bars
counts its domain as binom(floor(l/H)+d,d), proving (1).
For d=0 this is the single witness M^l and binom(l,0)=1.

For d>=1 and l>=H, floor(l/H)>=l/(2H), and
binom(n+d,d)>=n^d/d!. This supplies an explicit positive multiple of
l^d. The finitely many earlier levels contain M^l. The cited upper
theorem now gives (2). In particular, for every epsilon>0 there is no
bound J_l=O((l+1)^(r-1-epsilon)) for any one such fixed alphabet.

## Exact formula in the independent case

The same injection has an exact boundary. If Gamma is generated
by precisely M,g_1,...,g_d and those valuation vectors are independent,
then

    J_l = #{b in Z_>=0^d: product_i g_i^b_i < M^(l+1)}.       (3)

For any tuple counted on the right, choose the least nonnegative power
of M taking its product into the shell, allowing a=0 when the product
is already there. The argument above proves membership and injection.
Conversely every shell member has a unique expression M^a product g_i^b_i.
For that b there is exactly one allowable a because adjacent powers
differ by the shell ratio M and the upper endpoint is excluded. This
proves surjectivity and (3). Formula (3) counts finite exponent tuples;
it is not asserted for arbitrary dependent alphabets.

## Consequence and limit for adaptive widths

PR6086 bounds the number of possible multiplicative plateau starts in
each level of codyw912's PR4638 adaptive compiler. Its reported historical
p16 alphabet has augmented valuation rank eleven. Using that reported
rank as a premise, the present theorem gives J_l=Theta((l+1)^10) for
that monoid. No predecessor rank computation or scientific replay is
claimed here.

Therefore replacing PR6086's shell estimate by a smaller polynomial
degree for the same monoid cannot improve its width estimate. Actual
plateau availability, source-count valuations, LCM cancellation and
chronological reuse remain possible routes. There is no implication
that the product upper bound for the LCM is attained. Nick Petalas's
PR6612 already demonstrates the separate role of cancellation in a
homogeneous model; its result is cited, not extended or rechecked here.

No conclusion follows about a changed height base depending on l,
an alphabet changing with l, a restricted reachable subset, a monoid
that does not contain M, physical memory or runtime, native suppliers,
any selected framework assumption, or a multiplication exponent.
No selected construction or frontier is changed.

## Evidence and credit

The universal result is the written injection proof. The exact checker
enumerates its own small finite monoids, independently constructs their
membership by integer divisibility, checks all displayed lower witnesses,
and tests (3) on independent alphabets. It does not import or execute
any predecessor verifier. Positive finite cases corroborate the proof;
they do not exhaust all alphabets or levels.
