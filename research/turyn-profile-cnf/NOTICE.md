# Attribution, license and assistance

New independent verification package prepared by an OpenAI assistant at
the user's request, with substantial AI assistance throughout the
mathematics, implementation, certificate generation, testing and writing.
No independent human peer review is claimed. New files are under the
repository's Apache-2.0 license.

The prior theorem, original 24-port witness, Turyn coordinates, fitting
model, and scope come from **DominikScholz's PR598**, exact incorporated
head `615ca6f06fdea0c50dbef733370c47349e25c555`, and the packages
`research/leech-lattice`, `research/turyn-code-space`, and
`research/turyn-port-labels`. Their notices disclose OpenAI GPT-6 Astra
assistance. Their source files and notices remain unchanged. This package
claims no priority for the original profile dimension-nine result.

The source geometry credits R. J. Turyn, J. H. Conway, N. J. A. Sloane,
E. Bannai, Gabriele Nebe, Vincent Corlay, Joseph J. Boutros, Philippe Ciblat,
Loic Brunel, and SageMath contributors. The mathematical definitions used
here retain that lineage. Source hashes and the exact base are recorded
in `SOURCES.json`; no predecessor executable code is imported or run.

The selected conditional frontier at the pinned base is Jeremy Kothe's
PR799 with Chafik Boukhalfa's PR741 frames, DreamingOfClouds' PR589 bit
supplier and eumemic's PR555 construction. It is context only, unchanged
and not reverified by this contribution. The repository is maintained by
Douglas Colkitt / CrocSwap and preserves the broader source lineage.

Recent PR898, by CubeJerry, accelerates different historical Leech-minor
checks. Its title and scope were reviewed for overlap; no code is imported
and no performance claim is borrowed from it.

ShikeChen01's PR634 supplies DRUP proof logs for different Lorentzian
minrank graphs in characteristic other than two. Its body was inspected
during the final CNF overlap check. This package is not the first use of
SAT proof logs in the repository; it adds a separate standard-library
decision-tree certificate for the PR598 characteristic-two profile graph.
No PR634 code or proof artifact is imported.
