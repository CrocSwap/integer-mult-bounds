## 2. Diagonal wire gauges transferred to coefficient-changing circuit sharing

**Classical concept.** Preserve the exact endpoint while exposing a different elementary-matrix factorization. The matrix-decomposition viewpoint of Patel–Markov–Hayes above is relevant; phase-polynomial synthesis provides a second primary precedent for separating an intermediate representation from its exact final linear endpoint: Amy, Azimzadeh and Mosca, [On the CNOT-complexity of CNOT-PHASE circuits](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.01859), Quantum Science and Technology 4 (2018). Their Boolean phase-polynomial results are not a theorem about dyadic field ADDs. The transfer here uses an explicit diagonal-gauge invariant, proved below, rather than assuming their quantum gate model has the native supplier tariff.

**Exact algebra.** Let D be a diagonal map with nonzero dyadic-unit entries d_r, and maintain old state z_old = D z_new. An old update z_t += a z_s becomes z_new,t += a(d_s/d_t) z_new,s. An old scale on wire r multiplies d_r. A scalar d_r commutes with every linear family operator K_G on that same wire, so a recorded warp does not require a free change of operator. At a prescribed endpoint emit each remaining actual scale d_r; it is not omitted. These three direct substitutions prove the invariant by induction over every instruction and preserve the full arbitrary-dirt endpoint. In particular, moving a chronological scale λ past a target-wire ADD divides its coefficient by λ, while moving it past a source-wire ADD multiplies its coefficient by λ. A reciprocal scale pair may then be removed. This changes dependent coefficients and therefore is not the pure commuting transformation in Section 1.

**Source and prior-use boundary.** Our existing N076 already proves a paid dyadic wire-gauge construction; that generic mathematics is not a new discovery. The canonical ABI results cited above give the explicit encoding/decoding contract, but they do not prove a cheaper concrete embedding. `Mathproof/LiGuanCNOTPhaseBridge20260905.lean`, SHA-256 `df29fb9c346d065538c2a446d11f75d6e76c717bbf79897abb8d29a4bb4d96a1`, contains `phaseRun_linear` at line 24 and `paper_PhaseZeroEquivalence` at line 59: a phase circuit retains its actual linear endpoint. `Mathproof/External/IntegerMultBounds20261008/Matrix/CommutingPhases.lean`, SHA-256 `95d64270a9973e00b75d2b1f8a29ed95d15f0c3013e604f2ef14249a2a7c9d48`, proves `xor_translation_commutes`, `raw_phase_commutes`, and `raw_phase_translation_commutes`, and supplies `gauge_phase_noncommuting_witness`. Thus a diagonal gauge is not generally allowed to cross a phase kernel unchanged. The present experiment transports scalar field coefficients through the actual linear native program and preserves recorded frame/warp anchors; it does not infer raw-phase commutation from these restricted statements.

## 4. Dependent three-wire resynthesis with actual frame bills

If `E_k … E_1 M = I`, the generated chronological inverse list realizes exactly `M = E_1⁻¹ … E_k⁻¹`. Every matrix equality is recomputed over exact rational numbers.
