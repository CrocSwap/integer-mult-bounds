# Two compatible mechanisms for a sharper conditional supplier

This is an informal mathematical proof with exact finite certificates. It is not a Lean formalization or an unconditional integer-multiplication theorem. The new result is the checked composition of Marius-Paul Dumitrean’s four normal-45 paired extractions (PR #2942), Jeremy Kothe’s fourteen gate rewrites (PR #2957), extending his nine-rewrite PR #2948, and an explicit chronological allocation at replica count 370. The constituent mechanisms retain their original attribution. The native, owner and outer evidence must be read with the final validation record; an algebraic word audit alone does not establish supplier admission.

## 1. Algebraic setting and the dirty-input guarantee

There are 632 independent live input coordinates after removing the declared unused helpers. A register holds an arbitrary element of a vector space over the rationals; for symbolic warp checking its entries lie in a free module over the algebra generated by the declared warp operators. Thus no relation among distinct warp words is silently assumed. A scalar update E_ab(c) sends z_a to z_a + c z_b, leaves every other register unchanged, and requires a ≠ b. A scale D_a(c) multiplies z_a by the nonzero scalar c. All actual coefficients in the construction are explicit dyadic rationals. Matrix identities over this algebra specialize to every compatible vector-space interpretation of the warp generators.

**Theorem 1 (basis-complete arbitrary-dirt certification).** If two such linear programs have identical formal coefficient columns on every live input coordinate, their outputs agree on every input, including every dirty helper and every source. If their dual maps agree as well, the asserted backward interface agrees on every dual input.

*Proof.* Write an arbitrary input as the sum of its coordinate vectors with its coordinate entries. Each scalar update and warp is linear, so the output is the same linear combination of the corresponding output columns. Column equality therefore gives equality of each output register. Apply the identical argument to the dual free module. No claim that dirty helpers are initially zero enters the argument. The finite certificate checks all 632 live columns in both orientations, rather than a sample of integer inputs. Private zero buffers are a separate declared ABI resource and are checked separately. ∎

**Theorem 2 (safe elementary retiming).** E_ab(c) and E_de(f) commute when a ≠ e and b ≠ d. A scale on a register outside {a,b} commutes with E_ab(c). Hence a gate may cross an interval in which each of these conditions holds without altering the arbitrary-input map.

*Proof.* In the product expansion, the only possible cross terms are cf δ_bd e_ae and fc δ_ea e_db. Both vanish under the two inequalities, and scalar addition commutes when the targets coincide. The scale claim follows because neither coordinate read or written by the addition changes. Induct over the crossed gates. When a crossed scale acts on a participating register, a coefficient change is required; it is not licensed by this commuting theorem. PR #2957’s inherited sign-corrected restore moves are checked with their actual negative coefficients. ∎

## 2. Paired extraction with explicit compensation and recovery

The paired-extraction construction is Jeremy Diviney’s PR #2908 method, extended by Mason Hale in PR #2917 and by Marius-Paul Dumitrean in PR #2942. Let M be the non-target middle program, C its target compensation matrix, and h a newly declared cold helper. Let S and T be explicit invertible scalar words at the input and output. The exact two-sided certificate provides vectors and covectors v,u,y,α satisfying

S e_h = v,   u S = e_hᵀ,   M v = y,   α M = u,
α y = 1,   T y = e_h,   e_hᵀ T = α.

Here v and u are supported on the declared unprotected helper coordinates. The input and output words are literal transvections and scales, so their inverses are explicit. The output word lies in the rank-8 hyperplane E_45 = {x ∈ F₂⁹ : x · 45 = 0}. No unknown chart-finding operation is used in the delivered construction.

**Theorem 3 (paired isolation).** Under these identities, the h-th column and row of TMS are respectively e_h and e_hᵀ. If the old helper-to-target dirty response is D = −C, then replacing the compensation by CS cancels the transformed response DS. Protected source and initial-correction coordinates must remain fixed, or be checked separately.

*Proof.* TMS e_h = TMv = Ty = e_h. Also e_hᵀTMS = αMS = uS = e_hᵀ. Thus h is an identity coordinate with no incoming or outgoing cross term in the transformed middle map. For original input coordinates z, the transformed dirty response is DSz and the transformed compensation is CSz. Their sum is (D+C)Sz = 0. This is a cancellation identity, not the assertion CS=C. Right multiplication by the literal S gives exactly the new compensation matrix; processing the elementary input gates in reverse order is the explicit column-update algorithm used by the producer. The new h compensation column is checked to vanish, so no undeclared read from the new cold input is introduced. ∎

**Theorem 4 (inverse-word identity and certified substituted cleanup).** If a finite invertible word W is followed by its reversed sequence of inverse gates, every input coordinate is restored. The delivered cleanup is a filtered and buffer-substituted inverse construction: target writes are excluded and declared private buffers replace the selected restored coordinates. Its restoration claim therefore requires the exact all-column and buffer-endpoint check of that actual substituted program.

*Proof.* For W = g_k ⋯ g_1, the inverse is g_1⁻¹ ⋯ g_k⁻¹. Adjacent inverse factors cancel, giving the identity on arbitrary inputs. The raw inverse identity explains the cleanup constructor. Filtering target writes and substituting private buffers require an additional argument: the delivered full substituted program is linear, and its actual matrix columns and buffer endpoints are checked exactly, so Theorem 1 establishes its asserted restoration for every dirty input under the declared zero-buffer ABI. Reusing a predecessor cleanup would not satisfy this check and is rejected by a negative control. The substituted program is not identified with the unfiltered matrix W⁻¹. Its ordinary work and stock costs remain in the complete invoice. ∎

## 3. A sufficient composition criterion

**Theorem 5 (source-bound compatible surgery).** Suppose an exact paired-extraction program is modified by a declared set of literal gate edits. Suppose every old edit preimage matches, edits have the declared nonoverlap, every warp keeps its operator/lender/frame, its position is transported exactly past the deleted B gates, and the final program satisfies all-column forward/dual equality, legal monotone frame chains, target compensation, and the full restored-buffer ABI. Then the modified program realizes the same mathematical supplier interface. Its recursive bill is the invoice of its actual frame chains.

*Proof.* Matching preimages fixes which old operations were replaced; the producer cannot silently edit a different repeated gate. The exact all-column comparison and Theorem 1 establish the mathematical interface. The warp transport preserves execution relative to every retained B gate; its new position is old_position minus the number of deleted B indices strictly before old_position. The frame check establishes that every actual operation is legal at its assigned frame. The target and buffer checks establish the required final ABI, and the actual substituted cleanup is certified as required by Theorem 4. Sum the rank increments over the actual chains to obtain the bill. There is no premise asserting that independently good transformations always commute: the combined program itself must satisfy all these sufficient tests. Native Clifford phases, scalar lowering, numerical prefixes and the outer compiler remain separate parts of complete admission. ∎

In the instance, PR #2942 inserts 122 zero-frame input gates after the inherited 84-event prelude. Every #2957 A edit maps to old index + 122; B and inherited restore indices map unchanged. The two deleted B indices are 606 and 631. Every old preimage matches exactly. The four normal-45 endpoints remain present, and all 86 private buffers remain charged. The two commuting restore moves found in our first search are already #2957 IDs 5997 and 6000, inherited from #2948 and are applied only once, with Jeremy Kothe’s credit retained.

There is a stronger compatibility certificate than disjoint edit addresses. Let M_K be the raw middle operator after all fourteen rewrites and M the shared PR #2917 middle operator. The target response and its compensation are preserved by this same equality. This proves that the two surgeries compose mathematically; it does not assume that arbitrary individually valid optimizations can be combined.

## 6. Exact moment comparison and meaningful ablation

For a complete profile (c_r) at replica count T, define
M(b) = Σ_r c_r r (73/r)^b / (73·896·T).
The tariff exponents are related by p = 1 − b: r(73/r)^b = 73^b r^(1−b). At fixed b the common positive factor 73^b preserves every comparison of complete H tariffs. Every supported r is strictly less than 73. Rank-mass equality gives M(0) = 65,088 / 65,408 = 1,017 / 1,022 < 1. Since M(b) tends to infinity, there is a unique positive solution of M(b) = 1. This is the complex supplier endpoint; its transfer to multiplication uses the inherited outer framework. A root or a local H-saving alone is not a full multiplication theorem.

**Theorem 8 (directed root comparison).** For positive coefficients and ranks 0 < r < 73, M is strictly increasing in b. Therefore a directed exact lower/upper bracket around its required threshold certifies the unique corresponding endpoint, and disjoint brackets certify strict improvement.

*Proof.* Each factor (73/r)^b is strictly increasing, and at least one coefficient is positive. The sum is continuous and strictly increasing. An exact bracket with opposite strict endpoint inequalities contains exactly one solution by continuity and strict monotonicity. Directed rational enclosures of the logarithms/exponentials certify the endpoint inequalities without relying on floating-point solver output. ∎

The final validation records all four complete profiles: the common anchor, normal-45 only with its legal packing, rewrites only with PR #2957’s unchanged inherited PR #2930 packing, and the joint construction.
