Clean helper with zero active off-diagonal patterns
=================================================

The active contract is only the upper-left 3 by 3 scalar block:

  J^T P J = diag([C_1],[C_2],[C_3]).

The fourth input is zero and its output is unused. The target frames
are invertible lower-triangular rational 3 by 3 matrices. Each of the
eight paid frames is nonzero and rank one. Scalar factors are binary
rank-one idempotents on four registers. This result adds the hypothesis
that every paid scalar projector has zero off-diagonal entries in its
active 3 by 3 block.

Alphabet and multiplicity reduction
-----------------------------------
Write N=xy^T, with active parts x',y' and helper components x_4,y_4.
If the active block is nonzero and diagonal, x'=y'=e_i for one active
i. Idempotence requires x_4 y_4=0. There are exactly three choices:

  E_ii,  E_ii+E_i4,  E_ii+E_4i.

If the active block is zero, x'=0 or y'=0. Idempotence requires
x_4=y_4=1. These are the helper-star projectors e_4(e_4+v)^T and
(e_4+u)e_4^T. There are 15 distinct choices, including E_44. The full
zero-active-off-pattern alphabet therefore has 24 projectors.

Let d_i count calls from active-diagonal family i, and D=sum d_i.
Every such call contributes e_i to both active visible codes. A
helper-star call contributes a nonzero vector of F_2^3 to just one
code, or contributes zero to both. Each nonzero active row must see
at least three calls in each code. This follows by projecting away
at most two visible paid frames: the active row of the circuit is
fixed, but an active invertible target in its support is not.

For fixed d, let m be the smallest number of extra nonzero vectors
that makes one code have distance at least three. Both codes need
at least m extra calls, so D+2m<=8 is necessary. A binary dimension-
three distance-three code has length at least six: its eight disjoint
radius-one balls do not fit in binary space of length at most five.

If m>=3, D+m<=8-m<=5 contradicts this code bound. If m=2, each d_i
is at least one and D<=4. At least two counts are one. Both extra
vectors must contain those two coordinates, so their pair has weight
two, a contradiction. If m=1, each d_i>=2 and D<=6, hence d=(2,2,2)
and the extra vector is (1,1,1). If m=0, all d_i>=3 needs D>=9.

Thus the word has exactly two calls from each three-choice active
family, one fixed U=(1,1,1,1)e_4^T, and one fixed V=e_4(1,1,1,1)^T.
No E_44 call or other helper-star direction fits the call budget.
There are binomial(4,2)^3=216 multisets. This analytical reduction
was found independently by the audit agent. The exact finite code
check in ../clean_zero_off_distance.py gives the same classification.

Complete word and relation check
--------------------------------
generate.py lists every order of all 216 multisets, modulo swaps of
adjacent factors whose scalar projectors commute. It uses actual
projector products. It makes no reversal reduction. The resulting
counts are 3,674,160 raw orders and 314,666 commuting normal forms.

CLEAN-SPAN-FOUR.txt proves that paid-frame span at most four needs
at least nine calls for the compressed active-block contract. Hence
an eight-call solution has relation dimension at most three. Search
all required subset collisions up to that dimension. A nonzero
active off-diagonal term must merge with a partner in its entry.
If an active diagonal has multiple terms, at least one of its first
two terms must merge with another current term; branch on every such
partner. This branch rule includes the case where those first two
terms merge with each other.

At a terminal, all three active entries are monomials. Reject zero
paid frames and any active target in the span of at most two paid
frames. These necessary tests exclude an invertible target. Only the
active block is constrained; all entries involving the helper remain
unrestricted. No returned-helper equation is imposed.

Two exact engines check every retained word. One uses primitive
integer rows. One uses F101 with the bounded-minor rational-equivalence
argument in F101-ARITHMETIC.txt. Both use at most three original cube
difference relations. After deterministic branch ordering, every
cumulative count and every terminal word/count agrees:

  words:              314,666
  states by relation rank:
    314,666; 1,005,125; 1,024,411; 2,160,151
  monomial terminals:  20,430 on 17,799 word IDs
  accepted spaces:          0

An earlier integer run used another complete branch order. It also
rejected every word and found the same terminal word IDs. Its terminal
space count differed because it stopped at different already-rejected
subspaces. The canonical run resolves that diagnostic difference.

Therefore no eight-call solution exists in this full zero-active-off-
pattern case. This is not yet an exclusion of all clean-helper words.
Together with CLEAN-ONE-RELATION.txt, after its analytic proof review,
it also excludes clean four-register words of frame span seven or
eight. General clean frame spans five and six remain unresolved here.
