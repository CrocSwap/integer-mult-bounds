Span-four bound with clean workspace
===================================

Claim
-----
Let the physical address dimension be three. Let W active scalar
registers have invertible lower-triangular rational target frames.
Permit any number of additional scalar registers with zero input and
unused output. If all paid rank-one frame matrices span a rational
space of dimension at most four, at least 3W calls are required.

This claim does not require the complete scalar-register product to be
diagonal. Let J embed the active input and output registers. The required
condition is only

  J^T P J = diag([C_1],...,[C_W]).

The extra rows and columns of P can be arbitrary.

Compressed compact-rank bound
-----------------------------
For any selected nonzero projected frames, the full product has a compact
expansion P-I=X T Y^T. The matrix T is triangular with nonzero diagonal,
so it is invertible over the fraction field of the relevant group ring.
If the active block of the projected target is I, then

  (J^T X) T (Y^T J) = 0.

If U calls remain, Sylvester's inequality gives

  rank(J^T X) + rank(Y^T J) <= U.

The active projections of x and y can be zero. The bound remains valid.
For any other projection, the rank of the active target difference is
at most the rank of its active image vectors and at most the rank of its
active row vectors. No condition is imposed on the workspace blocks.

Two-dimensional flag bound
--------------------------
For a two-dimensional address block with lower-triangular targets, let
U be the calls whose upper frame entry is nonzero. Let r_X and r_Y be
the ranks of their active scalar image and row vectors. Upper-entry
projection has active target I, so the compressed bound gives
r_X+r_Y<=U. Let l_i count lower frames with nonzero diagonal entry i.
Each diagonal target difference has rank W, hence

  W <= r_X+l_i,   W <= r_Y+l_i    for i=0,1.

A lower rank-one frame has at most one nonzero diagonal entry. Therefore

  4W <= 2(r_X+r_Y+l_0+l_1) <= 2E.

A two-dimensional physical block needs at least 2W calls. A one-dimensional
block needs at least W calls by the active target rank alone.

Span-four reduction
-------------------
Choose three paid rank-one frames whose left vectors and right vectors
are each independent. They exist because the frame span contains an
invertible active target. Common rational row and column maps turn these
three frames into the diagonal coordinate matrices. A fourth basis
frame is u v^T.

If all coordinates of u and v are nonzero, all rank-one frames in this
four-dimensional space lie on the four independent basis lines. Project
onto each line's coefficient. The active target rank gives a lower bound
on calls from that line. Each invertible target uses at least three
lines, so the sum of the four bounds is at least 3W.

If a coordinate of u or v is zero, a simultaneous permutation makes the
physical frame space block upper triangular with diagonal blocks of
size at most two. Each rank-one frame has at most one nonzero diagonal
block. The active targets have invertible diagonal blocks. Their common
ratio flag descends to each invariant subquotient, so common row and
column maps within each block make its active targets lower triangular.
Assign each paid call to its nonzero diagonal block, if one exists.
The preceding one- and two-dimensional bounds sum to 3W.

Thus a clean-workspace eight-call word for three active targets must
have frame span at least five. Its rational relation space has dimension
at most three. This claim does not permit reuse of the returned-register
scalar irreducibility or full off-diagonal pattern restrictions.
