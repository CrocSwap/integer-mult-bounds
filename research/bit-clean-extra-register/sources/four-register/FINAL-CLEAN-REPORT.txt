CLEAN-REGISTER RESEARCH CHECKPOINT
==================================

No eight-call construction was found. There is no new positive value
of kappa from this work. The general clean-register problem remains
open here, with the restrictions below.

Exact model
-----------
There are three active scalar registers and one extra register. The
extra input is zero, and its output is unused. Only the upper-left
3 by 3 block of the full scalar product must equal

  diag([C_1],[C_2],[C_3]).

The extra row and column are unrestricted. Each C_i is an invertible
lower-triangular rational 3 by 3 address frame. Each of the eight paid
factors is I+([A_j]-1)N_j. Each A_j is a nonzero rational rank-one frame;
its entries have no size bound and need not be lower triangular. Each
N_j is a binary rank-one idempotent on four scalar registers. The
coefficient ring is the additive group algebra F_2[M_3(Q)].

General restrictions
--------------------
CLEAN-SPAN-FOUR.txt proves that paid-frame span at most four requires
at least nine calls. The proof permits any number of clean extra
registers. Its compact-product rank bounds use the active block only.
The root and architecture agents independently reviewed this extension.

CLEAN-ONE-RELATION.txt proves that span eight is impossible, and that
span seven requires every paid projector to have zero off-diagonal
entries in its active block. The open_pr_survey agent independently
reviewed its rank-one completion classification and variable removal.
A direct enumeration also confirms all 28 nonzero completion patterns.

The complete finite check below excludes the zero-pattern case for one
clean extra register. Its independent family and order audit passed.
These results leave only paid-frame spans five and six in the stated
four-register eight-call model. That is a restriction on an unresolved
problem, not a construction or a complete impossibility theorem.

Complete zero-pattern case
--------------------------
The full alphabet has 24 binary projectors. Necessary active distance
bounds reduce every eight-call word to 216 scalar multisets. The reviewer
gave a separate analytic proof of this restriction. Each multiset has
two calls from each of three active-diagonal families and one call from
each of two fixed helper-star directions. All factor orders were checked,
with only swaps of adjacent commuting projectors removed. No reversal
symmetry was used.

Complete coordinate-plus-one-basis case
---------------------------------------
Fix the coordinate basis I. Let B be any binary basis of F_2^4. Allow
all eight-call words, with repetitions, over the union of their rank-one
basis projectors. The 840 unordered choices of B give 166 classes under
permutations of the three active coordinates. All 166 were checked.
Fixing one basis to I is a restriction of this class. It is not a valid
normalization of two arbitrary bases for the required active target.

  Scalar words checked:                 7,406,643
  Relation states:                    181,099,930
  Active monomial terminals:               262,735
  Terminals without a rank obstruction:         0

The generator uses actual commutation and the fourteen active distance
conditions. It uses no reversal or inverse symmetry. A second agent
independently reconstructed the basis catalogue, reviewed the driver and
collision rules, and checked the totals. A separate Fraction engine
agreed on 48 selected nonsymmetric and repeated-factor words. The full
7,406,643-word search was not repeated with a second arithmetic engine.
The two-basis engine reuses the clean F_101 relation core. Its result is
therefore not an independent full replay of the earlier H/I special case.

Remaining work and cost implications
------------------------------------
Any remaining candidate in this model must have frame span five or six,
must have a nonzero active off-pattern, and must use an alphabet outside
the coordinate-plus-one-basis class. Arbitrary scalar words with those
properties have not been excluded. More clean registers, more general
endpoint contracts, and non-abelian suppliers are outside this checkpoint.
The separately proved returned-register obstruction cannot be applied to
the clean endpoint: it has stricter conditions on the extra row and column.

Even a future exact eight-call word would still need an all-size compiler.
That proof must normalize the three target frames, define the recursive
mixed active/temporary interface, and charge initialization, copies,
readout, erasure, address operations, and finite base cases. Existing
copy-transform-read-discard primitives support temporary workspace, but
do not by themselves compile every such algebraic word. The complex
supplier and the full outer construction must also meet the cost bound.

Conditionally, eight child calls of volume V/3 for three active payloads
would give the ideal moment 8*3^(a-1)<3, or

  a < log(9/8)/log(3) = approximately 0.10721.

This is a conditional supplier margin. It is not a proved global kappa,
and it does not establish the requested 1/256 milestone or 0.01 target.
