CLEAN-ANCILLA SEARCH: INDEPENDENT SCOPE AND PRUNING AUDIT

No incorrect pruning was found in the reviewed rational engine or in the
all-order field-engine driver. This audit is conditional on rational frame
span at least five, so the relation space has dimension at most three.

The target condition is only the upper-left 3 by 3 block: it must be diagonal,
with one group monomial at each diagonal position. The fourth input is zero
and the fourth output is discarded. No other entry is constrained.

RATIONAL COLLISION SEARCH

Each paid scalar matrix is a nonzero rank-one binary idempotent. The engine
expands all eight factors exactly over the binary group algebra. Its exponents
are the 256 subset indicators. A state is a rational row space R0 of imposed
relations. Exact Fraction row reduction gives a canonical representative for
each exponent modulo R0. Group coefficients are added by XOR.

For a nonzero active off-diagonal coefficient, its exponent must collide
with another exponent that has that bit. Pairing its first exponent with
every possible partner is exhaustive for all extensions of R0. At an active
diagonal with more than one exponent, some pair must collide before it can
become one monomial. The rational engine branches over every pair. A zero
diagonal or a single off-diagonal term cannot be repaired by more relations.
Each collision adds one independent relation, so at most three additions
are required under the span hypothesis. Repeated states can be omitted.

Rejecting a state that contains a coordinate vector e_j is sound: it forces
the corresponding paid frame to be zero. The primitive integral version of
each relation belongs to the saturated physical relation lattice L. Its
parity therefore lies in L modulo 2. Each active off-diagonal first-order
row also lies there. Their binary span cannot have dimension greater than
rank(L)<=3. The parity test uses only these necessary rows; it does not
assume that a chosen rational basis generates the full integral lattice.

At a terminal, a target exponent in R0+span(e_i,e_j) is a linear combination
of two paid rank-one frames. Its matrix rank is at most two. It cannot be
an invertible 3 by 3 target. More relations do not remove this obstruction.
A terminal that passes this test is only a candidate, not a construction.

CLEAN-ENDPOINT DISTANCE FILTER

Let w be any nonzero binary vector on the three active coordinates, extended
by zero on the fourth coordinate. Suppose w pairs with at most two scalar
left factors. The span of the corresponding rational frames contains only
matrices of rank at most two. A rational functional can annihilate this span
while remaining nonzero on all three invertible target frames. After this
projection, the touched factors become identity; every other factor fixes
w on the left. The product must fix w. Its active diagonal block does not,
which is a contradiction. The unknown fourth output entry does not affect
this contradiction on the first three columns. Transpose and reverse the
word for the column version. This proves all fourteen distance constraints.

SPAN HYPOTHESIS AND ACTIVE COMPRESSION

The existing low-span proof uses the factorization P-I=XTY^T, where T is
upper triangular with nonzero diagonal. Compressing to the three active
coordinates gives P_active-I=X_active T Y_active^T. If this block is zero,
Sylvester's rank inequality gives rank(X_active)+rank(Y_active)<=k. If its
rank is three, both active ranks are at least three. These are precisely
the rank tools used in the span-at-most-four argument. The lower-triangular
target or common-flag assumption in that argument must be retained. The
search itself does not establish that assumption.

CATALOGUE

A separate implementation finds each dual basis vector by testing its four
binary inner products. It does not use the production matrix inverse.
There are 840 unordered binary bases and 166 orbits under permutations of
the three active coordinates. Orbit sizes are 1 (6 orbits), 3 (42), and
6 (118). The independently generated job set equals all 17106 saved jobs.
The declared layer shapes contribute 14148, 1479, and 1479 distinct jobs.

These are the shapes (2,2,2,2), (3,3,2), and (2,3,3), with alternating I/B
layers, either initial basis, and distinct projectors within each layer.
This is a finite layer ansatz. It does not cover all two-basis words.
The separate all-order run removes this layer-shape restriction. Its 166
alphabet rows match the independently generated basis orbit representatives.

ALL-ORDER C++ DRIVER

The driver deduplicates identical scalar projectors. It enumerates words of
length eight over the remaining alphabet, allowing repeated labels. Its
fourteen distance counters are exact. A prefix is discarded only when its
current count plus all remaining positions is less than three.

The commutation test calculates both products of the actual rank-one scalar
matrices over the binary field. Commuting scalar projectors give commuting
paid factors for all frame variables. A rejected word has a smaller label
that can move left through commuting labels. Thus its trace class has a
lexicographically smaller representative. Every trace class has a least
representative, and every prefix of that representative passes the test.
This reduction preserves coverage. It does not use word reversal.

Fixing the first basis to I is part of the ansatz. General simultaneous GL4
conjugacy need not preserve the required active diagonal target. Consequently
the complete driver covers I plus one arbitrary binary-basis alphabet, not
every pair of bases in every scalar coordinate system.

MODULAR ENGINE

Every state has at most three rows that are differences of subset indicators.
Their entries are -1, 0, or 1. Nonzero minors of size at most three have
absolute value at most four. State row-reduction entries therefore have
reduced numerator and denominator of absolute value at most four. The
implemented reconstruction box [-5,5]/[1,5] contains them and is injective
modulo 101, since a difference of cross products has absolute value at most
50. Denominators remain nonzero modulo 101.

Testing an exponent collision adds one further cube-difference row. Every
relevant 4 by 4 minor has absolute value at most 16 by Hadamard's inequality,
which is less than 101. Zero-frame checks add a coordinate row. Target rank
checks add two coordinate rows and one subset row; expansion on the two
coordinate rows again leaves a minor of size at most four and absolute
value at most 16. Thus rank tests and exponent collisions agree over the
rationals and F101. Rational reconstruction makes the parity test exact.

The field engine handles a nonmonomial diagonal by pairing either of its
first two terms with all possible later partners. In any valid completion,
at least one of those two terms shares its final exponent with another
term: at most one singleton can remain. Therefore this smaller partner
set is exhaustive. It can visit fewer states than the all-pairs engine.

SELECTED INDEPENDENT ARITHMETIC COMPARISON

Forty-eight fixed words were run through both engines: 24 nonsymmetric
catalogue words with many terminals, and 24 fixed-seed words that permit
repeated projectors. Both engines found zero survivors and 54 terminals.
The field engine visited states at all relation depths zero through three.
This is a selected-case comparison, not a second full all-alphabet replay.

The completed 17106-job log is also checked for unique job indices, exact
word correspondence, zero survivors, and agreement with the stored totals.
No production search source was edited during this audit.
