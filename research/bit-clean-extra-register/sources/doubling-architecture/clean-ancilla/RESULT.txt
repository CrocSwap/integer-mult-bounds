EXACT RESULT FOR A FINITE CLEAN-ANCILLA CLASS

There is no eight-call word in the class below. This result does not
exclude arbitrary four-register scalar words.

The endpoint has three active outputs. Their address operators are
three invertible lower-triangular rational 3 by 3 frames. A fourth
input starts at zero. Its final output can be discarded.

Fix the coordinate basis I of F_2^4. Let B be any binary basis. The
allowed scalar projectors are the four coordinate projectors and
the four projectors onto the basis vectors of B along their dual
covectors. The word can use any eight ordered factors from this union,
with repeated projectors. Each paid address frame can be any nonzero
rational rank-one 3 by 3 matrix. There is no bound on its entries.

The catalogue has 840 unordered binary bases. Permutations of the
three active coordinates leave 166 basis orbits. The fourth coordinate
stays fixed. All 166 alphabets were checked. Fixing the first basis to
I is part of the stated class. It is not a normalization of an arbitrary
pair of bases under a general change of scalar coordinates.

The scalar generator uses two necessary filters. Each of the seven
active binary probes must touch at least three calls on each side.
Also, adjacent factors with commuting scalar projectors can be sorted.
The generator retains one least word in each such trace class. It uses
no reversal or inverse symmetry. The two distance arrays are separate;
the projectors need not be symmetric.

Every remaining scalar word goes through a complete relation search.
The clean version of the span-four lemma requires rational frame span
at least five. There are therefore at most three independent relations
among eight frames. The search branches on exact collisions of subset
sums. It requires zero active off-diagonal entries and one monomial in
each active diagonal entry. It imposes no other condition on the fourth
row or column. A terminal is rejected only after a target is proved to
lie in the span of at most two paid frames, or an earlier necessary
condition fails.

The field calculation uses F_101 as an exact representation of these
rational rank tests. Each tested minor has, after expansion along any
coordinate rows, at most four rows with entries in {-1,0,1}. Its absolute
determinant is at most 16. Rational echelon entries for the three original
relation rows have numerators and denominators at most four. Thus the
field calculation preserves the needed zero tests and permits unique
rational reconstruction before the parity test. The calculation does
not reduce physical rational frame entries modulo two.

Result:

  scalar trace words checked:      7,406,643
  relation states checked:      181,099,930
  active monomial terminals:        262,735
  terminals without rank obstacle:       0
  basis alphabets completed:           166

The exact counts are in ALL-BASIS-SUMMARY.json. The per-alphabet records
are in all-basis-results/. Reproduction uses ALPHABETS.txt, two_bases.cpp,
and run_two_bases.py. The C++ relation core was copied from the separate
bit8 agent's clean_f101.cpp. Its scalar driver was replaced for the
present class. The smaller Fraction implementation is search.py.

Independent checks: a second agent reconstructed the 840 bases, their
166 active-coordinate orbits, and the earlier 17,106 layer jobs. It also
compared the Fraction and field engines on 48 selected nonsymmetric
words. All terminal and survivor counts agreed on those words. This is
a selected-case second-engine check, not a second full replay of all
7,406,643 words. See ../rank-independent/clean-audit/.

No positive supplier gain follows from this result. The wider clean
ancilla problem, with arbitrary scalar rank-one idempotents, remains
open in this work.
