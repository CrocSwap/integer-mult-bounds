## Mathematical checks

Every instruction of the old fixed 70-role binding acts on one of those
owners. Instruction induction leaves the three outside record owners
unchanged, so that binding cannot remove their L markers. The extension
binds those existing owners as roles 70--72 once, before selector and stack
initialization. It adds addressed roles, not physical backings.

The unchanged prefix hash and fixed target relocation establish graph
composition. The independent terminal reviewer checks every suffix target
and the entire addressed role set, then executes the shifted graph with a
separate raw-array interpreter. The complete integration executes that same
suffix after the actual source consumer, rather than using an old numerical
producer merely to prepare a terminal fixture.

The 56-record guard consumes the inherited distinct-free-record invariant.
It proves the terminal live set by a physical length check, before any write.
It does not prove uniqueness or supply blank historical tails. Template and
record loops retain L until their backward scans finish; these invariants
prove all-width termination, erasure of the admitted prefixes and head
return. The inactive historical regions remain blank by their entry premise.
Metadata is erased only after the terminal guard and owned marker work.

Direct instruction counting gives, for u=P+n70+n71+n72,
R=848+2u, W=399+u, M=1680+2u, J=2u and H=1. The sum is 2928+7u.
The independent interpreter checks the individual counts, and its returning
mutation replaces a required write by an equally priced jump. The total
price alone would miss that error; the complete cell postcondition and
separate opcode counts detect it.

## Source attachment and accounting

The machine accepts the original service tuple only, with finite setup,
active and terminal phases. Old acquire/initialize/retire entries cannot be
re-entered during the active phase. Successful terminal return supplies the
source Boolean closed flag; neither this flag nor the phase wrapper is
represented as an already lowered complete higher controller.

Old arithmetic/lifecycle providers are blocked during their replacement
interval, and original Entry.close is blocked throughout the complete
invocation. The terminal additionally blocks raw Word/Port methods and host
live/free membership. The raw literal bridge does not call those methods.
Ghost ownership snapshots and counters are observer checks and do not select
scientific operations or supply values to the graph.

The fresh P=7 source result matches the exact CRT oracle. Its 2,339 literal
calls execute 3,120,094 instructions, of which 2,977 belong to the terminal.
All 76 physical identities and capacities persist, totaling 8,991 supplied
cells. Each source primitive also checks its backed window, so an implicit
out-of-range blank is not counted as a grant. These larger explicit windows
are not presented as a storage improvement over the predecessor fixture.

The observed retained native actions sum independently to 2,050: 1,011 Word,
151 guide, 782 owned record and 106 borrowed record actions. Subtracting the
literal contributions from the source meters gives the same total. The old
468 Entry-close actions are inside the new terminal bill; they are not
charged again as residual work. Higher Python call diagnostics are not a
physical controller price, and the proof does not claim a full caller tariff.
