What the result supplies
------------------------
The package gives one finite complex decoder on eleven streams of 64
values. Three carrier inputs have the specified line-frame interface.
All eight target inputs are arbitrary; a zero target frame means the
identity operator, not a zero value. In scalar coordinates it implements

X_i -> X_i,
Y_t -> Y_t + sum_i bit_i(t) X_i.

The physical carriers finish in the full frame, so they are not returned
as the same physical arrays. Each target finishes in its canonical
q(t)-perpendicular frame. The separate saved extension changes those eight
target operators to F C_q^-1 with exact monomial adapters.

The new capability is an explicit legal schedule in which a carrier can
grow after an earlier target is served. One final read frame does not fit
all earlier reads. This invalidates an extension of fixed-read-frame
counting to arbitrary growing frames. It does not invalidate the stated
fixed-frame theorem or prove a bound for every decoder.

Exact local account
-------------------
There are 39 base events: 12 scalar ADD scans and 27 positive-rank children.
Their profile is 15 H_1 + 2 H_2 + 5 H_3 + 4 H_4 + H_5, with rank mass 55.
The 27 calls use 23 distinct saved recipes. Each applied positive-rank
recipe has left and right monomial factors: 54 applied monomial records
in the base word. There is no COPY. The saved extension adds eight more
monomial records and zero positive child rank. This count of records is
not a primitive-operation invoice.

Every stream pays five units of rank growth. A carrier grows from rank
one to rank six; a target grows from rank zero to rank five. All moves are
strict nested ascents. This explains 3*5 + 8*5 = 55 without omitting a
nonmonotone transition.

The count assumes the prepared carrier interface. It does not price the
source factory, initial frame preparation, scalar or monomial primitives,
routing, workspace management, precision, owner controller, or recursive
hosting. No complete supplier, strict total-cost improvement, or verified
kappa improvement follows.
