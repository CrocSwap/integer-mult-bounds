# A restored third dirty role does not shorten actual width-one GL2 targets

This scoped proof and finite certificate were produced by Nick Petalas.
For every ordered pair
of GL2(F2) address permutations g,h, the actual full-stream endpoint
`diag(P_g,P_h,I_4)` has exact paid minimum `ell(g)+ell(h)`, where ell is
single-role coordinate-CNOT length. All three streams start with arbitrary
dirty payloads. Scalar GL3(F2) mixing is free, both coordinate labels cost
one, and the third role must be restored completely.

The result answers Open next question 1 of ywz65's
[PR2620](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/2620).
That two-role result and
[PR2599](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/2599)'s
nonfaithfulness distinction are cited predecessors, not audit or replay
targets. The new step is an actual-stream transfer and geodesic completion of
commdata2338's [PR1495](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/1495)
natural-image two-SWAP exclusion to the full mixed-target table. That
six-call exclusion is inherited, not claimed anew. The fresh finite
three-role classification corroborates the new transfer. The width-one
actual action matters: a formal group-algebra lower bound cannot simply be
transferred through this nonfaithful representation.

[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) gives the actual-stream normalization and finite-join
argument. The independent implementations use packed nine-row operators and
full twelve-column operators. One derives the 56 paid calls from all 28
rank-one idempotents; the other derives them from all 168 free scalar bases.
The explicit module bridge passes all 3,584 active-vector checks and the six
geodesic completions. Each engine constructs the complete radius-three ball
of 46,951 operators. They
agree on every operator, distance and all 10,073 target join probes. All 36
attaining endpoints pass all 432 full input columns, including the dirty
spectator. The 113 rejection controls include every one of 108 call omissions,
a spectator leak, incomplete/false ball data, a false short target and a known
positive short-word join.

Every possible shorter word is covered because the largest direct bound is
six and a word of length at most five splits into radius-two and radius-three
factors. This proves the stated exact minima, while avoiding enumeration of
the entire active GL6(F2) group. It is a finite certificate with a written
reduction, not a complete multiplication theorem or a new kappa.

## Scope and implementation limits

The claim has exactly three roles, one binary point column, unit-priced
coordinate calls and free binary scalar role mixing. A fourth role, other
address widths, alternate paid alphabets, clean inputs or incomplete
restoration change the question. Physical mixing, native conversion,
compiler, precision, stock, tape, paid/productive invoice and outer transfer
remain separate obligations. All five selected framework assumptions remain.
The selected frontier is unchanged. No selected cold replay, Lean, Docker,
independent human review or formal proof-kernel acceptance is claimed.

Every loop is bounded by the fixed domain: three BFS layers, 56 calls,
at most `1+56+56^2+56^3` candidate states, twelve columns, 4,096 vector values,
168 scalar bases, 36 targets and at most 1,807 small-ball join candidates per
target. Python's managed allocation is a documented departure from static
allocation; live collections are bounded by these finite domains. There is
no recursion, dynamic code generation, external input or persistent cache.
Functions stay below 60 physical lines. Checks use explicit exceptions and
remain active under optimized execution. Warning-strict normal and optimized
runs are the available diagnostics; no repository-wide static-analysis
configuration is changed.
