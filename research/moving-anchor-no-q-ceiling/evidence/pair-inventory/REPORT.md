## Axes are not owners

Let q be the central axis, and let 0,s,t be disjoint eight-axis blocks.
All statements lift columnwise to f digits per axis. A field is an arbitrary
function of every address coordinate, including all 8f anchor bits. The new
anchor creates no new owner array and no extra independent Gaussian output.
It enlarges the surrounding address volume by 2^(8f) relative to a comparable
two-fresh-block field without the anchor. Those arbitrary fibres cannot be
discarded, reset to zero, or counted as eight extra productive owners.

One main bank contains 112 arbitrary owner fields. A FULL has two banks,
224 main owners. Two sequential FULLs with the same main owners and fresh
independent native helper banks have 224+2*408=1040 original owner fields.
The eight extra axes are present within these fields; they are not another
eight fields. Under source helper autonomy, the 816 helpers retain independent
outputs once, on their source requested axes, with every other coordinate
including the anchor treated as an arbitrary spectator. If a different helper
reuse is proposed, this disjoint inventory no longer applies automatically.

## Literal pair and its exact boundary

Use D_b for the product of the eight actual Gaussian kernels on block b,
and P_s for the real coordinate swap (0,s). The source defines

    E_s=P_s D_0,       E_t=P_t D_0.

For s,t distinct and nonzero,

    W=E_t E_s=P_t P_s D_0 D_s.

This is an identity of literal operators, not merely of frame labels. The
Gaussian factors have their inherited actual phases; the coordinate swaps
have no added phase. They act on whole blocks at each column. The pair
contains no Q factor.

At each of the eight corresponding bit positions, the output coordinate
permutation P_t P_s sends label positions 0->s, s->t, t->0, fixing q and all
other labels. Before that permutation, D_0 D_s acts on blocks 0 and s and
leaves block t untouched. Therefore applying the explicit output permutation
(P_t P_s)^-1 leaves the exact rank-16 Gaussian D_0 D_s. It does not return
the anchor as identity: anchor inputs have been transformed. The last fresh
block t and q are spectators. Rank is 16, not 17, and no new reward follows
from the eight anchor axes alone.

For the pure diagonal word there is also a useful two-sided boundary fact:
with L=P_t P_s P_t, which swaps s and t,

    L W P_t = P_t D_0 D_s P_t = D_t D_s.

This returns the anchor identically for arbitrary anchor-dependent input,
using one input and one output coordinate permutation per affected field.
It proves that an extra H8 completion is not forced by this isolated diagonal
word alone. It is NOT a zero-cost recycling theorem or a full FULL-pair
identity. Its input chart is changed, its two boundary permutations must be
paid, and it does not remove the enlarged address volume or prove that a
larger caller accepts those charts. Nor does it straighten the coupled code
blocks of the actual FULLs.

The source moving FULLs are

    Ftilde_s=diag(E_s,E_s) L(C0 Q^-1),
    Btilde_s=diag(E_s,E_s) U(-Q C0).

Their entry is the inherited source ABI with P_s appended on both main banks.
All main arrays remain arbitrary and helper autonomy is inherited pointwise
over their spectators. For two forward FULLs, X_out=W X_in is pure on all
112 original X owners. But

    Y_out=W Y_in + (W C0 Q^-1 + E_t C0 Q^-1 E_s) X_in.

Thus the other 112 owners are not independent Gaussian jobs merely because
their diagonal block is W. For two backward FULLs the analogous pure bank
is Y; mixed orientations need their own full block calculation. This report
does not enter the active p2 caller question.

## Paid and finite ledger

The source center-free native price and helper reward are

    P=2201H1+412H2+510H3+127H4+40H5,
    H=28H4+7H7+47H8+326H9.

These are inherited local profiles, not a newly admitted moving-stage supplier.
Two FULLs pay at least 2P and retain at most their two disjoint helper banks
2H, with all those helper inputs distinct. No removed rank-eight center call
is credited again. Unknown closure costs are not subtracted from this bill.

precision and complete ordinary bill. All original native/internal movement,
rounding and helper allocation remain inherited; no such cost is zeroed.

## Strong optimistic pair screen, without a caller claim

The source does bind the diagonal pair W to rank 16. Give the proposal the
unrealized favorable grant that BOTH main banks become independent rank-16
jobs with all code cleanup, output permutations and other costs free. Its
optimistic moment is

    M(theta)=224*16^theta + 2H(theta) - 2P(theta),
    theta=1-C.

The paid rank mass is 2*5263=10526; the granted reward mass is
224*16+2*3471=10526. After cancellation,

    M(theta)= -4402 H1 -824 H2 -1020 H3 -198 H4 -80 H5
                +14 H7 +94 H8 +652 H9 +224 H16.

Every positive coefficient has rank strictly greater than 5 and every negative
coefficient has rank at most 5. Therefore M(theta)/5^theta is strictly
increasing in theta: each positive term has positive derivative, each negative
term below rank 5 also has positive derivative, and the rank-5 derivative is
zero. Since M(1)=0, M(theta)<0 for 0<=theta<1. In particular the optimistic
pair grant loses for every 0<C<1. The actual one-pure-bank F-F contract earns
less, so it cannot repair this inequality. This is an isolated rank-16 pair
screen, not an obstruction to changed terminal actions or a general caller.

No extra Q is granted: the Q and Q^-1 symbols in the code off-diagonal blocks
do not turn either pure diagonal bank into a rank-17 Gaussian job. A changed
consumer that genuinely supplies another Gaussian factor must price and prove
it before changing this reward offer.

## Annotation for the separate terminal-anchor design lead

Astra separately owns the hypothesis of using the existing anchor as the
terminal stage E0=D0 after the two distinct nonanchor stages. Algebraically
D0 W=P_t P_s D0 Ds Dt, so that word has 24 Gaussian axes after its explicit
permutation, not 25. For a native FULL on anchor, P0=I, hence there are zero
*additional moving-P* maps. That does not remove inherited native/port costs,
and the FULL still contains a nonzero L or U code. It cannot grant both banks
a free D0 action. Directly applying D0 to all 224 main fields would instead
pay 224 H8 calls. The separate design lead owns any complete cost comparison;
no triple search or physical source substitution is performed here.

## Boundary and credit

The isolated inventory is enough to reject the favorable rank-16 two-stage
offer; it does not solve anchor recycling, certify a complete target census,
or price a new caller. It neither proves that every anchor must be completed
with H8 nor that coordinate permutations are free. The relevant next question
belongs to the separately scoped changed terminal design, with no free Q
factor and all original fields/helper returns retained.
