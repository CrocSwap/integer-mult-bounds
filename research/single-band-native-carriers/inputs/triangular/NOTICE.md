# Attribution and source history

Ali Romman prepared the same-order native supplier, the triangular quotient router, its staged use, the completed Fourier pack schedule, the product integration and its verification, with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. The new work includes the reduced working grid, the measurements on matched payloads, and the new Lean arguments. This package uses Apache License 2.0.
