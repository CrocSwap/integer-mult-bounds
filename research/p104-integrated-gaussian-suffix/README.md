## Reproduce

The sparse registered runner uses CPython 3.12 and the standard library:

```sh
python3.12 -I -B scripts/research_package.py --package research/p104-integrated-gaussian-suffix
```

Each of the seven declared commands also works as `python3.12 -I -B research/p104-integrated-gaussian-suffix/verify.py MODE`, with `MODE` one of `price`, `calendar`, `raw`, `exterior`, `nested`, `product`, and `setup`. The runner rejects optimized Python, authenticates both archives and their whitelisted members, checks the review copies against those bytes, and reconstructs in fresh temporary directories. Each requested named scope must return zero with a matching recorded stdout digest; successful completion of one mode does not assert success of another. The `price` mode checks the #8073 prices and the #8076 addendum's source invoice, ordinary tariff, stage caps, composite invoice, independent price, cutoff and corrupted-owner control. The addendum economic checks can also be run alone with `MODE=addendum`. The other six registered modes exercise the #8073 construction; they are not represented as fresh #8076 physical replays. The separate #8076 scientific replays are reported in [EVIDENCE.md](EVIDENCE.md). The dense physical product is expensive; run one product job at a time on a shared worker.
