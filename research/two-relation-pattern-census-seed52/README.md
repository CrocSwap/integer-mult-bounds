# Two-relation four-register bit patterns (seed 52ca)

**Result: finite structural census and a scoped candidate obstruction; no new κ.**
Among the 120 rank-one idempotents on four binary scalar registers, there are
105 distinct off-diagonal patterns including zero and 196 realizable
two-dimensional pattern planes. In 176 planes the three nonzero patterns share a topological order.
The other 20 planes have cyclic union; every nonzero pattern in each of those
planes has exactly one idempotent lift. A fixed 15-call candidate family using
those 20 planes has no exact word under the tested linear relation-signature
assignment. This does not prove a general two-relation lower bound.

## Question and model

The task is the next structural step after the one-relation result in
[`bit-one-relation-seed04`](../bit-one-relation-seed04/README.md): can a four-register
rational-address construction exploit two independent frame relations to save
one paid rank-one call on four rank-four targets?

The finite pattern census uses

\[
N=xy^T,\qquad x,y\in\mathbb F_2^4,\qquad y^Tx=1.
\]

There are 120 such rank-one idempotents. A pattern records the 12 directed
off-diagonal entries of N, ordered lexicographically by (row,column);
diagonal entries are not included. Every enumerated N has an odd-denominator
rational idempotent lift: for the 0/1 representatives x,y, let d=y^Tx in
{1,3} and P=xy^T/d. Then P has rank one, is idempotent over Q, and reduces to
N modulo 2. An exact rational word using odd-denominator projectors and these
integral addresses must reduce to its checked F2 word. The census does not
cover rational projectors whose entries have even denominators.

A two-dimensional pattern plane is a set
{0,p,q,p+q} whose three nonzero patterns are all realized by idempotents.
One implementation enumerates outer products and tests acyclicity by
topological sorting. A second builds the matrices first and checks cycles by
transitive closure; both enumerate the same projector fibres and planes.

The exact 20 exceptional planes and their unique (x,y) lifts are recorded in
[`CERTIFICATE.json`](CERTIFICATE.json). For example, patterns (19,200,219)
have lifts (3,6), (6,3), (7,7). The first two are opposite acyclic
three-edge patterns on coordinates 0–2; their sum is the cyclic bidirected
triangle pattern. The full-plane and common-order-plane lists are bound by
the SHA-256 values in the certificate. The canonical certificate SHA-256 is
`690de78db74c1ba59a4092b8f7b9f8f54c5d53d3650d9e8d78448cdddda18321`.

For every common-order plane, each rank-one idempotent with one of its patterns
is triangular in the same coordinate order. Such an idempotent has trace one
and can have at most one nonzero diagonal entry: two diagonal ones would force
the corresponding pair of opposite off-diagonal edges, creating a cycle. So
each factor contributes its address to exactly one diagonal coordinate. In a
product, the address assigned to each output coordinate is the sum of its
frames. If that output address has rank four, subadditivity of matrix rank
requires at least four rank-one frames assigned to it. Four rank-four outputs
therefore require at least 16 calls. This excludes a 15-call saving inside
that common-order pattern class. The rational interpretation is limited to
the odd-denominator projector class described above. This is a statement about
the declared pattern class, not every possible word with two rational relations.

## Fixed 15-call construction attempt

The four targets are

C1=I, C2=I+E12, C3=I+E23, C4=I+E34.

All have rational rank four. The ordered rank-one address list is

    A1=E11             A5=E33             A9 =E12
    A2=E22             A6=E11+E31         A10=E13
    A3=E11+E21         A7=E33-E31         A11=E14
    A4=E22-E21         A8=E44             A12=E23
                                             A13=E24
                                             A14=E32
                                             A15=E34

Its rational span has dimension 13 and its relation lattice is generated by
the following two independent relations. The address matrix also has rank 13
over F2, so they generate the complete relation space after reduction modulo
2 (verified directly).

    A1 + A2 = A3 + A4
    A1 + A5 = A6 + A7

Each target is in the address span. A direct row decomposition gives an exact
16-call baseline, four rank-one factors per rank-four target. The proposed
15-call word would save one local paid call (6.25%) if realized.

For the bounded construction pass, choose one exceptional plane and a
permutation (a,b,c) of its three unique projector lifts. The first seven
projectors are fixed by N1=a, N2=N3=N4=b, and N5=N6=N7=c, matching the two
four-term relation blocks in the address list. The remaining eight factors
have zero off-diagonal pattern and are coordinate projectors. There are 20
planes and six assignments per plane, for 120 tested prefixes.
Exact sparse multiplication in the full rational-address group algebra finds
no first-seven prefix that is diagonal with one monomial per output. Since the
last eight factors are diagonal monomials, none of their 4^8 coordinate
assignments can repair that prefix. The 16-call baseline passes the same exact
product engine; dropping a baseline call and corrupting an address are both
rejected.

This search does **not** show that every two-relation word has a pattern map
linear in the two relation signatures, nor does it cover other address lists
or factor orders. Those are the main remaining openings.

## Open next questions

1. **Is the linear relation-signature pattern assignment necessary for every
   exact two-relation word?** This would turn the finite candidate screen into
   a general reduction. First step: extract the coefficient constraints from
   the Laurent group algebra modulo two primitive relations. Blocker: unlike
   the one-relation case, the resulting coefficient ideal has two generators
   and permits interleaving.
2. **Can a word outside this fixed 15-address schedule realize a rank saving?**
   First step: use one of the 20 exceptional pattern planes to choose a new
   address list with two independent rational relations and run exact sparse
   formal expansion. Blocker: any positive word still needs a full physical
   frame, copy, hosting, precision, restoration, and recursive invoice.
