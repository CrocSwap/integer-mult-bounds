# Ten deletions at residual rank six

Use the pinned P112 label map `ell(t)` into `F2^9` and doubled E8 root
representatives `r_t` in `Q^8`. Write

```
Lambda(V) = {t : ell(t) belongs to V}
Off(h) = {t : r_t dot h != 0}.
```

## The finite theorem

For every pair of six-dimensional binary label spaces `V1,V2`, let `H` be
the projective directions for which there is a separately chosen set `D_h`
of at most ten P112 lines such that

```
rank_F2 ell(Off(h) \ (Lambda(V1) union Lambda(V2) union D_h)) <= 6.
```

Then `dim span(H) <= 4` over the reals and every characteristic-zero field.
Four is attained with separate deletion sets. The upper bound also holds
when one common deletion set is required. Common-set attainment is not
claimed. There can be ten qualifying projective directions; a bound of four
on their count would be false.

The proof combines the direction-completeness argument below with an exact
finite census. It is a finite geometric theorem, not a multiplication theorem.

## Complete direction coverage

The native replay enumerates all 788,035 three-dimensional annihilators in
`F2^9`. Their kernels are all six-dimensional label spaces. It verifies that
every six-flat contains at most twenty P112 lines. A residual set of binary
rank at most six extends into a six-flat, so every admissible direction has

```
|Off(h)| <= 20 + 20 + 10 + 20 = 70.
```

At least 42 of the 112 live root lines, and therefore at least 42 of the
120 E8 root lines, are perpendicular to `h`.

Here is a finite specialization of the usual Weyl-chamber argument. The
displayed eight simple roots in `geometry.py` form an E8 simple system.
Exact coordinates verify that every one of the 120 root lines is positive
or negative in that basis. In a closed Weyl chamber, the perpendicular roots
are generated by the simple roots on which the direction vanishes. For a
face with at most six vanishing simple roots, enlarge that set to six.
The replay checks all 28 such six-node subsets and finds at most 36 root
lines. Thus at least 42 perpendicular lines force exactly seven vanishing
simple roots. The direction is one of eight fundamental-weight rays, up to
the Weyl group.

The eight ray counts are `42,28,22,14,16,23,37,63`. Only the first and last
can qualify. Closing their projective directions under the eight exact simple
reflections gives 1,080 D7 normals and 120 E7/root normals. Closure is checked,
with a hard capacity of 1,200. The actual P112 support cutoff of seventy
retains 128 D7 normals and all 120 root normals, hence 248 candidates. In
particular, extending the predecessor's root-only direction list would have
left an unproved completeness step.

The same catalogue certifies the code distance 53 used below. Any direction
of smaller support would meet the cutoff of seventy and hence appear in the
catalogue, whose minimum support is 53. Root directions attain that minimum.

For an arbitrary characteristic-zero field, the rational root vectors
perpendicular to `h` have rational rank at most seven. They cannot have rank
at most six: a generic real normal to their span would give a perpendicular
root system of rank at most six with at least 42 lines, contradicting the
six-node check. Their rational kernel is therefore one-dimensional. The
field-valued `h` is proportional to a rational generator of that kernel.
Apply the real chamber argument to this generator. Ranks of the resulting
rational direction vectors are unchanged by scalar extension.

This argument uses the standard Weyl-chamber theorem for a finite root
system. It is not a proof-kernel formalization of that theorem.

## Pair coverage and rank-preserving symmetries

The 788,035 flats give 776,805 distinct nonempty P112 masks. Empty masks
cannot qualify a direction: one remaining covering flat, one residual flat
and ten deletions contain at most fifty lines, below the minimum nonzero
P112 support of 53.

The twelve pinned generators are checked to be binary-label linear and to
permute all 248 direction support masks. For a span bound, support closure
alone does not justify reducing pairs by symmetry. The new signed-Gram check
also assigns signs to all 112 root lines and verifies every pair's Gram
entry after permutation. The P112 root vectors span `Q^8`. Equal Gram
matrices therefore define an invertible rational orthogonal map realizing
each signed permutation. The map carries `Off(h)` to the permuted support
of the transformed direction and preserves rational direction rank.

Union-find under these checked generators gives 159 first-flat orbits.
One representative from each orbit, paired with every second mask, covers
all pairs. This is 123,511,995 representative pairs. The replay retains
every orbit row; a prefix or inconsistent total is rejected.

For a fixed first flat, a direction is screened out only if its remaining
support exceeds fifty. For a pair, it is screened out only if its remaining
support exceeds thirty. These are necessary size inequalities from the
twenty-line residual-flat bound and ten-line deletion budget. Every surviving
test is decided by the exact oracle below. No floating-point decision occurs.

The complete ten-deletion result is span at most four, at most ten directions,

## Deletion and rational-rank oracles

The deletion oracle is retained from PR3161. If a residual label set has
rank `r>6`, put `k=r-6`, which is at most three. A deletion of at most `p`
lines leaves rank at most six exactly when `k` independent words of the
restricted binary character code have support union at most `p`. In one
direction, take independent annihilating forms of the retained span. In the
other, retain their common kernel. Enumerating all 511 nonzero ambient
characters and deduplicating their restricted words implements the quotient
by the annihilator of the residual span. Every chosen word has weight at
most `p`. Distinct nonzero words are pairwise independent; three are
independent exactly when the third is not the XOR of the first two.

The native self-test compares all 4,096 subsets of a twelve-line fixture
with literal exhaustive deletion, at every budget zero through ten. This
gives 45,056 comparisons. Positive witnesses retain actual cuts, separately
checked in Python using explicit XOR closure.

Rational direction rank uses integer elimination, with each row divided by
its coordinate gcd. Stored entries have absolute value at most `10^9`;
each multiplication/subtraction is bounded by `2*10^18`, inside signed
64-bit range. The post-division bound is checked after every reduction;
exhaustion fails rather than returning a rank. Python Fraction elimination
checks 248 literal catalogue triples independently. A determinant-minus-two
triple distinguishes rational rank from binary rank, and its dependent
mutation is a separate control. The sharp witnesses have independently
checked rational ranks three and four.

## One newly excluded beta shape

Retain the pinned TWOSIDE low-piece hypotheses. Let `B` be a basis of pieces,
`T=span(B)`, `C` the P112 root code, and `d=dim(T intersect C)>=b`. Every
nonzero element of `C` has support at least 53. Write `|B|=rho+b`.

If `rho` chosen pieces have combined support below 53, their images in
`T/(T intersect C)` are independent: a nonzero combination cannot be a
codeword with so small a support. The quotient has dimension at most `rho`,
so these images form a quotient basis and `d=b`.

For each nonbasis piece `q_y`, subtract the unique combination of quotient
basis pieces with the same quotient image. This gives a nonzero element
`c_y` of `C`, supported on the chosen pieces and `q_y`. The `b` elements
`c_y` are independent because their coordinates on the nonbasis elements
of `B` are distinct unit vectors. This proves the fundamental-circuit step
used in the predecessor; the map from directions to `C` is injective since
the live roots span eight dimensions.

In `(b,t)=(5,4)`, the newly excluded weight multiset is

```
B: 2,4,5,6,6,6,6,6,6
X0: 2,4,5,6
```

Here `rho=4`, and the chosen support cap is `2+8+12+20=42<53`.
The weight-five and weight-six supports lie in two six-flats. The weight-two
and weight-four supports supply one common deletion set of at most ten
lines. Every nonbasis piece has weight six, so the five independent
fundamental-circuit directions all satisfy the finite theorem. Its span
bound of four is a contradiction.

This exclusion does not depend on the inherited weight-five capacity twelve.
Alternatively choose `X0=(2,4,6,6)`. Since every P112 label has first bit one,
a rank-two or rank-four flat has at most two or eight live labels. Together
with the replayed six-flat capacity twenty, the support cap is fifty, still
below 53. The complement has weights `5,6,6,6,6`, all at most six, and the
same ten-line deletion argument applies.

The new weight enumeration reproduces the pinned 15 BK/BK7 survivors over
all 4,795 weight multisets of lengths five through nine. The preceding
eight-deletion theorem excludes twelve. The ten-deletion theorem excludes
exactly the additional shape above under this covering rule, leaving

```

3,4,4,6,6,6,6,6,6.
```

The minimum extra-line budgets certified by weight-based two-six-flat covers
are ten for the newly excluded shape and fourteen/twelve for the two
remainders. These minima do not rule out covers exploiting the actual
