# Rank-9 realisations of the E8 orthogonality pattern are B-form or the A2 family (classification; no κ claim)

> **Scope, stated first.** Pure matrix classification on the **E8 unit** (120 lines). **No κ claim**, no window, no supplier, no cost of any decoder. It completes the *existence step* that #2784 (*Decoder floors, case (c): the A2 family of non-B-form rank-9 realisations*, open) left open as "Conjecture B\*": #2784 proves floors for the A2 family and says explicitly that it does not prove that every rank-9 pattern realisation is B-form or A2. **P112 / P104 are not claimed here** (see "Not claimed").
> Labels: **PROVEN** (argument in this README or the shipped card text), **CHECKED** (recomputed by `verify.sh`; SAT steps with a DRAT proof checked by the standard `drat-trim`), **RECORDED** (a run whose log is shipped and whose headline line `verify.sh` asserts, but which the default `verify.sh` does not repeat), **CONDITIONAL [verify]** (an external fact not checkable here). kissat 4.0.4 only, never CaDiCaL.
> **Overlap.** A search of PR bodies for rank-9 / rank-nine realisation classifications found none besides #2784 (this author's, the A2 family) and #1212 (burntout76, merged: rank-8 rigidity; rank 9 stated open). #2338 / #2724 / #2639 are decoder floors, not classifications.

## 1. Result

Setting (that of #2338 / #2784): a field `K` of **characteristic 0**; `M` a `120 × 120` matrix over `K` indexed by the E8 root lines, with `M_tt ≠ 0` and, for `t ≠ s`, `M_ts = 0 ⇔ r_t ⊥ r_s` ("iff pattern"); `G` the Gram matrix of chosen roots.
**B-form:** `M = D₁ G D₂ + c βᵀ` with `D₁, D₂` diagonal and `c_u β_s = 0` whenever `u ⊥ s`. **A2 family:** `M` is Gram (up to diagonal scalings) off the three rows of one A2 triangle, and row `u_i` is the Gram row rescaled by one factor on `Nbar(u_i) ∩ O_{u_j}` and another on `Nbar(u_i) ∩ O_{u_k}` (#2784 §2.2; construction `checks/a2e8.py`).

## 2. The proof chain (E8; pivot coordinates)

Full text: `evidence/BSTAR_THEORY_snapshot.md` (§2 frame calculus, Theorems P, A), `evidence/CONJB_THEORY_snapshot.md` (Theorem G, A2 family), `evidence/KSTAR_THEORY_snapshot.md` (§1 S, §2 Σ, §7 R). Working-card texts, copies; their status lines are working-time statements.

* **Pivot coordinates.** For a frame `F` (8 pairwise orthogonal lines), `M = M[:,F] M[F,F]⁻¹ M[F,:] + x yᵀ` with `x_F = y_F = 0`; row `u` is `a_u = (α_u, x_u)` with `supp α_u` the `F`-tetrad of `u`. `D_F = supp x`, `D′_F = supp y`. Schur rule: `x|_T ≠ 0 ⇒ y|_{T^c} = 0`. `Σ_a = {u : dim span a(O_u) = 7}`, `Σ_b` likewise; `f ∈ Σ_a ⇔ D_F ⊆ Nbar(f)` for `f ∈ F`.
* **Theorem P.** If the "unpinned" pairs of a P-compatible frame form a matching, the rows off `D_F` are Gram rows (pair pinning `φ(u) = ψ(s)^⊥`, triangles force `B′ = −B` since char ≠ 2, gauge `μ`, then S1); Theorem G finishes.
* **Theorem A.** `f₁ ⊥ f₂` both in `Σ_a` in a frame ⇒ `D_F` lies in the three tetrads through `{1,2}` ⇒ only `{1,2}` is unpinned ⇒ Theorem P.
* **Theorem S** (`Σ_a ∩ Σ_b ∋ f₁`). Off index 1 everything is Gram-shaped; the only remaining constraint is `P_u · Q_s = 1` for `u ⊥ s` in the 56 lines `Nbar(f₁)`, i.e. points `P̂_u` and lines `Q̂_s` of `P²(K)` incident whenever `u ⊥ s`. The Boolean abstraction keeps only necessary conditions (equalities of points/lines with transitivity; two distinct points share at most one line, and dually) and negates the conclusion; it is UNSAT, so some point class meets `Γ(s)` for every column (or the dual), which makes its rows Gram rows and Theorem G applies.
* **Theorem Σ** (`f₁ ∈ Σ_a`). Columns are Gram-shaped off index 1; for `u ∈ D` the relation `y_s = g_u · r_s` on `E6_u`; the cocycle lemma of Theorem G (using (S2), (S3′), (W2): the 56 vectors `w_u` give 28 directions, no three in a plane) glues the `g_u` to one `g`; then either `f₁ ∈ Σ_b` (Theorem S) or some `s ∈ O_{f₁}` is in `Σ_a` orthogonal to `f₁` (Theorem A). The transposed case is symmetric.
* **Theorem R** (`Σ_a = Σ_b = ∅`). Both sides non-Gram-shaped (else Theorem G and B-form / A2 both have `Σ ≠ ∅`), so `D, D′` meet every `O_s`. The pinning model with ratio equalities (§4) plus these hypotheses is UNSAT on `frames()[0]` (`W(E8)` is transitive on frames).
* **Conclusion.** `Σ_a ∪ Σ_b ≠ ∅` ⇒ Σ ⇒ B-form or A2; `Σ_a = Σ_b = ∅` is impossible by R. Lemma E (CONJB) is not used.

## 3. What the finite steps are, and what `verify.sh` does

`bash verify.sh [/fresh/outdir]` (needs `numpy`, `sympy`, kissat 4.0.4, drat-trim, `cc`, `xz`; one heavy process at a time, `nice 10`; missing tools give SKIP lines and exit 1, never PASS):

0. `MANIFEST.sha256`.
1. Exact finite facts: both families exist (`a2e8.py`); S1, S2 (`spans.py`); S3′ on 1512 pairs and W2 (`spans3.py`); `pframes.py` (2025/2025 frames P-compatible, 3780/3780 orthogonal pairs covered).
2. **Theorem S step, in full by default.** `s56.py` regenerates the 56-problem CNF (sha256 pinned for `f₁ = 0`), kissat `--factor=false --preprocessfactor=false` gives UNSAT in under a second, and the fresh DRAT proof is checked by the standard **drat-trim** and by the auxiliary `rupc` (a C forward RUP checker that honours deletions and rejects RAT; `rup.py` is an older Python variant, shipped, not used by `verify.sh`). `f₁ = 0..15`; `FULL=1`: all 120 lines.
3. **Theorem R step.** `pin.py` regenerates `pin3.cnf`; its sha256 equals the pinned one (byte-identical to the working card's file). The shipped proof `evidence/proofs/pin3.drat.xz` (11.7 MB; 162 608 912 bytes decompressed) is checked against its pinned sha256. **`FULL=1`**: drat-trim on the shipped proof (about 2 to 3 minutes), then a fresh kissat solve (limit 1200 s; about 10 minutes originally) and drat-trim on the fresh proof. Flags as for the shipped proof: without `--factor=false --preprocessfactor=false` kissat adds extension variables, which the auxiliary `rupc` rejects.
4. `soundtest.py` (rules on true data) and the mutation control.
5. Recorded logs (asserted, not re-run): the audit's drat-trim on `pin3` (`s VERIFIED`, 3 513 012 clauses, 125 s) and our own drat-trim run on the decompressed shipped proof (`evidence/logs/dratrim_shipped_pin3.log.txt`, `s VERIFIED`, 137 s), the working card's `rupc` run (`VERIFIED` after 2 382 724 lemmas, 890 s), `s56` for `f₁ = 0..15`.

The CNF generators are the certificate's trust anchor: the proof is a refutation of *that* formula; the soundness of the formula (§4) is a separate, human-checkable obligation, which is what the soundness test and the audit probe.

## 4. The pinning model (Theorem R): what each rule asserts

Variables: `x[u]`, `y[s]` (nonzero status of `x_u`, `y_s`), equalities of the pair ratios `ρ_p(u) = α_u(b)/α_u(a)` (rows) and `τ_p(s) = −β_s(a)/β_s(b)` (columns) over each frame pair `p = {a,b}`, equalities of `rat_i(u) = x_u/α_u(i)`, all transitive. Since `α_u|_p · β_s|_p = α_u(a) β_s(b) (ρ_p(u) − τ_p(s))`: ORTH / NON (orthogonal: `x_u y_s = 0 ⇒ ρ = τ`, `x_u y_s ≠ 0 ⇒ ρ ≠ τ`; non-orthogonal same sign: `x_u y_s = 0 ⇒ ρ ≠ τ`), TRI (triangle products, char ≠ 2), K³ and ZZ (the local lemma: two rows of one ε-class with independent `(φ, x)` force one common direction for the opposite-sign columns), D1, TW, RATIO and the common-partner rule, Schur, rank 9, the residue clauses, and "rows (columns) not Gram-shaped". Each has a one-line proof in `evidence/KSTAR_THEORY_snapshot.md` §7; sanity there: SAT with the residue dropped, SAT with "not Gram-shaped" dropped. **Note** NON uses that non-orthogonal entries are nonzero (the iff pattern).

## 5. Downstream remark: what this does and does not close (case (c) on E8)

With #2784 (A2-family decoder floors, E8 ≥ 72) this reduces case (c) (rank-9 square decoders) on E8 to decoders on **B-form** realisations. Exactly what is known about B-form (working cards MASK-RANK9 / MASK9, not shipped here; stated, not claimed):

* **Proven in the working cards:** a term with `p ∝ c` and `q ∝ β` (Case C) costs ≥ 64; Case A with `|supp c| = 1` (`c = e_t`, the #2321 / #2385 `K_λ` family, and its transpose) costs ≥ 64, modulo a CHECKED single-line puncture census; the generic 8-clique type (random integer `c, β`) is **CHECKED** ≥ 65 for the sampled values.
* **Open there:** B-form with every `x_j, y_j ≠ 0` (Case B) for `|supp c| ≥ 2`; non-generic `c, β` (special values add deficient classes); singular `D`; and `m ≥ 10` on the B-locus (#2784 does not claim it either).

So **a decoder floor of 72 for all rank-9 square decoders of all rank-9 realisations on E8 is not proven**: A2 family: #2784; B-form: only the cases listed above. Nothing about `m ≥ 10`, `h ≥ 10`, or rank-8 `N` with nine terms (#2724) changes.

## 6. Relation to philippeVV's open item (R1), Discussion #1587 follow-up 8

Their follow-up 8 (artifact `decoder-law/lift-family-2` @ 93766d7d, with expSM / expSG behind it) lists as open (R1): "classification: every rank-9 completion is a lift" (their κ-family normal form; quoted verbatim from the follow-up), and proves credit ≤ 9 (cost ≥ 72) for every lift on a support with independent roots, plus non-concurrent A2 triples. **Credit:** the lift normal form, the credit/cost machinery, and the 28-completion census with tangent dimensions are theirs; nothing here uses them. The exact relation:

* **Different hypothesis (so R1 does not follow as stated).** Their model (`decoder-law/README.md`, §0): `N = Σ v_k w_kᵀ` over `Q`, `N_aa = 1`, `N_ab = 0` for `a ⊥ b`, and **free when `ip = ±1`**. So a "completion" may have zeros on non-orthogonal pairs. Our theorem assumes the full iff pattern (`M_ts ≠ 0` for non-orthogonal `t ≠ s`). Hence it classifies the completions whose non-orthogonal entries are all nonzero, and says nothing about the rest. Their field is `Q`; ours is characteristic 0, which includes it.
* **"Lift" versus B-form ∪ A2 (unverified identification).** Their lifts are defined relative to a rank-8 completion on `T = V∖S` (their R2, rank-8 rigidity on `T`, which they assume), with `S` independent roots and κ-factors `(A, B)`; their non-concurrent A2 triples correspond plausibly to our A2 family, and `|S| = 1` / clique supports to B-form with small supports. We have **not** compared the normal forms, so we do not claim that every B-form realisation (arbitrary `supp c`, `supp β` with the orthogonality condition) or every A2 member is a κ-lift in their sense; their open (R3) is "lifts on dependent non-clique S"; we do not identify which B-form realisations, if any, it concerns.
* **Therefore:** our result gives R1 *for the iff-pattern subclass, provided B-form ∪ A2 ⊆ lifts*. Two steps remain for R1 itself: (i) show every B-form and A2 realisation fits their κ-family normal form (their R3 for dependent supports); (ii) remove the iff assumption. For (ii) the proof would have to be redone with "maybe zero" non-orthogonal entries: Theorem G survives (it uses non-orthogonality only through `d₁ ≠ 0`, `D₂` invertible, `M_uu ≠ 0`), but Theorem P (supports of `α_u`, `β_s` exact so `φ, ψ ∈ P¹∖{0,∞}`), the NON rule and the S-step reduction use nonzero entries, so the two SAT models would need to be rebuilt and re-certified. Not attempted.
