# Observation, state, and geometry: building complete suppliers for larger κ

Local identities and decoders can lose their savings when the caller pays for observation access, restoration, transport or precision. The ten targets below address those boundaries through outside mathematics and reusable search tools. The [detailed assessment](README.md) expands the literature, predecessor work and stopping conditions.

The baseline is canonical commit [`1a3ced6`](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/commit/1a3ced60b2f7a46bc70280a04f2eeb8ca027c700): **conditional κ = 0.003027288235**, ordinary BIT saving **0.0034363538396**, conservatively admitted as **0.0034**. The binding outer row is `main_transform`; the [selected record](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/1a3ced60b2f7a46bc70280a04f2eeb8ca027c700/certificates/selected-result.json) identifies the five retained framework assumptions. This is an exploratory research agenda with diagnostic tooling; no new κ or admitted supplier is established.

## Portfolio tradeoffs

**1, 6 and 4** are the suggested construction starting points: changed complex endpoints, complementary BIT progress and paid-cost geometry. **5** offers a structural alternative when immediate format restoration remains too expensive. These routes have substantial upside and can still fail their complete-cost gates.

**2, 8 and 9** offer reusable optimization or verification results with less dependence on a new profitable geometry. Scoped negative outcomes can retain value, although infrastructure alone does not raise κ. **7** should accompany approximate constructions; **10** merits a separate foundation effort with a named lemma and budget. Existing active scopes should be checked before assignment.

The decisive comparison is the whole caller: a query saving can return as cleanup, a complex gain can expose the BIT bottleneck, and a low-rank factor can require an expensive physical realization. The [detailed assessment](README.md) records these dependencies and the evidence that would justify expanding each family.

## Diagnostic evidence

The [probe](probe.py) uses the complete selected histograms: **64,260 paid jobs**, **10,928 productive fields**, and exact rank-mass surplus **752**. For `F(C) = Σ_targets n_r r^(1-C) − Σ_paid n_r r^(1-C)`:

| Candidate saving C | Approximate raw deficit | Isolated H1 deletions for positive raw margin, all else fixed |
|---|---:|---:|
| .0033 | 67.256506 | 68 |
| .0034 | 91.898559 | 92 |
| .004 | 239.525048 | 240 |
| .006 | 728.828475 | 729 |
| .01 | 1694.706712 | 1695 |

These are numerical necessary screens **before compiler reserves, physical feasibility and outer admission**. Decimal powers use 65 significant digits without outward error bounds. The counts exhibit no legal deletions and do not transfer to changed profiles. The selected 144 paid/target H8 pairs cancel; every new target needs a producer and every lost target its full debit.

A second, exact scalar diagnostic computes `rank([S; H]) − rank(S)` for available answers `Sz` and demanded answers `Hz`. It detects missing linear information, including correlated responses, source-version changes and Q/F2 differences. Native frames, oracle legality, acquisition, volume and restoration remain outside this scalar model.

```sh
python3 -B scripts/research_selected_baseline.py
python3 -I -B research/outside-input-research-targets/probe.py --check-source-root .
python3 -O -I -B research/outside-input-research-targets/probe.py --check-source-root .
```

On **CPython 3.14.5, macOS 26.5.1, arm64**, normal, optimized and portable runs matched byte-for-byte. The custom-cut control passed; an altered histogram with unchanged source pins rejected under optimized Python. [PROFILE.json](PROFILE.json), [RESULT.json](RESULT.json) and [VALIDATION.json](VALIDATION.json) retain source bindings, output and checks. The selected metadata baseline passed. Native engines, the full selected replay, Lean, Docker, SDP, heavy censuses and hosted CI were not run; their obligations remain outside these diagnostics.
