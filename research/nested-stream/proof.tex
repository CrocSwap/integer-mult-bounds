\subsection{Nested corners on the smaller dimension-thirty stream producer}
\label{sec:stream-nested-composition}
This composition is by Rohan Arun with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance.
It combines eumemic's smaller producer and all-complex batching (PR~\#15),

\paragraph{The preserved producer contract.}
Use $h=30$, $H=h^2=900$, $m=h^3=27000$, $v=\binom{30}{5}=142506$,
and the PR~\#15 physical producer with $R=13056812$ auxiliary roles.
The complete producer was rebuilt and independently audited for this
composition. Its source supports, nondegenerate source/dual frames,
bilateral physical allocation and all designated outputs are checked.
The full matching is a bijection on all $142506$ labels with intersection
two. The downward rank per orientation is $\binom{30}{2}28=12180$;
there is no extra frame loss. These are finite checks, not a replacement
for the general compiler argument.

Here is why the change of producer does not invalidate the geometric
profiles. PR~\#15 changes the internal scalar DAG and the number of physical
auxiliaries by continuing wires along nested frames. Each physical
auxiliary still enters its invocation at $D_0$ and exits at $D_1$ in both
orientations. All data endpoints and the interstage partner permutation
are unchanged. Invertible gates preserve arbitrary dirty scratch; the
scalar map remains the same transparent wrapper. Thus source relocation
still changes the stage-two auxiliary entrance and exit to $0$ and
$I+P_{D_0}-P_{D_1}$, respectively, preserving total rank $m-h$.
The shared join and the two data entrances have exactly the projector
forms required by Lemma~\ref{lem:nested-source-basis}. Only their
multiplicities and the count of other singleton edges change.

The nested-basis lemma is dimension-parametric for $h\ge4$. Apply it
at $h=30$ to the entire finite family of these endpoint projectors and
their duals. All remaining edges can use the singleton compiler for
arbitrary rational matrices. Its nonzero rational polynomial conditions and
invertibility conditions admit one rational specialization. Conjugating
all fixed matrices by that one basis preserves endpoint identities,
scalar maps, ranks and dirty-scratch restoration. The address prime is
chosen after the rational factors, as in the retained interchange proof.
No choice of a different basis for each edge is permitted.

Put $B=v^2R=265157216293565232$ and $N=v^3=2894006152890216$.
The four disjoint physical edge classes have profiles

\text{class}&\text{copies}&\text{rank}&\text{singletons}&\text{blocks}\\\hline
\text{shared auxiliary join}&B&26940&60&26880\\

The zero auxiliary entrance is not counted again. In particular, the
historical auxiliary entrance block before source relocation cannot be
added to this table. Every other rank unit uses the retained singleton
compiler. The nested exit block replaces its $h$ singleton pivots; it
does not reduce rank. The two data-corner blocks similarly replace only
their own singleton pivots.

The resulting exact counts are
\[
 W=2v^2(v+R)=536102444892910896,\quad
 L=3v^2\binom h2(h-2)=742052859715440,
\]
\[
 s=Wm-N+2L=14474764602208160732664,\qquad
 \eta=\frac{Wm-s}{Wm}=\frac{3857}{39597954000}.
\]
Writing $(B_i,t_i)$ for the six blocks in the table, the residual singleton
count is $S=s-\sum_iB_it_i=41126567423153946552$. Define
$r_0=1/m$, $w_0=S/(Wm)$ and $r_i=t_i/m$, $w_i=B_it_i/(Wm)$.
The supplied exact rational logarithm enclosures $\ell_i>\log(1/r_i)$
give
\[
 a_{\rm b}=\frac{2496689}{10^{12}},\quad
 \sum_i w_i=1-\eta,\quad 0<a_{\rm b}\ell_i<1,\quad
 \sum_i\frac{w_i}{1-a_{\rm b}\ell_i}<1-\frac{19}{10^{15}}.
\]
Since $e^x\le(1-x)^{-1}$ for $0\le x<1$, the exact mixed-width
moment is strictly below one at $\tau=1-a_{\rm b}$. Every block is a
contiguous interval in the common physical order and has width below $m$.
The finite-alphabet row-reservation and strong-induction construction in
Section~\ref{sec:mixed-width-rows} therefore applies with these constants.

\begin{lemma}[Nested-stream arbitrary-width interchange]
\label{lem:stream-nested-chunk-swap}
The arbitrary-width interchange interface of
Lemma~\ref{lem:nested-chunk-swap} holds with
$\tau=1-2496689/10^{12}$ and the dimension-thirty producer above.
It includes arbitrary spectator gaps, arbitrary payload values, the
one-bit unequal-width extension, row and radix padding and removal,
finite-alphabet preparation, and fixed-tape workspace cleanup.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The preceding preserved-boundary argument verifies the hypotheses of
the dimension-parametric nested-basis lemma for this producer. The
profiles preserve rank mass and give the strict moment displayed above.
Substitute this finite child list into the same integer-width recurrence
and complete-row proof used for Lemma~\ref{lem:nested-chunk-swap}.
All children are smaller, all selected coordinates are contiguous, and
all remaining work is the same linear scan work. The unequal-width
extension uses this new equal-width procedure in the two retained layouts.
\end{proof}
