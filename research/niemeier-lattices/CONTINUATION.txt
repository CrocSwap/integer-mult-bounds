Native tensor lift: concrete, but not competitive
------------------------------------------------
Remove an orthogonal eight-root frame from each E8 factor. The remaining
112 x 112 relative-sign pairs give 25,088 mixed ports in binary dimension
17. A same-label Walsh change separates two sectors. Each sector is a
disjoint collection of 112 native E8 blocks.

The construction has 224 native invocations, center charge 14,336 and
112,896 inherited helpers. Exact checks verify the signed transport to the
inherited Hamming-coordinate prototype, all 224 isometric frame embeddings,
the eight-dimensional spectator complements, and rank-eight center descents.
Virtual entrances and hyperplane endpoints retain their native residual
widths. The native scalar word is inherited, not re-emitted and replayed here.

Pricing the actual PR #600 profiles and paid spectator banks gives best
nominal kappa about 0.000872182222 over odd stage counts 3 through 101
(seven stages, ATM3 variant). CK is slightly worse. This is about 46% below
the comparison, not a gain. It does not exclude a different shared circuit
or prove a global bound over all stage counts for this tensor construction.

An exact edge partition provides a possible shared-circuit ingredient:
135 orthogonal eight-root cliques cover every E8 orthogonality edge once,
each root occurring nine times. If G is the root Gram matrix and H is
(G entrywise-squared minus J)/4, then

  H = 3 I - (1/4) sum_C 1_C 1_C^T.

The decomposition is dyadic. Applying its clique words independently
duplicates source inputs across incomparable frames; the identity alone
does not make those repeated gathers free.

Spinor routing: actual reusable subwords
---------------------------------------
A fixed pairing of the 16 coordinates partitions the spinor ports into
128 complementary rational eight-dimensional packets, each with 128 ports.
At a common packet frame, a reversible sum of existing source registers
can feed the opposite packet's targets and then be undone. This single
matching layer needs no extra helper roles. Different pairings generally
require incompatible frame histories, so their costs cannot be combined
by simply reusing the same data registers.

A complete orthogonal 16-port clique-adjacency word uses 34 dirty helpers.
The three helper classes have paths [2,1,13] eight times, [4,3,9] twelve
times, and [8,7,1] fourteen times. Exact integer replay checks every input
column, nested/common frames, target endpoints, and restoration of all
source and helper values. Across 1,024 Hadamard cliques this is 34,816
helpers. This is a compensation subword; a compatible center phase is
still required for a full supplier.

One matching layer can be fused at the clique word's eight-dimensional
stage. Sixteen source pivots at a packet frame hold its clique-half totals.
The invertible binary mixer I+J on those 16 pivots changes each total into
the sum of the other 15. The existing fanout then supplies clique adjacency
XOR matching adjacency, with no additional paid helper paths. The mixer
must be reversed during cleanup; this fusion is over F2.

The final fused variant cleans each copied helper immediately at the
parent span, before its source pivot joins the sibling total. Its helper
paths become [2,1,1] eight times, [4,3,1] twelve times, and [8,7,1] fourteen
times per clique. The checked rank mass falls from 1,024 to 832 per clique
(18.75%). This includes source and target paths, which remain unchanged.
Across the shell, helper endpoint widths are 4 for 8,192 roles, 8 for
12,288 roles, and 16 for 14,336 roles. The final fused replay checks all
1,056 input columns of a representative 256-port block and rejects an
omitted cleanup operation. All 64 blocks have the same enumerated geometry.
Native admission still needs the exact endpoint wrappers and their paid
spectator banks; this is not an exponent certificate.

The associated alternative decoder C' = J_H + M has binary rank 1,024.
Within each complementary packet pair, its quotient is

  [[I, A], [A, I]],  A = I+J_16,  A^2 = I.

Its image consists of (z,Az). Every nonzero vector has support on both
packet halves, each of which spans its full eight-dimensional space.
Therefore every nonzero image column spans all 16 rational dimensions.
Every rank-minimal center factorization costs 1,024 x 16 = 16,384, equal
to the spinor port count. This fused route has no positive rank surplus
under that center model, despite its cheaper compensation circuit.

Changing the fitting kernel
----------------------------
The full-shell hybrid retains the original sparse/sparse and sparse/spinor
orthogonality entries, but replaces spinor/spinor entries by Hadamard-clique
blocks. Exact binary rank is 1,851, versus 815 for the original full kernel.
Its full-width center bill is 29,616 on 30,960 ports. An exact active-pressure
bound gives kappa < 0.000448646990529330 at every odd stage count, even
omitting all helper, center-pressure and idle-pressure costs.

Further exact section searches retain all quadratic Boolean functions on
four coordinates and k independent cubic terms:

  k                         0      1      2      3      4
  spinor ports           1024   2048   4096   8192  16384
  binary decoder rank     112    180    272    392    576

Pruning the full-shell spinor interactions to the corresponding disjoint
sections, while keeping all sparse and cross entries, gives full-shell
ranks 2,407, 2,033, 1,675 and 1,359 for k=0,1,2,3 respectively. These are
valid fitting matrices but no cheap complete routing words were found.

The sparse/spinor cross block has exact rank 470. A tested natural-row
factor has every decoder support spanning the full rational 16-space:
low scalar rank again fails to guarantee a cheap physical decoder.

The earlier residual D = C + J_H has a stronger obstruction than the
natural-column check: every nonzero vector in its image spans at least
nine rational address dimensions. The proof uses the degree-six Boolean
polynomial for C, invariance of J_H under H, and the minimum-weight bound
for degree-five Boolean polynomials. A support in at most eight rational
dimensions would have at most 128 spinor lines. Its H-derivatives would
have at most 256 entries, below the 512 minimum for a nonzero derivative,
forcing a whole orthogonal 16-port H-clique into that small subspace.

Consequently any rank-minimal 1,268-column factorization implemented by
full-frame aggregates and private decoder copies pays at least 11,412
descent ranks. This exceeds the spinor surplus of 7,168. The result covers
every minimal basis of that residual, but not redundant factorizations,
ordinary helper computations or fused circuits with different endpoints.

Reproduction and scope
-----------------------
workbench/continuation/ contains the scripts and exact receipts under
tensor-routes/, endpoint-costs/, spinor-routes/ and section-search/.
Portable drivers replay the published receipts with outputs outside the
checkout; use the top-level verify.py --workbench command in README.txt.
The native tensor interface is inherited rather than independently
re-emitted. Optional search scripts and partial exploratory receipts are
identified in the subtree READMEs and are not elevated into global bounds.

The experiments do not establish a new integer-multiplication exponent,
a universal obstruction to Niemeier lattices, or an O(1) indexing result.
