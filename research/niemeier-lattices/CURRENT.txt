Initial construction experiments, 2026-10-12
========================================

Follow-up construction pass: see CONTINUATION.txt and workbench/continuation/.
That pass adds native tensor lifts, a fused spinor compensation subword,
and alternative fitting-kernel searches. No new kappa is certified.

This research is submitted separately from the earlier Leech/Turyn PR #598.
There is no new certified kappa. These experiments seek a complete paid
construction. CONTINUATION.txt records later results, including a stronger
residual obstruction and the fused proper-ending compensation subword.

Positive local results
----------------------
1. E8^2 has a matching small rational bit geometry: use its original
   norm-four vectors divided by two as norm-one address vectors in Q^16,
   and the F2 scalar decoder C(v,w)=[<v,w>=0 mod 4]. It has diagonal one
   and its off-diagonal support is exactly rational orthogonality. Exact
   elimination on all 30,960 rows gives binary rank 815, so full centers cost
   13040. D16+ has the same 30,960-port/rank 815 pair. This supersedes the
   expensive h816 dual as the useful bit candidate in INTERFACES.txt.

2. A legal Pauli encoder PARTIAL word can use 43,056 roles. Fixed-root input
   groups first aggregate in 9-dimensional frames, then fan out their eight
   totals to the 15-dimensional Pauli frames. The selected root minor has
   determinant 256, so the mixer and inverse are dyadic. Exact frame
   inclusions and affine dirty-state restoration are checked. It implements
   the K contribution, not the missing I-K compensation.

3. Financially, keeping all 816 complex centers at full width 17 is better:
   C = 13,872 avoids 42,240 encoder roles. The bit analogue uses 815 full-width 16
   centers. At a +20% one-supplier moment target, a specified five-stage
   banked invoice leaves room for 48,780 complex compensation helpers of
   path [1,7,8,1], or 64,651 bit helpers of path [1,7,7,1]. These depend on the
   stated source/target chains. They include restored stock and padding;
   they are budgets awaiting actual words, not supplier certificates.

4. On the 16,384 spinor ports of D16+, a Boolean group-basis change turns
   the decoder into multiplication by e8 in 14 square-zero variables.
   An explicit Darboux transformation gives e8=e4(x1*y1,...,x7*y7).
   The correction therefore factors into 35 commuting degree-eight
   creation shears, 64 additions each: 2,240 binary additions in that basis.
   Exact graded ranks [1,14,91,364,91,14,1] sum 576. The basis changes have
   physical costs and are not included in that addition count.

5. Orthogonal 16-port Hadamard blocks reduce the spinor correction to
   clique adjacency plus a residual of rank 1,268. This is exact, but its
   literal natural-column decoding remains too expensive.

Tested constructions that fail
-----------------------------
* E8^2's complex I-K has full rank 30,960. The exact spectrum rules out a
  low-rank scalar correction, not a reusable arithmetic circuit.
* The direct 14-layer Mobius transform forces BOTH operands of each gate
  into their common frame. Ultimately every wire has visited FULL16.
  Output caps have dimension 15, so at least 16,384 extra descent ranks are
  needed. The spinor-only rank deficit is negative; the full-shell bit
  version has kappa<0.000541880688 at every odd stage count, even with
  omitted costs made free. The analogous complex upper bound is
  0.000186502122. Partial and fused transforms are outside this result.
* The specified independent rectangle factory requires at least 1,640,925
  roles for spinor orthogonality. Its ordinary per-role rank 16 accounting
  fails even if all center costs are removed. This assumes a+b-1 separate
  input/output carriers per rectangle; shared outputs, reusable DAGs,
  fused children and different endpoints are not covered by that bound.
* Each natural column of the rank 1,268 residual has 6,420 targets spanning
  a 15-dimensional cap. A full-frame aggregate can scatter to that column
  at a common line only after paying 15 ranks. Thus the literal column-
  basis construction needs 19,020 descent ranks, before other charges.
  Different bases and fused routing remain open.

Reproduction and limitations
----------------------------
workbench/ retains scripts, exact receipts and derivations from the initial
experiments. Portable replay drivers now regenerate their outputs outside
the checkout and compare them with the frozen receipts. The top-level
README gives the complete normal and optimized commands. Initial sampled
search receipts remain explicitly distinguished from final certificates.
No formal review or end-to-end Niemeier word is claimed.

Remaining substantive task
-------------------------
A cheaper joint routing/restoration circuit is needed. Neither a small
kernel rank, the 35-shear transformed computation, nor the rank 1,268
residual suffices if moving into and out of their frames consumes the
saving. A useful next construction must avoid the tested full-frame
round trip and emit its complete scalar word with all helper paths.
