All-stage obstruction for the tested unfused Mobius schedule
===========================================================
The finite frame replay in workbench/helper-routes/MOBIUS-FRAMES.json
finds that all 16,384 spinor helper roles reach the full 16-space during
T^{-1}ST, whereas every output cap has dimension 15. The direct unfused
schedule therefore requires at least 16,384 additional rank-one copy
or descent operations per local invocation. This statement concerns the
replayed schedule; it does not exclude fused or differently labelled
transforms.

With the existing full-shell center factors this yields:

  bit:     N30960,h16,C=815*16+16384=29424;
  complex: N30960,h17,C=816*17+16384=30256.

Let d=h-1,m=dL+1 for odd L>=3. For any number of restored banked helpers,
the inherited paired model has deficit D=L(N-C)-N. Write delta=(N-C)/N.
Positive D first occurs at odd Lmin=21 in the bit case and 45 in the complex
case; every smaller odd stage count has nonpositive deficit.

For alpha=kappa/(1-kappa)>0 the unavoidable active data climbs alone have
pressure

  P_active=2NLd*((m/d)^alpha-1).

Splitting those climbs can only increase this pressure. At every L>=Lmin,

  P_total/(NL) >= 2d*((Lmin+1/d)^alpha-1),
  D/(NL) < delta.

Consequently a necessary condition at every positive-deficit stage is

  alpha < log(1+delta/(2d))/log(Lmin+1/d).

Exact rational log bounds give the following upper limits on kappa:

  bit full shell:     0.000541880687595200;
  complex full shell: 0.000186502121809012.

Both are below the pinned frontier 0.001612719427075359. These bounds
already discard every center's pressure, every helper's pressure and all
idle costs. They therefore prove failure at every odd stage count without
an optimizer or a finite stage cutoff. They are convenient upper bounds,
not claims to be the optimal roots of the complete necessary model.

For the spinor-only bit shell, C=576*16+16384=25600>N16384, so D<0 at every
stage immediately.

verify.py replays the rational inequalities with explicit checks under
both ordinary and optimized Python. Its negative controls remove the
16384 extra descents; the same rejection test must then fail, preventing
this schedule-specific obstruction from being asserted for the original
unaugmented factors. No PR, commit or released-package edit was made.
