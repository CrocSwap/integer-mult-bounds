Scoped lower bound for independent orthogonality rectangles
==========================================================
This certificate concerns the spinor orthogonality side of D16+ and a
specified independent rectangle-to-carrier compiler. It does not lower
bound all scalar circuits or all ways to use the lattice.

Canonical spinor ports are antipodal sign-vector lines in R16 with the
even-parity condition. There are 2^14=16384 such lines. Orthogonality means
that exactly eight signs disagree. Each vertex therefore has
binomial(16,8)/2=6435 orthogonal canonical neighbors. The directed bipartite
side matrix has 16384*6435=105431040 nonzero entries.

Sign-vector subspace bound
--------------------------
A k-dimensional rational subspace U of R16 has a set of k coordinate
positions whose projection is injective on U. A sign vector has only two
choices at each projected coordinate, so U contains at most 2^k sign
vectors. U is closed under negation, and canonical ports select one from
each antipodal pair. Hence U contains at most 2^(k-1) canonical ports.
The even-parity restriction can only reduce this bound.

For a nonempty orthogonality rectangle S x T, put a=|S|, b=|T| and
k=dim span(S). Its target span lies in span(S)^perp, so

  a <= 2^(k-1),    b <= 2^(15-k),    1 <= k <= 15.

The function ab/(a+b-1) increases in both variables when a,b>=1. At the
displayed limits its numerator is always 16384, and its denominator is
minimized at k=8. Thus every rectangle satisfies

  ab/(a+b-1) <= 16384/255.

The numerical maximum does not assert that a 128-by-128 rectangle meeting
the parity restrictions exists. It is an upper bound, which is enough.

Compiler assumption and role count
----------------------------------
The tested independent rectangle factory allocates a binary addition tree
with a-1 internal additions and b distinct terminal output-use carriers.
Under its retained role accounting c+q, it costs a+b-1 helper roles. No
carrier is reused between rectangles, and no output-use carrier is shared
between distinct target uses. This is a compiler cost assumption, not an
information-theoretic lower bound for arbitrary rectangle implementations.

Even when rectangles overlap and their contributions cancel over F2,
every nonzero matrix entry must occur in at least one rectangle. Therefore
sum ab >= 105431040, and the independent factory needs

  R = sum(a+b-1) >= 105431040 * 255/16384
                    = 6435*255 = 1640925 helpers.

Ordinary-role moment obstruction
--------------------------------
Now retain the ordinary h=16 helper interface: each helper runs from frame
zero to the full local frame, with separately paid local children inside
that role. Its nested increments r_j sum to 16 and each child has rank at
most 16. For 0<alpha<1, subadditivity of x^(1-alpha) gives

  sum_j r_j*((m/r_j)^alpha-1)
       >= 16*((m/16)^alpha-1).

Cross-role child fusion into a rank 32 or larger transform is not admitted
by this scoped estimate. Neither are partial or virtual helper endpoints.

For the paired controller m=15L+1 with odd L>=3, the complete banked mass
identity gives D=L(N-C)-N. All other pressure is nonnegative, so

  P_total/L >= 16 R *((46/16)^alpha-1),
  D/L < N-C.

At the pinned frontier kappa=0.001612719427075359, with
alpha=kappa/(1-kappa), exact rational interval arithmetic gives

  16 R *((46/16)^alpha-1) > 44825.4388635721.

This exceeds the full-shell allowance N-C=30960-13040=17920 and the
spinor-only allowance 16384-9216=7168. It even exceeds the full-shell
port count 30,960 with every center cost omitted. Thus this ordinary
independent rectangle factory fails for every odd stage count before
charging any active data, center or idle pressure.

Convenient global necessary kappa upper bounds are:

  full-shell bit with C=13,040:  0.000645676583105551;
  spinor-only bit with C=9,216:  0.000258423602940770;
  full shell with C=0:         0.001114721384339448.

These are weaker than optimizing the actual invoice but are sufficient
for the all-stage exclusion. Idle hosting does not remove the separately
paid local helper children. Shared DAG roles, reused output carriers,
fused transforms, changed endpoint widths and different controllers can
escape these assumptions and are not excluded.

Reproduction
------------
verify.py checks every possible source-span dimension, the exact edge
and helper counts, and the rational moment signs. A one-helper negative
control must fail the same sufficient rejection test. Both normal Python
and python -I -O pass. The inherited rational enclosure source and its
SHA256 are recorded in RESULT.json. No repository files are modified.
