Paid controller targets for E8 squared
====================================
Scratch work by DominikScholz with OpenAI GPT-6 Astra assistance.
This is a conditional invoice for a future native word, not a construction
of that word, a compiler admission, or an actual improved kappa.

Ordinary restored helpers
-------------------------
Let h be the local dimension, N the port count, C the paid center-copy bill,
and L>=3 an odd stage count. Put m=1+L(h-1). The data stock before helpers
is W0=(L-1)N. Each original data path has nested increments totaling h-1.
Each ordinary helper runs from frame zero to the full h-frame; its nested
increments total h. The private center-copy descent contributes C per call.
The inherited paired data idle slots are retained and paid at their actual
ranks, with no idle hosting credit. Long idle ranks are split into balanced
children satisfying m^2>2r^2.

Set q=floor(m/h), p=m-qh. Replicate the entire data template by a multiple
T of q. At each stage tile all TR helper residuals into width-h slots, q
slots per full physical bank. There are TR/q banks per stage, each with a
paid rank-p pad when p>0. The ordinary zero-entrance helper has no wrapper
spectators. Different slots are disjoint; replicas are invoked separately.
The effective stock per replica is

  W = (L-1)N + LR/q.

All helper paths plus pads have rank mass LR(h+p/q)=LmR/q. Hence

  D=mW-total_rank_mass=(L-1)N-LC

is unchanged by the additional restored helpers. A helper must not be
charged as added child work without also adding its banked stock. Conversely,
adding stock without its residual and pad children would be equally wrong.

For alpha=kappa/(1-kappa), the exact per-helper pressure addition is

  L [sum_j r_j((m/r_j)^alpha-1)
     +(p/q)((m/p)^alpha-1)],

where sum_j r_j=h, and the second term is omitted if p=0. All quoted
thresholds use outward rational logarithm/exponential bounds, not floating
sign tests. The complete rank-mass identity is checked. Fractional stock in
the report is shorthand for the exactly integral T-replicated construction.
The geometry of the actual address cover, finite arithmetic, dirty circuit,
and compiler still have to be supplied for this new h and port set.

Splitting a path increases pressure: x^(1-alpha) is subadditive for
0<alpha<1. Unit-rank increments therefore bound every nested helper path
with total h, but the same issue affects the data paths. The helper count
alone does not determine the invoice. In particular, an attractive R bound
with unverified coarse data paths is not a complete cost proof.

Targets before a word exists
---------------------------
The complex candidate has N=30960,h=17,C=12752. At L=5, m=81, q=4,
p=13 and D=60080. The bit candidate uses the original rational E8^2
vectors with h=16, the newly computed binary rank815 factor, and C=13040.
At L=5, m=76, q=4,p=12 and D=58640. Every individual center is charged
at its proved complex support width or, on the bit side, ambient width16.

For data paths [1,h-2] and worst-case unit-step helper paths, the maximum
ordinary helper counts at L5 are:

                      current frontier       +2%             +20%
  complex                 55,826            54,016            40,443
  bit                     60,062            58,158            43,879

Thus a joint +20% target is plausible if both legal words can be built with
roughly N ordinary helpers and this coarse data chronology. These are
construction targets, not gains. The bit thresholds also include the
retained 10^-16,32m^2-per-child bad-branch envelope conditionally; proving
that envelope for the new rational frames remains necessary.

If all data paths also fragment entirely into unit steps, the complex
frontier threshold is only24592 helpers, and its +20% threshold only9200.
The analogous bit thresholds are29443 and13250. This is why the emitted
data chronology must be priced, not inferred from total rank mass alone.

The numerical stage scans cover odd L=3,...,101; no claim that these
helper-count maxima are globally optimal in L is needed for the selected
explicit L5 invoices. The older cost package's all-L proof concerns its
zero-helper comparison and is not silently reused here.

Concrete complex encoder, compensation still missing
----------------------------------------------------
The current proposed encoder has the following ordinary helper paths:

  28800 source carriers       [1,8,8]
  13440 Pauli fanout carriers  [9,6,2]
    560 width15 centers       [15,2]
    256 full centers          [17]

This gives R43056. The conservative version adds1920 linear-total roles
with path[9,8], avoiding any assumption that a120-input mixer completing
the eight E8 linear forms has a dyadic inverse. Its total R is44976.
Both versions are priced in ENCODER*.json. They are proposed encoder
schedules; their scalar/frame legality is being checked separately.

For the conservative R44976 version at L5 with data paths[1,15], the
encoder-only nominal kappa is0.002367398161. At a+20% target its remaining
pressure is11009.77025476. This can pay at most14023 additional ordinary
helpers if every helper takes17 unit steps, or31231 if each takes[1,16].
At a+2% target the corresponding remaining budgets are27588 and61425.
These budgets include the new helper stock and its pads. Any additional
data splitting or repeated center descent must also consume this slack.

The missing K-I compensation cannot be supplied by treating each Pauli
quadratic channel as independently orthogonality-preserving. Its zeros
arise only after summing channels. A valid compensation network must
respect common frames gate by gate and restore arbitrary helper dirt.
The71 center-frame classes justify shared scalar work at those frames;
they do not, by themselves, justify one reusable helper per class or a
cost-neutral compensation network.

Virtual entrances
-----------------
A helper starting at E with residual w=h-dim(E) has a smaller nested rank
sum w. The inherited one-owner rule requires the entering helper to be
the only owner of its bank during its invocation, and dim(E) spectators.
Across separate replicas, q=floor((m-dim(E))/w) residuals can be packed
in a bank, retaining the spectator pad. The same formula holds with h
replaced by the residual width w in the helper path. This can tolerate
far more helper roles, but only after an exact dirt-column cancellation
or legal nonzero-frame target compensation proves the virtual entrance.
It cannot be assigned merely because the first useful gate has large frame.

Simpler current leading encoder: full-width centers
--------------------------------------------------
The operator factorization need not use its narrow frames in the first
native construction. Move each source directly to FULL, gather816 dirty
centers there, pay816 full-width17 center-copy descents to zero, scatter
while targets are still at zero, and undo the gathers at FULL. The old
center dirt is precompensated into targets at frame zero. This trades the
smaller bill12752 for13872 but avoids all42240 non-center encoder roles.
Its viability still depends on cloning or aggregating whatever fresh source
information the K-I compensation needs before those sources reach FULL.

FULL-CENTERS.json prices this baseline with816 width17 center roles. With
source path[16], target path[1,7,8], and L5, a+20% complex-side target allows
48780 additional compensation helpers with path[1,7,8,1],61430 with
path[1,15,1], or30434 with unit paths. If targets require16 unit steps,
those allowances become30117,37928,18790 respectively. This explicitly
separates source and target profiles instead of assuming identical chains.

The analogous bit baseline has815 full-width16 centers. Source path[15]
and target path[1,7,7] at L5 allow64651 compensation helpers[1,7,7,1],81240
helpers[1,14,1], or41055 unit-path helpers at the same+20% target. The
current bottleneck is therefore a legal complex compensation DAG and its
target chronology, rather than the center factorization alone.

A helper may also end below FULL. A completed subword inside a fixed-root
nine-space could retain a terminal frame of dimension9; the inherited
endpoint normalization applies to T_U T_E^-1, not only FULL T_E^-1.
This would lower its residual width if every cleanup incidence really
stays in that nine-space. The stricter spectator/one-owner bank rule must
then be retained unless an exact smaller-footprint chart is supplied.
For comparison, with complex sources[16],targets[1,7,8],L5 and the full
center baseline, a+20% target permits86525 such width9 helpers having
path[1,7,1], or58408 with nine unit steps. This is conditional on an actual
small terminal frame, not a license to drop final climbs from an invoice.
