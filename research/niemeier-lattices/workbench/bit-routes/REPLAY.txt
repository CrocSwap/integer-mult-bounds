Portable replay for the bit routes and spinor continuation
========================================================

From any working directory, run this file's sibling verifier using a Python
interpreter with NumPy and SymPy installed:

 python3 workbench/bit-routes/verify_portable.py
 python3 -I -O workbench/bit-routes/verify_portable.py

The command paths above are relative to research/niemeier-lattices. The
verifier itself resolves all inputs relative to its file, copies the package
to a temporary directory, regenerates both coordinate NPZ files, and runs
all18 generators in dependency order. It never writes checkout receipts.
Direct generator execution is a regeneration operation: generators write
beside themselves, so run them in a copy if preserving frozen receipts.

The verifier compares all JSON data, excluding only the measured `seconds`
field in MOD4-RANK.json. CROSS-FACTOR.npz is compared by named array content,
not archive bytes. ports.npz and d16-ports.npz are generated intermediate
inputs and need not be committed. PR589.json and the historical *.log files
are context snapshots, not regenerated mathematical receipts.

Exact final computations
------------------------
MOD4-RANK, MIXED-ORBIT-RANKS, D16-RANK, D16-SECTIONS, SPINOR-OPERATOR,
CLIQUE-RESIDUAL, CLIQUE-RESIDUAL-SUPPORT, and CUBIC-RELATIONS are the final
finite computations from bit-routes. CUBIC-RELATIONS uses a modular rank
lower bound on a specified deterministic subset, then checks the complete
integer kernel relation on every orthogonal neighbor. Its resulting rank679
supersedes the earlier mixed-neighbor sample rank678.

SECTIONS, HYBRID, HYBRID-SECTIONS, FRAME-BOUNDS, CROSS-RANK, and CROSS-FACTOR
are the final continuation receipts. FRAME-BOUNDS checks the finite premises
of the written mathematical proofs; its rank1268 is verified separately by
CLIQUE-RESIDUAL in the same run. A rank or center bill is not a complete
supplier or an improved exponent certificate.

Exploratory computations preserved and replayed
-----------------------------------------------
NEIGHBOR-CUBICS records the original fixed-seed sample only. Its ranks674
and678 are sampled lower bounds, not exact full-neighbor ranks. The later
CUBIC-RELATIONS computation establishes679 for the selected mixed port.

CLIQUE-LEADING is an explicit failed candidate: an odd433-clique relation
cancels its leading4-form, but its exact convolution rank2942 is too high.
CLIQUE-SMALL and CLIQUE-HALF record bounded permutation-orbit searches.
Their negative outcomes do not exclude other clique collections. All of
these deterministic experiments are replayed without being promoted to
universal mathematical claims.

The helper/schedule and rank-minimal-factorization scopes are stated in
continuation/spinor-routes/PROOF.txt. Nonminimal factorizations and other
fused schedules remain open.
