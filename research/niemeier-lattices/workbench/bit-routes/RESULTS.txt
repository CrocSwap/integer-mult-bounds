Niemeier bit routes: exact local results, no new kappa
====================================================
All work here is scratch. No PR or published package is changed.

A genuine small-dimensional bit pair
-----------------------------------
For norm-four lattice lines use rational address Gram K(v,w)=v.w/4.
Define the binary scalar central matrix C(v,w)=[v.w=0 mod4]. Since distinct
lines have inner product in {-3,...,3}, C=I+A, where A is the ordinary
orthogonality adjacency. This is a legal bit pair with rational frame
size equal to the lattice dimension. It does not use the complex F2 frame
as a rational address frame.

The integer-valued polynomial binom(t-1,3) modulo two equals [t=0 mod4].
Its degree-three multivariate binomial basis gives rank at most
binom(d+3,3), independent of a floating-point computation.

Exact exhaustive integer dot products and packed GF2 elimination give:

  geometry                    ports   rational h  binary rank  full center bill
  E8^2 norm-four shell         30960       16          815           13040
  E8^2 mixed block ports       28800       16          782           12512
  D16+ norm-four shell         30960       16          815           13040
  D16+ spinor lines            16384       16          576            9216
  D16 norm-four sparse lines   14576       16          575            9200

The full-center bill is h times the displayed rank, not a complete local
rank histogram. The graph compensation still needs an efficient legal
transparent word with arbitrary dirt restored.

On the E8^2 mixed ports, there are three invariant orthogonality types:
block pairings (0,0), opposite unit pairings, and opposite norm-two
pairings (the relative-sign partner). Including each type or omitting it
independently gives binary central ranks, in three-bit mask order:

  [28800,28800,28800,28800,14400,1998,14400,782].

Thus retaining all three types is best within this eight-member family.
This is not a global minrank claim.

D16+ spinor operator transformation
----------------------------------
Index the canonical spinors by x in F2^14:

  v(x)=(1,(-1)^x_1,...,(-1)^x_14,(-1)^(sum x))/2.

Distinct ports are orthogonal precisely when wt(x+y) is 7 or 8. Their
central decoder is group convolution by delta_0 + [weight7] + [weight8].
Write g_i=1+z_i in the F2 group algebra, so z_i^2=0. The exact upper
Boolean zeta transform turns this kernel into e8(z_1,...,z_14).

For zero-based i=0,...,6, use the invertible Darboux coordinates

  x_i=z_(2i)+z_(2i+1),
  y_i=z_(2i+1)+sum_(j>i)(z_(2j)+z_(2j+1)).

Then e2(z)=sum x_i*y_i and e8(z)=e4(x_0*y_0,...,x_6*y_6).
The latter has 35 monomials. Each creates eight variables and has rank64
on the full 16384-dimensional exterior algebra. Every two such monomials
have product zero, since their four-element pair subsets intersect.
Therefore I+e8 factors into 35 commuting shears, totaling 2240 scalar
CNOT operations IN THE TRANSFORMED BASIS. Exact graded ranks are
1,14,91,364,91,14,1, whose sum is576.

The two basis changes are not free common-frame operations. An unfused
14-layer in-place Mobius transform forces both operands of every addition
to the same union frame, ultimately FULL16 for every role. Lowering each
output to a target cap requires at least16384 additional rank-one copies,
which the spinor-only margin16384-9216=7168 cannot pay. This blocks that
direct schedule, not every possible fused transform.

A compatible Hadamard-clique subtraction
---------------------------------------
For a in F2^4 define the14-bit mask H(a) by

  H(a)_(j-1)=parity(a & j), j=1,...,14.

The four subgroup generators are5461,9830,14456,16256. Each nonzero shift
has canonical weight7 or8. Thus the1024 cosets of H are sixteen-port
orthogonal cliques in rational dimension16.

If J_H is convolution by the whole subgroup, its rank is1024. The legal
clique adjacency is P=I+J_H. The residual from the full spinor adjacency
is A+P=C+J_H and has EXACT binary rank1268 (not merely the upper bound1600).
This may be useful for a fused schedule, but low algebraic residual rank
does not itself give compatible encoder and decoder frames.

A scoped E8^2 packet-cover obstruction
------------------------------------
For one mixed port (u,v), its11831 orthogonal neighbors lie in the
15-dimensional cap a.u+b.v=0. Their homogeneous cubic evaluation rank is
679, out of680 possible cubic monomials. An exact integer vanishing
relation supplies the matching upper bound, so this rank is over Q too:

  ell*(||a||^2-||b||^2)=0, where ell=a.u=-b.v.

Indeed, mixed neighbors have equal block norms and pure neighbors have
ell=0. This spans the entire vanishing-cubic space. The quadratic factor
on the cap has rank14 and signature(7,7), with radical spanned by(u,-v).
It cannot be a product of two linear forms. Therefore these neighbors
cannot be covered by three proper rational linear hyperplanes. Block
Weyl symmetry gives the same conclusion for every mixed port.

This is an obstruction to such a three-hyperplane packet-cover plan,
not to every mutable-source algorithm or every supplier.

File: cubic_relations.py/CUBIC-RELATIONS.json. A modular rank679 sample
includes the unique orthogonal sign-partner; omitting that rare point
can give the misleading sample rank678. The integer relation is checked
on ALL11831 neighbors. The earlier NEIGHBOR-CUBICS.json records that
initial bounded sample and is not the final certificate.

----------------------

Literal natural-column implementation of the rank1268 residual
---------------------------------------------------------------
A column of D=C+J_H is supported on6420 spinor targets: the6435
orthogonal neighbors with the15 nonzero H clique shifts removed.
For the all-positive column, every support vector lies in the sum-zero
rational cap. Fifteen listed support vectors have a nonzero exact integer
15-by-15 minor after deleting the first coordinate. Consequently the
support spans the entire15-dimensional cap. XOR translations are diagonal
real sign isometries, so every column has the same exact span property.

Therefore the intersection of all target caps for one such decoder
column is precisely the one-dimensional line of that column's port.
If a rank1268 column-basis factorization uses full-frame aggregates and
one private decoder copy per retained natural column, each copy must
lower from dimension16 to at most1. Its rank mass is at least
1268*15=19020 per invocation, already larger than all16384 spinor ports,
even before charging the original576 full-frame centers or the clique
subroutine. This rejects that literal implementation of the1268 residual.
It does not reject different encoder/decoder bases or fused shared DAGs.
