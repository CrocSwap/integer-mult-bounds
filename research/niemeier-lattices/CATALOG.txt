Derivation and independent checks
---------------------------------
For q^{norm/2}, the primary-source identity is

  theta_L = E4^3 + (24H-720)Delta.

Using E4=1+240 q+2160 q^2+... and Delta=q-24 q^2+... gives

  number of norm 2 vectors =24H,
  number of norm 4 vectors =196560-576H,
  number of norm 4 lines   =98280-288H.

For an irreducible root lattice, the norm 4 vector counts are

  A_n: 6*C(n+1,4),
  D_n: 2 n+16*C(n,4),
  E6:270, E7:756, E8:2160.

The A_n vectors have two +1 and two -1 entries. The D_n vectors are
+/-2 e_i or have four +/-1 entries. The exceptional counts are explicitly
enumerated inside E8 by catalog.py, also recovering their 72,126,240 roots.
In a sum R=directsum R_i, a norm 4 vector is either a norm 4 vector in one
component or a pair of norm 2 roots in two components. Hence

  |R_4|=sum_i |(R_i)_4| + sum_{i<j}|(R_i)_2| |(R_j)_2|.

Subtracting half this count from the theta-derived line count gives the
last column. The script also verifies component ranks sum to 24, common
Coxeter numbers agree, root count is 24H, and determinant(R) is a square.
The glue group order is sqrt(det(R)) and is recorded in catalog.json.

What is actually different from Leech
------------------------------------
The norm 2 shell is exactly the orthogonal union of the ADE root systems.
Its ordinary Gram kernel has no cross-component entries. Merely taking
more copies of an old component repeats the old supplier; its 24-dimensional
ambient realization does not by itself produce a new interaction.

The norm 4 shell adds two genuinely relevant ingredients: pairs of roots
in different components, and glue-coset vectors. These have sharply
different tensor support. The last column shows that a root-sublattice
calculation represents the whole norm 4 shell only for D24 and E8^3.
For the other 21 systems, ignoring glue can discard most of the shell.

The same cubic parity gate is legal on every norm 4 shell:

  K(v,w)=[<v,w>^3-4<v,w>]/48.

Its diagonal is 1. Every nonzero off-diagonal has odd inner product, so
B(v,w)=1+<v,w> mod 2 is a fitting binary matrix. Unlike Leech, rooted
lattices may also have off-diagonal inner products +/-3; these are allowed
by this gate and must not be accidentally omitted in finite checks.
The generic real cubic-feature bound is 2600 and the generic binary bound
is 25. These are upper bounds for a particular kernel/fit, not lower
bounds for all constructions.

Concrete structural cases
-------------------------
* D24+: the added half-integral coset has minimum norm 24/4=6. Thus the
  norm 4 shell is exactly 24 axis lines and 8*C(24,4)=85008 four-support
  lines. Sparse coordinate support makes this an explicit baseline.

* E8^3: norm 4 vectors occupy one block with norm 4, or two blocks with
  norms 2+2. There are 3*1080+3*(240*240/2)=89640 lines. Three-block cubic
  features vanish, removing 8^3=512 dimensions from the 2600-dimensional
  homogeneous cubic space. The surviving 360 one-block and 1728 two-block
  features explain the 2088 real-rank construction. Two-block support also
  gives a binary frame-width bound 17 for those factors. The exact cost
  and dyadic factorization are treated separately in this package.

* D16 E8: this is D16+ orthogonally summed with E8. The additional 16384
  norm 4 lines come from the D16 spinor coset, whose 32768 minimal vectors
  have sixteen coordinates +/-1/2. There are only two coordinate blocks;
  the E8^3 three-block-vanishing shortcut therefore does not apply.

* D12^2: the two glue classes (s,v),(v,s) have norms 3+1, with 2048 spinor
  vectors and 24 vector-coset vectors per component. Together they add
  2048*24=49152 lines. Their sum class (c,c) first appears at norm 6.
  These low-rank block products deserve separate kernel/label treatment.

* D8^3: a glue code generated by (s,v,v),(v,s,v),(v,v,s) has three
  norm profiles 2+1+1 and three profiles 2+2+0 at norm 4. Here a spinor
  coset has 128 minimal vectors, and a vector coset has 16. Consequently
  the glue contributes [3*128*16^2+3*128^2]/2=73728 lines. The all-spinor
  sum class first appears at norm 6. Both kinds of profile must be counted.

* The remaining entries are candidates for a glue-aware shell treatment,
  not rejected lattices. D4^6 offers a small 64-word hexacode glue group;
  E6^4 has a 9-word group and triple-component norm 4 glue; A2^12 and A1^24
  have large coding-theoretic glue contributions. The census identifies
  their available geometry but does not claim a competitive factorization.

Natural root-only binary ranks
------------------------------
For the norm 2 roots, B=1+<r,s> mod 2 is legal. After quotienting the
radical, its rank is 1+rank_F2(Cartan), namely

  A_n: n+1 for even n, n for odd n;
  D_n: n-1 for even n, n for odd n;
  E6: 7, E7: 7, E8: 9.

These are ranks of this specified fitting matrix, not proved minimum
frame dimensions. They are recorded per component in catalog.json.
For D_n, the coordinate factor has n centers of width n-1, so its center
bill equals its n(n-1) root-line count; this particular unpunctured factor
has no rank deficit. Other factors, puncturing, and shared helper
structures require their own accounting.

See SOURCES.txt for exact primary-source locations. No conclusion here
rules out all suppliers, all kernels, or all schedules of any lattice.
