Niemeier supplier budgets: scope and exact comparisons
====================================================

These are necessary one-supplier moment screens in the inherited complex
binary-frame model. They are not complete words or overall exponent gains.
The scalar factorizations, binary fits, and center supports are checked
separately by the geometry modules. A native circuit, restored dirty
helpers, physical hosting, a bit-side construction, and outer transfer
checks are still missing.

The retained bit supplier is already a separate bottleneck. PR592 at
9092320f7932dbe61a78f15b6c5fd6a0cbd41307 cites PR543's bit exponent

    c = 413456747036699736067 / 250000000000000000000000
      = 0.001653826988146798944268.

Its assembly takes the minimum of the bit and complex exponents, and
reports rejection of the next bit endpoint c+10^-24. The corresponding
nominal outer cap c/(1+c) is about 0.001651096360425896: only 2.37964%
above PR592's declared kappa 0.001612719427075359. A larger overall gain
therefore also needs a better bit supplier. The retained bit input and
assembly source are pinned here as provenance, not freshly replayed.

Exact scalar inputs and the useful comparison
--------------------------------------------
The Pauli construction transforms the traceless quadratic factors before
combining them with the linear factor in the other E8 block. This changes
the real center basis and reduces binary support widths on both encoder
and decoder sides. It is not merely a reduction in real matrix rank.

The main comparisons below all use the same hosting-aware idle model and
the actual heterogeneous center widths. Each optimum is certified over
every odd stage count L >= 3, including the infinite tail.

The smaller 2,088-center literal cubic factor without its necessary

Moment model
------------
Let n_w count centers of support width w, C=sum_w w*n_w, and d=h-1.
For odd L, the inherited paired schedule has

    m=dL+1,  W=(L-1)N,  D=W-LC.

Required active data mass is 2NLd at child rank d. Center mass is L*w*n_w
at rank w. For K=(L-1)/2, the idle slots are the first pair's tail
2(K-1)d, and, for p=1,...,K-1, the pre-stretch 2pd, bridge d, and any
positive tail 2(K-p-1)d. An idle slot of rank r carries mass 2Nr and is
optimistically allowed child rank max(r,h), as in PR595's slot relaxation.
The code checks total rank mass = mW-D exactly. No actual owner packing
or helper inventory is inferred from those fractional idle capacities.

With alpha=kappa/(1-kappa), a child of rank r and mass M has pressure

    g(M,r,alpha)=M*((m/r)^alpha-1).

The nominal root solves sum g=D. A second, weaker necessary screen
relaxes every center's child rank to h while retaining its actual bill C.
This is separately named relaxed_center_cap in the receipt; it does not
silently replace every center's support width by h in the primary model.

All bounds use exact rational log and exponential series with explicit
tails. Outward rounding to denominator 10^40 controls intermediate sizes
without sacrificing rigor. Floating arithmetic only nominates a root.
The selected stage's lower root bracket exceeds every other candidate's
upper endpoint in the finite search. To cover all later L, use only the
active climbs and the (L-3)/2 bridge slots:

    pressure/(NL) >= d*alpha*[2 log L
                     +(1-3/L) log((dL+1)/h)].

The right side increases for L>=3. Once it exceeds 1-C/N, it exceeds
D/(NL) at every subsequent stage. This proves the infinite-tail exclusion.
When C>=N, D=L(N-C)-N is negative for every stage immediately.

Helper and extra-operation budget
--------------------------------
All 70 distinct width-15 Pauli support frames in E8^2 have restricted
standard-dot rank 13 and radical dimension 2. This does not force padding
to width 17 in the complex framework. The pinned inherited note
notes/general-clifford-frames.tex constructs exact Gaussian-dyadic
representatives for every binary subspace U, including degenerate U.
It proves Fourier rank(T_U)=dim U and nested transition rank
rank(T_V T_U^-1)=dim V-dim U when U is contained in V. Thus a private
center descending from U to zero costs dim U=15. The invertible binary
map used in the construction need not preserve the standard dot product;
its Clifford lift includes the required quadratic phases. No irrational
extension or additional recursive padding is necessary.

Consequently 12,752 is a justified sum of individual complex center-child
ranks, not merely an unsupported label-dimension estimate. It is not a
realizable total invoice yet: a complete native scalar word, source and
target incidence, adapters, dirty-helper routing and restoration still
need to be supplied and charged. The frame note credits the construction
to icekylinx with OpenAI GPT-6 Astra assistance and its exposition and
integration to Codex.

For the strongest E8^2 case at L=3, m=49, W=61920 and D=23664. At the
frontier, +20%, and +100% one-supplier targets, unused pressure is about
18,215.1281, 17,122.0550, and 12,738.7135 respectively. This schedule
has no idle slots; there is no free hosting capacity to assign to helpers.

If an invoice adds one paid rank-r child in every local call, adds no
stock, and removes no previous child, it consumes

    L*r*(m/r)^alpha

of that unused pressure, including the loss of deficit. At the +20%
target, E8^2 allows at most 5,664 additional rank-one children per call,
or 177 pairs of rank-16 children per call. The latter is far fewer than
816 centers. Thus a factory needing a full enter/exit pair for every
center fails this particular budget. These are exact invoice scenarios,
not universal lower bounds on all possible helper factories.

More generally, if D changes to D-e and the net pressure changes by
DeltaP after crediting removed children, the necessary condition is
e+DeltaP < the recorded slack. A banked helper, changed controller, or
shared helper must be priced with its actual stock and residual changes.
The receipt also gives L=5,7,9 budgets, where idle slots are available.

There are exactly 70 distinct width-15 frames, each shared by eight
centers, and all 256 width-17 centers share the full frame. This makes
batching by 71 frame classes a concrete next experiment. For scale only,
71 paid rank-16 enter/exit pairs would fit the 177-pair budget above;
there is no construction showing that one pair per frame class suffices.
Equal center frames allow common-frame scalar additions and can share
encoder work. They do not supply a complete local word automatically.
The scalar shadow must still implement the identity shear and restore
all dirt; this includes the K-I compensation network and its legal target
chains. A target ending at p_t^perp cannot visit the full h-dimensional
center frame. Private center copies descend and scatter, while helpers
must obey their own frame and restoration rules. Sequential reuse is
useful only if those transitions and the intermediate dirt response are
actually restored or charged. Native batching and low-width compensation
are therefore the next concrete circuit tasks.

Catalog baseline and root-only units
-----------------------------------
As a uniform comparison only, assign h=25 and 2,600 full-width centers
to each norm-four shell. No such rank or factorization is asserted for
the other lattices. Every one of these 23 hypothetical models fails the
frontier, because its C/N exceeds 0.591018161737. This is the globally
optimized necessary center-bill threshold at h=25; the best stage is 5.
For a +20% target that threshold is 0.561851627124, attained at stage 3.
At h=23 the corresponding frontier threshold is 0.608211423703.

Root shells split orthogonally by irreducible component, so repeated
isomorphic components merely repeat an existing local unit. For the
direct D_n root-coordinate construction, N=n(n-1), each of n coordinate
encoders has width n-1, and C=N. Its deficit is always -N. This excludes
that construction, not all alternate encoders or controllers. The useful
new behavior here comes from norm-four mixed-component ports and their
quadratic operator basis, not from the Niemeier shell count alone.
