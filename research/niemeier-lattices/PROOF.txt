Exact local constructions and scope
==================================

1. Shells and the common parity kernel

Take one canonical representative v from each antipodal pair of squared
norm four. For integral lattice pairings t=<v,w>, define

    K(v,w)=(t^3-4t)/48,       B(v,w)=1+(t mod 2).

Both diagonal entries are one. Distinct canonical lines have |t|<=3.
The even off-diagonal values t=-2,0,2 vanish in K, while odd values have
B=0. Thus K and B form a fitting pair. The values +/-3, present in rooted
lattices, are retained. This observation is an exact universal argument,
not an inference from sampled pairs.

CATALOG.txt derives the 23 shell counts from the cited theta identity.
The two fully constructed 24-dimensional shells are D24+ and E8^3.
E8^2 denotes the norm-four section with the third E8 block zero; it is
16-dimensional, not another 24-dimensional Niemeier lattice.

2. D24+

The half-integral glue coset has minimum squared norm six, so the norm-four
lines are 24 axes 2e_i and 8*C(24,4)=85008 signed four-coordinate lines.
Modulo two their supports lie in the even-weight subspace of F2^24.
Its radical is the all-ones vector. Quotienting gives an alternating form
of dimension 22, and adjoining the constant direction realizes B in the
standard dot product on F2^23. code/d24.py checks this isometry on a basis
of the whole even-weight space, and checks the full label span is 23.

Let A_j(v)=v_j/2 on the axes and zero otherwise; let Y_j(v)=v_j off the
axes and zero on them. Use M_ij(v)=v_i^2*v_j for i!=j and
T_ijk(v)=v_i*v_j*v_k for i<j<k. Expanding the cubic gives

    K = A A^T + (M M^T - Y Y^T)/16 + T T^T/8.

Indeed the unary coefficient v_j^3*w_j^3-4v_j*w_j is 48 A_j(v)A_j(w)
-3 Y_j(v)Y_j(w); mixed and distinct cubic coefficients are 3 and 6.
All coefficients are dyadic, and this proves the identity on every pair.
All support sets are enumerated for each coordinate, pair and triple:
24 axis centers have width 1, 24 unary centers width 23, 552 ordered mixed
centers width 22, and 2024 triple centers width 21. The bill is 55224.

Independent sign characters separate the triple sectors from 24 unary
sectors. In unary sector j, the 23 inclusion functions on 3-subsets of
the remaining 23 coordinates are independent over Q. Since their sum is
3Y_j, the coefficient form is (I-J/9)/16, of rank 23. Hence the off-axis
rank is 2024+24*23=2576; the isolated 24 axes add an identity block, giving
rank 2600. Omitting the axes leaves 85008 ports and support bill 55200.

3. E8 block geometry and the binary fit

The code uses doubled integer coordinates u=2v. E8 roots have squared norm
8 in this convention: two +/-2 entries, or eight +/-1 entries with sum
divisible by four. The norm-four vectors have squared norm 16: one +/-4,
four +/-2, or one +/-3 and seven +/-1, again with sum divisible by four.
There are 120 root lines and 1080 norm-four lines.

For b=2 or 3 blocks, a norm-four line occupies one norm-four block, or a
pair of root blocks. The count is 1080b+28800*C(b,2), giving 30960 and
89640 ports respectively. The generator checks norms, membership, unique
canonical orientation, and the three-block profile counts.

Eight selected E8 roots have an odd Gram determinant and form a basis
over Q. Their inverse Gram matrix reduces modulo two to the inverse
computed by binary elimination. Thus lattice pairings are represented
by an alternating rank-eight form in each block. The code explicitly
constructs an isometry to the even-weight subspace of F2^(8b+1). Adding
its orthogonal all-ones vector gives labels p with

    p(u).p(w)=1+((u.w)/4 mod 2).

The label span has dimension 8b+1. The basis/isometry identity proves the
pairing formula globally; additional diagnostic rows check the concrete
enumeration against it. This does not claim a minimum among all possible
fitting matrices.

4. Exact scalar ranks and a dyadic starting factor

In doubled coordinates, K=((u.w)^3-64(u.w))/3072. A cubic monomial using
three blocks vanishes. The retained homogeneous cubic space has dimension

    r_b = 120b + 288b(b-1),      r_2=816, r_3=2088.

The pure norm-four sectors give rank 120 per block. On each root-pair
sector, opposite relative signs separate the two parities, and root
quadratics and root linears have ranks 36 and 8. The code certifies all
three finite rank statements by full-column-rank minors modulo 101;
they imply the corresponding ranks over Q.

Let F be the retained cubic features and D their positive multinomial
weights. The sphere relation u_i=sum_j u_i*u_j^2/16 expresses each linear
feature by a column L_i. The kernel coefficient matrix is
(D-64LL^T)/3072, with

    L^T D^-1 L = (8b+2) I / 768.

The nontrivial correction eigenvalue is 1-(8b+2)/12, which is -1/2 for
b=2 and -7/6 for b=3. It is nonzero. This proves rank r_b and signature
(r_b-8b,8b), without a floating rank estimate.

A direct dyadic starting factor removes the denominator three in each
pure cube together with its linear correction. With q(u)=(u^3-u)/3,

 (u^3*w^3-64*u*w)/3072
   = (-(5u-q(u))(5w-q(w))+4(q(u)-u)(q(w)-w))/1024.

q is integer on integer coordinates. The 2112-center E8^3 baseline has
384 centers of width 25 and 1728 of width 17, bill 38976. Its complete
support orbits are checked. Restricting to two blocks gives 832 centers
of width at most 17, bill 14144.

5. Fano elimination and the trace directions

Fix coordinate i in a block. For the other seven coordinates j of that
block set m_j=u_i*u_j^2, and set

    f=(16u_i-u_i^3)/3,       g=(u_i^3-4u_i)/3.

For each other block let b_0=sum_j u_i*u_j^2. The sphere relation is
3f=sum_j m_j+sum_other b_0. Index the seven m_j by the nonzero points of
F2^3. For each of the seven Fano lines T put h_T=sum_{j in T}m_j-f.
The line incidence identity gives, with primes denoting evaluation at w,

    sum_j m_j*m'_j-f*f'
      = (sum_T h_T*h'_T-(sum_other b_0)(sum_other b'_0))/2.

code/e8_sections.py verifies the full coefficient-matrix identity. Thus
it applies to all points, using the displayed sphere relation. An
eight-coordinate Walsh transform separates the trace from the seven
traceless diagonal quadratics in every other block. The remaining trace
coefficient for b=2 is -3/8192; for b=3 the sum and difference of the two
traces have coefficients -7/16384 and 1/16384. Retain g with coefficient
1/256, each h_T with 1/2048, and pure distinct cubics with 1/512.
This already gives a dyadic rank-minimal factorization.

6. Transform the quadratic operator space

Index eight coordinates by x in F2^3. For a,b in F2^3 with a.b=0, define

    P_(a,b) e_x = (-1)^(b.x) e_(x+a).

These are 36 symmetric real signed permutations, including the identity.
The code checks symmetry, P^2=I, trace zero for the 35 nonidentity P,
and their Frobenius Gram matrix 8I. They therefore form an orthogonal
basis of the 36-dimensional space of symmetric 8-by-8 matrices. Applying
the resulting resolution of identity to uu^T and ww^T proves

 sum_(P != I) (u^T P u)(w^T P w)
      = 8(u.w)^2-(u.u)(w.w).

Replace each block's seven traceless diagonal and 28 off-diagonal
quadratic factors by these 35 factors. For each ordered pair of blocks
A,B, and coordinate i in A, the new mixed center is

    u_(A,i) * (u_B^T P u_B),       coefficient 1/8192.

The trace correction in Section 5 is unchanged. Every coefficient stays
dyadic and the number of centers stays rank-minimal. Crucially, the
nonzero support of every one of the 35 new factors has binary span 15.
The code exhausts the actual port support for every operator, including
all zero tests. Permutations of blocks and coordinates in the LINEAR
block cover all remaining choices; no unproved Pauli support symmetry
is assumed. Each representative support contains 3744 ports.

For E8^2 there are 35*8*2=560 narrow centers and 256 retained width-17
centers: C=560*15+256*17=12752. For E8^3 there are 35*8*6=1680 narrow
centers and 408 width-25 centers: C=35400. The symmetric diagonal form
makes decoder supports equal encoder supports. The universal identities
prove exactness; an additional integer replay of the complete evaluated
factor matrix on up to 192 selected ports is a diagnostic, not an
all-pairs proof by sampling.

For E8^2 every coordinate partner is also checked explicitly. The 560
narrow centers use exactly 70 distinct dimension-15 frames. Their
restricted dot form has rank 13. These are degenerate subspaces, not
orthonormal I15 spaces. notes/general-clifford-frames.tex explicitly
constructs exact representatives for arbitrary binary U, including
degenerate U, with a rank-dim(U) transition to zero. This justifies the
potential center price. It does not emit a schedule that gathers all
sources, scatters into all targets, and restores arbitrary helper dirt.

7. Relative-sign transform and compiler limits

The ports (a,b) and (a,-b) have the same binary label. H2 on one side
and H2/2 on the other is a dyadic similarity within this equal-label
group. It separates the odd-block characters, giving two sectors of
15480 ports/rank408 for E8^2 or three sectors of 29880 ports/rank696 for
E8^3. Their kernel within an AB profile in the odd-A sector is
s_A*(s_A^2+3s_B^2-4)/24. Fixing a gives s_B^2/4, a rank-36 quadratic
root kernel, so this is not just replication of the rank-eight E8 unit.
The price table does not assume any unbuilt saving from this transform.
