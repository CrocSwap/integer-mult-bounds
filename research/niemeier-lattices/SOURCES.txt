Primary sources for the Niemeier census
======================================

1. Gabriele Nebe and Neil J. A. Sloane, A Catalogue of Lattices.
   https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/index.html
   The Niemeier section lists all 23 rooted systems and links their lattice
   entries. Used to cross-check the classification and component labels.

2. Shoyu Nagaoka and Sho Takemori, Notes on theta series for Niemeier lattices,
   arXiv:1504.06715.
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1504.06715
   Equation (4), printed page 5, gives the genus-one theta identity
   theta_L=E4^3+(24H-720)Delta. Table 1, printed page 9, independently lists
   the 24 systems, including Leech, and their common Coxeter numbers.
   The coefficient extraction and antipodal line counts in catalog.py are
   calculations made here, rather than numerical performance claims in
   that paper. Our H denotes the Coxeter number, not the binary frame size.

3. J. H. Conway and N. J. A. Sloane, On the Enumeration of Lattices of
   Determinant One, Journal of Number Theory 15 (1982), 83-94.
   https://neilsloane.com/doc/Me81.pdf
   Table V on printed page 91 lists the Niemeier root systems, glue generators,
   and root counts. Section 4 and Table VI give the completeness check.
   The paper identifies the D4^6 glue with the length-six hexacode over F4.
   Root-system and discriminant-coset descriptions appear in Section 2.

4. Nebe–Sloane entries for the exceptional root lattices:
   https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/E6.html
   https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/E7.html
   https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/E8.html
   The E8 page supplies a standard integral/half-integral coordinate basis.
   The E7 entry identifies the sum-zero hyperplane inside E8; the E6 entry
   adds x_1+x_8=0. catalog.py enumerates their norm-two and norm-four shells
   exactly in integer coordinates x=2 v, independently checking all counts.

5. Individual catalogue entries for the first explicit constructions:
   https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/NIEM_D24.html
   https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/NIEM_D16_E8.html
   https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/NIEM_3E8.html
   https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/NIEM_2D12.html
   https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Gabriele.Nebe/LATTICES/NIEM_3D8.html

All geometric kernels, factor counts, support-width estimates, and algorithmic
screens in this research package are our deductions/computations. The sources
above establish lattice data; they do not establish a matrix-multiplication
exponent or prove optimality of the screened supplier families.

Related primary literature consulted during the continuation
-----------------------------------------------------------
M. Waegell and P. K. Aravind, https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.04350.
Simon Schmidt, https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.14906.
K. Abdukhalikov, E. Bannai and S. Suda,
https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.1425.
A. R. Calderbank, P. J. Cameron, W. M. Kantor and J. J. Seidel,
Z4-Kerdock codes, orthogonal spreads, and extremal Euclidean line-sets,
https://doi.org/10.1112/S0024611597000403.

These provide context for orthogonal bases, Pauli structure and binary
code constructions. The specific finite partitions, rank receipts, frame
obstructions and paid circuit deductions in this package are checked here
and are not attributed to those papers. Pinned computational dependencies
are separately recorded in sources/COST-SOURCES.json and the continuation
subtrees; reading a source or a PR is not a fresh supplier replay.
