## Mixing and the epoch graph

For a stationary mixing matrix `M`, the diagonal shifts on its two sides satisfy `M G_in = G_out M`. In entry `(d,s)` with nonzero unit coefficient `M[d,s]`, this forces the shift on input lane `s` to equal the shift on output lane `d`. Ordinary ADD and QUERY have both diagonal entries one and one nonzero off-diagonal entry, so all four body shifts agree. The Type-A native stage has two independent such pairs. For literal roles `(x,y,u,v)` their nonzero off-diagonal terms are `q^-1` from `x` to `y`, and `alpha2*q` from `v` to `u`, both with the native body factor `D_j` on the pair. The Type-B pair `(s,t)` has matrix `D_j [[1,-q],[q^-1/2,1/2]]`; the two constants come from literal `alpha=-1`, `beta=1/2`, and `1+alpha*beta=1/2`. These entries are units in the Laurent body ring with `q^4=1`. SCALE and LAMBDA commute with body monomials. SWAP transports the lane exactly. IDLE_ODD has the same positive body power as IDLE_ONE and also an independent signed q power.

Collapse the four lane ends at each mixing pair to one node. An edge is one field's epoch from its preceding to its next mixing node, following SWAP permutations. Initial and terminal ends have zero gauge because the operator on arbitrary input columns is fixed. For a body label `j`, let `x` be its integer gauge exponent at a node, and let `d,e` denote the old and replacement powers on an epoch `a→b`. The transported operator equation gives `e=d+x_b−x_a`. On an edge with no old payment for `j`, nonnegativity of replacement powers forces `x_b≥x_a`. If a node lies on a source-to-sink path of such edges, both endpoints are pinned to zero and monotonicity forces its exponent to zero.

The exact scan verifies this path condition at every node for all 17 labels. It finds 688 mixing nodes and 1,412 epochs in Type A, and 3,493 mixing nodes and 7,022 epochs in Type B. All 94 paid idle events occupy separate epochs. Thus every epoch retains its original body powers and no two paid idle events can be fused by this diagonal transport. Phase-only retiming of the extra signed factor remains open. The shared-body mixing control passes, and unequal body exponents fail. Without nonnegative replacement powers, a zero-payment two-edge path admits potentials `(0,1,0)` by introducing `+1,−1`. Without the terminal pin it admits `(0,1,1)` with nonnegative new power. These counterexamples show the role of each condition.

The result covers simultaneous diagonal gauges, including coordinated relabeling along the unchanged SWAPs. Prefix differences that become non-diagonal, changed HOST factors, inverse-body synthesis and packing distinct arbitrary field values into a new larger recursive input require different contracts. Independent Laurent symbols have no relation `D_j^4=1`; a finite phase-only specialization cannot justify canceling their powers. Same-field tensor batching acts on one existing field and uses its existing axes. Combining separate fields requires a separate direct-sum or tensor interface with exact input embedding, output recovery and storage accounting.
