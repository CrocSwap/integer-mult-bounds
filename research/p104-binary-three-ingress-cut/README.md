# Three-to-two binary ingress exclusion at P104 adjoint charts 510–518

All **27 fixed-port three-to-two recodes** fail on the binary native word
retained by PR #7830. Allowing different grade-one sender charts gives **90
source-pair multisets** across the nine receivers, including same-sender
pairs. Of those, **27** have two independent directions when all stock is
granted without clock restrictions. Every one requires a protected reader
that cannot be produced before the new send that needs it. The result holds
when a subset of the nine receivers is edited jointly under the contract
below. Higher grades leave the full binary invoice short at the stated
target even if all nine receivers attain the most favorable price.
The result carries zero κ credit. [#7830](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/7830)

## Contract

Use one native call with 680 arbitrary independent input bits and fixed
public start and end charts. Local operations are GF(2)-linear on data
already present at the chart. Every new interchart send has a legal nested
positive grade and carries one scalar. Edit only the three original
ingresses at each selected receiver. Their replacements terminate at the
same receiver. Preserve every other paid payload's exact input-dependent
row, every public terminal row, the chart atlas, the productive targets and
the caller. The owner-411 bridge and return remain protected. Each changed
bank has no other stock after its three original arrivals are removed.

The fixed-port check retains two of the original sender endpoints and
clocks and gives each sender any linear combination of its earlier stock.
The alternate-source check allows arbitrary retiming with finite causal
order, granting unlimited local retention. The conclusions concern the
named receiver and payload contract. Transfers into other charts,
altered protected payloads, joint coding across calls, and changes to the
productive or physical boundary are outside it.

## Replay of the cut

The literal replay evaluates all 462,400 public operator coefficients in
each direction and checks all 3,554 dirty private retirements per direction.
Reversing the literal word transposes ADD, reverses MOVE and exchanges
INIT with CLEAR. Packet IDs start at 680 in forward MOVE order. Among
receivers with at least two editable ingresses, the only cases of deficient
protected demand rank occur in the adjoint at charts 510–518. Each has three
independent ingresses and two independent outgoing demands, $u$ and $v$.
In original packet order $a,b,c$, these are $a+c$ and $a+b$.

With two replacement scalars at a receiver with empty stock, they must span
the plane generated by $u$ and $v$. Each scalar is one of $u$, $v$, or $u+v$, and
their directions must differ. Both original chart-1 ports can supply only
$u+v$. The original exterior sender supplies none of the three, even if
all its original observations are available. Exact stock intersections
strictly before the original send clocks give the same answer.

A grade-one sender must differ in dimension by one and have nested
rational chart subspaces. The replay checks all 636 labels. The four legal
senders at each receiver are chart 1, the exterior sender, and the two
destinations of the receiver's outgoing readers. With clocks ignored
the two latter senders supply $u$ and $v$, respectively. Recomputing
each sender's entire span with just its relevant reader value withheld
removes the corresponding direction in all 18 checks. This basis test
includes every alternative XOR representation over the remaining stock.

The required reader is itself unavailable until the new send carrying its
direction arrives. The other send supplies a distinct direction and cannot
stand in for it. Thus the required reader and send each have to precede
the other. A second check starts with the other stock granted, releases a
reader only if its row is in the span of the already delivered replacements,
and continues until no more events are enabled. It stalls in all 27
stock-only matches. The receipt also reports stock intersections strictly
before each old reader clock.

All grade-one senders lie outside the entire set 510–518. A new ingress at a
second changed bank therefore cannot add stock to any of those senders.
The protected readers are disjoint from the modified ingress packets.
The local closure already grants all other banks' fixed rows without clock
restrictions. These facts preserve the two-send obstruction for each bank
when any subset changes jointly.

## Complete price and incumbent ordinary boundary

At $\kappa=0.003889928046155$, a grade $g$ costs $p(g)=g^{1-\kappa}$.
The complete signed moment is the sum of productive tariffs less the sum
of paid tariffs. The replay recounts all paid and productive binary bins,
including 36 native invocations and the caller's QUERY and idle services.
It evaluates the original 48-call rational bins from the authenticated
application receipt for comparison. That rational calculation re-prices
the reported histogram. The rational graph and compiler are outside this
replay.

| Whole-application signed deficit | Directed lower bound |
| --- | ---: |
| Original binary invoice, 36 calls | 3.851191807063600004115975446503832338205869506 |
| Original rational invoice, 48 calls | 9.972939199998305926117330312624757785816908108 |
| Binary invoice with the most favorable higher-grade price at all nine banks | 2.106347527320494280004259933388909583704997895 |

Zero or one new send cannot span two scalar directions. Two grade-one
sends would save exactly 36 units at one bank across the caller, so that
case is economically relevant and is excluded by the cut. With two sends
and a grade of two or higher, the least possible replacement charge
per call is $1+p(2)$. The 36-call saving is bounded above by
$36[2-p(2)] \leq 0.193871586638122858234635057012769194944541291
$ per bank. Three or more sends cost at least the original three grade-one
sends. The edit sets are disjoint by receiver, so the upper credit summed
over all nine banks is at most 1.744844279743105724111715513114922754500871611.
The receipt computes directed bounds with rational logarithm and
exponential series and gives the entire optimistic paid histogram.

The incumbent has 111 physical contact quartets, 934,182,831,504 raw
ordinary records and 6,998,544 owned fields against limits of $10^{12}$
and $10^7$. The replay checks the paired caller's full polynomial
coefficient matrix in both orientations and reproduces the incumbent's
raw ordinary census. The mutation controls reject a missing dirty
retirement and detect exactly the owner-411 coefficient when its feeding
ADD is omitted. This certificate provides the cut and the signed price
bound. Physical realization and ordinary pricing of changed words remain
separate obligations.

## Reproduction

Use Python 3.10 or newer and the standard library. From this directory,
download the three pinned inputs and compare the output to
[`RESULT.json`](RESULT.json).

```sh
raw=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds
curl -fLsS "$raw/89a67c404226d51028e135de2b9169e9020d57af/research/p104-iterated-returns/binary/WORD.json.gz" -o WORD.json.gz
curl -fLsS "$raw/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/paired-query-only-bit/EFREE-CALLER.json.gz" -o EFREE-CALLER.json.gz
curl -fLsS "$raw/14c74c6a67108f675ac1196a5f2415ead0920e71/research/p104-chart306-chain/DOUBLE-APPLICATION.json" -o DOUBLE-APPLICATION.json
python3 -B replay.py --word WORD.json.gz --caller EFREE-CALLER.json.gz \
  --rational-application DOUBLE-APPLICATION.json > replayed.json
diff -u RESULT.json replayed.json
```

E-free BIT caller and predecessor contributors documented with their sources.
Separate Codex implementations performed the initial and independent
computational checks. [Binary proof and credits](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/89a67c404226d51028e135de2b9169e9020d57af/research/p104-iterated-returns/binary/PROOF.md)
