Structural result and compatibility
--------------------------------------
power-dominance.txt proves a new finite comparison: PR67 has a strictly
smaller complete power characteristic than PR64, PR65 and PR68 for all
0<a<1. The proof uses nonpositive prefixes of capped-mass sums and the
strictly decreasing discrete curvature of t^(1-a). Ordinary concave
dominance is false: for PR67 minus PR64 the cap at 16 is +3542. The JSON
contains exact prefixes and pinned complete/local input hashes. This
proof is mathematical prose plus exact integer checks, not a new Lean
theorem. Its scope is the supplied finite profiles.

PR67's witness enumeration and PR68's eligible live controls appear
compatible at the source level, but no combined construction is claimed
or tested here. For a live control with known next frame G, the matching
search score should compare H(F,G)-H(F,E)-H(E,G), not the retired-role
identity-cleanup score. Regardless of score, each proposed composition
requires regenerated legal words, complete paid profiles, CRT checks and
fresh arithmetic. PR65 region reordering could also compose after fresh
dependency/carrier checks; no gain is additive by inspection.

All four retain the inherited complex branch (saving 717/10000000) and
the existing projector/common-I+J framework. None closes the remaining
all-size physical transfer obligations. A generic framed-XOR execution
and charging theorem would apply across these finite search changes;
that proof closure is more substantive than another parameter-grid gain.
