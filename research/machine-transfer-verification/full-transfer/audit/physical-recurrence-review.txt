Adversarial review of the floor recurrence and physical volume contract
=====================================================================

Scope: source inspection of PR73 253ecc88faeed55a950c76026d1fe67a7f690123,
which contains the PR71 physical construction unchanged. This review does not
instantiate a complete finite-state tape machine. No counterexample to the
written floor/remainder/spectator construction was found.

1. Exact child width and volume

notes/batched-bit-rows.tex and notes/batched-algorithms.tex use the actual child
width t*floor(e/m), not floor(t*e/m). These differ in general; the FloorDepth /
FloorRecurrence / PhysicalCostTransfer sources use the former, correct quantity.
For e>=m and 1<=t<m, it is positive and strictly smaller than e. At e<m the
entire call is handled by individual digit operations, so zero-width children
are never executed. The strict characteristic gives a uniform induction bound
with this floor operation because t*floor(e/m) <= (t/m)*e.

Write V=P*R*B where the row field precedes the complete suffix B. The split
r=W*g+w partitions complete rows, producing W streams each of volume V/W.
Each recursive edge call acts on an entire one of these streams. Reducing its
active width from e to t*floor(e/m) does not discard the other address digits.
They become spectators. Thus there is no extra factor q^(2(tf-e)) in its volume.
In a descendant at depth j, the volume is V0/W^j, and all original address
digit ranges are still represented. Neither copies on work tapes nor the
number of scratch roles is folded into this logical volume. They contribute
to the constant local work charge and to the fixed role count W respectively.

The child field is a contiguous identity run of the ordered pivot profile.
notes/projector-batching.tex explicitly uses coordinatewise additions modulo
q^f on the adjacent f-digit components. A single width-tf interchange swaps
their concatenations, preserving internal order. It is not addition modulo
q^(tf), which would introduce unwanted carries. Singleton pivots use width f.
The triangular factors have fixed dimension and update later fields from
earlier controls. Their rational coefficients are fixed before q is chosen;
q must avoid their finitely many denominators and unit numerators. CRT witness
primes used by the profiler are not a claim that any one of them is that q.

2. Remainder movement is explicit and consistent

For e=mf+r, the initial field order is H+ H- G D+ D-. Exchanging corresponding
r high digits gives D+ H- G H+ D-. Moving H+ before H- gives
D+ H+ H- G D-. The main interchange of H-,D- gives D+ H+ D- G H-.
Moving H+ before the final H- gives the required D+ D- G H+ H-.
All unchanged high and gap fields are spectators of the main call. With
0<=r<m and fixed m, the number of individual digit exchanges/moves is bounded
independently of e, hence O(V) if the elementary stream theorem is instantiated.
There is no recursive padding of chunk ranges, no repeated rounding-up of
child widths, and no hidden sort of arbitrary growing coordinate tuples here.

3. Arbitrary spectators and uniform base cost

The mathematical source upstream/build/sections/02-streams.tex is more explicit
than a bare unit-cost permutation claim. A one-digit move demultiplexes into q
streams and remerges in the required order; a two-digit permutation uses q^2.
Removing the selected digits preserves the order of every remaining coordinate,
even for an arbitrarily long intervening gap. Fixed q permits a fixed finite
number of tapes. Nested counters have amortized ripple cost; the shape is a
fixed number of collapsed ranges. Binary shape preparation is polynomial in
log(2V), which is O(V) uniformly for V>=1 with a fixed degree.

For an affine target after its controls, a target fiber has Q=q^f blocks of B
bits. Preparing an offset costs f^C, absorbed into QB>=q^f. Spectator counters
are not reread wholesale for each bit. Negation reverses block order and then
reverses each popped block, so it does not silently reverse its record bits.
These details support the claimed mathematics; they are not yet a Lean
execution/refinement theorem for the described scans and counters.

In particular, finitely many base widths e<m do not alone imply a uniform base
bound over arbitrary spectator lengths. One needs the elementary stream result
uniformly in those lengths. PhysicalCostTransfer correctly exposes precisely
that assumption: cost(s)<=B*volume(s) for all base states s, with one B. It also
quantifies over actual state-indexed children and requires each child volume
V/W, so it does not assume existence of a normalized worst-case supremum.

4. Outer row stock and the new arithmetic proof

The new OuterRows source replaces the least suitable rho by the explicit
rho=14*D(e). For m575,tmax529, the additive decrease is at least46 at every
internal step: (m-tmax)*D(e)<=e. Hence 2rho=28D(e)<e for e>0 (and rho=0 when
D=0). W<=137151806<2^28 implies W^D <= q^(2rho) for every q>=2. This covers
the new PR71/73 W=135075665 as well as the earlier selected W.

For N=q^(2rho) and P=W^D, roundedRows=((N-1)/P+1)*P is the least multiple of P
at least N, and N<=roundedRows<N+P<=2N. Thus padding is strictly less than a
factor two even when N already is a multiple of P. The product
q^(2rho)*q^(2(e-rho))=q^(2e) shows that row stock comes from existing digits;
it is not polynomial duplication of the array. Depth monotonicity supplies
the needed stock for e-rho. The source also proves the exact logarithmic rho
bound and absorption into any positive real power of e. Its outer_cost_bound
explicitly assumes the physical gathering/padding cost. No unsupported
execution claim is hidden in the arithmetic conclusion.

The source inspected was work/full-transfer/outerrows/OuterRows.lean. Its
current inventory has 17 printed theorem audits. The reviewed-source hash is
saved in physical-review-source-pins.json; the parent owns compilation and
final packaging. Arithmetic interpretation and hypothesis signs are sound.

5. Exact remaining proof obligations and a decisive next step

(a) Formalize the elementary digit demultiplex/merge as an actual fixed-head
tape routine with arbitrary prefix/gap/suffix lengths, correct output order,
clean work tapes, parked-ancestor preservation, head return and a uniform
C*V bound including counters. The existing TapeStack/TapeStackPush proofs
cover the stack primitive but do not supply this permutation or its counters.

(b) Formalize whole-row cyclic split/merge with W fixed, including the inverse
role permutation, the bijection (p,r,b)<->(p,g,w,b), exact V/W size and head
interfaces. This is the direct bridge between a finite role schedule and the
state-cost child-volume hypothesis. Its correctness must retain every suffix
bit and row coordinate, including scratch rows with arbitrary input bits.

(c) Establish a finite-call-plan composition theorem: each literal finite edge
contributes exactly the recorded identity-run children, each of volume V/W,
and only a bounded number of linear scans. Remainder handling and the outer
layer then attach using the proved arithmetic contracts. The typed call plan
must include both phase directions, copied centers, exterior gauges and the
final data correction; these are the finite-agent's current ambient work.

(d) Close descriptor construction and stack scheduling uniformly. At depth j,
all root digit ranges remain present, so their exponential volume can pay for
polynomial arithmetic on O(log V0) descriptors. Prove the comparison with Vj
and ensure no growing-depth list is scanned once per data bit. A proof about
arbitrary width-indexed cost functions cannot create these implementation
invariants; they must be supplied by a physical state representation.

These are precise implementation refinements of the existing written proof,
not evidence of a new numerical obstruction. The scalar payload remains F2;
the older F3 specialization in notes/batched-algorithms.tex describes a
different historical network and must not be imported as the current scalar
gate table. Address arithmetic remains over Z/q^f for suitable odd q.
