Independent source review: OuterRows and DescriptorVolume
=======================================================

No mathematical or scope blocker found. This is a source/statement review;
the parent's fresh compiler/axiom receipt supplies build evidence. The exact
source hashes reviewed are in review-source-pins.json.

OuterRows has 17 printed theorem audits. It proves additive depth decrease,
explicit rho=c*depth, exact row-stock coverage, ceiling divisibility/bounds,
preservation of existing digit volume, subpower gathering-count growth and a
conditional outer cost estimate. For selected m575,t529,c14 and positive e,
28D(e)<e follows from46D(e)<=e when D>0, and rho0 when D0. Therefore both
e-rho and e-2rho are positive for all positive e; no asymptotic exception is
required. The selected W range includes PR71/73's135075665.

The rounding formula ((N-1)/P+1)*P is correct for positive N,P including exact
multiples. N<=R<N+P<=2N, so the strict factor-two padding claim is valid.
Stock for e-rho follows from depth monotonicity. The arithmetic volume identity
uses r<=e explicitly, avoiding Nat subtraction truncation errors.

The outer cost theorem has sufficient signs: A,C,V and paddedV are nonnegative,
p>0, reduced-width real power is at most e^p. Its explicit outer_contract
contains the physical cost of gathering/padding and the inner call. The theorem
does not derive execution costs from mere row arithmetic.

DescriptorVolume has five printed theorem audits. The complete retained root
main-address range X is an explicit hypothesis. At descendant depth j<=D,
W^j divides positive R, so R/W^j>=1. With positive S and W^D<=X, this proves
X<=Vj=(R/W^j)*X*S and W^j<=Vj, while V0=R*X*S=W^j*Vj. Hence V0<=Vj^2.
The selected theorem obtains W^D<=X from stock coverage and2rho<=e. It does
not mistakenly replace retained spectators by the current active child range.

The logarithmic step yields log(2V0)<=2log(2Vj). For positive natural k,
log(2V0)<=2k*(2Vj)^(1/k), and raising to k gives exactly
  (log(2V0))^k <= 2*(2k)^k*Vj.
The constant and power are correct, with explicit V0,Vj>=1 assumptions. The
case k0 is unnecessary for positive-degree descriptor arithmetic and trivially
handled separately if a caller needs it. The more general intermediate log
lemma uses Lean's real-log convention on negative arguments, but the cost
application only uses positive volumes.

These results discharge the arithmetic uniformity estimate needed to absorb
fixed-degree descriptor work measured at root scale into each descendant's
linear-volume budget. They do not implement descriptor multiplication/division,
the whole-row splitter, digit moves, affine scans or complete stack scheduler.
The remaining physical proof must produce their explicit state/volume and
cost contracts. The documentation currently preserves this distinction.
