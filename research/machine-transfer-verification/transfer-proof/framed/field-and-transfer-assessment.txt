A naive F3 reinterpretation is false
------------------------------------
Already L=J=V=1 in the all-plus dirty wrapper gives
  (x,y,z) -> (x, y+2z+x, z+2x)
over F3. For x=1,z=0 it leaves scratch 2, rather than restoring scratch.
The selected scalar incidence identity also uses parity: at S=T its two
terms are 0+3=1 in F2, but 0 in F3. A signed cleanup alone would therefore
not establish the actual selected graph's JLV=I over F3. No such lift is
claimed or needed by this bundle.

What transfers without a payload-field change
---------------------------------------------
A common address permutation commutes with any fixed pointwise finite-table
gate. For the selected word that table is XOR on Bool. Original dirty payloads
remain arbitrary; newly allocated copied-center temporaries alone start blank
and may be erased. FramedXor proves the typed decoded-state invariant and
fresh-copy fanout at this precise boundary. AddressGauges supplies the actual
partial-swap and original shear permutations over additive address modules.

Over ZMod(q^f), the address matrix identities are SUPPLIED hypotheses. A
finite rational matrix table can be reduced using one denominator localization
when the chosen common prime does not divide that denominator. To preserve
nondegeneracy and the certified profile, the prime must also avoid the finite
Gram determinant and prescribed nonzero pivot exceptions. In particular q
must be odd and avoid 3 for the literal G_h=I-J/9 formulas (further exceptions
include relevant factors of h-9 and all other finite table denominators).
The rational calculations do not imply the equations/profiles for every odd
prime. There is no unital ring homomorphism from all of Q into a finite ring.
The Lean modular gauge theorem only uses the exact reduced idempotence that
an eligible-prime argument must establish; it does not hide that argument.
