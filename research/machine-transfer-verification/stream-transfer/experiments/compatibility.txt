Compatibility proof (mathematical argument, not a new Lean theorem)

At a recursive block the groups are a partition of its points into sets of
size one or two. PR71 split_pair_graph.py lines 81-94 preserve that property;
lines 103-111 merely permute the noncommon points. Recursion still contracts:
for n>3, splitting one size-two group leaves at most ceil(n/2)+1 <= n-1
groups; at n=3 the default gives two groups; n<=2 is the inherited base case.

For any two distinct groups G_i,G_j, the old coarse edge is
    C_ij = sum_{a in G_i, b in G_j} e_{a,b}.
The new coarse edge is
    C_ij' = sum_{b in G_j} (sum_{a in G_i} e_{a,b}).
These expressions have identical coefficients by associativity and
commutativity; no characteristic-two cancellation is used in this local
identity. Singleton groups and zero-support terms cause no exception. For a
2x2 pair this is precisely two pair-star sums followed by their sum, instead
of the old sequential fold. The globally interned DAG may share either
pair-star sum with strip computations, so total node count can decrease.

Every e_{a,b} at this level represents a disjoint original input support.
Thus each newly introduced partial column sum and the final coarse sum is a
disjoint union. The recursive weight and coarse-edge partition is unchanged.
Induction through the unchanged recursion, strip formulas, and four-output
assembly gives exactly the same partial exclusion outputs and retained center
outputs. Consequently the unchanged source injection and scatter maps still
have J L V = I over F2 for the new L. The serialized word's independent full
basis replay checks this claim and arbitrary-dirty auxiliary restoration,
without relying solely on the induction.

Frames are canonical original envelopes: the core is the intersection and the
cover the union of source triples. All sources of one pointwise partial
circuit contain its distinguished common point. Any new intermediate support
therefore has a nonempty core. A disjoint union enlarges cover and shrinks
core, so the natural addition incidences remain nested. SharedPointCircuit's
verify() checks every active node's exact support union, core intersection,
cover union, and common point, plus all exact outputs. The actual compiled
word is then checked separately for every reframe incidence, source/terminal
contract, and literal output-to-scatter binding. Reassociation does not by
itself certify whichever carrier reuse the optimizer selects.

Cost boundary

Neither fewer additions nor fewer roles implies a better characteristic.
The new compiler can create different matching and reclamation opportunities;
the resulting actual ordered transition matrices must be profiled. The
experiment compares the full paid hybrid (new h23, unchanged h25), including
both exterior classes, data growth, fixed data corners, and final paid copy,
at the exact PR73 saving. All CRT minor bounds and actual finite transition
profiles are checked. The complete hybrid has m=575 and fixed deficit
m W - sum_t(t n_t)=1,846,900, but its width and distribution can change.

The two-axis ambient interpretation, inherited exterior/data pivot facts,
and all-size machine-cost contracts retain their prior conditional scope.
This experiment does not itself prove an integer multiplication theorem,
perform a general network search, or claim that failure of this one schedule
rules out every compatible reassociation/scheduler combination.
