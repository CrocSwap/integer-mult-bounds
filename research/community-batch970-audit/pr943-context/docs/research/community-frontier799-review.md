# X4 frontier and community verification/tooling round

Jeremy Kothe's [PR799](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/799)
composes four paired read exchanges with Chafik Boukhalfa's PR741 frames,
retaining DreamingOfClouds' PR589 bit supplier on eumemic's PR555 construction.
The reviewed **conditional** exponent saving is

κ = 1661634653669567 / 10^18 = **0.001661634653669567**.

That is approximately **0.026005%** above the PR741 checkpoint. It compares
exponent savings, not real-world multiplication runtimes. The construction
retains icekylinx's source gauge, the earlier cold-end, entrance and owner work,
and Rohan Arun's native-complex recurrence correction. Earlier source notices
and the cumulative [contributor record](../../CONTRIBUTORS.md) remain in place.

## Construction and finite review

X4 changes four pairs of read exchanges in the exact B word. Paired edits and
explicit readable frames change the local profile from
`{1:2556,2:334,3:430,4:194,5:56,8:8}` to
`{1:2548,2:338,3:430,4:194,5:56,8:8}`. Rank mass, helper endpoints and the 504-role
width inventory are unchanged. The physical owner bytes are identical to PR741.
Eleven of twelve global checker files are byte-identical; the assembly file
changes only its descriptive scope string. Admission checks were not relaxed.

The maintainer replay rebuilt the X4 word from the freshly reconstructed SW4
ancestor, all 728 scalar columns in both directions, frame chains, exact phases,
wrappers, 95 endpoint controls, physical owners and the full finite invoice.
The seven recipe controls include both rejected bad edits and valid neutral or
inferior alternatives; they are not seven rejection controls. The independent
rational all-column audit and two moment engines agree. All 47 strict outer
margins pass. The bit supplier is bound to the earlier complete fresh PR589
replay, including hosting and bridge controls. Reuse is explicit and source-bound.

`make frontier799-verify` rebuilds the ancestry as well as X4. The opt-in
`--ancestor` argument reuses a complete PR741 replay only after the existing
composition checker validates its source, receipt, artifact and log bindings.
The integration receipt records local execution and Linux CI separately.

This review accepts the explicit finite witness. Contributor reports of broad
read-exchange searches or frame optimality in PR799 are retained as search
reports, not promoted to independently established optimality theorems.
All-size compilation, common-label/mutable-source realization, whole-call
hosting, precision/tape and analytic transfer remain inherited hypotheses.
This is not an unconditional result or a full Lean multiplication theorem.
