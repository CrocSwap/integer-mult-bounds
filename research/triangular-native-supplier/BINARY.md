# Same-order native supplier

The external ordinary request interchanges two complete binary address fields. The caller supplies arbitrary independent records at every address, an optional prefix and gap, and an opaque suffix. The result restores their order, all saved row positions, and the prefixes while it returns the interchanged fields. The payload operation inside the binary program is XOR. The matrix ring is used for addresses. ## A change of chart on the actual payload

Use a product of cyclic ternary components with maximum width $u$. A single matrix over $R=\mathbb Z/3^u$ acts on all components through reduction. Consider one of the $f$ columns of a native call. Its head and tail have the same coordinate order. Let $A$ be the diagonal of inherited unit weights, which may differ at every coordinate, column and exposed ancestor. Write $x=A^{-1}h$ and $y=t$. In these coordinates the idempotent $P$ requests

$$D(P)(x,y)=(x+P(y-x),\;y-P(y-x)).$$

For a right matcher $PS=SQ$, applying $S^{-1}$ to both coordinates, running $D(Q)$, and restoring the original coordinates gives $D(P)$. On the actual head the forward action is $AS^{-1}A^{-1}$. These equalities are pointwise equalities of address permutations. Pullback therefore acts correctly on every function of the entire address, with arbitrary payloads and correlations. The two entries of a coupled root port use the same physical matcher and the same inherited weighting. The sum and exchange instructions still see the correct matched addresses. Private zeros, copied donors, independently returned productive helpers and the full chronology retain their original semantics. Inverting each entry route after the corresponding completed local call restores the original view. This induction is the retained all-column native proof with its concrete change-of-basis service replaced at each entry. It does not assert that the 36 root calls share a single free choice of chart. ## The quotient on growing bodies

Let $U$ be the whole lower unitriangular group over $R$. Store a representative $r$ of each left coset $U r$. For a right instruction $S$ compute $rS=Lr'$ with $L\in U$, and send the record to chart $r'$. Acting on its body by $L^{-1}$ on the tail and $AL^{-1}A^{-1}$ on the head completes its route. Indeed the two old/new represented projectors obey the preceding change-of-basis equation with $L$. The representative residuals multiply in time order and inverse routes restore every record. The left subgroup may be nonnormal. The new bank carries every chart representative with all its genuine high coordinates. The sorted data in distinct representatives and the data in every older or still unused row remain independently writable. ## Installation and movement clock

Each production carrier $N_i$ exceeds $4\cdot134^{480}$. The planner therefore takes its nonempty forest branch, with at least one source slot and one child-width multiple. It sets the productive batch width to the power-of-two ceiling of fifteen times that positive product. In particular $B_i\ge16$. Each direct child holds at most the parent's volume divided by $B_i$, even when it has no matrix chart. This pays the geometric sum of suspended frames used in the complete storage proof. No low representative table is installed. The odometer, Gaussian reduction, trit sorting and the variable-size unit scaler are fixed tape programs. Their count, width and frame metadata are computed at the actual invocation. A direct node with $c\le N_i^8$ can pay up to $N_i^{8C_i}$ in its fixed upper-reheading surcharge. It is less than $N_i^{0.032}$ and occurs in front of the summed subcritical recurrence. No inequality here predicts a uniform improvement in running time.
