# Six binary carriers in the complete product

The ordinary stages need successively stronger prices. A stage uses the
previous price on the fraction `1/250` of the width exponent. Give each
stage its own funded carrier. Decouple the width of the previous call
from the size of a matrix entry. This changes the machine and the
populations of its independent data records. The time exponent of the
complete product remains 0.00387992803556.

## Balance every real component scan

Let `e` denote the width in trits. Put `ρ=1/250` and
`η=97/25000`. At stage `i`, set `R_1=1`, `R_i=E_(i−1)+1` for
`i>1`, `w=max(R_i,ceil(e^ρ))`, `g=ceil(e^η)`, and
`m=ceil(w/g)`. Split the `w` trits into `m` contiguous cyclic
components as evenly as possible. Write `u=ceil(w/m)`. Since
`g≤w` and `m≤2w/g`, the actual largest modulus satisfies
`g/2≤u≤g`. Each physical component width is at least `u−1`.
The true address group contains all `3^w` ordinals, each carrying its own
arbitrary payload. The local matrix ring is `Z/(3^u)`. It acts on all
components by simultaneous reduction. The new same-order leaf and one
ordinary return inherit this product-module arithmetic. The old
whole-field shell required two reversed returns. [Earlier product-module execution](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/46783fbbfc219a3faffbea76f60a2810b85ab75a/research/native-stock-activation/MODULE-ATOMS.md)

There are at most `c≤e/w` coarse columns. A complete component pass
costs at most `2 e^(1−η)` full scans across all columns because
`mc≤2 e/g`. This estimate includes large predecessor floors as well as
growing atoms. The peeled stopped pairs cost at most a fixed carrier
factor times `w^(1−b_(i−1))`. Indeed `m≤2 w/e^η≤2w^(1−b_(i−1))`
because `w≤e`. A complete high-field request has width `u−1≤w`.
In the quotient regime each placement and its inverse, including
their triangular body corrections, have the separately proved charge
`O_F(V(mc+1+(u−1)^(1−b_(i−1))))` per native site. In the direct
regime the full physical reheading, wide-field transfers, return and
scrubbing cost `O_F(Vc(m+w^(1−b_(i−1))))`. Let `t_i=1−C_i`.
Every such body has `c≤N_i^8`, so
`c≤N_i^(8C_i)c^t_i`. This fixed multiplier is below `N_i`.
Apply the two funded moments to all recursive descendants. The direct
cost acquires this factor only once in front of the complete estimate.
Each node receives a smaller field from its parent, and the normalized
degree-`t_i` bills sum as a geometric series. The physical stopped
word calls the earlier ordinary algorithm twice on the entire `w`.
It counts every component shift, negation, return and scratch cleanup.

The rank-one children retain the previous sizes and paid moments.
Their induction gives
`O_F(V(mc+c^t_i w^(1−b_(i−1))))`.
The second term divided by `V` is at most
`e^(1−C_i) w^(C_i−b_(i−1))`. In the growing branch use
`w≤2e^ρ`. In the other branch use `w=R_i≤N_i` and
`C_i−b_(i−1)>0`. Both bounds, the unfinished tail and the restored
stock give `O_F(V e^(1−b_i))`. Below the entry use the greatest
affordable earlier stage, including the elementary stage. Any such
finite range also fits since `e^b_i≤E_i^b_i<N_i`. No call needs a
false admission.

The physical coefficient charge per prefix permits
`O(J_i²(w+1)^1024(D+1)^8)` before amortization over its true
component, with `D≤3+250log₂(w+1)`. This estimate includes division,
inverse, saved rows, parent positions, the true coefficient and its
propagation. Here `w≤e≤g^(1/η)≤(2u)^258`. Each live component and
high-field fibre has at least `3^(u−1)` positions, including a
one-position high when `u=1`. The ratio of the displayed variable
polynomial to that fibre is below the fixed `N_1`. For example
`D+1≤256(w+1)`, `w+1≤2(2u)^258`, and
`u^a / 3^(u−1) ≤ 3 a^a` for `a=266256`. Taking binary logarithms
gives less than 6,000,000, below `H_1`. All carriers therefore have
the fixed polynomial room for these calculations.

## Real reserves and the predecessor path

Let `a_i=log₂ E_i`. Start with `a_1=ceil((H_1+64)/(1−ρ))`.
At higher stages take the larger of `ceil((H_i+64)/(1−ρ))` and
`H_i+a_(i−1)+64`. The table checks `a_i<3H_i` and
`E_(i−1)+1<N_i`. At `e≥E_i`, write `t=log₂e` and
`D=2+ceil(log_(N_i)ceil(e/w))≤3+t/H_i`. There are two regimes.

* If `ceil(e/w)≤N_i^8`, the direct receiver needs the productive owner
  stock alone. Each of its `D≤10` levels reserves its own
  `B_i≤N_i^105` independent positions. Hence its rounded trit debt is
  at most `1+1050H_i<e/64` throughout this regime.
* Else `e>N_i^8`. The quotient's true chart count fits beneath the
  full `3^(N_i²u)` rows per owner. The rounded trit reserve obeys

    k_i ≤ 1+D(N_i²u+105H_i) ≤ 1+6(3+t/H_i)N_i² 2^(ηt).

In the second regime put `T=ceil((2H_i+64)/(1−η))<3H_i` and
`z=t−T≥0`. The bound
`3+t/H_i≤6·2^((1−η)z)` follows from
`1+z/4≤2^((1−η)z)` and `T≤3H_i`.
At `T`, `2^64 N_i²≤2^((1−η)T)`. These two inequalities give
`k_i<e/64`. Every nested public ordinary field is no wider than its
parent. The sum of all six rounded stage debts is therefore less than
`6e/64<e/4`. Each root borrows only its own debt and retains its
parent while a child runs. No older row is recycled as fresh stock.
Padding to the next multiple of the root divisor adds less than one
extra full slab. The quotient's correction work, scratch, retained
owners and any temporary chart padding have their separate physical
backing bounds.

The root restores the borrowed trits and the remainder by two cold digit
interchanges. They cost $O(V(k_i+r+1))$ with $r<w$. In direct mode
$k_i\le1+1050H_i$. In quotient mode the bound above gives
$k_i=O_F((1+\log e)e^\eta)$. Both fit $O_F(e^{1-b_i})$. The
remainder also fits since $w\le\max(R_i,2e^\rho)$. Opening and closing
the binary-to-ternary rectangle and the single root padding slab take
linear passes on at most a fixed multiple of the actual data. Thus the
price includes the work outside the compiled native body.

The implemented tree also uses direct receivers inside a quotient
root. Let `l=ceil(log_(N_i)ceil(e/w))`, let `Z` be the number of
quotient charts per owner, and let `b=max(0,l−8)`. The root reserves
`B_i^(2+l) Z^b` rows. After `j` divisions by `N_i` the remaining
number of body atoms is at most `N_i^(l−j)`. No level after `b`
needs a fresh chart. The earlier chart remains as an exterior spectator
with its true weight on the paid subfield. If a child reaches the
direct range early it treats unused rows as spectators. Thus all
suspended returns receive distinct rows. Compared with using an
identical quotient at every one of the conservative `2+l` reserved
levels, this removes a factor `Z^min(2+l,10)`. For `l>8`, at
least eight of the active coarse depth bounds can use direct bodies.
Two factors in the comparison come from conservative spare positions.

The body at an entry really issues a native call. At `i>1` the fixed
floor obeys `R_i≤2E_(i−1)`. The entrance leaves more than
`4N_i R_i` trits. The growing term is at most `2e^ρ`, and
`e^(1−ρ)≥2^64 N_i`. Thus `e/w>4N_i` at every entry and the body
after reserving `k_i`
has at least `N_i` whole atoms. In a complete covered call take any
positive rank-one paid occurrence and its central positive child.
Its number of atoms becomes `floor(c/N_i)≥1`. Repeat until the child
has between one and `N_i−1` atoms. Descending divides a positive
integer by a fixed integer at least two. The final nonempty stopped
word performs two genuine predecessor exchanges on the entire
`w≥E_(i−1)`. It restores their arbitrary input and all the intervening
cells as required by the original service. Induction gives six nested
native stages. A small high field may use a cheaper earlier stage.
The full-width stopped transfer establishes the positive chain
independently of those high-field calls.

## From ordinary width to a complete product

Let `A=355763540965264=log₂ E_6 + log₂ M + 1024`. Every public
precision `p≥2^A`, hence every operand length
`n≥2^ceil(2^A/6)`, reaches the six-stage binary path inside a genuine
numerical source of the complete multiplier. Use the predecessor's
actual sorted pair of axis widths, square bands, private compiler,
observed mixing layer and noncontiguous productive complex port.
For `s=ceil(log₂(p+2))` this yields a true native pack with
`h≥2^(s−100−16ceil(log₂s))` and one nontrivial interchange of
`floor(h/M) K'` bits. Its ternary equivalent has at least
`floor(h/M)K'/2` trits. The exponent arithmetic in the receipt gives
`s−100−16ceil(log₂s)−log₂ M−2≥log₂ E_6`. For increasing `s`,
use the continuous lower bound that replaces the ceiling by
`log₂s+1`. The function `s−16log₂s` increases when `s≥32`.
The one-bit slack in the receipt pays the ceiling at the initial
point. The public CRT shuttle at the same input has at least
`p/2424>E_6` trits. Its justified sufficient precision, if a
connected ordinary call inside the actual complex word is not
required, is `4096 E_6`. The numerical islands, input CRTs, paid
complex program, opposite normalization, inverse CRT and exact signed
carry are the previous complete construction with this ordinary
backend. [Outer source, all precision and geometry conditions](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/46783fbbfc219a3faffbea76f60a2810b85ab75a/research/native-stock-activation/OUTER.md)

The old strong sufficient binary logarithm of public precision was
3,840,000,000,000,000. Its actual sixth-stage admission guard alone
required at least 2,881,869,654,258,596 in the binary logarithm of
ternary width. Every outer request has at most `p` bits, and the
earlier calls cannot increase width. This provides a necessary old
bound for that specific gate as well as a sufficient new one.

At the first eligible precision `p*=6ceil(2^A/6)`, every ordinary
width obeys `log₂ e<A+1=s`. Put `U_6=s` and `U_i=a_(i+1)` for
`i<6`. Every selected stage-`i` root has `e<2^U_i`. If a public
entry or an inherited request skipped stage `i+1` on its way to
stage `i`, this follows from the actual guard. For an admitted parent
at stage `i+1` all cross-stage body and high-field calls have width
at most `max(E_i+1,ceil(2^(U_(i+1)/250))) < 2^U_i`. The table

`U_i−a_(i−1)≤8H_i`. The actual floor `w≥E_(i−1)` then gives
`ceil(e/w)≤N_i^8`. Thus **every ordinary call in this complete
product uses the direct regime**. Every earned wide native operation
still executes. The implementation can skip allocation of both the
low and high chart coordinate fields.

Along one active chain the binary logarithm of the product of all six

## Setup, peak and recurring charges

Each compiler accesses its own fixed stream on its own tape. Six
fixed programs still require only a fixed number of heads, and a small
stage can operate without traversing the larger stage's program. Set
`J_i=N_i^20000` and keep the separate complex stream. The inherited
generation bound and installed size for all six streams and the
complex stream are `O(J_6)`. For the rank-dependent factor,
`log₂ J_6=4736000000000000000`. Previously it was
4800000000000000000. These are upper bounds on the particular generated
programs and clocks. They do not give a ratio of observed compilation
times.

The physical sweeps, generated coefficients and previous calls cost
at most `O(J_i^11)(1+C_(i−1))` in the fixed part of each stage.
The outer service adds a factor `O(J_6^12)`. The resulting complete
rank envelope is at most `C_* J_6^100`. The fixed primitive constants
and price gaps enter `C_*`. [Derivation from the executed word](BINARY.md)
This comparison concerns upper envelopes. The least
recurring constant, elapsed time and crossover have not been measured
at either active scale. Input-independent storage for the compiler,
the two persistent numerical fields, linear input and output tapes,
scratch stripes, finite-rank storage factors and `2^O(s²)` private
per-input setup remain separate charges.
