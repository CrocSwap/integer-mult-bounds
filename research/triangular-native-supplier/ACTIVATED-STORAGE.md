## Reconstruct nonzero radix tags for the optional movement counters

The opaque payload data have no influence on a successful ordinary motion trace, but the quotient sort has **genuine nonzero tags**. Replacing those tags by zero would give the wrong buckets. The following finite symbolic simulator keeps every tag operation exact and proves the bit length of all hardware counters. Only its space bound is relevant. It is not the sorting algorithm used for multiplication and its work is never used as a substitute in the recurring time bill.

For one low group enumerate canonical source lows by their emitted ordinals $i=0,\ldots,Q-1$. Compute $t(i)=\operatorname{canonicalRight}(\operatorname{low}(i),S\bmod3)$, flattened as its actual row-major $N_i^2$-trit tag. Just before pass $j$ in the reverse digit loop, the tag order is the lexicographic order of
$$
             (t(i)[j+1:\ ],\, i).
$$
The initial suffix is empty. A stable bucket concatenation by digit $j$ prepends its value to the sorting key while retaining the indicated suffix/tie order, which proves the formula at the next pass. To supply the tag at stream ordinal $l$, enumerate candidates $i$. For each one enumerate all lows $i'$ and count the smaller key pairs. The unique candidate with count $l$ supplies precisely $t(i)$. This requires a constant number of low matrices, tags and counters: the source ordinal and comparison count take at most $2N_i^2+1$ bits since $Q<3^{N_i^2}$. The mod-three right word, finite pivot work, current group and pass, and nested unbounded-precision scalars already have room in the private budget. The sum of all bucket entries or all tags need not be stored in the simulator. Record each bucket's cursor and length and apply the actual concatenations, resets and erasures. Copies of tags that do not branch can advance their true extents symbolically. Whenever a tag is read to choose a bucket, generate its correct value by the rank enumeration. Full high-field transformation, prefix-dependent shears, scale branches, and earlier-stage calls proceed with their actual geometry, scalar and lifted row, with symbolic opaque zero payloads. The controller's only payload-dependent assertion checks a private zero. The zero data satisfy it and any successful actual call has the same tape sizes, branch sequence and transfer counts. This constructs the needed controller simulation with no unknown assumption on the size of the total run time.

Write $K=2^{(s+1)^8}$, $J=2^{4736000000000000000}$, $D=2^{2^{20}(s+1)^2}$, and $A_0=K^{8(s+130)}$. The per-level control bill $2^{64}(J+1)^8(p+1)^{64}(D+1)^8<K$ holds at $s_0$. The base-two logarithm of its crude upper bound, obtained with $\log_2(p+1)\le s$, is a sum of a nonnegative constant, an $s$ term and an $(s+1)^2$ term. On dividing by $(s+1)^8$ these terms are decreasing for all $s\ge s_0$. The new quotient scalar generation and simulated comparisons use at most a constant number of complete $N_i^2$-entry matrices per scope, with entries of fewer than $4p+8$ bits before reduction, including a summed inner product; $N_i\le J$, and $N_i<p$ on the entire range. The stack of scale descriptors has at most $4s+8$ entries (three return descriptors for an even split and one for its possible preceding odd step), with a constant number of address words per entry. Enumerating a high tuple keeps its $N_i(N_i+1)/2$ digits and one partial lifted row; the overall widened group cardinality is an actual funded shape. These termwise bounds fit the displayed control allowance without any full per-input chart list.

The sum of the private controls and the symbolic tape descriptors, before the write-only observer totals, is at most $A_0$, since $d(s)<s+128$. The unmetered symbolic stage terminates on a deterministic fixed-alphabet machine, so its configurations do not repeat.
