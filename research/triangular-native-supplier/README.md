# A general multiplier with fewer matrix records and smaller Fourier fields

The six-stage integer multiplier in [PR #7914](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/7914) carries a separate data row for every invertible low matrix at every native stage. This construction runs its compiled binary word directly on the payload near the first entrance. It carries **zero ordinary low or high matrix rows** there. At larger widths it shares one chart among an entire group of matrices and executes the missing row operations on the two actual data banks. A second change completes one normalized Fourier pack at a time. This reduces the size of the persistent data, which otherwise dominates storage even after the ordinary stock has fallen. [Binary supplier](BINARY.md) · [Complete storage and recovery proof](NUMERICAL-STORAGE.md) · [Verified results](VERIFICATION.md)

For arbitrary signed binary input intervals, the algorithm returns the exact signed product on a separate output tape. Let $n$ be the larger significant bit length, $p=6\lceil\log_2 n\rceil$ and $T$ the common padded tensor size. With fixed compilation and the paid sequential movement model of the predecessor, its time is $S+O_F(n(1+\log n)^{1-\kappa})$ at the same proposed **$\kappa=0.00387992803556$**. Here $F$ denotes the finite native program. Space is $S+O_F(n)$. The comparison below uses the same input and output convention and the same 480/264 source words. Both constructions await independent scientific review. The new sufficient input length is $n\ge 2^{\lceil 2^{355763540965264}/6\rceil}$ **bits**. The old actual sixth-stage guard needs $\log_2 e\ge2881869654258596$ for a trit width $e\le p$. The new bound therefore gives a genuine earlier entrance, even when compared with a necessary condition for the old implementation. At the first specified precision all ordinary sites are direct. The binary logarithm of all simultaneously reserved ordinary and complex row configurations is at most 532934501552439168. These counts describe row addresses. The payloads, fixed program, input, output, two numerical fields, stripe temporaries, padding and cleanup also enter the space proof. The cofinal 3,120-fold total peak bound has its own tail condition. [Activation and full reserve](STAGED-CARRIERS.md) · [Occupied tape calculation](NUMERICAL-STORAGE.md)

On every canonical input at the first specified precision $p_*=2^{355763540965264}+2$, the entire new fixed-head machine occupies **fewer than $2^{p_*}$ bits**, including setup and historical scratch. A single old low-matrix slab would already exceed that bound with one opaque bit per required row. This is a comparison at the two machines' own operating scales. ## Construction and evidence

1. The signed tape entry reads significant lengths, constructs the geometry, compiles the word for every true square side, and runs both input CRTs. It uses the finite scalar algorithm for the initial length interval. All eligible larger inputs use the Fourier path. An input can contain arbitrary leading zero bytes at the cost of reading those bytes. [Implementation](multiplier.py) · [Original geometry and compiler bounds](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/46783fbbfc219a3faffbea76f60a2810b85ab75a/research/native-stock-activation/OUTER.md)
2. The complex and CRT shuttles call the new ordinary receiver. It interchanges the two fields by one same-order native word. Its paid children use an inherited diagonal of independent unit weights. A wide call sorts true quotient charts and compensates each change on both full bodies. All setup, the earlier ordinary calls, conversions, seeks and restored storage enter the price. [Ordinary operation](triangular_ordinary.py) · [Binary proof](BINARY.md) · [Quotient movement](QUOTIENT-ROUTE.md)
3. Each disjoint pack finishes its normalized mean before the next one starts. The producer generates the same rational local gates at a lower working precision. Both normalized forwards, public-grid pointwise multiplication, the opposite transform, original-coordinate extraction, integer rounding, inverse CRT and carry use the two actual inputs. The error in every recovered coefficient is at most $144/B^2<1/2$. [Numerical proof](NUMERICAL-STORAGE.md) · [Kernel-checked gate-to-product implication](lean/GatePacks.lean)
4. Matched native bodies with 139,968 independent records used 10,171,008 versus 5,600,664 peak tape bytes and 3,017,776,176 versus 284,414,976 bytes of traversal. A signed Fourier multiplication returned $-345550037539$ through 138 charged rank-two CRT shuttles. Its geometry and carrier are diagnostic. It does not time the production ranks. The absolute entrance remains far beyond physical execution and a crossover is unknown. [Reproduction and coverage](VERIFICATION.md) · [Machine-readable evidence](EVIDENCE.json)

## Open next questions

Can a growing direct body replace the remaining quotient charts without the previous service's expensive upper additions? A complete answer needs a movement bound for arbitrary, independently weighted banks. Can a different physical binary word fund substantially smaller carrier ranks?
