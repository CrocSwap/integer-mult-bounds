# Numerical lifetime of the selected BIT recursive-child coefficient

For unchanged PR2697 `numeric.selected_coefficient`, the delegated **child
branch** uses at most **20,494 additional residue Words and 22 additional index
Words above all numerical residents on entry**. Literal 64-dimensional source
executions attain each bound separately. Successful calls restore entry
ownership, bytes, heads and input tapes, leaving one owned residue result.

This closes the branch explicitly excluded by Nick Petalas's
[PR2781 adapter audit](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/2781).
Together, the two results give its already established **37,672/22** allowance
for either branch of this entry point. The adapter result and its constants
remain Petalas's work. This is an occupied-numerical-content lemma for a pending
source, not a complete caller reserve or supplier admission. No new kappa.

## Proof by chronological lifetimes

The primitive inversion allowance of ten Words and the initialized
`Callback.weight` allowances of `5m^2+10` residues and ten indices are the
credited results of Nick Petalas's PR2759 and
[PR2770](references/PR2770.md). The source modules implementing them are unchanged.
The latter proof accounts for the actual high matrix, header, projector and
128-dimensional fallback residual. Its key depth invariant is that pending
operands are serialized before numerical matrices/scalars are freed. Only one
ancestor's preparation is resident at a time; serialized continuation growth
does not accumulate arena Words.

The additional step here is the **two simultaneously live callbacks** in the
closed child branch, rather than an initialized Page or the adapter constructor.

| Phase, measured above the closed call's entry | Residues | Indices |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Parent Callback construction/validation | at most 12 | at most 15 |
| Append child program, parent retained | 2 | at most 10 |
| Leaf Callback construction/validation, parent retained | at most 14 | at most 21 |
| Leaf.weight, both callbacks retained | at most `5m^2+14` | at most 22 |
| Optional negative inverse, both retained | at most 15 | 12 |

A completed Callback owns two residue Words (`root_unit,high_cap`) and six
indices (`root_size,depth,final_size,one,two,64`). The Ring and incoming request
already belong to `L_R,L_I`. Parent and leaf own distinct copies; the root size
and unit copied into the extension are serialized symbols, not extra retained
arena Words.

For constructor validation, prime-power division retains qword, high_cap and a
test beside at most four division temporaries. Root-unit inversion coexists
with high_cap and root_unit, giving twelve residues. Index validation has three
constants, root size/depth, current/count, then level, parent/child, f and the
halved-unit division's four temporaries, at most fifteen. Earlier step addresses
are freed before the next iteration. A resident parent therefore increases the
second constructor's counts to fourteen and twenty-one.

`extend` divides final_size by 64, frees the remainder, constructs the child
size, checks its coordinate, and serializes the new root/step. With six parent
indices, the four-Word division peaks at ten. A halved unit retains f while
dividing by two, still at most ten. Size multiplication uses at most two new
Words beside f. f is freed before new_depth is allocated, and size/new_depth
are freed after serialization. No new residue Word is allocated here.

At `leaf.weight`, both callbacks retain four residues and twelve indices.
Adding PR2770's above-entry bounds gives `5m^2+14` and 22. Good/unit branches
can be smaller. The fallback inverse phase retains the m^2 projector and 4m^2
residual while inversion adds ten. Neither the root depth nor the number of
pending records changes this numerical maximum.

If requested, the negative inverse retains the one weight result during a
ten-Word inversion: `4+1+10=15` residues. Later negation is smaller. Source
cleanup closes leaf, closes parent and clears the serialized extension. Every
numerical temporary has been freed except the returned result. Word operations
restore borrowed heads; record reads rewind both input tapes. The evaluator
ends with empty pending/value tapes. This proves the stated success contract
under the named primitive/source hypotheses, for all admitted program depths
and widths, rather than by extrapolating the finite fixtures.
