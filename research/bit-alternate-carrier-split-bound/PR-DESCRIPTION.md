Prove that every eligible alternate-carrier cut on the retained #7001/#6726 BIT

changed cocharted banks and 4,521 bank/group contexts, including 76 with nonzero
local pairing but zero endpoint pairing. The proof extends the zero form to
arbitrary finite positive multiplicity and ordered, possibly noncommuting,
linear operator coefficients at the same interface.

This closes the complementary ownership domain left by ToikY's #7304 at

**no original helper from the target group**. #7304 already handles an original
public pivot and arbitrary bank mixing; that domain and criterion are prior work.

## Why it matters and limitations

A productive split with the paid word fixed needs a clean paired channel before
it can improve the BIT moment. The zero-form certificate excludes this route
even with all proposed ordinary work free. Changed words or frames, additional
paid moves, mixed boundary groups, multiple times and nonlinear recodings remain
outside scope. No supplier is emitted or admitted.

**No new κ.** The selected conditional exponent remains
`363005817/100000000000`, with all five framework assumptions retained. This is
a source-bound negative theorem, not an unconditional multiplication theorem.
