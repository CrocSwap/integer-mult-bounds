# Prove the sharp six-drop cut for three 6D P104 depositors

Requires b_j⊆a_j and the original P104 odd-cell rigidity, two distinct low gathers and stated number of active gathers/depositors. Allows rational cancellation/comparable depositors. No Lean proof, universal network result or word realization.

A paper proof establishes T≥6 and D=50+E+B+T≥56+E+B for exactly three 6D and five ≥8D depositor inputs in the stated nine-gatherer geometry. Exact examples attain D=56 and D=57 with a real depositor relay.

Observed validation. The owner’s cold checks reproduced original/three-depot full Gram and odd-cell witnesses, the D=56/57 controls, 768 four-node and 10,240 five-node support DAGs, 32,256 signed minor samples and negative controls. These finite checks support the paper proof. Independent paper review status is reported separately.
