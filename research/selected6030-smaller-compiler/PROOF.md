## 5. Fresh finite constants, ordinary work, and precision

A rank bound is not a materialized word-size or runtime bound. The new unit
must supply its **own** finite constants. Here is the full finite prescription;
no numerical old selected constant is silently applied to the expanded word.

For the strictified product let `U_v` count raw caller instances. With
`P=66834` and `B=13444`, chronological substitution gives
`U_1=1`, `U_(v+1)=B*U_v+P^v`, hence

```

productive fields = 13444^370,
paid calls after exact splitting <= 240152^370+(13444*17)^370.
```

The last bound follows from paid rank mass: completion adds at most
`B^370*17^370`, splitting preserves mass, and every paid rank is at least one.
The source's raw ordinary allowance `10^9` bounds only each raw caller's
retained ordinary-pass descriptors, so those descriptors contribute at most
`10^9*U_370`. Added completion, split adapters and compiler routing are
separate counts. This bound is not a Turing-operation estimate at rank `N`.

Generate the strictified word by the chronological product and exact owner
completion/splitting above. Apply the finite compiler from exported
`FINITE-PRECOMPILATION.md`: at each integer rank, enumerate the finite
lockstep flow, clear all exit and complement widths with an actual integer
common multiple, allocate its source arrays, run disjoint complements, and
substitute complete smaller compiled programs before resuming parents.
Every recursive rank is smaller; finite induction terminates. There are no
fractional arrays or free routes in this prescription.

On that **newly expanded** fixed word count: productive width `W`, paid
rank-one calls `P_N`, raw-instance tags `U`, all ordinary/native/adapter
records `R`, scalar and actual movement passes `G`, all simultaneously
retained fields `Z`, and maximum fixed coefficient/descriptor height `D`.
Allocate a separate bank for every raw-instance owned label and its query
and movement scratch; keeping all banks disjoint is a finite conservative
allocation. Retain each bank until its creating instance clears it. All
initialization, COPY-as-ADD, movement and erasure are counted in `G`; owned
and interior slots are excluded from `W`. Further reuse needs its own
lifetime proof. Constants come from these counts at this `N`, not from the
old raw cap, old uniform word or its former precision numbers.

For a concrete stability prescription, give each elementary scalar operation

every prefix, suffix and subinterval. COPY into an ideally zero owned slot

Descriptor heights, all live banks, movement passes, prime exclusions and
ordinary storage are then substituted in the retained fixed-tape/precision

numerical precision schedule or a new proof of the precision/tape assumption.
