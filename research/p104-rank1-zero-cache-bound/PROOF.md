## Model and claim

Take one original helper h with rank-one entrance/final job. Impose only
X_h=0; retain every other original helper and all400 main inputs jointly
arbitrary. All original scalar operations, coframes and histories retain
their meanings on that domain. Permit deletion of whole original MOVEs
that become zero, or replacement of a whole target from strict original
destination history. Grant unlimited historical storage and deterministic
processing, all ordinary work and physical cleanup free. Retain original
coordinate targets only; h loses its complete rank-one productive credit.

For every such h, total possible recursive credit is at most f(1), and
therefore net native saving is at most f(1)-f(1)=0. This is singleton-only.
The claim holds for any nonnegative tariff on the recorded whole rank-one
jobs; no transcendental price estimate or new recurrence is required.
