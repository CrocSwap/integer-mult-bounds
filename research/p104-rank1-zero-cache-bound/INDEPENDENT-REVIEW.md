# Independent completeness review

The root reviewed the one-unit quotient proof, full source/rank methods,
original-target debit and final independent validator after requesting
explicit coframe and complete-latest-event checks. The final version derives
all18 rank-one helpers and the12/6 partition from the word, requires complete
unique helper/coframe case sets, and binds every unit-expression/provider to
its exact event, owner, physical frame and observation kind.

For an independent chronological bank, quotienting by e_h creates at most
one dependency. If e_h is outside the final bank span, the quotient remains
independent. Otherwise the unique exact expression identifies its latest
nonzero arrival, and singleton support identifies a zero arrival. This
classifies every earlier cut without assuming that a live subset was
simultaneously available: all history storage is granted optimistically.
The independent validator derives that complete event set itself and rejects
omitted, duplicated or mislabelled hits, then checks the target identities.

The modular argument is valid because each original rank equals its literal
row count. A modular augmented rank m+1 then forces the rational rank m+1;
a modular hit is accepted only after a directly checked exact-Q unit identity.
This avoids inferring rational nonmembership from a bare modular miss. The
second source implementation uses a distinct prime and ROW elimination;
it does not just rerun the author's column certificate. Actual wrong-frame,
wrong-latest, missing-case and other mutations exercise the validators.

Every original helper target is its own arbitrary input coordinate, as checked
at the source endpoint. Losing h therefore debits one original rank-one
target. All18 singleton cases supply exactly one whole rank-one credit, so
nonnegative rank tariffs give no net gain. No result about jointly zeroed
nonneutral helpers or changed/new histories is inferred.

The exact source and notices, complete scoped proof, and separate verification
methods are preserved. Registered normal/optimized and committed-byte export
execution is recorded in VALIDATION.json. This is substantial OpenAI-assisted
review for Jerry Chen / @CubeJerry, not independent human or formal-kernel
acceptance. All five foundational assumptions and the selected kappa remain.
