# Finite proof obligations

## Bidirectional physical frames

Each operation touches two physical register chains. Fixed source frames,
entrance gauges, root-read frames and full endpoints delimit those chains.
A downward proposal changes the equal-frame predecessor closure of an
operation. Its new frame contains the join of the incoming frames and all
source incidence vectors in the conservative supports of its operations.
An upward proposal changes the equal-frame successor closure. Its new
frame lies in the intersection of the outgoing frames and contains the
old frame. Degenerate proposals are rejected or replaced by an explicitly
tested nondegenerate subspace.

The boundary test retains both copies of an event when both register chains
touch it. For each boundary jump of width r, the unnormalized contribution
is r^(1-a), with zero contribution at r=0. All three chain copies and the
change in the number of fallback edges are charged. Exact outward rational
intervals must prove a strictly positive gain before a move is accepted.
The trial exponent is a search parameter, not a claimed supplier. Search
uses the older conservative envelope, 10^-16 times 32m^2 fallback children
per edge. Final acceptance independently evaluates the selected complete
profile with the tighter source-derived envelope below; the search score
is not substituted for the final certificate.

Independently of the search, the exported plan is checked for unique,
in-range integer indices and exact independent integer bases. Every final
operation frame must contain its full conservative source span. Every
physical role chain and the target read chronology must remain nested.
Neither descent from the original frame nor a smaller dimension is a
correctness requirement: support and the complete physical chains are the
relevant requirements. The word, including all delayed compensation reads,
is then replayed on all source, target and dirty columns.

For an integer basis B the local bilinear form is G=I-J/9. The checker
computes det(9 B G B^t) exactly. Explicit small-prime division and the
remaining positive factor must establish that every prime divisor is below
2^80. This establishes nondegeneracy over every retained prime q>2^80 for
each new frame; unchanged frames retain their source witnesses. A rational
nonzero determinant by itself would not establish this compatibility.

## Complete paid moment

For ambient dimension m, stock W and positive child multiplicities n_r,
the ideal moment is sum_r n_r*r*(m/r)^a/(mW). The retained theorem in
`notes/three-stage-cover-bit.tex` proves a per-edge bad fraction at most
2m^3/q, with q>2^80. We use the conservative rational upper bound
2m^3/2^80. This does not assume the bad set is empty or remove any edge.

That same theorem charges the full invertible residual of size N0=2m:

    6*N0*(N0-1) + 3*N0 + 6*(N0-1)

children, each of relative width at most 1/m. We retain this full count,
including reversals, swaps and diagonal pairs, before its later rounding
up to 32m^2. The fallback contribution is added to the entire ideal moment;
no displaced good contribution is subtracted. The exact source hash is
checked before these bounds are used. The complete child count is recounted
after every physical frame change.

The accepted coarse saving on the 10^-18 grid has moment upper bound below
one, while the next grid point has lower bound above one. The contaminated
rank mass also contracts. Given coarse saving c and old leaf saving o, the
least atom exponent theta on the 10^-24 grid strictly above c/(1+c-o)
gives actual ordinary saving b=(1-theta)c+theta*o. The checks enforce
b<theta<1-b and reject the preceding unpaid atom.

## Scalar equality and assembly

The complex checker retains PR163's exact adjoint and packed formal replay,
with its coefficient bound independently recomputed for PR168. It bounds
every intermediate coefficient and the final residual coefficient. Its
packing base exceeds twice that bound, so reducing a zero residual modulo
the base and inducting over its digits proves every formal coefficient is
zero. It verifies both shear signs, all aliases, actual compensation times,
the source involution and inverse dirty cleanup. This is deterministic
rational equality, not a modular random test. The bit checker separately
uses exact F2 bitsets and sparse integer coefficient dictionaries.

The bridge preserves the complete finite group and the logical scalar-role
reserve even though physical reuse reduces persistent stock. It recomputes
all router, semantic guard, exact-grid and external-row charges. The actual
transfer saving is the minimum of the paid bit supplier and a strict
weakening of (1-beta) times the certified complex supplier. This definition
is also used by the generated numerical report, avoiding a stale assertion
about which supplier binds. The retained PR168 balanced assembly checks
47 strict inequalities and seven margins. The next final grid point fails
at these particular supplies and parameters; this is not an optimality claim.

## Scope

All results above are finite identities, geometry, inventories and rational
inequalities. They do not prove the inherited all-size tensor/Clifford,
uniform weighted bit compiler, restored-row, balanced-layout, analytic
recovery or fixed-tape interfaces. Those assumptions are unchanged.
