The object of study is other people's work: the P104 native graph and 2048-call caller block of PR #7761 by
Clear1nsight, and the PRs it builds on (see the CREDITS.md files in #7761). This package states and proves facts
ABOUT that construction; it does not replace or endorse it. #7761's graph data is not redistributed here: it is
identified by its graph sha256 and rebuilt from #7761's posted files by our own `lean/tools/bridgegen/apply.py`.
The Lean sources, generators and checks are new; toolchain and Mathlib pin are those of the earlier Lean packages.
