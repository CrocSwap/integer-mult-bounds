## Why the outer is supplier-generic
PR1673's theorem (`vendor/pr1673/capped-phase-fusion/phase_fusion_note.tex`, Thm "Conditional capped-fusion transfer" and §"Updated T74 supplier") is "parameterized by the effective supplier rate". PR1673 was first stated on the T8 selected supplier (b_* = 0.001675462676808…), which is the controller class of PR2260's 72-call T8 supplier; T74 was a later substitution.

**Checked facts, not inferences:**
- *Whole-call row hosting.* PR2260's arithmetic recount (`vendor/pr2260-selective-borrowing-kappa/arithmetic/verify.py`) requires exactly 72 × 436 owner rows, one per (call, helper). Each row has one (owner_replica, child, port) bin and contiguous axes `range(offset, offset+width)`. Roles are therefore whole; "multi-bin repacking" describes the packing solver, not split residuals.
- *No hidden supplier rate in the outer evidence.* `struct_check.py`, `sched_test.py`, `sched_review.py`, `check_gaugefree.py` and `check_fine_padding.py` contain no supplier rate or kappa literal; their checks are structural.
- *The gain comes from the outer, not a looser claim.* PR2260's own `above_balance` control places its 3361/2000000 at the balanced-prefix outer's margin.

- **Original setting.** It was first stated for the T8 selected supplier (b_* = 0.001675462676808…) and then substituted with T74. The substitution needs only:
  - a fixed native dimension and maximum child rank (here m = 73 and r_max = 48, with 73² > 2·48²);
  - restoration of arbitrary dirty helper and spectator coordinates, together with the same normalized global transform at each completed-call boundary. PR2260 checks both native orientations under arbitrary dirty inputs;
  - the retained interfaces (common-frame / mutable-source realization, whole-call row hosting, semantic precision guard with C_1 = 1, fixed-tape compiler). PR2260 states it uses the same retained interfaces, with C_1 = 1.
- **What a larger stock changes.** A larger finite stock W, PR2260's fresh C_0 < 2^210000 and its row coefficient 16467 < 20161 change fixed constants, not powers of p.
- **Ordering.** The ordering q < a_eff < b < a_* also holds here.
