not a tape execution or a general impossibility result.

## Replays and limitations

`VERIFY.json` registers both suites normally and with assertions disabled,
120 seconds each. `verify.py` compares complete deterministic expected JSON;
`--emit` is an explicit regeneration action and is not used by default replay.
Failures throw explicit exceptions; no mathematical gate depends on `assert`.
Frozen predecessor files are not changed or regenerated.

The proof covers valid complete shapes, disjoint source/work/parent segments,
ordinary constructor contracts and named valid-provider returns. Arbitrary
interruption, malformed late output, hidden aliases or a provider violating
its return contract are excluded. Source correctness, local provider proof,
finite algebra, structural controls, cold replay and package integrity remain
distinct evidence types. The selected certificate and κ are unchanged.
