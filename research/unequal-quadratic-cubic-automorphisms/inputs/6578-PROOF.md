The structural input is apoursh's [PR5663](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/5663),

Its intrinsic coefficient field, local-type decomposition and autotopism

are not rechecked. The new step is the cardinality of all nonlinear substitutions.

## Local count

Let L be a finite field of cardinality Q and characteristic p, and let

```text
B = L[x_1,...,x_r]/(x_i^(p^h_i)),   h_i >= 1,
N = p^(sum_i h_i),
r_e = number of i with h_i=e,
k_e = N - p^(sum_j max(h_j-e,0)),
C = sum_i k_(h_i) - sum_e r_e^2.
```

Empty sums and products are allowed, so r=0 gives B=L and C=0.
For t>=0 define

```text
G(Q,t) = product_(a=0)^(t-1) (Q^t-Q^a),    G(Q,0)=1.
```

The exact order is

```text
|Aut_L(B)| = Q^C product_e G(Q,r_e).                         (1)
```

All factors in (1) count choices of images of the ordered generators, so no
division by permutations of equally high generators occurs. Their permutations
are already among the invertible tangent blocks G(Q,r_e).

### 1. Count the power kernel before projecting to the tangent space

Let J=(x_1,...,x_r). Its monomial basis has exponent ranges
0<=a_j<p^h_j. In characteristic p,

```text
(sum_a c_a x^a)^(p^e) = sum_a c_a^(p^e) x^(p^e a).
```

A monomial survives exactly when a_j<p^max(h_j-e,0) for every j.
The surviving exponent tuples remain distinct after multiplication by p^e.
The coefficient power map is a bijection of L. Thus no cancellation between
surviving monomials is possible, and precisely

```text
p^(sum_j max(h_j-e,0))
```

monomials survive. The zero-power locus K_e is an L-subspace of dimension
k_e. This statement concerns the full algebra, even when the power map is
semilinear rather than L-linear. The constant monomial survives, so K_e is
contained in J.

The image of K_e in J/J^2 is exactly the span of the variables of height at
most e. Put s_e=sum_(f<=e) r_f. Every prescribed allowed tangent vector
therefore has exactly Q^(k_e-s_e) lifts to K_e.

### 2. A valid substitution is invertible exactly when its tangent map is

Any choices y_i in K_(h_i) respect every defining relation and determine a
unique unital L-algebra endomorphism x_i -> y_i. Conversely every endomorphism
arises from such a tuple. The tuples are independent choices because these
are precisely the defining relations of B.

An automorphism induces an invertible map on J/J^2. For the converse, suppose
the tangent map is invertible. The graded algebra associated to the J-adic
filtration is generated by J/J^2. Hence the induced map on every graded piece
J^a/J^(a+1) is surjective. Also

```text
J^(1+sum_i(p^h_i-1)) = 0.
```

Successive lifting through this finite filtration makes the endomorphism
surjective on B. Since B has finite dimension, it is bijective. This proves
the equivalence without assuming that an arbitrary invertible tangent map
automatically respects the defining relations. The kernel restrictions above
are still required.

### 3. Count the invertible tangent maps and lift them

Order heights increasingly. The allowed tangent matrix is block triangular,
with diagonal block of size r_e at height e, and an arbitrary block of size
r_e by r_f when f<e. It is invertible exactly when every diagonal block is.
Writing T=sum_(f<e) r_e r_f, its number of choices is

```text
Q^T product_e G(Q,r_e).
```

For a fixed such matrix, the independent higher terms give
Q^(sum_i(k_(h_i)-s_(h_i))) choices. Finally

```text
sum_i s_(h_i) = sum_e r_e^2 + T.
```

Multiplication and cancellation of these powers proves (1). In particular
C is nonnegative: k_e>=s_e, and the uncancelled expression is
T+sum_i(k_(h_i)-s_(h_i)). This finishes the new local count.

## Residue extensions, products and multiplication tensors

Now fix K=F_q and characteristic p. Take a nonempty finite list of distinct types
tau=(d_tau, sorted height list h_tau), and m_tau>=1 copies of

```text
B_tau = F_(q^d_tau)[x_i]/(x_i^(p^h_tau,i)).
```

Let Q_tau=q^d_tau and N_tau=p^(sum_i h_tau,i). Let a_tau be the right
side of (1), evaluated over Q_tau. Put

```text
A = product_tau B_tau^m_tau,
n = sum_tau m_tau d_tau N_tau,
U = product_tau [(Q_tau-1) Q_tau^(N_tau-1)]^m_tau,
S = product_tau [(d_tau a_tau)^m_tau m_tau!].
```

Then the exact cardinalities are

```text
|A^times| = U,
|Aut_K(A)| = S,
|Stab_(GL_n(K)^3)(mu_A)| = U^2 S,
|GL_n(K)^3 . mu_A| = G(q,n)^3 / (U^2 S).                   (2)
```

Here mu_A is the multiplication tensor with the ordinary covariant action
on A* tensor A* tensor A.

The structural facts used for (2) are PR5663's sections 2, 4 and 6. The
coefficient field of B_tau is intrinsic, and

```text
Aut_K(B_tau) = Aut_(F_(q^d_tau))(B_tau) semidirect Gal(F_(q^d_tau)/K).
```

There are exactly d_tau coefficient automorphisms, generated by q-Frobenius.
Only equal full types can be permuted. For each chosen permutation the local
isomorphisms form independent sets of size d_tau a_tau. This gives S, including
the factorial for each repeated full type. Equal K-dimensions alone do not
permit a permutation. The field degree must enter both Q_tau and the factor
d_tau; it is not an additional multiplier in the exponent C of (1).

A local element is a unit exactly when its constant coefficient is nonzero.
There are Q_tau-1 choices for it and Q_tau^(N_tau-1) other coefficients,
giving U. The cited unique autotopism normalization is

```text
F=L_a sigma,  G=L_b sigma,  H=L_(ab) sigma,
a,b in A^times,  sigma in Aut_K(A).
```

It is a bijection, not just a generating description. The corresponding
covariant tensor stabilizer element is (F^(-T),G^(-T),H). Thus its order is
U^2 S. Orbit-stabilizer gives the last line of (2). Independent changes of
the three tensor bases conjugate the stabilizer and preserve these orders.

For example F8[x]/x^2 has 21 automorphisms over F2. F16[x]/x^2 has 30 over
F4, whereas allowing all F2-linear coefficient automorphisms gives 60.
Two copies of the first algebra have 882 automorphisms. Its product with
F64 has 126, despite the two factors having the same F2-dimension six.
These counts are corroborated by the new literal maps, not taken from a
predecessor's receipt. The unequal-height F2 algebra with cutoffs 2 and 8
has 33,554,432 automorphisms by (1) and the independent factorized census.

## What the proof does not establish

Equations (1)-(2) count this supplied algebra family and its tensor orbit.
They do not recognize arbitrary inputs, discover field embeddings, produce
physical native maps, reduce a paid recursive invoice or improve kappa.
They use the cited structural premises and classical finite-field, filtration
and orbit-stabilizer arguments. General literature priority is unclaimed.
Finite executable checks corroborate the proof; independent human and formal
proof-kernel review are unclaimed. All five selected framework assumptions remain.
