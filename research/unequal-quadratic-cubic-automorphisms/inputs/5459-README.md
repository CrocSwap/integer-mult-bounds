# Determinant characters for Frobenius-truncated local algebras

This package extends the promised tensor class in PR5316 to multivariable local
algebras with unequal characteristic-power cutoffs. It derives the full
stabilizer determinant image and the resulting special-linear orbit count.
It is an algebraic interface result. The complete kappa calculation is unchanged.

## The algebra and character formula

Let K=F_q have characteristic p. Choose r>=1 integers e_i>=1, set n_i=p^e_i,
and let

```
A = K[x_1,...,x_r] / (x_i^n_i for every i),
N = product_i n_i,
E_i = N (n_i-1) / 2,
h = gcd(q-1,E_1,...,E_r).
```

Each E_i is an integer. Let mu in A* tensor A* tensor A be multiplication,
with the ordinary covariant action on its three tensor factors. Then

```
Delta(mu) = {(u,v,w) in (K*)^3 : (u v w)^((q-1)/h) = 1}.
```

The image has (q-1)^3/h elements. The one GL orbit splits into exactly h
orbits under the product of the three special linear groups. These statements
hold after independent changes of basis in all three tensor factors.
They assume the tensor belongs to this algebra's GL orbit. They do not
recognize that orbit or produce a transporter.

For equal e_i=1 and odd p, h=(p-1)/2, independent of r and the extension degree
of K. For p=2 and e_i=1, h=1. Unequal or higher cutoffs can change the image:
over F4, the univariate cutoff four has h=3, whereas two cutoff-two variables
have h=1. Both algebras have dimension four, but their determinant images differ.

## Automorphisms must preserve the height flag

Write J for the radical. Every automorphism induces an invertible map L on J/J
squared. If the image of x_i has a nonzero linear x_j term, then e_j<=e_i.
Indeed the p^e_i power of that linear term gives a nonzero x_j^(p^e_i) term
when e_j>e_i. Frobenius removes mixed terms. Terms of degree at least two have
power degree at least 2p^e_i and cannot cancel that term. Thus L preserves the
flag given by the spans of variables of height at most e.

Conversely, every invertible flag-preserving linear substitution respects all
relations: each allowed x_j has n_j dividing n_i. Its inverse preserves the same
flag and relations, so it is an algebra automorphism. More generally, a valid
substitution with invertible tangent map is an automorphism by the finite
radical filtration. Validity of the relations remains a separate condition;
an invertible tangent matrix alone does not authorize forbidden height mixing.

An automorphism and its linear substitution induce the same map on the
associated graded algebra. Ordering monomials by total degree therefore shows
that they have equal determinants. Higher terms do not change the determinant.

## Compute the automorphism and unit characters

A diagonal change x_i -> c x_i has determinant c^E_i. The sum of the x_i
exponents over all N monomials is N(n_i-1)/2. An allowed elementary change
x_i -> x_i+c x_j has determinant one: order monomials by the x_i exponent;
every new term has a smaller exponent, and the diagonal stays one. The
flag-preserving linear group is generated by these elementary changes and
diagonal changes. Within an equal-height block, they generate its full GL group.
If L_e is the tangent block for height e, this proves

```
det(sigma) = product_e det(L_e)^(N(p^e-1)/2).
```

Independent diagonal scalings attain all products of these power subgroups.
Since K* is cyclic, the exact automorphism determinant image is H_h={c^h:c in K*}.
No determinant of a formal untwisted group algebra is used.

For a unit a, multiplication by a is triangular on the radical filtration
with constant diagonal a_0. Thus det(L_a)=a_0^N. Since N is a power of p,
N is coprime to q-1, so these unit determinants fill K*.

The classical unital commutative autotopism normalization, retained in PR5316
and PR5252, writes every F(x)G(y)=H(xy) as

```
F=L_a sigma, G=L_b sigma, H=L_(ab) sigma.
```

For completeness, evaluate at 1 to get H(x)=F(x)b=aG(x), where a=F(1), b=G(1).
Bijectivity makes both multiplication maps bijective, hence a,b are units.
Then sigma=(ab)^-1 H is an algebra automorphism. The converse follows by
multiplication. The covariant tensor action uses (F^-T,G^-T,H). If x,y are the
unit determinants and z the automorphism determinant, its ordered triple is

```
(u,v,w) = (x^-1 z^-1, y^-1 z^-1, x y z).
```

The inverse equations x=vw, y=uw, z=(uvw)^-1 prove the displayed image formula.
Conjugation preserves determinants under independent basis changes. Finally,
the product SL group is the kernel of the determinant map from the product GL
group; its orbits in the fixed GL orbit are the cosets of Delta(mu). This proves
the image size and orbit count.

The promised character equation can be computed without constructing A. To
compute E_i modulo m=q-1, multiply p^(sum e_i) and p^e_i-1 modulo 2m, then
halve the even residue. Use modular powers and gcd. This uses polynomially
many bit operations in the supplied parameter sizes and avoids constructing
the potentially huge integer N. The characteristic and algebra-class promise
are inputs; this calculation does not factor q or recognize a tensor.

## Evidence and reproduction

```sh
python3.12 -B scripts/research_package.py --package research/frobenius-tensor-characters
```

The standalone Python 3.12 checker uses its standard library. It implements F4
as F2[t]/(t squared+t+1), as well as prime fields. It builds literal quotient
multiplication tables and substitution matrices, checks all defining power
relations, compares ambient determinants with the tangent-block formula, and
checks basis products. Three complete automorphism domains include nonlinear
substitutions and unequal heights. Additional bounded domains check elementary
linear and nonlinear maps through algebra dimension 49. Unit multiplication
matrices and the full determinant-triple sets are checked independently.

Controls distinguish forbidden height mixing, equal dimensions with different
images, the inverse-input convention, and modular exponent extraction. Normal
and optimized checks use explicit exceptions. The written proof supplies the
general finite-field theorem; finite matrix checks are corroboration, not
proof-kernel verification. No predecessor executable is imported.
