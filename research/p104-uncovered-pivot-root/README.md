# Uncovered-pivot root: explicit identity and transport obstruction

**No new kappa.** The minimal singleton-host uncovered-pivot extension of
pending #6868 has a valid arbitrary-input root at every even L, but its best
complete optimistic L18 invoice has raw crossing in
**(.0033334095468, .0033334095470)**, below pending #6833's .00337180.
This excludes an improvement within the stated whole-field idle contract and
unchanged native profile, including carriers transported by the literal queries.
The optimistic idle cover need not be physically attainable; its unfavorable
price is already sufficient for this scoped exclusion.

A separate finite extension also excludes 90 explicit opposite-arm mask
enlargements. Its largest complete margin upper bound at kappa=.00337180 is
**-0.210890121535325573**, and a uniform negative derivative excludes re-entry
through .01. This is a raw caller exclusion; it supplies no improved BIT stock.

The contribution combines an explicit integer-coordinate root, a no-cross-side
transport theorem, a universal idle-cover majorization bound and independent
complete accounting. The root has one thin query and exactly L independent
fields. No nonzero native-neutral source line can reach the other arm's mask
through all intervening literal queries. Data-independent ordinary frames (or
a nondegenerate specialization at D=1) are required for that implication.

The field proxy in #6868 counts one too many idle dimensions at the uncovered
pivot. Its L18 rank surplus is 297; the complete rank-two-stage root must have
surplus 305. The corrected majorization relaxation removes that phantom paid
incidence and proves balanced arms 8|9 best for every concave power moment.
All native helpers/requests, main targets, thin queries and required idle
incidences are included. The source's .43858 proxy saving at L6 is not constant
in this minimal family: at L18 the proxy itself loses 8.4803785672596... per port.
