## Exact spectra and all tensor widths

For signed independent axes with coefficients `epsilon_j = +/-1`,
the residue multiplicity is
```text
N_k = 2^(r-2) + (1/2) Re(i^(-k) product_j(1 + i^(epsilon_j))).
```
This follows by the four-root filter. The negative root's product
vanishes. For odd `r` the real and imaginary parts have equal
magnitude `2^{(r-1)/2}`. For even `r` exactly one part is zero,
and the other has magnitude `2^{r/2}`. The four fractions differ
from `1/4` by at most `delta_r = 2^(-(r+3)/2)` for odd `r` or
`2^{-(r+2)/2}` for even `r`. The two residue parity classes
have equal mass. For the literal pair at width one the fractions
come from `N_9=(136,120,120,136)` and
`N_{64}=(2^{62}+2^{31},2^{62},2^{62}-2^{31},2^{62})`.

`64f` stays even, the same bounds hold uniformly. The
all-width assertion covers repeated phase cores. Other compiler
representations require a separate argument.
