# Sources and credit

Ali Romman (@aliromman) prepared the new reconstruction algorithm, support certificates, two-register example, implementation and campaign synthesis with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. New material is Apache-2.0. Separate Codex reviewers attacked the cut and phase arguments, implemented independent character, Pauli and dense-minor computations and tested the executable. Their checks do not constitute independent human review or formal proof-kernel acceptance.
