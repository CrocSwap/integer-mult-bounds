## Discharge a uniform tape or precision assumption

Results 13–16 construct scalar tables, a guide and a paid bank reset. Results 61–63 and 67 deliver a canonical rotation bit or row with restored heads. Results 70–71, 74, 85 and 91 supply an exact finite-window sampler. Results 93–94 identify a formal graph and invoice. A compiled child would have to produce the descriptors, share the same owner map across recursion, service the numeric operation and return every head and private cell. A gate on a resident coin cannot pay for bringing the coin to the head. The checkpointed sampler still has a censored branch. The 86N hidden-bit lower bound in result 10 applies to the pinned same-grid stochastic map at fixed seed, whereas the selected caller uses exact common-grid service.

A complete proof on the selected implementation could remove an inherited assumption even at the same κ. The optimistic local bills include 468 shared-guide resets on one fixed tuple, at most 1280N(h+4) transitions for one rotation row, and 1,375 clean bits plus 64 resident coins for one length-64 window. They are in different models. A complete finite upper invoice for the proposed recursive composition is unavailable. Its decisive test is a physical child call that consumes the actual parent tape layout and restores the same tuple, including its history. None of the three input contracts supplies that map. ## Recover every unknown Clifford term from a compressed observation

Results 79 and 82–84 identify an exact, conditioned four-qubit dictionary of 12,128,668,876,800 projective classes. Results 95–96 give sharper constants in two width classes. Result 99 concerns odd division in a canonical linear inverse. Subsequent work gives a dyadic left inverse at width 13, a compiled row for a named fibre, an identity-row contraction on a compact affine-quadratic input and two sharp Gram-pass counts. Five-qubit work now also proves its dyadic threshold is 15. These are substantive predecessor results. None of these particular input/output contracts lists every unknown nonzero label of an arbitrary exact tensor train. The submission refresh added an important comparison. On a compact affine-quadratic observation, apoursh now returns all 256 coefficients of the identity symplectic fibre with one Gauss sum in at most 48 variables. That algorithm also defines a linear output on arbitrary off-span observations. Its coupled phase-mask producer takes 104 probes and 206 adjacent moves. The decoder here instead accepts general Gaussian-rational train cores, finds the occupied fibres itself and tests membership. These access models have different complete input costs. [Identity-fibre proof](https://github.com/apoursh/integer-mult-bounds/blob/65573785774c6bbac12485a4d90cd82778d25e36/research/clifford-compact-pauli-fibre/PROOF.md) · [PR 7813](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/7813)

Choose support recovery. The matching contracts are exact Gaussian-rational tensor entries, distinct projective labels and two independent flanks of tensor powers. The missing implication is a way to identify unlisted labels through small cut matrices, including when individual slices vanish, phases cancel or a train has redundant states. Test it first on a signed parity observation that has a zero all-zero entry and unknown labels from different symplectic fibres. A supplied list, a single named row, or a comparison only on sampled entries would fail this test. An exact full-tensor residual must detect an unmodelled term.

The cost target is deterministic polynomial work in the train description, its bond dimensions and the physical alphabet, with output storage for the labels actually recovered. Global rank at width 13 supplies two independent flanks at width 27. Shorter widths can use the support-size bound in the proof. The compressed-input assumption changes the access model. A dense producer may still have to read every entry, noisy data require a different identification method, and no multiplication caller supplies this train for free. The five selected assumptions still apply. This construction contributes zero change to the two supplier budgets and the complete κ.
