# Joint rank-one phase schedule: design and acceptance

The completed sibling package `kappa-round7-4610-scalar-contract-20261022`
rules out every one-physical-edge phase defect on the declared fixed graph.
The present scope allows multiple edges to change rank together. It is a new
bounded experiment, with no positive result assumed.

**Research decision after source review:** the two cheap unrestricted-form gates
were completed and found no helper gain. Newly opened PR4696 supplies a general
owner-wise paid-phase rank-debt theorem, and PR4690 already screens the 511 common
rank-one forms with fixed interfaces. Exact upstream replay and an independent
actual-path audit confirm that removing inert marker freezes does not escape
the debt hypotheses. The planned 511 paid-phase scan is therefore not run.
Remaining work in this scope is preservation/audit, not a duplicate search.

## Construction family

First run an unrestricted homogeneous symmetric-form gate on the actual graph
with main/owned boundaries fixed, then with only main boundaries fixed and owned
source phases optimistically released. Enforce unchanged ranks at every paid
edge and native warp and query every helper form difference. Adapter markers
are inert descriptors; their textual positions are not additional chart freezes.
A useful free direction takes priority over the 511-covector ansatz.

Use the actual PR4610 round-three word, its scalar contact components, unchanged
subspaces and shared native G maps. For each nonzero ambient covector
`ell in F2^9`, choose a binary switch `s_v` at every contact component and form
`b_v=s_v*(ell|U_v) tensor (ell|U_v)`. Main and owned boundaries remain zero for
the first cut family. Scalar operands share their actual common chart. Actual
endpoint adapters retain their contract, while inert descriptor positions do
not impose fictitious intermediate gauge conditions.

This is the fixed-ambient rank-one mincut grammar of Dugongue's PR4654, explicitly
credited as prior work. The contribution sought is a complete, source-bound
global enumeration of 511 covectors on the current word with actual costs and
verified witnesses, rather than proposing that grammar anew.

On a nested paid edge, charge its old rank plus the rank of the restricted form
difference. On a formerly monomial warp, transport the source covector by the
actual shared G and charge its exact rank 0, 1 or 2. Include every original
owned center and paid call. A helper's reward uses its true entrance/end form
difference, not a changed chart dimension alone.

## Optimization and modes

First run two separately valid modes: all helper entrances fixed and helper
exits variable, and all helper exits fixed with entrances variable. Rewards are
unary in these modes. Paid/warp binary tables must be checked submodular before
using a cut solver. Enumerate all 511 covectors. Discovery uses rational or
integer approximations; exact outward-rational moments decide any emitted gain.

Allowing both helper endpoints to vary introduces an XOR reward and may break
submodularity; it requires an explicit signed-energy/MILP treatment and cannot
inherit the unary cut proof. Coordinate the graph loader with the geometry
agent and retain its exact source/model pins.

## Acceptance

1. Reconstruct the original paid/productive profiles, fixed boundaries and graph
   multiplicities before searching. Check every four-entry edge rank table
   against direct binary matrix ranks, including transport orientation.
2. Validate the cut reduction against exhaustive tiny graphs and explicit
   negative/submodularity controls. The all-zero schedule is a zero-gain control.
3. For every candidate, directly recompute all edge/helper ranks and the complete
   invoice from its switches. Certify strict gain by independent rational
   intervals. A solver objective or approximate optimality is not admission.
4. A positive invoice must then emit literal columnwise Clifford phases and
   whole-job normalizers, replay all arbitrary main/helper inputs and owned
   cleanup, and pass compiler/outer/Lean gates before any kappa claim.
