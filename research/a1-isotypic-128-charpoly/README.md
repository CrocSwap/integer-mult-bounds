# Characteristic polynomial of one 128-dimensional isotypic component

Prepared by unpaidmoon with substantial Cursor Grok assistance. Apache-2.0.

For the historical 8-by-112 centre query, let `E` be the 216-dimensional
joint `(+1,+1)` space of the `c=1` sign stabilizer and the involutions `g`
and `t`. Let `G` be the order-12 dihedral image generated by the signed
permutations `r` and `u` inside that space. The rational character with
values `2, 1, -1, -2, -1, 1` on `r^k` and value 0 on the reflections cuts
out a 128-dimensional `A_1`-invariant subspace `W`. Its monic characteristic
polynomial is the square recorded in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md).

The selected conditional kappa stays `781451/250000000`.

## Result

`B = 4 A_1` preserves `W`. On a Hermite `Z`-basis of the column lattice of
the integer projector `6P`,

```text
chi_{A_1, W}(x) = q(x)^2,
q(x) = x^6 (x-2)^4 (x+2)^4 f(x) g(x) h(x) k(x),
```

where `f` and `g` are the published degree-11 pair and `h` and `k` are the
published degree-14 pair that already appears squared in the degree-216
factorization. `g(x) = -f(-x)` and `k(x) = h(-x)`. The four higher factors
are irreducible over `Z`. Ascending-coefficient SHA256 of `chi_{A_1, W}` is

```text
bf10fad08aa427ff461bc3a7a7377f5b90ab7a810a1004d81e550a2a328bbb65
```

Eigenvalue 0 has algebraic multiplicity 12 on `W`. The multiplicity-one
factors of the published degree-216 polynomial do not divide this
characteristic polynomial.

From the repository root, with CPython 3.12 and the standard library:

```sh
python3 -B -W error research/a1-isotypic-128-charpoly/verify.py
python3 -B -O research/a1-isotypic-128-charpoly/verify.py
python3 -B scripts/research_package.py --package research/a1-isotypic-128-charpoly --check-only
```

checks, so `-O` does not skip a theorem or a rejection control. No Lean,
