This note assembles the conditional witness
\[
 T(n)=O\!\left(n(\log n)^{1-\kappa}\right),\qquad \kappa=\frac{13086957581}{3125\cdot10^{12}}\approx4.1878\cdot10^{-6}>2^{-18},
\]
from four components. It points to where each one is proved or checked.

\section{Components}

\paragraph{The stack.} Ideas A--E (\texttt{stack-notes.tex}, \texttt{segmented-inverse.tex},
\texttt{fine-field-lemma.tex}) drive $\epsilon$ toward $1$. The binding row is then the simultaneous
butterflies, $\epsilon(1-\lambda')$, with $\lambda'>\max(\tau,\sigma,\chi,\text{leaf})$. The reservations force
$\epsilon<1/(2-\lambda')$, so the supremum of $\kappa$ is $\min(a_b,(1-\beta)a_c)/(1+\cdot)$.

\paragraph{Bit side: the batched two-stage interchange.} This is the two-stage bit motif with partial-swap
frames, with the batched recursion extended to partial-swap frames (\texttt{partial-swap-batching.tex},
its Lemma~1). At $h=32$, $m=1024$, it certifies $a_b=22157/(5\cdot10^9)\approx4.43\cdot10^{-6}$.
This side does not bind.

\paragraph{Complex side: PR~\#7's network, fully batched.} PR~\#7's complex network at $h=28$
($m=21952$), evaluated under PR~\#15's full batching: every nonzero residual edge, of every rank, is one
whole-residual child call. We rebuilt the residual histogram from our own producer, labels and role
compilation (\texttt{independent/complex-network/fullbatch\_hist.py}) and certified the saving by exact
bisection on the moment equation with certified upper bounds on $\ln$
(\texttt{fullbatch\_cert.py}). This gives $a_c\ge1048009/(2.5\cdot10^{11})\approx4.192\cdot10^{-6}$ (PR~\#15 reports
$4191487/10^{12}$ from its own labels), and this side binds.

\paragraph{Guard.} The coefficient-depth guard needs no path-topology assumption. Every child call of the
batched recursion acts on at most $(m-1)/m$ of its parent's axes, and a node has at most $s_c$ children in
sequence. So the dependency depth is at most $d^{C_1}$ with $C_1=1+m_c\ln s_c$, and Idea~D takes $x$ with
$\epsilon C_1<1+x$ (\texttt{scripts/certificate\_round3.py}, guard mode \texttt{crude}). Any finite $C_1$
gives the same $\kappa$, since $x$ does not enter it; with $x\approx8.4\cdot10^5$ the packed-product and
record-regime rows hold for sufficiently large $L$. The witness is stated with this guard only.

\section{Certificate}

\texttt{scripts/certificate\_round3.py} evaluates the rows and constraints exactly for any pair $(a_b,a_c)$.
With $a_b=22157/(5\cdot10^9)$, $a_c=1048009/(2.5\cdot10^{11})$, $\beta=1/1000$ and the crude guard:
\begin{itemize}
\item every constraint is strict;
\item the binding row is the simultaneous butterflies;
\item $\kappa=13086957581/(3.125\cdot10^{15})$.
\end{itemize}
\texttt{independent/complex-network/fullbatch\_hist.py 28} followed by \texttt{fullbatch\_cert.py}
recomputes $a_c$ and $\kappa$. Our histogram differs from PR~\#15's in the smallest ranks, with the same total
$s_c$, and certifies at least its value.
