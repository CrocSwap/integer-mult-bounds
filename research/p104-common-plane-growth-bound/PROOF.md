# Common-plane closure with fixed endpoints

Work over the nine-dimensional binary chart space of the exact word. Cocharted ADD/COPY contacts connect segments into groups. A group is eligible only if every segment belongs to a public helper and is interior, with its initial and terminal endpoints untouched. The forward inventory and the reverse segment index both produce 482 eligible groups and 2,292 incident paid MOVEs. MOVE edges join strictly nested old chart spaces. No productive endpoint price changes.

Fix a plane P. Each eligible chart A may stay at A or grow to A+P. Write a for the dimension increase dim(A+P)−dim(A), which is 0, 1 or 2. On an old MOVE A⊂B of rank d, the corresponding upper increase b satisfies b≤a. If the lower chart grows and P is already in B, the new rank is d−a. If the lower grows while B does not contain P, the upper must grow too. If both grow the new rank is d+b−a. If only the upper grows it is d+b. A lower grow with a fixed upper lacking P is forbidden.

At any κ in the pinned old crossing bracket, use outward-rounded upper bounds for H(d)−H(d+b−a) and lower bounds for H(d+b)−H(d), with H(r)=r^(1−κ), H(0)=0. The independent cut puts a source arc to the lower vertex for the upper bound on the valid lower-grow reward, an upper-to-lower arc for the lower bound on the upper-only penalty, and a hard lower-to-upper implication when b>0. A fixed upper lacking P sends the hard implication to the sink. If the lower is fixed, the upper-only penalty is an upper-to-sink arc. Each hard capacity exceeds all offered source reward. Maximizing the bounded saving is equivalent to minimizing this cut. This relaxation charges no extra realization cost and allows rank zero at zero price, so its zero upper excludes every corresponding paid improvement in the tested domain.

The lead obtains equivalent directed vertex weights and solves maximum closure. The independent engine rebuilds segments backward from the literal tape and uses direct edge capacities with shortest augmenting paths. Both rank the source directions by the sum of the available one-rank source-side relief over the old edges. The first four by score and then by direction ID are 365, 244, 327 and 371. The sample consists exactly of all distinct planes through at least one of those directions. There are 511·510/6=43,435 total binary planes. The sample contains 1,014, leaving 42,421 untested. On each of the 1,014 planes, exact integer flow saturates the offered source reward in the independent calculation, giving zero upper saving. No literal changed word is built.
