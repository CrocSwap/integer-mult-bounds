# Validation and limits

The all-field statement is the written criterion and constructive proof in
PROOF.md. Finite enumeration corroborates it; it does not establish the
arbitrary-field theorem or implement a general completion constructor.
Separate AI reviews checked the exact sequence, the coupled lifting condition,
the quotient capacity extension and the final gluing argument. No independent
human review or proof-assistant acceptance is claimed.

Two separately written standard-library engines agree on 280,228 normalized
capacity decisions, of which 62,153 are feasible. The domains are F2 dimensions
0,1,2,3, and F3 and F5 dimension 2, with every partial-identity rank and every
ordered quadruple of subspaces. Independent domain/codomain basis changes
normalize every prescribed injection; these counts are not counts of all
labelled injections. All 16 rank streams agree in ordered membership masks,
counts, raw 0/1 SHA256 streams and their six domain concatenations.

`verify.py` uses RREF, nullspaces and the seven criterion checks. Seven actual
single-check omission counterexamples are rejected by complete GL enumeration;
five invalid-domain controls and named oblique and nonzero-delta fixtures pass.
`oracle.py` instead builds literal vector-set subspaces and enumerates actual
invertible maps extending the prescribed columns. It uses no candidate
criterion and imports neither primary nor predecessor code. Its first source
and receipt were sealed before reading primary results. Its later changes only
align schema names, add the concatenated digest of the same bits, and compare
the common primary census projection. The first receipt is preserved externally.
No scientific execution failed. Root's initial agreement harness compared whole
rank dictionaries despite oracle-only extra fields; the corrected projection
agrees without changing computed values. That harness history is retained.

Both final engines pass normal and optimized emissions and strict retained
receipt checks with warnings treated as errors. Emissions are byte-identical
between modes. Source objects authenticate before use; retained JSON rejects
duplicate keys, nonfinite numbers and boolean schema versions. The named
three-dimensional obstruction has 4,18,100 prescribed extensions over q=2,3,5,
2,6,20 extensions satisfying either individual capacity, and zero joint
extensions. Only the oracle enumerates the q=5 three-dimensional named fixture;
the full F5 dimension-3 capacity census is not claimed.
