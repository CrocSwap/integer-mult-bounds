# Consecutive paired-star schedules need total thin rank at least `g q`

This is a proof in characteristic two under the hypotheses below. It is not a
search, not an exhaustive census of words, and not a heuristic. It does not
say that the profile `g=31`, `q=26` is impossible for every call order.

No new kappa is claimed. The selected frontier stays
`781451/250000000 = 0.003125804`.

## 1. Contract

Use the paired-star contract recorded in
`research/coupled31-chronology/PROOF.md` at base
`7c49fe32c55de02c2c873655c804b16e4c3fef0a`. The core and each of the `g`
leaves have dimension `d`. Stage `j` has two orthogonal projectors resolving
the core-plus-leaf support. In the core/leaf blocks their legal form is

```text
P = [[A+B, B], [B, A+B]],    R = [[A, A], [A, A]],
```

with `A^2=A` and `AB+BA=B`. The complementary half uses `A' = I+A` and the
same `B`. A full call is `(I+x P)(I+e R)` with `x = D+1` and `e^2=0`. Thin
calls are `I+e Z` of any constant matrix `Z`. The variables `D_j` are
independent. The target is `(I+e I)` times `product_j (I+x_j E_j)`, where
`E_j` is the identity on the core and on leaf `j`.

This note specializes to two equal halves, so `d=2q`, and to thin maps of
rank at most `q`. Square-zero thin maps are not assumed. The first-applied
factor is the rightmost factor.

## 2. Theorem

Assume every stage's two full calls are adjacent, and no thin map sits
between them. Let `R` be the sum of the thin-map ranks. Then a word that
meets the endpoint satisfies

```text
R >= q g.
```

For `g=31` and `q=26`, five thin maps have total rank at most `130`, while
the bound is `806`. No such word meets the endpoint. The projectors may
fail to commute, and `B` may be nonzero. The same bound holds when they do
commute; that is a different hypothesis from the unrestricted-rank
commuting-projector theorem in `COMMUTING.md`.

## 3. Local stage factor

Work in the algebra generated by `A` and `B` modulo `A^2=A` and `AB+BA=B`,
with coefficients in characteristic two. Both products of the complementary
halves satisfy

```text
(I+x P)(I+x P') = (1+x) I
```

on the core/leaf blocks, and the coefficient of `e` in that product is
`(1+x) T` with `T = [[I,I],[I,I]]`. The off-diagonal block `B` cancels.
Thus each completed stage, with nothing but the pure identity between its
halves, may be replaced by the scalar block factor

```text
G = D * diag(I, I),    H = D * T
```

on its own core and leaf, and by the identity on every other leaf. These
factors are diagonal in the leaf decomposition. They commute.

The verifier reduces words by `AA -> A` and `BA -> B+AB` and checks both
orders, projector idempotence, and the relation itself. Dropping the
rewrite `BA -> B+AB` makes the pure product fail. That control is part of
the registered replay.

## 4. Full-call contribution

Multiply the scalar stage factors from the right. The pure product is

```text
diag(product_k D_k, D_0, D_1, ..., D_{g-1}).
```

That is the pure part of the target. The coefficient of `e` has, on the
core-to-leaf-`j` block, exactly the monomial

```text
M_j = product_{k>=j} D_k
```

times `I`. The leaf-`j` diagonal is `D_j I`. The leaf-to-core block is
`product_{k<=j} D_k` times `I`. For odd `g`, the core diagonal is the full
product; for even `g` it is zero. Leaf-to-leaf blocks stay zero.

The endpoint requires the off-diagonal blocks of the `e` coefficient to be
zero. The full calls already contribute `M_j I` on core-to-leaf `j`, so the
thin maps must contribute the same matrix.

## 5. Thin maps between completed stages

A thin map in the gap after `t` completed stages, and before the remaining
stages, is conjugated by those scalar factors. If its core-to-leaf-`j`
block is a constant matrix `Z_{c,j}`, that block is multiplied by the
monomial

```text
mu(t,j) = (product_{k>=t} D_k) * (D_j if j<t else 1).
```

No other block of `Z` enters this entry: the pure prefix and suffix are
diagonal. Because `Z` is constant and `mu` is square-free, the matrix
coefficient of `mu` is exactly `Z_{c,j}`.

The monomials `mu(t,j)` and `mu(t',j)` agree if and only if `{t,t'} = {j,j+1}`,
in which case both equal `M_j`. Every other gap has a private monomial.
Its coefficient must vanish, so `Z_{c,j}=0` at every nonadjacent gap.
The adjacent gaps therefore satisfy

```text
Z^{(j)}_{c,j} + Z^{(j+1)}_{c,j} = I_d.
```

Several thin maps in one gap add before conjugation, because `e^2=0` and
the pure factor between them is `I`. Let `r_t` be the sum of the ranks of
the maps in gap `t`. A block of a matrix has rank at most the rank of the
matrix, so each block above has rank at most `r_t`. Rank is subadditive, and
`I_d` has rank `d=2q`, hence

```text
r_j + r_{j+1} >= 2q    for every leaf j.
```

Sum over the `g` leaves. Gap `0` and gap `g` each appear once, and every
intermediate gap appears twice, so the left side is at most `2 R`. Therefore
`R >= q g`.

The verifier checks the scalar transport and the private-monomial claim for
every gap and every leaf at `g=31`, and the same identities at `g=1,2,3,4`.
Those smaller leaf counts are the same formal identity. They are not a census
of projectors and they do not transfer a finite-field search from another
profile. It also checks the incidence identity on every basis vector of the
gap ranks, and it checks `rank(I_52)=52` and `rank` of an explicit rank-26
matrix over `F_2`.

## 6. The profile

`g=31`, `q=26` gives `R >= 806`. Five maps of rank 26 give `R <= 130`.
The inequality is strict, so this schedule family has no endpoint.

The known commuting construction uses `ceil(g/2)` maps of rank `4q`, total
rank `1664` at these parameters. That total sits above `806`, so this bound
does not contradict that construction and does not improve its map count.
`COMMUTING.md` already excludes five maps of unrestricted rank when every
full projector commutes, for every order. The new statement is the rank
bound for consecutive halves when the projectors need not commute.

Passing the older chronological floor `5q >= 4q+20` is unchanged: `q=26`
still passes that floor. The obstruction here is the consecutive schedule,
not that floor.

## 7. What this does not prove

- A thin map may still sit between the two halves of a stage. The prefix is
  then no longer scalar on that stage, and Section 5 does not apply.
- The two calls of a stage may be separated, nested, or crossed by another
  stage. The local replacement by `D I` and `D T` uses adjacency.
- No witness for `g=31`, `q=26` is constructed on any other schedule.
- No exhaustive or sampled search of call orders, field elements, or
  projectors at `q=26` was run. Absence of such a search is not
  nonexistence.
- The `g=3` finite censuses in open PRs #6922 and #6940 are a different
  profile. They are not used, and they do not lift.
- The same algebra applies at every `g` and `q`. This package certifies the
  `g=31`, `q=26` consequence only. It states no `g=13`, `q=8` result.
- Native transport, frames, and the selected frontier are untouched.
