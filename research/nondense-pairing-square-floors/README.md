# Non-dense fitting B: per-term rank ≥ 9 for every E8 term with x·y ≠ 0, and the square root-code floors E8 ≥ 72, P112 ≥ 64, P104 ≥ 56, D4 ≥ 12 for any fitting B (no κ claim)

> **Scope, stated first.** A research note in the line of `research/ideal-decoder-floors/` (Theorem 4, public: the floor T·r_min for the *dense* pairing), #968, #1744, #2176 and #2338 (Theorems P, R). It removes "dense" from the floor in the square case. **No κ claim, no window, no supplier word, no decoder below any known floor.** Only `research/nondense-pairing-square-floors/` is added.
> Labels: **PROVEN** (written proof here, with the `verify.sh` step for each numerical input), **CHECKED** (an exact script that `verify.sh` re-runs and asserts, DRAT proofs replayed with the vendored drat-trim), **CONDITIONAL**, **OPEN**. Every claim is in `PROOF_MAP.md` with the command that checks it.
> Overlap, honestly: #1744 already gives rank ≥ 9 for any fitting B on P111/P112 in its scope. What is added here is the *per-term* statement on the whole E8 root unit via root-spanned hyperplane pairs, the proof structure (Lemmas 6.1-6.3), the P104 and D4 corollaries, two independent implementations, and the explicit list of where the per-term claim is false.

## 0. Setting
Unit: `v` root lines `t` (E8: 120). A decoder `(T, P, Q, B)` has `P = D₁RX`, `Q = D₂RY` (root-code factors: columns `x_j, y_j` of `X, Y`, `R` the root matrix), `R(x) = {t : r_t·x ≠ 0}`, `R_j = R(x_j)`, `A_j = R(y_j)`, `D_j = R_j ∩ A_j`. `B ∈ F2^{v×v}` is **fitting** if `B_tt = 1` and `B_ts = 0` whenever `r_t·r_s ≠ 0` (t ≠ s); entries on orthogonal pairs are free (the dense pairing sets them to 1). cost = Σ_j rank_{F2} B[R_j, A_j]. For `D ⊆ lines`, `G⊥[D]` is the orthogonality graph and `mr₂(G⊥[D])` the minimum F2 rank of a fitting matrix on `D` (fitting minrank).

## 1. Lemmas 6.1-6.3 (PROVEN)
**Lemma 6.1 (per-term reduction).** `min_{fitting B} rank B[R, A] = mr₂(G⊥[R ∩ A])`.
*Proof.* (≥) `B[D,D]`, `D = R∩A`, is a submatrix of `B[R,A]` and is fitting on `D`. (≤) Every entry of `B[R,A]` outside `D × D` sits at `(t,s)` with `t ≠ s` (if `t = s` then `t ∈ R ∩ A = D`), so it is forced to 0 or free; set all of them to 0 and put an optimal fitting matrix on `D × D`: the block is `B_D ⊕ 0`-padded, of rank `rank B_D`. ∎ (Per term only: a decoder shares one `B` across all terms.)

**Lemma 6.2 (normal-pair reduction).** If `x·y ≠ 0` then `R(x) ∩ R(y) ⊇ R(n₁) ∩ R(n₂)` for root-spanned hyperplanes `n₁^⊥ ⊇ F_x := x^⊥ ∩ Φ`, `n₂^⊥ ⊇ F_y` with `n₁·n₂ ≠ 0`.
*Proof.* `S = span F_x`. Extend the images of the roots in `V/S` to a basis of `V/S` consisting of roots; deleting one basis root at a time gives hyperplanes `S + span(b_l, l ≠ i)`, each spanned by roots, with intersection `S`; their normals span `S^⊥`. Likewise `S' = span F_y`. Now `x ∈ S^⊥`, `y ∈ S'^⊥` and `x·y ≠ 0`, so the two normal sets are not mutually orthogonal: some `n₁·n₂ ≠ 0`. Since `F_x ⊆ n₁^⊥`, every root non-orthogonal to `n₁` is non-orthogonal to `x`: `R(x) ⊇ R(n₁)`; same for `y`. ∎

**Lemma 6.3 (orthogonal-set deletion).** If `S ⊆ D` is pairwise orthogonal then `mr₂(D \ S) ≥ mr₂(D) − 1`.
*Proof.* `S` is a clique of `G⊥`, so the all-ones `J_S` is fitting on `S`. If `B'` is fitting on `D \ S` then `B' ⊕ J_S` (zero cross blocks, allowed since cross entries of distinct lines are forced-0-or-free and 0 is admissible) is fitting on `D` with rank `rank B' + 1`. ∎ (Per pairwise-orthogonal set, not per line.)

## 2. Theorem 6 (CHECKED, kissat + drat-trim)
**E8.** Let `n₁` range over the 8 W-orbits of fundamental coweights ω_j (together the 440 880 root-spanned hyperplanes), and `n₂` over the orbits of `Stab(ω_j) = W_J` (Steinberg: the parabolic generated by the simple reflections ≠ j) on hyperplane normals with `n₁·n₂ ≠ 0`. There are **4022** such types (76 + 214 + 485 + 1610 + 1009 + 454 + 145 + 29; 318 orthogonal types are not used), with `|D| = |R(n₁) ∩ R(n₂)| ≥ 30`. For each, the CNF "a fitting matrix of F2-rank ≤ 8 exists on `G⊥[D]`" is **UNSAT** with a drat-trim VERIFIED proof. (The CNF is normalised by a pairwise non-orthogonal 8-set `S ⊆ D`: fitting forces `B[S,S] = I`, so after a change of basis `U_S = V_S = I`, and `U_t[k] = B[t,S_k]`, `V_s[k] = B[S_k,s]`; non-orthogonal entries are 0, orthogonal ones free, diagonal 1, with Tseitin AND/XOR clauses. A missing 8-clique would be reported `noS`, counted as a failure.) Using the `W_J`-orbit rather than the orbit of the *pair* is finer, hence conservative.
**Consequence (6.1 + 6.2 + monotonicity of mr₂ under inclusion of D).** Every E8 term with `x·y ≠ 0` has rank ≥ 9 for every fitting B. (Monotonicity: `D ⊇ D₀` gives `mr₂(D) ≥ mr₂(D₀)`, a principal submatrix.)
Hyperplane count: `Σ_J |W|/|W_J| / 2 = 440 880` (stabiliser orders 2160 + 17280 + 69120 + 483840 + 241920 + 60480 + 6720 + 240 = 881 760, halved).
**Checks.** Two implementations of the orbit enumeration (`checks/orbits_a.py`: the IDEAL code, hyperplane normals as W-orbits of coweights, union-find over 7 simple reflections; `checks/orbits_b.py`: the finisher's code, ambient integer coordinates, all 120 reflections, scipy connected components) and two CNF writers (`checks/satA.py`; `checks/satB.py`, chunked direct XOR, own clique routine and SAT-model decoder); `compare_orbits.py` asserts identical per-type invariants; `brute_agree.py` asserts exact agreement of exhaustive minrank over all fitting B with both writers on 60 instances.

## 3. Corollary 6.4: square root-code decoders, any fitting B (PROVEN given Theorem 6; sharp)
Square means `m = T` and `X Yᵀ = I`, so `y_j·x_j = 1 ≠ 0` for every `j`. Hence every term has rank ≥ 9 on E8; on a live set obtained by deleting lines the same bound holds with `D` replaced by `D` minus the deleted lines, and Lemma 6.3 removes at most 1 per pairwise-orthogonal deleted set:
* **E8 ≥ 72** = 8·9. **P112 ≥ 64** (E8 minus one 8-frame: ≥ 8 per term) and **P104 ≥ 56** (E8 minus two disjoint orthogonal 8-frames: ≥ 7 per term); T ≥ 8 terms are needed (place bound of `ideal-decoder-floors`, Theorem 1). *Scope:* this proves the floor `T·r_min`; it does **not** say each live term attains its own dense value.
* **D4 ≥ 12**: all 24 root-spanned hyperplane normals of D4, all non-orthogonal pairs: 192 distinct `D`-sets, each with `mr₂ ≥ 3` (brute force over all fitting B on 180, SAT at rank 2 UNSAT with DRAT on 12), 4 terms × 3.
* **Sharp** (exact decoders, `checks/decoders.py`): E8 coordinate decoder `X = Y = I`, Gram exact, term ranks 9×8 = 72; P112: `x_j = y_j` = the 8 lines of the removed frame, `D Tᵀ = 2G` exact on the 112 live lines, ranks 8×8 = 64; P104: the frame-pair decoder of #968 rebuilt (`build56` part of `decoders.py`), `D Tᵀ = 2G` exact on the 104 live lines, 8 terms of rank 7 = 56, dead lines = two disjoint pairwise-orthogonal 8-frames (asserted).

## 4. Where the per-term (block) claim is FALSE (CHECKED; Lemma 6.1 B is `0` outside `D×D`, optimal on `D×D`)
* **E8, orthogonal normals α ⊥ β:** `|D| = 24`, `mr₂ = 6` exactly (rank 6 SAT, model decoded; rank 5 UNSAT with DRAT, both writers), while the dense block value is 9 (`counter_e8orth.py`). Such a term needs `x ⊥ y`, i.e. only an overcomplete decoder (trace Σ x_j·y_j = T) can use it.
* **D6, non-orthogonal pairs:** `|D| = 7` types with `mr₂ = 3` (240 sets, brute force) and sets with `|D| = 10, 15, 16, 19` and `mr₂ ≤ 4` (SAT, decoded and re-verified as fitting). **The scan covers only `|D| ≤ 19`** (26 096 distinct non-orthogonal D-sets in total). FULL=1 runs the whole `|D| ≤ 19` scan.
* **E7:** the type `n₁` = E6-flat (36 lines orthogonal), `|D| = 11`: `mr₂ = 5` (rank 5 SAT, rank 4 UNSAT with DRAT), 756 pairs, 378 distinct `D`.
* **No decoder below a floor is shown or claimed.** These are per-term counterexamples only; a decoder shares one `B` across all its terms. For D6, a shared-`B` test (one of the earlier runs, not part of `verify.sh`, **CONDITIONAL**) found that the shared `B` blocked the saving on one tested allocation.
