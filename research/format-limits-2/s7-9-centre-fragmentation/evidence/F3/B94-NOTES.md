# B94 (LIT): group-FFT over block-permuting symmetry, one shared S1 program for 14 conjugate blocks. VERDICT: KILL at gate, PROVEN [scope: shipped #656 S1 blocks; B90 frame-size histogram + fleet triage (c)]. ship=false
Lit (from memory, not re-fetched): Clausen-Baum, Fast Fourier Transforms (BI 1993); Maslen-Rockmore, Generalized FFTs survey (DIMACS 1997). Technique: direct sum of G-conjugate copies -> change to group-Fourier basis (linear combos ACROSS copies), run each isotypic part once, transform back. The saving needs the cross-copy butterflies (ADDs between registers of different copies) to be legal.
## Gate (argument + existing numbers, no new compute, ~8 min)
- Cross-block combination of corresponding forms = ADD between registers of blocks i != j. Legal only at a frame inside both blocks' frames. Fleet triage (c): block frames meet pairwise in dim 2 (91/91). A Fourier mix over an orbit of size k needs a frame inside the intersection of all k frames, so dim <= 2 (can only shrink; even the 2-block "isotypic" case is capped at 2).
- B89/B82: a register's frame must contain its content label span. B90: per-block S1 register frame-size histogram {4:72, 1:28, 2:24, 3:8} (132 regs): 80/132 (4 and 3) exceed dim 2 outright; the 24 forms of the 24-form frame have rank-8 content (R^8), and Haar/4-term/8-term sums span more labels than 2 dims. Only 52/132 can even sit in dim <= 2, and a mixed register must hold the content of BOTH constituents (union span larger than either), so the bound is stricter.
- Hence no cross-block combination of S1 forms lives at a legal common frame. B90 h-a already showed the same obstruction for the 8-line Hadamard (B69/B23 common-frame ADD illegal). Group-FFT reduces to the same illegal cross-frame ADD.
- Also (B90 h-c): even without mixing, sharing needs time-multiplexing; scratch of every block is read across all of B (windows 2245-3727), and B93: no group-frame structure (symmetry order 8 per block; 22 forms/7 orbits).
## Numbers: common frame dim <= 2 vs required >= 3 for 80/132 registers per block; 0 legal orbit combinations for those. No pricing run (gate killed).
