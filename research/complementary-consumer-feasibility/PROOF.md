## Actual two-call word

Use a shared central Gaussian Q and two disjoint seven-axis Gaussian blocks D1,D2. They commute because they act on disjoint address axes; the field matrices below are not tensor-powered. Let L(c)=[[1,0],[c,1]]. At stage s:

    A_s = D_s [[1,0],[Q^-1,1]],
    B_s = D_s [[1,0],[1,Q]],
    C_s = D_s [[Q,0],[1,1]].

The whole first invocation is the C provider and the whole second is the B provider. Use the same 56 exceptional ports in both. Each call has its own 408 independent helper inputs. Every main input remains arbitrary. On exceptional pairs the actual chronological word C1, B2, L(-2) gives

    L(-2) B2 C1 = diag(D2 D1 Q,D2 D1 Q).

On the 56 ordinary pairs the chronological word A1,A2,L(-2Q^-1) gives

    L(-2Q^-1) A2 A1 = diag(D2 D1,D2 D1).

The second terminal is a genuine rank-one query on each pair, implemented with the source contract's owned temporary, Q^-1 child, scalar multiply/add and owned clear. It cannot be replaced by L(-2). All monomial source wrappers and actual inverse-Gaussian normalizers are inherited. The helper fields are untouched by these main-only terminals and each returns its original independent Gaussian job exactly once. No helper is credited as a recovered main job.

For any matrix W=T B2 C1, the actual raw dual is

    W^-T=T^-T B2^-T C1^-T.

The execution order is C1^-T, B2^-T, T^-T, the same chronological order with every gate replaced by its inverse transpose. Explicitly

    B_s^-T=D_s^-1 [[1,-Q^-1],[0,Q^-1]],
    C_s^-T=D_s^-1 [[Q^-1,-Q^-1],[0,1]],
    A_s^-T=D_s^-1 [[1,-Q^-1],[0,1]],
    L(c)^-T=[[1,-c],[0,1]].

These expressions use symmetric canonical D_s,Q and retain their inverse powers; they do not identify Q with Q^-1. The verifier computes the inverse transpose algebraically and checks both complete endpoints over rational Laurent polynomials in D1,D2,Q without finite-order assumptions.

This is a fully specified typed consumer with paid query children. It is not a newly emitted 1040-field native trace. Physical validity remains conditional on the audited supplier/normalizer/owned-temporary interface; no claim of new absolute-phase native replay or compiler admission follows.

## Refreshed direct-price lemma

Let t=1-C and P_B,H_B,P_C,H_C be the authenticated current profiles. Define

    g_J(C)=P_J(t)-H_J(t)-224*7^t-112.

The direct primitives of either actual A/B or A/C interface cost224 rank-seven jobs and112 rank-one jobs, with helper jobs paid separately. B's exceptional output scalar shear and C's exceptional input scalar shear give their direct implementations. This is the existing PR4674 comparison extended to the current C profile.

At C=0, g_B=8 and g_C=0. Normalize g_J/7^t. In its derivative, every signed-rank term is nonnegative except the negative rank-six helper term. The rank-seven term is constant. The rank-one term alone contributes at least (P_J[1]-112)/7 * log7; the rank-six term has magnitude at most H_J[6]*log(7/6). Since log7>1 and log(7/6)<1, the derivative is strictly greater than

    B: (1956-112)/7-7 =1795/7 >0,
    C: (2011-112)/7-6 =1857/7 >0.

All other signs are checked against the authenticated profiles. Therefore for0<=C<1:

    g_B(C)>=8*7^-C,
    g_C(C)>=0,

and both are strict relative to their starting normalized value for C>0. This proves the domain, not a numerical sample or monotonicity extrapolation. It also explains why the C local improvement does not supply a positive primitive discount at positive saving.

## Scoped serial ceiling

Fix n>=2 whole invocations on the same224 main owners, one shared Q, and n fresh disjoint seven-axis blocks. The only other productive fields are the independent408 helpers per invocation, preserved as their audited outputs. Require every completed main Gaussian job to act within these7n+1 axes. Allow arbitrary main permutations and scalar boundary programs, but no extra productive inputs, helper repurposing or uncharged rank outside that domain. Any additional recursive cleanup is charged; grant all cleanup and finite work free for this upper bound.

Each main target has rank at most7n+1. Thus with n_B B invocations and n_C C invocations, n_B+n_C=n, the complete target-minus-paid moment has upper bound

    M(C)<=224*(7n+1)^t -n*(224*7^t+112)-8*n_B*7^-C.

For0<t<1, strict subadditivity and n^t<=n give

    (7n+1)^t < (7n)^t+1 <= n*7^t+1.

Consequently M(C)<224-112n-8*n_B*7^-C<=0. At C=0 the exact same optimistic mass bound is

    M(0)<=224-112n-8*n_B.

The best case n=2, n_B=0 only ties zero at C=0 and is strictly negative at every positive saving. A B/C pair loses at least8 rank units even if every main field is optimistically awarded rank15 and all cleanup is free. The actual cancellation word loses176 rank units. All B/B, B/C, C/C serial two-call choices are therefore screened without a search.

This statement is confined to the declared serial whole-call fixed224-owner domain. It does not exclude a controller with more independent main owners, interleaved extra productive jobs, changed helper ownership, different common/transverse geometry, or genuinely shared producer work. A new controller must identify which premise changes and price that change. More digits of the #4610 fixed root or more serial calls do not reopen this screen.
