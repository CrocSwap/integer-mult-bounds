This is a finite bank-allocation extension of the PR197/PR200 completed-bank construction to PR234's five-stage bit geometry, retained from eumemic's source527 package and applied here to the gen5 independent helper census. It does not claim a new all-size compiler theorem or replace the scalar admission.

For one complete local invocation, the source-bound physical telescope has no data/helper cross blocks. This holds on arbitrary correlated dirty contents. The complemented inverse invocation has the same residual: its reversed endpoints are 0 and I24-sigma. Source-owned gauges are visible-data paths and are excluded from the independent helper census.

In stage i, PR234 routes the helper at local class d to physical class g=d*tau_i. Relative to g, the residual is P_i=tau_i*diag(I24-sigma,0,...,0)*tau_i, supported only in H_i. For the actual gauge chart B whose columns are residual columns followed by gauge columns, set

    N_b=(b+1)*Pi_b*embed_i(B^-1).

For an ungauged role B=I24. Pi_b is the explicit coordinate permutation sending the first residual coordinates in H_i to the assigned bank interval. Thus N_b*P_i*N_b^-1 is exactly that coordinate projector. The scalar leaves conjugation unchanged. The checker reconstructs every actual chart, checks both inverse identities, the G-orthogonality of its two summands, its residual action and its elementary factorization. Therefore this is a projector identity for the actual bases, not a rank substitution.

The bank route is g -> g*N_b^-1, hence d*tau_i*N_b^-1 in the original local invocation. If the bank class is h, its residual is h*N_b*P_i*N_b^-1*h^-1. Every occurrence visits every bank class once because right multiplication is a bijection on the complete GL cover. If two roles in one local invocation are assigned the same bank family, their distinct N_b give distinct bank classes. Distinctness is literal: choose a coordinate j outside H_i. The embedded inverse fixes e_j; N_b*e_j=(b+1)e_(Pi_b(j)). The resulting columns differ for all b, at every eligible prime q>2^80 and every local ring Z/q^w. All scalars are between 1 and 40 and remain distinct units. The proof uses the full GL cover, not a projective quotient.
