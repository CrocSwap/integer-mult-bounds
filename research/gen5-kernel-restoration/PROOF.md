# Proof and verification boundary

The starting object is the actual binary word emitted by the pinned, untouched
PR290 verifier. All ten stages run freshly: virtual, raw, bit, kernel, scalar,
primes, banks, complex, math and finite. Its manifest and output word are checked.

## Coordinate binding

`export.py` rebuilds the physical emitter and parity word from PR290 source,
then applies its descent, target and kernel stages. The ordered helper roles,
physical index mapping and final endpoints must agree before and after those
stages. The resulting kernel word and initial frames must agree with the
separately completed original verifier. The original coordinate tables are also
bound to exact hashes. `export290.py` checks inherited frame bases and
annihilators, derives kernel annihilators using exact rational arithmetic, and
recomputes the paid histogram from the binary word. Original initial frames are
retained separately to measure entrance savings; post-kernel initial frames
remain the construction's actual inputs.

## Early restoration

`restore.cpp` scans the literal cleanup suffix. Each selected rank-20 entrance
helper has exactly one remaining use, a signed restoration from a distinct donor.
Both frames at the new cut have rank 22. Exact rational elimination produces
rank-23 unions and checks nondegeneracy under `I-J/9`. Every emitted frame
transition is nested with its exact rank increment. The helper's final frame is
changed only to its union frame; all remaining final endpoints are retained.

`audit.py` independently reads the old and new words. It checks that no copy or
erasure is crossed, each destination is untouched and each donor is unwritten
throughout the interval, the donor/destination sets are disjoint, and the entire
surviving signed scalar/copy projection equals the prescribed relocation.
Elementary signed shears therefore commute over the integers. This preserves
PR290's signed decoder and dirty-restoration semantics; integer equivalence is
not inferred from parity alone.

The same audit replays all 19,406 independent columns over F2 in both directions.
Omitting all moved restorations corrupts exactly 440 rows in each direction.
Native global lowering checks all 22,926 formal columns over all five stages,
with six omitted-bridge controls. Copy lifetimes and the signed reverse convention
are preserved.

## Geometry and costs

`banks290.cpp` computes exact residual projector differences and complete endpoint
charts, checks conjugacy and weighted splits, assigns every physical helper,
replica and stage to a bank without overlap or padding, and tests omission and
repetition controls. The 440 smaller final frames shift 440 original residual
roles from width 4 to width 3. Kernel entries remain unchanged. The measured
chart maximum is 318; the inherited ceiling 744 plus 239 normalizer factors is
conservatively charged. The resulting literal stock is 2,486,830.

`check.py` independently derives endpoint residuals, rank histograms, bank slot
counts, scalar and unit-expanded ADD counts, cancellation-free signed prefix
bounds, selector costs and the full primitive coefficient from the actual word
and frame tables. It binds these values to both rational pricing engines and
the finite invoice. The fixed-prime checker recomputes eight levels and all 47
strict constraints; the adjacent grid point is rejected at both tested caps.

## Conditional scope

The common-ancestor compiler, rational address grid, restored rows, routing,
selectors, recovery, complex correctness and analytic interfaces inherited from
the source chain remain assumptions at the all-size level. Finite replay does
not independently certify those public theorems, prove a Lean theorem, establish
global optimality, or measure practical integer multiplication speed.
