# Notice — disjoint-tensor support masks

Douglas R. Colkitt authored the disjoint-tensor compression screen, the
`DisjointTensor` recurrence and embedding, and the original expected-support
coefficient checks consumed by this tooling package.

This package adds an equivalence-preserving closed-form rewrite of the
historical leave-intersecting-inputs expected-support genexpr inside
`DisjointTensor.verify`, with identical receipts and updated source pins only.
