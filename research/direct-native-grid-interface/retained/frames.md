# Physical frames and exact phase changes for the mixed-cut supplier

This note concerns the two suppliers of the [mixed-cut multiplication claim](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/87fbf4f7b35b0f18ab12b5767775a9a424cf0b15/research/p104-mixed-rank-supplier/PROOF.md) at κ = 0.00387992803556. It describes a fixed-tape realization of the ownership boundary and a common-format invariant across the deferred complex phases. All quantities denoted by a fixed constant can depend on the compiled finite word and its prime. They cannot depend on input length.

## A physical recursive call

Use a finite number of physical one-head tapes. Each private work tape holds a stack of adjacent intervals. One interval has a beginning marker, at least one data cell, and a terminal marker. Data cells represent a symbol of the working alphabet, including its blank symbol. One additional bit can hold a suspended-head mark. The right terminal is one cell beyond the largest coordinate visited in this frame. Use one physical outside blank for unused tape. Two tracks and a sign bit simulate a two-sided logical tape with a half-line of cells: fold position -1-j over j for j≥0. Switching across the origin changes the sign and keeps the head stationary. The larger alphabet and table are finite. Alternatively the stream algorithms in the present suppliers operate on marked half-lines directly.

Installation at the top of a blank tape writes beginning, data-blank and terminal and returns to the data cell. A move to the right at the terminal replaces that marker by data-blank, writes a new terminal on its right and returns to the new data cell. A left move at the beginning stays at the first data cell, or uses the sign track for a two-sided logical tape. Each ordinary local step takes a fixed number of physical one-cell transitions. Eager clearing rewrites to physical outside blanks. No position, extent, free list or depth is a selection key for these transitions.

On suspension, mark the actual parent head, walk right to the terminal and install the next frame immediately after the terminal. After the child has delivered its arguments for return, walk right to its terminal, erase it, walk left erasing all child cells, erase its beginning, pass the parent terminal, and walk left to the first saved head. Unmark it. All saved data closer than any older suspended head are in the immediate parent. An older frame is never scanned. Lengths can exceed or fall below every prior length. A previous wider sibling leaves real blank tape to the right.

At an arbitrary parent head with a data cells to its right, push takes a+5 transitions. At an arbitrary child head with b data cells to its right and m≥1 child data cells in all, pop takes (1+b)+1+m+2+a+1 transitions. These formulas include erasing every physical cell of a dirty child. The Lean theorems `push_correct` and `leave_correct` derive the final tape and these iteration counts from the one-head table. They quantify over both outer pieces of the tape. No admissibility assumption on their contents is used, apart from the three fresh physical cells written by a push. Their existence follows from the initial blank work tapes, closest-first extension, serial nesting and the pop restoring its entire historical interval. At any instant no currently active frame can have a live descendant to the right of its terminal. Prior siblings have been physically cleaned. This is an induction on the actual execution, not a semantic promise that the allocator grants blank space.

Pass arguments through a fixed set of initially empty transfer tapes. A fixed site selects its actual complete argument banks. Copy the selected arguments and their shape descriptors to the transfer tapes by full signed or binary payload scans. Mark-and-rewind restores each borrowed head. Select the fixed number of work tapes used by the callees and suspend each as above. Install the arguments in the child and clear the transfer tapes through their terminal. Empty the transfer tapes before calling any recursive subroutine. At return perform these transfers in the opposite order, install every completed result into its designated held owner and clear the transfer buffers. A primitive that overwrites an output physically writes the whole output before exposing it to its consumer. A local numerical transducer used per scalar or per row is inlined in the current frame, or uses its private numerical tapes while leaving the large data tapes stationary. It does not suspend and copy the entire parent array per scalar.

All possible argument lists are finite for the fixed precompiled templates. Cases whose choice depends on a low matrix select from a fixed finite list. Record lengths vary. A split side with no numerical payload emits its delimiter and performs no data iterations. The template uses no tape per axis, recursion depth, high matrix, operand digit or logical copy at runtime. The mathematical machine may have an enormous fixed number of tapes and a large finite-state control. This is the upstream machine model.

If the total private extent at a node is at most K V, where V includes the full positive bit volume of the presented call, one suspend/return pair costs O(K V) plus O(child extent). Budget the child's final reverse sweep also to the child. The number of sites is fixed at a compiled word and can multiply that local cost. Repeated slot sweeps with a number of occurrences proportional to the local width charge that number explicitly. A transfer tape is only live at one edge and only until its handoff is complete. At any time the historical extent reached so far on a physical tape is bounded by the **maximum** over earlier active chains of the sum of their frame extents inclusive of delimiters. Each new interval begins immediately after its live parent. A completed sibling never shifts the next interval farther away. For one recursion with V(child)≤V(parent)/2, the sum including the root is at most 2 V(root). The induction in `chain_space` proves the bound for every finite chain. This bound applies to the previously visited cells as well as to current nonblank payloads. Distinct component tapes may reach their maxima at different times. Their count is fixed. At a call to an earlier ordinary stage the volume can be comparable to the parent and its radial padding can introduce a fixed factor. There are at most six nontrivial ordinary stage indices on a chain, plus the elementary stage. Multiplying their constants retains O(V) storage. Repeated outer phases are sequential with all temporary frames released between phases.

The [earlier parking table](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/finite-parking-frames/PROOF.md) proves matched serial transfer to a dedicated parking stack. The [complete-row caller](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/literal-complete-row-parking-caller/PROOF.md) reserves eight supplied ports and explicitly leaves recursive reuse of their complete backing open. The machine here instead grows adjacent intervals on each fixed tape and preserves arbitrary interior parent heads.

Use the source-bound root grid from the [expanded complex application](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/87fbf4f7b35b0f18ab12b5767775a9a424cf0b15/research/p104-chart306-chain/DOUBLE-APPLICATION.json). The caller odd scale is 2025 and the native scale 763745901562474065860625 is installed as factors 15658669, 13190315, 15217125 and 243. Every factor has at most 24 bits. H_C=15171300000000000000. The exact positive integer 856·1920·153^1920·(H_C+1)^2 has 14,083 bits, hence is less than 2^H_C. For D active axes and ℓ=floor(log_(2^H_C)D), at most t=1920·153^1920·(H_C+1)^2·ℓ contexts can lie on a single open source path by the finite compiler's shrink/divisor argument. The noncommutative temporal structure matters: no numerator certificate on one column alone proves the all-column chart identities, and no rounded physical child may be inserted into this induction.

There is a distinction between the old selected preprocessing, which describes paying individually for prior reserved axes, and the deferred shrinking phases required by the present movement bill. Suppose m axes have been completed and the next phase will process a active axes, with m+a≤d. Start on signed complex components with fractional precision p and magnitude exponent h. On each actual one-axis kernel the two output numerators at a doubled denominator are the components of x+i x+y-i y, where y is the input at the partner obtained by flipping this axis bit. If all incoming numerators are componentwise bounded by M, the next numerators are bounded by 4 M. Thus m completed axes have increased the dyadic grid exponent by at most m and each component's actual magnitude by at most 2^m. This uses arbitrary independent operand arrays. The exact ordered phases are disjoint C tensor factors, and the incoming S phases are signed quarter-turns.

At that completed cut, re-anchor the proof on the whole *new* values with parameters p+m and h+m, including complete now-reserved external labels. Every interior local bound uses fractional precision at most (p+m)+a≤p+d, magnitude exponent at most (h+m)+a+2≤h+d+2, and signed width at most (p+m)+(h+m)+2a+3≤p+h+2d+3. A single global format with these maxima therefore suffices. This change of proof reference requires no truncation, repack or additional kernel. It does not assume that a projection onto a changing exterior prefix is itself a Clifford operator. The remaining h/16 or smaller reservation subtree receives its next ordinary phase. Only the final polylogarithmic regime is processed one axis per scan. The terminal b₀^d S C S conversion and any 2^d normalization of H are included at their actual final boundary, with O(d) more guard bits as in the upstream layer. Since d=O(p^ε), ε<1, all these widths are O(p). `PhaseBudget.kernel_numerators` and `same_guard` check the Gaussian recurrence and the common physical format.
