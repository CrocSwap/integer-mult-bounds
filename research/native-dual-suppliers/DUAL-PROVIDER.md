## 1. Port duality and complementary incidence

For each original port i, let c_i be its central entrance vector and F_i its terminal main hyperplane. The exact pinned source data satisfy

    F_i=c_i⊥,    c_i·c_i=1.

This is checked on all 112 pairs, including the entire 512-point hyperplanes. The 112 vectors c_i are distinct and span the whole nine-dimensional space: they must not be treated as one common physical entrance line.

Let v be a nonzero vector different from every c_i. Quotienting by span(v) gives the A/B distinction:

- If v·c_i=0, the image of F_i has dimension 7 and the image central line lies outside it: type A.
- If v·c_i=1, the image F_i fills the eight-dimensional quotient: type B.

Now intersect every frame with H=v⊥ instead. Since v≠c_i, the two hyperplanes F_i and H are distinct, and A_i=F_i∩H has dimension 7. This gives the complementary A/C distinction:

- If v·c_i=0, the central line survives in H and lies outside A_i: type A.
- If v·c_i=1, the central entrance disappears, both logical starts are ground, and the physical diagonal blocks have ranks 8 and 7: type C.

Thus the B ports of the quotient and the C ports of the orthogonal restriction are EXACTLY the same set. At v=153 this set has 56 elements. This is an elementary universal incidence argument, not a claim inferred from a numerical moment root.

## 3. Explicit monomial normal form of C ports

At a C port both starts are ground. Let A_i=F_i∩H, and write C_H,C_A for the canonical frame operators. Its forward physical matrix is

          [ C_H     0 ]
      M = [ C_A   C_A ].

Choose any c′∈H∖A_i and an invertible G taking standard central axis e₀ to c′ and the standard seven-plane E to A_i. Let N=K_G, Q=C_e₀ and D=C_E. Define

    M_X=M_Y=N,
    O_X=QD N⁻¹ C_H⁻¹,
    O_Y=D N⁻¹ C_A⁻¹.

These four operators are monomial. The canonical-frame Lagrangian identity

    L_U={(x,z):x∈U, x+z∈U⊥}

and K_G L_U=L_GU show that both output operators preserve the computational Z Lagrangian. The exact Clifford lowering determines their monomial address maps, quadratic phases and absolute phases.

Direct multiplication gives

    O_X C_H M_X = QD,
    O_Y C_A M_Y = D,
    O_Y C_A M_X = D.

Consequently the normalized forward matrix is exactly diag(QD,D)S_y(1). A ports use the already proved A-type normalizers. All 112 pairs were checked with 448 exact monomial normalizers and 336 exact block identities, including global phase. Inverse transpose gives the backward interface; its extra inverse Q is on the same physical X field. Individual operator-entry identities tensor to every digit-column width, while the scalar word's all-width identity follows independently from formal free-group-algebra evaluation.

## 4. Why the two special types can cooperate

Relative to the desired common logical A primitive, a B port can be consumed with equal input central-gauge parities and flips Y, leaving opposite output parities. A C port can be consumed with opposite input parities and flips X, leaving equal output parities. The two types are therefore complementary parity transitions.

This does not by itself make a controller free. Every scalar ADD across unequal gauges can require a paid rank-one bridge; query shear cost depends on its source/target parity; scalar boundary programs and final productive central parity must be included. A B/C choice is a choice of a whole supplier invocation with its actual P/H invoice, not an independent free choice at each port. Pair permutations may choose which 56 ports are special, but the resulting supplier and all external normalizers must be charged as finite nonrecursive operations.
