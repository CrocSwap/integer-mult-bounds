## Exact substitution

The composition theorem gives, for at most m=7 installed stages,

\[
H_{native}\le 2T+
\sum_{j=1}^{m}[G(B_j,2)+8+A(B_j)]2^{m-j+1}T+3\,2^mT.
\]

The named-head coefficient, the root/cold-root coefficient, and the complete
67-position native transient coefficient are respectively

\[
G(B,2)=6+\frac{123}{8(B-1)},\qquad 8,\qquad
A(B)\le17+\frac{54}{B}.
\]

Thus their sum is at most

\[
31+r(B),\qquad r(B)=\frac{123}{8(B-1)}+\frac{54}{B}.
\]

The source sets B=N^92. Its installed rank logs are at least 5610, so the
very weak bound B>=32768 suffices. Both summands of r decrease for B>1.
The exact rational margin is

\[
254r(32768)=\frac{144349851}{268427264}<1.
\]

Since the stage weights sum to at most 254, and the terminal weight is at
most 128, the complete coefficient is strictly below

\[
2+31\cdot254+3\cdot128+1=8261.
\]

Multiplication by T<9X gives 74349X. No production-sized integer, calendar
or field is executed to establish this inequality. `check_joined.py`
authenticates both frozen arguments, checks their common source pins and
the installed rank list, and evaluates this rational margin exactly.
The universal part is the source-bound induction in the component proofs.

## Warm reservations and the public cold branch

The inventory contains 91 transient positions: 2 public rectangle, 5 stage
root, 11 cold ternary, 67 native transient and 6 public cold binary
positions. The last six are not called by a native weighted activation.
Their individual historical coefficients are `(1,1,1/4,1/4,1/4,1/4)X`,
summing to 3X. They are disjoint within one public call but can retain old
capacity when later calls enter native stages.

Fix one installation and physical role map, and let X_max bound the binary
slab volume of every native or cold public call in the reservation history.
The component proofs give uniform **per-role** envelopes before summing,
so each is valid across different shapes and different maximizing calls.
Adding the six cold-only positions gives, for an initially empty reserve,

\[
\boxed{H_{history}<74352X_{max}.}
\]

This conclusion does not follow from a scalar per-call total alone. It uses
the coordinatewise envelopes of the named-role table, all local work sites,
root/cold roles and the strict-stage argument. Their constants are fixed
throughout this history. Changed installations or additional role families
require their own bounds.

For an initial historical-capacity vector K_init, the exact update is

\[
K'_r=\max(K_{init,r},\sup_i h_{i,r}).
\]

A sufficient aggregate grant is therefore

\[
H_{history}\le\sum_r\max(K_{init,r},c_rX_{max})
<\sum_rK_{init,r}+74352X_{max}.
\]

Untouched roles retain their initial capacity. Erasure proves the required
zero/return state, not deallocation of a historical tape reservation. The
source-executed shrinking-call control in `composition/ENTRY-HISTORY.md`
demonstrates this distinction explicitly.
