## Mathematical review

The census partition is correct. The installed 91 work positions split into
67 positions reached inside a native weighted activation, six public
binary-cold positions, two public rectangle files, five stage-root files,
and eleven ternary-cold positions. Within the 67, eight helper positions
require the complete weighted volume `W`, 54 are bounded individually by
the owner volume `V=W/B`, and the five type positions have uniform
coefficients `(3,3,1,1,1)W`. Thus the coefficient is `8+9+54/B`.

I checked the actual caller products and coefficient-run domains. Residual
atom factors occur in both the gap and suffix, so the owner is exactly
`W/B`. Direct routes act on the whole owner. Stopped pairs act on the whole
weighted argument; assigning them the smaller owner coefficient would
fail. Compensation, direct routes, stopped pairs and covered shears each
emit coefficient lengths summing to their actual `pair_shape` prefix.
These conclusions do not discard spectators, older coordinates, high
representatives or byte suffixes.

The direct factor proof bounds every one of the twenty bucket positions
uniformly across positive/negative numerators, inverse denominators,
component geometries and prefix history. A fixed bucket is bounded by one
complete component interval; clearing before the next use prevents
accumulation. It is valid to sum the twenty separate bounds even though
their peaks occur in different calls. The two main spools need one full
interval each. This argument does not infer a sum of historical maxima from
a single simultaneous measurement.

The shared lower, negate and pair helpers similarly have complete-interval
bounds. A shift's scratch and a negation's reversed suffix fit within their
complete input. The scaler's invariant `front*3**v*tail=E` is maintained by
both odd and even steps, and each saved finish retains its own full shape.
Its eleven fixed roles do not turn into one payload allocation per halving
level. The saved descriptors are control and remain outside this byte bill.

The quotient tag guard is attached to the installed caller. Each high body
has `D>=3**(2N)>=N**2`, so a group of `Q` tags fits within its complete data
group. Every data or tag bucket is a subset of that group. Serial high and
affine subpackets retain their full interval and do not exceed the owner.
The standalone `N=2`, one-byte body example has 64 tag bytes for 16 payload
bytes; it correctly refutes an unrestricted generic-API envelope and does
not occur at the compiled caller. Positive component widths and `f>=1` are
used explicitly rather than assumed from a small successful fixture.

The type-route coefficient is coordinatewise and uniform in both
directions. The two stream positions are bounded by three times the true
input and each bucket by one times that input. A whole compiler transpose
can use `W`; a line transpose uses at most `V`. Taking a common bound relative
to `W` therefore covers the same positions across both uses. The new

The six public binary-cold positions are unreachable from a native
predecessor: that callback enters `ternary`, never the public binary wrapper.
