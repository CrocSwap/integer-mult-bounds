## Claims and proof

The general result is a conditional local protocol. It requires the
admitted raw interface, arbitrary helper and mode contracts, legal
monomial access to `Q^2` and `Q^-2`, matching replica views, and the stated
productive-frame intertwiners. These conditions are explicit. The proof
does not construct a raw interface for an unspecified Q.

For invertible Q, the definitions give
`T=[[0,Q],[Q^-1,0]]` and `T^2=I`. The conversion from each physical graph
to its logical map uses the stated frames in the correct order. The
forward target graph uses the updated mode. The backward source graph
uses the old mode before the mode preload. Both displayed inverses are
correct. The two local words are not asserted to be mutual inverses.

The all-stage conclusion uses one transported global T. It does not
require R or K to commute with Q, or equal centre row spaces. The
cancellation is the adjacent product `(I-T)(I+T)=0`. The proof correctly
restricts an intervening data map and requires matching replica views.
It does not infer a common mode action from the data-side map A.

For the fixed native instance, the line formula includes the scalar
phase and gives `C_p^2=X_p`. The route argument uses `gp=p`, including
owner-dependent direction and the entry and exit translations. Exact
tensoring supports the stated positive-depth extension. Two replicas
are used at each depth; the replica index is not tensored. The complete
old core kernel and helper-frame contract remain inherited premises.

The README, proof, and notices agree on the scope. All 16 mode inputs
are arbitrary. Their outputs are part of the map and are not claimed
restored. The 944 ordinary helpers are restored in their specified
frames. The private fields are preserved by non-use. Final odd-phase
H/G access, mode restoration in a complete controller, and full global
closure remain open. The package claims no complete supplier, kappa
change, or selected-frontier change.
