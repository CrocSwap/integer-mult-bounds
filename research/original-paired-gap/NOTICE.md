Within the centre-encoding campaign, history-encoding supplied the
full-mode core, exact sparse replay, and physical phase-gap graphs.
Coherent-port supplied the complementary-gap argument and exact native
review. The coordinating agent supplied the original-complex replica
construction and its monomial-square abstraction. Residual-syndrome
supplied the literal paired packet and proof. Its independent reviewer
checked the operator order, routes, and corrected frame hypotheses.
Encoded-controller supplied the prior arbitrary-mode decoder consequence;
that conditional controller is not an executable dependency here.
Source-domain supplied the separate owner binding, and circuit-interface
reviewed its composition with the local pair.

The portable pair checker adapts the history and residual-syndrome
replay methods. The native checker adapts the coherent-port independent
review, with the direct original-source route reconstruction described
above. The general checker records the ordered proof identities and
counterexamples. It does not replace the hypotheses by a numerical test.
Exact source paths and hashes are recorded in [SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json).

The result is a local typed mechanism and an enabling theorem. Full
global closure, a complete supplier, and a kappa improvement are not
claimed. The package does not change the selected frontier.
