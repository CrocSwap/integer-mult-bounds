1. Scope, source and outcome
---------------------------
This replaces the local formation of A0 and G12 inside each paired cube.
It preserves the query modules, center functions, target root functions,
all high-frame gauges, and the existing compensated-reuse matching.

The input is eumemic/integer-mult-bounds, PR168, commit
fd25adb7fbaa12ee761d02c733c54d1d2a7687ee. The source retains its own
credits, including Anthropic Claude assistance, jamesyc's reuse, and
the cited frame/gauge work. This note contributes the source-assisted
local replacement and its fixed-boundary completion argument.

At p=12 the replacement removes 440 unpaired auxiliary rows. The
physical count becomes R=17,852, W=21,372, m=72, while Delta=1,936
is unchanged. Its combined coarse bit exponent is

    0.0006656467179910197,

compared with 0.0006549403327479886 for the pinned physical input,
a factor 1.016347115466396. This is a coarse bit-supplier improvement,
not an assembled final exponent and not the fixed twofold target.

2. Local algebra and actual source frames
----------------------------------------
Write a cube's eight sources as x_(a,b,c), with selectors a,b,c in F2.
Here selector0 means the first of that cube's three pair labels.
Define the four source-pair sums

    p_(a,m) = x_(a,0,m) + x_(a,1,1+m).

Then the four replaced channels are exactly

    A_(0,a)  = p_(a,0) + p_(a,1),
    G_(12,m) = p_(0,m) + p_(1,m).

Each pair defining p is an existing original-source K pair. Its two
source vectors are orthogonal and span the already-paid nondegenerate
rank2 frame M_(a,m). This frame lies in each relevant rank3 A or G
formation frame. No four-dimensional common cube frame is introduced.

First finish every old singleton V injection while the original X
registers are on their source lines. Advance each original pair to
its existing M frame and leave both source registers there, unmixed,
through the local A/G formation in both phases of the center cut.
At M, a fresh pair-sum channel is injected into an arbitrary dirty
auxiliary row by two source-controlled XORs, one from each original X.
The auxiliary's frame-zero to M ascent is paid; it is not a zero-cost
birth. Its subsequent M to rank3 ascent is also paid.

The original K mixing and delivery can start after these source
controls. Waiting at M creates no additional recursive call. The
original source chains and original-source rank histogram are
unchanged. At full frame, restore the original K mixing first; reverse
the new auxiliary scalar operations and every new source-controlled
XOR there, then remove the remaining V injections. The original X
registers are controls of the new operations, and are restored exactly.

3. Arbitrary dirty rows and the local lift
----------------------------------------
At a common actual frame let C describe n incoming physical auxiliary
rows, D the t demanded outgoing rows, and E the linear forms available
from original-source controls at that frame. Put

    d = rank(C+E) - rank(E),
    r = rank(D+E) - rank(E).

Independent physical lifts require and suffice for

    b = max(0, t+d-r-n)

new dirty rows. This is the usual independent-lift argument applied
modulo the source-control span: source-controlled additions can remove
the E part of any auxiliary row, while an invertible auxiliary row
basis change supplies independent lifts of all outgoing forms. The
whole transformation on (auxiliary rows, original X) is invertible.
It never erases an independent physical dirty coordinate.

After the local map, n+b-t rows retire. Keep all d-r independent fresh
complements for full-frame inverse cleanup. Only the remaining rows
could be fresh-zero recycling candidates. The direct-injection version
used here leaves no such surplus, so no further recycling credit is
claimed.

4. Fixed high boundary and preservation of the paid mechanisms
-------------------------------------------------------------
The checker compares exact phase/frame/source-form triples. All 37,436
edge forms entering or lying inside frames of dimension greater than3
are identical before and after replacement. Their common SHA256 is

    322f2dbbaa0177fa9c4fc8ea3d592bd20074a2d3ff8d0e98c3230d5f1fdd58ec.

At each of the 2,832 phase/rank3 vertices the complete resource tuple
(n,d,t,r,b,retired) is also unchanged. Hence the old high word can be
kept literally: label each new low-region exit by the old physical
role of that boundary request. A low-region row path crosses this
high boundary at most once, because actual frames ascend. Thus these
exit labels impose no contradictory physical-register assignment.
Scalar basis changes at a common frame can realize the specified
outgoing forms on those exits.

The only removed endpoints are 440 rank2 retirees. None is a donor
in the pinned matching: all 1,760 donor terminal frames have dimension
at least3. Rank3 donor rows still exist in the same frame and phase;
their fresh complement value is allowed to change. A compensated
recipient read subtracts the donor's actual current value, so that
mechanism accepts an arbitrary changed complement. The donor frame
containment and its lifetime before the recipient read remain valid.
The new low operations may be completed earlier within their phase;
they do not delay any inherited high operation or recipient read.

All 3,960 gauged roles are born in the unchanged high suffix and have
the same future response columns, target supports, gauge frames and
read deadlines. Their 1,760 reuse pairings therefore remain; the live
exterior-tail bank remains the 2,200 rank20 gauges. The nongauged
frame-zero old-value response must be recomputed for the new low word.
It is an unrestricted scalar correction at target frame zero and
introduces no recursive charge. This is the place where old-value
coefficients change; no old response is silently reused there.

------------------------------------
Run:

    python common_frame_network.py --source-aligned --compact \
        --output source_aligned_profile.json

The pinned source is found at ../public/pr168_fd25adb7 relative to
the round13 directory, or can be passed with --source. The calculation
uses exact F2 elimination, verifies fresh-span membership and all
local/global rank identities, and checks the high boundary literally.
The geometric dependency is the pinned exact frame witness; the new
frames are existing source-pair M frames. New scalar coefficients are
binary, introducing no additional bad-pivot set.

This increment supplies a constructive certificate on all physical
columns through the explicit low lifts and fixed-boundary lemma,
together with the exact combined ledger. It is stronger than a check
only of fresh source values or rank counts. The retained high word and
the new low transcripts specify an invertible full construction.

The fully renumbered global elementary-operation transcript has not
yet been exported, and no new end-to-end arbitrary-dirty lane replay
is claimed. Emitting that transcript, computing its new nongauged
entrance response, and running a full replay remain concrete software
validation tasks. They are distinguished here from the all-column
algebraic certificate supplied by the local matrices and boundary
construction.

The full source_aligned_frame_network.json additionally contains
regenerable per-vertex diagnostics. Omitting those statistics does not
remove any calculation: --compact performs the same reconstruction,
checks and accounting, then suppresses only their serialization.

7. Explicit low lifts, including all dirty coordinates
-----------------------------------------------------
source_aligned_lifts.py and its compact JSON certificate provide literal
XOR/swap transcripts for every changed low interface. They cover 1,760
source-control births and880 A0/G12 sum vertices. Their full instance
manifest SHA256 is

    162503d35f76776308baec3410757bebb7a1f106ae45fee907c75f6edb01b4de.

For a source birth the incoming coordinates are (z,X_c,X_d), with z
arbitrary dirty. The row matrix has binary row masks (7,2,4): perform
z+=X_c and z+=X_d, leaving both source controls unchanged. Its inverse
is the reverse pair of XORs. The auxiliary's rank2 ascent is paid.

For a sum vertex with t outgoing copies, use the two incoming pair rows
a,b and t-1 new dirty rows z_1,...,z_(t-1), all at the same rank3 frame.
For each j first add a and b to z_j; then perform a+=b. Order the
outgoing rows as a,z_1,...,z_(t-1) and retain b as the complementary row.
The physical output row masks are

    e_0+e_1;
    e_0+e_1+e_2, ..., e_0+e_1+e_t;
    e_1.

These rows form an invertible matrix. The first t fresh rows are the
required channel, while the last retains the second independent input
mode. The certificate contains the explicit swaps implementing the
displayed order and the exact reversed cleanup transcript. There are
500 instances with t=2,306 with t=3 and74 with t=4.

Every instance is checked on the full physical coordinate basis, in
both composition orders, not only on fresh source values. Its fresh
output bitsets and source-plane containment are also checked exactly.
The JSON is about8KB; --instances optionally emits the full manifest
for a downstream elementary-word generator.

8. What is exhausted at the fixed source-pair interface
------------------------------------------------------
Both phases of all880 original-source rank2 M planes already have the
full two-dimensional source-control span E available. After exact
backward pruning, each such phase/frame vertex has n=0 and one physical
outgoing row. Its minimum dirty birth count is b=t=1, and it has no
retired surplus. Providing E to additional occurrences of the same
actual M therefore adds no capability in this network.

Before replacement, no rank2 auxiliary operation frame equaled any
original-source M plane. All rational frame signatures were checked
to be distinct. Thus there is no overlooked old same-M operation or
retiring row that could be cancelled by those controls. Among the
cube's local A/G rank3 planes, precisely A0 and G12 contain a given
entire source-pair plane; the other channels exclude its partner port.
The selected rewrite covers all of these local opportunities.

At every remaining vertex the incoming fresh rows, modulo any available
E, are independent. The outgoing edge forms are already independent
edge by edge, all used incoming rows are necessary for their unique
expressions, and every minimum-lift inequality is attained. Every
remaining retiree carries an independent fresh complement; there is no
fresh-zero kernel left for further recycling. Consequently there is no
additional scalar-rank saving from reapplying the same quotient or
pruning at these fixed actual frames and edge interfaces.

This is a scoped saturation statement. It does not assert optimality
over changed source paths, new intermediate frames, a different rank3
boundary, or a different routing of partial forms into later query
modules. Such changes require a new physical word and a new ledger.
