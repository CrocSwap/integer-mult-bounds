Prepared for icekylinx by GPT-6 Astra, 9 October 2026.

This note gives the algebraic interface for round 13's single selected
route. Sections 3 and 4 describe the zero-gauge reconstruction used by
the complex branch. The selected bit branch instead retains its proved
high-boundary gauges and compensated aliases, as specified below and in
SOURCE_ALIGNED_BIT_WORD.txt. A construction earns a numerical claim only after the actual
two-phase frame-flow compiler supplies its complete paid histogram.
No local rank reduction alone is a multiplication-exponent certificate.

1. The local problem

Work over the scalar coefficient field K: Q for the complex supplier,
F_2 for the bit supplier. Fix one actual frame representative T_U and
one chronological phase. All scalar combinations below occur at this
identical representative; equal dimensions are not sufficient.

Let the n incoming physical rows have fresh response C in K^(n by v),
of rank d. Their arbitrary dirty components remain independent physical
coordinates in the complete word. The desired t outgoing fresh rows
form D in K^(t by v), whose row space is contained in row(C), of rank r.
Rows destined for different future frames are separate physical rows.

Before counting t, replace the requested forms for each identical next
frame by a basis of those forms. The receiving frame reconstructs all
requested scalar expressions from this basis. A value needed at two
different frames still contributes to both outgoing boundary spaces.
Delayed reads and the center cut also count as actual destinations.

The exact minimum number of new arbitrary dirty rows needed at this
block is

    b = max(0, t + d - r - n).

After the block, n+b-t complementary rows remain; they are not discarded
or declared clean. In particular n+b-t >= d-r. Exactly d-r can be chosen
to carry the missing independent fresh forms. The remaining

    k = n+b-t-(d-r)

can be chosen to have zero fresh response while retaining arbitrary
independent dirty contents.

2. Necessity and constructive sufficiency

Adjoin b new arbitrary rows with zero fresh response, writing C'=[C;0].
An outgoing scalar row is a coefficient vector in K^(n+b) mapping under
L: a -> a C' to its prescribed row of D. The kernel of L has dimension
n+b-d. If t independent physical output rows have image rank r, at
least t-r independent directions must lie in this kernel. Therefore
n+b-d >= t-r, proving the lower bound.

For sufficiency, use exact elimination to choose P in GL_t(K) such that
PD consists of r independent rows followed by t-r zero rows. Lift the
first r rows through L to any chosen complement of ker(L). Lift the
remaining t-r rows to distinct basis vectors of ker(L). These t lifts
are independent. Multiply them by P^(-1) to recover exactly D, and
complete them to a basis of K^(n+b). The completed matrix Q is invertible.

Apply Q pointwise to the n+b rows at T_U. Keep its first t rows for
the specified outgoing boundaries and retain the complementary rows.
All fresh forms are exact, including signs and cancellations, while
every arbitrary dirty coordinate is retained in an invertible scalar
word. This construction is locally optimal for the fixed boundary
spaces. It is not a proof of global optimality over frame geometries,
phase cuts, target schedules, or other multiplication constructions.

2a. Recycle the zero-fresh kernel through the same frame network

A complementary row with zero fresh response may supply a later new-row
demand at a frame V containing U. This is a real transition T_U -> T_V.
The row's dirty value need not be zero and need not be restored before
that use. It is an independent coordinate of the new global scalar word,
whose response and inverse are recomputed in full. This lemma does not
by itself inherit an old selected gauge or compensated-birth certificate.

For each such compatible match, the separate retirement U -> F and new
birth 0 -> V are replaced by U -> V. The changes in the local ledger are

    R <- R-1,
    H_(h-dim U) <- H_(h-dim U)-1,
    H_(dim V)   <- H_(dim V)-1,
    H_(dim V-dim U) <- H_(dim V-dim U)+1,

with a zero-width transition charged only as scalar/router work. Hence
the local rank mass drops by exactly h and the global deficit is
unchanged. The recycled row can be reused again only after a later
invertible lift exposes an actual zero-fresh complementary coordinate.
This is a directed flow of physical rows, not simultaneous aliasing.

The d-r complementary rows with independent nonzero fresh responses
cannot be treated this way. They retain information needed for the
eventual inverse and remain live until a proved later basis operation
changes their status or until they reach F.

2b. Original-source controls can create a usable kernel

Suppose the original source bank exposes a known space E of fresh
linear forms at the same representative T_U, without changing its paid
chain or its pending K obligations. A complementary auxiliary form in E
can be subtracted using these controls. It then has zero fresh response
and can enter the physical kernel flow of Section 2a. The register's
arbitrary dirty component is retained. The control must exist at U at
that actual time; source support containment by itself does not provide
the control or pay to expose it.

More generally, eliminate fresh components in E before choosing the
outgoing basis. The relevant ranks become the ranks modulo E,

    d_E = dim(row(C)+E)-dim(E),
    r_E = dim(row(D)+E)-dim(E).

If all required components in E are supplied by the actual source
controls at U, the minimal additional dirty-coordinate count is
max(0,t+d_E-r_E-n). Components subtracted from outgoing forms are
restored through those same controls before the forms leave U. This
quotient changes the fresh rank problem; it does not remove any frame
transition needed to make a source control physically available.

With such controls the complete scalar auxiliary map is affine-linear
in the original inputs:

    z_final = M z_initial + L x,       M invertible.

At the full frame, after K has been undone on the original X bank,
apply z <- M^(-1)(z-Lx). The exact new M and L include every controlled
erasure and every initial injection. Thus the new cleanup restores all
arbitrary z. Reusing an old M^(-1) while omitting the controlled erasures
would be incorrect.

3. Sources, phases, centers, and late target reads: zero-gauge reconstruction

Allocate every independent auxiliary input at frame 0. Before any
source injection, subtract the complete old auxiliary response from
the target bank at frame 0. This is a fixed scalar matrix, computed from
the new literal word; an old compiler's response must not be reused.

Each original source injects once into its true rank-one auxiliary
entrance while the original source itself remains at its rank-one
frame. All such injections precede the scalar producer. Original-source
K mixing and its restoration follow the inherited completed-core
chronology. A later row demand is supplied either by an independent
arbitrary row that has waited at frame 0 or by a compatible zero-fresh
kernel row from Section 2a. Neither case creates a new input during the
computation.

The first chronological phase includes all dependencies of the copied
centers. Each center is evaluated as an exact scalar form of live rows
at its retained center frame U_i. The retained copied-center interface
pays dim(U_i) to expose that value at frame 0, and its scalar scatter
occurs before any target leaves frame 0. The total extra rank is ell.

The second phase contains the side response and follows the retained
nested target-read chains. A side form computed early must be held
through the center cut at an admissible actual frame. Such bridge rows
are physical boundaries in the compiler and are charged. A scalar
expression cannot be read from a row after the row has advanced to an
incompatible frame. The compiler records the live basis used for every
read. Center and side forms evaluated before an outgoing basis change
need not become separate persistent physical root registers.

4. Arbitrary-input identity and cleanup: zero-gauge reconstruction

Let z be the vector of all new independent arbitrary auxiliary inputs.
The complete transcript, including both the initial rank-one injections
and all later original-source controls, has final auxiliary response
Mz+Lx for a known M in GL_R(K). Its actual scalar transcript, not only
its ranks, determines every intermediate read.
Let A be the total target response of this transcript to z, including
the copied-center scatter, and let B be its source response.

The initial subtraction y <- y-Az followed by the literal producer
therefore leaves y+Bx, independently of z. Once all reads are complete,
raise every live and retired row to the full active frame F. Restore K
on the original X bank, then apply z_final <- M^(-1)(z_final-Lx).
Equivalently, reverse every actual auxiliary gate and source-controlled
injection in the new transcript at F, using the restored original X
as controls. The auxiliary logical contents are exactly z. Subtracting
only the initial injection Vx after M^(-1) would be valid only in the
uncontrolled special case L=MV. This is an all-column algebraic identity; a
random scalar replay can check an implementation but does not replace
this identity or the frame checks.

The required scalar identity remains K+B=I for the chosen paired-cube
producer. Its original sources, target endpoints, center ranks, and
the exact completed-core action are inherited only after this equality
and the above chronology are verified for the new transcript.

All entrance gauges in the selected complex reconstruction are zero. Old compensated
aliases, old physical gauge tails, and terminal-sink deductions are
not carried over as numerical discounts. They require separate new
proofs if ever reintroduced.

For the selected bit construction, SOURCE_ALIGNED_BIT_WORD.txt proves
that the high boundary and suffix, including every gauged future
response column, remain literal. That separate boundary proof retains
3,960 gauged roles and 1,760 compensated aliases. The 2,200 unpaired
rank20 gauges retain their existing nonzero-frame old-value corrections
and their width60 exterior completion children. Only the nongauged
entrance response is recomputed at target frame zero. A paired donor's
changed complement is accepted by its actual compensated old-value read.
The new low XOR templates are inverted with their source controls, and
the retained high word is cleaned by its inherited exact contract.
The all-at-zero old-response argument above is not substituted for this
nonzero-gauge response mechanism.

5. The paid rank telescopes; the entropy need not

In the zero-gauge complex reconstruction, every new physical auxiliary begins at frame 0, follows a genuine
ascending chain, and ends at the full h-dimensional active frame.
Its chain therefore pays total rank h. Copied centers add ell. Thus,
for the actual local internal histogram H,

    sum_r r H_r = h R + ell.

Let D and T be the actual source- and target-data histograms, each
of total rank v(h-1). The retained three completed cores, shared over
orthogonal active blocks, give

    m = 3h,
    W = 2v + R,
    n_r = 3(H_r + D_r + T_r) + 2v [r=2].

All complex auxiliary gauges are zero, so there is no gauge-completion tail.
The exact global deficit is

    Delta = mW - sum_r r n_r = 2v - 3ell.

For the selected bit word, let sigma_j be the nonzero entrance-gauge
rank for each live, unpaired gauge. The corresponding identities are

    sum_r r H_r = h R + ell - sum_j sigma_j,
    n_r = 3(H_r + D_r + T_r) + 2v [r=2]
          + sum_j [r=3 sigma_j].

The exterior tails restore exactly the missing rank mass, so the same
Delta=2v-3ell holds. Here there are 2,200 terms with sigma_j=20.
The complete bit child histogram includes all 2,200 width60 children.

A reduction in R lowers both the denominator and total rank. It is
not by itself a factor-of-R exponent improvement. The relevant test
at a proposed saving a>0 is the complete normalized moment

    Phi(a) = sum_r n_r (r/m)^(1-a) / W < 1,

equivalently

    sum_r n_r r ((m/r)^a - 1) < Delta.

Every initial birth, boundary transition, retired complement, source
transition, delayed read, copied center, and final exterior child must
appear in this histogram. A local rank ratio cannot replace this test.

6. What a global claim additionally requires

The complex construction retains exact binary Clifford frames and their
rank-zero endpoint reconciliation. The bit construction retains its
actual rational nondegenerate frames and weighted q-adic normal forms.
The bit supplier must pay exceptional residue classes and the inherited
atom stopping conversion; the complex supplier must support the phase
stopping parameter. The fixed-tape router, row stock, scalar coefficient
work, precision and all-size semantic bridge remain part of the claim.

For a successful profile, rational upper bounds on the moments and the
complete 47-constraint balanced assembly will be supplied separately.
The target remains kappa=0.0009218338, twice the reviewed public
baseline 0.0004609169. A finite numerical root is a discovery statistic,
not the selected final theorem exponent.
