## 5. Complete time and retained histories

Let E_g be the six-Gamma header length. All first-five parameters
`f,r,P,S,b` are at most V_p. The domain has `a>=9f>=9`, hence V_p>=2048.
With `m=floor(log2 V_p)>=11`, their five Gamma lengths total at most
`10m+5<=2^m<=V_p`. The middle inequality holds at m=7 and persists by
induction: increasing m adds 10 on the left and at least 128 on the right.
The cutoff Gamma length is `2 ell(cutoff)-1`, whereas the old cutoff field
has length `2 ell(cutoff)+1`. Thus, even for arbitrarily large cutoff,

\[
 E_g\le V_p+E.
\]

The inherited bridge bill includes both directions, all 9f forward and
9f reverse address passes, codec scans, guide construction, complete source
access, original-owner commit, cleanup and head returns:

\[
 T_b\le2^{20}((9f+1)V_o+E+1).
\]

The inherited literal child bill includes header access, guard installation,
source clone, every one of f axes, numerical work, full-bank transport,
same-owner commit, and private/guard retirement:

\[
 T_c\le2^{16}(fV_p+E_g+1).
\]

The two changed continuations do not add a transition. Therefore

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 T&=T_b+T_c\\
  &\le2^{20}(10fV_o+E+1)
       +2^{16}(\tfrac23 fV_o+E+1)\\
  &\le2^{24}(fV_o+E+1).
 \end{aligned}
\]

Here `f+1<=2f` and `V_p<V_o/3`. This is a complete direct-order local
service bill. Transport or acquisition *before* the supplied old entry
heads, the right/left monomials, and enclosing owner dispatch remain
separate caller work; they are not included as zero-cost operations.

The bridge and child each give an incremental historical-backing allowance
of 64 times their own volume plus header plus one. Their public packed bank
and header are the bridge's existing private owners; the child adds only
27 fresh owners. Counting the inherited upper bounds additively may
overcount shared cells but is safe. Hence

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 K_{after}-K_{entry}
 &\le64(V_o+E+1)+64(V_p+E_g+1)\\
 &\le64(\tfrac53V_o+2E+2)\\
 &\le128(V_o+E+1).
 \end{aligned}
\]

On reentry the history is the union of actual absolute cell intervals with
the entry history. A blank write or erased guard does not refund backing.
The bounds include endpoints and guards. The verifier records the minimum
and maximum visited positions, including reads and all unit moves, on
every owner; its complete finite histories lie within this allowance.

The bridge also supplies a graph-specific lower bound: its 18f address
passes erase and rewrite PACK, for at least `36f V_p>=8f V_o` writes. The
old E bits are actually read. Thus this particular joined graph has

`u=rho=0`, `cutoff=f`, `p=h=t=0` and `b=18f+3`; these also satisfy the
common-root numerical specialization.

This rules out assigning the unchanged graph a pure

It is not a lower bound on every codec or every multiplication algorithm.
The selected nonleaf tariff includes other parameters and logarithmic
