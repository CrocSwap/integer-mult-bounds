# Gauge44 attribution and scope

New contributions by icekylinx, with OpenAI GPT-6 Astra assistance.
Local review and packaging used OpenAI Codex. Apache-2.0 applies;
the license is included. The full original PR635 notice is preserved as
UPSTREAM-NOTICE.md and construction/UPSTREAM-NOTICE.md.

The new contribution is the changed source-branch gauge and retired-row
completion, source-carried coordinate totals, exact 44-cold-end realization,
and explicit all72 whole-family assignment using concave capacity matching
with integer majorization certificates. The center-carrier generalization
retains the exact ordered common frames and complete native local contract.
Independent local, price, interface and owner fixed-point checks are included.

The construction retains the credited PR635 K-triangle and porting machinery,
earlier joint-prefix/HPE/frame ancestry and icekylinx's prior factory work.
#645 used the same cited PR543 bit supplier as PR635. This package (research/gauge44-leaver8) cites the merged PR589 bit supplier instead, with a derived rank-mass deficit (PROOF-NOTE-BIT.md); #645's retained PR543-based evidence is historical. It builds #645's word without K-triangles (PartialK) and stacks earlier community jp8 layers on it (G44Stack), adds the p6n ends (NoTriReopen), entrance promotions (BlockInvert) a read-set exchange (BRoute), chafreaky's #741 frames, JointXF's joint exchange, NewBArch's triangle-hybrid B rewrite and LeaverSAT's eight k8 leaver cells (with LeaverSAT2's frame re-optimisation); see README. All-size, realization,
hosting, precision/tape, analytic-transfer and bit H1-H5 assumptions remain
conditional. No upstream license, credit, source pin or AI disclosure is
replaced by this notice.

PR641 and PR642 were checked as concurrent reported frontier comparisons.
Their endpoints and owner maps are not included here. See CONSTRUCTION.md,
hosting/PROOF.md, independent/ and README.md for the exact contribution scope.
