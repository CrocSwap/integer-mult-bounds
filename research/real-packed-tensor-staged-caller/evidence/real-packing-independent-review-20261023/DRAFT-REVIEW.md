## Changed methods and exact widths

`pack_real` reads both real/imaginary words of each converted input, checks the two imaginary words are zero, and writes the two real words as real/imaginary components of h. The operands remain independent. Both original forward CRT calls are retained. The source ingress and contraction bounds supply the packed half-disk premise; zero-imaginary checks alone are not a general magnitude admission for arbitrary external banks.

For one J axis with s groups of g bits each, the pointer sequence is correct. It copies group0, advances to the block end, then copies groups s−1,...,1 in descending group order while preserving the g bits within each group. It ends precisely at the next block boundary. Across each prefix block, output therefore equals coordinate negation on that axis. Source axes are positive and the actual D is positive, so the final route bank is distinct from H. Each intermediate route lease is cleared only after its replacement has been completed. H itself is preserved and rewound.

The routing loop makes `(5s−4)g` source moves and writes sg output bits per block, including source moves performed by copy_bits.
