## (a) Lemma Q4 (two-7-flat leftover fact), 15 rows
ClassI (slack 7), `P` = pieces of weight 7, `S` = the other pieces, `c_S = sum g(S) <= 13`, `|P|-2 <= d-1`: each pair of `P` gives a root `r_ab` (Circuit Lemma, E8 version, as in #2985); the roots are distinct and a covered root needs a minimal leftover of size 8 (`Q`) or 12 (`M`) inside `E_S`. Hence `C(|P|,2) <= c_S` (c_S<=7), `c_S+4` (<=11), `c_S+20` (<=13). The finite facts (non-root minimal leftover 14; for a covered root the minimal leftovers of size <=13 are 315 Q (F2 rank 4, each Q is the leftover of 4 roots, 9450 Q in all) and 5040 M (219240 in all), no root inside its own leftover, max 4 roots served by `E = M` or `M+{l}`) are recomputed by three implementations (`checks/g777`: A = lo2/nq/m13, B = implB/implB2, audit = a2_g777/a2b_m13). Kills: rows 18, 582-595. `checks/new_kills.py` applies the criterion; it also holds for 19 more rows (103-111, 249-258), which are not in the audited list and not claimed here (they are closed in (c) anyway).

## (b) Count lemma and Dim-Cover E6, 7 rows

Rows 168, 324, 325, 328, 457: every scenario is killed by IMM/COUNT/RRC (`checks/e6/asm.py`) except those needing the six flats `(6,6,5,5,5,4)` to cover the 84 lines outside E6 (`sum g = 84`); that cover does not exist (`tight.py`, and independently `cov2.py` via `verify_e6.py`). **Not claimed (CONDITIONAL, single implementation or open covers):** rows 169, 332, 333, 464, 424, 426, 427 (E6-slack covers, one implementation only).
