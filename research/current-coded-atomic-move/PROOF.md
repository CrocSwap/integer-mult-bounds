Each record contains r>=1 complex coefficients. Each real/imaginary component
has h>=2 least-significant-first two's-complement bits and delimiter 2. Each

    b=record_bits=6r(h+1), row_bits=2^a b, V=C_rows*2^a*b.

The root common-grid theorem, actual producer conditions such as H=dG and

separate premises when this local service is used there.

The numerical admission domain requires every phase negation selected by the
two monomials to fit the signed h-bit component and every complete C pair
division and intermediate result to be exact and signed-representable. Final
output representability by itself does not imply this domain. These are the
inherited arithmetic guards; this wrapper does not round or repair a failure.

A sufficient, conservative common-grid condition is that every input integer
component is divisible by 2^f and has absolute value strictly below
2^(h-f-1). A monomial preserves the component bound and divisibility. Each C
stage uses four signed summands divided by two, so it doubles the component
bound at most and spends one binary divisibility bit. After k stages the
components remain divisible by 2^(f-k) and bounded by 2^k times the initial
bound. All f stages and both monomials therefore fit strictly inside signed
range. The exact guard domain is larger; the finite fixture uses that larger
domain and is checked pair by pair by the actual kernel.

## 3. Complete-coordinate algebra and current geometry

For each j in 0,...,f-1, its rank-nine column consists of current address bits

    p_(i,j) = u+(if+j)K+rho,  0<=i<9.

The monomial predecessor applies the source affine map and its Gaussian phase
independently to every such column. Its old low-bit residue is K-1-rho.
Its folded suffix comprises complete original coefficient fibres; no original
coefficient, delimiter, spectator, row or outer-row label is omitted.

The leaf receives the **same unchanged descriptor**. Its selected interval is
the first fK-bit vector interval beginning at u. It acts at
u+jK+rho, precisely p_(0,j). Every other address bit, including all other eight
vector intervals and the front/back reservations, is a complete spectator.
The leaf visits these disjoint factors in reversed column order after the
low-bit conversion; they commute. It therefore implements C on coordinate
zero in every rank-nine column, with the exact positive normalization above.

Let U be the authenticated original frame transition. The source lowering gives

    U = M_left (C tensor I_8) M_right.

Tensor functoriality on the f disjoint selected columns now gives

    M_left^[f] C_0^[f] M_right^[f] = U^(tensor f).

This is an equality of linear maps on the complete 9f selected factor,
tensored with identity on every spectator; hence it holds on dirty,
correlated and entangled input coefficients as well. No input independence is
needed for the numerical equality. Both monomials already repeat their
constant phase per column. In particular the right constant 1 contributes
i^f, not one factor i independent of f. C's half denominator and absolute
phase are retained. No projective-phase identification is used.

## 4. Protected execution and named failure return

`AtomicMove.bind` partitions the fixed private inventory into the one raw
staging port and disjoint complete entrance, leaf and exit cohorts. Each
provider sees the staging port as its current field; it never receives the
public original. Its own work roles are distinct from that field and the
descriptor, as required by its predecessor. The three provider inventories
are not reused as if erased backing were free capacity.

The executed sequence is:

1. Use the retained administrative `transfer` to copy all V physical data
   bits into the initially blank stage and restore both heads.
2. Run the right monomial, the positive leaf, then the left monomial on that
   complete stage and the same read-only descriptor.
3. Only after all three return successfully, copy all stage bits back over
   the same-length public field. Restore the public and stage heads.
4. Physically retire every stage byte, restoring its head. Each factor has
   already retired every byte in its own complete private cohort.

Administrative copying preserves encoded zeros and delimiters literally;
it is not a free arithmetic COPY and is fully charged in section 5. Runtime
control reads only physical symbols and moves one cell at a time. It does not
inspect a host address, extent, payload array or decoded numerical value.

On admitted input, induction over the three completed services gives the
endpoint of section 3, unchanged length/format/header, restored field/context
origins and clean completed private cohorts. This proves the conditions for
the commit loop: every source and target position contains a physical bit,
and their blank frontiers coincide. The final retirement erases the entire
protected copy including all physical codewords.

For a named signed-phase or Gaussian parity/headroom rejection on otherwise
valid input, the failing provider preserves its entrance stage, restores its
field/context heads and cleans its own cohort. Earlier factors have already
returned and cleaned; later factors have not run. The public original has
not been written at all. The final retirement erases the stage at its returned
origin. The original complete public word, descriptor and every ancestor are
unchanged; every owned private body is blank and can be reused. A valid-format
f>cutoff rejection at the positive leaf has the same outer staging conclusion.

This is the precise atomicity claim. A corrupted or truncated supposedly
successful result could make the late commit fail after writing a prefix;
hardware interruption can do likewise. Neither is a named inherited
arithmetic rejection, and arbitrary malformation/crash rollback is not claimed.
The protected-stage blankness and origin premises are necessary even before
the first transfer.

## 5. Complete physical bill and retained backing

Count one raw physical read, write or unit move as one action. On a contiguous
binary body of V bits at its origin, the retained functions give:

| Administrative step | Physical actions |
|---|---:|
| Borrowed field to blank stage, with both heads restored | 8V+14 |
| Successful same-length commit, with both heads restored | 9V+14 |
| Stage retirement from its returned origin | 5V+7 |

For example `origin` from displacement L>=0 costs 2L+3 actions. Transfer pays
its two starting origins, blank-target check, every source read/target write/
unit move, and both terminal rewinds. The commit similarly pays both complete
rewinds and its two frontier checks. Retirement includes all encoded bytes.
Thus the new wrapper contributes exactly **22V+35** on successful return and
**13V+21** on the named rejection path after a factor restores the stage head.
These constants exclude the executed factor bills, not their shared-field
accesses. A direct independent arithmetic count and executable administrative
controls check the formulas.

Let T_R,T_C,T_L be the complete current-physical bills of the three factor
providers, including their stage and descriptor accesses, format conversions,
source construction, setup, restoration, private cleanup and their native
children. The complete successful invoice is

    F_join + T_R + T_C + T_L + 22V+35.

F_join is the positive fixed source/port preparation cost. A rejecting invoice
includes each actually executed complete factor bill and 13V+21. No unfinished
factor or failed scan is free. Each current factor already reads and charges
the full E-bit descriptor, including otherwise unused large metadata.

For the leaf,

    T_C = F_C + O(fV+(E+1)^4+349),  f<=cutoff.

It is a direct positive leaf, with no ordinary call. For each monomial retain
its **entire** predecessor invoice. Write s=a-z-(f-1)G, a'=z+(f-1)G,
R=2hr*2^s, M=C_rows*2^a', v0=2^a'R, and
A=2+a'+ceil(log2(R+1)). If its affine word has g_E early and g_L late gates,
g=g_E+g_L and S=4g_E+12g_L, its old-symbol bill for f>1 is

    S * sum_(row=1)^C_rows T_B(v0,(f-1)G)
      + O_9((g+1)*(Vraw+M*A^6)
             +g*delta*M*A*(R+A)),

where Vraw=C_rows*v0 and
delta=min(1,(f-1)*(2*2^(-G)+8*2^(G-K))). Apply the predecessor's fixed
whole-source codec factor, add O_9(V+(E+1)^4+Q), and its explicit
64*(Z+Q) retirement allowance for this call's reached extents. At f=1 the
ordinary and exceptional terms disappear; the complete affine, phase,
metadata, conversion and retirement work remains. The exact source census is

| Factor | Early gates | Late gates | Total gates | Ordinary calls per row, f>1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Right entrance | 5 | 6 | 11 | 92 |
| Left exit | 5 | 7 | 12 | 104 |

Thus the two factors together retain 196 ordinary calls per outer-row when
f>1. At f=1 both counts are zero. Neither a finite fixture nor this join
strengthens the inherited ordinary exponent 99830289/100000000.

For clarity, these are worst-case source bounds, separate from any measured
fixture. They can be written without asymptotic notation by retaining the
fixed positive constants from the predecessor proofs. For i in {R,L}, let
g_R=11, g_L=12, S_R=92, S_L=104. Choose fixed program constants c_old,i,
c_codec,i, c_wrap,i, c_reach,i that dominate the corresponding proved source,
codec, wrapper and reached-extent bounds. Their existence and independence of
all runtime parameters are inherited premises, not measured from the traces.
Define

    B_i = c_old,i*((g_i+1)*Vraw+M*A^6),                    f=1,
    B_i = S_i*sum_rows T_B(v0,(f-1)G)
          +c_old,i*((g_i+1)*(Vraw+M*A^6)
                     +g_i*delta*M*A*(R+A)),              f>1,
    B_C = c_old,C*f*Vraw,
    U_i = F_i + c_codec,i*B_i
          +c_wrap,i*(V+(E+1)^4+Q_i)
          +64*(c_reach,i*(B_i+V+(E+1)^4+Q_i)+Q_i).

Use the actual constructed Q_R,Q_L and Q_C=349, with the leaf's own fixed
constants for U_C. The exercised capacity-64 template has Q_R=Q_L=1878.
Each complete factor is bounded by its U_i. Hence a finite symbolic upper
bound for the successful whole service is

    F_join+U_R+U_C+U_L+22V+35.

This states the complete inherited constant-dependent bound explicitly. It
does not claim an extracted numerical value for the predecessor program
constants or a supplied numerical ordinary-service ceiling. The literal
finite action counts below are exact observations on one input and are not
used as replacements for any of these worst-case bounds.

The constructor-fixed inventory is 1+Q_R+349+Q_L private ports, equal to
4106 for the exercised capacity-64 binary template. The capacity-65 control
has Q_R=Q_L=1884 and 4118 total ports, so 4106 is not a universal constant for
every ordinary constructor. No per-input tape allocation is introduced.
Fresh occupied/
backing bounds add the actual public field V and header E, the new V-bit
stage with constant frontier/origin allowance, and **both** monomial cohorts'
and the leaf cohort's source backing allowances. Summing the per-family
allowances is safe; taking only the largest active family would forget the
other families' retained blank allocations. After repeated calls, each role
is charged by its historical maximum reached extent. The new stage similarly
retains max_previous V plus its fixed origin/frontier allowance. Erasure
does not shrink backing. A smaller later call stops at its own fresh blank
frontier and pays only its current V; it does not rescan an older blank tail.

## 6. Evidence, model limits and remaining work

`check_move.py` independently evaluates the original source P/D/C circuit on
all 512 basis columns, covering all 262,144 matrix coefficient positions and
all absolute phases. It never computes the expected complete answer by
executing the lowered monomial/C/monomial recipe. The source's direct matrix
routine is a second comparison. The resulting sparse matrix has exactly
1,024 nonzero entries, each with the exact half denominator.
The literal monomial/positive-C/monomial map is then compared with this
independent original-source oracle on all 512 columns. Every zero and
nonzero position and absolute phase agrees. Four changed-phase, reversed-C,
wrong-axis and omitted-exit controls are rejected by that complete matrix
comparison and separately by the full reference-packet comparison.

The smallest permitted local fixture still has 4,096 original records:
H>=1, f,K>=1, u>=2H, z>=u+9fK and a>=z+H imply a>=12. The proposed
512/1024-record fixtures would violate the published adapter's reservation
guards and were rejected as a test design, not reported as reductions.
H=G=K=1 is a reduced **local** descriptor; it is not the actual producer's
H=dG, K=128G allocation. Even a complete successful local packet would not
execute that enclosing caller.

Exact completed finite evidence and interrupted attempts are recorded in
`VERIFICATION.md` and generated receipts. An interrupted run is not a
completed whole-MOVE witness. Any bounded factor-wise evidence is labelled
sliced execution and joined by exact intermediate bytes; it is not described
as a completed uninterrupted process. The all-width theorem above instead
uses the explicit predecessor contracts and current interface join.

The actual physical evidence in this package is **partial**: the complete
4,096-record entrance and leaf slices passed, as did a separately seeded
reachable final-pair leaf rejection. The exit slice timed out with no
complete receipt. Therefore no complete three-factor sliced witness and no
uninterrupted whole-MOVE receipt is claimed. The exact original-source matrix
factorization and reviewed predecessor-contract composition establish the
scoped mathematical statement independently of that unfinished finite run.

The sliced join has a state equivalence stronger than matching payload
hashes. The returned stage and unchanged descriptor are compared byte for
byte with the complete next-slice inputs, with their heads at their origins.
The original public field and every protected ancestor agree. Completed
providers have closed engines, empty live-role pools and completely blank
private bodies, with every private head at its origin. The next provider's
cohort and finite control are still unused at the actual composite boundary,
so they agree with its fresh slice state. Earlier completed providers may
retain different diagnostic call counters and historical highwater values;
their disjoint roles are never read again by this invocation. These values
are observations, not numerical inputs or runtime control read by a later
provider. Reinsert their blank retained tails into the fresh slice state:
the later sequence of literal actions and numerical result is unchanged.
The corresponding backing invoice is the componentwise maximum extent of
each fixed role over all compatible slices, then the sum over roles. Taking
the maximum total slice size would omit other cohorts' retained backing.

The recorded arrays measure materialized cells, not the largest head position
ever visited: a unit move can traverse an implicit blank without extending
the array. The receipt therefore gives separately the exact rolewise union
of materialized extents and a conservative upper bound on all visited cells.
Start from the fixed local origins, one blank private body cell per role,
and the complete public/header regions with a frontier cell. Each additional
visited cell requires at least one literal unit move, and hence at least one
action. Adding the complete joined action count to those initial regions
is a valid, deliberately loose visited-backing upper bound. No asymptotic
space improvement is inferred from it; the predecessor's per-role source
backing proof remains the size-dependent bound used in section 5.

`physical_hardware.py` changes only Python observation overhead. Its legal
read, write and unit-move definitions have the same state transition and
counter increments as the retained primitive hardware, as can be inspected
directly. The 30,000-command comparison is finite regression evidence for
those literal definitions; it is not a new implementation theorem, bulk tape
operation or algorithmic time improvement.

The controller remains a source-level structured finite-control refinement.
No literal `Controller.execute_table` export is supplied. Enclosing caller
token normalization, live placement/stock, the producer's common-grid proof,
full ordinary tariff, nonleaf recursion, analytic recovery and complete
selected multiplication admission remain open. All five selected assumptions
and the selected certificate are unchanged. No new kappa follows.
