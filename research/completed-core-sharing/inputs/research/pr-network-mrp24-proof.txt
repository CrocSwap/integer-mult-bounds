Explicit construction

Points are (g,a) with g in{0,1,2,3}, a inZ6, encoded6g+a.
For each u,v inZ6, take the six disjoint quadruples
  {(0,a),(1,a+u),(2,-a+v),(3,a+u+v)}  (a inZ6).
These36 parallel classes cover exactly the triples meeting three distinct
six-point groups: the fourth coordinate obeys d=a+b+c, hence is uniquely
determined by any three coordinates.

Use the five perfect matchings F_j of K6 from the circle formula:
  F_j={(5,j),(j+1,j-1),(j+2,j-2)}, with arithmetic modulo5
on the latter endpoints. For a paired pair of six-point groups g,h, blocks
F_j[k] in g union F_j[k+s] in h, k inZ3, cover every triple of type(2,1)
once over j=0,...,4 and s inZ3. Apply this simultaneously to group matchings
{02,13} and{03,12}, giving30 more full classes. The first66 classes leave
only the two independent twelve-point sets01 and23.

On each twelve-point set apply the14 pair classes (j,s) except(0,0), then
three trade classes indexed t inZ3. A trade class consists of unions of
F_0[t] and F_0[t+1] inside each six-point set, and the cross-set union of
F_0[t+2] with its counterpart. Synchronizing the two twelve-point sets
gives17 more full classes, for a total83. The only uncovered triples choose
one endpoint of each of the three F_0 pairs inside one six-point set.

There are8 such triples in each of4 six-point sets. Pair each with its
disjoint complement, producing4 pairs per set. Combine corresponding pairs
from the four disjoint sets into4 groups of8 triples. Every pair of triples
in these last groups is disjoint, so its binary inner product is zero.

Every full class has6 disjoint quadruples. The four triple faces of one
quadruple have pairwise intersection2, and triples from different blocks
are disjoint. Thus all87 groups have binary Gram matrix I. The exhaustive
checker verifies exact coverage of every lexicographic triple, not just
the counts, and all48064 Gram entries.

Why83 full tetrahedron classes is maximal

Each point is incident to253 triples, so r full parallel classes cover3r
of its triples and r<=84. If r=84, the leave has degree1 at every point,
and is eight disjoint triples. But each pair belongs to22 original triples,
and a quadruple covers its incident triples two at a time, so every pair
must have even leave-degree. A disjoint-triple leave has pair-degree1 on
its edges, a contradiction. This is only a maximality statement for full
parallel quadruple classes, not optimality of the final physical compiler.

Preferred equal-cost signed-basis variant

pr-network-mrp24-signed.json, SHA256
9117d3f010354fa2f32d5c5fb324b280977e82e76060a70a867767a90989ccaa,
replaces only the last four groups. Instead of pairing a transversal triple
with its disjoint complement, pair it with the triple obtained by toggling
the endpoint of F_0[0]. For fixed choices c,d in the other two F_0 pairs,
and F_0[0]={a,b}, the two triples are{a,c,d} and{b,c,d}. These are two
faces of a tetrahedron. The other faces{a,b,c},{a,b,d}, together with the
two unused singleton coordinates of the six-point set, form a signed
orthonormal complement. Repeat in all four six-point sets.

Each of the four rank16 complements therefore has an explicit basis of
eight weight3 and eight weight1 vectors. The JSON includes their supports.
The generator checks the combined24-vector Gram matrix equals I exactly.
Both variants have identical counts and correction ranks; the signed
variant uses the already-audited signed orthonormal interface, avoiding the
wholly alternating16-space in the initial disjoint-pair variant.

The actual mod4 phase closure has a direct four-coordinate check. For any
bit vector x on one tetrahedron, the sum over its four faces T of
3*parity(T dot x) equals wt(x) modulo4. This follows immediately by checking
wt(x)=0,1,2,3,4; the numbers of odd faces are respectively0,3,2,1,4.
Thus a full tetrahedron class has exactly the full chirp, with no residual
linear character. In a partial group, its two faces plus the two displayed
complementary faces have the same identity, and the two unused singleton
directions supply their ordinary positive phases. This establishes phase
closure for the signed variant in both tensor orientations; conversion of
negative one-direction chirps still retains the inherited sign adapters.
