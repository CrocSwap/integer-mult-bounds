Standalone finite proof
-----------------------
pr-foundations-mrp-phase.py takes the partition JSON as its sole input and
uses only the standard library. It verifies coverage of every triple,
every full 24-vector Gram matrix, and all actual quadratic phase identities
on zero, every coordinate vector and every pair of coordinate vectors.
These 301 values determine a weight-of-linear-map quadratic modulo four.
The checker deliberately does not pin the SHA before checking geometry;
the separate interface/certificate pins the accepted input.

Each of the four partial groups is supplied with a 16-vector complement:
eight vectors of weight three and eight unit vectors. Together with its
eight group triples these form an actual orthonormal basis of F2^24.
The checker independently verifies that fact and the exact projections,
rather than trusting a claimed rank. In those coordinates the complement
operator is eight C^-1 directions and eight C directions. The exact
identity C^-1=-i Z C Z implements it using one positive C16 child,
paid diagonal Z signs and paid binary orthogonal basis adapters. Since
(-i)^8=1, the scalar phase is already one before tensoring with the other
24-dimensional factor. Both stage orientations and parallel columns give
one width-384 child with no new scalar, odd divisor or recursive rank.

Full groups span F2^24. Their accumulated actual phases equal the full
weight modulo four, so their complement operation is the identity and
has no child. Actual phase checks matter: treating the weight-three
directions as positive C would not implement the required operator.
The exact 16-by-16 Gaussian tests also verify orthogonal composition and
C_F C_(A-perp)^-1=C_A, and reject the wrong stage-two source sign.

A corruption control swaps one triple between two full groups. This
preserves group sizes and exact coverage but breaks their Gram matrix.
The generic checker rejects it at 'non-orthonormal completed group basis',
not at a hash comparison. The canonical receipt stays unchanged. The
control is recorded in pr-foundations-mrp-control.json.
