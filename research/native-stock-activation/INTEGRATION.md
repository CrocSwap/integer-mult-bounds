## One complete window move and its inverse

Let $P$ be the actual private grid of the global arithmetic, $s'=\lceil\log_2(P+2)\rceil$, and $G=4s'+6$. For every native pack of size $h$ in the full regime, the entire original width $K'=\ell_i$ is common to all its members. The selected significance can be its highest bit. Freeze $r=\lfloor s/2\rfloor$ high bits of one outside axis and process each resulting full numerical field with $L'=L-r$ bits of address. The chosen axis exists since $h<d$, and has at least $64s^2$ bits. Removing these $r$ bits leaves the $h$ original windows contiguous in exactly their order. Every value of the spectator label has independent data.

A single slab needs the following consecutive bit ranges, from high to low: the complete dyadic stock, two controls of $hG$ bits, the entire $hK'$ block, one back control of $hG$ bits, and $(s')^2$ trailing bits. Let $A=hK'$ and let $v=(\log_2 B_c)(2+\lceil\log_M(h+1)\rceil)$, where the installed complex planner supplies the power of two $B_c\le M^{448}$. The required length is $U=A+3hG+v+(s')^2$. The intact pack itself occupies $A$ bits in the original slab. A disjoint contiguous exterior of at least $(L'-A)/2$ exists, including for packs at the two boundaries. At a native entrance $s\ge512$, $K'\le128s^2$, $L\ge64ds^2$, $h\le d/(1024s)$ and $G\le6s$. Hence $A\le L/(512s)$ and $3hG\le18d/1024$. Also $\log_2 M\le s+1$ whenever $h\ge M$ and $v\le448(3\log_2 M+s+1)\le1800(s+1)$. The terms $v$ and $(s')^2$ fit in $8(s+1)^2$ on this range, which has $d\gg512$. These explicit inequalities give $U<L/4<(L'-A)/2$. The original and destination $A$-bit ranges are therefore disjoint. Choose the larger exterior, use its low end, and keep all untouched lower bits as part of the service suffix. ## The three CRTs, precision and the integer result

Each CRT uses the balanced tree on the actual ordered squares. At a cross-child update the restored left ordinal determines the right local offsets. The right switch is held fixed by the three reservoirs from a disjoint subtree or other right quarter. Exchange them with the two leading fields and the back field. At every color, perform the complete compact word. Record its bad cells, compute the inverse raw control word, sort by true target and put the changed payloads into the holes. Undo the original placements. Reverse level and row order for the inverse. The record permutation is independent of its data, so complete CRT stripes concatenate to the full numerical or coefficient word. The guarded singles include all omitted positions. At large $d$, only $O(\operatorname{poly}(s))$ tail positions per batch remain there and their control generation is amortized on the entire $2^{H_0}$-record back range. For the finite one-axis/small-tree branch, the capacity and $d<51200$ tests bound $s$ by an absolute constant. The elementary leaves there have finite cost. Give the first transform the digits divided by $4B$, where $B=2^b$, and zero all owned holes. Each axis executes the chirps, two stored rational root passes and exit scalar that approximate its own Fourier mean $F_{r_i}/r_i$. Conjugate input and output for the opposite mean. The three tensor means give two source-size factors in the product, and the two digit scalings give $(4B)^2$. The final recovery factor is $(4B S_*)^2$. The intermediate disk radii are at most $1/4$ at each exact forward, at most $1/2$ before the pointwise operation, and at most $1/2$ on input to the opposite transform. With $E=4096(L+6d)$ and $J_p=20000(d+1)(\ell+2)^6$, the executed working grid is $P=4p+8E+4\,\mathrm{bitlength}(J_p(d+1))+64$. Each mean's raw error is below $2^{-4p}$. Its exact native H uses an extra $h$ fractional bits and at most $2h+O(1)$ extra signed bits, including the exact odd divisions. After conversion by truncation toward zero the error per mean is at most $e=3/B^6$. Forward values in exact zero sectors truncate to zero. Park the first forward on this public grid. Read the second forward on the identical grid, use signed short-word products, and convert back for the last transform. Compression of the stored first forward moves exactly that conversion to the write boundary. Apply the two product-error terms and the opposite mean's contraction. Before integer rounding every error is at most $(4BS_*)^2(3e)\le144/B^2<1/2$. The inverse CRT, original-coordinate filter and radix carry emit the exact magnitude. Sign handling covers zero and either input order. All temporary banks and owned holes are scrubbed.
