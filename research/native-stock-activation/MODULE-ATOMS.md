# Ternary product atoms and the ordinary receiver

The six-stage binary address word exchanges the image of a split projector. A cyclic atom of width $w$ requires every lift of a ternary matrix to modulus $3^w$. Replace its $3^w$ address values by a product of smaller cyclic groups. One chart serves all components of the atom. Each resulting address still holds its own arbitrary payload. $$A=\prod_{j=1}^m \mathbb Z/(3^{b_j}).$$

## Sequential passes and the stopped pair

Consider the complete stream $[P][3^w][G_0][3^w][S_0]$. For the lower update $y\gets y+a x$ run one forward pass per component. If the component has width $b$ with $l$ preceding and $r$ following trits in each atom, use the original shift procedure with

$$\mathrm{prefixes}=P3^l,\qquad \mathrm{side}=3^b,\qquad
\mathrm{gap}=G_0 3^{w-b},\qquad \mathrm{piece}=S_0 3^r.$$

The middle field consists of the trailing source trits, the complete external gap and the leading target trits, in their existing order. At the exterior prefix generate $a\bmod 3^b$. For source component $z$ the target rotation is $az\bmod 3^b$. Spool its initial segment, emit its terminal segment, rewind, append the initial segment and erase the spool. Include returning the heads in the linear movement cost. The updates on different components commute. A constant number of spools suffice for all $m$. Negation uses one unary pass per component. It keeps the zero block fixed and emits the other blocks in reverse order while preserving their opaque suffixes. Its full tape travel and scratch erasure are linear. $$L(a^{-1}),\quad J,\quad L(-a),\quad J,\quad L(a^{-1}),\quad \text{negate the head}.$$

The first lower and middle upper updates give $(-at,t+a^{-1}h)$. The last lower leaves $a^{-1}h$ in the second position. The head negation produces $(at,a^{-1}h)$. ## Admission and complete ordinary price

Write $N=2^H$, $H=240000000000000$, and retain the six stages, $2\le B\le N^{105}$, and the previous machine allowance $J=N^{20000}$. Define

$$\rho=1/250,\qquad \delta=3/100,\qquad \eta=\rho(1-\delta)=97/25000,$$
$$E_1=2^{\lceil25000(2H+12)/24903\rceil},\qquad E_i=4096N^2E_{i-1}\quad(2\le i\le6),$$
$$R_1=1,\qquad R_i=E_{i-1}+1,\qquad
w=\max(\lceil e^\rho\rceil,R_i),\qquad
m=\min(\lceil w^\delta\rceil,\lfloor w/R_i\rfloor).$$

Here $e$ measures ternary digits in an ordinary field. Use the highest admitted stage up to the requested stage. If no such stage is available, use the elementary receiver. Stage zero is always elementary. Width zero is the identity. Write $w$ as the sum of $m$ nearly equal positive components. The first $w\bmod m$ have width $\lfloor w/m\rfloor+1$. Store just the two widths and their counts. The actual maximum is $u=\lceil w/m\rceil$. Integer powers and comparisons suffice to compute the ceilings. Then

$$m\le2w^\delta,\qquad u\ge R_i,\qquad
w\le(2u)^{100/97},\qquad u\le\max(2R_i,1+2e^\eta).$$

For the last bound, if $m=\lfloor w/R_i\rfloor$, then $w<(m+1)R_i$, so $u\le2R_i$. Otherwise $u\le1+w^{1-\delta}$. When $w=R_i$ there is one component of that width. These bounds include integers of the smallest admitted sizes.

At one fresh level the true stock is $W=BGH_u$ with $H_u=3^{N^2(u-1)}$. A root divisor $W^D$ leaves $W^{D-j}H_u^j$ rows per slab at depth $j$. Each child takes $1/(BG)$ of the parent's volume. The complete forward and inverse route, temporary singular cube, low split and merge use the original sequential algorithms on $u$. Keep the parent banks while each current child uses its own full padded volume. The active coarse chain is bounded by the geometric series with ratio $1/(BG)$. Multiply reserve divisors for all up to six simultaneous stages. Include both complete radix conversions on an earlier binary call. All lower roots have input width at most $e$ at a fast entry and have floors at most $R_i$. If $k$ is the ceiling of the total base-three stock logarithm, then

$$k\le 1+6(3+\log_Ne)N^2(1+\max(2R_i,1+2e^\eta)).$$

At $E_1$, $3+\log_NE_1<6$ and $E_1^{1-\eta}\ge4096N^2$. At $E_i$ for $i\ge2$, $3+\log_NE_i<16$, $R_i\le2E_{i-1}$, and $E_i^\eta\le E_{i-1}$ because $\log_NE_i<13$ and $13\eta<1$.
