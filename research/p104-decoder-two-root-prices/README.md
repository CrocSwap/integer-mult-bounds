# Classify all 784 two-root exchanges for the P104 f22 loose row

Extends #7917 and #7902 with two changed roots, the same fixed order-four f22 loose row and tested eight-root complement family. Full integer witnesses hold for 192 unit determinants; 12 determinant ±3 cases are certified modulo four.

Of 784 exchanged complements, 580 fail mod-2 coverage. The attained exact minima of the other 204 are 63, 64 and 65, in counts 36, 120 and 48.

Observed validation. A clean extraction verified the manifest, byte-identical coverage and price replay, a separate check of all 204 survivors and four corruption controls.

The source archive contains the exact pins, proof, licenses, commands and control outcomes.
