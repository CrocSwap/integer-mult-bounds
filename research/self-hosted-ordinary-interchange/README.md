This construction closes those calls inside one finite program. Different stopped words can call one another at decreasing widths until single-digit moves suffice. A word that earns no coarse saving can participate in a cycle that funds a complete multiplier. [Width and space proof](PROOF.md) · [Application and full costs](APPLICATION.md)

## Complete contract

Both fields and all spectator ranges carry arbitrary bits. The program calculates its row reserve, pads and restores it, preserves record offsets, returns every independent productive output and charges private cleanup. Balanced intervals of a genuine spectator make the active ordinary frames shrink in bit volume. The charged stopped-tree recurrence gives the exact characteristic upper exponent with a fixed power of the logarithm, and a pure power at the strict prices above. Visited backing is linear in the entry bit volume plus previously visited backing. The complex caller retains its full dyadic tensor, source precision and rational invoice. The proof re-prices simultaneous stock and all four functional guards for its changed BIT service. [Producer and consumer bindings](APPLICATION.md)

The selected-word example has a large eventual threshold. One of its high matrices alone needs `log₂(e) > 59,326,000,000,000,000` in an active cover. A four-stage finite alternation already reaches the pending tested price. One finite selected stage supplied by the stronger original pending service even exceeds the cyclic selected-entry price. This package proves a change to the recursive contract. The strongest comparable complete numerical proposal remains that in the four-return source.
