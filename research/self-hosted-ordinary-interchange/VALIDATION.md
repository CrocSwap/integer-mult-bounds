# Verification and objections

The proof is an induction on widths and stopped ranks. These finite checks test its mechanism, its two source applications and several ways the proof could fail. A reader can run the seven commands in [VERIFY.json](VERIFY.json) with CPython 3.12. No third-party library is needed.

| Check | What passes |
|---|---|
| [Complete streams](check_streams.py) | The original two tags on 158,219 labelled positions across the primary shapes. The maximum observed ordinary nesting below the root is two. Independent address formulas check arbitrary payload positions, two restored body calls, four weighted-leaf swaps, prime-radix and binary fields, foreign-cardinality padding, a high column swap, record positions and 15 ragged slabs. Inserting one or two fixed-count entries tests another 403,530 positions at every public tag. |
| [High matrix](check_routes.py) | A complete shear and inverse on all 48 low classes of GL₂ over F₃. An additional width exercises the second high digit, two old prefixes and three record positions. Independent reduction of the full matrix modulo 9 or 27 checks 97,686 positions. Both low-class changes and nonzero carries occur. |
| [Time and space](check_bounds.py) | An exactly critical weighted tree at 2,049 positive ranks, volume contraction on 1,125 shapes, a full-volume counterexample to width-only contraction, the fixed cost of unbatched parking, the necessary high-matrix fit, and three power-split descriptor traces and 1,536 fixed-count shapes with exact continuation tokens. |
| [Complete callers](check_application.py) | Twelve substitutions at six distinct service prices, each with both service budgets, 54 strict inequalities and four revised functional inequalities. ## What adversarial review changed

Three separate OpenAI Codex workers reviewed timing, space and the consumer binding. They used the same model family. Their numerical and finite checks are independent implementations where described below. They do not constitute independent human acceptance.

- A timing reviewer expanded a different exactly critical four-edge tree at 2,072 ranks. Two regenerated body costs at each square-root width made the normalized cost grow by 4,096 after 12 levels. The proof keeps a power of the logarithm at the characteristic. The reviewer also produced a four-stage finite competitor and a one-stage construction seeded by the pending backend. Both comparisons are in the application.
- A space reviewer exhibited an 8-digit to 2-digit call that preserves all 43,046,721 bits. Stable spectator splitting restored all 8,483 labelled positions in seven independent shapes. Separate alternating-word fixtures covered 128,681 labelled positions. Another check reconstructed ordinary record payloads in 54 shapes and 27,579 bits. A full-frame parking test charged quadratic work for linearly many tiny requests. The open-word conditions now charge frame suspensions. The high-factor schedule batches all equal old-prefix work, and the neutral entry pays for every entire-array request. At the actual neutral exponent, opaque continuation tokens checked the ragged returns at widths 2,000, 8,192 and 12,000. Those descriptor tests did not allocate the full arrays.
- A binding reviewer reconstructed the selected invoice and three successive pending rational invoices at both asymmetric entry prices, with the source-locked gate and physical depth calculation. The fourth historical functional inequality fixes the stock exponent at 1/250. In the asymmetric cycle the proof revalues it using 99/100. The revised inequality is positive and the increased outer stock fits after a larger polylogarithmic cutoff. Checking the old gate alone would miss this cost.

The final challenge checked fixed-count insertion separately. A timing reviewer tested 6,000 widths with block counts 2, 3 and 5, and detected a missing last block with an independent address oracle. A space reviewer exercised one, two and three intervening tags on 2,724,678 labelled positions. The reviewer also checked 7,232 descriptor configurations with block counts from 2 to 17 and a four-factor GL₃ route on 157,464 positions. The binding reviewer reran the pending source's original primary and independent finite binary verifiers. Both reproduced the corresponding archived JSON byte-for-byte. Their scope is the two finite certificates and the lower or primary supplier they certify. A separate original-gate replay at both full prices gave the same margins, all four functional tests, and 14,083 bits in the pending depth guard. A finite four-stage countercomparison using the actual cover headroom is recorded in the receipt.

## Limits

The reference interpreter uses Python memory. It does not measure tape-head actions or execute the final astronomical native templates. The fixed-tape statement comes from the explicit slab, local stream, dispatcher and geometric backing arguments under the listed source contracts. A finite calculation checks a supplied native invoice. It cannot establish the physical semantics of all its instructions. The complete multiplication implication uses the original five assumptions. This contribution supplies a different implementation of their ordinary movement interface. It does not assert separate proofs of any of the five.
