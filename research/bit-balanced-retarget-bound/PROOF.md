## Balance before the clock filter

Chart-count preservation forces equal in- and out-degree at each vertex in the selected multigraph, so every selected arc belongs to a directed cycle. It suffices for an upper bound to retain every arc with a return path in the full geometric graph. This admits arcs that cannot occur together and ignores competition for any slot.
