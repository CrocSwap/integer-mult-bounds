Exact statements and scope
==========================

All geometry is regenerated from the pinned leech-lattice source. Each
port has one block of squared norm 2 and two blocks of squared norm 1.
The scalar kernel multiplies the three real inner products. Grouping
ports by their profile and both low root lines leaves 8 orthogonal high
lines. These eight ports have no internal active edges. Two glue classes
are adjacent precisely when some concrete pair has nonzero scalar kernel.
A binary label constant on each class is therefore valid exactly when
it has odd diagonal and is orthogonal across every quotient edge.
The generator checks every concrete pair when building this existential
quotient; it does not sample or average the scalar kernel.

There are 960 classes in each of 3 profiles. The exact quotient degree
is 1136, giving 1,635,840 unordered edges. Its included 24-clique already
requires 24 independent odd orthogonal labels. The following stronger
certificate proves the sharp bound.

The sharp parity lower bound
---------------------------
PARITY-CERTIFICATE.json lists nine cliques of size 24. The verifier checks
all 4968 ordered adjacency incidences and 60 distinct vertices: 36 occur
twice and 24 occur six times. It independently checks all 1176 distinct
edges from the integer coordinates of concrete port pairs.

This gives a rank bound even for asymmetric block fitting matrices over
any field of characteristic 2. Factor such a matrix through dimension r,
using factors A_v of size d by r and B_v of size r by d. The fitting
conditions are A_v B_v=I_d and A_v B_w=0 across every ordered edge.
Set P_v=B_v A_v. These are rank-d idempotents, mutually annihilating on
each clique. For a clique C, Q_C=sum(P_v: v in C) is consequently an
idempotent of rank 24d, and R_C=I+Q_C has rank r-24d. Every vertex occurs
evenly across the nine cliques, so their R_C sum to I in characteristic 2.
Rank subadditivity gives

    r = rank(I) <= sum_C rank(R_C) = 9(r-24d),
    hence r >= 27d.

The existing scalar standard-dot fit has dimension 27, so the minimum
fitting rank in characteristic 2 is exactly 27; normalized block rank
cannot improve it. This includes arbitrary nonlinear class-constant
labels. It excludes dimension 25 throughout the quadratic family without
relying on SAT or on that parametrization. It does not exclude changed
scalar kernels, punctured port sets, or labels varying within a class.

LIFT-SCOPE.json demonstrates why this quotient proof does not automatically
apply to unrestricted port labels: in its second clique, class 0 needs
high-line choices {3,5}, {3,6}, and {5,6} to meet classes 2295, 2326, and
2812 respectively. Their intersection is empty. Even that individual
clique cannot be lifted by choosing one concrete port per class; allowing
different representatives in other cliques does not fix it. Other
full-port certificates are not excluded.

Affine glue features
--------------------
Write both low roots in the same 9-dimensional E8 fitting coordinates.
Their combined 18 evaluation columns have rank 13 in each profile.
Choose independent columns to obtain Z_i, a 960 by 13 matrix. Every affine
labeling covered here is Z_i T_i for arbitrary binary matrices T_i. Its
Gram blocks are Z_i G_ij Z_j^T, where G_ij=T_i T_j^T.

For each pair of distinct profiles, 169 independent active-edge outer
products are regenerated. They span every entry of a 13 by 13 matrix,
so G_ij=0. Within a profile a symmetric matrix has 91 entries. The complete
edge and diagonal equations have rank 90 and exactly two solutions. Both
have rank 9; the verifier checks both against the full adjacency block.
The complete Gram therefore has rank 27. Since diag(Z_0,Z_1,Z_2) has full
column rank 39, evaluating the Gram preserves its rank. This excludes
dimension 25 throughout the affine family, even with changed internal
Grams. The later parity certificate proves a stronger statement.

Constraint compression is exact: for a fixed left vertex, its edge
equation is linear in the right feature row. An actual basis of all its
neighboring right rows generates every edge equation for that vertex.
Every neighbor is included in the span calculation; none is sampled.

Quadratic family
----------------
All 91 products of pairs of the 13 affine columns, including squares,
span a 77-dimensional space Q_i. Constants and affine functions are
retained, with 64 independent nonlinear functions added per profile.
Allow arbitrary symmetric within-profile Gram blocks and unrestricted
cross blocks. The diagonal and active-edge conditions remain linear in
their entries. Exact solution dimensions are 168 for each within block
(3003 variables, rank 2835), and 46 for each cross block (5929 variables,
rank 5883). The complete symmetric family has 642 binary parameters.

The within-profile particular is shifted to the old rank-9 map: solve
Q_i R_i=P_i exactly and choose R_i R_i^T. Cross particulars are zero.
Every homogeneous direction satisfies all compressed equations, and the
directions are verified independent. Their counts match elimination's
nullities, so these are complete solution spaces. Recentering changes
no solution family. The separate parity theorem proves every assembled
231 by 231 fitting Gram in this family has rank at least 27. The free
parameters do not establish an exponent gain.

Fixed-profile radical obstruction
----------------------------------
The earlier ansatz uses a common I9 space plus a hyperbolic H_r space.
It keeps the old profile maps P_i and appends (f_0,0), (0,f_1), and
(f_2,f_2) in specified isotropic sectors. Its first pair of profiles needs
f_0(v).f_1(w)=P_0(v).P_1(w) on active cross edges. The included 9 by 9
biclique has invertible old-label matrices on both sides; its prescribed
cross matrix has rank 9. A product through r coordinates has rank at
most r, so r >= 9. This excludes r=8 in this ansatz even for arbitrary
nonlinear functions on glue classes. It is not a classification of all
embeddings preserving the old internal Grams. The separate parity theorem
supersedes this restricted obstruction for class-constant dimension 25.

Binary solver format and validation
-----------------------------------
Input: little-endian uint32 variable count n and row count. Each row then
uses ceil((n+1)/64) little-endian uint64 words. Coefficient bits occupy
0 through n-1; bit n is the right-hand side; higher padding bits are zero.
Within blocks serialize the upper triangle in NumPy triu_indices order.
Cross blocks serialize all entries in row-major order. Output: uint32 n
and solution count, then a particular solution and one vector per free
variable, with the same padding and a zero right-hand-side position.

The C++ implementation rejects inconsistent equations, truncated input,
nonzero padding, and trailing bytes. Python replays every returned basis
vector by integer parity against regenerated equations and checks basis
independence. Fitting controls reject even diagonals, all-one labels,
equal labels on an active pair, and the zero-radical candidate. Parity
controls reject missing cliques and duplicate vertices. Source and
artifact hashes pin the inputs and expected receipts.

Relation to coding
------------------
Golay self-duality and its 12-bit code dimension do not establish a fitting
representation of dimension 12. Self-dual codewords have zero mutual dot
products; appending 1 makes every pairing 1, including forbidden edges.
The useful transformation is into an exact code-feature space on which
the actual fitting equations can be solved. Parallel concatenated
encoders may suggest reusable scalar circuits, but probabilistic decoding
quality or fewer fixed encoder additions do not prove a recursive
exponent gain. No such circuit or complete supplier is supplied here.
