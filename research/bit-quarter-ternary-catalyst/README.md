# Quarter-integral ternary frames still require nine calls

**Result:** Adding all quarter-integral ternary frames to the half-integral
alphabet does not permit eight calls, even with any finite restored dirty
workspace. This is a scoped computer-assisted theorem, not a multiplication
lower bound or a new exponent saving.

## Model and proof reduction

For any finite R >= 3 work in F₂[M₃(Q)], the group algebra of the additive
rational matrix group. Write [A][B]=[A+B]. A paid call is

    D_N([A]) = I_R + ([A]-1)N,
    N²=N, rank_F₂(N)=1,
    A ∈ {uvᵀ/2, uvᵀ/4 : u,v ∈ {-1,0,1}³ \ {0}}.

The required formal endpoint is diag([I₃],[I₃],[I₃],1,…,1). Incoming dirty
registers are arbitrary and restored without residual influence. Intervening
free address transformations are not part of this alphabet. Scalar mixing is
already allowed by arbitrary rank-one idempotents N.

Scale exponents by four: B=4A, T=4I₃. The 676 distinct frames are uvᵀ and
2uvᵀ; their entries lie in [-2,2]. For N=xyᵀ with yᵀx=1 the determinant
lemma gives det(D_N([B]))=[B]. Thus every k-call solution has ΣB=3T=12I₃.
Expanding calls as (I-N)+[B]N, each nonempty positional subset contributes a
matrix of rank at most one. The target coefficient at [T] has rank three,
so at least three positional subsets must sum to T. Repeated occurrences
count separately; cancellations only weaken this necessary condition.

A target subset has at least three members by rational rank. Its complement
sums to 2T=8I₃ and has at least four members by the diagonal bound B_ii<=2.
Therefore k<=6 is impossible. For k=7 the only split is 3+4; for k=8 the
splits are 3+5 and 4+4. Four frames summing to 8I₃ all have diagonal (2,2,2).
They are 2wwᵀ with w=(1,s,t), s,t∈{±1}; the off-diagonal equations force
one of each. The verifier explicitly checks uniqueness with repetitions.

For five frames summing to 8I₃ every diagonal entry of each frame must be
nonnegative: one negative entry plus four entries <=2 would sum to at most
seven. Their total trace is 24. Each frame has trace <=6; thus in the primary
three-plus-two enumeration the triple trace is >=12 and pair trace >=6.
These are necessary filters, not symmetry assumptions. The second census
uses only recursive coordinate bounds and a final-frame lookup instead.

| Exact decomposition | Count |
|---|---:|
| T in three frames | 8 |
| T in four frames | 552 |
| 2T in four frames | 1 |
| 2T in five frames | 28 |

Union and deduplicate these decompositions. There are eight seven-call
candidates, each with one positional target subset. The eight-call census is:

| Positional target subsets | Candidate multisets |
|---|---:|
| 1 | 690 |
| 2 | 80 |
| 4 | 6 |

These 776 are all determinant-compatible multisets *having a target subset*,
not all determinant-compatible multisets. Those without a target subset fail
immediately. The first 770 fail coefficient rank. All orders and repetitions
are covered; no scalar-projector enumeration or register bound is imposed.

For each of the six survivors CERTIFICATE.json supplies a rational linear
functional ell with integer values on its eight frames, exactly one odd
frame value, and ell(T)=1. Let L be the subgroup generated by those frames.
A target subset puts T in L. The character B↦g^(ell(B) mod 2) maps L to C₂
and induces F₂[L]→F₂[C₂]. Seven paid factors become identity; one remains
I+(g-1)N. Comparing the formal coefficient of g with the endpoint gives
N=diag(1,1,1,0,…,0), contradicting ranks one and three. No character on the
full divisible M₃(Q), fraction field, or rank over F₂[C₂] is asserted.

A triple summing to T exists. Apply its three calls to each of the first
three coordinate projectors: this gives nine calls and the exact endpoint.
All other registers are untouched, proving attainability for every finite R.
The primary verifier replays formal matrix coefficients at R=3,4,6; the
block-diagonal argument extends this explicit word to arbitrary R.

## Delta, budget and predecessors

Tuafo's PR #1604, head c5f5bef84f6648d5573f801c283e01a333ea5f89,
proves the half-integral 338-frame result. Its exact census has 473 four-frame
target decompositions, 24 five-frame complements and 665 eight-call candidates.
Our extension adds 79, 4 and 111 respectively. The six survivors are the same
physical frames. We reuse and adapt its primary and independent implementations;
these are two different enumeration methods, **not fresh independent authorship**
or independent human review. The local theorem replays without downloading or
importing the predecessor. Exact original-file SHA256s are in SOURCES.json.

Credit Ali Romman's #468 and its eumemic #288 / huxint #203 lineage for the
three-register obstruction; arbitrary dirty-register scope is different.
Credit woklerx #1456 (83b6ad2ace819a96128131a3600ee914a66dd0dd) for broader

#1541 (194724d87505ec2517fdca1c1884da12398f4c33) for a different nonabelian
model. No novelty of the determinant, rank, or character technique is claimed.

The favorable eight-call recursive moment is (8/9)3^a for the three requested
outputs. Nine calls instead give 3^a>=1 for a>=0. Nonnegative additional
hosting, transport and restoration charges cannot improve that budget. Restored
spectators do not count as additional completed outputs. This rejects only this
explicit enlarged alphabet, not unrestricted rational frames, larger vector
entries, nonabelian operations, or integer multiplication. The selected
supplier/frontier is unchanged and its complete cold replay was not run.
The orientation-only `scripts/research_baseline.py` was attempted and failed
because `research/community-frontier1094-audit/ASSEMBLY.json` is absent in this

## Reproduce

Standard library, CPython 3.12 (locally 3.12.8 on macOS):

```sh
python3 -I -O -B research/bit-quarter-ternary-catalyst/verify.py
python3 -I -B research/bit-quarter-ternary-catalyst/independent.py
python3 -B scripts/research_package.py --package research/bit-quarter-ternary-catalyst
```

Default replay is read-only. Primary `--write` explicitly regenerates the
certificate. Eight corrupted-certificate controls are rejected by the primary;
two more by the second checker. The latter also checks subset multiplicities
by binomial weights and deliberately refuses -O because it uses assertions.
No floating-point or finite-address test is used as acceptance evidence.

## Open next questions

Can the same exclusion be proved uniformly for {uvᵀ/2^j : j>=1} with ternary
vectors? It would replace repeated denominator extensions by a symbolic theorem.
A feasible first step is to classify quarter-frame occurrences in the new
3+5 and 4+4 decompositions and derive a denominator-independent elimination.
The blocker is that smaller dyadic entries introduce new diagonal-deficit
patterns; this finite census does not cover them. See TASK.json for the
structured question. We deliberately do not launch a further alphabet search.
