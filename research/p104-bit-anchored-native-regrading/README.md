# Prove the global anchored BIT regrading bound

With the original native packet graph, original containment directions, and 229 fixed anchors replicated as A tensor Q^m, every rational regrading of the remaining charts in dimension 8m has normalized signed gain at most zero at both trials. The original replicated geometry attains zero.

Exact rational hull reconstruction forces 606 of 656 components. The 50 free components form eight four-node stars and nine pairs. The proof uses 24 leaf quotient injections and 477 outward Jensen/tangent corner checks, allowing arbitrary coupled rational subspaces inside the free charts.
