capture before destructive pop, all final states/heads and retained backing.

The expected bit endpoint remains the separate iterative original-index XOR
mask: for each original index x, compute a=x//(L*R), low=(x//R)%L and r=x%R;
at an internal node stop without a flip if low is outside {0,1}, otherwise
set x=a*R+r, A=A/(L*H), R=H*R and decrease remaining depth; at the leaf flip
exactly low0. No recursive expected bit-vector transformation is introduced.

given back to the emitted program.
