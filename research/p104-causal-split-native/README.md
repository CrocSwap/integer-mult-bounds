## What is new: ten further helper splits
* **What a split is.** A helper register p is carried along one chain of nested frames from its initial frame to its final frame and returns its input exactly. A split cuts that chain at a frame F strictly between the two: **p keeps the path up to F and stops there; a new public helper register z starts at F and takes the path from F to the old final frame.** Around the cut a basis change with dyadic coefficients and unit determinant is made on the group of helpers that share the cut, and its inverse after it, so that every other row of the word is unchanged and p returns its input at F. The credit of the helper is cut in two, not doubled: the rank mass of p plus that of z equals the rank mass the register had. In the invoice a target of rank r becomes two targets of ranks r₁ + r₂ = r; no paid move is added or removed.
* **Why a split is exact.** Exactness is a property of the finished word, and it is what is checked: the exact replay of every event on Fractions (`check.py`) leaves every public row of the word at its declared endpoint (identity on helpers, the port maps on mains) and every owned row empty; a second replay on a random vector mod 2⁶¹ − 1 (`second_engine.py`) and the replay as 512-component streams over F_p[i] (`physical.py`) agree, and the frame law holds at every event (`cover_check.py`). The lemma of #5599 supplies the identity for one split on one chain; this package does not re-derive it. A cut is available only where the cut group has a dyadic unit pairing (a determinant that is a unit of Z[1/2]); the ten cuts of this word are those where it holds, and no claim is made about other places.
* **Which ten, counted on the word** (`cover_check.py`, against the #6919 word in `data/prior_word_6919.json.gz`): labels 1431 to 1440 are new, none is a main; exactly ten registers of the #6919 word end at a different frame, and the ten pieces are matched to them one each (where several parents have the same frame labels the matching is not unique, and no fact below depends on which one is taken). **Frames are strictly nested on all ten** (initial frame of the parent, strictly below its new final frame, which is the initial frame of the piece, strictly below its final frame, which is the old final frame of the parent), as subspaces. By (initial rank of the parent, rank climbed by the parent, rank climbed by the piece): (0, 4, 5) × 5, (0, 5, 4) × 3, (0, 3, 6) × 1, (0, 3, 1) × 1; the last is a split of a rank-4 register into 3 + 1. All other registers keep their initial and final frames; the frame list is that of the #6919 word.
* **Five ordinary and five after a reordering.** Five of the ten cuts (labels 1431 to 1435) are made at one instant of the event order, as it stands. The other five (1436 to 1440) are made after reordering events that commute. **The reorder that is allowed** swaps adjacent events that commute and nothing else: two ADDs into the same register (as operators they commute), two ADDs out of the same register, or two events on disjoint registers. Nothing commutes past a MOVE of a register it touches (the frame law ties an ADD to the frame of its registers), nor past an INIT, CLEAR, SCALE or COPY of it. After such a reorder the events of a cut group before the cut form a prefix, and the cut is an instant of the new order. A reorder of this kind does not change the linear map of the word, but it can change the order of owned INIT and CLEAR events: that is why the peak of live owned fields is 36 here (30 in #6919; the number of owned lifetimes, 54, and of rises, 23, is the same), and why owned registers read mains in 19 events (16 in #6919). Both are declared constants of the word, not priced, and covered by the exact replay. The intermediate words are not in this package: what is claimed is the finished word, and the five and five are the history of the edit, not something the checks rely on.
* **The event list is rewritten as well.** Against the #6919 word the event list has 62,921 events (74,565), ADD 53,150 (65,902), SCALE 3,288 (2,180), MOVE 6,374 (unchanged), INIT, CLEAR 54 each, COPY 1: same-frame stretches of ADD and SCALE events are recompacted into equivalent ones and ADDs are merged. The compaction changes no frame, no register's initial or final frame and no MOVE, hence neither T nor P; it changes the raw counts and the coefficients (below). Correctness of the rewritten list rests on the replay alone.
* **T change** (per call, against the #6919 word, both invoices by the same code): +1 at rank 1, +2 at rank 3, +7 at rank 4, +8 at rank 5, +1 at rank 6, −9 at rank 9; ten more productive fields; the rank mass of T is unchanged. **P is unchanged** (the multiset of MOVEs, as (initial frame, final frame, owned flag), is equal, 6,374 MOVEs).

## Inherited from #6917 and #6919: valves, deletions, flipped mirror pairs, earlier splits and sinks

The individual valves, flips and deletions cannot be read off the word by identifier after renumbering, so their effect is what is counted and replayed.
* **The flip.** The word h += a·u*; h += c1·u; u += c2·h; u* += b·h has an inverse whose (h, u*) block is [[s + ab, −a], [−b, 1]] with s = 1 + c1·c2; with ab = s·c1·c2 the loop gain is s². The flips use SCALE gates of weight 1/2, so a wrong scale must fail, and it does (`cover_check.py`, sampled SCALE events: doubled, missing, replaced by ±1/2: all rejected).
* **R alone is not F⁻ᵀ.** Nothing here relies on a relation of the R block: correctness rests on the exact replay of every event (`check.py`), on the second replay as streams (`physical.py`), and on the controls. A reader should not infer the inverse-transpose property from the flip identity.
* **The #6919 splits and sinks** (69 splits, 16 sinks, 11 extra paid stops against the #6917 word) are part of the word this one extends. Then LAMBDA = aI + bT with a, b = (1±i)/2 has row support 2 at every f (it is not the f-th tensor power), and compiles as `INIT t; COPY t = x; T(t); x := a x + b t` (numerator first); `CLEAR t`, with T the native translation: the macro of the thin query of the open #6900 (not on main) with its paid Q⁻¹ replaced by the ordinary translation. The lift is "substitution of the fixed coordinate, phase and scalar generators. It does not tensor a sum of matrix operators" (`research/higher-moment-strictification/vendor/p7-TENSOR.md:42-44`), native monomial maps and quadratic phases "lift column by column" (`research/kappa-rebuild/architecture/FINITE-PRECOMPILATION.md:243-248`), and finite compositions of permitted words are permitted (FP:45-46). Under this reading LAMBDA costs ordinary work and S does not go negative; I have not verified the tape-level implementation, and the κ is unchanged by it.
