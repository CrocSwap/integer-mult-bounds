The rank formula and its stated scope are correct. The proof in

An alternate proof of the deletion step
--------------------------------------
Let B=I+A_h, n=2^h-2, u=(1,...,1)^T, and J=uu^T over F_2.
Each row of A_h has 2^(h-|S|)-1 entries equal to one. Thus B u=0.
The full matrix M=I+T_h has block form

  [0  u^T  1]
  [u   B   0].
  [1   0   1]

Since T_h^3=I, we have M^3=M^2+M. The central block of this
identity, together with B u=0 and u^T u=0, gives

  B^3+B^2+B=J.

If Bz=0, then Jz=0, so u^T z=0. For any unit column e, put
w=(I+B+B^2)e. Then B w=u and u^T w=1. Hence

  ker M = {(0,z,0): z in ker B} + span{(1,w,1)},

where the sum is direct. Thus nullity(M)=nullity(B)+1 and
rank(M)=rank(B)+1.

The full matrix rank
--------------------
The factor L=[[1,1],[1,0]] satisfies L^2+L+I=0. Over F_4 it
has distinct eigenvalues omega and omega^2, where omega^3=1.
The tensor power T_h=L tensor ... tensor L is diagonalizable.
Let a_h count its eigenvalues equal to one. In T_(h+1), each
eigenvalue omega or omega^2 contributes one eigenvalue equal to
one, while an eigenvalue equal to one contributes none. Hence

  a_0=1,  a_(h+1)=2^h-a_h,
  a_h=(2^h+2*(-1)^h)/3.

It follows that rank(M)=2^h-a_h. A field extension does not
change matrix rank. The deletion step therefore gives

  rank_F2(B)=(2^(h+1)-2*(-1)^h-3)/3.

Verification by a different method
-----------------------------------
verify.py counts every kernel input, without row reduction.
For h=2,3,4, it checks all 4, 64, and 16,384 inputs to B and all
16, 256, and 65,536 inputs to M. It gets B ranks 1, 5, and 9.
It also checks every kernel parity, every preimage of u, and the
matrix identity B^3+B^2+B=J. All checks pass. This finite check
supports the proof; it does not replace the proof for all h.

Application and limits
----------------------
If K=B is a sum of ell rank-one additive corrections, rank(K)
is at most ell. With v=2^h-2 and the assumed ledger D=2v-3ell,
the formula gives D <= 2*(-1)^h-1. This is 1 for even h and -3
for odd h. A copied scalar centre must have the stated additive
rank-one effect for this argument to apply. A product of general
maps is not being treated as an additive sum without proof.

This does not exclude a new source basis, a new port design, a
new compiler, or another method of correction. It is not a bound
on all Boolean-lattice constructions.
