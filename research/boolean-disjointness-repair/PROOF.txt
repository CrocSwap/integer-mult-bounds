Scope: this is an obstruction to one repair of PR172. It is not a bound
on all Boolean-lattice constructions or on a new source-basis compiler.

Let h >= 2. Index A_h by the nonempty proper subsets of [h]. Set
A_h[u,v] = 1 if u and v are disjoint, and 0 otherwise, over F_2.
The literal side map in PR172 is A_h. If an additive correction K is to
make its endpoint the identity, it must equal B_h = I + A_h.

THEOREM
rank_F2(B_h) = (2^(h+1) - 2*(-1)^h - 3)/3.
Thus a sum of ell scalar rank-one corrections needs at least this many
terms. Under the stated ledger D = 2v - 3ell, v = 2^h - 2,
  D <= 2*(-1)^h - 1.
The bound is 1 for even h and -3 for odd h. It is not the projected
centerless deficit 2v in PR172.

PROOF
Include the empty and full subsets. The full disjointness matrix is
T_h = [[1,1],[1,0]] tensor ... tensor [[1,1],[1,0]]. It satisfies T_h^3=I.
Over F_4 the two eigenvalues of its 2-by-2 factor are omega and omega^2.
If a_h is the multiplicity of eigenvalue 1, then a_0=1 and
  a_(h+1)=2^h-a_h,
so a_h=(2^h+2*(-1)^h)/3. The polynomial X^3-1 has distinct roots over
F_4. Hence M=I+T_h has rank (2^(h+1)-2*(-1)^h)/3. Extension from F_2
to F_4 preserves matrix rank.

It remains to prove rank(M)=rank(B_h)+1. Order the indices as empty,
proper nonempty, full. For a column (x0,z,xF),
  M(x0,z,xF) = (1^T z+xF, x0*1+B_h z, x0+xF).
Put P=I+T_h+T_h^2. Then PM=0 and P e_empty is the nonzero column
that is 1 exactly on the proper nonempty subsets.

If B_h z=0, M(0,z,0)=(1^T z)e_empty. Apply P to obtain 1^T z=0.
Because B_h is symmetric, its image is the orthogonal complement of
its kernel. Thus 1 is in its image. Choose w with B_h w=1.
Then M(1,w,1)=(1^T w+1)e_empty. Apply P to obtain 1^T w=1.
Every kernel column of M has x0=xF. The kernel columns with x0=0
are exactly (0,z,0) for z in ker(B_h). The column (1,w,1) supplies
one extra independent kernel column. Therefore
  nullity(M)=nullity(B_h)+1,
and rank(M)=rank(B_h)+1. This proves the rank formula.

Each scalar rank-one additive correction raises matrix rank by at most
one. Substitution in D=2v-3ell gives the final inequality.

LIMITS
This does not prove that all variants of PR172 fail. A different endpoint,
a new source basis, a new port label design, or a correction implemented
by another method can evade this stated model. It does show that the
missing scalar identity cannot be repaired by a small number of copied
scalar centres while retaining PR172's projected deficit.
