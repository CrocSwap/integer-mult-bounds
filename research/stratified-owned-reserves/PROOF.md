## 1. The remaining composition question

The selected #7000/#7001 application uses the complex core, ordinary BIT
backends frozen in the order `.0034 -> .0038507 -> .00385248`, and the latter
backends' covered kernels. Section 3 of the application explicitly makes the
ordinary dependency acyclic and retains `rho=1/250`. Section 6 requires the
product of simultaneous BIT and complex reservations and retention of
suspended parents. Neither a count of two bootstrap improvements nor the
raw peak of 43 native private lifetimes is a recursive storage bound.

#5814 already proves a single-provider geometric ownership implication.
#5829 supplies the whole-high `V/L` child geometry; its divisor is the
fixed low-type factor **L**, not the variable row factor `W(w)=L H(w)`.
#5404 already counts coordinatewise historical backing of the continuation.
#6719 proves a literal two-site shape recursion and its own geometric bill.
#6879 supplies a source-faithful reusable-origin space construction for the
old ordinary helper. #6903 supplies the chronological field producer and its
separately quantified volume-absorption conditions. None of those results is
claimed anew. In particular, #6879 does not by itself prove a space contract
for the selected `.0034` backend.

The new obligation treated here is composing **different simultaneously
active providers**. An ordinary map can receive the same complete bit volume
as its complex parent. A radix/row wrapper may enlarge that volume by a
fixed factor. During its execution the parent copy, private banks and return
state remain allocated. A one-provider geometric series cannot simply
replace these noncontracting edges by recursive `V/L` edges.

We give an exact finite certificate for that stack and an explicit safe
reservation for all smaller sibling widths. The latter needs an envelope:
the raw stopped-stock formula is not generally monotone in ordinary width.
These close an accounting implication under the listed physical and source
interfaces. Local machine realization, complete generated dispatch,
precision, actual stock fit and the base backend remain separate premises.

## 2. Model and its physical boundary

Fix a finite set of backend stages before input size varies, ordered
`0,...,s`. Fix a finite physical tape inventory. At a stage-i invocation
of **complete encoded bit volume** V, let `c[i,t] V` bound the new relative
extent needed on tape t. It includes all public and owned copies, erased
interiors, coded blanks, delimiters, descriptors, setup and local output
staging. The same local bound must hold at every prefix, before allocation;
a bound conditional on passing the same capacity guard would be circular.

The volume includes all external complex spectators and every component
bit. A bare number of records U must be replaced by `r(p) U`, where r(p)
is the actual encoded record width. The selected common-grid route retains
the same global p through a complex recursion; an unbounded r(p) is never
part of a fixed c. Different local encodings and markers can be accounted
by explicit fixed coefficients and interface inflation factors.

There are two types of active-child edge:

* Same stage i: `V_child <= rho[i] V`, where `0 <= rho[i] < 1`.
* Strictly lower stage j<i: `V_child <= a[i,j] V`, with fixed positive
  rational `a[i,j]`. These factors may equal or exceed one.

An ordinary shell with no recursive self-call has rho=0. Each vertex may
have any finite source-prescribed number of sequential child calls. That
multiplicity remains in the time invoice; one child is active at a time.
No recursive edge may go back to a higher stage.

Assume the #5814 coded-segment/bridge discipline, with the local predicates
just stated: child frames are placed after actual suspended parent frames,
no child accesses an ancestor, output is protected before destructive pop,
completed siblings restart at the same restored suffix origin, and every
local private lifetime is confined to its current invocation. Every global
bridge is empty during descent and is included in the local envelope when
used. This is a named implementation premise, not something derived from
the source's algebraic endpoints or from a passing finite trace.

For each tape t, K[t] is the entry backing already visited/allocated, including
erased tails and dirty inaccessible prefixes. A completed return never
subtracts from K. If a terminal stage abbreviates a complete existing
subalgorithm, its c is a *relative all-history space contract* including its
own internal recursion, not merely the size of its formal input.

## 3. Least stratified stack certificate

In increasing stage order define, separately for every physical tape,

```
M[i,t] = max( c[i,t]/(1-rho[i]),
             c[i,t] + a[i,j] M[j,t] for each lower edge i -> j ).       (1)
```

An empty lower-edge maximum leaves only the first term. All values are
finite rationals when c,rho,a are rational; they are computable without
executing an input-dependent call tree.

**Theorem 1.** Every finite properly nested execution satisfying Section 2
has

```
retained_total <= sum_t K[t] + V_root * sum_t M[root,t].                (2)
```

The bound holds over the complete history, not just at the point of maximum
live content, and is independent of the number of completed siblings. For
successive root calls at the same origins it holds with the maximum root
volume (or the maximum of the corresponding per-tape root bounds) and K
measured once at sequence entry.

**Proof.** From (1), `c[i,t]+rho[i] M[i,t] <= M[i,t]` and
`c[i,t]+a[i,j] M[j,t] <= M[i,t]`. Strong induction on the length of any
finite active call path therefore bounds its sum of local tape extents by
`M[i,t] V`. Equivalently, telescope the remaining potential along that path.
This is a proof for arbitrary permitted edge choices, including every
finite termination pattern; it does not assume termination of the actual
numerical program.

At a push, the absolute child origin is the parent's origin plus its actual
parked local extent and delimiter. That extent is no larger than the local
coefficient already counted. At any write, its coordinate is consequently
bounded by the path sum to the current invocation. Pop and erasure stay
inside previously bounded extents. A later sibling restores the earlier
origin and uses that same bounded interval; it does not append at the
historical high-water mark. A nonsegmented bridge has its fixed origin and
is bounded by the local term of the node using it. Thus every address ever
visited on tape t lies in a fixed interval bounded by the right side of (2).
Take the union over time **on each tape first**, then sum. Extents attained
on different sibling paths remain counted. This proves the theorem. The
same interval argument with a history-wide maximum proves the last claim.

**Sharpness of the relaxation.** Formula (1) is the least nonnegative
coordinatewise solution of its Bellman inequalities. A same-stage path
of arbitrarily many edges approaches `c/(1-rho)`. Taking an immediately
descending edge and an arbitrarily near-extremal lower path approaches
`c+a M_lower`. Induction on stages proves minimality. This is sharpness
for the abstract real-volume path relaxation, not a claim that an actual
integer-sized supplier attains every local bound or that (2) is optimal
physical storage. Fixed finite integer histories still obey (2).

The exact fraction calculation in `stack_model.py` is a certificate
constructor. Written induction supplies the universal statement. Independent
finite path enumeration and interval histories only corroborate it.

## 4. Actual selected source graph and complete local costs

Use the following six stages. `B0` is the selected completed `.0034`
interface. `Oi` is the ordinary wrapper at the ith new bootstrap stage;
`Ki` is its covered weighted kernel. `C` is the complex uniform core.
The source context fixes `O1=.0038507`, `O2=.00385248`, and the same weaker
`.00376669` BIT premise in the complex outer ledger. No saving is changed.

| Stage | Lower calls | Same-stage contraction | Source and qualification |
|---|---|---|---|
| B0 | Internal implementation kept in its own contract | Absorbed into B0 coefficient | Application Section 3; a relative linear-space certificate for this exact interface remains a premise. The old helper in #6879 is narrower. |
| K1 | B0, factor at most 4 for a radix atom's binary embedding | `1/L1 <= 1/2` | Delayed-cover whole-high theorem and stopped-product paid atom interface; the complete target/spectator fibre remains present. |
| O1 | K1 and B0, factors at most `P1=2q1^2` | No self edge | One binary-to-radix enlargement below q1² and one row padding below two; the two completed body calls are sequential. Residual/high-part/finite-cutoff predecessor calls are included. |

| C | O2, factor at most 1 at its binary entry | `1/W_C <= 1/2` | Complete complex fields passed to ordinary chunk interchange in compact movement; internal O2 padding is already its own edge. |

L1,L2 are the final compiled low-type/role divisors, not 134 and not
`W(w)=L q^(N^2(w-1))`. W_C is the final complex productive width, not the
raw 23,392. The new current BIT ambient cap **134** is a source pin; it
does not determine any of these final compiler widths or tape coefficients.
The conservative 1/2 contractions require only their retained lower bound
two. The larger exact divisors can be used in (1) when actually supplied.

The complete encoded volume at a lower entry contains every upper spectator
and record bit. The upper suspended copy and its private pools remain in
`c[parent,t] V`; the child's copy, output staging and scratch are charged
again in its own local coefficient. Equal mathematical values are not
merged physical storage. Thus the graph does not hide payload duplication.

The factors in the table are sufficient geometric bounds, not new finite
runtime tariffs. An implementation using larger extra encoding factors
must put them in a or in its local c before applying the theorem. The
factors 4 and 2q² come from the retained source interfaces; no assertion
that q=3 is a sufficient source cover prime is made by small controls.

For example, if every local coefficient on one tape is one, and the
conservative contractions are 1/2, (1) gives

```
M[B0]=1,              M[K1]=5,
M[O1]=1+5P1,          M[K2]=5+20P1,
M[O2]=1+5P2+20P1P2,   M[C]=2+5P2+20P1P2.                              (3)
```

These are a transparent symbolic unit-coefficient example, **not** the
selected program's numerical cell bound. At q1=q2=3 the example is 6572.
Actual source constants, record encodings, private inventories, descriptor

`51J+U+O_aux`, `237J_B+U_B+O_B,aux`, and all compiler and primitive

The extra administrative **time** is the occurrence sum

At most twelve ordinary swaps in a compact-control macro do not mean twelve

The existing source recurrence must fund this sum. A root-space bound is
not a proof of root-linear running time or a new κ.

## 5. A history-safe stock envelope

For one frozen ordinary backend, write

```
w(e) = ceil(e^rho),       0 < rho < 1,
H(w) = q^(N^2(w-1)),      W(w) = L H(w),  L=B |GL_N(F_q)|,
D(e) = 2 + ceil(log_N(max(1,ceil(e/w(e))))),
S(e) = W(w(e))^D(e) * S_old(e).                                       (4)
```

The selected new stages use rho=1/250. The compiler constants N>=2,
B>=1 and the eligible prime q are fixed before e varies. `S_old(e)` is
the predecessor's own complete reserve and is not silently set to one.
Equation (4) is the application Section 3 / delayed-cover source stock
prescription, with the generous depth offset used by the current review.

Suppose every invocation of that backend in one enclosing call has `e<=E`.
Supply a justified bound `S_old(e)<=S_old_bar(E)` over the same domain.
Define

```
w_bar(E)=ceil(E^rho),
D_bar(E)=2+ceil(log_N(max(1,E))),
S_bar(E)=[L q^(N^2(w_bar(E)-1))]^D_bar(E) * S_old_bar(E).               (5)
```

**Theorem 2.** `S(e)<=S_bar(E)` for every positive integer e<=E. If the
predecessor envelope is nondecreasing, (5) is nondecreasing. For fixed
template constants its additional logarithmic size is
`O(E^rho log(2E))`; in particular it is o(E). This is a sufficient envelope,
not a sharp maximum of (4).

**Proof.** Atom width is nondecreasing. Also `ceil(e/w(e))<=e<=E`, so
`D(e)<=D_bar(E)`. Both the base and the exponent in the new stock factor
are bounded by (5), and their bases are at least one. Multiply the inherited
predecessor inequality. The logarithmic statement follows by taking logs
and using `w_bar(E)<=E^rho+1`. These inequalities do not assume monotonicity
of the raw quotient `ceil(e/w(e))`, the raw depth, or their product. ∎

The actual source width bound E must be supplied by the caller; neither a
largest observed test width nor the number of bootstrap levels supplies it.
If different stages have different maxima E_i, use a separate envelope for
each. The asymptotic statement permits enlargement of a fixed threshold
when the enclosing source has the requisite strict stock-fit margins. It
does not prove those margins, choose their constants, or establish actual
input-dependent root generation.

### Why replacing the envelope by the endpoint is not automatic

Keep rho=1/250 and set the **stock-formula dimension** N=2. Put
`k=3215`, `e0=k^250`, `e1=e0+1`. Exact integer inequalities give

```
w(e0)=3215, w(e1)=3216,
2^2901 < ceil(e0/3215) <= 2^2902,
2^2900 < ceil(e1/3216) <= 2^2901,
D(e0)=2904, D(e1)=2903.
```

For the new stock factor alone (or constant S_old),

```
S(e1)/S(e0) = L^(-1) q^(4*(2904-3215))
             = 1/(L q^1244) < 1.                                   (6)
```

This calculation holds for **every** fixed eligible q and positive L of
the stated form; it does not rely on an inadequately small cover prime.
Taking B=2,q=3 gives L=96 only as a finite algebraic control. We do not
claim that these parameters meet a supplier's moment, prime-avoidance or
admission conditions, or that the actual selected enormous N has this
discontinuity. Equation (6) refutes a blanket monotonicity justification
based solely on the displayed stock formula. For the selected N, (5)
remains valid whether or not any discontinuity occurs.

## 6. Simultaneous reservations and source-preserving reuse

Let S_C be a complete reserve for the current complex recursion, and let
the simultaneously callable ordinary stages have bounds `S_bar_i(E_i)`.
These are numerical row cardinalities, including their internal fresh
depth stock. A safe independent reserve is

```
S_total = S_C * product_i S_bar_i(E_i).                               (7)
```

One may avoid duplicating a predecessor factor already included in an
enclosing S_bar only after proving which exact coordinates it supplies.
Equation (7) intentionally allows overcounting; it never removes an
unidentified factor because a similarly named predecessor appears twice.
The `.0034` backend's internal reserve remains explicitly present.

For Q>=S_total, pad once to `P=S_total ceil(Q/S_total)<2Q`. Euclidean
division identifies `[P]` with a slab factor and the Cartesian product of
all reservation coordinates. Assign each stage its own coordinate factor.
A stage's fresh fields consume only its own remaining stock; all upper and
other-stage factors are external spectators. On invoking a lower stage,
the complete upper factor is retained in the lower physical field and is
never relabelled as a free fresh lower digit. The #5829 whole-high map
moves that stage's exposed high coordinate into its body while fixing the
other factors; it is a bijection and changes no other reservation.

Assuming each completed call restores its full assigned row endpoint,
sequential sibling calls reuse the same coordinates. They do not require
a product over historical invocations. Concurrent ancestor stages still
require their full product. This is the exact distinction needed by the
application's simultaneous-reservations clause. A maximum of two nontrivial
cardinalities has too few points for their independent Cartesian product.

This argument concerns row allocation and complete-fibre preservation.
It does not identify that row range inside an already materialized physical
input, prove it fits the compact-control/precision parameters, synthesize
the chosen prime, or justify deleting padded rows at intermediate cuts.
Only a completed row-restoring endpoint permits restriction to the genuine
input range. Those source obligations stay explicit.

## 7. Necessary failure controls

1. If a same-stage edge has factor one and positive local extent, a chain
   of h nodes keeps h copies of that extent. There is no uniform bound
   proportional to root volume independent of h. A cycle of stage-changing
   edges with product at least one has the same obstruction. The actual
   frozen bootstrap DAG excludes such a cycle; completed backend names
   alone do not establish this.
2. If a shrinking child drops an external factor before defining its local
   V, the same source packet can have an arbitrarily large ratio of encoded
   bits to the falsely quoted V. The Bellman certificate then prices a
   different interface.
3. If siblings append after high-water backing instead of restoring the
   segment origin, h equal children retain h blocks despite unit path depth.
4. On distinct tapes, historical maxima can be reached on different
   siblings. `max_time(sum live)` is insufficient; (2) sums per-tape
   historical bounds. K at entry cannot be discarded after erasure.
5. Equation (6) rejects endpoint-only stock reuse. Replacing actual
   `D(e)` by the two frozen bootstrap improvements also fails as e grows.

These are scoped countermodels to missing hypotheses, not observed failures
of the admitted #7000 construction and not multiplication lower bounds.

## 8. Consequence and remaining work

The current six-stage dependency pattern has a finite exact stack
certificate once its local relative-space interfaces are supplied. Every
complete upper payload copy, padding factor, private bank, suspended frame,
fresh stock coordinate and retained historical tail has a place in one
common bill. No extra logarithm of execution depth or count of completed
siblings is required for backing. The source-compatible envelope (5)
avoids an unjustified endpoint-monotonicity step in supplying that bill.

The next concrete task is to instantiate c[i,t], E_i, the `.0034` backend
history contract and the common root row/precision layout for an emitted
selected dispatcher. #6719 and #6903 supply partial implementation pieces,
not that complete caller. No numerical upper tariff, complete runtime,
global `owned_erasure` discharge, new κ, independent human acceptance or
formal-kernel proof is claimed. All five selected assumptions remain.
