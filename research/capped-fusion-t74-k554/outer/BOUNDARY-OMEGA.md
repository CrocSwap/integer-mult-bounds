# Boundary matrix-product exponent: from Strassen to the exact <3,3,6;40> scheme

PR1673 (Vlado Vojdanovski) charges the batched boundary algebra `O(V (d/B) W^(omega-1) polylog p)` (note, section "Five factors, sparse windows,
and batched boundaries"; CAPPED-INTERIORS.md), where omega is the exponent of the W-square matrix products used for the banded block updates, and
takes Strassen, omega < 45/16, giving c = (3-omega)/2 = 3/32 and the ledger row `boundary = c + xi - eps`. The proof only uses that exponent
through a fixed exact bilinear scheme with rational coefficients applied recursively to W-square matrices padded to a power of the scheme's base
(the note: "Only the current square product is padded to the next fixed-base power, at constant volume ratio"; the dyadic width between W and 2W
becomes a width between W and 54 W, a constant). A scheme with rational dyadic coefficients needs no coefficient search or rounding; the rounding
envelope is the Strassen one: E_j <= K E_{j-1} + K m^(j-1) with a scheme-dependent fixed K.

Scheme: <3,3,6;40> over Q (A. V. Smirnov's rank-40 scheme in the rational dyadic variant of J.-G. Dumas, C. Pernet, A. Sedoglavic; files vendored
unmodified in `vendor/fmm336`, provenance and sha256 in `SOURCE.json`). `check_tensor.py` verifies in exact rationals the full tensor identity of
<3,3,6;40>, of its cyclic rotations <3,6,3;40> and <6,3,3;40>, tamper controls that fail, the Kronecker product <54,54,54;64000> (dimensions 54 in
every mode, rank 40^3) and the integer inequality 64000^10000 < 54^27743, i.e. omega <= 2.7743 (true value log_54 64000 = 2.77429998...). The
independent balance check re-asserts the same inequality in its own code.
