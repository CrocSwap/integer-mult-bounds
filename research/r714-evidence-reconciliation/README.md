# R714: reconciling the archived pending local replay

The hierarchy archive leaves candidate-201's independent dirty-column replay pending. The later R714 follow-up records that replay as complete. This package binds the two records to the actual words and independently reproduces the local result. It changes no selected certificate and claims no new kappa.

The earlier note describes its historical checkpoint. The later local evidence resolves that pending obligation. The resolution is precise: the raw R714 word and its mixed successor pass the finite local interface. The archived retiming makes zero moves, so the three receipt names describe two distinct words. This package leaves native execution, physical hosting, a full supplier invoice and final assembly unverified.

[INPUTS.json](INPUTS.json) pins 20 files, including the immutable source archive and the interface reference. The checker imports no historical producer, replay engine or solver. It streams the archive without extracting or executing it. It checks the exact inventory of 850 members, matches every RESULT to its ledger row, recomputes every word's canonical JSON SHA256, binds all 424 rewrite rules, and reconciles the helper-count summary. Candidate-201 is the unique 714-helper case among 425 records. The other 424 words receive provenance checks only, not new mathematical replay.

For candidate-201 and the two follow-up payloads, the checker propagates all output covectors backward through the F2 circuit. This independently checks all 954 input coordinates, including arbitrary helper dirt, against the exact target map. Fraction-free integer elimination checks rational frame containment, source and target boundaries, the COPY charges and the paid-rank histograms. This is not a modular approximation to rational geometry.

## Failure detection and scope

Nine negative controls reject missing compensation, missing helper cleanup, a missing COPY operand, a scalar-preserving illegal frame, a scalar-preserving wrong target hyperplane, a wrong paid profile, a wrong word binding, a missing archive member and a duplicated member.

The focused tests compare the integer span routine against exact Fraction elimination and compare both complete words against a separate forward implementation using sets of input indices. A control explicitly demonstrates that omitting raw-word compensation passes when helpers start clean, but fails for arbitrary helper inputs.

```sh
python3 -B -m unittest discover -s research/r714-evidence-reconciliation -p 'test_*.py' -v
```

The two later kernel ledgers contain 20 fresh trials and 20 transferred-seed trials, plus the transferred starting record. Their IDs and record counts are checked. Their solvers, kernel candidates, projected prices and physical feasibility are not replayed here; a historical solver failure is not an impossibility theorem. No new search is performed, and none of the 40 trials is promoted to a full supplier verification.

The existing archive, its manifest, upstream sources and selected frontier remain untouched. This result does not prove an all-size compiler, native multistage precision, analytic transfer or the multiplication theorem. It does not close or approve the remainder of PR463.
