# Research scope and attempts

The initial assignment concerned two paid accumulators, arbitrary partitions
of one four-term packet reconstruction, and joint formation/reader schedules.
Three candidates were recorded before search: split pair sums, shared
accumulator lifetimes across groups, and rational changes of basis. The
initial target was the 16 packet-copy roles in PR1238. The broader class is
not closed by this package.

1. A frame search on the raw CF0 cluster found a feasible 21-operation order
   after 57,478 distinct states. This is a positive control against an
   inference that PR1238 already excludes two accumulators. It does not give
   a complete admitted supplier. The control keeps the source-version
   precedence relations and target caps of that local cluster.
2. An attempted no-return argument for both partial packet versions did
   not transfer to the selected word. The selected factorization can keep
   one arm clean while its reader operations occur. Thus no rank-two
   first-formation theorem or universal copy-necessity theorem is claimed.
3. The first selected saved-arm schedule passed the frame search but had
   local change `+8 H2 -4 H3 +2 H4 -2 H5`. It lost even under a favorable
   rank-51 idle relaxation. That calculation was a reason to change the
   schedule, not evidence of an all-order obstruction.
4. A search that always chose the smallest immediate common frame excluded
   useful choices. Moving some frame growth earlier gave the final paid
   changes in `RECIPES.json`. The full arbitrary-input cleanup added four
   full-frame corrections per group. These are part of the final word.
5. The local improvement looked favorable if each extra two units could
   replace two rank-one idle units. The PR1129 signed-price inequality
   rejects that credit. The new width-9 helpers lose some width-7 plus
   width-9 fills. The resulting all-replica moment penalty is the theorem
   in this package.
6. The raw and selected numbering of the other eight roles differ. Their
   selected first-arm carriers are
   `[482,483,506,507,518,519,494,495]`, the second-arm carriers are
   `[554,555,530,531,542,543,566,567]`, and their later packet copies are
   696 through 703. A first attempt to treat 456 through 463 as the same
   selected roles was rejected during source binding. Those labels must
   not support a theorem about the selected word.

The final construction family is eight explicit recipes, with all subsets
and arbitrary mixtures across calls. The completed coverage is 256 exact
local words and an all-replica cost proof. There is no randomness in the
final check. The stopping rule for the family proof is exact positivity of
both complete increments at the known optimal baseline root. Once this
holds, a search for a better owner of these words cannot help.

The other eight roles, new partitions, arbitrary rational encodings, and
other frame schedules remain open. This is a change from the broader
initial research objective, not a proof that the larger class fails. The
eight completed rewrites and the exact obstruction are retained because
they show which local mechanism works and why its complete price loses.

The all-eight word also passes a 712-column scalar check. The extra eight
columns are independent additive values at the private centre reads. This
check addresses arbitrary private input values. It is not an operator-level
test of a new global host assignment. No new host assignment is proposed.
