## Domain

The generic constructor requires a cold helper h, no existing h warp or restore
touch, and a live helper L with zero initial frame, FULL final frame, no cold
buffer, no non-B native touches, and first B use after h's last B use. Existing L
warps are retained and retagged. Both providers give 26 host labels, two borrowers
530/542 and 32 eligible pairs. All hosts have end dimension 4 and start dimension 0.
A successful transport would replace L's width-nine role by h and delete h's old
width-four role. Structural eligibility does not establish native feasibility.

## Necessary native bound

For each framed gate, its common frame lies below the intersection of the next
upper frames on its registers. At an existing fixed warp U→V, check that V fits
the future upper; the incoming upper is U. The older loose bound instead resets
to FULL and discards that exact input constraint. Reverse induction gives a
necessary upper under any gate-frame refit retaining the fixed warp interfaces.
Every actual baseline B/restore gate and warp passes the stronger bound.

The upper at first use of either borrower has dimension 3 (L530 at B651; L542 at
B675; exact bases in STRONG-BOUND.json). An invertible new pre-life warp maps the
host's dimension-four end to a dimension-four image E. The first borrower gate
must contain E. Hence no image E fits below the necessary dimension-three upper.

For a single replacement, the future h chain is exactly old L's chain renamed,
with the same FULL end. Former h touches precede the new warp. Existing warp pins
are unchanged. Thus the original L bound transfers after retagging. Re-derived
cleanup is at FULL and supplies no missing non-FULL frame. This is a native
necessary-condition rejection, not a search failure or an unpriced claimed gain.

## Controls and limits

Two initial fixed-envelope trials fail constructive taint repair. Full 63-image
trials on two representative pairs also fail. The loose upper has dimension 4;
all 32 pairs on both providers were tested at its unique possible image: 64
constructive failures, zero errors. Those failures alone do not exclude a global
refit. Independently, the raw fixed-warp graph solver rejects both representative
pairs on both providers with rank-four lower versus rank-three upper witnesses;
its original-word, stale-frame repair and zero-end controls pass.

The strong bound uses incidence and fixed start/end/center/target/warp pins,
not old assigned gate frames. B1149's frame-only improvement cannot remove it
while those interfaces stay fixed. Changing existing warp interfaces, entering
borrowers, extra scalar topology or other pre-life grammars remains outside this
result. Compatible-group projection is separately reviewed; it is not inferred
from the single-host failures. No valid native additional-host word reached the
complete paid-cost gate, so no speculative profile or kappa is promoted.
