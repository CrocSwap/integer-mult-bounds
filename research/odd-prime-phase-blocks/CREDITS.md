# Credit and source record

Ali Romman prepared the general product argument, two-block classification,
capacity reduction and checker with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance in
research, implementation, verification and writing. New material is under
Apache-2.0. No global literature priority is claimed.

T. Y. Lam and K. H. Leung supply the classical two-prime cycle theorem in
[On Vanishing Sums of Roots of Unity, Corollary 3.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9511209#page=8).
Their theorem is an explicit mathematical input. The new proof does not claim
to reprove or originate that classification.

Ali Romman's [PR #7775](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/7775)
supplies the [p=3 phase descent](https://github.com/aliromman/integer-mult-bounds/blob/00daa049c349349f7db27ae7ddaf2f56116aaf79/research/two-prime-alphabet-descent/PROOF.md)
and exact predecessor checks. The complete package is retained without changes.
Its nested source proves the exact eight-row capacity. This work replaces the
p=3 two-line step with a product invariant valid for every odd prime, and adds
the full block normal form and capacity equality.
