The compiled complex rank-one invoice must satisfy P/B<N^(1−b) for b=κ+5g. All subsequent stopping, stock, root, precision, aggregation, and CRT inequalities keep these original rates. ## Fund every rank

Put X=4A. Write F(n)=P_n/B_n for the exact normalized number of paid rank-one leaves. The primitive at n<X costs n. For n≥X let n'=4 floor(n/4) and use the flow at n'/4. A source has n'/X<s≤n'/4. Its period k satisfies n'≤ks<2n'. Consequently

    s^t ≤ [2 X^C (n')^t] / k.

This follows from (ks/n')·(n'/s)^C·(n')^t=k s^t. Periodic incidence computes the n'-axis job exactly. A schedule of length T supplies T/k independent owners at this source. Replicate every full round by a common child-width multiple D. Round 15 times the raw owner count DR upward to a power-of-two B. With z=ceil(B/(DR)), run zD copies. Then B≤zDR<B+DR≤16B/15 and z≤30. The output head serves the remaining n−n'≤3 axes only on each retained owner. The finite forest cancellation gives Σ_e mult_e e^t ≤ Σ_s s^t with slot multiplicities. The real weights q_e in the paid normalized debit therefore obey

    Σ_e q_e e^t ≤ (16/15)·2 X^C n^t,
    F(n) ≤ 3 + Σ_e q_e F(e).

Only the bottom exits enter this recurrence. Each exit is at most r(n'/X)^θ when r≤k_min^θ. This uses the retained finite-flow horizon, and is weakly smaller than r(n/X)^θ. The actual exit is strictly below n. Periods and rank-one rounding alone do not bound the other work in the final algorithm. All generated fields, banks, complete routes and setup remain payable at the separate physical stage.

Let h(x)=1+log₂(x)/b₀. To close the induction at small ranks, let U≥log₂ X and ensure C(b₀+U)≤p. For 1≤x≤X put y=log₂ x. Then log(1+y/b₀)≥y/(b₀+y) and log(x)≤y, so x^C≤h(x)^p. At an exit with e≤r(n/X)^θ, the guard (1−θ)b₀≤θlog₂ X−log₂ r implies h(e)≤θh(n). Choose (16/15)·2 X^C θ^p≤3/4. At n≥X the elementary bounds t>1/2 and X≥256 give n^t h(n)^p≥12. Strong induction yields F(n)≤n^t h(n)^p at every rank. This argument permits a nonzero shift while keeping the original primitive down to n=1. An unsurcharged clamp of the old log at the base threshold would lose the contractive factor for a terminal exit.

## Prime exclusions and a necessary input length

The old binary criterion q>2^45 N^5 paid for a bad-pivot frequency. A uniform correction removes this surcharge. For unrestricted rational bases, taking the product D≥1 of the nonzero denominators, determinants and required rank minors gives a finite prime q≤2D+1 outside the exclusions. The stronger ternary construction in this package chooses the native frames and all their matchers over the rationals with denominators coprime to 3. Its factored right routes preserve every complete field over Z/3^w. This separate result fixes q=3 at every ordinary stage and gives nine elementary digit buckets. The true row stock still includes the entire general linear group and every high fibre. [Previous cover and catalogue order](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/87fbf4f7b35b0f18ab12b5767775a9a424cf0b15/research/p104-mixed-rank-supplier/PROOF.md)

The current ordinary stage needs an additional input reserve even when the base prime is small. Its atom width is w=ceil(e^(1/250)) at ordinary input width e. The source allocates W=B·|GL_N(F_q)|·q^(N²(w−1)) for each of D=2+ceil(log_N d) levels. Distinct unit upper triangular matrices already give q^(N(N−1)/2) elements. The borrowed k digits must hold at least W^D, and satisfy k<e/2. Consequently e>4N²(w−1)+2N(N−1). For e≥2^250, w−1≥e^(1/250)/2 and hence e^(249/250)>2N². This necessary input width is independent of q. [Exact ordinary stock and guard](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/343f7b1817e26ad3f89d2ddad313b12741d53602/research/mixed-rank-uniform-composition/ORDINARY.md#L13-L33)

At log₂ e≥481927710843386 the six simultaneous ordinary stock reserves fit inside half the input width. Put u=log₂ e, x=2^(u/250), H=240000000000000 and N=2^H. The binary-to-ternary wrapper for an earlier width-w exchange uses at most w+1 ternary digits. This is smaller than the top e on the stated range. Each of the six stages has depth at most 3+u/H, atom width at most 2x, and base-three logarithm of the ownership width at most 105H≤N². Since |GL_N(F₃)|≤3^(N²), adding the six debts and the final integer rounding gives k≤1+18N²x(3+u/H). At the stated lower endpoint T, 2^(249T/250)≥4096N² and 3+T/H<6. The ratio 2^(249u/250)/(3+u/H) increases thereafter. Thus k<e/2 for the whole tail. CarrierStock.current checks the six separate debts, the precise threshold and the strict inequality. This is a sufficient width for the ordinary stock guard.

There is a separate lower cutoff on the unchanged Gaussian outer fast regime. For p=6 ceil(log₂ n), d≥p^(1−g)/2, and log₂T≤p/6+3. The maximal axis width ℓ=ceil(log₂T/d) obeys ℓ≤1+(1/3+6/p)p^g. Set z=log₂ p. For z≤5·10^15 the secant bound for the convex function 2^x gives p^g≤1+(2^50−1)z/(5·10^15)≤1+z/4. If p≥64 this implies ℓ≤3/2+z/8. Yet log₂d≥(1−g)z−1≥3z/4−1, strictly larger for z≥6. Every candidate fine prime is below 2^ℓ<d. Thus the required d distinct primes do not exist for any p in [64,2^(5·10^15)]. This is a necessary obstruction for the specified outer rectangle. It establishes neither a sufficient activation threshold nor an obstruction to a different outer geometry.
