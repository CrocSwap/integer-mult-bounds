# Route the ordinary cover without a directory of low matrices

The physical stream uses row-major lexicographic order on ternary matrices after deleting the singular matrices. This order applies to every productive role, private role, recursive packet and returned packet. A group contains all its invertible low matrices; for each such matrix it contains **every** high matrix followed by the entire old-high and body suffix. The byte-stream implementation uses fixed-width opaque records. On binary tapes each byte is eight bits; arbitrary bit packet lengths can equivalently use the unit-bit alphabet, or one byte per bit at constant factors. No operation tests an opaque payload. [Physical stream implementation](streaming_routes.py) · [Independent address and return checks](check_streaming.py)

## Complete right movement

Let M = 3^(w−1), d=N², N≥1 and w≥1. Let G=|GL_N(F₃)|. The regular lows occupy the fraction

    G/3^d = ∏_{i=1}^N (1−3^(−i)) ≥ 1−Σ_{i=1}^N 3^(−i) > 1/2.

The inequality follows by multiplying one factor at a time and keeping the nonnegative product term. Thus all ternary lows, including singular lows, have volume less than **twice** the genuine input. This temporary padding is local to this router and differs from the recursive stock and the last partial slab. None of these three quantities is divided out of a child invoice.

Enumerate the full ternary cube in order with an N²-trit counter. Test the current matrix by modular elimination at 3. A nonzero pivot is its own inverse. This costs O(N³) operations on trits, O(N²) trits of scratch, and at most O(N⁵) steps on a simple scanning tape simulation. Invertibility is a physical algebraic test of the index. It never reads the payload. For a singular low write a complete initialized zero fibre. For a regular low consume exactly one genuine high fibre, once, with all its suffixes. Apply the following high operation before emitting that fibre at its original low position. The production implementation does not allocate a list indexed by G or by the number of possible high matrices.

Let S be the actual ordered elementary frame word. For a regular g₀, perform g→gS in the high columns using one running low matrix. At each elementary step on a row, compute the new low target t' and its rational carry (t−t')/3. The numerator reduces to zero modulo three, so the carry has denominator prime to three. A swap exchanges both full high coordinate fields. An addition sends the high target y to y+c x+carry mod M, where x is its high source. A scaling sends y to c y+carry mod M. The tested low header advances after every factor even when an intermediate high permutation is the identity. Thus the composed maps have the complete carry of g₀S. The earlier low matrix is used for every input record in that fibre; no already-permuted low header is substituted.

An ordered addition writes two complete segments of the target fibre in rotated order. Prepare the offset once per exposed source value, advance it by c for the next source value, and pass it through any intervening spectator range. The target contains M full records; this absorbs polynomial-in-w offset preparation at the ordinary tariff. If the high source follows its target, invoke the *completed preceding ordinary stage* on the two complete fields, perform the ordered rotation in exchanged coordinates, and invoke that same stage to restore them. A high-coordinate swap also invokes the previous stage. The callback takes the actual field width w−1, the full prefix, full gap and full suffix. For w>1 the router requires this callback explicitly. At the cold starting stage it is the implemented elementary digit exchange. At each of the subsequent five stages use the already completed preceding stage. No warm-stage proof charges elementary width-w work at a sublinear-in-width price.

For completeness, use a bounded integer-run implementation for each unit scale. To multiply by k>1 modulo M, the source range divides into the k consecutive runs with endpoints ⌈jM/k⌉. Run r contains the outputs congruent to −rM modulo k. Writing outputs y in increasing order reads run (−M⁻¹ y mod k), whose selector has a period of k. The inverse operation distributes by this selector and concatenates complete runs. Prefixes are handled separately. For c=a/b first multiply by |a|, then negate if needed, then apply the inverse scale b, and rotate by the carry. Negation holds coordinate zero fixed and reverses the order of the remaining opaque blocks. Two-way tape traversal reads each block in its original internal direction and walks back at most twice its length to the preceding one. The sole relative seeks in that operation move exactly those distances. The factored compiler bounds |a| and b by 20 at every scaling, including inverses. The program uses two reusable complete packet tapes and twenty run tapes for the high operation. It has no large-radix bucket array. A factor's actual scans and all preceding-stage calls are charged.

After the high step, the padded cube stores the correct final high permutation at each regular *original* low. Each singular fibre remains zero. Now apply S to the low part alone, treating the complete transformed high-and-suffix block as opaque. Expand each row operation into at most three whole-cube passes.

- A ternary swap distributes the nine possible pairs of digits into nine sequential buckets, with all intervening coordinates and suffix records intact. It returns the buckets in second-digit, middle, first-digit order.
- A scaling at three permutes three target blocks. An addition from an earlier field cyclically permutes three target blocks per exposed source digit. A backwards addition uses two ternary swaps surrounding one such rotation. Coefficients that vanish at three may skip a low pass even if their recorded full high coefficient was nonzero.

Every step is right multiplication by an invertible matrix on the full low cube. It sends regular lows bijectively to regular lows and singular lows to singular lows. The spectator-preserving high movement followed by the low movement therefore sends each genuine (g₀,X,z) to its correct complete (h₀,Y,z). Enumerate all low indices again, copy each genuine fibre in lexicographic output order and consume all padded fibres. Wipe temporary tapes through their visited extent, rewind and retire them. The child sees exactly G genuine low fibres. Right routing by the reversed inverse word restores any arbitrary returned payload. Getter and writer streams expose their ordinary sequential cursor; a held parent retains all its source symbols and pays the linear rewind on its owned wrapper.

Let K be the number of elementary gates in S and V be the genuine data volume. Each full-cube low pass moves <2V data bytes; distribute, merge, return and erasure perform a constant number of scans. At most 3NK such passes are needed. The two regularity enumerations test 2·3^(N²) matrices per group and therefore cost O(N⁵V), since every genuine low has at least one payload record. The local low component costs O((N⁵+NK)V), including padding and restoration. It uses 11 sequential temporary tapes and O(V+poly(N)) total scratch bits when the current group has volume V. The total contents of the nine buckets never exceed one padded group. No table has one entry or one physical head per low matrix. At most one genuine high fibre has a high engine active at a time. High scans, bounded scalings and at most 2NK full preceding-stage swaps per genuine fibre require O(NK·V(1+w^(1−b))) at prior saving b, in addition to the per-prefix arithmetic allowed by the ordinary descriptor bound. This factor is a real cost. Previously parked parent and child frames continue to obey the separate allocator bill. ## Split and merge the true type banks on one stream

A true type is l=(productive role, regular low), in role-major order. Put L=BG. In the source child boundary, the parent row is ((uL+l)H+h)Hʲ+a, where H=3^(N²(w−1)); the child row is (uH+h)Hʲ+a. Thus the complete block after l comprises the current high h, all older high a, and the unmodified body. Let R be its full physical length and U the number of u values. We need to turn the order [u][l][R] into [l][u][R], with one contiguous true output for each l. Iterating through a role's regular low matrices by the same lexicographic cube scan labels these packets without a directory. Scientific roles and independent productive width B keep their original identities. [Physical boundary in the predecessor](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/delayed-cover-physical-batch-routing/PROOF.md)

Set k=⌈log₃ L⌉ and M₀=3^k. Pad each row's final interval of L true types to M₀, so the temporary volume is less than 3ULR. After the first i trits have crossed u, the order is [first i type trits][u][next type trit][remaining trits][R]. For each prefix send each complete rest block to one of three streams selected by the next trit and concatenate those streams in 0,1,2 order. Induction gives [padded type][u][R] after k passes. Remove all padded type blocks before any child is offered an input. The genuine l-th packet now has exactly UR=V/L bytes in the required child row order. R includes every high digit and genuine old spectator. Type padding never changes the bank size used for recursion. Each chronological invocation can consume all its type packets in this order and stage their returned arbitrary complete contents in the same serial order. A full invocation that needs several independent source roles must retain those distinct role banks or pay the actual separate selector passes. This transposition removes only the per-low tape catalogue.

For a return, append complete padded type blocks, move the trits in reverse order, transposing three tapes by round-robin full rest blocks for each u, and strip the local tail pads from every reconstructed parent row. The proof uses only the shape and permits arbitrary changed child outputs. Both directions use five temporary spools, at most k whole padded passes, O((1+log₃ L)V) scanning and O(V) payload scratch. Positive descriptors use O(log(U+L+R)) binary work bits. Whole-array cursor seek lengths, gaps, scrubbing, padding and return transfers are charged at their actual linear extents. The older literal implementation proved at most 94V steps using L+7 physical tapes. Fewer fixed heads cost these additional passes. A polynomial-in-N bound for log L also needs the independent B bound of the complete density compiler.

## Verification

Run `python3 -B check_streaming.py` under Python 3.10 or newer. The oracle independently enumerates the genuine group with the ordinal decomposition, sorts those matrices for stable lexicographic comparison, and applies the exact modular matrices directly to every original coordinate. All address maps are compared before testing their inverses. The high tests pass an instrumented cold predecessor at the permitted stages; they verify its exact width, complete prefix/gap/suffix volume, invocation count and changed data.
