# Remove the fixed low directory

The finite controller uses the complete group $G=|\mathrm{GL}_N(\mathbb F_3)|$ in its row stock. It stores zero group-indexed tables and uses a fixed number of physical tape heads independent of $G$, $N$, the input length and the recursion depth. The external packet has exactly the true $G$ low rows before and after each matcher. This section specifies the implementation used in the theorem. The more expensive eager directory in [the machine bill](MACHINE.md) is a comparator and a test oracle.

## Change the physical representation

Give the true low matrices their row-major ternary lexicographic order, with singular matrices skipped. A counter supplies all $N^2$ trits. At each candidate, Gaussian elimination on its small private copy tests nonsingularity. The full group cardinality is $\prod_{i=0}^{N-1}(3^N-3^i)$. Its binary length is less than $2N^2$. Products and counters for the independent role and stack depth also have polynomial fixed length. The reference implementation of a different bijection reconstructs a true low from its ordinal by successive pivot-column reductions. It checks the stream generator on finite examples.

* **Right action.** Start with one complete block of genuine lows at a fixed external prefix and productive role. For a genuine low $g$ execute the full high affine right action, carrying every opaque suffix in its original position. If the factor word is $S$ this gives high part $XS+(gS-h)/3\bmod 3^{w-1}$ for $h=gS\bmod3$. Compute each elementary step with its actual incoming low and both full forward/return routes. For each singular low insert a private all-zero fibre of the same size. The resulting low-coordinate cube has exactly $3^{N^2}$ rows. On this cube apply the elementary right factors to the low trits. These are complete coordinate swaps, scales by a ternary unit and additions. The splitter for a pair of trits uses nine buckets even across an arbitrary gap. At the resulting ordered cut remove all singular rows. This is legal at this cut since multiplication on the right by a unit both preserves and reflects nonsingularity. The reverse uses the inverse elementary factors and their full high carries. Each product of factors processes each real source and each chronological placement. No factor lookup uses a $G$-entry index. [Streaming implementation](streaming_routes.py) · [Bound on the elementary factors and their scalar values](FACTORED-ROUTES.md)
* **Volume.** The nonsingular proportion is $\prod_{j=1}^{N}(1-3^{-j})\ge1-\sum_{j=1}^N3^{-j}>1/2$. Temporary low padding therefore grows the local volume by strictly less than two. A genuine low input always contains at least one payload cell. Enumerating even the rejected candidate matrices costs at most twice the number of genuine rows times the polynomial work for one determinant. At $w>1$, a genuine fibre has exactly $3^{N^2(w-1)}$ high records. Consume it *once* in sequence at this part of the computation. Apply each elementary swap, ordered add, proper small-rational scaling and affine translation to complete fields on reusable tapes. An earlier completed ordinary stage supplies each high field exchange. At stage zero the explicit cold receiver supplies it. Charge the whole $O(Vw^{1-b})$ predecessor bill for high routing and the $O(\mathrm{poly}(N)V)$ low-cube bill. Computing a pivot and the terminal factors visits the full high matrix separately, as proved in [the ternary leaf construction](TERNARY-COVER.md).
* **Type split.** The child selects one true type among $L=BG$ and gets $V/L$ payload cells. Given $[u][L][r]$ records in order, temporarily extend $L$ to the next power of three. There are less than three times the genuine number of slots. Move one trit of the type past the whole $u$ range in each pass. For every earlier prefix, separate the three cases on three sequential tapes, then concatenate them. The inverse splits the three contiguous banks and reads the original blocks in round-robin order. No $u$, old high or variable-length address tag is attached to the payload. Remove the $l\ge L$ packets before calling any child. Regenerate the temporary intervals on ascent and remove them on return. The cost is $O(V(1+\log_3 L))$ moves. Here $\log_3 L<2N^2+105H_b$. The old fixed-control interface bounded this split by $94V$ using $L+7$ physical tapes. This is a time/number-of-heads tradeoff. [Prior fixed-control physical splitter](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/delayed-cover-physical-batch-routing/PROOF.md)
* **Sibling calls.** Park the other large working banks once per paid site. Reverse the single type-major stream into a pending stack. Pop and erase one complete argument in forward order, execute that child, append its returned stream to a separate answer stack, and advance. The two stack heads sit on their exposed tops. A descendant appends a marker above each top, uses its own interval and retires it. The suspended heads are already at the two markers. A completed child transfers its full return to the caller and clears its own interval. No call walks across the other $G-1$ packets. Progress counters, the current low and scalar descriptors live on a third stack of small active frames. Park or regenerate them once per cut. At the end scan the answer stream once and run the inverse type split. For a non-destructive ABI retain the source once for the whole site. Every child gets all high values and all outer data at that true low, regardless of previous pivots or operand values. The total live data down one chain are proportional to $V\sum_j (BG)^{-j}$. The independent executable changes every child's payload and checks the restored sibling order. [Serial cut and backing](SERIAL-LOW.md) · [Serial transfer check](stack_cut.py)

The other native banks can also use fixed physical heads. At each complete source opcode, stream its tagged bank store onto a second tape. Copy the bounded number of input fields for the opcode onto dedicated active tapes and restore the other full banks together. Run the complete opcode on these active fields. Install its results with another bank scan. There are at most $J$ banks, each with volume at most a fixed number of current array volumes, and at most $J$ opcodes before recursive requests are counted. Charge this $O(J^2 V)$ bank motion at each current frame. At a paid cut collect the whole batch once and park all other banks once. Process the already contiguous child packets using the two stacks above. For a numerical operation within one field keep the large bank store stationary until the entire field pass returns. The scalar and guide transducers have their own fixed tapes. This gives a serial implementation without a scan of the entire saved array per scalar or per low. A conventional machine with separate fixed tapes per raw role is another valid implementation when those tapes are counted.

As a result the installed control only contains the pre-cover factor words, clocks, fixed-tape programs, and the small primitive rules. The generated structural bounds before numerical lowering are $N^{1200}$ and $M^{4920}$ one-head steps, with $M=2^{3300000000}$. The factor expansion described in the machine bill fits $J=N^{20000}$, including its private setup high-water space. A universal, fixed-alphabet scan interpreter reads the finite opcode stream. Its transition table has a fixed number of states and heads. The word, numeric phase constants, $N^2$-trit counters, determinant scratch and group-size calculation also fit this bill. Constants from the fixed raw words are already charged in the $2^{64}$ baseline per occurrence. The machine is generated by actual nested finite loops. It does not compute or store $G$ low programs during setup.

$$
 S_{\rm new},\quad \text{stored instructions and tables},\quad
 \text{peak fixed setup cells}\quad \le N^{20000}=2^{4800000000000000000}.
$$

For the literal old eager low directory alone, $\log_2\log_2 S_{\rm old}>2570399999999999$. For the production upper bound here, $\log_2\log_2 S_{\rm new}<63$. The same bound includes the compiler's computation. The per-input scan of actual low rows recurs only when they are present in the actual volume. It costs polynomial work in $N$ per genuine row plus the high and leaf tariffs. It does not cost $G$ steps per cell or per child. This distinction matters at $w=1$, when a true packet can contain only a single cell.

## Recurring constants

One can track the dependence on the native machine separately from the common outer constants. Here is a deliberately loose sufficient bound. Use $J=N^{20000}$ and let absolute constants below depend on the retained mathematical libraries, fixed raw words, and on the fixed price gaps. For each issued ordinary site, a scan of the entire installed word costs at most $O(J)$. There are at most $J$ site and factor records. Each visit needs at most $O(J)$ fixed scans to decode its factors, coordinates and placement. The low determinant, possible modular solve, fixed coefficient chains, enclosing counters and temporary pointer-free tapes require at most $O(J^2)$ fixed work per single coefficient in addition to a polynomial in $w$ and $D$. One explicit variable envelope is $(w+1)^{64}(D+1)^8$: schoolbook operations on $O(w)$-bit residues and their inverses by repeated long division cost $O((w+1)^4)$ steps per elementary operation, indexed elimination can use a full matrix scan per operation, and at most two single-ancestor chains per current coefficient have length $D$. Complete earlier descriptors can be regenerated by scans rather than indexed across other held data. Since $D\le3+250\log_2(w+1)$, the supremum of this envelope divided by $3^{w-1}$ is a finite absolute constant. Table generation, full payload, private arithmetic and routing are separate costs. The local ordinary clock multiplier can be bounded by $O(J^{10})(1+C_i)$ given earlier-stage multiplier $C_i$. This pays both reciprocal coefficient passes, the $O(N^2)$ low passes, all full type transposes and every actual native-frame bank scan. Bank count is bounded by the number of causal site records. High-field operations that call the previous receiver only multiply its own clock once per issued route. The cutoff for insufficient row stock has $e\le2^{481927710843386}<N^3$, so the linear-in-$e$ elementary receiver on that interval costs at most $O(N^3 V e^{1-b'})$. A smaller earlier working width that stays elementary has the same uniform bound. Six stages and their geometric factors thus fit $O(J^{61})$ in front of the priced ordinary clock. This bounds a dependency on $N$. It does not estimate the least constant for the original or new machine.

For complex width $d$ a child at depth $j$ has $e_j=\lfloor d/M^j\rfloor$. Stop when $e_j\le\max(M,d^g)$; widths below $M$ cannot start a complete native copy. For the stopped cost, $(P/B)^j e_j\le d^\sigma e_j^{1-\sigma}\le M d^{\sigma+g(1-\sigma)}$ for $e_j>0$, with $\sigma=1-\kappa-5g$. This proves the same exponent uniformly across the small dimension range. The internal geometric multiplier is less than two. Row-stock needs in an exposed complex pack have the preceding six-stage logarithm, together with the complex batch log and stopped depth. From $B_c\le M^{448}$, $\log W\le O(N^2 w)$, $w\le2e^{1/250}$, and the complete previous-stage width $e=O(hs^2)$, their total is $O(N^4[(1+s)^3h^{1/250}+\log(h+1)])$, where $s=\lceil\log_2(p+2)\rceil$. To bound the sequential tail choose a fixed threshold $K N^{12}(1+s)^5$ with sufficiently large absolute $K$. Above it these reserves occupy at most $h/64$ by the two strict power inequalities $12(1-1/250)>4$ and $5(1-1/250)>3$. The scheduled front controls use at most $3h/128+O(1)$ further chunks. Below it charge the individual kernels and all their scans with this actual polynomial coefficient. This changes an internal stopping decision only. It never sends a growing whole product to elementary multiplication for lack of native rows.

For the direct outer caller the window sizes, total suffix, number of matched stages, numerical table generation and physical precision are as in its source. The coefficient of $hs$ in the window exposure follows from the explicit $2h\cdot128(4s'+6)$ locations with $s'=\lceil\log_2(P+2)\rceil$ at $P=O(p)$. Thus its finite initial exposure cutoff and its square-geometry test are independent of these native dimensions. Per compiled instruction use at most the previous-stage multiplier, a full finite-control scan, the paid layouts, and constant-many complete site passes or private requests from this expanded record. Bounds by the full $J$-record stream and its literal factor lengths still give at most $O(J^{10})$ additional factor over the six-stage cost and its terminal coefficient. Every fixed power of $s$ from the same outer proof can be absorbed into its strict price surplus at a constant independent of $N$ and $M$. The square selection and the private $2^{O(s^3)}$ term likewise retain their original constants. We obtain a conservative complete upper envelope $C_{\rm shared} J^{100}$ for the recurring time constant and the input-proportional storage coefficient. Here $C_{\rm shared}$ includes the unchanged outer initial range, coefficient producer, fixed raw arithmetic and price-gap constants. No numerical value for this common factor is certified. This envelope is a bound for the specified implementation. A lower bound on old optimal $C$, uniform multiplicative improvement in $C$, or practical crossover for the cofinal native theorem would require additional evidence.
