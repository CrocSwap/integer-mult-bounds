# Executing and measuring the integer product

Fix the signed convention by multiplying the magnitudes and returning their sign XOR (zero has sign zero). Length chooses the outer branch. The existing direct caller generates its CRT sides, coefficients and self-normalized forward and opposite words. It spends the same precision budget on arbitrary input, rotates the original cyclic addresses, computes both source-size factors and returns the exact carried product. The local extension allows the same finite scalar multiplication routine at each diagonal multiplication, public pointwise product and final gain multiplication. Its source phase certificate holds exact rational objects until a diagnostic report is requested. This avoids converting an unused multi-thousand-digit error fraction to a decimal string while preserving its construction and numerical checks. ## Finite packed multiplication

Below the first direct geometry, and at the short scalar service inside it, the compiled route reads ordinary binary operands. The terminal of at most 192 bits uses a primitive product. For represented magnitudes of at least 4,096 bits, split into true base-$2^{16}$ digits. With digit lengths $a,b$, select the smallest power of two $L\ge a+b-1$. When $L\le2^{26}$, allocate three arrays of $L$ 32-bit residues and two temporary streams of $L/2$ entries. No root or permutation table grows with $L$. Load the actual input digits, append zeros, then for each of the two primes carry out two forward radix-two DIF transforms, the pointwise product and the corresponding inverse DIT transform. Compute one principal root for each stage and advance its powers in order for each block. The forward butterfly at half-width $h$ is $(x+y,(x-y)\omega^j)$, at opposite half-width it is $(u+\omega^{-j}v,u-\omega^{-j}v)$. Splitting the Fourier sum into its even and odd output indices proves the DIF recurrence; its natural result order is bit-reversed. The opposite recurrence cancels each forward pass up to its factor two. Thus multiplying in that common order and scaling the inverse by $L^{-1}$ gives precisely the cyclic convolution residues. As the support length is at most $L$, no linear coefficient wraps.

The prime/generator pairs are $(2013265921,31)$ and $(1811939329,13)$. Their predecessors have factorizations $15\cdot2^{27}$ and $27\cdot2^{26}$. The certificate tests the unit-power identity and $\gcd(g^{(p-1)/f}-1,p)=1$ at every prime divisor $f$ of $p-1$. For every prime divisor $l$ of $p$ the residue of $g$ modulo $l$ consequently has order $p-1$, forcing $l=p$. The same tests establish primitive roots for every admitted $L$. The maximum coefficient is $\min(a,b)(2^{16}-1)^2\le2^{25}(2^{16}-1)^2$, strictly below the product of the two primes. Reconstruct it as $u+p[((v-u)p^{-1})\bmod q]$. Carry in base $2^{16}$, handle the final byte exactly, and return every output byte. All modular products fit in 64 bits and every butterfly sum fits an unsigned 32-bit word. CRT reconstruction plus carry stays below $2^{63}$. Signed inputs, discarded leading zeros, zero, odd byte lengths and unequal operands are covered.

The two primes require $6(L/2)\log_2 L$ butterflies, two sets of pointwise products, two inverse scale scans and one CRT/carry scan. Each stage copies contiguous half-blocks to the temporary streams, emits the first half sequentially, then appends the second. Return each tape head across its actual interval. These are $O(L)$ moves per layer for bounded-width cells on a fixed number of tapes. Initialization and final wiping cost $O(L)$, including every unused cell of the arena. Word arithmetic has constant bit cost here because the prime sizes are fixed. The packed C arena is exactly $16L$ bytes, excluding inputs, outputs, stack locals and FFI copies. At the maximum $L$ this is 1 GiB. Equal operands can each use 536,870,912 bits. If the shorter operand has at most 192 bits, stream it across 192-bit limbs of the longer operand with a bounded carry. Beyond the transform cap, split into halves, run three difference-form Karatsuba children, and use the full signed difference in the middle coefficient. Every child has at most $\lceil n/2\rceil$ bits. This preserves the general algorithm and the $O(k^{\log_2 3})$ scalar upper bound at $k$ bits. The best crossover for other unequal lengths is unmeasured. An unsuccessful scratch allocation is reported to the caller. ## Native receiver in a real multiplication

On two short cyclic axes the complete product has an explicit row receiver. Serialize the real or complex signed input records into their component bit planes using a fixed signed format, pad the last 48-plane group with separate zeros and extend the two genuine address extents to nine. Each group uses all 48 genuine $2\times2$ ternary lows. Call the physical zero-singular extension/right route and the true type split on the complete opaque address bodies. The only raw rank-one line is the first coordinate. Matched via a column transposition, the second occurrence exchanges its complementary projector. At each true low $g$ the two actual projectors are $gE_{00}g^{-1}$ and $gE_{11}g^{-1}$. Their sum is the identity and their cross products vanish. Their product therefore exchanges the full head and the *reversed* tail. The ordinary wrapper with two such calls and its three trit corrections gives the unreversed exchange. Each exchange actually permutes the payload bit planes. In row-major layout read the source CRT control and run the actual Beneš row on the contiguous right coordinate. Transpose back, reject any data in the temporary address or plane holes, and reassemble signed components. The inverse accepts arbitrary changed products. The bit planes supply actual independent fields for this finite receiver. The full all-size theorem obtains its stock from the earlier portion of an operand address as proved in the machine bill. ## Input lengths

For the composite caller put $b=\lceil\log_2 n\rceil$, $s=(6b+1).\mathrm{bit\_length}()$, $W=65536s^3$. The first nonempty axis count requires $4n>b2^{W-1}$. On a fixed $s$ plateau some $n\le2^b$ satisfies it exactly when $2^{b-W+3}>b$. The integer comparison first succeeds at $s=34$, $W=2575826944$, $b=2575826973$. The first operand length in bits is exactly
$$
 n=2575826973\cdot2^{2575826941}+1 .

There is one axis there and its square band contains an eligible odd square. The direct proof bounds each subsequent nonempty band's coprime supply, including multi-axis cases. The baseline uses the published direct program's finite Karatsuba branch on identical operands. The GMP comparison uses its C import/multiply/export API through the same Python boundary. CPython built-in multiplication provides a third comparison. The expected product was computed outside the timed region, and every returned value was compared.
