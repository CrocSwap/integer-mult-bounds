# Bounded coefficients for all binary right matchers

The terminal and frame proofs are in [the ternary cover](TERNARY-COVER.md). Dense elimination over the three-local rationals proves that an admissible frame exists, but its intermediate numerators can depend on the full compiled dimension. The executable [factored compiler](factored_frames.py) avoids this cost. A matcher for the actual complete binary compiler needs only swaps and elementary additions or scalings with reduced rational numerators and denominators of absolute value at most **20**. This bound includes inverse routing. It holds for every product, partial block, split, dilation, positive completion and lower-rank invocation.

## Representation

Write the coordinate projection onto the ordered mask J as E(J). Keep the true frame F as a chronological list of elementary right operations. The represented address projector is F E(J) F⁻¹. A leading mask is unnecessary. Here are the construction rules.

1. For one of the 636 native frames use the original eight-axis basis when the source specifies it. For a nested MOVE or helper difference, compute its eight-axis frame over the three-local rationals once. Every generator and port was included in the authenticated finite census. A binary QUERY uses its port head line, an idle body uses its stage coordinates and a root target uses that head with its specified disjoint stages. Positive native operators in the eight-axis inner network use their own computed frame and full spectators. The fixed port bridge keeps its actual native basis, seven transpositions and the inverse basis.
2. Embedding places each factor on its original ordered coordinate subset and fixes every exterior coordinate. If the ambient is enlarged, append its zero complement to the **mask interpretation**, keeping the frame the identity on the new axes. Dilation by f uses F ⊗ I(f). Its word has f disjoint copies of each gate. More generally F ⊗ G = (F ⊗ I)(I ⊗ G). Each factor of the first word is issued once per second coordinate, and each factor of the second once per first coordinate. The product mask J × K is lexicographic. This gives the selected Cartesian axes of all 480 factors, including the native working spectators.
3. In a positive split replace J by its successive stipulated sublists and retain F. A complement within an active ambient uses the complement of J there in the same frame. A partial last block gets precisely its remaining indices. No coefficient is generated. The sum of several disjoint masks in this one frame is their union. Native cuts use the specified raw port coordinates. For an internal difference with no external basis, using the chosen three-local basis fixes its selected coordinates once, before any split. Each whole split composes to the original idempotent on the original owner.
4. To match source P = F E(J) F⁻¹ and target Q = L E(K) L⁻¹ of equal rank, generate a permutation U that maps the j-th basis vector of K to the j-th basis vector of J, and matches their complete ordered complements. At most N−1 transpositions suffice by the cycle decomposition. Emit the words F, U, L⁻¹. Their product S satisfies P S = S Q. Undo with the exact reverse word and inverse coefficients. At a completed call the child receives all its roles at their own transported frame. It can mix the resulting arbitrary payloads. Once the child returns, corresponding inverse routes restore every parent field.
5. A smaller uniform program has the full rank-e endpoint on its own standard e-axis space. Place it in the canonical selected coordinate block, keep the other parent coordinates as complete spectators and perform the parent/child adapter at this boundary. The paid invocation returns before the next one. A child calendar need not inherit a new dense rational basis computed from an ambient parent product. If a flat representation stores intervening adapters, retain their factor lists. Multiplicity scaling, fresh roles and denominator clearing clone complete programs and counts of fields. They never multiply a gate coefficient.

These operations cover the binary grammar in the source, including the parent map at a flow cut and the complement n−s. The separate Gaussian compiler matches its coordinate sets by binary coordinate permutations, and uses its existing Clifford scalar tables and guards. Gaussian factors do not occur as three-local right multipliers of the ordinary cover. They remain a separate setup and movement debit in the complete comparison. ## Size and movement

An elementary matcher consists of a swap, a unit scaling, or an addition of a rational multiple of one coordinate to another. Its inverse coefficient is its reciprocal for a scaling, and its negative for an addition. At the eight-axis generators the checked absolute numerator/denominator maxima are 8/20 for scalings and 13/20 for additions, where the slash separates two independent maxima. Copying, permuting and inverting these instructions gives the asserted bound 20 in every compiled factor. At most 820 signed rational encodings are needed even if all pairs of nonzero denominator 1 through 20 and numerator −20 through 20 are allowed. This pays for the complete set of coefficient values. Gate occurrence lists, axis indices, ownership, right-route scans and low-control lists remain separate.

One literal port route has at most 115 elementary instructions (54 + 7 + 54). A native frame or a difference has at most 54. Even the deliberately loose estimate of 256 instructions per raw projector frame allows one port route followed by the eight-axis frame. Repeated internal MOVEs are restored at their own boundary and do not concatenate into a raw projector. On ambient at most N, each gate of a raw factor in a single 480-fold product is copied at most N times. Its product frame therefore has at most 256·480·N elementary gates. Dilation and zero padding are included since the number of repetitions in the final ambient is still at most N. A pair of such product frames plus an arbitrary coordinate alignment uses fewer than 2¹⁸ N elementary gates. A split changes only the mask. If the compiler chooses to keep ordered active indices explicit, a gate fits 2⌈log₂(N+1)⌉+16 bits for its type, two addresses, two magnitude fields and the sign. Each mask fits N⌈log₂(N+1)⌉ bits.

This is a bound for one local placement. The nested computation performs and pays each placement separately. For a coarse bound on a single nested path, the uniform rank argument decreases the integer n strictly on a call for a positive complement or deepest exit. Within one rank n there are at most 1+⌊log₂ n⌋ proper flow levels because each productive rank is at least two. A flow edge from one fixed unit to the next contributes one local matcher, plus its inverse for restoration, and the 480 raw product levels can each add one such edge and its return before its parent resumes. Even allowing four local matchers per product level, this gives fewer than 2³¹ N² (1+⌈log₂ N⌉) elementary gates over all open ranks and one path of raw labels. At the stated carrier its gate and mask records fit in 2³⁶ N² (1+⌈log₂ N⌉)² bits. This is less than 3^(N²). It bounds a full flattened open-path descriptor. The total across chronological repeat occurrences and independent fields additionally needs their actual multiplicities. This argument cannot bound the full unrolled program by 3^(N²) without those multiplicities.

There is also a bound on the coefficient and coordinate dictionary for **all distinct local binary matchers**. It does not count a different duplicate payload for every reference. Put H = log₂ N. One raw frame choice takes 32 bits, even if the source-owner and occurrence labels are retained. The 107,460 paid and 20,706 productive labels are both below 2¹⁷, and each type has at most the 111 port and 18 stage choices. For each of at most 480 factors of a local product frame, record its type, and a list of ordered disjoint embedded copies of its raw coordinates. This list contains at most N distinct indices, plus at most N markers for the ends of groups. Allow up to two further arbitrary coordinate permutations per factor to describe axis reordering. Together these fields cost no more than 4N(H+2)+40 bits per factor, using explicit complete arrays. Record the resulting positive mask as a single list of at most N coordinates, in order. Do this for the two frames of the matcher and also record its alignment permutation and its ambient. Thus a key for a distinct local matcher fits in 2¹³ N(H+2) bits. This counts a superset of the required keys. A lower-rank call re-enters the standard local rank program and pays the two prescribed surrounding routes. No higher-rank ancestor frame is appended to its dictionary key. With lengths and the expanded elementary lists included, the total data of all these distinct local keys and their compiled words is less than 2^(2¹⁴ N(H+2)) bits, hence below 3^(N²) for the actual H. This is a storage ceiling if the dictionary is emitted, or an enumeration ceiling if it is generated sequentially. Labels, pointers and repeated passes on the actual chronology still cost time and space under their separate counts.

On a physical low/high cover, run each elementary factor separately. For a low representative with source coordinate s and target t, compute the new low representative before acting on its high coordinate. Add the integral carry (old rational target − new low representative)/3 to the high result for that factor. For addition the high result is the old target plus the indicated rational times the source. For scaling it is the indicated rational times the old target. For a swap the complete high fields exchange. The implementation uses the retained complete target pass, small-rational scaling, ordered shift, and the previously funded ordinary exchange when the source follows the target. Each row of the N-row cover executes its affected column instruction. Untouched high digits, original slot order, old high stock and all outer spectators stay in the carried records. The low dispatcher for each intermediate factor is a permutation of full low packets. At the terminal rank-one occurrence the pivot finds only the current low. Therefore high digits affect carries and full coefficients, but never the child count or the selection of an operation.

A scaling needs at most 20 of the predecessor's integer-run buckets in either direction. A scale and its inverse may perform the two bounded numerator and denominator passes. A high addition needs a complete ordered rotation and at most two genuine earlier-stage exchanges per row. Every actual factor and reverse must be charged; using only one sweep for the entire product would give a false resource bound. At a terminal one can compute the native column and covector modulo 3^w by applying the factor and inverse-transpose lists to the indicated coordinate vector. The full g still requires the charged linear solve in the ternary proof. Thus bit growth of a dense rational product is absent even during setup and evaluation of this factor program.

The check also uses odd partial masks, zero exterior coordinates, tensor lifts, complement, direct full payloads, and a covered rank-one output obtained from the factor sequence.
