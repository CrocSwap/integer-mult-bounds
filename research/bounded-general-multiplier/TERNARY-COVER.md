# A ternary ordinary cover for the mixed-rank multiplication construction

The binary address ring can be fixed to `Z/3^w` for **every** ordinary stage of the mixed-rank supplier. Its terminal rank-one request uses at most nine full reversed children. Its nonrecursive adapter has exactly $8(N-1)$ ordered shear slots. The finite compiler chooses all split frames at 3. This changes the radix of the ordinary address cover, including its cold start, while preserving the two required child-width moments and every independent output. Payload addition remains XOR. The complex scalar ring, outer Fourier primes and precision contracts retain their own definitions. [Mixed supplier](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/7830) · [Native realization](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/7837) · [Complete construction](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/7846)

## 1. Fix the address prime before the binary compiler

Write $A=\mathbb Z_{(3)}$ for the rationals with denominator coprime to three. Use the compressed source words in `vendor`. The binary word SHA-256 is `d28f0d28d9d87d5fe57b8ef19e12faf98014f0bbef781987ce22daaa82f21cb9`. The binary root SHA-256 is `757b3523798f840bdb02028b23708c3416627a99ea09e7a3c4bcdc5cd0c7ce4b`. The check reads all 636 native image/kernel bases and every one of the 111 *joint* port bases. Their absolute determinants have the following multiplicities. The checks use the exact rational columns of the sources.

| Absolute determinant | 1 | 4 | 16 | 64 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Native basis | 28 | 326 | 250 | 32 |
| Joint port basis | 8 | 103 | 0 | 0 |

The native bases are integral with maximum absolute entry 2. The port bases have maximum absolute entry 1. In their inverses, native projectors and nested differences, the largest absolute reduced numerator is 3 and the largest reduced denominator is 4. All denominators are units at 3 because the bases are integral and have unit determinant there. The check recomputes all 2,874 MOVE occurrences and 458 productive helper differences. There are 2,382 distinct unordered pairs. Each pair satisfies $PQ=QP=P$ after ordering by rank, so its difference is an idempotent of the required rank.

The actual root word has 38 roles, 18 stages and 798 events. It contains 364 SWAP, 362 ADD, 36 LOCAL, 32 IDLE_ONE and four QUERY instructions. Each LOCAL places the same native binary job at its specified ports. At port $i$ and stage $s$, apply the inverse of the eight-axis joint port frame, interchange its seven body coordinates with the seven fresh stage coordinates, and apply the frame. These operations preserve the fixed head coordinate and all exterior coordinates. The 127 logical root coordinates occupy a physical 134-axis work space. All 1,998 port-stage placements act on 15 of these axes. ADD and QUERY use the binary payload operator, and IDLE_ONE composes existing exchanges. They introduce no further rational address coefficients. The paired root is separate from the complex scalar caller. [Native projector and port code](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/a7f99a5f5f919dd3ffb2987f90c1e7d6527feb1e/research/grade14-native-realization/projectors.py) · [Root polynomial and physical placement check](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/a7f99a5f5f919dd3ffb2987f90c1e7d6527feb1e/research/grade14-native-realization/check_binary.py)

For any finite idempotent $P\in M_n(A)$, reduce its columns modulo three. Scan first the columns of $P$, then the columns of $I-P$, taking each column whose residue increases their joint span. Keep the **original rational column** when its residue is selected. Over the residue field the two images are complementary and span the whole space. Thus the selected frame $F_P$ has $n$ columns and its determinant is a unit at three. Each original column from the first list satisfies $Px=x$, and each from the second satisfies $Px=0$. Therefore

    P F_P = F_P diag(I_r,0).

The rational rank equals the residue image rank $r$. The inverse is over $A$ by the adjugate formula. Equal-rank jobs use $S=F_P F_Q^{-1}$, which satisfies $P S=S Q$. The compiler can split any positive rank by taking any specified number of the first $r$ frame coordinates, forming the two corresponding conjugate coordinate projections. The resulting two projectors multiply to zero in both orders and sum to $P$.

Tensor products, coordinate padding, transport through these frames, identity and zero blocks, complements on the stipulated active space, and disjoint sums all preserve these identities in $A$. In particular a partial final block and its zero exterior use the same construction. Apply this induction to the finite chronological products, their cuts, all 480 binary factors, and the arbitrary original uniform dimension. Each matcher and inverse reduces to an invertible map over every `Z/3^w`. Integer denominator clearing in the *multiplicity* census supplies counts of distinct fields. It does not divide a binary address or payload. This identifies the exact change to the predecessor's choice of arbitrary rational frame followed by finite exclusions. The function `frame_at_three` computes these frames, `split` produces their positive parts, and `matcher` constructs the whole-coordinate conjugacy. [Compiler and finite uniformization](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/343f7b1817e26ad3f89d2ddad313b12741d53602/research/mixed-rank-uniform-composition/COMPILER.md)

The integer operation list for one native 8-by-8 source or port factor comes from the retained `row_program`. This includes every raw frame, the selected frame for each frame projector, every selected nested-difference frame, and each joint port basis. The longest emitted word has 54 instructions. Reduced scale numerators and denominators are at most 8 and 20 in absolute value. The two maxima for an addition are 13 and 20. A reverse factor takes the inverse word. For the resource-bounded construction, keep the actual eight-axis generators as factor lists through the entire compiler. This gives bounded primitive coefficients and their full transport cost at larger dimensions. [Factored compiler and size bounds](FACTORED-ROUTES.md)

## 2. Place the actual leaf

Let $R=\mathbb Z/3^w$, $w\ge1$, and let $P=u v^t$ be a split rank-one leaf, with $v^t u=1$. Such factors are available constructively. Before reducing the native rational leaf, choose a unit diagonal $p_{ii}$ at three and take $u_*=P_{*,i}/p_{ii}, v_*=P_{i,*}$. This factorization holds because the image is a free line and the pivot is a unit. At the actual complete cover row $g$, use $u=gu_*, v^t=v_*^t g^{-1}$. The routine `covered_factors` performs the matrix-vector multiplication and solves $g^t v=v_*$ over the full ring using unit pivots. It includes every high digit of $g$. This costs at most $O(N^3)$ ring operations and $O(N^2)$ ring words for one exposed matrix, plus reduction of the actual compiled rational factors. These operations belong to coefficient production. If a tape scan recomputes factors for later slots, charge the repeated solves. In particular a complete coefficient-generation procedure does not have linear cost in $N$ merely because the terminal coefficient list does.

Select the first $i$ with $u_i v_i\ne0\pmod3$. Such an $i$ exists since the trace is one. Its selection uses the current fresh low matrix and the native rank-one factors. The truth of the test is constant on its entire high fibre. Unit entries of $u_i$ and $v_i$ lift to all $w$. An arbitrary diagonal ancestor weight, raw data, past branches, spectators, high digits and input bits never affect this choice.

When $i>0$, name four distinct width-$f$ packets $a=H_0,b=H_i,c=T_{N-1},d=T_{N-1-i}$, using the actual physical tail order. In chronological order execute four unit-weight reversed children on $(a,c),(b,d),(a,d),(b,c)$. Each crosses the two banks and fixes the full gap and suffix. Each original packet follows two reversals. The result is the simultaneous internal-orientation-preserving interchange $a\leftrightarrow b,c\leftrightarrow d$. The physical backing seen by the middle operator has its projector **and** its arbitrary head weights reindexed by the same transposition. The controller reads the original ancestor weight at the new source index. It changes the address lookup and does not copy or synthesize another weight bank. After the middle operator return, execute the inverse four children in reverse order. Use neither adapter when $i=0$. There are no private data packets, same-orientation primitives, half-field calls, fixed field permutations at zero cost, or presorted inputs. All auxiliary descriptors are discarded and all coordinate positions restored. Width zero is the empty operation.

## 3. Compute the lower slots directly

Work on one aligned column. Column $j$ of the head pairs with physical column $f-1-j$ of the reflected tail. Relabel the chosen pivot to zero. Normalize the line as $P=b c^t$ with $b_0=1$, $h=c_0$ a unit and $h+\sum_{i>0}c_i b_i=1$. Use the normalization $X_i=H_i/A_i$ only for the proof. The code emits the following physical instructions. Let $Y_i=T_{N-1-i}$ in reflected block and atom order. Each `+=` takes an earlier physical source into a later target. Perform each whole sweep in its stated order before starting the next sweep.

| Sweep | Slots, $i>0$ | Action on the stored fields |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | $N-1$ | $H_i\mathrel{+}=-(A_i/A_0)b_iH_0$ |
| 2 | $N-1$ | $Y_0\mathrel{+}=(c_i/h)Y_i$ |
| 3 | $N-1$ | $Y_i\mathrel{+}=(b_i/A_0)H_0$ |
| 4 | $N-1$ | $Y_0\mathrel{+}=-(c_i/(hA_i))H_i$ |

Perform one reversed central swap on the head and tail pivot packets with physical weight $A_0h$ at that column. Its tail output contains the reciprocal weight. Undo all four sweeps in reverse order with their coefficients negated. This action has the original graded topology, every zero high coefficient included. The one identically zero coarse link at the end of the predecessor's third sweep is omitted for all columns.

Put $d=h(Y_0-X_0)+\sum_{i>0}c_i(Y_i-X_i)$. In normalized coordinates the requested map sends $(X_i,Y_i)$ to $(X_i+b_i d,Y_i-b_i d)$, including $b_0=1$. The four forward sweeps leave pivot head $X_0$, change the other heads to $X_i-b_iX_0$, and change other tails to $Y_i+b_iX_0$. At the tail pivot they give

    Y_0 + h^-1 Σ_{i>0} c_i (Y_i - X_i + b_i X_0) = (X_0+d)/h.

The requested exchange sends $d$ to $-d$, since $c^t b=1$. The prepared nonpivot fields are invariant under it. Its effect on the two prepared pivot fields is hence the normalized swap of weight $h$. Conjugating by the four preparatory sweeps proves the desired exchange on every raw column. The scaled head and reversed ordering give exactly the physical weight $A_0h$. This proof allows arbitrary ancestor unit weights at each atom and each column. It is valid over a commutative ring with the stated units. At $N=1$ all four sweeps are empty.

Each physical slot needs at most two original ancestor weights and two chart entries. After obtaining the two line factors, selection and normalization need $O(N)$ ring words and operations. Given the normalized vectors, a single forward column uses $6(N-1)$ modular coefficient multiplications and at most $3(N-1)$ modular unit inversions with the displayed formulas. The backward sweep can reuse coefficients or recompute them at this cost. This count excludes the central weight, the linear chart normalization, the paid address operations themselves and the full cover solve above. The overwritten data storage belongs to the original fields. The controller can produce a single coefficient by its slot index and aligned column without building even a per-column operation list.

The old dense executable performs two $2N\times2N$ multiplications in `good_column`. The sparse test in its `multiply` skips zero left entries. To obtain a lower bound on its actual arithmetic, take $N\equiv1\pmod3, w=1, u=v=\mathbf1$ and unit ancestor weights. This is a genuine rank-one instance of every residue cover with this $N$, including the stated binary carrier. Of the tail rows in its left argument to the second multiply, the first $N-1$ are original residual rows. Each has $2N-1$ nonzero entries. Each such entry executes all $2N$ modular multiply-add iterations, hence at least $2N(N-1)(2N-1)$ updates before the other rows. The temporary arrays hold at least $4N^2$ entries. The comparison refers to building the good-leaf coefficients from a placed projector or its factors. Other real-word costs, including generating the placed factors from $g$, remain charged. ## 4. Fund the complete ordinary stages

At most nine genuine width-$f$ children are paid. For one terminal call $f\le\lfloor c/N\rfloor$. In the notation of the mixed compiler its uniform priced ratio satisfies $P/B<N^{1-c_0+\delta_u}$, with $c_0=0.003879928046148$ and $\delta_u=1/400000000000$. Let $C=c_0-\delta_u-\delta_c$ with $\delta_c=1/125000000000$. The complete child moment at exponent $1-C$, with both productive volume and all child calls counted, is bounded by

    9(P/B) N^(-(1-C)) < 9 N^(-δ_c).

At $N=2^{1285200000000000}$ we have $\log_2(N^{\delta_c})=10281.6>4$ and $9<16$. The moment at exponent 1 is no larger. These estimates are uniform on *every* low and high row. They need no frequency estimate for good pivots. All productive outputs, paid occurrences, row stock, padding and spectators retain their original multiplicities. The strict funded exponents can therefore be used unchanged by all six previous ordinary stages, including the stage beginning with savings zero. [Exact funding and stage numerals](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/343f7b1817e26ad3f89d2ddad313b12741d53602/research/mixed-rank-uniform-composition/lean/Funding.lean)

The right route still sends $g_0+3X$ to low representative $h_0=g_0S\pmod3$ and high matrix $XS+(g_0S-h_0)/3\pmod {3^{w-1}}$. This division is legal since each rational numerator after subtraction has residue zero. The added rational carry is integral at 3. The inverse follows the full inverse matcher. The unchanged row program acts on $N^2$ true high fields and all older high coordinates. Its ordered operations and paid earlier-stage exchanges transfer every record, then restore its layout. Single-digit cold exchanges use nine physical buckets and traverse all genuine and padded records. The radix conversion, complete gap, return ranges and final order obey the same whole-field construction for $q=3$. [Physical high route and cold implementation](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/a7f99a5f5f919dd3ffb2987f90c1e7d6527feb1e/research/grade14-native-realization/cover.py)

The repeated per-prefix arithmetic is polynomial in $w$ with constants at the generated geometry. Current mutable modular matrices use $O(N^2w)$ bits. Ancestor access branches into at most two head coefficients per terminal slot. A depth-first descriptor traversal reuses storage within each of the $O_N(\log e)$ ancestors, including its indices and full exposed matrix. The fixed finite routing, rational numerator and denominator data are charged at their actual size. Since $e\le w^{250}$ and each target fibre contains $3^w$ records, or $3^{w-1}$ on a high field, these fixed polynomial charges are absorbed for all sufficiently large widths. Every coefficient is prepared once per relevant prefix and propagated through the gap. This gives the same nonterminal $O_F(V(c+w^{1-b}))$ time tariff at earlier-stage saving $b$, with changed fixed constants. The cutoff must include the actual polynomial and tape machinery. Small widths use the exact elementary or earlier stage service. This proves a fixed threshold exists. No numeric practical crossover or universal relative bound for the complete hidden constant follows from this induction alone. ## 5. Verification and credit

Run `python3 -B check_ternary.py` with Python 3.10 or later. It authenticates the two native input archives and checks each native projector and port determinant. It compares direct independent physical outputs with the changed leaf on 1,216 examples, including 497 nonleading pivots, and compares every lower coefficient with the actual old dense good-branch word on 738 instances. The cases exhaust all split factorizations in two dimensions over `Z/3` and `Z/9`, then cover other dimensions, higher digits and unequal ancestor weights. The actual MOVE at instruction 12 of the native source supplies an additional 144 included cases. Its first diagonal is nonunit in 98. The complete high and cold checks call the retained published physical implementation. They check 97 full high routes with 10,497 records and 95,940 cold records.

Lean checks the four-exchange adapter, the existence of the required nonzero low pivot, the four-sweep factorization over every commutative ring with the indicated units, and its physical specialization to an arbitrary unit at each head index. The official standalone rechecker accepted the compiled module. The reported endpoints use only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound, with only propext used by the packet reversal lemma. The written proof connects the fixed program’s address indices, raw frames, rational splitting, cover row selection, spectator-safe scan tariffs and six-stage induction. The executable comparisons test the Python implementation. They do not constitute a formal refinement of the Python interpreter or a new formal proof of the outer multiplication theorem. [Lean proof](lean/TernaryRouting.lean)

Nick Petalas previously gave a general rank-$r$ pivoted fallback with at most $1+12r$ children, including smaller half-width children, arbitrary dirty inputs and arbitrary ancestor weights. At rank one that theorem itself bounds the child multiplier by 13 and is sufficient for the available cover margin. The present result contributes the four opposite-bank exchanges, the thin coefficient generator, the fixed ternary native compilation and their use across this specific complete multiplier. The prior linear-width charge was already at most nine for rank one. The symbolic Laurent audit checked individual fallback words over several local rings. It supplied neither the ternary compiler nor this complete prime binding.
