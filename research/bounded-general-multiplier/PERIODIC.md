# A finite-order compiler for the mixed native words

The input is a strict chronological rule with true ambient A, separate
productive ports of ranks k_i, and ordered paid requests of ranks r_j.
Write k=min_i k_i>max_j r_j=r. Each port is an arbitrary complete field.
After its own full boundary adapters, each job acts by commuting coordinate
operators of order dividing four. It fixes all other coordinates upon return.

## Literal schedule

At rank n put u=4 floor(n/4), m=u/4. If m<A, use one public field and apply
its n one-coordinate jobs in sequence. Otherwise run the live forest at cap
m. Its height G is the largest g with A k^g≤m. At paid prefix p and
productive suffix a with |p|+|a|=G, a unit has c=prod(r[p])prod(k[a])
columns and is live exactly when Ac≤m. At port i its only possible lender
has the preceding paid prefix and suffix (i,a). That ancestor's current
paid request has the required rank k_i c. At the root, or if that parent
is ineligible, take a separate source. Every external rank s satisfies
m/A<s≤m. The missing parent's ambient exceeds m; for root ports use
A k^(G+1)>m. At the final paid prefix there is one unit. Give each paid
occurrence its own chronological exit. Its rank e≤r^(G+1)≤m^θ if
r≤A^θ and r≤k^θ.

For each external source i, let q_i be the least power of two with q_i s_i≥u.
Set T=max_i q_i. Then u≤q_i s_i<2u, and 4≤q_i<8A. Reserve T/q_i complete
owners per forest copy for port i. In round b<T choose owner floor(b/q_i)
from that block. Set q=q_i, s=s_i, j=b mod q, and x=(qs-u)/4. To emit
the actual ordered axis subset, visit t=0,...,s−1 and let z=j+qt.
If z<5x return floor(z/5); otherwise return z−4x. The integer x satisfies
0≤x<u/4. Each coordinate below x occurs in one run of five tickets, and
every other coordinate occurs once. If x>0 then q≥8 since q=4 forces
s=u/4. The tickets in a row differ by at least q, so each row has s
distinct legal axes. Euclidean division assigns every ticket to exactly one
row and one position in that row. The per-coordinate powers in a complete
period are 5 or 1. After all rounds apply at most three one-coordinate jobs
to the unprocessed n−u axes of each public field.

The positive complex axis is ((1+i)I+(1−i)X)/2. Its square is X and its
fourth power is the identity, including the absolute scalar. The adjoint
case follows by complex conjugation. Distinct tensor coordinates commute.
For BIT, work in (head, physically reversed tail) order. In a diagonal
chart D(P) exchanges its two P components. Each exchange squares to the
identity, and disjoint-axis exchanges commute. Conjugating all these
operations by one basis and one exterior weight preserves these equations.
For a paid rational projector use its ordered image/kernel basis and place
each child with its own full right matcher. Restore that placement before
advancing the source. Both two-sided complex native normalizers retain
their source signs and order. This argument only compares completed forest
calls; no native instructions or distinct cuts have exchanged chronological
positions.

Let h=sum_i T/q_i. Smaller exported widths B_e are already powers of two.
Set D=max_e B_e, M=D h. Export the least power B of two ≥15M. Use zD full
replicas in every round, where z=ceil(B/M). Then z≤30 and the actual R=zD h
owners obey B≤R<B+M≤16B/15. Initialize R−B separate private fields
including their full spectator extents, perform all their real paid calls,
and erase their complete zero endpoints. Their tails can be omitted since
a coordinate job fixes zero. Each individual cut produces exactly zD
requests, divisible by its own B_e. Return every group to its held owners
before continuing. Distinct simultaneous inputs have distinct owners by
the forest partial bijection and disjoint source blocks. A loan can contain
arbitrary correlated intermediate data. Only B root fields earn output
credit. All padding, visits and routing are charged below.

The reference generator in periodic.py enumerates the forest and paid cuts,
binds the source tickets, opens each replica's actual native iterator,
serves its yielded requests and resumes it. A subsidiary zero allocator
receives an entire exemplar, including physical geometry and active axes.
The numerical regression words in check_periodic.py are short independent
positive words. Their measurements apply only to those fixtures.

## Price and precision

With L(n)=P(n)/B(n) and P(n) the number of rank-one calls, the exact bill is

~~~
P(n)=(n−u)B + T sum_(individual exits e) (zD/B_e)P(e).
~~~

For 0<t<1 the forest's rank-by-rank cancellation applied to the strict
native moment, multiplied at each live unit by c^t, gives
sum_e e^t≤sum_i s_i^t. The period bound and s_i>u/(4A) give
T sum_i s_i^t/h≤max_i(q_i s_i^t)≤2 (4A)^(1−t)u^t. The ratio R/B
adds a factor strictly smaller than 16/15. The elementary branch costs n.
These are the terms in the separate funded-carrier induction. There is
no recursive complement.

The source common-grid argument expands a live row in terms c U x, where
each U is Clifford on the same entire D-bit root factor. Every completed
subset left-multiplies U by another such Clifford. It adds zero exterior
denominator or mass debt, including for the fifth visit to a coordinate.
A fresh helper at an unfinished native context retains its actual entrance
debt relative to the root. Its own odd scale is installed and removed as
in the immutable source. Every completed period, or one completed call
inside that period, returns before the next dependent instruction. Thus 140
fractional and 428 mass bits per open raw context still suffice. At n≥4A
the heads have n^(1−θ)≥2 and e≤n/2. A path at N=2^H has at most H+1 rank
frames and each forest frame at most v(G+1)≤v(H+1) raw contexts.
K≤v(H+1)^2 raw contexts suffice per ordinary recursion level, including
all replicas and rounds. Check 856 K<N at the chosen carrier. With
l=floor(log_N D) open ordinary levels, Nl≤D. The source fixed-root Clifford
row bound gives common F=p+D, W=p+h+2D+3 and temporary W+26. Completed
outer phases still transform each of their selected coordinates exactly
once. The old phase proof reanchors only at those true outer cuts.

## Width, expanded records and generator clock

For BIT v=480,a=134; the current complex strict word has v=264,a=153.
Both also satisfy the bounds below if the predecessor complex
v=1920,a=153 and its full completion are used. Take A=a^v.
Raw target count W0≤52,512<2^16, raw paid count P0≤283,152<2^19.
In a strict unit, W≤W0^v. Splitting every original paid tuple to individual
axes would give at most (a P0)^v paid slots. Completing every small target
in the earlier complex grammar could add at most (a W0)^v separate axes.
Dropping low targets adds no paid slots and keeps all original work.
Thus P<2^(28v), W+P<2^(29v), k≥12^v>2^(3v), A≥2^(7v).
Taking H=log2 n gives G≤H/(3v). The following counts include candidates
which fail the ambient check, with U for units, S for sources, J for exits.

~~~
U ≤ sum_(d=0)^G P^d W^(G−d) ≤ (P+W)^G ≤ n^(29/3).
S ≤ UW < n^(29/3+16/7)<n^12.     J ≤ UP<n^14.
~~~

Now B<30DTS<60 n^13 D≤n^14 D as T<8A≤2n. Induction gives B_n≤n^105 on
BIT with θ=13/15 and B_n≤n^448 on complex with θ=31/32. These degrees
also hold with a smaller certified θ.

A frozen raw occurrence has a conservative 2^64 structural record allowance.
The BIT raw ordinary and owned allowances are 10^12 and 10^7. The complex
ordinary receipt is 151,912,932,864, the two raw selected-step receipts
13,066,144,414,176 and 58,832,521,372,992, and the raw numerical home count
including dummies 397,968. The physical expansion of one basis instruction
and each operation on a whole input field have separate bills.

The v-factor chronological product contains sum_j W0^(v−1−j)P0^j raw
instances, for 0≤j<v. An internal binding has a target prefix, one
paid site and a paid or target suffix of total length at most v. Include
both sides of every binding, all ports, zero allocations/erasures, every
disjoint split and any completion job. The total is bounded by
2^64(v+1)[2(a+1)(W0+P0+2)]^v < 2^64(v+1)2^(28v)<n^5. The constant
also absorbs both endpoint records per site. The chronological traversal
does not materialize a cross product of all target and paid tuples.

One copy-round uses at most U macros. At every native instruction, port,
paid cut or binding, allow n+32 records for listing selected coordinates,
tagging, staging, returning and retiring. This gives <n^17 records per
copy-round. Since zDT/B<3n, local records per productive lane total <n^20,
including preparation and retirement of its share of the private fields
and the three tails. The recursive term is at most 3n J times the largest
normalized child bill. Thus for total records E and X(n)=max(1,E(n)/B_n),
X(n)≤n^24 max_e X(e). The base uses at most 4n records per lane.
Induction on e≤n^θ gives

~~~
                  productive B       total generated E       normalized X
BIT               ≤ n^105            ≤ 4 n^285               ≤ 4 n^180
complex           ≤ n^448            ≤ 4 n^1216              ≤ 4 n^768
~~~

To generate the whole owned structural schedule on fixed binary tapes take
r=285 or 1216 and N≥4A. Store the source lists by ordinal. Build plans in
increasing rank (or use a memoized depth-first traversal). At each rank the
planner examines fewer than n^10 candidates, n^12 source ports and n^14
exits. Even an ineligible path has c≤A^G≤N^(8/3), so its intermediate
product takes at most 3log2 N+1 bits. Base plans consist of the rank and
its one-lane base bill. Preparing all ranks costs fewer than N^20 indexed
integer/list operations, before physical lookup. On the strictification
pass there are ≤2^(19v) raw paid and ≤2^(16v) raw target tuples. The raw
macro bound pays each block, raw route and zero assignment. Keep its
full underlying label with each retained or paid ordinal.

Use serial integer owner names. Each counter, width and leaf sum has fewer
than 2000(H+1) bits (H=log2 N). Each tensor or forest path has at most
H+v+1 positions of at most 28v+2 bits. There are at most H+1 such forest
depths on a path. Each explicit root selector lists at most N coordinates.
Consequently the full structural tag, comparator key or staged selector
at one access occupies less than N^3 bits. A basis instruction gives its
coordinate operands and exact source indices. Computing the numerical
entries of those matrices is charged in the separate numeric compiler.

Turn the Cartesian loops into binary odometers. Charge one indexed visit
per bound port, yielded job, actual issued primitive, row ticket, native
frame, owner or return, all counted in E. Scanning each selector and its
path and running schoolbook integer arithmetic uses fewer than N^7 logical
cell operations per billed visit: there are at most N axes, (H+v+1)(H+1)
path labels, and all index integers have ≤2000(H+1) bits. Copying/comparing
a whole N^3-bit key fits this bound. All Python-style set or dictionary
operations can be implemented as finite linear searches, charging the head
travel below. The total number of issued logical accesses is therefore
<N^20+4N^(r+7)<N^(r+8). Each entry is smaller than N^3 bits. Even retaining
every entry ever created plus all output records and a second copied stream
uses less than L=N^(r+14) tape cells. To search or update an entry, scan the
whole store, rewind the candidate-key tape at each comparison, write all
updated rows to a second tape and rewind/erase the old stream. Fewer than
L^2 one-head moves suffice per access. A further factor L pays transfer and
initialization. The resulting structural generation uses at most N^300 cells
and N^1200 transitions on BIT, or N^1230 cells and N^4920 transitions on
complex. These are conservative constructive bounds. Numeric rational-chart
work, good-prime selection, the installed low-type programs and executing
the full routes on input fields belong in the complete physical invoice.

## Comparators and checks

For the literal #7846 compiler, every smaller width is multiplied at every
distinct cut. Its original raw paid count at rank two is 46,116 on BIT and
37,920 on complex. Each 2-only powered paid path of rank 2^v is unchanged
by either cutoff. In the predecessor's G+1 levels there are at least
(2^15)^(v(G+1)) separate cuts with exit e=2^(v(G+1)). At its funded roots
e≥A and the child width is ≥2 because a live root unit has at least two
different productive ports. With k≤2^(4v), A<2^(8v) for those old two
heads, G≥floor((H−8v)/(4v)). The old literal policy thus has
log2 B_old(2^H)>2^(15(H−8v)/4). No lower bound for a different
denominator-clearing policy is inferred.

The least-LCM #4507 policy and its adaptive extension already bound log B
by a polynomial in log N under their own conditions. They do not multiply
duplicate cuts. The single-grade #7828 has width ≤N^16 and O_source(N^51)
structural records at its own carriers. The new bounds have larger degrees.
The new mechanism supports multiple simultaneous productive grades and
eliminates every complement from the selected mixed raw construction.

Lean checks the ticket fibres, injective rows, Euclidean incidence, replica
padding, positive complex fourth power and the complete nested round/slot action in
an arbitrary monoid with pairwise commuting coordinate generators. Its
ordered_actions conclusion follows the exact transposed ticket formula
b+k j; the ambient operator algebra can be noncommutative. The bridge from the native adapters,
the forest compiler and its physical resource estimates are written proofs.
The Python checks use exact Gaussian integer numerators and an independent
Walsh spectrum oracle on 64 arbitrary 4096-entry complete fields. Weighted
BIT runs use an oblique chart over Z/27, a nontrivial exterior weight and
physical reversed tails. Each final owner is checked on two nested forests.

## Binding the real raw words

tagged_sources.py authenticates the eight source objects from the
36-call binary and 48-call complex constructions. Every root output and
fresh helper has a distinct tag. For BIT a MOVE tag contains the caller
clock, native clock and bank. QUERY and IDLE_ONE tags name the caller
clock and actual port. For complex the issuer follows the scheduled DAG
rows, selects only paid returns, and includes their native occurrence and
node. The fixed caller targets and requests retain their real instruction
locations. The true whole raw caller has separate diagonal productive
endpoints. Inside that caller the coupled main pair uses its common right
matcher throughout. Raw matching occurs only at a complete endpoint.

powered.py enumerates tensor tuples lazily. It retains the full raw label
in each kept or privately initialized port and in each paid slab. BIT uses
the first paid occurrence's grade to select either 10^480 or 11^480. It keeps
all productive grades ≥16^480. The new complex rule uses cap 10^264 and
keeps all productive grades ≥17^264. At a raw occurrence the interpreter
suspends every suffix instance at the actual paid tag and checks its
per-owner extent before entering the next raw factor. The final paid tuple
is split into concrete subranges of its own normalized axes, scaled by the
current column count. After returning from every split, the raw source
performs its own original exit chart. Every discarded productive field is a
distinct complete zero with actual intermediate work. Separate full private
centres can take arbitrary intermediate contents.
