# The retained center protocol cannot be made cheaper by chart retiming

**Exact finite structural theorem; no higher K.** Every one of PR4610's
eight retained center calls still has Gaussian rank at least eight under
any feasible chart reassignment in the monotone nine-axis model below.
The center bill is therefore at least **64 rank units**, and this floor
is attained by the submitted word.

This permits arbitrary binary subspaces at every nonfixed contact,
including all helper boundaries and both endpoints of every center call.
It is broader than one-contact moves, 511 one-axis tests, or a search
through superspaces/subspaces of the original charts. Logical warp charts
may change; the actual G maps remain fixed. The proof uses global lower
and upper subspace closures, rather than a failed candidate search.

For both complete emitted orientations, all eight originally low center
endpoints have upper bound `{0}`, while every high endpoint has a forced
eight-dimensional lower space. The calculation does not even impose a
relation between the two endpoints of a center call. Its rank floor is
forced by the surrounding computation.

[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) defines the model and proves the universal interval
argument. [RESULT.json](RESULT.json) records both exact source certificates:
22,255 nodes, 38,280 directed inclusions and 761 fixed nodes per orientation.
The scalar order, coefficients, references, main/adapter boundaries and
logical maps stay fixed. Persistent transports remain ascending. The owned
center keeps its clear/copy-or-gather, one call, scatter-or-copy-back and
erase protocol, with no added center calls between contacts.

New scalar circuits, descending persistent moves, inserted center calls,
changed main contracts or warp maps, larger ambient spaces and maps between
digit columns are outside scope. Other paid profile improvements with the
same center bill remain possible. This is not a universal 64-unit lower
bound on multiplication suppliers.

Historical [fixed-interface floors](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/1517)
