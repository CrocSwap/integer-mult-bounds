# Finite correctness argument and its limits

## Fixed matrix and circuit

The pinned generator derives the ordered 12-row binary transfer `N` for
phase-B block 6 from PR543's frozen geometry and plan. The search program uses
the upstream pivoted-LU sampler: it chooses an ordered wire permutation,
randomly pivots the output rows, applies `lu_ops`, and records the destination
wire for each target row. For each sampled candidate, `nodeopt.apply` replays
the local XOR operations from the twelve unit vectors and checks
`current[output_to_wire[k]] == N[k]` for every row.

The baseline word rebuilt from the frozen plan has the exact pinned canonical
hash. The selected 47-gate candidate has the same `N`, remains at frame 548,
and is emitted in the exact phase-B interval beginning at record 417. The word
generator propagates the selected output assignment to the subsequent Q-delivery
and needer writes, then regenerates the literal reverse cleanup. No hand-edited
downstream route is trusted.

## Whole-word interface

`verify.py` checks every live source pin against both its SHA-256 and Git blob
at the recorded base. It regenerates the baseline and candidate, checks all
proof-relevant JSON fields against the committed search certificate and
candidate word, and runs the selected-record binding checker.

The inherited R420 `xcheck.py` then checks exact F2 action on all 660 symbolic
columns: 120 source columns, 120 target columns, and 420 arbitrary dirty-helper
columns. Its H1–H5 hypotheses and the interleaved interface conclusion all
pass. The companion mutation suite rejects nine invalid mutations and accepts
one harmless cancelled pair. Literal cleanup restores every mutable source
and helper while preserving the target action.

The adapted full R420 pipeline additionally rebuilds native records and checks
the five-, nine-, and eleven-stage cohorts, frame geometry, whole-residual
hosting, exact invoices, and historical controls. The retained paid histogram
and rank mass are exactly the published R420 values. The nine-stage coefficient
changes from 416 to 418 bits but remains under its 512-bit charge; the
eleven-stage coefficient remains under its existing 14,208-bit charge.

## What is not proved

The search enumerates exactly 20,000 candidates generated by one randomized
pivoted-LU sampler and selects from its observed Pareto set. It is not an
exhaustive search over all 12-wire XOR circuits, does not prove a minimum gate
count, and does not show that another sampler cannot do better. The full R420
checks establish this candidate within the inherited finite construction and
accounting contracts; they do not prove the all-size compiler/realization,
mutable-basis/common-label, physical, precision/tape, or analytic assumptions,
or the integer multiplication theorem.
