# Reviewed general fixed-prime descriptor supplement

## Result, scope and earlier work

The accepted proof is `PROOF.md`, SHA256
`cbf42e276c2d1b25d72064801b6f0c0fb1ecab2d7cb0f771640a8bc4301dc14b`.
`review/REVIEW.md` accepts its local-ring construction, source alphabet,
coefficient-height argument, chronology and tariff interpretation within
the stated fixed-template contracts. This is separate model-assisted
review, not human or proof-kernel acceptance. The original author-stage
README, FROZEN and INTEGRATION JSON files retain their historical
pending-review wording because their bytes are frozen.

The proposition applies to every complete dyadic matcher
`S in GL_N(Z[1/2])` and every fixed odd prime q. It constructs a rational
elementary full-GL descriptor whose coefficients are independent of the
exponent w. Unit pivots are selected over the local ring of q-integral
rationals. The selection may depend on q. Additive numerators need not be
q-units; scaling numerators and all denominators must be q-units. This is
the source alphabet, without an unrelated bounded coefficient alphabet.
The executable treats primality as a caller premise and checks its actual
pivots; it does not certify that an arbitrary supplied q is prime.

The new descriptor direction and its inverse cover all high fields and
every localized carry. Each coupled X/Y contact uses one common descriptor;
both separately owned payload applications retain their service charges.
For one directed full route there are at most `2N^3` ordinary interchanges,
`N^3` ordered shifts including all `N^2` carry translations, and `N^2`
rational-unit scaling records. The proof supplies coefficient and carry
height bounds in terms of the fixed dyadic input, uniformly in q and w.

The existing `../routing/` package, already published at
`67d18a45fdf3f675bd88cb6f52e7d0356a9ace36`, is a distinct finite result. Its
q-independent short-repair words cover the exact 513 difference matchers,
and its explicit counterexample prevents generalizing that short repair
heuristic. This supplement preserves all of that directory byte-for-byte.
It adds a general fixed-q construction and input-sensitive size accounting;
it does not claim that elimination or the inherited full-GL bridge is new.
The two catalogues use different words and neither finite invoice is
presented as a universal improvement over the other's operation count.

## Registered replay and fresh evidence

The parent `VERIFY.json` still has eight explicit commands with a total
declared cap of 1,200 seconds. The first six invoke the corresponding
original commands in disposable identical copies, preserving their
scientific flags. Command seven runs both original finite-route reviewer
modes. Command eight regenerates the new compressed descriptor catalogue
and examples, then runs the new author and independent reviewer in normal
and optimized Python. The exact original registry is retained and
hash-authenticated as `reconciliation/REMOTE-VERIFY.json`.

`replay.py` removes stale output files before a checker runs, redirects
every reviewer output into a fresh private path, and compares the actual
new evidence. The prior complete-basis catalogue and both new constructor
outputs are compared byte-for-byte. Only the elapsed `seconds` field is
excluded from the two original finite-route reviewer receipt comparisons;
all their scientific fields must match. Other evidence is compared in
full. The original package's complete byte-and-mode snapshot must remain
unchanged, and every frozen/provenance binding is authenticated before and
after each registered replay.

The new author evidence covers 629 matrices, 2,516 descriptors, 5,032 exact
word products, 15,096 prime-power word actions, 7,548 affine charts and a
complete 3,888-case GL2(Z/9) carry check. The independent reviewer checks
5,032 exact words and 23,112 fraction-free pivot-minor identities, together
with coefficient domains, all stated height and count bounds, and explicit
wrong-order, low-zero-shear and composite-pivot controls. Neither reviewer
imports the new constructor or author scientific checker.

## Unchanged obligations

The record counts are the inherited service categories, not bit-step
counts. A rational scaling still expands into integer numerator and
denominator services and possibly a sign operation. Its bucket count can
grow exponentially with the fixed input height, and the fixed low table
can have up to `q^(N^2)` entries. Preparation, lookup, complete payload
scans, ownership, cleanup and previous ordinary services remain charged.
No new polynomial physical bound in N or coefficient height is asserted.

The quantitative physical condition `q > 2^45 N^5` remains in force for
the true completed carrier N. Existing fine-prime supply and transfer
results must still be applied at the exact analytic-recovery caller for
the newly chosen template; this supplement does not perform that retained
caller-specific transfer. References in the frozen files to remaining
outer-prime work concern this framework obligation. Global storage, all
retained ownership/precision/tape/analytic contracts and the full
multiplication theorem remain outside this result. No new kappa is claimed.
