\subsection{Scalar, frame and temporary-stream contracts}
The retained source-copy, reversible mixer and scatter maps satisfy
$JLV=I$. Over $\mathbb F_2$ the chronological word
\[
 L,\ J,\ L^{-1},\ V,\ L,\ J,\ L^{-1},\ V
\]
adds the input to the target and restores every arbitrary auxiliary.
Over Gaussian dyadics use its corresponding old-value subtraction and
new-value addition. A designated retained output has a distinct terminal
output-use carrier: a matching may feed that carrier, but cannot merge
two designated terminals or append a later middle-producer consumer.
Its remaining uses before cleanup are its scatter reads, all of which
leave its scalar value unchanged.

Within one tensor invocation suppress the fixed future line and put
\[
 D_0=B\otimes F_h,\quad D_1=A\otimes F_h,\quad
 A=B\mathbin{\perp}P,\quad D_U=D_0\mathbin{\perp}(P\otimes U),
\]
where $P$ and the suppressed future factor are one-dimensional and
$U$ is a nondegenerate retained-center space of dimension $r$.
Thus a local change of rank $d$ has ambient rank $d$.
Write $\mathcal F_Q$ for the address frame: it is the rational partial
swap $D_Q$ in the bit construction, and the binary phase $C_Q$ in the
complex construction. Changing a copy from frame $Q_1$ to $Q_2$ means
applying $\mathcal F_{Q_2}\mathcal F_{Q_1}^{-1}$ to its physical stream.
Orthogonal backgrounds cancel in this quotient.

Copies use Paureel's retained copy/transform/read/discard interface
\cite{paureel,pr29}. A copy has precisely the original complete role
stream, including all control and spectator ranges, and volume $V/W$.
It is processed while inactive original streams are parked on the fixed
depth-first stack tapes. Copying, pointwise reads and erasure are linear
in that stream's volume. Sequential processing needs one additional
fixed work stream, not an independently varying row coordinate.

\begin{lemma}[Copied retained-center schedule]
In either invocation orientation a designated retained center's
physical rank profile can be changed from $z^r+z^h$ to
$z^r+z^{h-r}$, with the same scalar action, original role endpoints,
and arbitrary-scratch restoration. The rank-$r$ transform on the copy
is included in the recursive call list.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
In the forward orientation the original center has scalar value $w$
at frame $D_U$ after the middle mixer. Its old sequence was
$D_U\to D_0\to D_1$: scatter at $D_0$, then cleanup at $D_1$,
with ranks $r,h$.
Keep the original at $D_U$, copy its physical stream, and transform
only the copy to $D_0$, paying rank $r$. Supply every scatter read
from that transformed copy while its data targets remain at their
unchanged common frame $D_0$, then erase the temporary. The reads
are exactly those of $w$ in the old schedule. Since they do not change
$w$, move the original directly from $D_U$ to $D_1$ before its first
cleanup incidence; this costs $h-r$. Every subsequent original gate
therefore receives the same scalar value at its required frame.

In the reverse complementary orientation the old sequence was
$D_0\to D_1\to D_{U^\perp}$, with ranks $h,r$ and scatter at $D_1$.
After the preceding all-$D_0$ operations, advance the original directly
to $D_{U^\perp}=D_0\mathbin{\perp}(P\otimes U^\perp)$, paying
$h-r$. Copy it and transform the copy to $D_1$, paying $r$.
Use that copy for all early scatter reads, then erase it. The original
already has the exact frame and unchanged scalar value required by the
following inverse mixer. Terminal output-use separation ensures no
intervening producer consumer has been bypassed.

For partial swaps either direction of the rank-$r$ change is the same
involution. For complex phases the forward copy uses $C_U^{-1}$,
the direct original growth uses $C_{U^\perp}$, and the reverse copy
uses $C_FC_{U^\perp}^{-1}=C_U$. These are the same charged ranks,
implemented by the retained forward/inverse residual interface.
All tensor backgrounds cancel as stated. Every original scalar
incidence, final source-copy cancellation and auxiliary source/sink
operator is unchanged. Thus the scalar identity and all endpoints
remain exact on arbitrary initial auxiliary values.
\end{proof}

For the selected centers $r=h-1$, and there are $h$ distinct retained
output uses per invocation. Their old histogram has rank mass
$hR_h+2\ell_h$, where $\ell_h=h(h-1)$. The new histogram is exactly
\[
 H'_{h,1}=H_{h,1}+h,\qquad H'_{h,h}=H_{h,h}-h,
 \qquad H'_{h,r}=H_{h,r}\quad(r\ne1,h),
\]
and hence $\sum_r rH'_{h,r}=hR_h+\ell_h$.
Every old rank-$(h-1)$ residual remains as a paid transform of a copy;
its projector and ordinary bit pivot profile are unchanged. Only the
full-rank original transition becomes the rank-one complement.
The physical role count $R_h$ is unchanged.

For two factors, put $N=v_av_b$, $B_a=v_bR_a$, $B_b=v_aR_b$,
$W=2N+B_a+B_b$ and $L=v_b\ell_a+v_a\ell_b$.
All exterior, data-front and endpoint-correction calls remain in place.
The resulting complete rank mass is
\[
 s=Wm-N+L,\qquad Wm-s=N-L.
\]
This follows by subtracting exactly $L$ from the retained two-stage
physical mass $Wm-N+2L$. The $N$ rank-one endpoint-copy calls are
separate and remain fully charged. Because center copies retain the
same complete role-volume fraction $1/W$, the existing complete-row,
integer-width remainder and fixed-tape recurrence proofs apply.
