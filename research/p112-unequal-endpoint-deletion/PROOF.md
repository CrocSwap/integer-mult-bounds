# Two unequal-endpoint P112 exclusions

## Statement and exact change

Use the fixed 112 nonframe E8 root lines `r_t`, their nine-bit binary labels
`ell(t)`, and the code

```
C = {c_h : c_h(t)=<r_t,h>, h in K^8},
w(q) = rank_F2 {ell(t) : q(t) != 0}.
```

The field `K` has characteristic zero. All intersections and independence
below are over `K`; only support weights use binary rank.

**Nine-piece theorem.** Nine independent pieces with weight bounds either
`3,5,5,5,6,6,6,6,6` or `4,4,5,5,6,6,6,6,6` span a space `T0` with
`dim(T0 intersect C)<=4`.

**Ten-piece consequence.** Ten independent pieces with code intersection
of dimension at least six cannot have either TWOSIDE profile
`3,5^3,6^5,7` or `4^2,5^2,6^5,7`. The last piece can actually be unrestricted.

This applies Nick Petalas/PR3169's existing A5 coordinate inequality after
the dimension-deletion step of PR3197. The new callers have six unequal
endpoint capacities `12,20,20,20,20,20`, rather than six twenties. No new
geometric inequality, full C5 result, decoder floor, supplier or exponent
is claimed.

## Replayed geometric premises and written dependency

The unchanged, declared `geometry.py` input reconstructs the roots and
labels and binds them to the declared P112 data. It checks the following
finite premises, inherited from PR3164/3169 and PR3161/3162.

* Weight-three and weight-four supports have at most four and eight
  coordinates. The labels are distinct and odd, so a binary `d`-space
  contains at most `2^(d-1)` of them. Weight-five and weight-six supports
  have capacities twelve and twenty. The replay enumerates all 3,309,747
  five-flats and 788,035 six-flats in the nine-dimensional label space.
* A nonzero codeword has at least 53 live coordinates. A codeword supported
  on fewer than seventy coordinates is proportional to a root codeword.
* The Gram sum in doubled root coordinates is `112 I`, so evaluation from
  `K^8` into `C` is injective.
* A plane containing three distinct E8 root lines is A2. Relative to the
  first two roots, the third has coefficient ratio `+1` or `-1`.

The support classification uses the standard Weyl-chamber theorem, not a
short-vector enumeration. In a closed chamber, perpendicular roots are
generated by vanishing simple roots. The 28 six-node parabolics have at
most 36 root lines. The eight seven-node faces have counts
`42,28,22,14,16,23,37,63`; only the last admits 43 perpendicular live lines,
and its normal is a root. Root directions have live support 53 or 56.
This proves the distance and the below-seventy classification over the
reals. Reflection closure, simple-root positivity, the face counts and
all 7,140 root-line planes are replayed exactly.

For an arbitrary characteristic-zero field, the rational roots perpendicular
to a field-valued direction have rational rank at most seven. If their rank
were at most six while there were at least 43 of them, a generic real normal
to their span would contradict the rank-six parabolic bound. Their rational
kernel is therefore one-dimensional. The field-valued direction is a field
scalar times its rational generator, to which the real classification
applies. This includes complex coefficients without a positive-definite
field-valued norm. The standard Weyl-chamber theorem and the original
decoder reduction remain written dependencies, not proof-kernel results.

## Nine pieces and six root-simplex vertices

Suppose `dim(T0 intersect C)>=5`. In either weight profile take the first
three pieces as `s1,s2,s3`, and call the other six `q0,...,q5`. Put

```
S = supp(s1) union supp(s2) union supp(s3),    s=|S|<=28,
U = sum_i |supp(q_i)| <= 12+5*20 = 112,
D0 = T0 intersect C.
```

For the first profile, the small-support bound is `4+12+12=28`. For the
second it is `8+8+12=28`. Their endpoint capacities are identical.

The quotient `T0/D0` has dimension at most four. For every `i`, the four
images of `s1,s2,s3,q_i` are independent: a dependence would give a nonzero
codeword supported on at most `28+20=48<53` coordinates. Nonzero follows
from independence of the original pieces. Consequently the quotient has
exact dimension four, and `dim D0=5`.

After quotienting by the three small images, all six endpoint images are
nonzero in one dimension. Rescale them to agree. Their supports and
independence do not change. For suitable small-piece combinations `a_i`,

```
c0=0,    c_i=q_i-q0-a_i in D0,    i=1,...,5.
```

The five codewords are independent, as witnessed by their distinct nonzero
coefficients on `q1,...,q5`. Every nonzero edge `c_i-c_j` has support at most
`28+20+20=68<70`, and hence is a root direction. Injectivity gives five
independent direction vectors.

Normalize real E8 roots to squared norm two. Write those vectors as
`h_i=b_i alpha_i`. Every difference is also a root direction, so the A2
plane fact forces `b_i/b_j` to be `+1` or `-1`. Absorb signs into the roots.
There is one common nonzero scalar `b`, and all distinct
`<alpha_i,alpha_j>=1`. Thus the six vertices `0,alpha_1,...,alpha_5` form
an affine root A5 simplex. This argument remains valid over `K`: a rational
root in the field span of two independent rational roots lies in their
rational span, by a nonsingular rational coordinate minor.

## The inherited coordinate inequality, with the correct endpoint bill

At any root coordinate the six simplex values lie on at most two adjacent
integer levels, except when that coordinate is an edge root. In the latter
case their equality multiplicities are `1,1,4`. In either case at most nine
of the fifteen pairs have unequal values. The common field scalar changes
no equalities.

Outside `S`, edge values are exactly `q_i(t)-q_j(t)`. If `k` of the six
endpoint values are nonzero, their number of unequal pairs is at most

```
k(6-k) + k(k-1)/2 = k(11-k)/2 <= 4k+1,    0<=k<=6.
```

This last inequality follows from `(k-1)(k-2)>=0` for integer `k`. It needs
no restrictions on the nonzero field values. Summing inside and outside
`S`, and using the distance 53 on all fifteen edges, gives PR3169's bound

```
795 = 15*53 <= 9s + 4U + (112-s) = 112+8s+4U.
```

The present endpoints give

```
795 <= 112+8*28+4*112 = 784,
```

a contradiction of eleven. This proves both nine-piece statements. The
older `9s+5U` bound gives 812 and is insufficient. Charging six twenties
instead gives 816 even in the coordinate bound. Both losing controls are
retained; neither is an exclusion. The replay also enumerates all `7^6`
assignments of six values with zero distinguished, representing every
possible equality pattern.

## Delete the unrestricted piece

Let `T` be the span of ten independent pieces and `T0` the span after
removing the last one. Its codimension is one. Restricting the map
`T intersect C -> T/T0` shows

```
dim(T0 intersect C) >= dim(T intersect C)-1 >= 5.
```

Either retained profile contradicts the nine-piece theorem. No weight or
support assumption on the removed piece is used. Removing two pieces
would guarantee only four code dimensions, not five.

Both targets pass DreamingOfClouds/PR3154's original TWOSIDE `(6,4)`
filters, with excess `35<36`. A direct transcription reproduces all 69
historical survivors; the unchanged declared `bk2b.py` corroborates the
count. This is not a claim that 69 profiles remain open today.

groups plus paid cuts are checked. For `3,5^3,6^5,7`, support-certified basis
types are `3,5,5,5` and `3,5,5,6`, each needing at least sixteen paid cuts.
For `4^2,5^2,6^5,7`, the basis types are `4,4,5,5`, `4,4,5,6` and
`4,5,5,6`, with minima sixteen, sixteen and twenty. Thus none of the
weight-certified covers reaches the earlier fourteen-cut rule, or the
smaller eight/ten-cut rules. The only nonbasis weight-four anchor occurs
in the twenty-cut case, outside the twelve-cut anchor theorem.
These are comparisons of named sufficient routes, not exclusions of
unknown actual support overlaps or every possible earlier proof.

The targets are not componentwise consequences of PR3197's
`4^3,6^6,7` caller, nor PR3195's shorter `5^2,6^6,7` caller. The unequal
endpoint bill is essential for this particular application. No full C5,
`C(P112)>=64`, physical realization, supplier admission, multiplication
theorem or new kappa is established. Evidence is author-checked; independent
human review, hosted CI and proof-kernel verification are separate.
