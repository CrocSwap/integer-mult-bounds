## Change and mathematical scope

The finite mixed-target compiler currently obtains its owner matching by
materializing per-rank occurrence buckets. This adds an exact arithmetic
codec for that same ordered Pascal bijection and a direct physical-owner
lookup using at most G+1 ancestry decisions. It supplies one concrete part
of #6878's outstanding final-occurrence-emitter obligation.

The proof covers arbitrary separated finite rules, every valid integer top
rank, actual ambient caps above the largest target, arbitrary native role
order, owned fields and distinct invocation/replica namespaces. It includes
descriptor size and a separately charged offline big-integer work bound.
This is an enabling compiler lemma: **no new kappa or assumption discharge**.
The selected conditional bound remains 0.00363005817.
