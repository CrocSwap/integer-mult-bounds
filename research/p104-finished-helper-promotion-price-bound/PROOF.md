# The price of adding one finished helper to an endpoint bank

The frozen 119 cases use the exact #7334 word. Each has an exact rational separating input for the old peer row against its retained endpoint bank, with the moved role excluded. The one-coordinate promotion model retains the ADD cut immediately after the second MOVE and adds the completed normalized row e_r of one distinct public helper. If the separator vanishes on e_r it still annihilates the augmented bank. Therefore a useful promotion requires nonzero separator coordinate r. The separate forward replay checks both exact separator sets on every bank and target. Their witness coordinate counts can differ because rational separating inputs are not unique.

Take a non-main public helper r with old initial chart A, final chart D and last operand contact before the candidate cut. Both source and destination count as contacts. Require A strictly nested in D and D strictly nested in the endpoint bank chart C. The source scan authenticates these chart containments and the full literal chronology. It grants one direct paid lift D→C of rank d and the change of the complete independent helper target from rank t to t+d, where t=dim(D)−dim(A)>0. Grant the required physical maps, ordinary ADD, routing, ownership, restoration, precision and tape work free for this price exclusion.

Write H(r)=r^(1−κ). A fusion of two paid rank gaps u,v into u+v would save H(u)+H(v)−H(u+v) per invocation. The promotion pays H(d) and raises productive credit by H(t+d)−H(t). The optimistic combined price over 17 invocations is

```text
17 [H(u)+H(v)−H(u+v)−H(d)−H(t)+H(t+d)].
```

The two independent source and price scans find exactly one eligible donor on their respective separator coordinate sets. Since H is strictly concave for 0<κ<1, the increment from 8 to 9 is strictly smaller than the increment from 3 to 4. This gives a uniform negative sign and the reproduced directed incumbent interval lies between −0.055 and −0.054 raw.
