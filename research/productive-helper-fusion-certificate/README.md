## Proof certificate

The verifier independently rebuilds the chronological graph. At each affected
edge it computes the four exact rank costs from binary subspaces. Paid edges
have positive sign; each helper's whole endpoint conversion has negative sign.
Warp inputs are mapped before measuring distance. Former zero edges, parallel
incidences and self-incidences are included. These costs give a binary quadratic
energy `E(x)` relative to the original assignment.

Complementing selected bits makes the pair terms submodular. The supplied graph
represents exactly `2 E(x)`, coefficient by coefficient in `H_1,...,H_9`; the
factor two avoids half-integral capacities. The independent checker expands
the cut graph back into the original variables rather than trusting its builder.
A symbolic feasible flow equals a cut identically, and their value plus the
graph constant is the zero polynomial. Every capacity, forward residual and
reverse residual is nonnegative on the entire C interval. Weak flow/cut duality
therefore proves the lower bound simultaneously for all assignments and all C.
The original assignment supplies the matching upper bound.

Signs use either the exact `min(r,k)` basis for increasing concave tariffs or
independent rational logarithm/exponential enclosures. A polynomial vanishing
at C=0 can be certified by a positive derivative enclosure. Floating search
statuses are not acceptance evidence.

For a selected late map, let input/output subspaces have dimensions u,v and
let its optional prelude constraint be `G(a)=b`. Set
`m=min(u-[a in U and b not in V],v-[b in V and a not in U])`; without a line,
set `m=min(u,v)`. The minimum Gaussian rank is `u+v-2m`: maximize the intersection
of `G(U)` with V subject to the required membership of b, then extend bases to
an invertible binary-linear G. This is the credited #5144 formula. The source-derived check
verifies that all four minima at every selected map equal the fixed-G tables.
Other warp occurrence charts remain unchanged, so sharing one G imposes exactly the
retained line constraints, with no independently chosen per-occurrence maps.
