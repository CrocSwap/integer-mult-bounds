## Six arenas, no new engine and no reclaimed storage

The inherited Ordinary constructor creates four `PhysicalArena(capacity)`
banks and two further arenas for `RootSession` numerical/index roles. The
history relation covers the six available queues, live roles, word templates,
codec state and canonical heads. A word's slot number identifies a fixed
physical tape role, not a head coordinate on one long tape. Permuting roles
therefore does not by itself move a head a distance equal to the slot number.
Serialized identifiers still have real tape actions; those are covered by the
codec theorem rather than erased from the invoice.

No new engine, queue copy, sorting pass, canonicalization/reset, numerical
reserve, scratch bank or persistent record is added by the action relation.
The checker fingerprint is observation only. The relation does not require
initializing a precision-dependent state at no cost. Existing preparation,
markers, encoded maxima, codec and template erasure remain in actual calls.

Historical backing **does not** become equal from equal current contents or
from action equality. For physical role t let H_t be the greatest visited
coordinate (with the source convention for negative marker cells). Its storage
cost is retained after erase; aggregate backing is the sum over all physical
roles and concurrently live engines. Different histories can distribute old
high-water values differently. A role bijection maps current word lengths and
heads, not necessarily H_t. Consequently the history theorem proves neither
space equality nor storage dominance. Preserve each run's historical backing
receipt, and keep the inherited geometric/long-record bound separately.

## Actual reset sites if a later protocol changes state

Ordinary is called only through the positive-width Binary branch inside
Compact swaps. A successful monomial with f columns, r outer rows, e early and
l late gates makes zero Ordinary invocations when f=1 and r(4e+12l) when f>1.
The latter count repeats for every source monomial and original C descendant,
including producer/service maps and line wrappers. Neither root callback
counts nor an unfiltered Compact.calls counter is a legal reset-site census.

A future canonicalization policy would have to pay each selected invocation's
six-arena/control/template/codec work and provide successful closure, borrowed
preservation and marker contracts. A common upper bound on B and D resets
cannot establish exact cancellation. Here that work is absent because no
runtime reset is introduced; the compiler and source action model remain the
original ones. Compiled finite slot selection may be modelled separately from
R/W/M, and no Python list operation is asserted to have zero runtime cost.

## No unsupported resource consequence

This package removes a context cost uncertainty for related successful source
executions. It does not remove D's extra engines, give a complete physical
invoice for all new arithmetic, or show that those engines are affordable in
an all-node supplier. It also does not turn the current selected exponent into
a proved improved exponent. The stopping rule remains: no fixed-mechanism
recovery expansion while the reduced complete necessary inequality is failed
or unpriced.
