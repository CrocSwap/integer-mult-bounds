# Selected BIT local-ring sweeps and live-row allocation

We extend the selected PR589 whole-call argument to complete high packets
over GL64(Z/q^w), with an explicit live-row layout and a charged procedure
for same-family XOR. We retain completed address callees, recursive work
allocation and finite routing admission as hypotheses. We leave the selected
conditional kappa at 0.001672660197076918.

## Result

In [PROOF.md](PROOF.md) we bind the 636 dyadic frames, rank-seven COPY/ERASE
guards and chronological hosting schedule to arbitrary odd prime powers.
We prove separation of complete owner packets for the selected helper
completions. We complete one local word per low class while batching its
whole high fibre, then compose the global schedule and its terminal exchange.

For row allocation we distinguish 28,416 live families from nine private
work slots. With N=|GL64(F_q)| and H=q^(4096(w-1)), we use the divisor
K^D where K=lcm(28416,28425)NH=269241600NH. A complete live low packet has
volume V/(28416N). After exposing its high matrix, we retain a complete
unused row range divisible by each smaller child's reserve. For q>2^80,
we retain log_q K<4096w+1. We allocate private copies as separate work
streams; divisibility alone does not allocate that workspace.

## Evidence and reproduction

From the repository root, use Python 3.12 and its standard library:

```sh
python3.12 scripts/research_package.py --package research/selected-bit-local-ring-sweeps
```

The registered commands run normally and with `-O`, authenticate the imported
PR1627 verifier and its inputs, replay it, and compare the new deterministic
[RESULT.json](RESULT.json). We reuse that predecessor check; we do not call
it an independent new audit of the scalar word.

Our additional checks cover:

- 4,668 split image/kernel bases and 14,370 weighted frame transports.
- 181,056 same-family helper pairs across 288 selected calls, including
  11,072 same-family ADDs; one mutation aliases two selected owners and fails.
- 888 data-chart boundaries, 111 ambient endpoints and eighteen omitted
  bridge controls.
- One sequential restricted-fibre XOR on all 3,888 GL2(Z/9) rows and
  198,288 payload bits, with untouched-packet and omitted-carry checks.
- A complete 1,296-row layout model and child-divisibility checks.

We also reject two wrong GL transports that agree modulo 3: using a
transpose as an inverse, and replacing complementary oblique projectors
with orthogonal projections onto their ranges. A separate 81-row example
shows that point-owner injection does not suffice for high-packet batching.

The finite cases test the written proof and specific failure modes. We do
not execute the selected GL64 cover on physical payload tapes, measure the
complete controller, or prove the full multiplication theorem. The literal
physical fixture uses an ordered shear whose target/return charts are
identity; general routes retain the three-action bill in PROOF.md.
