# A restored dirty spare reaches every principal two-row demand

This contribution was produced by Nick Petalas. Apache-2.0.

Claim type: scoped algebraic proof with an exact free-associative polynomial
certificate and finite corroboration. Over every associative unital ring R,
for two demanded rows and one arbitrary dirty spare restored to its original
value, the minimum source-insertion count is

```text
0 for zero demand;
1 for a nonzero principal left demand ideal;
2 for a nonprincipal left demand ideal.
```

The new step is arbitrary-ring sufficiency with the restored spare, using
one source insertion and at most eight elementary row additions. Finiteness,
commutativity, invertible demand coefficients and a clean spare are unnecessary.
[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) gives the ordered-ring construction and all-input proof.
The bound of eight ADDs is constructive, not an ADD optimum or exact tariff.

This answers the feasibility part of Open next question in Nick Petalas's
[PR #5211](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/5211). For its
polynomial demand `(1+uv,u²)` over every field K, one restored dirty spare
removes the two-row one-insertion obstruction. The literal new word has
exactly eight nonzero ADDs and one insertion. Its complete declared abstract
gate price is `mu_2+2c_0+2c_1+4c_2`, with c_i charging each ADD into row i
and mu_2 charging the insertion at the spare. At prices mu_0=100, mu_1=1000,
mu_2=100 and all c_i=1, the word costs 108. The cited two-row optimum is
202 at the matching original prices. The workspace contracts differ; no
three-row optimum or physical saving is inferred.

## Construction and evidence

Given witnesses `x=a beta`, `y=b beta`, `beta=s x+t y`, execute the following
nine gates on arbitrary incoming A0,A1,H,p:

```text
H += s A0; H += t A1; A0 -= a H; A1 -= b H;
H += beta p;
A1 += b H; A0 += a H; H -= t A1; H -= s A0.
```

The endpoint is `(A0+x p,A1+y p,H,p)`. The first and last four gates cancel
on every original dirt column. Without any witness relation, the residual
increment at H is exactly `(1-sa-tb) beta p`. Thus witnesses need only satisfy
an annihilator relation, not `sa+tb=1`. Coefficient preparation and physical
access remain separately paid obligations.

The standard-library verifier imports no predecessor scientific code. It
checks the full four-by-four affine map over the free associative integer
polynomial ring, including the precise product orders, and the literal
inverse. A separate commutative integer polynomial specialization checks
the Cohn word, its inverse, all nine individual gate deletions, every
nonzero coefficient and destination counts `(2,2,4)`.

Exact finite substitutions cover every five-tuple `(beta,a,b,s,t)` over Z/4
and UT2(F2): 1,024 and 32,768 tuples, respectively. Every full affine map and
inverse agrees with the unconstrained residual identity. Among these are
192 and 6,720 nonzero valid witnesses; 48 and 4,704 of those have the chosen
`sa+tb != 1`. A further 3,420 selected witnesses in M2(F2), including 2,160
with the chosen `sa+tb != 1`, check genuinely noncommutative substitutions.
These finite tests check the new word, not predecessor source-count theorems.
Eight invalid-domain controls and explicit wrong-order/nonzero-residual
discriminators also pass. Three disposable optimized-script mutations are
rejected at the free-associative endpoint check: lost restoration, wrong
preparation sign and reversed multiplication. The universal criterion is
the written proof.
