# Conditional sufficient target-read extension

This is a finite source-admission argument, relative to the inherited local,
compiler, precision, arbitrary-bank and global assembly interfaces. It does not
establish an unconditional multiplication exponent or a Lean theorem.

Order abstract live coordinates as w=(X,S), followed by Y. A candidate is sufficient
for the same local endpoint action as its admitted base when its exact raw map is
    T = [[U,0],[V,I]],  V=[M,K],
its retained totals and complete non-Y-target shear subword equal the base, its
clean-X response M equals the base, and its frame walk and literal coefficients
are admitted with their actual paid invoice. K is allowed to change. X cannot read
helpers or Y, and helpers cannot read Y; only Y reads Y in the extension.

Delete X-source adds from the actual candidate and route helpers to disjoint work
T. The resulting literal zero-X circuit is Q=[[J,0],[K,I]] on (workS,Y). This is
an extracted circuit, not an assumed oracle. Its full initial-Y block must be I.
For forward correction conjugate Q by the sign involution D_Y. This negates
helper-to-Y coefficients and keeps Y-to-Y coefficients unchanged. With workS
copied from the dirty live helpers it subtracts exactly K*S_initial from Y.
Erase all work, execute the candidate main, then the fixed U inverse cleanup.
The final live map is X,S unchanged and Y -> Y+M*X.

For backward correction, run literal Q transpose (reverse operations and swap
source/target) from workS=0. It leaves Y unchanged and writes K transpose*Y into
workS. Add this into the live helpers, erase work, execute the candidate main
inverse transpose, then the fixed U transpose cleanup. Its w result is
    U transpose * U inverse transpose * (w - V transpose*Y + (0,K transpose*Y))
  = w - (M transpose*Y,0).
Thus the final map is X -> X-M transpose*Y with Y,S unchanged. Both corrections
use only paid scalar operations in the common old frame, and add no recursive
address child or clean live helper assumption.

quotient-compose.py checks the exact clean-X/Y blocks, all X/helper rows, retained
totals and preserved non-Y subword. It extracts and emits literal Q independently
of the main scalar schedule and requires exact physical projection equality,
full all-live-column replay in both orientations, erased private work, nested
frames, paid coefficients and rejected mutation controls. The full primitive
invoice and two rational moment calculations are rebuilt from the candidate.
complex-clifford.py separately verifies canonical frame representatives, every
used directed child, endpoint adapters and their Gaussian global phases.

The original frozen-K star candidate is independently retained. Replaying the
Q adapter against that known candidate validates integration only; it is not an
additional discovery or evidence that a dirty-K-changing candidate exists.
