# Core-aware cyclic strips with deferred readouts

Conditional **κ = 9.4744627892e-5 = 23686156973/250000000000000**.
Human-readable form: **κ ≈ 94744627892/10¹⁵ > 2⁻¹⁴**.

Concurrent PR113 independently publishes the direct PR110+PR111 combination.
This package adds the core-aware pair refinement and independent reflection audit.

This combines PR111's cyclic strip / dual-suffix complex producer with PR110's
lifted frames and deferred readouts, then replaces the contracted pair
subproblems with PR62's core-aware pair assembly and balanced column coarse
sums from PR69. The bit supplier remains Swapnil Jain's pinned round-seven
word under PR104's stopped product-ring interface.

The resulting complex word has 68,800 additions, 39,061 carrier links,
37,859 roles, 5,182 deferred roles, W = 161,446,384 and largest child 574 of
576. Every center, copied-center loss, exterior child, data correction and
endpoint copy remains charged. Its strict complex saving is
47381341/500000000000 = 9.4762682e-5. The stopped bit saving is
1240189553/10¹³ = 1.240189553e-4, so the complex side still limits κ.

On the same deferred compiler, the direct PR110+PR111 combination has
38,506 roles and complex saving 9.3925307e-5. The core-aware pair change
improves that complete profile by about 0.89%; it is not a scalar-count-only
claim. A cyclic pair-star variant reduced roles further but worsened the
moment and was rejected.

Run `python3 research/cyclic-deferred/verify.py` from the repository root.
The default verifier preserves frozen inputs and regenerates into a temporary
checkout. `test_controls.py` exercises corrupted inputs and unpaid costs.
`certificate.py` checks the frozen exact arithmetic certificate; its explicit
`--write` option is for authoring a new certificate.

The producer checks all exact source supports, rational center scatter and
inherited binary-frame rules. The role compiler checks every chain,
nondegeneracy and nested deferred target chain, and replays arbitrary scratch
and data over Z/(2^61-1). The separate reflection audit checks the literal
inverse word after swapping data banks and the complemented frame chains.
These finite checks do not formally prove all-size transfer.

The exact assembly includes both moments, the stopped ordinary-bit saving,
three product-row reserves, all 47 strict constraints and seven margins, and
next-grid rejection. Expanded old-readout scalar operations are charged with
an explicit conservative upper bound, rather than reusing the original DAG's
addition count. Enlarging this guard changes precision constants and eventual
thresholds; it does not increase the claimed exponent.

All PR104/PR110 transfer hypotheses remain: simultaneous rational bases and
opposite-bank factorization for the listed residuals, stopped atom streaming,
ordinary wrapper, translated complex endpoint gauges, exact odd-denominator
grid, routing, recovery, prime selection, analytic estimates and fixed-tape
implementation. This is a finite conditional witness, not an unconditional
multiplication theorem or a practical running-time claim.
