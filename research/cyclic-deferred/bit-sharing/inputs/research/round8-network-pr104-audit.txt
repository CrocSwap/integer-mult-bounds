1. Common basis and full involution.
For each finite rational projector P of rank r, every leading principal
minor of size<=r is a nonzero function of a generic common similarity:
diagonalizing P witnesses nonvanishing. Their finite product is nonzero on
GL_m. Thus a single rational basis satisfies all these conditions. This is
an existence/terminating finite-enumeration argument, not an explicitly
supplied numerical common basis. P C_m has northeast ranks min(i,j,r), so
physical lower row/column factors normalize it to E F^t with reversed F.

For the full involution g, rank(g-I)=r and its upper-right block E F^t has
rank r. Hence g-I=[E;B][A,F^t] and AE+F^tB=-2I. The proposed
K=-(B+F)E^t+FA(I-EE^t) satisfies KE=-B-F and F^tK=A+E^t.
Consequently SgS^-1=I+[E;-F][-E^t,F^t], exactly the selected cross-bank
swaps, with the complementary diagonal blocks correct. Q and S are lower
triangular in the physical head-then-tail order. Symmetry of P is not used.

2. Atom lift and recursive closure.
On f atoms, Q acts with tensor I_f and the cross shear with K tensor C_f.
All identities lift because reversal commutes with componentwise scalar
arithmetic. C_r tensor C_f equals C_(rf), so the normalized middle move
is one contiguous child of the SAME reversed-interchange family. Rational
coefficients are reduced only after a fixed odd prime avoids all finite
denominators and pivot numerators. Ring identities then hold modulo q^w,
without treating the product ring as one integer field with inter-atom carries.

3. Actual cost of adapters.
The claim is not that an atom reversal is free. A lower-triangular formal
matrix is expanded into fixed scalar operations; for each atom index, every
control lies in a physically earlier formal field. In K tensor C_f the
control atom index reverses, but every head atom still precedes every tail
atom. Each ordinary width-w atom update therefore fits the retained earlier-
control ordered-affine primitive. O(f) passes per fixed matrix costs O(V n)
per node. Target fields can be processed in descending order to preserve
original earlier controls. Descriptors select indexed atoms and use the
inherited fixed tapes/parking; they do not imply a standalone reversal pass.
This is the exact point at which the inherited streaming contract is used.

4. Remainders, stopping and ordinary conversion.
Peeling the first delta head atoms against the last delta tail atoms in
reverse order leaves two contiguous mf-atom intervals for F_(mf). At most
m ordinary width-w calls pay the peel or a bounded base case. Padding two
radix-q atoms to binary ranges costs a factor<4 in volume and ordinary width
O(w); the constant prime is fixed. With both moments mu_1<1 and mu_tau<1,
induction gives T(n,w)<=A n+B n^tau w^(1-a_old), absorbing the O(n) adapter
toll with the first gap and the peeled-atom toll with the second.

The chronological D-=H;F;D+=H;F;D=H-D is exactly ordinary swap because
F(H,D)=(C D,C H) and C^2=I. It is performed only outside the entire reversed
recursion. Its two calls sequentially reuse restored row stock. At
w=ceil(e^theta), theta=1/1000, the ordinary saving is
(1-theta)*4019/50000000+theta*384599/10^10=803380799/10^13.
The O(e^(1-theta)) adapter term is subordinate since theta exceeds that
saving. Fewer than w residual digits use the old ordinary interface.
High-digit row borrowing applies to the completed ORDINARY wrapper, not
to a naked reversed call. The claimed three-factor row reserve is thus
the appropriate form; its exact numeric assembly is audited separately.
