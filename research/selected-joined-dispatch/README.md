# Full-ring preparation and completed embedded children

We connect actual full-ring preparation, generated control evaluation and
completed child execution on the same high packet. The child runs sequential
digit swaps, controlled ADDs and signs, or a smaller complete generic caller.
We use no child address-permutation oracle in the payload path.

[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) gives the general embedded-field interpretation: retain
the complete prefix, collapse surrounding fields into spectators and preserve
the exact volume without moving records. The executable backend uses two
complementary projector residuals in dimension two. Their product gives the
weighted interchange. This tests the pending selected-caller interface; it
does not execute the selected GL64 family/owner/hosting schedule or claim a
new saving.

## Evidence

We check 1,456,542 distinct 32-bit records at both the first residual and the
completed weighted endpoint:

| Case | Complete domain | Records |
|---|---|---:|
| Good high packet | All 81 high lifts over Z/9 and all 9^4 body addresses | 531,441 |
| Fallback high packet | The same full sizes, including nonzero nonunits | 531,441 |
| Nested unit reset | 64-dimensional preparation, 48 fresh GL2 rows and a real internal child | 314,928 |
| Nonbinary spectators | Unused range 2, gap 2 and suffix 3 with completed leaves | 78,732 |

The call trace accounts for 872 children: 871 completed leaves and one
internal caller. The latter visits all 48 fresh GL2 low classes after a unit
reset. It retains global field slots through depth three and returns a buried
nonzero caller frame. The nested fixture obtains weights `(2,1,1,1)` from an
actual 64-dimensional full-ring preparation, rather than a supplied weight
table. Its width-one parameters test endpoint algebra outside the production
root-coupled asymptotic setup.

Six controls target low-only coefficients, an omitted orientation child,
compacted global slots, a sliced high-packet request, insufficient fresh stock
and a chunk crossing the old gap. The checker uses direct adjugate/projector
and weighted-endpoint formulas separately from the executor.

## Reproduction and scope

Use Python 3.12 and the standard library:

```sh
python3.12 scripts/research_package.py --package research/selected-joined-dispatch
```

The registered hook runs complete normal and optimized replays, each with a
600-second limit. A replay takes several minutes on the development machine.
`verify.py --emit --progress` prints a fresh receipt and stage progress without
writing files. [RESULT.json](RESULT.json) retains exact endpoint hashes,
child profiles, payload traffic and rejection outcomes.

The interpreter copies opaque records through sequential tape macros and
charges their full bit volume. It executes payload parking and cleanup.
Host integer arithmetic, memoized immutable preparations and control objects
remain outside the measured payload machine; their binary encoding and cost
need the separate control/parking proof. We claim no full transition-table
compiler, selected-supplier admission, new formal proof or frontier change.
