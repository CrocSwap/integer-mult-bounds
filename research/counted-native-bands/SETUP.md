# Count ranks during installation and retain labels during execution

The paid and productive ports of a raw word have distinct time and owner labels. Installation needs the number of external periods and the two leaf totals. These quantities depend on ranks. The executable word needs all labels. The following construction uses this difference.

## Paid block rule

Let the tensor power be $v$ and the cap be $D=12^v$. Leave a paid rank $R\le D$ intact. For $R>D$ issue $\lfloor R/D\rfloor$ disjoint intervals of size $D$. Issue the binary digits of the remainder as intervals in descending size. Move the left endpoint by the length of each interval. Multiply both the start and the length by the actual column count. These cuts act on distinct ordered axes in the same suspended chart. Each returns before the next cut. They cover the original request once. Entry, exit, complementary data and the original scalar program are those of the powered word.

Here and below use the coefficient $c\le 1/250$, $t=1-c$, and any tilt $z\ge1/4$. For a binary expansion of an integer $b>0$, let its nonzero parts be $b_i$. Then
$$
  \sum_i b_i^t\le K b^t,\qquad K=(2^t-1)^{-1}.
$$
Indeed the largest part is a fraction $w\ge1/2$ of the whole. The derivative of $w^t/(1-(1-w)^t)$ has the sign of $1-(1-w)^{t-1}\le0$. Its value at $1/2$ is $K$. Induction on the remaining digits proves the inequality, including the one-part case. The integer comparison $2^{249}129^{250}>257^{250}$ gives $K\le129/128$. For $0\le x\le1$, concavity at 1 gives $x^t\le x+c(1-x)$. Hence $Kx^t-x\le\max(K-1,Kc)\le1/128$.

For $u=R/D=q+x>1$, the *extra* charge over the unsplit moment, in units of $D^t$, is at most $u-u^t+1/128$. Now $1-u^{-c}\le c\log u$, and the maximum of $y\exp(-(z-c)y)$ over $y\ge0$ is $1/(\mathrm e(z-c))$. Using $\mathrm e>5/2$ gives
$$
 \frac{u-u^t+1/128}{u^{t+z}}
 \le \frac{4}{615}+\frac1{128}<\frac1{64}.
$$
Sum over ordered raw paid tuples. The extra debit is at most $1/64$ times the exact tilted tail term of PR #8033. The original productive cutoff and every other term of the PR #8035 certificate agree. The two raw words and all six installations pass the directed rational moment check. This preserves even the smallest signed margin. The new block rule is the construction change that makes the following count small. The predecessor's single residual interval can have prime factors outside the raw alphabets.

## Counting the band

Use the same integral band with active rank $N'=4\lfloor N/4\rfloor$, upper limit $m=N'/4$, ambient $A=a^v$, floor $L$, largest paid grade $l$, and smallest retained target $k$. The height $G$ is the first integer with $A k^G>m l^G$. The virtual column numerator at depth $j$ is the product of $j$ ordered paid grades and $G-j$ ordered target grades. Its denominator is $l^G$.

A table stores pairs (grade, multiplicity). Join two tables by multiplying grades and multiplicities and adding the multiplicities at equal resulting grades. Iteration from the unit table gives the exact number of ordered words of each grade at a given length. Keep the last paid grade $r$ and first future target grade $k_1$ separate. For each interior grade $y$ at depth $j$, the numerator is $x=yrk_1$. Its multiplicity includes the numbers of labels for $r$ and $k_1$. Check $L l^G\le x$ and $Ax\le m l^G$, and take $b=\lfloor x/l^G\rfloor-1$. For a productive rank $k_i$, add its true period at size $k_i b$ exactly when $A x k_i>m l^G r$. Otherwise add the residual $r(\lfloor xk_i/(l^G r)\rfloor-1)-k_i b$. For each paid grade $r_j$, add the terminal $r_jb$ exactly when $xr_j<Ll^G k_1$. Multiply every increment by the corresponding multiplicity, including the port being tested. The depth-zero and depth-$G$ bands are empty. These cases are the full labelled band calculation. The proof objects in `lean/GradeCensus.lean` verify the product-of-multiplicities identity for an arbitrary nonnegative test on the resulting grade and addition of equal-grade counts.

From the period histogram take its maximum $R_0$, and sum $(R_0/q)\,\#q$ to obtain $h$. The width is the dyadic ceiling of $15h$. An empty band takes the same elementary branch as its source. The census retains no labelled schedule, path dictionary or offset vector.

## Running the native calls

At execution use one odometer for the paid prefix and nested odometers for the productive suffix. Visit every prefix with a possible deeper depth, even if the current layer has no active unit. At an active unit issue all labels in native order. Give the next $R_0/q$ disjoint row ordinals to each external source, in depth-first visitation order. Assign these ordinals once per unit and reuse them in each replica and round. The final ordinal is $h$ by the counting proof. This is a permutation of complete blocks of source rows relative to the old ordering. Each real owner still receives a full period of its own, including an owner in the last partly padded replica. An internal port uses its one actual suspended parent with suffix $(i,a)$. Return all excess axes to the leaf service before advancing that parent. The iterator stack, live physical jobs, loan maps, port ordinals, masks and native temporaries are private to each invocation. Nested calls can use the same immutable plan. No shared program head or expanded opcode image exists. The retained epoch interpreter consumes the emitted operations and verifies retirement of each padding owner.

Changes to the distribution of residual sizes can change the real width, fringe, leaf count and per-round chronology. The comparison above preserves the *proved budgets*. The reference tests compare the streamed schedule to the independently enumerated geometry of the new block rule, including label multiplicity and terminal versus fringe counts.

## Fixed setup and working control

Write $H=\log_2N$. In the six named installations $H\ge512v$, $v\ge128$, $k/l\ge(5/4)^v$, and $vG<13H/4+v$. The largest raw grade is below 256. Within either word all raw grades, the cap and all new blocks have at most ten distinct prime factors in union. The exact sets appear in RESULT.json. Every product in a count table fits in $32(H+2)$ bits, including the last unused prefix update. Each prime exponent is less than that bound. With $d=1000(H+2)$, every table has fewer than $K=d^{10}$ keys. A single paid tensor tuple produces at most $R/12^v+8v+1$ cuts. Across all words, $W+P_\#\le W_0^v+(8v+1)P_0^v+(\sum r/12)^v<2^{23v}$. Here $W_0,P_0\le281568$, $\sum r\le153P_0$, and $8v+3<2^v$. Thus all candidate units number at most $(P_\#+W)^G<N^{75}$. The power certificate uses $23(13/4+1/512)<75$. There are fewer than $N^{76}$ source labels, periods at most $N$, productive width at most $N^{79}$, and at most 30 replicas. All multiplicities, total leaf counts and keys, including intermediate products used to update the counters, have fewer than $d$ bits.

Use at most eight tapes for the live count tables and their rewrites. Recompute a target power at each depth. At most $4(G+v+1)^2$ joins or outer loops occur. A join costs at most $K^2$ logical updates. The main loop uses at most $2K^4$ incidences per depth. A logical update includes a fixed number of $d$-bit multiplications, a long division, a period, or an addition. Use binary schoolbook arithmetic and serial lookup. Including rewinds of both stores, copying or adding a record, and serial arithmetic, $2^{16}(Kd)^3$ moves suffice per update. For example, a lookup uses fewer than $2^8Kd^2$ single-symbol moves with a fresh key scan per comparison. The usual shift, compare and subtract division has at most $d$ steps, each cost below $2^8d^2$. With the outer histogram and live plan copies, the entire grade calculation including erasure fits in $d^{100}$ transitions and cells. Only the scalar plan data and the tables of the one-step grades can persist.

The raw prelude is independent of $H$. It reads the two literal words, reconstructs the rational journals and generates and checks the 2,971 Clifford pairs and 104 ports. Its absolute account is in RAW-SETUP.md. At one rank the combined allowance is $2^{4096}+d^{100}$. The total in RESULT.json sums this for every installed binary rank and the positive rank, even when they share raw data. In particular the fixed installer has polynomial cost in $H$. This bound includes input-independent setup only.

At an active call the labelled odometers visit fewer than $N^{75}$ word candidates per round, including rejected futures. Empty paid prefixes and terminal requests add at most $G(P+W)^{G}\max(P,W)$ visits. Restarting a raw labelled iterator can enumerate all raw tuples, including dropped tuples. The raw-word count $2^{64}(v+1)[2(a+1)(W_0+P_0+2)]^v<N$ pays each such restart per retained tuple or attempted visit. Frames, masks and chronological bindings occupy fewer than $N^{100}$ cells of private control per active invocation by explicit enumeration, even if each path/key takes $N^3$ cells. Allow $N^{85}$ control records across all replicas and rounds; the binary and positive payload steps have their own physical count. Three full passes over the control store per key, $N^7$ logical operations per record, and creation, search and clearing fit in $N^{500}$ moves for the purely structural work. Every active raw call contains at least $2^N$ physical addresses across its selected windows or atoms. Since $500H<N=2^H$, one full-volume pass funds the extra control generation even if it is regenerated at every activation. The masks and dependent native chart actions which move real fields still pay their full costs. The source owner-space proof uses its original conservative structural $J=N^{1024}$ as a bound on local control, separate from the new fixed installer. The chronological rule does not store an expanded image at first use.

## Genuine predecessor lower bound

The former installer materializes all labels. At depth one choose a real largest paid block $12^v$ and any $G-1$ minimum retained productive labels. Their virtual column count is $(k/12^v)^{G-1}\in[L,m/A]$ at each final binary rank here. It exceeds $L$ because $m/A\ge L(k/12^v)$ there. For $v\ne128$, all raw rank-15 factors attain the minimum. For $v=128$, use one raw rank 4, 84 rank-8 factors and 43 rank-64 factors. This attains $16^{128}$ exactly. At all these raw grades there are more than $2^7$ labels. Thus the predecessor writes at least $2^{7v(G-1)}$ real open records at this one depth. As $A<2^{8v}$ and $k/12^v<2^v$, its binary logarithm exceeds $7(H-2-9v)$. This is a lower bound on bytes the predecessor actually allocates and writes, independent of the degree-1024 published upper bound. Neither machine's production-size image was built in the experiments.
