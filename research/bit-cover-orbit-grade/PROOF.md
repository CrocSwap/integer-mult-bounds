# Middle bank capacity from a grade on each cover orbit

## Scope

Use the retained #7001 BIT whole-fibre interface and the two independent equal-mask fields counted in [#7201](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/7201). A field has complete cover labels g in GL_d(R) and a complete physical difference-address lattice R^d at admitted odd prime-power digit cardinality s, where R=Z/sZ. A common sum address, the other columns and spectators can be included in the per-field volume V. Choosing a grade on one fixed admitted column suffices, with the projector and right-route matrices acting componentwise. In a row g, the prescribed action on each field is T_g=g(I−2P)g⁻¹ with rank(P)=a. This leaves the cover label, role and other physical addresses unchanged. Pull back the permitted common weight.

The proposed replacement has three chronological paid projectors of positive proper ranks r₁,r₂,r₃. Every two ranks sum to less than a. Each paid projector Q_j acts by a reflection g(I−2Q_j)g⁻¹ in its current row, as an involutive permutation of its charged bank cells. All ordinary linear operations preserve physical addresses. Scalar mixing is within a cover row, and a finite list of fixed right multipliers S may move among rows. This list and the projector matrices are width-independent, with denominators and route determinants invertible at the admitted odd prime. All occupied bank cells, including extra role or spectator slices, are charged. The replacement acts on one pair at a time. Sharing a paid bank across distinct pairs or adding address-changing ordinary work changes the contract.

## An invariant subspace suffices

Let W be a nonzero rational row subspace invariant under every right multiplier, contained in

    { l : lP=l, lQ₁=0, lQ₃=0 }.

An individual row need not be fixed by the multipliers. Let K_w be the subgroup of GL_d(R) generated by their reductions. Its right cover orbits are g₀ K_w. Choose any nonzero primitive integer row l₀ in W by clearing denominators and dividing out the common factor. On the whole orbit choose the *physical* grade z ↦ l₀ g₀⁻¹ z. It has equal-sized residue fibres, since at least one coefficient is a unit mod the underlying prime.

For g=g₀S with S in K_w, the normalized row for this same physical grade is l₀S. Rational invariance extends to the group including inverses, and the required projector identities reduce modulo s because the fixed denominators are units. In particular l₀S P=l₀S and l₀S Q₁=l₀S Q₃=0. The outer children and ordinary operations preserve this orbit's physical grade. Both required external actions negate it. A right multiplier can negate or mix the normalized rows in W while leaving the orbit grade unchanged. Different orbits can use different physical grades, since no declared operation connects their rows.

The verifier computes the largest such W. In column coordinates, start with the span of the columns of I−P, Q₁ and Q₃. Close it under all S by adding the image of every new basis vector. Its annihilator is W. Closure under the invertible generators gives closure under their inverses. At most d independent vectors enter the queue.

## Middle bank lower bound

In each orbit select one grade from every nonzero pair {v,−v}. In the target matrix, select input columns on these grades and output rows on the opposite grades, including both independent fields. The target restriction is a permuted identity of rank V_orbit(1−1/s), where V_orbit counts one full field in that orbit. The grade-preserving operators contribute zero to the selected minor.

Modify the middle involution only by replacing each transposition whose two endpoints have different grades by two fixed points. This makes the entire reference network preserve the orbit grade. Each replaced transposition has a rank-one difference over F₂ on its two cells. Thus the full middle difference has rank at most V₂/2 over all charged cells, even if some middle transpositions are grade-preserving or change the grade by something other than negation. Multiplying this difference on each side by the ordinary and outer maps cannot increase rank. The selected target minor, summed across disjoint orbits, forces

    V₂ ≥ 2(1−1/s) V.

The asymptotic charged middle volume is at least 2V. This argument counts each cell once, regardless of the number of useful prefix address maps into it.

The fixed-physical-address input and transposed output cuts of #7201 also force first and last volume at least 2V asymptotically. Their bypass argument applies here because any path omitting a given child uses at most two paid reflections. Its rank defect is below a. The finite list of rational bypasses misses the target at all but a vanishing address fraction uniformly in the cover label. The exact target also needs some path with rank sum at least a. These are necessary all-width statements, including when the paid volumes differ.

That bill is strictly above the old 2V H_a. The result grants new setup, routing, ownership, restoration, precision and tape free. ## Next question

A candidate that could reduce the middle volume must escape this grade criterion. For rank sum exactly a there is a further exact path test. Express the three projectors in one physical coframe. If their reflected product equals I−2P, the telescoping image lies in the sum of their image spaces. Rank equality forces those spaces to be a direct sum equal to im(P). Project the telescoping identity onto each summand in chronological order. It gives Q₁ as the first component of P, then Q₂ and Q₃ as the remaining components, since earlier reflections act diagonally on the already determined components. Thus the useful-path projectors mutually annihilate and sum to P. A future width-independent table should check this identity and the full independent field response before crediting any saving. The charged physical compiler and tape remain outside this proof.
