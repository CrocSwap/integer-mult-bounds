chart contract. It still gives no paid Gaussian saving in the complete
single-contact, same-transvection adjacent-pair, or coherent-block families
checked here. **No higher κ, extra productive center reward, supplier admission,
or selected-frontier change is claimed.**

The strongest result covers arbitrary subsets of the 176 formerly owned scalar
contacts sharing the empty chart. For each of their 1,022 distinct one-transvection

change for every `0<theta<1`, at least `2-2^theta`. This includes long,
noncontiguous and cross-center blocks, not only single or adjacent contact moves.
