# Ordinary binary fields at the mixed-rank rates

The consumer exchanges two equal-width complete binary fields in an arbitrary rectangular prefix, gap and suffix. It accepts an arbitrary binary string on those addresses. The product below uses six finite ordinary stages, including the stage that starts with individual digit swaps. The prime and the uniform rank are fixed before operand width varies. The following explanation retains the exact order of the stock and the exact size of a paid stream. [Mixed-rank source](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/87fbf4f7b35b0f18ab12b5767775a9a424cf0b15/research/p104-mixed-rank-supplier/PROOF.md) · [Complete weighted and ordinary implementations](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/a7f99a5f5f919dd3ffb2987f90c1e7d6527feb1e/research/grade14-native-realization/APPLICATION.md)

## The two arithmetic bases and the array layout

For E≥1 choose the least d such that q^d≥2^E. The input ordinal i in [0,2^E) has the same ordinal in [0,q^d). Implement this embedding by a tape copy in increasing address order. For each genuine first-field value, copy the full genuine second-field section at each gap label and insert its missing terminal section. Then append the missing first-field blocks. Prefix, gap and suffix keep their order. The resulting full volume is less than q² times the original volume. A small constant number of unary length guides, or binary countdowns with reset once per whole block, generate these sections in time proportional to the full padded volume. Constructing the fixed-q powers, exact lengths and guides once costs polynomial in the bit lengths of the extents plus the guide lengths. For complete rectangular positive ranges, this is O(V) after enlarging the fixed constant. The constructed tapes contain the original payload, explicit padding and their markers. External labels never enter a ring-operation coefficient. Apply the exact completed q-ary ordinary interchange to these records, across the given complete gap. It sends (p,x,g,y,s) to (p,y,g,x,s). Both selected extents are the same, so the genuine rectangle and its complement are invariant at the completed endpoint. Only then remove padding by reversing the insertion scans. This procedure also lets a previous binary stage exchange two old q^w-fields by first extending their ranges to the next power of two, with volume factor below four. No per-record binary-to-radix arithmetic is required.

The elementary stage uses fixed q² digit buckets. For each digit position copy records by the two digit values, preserving gap and suffix order. Return in second-digit, gap, first-digit and suffix order. A pass traverses all genuine and padded records and resets the fixed bucket heads. The digit passes commute at distinct positions. This gives time O(Vd) and space O(V) on arbitrary full spectator fields. [Physical digit-bucket implementation](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/a7f99a5f5f919dd3ffb2987f90c1e7d6527feb1e/research/grade14-native-realization/cover.py)

## Exact allocations and fresh child types

Fix one stopped recursion with atom width w≥1, prime q and uniform dimension N=2^1285200000000000. Write G=|GL_N(F_q)|, H=q^(N²(w−1)), L=B G and W=L H. The low type has a productive role and a low matrix. The high matrix has H values for each low matrix. If the root reserves P=T W^D rows, a packet after j low selections has

    T (L H)^(D−j) H^j

rows. The unused stock still has (L H)^(D−j) choices per slab. Let a<H^j encode the old high values. In the parent a row is ((uL+l)H+h)H^j+a. Its child packet is l and its child row is (uH+h)H^j+a. For every u, l, h and a these encode distinct complete locations and the two Euclidean divisions invert the operation. CoverRows.lean proves both inverses for arbitrary radices and proves that each complete packet has parent volume divided by L. The actual parent array still contains all H^j older high values. The case H=1 is included. The physical splitter and its merge transport every one of these rows at the low-type tariff, without allocating an H-dependent number of tapes. [Whole-high allocation](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/delayed-cover-batch-allocation/PROOF.md) · [Fixed-control physical splitter](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/delayed-cover-physical-batch-routing/PROOF.md)

For a fixed ancestor address, every fresh low matrix and every fresh high matrix occurs. A parent's complete right route for S maps the current matrix g=g₀+qX to gS. Its low output is h₀=(g₀S mod q) and its high output is XS+(g₀S−h₀)/q mod q^(w−1). The inverse uses S⁻¹. This map fixes the unused stock, old high digits and body coordinates. At w=1 the high fibre is a singleton. With w>1, dropping the carry changes the map. Its coefficients are rational units and q-integral entries for fixed S. Gaussian elimination at the chosen q uses unit pivots throughout on the fixed rational matrix. Each fixed scaling, translation or ordered addition acts on complete fields. Upper additions use two previously funded complete ordinary interchanges. This charges O_F(V w^(1−b)) for a fixed list of high-field routes when the earlier stage has saving b.

The decision to enter a good or bad child depends on the old low pivot. The bad body uses an unconditional slot list at that pivot. A coefficient that is zero modulo q can be a nonzero element of Z/q^w. The full coefficient is evaluated for that fixed slot. No future stock digit is a predicate of the dispatch. The emitted chronology is the same for all high values in a low packet. Therefore every individual paid occurrence sees the complete low/high cylinder at its fresh input, regardless of past branch decisions and of ancestor-dependent weights. Between child calls, the parent applies the inverse row placement to the full return. It does not stop on zero data or remove padded rows. These facts exclude both published conditional-frequency counterexamples. In particular, even an identity route with the correct final address map would fail if it removed the final incomplete slab early. [Fresh-fibre theorem and counterexamples](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/selected-cover-fresh-fibers/README.md) · [Source low/high operator and fallback](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/a7f99a5f5f919dd3ffb2987f90c1e7d6527feb1e/research/grade14-native-realization/ROUTES.md)

At one request P is a conjugate of a split rank-one projector. Under the uniform low group, it is uniform on its similarity orbit. Write its low value as uvᵀ with vᵀu=1. Each projector has exactly q−1 representations. The chance of u₀=0 and the chance of v₀=0 both equal p=(q^(N−1)−1)/(q^N−1), by symmetry of nonzero vectors. Conditional on u₀=0, the value v₀ is free. Thus the bad pivot fraction equals (2−1/q)p<2/q when N>1. For N=1 this fraction is zero. A fresh low route preserves uniformity separately at each actual paid occurrence. The good child has at most floor(c/N) atoms for parent c. A bad low uses at most 32N² children of that same maximum width, including same-orientation correction children. The source's looser 2N³/q upper bound suffices. If P_calls/B is the uniform leaf ratio, the normalized recursive moment at s is at most

    (P_calls/B) N^(−s) (1+64 N^5/q).

This sums over separately counted paid edges. No probabilistic independence is needed. Keep unused active high digits in each paid volume V/L. Each frame has a fixed number of parent banks, their full projected fields, descriptors and temporary buffers. With the proven allocation and stack primitives, the occupied volume along one coarse chain is bounded by a fixed multiple of V Σ_j L^(−j)≤2V. There is one active stack per ordinary stage at any instant. Calls to a previous stage during a parent route finish and return before that parent schedules its next child. That previous stage can borrow from the earlier operand of its own old swap while the parent parks all other data. The returned old permutation preserves arbitrary outer prefix, gap and suffix. Therefore it realizes the desired route on the parent high coordinates even when the old operand lies inside a current high cube. ## Stock growth, conversion, and the ordinary root

Choose w as the least integer with w^250≥e and put d=max(1,ceil(e/w)). The bound D=2+ceil(log_N d) covers every coarse path, since its positive children have at most floor(c/N) atoms. Compute D by integer comparisons. Allocate W(w)^D before joining the body. For earlier stage stock multiply by its full reserve when both need to live. On the current stage log_q W(w)≤log_q(BG)+N²(w−1). The old stage exchanges O(w)-digit fields, up to fixed radix conversion constants. After finitely many older stages the sum of the logarithms of all simultaneous stock factors is O_F(w(1+log e)). This bound also follows by descending the finite stage index and bounding each older term by the same current w log(e+2). It includes high matrices, all low types and independent productive inputs. Because w≤e^(1/250)+1 and 1/250<1, the sufficient k=ceil(log_q stock) eventually satisfies k<e/2. All thresholds are effective by integer search for a verified exponential-over-polynomial tail bound. For smaller widths the completed predecessor or the elementary primitive supplies the exact result.

Borrow the first k digits from the earlier operand for row stock. Its counterpart remains a genuine range in the gap. The complete earlier high range Q=q^k≥stock pads once to ceil(Q/stock)stock<2Q. CoverRows.one_padding proves this strict bound. Body operations and parked returns run on the entire padded slab. Retain every old high coordinate and the untouched low/byte suffix. At each parent width, peel its fewer than N coarse remainder atom pairs into complete weighted old-operand exchanges and fixed full-field ordered shifts. N is a constant for this stage. Handle fewer than w remaining digits by elementary digit swaps. Shift and routing descriptors draw only on exposed address digits. Evaluate each current coefficient geometrically by the ancestor programme, reusing workspace. The depth is O_F(log e), so fixed finite fan-out gives a polynomial number of operations in e and w for one coefficient even if its parent entries differ. Since e≤w^250, this is a fixed polynomial in w. A full target has q^w records, or q^(w−1) for a high field. This absorbs each such polynomial at large w, uniformly over generated weights. Generate a coefficient once per relevant prefix and move it through any intervening spectator range in a scan. Arbitrary exterior prefix values need no long arithmetic. The exact pair exchanges and earlier placements then use the preceding-stage exponent b. These bounds give O_F(V(c+w^(1−b))) time at a nonterminal coarse node, including the constant number of remainder exchanges. Its stopped base costs O_F(V w^(1−b)).

At the root, the covered strict moments at 1 and t=1−C admit, by induction on the coarse input c≥1,

    T(c,w)/V ≤ K_F [c+c^t w^(1−b)].

Indeed the first recursive term leaves a positive fixed fraction of c for the local c charge, and the second leaves a positive fixed fraction of c^t w^(1−b) for the remaining local work. The induction is monotone even for shorter bad-branch children. Apply this action twice with identity root weight to the same restored stock. Between the calls use D←D−H, the reversed exchange, D←D+H, the reversed exchange, and D←H−D. Writing C for the involutive reversal of the coarse atoms gives successive pairs (H,D−H), (C(D−H),CH), (C(D−H),CD), (D,D−H), and (D,H). All full-field additions have their O_F(Vc) charge. After both calls, remove the padded high rows and complete their k digit swaps on the genuine fields. This proves the exact ordinary endpoint for arbitrary input. The three exponents for its full cost per unit volume are

    e^(1−ρ),  e^((1−ρ)(1−C)+ρ(1−b)),  e^ρ log(e+2),   ρ=1/250.

Strictly choose each b' below (1−ρ)C+ρb, ρ and 1−ρ. This gives O_F(V e^(1−b')) for e≥1. The stock output remains independent of any external spare rows. At the six rates in Funding.lean the preparatory source has b=0. The last rate exceeds the complete complex trial by 1/62500000000000. All of these bounds concern the binary ordinary bridge.
