At significance t the current subdivision length is u=2^(t+1), and 2r/u is an integer. For an active address j set b_j to its bit at t and k_j to its strictly lower t digits. The one combined twiddle exponent is E=-sum_(active j)(2r/u)b_j k_j mod 2r. In a forward round apply the H0 layer first, then multiply the polynomial record by y^E. For opposite order, undo y^E before applying H0 in increasing significance. Other axes' twiddles commute with a given butterfly since its two inputs share their other coordinates. If E=ar+v with 0<=v<r, split the record before r-v, negate the moved suffix as it wraps, and apply sign (-1)^a to both parts. Include the case v=0 as a signed copy. A one-record computation of E on the original restored address costs poly(p) bit operations, absorbed by its r p-bit payload as r dominates every fixed power of p. The two half streams and their resets cost O(V) per level. Repeating this preparation on every individual scalar would invalidate the estimate.
