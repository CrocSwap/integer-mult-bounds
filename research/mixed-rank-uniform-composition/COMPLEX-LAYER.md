Normalize total work by the phase's starting bit volume V. At depth j, its weight is (P/B)^j. The local width is e_j=floor(e/N^j), by nested integer division. The actual compact route and prepared unit phases together cost O_F((1+e_j^τ)polylog p) per local unit volume. In the nonterminal range e_j≥1. The complete internal sum is bounded by

    O_F(polylog(p) e^τ Σ_j ((P/B)/N^τ)^j).

The ratio is below one because σ<τ. This includes all N−1 possible remainder kernels at each node. Choose a common stopping threshold d^g, where d is the initial selected width. At the last depth j≥1 the parent has e_{j−1}>d^g. Since e_{j−1}≤d/N^(j−1), N^j<N d^(1−g). Each positive stopped child uses its e_j≤d^g individual kernels. Its aggregate normalized cost is therefore O_F(d^(σ+g(1−σ))polylog p). The top-level case j=0 costs at most d^g at this same threshold. If the local width is smaller than fixed N use the complete sequential implementation. For sufficiently large d the threshold exceeds N. The finitely many remaining widths change the fixed constant. Since each active packet retains its whole row, a depth-first frame of storage O_F(V/B^j) at depth j sums to O_F(V).
