# Weighted calls, fixed-root denominator and global space

This is a second proof audit for the 480-fold rule at [87fbf4f](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/87fbf4f7b35b0f18ab12b5767775a9a424cf0b15/research/p104-mixed-rank-supplier/PROOF.md). It focuses on three ways an abstract interface could fail on the actual words.

## Every emitted binary projector

The native binary proof uses rational split idempotents. For a complete head/tail address let C reverse all coarse and within-block indices. In the coordinates (head,C·tail), D(P) sends (x,y) to ((I-P)x+Py,Px+(I-P)y). If P, Q annihilate each other on both sides, D(P)D(Q)=D(P+Q). For nested P,Q with PQ=QP=Q the product is D(P-Q). Tensor dilation replaces P by P⊗I_f and C by C_N⊗C_f. The reversal inside each multiplicity block is required even after a block moves between head and tail.

The finite compiler can be audited by induction on its actual construction. Dilation, an embedding with zero on unused coordinate blocks, tensor product, restriction to a basis of the image with its kernel as a complementary half, positive subdivision and I-P complement preserve split idempotence over the rationals. These operations do not require that P remain diagonal in the common ambient basis. A positive split of R image vectors into any disjoint ordered blocks replaces P by the sum of mutually annihilating split projectors onto those blocks. It applies in particular to each separate cutoff after the first paid label of the 480-fold product has been determined. The equal rank matcher S=B_P B_Q^-1 identifies whole images AND kernels. The finite set of matrices, inverses, denominators and witnessing minors is fixed before selecting the single prime. After excluding them the same identities hold over every Z/q^w, including all lifted ranks and the last partial block. The compiler changes the symbolic projectors. It does not insert an unpriced coordinate permutation of varying width between individual roles. Fixed physical port routes and role matchers act on the *whole fresh cover row* only after the full compilation.

It is insufficient to recite equal rank at a coupled interface. The actual right action sends a row g to gS_i on ingress of role i. A nonzero block from i into j, returned at g on role j, reads its source at g S_j S_i^-1. For arbitrary independent functions of g this equals the required source only if S_i=S_j, or the target explicitly contains that displaced row. The two ports in every coupled native main pair use the identical S_(port,stage). Directed/adjoint ports use the matching inverse. The native helpers have autonomous diagonal completed endpoints. The complete *paired caller* in each product slot has independent diagonal productive outputs, as does the strictified mixed unit. Internal cross-role coupling at such a slot cancels before its individually chosen routes are undone. Never start re-matching an internal, partly executed native word as if its outputs were already diagonal. Specializing a discarded productive input to zero preserves the diagonal endpoint and its physical bank. Arbitrary other inputs stay independent. Chronological entire-child completion establishes the needed matches from native ports through source complements and exit batches. These checks use the actual [all-column port proof](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/a7f99a5f5f919dd3ffb2987f90c1e7d6527feb1e/research/grade14-native-realization/PROOF.md) and [same-row criterion](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/7840). They require the compiler to establish the claimed finite counts separately.

For a compiled invocation the common unit array A may depend on original atom j and exposed ancestor rows only. The symbolic whole-job routes g↦gS do not move the original physical body atom index j or its ancestor. Therefore conjugation by diag(A,I) commutes with every such route, inverse route and pointwise XOR on aligned roles, even when A varies with j and even when a parent owned bank carries correlated data. An arbitrary fresh-row-dependent weight does not obey this equality. In the chosen recursion it is never imposed. At an actually emitted terminal leaf lower the complete covered projector P_g=gP_0 g^-1 of rank one to weighted reversed exchanges. On its good low class, d=A_0(P_g)_00 is a unit. Write Δ=T M T^-1-I=[e;b][a,d z^t] as in [the source recipe](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/a7f99a5f5f919dd3ffb2987f90c1e7d6527feb1e/research/grade14-native-realization/ROUTES.md). Then Δ²=-2Δ gives a e+d z^t b=-2. A lower shear K with K e=-b-z/d and d z^t K=a+e^t exists by this compatibility. The explicit K in the recipe verifies both equations. It changes Δ into [e;-z/d][-e^t,d z^t]. Adding I is the required two-coordinate swap and fixes every other coordinate. The actual later-target shears on the head and reversed physical tail precede and follow this single reversed child. Its weight d varies over original columns and the *parent* cover. That cover becomes an earlier head-independent prefix at the child. Its values remain completely represented.

For a bad low class, unit-pivot elimination works on the complete invertible 2N-by-2N residual. Every remaining Schur complement is invertible, hence has a unit in its first column: reduction modulo q is a nonsingular field matrix. Under column-dependent diagonal conjugation and a row permutation π, the current reduced matrix changes to diag(b_π)M_red diag(b)^-1. Each slot coefficient therefore receives the appropriate ratio of units. The first eligible pivot and every swap position depend on the common low, even though the coefficients differ with high rows or columns. Zero additions and identity paired scalings retain their emitted slots. The final diagonal product has the original determinant sign modified only by the common swap parity. The sign is independent of the column. A same-orientation pair of fields needs two real half-field reversed exchanges around its middle reversed exchange, with an odd remaining atom untouched. All swap pairs in the lowering place the physically first field before the second. For a tail first field reverse the order in which its weight array is read. Every such child and every half child retains the full exterior, is called only after the aligned ingress, and has size at most f=floor(n_a/N). At f=1 the unit reversals on empty halves need no paid job. This accounts for all good and bad leaves after the two mixed cutoffs. No prime or native argument purports to make a nonunit pivot invertible.

Correlations in work are harmless only because the current `l,h` range is a complete fresh cylinder. At a bad or good parent edge, keep all earlier h in the child batch. Every child gets its own independent L H stock factor. Specialize only algorithmic target slots already marked as private. The root-generated descriptors recurse through earlier low and high rows and column indices. If a descriptor reads b parent entries at each of D=O_fixed(log e) generations, recomputation costs b^D poly(w,log e)=poly(w) on w=ceil(e^(1/250)). Prepare it once per complete later-target fibre of q^w addresses, or q^(w-1) for a high field. Both log e and the degree can be absorbed since q^w outgrows every fixed polynomial. Preserve earlier control state on its private tape through intervening spectator ranges. The actual address permutation acts on complete physical data, independent of bit values.

## Kernel proof of the complete good rank-one leaf

It applies to every commutative ring, module rank and legal weight column. Take R=Z/q^w, X=Y=R^N, a split rank-one projector P=uV with V(u)=1, diagonal head automorphism A and the physical tail reversal C. A conjugated projector gP₀g⁻¹ has column gu₀ and row V₀g⁻¹. Take e and z to be the first head and last physical tail unit vectors, E and Z their coordinate functionals. In the code these are unit vectors and duals by construction.

* The specification `weighted` applies A⁻¹ and C⁻¹ on entrance. It exchanges the P parts, fixes their complements, then applies A and C. This is the actual residual and diagonal conjugation. ## Interior resets and deferred external coordinates

At a complex phase start, designate its active selected axes as one root factor. Reserve *all* complex role rows for descendants in physically external complete coordinate fields before the phase starts. At an ordinary descendant no new role coordinate is taken from the active root factor. Complements and remainders that were selected at this root stay factors of the same root vector even after they become spectators of a child. Any fixed address changes inside temporary compact movement may cross active and inactive address positions, but they only move whole numerical values. No scalar instruction observes a partial permutation. The completed map is the required monomial on the root. Under these conditions a row on external label r can be expanded as a finite sum c U x_o where o selects one whole original entrance vector or the zero sentinel independent of the active coordinate, and U is a Gaussian-dyadic Clifford on the same root. This rules out the projected intermediate (P C P)^t whose denominator can grow indefinitely even on one active axis.

At a completed native return the child supplies its exact independent prescribed operator on the *actual* entrance row. Its ancestor mass and dyadic denominator debt remain present. Only the child's temporary scalar expansion is replaced by one completed Clifford on that row. It never resets the incoming row to a new independent magnitude-one input. Odd factors are cleared on every actual entry. The minimum product-grid certificate holds coefficientwise because paths from a fixed entrance to a fixed returned chart telescope to the same oriented T_b T_a^-1 on every multiplicity. Cross-entrance cancellations are unnecessary. A prefix before an ADD accumulates only already representable scalar products on this grid. The finite source upper-bounds its unsigned mass, including the one-sign wide Horner prefixes.

Here is one direct denominator argument. For a unitary Clifford U, take v=U|b⟩ or U*|b⟩ to treat a column or row. Its stabilizer has 2^D signed Pauli elements. The shift parts form an r-dimensional binary subgroup A. Stabilization forces amplitudes' moduli to be constant on every A-orbit. The diagonal kernel has size 2^(D-r) and contains no -I. Its distinct signs and support vectors give D-r independent binary equations on any supported address. Thus support occupies at most 2^r addresses, contains a full A-orbit since v is nonzero, and is exactly that orbit. By normalization the nonzero squared modulus is 2^-r. Express a dyadic entry in a reduced common denominator as (a+i b)/2^k. For k>0 at least one numerator is odd, and (a²+b²)2^r=2^(2k). Modulo four this gives k≤floor((r+1)/2)≤ceil(D/2). The k=0 case is immediate. The new theorem `PhaseBudget.clifford_denominator` kernel-checks this last arithmetic implication, using the absolute values of the integer numerators. The flat-support argument and intrinsic Clifford status of the native operators remain mathematical steps outside that Lean theorem. After a completed outer phase re-anchor as in [the frame proof](FRAMES.md). This permits deferred axes without an active coordinate projector.

## Historical outer tape space in terms of operand bits

For every length in the defining positive range, b=ceil(log_2 n), p=6b and 4n/b≤T<8n/b. Therefore 24n≤T p<48n exactly. a complex record using O(p) signed bits changes only this fixed factor. All three CRT conversions operate on one complete padded cube at a time with a fixed finite number of sibling banks. Its balanced topology is executed level by level, with stable order restored between successive gates. No full parent cube is recursively retained at every tree level. The [actual CRT router](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/unconditional-batched-crt/CRT.md) prepares a switch using at most O(2^(4 ell) (p+2)^16) private space by its time bound. Since ell=Θ(p^(1-e)), this is 2^o(p)=o(n). Whole-volume scans for guarded holes use O(p)-bit keys and an O(p)-bit address counter, so carrying every key on every record would already take O(Tp). The runtime actually computes the expensive keys only for exceptions. Stable radix passes overwrite and erase their previous buffers. The compiled previous-stage ordinary swap may temporarily open geometric frames as proved separately in PROOF.md. The exact exponent of the re-priced conversion is 1-a+2(1-e)=1-κ-2·10^-14 up to absorbable logarithms, with a=κ+4·10^-14. The old numerical value of a in the earlier #4100 example would not justify this comparison.

Three normalized transforms, one pointwise product and the two inputs can require several full fields simultaneously. Reserve one permanent O(Tp) extent per such fixed role and erase its current data after its last use. For middle ring products the scalar axis has length r=2^ell. One exact signed Kronecker convolution call on O(rp) bits has historical workspace O(rp log(rp)) from its independent ordinary multiplication time bound. The calls at distinct polynomial records take turns on the same work tapes. Thus this workspace is 2^o(p)=o(n). The same reasoning applies to the O(p)-bit scalar backend. The one-axis archive and all dyadic control-page setup use at most d·2^O(ell) poly(p)=2^o(p) and are also reused. During a sparse union round at most m stored split-indicator bits accrue per original record over all rounds, whence O(Tm)⊂O(Tp). Destination keys and coefficient records take O(p) bits apiece. Frame and transfer tape historical sizes depend on the *maximum*, over all completed or current calls, of the chain extent on that fixed tape. They do not sum full high-water sizes across three retained transforms, d level sweeps or repeated RHS batches. Including input, canonical zero padding and 2n output, these estimates give O(n) historical cells for these components.
