# From two bit strings to their product

Fix the price at κ = 0.00387992803556 and put g = 10⁻¹⁴. The fixed source word, transcript and compiler are part of the algorithm. Input data never select a different word. Use the operator and layout constructions in [COMPILER.md](COMPILER.md), [ORDINARY.md](ORDINARY.md) and [FRAMES.md](FRAMES.md). This section connects their outputs to a single fixed multitape integer machine. The resource unit V includes all bits in one complete external array, before a constant number of buffers or tapes.

## Layout, parameters and sources

Read two nonnegative binary integers of length at most n. Take the larger length including given leading zeros, and pad the shorter input. Treat empty or small operands by elementary multiplication. For the large branch set b = ceil(log₂ n), p = 6b, B = 2ᵇ. Obtain the unique power of two T in [4n/b,8n/b). Let ε = 1−g and β = 1−3g. Choose a power-of-two d within a factor two of p^ε, and partition it into power-of-two groups of m axes within a factor two of p^β. Choose integer powers and floors by exact power comparisons after clearing the fixed rational exponents. Use width ℓ or ℓ−1 on each of the d target axes so that their sum is log₂ T. Give axis zero maximum width. It is the contiguous suffix. Thus V = Tp satisfies 24n ≤ V < 48n. Also ℓ = Θ(p^(1−ε)), dℓ = Θ(p) and 2^O(ℓ) poly(p) = 2^o(p). All constants below are fixed before n varies.

For signed magnitudes, retain the XOR of the sign bits and apply it to the magnitude output, giving zero its canonical sign. A fixed-width two's-complement input or output uses a single inversion-and-add-one scan on a negative value. Both conventions add O(n) time and backing. The core theorem below is stated on the unsigned digit streams.

For each dyadic side t_i select a different odd prime r_i in ((1−1/(2p²))t_i,(1−1/(4p²))t_i]. Scan the intervals deterministically and use trial division. Trudgian's explicit bound for π(x) differs from li(x) by at most 0.2795 x (log x)^(−3/4) exp(−sqrt(log(x)/6.455)) for x ≥ 229. The main term between the two endpoints is at least t_i/(4p² log t_i). The ratio of the two errors to this term is O(p²(log t_i)^(1/4) exp(−c sqrt(log t_i))). It tends to zero because ℓ grows as a fixed positive power of p. The resulting count exceeds d. The two distinct target sizes have disjoint intervals. Full scans, trial divisions and reversals cost d·2^O(ℓ) poly(p) = o(n). This is the only large-prime counting input. [Primary theorem 2](https://arxiv.org/html/1401.2689v2#S2) · [Fine-prime construction](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/native-kappa-30201655/modern-sources/FINE-PRIME-PADDING.md)

Set S = product r_i. Then T/2 < S < T, since the relative loss is at most d/(2p²). For b ≥ 8, S ≤ B. Each operand has at most l = ceil(n/b) digits, 2l−2 < 2n/b ≤ T/2. Allocate S cyclic coordinates with zeros past the last digit. The raw radix-B digits are less than B. Their linear convolution is also their cyclic convolution. Its coefficients are bounded by l(B−1)² < B³. These tests depend only on the input length. Reading and padding costs O(V), including exactly representing each digit divided by 4B on the p-bit fractional grid.

## An actual router for three CRT conversions

Set P_i = product of r_j for j<i and μ_i = P_i⁻¹ mod r_i. The address permutation maps the cyclic integer k to (μ_i k mod r_i)_i. At input, consecutive k already have mixed-radix digits with axis zero fastest. Insert zeros at the ends of padded axis intervals in that same order. At output reverse the operations. No transpose of the full extent is implicit.

Construct the permutation by a balanced tree. Combine each left and right group A,B by adding k_A/(S_A P_(B,i)) mod r_i on each right axis. The identity k_(A,B) = k_A + S_A k_B proves the step. Reconstruct k_A from its current residues. On invalid left-child coordinates use the identity, and fix invalid right coordinates. The resulting maps permute the complete dyadic cube and preserve its valid subset. Reverse the level order and subtract for the inverse. Use false controls on the two unused switch layers for a shorter side. The full switch evaluators (including CRT arithmetic, coloring degree-two graphs, target positions, placement, inverses and a dirty address) can be performed by repeated finite-tape scans in E ≤ C·2^(4ℓ)(p+2)^16 steps. [Balanced CRT](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/unconditional-batched-crt/CRT.md) · [Mixed widths](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/crt-mixed-width-caller/README.md) · [Uniform evaluator](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/crt-uniform-evaluator/PROOF.md)

A completed target rotation preserves its valid subset. Its Beneš micro-switches can move a partial route through an invalid coordinate. Execute them there as well. Disable only the two genuinely idle switch layers on a shorter side and use the specified padding rule in the completed target map.

For one simultaneous layer, take G = 64(ℓ+ceil(log₂(p+2))), Q = 2^G, K = 64G, r_max = floor(log₂ T/(100G)), H = G r_max. Work in batches ≤ r_max. Prior to the root the other global half is unused. At the root the inactive quarter of the right half is unused. Split into a bounded number of batches and move three whole dirty H-bit ranges into two front and one rear ranges by the completed ordinary interchange. Each switch function omits all active targets and these reservoirs. Track the fragments produced by the three disjoint transpositions. Put the positions in colors modulo K **after** the move. [Explicit placement and batch count](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/crt-reservoir-placement/PROOF.md)

Use the reversible four-rotation switch F in the balanced CRT source. In step 3 read the *updated* middle digit of the t register. On a carry-free target with initial bit b and parity z, the two changes of the y field sum to z(1−2b)·2^j. The last step restores t. Execute F, add the control to the u digits, execute F again, subtract the control. The result on good addresses flips exactly the selected bits. The guard at position j examines **j+1 through j+K−1**. Its margins are measured on that K−1 bit integer. The next modulo-K target is outside this interval. A margin 2Q on each side gives failure density at most 8Q/2^K per target. Exclude all-ones u and t digits as well. Process truncated end guards by direct single-bit controlled swaps with the complete H-bit suffix available. The ideal map preserves the resulting good set. Each actual macro is a permutation even with carries. Consequently after the macro the correct key on every bad address x is ideal(actual⁻¹(x)). Sort exactly those full keys and merge the repaired records into their holes. This also restores all dirty reservoirs. Using K higher bits or reordering holes without those keys can change the answer. [Guard counterexample and correction](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/pr4100-guard-coloring/README.md) · [Complete-key repair](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/crt-arbitrary-source-repair/PROOF.md)

For an end target, move its bit next to the H-bit suffix by a paid width-one ordinary interchange. The target control omits that bit and every suffix bit even on invalid addresses. Prepare it once per prefix. When it is one, buffer the first complete suffix slice, copy the second, then drain and erase the buffer. When it is zero, copy the two slices in order. Reverse the interchange to restore the physical order. The evaluator runs once per pair of slices, and each slice has 2^H arbitrary records. A color has at most one target in the final K−1 positions. Thus this branch adds at most two ordinary moves and O(V) plus the amortized evaluator cost to that color. The source, working arena and saved heads meet the complete-fibre contract at every level. [Tail switch proof and predecessor credit](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/crt-tail-single-buffer/PROOF.md)

Test the guards in one O(p)-bit stencil scan per physical address. The sparse fraction is O(p·2^(−G)). Pay E for each bad address, and separately E once per complete dense preparation fibre of at least 2^H records. Both are absorbed since E·2^(−G) ≤ C·2^(−60ℓ)(p+2)^(−48). Charge the full ordinary swaps at exponent 1−a_BIT. A tree level has O(1) batches, O(ℓ) switch layers and O(ℓ+log p) colors. There are O(log d) levels, executed in sequence in a fixed number of complete arenas. All three conversions therefore cost O(V p^(1−a_BIT+2(1−ε)) polylog(p)), where a_BIT = 0.003879928035646. Even the weaker completed rate a=κ+4g would suffice. There is no use of an address-key sort of the dense array. The packed keys have O(p) bits and one sequential evaluator occupies 2^o(p) workspace.

## Three source transforms

Let F_r denote the kernel exp(−2πi j k/r)/r. For one axis put ρ = t/r and v(j) = floor(ρj+1/2). Write E for embedding at v(j), C for restriction at the same coordinates, and D_jj = exp(πα²(ρj−v(j))²). Let S_g be the broad expansion and T_g be the tall narrow Gaussian with kernels from [GROUPED-GAUSSIAN.md](GROUPED-GAUSSIAN.md). Respect the signed periodic occurrences in both. On valid rows N = C T_g D. On padded holes set the narrow extension to the identity. For R_r:x_j↦x_(tj mod r), Q_t:x_k↦x_(−rk mod t), the absolutely convergent Gaussian Poisson formula gives

    T_g R_r F_r = Q_t F_t S_g
    R_r F_r = D N⁻¹ C Q_t F_t S_g.

To see the normalizing constant, fix input residue l. Poisson summation of the narrow sum gives the common kernel (α t)⁻¹ sum_m exp[−π(l+rm/t)²/α²] exp[−2πi rkm/t]. The phase exp(−2πi kl) is one. Thus the scalar is fixed by the executed 1/t Fourier mean and the broad 1/α. It does not come from a round-trip assumption. The matrices S_g, N, C and D are real. Conjugating the two phase diagonals and the chirp kernel implements the opposite sign with the same retained R. [Derivation](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/unconditional-batched-crt/PROOF.md#L170-L208)

Tensor the identity over the actual axes. At the entrance insert **all** v(j) coordinates in a single ordered scan before the first native array operation. In the broad product keep the entire dyadic cube, including nonzero values on holes produced by other broad axes. In the narrow product apply all the extended inverses before the final restriction of all axes. Diagonal D extends by one on a hole. The factors on different axes commute. The two predicate scans cost O(Tdℓ² polylog(p)). Their exponent after dividing by V is 1−ε. For each balanced group expose its m log₂ L low bits as one contiguous core at the back, with L = 2^ceil(log₂(p^128)). The sparse coordinate placement alters at most 2m log₂ L positions. Its inverse descriptor reconstructs the true coarse addresses when a page is built. Complete the paid inverse exposure as required by each stage. No prefix with only part of a dyadic spectator is sent to the H layer. [Physical support placement](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/capped-phase-fusion/SUPPORT-ROUTER.md)

Before **each** global H undo the low-core gather and expose the original K-bit neighborhoods. Its log₂ L low bits alone need not cover K. Restore for the next coarse-controlled diagonal, including both movements for every layer in ε−aβ. Place the full short-record suffix outside all reservoirs. When BIT transports a guarded complex field, use fixed-width binary cells for the finite alphabet. Segment by the known record shape. Intermediate XOR words are opaque. Decode numeric components only after the entire permutation returns a valid record. The encoding and end resets cost a constant factor.

The producer in [GROUPED-GAUSSIAN.md](GROUPED-GAUSSIAN.md) constructs signed bands on true original coordinates, separate normalized interior circuits, and packed genuine Schur pages for current right-hand sides. The one-axis rows have direction-independent dyadic public divisor A_p = 2^ceil(log₂(p^100)). This is an integer power determined by a comparison with p^100. Use the same A_p on the face returns and interiors. If a factorization distributes the divisor over stages, every outlet has exactly this product. Implement the inner calculation as a direct sum over complete cores, using the fixed bit positions of the nested Beneš network. Coarse-dependent switches are controlled diagonals between global H layers. Short Strassen batches must persist across prefix changes on the same (core, visit, axis). Save the untouched original data for all face returns. Every dense or sparse stage uses the actual values returned by its predecessor. Unfinished norms within a group are at most 2^O(m polylog(p)), hence cost o(p) bits. The spectrum or invertibility of the broad full matrix is not required. Elementary-function evaluation, exact local elimination and rational generator selection to polynomial private precision take poly(p) per core/page, absorbed by L^m, even on the last full core. Only packed O(p)-bit words move online.

On the full intermediate cube form a(x) = product exp(−πi r_i x_i²/t_i). Then QF_t u is conjugate(a) · [a * (conjugate(a) · u)]/T. Expand a(x−j) to check the bilinear phase. Each t_i is even. Apply the per-axis phases by lexicographic odometer with cached prefix products. A carry of depth j requires O(j·2^ℓ) table words only once per product of j radices. All radices are 2^ℓ or 2^(ℓ−1). Summing gives O(1) word movement per scalar on average. Prefix coefficient errors traverse at most d factors. The opposite uses the conjugated kernel. Initial and final small root tables are generated by rational interval expansions of π and exp, with downward component truncation toward zero at bounded phase modulus. [Chirp tape and prefix error](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/pr4100-chirp-odometer-tariff/README.md)

Compute * on the cube via [SYNTHETIC-ACCESS.md](SYNTHETIC-ACCESS.md). Let r=2^ℓ for axis zero and M=T/r. Twist both operands on this axis by exp(πi j/r) before packing as r coefficients in the negacyclic polynomial ring. Undo that twist at output. Its r-th power is −1. For every outer-axis size u dividing r, y^(2r/u) is a principal u-th root in that ring: in any nontrivial geometric sum the terms separated by half the order cancel by y^r=−1. Two normalized forwards, ring products divided by r, the reverse normalized circuit and a final exact factor M give (f*g)/(rM). The common bit-reversed order in the middle is preserved through the product. At each ring product, use centered signed Kronecker slots wide enough for the sum of r products, O(p+log r) per slot. Four real integer products suffice for a complex product. Recover exact signed coefficients with carries, apply the negacyclic wrap, divide by r, and round. Sequential calls use the unconditional fixed-tape O(N log N) multiplier on O(rp) bits. Its time also bounds the historical scratch for each call. [Detailed packing and extraction](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/upstream/build/sections/06-transforms.tex#L448-L609) · [Harvey and van der Hoeven](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2021/193-2/p04)

Use maximum complex modulus as the public error norm. Signed monomial twists preserve it. For a ring product divided by r the maximum norm is at most the product of the operand norms, by the r-term triangle inequality. Its perturbation is at most (R+δ)δ'+R'δ. Apply this to bounded operands, scaling the known chirp input by a fixed dyadic factor when necessary and undoing it at return. The final M can amplify the residual once by at most 2^b. Within each group the arithmetic, coefficient, truncation and graph perturbations are ≤2^(−P+o(p))(1+R) at online working precision P=Cp. The conversion from a local circuit 2-norm costs at most sqrt(L^m), included in o(p). For the real Gaussian rows the same polynomial row-sum bounds apply to maximum complex modulus and to maximum component-l1. One global factor sqrt(2) suffices when interpreting complex component errors in the latter norm. Completed normalized broad and narrow maps are contractions, including holes and extraction. Errors over the O(d/m) groups add. Reversing the two sets of Gaussian divisors costs A_p^(2d)=2^O(d log p)=2^o(p) in the final scale. Chirp generation, FFT truncation and the polynomial number of sequential stages per scalar add 2^(−P+o(p)). The residual on the complete transform of any represented input in the half disk is ≤2^(−P+O(p)+o(p)). Choose one fixed C large enough that it is <2^(−4p) eventually. Increase the constant in the Gaussian bandwidth with C. Private setup words can have polynomial length in p. Online components, including temporaries, have O(p) bits.

All d prime axes participate in each Gaussian expansion and compression, including the axis that later stores the signed polynomial coefficients. The ring FFT has d−1 outer axes. In each of the three source transforms undo that call's own A_p^(2d) **before** its p-bit public outlet. The final decoder therefore uses exactly (S·4B)². A single Gaussian compensation at the final product would be incorrect.

The formal transform is defined on all complex signals. Extend the finite-grid program there by applying componentwise truncation toward zero to p fractional bits at entrance. This mathematical extension agrees with the actual program on every represented input. It preserves the half disk. Both the entry conversion and the final p-bit output conversion have maximum error at most sqrt(2)/B⁶. Each exact source mean is a contraction. Thus the returned error on the full disk is at most 2 sqrt(2)/B⁶ + 2^(−4p) < 3/B⁶ for B ≥ 32. The two operand streams and the rounded pointwise output are already on this grid. No real-number oracle or extra high-precision input conversion is executed.
