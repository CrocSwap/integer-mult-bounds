Read the dependency order from the ideal algebra through physical implementation. Prove the closed finite-word identities for formal fields first. Compile their occurrences and choose the finite prime and sites. Prove exact common-grid simulation using the ideal completed returns. Implement ordinary calls by induction on the earlier stage and, inside a stage, decreasing atom count. Only then use ordinary movement in the complex service. Use those completed primitives on the globally padded outer arrays, and finally invoke the product-error theorem. The previous four-premise grade-14 theorem and the earlier complete outer theorem are never taken as assumptions in this order.

The claimed closure of these rows is a **written mathematical result** with the formal and finite boundaries stated in [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md). The remaining external results include the classical CRT and character identities, finite linear algebra and Clifford stabilizer facts, Poisson summation for Gaussians, the Jacobi triple product, the explicit prime-number estimate, Strassen's identity, and the independent multitape O(N log N) multiplication theorem on short words. The proofs identify where each is used. None asserts the multiplier or the transform supplier being constructed.
