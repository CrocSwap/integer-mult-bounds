## Sufficient composition theorem

Fix a finite controller built from complete, indivisible normalized invocations. Each local provider has the required data endpoint, arbitrary-dirt restoration, actual helper entrance frames, normalized full-chart recipe and paid local recursive profile. Let every helper occurrence be assigned to a legal whole physical family and a pending child on that family's actual idle timeline. Require:

1. Each call uses every required helper once, on distinct whole families, excluding its active data families.
2. Every occurrence lies strictly inside the assigned pending child's actual legal interval.
3. Within each pending child b of rank R_b, assigned residual widths sum to at most R_b. Their actual physical supports are chosen as disjoint consecutive intervals in that child's orthogonal axes.
4. Each assigned helper is emitted with its source-specific full ambient chart and normalized wrapper, which restores every borrowed spectator after the complete invocation.

Then these assignments compose legally under the same inherited complete-wrapper interfaces. The exact recursive profile is

    H = sum_calls H_local + sum_{b:q_b>0} delta(q_b),
    q_b = R_b - sum_assigned_widths.

Proof. Conditions1 and2 establish actual storage and chronology. Condition3 permits the residual operators to commute on disjoint coordinate supports; their product followed by the paid remainder equals the original pending operator. Condition4 restores all temporary spectator state before later invocations. Substitution at complete-call boundaries therefore preserves the controller's data endpoint and arbitrary-dirt semantics. Scalar words, every recursive local call and every positive remainder appear in the displayed bill. No helper family, recursive splitter or storage oracle is omitted.

The theorem allows uniform or heterogeneous local providers, and allows the whole owner map to change. It does not assume pointwise dominance of old and new widths. Disjoint unions of certified contexts compose by addition of profiles. The finite source-specific charts and invoice are required constructive evidence, not hidden assumptions introduced by the calculus.

## Exact pattern realization

For a group of n physical ports sharing the same owner timeline, choose a finite multiset of residual-width patterns, each fitting its pending rank. Assign each pattern position to a legal invocation time, preserving the provider's exact width multiplicities. Within n copies of one pattern, let a(t,j) be the integer number of positions j assigned at time t. Each position has column sum n and each time has row sum at most n.

View a(t,j) as a bipartite multigraph between times and pattern positions. Add dummy vertices and edges until both sides have equal size and every degree is n. This completion exists by repeatedly pairing a deficient vertex on each side; total deficiencies agree. Hall's condition holds because n|S| edges leaving any left set S enter at most n|N(S)| slots. Hence a perfect matching exists. Remove one perfect matching at a time: the remainder stays regular, yielding n matchings. Matchings are port colors. Thus each physical port realizes one copy of the pattern and participates at most once at each call time. Dummy edges contribute no helper occurrence.

The literal output is additionally checked for whole-family injection across pattern groups and exclusion from active data. Therefore a finite integer pattern/time certificate plus the reconstructed and checked owner map is a sufficient physical construction. The final verifier requires neither the optimizer nor any optimality claim.
