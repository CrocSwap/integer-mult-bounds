# Derivative and transport changes

The root scripts add actual multi-dimensional response construction, a concrete
nonzero-response rewrite, an explicit two-provider registry, new physical owner
binding and independent per-call checks. The inherited scalar/phase/outer engines
are retained and attributed. No public gain is added arithmetically to another.

Independent proof review corrected an important distinction: scalar-body dirty
restoration does not mean the complete physical helper endpoint is identity.
The endpoint includes its paid residual operator and restores only borrowed
spectators. The gluing proof now uses explicit pending-operator factorization
and legal idle windows. The final two variants have identical residual factors,
so their call-by-call substitution is valid. No executable check changed.

The first transport run exposed nondeterministic elapsed seconds in phase review
bindings. Registry fingerprints now omit only that top-level timing field from
canonical JSON; every mathematical field remains. Raw receipts stay hash-bound
in source_artifacts. The subsequent complete18-stage CRLF replay passed exactly.

After that replay, the outer driver gzip.compress call was made explicit about
compresslevel=9 (subprocess gzip already was). The resulting owner bytes were
recomputed and compared byte-for-byte with the complete replay; they are identical.
This future-proofs runtime-default changes, not a mathematical or payload change.
All other executable sources and immutable inputs match the CRLF replay copy.
