Geometric reflection lemma (conditional on exact forward frame certification)

Let G=I-J/9 on Q^24. It is nondegenerate (its eigenvalues are 1 and -5/3).
For any G-nondegenerate used frame U, Q^24=U direct-sum U^perp_G, the complement
has dimension 24-dim(U), and its restriction of G is nondegenerate. Inclusions
U subset V reverse under complement. Thus the reversed chain of complemented
frames is ascending and each rank gap is exactly the original rank gap:
 (24-dim(U))-(24-dim(V))=dim(V)-dim(U).
Every same-frame scalar gate stays a same-frame gate when all its incident
registers are reflected. Read boundaries, source controls, gauge reads and
physical alias transitions must all reverse as events, including the phase cut
and both original-X control ports at their common reflected frame; reflecting only the
producer or omitting a gauge read would not establish this result.

A concrete rational basis for the complement is A G^-1, where A is any full-row-
rank annihilator matrix of U and G^-1=I-J/15. Multiplying by 15 gives the integer
rows 15a-(sum a)1. These rows pair to zero with the original B basis because
G(15 G^-1 A^t)=15 A^t and B A^t=0. Their rank is rank(A)=24-dim(U), since G^-1
is invertible. This is an explicit algebraic certificate for every used frame,
not a heuristic test on frame dimensions. Over allowed residue fields the same
argument applies whenever the inherited denominators and Gram pivots are units;
no additional primes beyond 3 and 5 enter this complement formula.
