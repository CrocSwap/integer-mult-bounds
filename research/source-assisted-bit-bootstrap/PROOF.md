# A literal source-assisted BIT word with fixed high boundary

The scalar payload in this package is F2, as in the retained
`research/paired-cube-bit/FORMAT.md` and `LEMMA.md`. The exact rational
frame geometry is a separate object. This package does not claim an integer
decoder identity by interpreting XOR cancellation as integer cancellation.

## Source-assisted low circuit

We implement icekylinx's PR184 construction, developed with GPT-6 Astra,
on eumemic's later immutable PR168 input
`2c4a380126640abfcdce398ced255d1dd5d1d007`. The older PR184 input is
`fd25adb7fbaa12ee761d02c733c54d1d2a7687ee`; it is not substituted silently.
The pinned newer input files are byte-identical in the PR185 base checkout.
`common_frame_network184.py` and `SOURCE_ALIGNED_BIT_WORD184.txt` retain
PR184's source and argument; the new emitter and full checks supply the
previously missing global elementary transcript.

In each cube, the A0 and G12 rank-three channels are sums of two original
source-pair values. Each pair lies in an already paid, nondegenerate rank-two
K frame M. All singleton V injections occur first at their source lines.
The original source registers then remain unmixed at M through both phases
of low construction. Every direct injection is two literal X-controlled
XORs into an arbitrary dirty auxiliary. Its ascent from frame zero to M is
paid. All subsequent source controls finish before any original K mixing
or delivery. The original source chains are unchanged.

At an exact low-frame vertex, the incoming fresh forms C are independent.
Let t be the number of outgoing requests and r their rank. The compiler adds
exactly t-r dirty coordinates. Express each request on C. Complete dependent
requested rows with distinct new dirty basis vectors, then append independent
coordinate rows to obtain an invertible square binary matrix. Exact Gaussian
elimination emits its elementary XOR factorization; swaps use three XORs.
Every operation takes place at that vertex's common nondegenerate frame.
Complementary coordinates remain physical rows and are retained for cleanup.
The special source-control vertices directly inject the demanded pair forms.

A low path exits the rank-at-most-three region at most once. The outgoing
physical coordinate is renamed to its exact old high-boundary role. The
complete higher-frame operation sequence is retained literally, in each
phase. Every new high fresh value and root value is checked against the old
scalar graph. The 240 old paired donors that terminate at rank three are
assigned distinct new complement coordinates at their exact old terminal
vertices; the other donors retain the high word. All 1,760 matching pairs
and 3,960 gauges remain. This construction does not assume that the changed
complements have their old dirty values.

## Dirty columns and chronology

The emitter computes the entire new auxiliary response by transposing the
new elementary XOR word over F2. The nongauged initial corrections therefore
use the new word, not the old graph's response. Every gauged response is
checked equal to its retained target set. A reused recipient subtracts the
actual donor content at its retained read, after the donor's final operation
and before the recipient's first operation. Read order, donor-frame inclusion,
and every target chain are checked again on the emitted transcript.

`audit.py` independently interprets the exported word, without importing the
emitter or its response generator. It checks all source, target and arbitrary
dirty physical columns, both forward and under literal time reversal, and
rejects omitted source injections, omitted inverse source injections and
omitted compensated reads. After all target deliveries, original K mixing is
undone first at full frame; only then are the complete new auxiliary and
source-controlled operations reversed. Thus original X and all dirty
auxiliary registers are restored and Y receives X over F2.

## Frames and complete paid work

Every operation is checked at a nondegenerate retained frame. The checker
propagates conservative source ancestry with union, even across XOR
cancellation, and checks each source label inside its operation frame. This
also bounds the support of a noncancelling defining integer lift. It checks
all nested physical role chains, including donor/recipient splices; all
original-X histories; and gauge/root/partner target events in chronological
order. All used frames belong to the retained newer PR168 geometry, but the
PR182 witness list covered only 23,869 of the 24,161 selected bases.
`prime_coverage.py` supplies exact Bareiss Gram determinants and factor
witnesses for the remaining 292 bases, keyed by canonical basis hashes.
Their largest determinant has 79 bits and their stripped residual is one.
All 24,161 bases are thus covered, with four corruption controls; the
inherited prime guard q > 2^80 remains unchanged. The reflected-complement
denominators 3 and 5 are already excluded.

The independent recount charges three copies of every local, source and
target frame transition; every centre copy; every remaining gauge exterior;
and all 2v finishing children. The physical stock is 17,588, total W is
21,108, m is 72, and the deficit remains 1,936. The exact new histogram equals
the independently reconstructed low-network prediction, but this equality
is checked after emitting the actual physical word.

`scalar_cost.py` counts all forward/inverse gates, all old-value corrections,
root reads, K mixing and K deliveries: 890,560 scalar XORs. Every payload
shear already has unit coefficient. It separately derives conservative
noncancelling integer path majorants; these are not an integer adjoint
obtained by reducing the stored F2 response. Exact affine inverse and
complemented reflection scope are stated in `REFLECTION_AUDIT.txt`.
The final arithmetic certificate must retain sufficient fixed scalar guards;
this finite operation count is not a substitute for the all-size adapter.

## Scope of the assembled exponent

The BIT word above supplies a better coarse child histogram. The final
certificate uses the unchanged PR182 complex supplier and finite-depth
ordinary-leaf bootstrap from PR185. It preserves their conditional
all-size weighted-prefix, restored borrowing, padding, uniform-constant and
fixed-tape interfaces. Successful finite word checks do not independently
prove those inherited analytic interfaces. No global optimality or practical
speedup is claimed.
