# Canonical raw prefixes at a selected coefficient site

We prove a refinement from selected raw-header preparation to a complete
controlled-ADD site. We keep the physical header g in place and compute
g sigma B0 in control. We decode generated fields from their canonical
mixed-radix coordinates and encode the program, field schema and local
continuation stacks as delimited binary words.

For each fixed low class, the prepared high field is an affine bijection
of the raw high cube. We use that fact to preserve complete packets while
pulling each coefficient back to raw-header order. Induction over the
fixed-low instruction trace extends the substitution to the selected
residual, under its completed-child contracts. [PROOF.md](PROOF.md) gives
the quantified statement and the retained uniform local charge.

## Evidence

We check:

- All 81 high matrices in a two-dimensional Z/9 cube, with a nonzero carry
  and nontrivial high-coordinate map. We execute the complete finite site
  on 6,561 opaque 16-bit records and its inverse.
- Actual selected COPY510 and nonidentity-owner MOVE requests from the pinned
  word and hosting inputs. Exact raw fixtures from PR2234 prevent matching
  chart errors in preparation and fixture generation from canceling.
- A selected head coefficient with older generated weights. The coefficients
  4,7,1 feed the actual split/merge preparation callback. We check all 1,944
  records in the reduced finite site rectangle and inverse restoration.
- Four serialized Good/Pair/reset chains with 36 coordinate comparisons
  against the attributed recursive grammar. We retain global field slot 2
  after a reset and check that dead high fields do not affect the value.
- Complete odometers with 139 states across four schemas. We check identical
  local symbol counts after translating origins as far as 10^40, replacing
  buried data and growing a separate spectator-metadata band to 4,096 bits.

Controls expose wrong raw/prepared interpretation, a second COPY chart,
reordered coefficient prefixes, current-field capture, wrong source codes,
compacted reset slots, wrong pair orientation, an inadmissible branch and a
wrong selected rank. We retain exact output hashes and counters in
[RESULT.json](RESULT.json).

## Reproduction and measurement boundary

Use Python 3.12 and the standard library:

```sh
python3.12 scripts/research_package.py --package research/selected-prefix-coefficient-site
```

The registered hook authenticates source bytes before importing the retained
reader and runs normal and optimized replays. Use `verify.py --emit` to print
a fresh receipt without changing files.

We count single-symbol payload and local-control tape reads, writes and moves.
We retain host integer arithmetic, decoded local operands, finite source
dispatch and outer loop bounds as macros. Their fixed-tape lowering and
polynomial cost use the written schoolbook/counter argument and PR2310's
existing allowance. We claim no measured transition count for the whole
compiled machine. We also retain the exact request/low vocabulary as an
admitted source context; a caller must supply its correct physical schema.

The selected64 execution uses three raw-prefix snapshots and a reduced
complete two-atom site rectangle. We do not enumerate its full high cube or
128-atom body. The complete 81-lift cube has dimension two. The written
refinement covers the stated general domain; the finite tests do not prove
that theorem or the selected global caller. We retain the selected frontier.
