# Fixed-rho occurrence routing preserves the retained node tariff

**Scoped positive conclusion.** The constructed routes need no extra K-plane
sweep inside a retained complex-layer invocation. The caller supplies one rho;
native columns and recursive children keep that rho. Under the retained
current-volume, compact-primitive, record-fiber and ownership contracts, a
fixed finite routing word changes the constant in the ordinary node tariff.
The source already charges the separate outer bit-plane rounds. This is
asymptotic accounting, not a numeric final-word bill or a new primitive proof.
All five selected assumptions remain.

## 1. The actual caller requests one plane

The opening and formal proposition of `upstream/build/sections/05-layers.tex`
specify `[P] x [2^K]^D x [S]`, one common offset rho, and D kernels on exactly
those D bits, fixing every other address bit. The header contains rho and the
bound is uniform over rho. Its phase frames define f columns as the selected
bits x_(h,j) in m slots of f K-bit chunks. An n-axis monomial lifted to f columns
therefore acts on n*f selected bits, not n*f*K bits. Other chunk bits remain
complete spectators.

The compact replacement retains this ABI. Its positions are j_i=rho+i*K.
One primitive handles all f positions at that rho and restores the remaining
bits and controls, at `O(V*((f*log p)^tau+1))` in its stated domain. Three XORs
exchange two selected f-bit vectors, not their whole K-bit chunks. The selected
monomial lift and final4610 specialization use this grammar and phase
`a*f+sum_j q(x_j)`.

This distinction is observable: with K=2, Hadamard on bit0 sends a basis vector
at00 to support {00,01}; Hadamards on both planes give {00,01,10,11}. An inner
sweep changes the requested endpoint and its spectators.

## 2. Closure through the occurrence grammar

Fix the full finite uniform template first, with rank N, width W and paid
rank-one count P. Its compiler occurrences and coordinate lists are fixed data.
For e=N*f+r, r<N, a logical block permutation moves only positions
`(a*f+j)*K+rho`, a<N, j<f, inside the admitted active interval. It extends by
identity to every other address bit.

Whole-field pre/post conjugation aligns native scalar contacts in the common
selected-bit chart. No instruction requires the other K-1 planes to be moved.
Product transposes permute selected axes of the (a,b) rectangle. Product children
keep rho and change column counts. Completion adds disjoint selected axes;
splitting partitions their list. Source complements, exits and the fewer than N
ordinary remainder kernels inherit rho. Each inverse route returns before the
parent resumes. This is the retained selected-bit ABI, not a full-chunk ABI.

Reading K, computing positions and scanning O(e) selected bits are polynomial
per-record descriptor operations, already paid by the superpolynomial record
fiber. They are not K full-volume executions of the native program.

## 3. A complete finite syntactic ordinary bill

Let T be the emitted finite template, including original bodies, new adapters,
owned operations and returns. Count instruction occurrences, not unique matrix
names. Expand the compiler substitutions in T before lowering ordinary rank-one
leaves to f-axis children. The following sum defines a new allowance; no raw
10^9 cap is reused.

For each coordinate wrapper omega, let t_omega count its entry transpositions.
Its reverse word is the return. Charge exactly 6*t_omega selected-bit-addition
records, with t_omega<=n_omega-1 and n_omega<=N. Sum over every role occurrence,
including repeated wrappers at different paid positions.

For each whole-field role permutation eta, let s_eta count its transpositions.
On aligned equal-shape fields a swap uses three signed scalar ADDs (coefficients +1, -1, +1) and
one negation:
`(x,y)->(x+y,y)->(x+y,-x)->(y,-x)->(y,x)`.
Charge four scalar scans per transposition, subject to the retained same-chart
and owner contract. Required alignment wrappers are in the coordinate sum.
An alternative tape stream-swap must contribute its actual scan list instead.

Also count every original and new binary-monomial row addition/translation;
all ADD/SCALE/COPY and phase applications; every INIT/CLEAR and owned scan;
park/load/reorder/return scans; and descriptor construction, push, inverse lookup
and restoration. Movement scratch and cleanup use the retained primitive bill.
A finite multiset can be encoded by a sum/product expression without dropping
occurrences. Let A be the address records, L scalar/phase applications, S stream
and owned scans, and D descriptor/phase-setup events. The 6*t and 4*s terms are
included in these multisets, not counted twice.

For each event z, let v_z be its fixed current-volume multiplier: its stream
has volume at most v_z*V at an ordinary node of logical volume V. These constants
come from the retained productive/owned/parking contract, not address geometry
alone. Let k_z be its admitted primitive constant. An address event uses c_z*f
selected columns, with c_z*f<=e<=d<=p, so it costs at most

```
v_z*k_z*V*((c_z*f*G)^tau+1) <= v_z*k_z*V*((e*G)^tau+1),
G=4*ceil(log2 p)+6,  0<tau<1.
```

Every L or S event contributes v_z*k_z*V in the retained coefficient format.
Every D event costs a fixed polynomial in the O(p)-bit descriptor per complete
record, plus node setup. Sum these finitely many polynomials first. The retained
superpolynomial record fiber eventually exceeds that sum, making their combined
cost at most V after increasing the common cutoff. Thus

```
C_T = 1 + sum_(z in A union L union S) v_z*k_z
```

is an explicit symbolic finite ordinary allowance, with the descriptor cutoff
just specified. The full node bill is `C_T*V*((e*G)^tau+1)`. Full-volume scans
are counted in C_T; only polynomial per-record descriptor work is absorbed by
the record fiber. The permutation-only increment obeys
`|A_added|=6*sum_omega t_omega<=6*(N-1)*R_pair`, but this term alone is not a
complete added bill. R_pair and the event sum need not be numerically emitted.

This is **the same node-tariff shape as the retained compact-control and mixed
complex-layer recurrences**. Address permutations add no positive-rank children,
so paid/productive ratios, child widths, stopping rule and moments are unchanged.
One must reuse the already-selected strict exponent inequalities, replacing
the ordinary constant and its eventual cutoff. No new chi or lambda is chosen
and no numerical raw10^9 allowance is asserted for the expanded program.

The selected specialization is already proved in #6115,
`research/p104-6030-deferred-layer/PROOF.md` sections2--5. It uses the compiled
uniform tree, not the raw P104 histogram. With its notation
`delta=1/1400000000`, `sigma=1-b`, `tau=1-a`, `eta=stop=delta`, and
`lambda_prime=1-q`, it certifies
`mu_sigma<2^-47005` and `mu_tau<2^-94010`.
These supplier moments are untouched by rank-zero routing.

Here tau is #6115's retained node exponent. The underlying movement primitive
may have a smaller admitted exponent; increasing an exponent on e*G>=1 only
weakens its upper bound. This note assumes the same admitted primitive tariff
used by that node premise, rather than substituting a historical BIT exponent.

Its internal-node premise is `c_N*Lambda(p)*v*e^tau`, including remainders,
parking, ownership and monomials. Add the newly emitted event sum C_added
(the submultiset of the complete bill above). Since e>=1, the increment is at
most `2*C_added*G^tau*v*e^tau`. Replace Lambda by a fixed polylogarithm also
covering G^tau and replace c_N by c_N+2*C_added. This directly instantiates
#6115's existing implication, with its same selected exponents, moments,
stopping and deferred-phase schedule. No new tree summation is claimed. Recompute the compiled owned/parking
inventory, descriptor allowance and finite product-stock constants as needed
for these added operations, using the retained stock laws and fresh cutoffs.
No historical numerical stock constant is reused merely because W/P and
its moment exponents are unchanged. Any changed prefix-norm, dyadic-height,
precision and record-width constants must also be substituted through the
admitted precision interface. The ordinary event tariff does not establish
rounded numerical closure or complete machine admission.
Its cutoff is s=max(N,h^eta); an internal e>s has floor(e/N)>=1.
Smaller widths use the retained elementary leaf contract.

The selected deferred transfer uses K=128G, not the old power-K reservation
schedule. In particular it does not literally assume K/log p tends to infinity.
The compact movement proof gives the explicit sufficient guard
`K>=G+4*ceil(log2 p)+10`. With L=ceil(log2 p)>=1 and G=4L+6,
K=128G satisfies this guard because 127*(4L+6)>=4L+10. More explicitly,
the proof bounds its bad fraction by
`delta<=n*(2*2^(-G)+8*2^(G-K))`, n=f-1<=p. The first term is at most
1/(32*p^3); the guard makes the second at most 1/(128*p^3). Hence
`delta<=5/(128*p^3)`. With A<=C_A*p, the repair term
`delta*M*A*(R+A)` is O(V/p^2), where V=M*R, and `M*A^3=o(V)` because R
exceeds fixed powers of p. Rotations retain their record-paid polynomial
setup; the compact swaps have width n*G and therefore the same
`O(V*((f*log p)^tau+1))` bound. These are the actual proof steps: the stronger
limit K/log p->infinity is not used once this explicit guard is supplied.
This extends that written implication to K=128G under the same f<=p,
A=O(p), record-fiber and complete-control hypotheses; it does not claim the
original proposition literally has that domain or admit a new physical repair
implementation. #6115's deferred stock and support
router premises remain exactly as retained, rather than importing the obsolete
eager reservation term or a new positive power of d for K.

For an explicit eventual absorption, given a certified finite C>=1, q>=0 and
strict power gap nu>0, it suffices to put x=log2 p and require

```
x >= max(16, (2*q/nu)^2, 2*ceil(log2(2*C))/nu).
```

Indeed log2(x)<=sqrt(x) for x>=16, so
`log2(2*C)+q*log2(x)<=nu*x`, proving `C*(log2 p)^q<=p^nu/2`.
The selected strict gaps supply nu after the fixed outer width substitution.
This gives a symbolic new threshold, not a materialized numeric one or a
license to absorb K into a fixed constant.

## 4. The outer plane schedule is already paid

`upstream/build/sections/06-transforms.tex` sets b=(ell-1) mod K and
q=floor((ell-1)/K). Main rounds h=ell-2,...,b supply
`chunk_index=floor((h-b)/K)` and `rho=(h-b) mod K` to the layer header.
These schedule identities use only 1<=K<=ell-1, so they carry through the
retained outer transfer without literally instantiating the old transform
theorem's power-K parameter restrictions at K=128G. The q*K pairs enumerate
every chunk/plane pair once. These are separate outer
rounds with their transform/twiddle semantics, not an inner sweep.

The source charges O(T*p*d^lambda') per round and O(T*p*ell*d^lambda') in total.
The at most b+1<=K prefix rounds and outer whole-chunk layout moves retain their
separate O(T*p*d*K) and O(T*p*p*K^(tau-1)) terms. This note does not remove them.
Multiplying the per-layer route cost by K again double-counts the plane index
and implements a different inner operation. The actual source schedule, not
absorption into a constant, excludes that multiplier.

## 5. Evidence, limitations and credit

`rho_tariff.py` authenticates exact sources and caller fragments, then enumerates
schedules for 2<=ell<=70 and 1<=K<ell. It checks chunk/plane coverage,
prefix/main counts, selected-bit permutations on coordinate basis vectors,
a whole-chunk counterexample and an exact K=2 endpoint witness. An integer
fixture checks the full ordinary event sum and four-scan role swap. Finite
checks corroborate this argument; they do not prove the all-size primitive tariff.

A later selected emitter must preserve rho through every child and retain the
complete event sum. An emitter looping over K planes violates the contract.
Numeric final constants, row dispatch/backing, compact repair, rounded precision
and analytic recovery are unverified here. No selected source, kappa or
assumption status is changed.
