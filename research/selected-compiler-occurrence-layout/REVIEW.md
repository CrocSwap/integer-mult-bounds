# Independent review of occurrence geometry

Prepared for @CubeJerry with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. This is a
separate source-aware AI review, not human or proof-kernel acceptance. The
reviewer did not implement or import the package's source/layout verifier.
The independent checker was written from the proposed theorem and retained
product/compiler interfaces. No selected native word or final uniform template
was materialized.

## Conclusion and corrected findings

The coordinate-extension and composition argument is sound under its stated
native, whole-job, chronological, ownership and compact-primitive premises.
It constructs occurrence-dependent coordinate choices and charges their
movement. It does not remove the complete native or compiler assumption:
raw semantic admission, exact occurrence/word counts, descriptor production,
physical owned backing, row dispatch, precision and analytic closure remain.

Two substantive wording corrections were requested and incorporated: three XORs
swap the f selected axes at a **fixed rho**, not all f*K chunk bits at that price;
and every unit occurrence needs its complete owned-bank inventory, not p7's
one-center convention. For the selected local word, 31 lifetime fields are a
conservative choice; peak-13 reuse needs its retained lifetime argument.
Complete caller/product nesting must count every invocation of that inventory.

## Reconstructed argument

Let the admitted multi-field unit have endpoint `diag(G_(k_i*c) tensor I)`
on its productive fields and its prescribed owned entrance/return. Choose
complete coordinate permutations P_i extending the requested ordered supports.
Applying `diag(P_i^-1)` before the **whole** unit and `diag(P_i)` afterward
gives `diag(P_i G_(k_i*c) P_i^-1)`. This holds on arbitrary full values,
including correlated inputs and spectators. All scalar ADDs execute in the
common internal chart. Changing paid-kernel supports alone is insufficient.

In product coordinates (a,b), a common U paid position requests `a<r,b<h_j`
on the V-role owner fields. V at multiplicity r has precisely these endpoints
and fits `a<r,b<n` since r<=m. The traversal-order permutation must return
before U resumes. Distinct owner fields and common-position chronology are
retained premises, not consequences of rectangle sizes. After return, completion
on [K,L) and disjoint splitting preserve the stated endpoint. Repeating a split
axis is invalid. This argument does not tensor a scalar sum as an operator.

For each compiler occurrence, m*c<=N permits extending its k*c endpoint list
to a complete ambient list. The common-chart conjugation then applies. Smaller
source complements and exits terminate by the retained precompilation theorem.
This review checks the geometry implication; it does not independently reprove
all Pascal matching, batching or rank descent. Ordered exit batches cannot be
pooled merely because ranks coincide. The package's clearing factor divisible
by every required smaller width avoids that ambiguity.

Whole-row division is a bijection preserving within-row coordinates. The product
of unreleased divisors must divide the declared path stock; individual size
comparisons are insufficient. Extra labels do not initialize arbitrary rows to
zero or supply owned backing. Only W original arrays receive productive credit.

The raw265/145 reduction uses the source's unused-spectator deletion statement
and actual active-stage support. Complete old canonicalizers cannot simply be
restricted: re-emission on the smaller full space is necessary. The primary
checker binds those source supports and phases. The independent dense tests
below use an exact toy word, not P104 native coefficients.

## Independent executable evidence

`independent_check.py` acts on every integer basis input and compares **all**
output coefficients, including zeros. Its two-field local word is
`y+=x; H_A(x); H_A(y); y-=x; H_B(y)` for disjoint A,B. Its endpoint is H_A
on x and H_(A union B) on y. Scalar contacts expose wrong chart choices.
The self-product substitutes this literal word at each common paid position;
expected endpoints are separately computed Cartesian coordinate subsets.
The full-field permutation oracle is exhaustive search, not stable-prefix code.

Executed checks:

- f=1, four fields, six axes: 65,536 product coefficients, including two
  arbitrary spectator axes.
- f=2, four fields, eight axes: 1,048,576 product coefficients.
- Completed product with four different whole-field conjugations: another
  65,536 coefficients, including spectator fibers.
- Two-column raw and singleton-split calls: 4,096 coefficients each.
- A doubly nested child transform followed by a parent transform, on all 64
  address basis vectors, and exact inverse-order restoration.
- 21 selected-bit swap cases over f in {1,2,3}, K in {1,2,4}, every rho;
  independent coordinate basis masks check unselected and control bits.
- All 36 rows split through divisors (2,3,2), with no loss or cloning.

Ten negative controls reject: output-only routing; changed internal supports
without whole conjugation; wrong product coordinate order; overlapping splits;
wrong LIFO return; field alias; support alias; full-chunk swap at fixed-rho price;
maximum instead of simultaneous product stock; omitted repeated path divisor.
These bounded witnesses do not test selected native admission.

Normal and optimized Python3.12.14 runs produced byte-identical JSON. Explicit
exceptions remain active with -O. The saved result is INDEPENDENT-RESULT.json.
Commands from the package: `python3 -I -B independent_check.py` and
`python3 -I -O -B independent_check.py`.
