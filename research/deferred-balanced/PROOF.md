## Sharper moment enclosures

For a complete child histogram n_t, use

    M(a)=Σ_t n_t t/(mW) · exp(a log(m/t)).

The retained PR65 enclosure computes log through a rational atanh series with a geometric tail and directed rounding. For 0≤u≤v<1, the degree-eight Taylor polynomial at u is a lower bound. At v, the first omitted term divided by 1−v/10 bounds the tail, because every subsequent term ratio is at most v/10. Directed rounding to denominator 10^40 preserves these inequalities.

The independent audit uses reduction by 3/2, sixty atanh terms, and a degree-twelve exponential series with twice the first omitted term as its upper tail. Its logarithm interval must lie inside the primary interval. Both methods prove the accepted moment below one and reject the next 10^-18 saving grid point. Function bodies are preserved without changes from PR65 snapshots and checked against them.

For the bit histogram this gives

The stronger complex moment is also audited, but the assembly deliberately retains PR97's already certified b=36926111/500000000000. This separates the improvement from any changed complex-network saving. The complete paid histograms are the inputs, not a lower cost inferred from a heuristic.

## Balanced positional transfer

The stronger result additionally invokes the balanced physical layout retained in `../copied-fixed/PROOF.md`, its pinned PR34/RaD sources, and `balanced_assembly.py`. This is an extra inherited construction, not an algebraic omission of the old prefix work.

Process the extra top bit of each long FFT axis individually. Use common named low-coordinate groups with widths K_j∈[K,2K), rather than repeating each full prefix separately for every group. The resulting extra work is O(Tpd), with margin 1−ε. The full axis-major/group-major permutation and its inverse are performed by the paid arbitrary-coordinate router. Bulk Gaussian gathers and inverses retain the bit saving a. Whole active, control, row and spectator fields remain complete, with FFT level/twiddle order and forward/inverse alignment preserved. The geometric inequality 1−ε(1+c)>0 remains necessary even though it is not this layout's prefix-work cost margin.

PR97 supplies the interfaces this outer layout requires. Its bit word implements the complete shear on ordinary and arbitrary dirty inputs and has the stated full paid profile. Its corrected signed complex endpoint proof gives the same completed C^tensor-u contract on every returned role, including scratch and temporary copies. Thus the semantic row bound and C1=1 apply to completed children. Coordinate routing carries every phase/address descriptor with its complete field; it does not erase the translation or discard a signed correction. The changed local arities 529 and 576 enter the regenerated halving and row-stock calculation, not the outer FFT regrouping argument. No local gate is reordered or crossed by this transfer.

## Provenance and limits

The original PR97 manifest is preserved as `pr97-source-manifest.json`. Four unavailable historical log artifacts are reproduced by running their unchanged scripts; fresh timings, hashes and exit statuses are preserved. Only their manifest entries may change, and no mathematical input or implementation byte may change. A passing new log is not represented as the missing original log when its time-stamped bytes differ.

All-size ordered-frame compilation, common-basis existence, effective setup, routing, exact recovery, fixed alphabet/tape simulation and analytic transfer remain inherited hypotheses. The finite checks support the explicit instance and interface conditions; they do not prove those hypotheses. There is no claim of global optimality, a new formal Lean theorem or practical speedup.
