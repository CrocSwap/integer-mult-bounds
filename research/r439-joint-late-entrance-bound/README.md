# Joint late entrances: at most one independent direction on PR589

**Scoped negative result; no new kappa.** The two helper-entrance supports that
survive PR1932's individual late-compensation test cannot be used together for
two independent elevated helper inputs. The complete common-first-contact
support domain therefore admits **joint rank at most one**, even allowing
arbitrary monotone post-scatter linear compensation and restored dirty relays
under the stated boundary contract.

The supports are `{444,470}` and `{444,470,578}`. At eight targets their required
joint response is `(0,1)`, but every accessible source-free helper value gives
only `(0,0)` or `(1,1)`. The dual witness `(1,1)` separates them. In particular,
separate per-column acceptance misses the obstruction.

This answers the explicitly unrun simultaneous-entrance test in Sagar Thakrar's
[PR1932](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/1932), pinned at
`d5e6ac0e442a1acfd0ac933af90a2adfb3417138`. **The single-triple construction
remains open.** This is neither a new bit circuit nor a general obstruction to
changed first contacts, source donors, new COPY operations or changed A.

Together with the rank ceiling, the most favorable local saving is at most one
unit of initial helper-climb rank mass. The known PR1179 kernel entrance already
