# Research status: retired

Retired at Douglas Colkitt's direction on October 8, 2026.

This status applies to the clean aggregate-producer branch with distinct
rank-one source frames, central outputs delivered at frame zero, and a
separately charged output readout. It is a research-direction decision,
not deletion of a Git branch or withdrawal of an established result.

The [follow-up report](REPORT.md) establishes the scoped obstruction:
producer rank at least n plus separate readout rank n(h-1) exhausts the
useful rank capacity nh. Sharing and cancellation within this interface
do not restore a contracting recurrence. No stronger kappa was established.

Do not resume parameter tuning, larger producer searches, or output-merger
optimization under this unchanged contract. The earlier hypothetical
headroom in the [design session](../hybrid-clean-design/REPORT.md) is closed
by this follow-up; it is not an outstanding construction target.

A proposal worth reconsidering must explicitly change the central-frame
interface, share physical work between production and readout so the
separate charges no longer apply, or replace the frame/child contract with
a proved implementation. It must specify the complete operation and charge
all transitions. No such proposal is currently selected.

Reports, code, certificates and validation records remain preserved as
historical evidence. This retirement makes no claim of a general barrier
to integer multiplication and changes no published bound.
