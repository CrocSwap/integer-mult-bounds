Exact bit operators and compressed ambient space

Over an admitted odd address field, the E8 form is G=I_8/2. For a nondegenerate
subspace U, write P_U for its orthogonal projector. On the doubled address
space define
  D_U = [[I-P_U, P_U], [P_U, I-P_U]].
Thus D_U is an exact involution. If U is contained in V then
D_V D_U = D_(V intersect U-perp), with rank dim(V)-dim(U). Also
  D_I D_P = D_(I-P).
These are literal address matrices. Unlike the complex C operators, they
have no hidden scalar phase. The auditor reconstructs all 944 rational
projectors and checks all 3289 used nested pairs, in both orientations.

Set V_global = Q^8 direct_sum (Q^7)^8, m=64, with form I_64/2.
Let E_s be the first s exterior 7-blocks, s=0,...,8. At stage s lift the
entire local word by replacing U with U direct_sum E_s. Both ends of every
local transition contain the same E_s, so it cancels exactly; the child
projector and rank are unchanged. This holds for copied totals as well:
the temporary transforms between the source and scatter frames, whose
common E_s cancels. COPY/ERASE windows remain sequential and immutable.
No assumption that m is a multiple of 9h is used.

Port routes

The reference root is q*=2e_0. For every root q, the checked product g_q
of at most two E8 root reflections sends q* to plus or minus q. Let A_q be
its last seven columns. They form an orthonormal basis of q-perp:
  A_q^T A_q=I_7, A_q A_q^T=I_8-P_q, P_q=q q^T/4.
For stage s>0 use, on the local block and exterior block s,
  rho_(s,q) = [[P_q, A_q], [A_q^T, 0]],
and fix all other coordinates. For s=0 use the identity. These matrices
are orthogonal involutions. Conjugating the lifted endpoints gives
  (P_q,E_s) -> (I_8,E_(s-1)),    (I_8,E_s) -> (I_8,E_s),
  (0,E_s)   -> (I_8-P_q,E_(s-1)),
  (I_8-P_q,E_s) -> (I_8-P_q,E_s).
These equalities are checked as exact rational matrices for every port.
Each nontrivial route has a replayed elementary factorization, at most
77 factors, with denominators 1,2,4. Omitting a factor fails. Thus all
routes remain defined at every odd address prime; there is no new odd
exceptional prime. The existing local prime witnesses are rerun separately.

Nine-stage scalar recipe and deferred endpoint completion

Use four data pairs (X_j,Y_j). Start with F_0: Y_0 += X_0. For j=0,...,3,
apply B_j: X_j -= Y_j; when j<3 insert the two shears
  X_j += X_(j+1), Y_(j+1) -= Y_j;
apply F_j; then undo those two shears. After all stages, for j=2,1,0 apply
  X_(j+1) += X_j, Y_j -= Y_(j+1).
Finally replace (X_j,Y_j) by (Y_j,-X_j). The complete signed integer
8-by-8 scalar matrix is the identity. Omitting any of the eighteen
individual bridge shears changes it.

Each B_j is the reflected inverse of the actual local record stream:
reverse the order, negate ADD coefficients, replace U by U-perp, and
turn ERASE into a fresh COPY from the original unchanged center. No erase
is inverted. PR #370 stores zero in the ERASE rank field; the reflected
COPY rank is freshly derived from its two frames, giving rank 8. Both
oriented streams replay all 1105 independent input columns and close work.

Before each bridge the newly used pair is advanced to the active level.
The two idle increments are (14,7), (28,7), (42,7). Endpoint completion of
the earlier pairs is deferred to the final stage, with ranks 42,28,14,0.
Every increment acts on both members of every port pair and every replica.
The schedule checker requires equal complete frames at every bridge.
The signed terminal exchange completes the data transform using the exact
identity D_I D_P=D_(I-P). Scalar replay on 8745 independent columns includes
960 data and 7785 separate stage-helper inputs. All are restored after
the exchange; the reflected copy work closes to zero.

Whole-block banks and arbitrary correlated dirt

Use eight replicas. There are 4*2*120*8=7680 data families. For each stage,
number a local helper occurrence by z=865*replica+helper, and assign it
to bank floor(z/8), slot z mod 8. There are 865 banks at each of nine
stages. Each slot is one whole 8-dimensional coordinate block in Q^64.
The eight slots are mutually perpendicular and fill the ambient space;
no padding child is needed. Live recursion stock is 7680+9*865=15465.
Nine private center slots are reserved and excluded from recursion volume,
so charged physical stock is 15474. Only one such slot is used at a time.

Let Pi_slot exchange the first eight coordinates with its bank block.
For a cover class d in O(I_64/2,F_q), the helper owner is the bank class
g=d Pi_slot^-1, and a data owner is d rho_(s,q). Right multiplication is a
bijection of the finite cover. Within every local invocation, helpers in
the same bank have different slots, hence different actual cover classes;
all data and work families are disjoint from banks. All 62280 assignments
are checked, and every physical bank receives each of its eight slots once.

The local all-column theorem permits arbitrary helper inputs, including
correlations created by earlier invocations. After its endpoint each helper
has only the known residual D_H. The common E_s cancels, so no cleanliness
assumption is imposed by a previous bank action. Complete cover sweeps run
sequentially. At a fixed bank owner g the residuals are D on the eight
orthogonal coordinate blocks conjugated by g. They commute and multiply
to D_I. Since diag(g,g) commutes with the literal head/tail exchange D_I,
no complex-style cover phase correction is needed on the bit side.
This is a symbolic finite cover proof, not an enumeration of O(64,F_q).

Finite cost, precision allowance and two-level halving

The largest child is 42, so one-level halving is false. Two levels suffice:
  2*42^2=3528 < 64^2=4096.
Floors only reduce descendant widths. The exact one-level moment, including
the inherited 10^-16 bad-branch envelope, is strictly below one; composing
two levels squares this contraction. The finite composite may be used with
the inherited halving-based compiler and direct small leaves.

The old five-stage 104-bit payload-prefix guard is not reused. Fresh local
cancellation-free row bounds are 742 forward and 6308 reverse. For five
forward calls, four reflected calls and eighteen bridge shears, the bound
  2^18 * 742^5 * 6308^4
has 117 bits. We explicitly charge 192 bits per nine-stage block. The actual
source operations, copy episodes, 77-factor routes, selectors, all owner
adapters and this prefix bound enter certify.py's finite coefficient. It
has 117 bits; the charged two-level coefficient has 269 bits and is bounded
by 2^512. This deliberately increases the finite cutoff, rather than passing
the old 104-bit assertion. The exact three bootstrap cutoff inequalities
are checked using the changed coefficient.

For cover/routing enumeration use the inherited coarse q^(m^2+1) bound;
two levels and the retained additive row charges give
  2*(64^2+1)+9909+252 = 18355 < 20161.
The explicit rational bit routes introduce only powers of two. The new
finite constants fit well below the companion bridge's 2^30000 reserve,
and the common external C0=2^210000 and row allowance 20161 are retained.
As for the companion complex component, the general compiler/common-grid
interfaces are assumptions; no new Lean formalization is asserted.
