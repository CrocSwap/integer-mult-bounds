## Ordered leaves and the weighted suffix proof

The preliminary source-complement phase followed by depth-first lockstep
paid-site calls is permitted by retained FINITE-PRECOMPILATION sections 3--4.
The earlier complementary coordinates commute with the source job; whole
child calls must complete before parent resumption. The new theorem explicitly
fixes this permitted order instead of claiming a canonical arbitrary native
word. It indexes its chronological projection onto base paid leaves only.

At deepest height G, every ordered paid prefix is present: its capacity is
at most A r_max^G, bounded by A k_min^G <= n. The zero target point then
has multiplicity one. Consequently an exit cut is the full length-G+1 paid
word, not a rank or owner. Each individual cut contributes a/B_e whole calls
of P_e leaves. The recurrence H(h,rho) is a finite ordered-tree suffix count;
merging equal products is safe for arithmetic because the selection still
visits every distinct paid site in its original order. Repeated equal ranks
retain their multiplicities. Product coincidences do not merge paths.

The complement intervals have whole-call widths P_(n-s), and each exit
subinterval has whole-call width P_e. The exact supplied leaf recurrence
proves coverage without gaps or overlaps. Successive subtraction selects
one ordered interval and divmod selects one invocation/local ordinal. The
inverse sums those same preceding intervals. Strong induction on the
strictly smaller child rank establishes full path/ordinal inversion.

The code implements this proof: `prefixes` is the sorted complement order;
`locate` subtracts actual paid-site intervals; `step_data` reconstructs the
before-call offset from actual preceding sites and invocation; `rank_leaf`
adds these offsets from inside out. Its field check is additional identity
validation, not a replacement for the path. Base rank n<A contributes n
canonical axis leaves on its width-one program. Global axis embeddings
remain a supplied adapter premise, clearly separated from these indices.

## Owner composition and exact injection scope

For one complement invocation, the inherited port inverse maps the consecutive
B_e slots to distinct parent source rows. For one exit invocation, consecutive
slots yield distinct replica numbers b=v B_e+i. The complete owner includes
that replica and is checked against the qualified cut; equality of rank,
value, or unqualified owner is never used to recover an exit.

Upward resolution has two cases. A productive source is converted to the
parent source-row index and continues. An already owned field stops at the
parent that actually allocated it, carrying that parent's call-prefix tag.
This avoids relabelling it as newly allocated in a child. The root namespace
must distinguish simultaneously active roots, as the theorem explicitly
requires. The internal tag also retains unit path, point, ordinal and bank
name, so distinct physical banks do not merge under equal numerical ranks.

Induction gives injectivity among the productive ports of **one whole
invocation**: immediate source/exit maps are injective; inherited productive
rows are injective; internal banks are disjoint from those rows and each
other. It does not give global leaf-to-owner injectivity. The documented
two-sites-on-one-role counterexample correctly refutes that stronger claim.
Ordered child return uses the same port correspondence. No payload scatter,
parking, copy or bank erasure follows for free from this logical identity.

## Descent, size and cost review

A positive complement is below (A-1)n/A. Every exit is at most
r_max k_min^G <= r_max n/A <= (A-1)n/A. Since ((A-1)/A)^A < 1/2 for A>=2,
the stated A ceil(log2(N+1)) bound safely limits the number of call edges.
The proof appropriately treats A as fixed for fixed-rule polynomial claims;
it does not describe this factor as polynomial in log A.

The Z cap includes all supplied library integer lengths, role/site lists,
namespace and path digits. Source counts are bounded by B_n, positive suffix
counts/offsets by P_n, and operand widths needed by intermediate products and
divisions fit its stated O(Z) allowance. Actual huge replication is therefore
not a unit-cost hidden parameter. A path requires O(DZ) bits.

The new suffix levels have at most binom(G+q+1,q) product records. Sorting
and comparison costs are included, rather than relying on expected hash
behavior. Source setup, bucket replacement and queries explicitly use the
retained predecessor envelopes. Per-node setup adds the new weighted levels,
bottom quotients and complement intervals. Per-query work covers each rank's
source translation, paid-site scans, group scans and path copies. Repeated
`step_data` and `validate_calls` traversals in owner resolution fit the
conservative D/path terms; they are not hidden constant-time operations.

The complete bound includes O(NZ) initialization of all N supplied rows and
the N-entry cache. Retained cache space permits all N source/node tables,
not merely those touched by the first query. The predecessor table envelope
includes transient replacement storage; new level and suffix storage is
covered by the additional (q+1)HZ term. The serialized companion explicitly
replaces logical metadata access with full scans/copies and O(L^2) local
actions, retaining historical space after erasure. This is a constructive
loose simulation envelope, not a literal state table or a multiplier tariff.

The general interface assumes a valid evaluated library; its recurrence is
checked as new nodes are built. The unchanged generator's sharper bounds
are imported only at A=k_max. No transfer to arbitrary A or the selected
153^1920 instance is claimed. These distinctions are essential and present.

## Separately written direct recursion oracle

The new `direct_recursion.py` specializes to one productive role of rank two,
three ordered rank-one sites on `(x, bank, x)`, and ambient A=3 or A=5.
Thus A exceeds all target ranks. This family has one fresh source at
s=2^(G+1), one positive complement e=n-s, and 3^(G+1) qualified exits.
It is simple enough to derive expected answers without any source codec.

Rather than adopting the author's minimal library, the oracle chooses
`t_n=1+(n mod 3)` and `a_n=t_n B_e`, with `B_n=a_n` and
`P_n=t_n P_e+a_n 3^(G+1)`. This is a valid, often nonminimal supplied library.
Direct recursion first slices the explicit current source vector into t_n
consecutive whole child blocks. It then literally enumerates all ternary
paid words and replicas; each bank site changes the carried field to that
unit's explicit new bank, while x sites preserve it. It never asks the
production resolver for an expected owner. Expected inverse descriptors are
constructed directly, not copied from the implementation's locate result.

The executed domains are every rank 1--36 at A=3 and every rank 1--18 at A=5:

- 24,137 complete leaf locations, full paths, base axes and physical fields;
- 24,205 whole-call occurrences, including root invocations;
- 25,120 input ports, including 272 invocations of width greater than one;
- 16,376 owned-field leaves, including 4,988 with a nonempty allocating-call
  namespace (nested bank namespaces, rather than a claim of every possible
  parent-owned-to-recursive-child configuration);
- all eight source pins and three independently inserted semantic mutants.

The mutants lose the complement invocation number, rotate the base axis,
or omit the allocating call prefix. Each is rejected by direct expected
leaf/field mismatch. Equal-owner/different-instruction behavior is retained;
per-call port vectors are separately checked for injectivity.
