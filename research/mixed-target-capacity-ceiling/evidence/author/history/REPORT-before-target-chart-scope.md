## Every negative endpoint term has Fourier rank at most 16

For a one-column Clifford let its Fourier rank be the rank of its symplectic
upper-right block, equivalently `17-dim(T L0 intersect L0)`. Monomial left or
right multiplication preserves this number. A genuine H17 has rank 17.

The reviewed first-three-control argument and finite tensor separation place
each surviving letter k of t in the transported class Ht. Its monomial binary
linear block R preserves W and `span(q)+W`. Consequently `R^-T` preserves U,
the orthogonal complement of `span(q)+W`.

A nonzero expanded negative endpoint term has the precise order

    T = Q^-1 N3 k N1 D1 D3,
    N3=K3_i^-1 K3_r,   N1=K1_s^-1 K1_j,

with source coefficient containing `A[i,r] A[s,j]`. This follows from
`Q^-1 G=D1 D3`; no Q-order-two replacement is used.

The pure Z vector `(0,q)` belongs to `D1 D3 L0`, since those factors omit q.
The signed orthogonal N1 carries it to `(0,a)`, where

    a=G1_s^-1 q_j,
    q dot a = q_s dot q_j = 0.

Thus a is a nonzero vector entirely in U. The monomial k carries this vector
to `(0,R^-T a)`, still a nonzero pure Z vector in U. N3 acts only on q+W and
therefore fixes it. Q^-1 likewise fixes every pure Z vector in U. Hence
`T L0 intersect L0` contains a nonzero vector, and the Fourier rank of T is at
most 16. This holds for every nonzero complete term, before cancellation.

This proof uses only the right source edge; it does not assert that transported
k fixes q. For scalar-owner t0, the stronger two-edge calculation gives rank
15: up to outer monomials the Fourier block is `I+v v^T+q q^T`, with v and q
distinct orthogonal odd vectors. The general rank-16 bound is the one used.

## Arbitrary FREE input encoding cannot create more than 112 H17 jobs

This is an image-dimension argument over the full FREE algebra, not scalar
coefficient rank. At sufficiently large column width f, take B to be the
complex span of the lifted projective monomial Clifford group H on 17 bits.
H is finite. Tensor separation makes its projective representatives linearly
independent; its multiplication retains the scalar cocycle raised to f.
Thus B is a finite-dimensional twisted group algebra. Write `d=dim_C B`.

Similarly, the finite projective Clifford group gives an ambient operator
space with distinct projective classes independent at sufficiently large f.
Let pi17 retain exactly the classes of Fourier rank 17. Since left and right
monomial multiplication preserves Fourier rank, pi17 is a B-bimodule map.
Width-dependent outer coefficients do not affect this support statement.

Let U be any FREE input encoder and L any FREE output readout. On the main
input columns the proposed output is `L M U+B0`, where B0 is any saved-input
FREE bypass. The preceding lemma kills the entire negative bank under pi17;
B0 has grade zero and also vanishes. The positive bank contribution factors
as

    B^224 --U_positive--> B^112 --diag(G)--> ambient^112
          --t and positive readout--> ambient^output.

Its image therefore has complex dimension at most 112d, irrespective of the
number of clean buffers, copies or readout rows. No claim that the raw physical
data dimension itself is d is being made; the inputs here are the FREE algebra
module, after the justified coefficient separation.

Suppose m original inputs receive independent H17 outputs. Select their m rows
and their matching original input columns. The desired projected map on B^m
is diagonal in their actual rank-17 Gaussian operators G_j. Each G_j is an

## Extension: arbitrary monomial letters with fixed complete-product support

This extension reuses the separately reviewed partition lemma in
`outputs/nonlinear-monomial-control-ceiling-20261023/PROOF.md` and its review
`outputs/nonlinear-monomial-control-review-20261023/REVIEW.md`. The predecessor
does not itself prove this changed-input/changed-target result.

Let H now be the group of all invertible monomial matrices on the actual
17-bit one-column address space. It includes arbitrary nonlinear permutations
and arbitrary nonzero complex weights. Every proposed control, input encoder,
readout and bypass has a fixed finite set of complete one-column products in
its expansion, independent of width f; its outer scalar coefficients may
depend on f. A finite generating alphabet with unbounded width-dependent word
length is insufficient. The native paths, source and Gaussian target contracts
remain exactly those above.

**Step 1: transport of the join partition.** Finite tensor separation applied
to the first three memberships again puts each surviving h of t0 in

    h monomial, Q^-1 h Q monomial, k=D3 h D3^-1 monomial.

The predecessor partition lemma shows that the address permutation of h
preserves both the q-coset and W-coset partitions. Therefore it preserves their
join, the `(q+W)`-coset partition indexed by U. Since D3 and its inverse act
inside these join blocks, k preserves the same join partition. Its permutation
may act nonlinearly on U; it still induces a bijection of those blocks.

**Step 2: sparse negative endpoint terms.** In a fixed column, D1 D3 has exactly
2^16 nonzero entries, at addresses whose q coordinate has one fixed value.
The orthogonal signed N1 sends that support to the affine hyperplane

    a dot x = c,  a=N1 q in U,  a!=0.

This is a union of exactly half of all `(q+W)` join blocks, each entire block
having 2^9 addresses. Since k preserves the join partition, it maps this support
to another union of exactly half the join blocks. N3 and Q^-1 operate only
inside q+W and cannot move support into any missing U block. They may cause
cancellation inside existing blocks, which only decreases support. Thus each
complete negative-endpoint term `Q^-1 N3 k N1 D1 D3` has at most 2^16 nonzero
entries in every column. Left or right monomial multiplication preserves
this per-column bound (right multiplication merely reorders/scales columns).

In contrast, G has every one of its 2^17-by-2^17 entries nonzero. Every member
of its projective monomial double coset `H G H` remains fully dense. Every
Gaussian of rank at most 16 with monomial charts has at most 2^16 nonzero
entries per column, so lies outside this double coset. A genuine full-rank
Gaussian target with the prescribed monomial entry and exit charts belongs
to `H G H`. These are support facts, without assuming every dense operator
belongs to that double coset.

**Step 3: project formally before applying physical rank.** Choose one exact
representative per relevant projective operator class. At width f, use the
twisted group algebra in which the multiplication scalar of the chosen
one-column representatives is raised to f. This algebra need not have finite
dimension or a finite group. Its evaluation map sends a representative to its
f-column tensor lift and is a unital algebra homomorphism.

Expand the proposed complete endpoint identity into its fixed finite union
of one-column products and combine projectively equal terms. Tensor separation
at sufficiently large f makes this identity an equality of coefficients in
that twisted algebra. This is essential: projection by one-column support
classes is not asserted to be a well-defined map on arbitrary physical sums
at small widths.

Let pi retain the projective classes in `H G H`. This is a B-bimodule map for
the monomial subalgebra B, since left or right multiplication by H preserves
its double cosets. All negative endpoint terms, all FREE bypass terms and all
lower-rank target terms vanish under pi. Every positive-bank term and its FREE
input/output products belongs to `H G H`, so those terms are unchanged.

Select the m claimed H17 output rows and their matching original input columns.
After this formal projection, their exact coefficient identity is

    diag(G_1,...,G_m) = L_positive * t * diag(G,...,G) * U_positive,

where the middle diagonal has exactly 112 entries; all dimensions and products
retain their actual order. No inverse of U or t is assumed. Evaluate this
projected identity at the same sufficiently large width f. Each side is now
an ordinary finite matrix acting on address fields of dimension N_f. Every
G_j is invertible, so the left side has rank m*N_f; the right side factors
through 112 such fields and has rank at most 112*N_f. Hence m<=112.

Formal projection precedes evaluation; no faithfulness of an infinite algebra,
uniform inverse oracle, group enumeration, semisimplicity or scalar coefficient
rank is used. The same argument permits owned copies and arbitrary rectangular
readout because they only alter the outer factors. Fresh extra paid observations
or changed helper paths would add new channels and are outside this equation.
