## Distinct enumeration and pair law

The model never imports Flow, calls source points/demand/merged, computes per-b tuple minima, or uses a source target-result histogram. It enumerates all target-index multisets of each degree via combinations_with_replacement; a direct factorial quotient gives each exact multinomial weight. Column products are evaluated only after a complete multiset is formed, without source recursive partial-cap pruning. Paid support words are enumerated in order, with exact product multiplicities, rather than expanding tens of thousands of labelled letters or copying source path_counts recurrence. Different multisets or paid words with equal products retain their independent multiplicities.

At nonterminal paid-prefix depth d, take a target multiset a of degree h=G-d-1, its coefficient u_h(a), paid-prefix product rho/path weight, and z=rho*c(a). For each target k and paid r, form an integer pair of weight paths*u_h(a)*T_k*P_r at rank k*r*z.

- Parent presence is k*z<=C.
- Child presence is r*z<=C.
- Child only adds the complete weight to sources.
- Parent only adds it to exits and premature boundary exits.
- Both add it to matched_jobs.
- Neither contributes.

This is a factorial/Pascal decomposition, not an alternative spelling of tuplewise min. For a fixed parent tuple b=a+e_k, all k-terms sum to u_(h+1)(b). Parent presence depends only on rho*c(b), while child presence selects the eligible last-letter terms. If the parent is present, matched terms exactly sum to the truncated child demand and remaining terms are exits. If absent, the eligible terms are fresh sources. Sources and exits are accumulated separately, so equal numerical ranks on both sides are not improperly net-cancelled.

Root demand is added separately from present degree-G units. Units and column_mass sum all present degree-(G-d) multisets for every ordered paid support word. At depth G, each present zero-degree unit emits **every** paid letter, even when rho*r exceeds C. There is no empty-batch prune or terminal rank cap. All histograms are sorted positive count lists; mirror starts with exits and adds every source complement including rank0. source_count retains all source occurrences.
