## 2. All mixed assignments, ADD destinations and native scope

The actual source proof and peer code review match the invariant in my abstract derivation: every active selected field retains its own independent binary real/imaginary coordinates, and every other live field is zero. For each active fractional source, the chosen sign seed places both p*0+u and p*1+u in [0,D−1]; hence the actual floor expression is zero for either bit. Inactive zero sources likewise give zero for every legal seed. Every ADD therefore leaves its destination unchanged, including an active destination and an inactive-source/active-destination event. This is precisely why destination use need not be prohibited.

An active signed-half SCALE kills its own field; other SCALE operations preserve zero. COPY, INIT and CLEAR touch only inactive zero fields. Each MOVE is forbidden on an active declared field and receives a zero inactive field. Its zero output follows only from the inherited whole-field, fixed-native-seed zero-preservation/read-set contract. A MOVE may mix coordinates inside that field; no componentwise native model is required. A hidden second data-field read would violate this premise, not be detected by treating arbitrary serialized MOVE operands as fields.

The local scalar argument holds for every mixed assignment simultaneously, before and after every erasure, at each admitted N=512^f and fixed h>0. It is not an extension from one-bit basis tests through a nonlinear map and does not extend the native ABI to arbitrary unadmitted widths. The fixed word's live-field/shape/owned-initial-zero conditions and input-independent final layout are inherited. run_data models INIT/CLEAR by resetting array slots and MOVE by asserting a zero operand, so it is not an ownership implementation or native execution.

## 3. Exact seed and residue association; no free deletion of other records

The actual seed function is denominator−1 for negative p and zero otherwise. This agrees exactly with the abstract fixed schedule; it is independent of data, coordinate and earlier outcomes. Legal native seeds may be fixed subject to their zero contract. This proves a fiber for one realization, not for every seed and not a stochastic independence/error assertion.

The retained signed-half residue is (p*b+u) mod 2: it equals b for p=1 and 1−b for p=−1. The corresponding sixty erasure residue fields therefore bijectively encode all original chosen bits on the displayed family, assuming the inherited literal residue ABI. ## 4. Capacity and dirty-bank cancellation retain their complete interfaces

Independent bit choices at distinct original coordinates give exactly 2^(2KN) distinct permitted public inputs. The common endpoint in this proof is the complete declared numeric data output with the same final layout and unchanged fixed seeds; it is not a claim that arbitrary diagnostic metadata of a wider caller is constant. Any data-dependent digest, original-input copy, seed overwrite, record length, allocation or address choice that recovery can inspect must be counted as retained information. With no record feedback, a fixed visible output and fixed seeds, injective exact recovery then needs at least 2^(2KN) possible complete retained states.

For a dirty bank with finite Q initial states, cancellation is valid on the unrestricted product family only if its old state must also remain recoverable, its final capacity remains Q and no extra uncounted variable channel appears. Then 2^(2KN)*Q <= 2^b*Q gives b>=2KN. Correlating the bank with inputs, restricting the public entrance image, requiring recovery of less state, changing bank capacity or allowing a free variable channel changes these assumptions. No initially clean physical-storage minimum, routing schedule or erasure mechanism is proved.

With the unreplayed source membership K=60 this specializes to the source-owned 120N bound for one fixed sign-dependent seed schedule. PR6547's source-owned every-seed 86N statement has a different quantifier and remains unreverified here. There is no global maximum-fiber, multiplication-entrance reachability, full physical caller, all-five-assumption discharge or frontier implication.
