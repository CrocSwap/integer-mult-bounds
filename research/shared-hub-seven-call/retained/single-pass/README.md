# Six nonzero calls on the shared-hub projector pool

**Scoped proof.** A six-call word on this five-register alphabet, with each
projector used once at a nonzero exponent, has a diagonal endpoint exactly when
the two triples are consecutive blocks with a common exponent within each
block. This holds in every torsion-free abelian exponent group. In particular,
allowing a fifth exponent relation cannot produce an interleaving in this
six-call model.

## Model and result

Write `(u,v)` for the rank-one matrix `uv^T` over F₂, with the masks interpreted
in coordinate order 0 through 4. The alphabet is

```text
A0=(6,5)   A1=(5,6)   A2=(7,7)
B0=(12,20) B1=(20,12) B2=(28,28)
```

Each triple consists of mutually orthogonal idempotents. Their sums are
`DA = diag(1,1,1,0,0)` and `DB = diag(0,0,1,1,1)`. Coordinate 2 is the common
coordinate. The A-private coordinates are 0 and 1, and the B-private coordinates
are 3 and 4. The alphabet comes from Ali Romman's
[PR #7707 at `57e2492`](https://github.com/aliromman/integer-mult-bounds/blob/57e24923b609d2588b83321f530fa2a1e75ed364/research/edge-matching-even-attainment/PROOF.md).

Each of the six calls is `I + ([a]−1)P`, with its own nonzero `a` in the
additive group `H`. Here `[a]` is the monomial for `a` in the group algebra
F₂[H]. Use the original coordinates and let later calls act on the left. Any
additional unused coordinates are fixed. Diagonality on all five coordinates
forces either A then B or B then A. There are exponents `S,T` such that all
three A calls use `S` and all three B calls use `T`. The endpoint is
`diag([S],[S],[S+T],[T],[T])`. Both block orders attain this endpoint. If all six
labels are the same nonzero element, their generated group has rank one and
there are exactly five independent integral relations.

The five target labels use disjoint sets of calls. The private coordinates
receive A0, A1, B0 and B1 respectively, while the common coordinate receives
A2 and B2. For rational matrix exponents, rank subadditivity bounds the total
target rank by the total paid rank.

## Proof

Let `Q` be the F₂-span of the off-diagonal parts of the six projectors. Use the
cross-commutator lemma from Ali Romman's [PR #7711, section 3 at
`ac185e4`](https://github.com/aliromman/integer-mult-bounds/blob/ac185e4529a01156f7b574555b50827f8cb645bb/research/shared-hub-triple-word-obstruction/PROOF.md).
In the off-diagonal matrix space modulo `Q`, the nine vectors
`off(Ai Bj + Bj Ai)` have only one nonzero dependence, the sum of all nine.
PR #7711 proves a two-block theorem for arbitrary repetitions with at most four
relations. Its interleaving at five relations uses zero exponents.

**First nonzero term on each triple.** Replace `H` by the subgroup generated by
the six labels. It is free abelian of finite rank. Choose a basis and expand
each corresponding Laurent variable as `1 + x_k` in the formal power series
ring over F₂. This expansion is injective. To see this, multiply a Laurent
polynomial by a monomial to clear negative powers, then use injectivity of
translation on polynomials. Every nonzero label therefore gives a factor
coefficient `[a]−1` of finite positive order in total degree.

Let `p` be the smallest such order among the A calls, and let `q` be the
smallest among the B calls. Any product term involving both triples starts
in degree at least `p+q`. In the A-private square at degree `p`, pure B terms
vanish, and pure A terms are linear combinations of the three A projectors
since distinct projectors within a triple multiply to zero. In the two
private off-diagonal entries, A0 and A1 each contribute to one entry and A2
contributes to both. Diagonality gives equality of the three degree-`p`
coefficients of `[a]−1`, including any zero coefficients at that degree. By
choice of `p` at least one coefficient is nonzero. Thus every A call has
order `p` and the same nonzero initial form `f`. The B-private square at
degree `q` gives order `q` and a common nonzero initial form `g` for B.

**The first mixed degree.** Compare the candidate word to the order with every A
call before every B call. Pure terms agree in the two words. In degree `p+q`
the comparison order has mixed part `fg DB DA`, which is diagonal. Its pure
off-diagonal parts lie in `Q`. Moving any B call before an A call adds that
pair's commutator times `fg` in this degree. No higher-order mixed products
can enter. If the candidate is diagonal, the sum of its inverted-pair
commutators times `fg` vanishes modulo `Q`. The polynomial ring over F₂ is an
integral domain, so `fg` has a nonzero coefficient. At that coefficient the
cross-commutator lemma applies. Either none of the nine pairs is inverted or
all nine are inverted. This proves the block separation even when every
linear coefficient of one triple vanishes.

**Exact labels.** In order A then B, the A-private rows equal those of the A
block alone, and the B-private columns equal those of the B block alone. The
same private off-diagonal entries used above now give equality of the three
full monomials per block. Reverse the roles of rows and columns in order B
then A. Equality of group monomials gives equality of labels. Orthogonality
within each triple reduces the two blocks to `I+([S]−1)DA` and
`I+([T]−1)DB`. This gives the endpoint and proves sufficiency.

## Evidence and reproduction

The proof covers arbitrary labels under the stated hypotheses. The finite
[verifier](verify.py) independently multiplies full five-coordinate matrices
over F₂[z,z⁻¹]. It uses all 720 orders for each label tuple in
`{-2,1,2}^6`, plus three signed tuples that exercise a wider gap in augmentation
orders, equal initial forms at different labels, and a zero sum at the common
coordinate. It checked 527,040 words and found 792 diagonal endpoints. Every
passing matrix had the exact predicted diagonal. The finite check uses the
infinite cyclic group, so all the words checked have five exponent relations.

From the repository root with CPython 3.12.15 and the standard library:

```sh
python3.12 -B scripts/research_package.py --package research/shared-hub-single-pass
python3.12 -B scripts/check_research_commit.py --package research/shared-hub-single-pass --check-source-history
python3.12 -I -W error -B research/shared-hub-single-pass/verify.py
```

The verifier prints `PASS_SHARED_HUB_SINGLE_PASS cases=527040 diagonal=792`.
[SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json) pins the catalogue and selected record at the base
commit. Full Git history is needed for those provenance checks. The replay
requires only this package and the standard library. The scope is exactly six
nonzero calls in the formal alphabet. Repeated projectors, zero labels and
arbitrary changes of register basis lie outside it. Native realization,
physical storage and restoration, and the complete recursive invoice are open.
The predecessor replays, selected cold replay, Lean and Docker were unrun.

## Open next questions

Can a seven-call word interleave the triples at five relations with all labels
nonzero and the two occurrences of its repeated projector separated by a call
from the other triple? This is the first length at which per-type cancellation
could defeat the proof. Start with a single repeated A type and compare its
two leading coefficients in the private square with their separate contributions
to the mixed degree. The current argument only constrains their sum there.
