# Three larger-simplex exclusions

## Proposition

Let `K` be a characteristic-zero field. Let `r_t` and `ell(t)` be the pinned
P112 root representatives and binary labels. On its 112 coordinates put

```
C = {c_h : c_h(t) = <r_t,h>, h in K^8},
w(q) = rank_F2 {ell(t) : q(t) != 0}.
```

For each `b in {6,7,8}`, there do not exist `b+4` independent pieces spanning
`T` such that `dim(T intersect C) >= b` and their weights are bounded by

```
2,5,5,5, followed by b copies of 6.
```

Thus the proposition excludes the named `2,5^3,6^b` weight shape in each
pinned TWOSIDE cell `(b,t)=(6,4),(7,4),(8,4)`. It is a theorem about these
piece configurations, not the whole C5 conjecture or a decoder lower bound.

## Replayed geometric premises

The unchanged geometry implementation from PR3164 reconstructs the 120 E8
root lines, removes the eight frame lines and reconstructs all 112 labels.
The data match the exact main-branch input. The labels have a leading one,
so `g(2)<=2`, where `g(d)` is the maximum number of live labels in a
`d`-dimensional binary linear space. Complete unique reverse-echelon
annihilator enumeration gives `g(5)=12` and `g(6)=20`. It covers 3,309,747
five-flats and 788,035 six-flats. Every lower-dimensional support can be
extended to a flat of the stated dimension.

Every nonzero codeword in `C` has support at least 53. Every codeword with
support below 70 is proportional to a root codeword. For completeness, the
argument behind the retained finite quantities is as follows. Below 70
means at least 43 perpendicular live root lines. Move a real direction into
a closed Weyl chamber. The perpendicular roots are generated by vanishing
simple nodes. Every six-node subsystem has at most 36 root lines. The eight
seven-node subsystems have `42,28,22,14,16,23,37,63` root lines. Only the last
can contain 43; its normal is a root direction. Root directions have live
supports 53 or 56, which also proves the distance 53. The replay checks all
960 simple reflections, simple-system positivity and these subsystem counts.
The standard finite-root-system Weyl-chamber theorem is used, not proved by
the executable.

The statement extends to `K`. The rational roots perpendicular to a nonzero
field-valued direction have rational rank at most seven. If the rank were
at most six, a generic real normal to their span would contradict the
36-line bound. Hence a direction with at least 43 perpendicular roots is in
a one-dimensional rational kernel, and is proportional over `K` to a real
rational direction. Apply the real argument. The replay also checks that
the P112 root matrix has full rank eight, making `h -> c_h` injective.

## A quotient with seven through nine vertices

Choose three small pieces of weights `2,5,5`, and let `S` be the union of
their supports. Write the other pieces as `q_0,...,q_b`, with weights
`5,6,...,6`. Let `D=T intersect C` and `lambda:T -> T/D`. Then

```
|S| <= 2+12+12 = 26,
dim(T/D) <= (b+4)-b = 4.
```

The small pieces together with any one `q_i` have support at most
`26+20=46<53`. Their quotient images must be independent. Otherwise a
nonzero combination of four independent pieces would be a nonzero
codeword supported on fewer than 53 coordinates. Consequently the quotient
has dimension exactly four, the three small images are independent and
every `q_i` has nonzero image after quotienting by their span.

This last quotient is one-dimensional. Rescale the `q_i` so that they have
the same nonzero image. Supports and piece independence are unchanged.
There are unique small-piece combinations `a_i` for which

```
c_0=0,
c_i=q_i-q_0-a_i belongs to D,  i=1,...,b.
```

The `b` codewords `c_i` are independent, since their coefficients on the
independent pieces `q_1,...,q_b` are distinct unit vectors. Each nonzero edge
`c_i-c_j` is supported on `S union supp(q_i) union supp(q_j)`, hence has
support at most `26+20+20=66<70`. All edges are root directions.
Injectivity supplies `b` independent directions `h_i` with `c_i=c_{h_i}`.

## The larger root-simplex incidence lemma

Normalize roots to squared norm two. In the plane of two independent E8
roots, any third root line is an A2 sum or difference, with coefficients of
absolute value one. All 7,140 root-plane checks are replayed exactly. This
fact holds over `K` too: a rational root in the `K`-span of two rational
roots is already in their rational span, by solving a nonsingular rational
coordinate minor.

Write `h_i=z_i alpha_i` for roots `alpha_i`. Every `h_i-h_j` is a third
root line, so `z_i/z_j` is `+1` or `-1`. Absorb signs into the roots. There
is a common nonzero `z` such that `h_i=z alpha_i`; moreover
`<alpha_i,alpha_j>=1` for distinct indices. Thus
`0,alpha_1,...,alpha_b` is a root A_b simplex with `n=b+1` vertices.

For any E8 root `r`, its evaluations on these vertices are integers and all
pair differences lie in `{-2,-1,0,1,2}`. If a difference has absolute value
two, equality in Cauchy-Schwarz makes `r` that edge root. The evaluation
multiplicities are then `1,1,n-2`, so there are `2n-3` unequal pairs. If no
difference has absolute value two, there are at most two consecutive integer
levels, giving at most `floor(n^2/4)` unequal pairs. Therefore each live
coordinate occurs in at most

```
M_n = max(2n-3, floor(n^2/4))
```

edge supports. For `n=7,8,9`, these bounds are `12,16,20`. They are universal
upper bounds; sharpness in E8 is not asserted for every size. In particular,
the literal nine-vertex fixture has maximum 18, not 20. Multiplication by
the common field scalar `z` does not change which evaluations vanish.

## Incidence contradiction

There are `E=n(n-1)/2` edges, each with at least 53 support coordinates.
At most `M_n |S|` edge incidences occur in `S`. Outside `S` each edge is
exactly `q_i(t)-q_j(t)`. Every unequal pair has a nonzero endpoint, and one
nonzero endpoint can meet only `n-1=b` pairs. Set
`U=sum_i |supp(q_i)| <= 12+20b`. It follows that

```
E*53 - M_n*|S| <= outside edge incidences <= b*U.
```

Substituting upper bounds only weakens the contradiction:

| b | n | E | M_n | S cap | U cap | Required inequality | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | 7 | 21 | 12 | 26 | 132 | 801 <= 792 | 9 |
| 7 | 8 | 28 | 16 | 26 | 152 | 1068 <= 1064 | 4 |
| 8 | 9 | 36 | 20 | 26 | 172 | 1388 <= 1376 | 12 |

All three are impossible. Smaller supports or overlaps strengthen the bound.
Increasing the allowed small-support union by just one removes each of these
contradictions; the checker retains this failure control.

## Actual residue and overlap boundary

The unchanged original `bk2b.py` reproduces all 24 pinned survivor counts.
A separate implementation reproduces the three relevant counts
`69,154,251`, and retains each target shape. The original filters are source
context, not new universal proof obligations established by this computation.

There are exactly two four-piece weight multisets whose support capacities
are below 53 in each target: `(2,5,5,5)` and `(2,5,5,6)`. Exhaustively assigning
each to two groups of total weight at most six, with the rest paid as line
deletions, gives minimum deletion budget fourteen. The nonbasis pieces have
weight at least five, so this weight-certified calculation cannot invoke
PR3167's rank-four anchor. Consequently the earlier eight-/ten-deletion and
twelve-deletion/rank-four-anchor theorems do not themselves certify these
shapes by this weight-only cover route. This is not a claim that all other
possible proofs or geometric covers were excluded.

Concurrent PR3169's five `(5,5)` exclusions and stronger six-vertex
coordinate budget are outside this scope; its proof was not cold-replayed
here. The six-vertex
PR3164 inequality is a positive control, not another new result. No complete
C5, `C(P112)>=64`, supplier, native/outer implementation or kappa improvement
follows from these three exclusions. Independent human and proof-kernel
review remain separate from this author-checked proof and exact replay.
