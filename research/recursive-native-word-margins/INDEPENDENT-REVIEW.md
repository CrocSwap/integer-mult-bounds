## Method and independently derived obligations

The reviewer read the actual PhysicalArena/Ordinary producer, recursive
Machine, physical Layout, residual/leaf traces, generated Callback records,
ring/matrix helpers, high routes and parking codec. The following bounds
were derived directly from those sources, independently of the author's
finite numerical fixtures and affine-certificate implementation.

For canonical positive root Counts, `PhysicalArena.prepare` copies one
zero per digit and adds one high zero. Thus the actual native widths are
`I=bitlength(n0)+1` and `L=bitlength(M)+1`, with the strict bounds
`2n0<2^I` and `2M<2^L`, including powers of two.

Positive strict child contraction gives `h<=n0-n`. Consequently actual
chronological counters, child depth extension and the leaf's next record
slot fit the original index template. Child ROOT records retain the
original root size; they do not substitute a narrower child template.

Writing `n=64f+delta`, the original physical embedding is `x` in the
head compact bank and `x+2delta` in the tail. The good, pair and halved
orientation chunks remain in their respective banks. All physical starts,
last atoms, one-past ends, differences and peel coordinates lie in `[0,2n]`.
The actual code maps last atoms, rather than incorrectly mapping a head
chunk's one-past endpoint through the gap. External gap factors enlarge
growable cardinality tapes, not fixed-word atom positions. Nonnegative
fixed-multiplier prefixes are bounded by their final products.

Ring values and matrix/form coefficients preserve the inherited canonical
residue bounds. The final high-route mask is deliberately different: it
reaches `2^(L-1)`, potentially exceeding M. The actual top-bit test stops
before another shift. The mask is neither a Ring operand nor a divisor.

The reviewer classified all 19 actual fixed-word division sites. Index
divisors are `2`, `64`, or positive `f`; residue divisors are `q` or a
positive Euclidean remainder. These are strictly below the relevant
half-word limit. The doubled body endpoint and terminal route mask never
enter division. The remainder invariant then bounds each pending division
shift before its guard.

Finally, grants and record copies retain the same full digit extent.
Parking restores the saved original Word slot after free-list reordering
and copies exactly its unchanged template. This establishes value/extent
compatibility; it does not replace the separate register-capacity result.

These invariants support a shortest-first-width-failure argument. They do
not assume a completed successful reference execution. The semantic
premises must be interpreted over unbounded integers, independently of the
finite guards whose sufficiency is being proved.
