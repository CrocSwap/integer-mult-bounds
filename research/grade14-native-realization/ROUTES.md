# Complete binary routes and stopped leaves

All counts in this proof refer to physical fields of the compiled invocation. One binary payload can be any complete suffix record. The implementation transports its contents as an opaque string. A route is exact on the entire address set.

## The right action

Fix an admissible prime q and a rational constituent S of a whole-job or port matcher. Let w≥1. Each g in GL_N(Z/q^w) is uniquely g_0+qX, with the stated fixed representatives of GL_N(F_q) and N² high fields in Z/q^(w−1). Put h_0=(g_0 S mod q) in the same representatives. In the localization at q, entrywise division gives the q-integral rational C=(g_0 S−h_0)/q. Route the low type to h_0 and the entire high record to XS+C modulo q^(w−1). This is a bijection with the independently constructed inverse for S^−1. It preserves arbitrary exterior prefixes, gaps and suffixes. At w=1 the high domain is a singleton. In particular it needs no width-zero interchange. [cover.py](cover.py) implements the low computation, high carry, factorization and full high permutation. In its examples omitting the carry moves 30,051 high records to an incorrect address.

Eliminate the columns of the actual rational S by elementary operations, or equivalently eliminate the rows of its transpose as in the implementation. Choose a pivot that is a unit at q. A pivot exists at each step because the active submatrix is invertible modulo q. All operations have q-integral rational coefficients independent of w, including the inverse unit scalings. This choice introduces no new exclusions even when a rational minor vanishes at the selected q. In reverse order the inverse reduction gives a finite word for the right action on one high row. Process each of the N rows, and apply its carry at its original coordinate. A lower addition is one later-target rotation controlled by an already exposed field. An upper addition exchanges the two fields, performs that rotation, and returns them. A swap exchanges the two fields with the entire gap and suffix in place. These swaps use the completed ordinary backend of the **previous** stage. One route uses at most 2N³ such swaps, N³ ordered shifts including carry, and N² fixed unit scalings, in addition to the fixed low-type route. Allow the same costs for the inverse. The low types use fixed tapes or fixed serial buckets. The implementation never allocates one tape per high matrix.

For completeness, let M=q^(w−1) and let a>0 be an integer unit fixed by this program. To map x to ax mod M, deliver the range of x for which floor(ax/M)=k to bucket k. There are at most a buckets, independent of w. Each bucket already has increasing output addresses, all congruent to −kM mod a. Interleave them with the periodic selector −y M^−1 mod a. For the inverse, distribute by the selector and concatenate in increasing k. A sign reverses the order of whole records except record zero. For a rational unit a/b compose the numerator and the inverse denominator procedures. This is the [retained fixed-rational stream construction](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/upstream/build/sections/02-streams.tex). The bucket breaks, row lengths and signed prefix offsets are generated with polynomial work in w. Fixed-radix counters and entire-suffix streams cost O(V) per pass at bit volume V. The complete target fibre absorbs polynomial preparation per prefix for growing w. Use fixed elementary operations in the finite small-width range.

For a fixed low type, batch all H=q^(N²(w−1)) high matrices in one stream. Let L=B |GL_N(F_q)|, where B is the productive width of the compiled word. A coarse child has volume V/L. It contains all H high values and all previously exposed ancestor high values. On descent reserve a fresh factor LH in the independent row coordinate. On return use the inverse split in its entirety. After D levels the required power is (LH)^D even though the high values are batched. The [complete address and inverse](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/delayed-cover-batch-allocation/PROOF.md) give this map on padded as well as genuine rows. A parent operation and its inverse use the same outer coordinates. Access to the parent remains suspended under the retained ownership contract. The finite opcode order is fixed. A branch reads the parent low type and retains every unused fresh-row digit. Zero and padded fields traverse the same slots. Each entered child therefore contains the full fresh low/high cylinder, and its inverse return restores that cylinder before the next paid position. The complete low classes are equally numerous at each individual paid edge. Ancestor-dependent weights may correlate with past choices.

## A rank-one good leaf

After finite compilation the only remaining positive query is rank one at multiplicity f. In one atom column let P=gP_0g^−1, C be coarse reversal and A be its diagonal of generated unit weights. On the head and physical tail the required matrix is M=diag(A,I) D(P) diag(A^−1,I). The tail at logical column j is at physical column f−1−j. A good low class has P_00 a unit. Set d=A_0 P_00. Ordered additions eliminate the upper-right block to e d z^t, with e the first head unit vector and z the last tail unit vector. The head change adds −A_i P_i0/d times row zero to row i for i>0. The physical tail change adds P_0,N−1−i / P_00 times earlier row i to its last row. These formulas give T and its inverse on the two banks. They work because the split rank-one P factors as its first column times its first row divided by P_00.

Write Δ=T M T^−1−I. Its upper-right block is e d z^t. Its image is free of rank one: before this change D(P)−I factors through im P, and its cross block exhibits a unit. Hence Δ has the full-ring factorization [e;b] [a, d z^t], with a the first row of its head block and b its last tail column divided by d in the tail block. The involution identity Δ²=−2Δ implies a e+d z^t b=−2 by the split inclusion and projection. Define

```text
K = −(b + z/d)e^t + (z/d) a (I−ee^t).
```

Thus K e=−b−z/d and d z^t K=a+e^t. Conjugation by the cross-bank lower shear with block K now gives identity on the other coordinates and the two-by-two weighted swap with coefficients d, d^−1 on e,z. Execute T, the K shear, the one reversed weighted child, then the inverse ordinary shears. The child's weight at j is A[j] P_00 when the first coarse head is indexed from zero. For a general location use its embedded first head index in the parent. The physical child consumes the first f head and last f tail atoms, with their actual reversed ordering. It fixes every other atom and all exterior spectator fields. [leaf.py](leaf.py) constructs these later-target slots from the full-ring entries. The independent address oracle in [check_binary.py](check_binary.py) compares the physically ordered result with M on every component of its test cases.

The first pivot suffices for this final rank-one list. Over F_q write P=u v^t, v^t u=1. All split rank-one projections occur equally often by conjugation. Each projection has q−1 such factorizations. Both u and v are uniform among nonzero vectors. With p=(q^(N−1)−1)/(q^N−1), each first coordinate vanishes with frequency p. Conditional on u_0=0, v_0 is free. The bad fraction is (2−1/q)p < 2/q for N>1 and zero for N=1. Each low type has exactly H high lifts. This is a specialization of the [complete-cover counting theorem](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/bit-deterministic-bad-headers/README.md). It implies the source's looser 2N³/q bound without changing any price. The argument holds for each requested projector separately after every fixed complete right-route bijection.

## All bad leaves, including nonzero nonunits

A bad low pivot can be nonzero at higher precision. Keep the original fallback. Perform unnormalized elimination of the entire invertible 2N by 2N residual using the first unit pivot in each column and retaining every lower/add slot, including zero coefficients. Each remaining Schur complement is invertible over the same local ring. With accumulated row permutation π the weighted matrix after each reduction is diag(b_π) times the unweighted reduced matrix times diag(b)^−1, where b=(A,I). A row addition has the corresponding ratio of units, while a pivot row swap updates π. This proves by induction that the itinerary and instruction slots depend on the unweighted low only. Use the full high precision and the intermediate π at *each* coefficient. The final diagonal has product ±1, because D(P) is conjugate to a single interchange and each row swap changes the sign. Implement this diagonal by 2N−1 paired unit scalings and one fixed sign. Undo the elimination slots in inverse chronological order. [The retained fallback](retained/fallback.py) constructs these schedules and lowers each paired scaling to two weighted swaps, and each upper addition to a lower addition between two unit swaps.

A same-orientation swap J_a of two f-atom fields reverses the later field, makes one reversed child, then reverses the later field again. Each reversal is a unit-weight reversed child on its two halves; an odd middle atom stays fixed. On opposite orientations use the physical reversed child directly. Construct the coefficient in the first field's physical order. Omit the half calls at f=1. Counting each swap at the conservative three-child price gives at most 6(2N)(2N−1)+3(2N)+6(2N−1)<32N² children, all of width ≤f. The middle child has no access to fresh dependent weights. In the good and bad cases the raw head, tail and every completed spectator are restored to their original placements. The source of this tariff and the weighted index rules is the [ordered local-ring proof](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/notes/three-stage-cover-bit.tex).

The whole normalized rank moment is bounded by (P_calls/B) N^(−τ) (1+(2N³/q)32N²). The exact grade-14 certificate pays this for its actual N and uniform loss. No rank or output credit changes. The high-row and lower-shear sweeps count separately in ordinary work. The number of coarse operations depends only on N. Each lower atom sweep uses the retained prefix-controlled rotation with its actual source index and possible reversed column index.

## Descriptors and return

At the root A=I. At a good child, computing one entry reads one parent column entry and multiplies by a pivot from the parent cover. At a bad child compute the required units from the constant-size local matrix, the recorded row permutations and a bounded number of parent entries. Covers, embedded projectors, selector types and topology have finite descriptions. Compute inverses of units by Euclid. Each ancestor contributes O_fixed(w) bits of matrices and indices, apart from O(log e) bits to locate indices. The number of ancestors at fixed old-backend level is O_fixed(log e). Even if a coefficient branches a constant number of times at each level, total work per prefix is polynomial in e and w. For the actual choice w=ceil(e^(1/250)) it is polynomial in w. Work through the ancestor tree depth first, recomputing sibling terms and reusing local storage. This takes O_fixed((w+log e)log e) descriptor bits. The full q^w target fibre, or q^(w−1) high fibre, pays this fixed-degree polynomial for large w. In a lower update a control in an earlier atom may be separated by a large gap: carry its prepared state through that gap with ripple counters and do not reread its whole prefix for each bit. The [source descriptor induction](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/e54523517d0f03e58352e647c403a5af3e525a57/research/selected-bit-recursive-closure/PROOF.md) gives the amortization. N, the scope of each projector, and all finite ordinary stage constants are changed to their actual values here. Algebraic correctness holds for any legal common ancestor weight. The time bound is for the weights generated by this algorithm. The finite interpreter materializes the test columns. On tape the slot selector is shared over the entire low class. For each slot and prefix generate only the current column's coefficient by this descriptor procedure. A selector pass consumes its entire target fibre before proceeding. Store the fixed low schedule and the current descriptor. The number of bank scans is bounded by the fixed program length.
