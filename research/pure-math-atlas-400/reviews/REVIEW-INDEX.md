## Corrections that changed the mathematics

The reports record explicit positivity, characteristic, domain, time and nonzero-space hypotheses. A059 now proves the correct chart-domain and symmetry equivalence. D043 requires a nonnegative rate, D086 a nonnegative time, and D099 a nonzero value space for norm attainment. B030 was read again after its weighted covariance replacement. D074 was read again after its rectangular variational extension. The three replaced C enumeration results were independently checked. No unresolved theorem-level finding remains in these reviews.

## Duplication and scope

Exact repeats of affine/projective construction, design rank and Hadamard existence were replaced. Shared lemmas such as Fourier orthogonality or polar error bounds remain where needed, but the affected entry’s main conclusion now adds explicit reconstruction, variational optimality, a different geometric domain, or a different certificate. This is not a claim that no two mathematical statements are logically related.
