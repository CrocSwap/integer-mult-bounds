# Primary bibliography and mathematical precedents

The links below record attribution and bounded source checks performed for this collection. The volumes contain their own proofs; an author abstract or source hash is not a proof audit. Exact retrieval scope is recorded in each source ledger. No comprehensive global novelty search is claimed.

## Volume 01

- [The Stacks Project, Fields, section9.20 Trace and norm](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BIE). Norm-as-determinant definitions and context; independently supplied proofs.
- [J.S.Milne, Fields and Galois Theory, v5.10(2022)](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/FT.pdf). Primary author notes for Galois/Hilbert90 context; no copied proofs.
- [Chen–Grochow–Qiao–Tang–Zhang, actions by classical groups, arXiv2306.03135v2(2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03135). Tensor-isomorphism research context only; no complexity reduction is inferred from orbitwise algebra.
- [Etingof and coauthors, Introduction to Representation Theory, MIT18.712 lecture notes, Chapters2 and5](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-712-introduction-to-representation-theory-fall-2010/pages/lecture-notes/). Classical Krull–Schmidt, semisimple endomorphism rings and quiver context; independent proofs supplied in A041–A050.
- [Stacks Project, section10.36 Finite and integral ring extensions](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00GH). Finite/integral background for A036; no copied proof.
- [Stacks Project, lemma15.112.4 Group actions and integral closure](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0H35). Modular invariant-quotient caution; A040 uses its own nonmodular averaging proof, not the stronger false exactness without that hypothesis.

## Volume 02

- [Wootters and Fields (1989)](https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-4916(89)90322-9). Prime-power MUB construction and tomography precedent; own proofs given in B051-B070.
- [Klappenecker and Rotteler](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0309120). Exponential-sum MUB construction precedent.
- [Klappenecker and Rotteler](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0502031). MUB/projective2design and SIC/projective2design precedent.
- [Bandyopadhyay, Boykin, Roychowdhury, Vatan](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0103162). Orthogonal commuting unitary construction precedent.
- [Abdukhalikov](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.3478). Symplectic-spread construction precedent. B098 is now an independently written proof of deficiency-one completion, without global novelty attribution.
- [Casazza, Fickus, Heinecke, Wang, Zhou](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.4061). Spectral tetris precedent; own >=2 proof in B037.
- [Boykin, Sitharam, Tarifi, Wocjan](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0502024). Real-MUB operator-space bound precedent.
- [Fickus, Mixon, Tremain](https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.5730). Sparse SteinerETF construction precedent; reconstructed in B079.
- [Brierley, Weigert, Bengtsson](https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.4097). Complete MUB uniqueness below dimension six, not dimension seven.
- [McNulty and Weigert](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.23997v2). March2026 author-review abstract retains nonprimepower complete-MUB existence as unknown. Bounded source-status check, not an exhaustive claim about every subsequent paper.
- [Appleby, Flammia, Kopp](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03970v2). Universal construction is conditional on Stark and special-value conjectures; not converted into unconditional existence.
- [Cati and Pasechnik](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.18897v2). Primary construction database supplies classical Paley-construction context. B094 proves the q=3mod4 construction; the duplicate all-order conjecture was removed.
- [Glebsky](https://arxiv.org/abs/1002.3082). Qualitative normalizedHS approximation precedent, not proof of B099 rate.
- [Bansil and Kachkovskiy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.31000). Aug2026 two-matrix1/3 rate includes [X,Xprime]=0; sharpness restricted to that pinned problem.
- [Jasper and Mixon](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.14938). Weighted Sidon-set designs distinguished from unweighted distinct-ray conjecture B100.
- [Arman, Bondarenko, Prymak, Radchenko](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01376). May2026 primary abstract gives explicit equalweight spherical5designs with <=72d^2 points. Generic quadratic design-size existence is not labeled newly open.

## Volume 03

- [Costa and Sadhu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02295). Universal proportion below one half; does not settle Frankl.
- [Montgomery and Sauermann](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05601). Asymptotically tight approximate rainbow basis packing/covering, distinct from exact Rota.
- [Counting Near-Spanning Matchings in Latin Squares and Steiner Triple Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11006). Near-transversals and even-order variant must not be promoted to odd full transversals.
- [What did Ryser Conjecture?](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.02893). Primary formulation distinctions.
- [Jean Paul Zerafa](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28118). Stronger five-even-subgraph double-cover problem and claw-free cases.
- [Sang-il Oum](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16356). Primary exposition of ordinary cycle-double-cover proof announcement; not a proof of five-cover target.
- [Letzter, Pokrovskiy and Ella Williams](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11331). Approximate graceful-labeling progress, not exact graceful tree solution.
- [Sichen Wang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13831). Claimed universal factor165/59; factor two remains a separate target.
- [Chahua and Gutiérrez](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.11409). Dense-graph sufficient conditions, not universal Tuza.
- [Anup Rao](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14790). Primary author survey of sunflower methods; factorial bound independently proved.
- [David Ellis, Yuval Filmus and Ehud Friedgut](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.28404v1). Section4 Conjecture I, supported symmetric matrices with nonnegative-eigenvalue inertia count; ordinary Chvatal proved in this paper.
- [Noga Alon](https://www.tau.ac.il/~nogaa/PDFS/null2.pdf). Original combinatorial Nullstellensatz, polynomial sufficient conditions.
- [Levent Alpoge](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.7574). Latin parity cancellation analysis; nonvanishing conjecture remains distinct.
- [UC San Diego primary seminar](https://math.ucsd.edu/seminar/alon-tarsi-conjecture-sun-matrix-integrals). Current discussion of Alon-Tarsi and matrix-integral routes.
- [Stefan Steinerberger](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.13202). Approximate sign orthogonality is not exact Hadamard existence.
- [Priyanshu, Majhi and Paul](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12315). Specific exact Hadamard constructions, not all orders.
- [Giorgis Petridis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.3507). Original short product-set proof; elementary minimizing-subset argument adapted additively.
- [Melvyn B. Nathanson](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0302155). Primary classical additive-basis formulation.
- [Yuchen Ding and Jie Wang](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0012365X26002670). Current introduction distinguishes natural-number additive-basis conjecture from unique representation bases over integers.
- [Bloom, Sawin, Schildkraut and Zhelezov](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28781). Counterexample to unrestricted real sum-product conjecture; excluded from open-target list.
- [Chang, Hong Liu and Miao Liu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19123). Earlier September proof announcement of Chvatal downset conjecture; not treated as open.
- [Gian-Carlo Rota](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/rota1.pdf). Primary incidence algebra and Mobius inversion; proof reconstructed directly here.
- [George Polya](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02546665). Primary group-action enumeration and cycle-index theorem; weighted proof reconstructed here.

## Volume 04

- [Boyd–Vandenberghe — Convex Optimization](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/). Log-sum-exp, entropy duality and convexity background; proofs D001–D030 are supplied in the volume.
- [Potts–Tasche — Parameter estimation for exponential sums by approximate Prony method](https://www-user.tu-chemnitz.de/~potts/paper/prony.pdf). Prony/Hankel reconstruction precedent; finite exact reconstruction is proved with its own assumptions.
- [Curto–Fialkow — Truncated K-moment problem](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0507067). Flat extension and representing-measure precedent; D059 gives a finite one-variable proof.
- [Curto–Fialkow — Recursiveness, positivity, and truncated moment problems](https://www.math.uh.edu/~hjm/v017n4/0603CURTO.pdf). Primary moment positivity and recursion precedent.
- [Al-Mohy–Higham — Computing the Frechet derivative of the matrix exponential](https://eprints.maths.manchester.ac.uk/1218/). Matrix-exponential perturbation background; D081–D087 use elementary integral and series proofs.
- [Blanes–Casas–Murua — Splitting methods for differential equations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.01722). Primary author survey for Lie–Trotter and Strang attribution; our stated finite matrix estimates are proved explicitly.
- [Nicholas J. Higham — Computing the Polar Decomposition—with Applications](https://eprints.maths.manchester.ac.uk/694/). Primary polar-decomposition and nearest-isometry attribution for D074; a full thin-SVD proof is supplied here.
