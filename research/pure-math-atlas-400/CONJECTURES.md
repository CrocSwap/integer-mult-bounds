# Conjecture register: 21 unproved targets

These targets are not used as proved leaves. Thirteen are identified in inspected literature; eight are precise atlas proposals whose global status was not established by the bounded retrieval. A proposal is useful to test or prove, but may already have relevant literature. None is advertised as a newly discovered globally open problem. Full evidence, special cases, citations and falsifiers are in the linked entries.

## [A010 — a uniform fixed-field determinant-automorphism compiler](volumes/01-algebra.md#a010)

Status: atlas proposal; unproved here; global literature status not established.
**Target.** For each fixed finite field F_q there are polynomial-size, uniformly constructed pairs of ordinary three-tensors C(A,d),D(A,d) such that C(A,d)≅D(A,d) exactly when A admits an automorphism with determinant triple d∈(F_q*)³. The polynomial bound is in the number of entries of A; no transporter is supplied.

## [A030 — intrinsic extraction of a bounded determinant equation table](volumes/01-algebra.md#a030)

Status: atlas proposal; unproved here; global literature status not established.
**Target.** Fix q. There is a deterministic algorithm using polynomially many field operations in the tensor size that, from an arbitrary tensor A over F_q, outputs three bounded monomial equations defining its stabilizer determinant image, without enumerating its automorphism group.

## [A060 — Uniform unmarked tensor recovery of paired flags](volumes/01-algebra.md#a060)

Status: atlas proposal; unproved here; global literature status not established.
**Target.** For each field K, seek a uniform polynomial-size construction from two finite flags in V to a single ordinary order-three tensor T, with no externally marked summands, such that a distinguished intrinsic quotient of T recovers V and both flags, and the induced map Aut(T)→GL(V) has image exactly the common flag stabilizer. The construction must also lift every member of that stabilizer. This precise package is **not proved here**; no assertion of global novelty or global openness is made.

## [A100 — Fixed-dimensional unipotent transporter fibres have polynomial-height points](volumes/01-algebra.md#a100)

Status: atlas proposal; unproved here; global literature status not established.
**Target.** Fix dimensions of three rational tensor factors. For tensors x,y over Q with reduced entry numerators and denominators bounded by H≥2, suppose a rational GL-transporter exists and the effective algebraic stabilizer G_x/Z is a split unipotent Q-group, where G=GL(U)×GL(V)×GL(W) and Z={(aI_U,bI_V,cI_W):abc=1} is the central kernel. Seek C depending only on the dimensions such that some rational transporter has all reduced entry numerators and denominators bounded by H^C. This is **not proved here** and is not asserted globally new or globally open.

## [B091 — A fourth MUB in dimension six does not exist [conjecture; classical direction]](volumes/02-geometry.md#b091)

Status: literature-identified conjectural direction; see the bounded dated source check.
**Target.** Every family of pairwise unbiased orthonormal bases in C^6 has size at most three.

## [B092 — Complete complex MUB families occur exactly in prime-power dimensions [conjecture; classical direction]](volumes/02-geometry.md#b092)

Status: literature-identified conjectural direction; see the bounded dated source check.
**Target.** For an integer d≥2, there exist d+1 MUBs in C^d if and only if d is a prime power.

## [B093 — Weyl–Heisenberg SIC construction in every dimension [conjecture; classical direction]](volumes/02-geometry.md#b093)

Status: literature-identified conjectural direction; see the bounded dated source check.
**Target.** For every integer d≥2 there is a unit vector x∈C^d such that the d² rays of X^aZ^b x, with cyclic shift X, diagonal clock Z and a,b∈Z/dZ, have squared pairwise overlaps 1/(d+1).

## [B095 — Uniqueness of a complete MUB family in complex dimension seven [conjecture; atlas proposal]](volumes/02-geometry.md#b095)

Status: atlas proposal; unproved here; global literature status not established.
**Target.** Every family of eight MUBs in C^7 is equivalent to B063's chirp family under a common unitary or antiunitary, basis permutations, vector permutations, and individual phases.

## [B096 — A quantitative stability exponent for the qutrit complete family [conjecture; atlas proposal]](volumes/02-geometry.md#b096)

Status: atlas proposal; unproved here; global literature status not established.
**Target.** For four unitary 3-by-3 matrices U_a, set Δ=∑_(a<b,i,j)(|(U_a*U_b)_ij|²−1/3)². Let dist be the square root of the sum of squared Frobenius distances to the orbit of the standard complete qutrit family under B074's equivalences. There exist constants C,ε>0 such that Δ≤ε implies dist≤CΔ^(1/4).

## [B099 — Square-root normalized-Hilbert–Schmidt repair for three Hermitian matrices [conjecture; atlas proposal]](volumes/02-geometry.md#b099)

Status: atlas proposal; unproved here; global literature status not established.
**Target.** There is a universal C such that for all d and Hermitian contractions A_1,A_2,A_3, writing δ=(∑_(i<j)||[A_i,A_j]||_(2,d)²)^(1/2), one can find commuting Hermitian contractions B_1,B_2,B_3 with (∑||A_i−B_i||_(2,d)²)^(1/2)≤Cδ^(1/2). Here ||X||_(2,d)=||X||_HS/√d, and all three matrices may move.

## [B100 — Unweighted projective 2-designs at the MUB cardinality scale [conjecture; atlas proposal]](volumes/02-geometry.md#b100)

Status: atlas proposal; unproved here; global literature status not established.
**Target.** For every d≥2 there exist N≤d(d+1) pairwise distinct rank-one projectors in C^d whose uniform measure is a projective 2-design.

## [C010 — union-closed frequency one half](volumes/03-combinatorics.md#c010)

Status: literature-identified conjectural direction; see the bounded dated source check.
**Target.** A finite union-closed family F containing a nonempty set has an element belonging to at least |F|/2 members.

## [C020 — Rota's basis rearrangement](volumes/03-combinatorics.md#c020)

Status: literature-identified conjectural direction; see the bounded dated source check.
**Target.** Given n labelled disjoint bases B₁,…,B_n of a rank-n matroid, their union can be partitioned into n bases, each meeting each B_i exactly once. Repeated underlying vectors may be represented by distinct parallel labelled elements.

## [C030 — odd Latin squares have transversals](volumes/03-combinatorics.md#c030)

Status: literature-identified conjectural direction; see the bounded dated source check.
**Target.** Every Latin square of odd order n has n cells meeting each row, each column and each symbol exactly once.

## [C040 — five even subgraphs double-cover edges](volumes/03-combinatorics.md#c040)

Status: literature-identified conjectural direction; see the bounded dated source check.
**Target.** Every bridgeless graph has five, possibly empty or disconnected, even-degree spanning subgraphs such that each edge occurs in exactly two of them. “Even subgraph” does not mean a single simple cycle.

## [C050 — every tree is graceful](volumes/03-combinatorics.md#c050)

Status: literature-identified conjectural direction; see the bounded dated source check.
**Target.** Every tree with m edges admits a bijective vertex labeling by {0,…,m} for which its edge absolute differences are {1,…,m}.

## [C060 — Tuza's factor two](volumes/03-combinatorics.md#c060)

Status: literature-identified conjectural direction; see the bounded dated source check.
**Target.** In every graph, the minimum number τ of edges meeting all triangles is at most twice the maximum number ν of edge-disjoint triangles.

## [C070 — a sharp inertia certificate for every downset](volumes/03-combinatorics.md#c070)

Status: literature-identified conjectural direction; see the bounded dated source check.
**Target.** Let D⊆2^[n] be a downset containing a nonempty set, and s=max_i |{A∈D:i∈A}|. There exists a real symmetric matrix M indexed by D, with M_AB=0 whenever A∩B≠∅, having exactly s nonnegative eigenvalues, counted with multiplicity. Negative and zero entries on allowed disjoint pairs are permitted; the empty-set diagonal is allowed.

## [C080 — Alon–Tarsi Latin parity](volumes/03-combinatorics.md#c080)

Status: literature-identified conjectural direction; see the bounded dated source check.
**Target.** For every positive even n, the signed sum over all Latin squares of order n is nonzero, where a square's sign is the product of the signs of all its row permutations and all its column permutations, using fixed ordered row, column and symbol labels.

## [C090 — all admissible Hadamard orders](volumes/03-combinatorics.md#c090)

Status: literature-identified conjectural direction; see the bounded dated source check.
**Target.** For every positive integer m there is a Hadamard matrix of order 4m.

## [C100 — Erdős–Turán additive bases](volumes/03-combinatorics.md#c100)

Status: literature-identified conjectural direction; see the bounded dated source check.
**Target.** If A⊆N₀ and every sufficiently large integer is a sum of two elements of A, then r_A(n)=#{(a,b)∈A²:a≤b, a+b=n} is unbounded as n→∞, meaning limsup r_A(n)=∞.
