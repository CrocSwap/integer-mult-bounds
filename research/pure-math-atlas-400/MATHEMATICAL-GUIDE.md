# Mathematical guide: sufficient routes and useful gaps

The organizing question is constructive: **what explicit hypotheses are enough to produce the object, reconstruction, comparison or exact certificate?** The volumes keep classical tools close to the concrete theory gaps they can help close. A theorem is available only after its actual hypotheses have been checked. Conjectures are research directions and are never premises in the proved routes below.

## 1. Recover intrinsic structure before normalizing a tensor

[A011–A020](volumes/01-algebra.md#a011) separate a tensor's minimal carriers, ambient padding, isotopy, radicals and centroid projectors. In particular an injective padding does not create a smaller tensor rank; padding a GL problem to an SL problem uses actual complement determinant freedom and includes a recovery argument. This does not supply a general unmarked tensor compiler.

After a transporter is supplied, [A001–A009](volumes/01-algebra.md#a001) express correction in the stabilizer's character image. [A021–A029](volumes/01-algebra.md#a021) turn a supplied integer character table into explicit power equations and finite fibre counts. The useful gap is to obtain that table intrinsically and efficiently: [A010](volumes/01-algebra.md#a010) and [A030](volumes/01-algebra.md#a030) state exact compiler and extraction targets. A short table's existence is not an algorithm to find it.

[A051–A059](volumes/01-algebra.md#a051) then provide actual flag bases, lifts and symplectic chart transitions. [A060](volumes/01-algebra.md#a060) asks for a construction that recovers both flags after external markings are forgotten. This requires proof of recovery and of every legitimate lift, rather than an unexplained tagging device.

## 2. Descent with explicit correction and recovery

[A061–A070](volumes/01-algebra.md#a061) use norms and coprime degrees to descend actual scalar corrections. [A071–A080](volumes/01-algebra.md#a071) isolate Frobenius correction equations and solve specified abelian cases. [A081–A090](volumes/01-algebra.md#a081) prove the independence of field homomorphisms and then construct semilinear descent bases; morphisms, exact sequences and algebra identities descend along with them.

This is a sufficient route when the field extension, action and compatibility data are supplied. It does not recover a missing action or produce an unproved tensor reduction. [A091–A099](volumes/01-algebra.md#a091) add finite rational charts, nonvanishing evaluations, rank certificates and deformation calculations. [A100](volumes/01-algebra.md#a100) asks for a uniform height bound in a precisely promised unipotent-stabilizer class; the promise includes the quotient by the unavoidable central scalar kernel.

## 3. Complete actual finite geometric objects

[B001–B010](volumes/02-geometry.md#b001) construct incidence geometries, count Lagrangians and describe checkable spread completion. The stronger positive bridge [B098](volumes/02-geometry.md#b098) is a universal deficiency-one completion theorem: in a 2n-dimensional symplectic space over F_q, q^n pairwise disjoint Lagrangians leave q^n−1 nonzero holes. Each hole's perpendicular contains all the holes, so their span is itself the missing Lagrangian.

Combine this with the explicit Weyl construction in [B068–B070](volumes/02-geometry.md#b068): a supplied deficiency-one symplectic partial spread produces a complete family of q^n+1 mutually unbiased bases in complex dimension q^n. All objects and compatibility hypotheses are supplied or constructed. This is a subclass result, not a theorem completing arbitrary partial MUBs in arbitrary dimensions.

[B081–B084](volumes/02-geometry.md#b081) reconstruct flat missing borders and preserve the relevant cross identities. [B097](volumes/02-geometry.md#b097) proves an additional prime-alphabet statement: the complement can stay within pth roots of unity. The key is that a sum of p−1 such roots with modulus one omits exactly one root, as shown by cyclic autocorrelation counts. Completion inside a number field and completion inside a finite alphabet are different conclusions.

## 4. Turn measurements into exact or stable reconstruction

[B021–B040](volumes/02-geometry.md#b021) give frame operators, duals, defects and constructive completions. [B030](volumes/02-geometry.md#b030) treats correlated noise: for full-row-rank F and positive covariance Σ, L₀=(FΣ⁻¹F*)⁻¹FΣ⁻¹ reconstructs exactly, and every other exact L has covariance S⁻¹+(L−L₀)Σ(L−L₀)*. The positive extra term proves the full comparison rather than assuming independent noise.

[B041–B060](volumes/02-geometry.md#b041) connect designs and MUBs to tomography. A commuting residual basis or suitable channel is a sufficient missing-basis certificate; it is not evidence that the required certificate exists in dimension six. [B085–B090](volumes/02-geometry.md#b085) quantify repair and provide an exact contraction certificate. A small numerical residual alone is not an existence proof.

The corresponding research directions are the actual dimension-six and complete-family problems [B091–B092](volumes/02-geometry.md#b091), SIC construction [B093](volumes/02-geometry.md#b093), exact classification/stability [B095–B096](volumes/02-geometry.md#b095), almost-commuting repair [B099](volumes/02-geometry.md#b099), and the sharp unweighted design scale [B100](volumes/02-geometry.md#b100). Each statement gives its own status and falsifier.

## 5. Produce finite witnesses from local certificates

[C001–C029](volumes/03-combinatorics.md#c001) take diminishing returns, rank axioms and Hall inequalities to explicit optimality or assignment witnesses. [C031–C049](volumes/03-combinatorics.md#c031) convert cut inequalities to integral flows, circulations, orientations and structural decompositions. [C071–C079](volumes/03-combinatorics.md#c071) show how a nonzero coefficient or degree inequality forces a finite-field solution, with explicit characteristic hypotheses.

[C081–C083](volumes/03-combinatorics.md#c081) recover exact counts by Möbius inversion and symmetry counts by orbit and cycle-index formulas. [C091–C099](volumes/03-combinatorics.md#c091) turn additive growth into actual covers, subspaces and zero-sum selections. These are reusable sufficient constructions; no open conjecture is required to run them.

The ten C conjectures put precise new demands on these tools: exact Rota rearrangements go beyond approximation, a full odd Latin transversal goes beyond a near-transversal, five even subgraphs go beyond an unbounded double cover, and Alon–Tarsi asks for a specific signed nonvanishing rather than an arbitrary coefficient criterion. The [register](CONJECTURES.md) keeps these distinctions visible. The unrestricted real sum-product conjecture and older downset assertion are not mislabeled open targets.

## 6. Recover a positive model from finite moments

[D051–D060](volumes/04-analysis.md#d051) distinguish node collision, signed cancellation and positive atomic recovery. A positive definite smaller Hankel matrix and a positive flat extension construct a self-adjoint shift. Its cyclic vector supplies simple real eigenvalues and positive weights; the annihilating polynomial proves uniqueness. Add the localizers in [D093](volumes/04-analysis.md#d093) to force the atoms into a prescribed interval.

[D061–D070](volumes/04-analysis.md#d061) supply quadrature, positive resolvent errors and energy-optimal compression. [D091–D097](volumes/04-analysis.md#d091) connect strict minors, symmetry certificates and eventually positive Bernstein coefficients. A source comment about an infinite object is not needed for any of these finite proofs.

## 7. Repair structure with a paid quantitative margin

[D031–D050](volumes/04-analysis.md#d031) account for block growth, switching, forcing and invariant metrics. Per-block activity and coherent gauges have explicit hypotheses; arbitrary mixing does not inherit the same estimate. [D071–D080](volumes/04-analysis.md#d071) repair intertwiners and almost-isometries. In particular the rectangular polar factor is the unique least-squares isometric repair, not merely a candidate within a bound.

A useful combined sufficient result is exact unitary recovery for a finite group. Suppose ρ,σ are finite-dimensional unitary representations and an invertible X has ||ρ(g)X−Xσ(g)||≤ε for every g, with σ_min(X)>ε. By [D077](volumes/04-analysis.md#d077), X₀=|G|⁻¹Σ_g ρ(g)⁻¹Xσ(g) is an exact intertwiner and ||X₀−X||≤ε, so X₀ is invertible. Its positive matrix X₀*X₀ commutes with every σ(g), because conjugation by σ(g) and unitarity of ρ(g) leave it unchanged. Its inverse square root therefore commutes too, by spectral interpolation. Thus U=X₀(X₀*X₀)⁻¹ᐟ² is an exact unitary intertwiner. This route constructs a genuine witness under an explicit singular-value margin; it does not turn an arbitrary small residual into exact equivalence.

[D081–D090](volumes/04-analysis.md#d081) and [D098–D100](volumes/04-analysis.md#d098) then quantify finite evolution, inverse reconstruction and mixing. These results explain what a precision or stability margin actually buys while keeping its conditioning and spectral requirements explicit.

## How to extend the atlas

For a proposed new result, record the exact object, assumptions, output and recovery map; identify which proved entries discharge each step. For an open target, check primary literature first, prove a useful special case, and give an explicit counterexample criterion. A finite search or numerical witness is evidence only at its checked size. A supplied transformation, chart or oracle is not a solution to constructing it. Preserve the exact source revision and credit the earlier theorem or PR mechanism when adapting it.
