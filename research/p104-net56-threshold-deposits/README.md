# Prove the P104 two-drop threshold per six-dimensional depositor

Assumes the pinned P104 original-net rigidity and low coefficients, two distinct 7D gather charts, seven gathers of dimension at least eight, eight active depositors of the prescribed dimensions, b⊆a and chronological network rules. Rational cancellation and comparable depositors are allowed. The legal-word bridge and a free-standing universal NET-D56 theorem remain open.

For 1≤r≤8 low depositors with 6D inputs, the specified original nine-gatherer/eight-depositor P104 network has T≥2r and hence D=56−2r+E+B+T≥56+E+B. The four-low application has T≥8. Exact networks attain T=8,D=56 and T=8,D=57 with an actual depositor relay.

Observed validation. Owner runs check complete Gram, all odd/diagonal cells, transfer and chronology for the network examples, including equality controls with up to eight low depositors. A distinct reviewer inspected the chart-block/circuit proof under its hypotheses and checked 759 reduced chart patterns. This is a paper proof and finite review; no new Lean proof or independent human approval is asserted.
