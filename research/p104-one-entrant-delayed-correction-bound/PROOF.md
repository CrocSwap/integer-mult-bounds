# Exact scope and separating inputs

For each historical key, the old source row is evaluated immediately before its old ADD. From the second MOVE, advance to immediately before the next operation that touches the target role as an operand, counting both sources and destinations of ADD and COPY. The bank consists of roles other than the target resident in its destination chart at that cut. The exact trace tracks INIT, CLEAR, SCALE, COPY, ADD and MOVE. It treats MOVE as a chart change of a normalized row.

The inventory is one #7434 coordinate exclusion, six #7434 exact controls, 62 #7436 small-bank exclusions and 50 #7436 larger-bank exclusions. The 119-case scan has a 25-second cap. It exhausts the scope with no literal proportional donor and 118 coordinate survivors. There are 106 unchanged cuts and 13 cuts advanced past precisely one intervening MOVE. In every advanced cut the intervening MOVE brings one distinct donor into the target chart without changing an old bank row. No case uses the word-end convention.

An old covector can be reused exactly when its value on the entrant is zero. It already annihilates the old bank and is nonzero on the same pre-ADD source. A nonzero value on the entrant invalidates that particular certificate and leaves the augmented span question open. This test is necessary for reusing the old certificate and sufficient for excluding the augmented bank when it passes.
