## Genuine preparation at the first and every active field

The receiver reaches a native body only on a genuine field with more than N selected original windows, including all its arbitrary spectator data. The check precedes preparation of its scale tables. The complete field has bit volume at least 2^N. At this point `Recipe.open()` enumerates every positive bin intersection, both link directions, source incidence and static delay; see [the transfer proof](TRANSFER.md). The code derives all these quantities anew. Its rounded-power computation has no class or global cache. The integer m[e] has at most 7H bits. Static transfers and output selections have fewer than 94H+512 bits each. There are at most 2^35 N parent/target/bin visits, at most two interior child bins and one exterior calculation per visit. Even if each grade multiply, square root and gcd on O(H) bits is allowed 2^50 (H+1)^3 tape transitions, the direct arithmetic costs less than N^2 for H>=3000. For serial lookup allow 2^42 N records, 2^10(H+1) bits per record and at least a full table scan for every requested link. One scan can compare a delimited key with a saved key on another tape and rewind that key, then restore or rewrite the value in sequential order. Fewer than 2^50 N(H+1)^2 transitions suffice per lookup. The complete fixed-rank incidence preparation, including sequential lookups, navigation and deletion after the data call, fits N^3. It resides with that invocation only. A suspended parent may keep its own table and its live raw state during a descendant. Every such parent is an actual active field.

Inside each physical epoch `Plan.events` yields the timed events on demand to the common physical decoder. Each suspended event generator, the raw continuations and their live owners retain their own cursor. The decoder completes the same native splits and returns as in the installed reference. No image is emitted or read in this variant. The control scans and raw data operations below pay for this change in preparation. Every subsequent invocation repeats these bills.

The old bound h<2KVWR, where W<2^16 and R=N^2, shows h<N^91. It implies B>=4096h for H>=3000. For a tighter upper bound on the actual executed copy count, the true external sizes per time sum to at least eta(1-1/N)(N/(4*1024A))^c V Phi > V, since N>4*1024A, eta>0.99 and Phi>P/5>2. The physical periods satisfy qs'/N<=1+4/1024. Thus h >= K(R/N)(1-1/N)sum(s')/(1+4/1024)>KVN/2, where the integer source loss has already been counted in the lower bound on sum(s'). With K=N^82,V=N^6 this gives

    1 <= z=ceil(B/h) < 3N^3,
    z*h/B <= 1+1/4096,
    B=N^92 < N^448.

Every one of these z copied programs really runs. A whole last group can have arbitrary fields and zeros at different sources. In an incomplete period releases occur at each native return. The shared decoder checks its real row ordinal against B, and fills every other required port with a distinct complete zero in the current cohort. These copies are included in both native price bounds. It makes B an explicit architectural parameter whose bit size is paid. At compact H=13000 it is 2^1196000 fields. At high H=36000 it is 2^3312000 fields. The earlier 2^91H *upper limit* on the number of slots does not make the extra rows or their copies free.

## Complete per-use control and per-field cost

There are <2K time instants and <N bins. Testing them and every chronological paid ordinal, including an empty one, takes <2KNQ visits per epoch. Actual opens per epoch are <=2KV and there are <2^19 paid ordinals and <2^16 target bindings per open. In a width-two bin a given target of one paid label has at most two possible destinations. Conversely, a destination bin can meet at most 259 parent bins of that paid label because k/r<=258. Scanning every positive and absent-parent interval when opening or issuing is bounded by 2^29 trials per binding or issue. Across Rz group-rounds, the structural comparisons, raw/target enumerations and event releases are fewer than 2^66 N^93 before expanded selector axes. Each real leaf expands at most N original axes per returned job. The installed source has the predecessor's fixed <2^64 primitive records per raw unit. Enumerating chart alignments, paid or target selections, all raw records, discards and native copies on every real occurrence fits N^96 logical visits at H>=3000. This includes both copies when a rank-one native request supplies two half-column loans. Iterator resume is sequential. For a sequential tape simulation allow even a scan of all fixed-rank control stores on every access. The current physical owners and descriptors, including the B real rows, have at most N^96 fixed-rank control bits: B rows can each record N ordered axes with O(H) bits per axis; uncompleted raw units number at most 2VN^77 and have fewer than 2^64 associated base field descriptors. On updates a second tape permits a complete sequential rewrite, with all seeks and erasure. Even charging the square of N^96 moves per logical visit and allowing two further powers of the store for encoding scratch and restoration uses fewer than N^700 purely structural transitions per *active numerical invocation*. Its actual field volume exceeds 2^N>N^700. This absorbs pure preparation into one field pass. It does not absorb or remove any movement of real payloads.
