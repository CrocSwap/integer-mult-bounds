# An absolute ordinary interchange clock

Fix one of the six chronological binary and numerical profiles in `installations.py`. The retained receiver uses the arbitrary-payload physical charts of PR 7981 and the signed general caller of PRs 8030 and 8035. The unpowered source is the 36-call binary graph from PR 8027. Write `N=2^H`, `J=N^1024` separately at each stage. Count one read, write, or one-cell move on one fixed-alphabet work tape as one transition. Counting the operations of simultaneous heads separately can only raise this bill. Use the installed raw labels, the per-use chronological schedule from `transport.py`, and the serial physical work tapes. This is a bound on that specified sequential implementation. Python `TemporaryFile` seeks are an executable description of its routing and do not define a unit-cost random-access primitive.

For two binary index fields of width `a>=0`, a positive prefix `P`, gap `G` and byte suffix `S`, let `V=P G S 2^(2a)`. With final ordinary saving `b`, the service takes at most

    2^40 ((7·376/10))^m product_i J_i^16 · V (1+a)^(1-b)

transitions, including admission, any cold range, the padded ternary rectangle and all returns. Here `m` in the final product of constants counts the selected ordinary stages. This is an upper bound on a recurring constant, without a matching lower bound or timing claim. The chosen fixed-tape implementation below makes the low-level normalization explicit. The computation in `check_complete.py` checks the numerical absorption, independently reads the raw moments, and evaluates every stage of the six actual installations. The proof of the chronological bill and the finite-width storage conditions are separate.

## Tapes and spectator dimensions

A physical byte and frame delimiters use distinct symbols on a fixed alphabet. Store variable counters least significant bit first with a delimiting high bit. In a sequence of `q` decrements from `q`, the carry lengths sum to at most `2q+ceil(log2(q+1))`. Moving the counter head back doubles that bound. Copying the initial counter costs at most `q+1`. Thus an opaque `repeat(q)` loop uses fewer than `16(q+1)` control transitions aside from its body. Copy an opaque `size`-byte run with such a counter. Rewind to the physical segment delimiter and erase through the old end. The forward travel, return and erase cost fewer than `64(size+1)` transitions together. Split the public input into its `P` consecutive slabs, with `P=1` in the outer stage of each invocation. `root_body` always has its required owner stock in one slab. Write the completed slabs consecutively. This costs one public pass and avoids an operation on a numerical external prefix for each coefficient. There is no byte change to the returned string.

`G` and `S` are opaque positive lengths. Store their factors as repeat counters or binary lengths. The returned weight at an internal row uses `older`, `atom^start`, its chart ordinal and the earlier weight. At entry `older=1`. A descendant increases `older` only by old head atoms and the true high chart. The external middle interval stays in the gap, and the exterior suffix stays in the suffix. Thus no algebraic coefficient uses the ordinal of a record in `P,G,S`. Addressed lower passes may have `G` inside a repeated prefix when both chosen fields are on the right. Restart the prefix counter once before processing the corresponding full interval. Applying an actual `G` or `S` as a length, including indexing its endpoint, is paid either once per whole slab or per complete contiguous interval containing that factor. Binary multiplication of lengths can use the bounded private factor as multiplier. If both opaque factors occur, pay the length arithmetic over their whole combined interval. For positive `Q`, `(1+ceil(log2(Q+1)))^5 <= 2^14 Q`; for `y=ceil(log2(Q+1))>=1` this follows by checking `y<=8` and using `(1+1/9)^5<2` thereafter. This bounds long descriptor arithmetic at interval creation even on one tape. A byte copy, nested loop, or a one-cell coefficient-charged operation never recomputes all public absolute addresses.

At each type cut keep its parent banks parked. Reverse the one split stream onto the pending stack; pop each packet, append its answer, then transfer the one answer interval. Put markers on the stacks. Child accesses are relative to their exposed tops. At the first child of a paid site or the return to its parent the shared compiler backing head can cross the suspended bank. Across all the low packets at this site the parent bank stays parked; intervening seeks are within the previously used child frame and the next one. Charge at most two additional complete parent-bank traversals per site. On separate tapes hold active program/row descriptors and compact saved coefficient chains. For normal sequential transfers keep the head at the next cell. To locate an owner, scan the installed owner descriptions or the full fixed number of bank extents, as permitted in [the local installation proof](INSTALLATION.md) and the retained `STREAMED-MACHINE.md`. A rewind or release uses its local delimiter; no per-byte subtraction of absolute positions on the entire ancestor tape occurs. `Banks.xor` copies both the donor and former destination to distinct sequential spools before writing the destination. This avoids a seek across both live owners on every opaque byte. A stage can reuse one set of spools per named role as in `Arena`. The other temporary spools have bounded call nesting per stage (`unit_scale` has a loop over its limb depth). Fixed names for all six stage sets suffice. These choices preserve arbitrary payloads, all permitted reads, and all zeros and erasures in the program.

## Local numerical control

For a coarse recursion inside one growing stage let `e` be its entry width, `w` the chosen atom width, `m` its number of physical components, `u` its maximum component width, and `D=2+ceil(log_N(ceil(e/w)))` the depth bound. Define

    X = 2^16 N^4 (D+1)(w+1)^252.

The inequality `e<=w^250` holds even on a predecessor floor. `D<=3+250 log2(w+1)`. In the original entered call, the actual stock divisor and its high range have fewer than `2e` index bits by the stage reserve proof. In an ancestor descriptor, the two head/tail coordinates use at most `4e` bits in total. Within one stage all current `older` ordinals consist of parts of those original fields and the paid stock, apart from a factor less than two for the root padding. Alternatively, summing uncompressed lifts gives at most `2D(N^2 w+105H)` additional bits. Word indices have at most `1024H` bits. The chart entries have fewer than `2w+1` bits. A temporary dot product adds at most `log2 N` bits to twice that entry width. `least_power`, repeated squaring, and the two integer roots at entry need at most `2^15` times a field bit width for their positive 250th or at most 25,000th powers. These bounds fit `X`, including a second operand and a guard bit. Scalar `3^e`, complete type counts, residue fields, compiled indices and all ordinary coefficient addresses fit. Unrestricted spectator lengths use the preceding separate interval bill.

One sequential implementation works with the following deliberately loose finite counts. In each list, materialize the indicated compact controls on distinct cells; an indexed access may scan the whole list. All constants below include decoding and operand copying.

* At a new paid head coefficient there are at most three linear inherited chains, each with at most `D` links. Each preliminary or central exchange inherits at most one older weight. The weighted preliminary swaps retain this property after the basis permutation. Each link scans at most `J` installed word cells to get a placed generator. Chart recovery uses at most `N^2` high-digit divisions and at most `8(N+1)^3` elementary matrix operations to solve the `N` augmented rows or form the triangular image. `row_action` may repeat a row reduction in at most `N` directions. `pair_shape`, placement, and the two operand positions require at most `4(e+1)` field lengths per link. Restarting `least_power` and schoolbook repeated-square control takes at most `2^16(X+1)^2` scalar steps. In particular, allowing `2^25 J (D+1)(N+1)^4(e+1)^2 + 2^16(X+1)^2 <= J^2 X^4` scalar operations per requested current prefix covers a coefficient, an exposed row action, a field shift parameter, or a setup of its local schedule. Raw loops counted once per paid site below are separate.
* Store up to `N^2` rows/slots at any solve, one chart and fixed-basis key per saved link, and at most `4(e+1)` coordinate lengths per active schedule. The actual native interpreter can have up to `J` live small fixed owner and continuation descriptors. Scanning even a store with `J^2 X^3` scalar words costs at most `4 J^2 X^4` transitions per access, including return to the saved mark. Replicate a read into a temporary word before moving either scalar head. At most a fixed eight lookups are needed for any scalar add, product, division, extended-Euclidean step or array update in the tests above; loops over a whole row have already been counted.
* For operands, temporaries, and result with at most `X` bits, a ripple add uses at most `16(X+1)` transitions. Repeated shift/add schoolbook multiplication or restoring long division uses at most `128(X+1)^2` transitions on a fixed set of tapes. One Euclidean modular inverse takes fewer than `2X+2` division steps because the residue decreases by at least a factor of two after every two steps. Update the modular Bézout coefficient with two products/divisions, staying on at most twice the operand length. Even treating each of these three costs at width `2X+2`, fewer than `2^14 (X+1)^4` transitions suffice for any modular arithmetic instruction. Temporary scratch is swept clean before reuse. Coordinate counters are included in the scalar operand bill.

Thus `J^5 X^20` transitions per true coefficient/prefix or one setup is sufficient, charging scalar operations times store lookups plus the scalar bit routine. Fixed raw word constants fit in the separately installed `J`-cell stream. In `lower_tape` the coefficient is generated once for a complete target component. The smallest component has width `u-1`. In `high_packet` a prefix for `row_action` still has at least one complete free high digit, while its optional compensation block contains at least one full body atom. In a direct stopped block both addressed atoms survive. Each such coefficient or setup is therefore charged across at least `3^(u-1)` distinct current byte-cells, once per respective sweep. The inequalities `D+1<=256(w+1)`, `w<=e<=(2u)^258` and `w+1<=2^259 u^258` hold on every profile. For positive integers `a,u`, `u^a/3^(u-1)<=3 a^a`: if `u<=a` it follows directly, and after `u=a` the consecutive ratio is at most `(1+1/a)^a/3<1`. With `a=258*5060` this gives

    X^20 / 3^(u-1) <= N^80 · 2^28,726,102 < N^1024 = J,

at every stage after the first, since their H is at least 144,000. At the first stage there is no predecessor floor, so w=ceil(e^(1/250)). The largest balanced component has u≥ceil(e^η)/2 and η≥.00388. Therefore w+1≤(2u)^2+2≤9u². Substitution gives $X^{20}/3^{u-1}≤N^{80}2^{162402}$ using the same inequality with a=2·5,060. Two short inner operations have a different fibre. In `unit_scale` at an even split `2v`, the `carry_step` forms `old_low`, an inverse or a carry from values of at most `4(v+1)` bits per low. For `z>=1`, `(z+1)^5/3^z <= 2^10`, by checking `z<=5` and the ratio thereafter. Thus `2^50` operations per visited byte, including the length factors in the full interval, covers these scalar updates and their copy/counter loops. No ancestor weight is reconstructed in these inner data loops. At each halving `unit_scale` uses at most six preceding ordinary transpositions, at widths bounded by `floor(u/2^j)` for successive `j>=1`. As `b_prev<.004`, the series of their `(1+v)^(1-bprev)` bills is at most `4u^(1-bprev)` before the multiplier six. Odd steps and ending shifts use at most ten full linear sweeps per halving plus one. `1+log2(u+1)<=4u^(1-bprev)` for `u>=1`. Together these bounds pay all inner-limb work. Full-rank earlier-stage calls and same-stage funded children are accounted separately below.

## Sweeps and contraction

* In a quotient native route, `canonical_lows` emits each of the `Q` real charts once. ## Roots, admission, and final rectangle
