## Scale bands and data

Let N=2^H, H>=3,000, L=32768 and A=134 or 153 on the binary and numerical sides respectively. Set U=2 floor(N/(2048A)), V=N^6, K=N^82 and R=N^2. The static scale coordinate x ranges from L to U. Put e=2 floor(x/2) and execute e-8 actual columns. Then x-10 < c(x) <= x-8, and A c(x) <= N/1024. The number m[e] of native copies at each time is the upward-rounded integral of V x^(-1-t) over [e,e+2), using an upper enclosure. At a paid j, for each target i, send the fraction pi[j,i] with y=x r[j]/k[i]. If L<=y<U, bind a real native input at y to the actual parent job. If y<L, execute its axes with the paid elementary service. If x>U but y is in the band, issue an external occurrence at y. There are no upward interior transfers. In each child port fill every unassigned input of a native unit with its own blank zero field, then run and retire it. This is essential because the normalized transport can leave unused target capacity in the interior. It gives no credit for the dummy inputs.

In the program, each nonempty parent-bin/target-bin intersection reserves floor of a lower enclosure of
V pi[j,i] integral x^(-1-t) dx. Source intervals use the same rule. At a paid cut assign different replicas consecutive disjoint ranges for their destinations. At the receiving label assign disjoint consecutive target ranges for its incoming records and its external occurrences. Under the change of variable x=k[i]y/r[j], the density factor is (r[j]/k[i])^t. Thus the sum entering a child bin and label does not exceed its unrounded integral or m[e]. Its outgoing ranges do not exceed m[e] by the row sum. Leftover parent replicas execute the entire paid job. The receiving criterion depends on the actual positive ranks and cells, not on an independent choice of input.

For an interior transfer k*y=r*x and r<=4k/5. Therefore r*c(x)-k*c(y)>=8k-10r>=0 and the difference is smaller than 10k. Lend the prefix of precisely k*c(y) ordered axes and execute the whole suffix at the parent's cut. Every helper retains all its spectator coordinates. The two actions on the selected prefix and suffix commute in that chart. The recipient returns before the parent advances to another operation. No field or data comes from an observation or lookup oracle.

The integral enclosures in `Measure` start from a rational reciprocal and use directed integer square roots and products. The grid step is $u=2^{-64(H+1)}$. There are at most 60 square roots and 61 products for the binary exponent, and at most 20 and 21 for the numerical exponent. All reciprocal endpoints before rounding lie in $[1/(258N),1]$. The lowest rounded base is at least $1/(512N)$ and a rounded square root preserves this bound. The derivative of the square root on that interval is at most $\sqrt{512N}/2$. Products of numbers in $[0,1]$ add the two error bounds. At each loop step the larger of the base error and accumulated product error therefore grows by at most $4\sqrt{512N}$ times the previous error plus one grid unit inside the same factor. Starting with error at most $u$, the final error is below $2(4\sqrt{512N})^{61}u$. Multiplying two endpoint errors by $V/t$, with $V=N^6$, leaves error below $1/4$ for $H\ge3000$. The integer count in each bin exceeds the real mass by less than two. Each allocated cut loses less than two replicas. This calculation applies also when the real interval has arbitrarily small mass.

## Chronological scheduling

Let level(e)=floor(log_(6/5)(e/L)), computed by comparing powers of 5 and 6, and D[e]=2^(19 level(e)). Start all m[e] true raw units of each band at each integer birth b from 0 to K-1. At t0=b+jD[e] issue paid ordinal j, starting at j=0. Close a unit at b+Q D[e], where Q is the actual paid count. For a positive edge e to d, d<=4(e+2)/5 <5e/6. Thus D[d]<=D[e]/2^19. At time t0 the child at d receives real jobs from parent births t0-jD[e]. Its last paid cut and return precede the next parent cut, independent of which other j values feed the same child. Missing negative births supply private zeros. For t0>=K, any parent with a later cutoff executes its whole loan locally. Since (6/5)^4>2, level(e)<4H. The maximum completion delay is smaller than N^77.

At each integer time close all due units first. Open and issue in order of decreasing e, including the first issue of a newly opened child. Different e on one delay level cannot lend to each other. Only a live parent creates a loan. Issue the suffix on that parent before passing its prefix. Consume the prefix once, on that same tick. A fresh zero has a new owner, and external occurrences have disjoint live fields. By induction on these operations, all concurrently lent owners are disjoint until return. This also follows by giving each complete root field a unique custody token and moving that token only to the current one child at its cut. Internal aliases of the physical raw emitter, such as its idle register, stay locked in that suspended context. Other fields with different true jobs cannot use the underlying physical storage before it returns. The last close and the final empty-pending check dispose of all temporary jobs. Each active `Plan.events` emits its own open, issue and close records as the physical decoder needs them. A recursive paid call retains its parent’s iterator and live fields until it returns.

## Price of the complete integer plan

Write P=sum r[j]^t and T=sum k[i]^t. Let Phi=sum_(j,i) pi[j,i] r[j]^t ln(k[i]/r[j]). Both the continuum terminal t-mass at L and the continuum input t-mass at U are V Phi. For a terminal, multiply x^(-1-t) by (r[j]x)^t and integrate x from L to L k[i]/r[j]. For an external source integrate from U to U k[i]/r[j]. The intervals are complete because U/L>258. This calculation counts only true paid occurrences and true external inputs. In particular the independent zero owners used in the interior do not enter the denominator. Phi>P/5 because ln(5/4)>1/5. The receipt gives P>0.99 T, sum k[i] <=129^c T<1.02T and t>.996.

A terminal has actual size at most k_max L. Hence its leaf cost is at most (129L)^c times its virtual t-mass. All interior residual axes at any receiving bin cost less than 10 m[e] sum_i k[i]. Since sum m[e]<=V/(tL^t)+2U, their cost per time divided by V Phi is less than 60/L^t plus the term for integer errors below. A parent's bin of width two maps into at most two bins for each i. Comparison against the real terminal part therefore leaves at most 4W+2 excess replicas per paid label and bin, where W<=52608. Each costs at most N axes. Rounding external sources loses less than two copies per (j,i,bin), each of virtual t-mass at most N. The fringe from the extra two copies per bin enters the same estimate. The sum of these three rounding errors is less than 2^41 N^2 per time. The final N^77 birth windows incur extra elementary axes. Their cost divided by K is at most (N^77/K) * (2V) * 282576*129*N < 2^27 V/N^4. The combined relative correction at either boundary is smaller than delta=1/N for these production H, since Phi>1. The initial absent parents add zeros but no paid work beyond the m[e] already counted.

The virtual external size s satisfies s>=U/2>=N/(4*1024A), since the smallest paid rank is 1/2. Its physical size s' satisfies eta*s<=s'<=N/1024, eta=1-10/L. The sum of physical external sizes per time is therefore >=eta*(N/(4*1024A))^c*(1-delta)*V Phi. This estimate includes both the t-mass and the extra c power of eta. Give each external slot q=4 ceil(N/(4s')) rounds. The first (qs'-N)/4 coordinates are covered five times and the rest once by the ticket bijection in `axes`. Each visit selects distinct axes because q>=1024. A full q visits acts on each axis as the required order-four operator. Set R=N^2. Give each slot floor(R/q) independent arbitrary fields for complete cycles. Fill the remaining partial cycle with independent true zeros. Then qs'/N<=1+4/1024 and q/R<=delta.
