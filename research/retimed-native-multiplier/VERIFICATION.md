## Kernel replay

The package pins Lean 4.34.1 and mathlib `d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612`. The fixed-column projection/fringe and dilated mass, offered capacity, equal boundary mass, timed return and final padding were checked in [Retimed.lean](lean/Retimed.lean). [OrdinaryClock.lean](lean/OrdinaryClock.lean) checks the disjoint-volume sum, contraction and finite stage clock. [OuterTickets.lean](lean/OuterTickets.lean) checks active/stopped inequalities, cutoff and cofinal stripe/stack rules. [RetimedGrid.lean](lean/RetimedGrid.lean) checks the heterogeneous entrance bound and final root reserve. All four modules were built and passed a separate `leanchecker` replay. The combined axiom list is `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. The proofs contain no added axioms or placeholders.

~~~sh
cd research/retimed-native-multiplier/lean
lake build
lake env leanchecker -v Retimed
lake env leanchecker -v OrdinaryClock
lake env leanchecker -v OuterTickets
lake env leanchecker -v RetimedGrid
~~~

The raw source interpretations, exact integral enclosures in the emitted schedule, absolute tape lowering, geometry/source bindings, all-input numerical execution and complete signed theorem remain outside Lean. The transfer proofs were challenged within the authoring team. Independent scientific acceptance remains open.
