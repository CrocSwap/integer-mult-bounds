# Execute a direct line with one recursive exchange and a covered line with at most five

An ordinary source occurrence exposes its actual singleton frame. The old leaf used up to nine recursive whole-field exchanges. In direct mode the new machine uses one. On a true low packet in quotient mode it uses one, three, or five. The number of high representatives, the dirty payload and the column multiplicity do not change the bound. At fixed rank N the quotient volume-weighted mean is at most

    7/3 + 16/(3(3^N-1)).

All counts include the inverse moves. The two routes feed the same arbitrary-input multiplication caller. This change affects the ordinary price. The independent numerical supplier, normalized transforms, signed recovery and the five research premises of the inherited complete claim keep their original scope.

## Coordinates and operation order

For one resident high representative, one aligned column and one cyclic component, let R=Z/(3^d). The column has two N-component vectors h,t. Let u,v be the actual placed factors of the issued singleton. Their pairing is vᵀu=1. Let A be the diagonal matrix of the old row's units a_i. The required map sends h to h+Au·δ and t to t−u·δ, with δ=vᵀ(t−A⁻¹h). Equivalently it is I−zwᵀ for z=(Au,−u) and w=(A⁻¹v,−v). Its pairing wᵀz is 2. Apply these ring identities to each physical component. Reduction from the largest modulus to any narrower component preserves inverses of the actual units. The oracle tests include a pair of unequal component widths.

This sends the head to AF⁻¹A⁻¹h and the tail to F⁻¹t. Next use the same coordinate child as the public empty-word branch on the pair H_k,T_k, with coefficient a_k. Finally execute direct_route(F⁻¹). The head and tail now receive exactly the desired correlated add/subtract with projector F E_kk F⁻¹. Invert the word with its actual reverse-order inverses. Every direct preparation is an invocation of the literal native matcher on the real entire field. Its add, scale and interchange instructions visit each genuine column, earlier prefix and full suffix. Its same-bank inverse direction uses its earlier-rank service twice where needed. The returned basis consists of the original paid frame word and selected coordinates stored by job_frame. Every new raw cut enters a real placed frame, and a selection within that job keeps the same word. The source verifies the 44,825 fixed basis factors, their inverses, the modulus prerequisites, and the magnitude limit 20 on individual numerators and denominators. Embedding changes addresses; extending by a column identity repeats actual factors. The streamed scheduler's N^96 operation estimate already counts every expanded selector, leaf and placed source word per node. ### Quotient source and lower shears

Put D=diag(A,I). An unweighted swap S of H_i and T_j on bare coordinates is realized by D S D⁻¹ on the real field. Run the existing weighted callback with weight a_i. The same weight restores the swap at return. Consequently after any preliminary sequence the primal vector is D times the permuted bare vector (u,−u). The dual is D⁻¹ times the correspondingly permuted (v,−v). In particular each coefficient at a physical head uses its own ancestral row weight, even when its scalar originated at another head or tail. The proof diagonalPair checks this cancellation. Every preliminary child inherits one earlier weight. The central weight uses one. Two ancestral addresses never branch at a recursion link. Rebuilding a coefficient needs at most three chains of depth D_stage; the chart solve and coverage clock already allow multiple such linear scans. For example increasing the old 2^22 scalar allowance to 2^25 still gives 2^25 J (D_stage+1)(N+1)^4(e+1)^2 + 2^16(X+1)^2 < J² X^4 at these ranks. The direct child also reads only one earlier weight.

Let a be the event that u_0 is a unit, b that v_(N−1) is a unit, and p the source's first common unit pivot. The following choices use only the reduction modulo three. That reduction is identical across the complete high packet, including when all g entries above the low are different.

* If a and b hold, use no preliminary pair.
* If only a is missing, try (H_0,T_j) with any unit u_j and j<N−1. If only b is missing, try (H_i,T_(N−1)) with any unit v_i and i>0.
* Also try (H_0,T_(N−1)) if u_(N−1) and v_0 are units.
* If none of these suffice and p is interior, use (H_0,T_p) and then (H_p,T_(N−1)). If p is zero, use (H_0,T_(N−1)) and then (H_0,T_0). If p is last, use (H_0,T_(N−1)) and then (H_(N−1),T_(N−1)).

The last two cases include all sparse edge ties at N=2. The empty case covers N=1; production uses N≥2. Each path puts a unit e of z at physical position 0 and a unit d of w at l=2N−1. In the sparse p=0 case, the first swap moves the head-0 dual to the last tail. The second puts the tail-0 primal at the first head. The p=last case is its reversal. For an interior p the two selected pairs are disjoint. A single preliminary swap that changes neither affected outside position cannot repair a missing unit, so the listed one-swap cases are exhaustive within this dictionary.

Apply lower shears in the following order:

1. For each middle i, add −z_i/e times position 0 to position i. This kills the middle primal.
2. For each middle i, add w_i/d times position i to l. This kills the middle dual. The primal at l stays unchanged.
3. Add (d⁻¹−z_l)/e times 0 to l.

A shear sending x_j to x_j+c x_i sends z_j to z_j+c z_i and w_i to w_i−c w_j. It preserves the pairing on every input. The final outside primal is (e,d⁻¹), and its dual is (e⁻¹,d) since their pairing is 2. The map on this pair is a weighted interchange with weight −ed and inverse −1/(ed), leaving every middle address fixed. Execute it, undo all lower shears in reverse order and undo the preliminary pairs. FiveExchange.loweredReflection proves the exact signs, units and arbitrary middle-coordinate identity over every commutative ring. No entry divisible by three has been inverted.

### Physical dimensions, movement and restoration

For a child (H_i,T_j) let start=i f and end=(N+j)f, α the full atom cardinality, U=unused, G=gap and S=suffix. Its real callback arguments are

    atoms=f, available=U,
    older=heights·older_origin·α^start,
    gap=G·α^(end−start−f), suffix=S·α^(2Nf−end−f).

The coefficient reader removes the entire α^start prefix, splits the next row into high and old, and uses exactly the relevant coordinate in that old row. Multiplying those dimensions by both exposed f blocks gives the original byte volume. In direct mode this is one native owner. In quotient mode it is one owner divided by the actual low count Q. Siblings and unfinished rows keep their full volumes while the parent is suspended. No high digit, arbitrary spectator or output credit disappears.

## Prove the wide average, and pay the direct case separately

Uniform lows mean the Q actual canonical matrices enumerated by quotient_route.low_rows. They quotient full GL_N(F_3) by left unit lower triangular row operations. Every full GL matrix has one reduction with exactly the same number of preimages. A flag has 2^N choices for its nonzero row signs at the successive one-dimensional quotients. The first row's line and the span U of the first N−1 rows therefore have the ordinary uniform flag law. This law applies to every fixed incoming rank-one frame before conditioning on the current low. Every one of those lows has exactly the same byte volume and high cardinality on that occurrence.

Let x=3^(N−2) and D=9x−1. Both events a,b have marginal 6x/D: they assert that a uniformly drawn row evaluates nonzero on the fixed primal, and that the original dual vector lies outside a uniform hyperplane. For the mixed event a and not b, v lies in U. There are (3^(N−1)−1)/2 such hyperplanes. Because vᵀu=1, u is a nonzero functional on each. Its first row is uniform among the 3^(N−1)−1 nonzero vectors there, of which 2·3^(N−2) have nonzero evaluation. Thus this mixed event has probability 2x/D. The other mixed event has that same probability by equal marginals. Both hold with probability 4x/D. Neither holds with probability (x−1)/D.

A mixed event that needs both preliminary swaps has either u supported solely on the last axis, which forces U=ker(u_original), or v supported solely on the first, which forces the very first row to span v_original. Each special subspace occurs with probability 2/D. All lows together thus satisfy Pr(five)≤(x−1+4)/D. The exact weighted sum is 3−2Pr(one)+2Pr(five) ≤ (21x+3)/D. This upper bound holds for every single paid frame without any distribution on the program, input values or high digits. FiveExchange.weightedChartSum checks its linear arithmetic. A direct frame separately uses exactly one same-rank child, independent of any average.

The upper bound is sharp for an arbitrary placed frame. Take the bare vectors u=v=e_0. Canonical rows eliminate their first remaining nonzero column; column zero can be nonzero only in its one pivot row. Therefore Cu has exactly that single nonzero coordinate. When the first N−1 rows span ker(e_0), column zero is the last remaining free column, and the last row equals ±e_0. The exceptional mixed case needs two pairs. When the first row spans e_0, the original dual is supported solely on that first row, and the other mixed exception needs two pairs. Each event has probability 2/(9x−1). If both corners fail, the one support of Cu is interior (its being last would force the previous case). A single pair cannot repair both corners there. Thus the count inequality is equality for this low law on e_0. This identifies the exact worst mean for the stated selector over all rank-one source frames. It makes no claim about the frequency of this particular frame in the complete supplier word. check_flags.py verifies sharpness at N=2,3,4, including every paired frame at N=2,3.

Every installed N exceeds eight. The complete mean is below 75/32. The actual chronological bound buys τ=47/20 within 11/8 bits of rate debit. The normalized child volume is at most (75/32)/(47/20)=375/376 at both required moments. Sum the finite tree by induction to get a factor at most 376. The earlier-rank basis motions belong to the local invoice. [Physical charge and geometric sum](ORDINARY-CLOCK.md) · [Directed inequalities](check_complete.py)

A useful lower obstruction remains. Conditional on a and not b, the last dual coordinate is a nonunit. Select a preceding unit v_j. Adding 3t times the final row to row j permutes the actual permitted high lifts of this low, since free columns decrease by inclusion. The second trit of the last dual cycles, while the first primal stays a unit. At least two thirds of that packet at modulus at least 9 has a nonzero nonunit upper right coefficient. In contrast the outside corner of L S R for any invertible lower L,R and a single weighted interchange S is either zero or a unit. The f-column version compares its extreme square block. Thus one cannot fund every true high with just one such child and free lower passes. The direct path's extra same-bank basis moves are paid on the bounded direct column range.
