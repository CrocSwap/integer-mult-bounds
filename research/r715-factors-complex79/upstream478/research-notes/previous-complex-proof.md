## 2. Borrow an inactive coordinate in the same owner array

Suppose a pending idle Fourier child uses a coordinate set S. Choose eight orthonormal active coordinates A inside S and one orthogonal coordinate z outside S, all in the same physical owner array. Execute the full nine-axis normalized invocation on A plus z. Its endpoint is C_A tensor I_z, so executing C_(S minus A) afterward gives precisely C_S on arbitrary inputs. The borrowed coordinate is restored by the whole operator identity; it need not be zero, uncorrelated, or initialized.

This is different from placing a nine-axis footprint wholly inside S and then paying for its inactive axis in the remainder. The entire physical footprint still exists. Only active coordinates are removed from the pending Fourier child. No operator is moved to another array and no nine-dimensional function family is compressed into an eight-dimensional one.
