# A rank-seven return into an existing line does not repair the native word

The 111-port #8027 graph has nine native downward paths of three scalar MOVEs. Replacing any one path by a single **1 → dimension-one** rank-seven contact, for any of the 111 existing line charts, fails in the fixed-interior binary model below: **999 of 999 combinations are excluded**. The receiver may recode its one surviving outgoing signal and terminal charts may decode their complete histories arbitrarily. This is a scoped negative result, not an exclusion of coordinated multi-contact redesigns, a physical implementation, or a new κ bound.

A literal diagnostic sends the entire first missing return from the top chart to line chart 8 and folds it into that line's surviving outgoing value. The resulting `630 × 630` normalized binary operator still has full rank **630**, but **54 public rows are wrong**. This distinguishes carrying the desired information, matrix invertibility, and the actual endpoint obligations.

## Contract and result

Delete that chain and allow one new contact from chart 1, the rank-eight identity projector, into one of the 111 original rank-one line charts. The new contact carries one binary scalar. Apart from deleting the selected chain, all other paid contacts, their clocks and all other interior computations stay fixed. The chosen line may use arbitrary local computation and may change the Boolean payload of any surviving outgoing contact after receiving the new bit. The old chart-zero completed return may be locally decoded anew, and every terminal may retain and deterministically decode its entire local history. No uncharged information may travel through an input-dependent schedule, control flow or another cross-chart channel; private initial state has no outside input information.

On the authenticated source the first informative top arrival occurs at native clock 6908. From then onward 109 of the 111 lines have exactly one outgoing contact and two have none; no line chart is a final public output. The 109 departures go to chart 0 eight times, dimension six 20 times, and dimension seven 81 times. A departure that belongs to the deleted path is unavailable. The verifier overgrants an arbitrary Boolean alteration at that single surviving departure even when the new bit might arrive too late, and grants chart zero its entire suppressed-return history including later arrivals. Those grants make the exclusion stronger within this fixed-interior class.

The exact proof and all assumptions are in [SEVEN.md](SEVEN.md); the full 999-site witness table is [SEVEN.json](SEVEN.json). The original return is independent of the entire zeroed-return chart-zero history in all cases. These facts do not rule out altered later interior operations, added outlets, changed upward traffic, multiple new contacts, larger alphabets, or new charged schedules.
