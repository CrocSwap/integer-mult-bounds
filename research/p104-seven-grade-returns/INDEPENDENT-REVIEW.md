# Independent audit of the 999 seven-grade return candidates

The 999 exclusions pass an independent native-program dual trace and
low-pivot witness check. The stated model replaces one of the nine original
three-MOVE downward chains with one binary `1 → rank-1` contact, keeps the
other contacts, clocks and interior operations, and permits the new recipient
to recode its surviving departure and terminals to use arbitrary full-history
decoders. The argument uses arbitrary Boolean discrepancies, not only linear
ones, but requires a fixed schedule, binary contact alphabet and no uncharged
cross-chart information. Changed other senders, earlier or extra traffic,
retimed old contacts or multiple departures remain outside the result.

This is distinct from #8041's fixed-history screen of cheaper **incoming**
chart-1 contacts. It is also broader than the separate 4,643-test feedback
rigidity result in one respect: it removes a complete three-contact chain and
releases one additional rank-1 departure. It remains a narrow, fixed-suffix
and fixed-incidence result. Its prospective price comparisons do not construct
a valid replacement and establish no κ improvement.

`check_independent.py` takes `pinned/strong-bit.json.gz` and the producer's `SEVEN.json` as optional positional arguments and emits `INDEPENDENT-RECEIPT.json` using an independent
low-pivot rank procedure and a separate error/data interpreter. It verifies:

* Exact rational calculations check the identity projector at chart 1 and the 111 rank-one idempotent projectors and their nesting into chart 1.
* Chart 1 holds no initial public input, its first incoming contact is clock
  6908, and no public output ends at a rank-1 chart.
* The 111 rank-1 charts have at most one outgoing contact after clock 6908;
  109 have one and two have none. Outgoing destinations have dimensions
  0, 6 and 7 in counts 8, 20 and 81.
* All nine removed paths are real consecutive nested chains. Each of the
  18 intermediate owners has no reference after its chain except one CLEAR;
  the within-chain original reader is present. The checker verifies the whole
  block uses only the three chain owners as destinations.
* For all 999 cases, each saved pair of terminal charts is insensitive to
  the other formal disturbance, has jointly independent full native stocks,
  and has an actually observed final target sensitive to the relevant error.
  It checks that each such target is generated by that chart's lifted stock.
  The original return is outside the complete suppressed-return chart-0
  history. When the alternative departure can affect chart 0, a second immune
  pair enforces constant disturbance there too.
* A line departure reaches chart 0 in 64 cases, 28 before the old receive
  clock; none alters the native producer of the missing return. Duplicating
  a terminal witness is rejected.

`check_example.py` independently constructs the chart-8 relay from the pinned
word and emits `INDEPENDENT-EXAMPLE.json`. It verifies the nine actual chart-1
donors deliver the first missing return, the second outgoing scalar is the
XOR of the first two native returns, and the resulting 630-row map has full
rank but 54 wrong outputs. Each discrepancy is the first missing return.
Dropping one of the donors changes the result. This is a diagnostic, not a
valid supplier.
