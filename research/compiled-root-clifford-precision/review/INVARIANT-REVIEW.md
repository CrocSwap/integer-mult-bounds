## Independent mathematical checks

For each external row separately, the correct invariant is a sum of scalar
coefficients times Gaussian-dyadic Cliffords applied to complete original
root-coordinate vectors. The Clifford and origin may depend on the external
row and occurrence. All initially selected axes, including ancestor
remainders later called spectators, stay in one fixed root factor. A
non-power-of-two row selector changes only the external origin; it cannot
project an active coordinate. Complete occurrence conjugations preserve
this type even when their injections differ between owners.

The single-caller coefficient cap acts on actual entrance expansions. A
completed caller removes only its temporary scalar debt and returns the
entrance expansion acted on by its positive job. It cannot reset a nested
source to a fresh independent mass-one input. Copies preserve correlations.
Original padded inputs have zero origin, while their current fields may
become nonzero during unfinished mixing and can be discarded only at the
completed endpoint. These two provenance distinctions are explicit in the
reviewed proof.

A vector-level Clifford-sum invariant is needed only at completed field
operations and restored returns. An intermediate in-place write mask can
be a projector; its stored components are nevertheless old or completed
new components, each with the same grid/range bound. A consumer cannot use
that unfinished field. Complete C numerators are accumulated before division;
termwise divisions are excluded. An uninterrupted four-step ADD/sign swap has
peak mass twice its entrance cap and resets to a permutation. Because no
child or second swap interrupts it, only one additional factor two is needed.

The compiler's source band gives `n/m<s<=n`, so a source complement has rank
less than `rho*n`, where `rho=(m-1)/m`. Deepest exits satisfy
`e/n <= (r_max/m)(r_max/k_min)^G <= rho`. For `m>=2`, the elementary binomial
inequality `(1+1/(m-1))^m>2` proves `rho^m<1/2`. With
`L=1+floor(log2 n)`, at most `mL` nonbase vertices, counting the root, can
occur on a contribution path; each contributes at most `vL` unfinished raw
contexts. Thus `K(n)<=vmL^2`. This counts numerical dependence paths, not
simultaneously live banks or expanded unit counts. Rank contraction is an
inherited result; its use in the current fixed-root precision bound is the
new application.

For the completed current product, `m=145^1024`, `v=1024`, and
`K_star=1024*145^1024*(H+1)^2`. With `t=K_star*floor(log_N d)`, exterior debt
is at most `4t` and mass at most `2*8500^t`. A Gaussian-dyadic Clifford entry
on the fixed d-bit factor has denominator at most `2^ceil(d/2)`: reducing
`(a+ib)/2^j` and using its equal-magnitude support gives
`a^2+b^2=2^(2j-k)`, whose exponent is zero or one by parity. Its row l1 norm
is at most `2^(d/2)`. These facts prove the draft's exact grid and component
magnitude bounds without recursively assuming a child's numerical format.

The simpler actual format also follows independently. Since
`32K_star<2^8299<2^H=N`, the ordinary depth satisfies `32t<d`. Therefore
`4t,14t<=floor(d/2)`. The enlarged common grid is `F=p+d`; the independent
magnitude bound is at most `J=h+d+2`. Recomputing signed width at this

This recomputation matters: comparing the old and new W alone would not
justify a change in F. Integer multiplication followed by a checked exact

pieces are Cliffords in that same expansion, rather than a new magnitude or

branches require no recursive interval. Partial preprocessing cuts obey the

## Discriminators and evidence

An active-bit projector is a genuine counterexample to dropping the
whole-fiber typing. For `P=|0><0|` and diagonal C entry `(1+i)/2`,
`(PCP)^j` has denominator `2^ceil(j/2)` on one fixed active bit. At j=3 it
exceeds the one-bit Clifford denominator cap without any exterior scalar
division. This is a countercontrol to a broader typing claim, not a defect
in the admitted complete-row source.

A separate exact-arithmetic review script, importing neither the author
verifier nor the compiler executor, checked 49,104 exit contraction cases,
15,872 conservative contracted-rank recurrences, root-inclusive node counts,
the 7,455-bit encoding of K_star, and 24,576 enlarged-grid/range cases. Normal and optimized runs were
identical. These are bounded corroboration of the written all-size proof;
they do not establish it by enumeration. The author's actual compiler
control traces and normal/optimized package gate are regression evidence,
not a numerical execution of the astronomical current template.
