## Exact parameters and all retained inequalities

Let b be the certified complex saving, A the proved ordinary bit saving, beta>0 the complex stopping parameter, eta>0 the balanced backoff, and zeta>0 a strict weakening. Set

    a = min(A, (1-beta)*b-zeta),
    q = a*(1-2*eta),
    c = q+eta/4,
    epsilon = (1-eta)/(1+q),
    lambda' = 1-q,
    lambda = ((1-a)+lambda')/2,
    g = epsilon*q,
    r = (g+1-epsilon)/2,
    delta = eta/8.

The seven margins are

    1-epsilon, a, g, a,
    min(1-epsilon-delta, r-delta),
    1-epsilon-delta, epsilon.

The minimum is g. The identities

    1-epsilon-g = eta,
    1-epsilon-r = eta/2,
    1-epsilon*(1+c) = eta-epsilon*eta/4 > 0

retain the scalar, Gaussian and geometric gaps. The stopped internal exponent and complex leaf exponent remain exactly those in PR104; (1-beta)*b>a ensures the leaf ordering. All 47 strict inequalities are retained. Source normalization shows the full adapter differs from the pinned balanced implementation only in three-stock validation, explicit old-leaf argument plumbing and inclusion of the old leaf in cutoff maxima. No constraint or cost margin is deleted.
