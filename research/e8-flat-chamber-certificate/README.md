# A finite certificate for the E8 root-flat classification premise

The inherited **F6** premise in `complex-frame-pair-decoders/PROOF.md` is now
supported by a self-contained chamber proof with an independently checked finite
certificate. Every real hyperplane spanned by the standard E8 roots is carried,
by a product of the eight certified simple reflections, to exactly one of the
eight fundamental hyperplanes. Their root-line counts are
`42, 28, 22, 14, 16, 23, 37, 63` in the source simple-basis order.

This answers Open next question 2 of `complex-general-normal-boundary/README.md`.
It removes the cited classification premise; it does not reprove the other
finite census facts or improve a decoder cost, physical word, supplier, or kappa.
The method is the classical dominant-chamber argument, specialized and certified
here, rather than a new classification theorem or an enumeration of all flats.

## Finite certificate and independent check

Use doubled coordinates: R consists of the 112 vectors with two entries ±2,
and the 128 vectors with all entries ±1 and an even number of minus signs.
All have squared norm 8. `certificate.json` supplies:

* the exact 240 roots and eight source simple roots alpha_i;
* each root's integral simple coordinates, all nonnegative or all nonpositive;
* all 1,920 images of the simple reflections s_i(x)=x-(x·alpha_i)alpha_i/4;
* eight positive dual rays w_j, with alpha_i·w_j=0 for i≠j and alpha_j·w_j>0;
* every root orthogonal to each w_j, of rank seven, with eight distinct line counts.

`build_certificate.py` uses exact rational elimination. `check_certificate.py`
imports no producer code: a separate sign-mask root generator, direct integer
reconstruction, fraction-free rank computation, and cross-multiplied reflection
identities check every certificate field used by the proof. SHA-256 and Git-blob
pins are checked before evaluating just the source root function, SIMPLE and
EXPECTED_TYPES assignments through an AST boundary. The complete source verifier
is not imported. This binds the alphabet, ordered simple basis and census keys
to the inherited F6 instance, without relying on its classification citation.

## Proof for arbitrary real normals

The finite checks establish hypotheses (a) R spans R^8; (b) the alpha_i form a
basis in R; (c) every r in R has a sign-coherent expansion r=sum c_i alpha_i;
(d) each s_i permutes R; and (e) the listed positive dual rays and flat counts.
The argument below is ordinary real linear algebra; rational sample tests do not
stand in for its quantifiers.

Let W be the group of linear operators generated by the s_i. Because alpha_i
has squared norm 8, s_i is an orthogonal involution. Its certified action permutes
R. The action is faithful: an operator fixing every root fixes the basis alpha_i
and therefore is the identity. Thus W injects into the permutations of R and is
finite. Equivalently, its operators are determined by eight root images, so
|W| <= 240^8; no externally known Weyl-group order or classification is used.

Normalize omega_j = w_j/(alpha_j·w_j), so alpha_i·omega_j=delta_ij, and put
rho=sum omega_j. For an arbitrary real x, its W-orbit is finite. Choose y in that
orbit maximizing rho·y. If alpha_i·y<0, then

    rho·s_i(y) - rho·y = -(alpha_i·y)/4 > 0,

contradicting maximality. Hence y is dominant: all alpha_i·y>=0.

Let J={i: alpha_i·y=0}. For a root r with nonnegative simple coordinates,

    r·y = sum c_i(alpha_i·y)

is zero precisely when c_i=0 for all i outside J. For a negative root apply the
same assertion to -r. Consequently the span of the roots orthogonal to y is
exactly span{alpha_i:i in J}: containment follows from sign coherence, and the
reverse containment holds because those alpha_i themselves are orthogonal roots.
Its dimension is |J|.

Now take a root-spanned hyperplane H and a nonzero real normal x to H. Its
orthogonal roots span exactly H, of dimension seven. Orthogonal root permutations
preserve that dimension, so |J|=7. There is a unique j outside J; its pairing
alpha_j·y is strictly positive. The dual-basis expansion gives

    y = (alpha_j·y) omega_j.

Thus the same product of reflections sends H to w_j-perp. Conversely, each listed
w_j-perp is root-spanned of rank seven by the finite certificate. Their distinct
root-line counts show that none are conjugate to one another. These eight flats
therefore exhaust the orbits, proving the precise F6 premise.

For completeness, any nonzero normal has J a proper subset of the eight indices.
Choose j outside J. Its orthogonal roots lie in w_j-perp, so every proper real
hyperplane contains at most 63 root lines. This also supplies the hyperplane-count
premise used in the existing live-root spanning argument (120-p>63).

## Reproduction and controls

```
python3.12 -I -O -B research/e8-flat-chamber-certificate/check_certificate.py
python3.12 -B scripts/research_package.py --package research/e8-flat-chamber-certificate
python3.12 -I -B research/e8-flat-chamber-certificate/build_certificate.py --out NEW.json
python3.12 -I -O -B research/e8-flat-chamber-certificate/check_certificate.py --certificate NEW.json
```

Default replay is read-only; output commands exclusively create a new path.
`RESULT.json` records the finite checks, 64 rational chamber fixtures with
zero-root ranks 0 through 7 (including reflected representatives of all eight
rank-seven types), and the excluded zero normal of rank eight. The fixture
iteration cap is a test budget, not a universal algorithm bound.

Seven mutants must fail: a missing root; a root basis whose expansions have mixed
signs; a wrong root expansion; a wrong but bijective reflection permutation; a
negative dual ray; a missing flat representative; and Boolean-as-integer data.
In the mixed-sign control one basis root and all corresponding coefficients are
negated, so root reconstruction remains valid: full rank and closure alone are
insufficient. Export validation additionally checks regeneration, source-pin
failure before AST execution, altered receipt rejection, output refusal and a
Git-free read-only replay. Recorded commands and outcomes are in VALIDATION.md.

complex-field reduction, all-pairs factorization assumptions, puncture-specific
cost arguments, and physical implementation obligations retain their own scopes.
No frozen source or selected record is changed.
