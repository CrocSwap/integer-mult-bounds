# Scope and exact inequalities

For the frozen histogram, write

```
F(a) = sum_t (n_t t)/(m W) * exp(a log(m/t)).
```

Every supported width obeys `0<t<m`, so `F` is strictly increasing.
The copied primary method reduces logarithms by powers of 2, takes 40
positive atanh terms with a geometric tail, and encloses the exponential
using degree 8 Taylor terms with a geometric tail. The independent method
reduces by powers of 3/2, uses 48 atanh terms and degree 12 Taylor terms.
All endpoints are rational and rounded outward. Both establish

```
F(63983013240044/10^18) < 1
F(63983013240045/10^18) > 1.
```

Thus the accepted point is the last feasible point on this specific
`10^-18` grid for this histogram. This does not prove optimality of the
network, of its histogram, or of a final assembly parameter.

For CrocSwap's unchanged balanced assembly, put
`a=63983013240044/10^18`, `b=36926111/500000000000`,
`eta=10^-8`, `beta=1/10`, `q=a(1-2eta)`,
`c=q(1+eta)`, and `epsilon=(1-eta)/(1+c+q)`.
The binding exact margin is `epsilon*q`. Flooring that margin on the
`10^-12` grid gives `63974824/10^12`. The supplied exact verifier
reruns the unchanged 47 constraints and 7 margins with the frozen bridge,
checks the PR #97 baseline is reproduced byte-for-byte as a JSON object,
and rejects the next grid point. Its source manifest pins the bridge,
histogram, assembly code, and both enclosure implementations.

The bridge and original physical receipt are not regenerated by this package;
`make deferred-signed-verify` checks the original package. Consequently
the status is exactly conditional on PR #97's finite physical argument,
the inherited analytic/fixed-tape interfaces, and its unchanged complex
saving. It is not a new unconditional complexity theorem.
