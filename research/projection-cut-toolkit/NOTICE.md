This package develops and tests finite research transformations for the conditional integer-multiplication supplier framework. It does not prove unconditional faster integer multiplication. Established maximum-flow/min-cut, rank-nullity, tensor gauge, matroid, reversible-computation and tropical-algebra facts remain attributed to their sources in the accompanying notes. OpenAI and Mathlib files were consulted as research sources; no claim is made to have newly compiled or kernel-certified those sources. The frozen frame graph is derived from the previously checked wavefront construction and its acknowledged public PR lineage; no change of license or attribution for that upstream work is intended.
