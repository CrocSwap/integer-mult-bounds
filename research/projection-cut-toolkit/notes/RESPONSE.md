# Two constructive cross-field transfers

Neither bounded search below produced a new paid improvement. These are sufficient
construction theorems and executable research gates, not a claimed new kappa.
All scalar spaces below are over Q. A "frame" remains an F2 subspace specifying
where the actual Clifford supplier operates; rational scalar similarity does not
supply a free change of that frame.

## 1. Cold-carrier holographic conjugation

Let W be an invertible straight-line linear word. Its background subsystem does
not read targets. Let T be any invertible matrix supported on a set S of cold
helper coordinates, fixing sources, targets, warm helpers and cut centres.
Replace each elementary gate G_i by an exact elementary factorization of
T G_i T^-1. Suppose every factor has an admissible dyadic scalar coefficient,
every scale and its inverse are allowed, and every resulting register frame chain
passes the actual compiler's nested-frame and boundary conditions.

Then the transformed main word is W'=T W T^-1. Its normalized target map agrees
with W on every source, target and warm-helper initial coordinate. Its cold
response becomes R T^-1. Recompute the ordinary frame-zero cold compensation
using this exact response, and invert the entire new background program for
cleanup. This gives the same compensated data endpoint and restores arbitrary
initial helper dirt.

Proof: cancellation of each adjacent T^-1 T gives the displayed whole-word
identity. Target projection and target normalization commute with T; restriction
to noncold input columns commutes with T^-1. Thus only cold-response columns
change. Subtracting their exact linear response removes all changed dirt.
Background evolution is closed, so reversing its elementary factors restores
its initial coordinates independently of target evolution. No clean-helper
assumption enters. Every added scalar factor, frame climb, normalization and
residual must be charged by the ordinary local/global audit; this theorem does
not assert a numerical saving.

This is the sequential-register analogue of inserting dual inverse maps on a
contracted tensor edge. The inspected OpenAI source gives a finite PEPS
contraction definition, not this gauge theorem. The direct proof above is the
actual transfer. Source provenance is in SOURCES.json.

Implementation: gauge_prepare.py and gauge_pairs.cpp identify full-lifetime
nested chain pairs; gauge_screen.py computes literal 2x2 rational conjugates,
Gaussian elementary factorizations and all new frame-touch costs. Only adjacent
identical-frame shear cancellation is applied. At a hypothetical accepted
candidate, cleanup must be regenerated and compensation/phase/dirty-column
audits rerun; no such candidate passed the paid gate in this round.

## 2. Causal representative transport followed by circuit elimination

Write the current row state before event i as P_i x. Replacing one shear by
another with the same destination changes that event matrix by e_t ell_i.
Assume delta_i=ell_i P_i is supported only on cold initial coordinates. Let u_i
be the normalized output response to a unit impulse at that event's destination.
The exact target response update is u_i delta_i; all protected initial columns
are unchanged.

For two events i<j, let M_ji be the unmodified state transfer from immediately
after i to immediately before j, and gamma_ji=ell_j M_ji e_ti. Simultaneously
making both changes gives the exact update

    Delta R = u_i delta_i + u_j delta_j + u_j gamma_ji delta_i.

Proof: expand the product containing the two changed matrices. Its four terms
are the original product, each of the two single replacements, and their ordered
cross term. There are no higher terms because each changed event occurs once.
All three differences have cold-supported input rows, proving preservation of
the complete protected input map. This is why independent gain addition or
independently chosen prefix syndromes would be unsound.

Now suppose the resulting exact response columns have a relation
c_p=sum_d a_d c_d among cold coordinates, with donor and pivot sets disjoint.
Suppose a common nonzero frame E lies inside the actual first frame of every
participant, every existing initial donor frame lies inside E, and all a_d are
compiler-admissible. Prepend d -= a_d*p at E. This replaces the pivot response
by zero. The pivot may therefore start at E without a dirt-compensation read;
the modified scalar map remains correct after ordinary cold compensation.
The prefix matrix is I-N with N^2=0, so its inverse is I+N. Reversing all changed
background instructions restores arbitrary dirt. Exact frame and full resource
audits complete the sufficient admission test.

This is a concrete use of a linear representable matroid: its fundamental
circuit supplies a reversible donor presentation of the pivot column. It is
not an application of nonlinear algebraic-matroid independence. The inspected
Mathlib fundamental-circuit theorem supports this abstract selection structure;
the rational coefficients, causal transport and compiler conditions above are
our explicit constructive specialization.

Implementation: prepare_mutations.py computes exact prefix cold errors and
suffix responses. mutate_circuits.cpp tests single changes. pair_circuits.cpp
propagates the actual intermediate impulse and includes gamma_ji for each pair.
The prime-field screen nominates response circuits; any positive nomination
would require exact rational reconstruction and full physical admission.

## Finite outcomes and scope

The baseline has 292 cold helpers. All 511 nonzero entrance lines were screened.
There were no eligible residual response circuits in the unchanged word, in
117 compatible single representative changes, or in 6,780 distinct-event pairs
with the exact causal cross term. This is a bounded source-family result.

Eight cold carrier pairs have nested full-lifetime frame unions. The six tested
dyadic GL transformations on each pair gave 48 frame-legal words, but none
improved the paid moment. The best changed histogram was +1 rank1 +1 rank2
-1 rank3, which strictly worsens every strictly concave moment at fixed rank
mass. No warm/cold pair passed the stronger full-lifetime chain screen.

These results do not exclude time-window gauges with paid boundary corrections,
larger compatible carrier groups, or other source representatives. They do not
certify a global optimum. No unchanged public verifier was rerun. Heavy pair and
circuit discovery was native C++; rational construction and proof bookkeeping
used Python. No Lean formalization or theorem compilation was performed.
