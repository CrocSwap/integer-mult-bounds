# Constructive dirty-register recycling and continuation quotients

These two sufficient transformations were derived and screened on the frozen weighted 504-helper complex provider. They are not claims of global mathematical novelty. Neither bounded screen produced an admitted paid-rank improvement. The proofs describe executable transformations with explicit finite checks, rather than an assumed reset oracle or free discard of dirty state.

## 1. Reversible pebbling with a protected-state reset

Consider a linear straight-line program whose target outputs do not feed back into background registers. Split the initial coordinates into protected data and warm dirt P, and cold helper dirt C. Assume the existing compiler explicitly subtracts the actual cold response, realizes the stated framed gates, and cleans the background by the literal inverse of its background program.

Let h and g be cold, non-centre helpers. Before a seam, h has completed all its raw uses and g has never been used. Its later lifetime can occupy h's storage under the following constructive conditions:

1. At the seam find actual available registers z_j other than h and g, and explicit permitted scalars a_j, such that the restriction to P of h's current coefficient row equals the restriction to P of sum_j a_j z_j. Compute this equality on actual prefix rows after all prior rewrites.
2. Emit h -= sum_j a_j z_j at a single frame E. Every donor's current frame is contained in E, which is contained in that donor's next frame. The old last frame of h is contained in E, and E is contained in the first frame of g. Every coefficient and inverse obeys the allowed arithmetic contract.
3. Delete g's physical role and replace every subsequent use of g by h. No old use of h may remain after the seam. Update all register indices, entrance descriptions, physical allocations and the complete rank profile.
4. Recompute the actual cold response and emit its permitted compensation. Regenerate literal inverse cleanup from the complete new background sequence, including the reset. Charge every reset, compensation, normalization, copied-state and cleanup operation and verify the resulting whole-owner allocation.

Proof: the reset makes h's current row zero on P, exactly as the unused cold g was zero on P. Substitution therefore preserves every subsequent protected column by induction through the later assignments. Earlier target outputs are unchanged. Differences on C are explicitly measured and cancelled by the changed cold-response preconditioner. Every background operation is invertible; its literal reverse restores all surviving initial dirt. The removed coordinate is absent from the new interface, so no claim is made to restore an unallocated old g coordinate. All retained registers and all data keep their required endpoint. The frame inequalities make the emitted forward paths monotone; cleanup uses the established full-frame convention. A missing reset equality is a failed candidate, not an assumption.

This is a precise reversible-pebble/register-allocation connection: storage reuse requires either reconstructing the initial protected state or explicitly preserving it elsewhere. Disjoint textual lifetimes alone are insufficient, because a retired dirty register can still contain clean source information.

An alternative sufficient completion is to compute the alias's entire protected target error and explicitly cancel it with legal source-to-target gates. The source frames must fit the full existing target and centre chains, and the complete changed profile must be billed. Target-hyperplane compatibility alone does not suffice.

Finite application: 284 eligible cold non-centre helpers yielded 2,682 ordered disjoint lifetime pairs. Eight pairs share a legal rank-three seam: (285,494), (301,506), (317,518), (333,530), (349,542), (365,554), (381,566), (397,578). Exact rational prefix rows showed each reset needs two protected directions while only one other donor is frame-legal; none solved the explicit linear equation. For the first pair the retired protected content is (x_14-x_13)/2. Its new lifetime has six target-response entries, so an initial correction needs 12 nonzero entries. All eight such corrections fit the final target hyperplanes, but each violates three or four retained centre-frame containments. No alias was accepted. The receipts retain those actual failing centres rather than treating the reset or wrapper as free.

## 2. Multi-carrier continuation quotient

At a seam, let h and a finite set of donor helpers S have no later background writes or background reads: their remaining raw uses are target deliveries only. Let C_r be the exact column of the remaining linear target map for the current value in register r, including any subsequent target mixing. Suppose explicit coefficients a_s satisfy

    C_h = sum_(s in S) a_s C_s.

Choose one common frame E containing the current frames of h and all s, and contained in every donor's first remaining frame. Insert s += a_s h for each nonzero coefficient, delete all remaining deliveries from h, and regenerate full inverse background cleanup.

Proof: h and each donor stay constant throughout the old suffix except for their deliveries. The inserted shifts contribute sum a_s C_s h to the final target, exactly replacing the removed C_h h contribution. This holds on arbitrary values of all helper and data registers, not merely clean inputs. Literal inverse cleanup restores the changed donors. The frame hypotheses admit all inserted transfers; h may then go directly from E to its required final full frame. Charge the inserted transfers, the changed climbs, compensation, and inverse cleanup. This proves equality of the completed operator; only a full profile and owner allocation determine whether it improves kappa.

The coalgebraic/bisimulation connection is observational equivalence under a fixed continuation: current states differing in the kernel of the continuation map produce the same final observation. The construction realizes that quotient by reversible shear transfers and explicit restored storage; it does not physically erase an unobservable direction for free. It strictly extends a single proportional-column merge by allowing several independent surviving carriers.

The native screen exported 305 suffix cuts and checked 586 common-frame nominations, restricting common frames to the actual first suffix frames of eligible carriers. It found no donor-span relation, hence no candidate. The screen uses a prime-field image only as a bounded nomination filter, not a characteristic-zero impossibility proof: allowed rational coefficients may have denominators divisible by that prime. Any positive nomination would require exact rational reconstruction and a changed-word replay. The present null result does not rule out other seams, multiple-frame transfer chains, different donor ordering, saved states, or controller redesign.

## Additional explicit completions tested

Centre routing: for each of the eight reuse pairs, compute every centre's protected coefficient row at the actual cut. The alias error direction is outside even their unrestricted rational span. Consequently none of the six target corrections per pair can be obtained by changing the existing cut coefficients alone. PEBBLE-CENTRE-CORRECTION.json also records all geometrically eligible donor sets. This is a statement about those eight fixed errors and those actual centre rows, not about redesigned centres.

Earlier continuation delivery: a second sufficient repair leaves the retired value unchanged and emits the negative complete new-lifetime response while the old value remains available. If the reused lifetime would add C*h to final targets, insert exactly -C*h before aliasing, at individually legal earlier gates; regenerate cleanup as before. This cancels arbitrary dirt as well as protected data directly. We exhaustively scanned positions in the existing post-cut B prefix for each of the 48 scalar deliveries, requiring the insertion frame to lie between both registers' current and next actual frames. Only one of each pair's six required target deliveries has any legal position. Thus this explicit completion also supplies no full candidate. The receipt is PEBBLE-RESPONSE-RETIMING.json.

A target-increment relay is another exact realization of the same repair when its two boundary shears are legal: z += a*y; y += h; z -= a*y changes z by -a*h while cancelling the old arbitrary y value. It must include both shears, their frame climbs and all intervening modifications of y. It is not admitted here: the first shear still has the restrictive before-delivery target frame, and no complete relay witness was built. No inferred numerical gain is attached to it.
