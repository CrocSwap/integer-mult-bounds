# Two constructive resource transfers

## 1. Transport potentials for complete-provider choice

Fix a finite set of physically verified complete-provider variants. Every variant must have the same ordered data endpoint and explicit helper residual action, with its own ordered residual widths and complete local paid histogram. Let x encode integer counts of pending-bin patterns and assignments to legal call times. Let A x = b(z) state all port counts, time capacities and per-call provider width inventories for a chosen integer provider selector z. All variables are nonnegative; each pattern fits the actual pending rank and its permitted time set.

For a fixed recurrence exponent a, let c be the exact power cost of each pattern's paid remainder and ell(z) the exact sum of all selected local calls. If x and z are feasible and the resulting complete profile has a strictly smaller rationally bounded moment than an admitted old profile, then the new supplier improves at that exponent after the usual physical chart and finite-invoice checks. Sufficiency is constructive: decompose each integral pattern/time incidence graph into matchings, assign one matching per physical port, biject actual helpers to equal-width slots, and rebuild every source-bound residual/spectator chart. No fractional owner is executed.

A dual vector y with A^T y <= c supplies resource shadow prices: y*b(z) <= c*x for every feasible allocation. This is proved by multiplying each nonnegative x_j into the corresponding inequality and summing. It is a search bound, not sufficient physical admission. Candidate scoring combines the changed local power sum with the changed inventory's shadow price. A negative reduced score merits a primal construction; it is not itself a kappa claim.

For the frozen width alphabet {5,8,9} and ranks {8,16,32,48}, all 145 pattern inequalities were checked with exact rational lower bounds from the retained moment engine. Floating optimization only nominated y. Each width potential was rationalized, then every group potential was decreased by the exact maximum inequality violation, making the final certificate rigorously feasible. SHADOW-PRICES.json records it. Thus the search can cheaply prioritize profile/inventory changes without conflating local savings and global feasibility.

The source OAI/Combinatorics/UniformKServer/RealFlowConvex.lean proves validity and linear cost of mixing actual causal flows, expressly distinguishing this from nonlinear row normalization. Here an integer realization is additionally required; a convex mixture of providers is never executed as a fractional physical helper.

## 2. Tropical composition of complete boundary states

A controller state records the next source event of every replica, all pending-child residual coordinate masks, the full live namespace, and every provider-dependent boundary interface needed to resume an execution. A transition is a concrete, independently checked segment of complete cover calls and their bridge/idle sweeps. It records its actual scalar/operator witness, source and destination states, and paid rank histogram. No transition splits a complete invocation or discards dirty spectator information.

For a fixed exponent a, define a matrix M whose entry M[s,t] is the minimum paid power cost among retained transitions from s to t, or infinity if none exists. Store an attaining witness index with every finite entry. Min-plus matrix multiplication composes paths: (M*N)[s,t] = min_u (M[s,u]+N[u,t]). Every selected finite term concatenates two executable witnesses because the intermediate full boundary state agrees exactly. Induction proves every finite matrix-power entry comes with an actual legal finite controller path and the sum of its paid histograms. Choosing a strictly lower-cost path, then rebuilding its literal complete-owner transcript and finite invoice, is therefore a sufficient improved-controller construction.

State compression is allowed only with a proved interface-preserving isomorphism carrying all future admissible continuations and their costs. Equal width counts alone are not such an isomorphism: owner chronology and residual axes must also transport. Terminal and initialization transitions remain charged; a favorable mean cycle alone does not establish an improved finite algorithm.

Mathlib/Algebra/Tropical/Basic.lean supplies the actual min-plus semiring identities (untrop_add, untrop_mul). The executable-witness enrichment and multiplication-controller boundary requirements are the additional sufficient proof above. This does not claim a state-space bound small enough for unrestricted exhaustive search; the intended implementation retains a bounded candidate transition set and exact physical states.

## Executable bounded trace-state search

The weighted-state view was applied to actual scalar transitions in the final weighted word, beyond the neutral PR550 transplant. For a Steinberg expansion at an earlier site, the new gates have touches(i,j),(i,k),(j,k). Thus their legal annotations form a flag F1 <= F2 <= F3. Let Lij be the span of the previous i,j frames and Lall the span of all three previous frames. Let U be the intersection of the three next frames and Ujk the intersection of the next j,k frames. Whenever Lall <= U, every rank triple

    dim(Lij) <= d1 <= dim(U),
    max(d1,dim(Lall)) <= d2 <= dim(U),
    d2 <= d3 <= dim(Ujk)

has an explicit realization. Extend Lij first toward Lall, then within U to rank d1; join with Lall and extend within U to rank d2; extend within Ujk to rank d3. The resulting flag makes each new register chain monotone. The scalar identity and the required commuting exchanges are independent exact obligations. Native search checks those and computes the entire affected per-wire paid histogram, including unchanged starts and ends.
