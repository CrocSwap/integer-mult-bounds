# Gaussian E8 nonlinear block-message copy floor

This composes Sagar Thakrar's Gaussian geometric certificate in [PR738](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/738)
with Alexander Templeton's conditional transcript argument in [PR785](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/785),
as also applied by Michael Fischler to Eisenstein geometry in [PR814](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/814).
The methods are inherited. The new coverage is Gaussian nonlinear block messages,
unequal demands, and explicit controls of the pricing and side-information assumptions.

## Model and result

There are 60 Gaussian E8 lines, with the exact ordering and complex structure
of the pinned PR738 geometry. Port t holds an independent arbitrary b_t-bit
string X_t; each b_t is a fixed nonnegative integer. There are finitely many
messages with fixed source sets A_j, reader sets R_j and integer widths q_j >= 0:

    Y_j = f_j((X_a) for a in A_j) in {0,1}^{q_j}.

All members of R_j receive the entire same message. Receiver t must recover
X_t on every input tuple using its received messages and all inputs X_u on
lines orthogonal to t. It has no free self-input. The functions, supports and
widths are fixed before inputs are chosen; encoders and joint decoders may
be arbitrary nonlinear functions. Unused declared sources can remain declared.

For S a line set, let V_S be any rational row basis of the span of the
representatives of S and their J-images. Set

    c(R,A) = rank_Q(V_R V_A^T)/2,
    C = sum_j q_j c(R_j,A_j).

**Additive per-bit pairing-rank pricing is an explicit premise.** A scalar
copy-tree argument does not by itself establish this pricing for arbitrary
physical nonlinear circuits. This result applies to the specified message
model, not every conceivable way to share work across coordinates.

For every such zero-error code,

    C >= max(ceil((2/3) sum_t b_t), max_Q sum_(t in Q) b_t),        (1)

where Q ranges over nonempty pairwise nonorthogonal cliques. In particular,
for uniform b_t=b, C >= **40b Gaussian / 80b rational units**. Direct per-port
messages give the abstract upper bound 60b. No cost-40b decoder or exact
nonlinear optimum is asserted. The nonlinear floor remains an interval [40b,60b].
For demands supported on a nonorthogonal clique, (1) is sharp: direct messages
cost sum b_t, which equals its concentration term.

Correlated inputs, errors, variable-length/average-cost messages, adaptive or
interactive encoding, sub-line supports, extra nonorthogonal side information,
and cheaper physical sharing between bits are outside this model.

## Exact inherited Gaussian premise

The doubled E8 roots have two nonzero coordinates +/-2, or eight coordinates
+/-1 with an even number of minus signs. The block complex structure sends
(x_0,x_1,...,x_6,x_7) to (-x_1,x_0,...,-x_7,x_6). It is orthogonal, squares to
-I and permutes the 240 roots. Its four-root orbits define 60 Gaussian lines.
Their spans have Gaussian dimension at most four.

A line-generated space is J-invariant. Its pairing kernel with another such
space is also J-invariant: J is orthogonal and preserves both spaces. The
rational dimensions of domain and kernel are even, so c(R,A) is an integer.
Changing row bases does not change the product rank. The self-pairing of a
nonzero line is nonzero; orthogonality excludes the port itself.

PR738 assigns weight 1/192 to all 640 maximum nonorthogonal cliques, each of
size 12. For any R,A, its certified geometric inequality is

    H(R,A) := number of those cliques meeting both R and A
    H(R,A) <= 192 c(R,A).                                     (2)

The new driver replays PR738's unchanged exact geometry and calculation;
it does not claim an independent implementation of (2). All 2,272 flats,
including the empty flat, are generated by adjoining a line and taking exact
closure. Every flat has a line basis, so induction on its basis size proves
completeness. Closing a support preserves its span and pairing rank and only
increases clique-hit indicators. Symmetry therefore reduces (2) for all
2^60 by 2^60 supports to 2,579,856 unordered nonempty flat pairs. Empty supports
have zero hits and charge. The retained checker partitions them into 2,725
zero-hit pairs, 1,827,741 rank-bound pairs, and 749,390 exact-rank pairs.
The committed sharp rank-two/rank-three witness still has pairing rank one
and 192 joint hits. Six inherited rejection controls are rerun.

Each of the 60 ports belongs to exactly 128 of the 640 cliques; the new driver
checks every incidence literally. Its total weight is 128/192 = 2/3.
The retained historical source-check entry point is not invoked: its old
selected-record pin differs from today's base. Instead the new source checker
binds every retained byte and current-base dependency in SOURCES.json before
calling the unchanged calculate() function. Original source metadata and
notices are preserved; no selected record is rewritten.

## Conditional transcript injection

Fix any nonorthogonal clique Q and any assignment of all inputs outside Q.
Let J_Q contain exactly the messages whose source and reader sets both meet Q.
The map from X_Q to the displayed transcript (Y_j) for j in J_Q is injective.

Suppose two different X_Q assignments gave the same transcript. Every message
received by a receiver in Q but absent from J_Q has sources disjoint from Q,
so its value is fixed and identical. Every receiver's orthogonal side
information is outside Q, so it too is identical. Each receiver in Q thus
has identical complete decoder input and returns the same X_t. This contradicts
the difference in X_Q. No assumption about linearity or separate decoding of
message coordinates is used.

Counting all possible inputs and fixed-width transcripts gives

    sum_(t in Q) b_t <= sum_j q_j [Q meets R_j and A_j].           (3)

This is an all-width written proof. Finite truth tables below are controls,
not a substitute for injectivity at arbitrary widths.

Multiply (3) by 1/192 and sum over the 640 maximum cliques. The literal
128-incidence property and (2) give

    (2/3) sum_t b_t
      <= sum_j q_j H(R_j,A_j)/192
      <= sum_j q_j c(R_j,A_j) = C.

Integrality of c and q supplies the ceiling in (1). For any clique, if both
supports meet it, choose one line from each intersection. They are the same
nonzero line or two nonorthogonal lines, so the Gram product has a nonzero
block and c >= 1. Equation (3) therefore also gives C >= sum_(t in Q) b_t.
For nonnegative demands the maximum occurs on an inclusion-maximal clique;
all 8,704 such cliques are enumerated, including those smaller than size 12.

## Nonlinear and boundary controls

The separate implementation independent_controls.py chooses three disjoint
orthogonal four-line frames using seed44. For their three parities z_0,z_1,z_2,
it broadcasts (z_0,z_1,z_2 XOR (z_0 AND z_1)) to those twelve active ports.
After inverting the Boolean shear, each receiver recovers its own bit using
the other three frame inputs, which are legal orthogonal side information.
The common source/reader span has Gaussian rank four and width three, so
charge is 12, while (1)'s averaged active-demand floor is eight.
All 4,096 input tuples and every receiver are checked. A mixed Boolean
derivative certifies genuine nonlinearity. All conditional projections of
the 640 weighted cliques are checked, including every outside assignment.

Charging that entire three-bit message only once would give four rather
than twelve, violating the certified active floor eight. This correct
code is a boundary example for the *pricing premise*, not a counterexample
to (1). Similarly, twelve mutually nonorthogonal ports can decode one parity
if granted all other eleven inputs as side information. The pairing charge
is four versus clique demand twelve; the code is correct but its side
information is inadmissible. Dropped message bits and failure to invert the
nonlinear shear are decoding failure controls. The exact finite coverage
and rejection counts are recorded in CERTIFICATE.json.

## Optimistic complete budget

Only if the implementation obeys both the declared message charge and the
separate inherited Dfc stage identity may one set v=60, rational port rank
k=2b and cst=2C. Then

    D/T = (s-1) 120b - s 2C <= 40b(s-3).

At three stages this is nonpositive. Helper preparation, source access,
encoding/decoding, frame changes, extra copies, restoration, and recursive
children have all been granted zero additional expense. Adding any
nonnegative expense cannot repair this screen. Thus this three-stage mechanism
cannot yield a positive supplier saving under those premises, regardless
of which other supplier would bind the assembly. Five or more stages and
changed accounting remain open; no physical invoice is built.

The original all-size/compiler, realization, whole-call hosting,
precision/tape/exact recovery and analytic obligations remain inherited.
No selected construction or kappa changes. Written mathematical argument
and finite replay are not a proof-assistant verification of integer multiplication.
