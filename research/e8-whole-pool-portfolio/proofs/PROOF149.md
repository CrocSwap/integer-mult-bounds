This supersedes the rank137 pricing experiment in PROOF.md. The local substitution
theorem there is unchanged; all ownership in this experiment is whole residual.

Base0 is PR451's rank149 local word, followed by PR450 target mixing and our two

For k=0,1,2,3, remove the first k entrance pivots of [963,970,948]. Reinstating each
pivot's original D-compensation reads and deleting its entrance/cleanup correction
sources gives a complete exact provider of the SAME data map. Each arbitrary dirty
helper is restored. These are complete programs, not pieces to mix inside a call.

Exact per-call histogram difference is k*{1:-1,4:-2,5:+2}. Thus the rank mass
increases by k and the residual inventory changes from
{4:8,6:4,7:109,8:619} to {4:8,6:4,7:109-k,8:619+k}. No discarded rank is free.
At the nomination exponent .0013497 and window78, local excess-moment cost changes
by approximately -0.0008865528910186986*k; the final whole-hosted moment must charge
the matching additional residual coordinates. This decimal is discovery only.

The choice k may differ for each (stage,replica). Since all four programs have the
same external map and full dirty restoration, controller composition is unchanged.
Each call's own complete program, residual dimensions/isometries, ownership and
finite instruction charge must appear in the final emitted word. This provides a
sufficient new optimization variable: select the complete provider together with
its legal whole-residual storage, rather than maximizing entrance rank first.
