# Sufficient composition and complete accounting

## Whole-family allocation

Fix a checked complete dirty-helper invocation. For each helper choose one idle physical
family, one allowed whole-slot isometry, and one interval containing its entire residual
block. The host pair is distinct from the active pair. The interval lies after the family's
last touch and before its named pending child. Blocks assigned to that family are disjoint.
Under the retained universal dirty-hosting interface, conjugating the complete invocation
by these routes preserves its data map and leaves the specified residual factors on those
families. Within the SAME family, disjoint orthogonal coordinate factors compose, and the
complementary child restores the original pending operator. Every route and child is paid.

The replica label is only part of the physical family selector. The argument does not
require a helper's replica to equal its host's replica. Pooling all eight replicas thus
enlarges the integer allocation domain without splitting any helper array. The independent
auditors check actual family and absolute coordinate identities, not just bin counts.

Do not confuse this with distributing the address coordinates of one array among several
independent arrays. Such a transformation would identify tensor-factor action with a direct
sum, and is false in general. For positive a,b, the trace difference between the proposed
operators is `q^(2m-a-b)*(q^a-1)*(q^b-1)>0`. The rejected split route is excluded everywhere.
