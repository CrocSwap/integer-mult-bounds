## What is most necessary to prove next

A useful next construction must emit a literal compatible changed native or consumer word, including arbitrary-dirt response and cleanup, then account for its complete recursive child profile and all adapter costs. For a tensor route it must return actual maps/labels and an implementable recovery algorithm; mere isomorphism existence is insufficient. For physical reuse it must prove equality of the complete operator, not only returned coordinates. For the all-size multiplier it must bind source access, precision, physical bank placement/erasure, stronger primitive time contracts and the outer borrowing/padding wrapper to the same caller. The present 400 theorems give constructive pieces and quantitative sufficient admission tests for these tasks. They do not assert that all these independent application premises have been simultaneously instantiated.

## Coverage and evidence limits

The entry refresh inspected the 100 most recently created and 100 most recently updated PR records. The final refresh inspected 30 in each order, plus the specific source files pinned in provenance and the source ledgers. These are overlapping bounded windows, not a census of all PRs/discussions or unpublished work. Read-only canonical DAG/registered-library searches and selected actual Lean-tree sources supplied precedents; source-only records and old checked snapshots were never promoted to fresh verification. A broad root moment/multiplication query completed with lexical source-only or unreviewed-note hits; those extra hits were not semantically reviewed or used as proof premises. No full-library semantic completeness, priority guarantee or claim that nobody previously used an idea is made. No external package replay, Lean/kernel run or numerical exponent computation was performed for this collection. Actual inspected sources, reported receipts and proved manuscript statements are kept distinct.
