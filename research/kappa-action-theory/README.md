# 400 constructive theorems for changed-contract κ improvements

Four volumes give **400 theorems with complete informal proofs and concrete actions**: 100 native/helper constructions, 100 physical consumers and routing results, 100 labelled tensor/recovery/arithmetic constructions, and 100 quantitative admission/precision/uniform-transfer results. The collection is pure mathematics. There are no conjectures in the count, no Lean formalizations and no exact new κ.

The output is a toolkit of sufficient constructions and charged admission bounds. It does **not** establish 400 realized κ improvements, a new record, 400 globally novel discoveries, or a complete faster integer multiplier. Many results are classical methods reconstructed for explicit dirt, label, cost or source interfaces. Their direct effects are specific: emitted words, recovered maps, complete finite algorithms, smaller certified error/resource bounds, or symbolic conditions under which the resulting complete caller improves. Whether those conditions hold for the current multiplier is stated separately.
