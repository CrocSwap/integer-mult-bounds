# Proof review and evidence scope

Exactly 400 unique numbered theorems have complete informal proofs, with 100 in each volume and no conjectures in the count. Statements and proofs were read by the author and by a different Codex agent. The coverage is full manuscript review, rather than sampling. This is separate AI-assisted review within the same team, not independent human peer review, a proof-assistant check, or certification of a new integer multiplier.

The author ledgers record corrections and source hashes. The cross-reviews record mathematical issues and their resolution. The root also reviewed the collection's interfaces, source scope and action map. Close results with different outputs are identified in ACTION-ROUTES.md and the author reviews; none is counted as an independent global discovery. No source-only DAG entry, numerical fixture or historical kernel receipt is treated as a freshly proved fact. Every manuscript supplies its actual proof in prose.

Mechanical checks validate exact IDs/counts, Statement/Theorem and Proof fields, concrete index actions, dependency closure/acyclicity, stable anchors and sealed file hashes. They cannot establish correctness, novelty, direct implementation compatibility or an exponent improvement. The reviewed drafts and public manuscripts have separate hashes because stable anchors were inserted in three volumes; mathematical text was not changed by packaging. Local personal host prefixes are normalized in public review provenance only. The release evidence records that transformation explicitly.

No Lean formalizations or fresh kernel/transitive-axiom checks were attempted, as requested. No Jev requests, GPU/model proof jobs, persistent-service starts/stops, changes to global configuration, or remote GitHub writes were used. Computation is limited to source retrieval and document/provenance checks. The full current global multiplier, physical/H2/outer premises and all prior packages were not replayed. A theorem's explicit interface hypotheses remain part of its conclusion's scope.
