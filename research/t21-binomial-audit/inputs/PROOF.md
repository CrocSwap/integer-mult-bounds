## Interleaving and the nine-role trade

The initial order expands each original `(stage,r)` to available copies
`r,r+8,r+16`, omitting third-copy labels above 20. Preserve the T16 and T5
subsequences separately. Place each T16 call at integer time `2*j`. If a T5
call originally followed `j` T16 calls, give it sort key `2*(j+4)-1`. Stable
sorting gives the final 189-call order. This is a four-call placement delay,
not additional execution. Every replica still projects exactly to the original
30 source events, with both global tail events after all replica-local work.

`data/EDITS.json` lists nine exact before/after locations. An early width-four
role leaves a cap-48 bin containing only that role and replaces a later
width-four role in a full cap-48 bin. The later role joins two cap-32 bins
previously filled to 26 and 30. They are repacked to 29 and 31; all offsets
remain contiguous prefixes. Thus


and the two existing rank-one remainders remain. The resulting profile is
`{48:6292,3:1,1:3}`. Offset-only changes count among the nine recorded edits.
The rank mass is unchanged by this trade. Every omitted edit is rejected;
removing the placement delay is rejected after recomputing that alternative
schedule's actual windows.

For each idle child, the independent audit locates the previous touch of its

must lie strictly between those events. The checker independently reconstructs

predecessor constructions; they do not establish its all-size realization.
