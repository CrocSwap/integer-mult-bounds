Why this angle differs from the earlier ones: HT §1 tracks only the images pi(C,w) in F2^Q, and those images can rise for free on public ascents (that is the h-term). KJ/RV tried to charge each K-jump to a rise. Here every register (public included) is tracked by its full CHART ⊗ VALUE tensor. A public's free climbs are then cancelled exactly by the dimension its own start and final take up, so no per-event charging is needed.

1 [P] TENSOR POTENTIAL (any field F; charts are F-subspaces of F^n; values in F^W; ADD only within one chart, with scalars in F; publics ascend; owned registers pay ascent; INIT gives 0; descents and discards are free).
  Let S(t) = Σ over the whole history of C_r(τ) ⊗ w_r(τ) ⊆ F^n ⊗ F^W. This sum is cumulative, so it is monotone.
  - An ADD at C: C⊗(w_d+λw_s) ⊆ C⊗w_d + C⊗w_s, so it adds 0.
  - INIT, a descent (D⊆C gives D⊗w ⊆ C⊗w) and a discard all add 0.
  - An ascent C→D adds at most dim D − dim C, whether the register is owned or public.
  Hence R ≥ dim(S0 + T) − dim S0 − Σ_publics (dim F_P − dim S_P), where T = Σ_P F_P ⊗ f_P (the finals) and S0 = Σ_P S_P ⊗ s_P (the starts). The support rule is not used.
2 [P] TOY EVALUATION. X_t: <q_t>, x_t → V, x_t + M_t·y. Y_t: {0}, y_t → q_t^⊥, y_t. Relays: S_j, z_j → F_j, z_j.
  - The x-part of S0+T is all of F^n⊗X, which has dim n|Q|.
  - The kernel is Σ q_t^⊥⊗y_t + span{q_t⊗M_t y}.
  - Modulo the q^⊥ part, F^n⊗Y/Σ q_s^⊥⊗y_s ≅ F^Q via v⊗y_s ↦ (v·q_s)e_s, and q_t⊗M_t y ↦ row t of Q∘M.
  - Relay terms cancel exactly (dim F_j − dim S_j on both sides).
  ⇒ R ≥ rank_F(Q∘M) for EVERY run. In GF(2) with M = I + (even-cell side info) this is R ≥ |Q|, and the per-port witness makes it tight: toy R_min = |Q|. This is toy HostToDec with h removed. 5 [P] BARRIERS for the dyadic case.
  (a) The mod-4 tensor with lifted charts Ĉ = preimage of C in (Z/4)^n is valid (length rises ≤ climb), but because Ĉ ⊇ 2(Z/4)^n it collapses exactly to rank_F2(Q∘N̄), with N = 2M. It cannot see carries.
  (b) LIFT LEMMA. If the charts a run visits admit a dimension-preserving Q-lift ℓ with ℓ(C) ⊆ ℓ(D) along every move actually made (same chart ⇒ same lift), then the tensor over Q gives R ≥ rank_Q(Φ∘M), where Φ_ts = φ_s(q̃_t), φ_s has kernel ℓ(q_s^⊥) and q̃_t spans ℓ(<q_t>). If ℓ also keeps the even-cell incidences and Φ_tt ≠ 0, then R ≥ |Q|. So any R < 96 run on Q96 (asc56 included) must traverse a chart configuration that is NOT Q-liftable along its own move graph. Carries = non-representability of the F2 chart geometry over Q. The boundary part alone needs a Q-matrix of rank ≤ 9 with zeros on even cells and a nonzero diagonal, i.e. the min-rank of the odd graph over Q.
