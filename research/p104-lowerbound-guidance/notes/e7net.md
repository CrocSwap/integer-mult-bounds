Code: lib7.py (shard decoder as netlib Net; labels overridden), lm7.py (numeric LM feasibility for a chart structure with ALL legal relay edges free), ctrl*.py, anneal.py, exp2.py, exp3.py, border.py, omega.py. Not a retread: NETVSWALL enumerated tiny h<=4 sets only (m(P)=n there); this runs the networks-vs-decoder test on the first sets with m(P)<n (E7 63 ports / 49; P104-orth-line 52 ports / 38), using netlib's DX evaluator plus a numeric feasibility solver.
## 1. Decoder as a network [C exact, mod p]
- E7 63: 7 nodes (a dim 8, b=<v> dim 1), legal, decoder condition holds, D=49, YC=DX=49, levels 2..8 each |C_l|=rank=7. loss/junk: window [2,8] fully paid, junk 0.
- P104 52: rows (7,1),(7,2)x4,(7,1)x2, D=38, YC=DX=38, level profile l=2:3, l=3..7: 7 each (level 2 is the cheap one: only 3 rows reach b dim 1).
- Hyperplane sections of the 8-dim label span (omega = odd clique number): E7: 99 of 255 hyperplanes hold an odd 7-frame (omega 7, 31/35 ports); so gather-side capacity does NOT forbid a dim-7 chart. P52: omega 7 hyperplanes exist too.
## 2. Numeric feasibility tool [C, control-validated]
lm7.Struct: given charts (a_k,b_k), F supported on ports with label in a_k, G on ports with label perp b_k, H on ALL legal edges b_j<=a_k (nodes sorted by dim a desc), LM on G^T(I-H)^-1 F = delta on all odd cells. Control: E7 decoder structure solved to 1e-10..1e-16 in 18/20 seeds (2 stuck at plateau 16); P52 decoder structure only 3/12 seeds per run, so P52 negatives are WEAK (4 seeds/case).
## 3. Searches below the decoder [all null; none exact]
- SA over chart structures with D<=48 (E7), 161 evals from decoder + random splits/merges (m up to 14): best residual stays at the starting 10.0 (== losing one rank-1 piece); random-start control at D<=50 did not even reach feasibility (resid 39) => from-scratch SA has no power; only decoder-neighbourhood tests are informative.
- Exhaustive-type one-unit neighbourhood of the E7 decoder, D=48, every row, both move types (b grown by a port-label x, i.e. deposit restricted to x-perp; a cut to a random hyperplane, i.e. gather restricted): b-grow: residual plateau 1.0 (or 12.0); a->hyperplane: residual 3e-4..7e-4 after 400 its and 1.7e-5..5e-5 after 1500 its with |x| growing 130->450 and cost ~ |x|^-4 (border-rank signature: infimum 0, NOT attained; border.py on one case 4000 its: c 9e-4->5e-6 as |x| 130->470). Relays between the 7 nodes were free variables (21 legal edges, slack 7 each) so slack-relay networks on this chart skeleton are included.
- P52 one-unit neighbourhood (D=37), all rows both moves: residuals 1.0..18 (plateaus; weak because control success is 25%); row 0/6 a->hyperplane 1.0 nearest.
## 4. What blocks gain (BOTTLENECK-CUT evidence) [C numeric + P for the reading]
- A shrunk node (gather in hyperplane H, price 6) is exactly a decoder row of price 6 (pairing rank V x H = 7-1): the near-feasible border solutions are the decoder-minimum question again, not a network gain; they never become exact, consistent with GIBBS8 / NETVSWALL (E7 optimum 49).
- Rank cannot be stacked: with 7 nodes at all levels 2..8 |C_l|=7=rank; removing any unit of drop from any node loses a rank-1 piece (plateau 1.0 / 10.0 = one piece), and no relay (slack up to 7 per edge) restores it. Levels refusing to stack: every level 2..8 on E7 (each carries the full 7-frame), and levels 3..7 on P52; only P52 level 2 (rank 3) is slack-shaped, and the neighbourhood moves on those rows (row 1..4, b dim 2) gave no solution (weak control).
- Not tried / limits: structures with D<=48 and MORE than 7 nodes were only sampled by SA (no power); no exact (GB) proof of infeasibility for any structure; zero-drop contraction says counterexample has <=48 nodes. So: NO LOUD result; NET-D56 is not contradicted; evidence that E8-type rigidity (7-frame at every level) already forces D>=decoder on these shards.
