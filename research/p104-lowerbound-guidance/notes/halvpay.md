5 MEANING. The Z/4 cumulative potential stores the half state as already-paid content: the preimage lift puts F2^9 (x) h_p into the 2-torsion at the moment of halving, and the later carries (half + half -> integer, free) cost nothing. The 56 is a CANCELLATION cost, not a creation cost. A potential that can see it must be non-cumulative in the half layer (carries must be able to cost), i.e. charge the U phase directly from the post-relay state, where E = 0 for every preimage-lift Z/4 tensor. That is HostToDec_Q restated (O3: public odd deliveries into X), not a way round it.
