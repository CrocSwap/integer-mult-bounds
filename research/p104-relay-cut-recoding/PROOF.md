## Rerouting a group and recovering the missing direction

Take arrivals $G$ from $P$ to $Q$, one departing edge $j$ from $Q$ to $T$, and a candidate receiver chart $R$. Choose a rational direction $v$ on $G$ with $v_t=1$. For every surviving $k\in G$, send $h'_k=h_k-v_k h_t$. Entries outside the support of $v$ keep their values. A row $L$ that uses $G$ has response $L v$. A zero response means that dropping coordinate $t$ and reading the rebased arrivals evaluates exactly the same row.

For a **grouped reroute**, choose $Y=\sum_{k\in G}g_k h_k$ with $s=g v\ne0$. Chart $P$ can send $Y$ on $t$ to $R$ because all group members originate there. The witness gives $h_j=Y+D$, with $D$ in the old banks at $R$. For another row $L_m$ at $Q$, subtract $z_mY$, where $z_m=(L_m v)/s$. The result has zero response. A row with $z_m\ne0$ must send its value to $R$. At $R$ restore $h_m=h'_m+z_mY$ wherever a later row needs it, including the banks in $D$. Edge $j$ now sends its original value from $R$ to $T$.

For a **feedback reroute**, take $Y=h_j$ and $s=L_jv\ne0$. The witness gives $Y=S+\sum_i d_i h_i$ with $S$ in the original banks at $P$ and $d_i$ on banks at $R$. Send $S$ on the redirected edge $t$. Form the same residuals using $z_m=(L_mv)/s$. The receiver solves

$$
\left(1-\sum_m d_m z_m\right)Y=S+\sum_i d_i h'_i.
$$

For unaffected receiver banks, $h'_i=h_i$ and $z_i=0$. The scalar determinant must be nonzero. Substitution recovers every affected old bank, and edge $j$ sends $Y$ from $R$. Here “feedback” refers to a scalar equation for the old values. Its solved expression uses only earlier banks in the final schedule.
