## What the retrospective predicted

The organizing objective is the **fully hosted recursive moment subject to
semantic liveness**, `Σ H[r] (r/m)^(1−a)`. A register's last syntactic read is
insufficient: later uses of its original input, recoverability of the value
needed by those reads, frame transport and cleanup must all be accounted for.
The successful sequence is:

1. Restore jointly recoverable source coordinates before either is handed off.
2. Carry the exact residual to a legal earlier target correction.
3. Recompute every frame transition and the complete width inventory.
4. Rebuild the whole-call ownership schedule and residual ranks for that inventory.

At fixed rank mass, concentrating residual ranks lowers the moment for a>0.
In the small-a expansion, the relevant cost is `Σ r H[r] log(m/r)`, not scalar
gate count. This explains why the largest retrospective gains came from changed
lifetimes/interfaces followed by physical rehosting, and why merely shortening
the scalar word often did not help.

The local-only fractional packing estimate for these eight aliases was
approximately 0.00171422836. Removing their old owners without repacking gave
only approximately 0.00170575101. The original order forced roughly two thousand
rank-eight bins out of use; the diagonal order leaves only 114
rank-eight bins unfilled in this construction. Those estimates were nominations;
only the literal witness and complete checks justify the reported result.

Earlier local probes in this session found 109 removable scalar operations
with unchanged paid profile, and no gain in several bounded cleanup/frame
searches. They are useful negative observations, not universal barriers. They
were superseded by the source-partner construction and are not proof inputs.
One initial scalar-deletion diagnostic shared mutable lists with its baseline;
one initial conjugation enumerator reused mutable candidate maps. Their early
outputs were withdrawn and the relevant bounded searches rerun after correction.
Neither diagnostic is used in this claim.
