## 1. Claim and exact boundary

Let `L=2^m>=2`, `g>=1`, `K=2^k>=1`, `D=L^g`, and `T=KD=2^n`.
For each of the K complete contexts and each of its g local axes, take an
admitted original-coordinate narrow window of the immutable rational Reference.
Its interior partition has the source's phase-pure dyadic blocks and exact
zero interactions between distinct blocks. The source validates the actual
accuracy, conditioning, cap, ordinary band and compressed bands. The boundary
J is nonempty. It may include original holes. The implemented new
admitted-partition provider also requires nonempty I. The assertion here is
restricted to admitted inputs of the implemented producer; no unimplemented
empty-interior extension or partially delivered context is needed.

Order I and J increasingly in original local coordinates and write

\[
 Q=\begin{pmatrix}A&B_0\\C_0&D_0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 F=D_0-C_0A^{-1}B_0.
\]

All of A, B0, C0, F and the exposed set E are obtained from that same Q. In
execution order the five axis factors are

\[
 D_-;\quad T_1;\quad D_+;\quad T_2;\quad D_-,             \tag{1}
\]

where `D_-=diag(A^-1,I_J)`, `D_+=diag(A,I_J)`,
`T1(u)_J=F^-1(u_J-C0 u_I)`, `T1(u)_I=u_I`, and
`T2(u)_I=u_I-B0 u_J`, `T2(u)_J=u_J`.
The exact product in matrix order is `D_- T2 D_+ T1 D_-=Q^-1`.
This identity and the factor-level numerical estimates are inherited; the
new result is their whole physical execution at one actual current state.

**Theorem.** Under the source admissions, common-grid/radius/width certificate
in [NUMERICS.md](NUMERICS.md), and the precise physical supplier contracts of
the pinned #6899, #6908, #6909 and complete union router, with production
observation disabled (`observe=None`), `CompleteCore.run`
executes all five complete stages of (1). Each dense stage is one global
T-record call. Each sparse stage traverses all K complete contexts using the
actual union router and actual numerical child. The returned words approximate
`direct_sum_c tensor_i Q[c,i]^-1` at the admitted incoming field within the
stated total Euclidean error. Every intermediate sparse call receives the
actual preceding rounded field. All conversions preserve the exact common-grid
values. Borrowed data, controls and metadata are restored at their origins;
one protected return remains until `consume` erases it. The complete time and
historical backing invoice is in sections 7–9.

`OriginalCore.run` adds the actual original-coordinate support gather and its
inverse around this whole packed word. For an admitted Layout and matching
family it computes the conjugate by that permutation. These are two actual
global routes, not implicit address relabellings. The full theorem retains
their source provider, free-block, reserve, constructor and stock premises.

The native global H source, complete Count implementation, scalar products,
codecs, live-operand and ownership/cost contracts are inherited admissions.
The #6895 numerical source implication for the corrected #3709 normalized
scalar is inherited for the byte-identical seven-file closure, as certified
separately. Broad Core execution, halos, chirps, final FFT/CRT recovery and the
whole multiplier are outside this theorem. A finite replay is evidence for
the implementation, not a proof of those premises or of the asymptotic rate.

## 3. Global layout and the axis convention

The packed field address for router coordinates is

\[
 cL^g+\sum_{i=0}^{g-1}x_i L^{g-1-i}.                       \tag{2}

The router's first axis is most significant. The dense source's first axis is
least significant. The producer reverses each context's list of immutable

## 4. Exact stage seams and complete context traversal

### 4.1 A common grid with different signed widths

All public scalar values are signed little-endian two's-complement words on
the common `2^-P` grid. The entry contract already supplies that grid. A caller
with coarser P0 must perform and pay an initial exact numerator shift; this
controller does not pretend a width change shifts the fractional grid.

The dense input width is its original sealed `W0=P+r2+2`; the dense return
width Wd is its larger prefix-derived width. Neither is changed to equal the
sparse common w. Before each dense call `convert_field` copies w to W0 and
checks every removed high bit equals the retained sign bit. After its actual
return it copies Wd to w with the same check or exact sign extension. No low
fractional bit is discarded. NUMERICS proves every completed stage fits R2,
which justifies the checked output shrink even when Wd>w. The real dense
consumer still performs its squared-radius test on the actual W0 input.

Each component converter visits exactly the source width and emits exactly
the target width. Each field loop copies both real and imaginary components
before checking the next end marker. Thus incomplete components reject. The
new complete-field scans, erasures and larger old backing are all billed.

### 4.2 No counter of exponential context size

`copy_context_payload` creates gm raw binary cells by g and m short loops.
It copies exactly two w-bit words per raw address. `increment_address` changes
the trailing run of ones to zero, flips the next zero, and signals completion
only on the final all-ones carry. Therefore it copies exactly D records.
For bit i the carry visits it at most `D/2^i` times plus the final reset; the
total address travel is `O(D+gm)`. Rewind visits only the just-traversed prefix,
and one final clear visits gm cells. It does not decode `D=2^(gm)` as Count.

The source catalogue frame ends with symbol 8, outside the page/header alphabet
`{0,1,3,5,6,7}`. `copy_catalogue_frame` copies until that marker. Its cost is
linear in the actual frame, without a long frame-length Count. Context keys
are copied to the independent key delimiter 5. Neither keys nor full addresses
are metadata arithmetic values. The full context count K is represented by
the sequence of framed banks, not by an online loop decrementing a huge K.

For each frame the parent copies precisely the next D current records into a
local input after its complete key, runs the child, compares the full protected
return key with that local key, and copies precisely D returned records to the
next global field. It consumes the child before recycling the local roles.
At the end it requires both current-field and key streams to end. This catches
missing or excess contexts, incomplete returns and seam length errors.
In the admitted stream the c-th frame is produced from exactly the c-th source
family. The end checks establish cardinality; source preparation establishes
that semantic order.

### 4.3 Literal current-input chronology

After each dense return the converted actual current field becomes the next
sparse input. After each sparse stage the fully reassembled current field
becomes the next dense input. There is no saved original boundary used to
overwrite J. The production default has no observer. The optional finite
instrumentation receives a live tape reference and therefore has a passive
observer contract; its return value is ignored, but malicious writes through
that reference are outside the theorem. The retained fixture observer reads
the external representation without changing a tape head/body and records
finished words only. No observer supplies a coefficient or payload value to
any numerical service.

Within a context the original whole-union code restores the full increasing
active-axis rounded schedule. Its proof uses flag classes and support, not
commutativity of rounded maps. That chronology is inherited from #6908 and the
public whole router. Exact tensor endpoint identification commutes only the
exact axis maps. The error argument compares each completed rounded stage with
the exact factor at its actual input, and multiplies its error by exact suffix
norms. Hence possible rounded boundary drift is preserved and budgeted.

## 5. The second sparse payload is a real complete service

For T2 the supplied E-by-E matrix is `M=I_E-N_B`, with N_B equal to original
B0 in I rows and J columns. It is identity on J. Coordinates outside E have
zero original coupling, so the source matrix plus the unchanged complement
implements the complete upper shear.

`UnionForwardWindow` retains the first service's complete-line header checks,
batch formation, scalar real/imaginary packing, protected return and line
restoration. It replaces the numerical batch transaction with the actual
#6909 BandedAction. For m' complete complex lines it packs 2m' columns, pads only
the final b-column batch, physically transposes, runs the supplied action,
transposes back, and returns only the admitted complete lines. No host matrix
multiply or arbitrary payload callback occurs.

The forward catalogue parser reads exactly the original E list and J mask,
then the four page extents for the actual `2a`-row lift, and four zero extents.
Fresh module namespaces bind this parser into the unmodified membership and
router bodies. The original inverse namespaces and pages are untouched. This
is a fixed page-format adapter, not a conditional choice of host execution.

In increasing E order, compression cannot enlarge a coordinate distance of a
nonzero original B0 entry. If its ordinary band is W, then M's E-order band is
at most W. The literal lift places M[i,j] at `(2i,2j+1)`, so its band is at most
`2W+1`. Its actual least dyadic block covers that checked lift band and 2.
It is O(W) and at most 4a, including a=1. Every call still pays the full row
and batch ceilings in section 7. This argument does not assert that an embedded
Schur F has the same band; broad T2 is outside the implemented claim.

## 6. Ownership, source restoration and historical backing

All top-level pools and physical children are constructed before online entry.
`sealed_constructors` forbids online Tape construction. Count temporaries use
leased cells of those preconstructed pools. Each parent suspends its pool
scope while a child runs, so temporary allocation uses the child's declared
pool. Port checks reject external data/control aliases with private roles and
require canonical origins. Immutable metadata may be borrowed by value without
turning it into payload data.

Two full working fields carry the five-stage state. The local context, bank,
raw address and protected output are additional fixed roles. Each child is
consumed before its roles are reused. On successful exit every scratch body is
cleared, markers are erased according to its owner's contract, and all borrowed
ports are rewound to origin. `consume` clears the final protected result.
Native preconstructed marker/configuration bodies are restored to their initial
state, not incorrectly required to have been absent before the call.

Tape.clear scans the live prefix to its first blank, writes blanks, and returns
to origin. A prior larger blank suffix remains allocated in the host tape
representation; it is not removed from the space bill. This makes repeated
local-bank use take the sum of live frame lengths, rather than K times the
largest historical blank suffix. The invariant is that every recycled role
has a contiguous live prefix or is completely erased. The original child
contracts and each clear/copy transaction preserve that invariant.

The finite cleanup check compares all historical tape cells, after stripping
only trailing blanks, with the frozen initial configuration. It checks origins
and not merely equality of a short saved prefix. The first completed numerical
run exposed a verifier error: it demanded blank native roles that already had
markers before any traffic. That check was corrected to this actual invariant;
no numerical/service body was changed to conceal the failure. Verification
history records it. The multi-context run also checks zero new online tapes
and an unchanged private-role inventory under reuse.

## 7. Complete online time invoice

The following invoice is generic and is retained before any asymptotic
absorption. Let C_inv and C_fwd be the whole global dense provider bills at
T records, including every global H/diagonal site, its native and scalar calls,
radius squares, control scans, movement, return, cleanup and its own two global
permutations. Let R be the actual original-coordinate route bill. Let A_src be
all input-independent setup in section 9. No provider is charged only by its
arithmetic inner loop.

For each sparse stage s and context c let C_cs be its complete catalogue/key
length, `q_c` the separate key length, `a_i=|E_i|`, and

\[
 S_c=\sum_i a_i L^{g-1},\quad
 H_{hdr}=2+2gm+g+2t_g,\quad B=2w+H_{hdr},\quad
 t_g=\lceil\log_2(g+1)\rceil.
\]

Let U_c be union size and R_c its number of nonempty rounds, at most g. Use the
whole-router definition `M_c=g[L(m+2)+2]` for its membership store and its
short arithmetic length `ell=1+ceil(log2(2+g+L+w+b_max+P))`. The inherited
transport bound is

\[
 O\bigl([DB+S_cB(1+t_g+\log(a_{max}+1))
          +M_c+(R_c+1)C_{cs}+1]\,ell^5\bigr).             \tag{3}
\]

This includes dense membership/header generation, dense reconstruction,
every sparse radix pass, non-dyadic line zip, restoration, source selection,
and local stack pops. The DB term is separate from the sparse S term. It is
never removed by an unproved sparsity inequality. Long headers are scanned
linearly; their whole H_hdr-bit value is not an arithmetic Count operand.

For the new parent operations take
`ell_join=ell+ceil(log2(2+n+W0+max Wd))`, explicitly including the global
address and dense signed-carrier widths. This remains O(log p) in the admitted
regime. The parent adds `O(T(w+W0+max Wd) ell_join^c)` for the six exact dense
seam conversions and fixed full-field copies/erasures; a fixed exponent c
suffices for its short Count operations. Context extraction/rejoin adds
`O(Tw+Kgm+sum_cs C_cs+sum_c q_c)` times the same short arithmetic overhead.
The two source bank streams and key stream are scanned at their actual lengths.
OriginalCore adds two R calls and a fixed number of complete Tw-bit copies.
Thus a safe total is

\[
 A_{src}+2C_{inv}+C_{fwd}+2R+C_{seams}
 +\sum_{c,s}\{C_{transport}(c,s)+C_{payload}(c,s)\}.        \tag{4}
\]

`CompleteCore` alone omits 2R. NUMERICS gives the safe larger four-extra-route
invoice; the concrete packed controller establishes the two-extra-route bound
here. All K contexts remain in the sum and each dense call remains global.

For m'>0 complete complex lines in one nonempty round/axis substream, with
a=|E| and j=|J|, the actual inverse block-product count is

\[
 Q_I=[2(2\lceil a/b_I\rceil-1)+2(2\lceil j/b_I\rceil-1)]
                      \lceil 2m'/b_I\rceil.                \tag{5}
\]

The actual forward action count is

\[
 Q_A=2(2\lceil 2a/b_A\rceil-1)\lceil2m'/b_A\rceil.        \tag{6}
\]

Each block product invokes exactly `7^log2(b)` actual scalar children. With
`Lambda=1+log2(b)+ceil(log2(w+2))`, each complete **block product** has the
inherited bound

\[
 O\{b^{\log_2 7}[M(w+\log_2 b+2)
             +(2w+4\log_2 b+4)\Lambda^3]\}.               \tag{7}
\]

Here M is the sparse FftWord tariff, including its complete setup, rather
than the distinct dense Karatsuba tariff. Add the original block solver/action
page scans, transpose, extraction, mask checks, padding, additions and protected
return costs. They are actual calls, not free matrix arithmetic. Equations
(5)–(7), including their singleton/last-batch remainders, are never replaced
by an unrounded `am'` count until the source regime justifies it. Empty
substreams make no numerical call. At most g squared substreams per context
pay the residual ceiling terms, denoted C_ceil after expansion. These remain
part of the actual online C_payload bill in (4); they are not input-independent
source preparation A_src. Section 9 bounds A_src and C_ceil by one common
per-context preparation/remainder envelope before asymptotic absorption.

## 8. Complete retained-space invoice

For a physical tape role r let `B_r` be the largest address ever allocated to
it, including older cleared runs. Count each role once, taking the maximum of
successive uses. This is the historical backing convention of the sources.
Besides the native root and child roles, the parent has

\[
 O(T(w+W0+\max Wd)+D w+gm+C_{max}+q_{max})                 \tag{8}
\]

cells of backing and the bounded short-counter pool backing. The global dense
child retains its own full-field/factor/product/stock bill at the largest
of its three calls. The two sparse children retain their separate own matrix,
scalar, page and transport maxima over every context and substream; one
cannot replace these with a sum of only currently live payloads.

For each union child the transport roles have inherited bound O(DB+C+q)
up to the short counters and numerical child storage. In particular
`S<=gU<=UH_hdr` and `Ut_g<=UB`, so the distinct transport terms
`DB+UB+S+Ut_g` are O(DB), without an extra source-density assumption. This is
a space simplification only; it does not remove the sparse visits in (3).

Additionally retain every borrowed prepared global coefficient/control tape,
the two concatenated context bank streams, separate keys, actual original
route descriptors, immutable Reference caches and exact-rational source
objects not explicitly disposed of. The producer currently prepares all
catalogues, so their lengths are summed over K. Source-page preparation's
historical temporary backing is also charged. No amortized time claim makes
these cells vanish. If a future streaming producer chooses to erase a source
cache, it must pay that complete scan before reducing this term.

More explicitly, for one dense direction with J_word source events, Hc H
events, Dc diagonal events, factor width Wfactor and
`bpos=max(1,ceil(log2 n))`, its retained source code has the exact length

\[
 2(J_{word}+1)+Hc\,g\,bpos+2Dc\,K g L\,Wfactor.           \tag{8a}
\]

There are two such prepared direction tapes, with the inverse tape reused
for stages 1 and 5. The outer route pair adds `4 k_support bpos` bits. Both
complete context banks and independent keys are additional. These volumes
are not claimed O(Tw) for an arbitrary fixed-g instance; their absorption

## 9. Uniform setup and the conditional rate

Use the explicit #6908 source height bound with `u=2+L+b`, source rational and
coordinate height h_src, and radius-description height h_R:

\[
 H_{rat}=O(u^8(h_{src}+h_R+P+w+\log u+1)),\qquad
 A_{window}=A_{ref}+O(u^8 H_{rat}^4+u^2w).                 \tag{9}
\]

Its matrix storage is `O(u^2 H_rat+C_ref)`. Charge the actual source carving or
admitted-partition validation, complete E/J generation, both source factors,
all physical page preparation and serialization. The literal lift has size
2a, so it changes this polynomial envelope by a constant-degree factor. All
source matrices and rational pivots in this setup are input-independent.
The dense source's complete raw factor construction, coefficient bit heights,
global source word and control setup remain its separate #6899 source bill.

There are at most g distinct axis source keys for one delivered context. A
loose sufficient uniform preparation/remainder envelope for that context is

\[
 2^{O(ell_{original})}\operatorname{poly}(g,L,b,P,w,n,J_{word},h_R).
                                                                    \tag{10}
\]

J_word is the dense source word length. The polynomial has a fixed degree
independent of c and K. It covers exact source generation and list/matrix
simulation, page and code writes, every catalogue/key copy and reset, the
g-squared ceiling remainders and retained source backing. Complete catalogue
lengths and emitted controls are bounded by their charged construction writes.
No unbounded supplied catalogue is declared free. The source part bounds
A_src; the g-squared online ceiling part bounds C_ceil separately.

One must multiply (10) by K, not apply a local theorem to the full field by
changing notation. A fixed-tape implementation can process independently
bounded context descriptions sequentially, emit their prepared blocks, and
make the polynomially many stage-major sequential passes required by the
global dense code. It can first determine common maxima by a sequential
short-metadata pass, then regenerate each context at that allocation. This
costs K times a polynomial per-context envelope. It does not simulate one
unbounded K-entry random-access object store with an unmentioned K-squared
scan. The Python source producer materializes those input-independent arrays;
the displayed constructive sequential simulation is the setup tariff being
invoked. The online physical bodies do not use those arrays.

For the conservative retained #6899 source family, `g=Theta(p^beta)`,
`L=p^O(1)` rounded dyadically, `ell_original=O(p^(1-epsilon))`, and all
remaining quantities inside (10) are polynomial in p. Thus its logarithm is
`O(p^(1-epsilon)+log p)`, whereas `log D=Theta(p^beta log p)`.
If `beta>1-epsilon`, K times this preparation/remainder envelope is o(Tw),
uniformly in source keys. This absorbs the separately charged A_src and C_ceil.
This statement applies only to delivered complete contexts. It does not
assert that edge/truncated microboxes are delivered or padded at that cost.

NUMERICS retains the source boundary density and W regime explicitly and
checks a favorable complete family with beta=39/40, xi=15/16 and
epsilon=1-10^-30. The sparse scalar is FftWord, giving the complete sparse
power below epsilon-1/32 from `7^16<2^45`; the distinct dense Karatsuba and
support-route powers remain the actually inherited historical ones. Three
global dense calls, both windows, all ceil remainders, setup and exact scans
fit that conditional bill. Native constructor/stock guards remain positive
admissions.

Crucially the dense acceleration needs global `n=Theta(P)`. Here all three
dense calls cover T records. Separate D-record dense calls with
`log D=o(p)` cannot inherit that rate at P=Theta(p). Outside its production
premises retain equation (4) unchanged. Neither the finite K1/K8 witness nor
this compatibility corollary identifies the old native rate with the selected
.0034 BIT regime or proves a new kappa.

## 10. Evidence and limits

The actual complete K1/g1/L2 physical replay executes 189 dense source events
and both real sparse services with P242, w264, W0 253 and Wd362. Normal and
optimized Python receipts agree on all scientific fields and read/write/move
counts. Its Q is nonidentity with genuine off-diagonal couplings; its scalar
interior A equals 1. Dense J drift happens to be zero in this fixture. It is
not evidence of nonidentity dense interior numerical performance or of a
general no-drift property.

The separate K2/g2/L4 semantic check executes all 2,553 emitted dense events
and actual staged sparse value algorithms with four nonidentity A2 interiors,
asymmetric source contexts and a direct original-Q tensor-inverse target. It
is labelled semantic and is not counted as physical tape execution. The
independent physical receipt observer regenerates the exact Reference and
dense source certificates and checks all five actual-input transitions.
It also compares every physical stage bitwise with independently interpreted
emitted H/diagonal words and independently inverted staged sparse blocks.

Independent transport tests use a separate tape representation and enumerate
signed widths, local raw addresses, complete context splits/rejoins and source
frames. Their Count replacement is observer metadata only, not evidence for
the inherited Count tariff. Independent original-layout tests execute the
actual outer wrapper and global routers with an explicit semantic Core double;
they check both routes and adverse omissions, without a numerical claim.
The extended original-coordinate physical replay is separately recorded with
its exact scope and runtime; it is not silently included in a bounded hook.

The executable checks, failed-check history, exact receipts and source
compatibility audit are detailed in [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md).
Finite checks do not substitute for the preceding invariants or inherited
provider proofs. The open steps are attachment to actual halo/context delivery,

narrow finite-Core theorem.
