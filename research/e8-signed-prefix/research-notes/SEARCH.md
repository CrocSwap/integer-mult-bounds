# Bounded search and transferable mechanisms

The frozen public sweep covers the PR catalogue through 393, new/updated discussion and changed sources for the current candidates. It does not claim every line of every historical vendored source was reread. All 393 titles were catalogued; downloaded updates are pinned locally. Unchanged public full verification pipelines were not rerun.

## Realized contribution

Signed target-prefix restoration: 154,938 candidate tests, 2,694 nested legal trials, 49 positive ordered pairs. The selected 25 pairs have distinct donors; 16 coefficients are +1 and 9 are -1. Discovery used C++ floating scores only as a nomination filter; exact rational reconstruction and full admission determine the result.

## Broader routes tested and dropped

- Two-donor prefixes: 661,032 candidates, 477,684 nested legal, 2,466 locally negative cost changes; none beat the best single donor.
- Interval target shears on the signed prefix word: 6,520,272 attempts, 44,664 nested and read-safe candidates, zero positive improvements.
- Normalized exact clean-row quotient tokens: no extra gain beyond the same 49 pairs and 25 selected rewrites.
- Cut-local helper readout: 37,109 exact clean-row matches, 19,713 nested paid replacements, zero positive improvements. Helper frame detours were charged. Only the existing helper/target cut frames were tested; this is not an exhaustion of arbitrary new frames or helper combinations.
- Source-dual shears: an exact scalar rewrite passed 1,023 dirty columns and eight retained totals, saving two primitive additions. No paid frame/physical admission was established; it contributes zero to the reported kappa.

## Structural retrieval

Five bounded structural queries ran against the canonical database containing 8,047,121 operations (243,925 typed). Transvection and commuting nilpotent identities supply useful sufficient algebra. Retrieved source-only matches were not kernel-certified here; a capped query is not an exhaustive search of all library mathematics. No new Lean formalization was performed.

## Useful sufficient route for future workers

The clean quotient theorem in `clean-quotient-proof.md` allows changes in helper-dirt response K when the entire clean X-to-Y map and Y-to-Y identity block remain fixed and the source/helper subword is unchanged. Its zero-X projection Q gives explicit compensation in both orientations. This can justify a broader synthesis search; it is not an assumption of an unknown correction oracle. The current priced construction preserves K and does not need this extension.

Every negative result here is limited to its enumerated family and fixed source. None is a universal impossibility theorem or an added exponent gain.
