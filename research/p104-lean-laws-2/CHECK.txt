METHOD (round 1 of this package)
 * Newly staged kernel-only files: each .lean compiled ALONE, in dependency order, one lean process at a time, `lean -j1 -o out/Nov15/X.olean Nov15/X.lean`, under a 3.5 GB RSS watcher
   (kill if exceeded; none triggered). Fresh output directory first on LEAN_PATH, then the pinned Mathlib packages, then an existing build of the already-released first package's modules
   and of the generated V2FloorKernel chunk files that were NOT recompiled here.
 * Compiled here: all 41 non-chunk new files (the laws, the specs, Budget, FreeEnd, the V2FloorKernel support/data/base files, and the two final theorem files V2FloorKernel_j and V2FloorKernel_b) and a SAMPLE of 20 of the 536
   generated chunk/base files (10 joint, 10 beta: E_*_a/_b and F_* slices). V2FloorKernel_j / V2FloorKernel_b import all chunk modules; for the other chunks the olean from the full build below was used.
 * Full build of all 539 V2FloorKernel* files (every chunk, base, data, cost and final file): see the log at the end of this file (rows are: file, rc, wall time, peak RSS in MB, error count). Every row rc=0, 0 errors,
   every peak <= 3,402 MB, 5,963 s summed lean time. (The two data files V2FloorKernelData_j/_b were built earlier and appear as SKIP; they were recompiled in this round, rows below.)
 * Axioms: for each compiled module, the union of axioms of all theorems declared in it (Lean.collectAxioms), and the headline theorems individually.
   Union over everything below: {propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound}; NO Lean.ofReduceBool, no sorry (the only occurrences of the word are in comments saying "sorry-free").
 * NOT rebuilt here: native/V2FloorNativeBeta.lean (verified earlier build, the run takes over an hour). The lake route (`lake build`, which would recompile ~620 files, about an hour) was not run.
