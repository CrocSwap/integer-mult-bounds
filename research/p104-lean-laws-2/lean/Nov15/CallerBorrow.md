The commutation FORCES `Kq pi = 0` and `pi U Lq = 0` (`OSData.forced`) and the sheared prefix `g pp(N) g^-1 = U (F0 + J)` commutes with pi (`OSData.os`, `near_side`). 3. **GS (R4 §1) with [p,KL]=0**: the algebra is formal (`gs_comm`, `gs_zp`, `gs_pz`, `gs_pp`), but the extraction of the seven block identities from `[P, pi] = 0` is only done by hand + numerics (gs_check.py: at (z,pre,suf) = (2,3,2), on the FULL solution space of `[G P' g, pi] = 0` with P' of the prefix shape, `[P', pi] = 0` holds whenever `[p,KL] = 0`, and FAILS (commutator ~2-3) for generic K,L with p not commuting). So the hypothesis cannot be dropped in the near-near (non-OS) case; it is consistent with R5 that E4** only lives where BOTH sides of the stage carry non-stage units.
