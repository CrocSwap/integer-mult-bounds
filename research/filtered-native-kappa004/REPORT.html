Observer Limits and Physical Costs in Native Constructions

This report offers tools and scoped findings for researchers trying to improve the integer-multiplication exponent saving κ. Its main contributions are ways to screen proposed reuse mechanisms, identify what a repair must change, and connect a local operator improvement to the full cost budget. No new κ is established.

The work extends the recovered filtered_native packet. It combines exact phase-aware history analysis, an observer quotient with rational certificates, literal program-to-invoice checks, exploratory feedback searches, and a conditional arithmetic budget. The practical value is to make subsequent construction attempts more selective and easier to verify.

The repository baseline at the pinned research snapshot was the conditional κ = 0.00302015 construction in PR4470. The findings are author-checked scoped research prepared for public review; they do not update the selected frontier. These findings have different strengths. The support argument concerns a fixed whole-field macro grammar. The observer exclusions concern specified finite families in a necessary quotient. The MILP and active-feedback runs are search results. None gives a universal barrier to increasing κ.

A cheaper native word comes with a repair problem

The recovered packet builds on the center-free coded-producer direction in PR4929. It uses

P = RᵀR/56, A = I − 14P, C_F = A + (I−A)F.

Deleting eight H8 centers reduces the paid inventory, but changes the native endpoint. The defect factors through the eight R modes. This gives a concrete repair target: recover or otherwise handle those modes while preserving the arbitrary original inputs and independent helper outputs.

For another researcher, the useful interface is the changed operator together with its complete input/output contract. A proposed observer, inverse code, or caller should be checked against that interface before its rank savings are included in an exponent calculation. A repair may consume the entire apparent saving.

The predecessor's paired inverse codes, causal companion, even-head resolvent, and encoded-mode controls remain verified components worth reusing. They do not discharge the missing odd-Q ordinary-caller obligation. Algebraic inverses and physical implementations must be connected through the actual phase and frame conventions.

Screen passive reuse with physical support

history_grade_probe.py replays all 632 independent original amplitudes, including 408 independent helpers, using the actual phase-sensitive K_G operators and conjugate raw-dual canonical frames. It checks the original endpoints and owned-zero return, and observes 21,480 row snapshots.

Among 2,965 distinct source-X physical terms, every Clifford grade is at most 8. A snapshot has at most 24 source-X terms in total. By contrast, the X coefficient needed for the following thin query has grade 9 at each of the 112 ports.

For column width f, a grade-r Clifford coefficient has at most 2^(rf) nonzero entries per row. Monomial recoding preserves row support. A sum of M recoded snapshots therefore has row support at most 24M·2^(8f) in a fixed X input column, whereas the required coefficient has exactly 2^(9f) nonzeros per row. Equality requires 24M ≥ 2^f.

If an expression is built from snapshot inputs using L whole-field binary additions, it has at most 2^L leaves, giving L ≥ f − log₂24. A fixed finite number of these operations cannot supply the following thin query at every width.

Research implication. For this unchanged history, passive whole-field linear combinations and monomial recodings cannot provide that query using a constant number of whole-field operations. A candidate must change an assumption of this argument, for example by using a new paid provider, a changed native word, or a different operation grammar. The argument does not bound arbitrary coordinate algorithms or multiplication. It also does not exclude every form of cache reuse.

The reusable technique is a physical grade census followed by a support comparison. A census based only on the binary incidence matrix G would not justify this conclusion; the K_G phase and raw-dual frame conventions matter. The implementation can serve as a template for screening a different history, with its own endpoint checks and coefficient census.

A separate bounded alignment screen, snapshot_probe.py, considers 424 rank-eight snapshot arrivals and native-word recodings. Each port has three independent eligible candidates; none recovers the target at any of the 112 ports. Native-Y exit positive controls pass. This exact result is limited to that recoding family and does not exclude additional group relations or more general ordinary recodings.

Use observer quotients to test repair ideas cheaply

The changed, center-free backward word can first be examined in the necessary specialization K_G |0⟩ = |0⟩. The observer probe keeps all 632 original amplitudes and the 408 independent helpers, checks the changed endpoint in this specialization, and grants full-frame snapshots as free side information. That side-information span has rank 482; eight R-mode directions remain missing.

This relaxation supports inexpensive tests before attempting a full physical operator identity. Its exact subspaces are exported in evidence/observer-subspaces.json.gz for other searches. A quotient-level candidate still needs phase-aware physical recovery, chronological access, helper preservation, and a complete invoice.

Individually clean observations

observer_probe.py examines 1,001 non-full contact frames. At ranks 1–8, no same-frame scalar gather individually supplies a pure missing-mode combination modulo the free side information. Ground-frame gathers recover all eight modes, but their full lifts cost rank mass 8·9 = 72, exceeding the removed mass 64.

This exact optimum applies to the individually clean lift family. It does not settle jointly decoded noisy observations. Its practical use is to prevent repeating a clean-observer search over the same contacts without changing the model.

Two noisy observations, with exact certificates

exact_pair_check.py --maximum-cost 8 examines 204,291 pairs from 871 contact frames, allowing arbitrary same-frame gathers and a summed full-lift rank at most eight. None yields a missing-mode direction in the necessary quotient.

For 202,749 pairs, a full-rank minor over F65537 proves that the nuisance projection is injective over Q. The remaining 1,542 pairs are checked by exact rational elimination. A deliberately planted joint-noise cancellation passes the positive control. The earlier budget-four calculation is retained too.

Research implication. Two-frame repairs within this cost budget are excluded in this family. Researchers can turn to other timings, three or more frames, larger budgets, or changed sources without treating those possibilities as already ruled out. The modular-minor shortcut plus rational fallback is also a reusable way to turn a fast modular screen into an exact finite certificate. A negative modular search alone would not provide that certificate.

Wider joint searches remain exploratory

observer_milp.py searches 986 frame selectors and 2,312 continuous row coefficients. With coefficient bound 32 and total lift rank budget eight, HiGHS reports infeasible for each of the eight target modes. A budget-nine positive control reconstructs over Q.

This is bounded numerical search evidence. It can help choose follow-up experiments, but it is not an unrestricted rational infeasibility proof and does not extend the exact two-frame exclusion to arbitrary collections of frames. The retained solver inputs and subspaces make that boundary explicit.

Bind literal programs to the claimed rank inventory

repeated_head_probe.py provides a concrete review example at PR5270, pinned head df9e5037587ce0cfbd23c2a944515526b301e820. Its two source files are retained byte-for-byte in inputs/. The probe traces the actual listing and dispatch with empty payloads.

Every LETTER and IDLE uses the same range(9), while the invoice credits fresh eight-axis stage blocks and a maximum target rank of 153. The p19 middle IDLEs execute 4,032 rank-nine field jobs, whereas their thinned invoice contribution is zero. Equality to a reference using the same reused head demonstrates gauge equivalence; it does not establish those target ranks or paid-job omissions.

There is a separate adaptation cost: turning native D into R = DQ by the direct two-bank post-Q recipe costs 4,256 H1 jobs at p19. Even a relaxation that grants H9 main targets and discards every idle charge has rank-mass surplus −2,240 after that direct upgrade. This applies to that explicit recipe, not every possible redesigned caller.

Research implication. A supplier certificate should connect its literal coordinate maps, its target operators, and every charged operation. Agreement between two programs sharing the same coordinate reuse does not establish an independent-stage invoice. This check is useful for other controller proposals, including ones with different pair counts or stronger headline savings.

Explore feedback while retaining its physical obligations

The active-feedback experiments allow selected-mode controls on six original banks plus one or two owned scratch banks. They test 128 width-two sectors with seven independent stage signs and one filtered stage. Three starts with one scratch bank and three with two banks plus internal selected feedback found no numerical hit.

These runs do not exclude feedback repair. Their reusable contribution is a small discovery model and recorded controls/residuals for a mechanism that is broader than passive observation. A promising continuation would change the physical contacts or observer timing, reconstruct any numerical hit over Q, and then replay every arbitrary input column with the actual phases. Owned restoration and ordinary work remain part of the cost contract.

Relevant primary literature suggests directions without supplying that compilation automatically:

  • Vinciguerra, An Operator Approach to Register Programs for Catalytic Computing (2026) treats closed operator loops and restoration, motivating actively written owned-bank feedback. Its register operations still need a compilation into the repository's Gaussian ABI and cost model.
  • Alekseev, Filmus, Mertz, Smal and Vinciguerra, Catalytic Computing and Register Programs Beyond Log-Depth (MFCS 2025) provides a restoration/register-program perspective relevant to that search.
  • Alman and Li, Kronecker Powers, Orthogonal Vectors, and the Asymptotic Spectrum (2025) is relevant to spectrum and depth-two rebalancing obstructions. Such results do not settle this changed operator endpoint or its tape model.

Translate a local saving into a joint κ budget

For a proposed saving κ, the raw inventory test compares

M(κ) = Σ_r T_r r^(1−κ) − Σ_r P_r r^(1−κ).

This is a useful first arithmetic screen once the target inventory T and paid inventory P are justified. If a fixed caller has sixteen backward invocations, replacing s rank-b centers per backward call by one H1 each increases this raw margin by 16s·(b^(1−κ) − 1), provided every endpoint and helper target is preserved. A changed endpoint requires a new inventory or a separately charged repair before this formula can be used.

acceptance_budget.py connects a hypothetical local replacement to higher moments, both charged tails, a logarithmic compiler, an ordinary BIT cover, and 54 strict plus four functional outer checks. independent_budget_check.py uses integer roots to check the raw margins independently of the retained logarithm/exponential engine. The retained scripts implement one fixed conditional invoice; they are reference implementations to adapt for another candidate, not a general parameter-search interface. Their evidence records retain the numerical parameters and unfulfilled implementation obligations.

Research implication. Compare repair ideas using their full marginal cost, then track which of the complex and BIT branches limits the joint bound. A complex improvement may leave the joint κ unchanged when the BIT branch is insufficient. Favorable moments alone do not establish a new supplier: the physical-word, arbitrary-input, phase/all-width, helper, ordinary-work, and precision/tape obligations remain separate.

Open next questions

  1. Can an observer be injected before later paid jobs? The end-only families above leave this route open. First attach a selected observer before a later climb and replay all 632 arbitrary columns with actual K_G phases. Progress requires a chronological repair word and its complete paid/ordinary inventory.
  2. Which BIT changes would complement a proposed complex gain? First build the analogous changed-source DAG and lower-frame observability data for BIT, then evaluate both branches against the candidate joint saving. Progress requires an endpoint-preserving BIT program and a charged account of global Q adaptation; local mass savings alone have already proved misleading.
  3. Can a broader observer or feedback grammar recover the missing modes? First test changed contacts, earlier timing, or more than two contact frames in the exported quotient. A surviving candidate needs rational reconstruction, full phase-aware replay, ownership restoration, and an ordinary-work budget. The numerical no-hit runs do not resolve this question.
  4. Can a coded-reverse caller use genuinely fresh stage coordinates? First list the coordinate map for every LETTER and IDLE and price D-to-R adaptation. Progress requires a physical fresh-stage word and a target/paid recount; a same-head gauge comparison does not discharge those obligations.
