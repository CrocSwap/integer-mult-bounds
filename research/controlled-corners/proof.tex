\subsection{Three further contiguous blocks in the controlled basis}
\label{sec:controlled-corner-refinement}

Retain the dimension-thirty scalar producer, matching, endpoint matrices,
and rank budget of Section~\ref{sec:dimension30-batching}. Write
$H=h^2$, $m=hH$, and use the controlled family
$S=T(I\otimes K)$, where $T(u\otimes e_i)=G_i u\otimes e_i$.
The following additional conditions are imposed on this same basis.
They are compatible with all previously required conditions by the
finite nonzero-polynomial argument.

\paragraph{A stage-two auxiliary entrance.}
Before changing basis, this edge has projector
\[
 A_4=\Pi_t\otimes\Pi_U,\qquad
 \operatorname{rank}\Pi_t=1,\quad
 \operatorname{rank}\Pi_U=H-h.
\]
Indeed its frame is $D_0=(B\otimes F)\otimes Q$, where at stage two
$\dim B=h-1$ and $Q$ is a line. Reordering the third tensor factor
first gives the displayed form. Every stage-two auxiliary role starts
at zero, so there are $B_0=v^2R$ such rank-$(H-h)$ edges.

Write $\Pi_t=t\varphi$, $\varphi(t)=1$, and
\[
 a_i=e_1^{\mathsf T}G_it,\qquad b_i=\varphi G_i^{-1}e_h.
\]
The existing stage-two sink condition ensures $a_i b_i\ne0$ for every
$i$. The first-$H$-row, last-$H$-column corner of $SA_4S^{-1}$ is
\[
 C_4=\operatorname{diag}(a_i)(K\Pi_UK^{-1})\operatorname{diag}(b_i).
\]
Normalize the nonzero row and column factors. In this $H$-dimensional
corner, $K\Pi_UK^{-1}$ is a projector of rank $H-h>H/2$. Require its
first-$h$-row, last-$h$-column corner to be nonsingular. This is a
nonzero rational-function condition on $K$ by the large-projector
argument, since $K$ ranges over all invertible matrices on $V$.
The lower/lower pivot profile inside $C_4$ therefore consists of $h$
corner pivots and an identity block of length $H-2h$ in matching order.
Those $H-h$ pivots exhaust the rank of the entire $m$-dimensional edge.
Since the selected columns are in its last $H$ positions, all the
eliminations are allowed in the original physical order. Thus this
edge uses $h$ singleton interchanges and one width-$(H-2h)$ block,
instead of $H-h$ singletons.

\paragraph{The stage-two data entrances use the same argument.}
More generally, the preceding corner argument applies to
$\Pi_t\otimes\Pi_U$ with $\operatorname{rank}\Pi_U=H-b>H/2$:
require a nonsingular $b$-corner after conjugating $\Pi_U$ by $K$.
The resulting profile has $b$ singleton pivots and one block of
length $H-2b$. This adds another finite nonzero-polynomial condition
and requires no change to the controlled family.

At stage two, the prefix complement $B$ has dimension $h-1$.
For either physical data wire its entrance residual is
$B\otimes\ell^\perp\otimes Q$, where $\ell$ is its current source
or target line and $Q$ is the future line. On the source wire this
is the difference between $B\otimes F+P\otimes\ell$ and
$(B+P)\otimes\ell$; on the target wire it is the difference between
$B\otimes F$ and $B\otimes\ell$. These are exactly the retained
rank-$(h-1)^2$ entrance edges. Reordering the third factor first
therefore gives $\Pi_t\otimes\Pi_U$ with
\[
 \operatorname{rank}\Pi_U=(h-1)^2=H-(2h-1).
\]
For $h\ge4$ this exceeds $H/2$. Apply the generalized argument with
$b=2h-1$. Each of these $2N$ edges uses $2h-1$ singleton pivots and
one block of length $H-4h+2$.

\paragraph{The first corner of a stage-three data entrance.}
Write $r=h-1$ and retain
$A_3=(I-\Pi_t)\otimes(I-\Pi_q)$, of nullity $H+r$.
Set $K\Pi_qK^{-1}=q'\psi'$, $\psi'(q')=1$,
$\ell_i=e_1^{\mathsf T}G_i$, and $w_j=G_j^{-1}e_h$.
Its first-$H$-row, last-$H$-column corner is
\[
 (C_3)_{ij}=\delta_{ij}d_i-q'_i\psi'_j\ell_i(I-\Pi_t)w_j,
 \qquad d_i=\ell_i(I-\Pi_t)w_i=-a_i b_i\ne0.
\]
After row normalization this is $I-Z$, where $\operatorname{rank}Z\le r$.
We additionally require $C_3$ to be invertible and its first-$r$-row,
last-$r$-column corner to be invertible. The following explicit witness
shows that both requirements are nonzero polynomial conditions.

In an input basis $f_0,\ldots,f_r$ of $F$, fixed rational changes
absorbed into $K,G_i$ let us take $t=f_0$,
$\varphi=f_0^*$, $q'=(1,\ldots,1)^{\mathsf T}$ and
$\psi'=H^{-1}(1,\ldots,1)$. Assign each multiplicity coordinate a
label $k(i)\in\{1,\ldots,r\}$, such that each of the first and last
$r$ coordinates contains the labels exactly once, in the same order.
Assign the remaining coordinates arbitrarily. Each label then occurs
$n_k$ times with $0<n_k<H$, since $r\ge2$.
Choose
\[
 \ell_i=f_0^*+f_{k(i)}^*,\qquad
 w_i=f_0-f_{k(i)}.
\]
They satisfy $\ell_iw_i=0$ and extend to an invertible $G_i$ with
first row $\ell_i$ and $G_iw_i=e_h$: use the other $h-2$ coordinate
rows between $\ell_i$ and the last row $f_0^*$.
Then $d_i=-1$, and the normalized corner is
\[
 I-H^{-1}ZZ^{\mathsf T},\qquad Z_{ik}=[k(i)=k].
\]
Its nontrivial eigenvalues are $1-n_k/H>0$, so it is invertible.
Its indicated off-diagonal $r$-corner is $-I_r/H$, also invertible.
This proves both required nonvanishing statements over $\mathbb Q$.

For a matrix $I-Z$ with rank-$r$ update $Z=UV$, an invertible
off-diagonal $r$-corner makes $U_L,V_R$ invertible. The same Schur
calculation as for a large projector makes the middle block exactly
$I_{H-2r}$. Explicitly, partition the corner indices into first $r$,
middle $H-2r$, and last $r$ indices, denoted $L,M,R$. Then
\[
 (I-Z)_{MM}-(I-Z)_{MR}(I-Z)_{LR}^{-1}(I-Z)_{LM}
 =I_M-U_MV_M+U_MV_M=I_M.
\]
Idempotence of $Z$ is unnecessary for this calculation.
Because $C_3$ itself is invertible, the remaining $r$-by-$r$ block
also has full rank. Thus eliminating the first $H$ rows uses $2r$
singleton pivots and one contiguous block of length $H-2r$.

Keep the previously required nonsingular first-$(H+r)$-row,
last-$(H+r)$-column corner of the full $A_3$ transform. After the
first $H$ pivots above, its Schur complement supplies $r$ further
corner pivots. Eliminating all these $H+r$ rows and columns gives
the same middle identity block of length $m-2(H+r)$ as before:
the Schur complement is independent of the pivot order inside the
eliminated invertible corner. All pivot columns of the first $H$
rows lie in the last $H$ positions, followed by the preceding $r$
columns, so the permitted lower/lower operations suffice throughout.
The complete profile is therefore
\[
 3r\text{ singletons},\qquad
 H-2r\text{ contiguous pivots},\qquad
 m-2H-2r\text{ contiguous pivots}.
\]
It has total rank $m-H-r$, as required. There are $2N$ such data edges.

\paragraph{One basis for the whole finite network.}
Each new requirement is a rational-function nonvanishing condition on
the original freely varying $K,G_i$. Each has a rational witness as
shown above. The old conditions likewise have nonzero numerators by
Section~\ref{sec:controlled-projector-basis}. Their finite product
with all denominator and invertibility factors is nonzero. Thus one
rational tuple meets every old and new condition simultaneously.
Witnesses for individual factors need not be the same tuple. This
reasoning also covers every transposed projector, since the forms
used above allow arbitrary rank-one idempotents and arbitrary
rank-$(H-h)$ idempotents. Rational enumeration with determinant tests
is a terminating fixed-machine preparation.

\paragraph{The stronger moment.}
For $h=30$, $H=900$, the auxiliary entrance profile has 30 singletons
and a block of length 840. The data-entrance corner has 87 singletons
and a new block of length 842, retaining its length-25142 middle block.
The stage-two data entrances each have 59 singletons and a block of
length 782.
Let $S_1$ be the singleton count of Section~\ref{sec:dimension30-batching}
and $B_0=v^2R$. Put
\[
 S_2=S_1-840B_0-(842+782)(2N)=65379670780393117512.
\]
The new moment is
\[
 \Psi_2(x)=\frac{S_2+B_0(26880^x+25200^x+900^x+840^x)
                    +2N(25142^x+842^x+782^x)}{W\,27000^x}.
\]
The rank sum at $x=1$ is unchanged. Sixteen-term positive logarithm
enclosures, rounded upward to multiples of $10^{-10}$, and
$e^z\le(1-z)^{-1}$ give the exact strict certificate
\[
 \Psi_2(1-40315/10^{11})<1.
\]
All child widths contract. Common gate matrices, endpoint identities,
row handling and the finite tape alphabet are preserved, so the same
arbitrary-width recurrence proof applies.

\begin{lemma}[Controlled-corner interchange]
\label{lem:controlled-corner-chunk-swap}
The arbitrary-width interchange conclusion of
Lemma~\ref{lem:dimension30-chunk-swap} holds with saving
\[1-\tau=40315/10^{11}.\]
The scalar producer and rank deficit are unchanged;
only the controlled basis and three recursive pivot profiles change.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the simultaneous rational basis and factorization above, with the
strict moment $\Psi_2(\tau)<1$, in the retained finite-network transfer
and arbitrary-width row construction. All additional contiguous blocks
use the same componentwise shear primitive and preserve their field order.
\end{proof}

The complex producer, whole-residual child sizes, path-depth guard,
and complex saving $7/10^7$ remain unchanged. Only the stronger bit
interchange is consumed in the following layer interface and assembly.
