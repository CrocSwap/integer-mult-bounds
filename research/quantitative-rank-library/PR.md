## Change and mathematical scope

The retained compiler proofs bound finite widths but leave numerical library
generation and full native-word generation as different obligations. This
package supplies an exact evaluator for all smaller-rank metadata under the
fixed separated default-height, least-chronological batching policy. It
constructs evaluated integer replication, productive width, expanded
rank-one leaf count and expanded mixed-unit count at every rank through N.

Ordered target-role and paid-position product tables recover the source
census without enumerating paid paths or individual unit slots. Every
source-complement divisor and every original one-job exit divisor remains
present. Repeated exits may aggregate additive cost, but their counts may
not cancel individual invocation denominators.

The written theorem gives explicit parameter-dependent K/Z bounds. For
each fixed rule, the indexed metadata computation takes N times a fixed
power of log N in bit work. A separately specified fixed-tape scan
construction takes N squared times a fixed power of log N, with library
access, arithmetic, sorting, copies, resets, erasure and historical backing
charged. This outputs N rows; it is not polynomial in the input length log N.

The gcd/LCM law is inherited from #4507 and #5071. The width divisor comes
from #4638, with #5962's sharper multiplicative-rank refinement credited.
The new result is compressed exact all-rank metadata generation and its
complete bit-cost accounting. A normalized contracting-chain argument
bounds fully expanded leaf and mixed-unit counts without assuming positive
rank surplus. No factorization or unevaluated arithmetic-expression oracle
is used. Old nested widths are a different policy and are not substituted.

physical implementation of the smaller native programs.
