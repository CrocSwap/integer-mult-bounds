# A complete first-range caller resident grant

This is a conditional source-array theorem for Jose Manuel/jmbarrancoml's
unchanged PR2697 Node/Driver at a53fdf4bcbe33c6732f0bec1badc8c023380f9d1.
It supplies the enclosing local resident premise of PR5135. It does not assert
an improved asymptotic stock admission or a sharp constant. Its deliberately
large constants buy a complete, source-before-execution capacity grant.

## Contract and distinction from the recursive ledger

Use the original successful selected first-range contract: dimension64,
64<=n<128, positive canonical complete extents, a fixed admitted odd prime q,
M=q^w, exact source topology, codebook, Program and coefficient implementations,
sufficient declared arena capacities, and retained arithmetic/endpoint premises.
The fixed selected vocabulary has F=28416 families and r=661 ordinary local

The Context, Ring, supplied Program, n/depth words and root ports are admitted

The metric counts lengths of distinct records.Tape.cells and words.Word.cells,
including LEFT, erased tails, free arena slots and arena templates. It excludes
Python object headers, tuples/dictionaries, allocator overcapacity, static
source tables and machine stack bytes. A Program/Fields/Callback or suspended
frame is counted through the arrays it retains; an alias is not another array.
An explicitly supplied K_external covers all foreign source arrays, including
suspended parents. Nothing in this theorem creates or bounds those parents.

PR2753 already proves F_q+sum[8V_j+32Delta_j+E_j], and <F_q+11V_root under

Write f=U*B*G*S*M^(2n), v=C*H*f and V=F*N*v. Require C,H,U,B,G,S>=1 explicitly. Incoming row_count is F*N*H*U.
Let h be Program depth, c the code width, R/I the numerical bit widths, and

    J = 4096*(R+1)
    E = c+2+J
    D = 1+C*H*(h+1)*E
    P = 2I+R+6+(h+1)*(c+2I+90)
    t = max(2I+4,R+3)

four-stack backing bound at depth h+1, so it bounds each individual new stack

Let B_input be the sum of the *declared entry backing lengths* of source,

## Every new noncount Tape has extent at most L

Payload routines only select, clone, split, merge, XOR or permute complete
packets, families and fibres of this root input. Their raw data lengths are at
most V. A decorated route cube has H*(f+J) symbols; partial partitions and
outputs have no more. This is at most V+H*J. The raw key has J symbols and is
also covered. Repeated routing does not append keys to an already decorated
caller packet: route_cube removes its keys before returning. LEFT adds1.

The largest fresh serialized directory has C*H records of h+1 fields, giving
D. Old-prefix copies contain h fields, new-prefix copies h+1, and constructor
empty arrays fit D. There is no directory per selected family or old row
retained simultaneously; produce replaces its saved prefix. New Program
serialization appends at most one STEP to the canonical depth-h program,
giving P. Repeated coefficient requests construct and release separate copies;
they do not extend a single descriptor cumulatively. The encoded callback
stacks satisfy the retained depth h or h+1 bound S; clear() preserves their
maximum extent, which this bound already includes. Thus all noncount extents
are bounded before execution, even after their content has been erased.

Counting input arrays, capacity-limited numerical banks, count arrays and the
complete noncount inventory proves (1), including partial construction writes,
returns and normal cleanup. No summation over total loop iterations is needed.
