## Caller contract and exact arithmetic

Inputs are two distinct borrowed streams, each containing q nonnegative
radix-2^b digits, least significant digit first and little-endian bits within
each fixed b-bit word. A supplied catalogue describes S original source
coordinates, with pairwise-coprime axes verified by the actual converter.
Require b,q>0, S<2^b, 2q-1<=S, and p>=b+2. The output is exactly 2qb bits,
most significant bit first, including leading zeroes. An external conversion
from arbitrary n-bit operands to this padded digit format is not implemented
here; q*b is the declared operand bit extent.

`ingress` reads exactly q digits, rejects trailing cells, writes
`a_j*2^(p-b-2)` and an imaginary zero, and appends S-q zero coordinates.
The represented values are exactly a_j/2^(b+2), strictly below 1/4.

The executed sequence is: forward Converter, forward Tensor, forward
Converter, forward Tensor, four real exact word products per coordinate
and inward p-grid truncation, opposite Tensor, two exact products by S
and a shift by 2b+4, nearest integer, inverse Converter at its actual widened
record width, and radix carry propagation. Every child return is copied into
an owned parent bank then cleared; the child's protected output marker is
retired before the next invocation. The owned parent banks are separately
retired after their final read. No returned child stream is silently aliased
as an uncharged permanent parent bank.

The two products by S are retained separately. Source07/source08's R F_s
convention gives the exact opposite sandwich `(u*v)/S^2`; the CRT map
preserves convolution. The two S products and 2^(2b+4) undo these two
normalizations and both digit scales. The code never substitutes an inverse
permutation for the opposite-sign Fourier call.

The temporary width W=(p+2)+4b+4 is deliberately conservative. The completed
opposite return has magnitude at most 2^p per component under the source
boundary. Multiplication by S<2^b twice and shifting by 2b+4 gives magnitude
less than 2^(p+4b+4), inside signed W bits. Every operand of the two word
products fits W. Pointwise products use the source p+2 operand width and
unbounded signed exact product results before their one p-bit truncation.
Since b<=p-2, W=O(p) even when p and b are independently supplied.

The true coefficient is at most q(2^b-1)^2, so it is nonnegative and fits
our conservative output width. Carry propagation maintains
`previous carry + coefficient = digit + 2^b * next carry` exactly. A negative
coefficient or a nonzero excess high digit rejects. Because 2q-1<=S, either
all 2q output digits were emitted while traversing S coefficients, or exactly
one final carry digit remains. The latter must fit b bits. Reversal emits
exactly 2qb bits; the cursor is returned from the left marker before erasure.
The product is less than 2^(2qb), so all guarded excess digits vanish under
the recovery theorem. The converse is not asserted: a false numerical
coefficient can sometimes pass nonnegativity/carry checks.

## Quantified conditional recovery guard

Let h=2^-p, D be the actual number of axes, u=alpha^2,
M=2^A with A the actual Tensor outer bits, and

    gamma = D(2u + ceil(log2(32p)) + 1),
    E = D*p^2 + 6*M + 22 + 2*D,
    epsilon = E * 2^gamma * h.

The actual catalogue supplies gamma; no old `2Du` value is reused.
Since 4sqrt(2)<6, the inherited PR3935 production approximation premise
implies each complete forward/opposite call differs from its exact normalized
map in sup norm by less than epsilon, on every computed half-disk input.
This remains a premise, not an independently established local analytic proof.

The strict encoded guard is

    2^(2b+5) * S^2 * (3 E 2^gamma + 2) < 2^p.             (G)

The implementation evaluates this with encoded Counts; no floating-point
logarithm, tolerance or host decoding participates. It additionally requires
a production-mode catalogue, whose original constructor enforces the
production geometry and parameter policy. A validated header does not prove
arbitrary page contents: pages must come from the declared source producer.

Put C=2^(2b+4)S^2. Since C>=64, (G) implies epsilon<1/384<.01.
Exact normalized transforms send the input quarter-disk into itself. Thus
computed forward outputs have norm below .26. Their product, after inward
component truncation, has norm below .26^2<.5, satisfying the actual opposite
Tensor entrance. Insert the exact product between the two computed forwards;
the product error is at most `epsilon*(1/4+epsilon)+(1/4)*epsilon`, hence
less than 2epsilon. Pointwise component truncation contributes less than
sqrt(2)h<2h. Insert the exact opposite map at the actual computed input;
its contraction and local approximation give total error below
`3epsilon+2h`. Scaling gives C(3epsilon+2h)<1/2 by (G).
The exact coefficient is a real integer, so source nearest rounding, including
its arbitrary half-tie convention, returns that unique integer. The strict
inequality means ties cannot occur under the theorem.

p=6b is source08's sufficient asymptotic *choice*, not a requirement for this
algebraic digit/rounding proof. Allowing independent p with p>=b+2 preserves
O(p) record widths, but does not import source08's n-to-parameter construction,
prime existence or asymptotic multiplication bound. Those require their own
family binding. In particular the new caller has not established a new
all-size n-bit multiplication theorem merely by checking (G).

## Full conditional cost and ownership ledger

There are exactly three complete CRT conversions and three complete Tensor
calls, including the opposite call. Each retains its full source invoice:
coefficient preparation (not free even when reused), catalogue scans, global
routers, four canonical tensor gathers, halo/copy/scatter/core work, all
physical children and their restoration. Preparation is separately paid
once for a reusable common catalogue.

Outside these inherited bodies there are four signed real word products per
pointwise coordinate and two per recovered real coordinate, at O(p)-bit
operand widths. The retained DigitWordProduct is a fixed-digit leaf inside
the exact recursive WordProduct; use its inherited constructive word bound,
not a rank-count credit. Even the available Karatsuba upper envelope
O(p^log2(3)) gives O(S p^log2(3)) for six scalar products per coordinate.
All remaining digit ingress, truncation, exact shifts, copies, nearest,
carry and bit reversal cost O(Sp+qb). No paid child tariff disappears.
The finite scalar proof/metadata construction is polynomial in p and encoded
shape length and must be retained as setup; absorption into a production
volume uses the inherited exponential-volume family hypothesis explicitly.
The conditional Gaussian target exponent is not re-proved by this package.

The parent has a fixed128-role scratch inventory, one protected output, one
word owner and two distinct inherited numerical/layout owners. Source and
catalogue remain borrowed. The largest simultaneously retained banks have
O(Sp) bits, plus inherited catalogue and child backing and polynomial
metadata. Failure cleanup retires every remaining fixed parent lease before
checking emptiness; it does not claim to erase or repair borrowed inputs.
Repeated-use, occupied-output and source-drift controls are separate evidence.

## Entry ownership and returned-stream protocol

Construction transfers exclusive ownership of fresh Tensor and Converter
objects. Their resident inventories must be disjoint. The host ABI traverses
the fixed, parameter-independent constructor graph once, stopping at Tape
objects without examining their cells. It retains only tape identities; this
is not a numerical operation or an uncharged metadata parser. The resident
graph is fixed by the authenticated constructors. Run-time sealed constructors
permit only leases on those existing banks, not additional resident tapes.
The fixed inventory and its comparisons contribute a fixed constant overhead.

Every transferred resident must be empty at its canonical constructor cursor;
primitive reads check the current blank and the preceding blank or left marker
(the source word owners retain some empty markers). Noncanonical arbitrary
head movements on transferred private banks are outside this ABI. Fresh top
level owners must be closed, lease-free and have no earlier invocation. Failed
preflight does not initialize any parent execution marker or alter child data.
After transfer the caller must not operate on private descendants directly.

Before even positivity reads, all five external underlying tapes (two payloads,
catalogue, b, q) are pairwise distinct and excluded from the full resident
inventory. Metadata alias rejection therefore cannot reset payload cursors.
The returned output is borrowed read-only, and a finite pending-return token
rejects another call regardless of its cursor. `consume()` performs the real
linear clear and marker retirement before releasing that token; flipping a
flag alone would not suffice. End-head occupation and all six metadata/data
aliases have explicit failure-and-reuse controls.
