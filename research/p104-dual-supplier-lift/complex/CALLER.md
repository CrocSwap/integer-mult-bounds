## Place the 312 roots

For each start $s=0,\ldots,23$, start eight mixed roots on the eight distinct B ports. End the same roots on the B ports at $s+24$. The two visits have different colors. For a fixed body color $c$, each remaining mixed root whose two endpoint colors differ from $c$ needs one interior A visit of color $c$. Process the three calls of that color in increasing time. At each call take up to twelve eligible groups of eight, ordered by their start. Each selected group receives eight different A ports. The finite verifier checks that every group obtains its required interior visit. The remaining capacity has 120 A ports of each color. Each index in those sixteen lists gives one A root when its sixteen visits are put in chronological order.

Each root sees all sixteen actual body colors. The native source and destination charts use the same canonical head and absolute coordinate views as the parent.
