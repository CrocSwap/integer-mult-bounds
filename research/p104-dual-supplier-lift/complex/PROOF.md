## Reconstruct the native graph

The source is the scalar graph and the complete public layout exported by the scientific commit of PR 7756. The two exports have canonical content hashes. Each paid transfer reads an exact rational linear form at its source chart. Each terminal reads an exact rational linear form at its return chart. A linear form at chart A represents a scalar combination of arbitrary public arrays in the numerical coordinates of A. Moving from A to B then applies their relative physical operator. A paired public change can mix independent helpers with the same two boundary charts. Use the inverse at their other ends. For each sent row, separate the terms that must cross between the charts. Send a basis for those terms. As soon as a new row is independent of the earlier rows, send its full crossing part. Express later crossing parts in the available basis. Substitute zero-distance transfers into their consumers. The edit checker reconstructs each form over the rationals and requires a topological order after each edit. It also checks all donor charts and the two graph digests. [Paid basis edits](BANK-EDITS.json) · [Public basis edits](PUBLIC-EDITS.json) · [Reconstruction](supplier.py)

Two further edits split an existing helper into two independent owners. The first output is a local observation of the original source direction. The second input changes a local pulse. A local calibration subtracts the disturbance from the other inputs. At the old return its nonzero pivot recovers the second owner. The verifier tests all source columns after all edits. This test checks the interaction of the pulse and the observation with the rest of the circuit. [Split operator](birth_event.py) · [Paid and public row operations](public_split.py)
