## Result and why it matters

DreamingOfClouds' [#6934](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/6934)
introduces an odd-scalar native word and a changed caller. Its precision argument
quotes bounds from an older caller and remains conditional on exact numerical
child returns. This package recomputes the actual source prefixes and proves a
common-grid implication through the retained ideal compiler and ordinary
recursion. The ideal endpoint, same-root-factor, ownership and scheduling
interfaces are explicit; no physical precision closure is assumed as a premise.

For the **per-call scale1125 program**, after a separately paid root boundary,
one sufficient component format is

`F=p+D`, `W=p+h+2D+3`, with `W+26` temporary bits.

Each nested local invocation scales its own entrances and unscales its completed
outputs. Completed endpoints reset only extra debt relative to their entrances.
Their inherited root debt stays present. This does not permit a one-time outer
scale1125 to stand in for all nested conversions.

The exact 71,069-event word yields height24, scaled mass
`1636447290138225/16384 < 2^37`, and 24-bit scalar numerators. The actual
3,221-event caller yields height163 and mass below2^126; joint entrances have
height154 and mass below2^124. Thus each unfinished raw context adds at most
**178 denominator bits and 161 mass bits**. The retained source-depth recurrence
and an exact integer threshold absorb that debt in the displayed common format.

This is a proof of a stated numerical implication, with finite source bounds as
inputs. It is not an independent verification of every native endpoint, a
physical supplier construction, or an admission of #6934's candidate kappa.
The selected bound remains conditional with all five assumptions retained.

## Reproduction and evidence

```sh
python3 -B scripts/research_package.py --package research/odd-native-common-grid
```

The registered normal and optimized commands print
`PASS_ODD_NATIVE_COMMON_GRID`, followed by the runner's
`PASS_REGISTERED_RESEARCH_PACKAGE`. A Git-free package copy can run

```sh
python3 -I -B research/odd-native-common-grid/verify.py
python3 -I -O -B research/odd-native-common-grid/verify.py
```

`audit_bounds.py` uses exact rational sparse rows. The separately authored
`independent_bounds.py` instead stores fixed-grid integer rows and propagates a
78-by-78 positive caller transfer matrix. Both methods check all 14,060,742 word
coefficient instances. The second also derives the minimum odd scale and
rejects225 at event1101 and375 at event36915. A separate subagent reviewed the
all-size argument; its methods, frozen draft pins and limitations are recorded
in `INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md`. This is author-side review, not human or formal-kernel
acceptance.

Controls detect changed source bytes, wrong frames, nonzero COPY destinations,
an unfunded odd prime, a nondyadic completed endpoint, caller aliases and a
changed type-B endpoint. A nested exact-division example falsifies the one-time
outer-scale shortcut. A D=1 projected Gaussian example falsifies applying the
Clifford denominator cap after selecting an active coordinate. Finite boundary
sweeps check arithmetic off-by-one cases; the written proof establishes the
quantified inequalities. Executed gates are in `VERIFICATION.md`.

## Complete invoice and limits

There are **52,272 complete-field scaling operations per raw caller**. Their
positive linear-word cost is added to the inherited word, caller, native,
source, setup, movement, restoration, owned backing and recursive costs. No
helper credit or conversion cost is dropped. This package does not recertify
the raw numerical allowance, BIT side, strictification, full outer ledger,
H0 preprocessing/output normalization, native phase identities or actual
physical format implementations. It begins after a paid root boundary.
