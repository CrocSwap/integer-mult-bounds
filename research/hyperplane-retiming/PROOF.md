## Why coordinated changes are searchable by a minimum cut

Fix any ambient subspace H and allow each chronological contact or endpoint U to use either U or U intersect H. Write a_U=dim U-dim(U intersect H), and s_U in {0,1} for that choice. Freeze the main and owned-instruction boundary gauges. Keep the original logical native maps, and require each actual warp to remain monomial.

For an original nested paid edge U contained in V of rank r, the new rank is

    r + a_U s_U - a_V s_V.

If a_U>0, nesting forces s_V<=s_U. On the three allowed assignments (0,0), (1,0), (1,1), its incremental paid moment is exactly the linear expression

    [f(r+a_U)-f(r)] s_U
      + [f(r+a_U-a_V)-f(r+a_U)] s_V,

where f(r)=r^t and f(0)=0. If a_U=0, only the second switch matters, with incremental cost f(r-a_V)-f(r). A productive helper edge has the negative of this expression, retaining strictly positive productive rank.

For a fixed logical native warp G:U->V, either both reduced subspaces satisfy G(U intersect H)=V intersect H and the two switches agree, or the nontrivial switches are forbidden. If one endpoint is unchanged by intersection, only the other nontrivial switch is forbidden. These are equality or fixed-zero constraints.

The total energy is therefore a sum of unary weights with hard binary implications, equalities and fixed values. It is a minimum-weight closure problem and has an s-t cut realization. Algebraic powers need exact enclosures for certified signs; floating min-cut discovery is not an exact optimality certificate. The admitted result is the explicit patch, independently replayed with outward rational arithmetic.

Zero-length chronological edges must be represented when the search permits them to become paid. Contracting them is a smaller legitimate search family, not the complete contact search. The first successful search used that smaller family. Subsequent exploration permits new paid transitions. No global ceiling or completeness claim is made for these bounded searches.

The dual construction U -> U+W has the same reduction: interchange the direction of the implications and use the corresponding dimension increments. Higher-dimensional H or W remains a fixed common choice in each minimum-cut instance; this is not optimization over arbitrary unrelated subspaces at every node.
