The admitted positive construction in this package uses the explicitly pinned **round-two** word. The source head also contains Cao Yifan's stronger round-three word. Confusing those two artifacts would overstate the contribution. SEARCH-ORIGINS.json pins the latter independently; #4635 proposes conditional κ=.0030217754 from it. The optional discovery code uses the newest word and explores fixed common kernels while retaining the actual main ABI. It includes every chronological zero-length bridge, allowing it to become paid. Owned center source/scatter gauges can move coherently; their complete new rank is included. Full-dimension warp gauges are kept fixed.

The shared native maps may change jointly. A pair of subspace contracts A->B and U->V can be realized by one invertible map exactly when their two dimensions and intersection dimension agree. Extend an intersection basis first to A and U, then to the ambient space; match the corresponding target basis. This constructs the map. The retained two-contract operators have one line contract and one late subspace contract. Under a common kernel deletion, a line is either unchanged or zero. Matching deletion dimensions gives switch equalities or fixed-zero constraints. If the retained line is inside the late subspace and only one of its two representatives lies in the kernel, a late deletion is forbidden. The other cases preserve incidence automatically. Repeated occurrences of the same line contract are consistent because deleting it adds only the zero-subspace contract.

Thus this restricted shared-map family still reduces to minimum-weight closure, rather than pricing each occurrence with a different incompatible G. An observed positive cut would require emission of the actual common maps, fresh all-column/phase replay, and the entire compiler/outer bill before admission.

The fresh bounded screen observed no profitable cut among 511 rank-one common kernels, 2,937 rank-two kernels and 512 rank-three kernels. Rank-two cases combine actual two-dimensional source frames with a seeded sample; they are not all 43,435 planes. The powers and capacities are discovery approximations. Rank-one capacities are adaptively scaled and every hard implication is funded above the feasible all-zero cut; SciPy's int32 edge-capacity limit is checked. Higher-codimension discovery used scale 100,000. These searches are neither exact ceilings nor an impossibility proof, and can miss very small gains.
