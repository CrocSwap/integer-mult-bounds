## Lemma: complete chart lifting preserves packet volume

Assume one source request acts on one family a and one cover value g, with a legal scalar chart on a fixed hosted set of k <= 73 axes. Its right and left monomials have invertible affine address maps and phase factors in {1,i,-1,-i}; its middle operation acts on r <= k independent coordinate axes. Assume the node realizes this request on all f columns as one completed job, retaining all other coordinates. Then its proper recursive call has width rf and payload volume exactly V/B.

Proof. Euclidean division gives the row bijection

    rho <-> (a,g,z),
    rho = (a Gamma + g)(R/B) + z,
    0 <= a < W, 0 <= g < Gamma, 0 <= z < R/B.

Fixing a and g leaves R/B rows. The request carries a and g as return labels; it does not divide by either label again. Every scalar monomial address map x -> Gx+b is a bijection on {0,1}^k. Apply it independently to the f columns, fix the other (73-k)f core bits and t remainder bits, and preserve z and S. This is a bijection on the entire selected e-bit cube, even when coefficient values are correlated with the spectators. Phase multiplication changes no addresses.

After the right chart, regroup coordinates by a permutation:

    {0,1}^e <-> {0,1}^{rf} x {0,1}^{(k-r)f} x {0,1}^{(73-k)f} x {0,1}^t.

The first factor is the child's active address. The other factors are complete child spectators. Thus its data rectangle is

    Q = [R/B] x {0,1}^{rf} x {0,1}^{e-rf} x S,
    ell |Q| = ell (R/B) 2^{rf} 2^{e-rf} |S| = V/B.

The inverse permutation, inverse chart and row formula give an explicit inverse address map. Fixed labels and all spectator values return unchanged at the child interface. The argument uses no divisibility of e by 73, no equality of child ranks, and no power-of-two assumption about R, W or Gamma. It applies independently to every source request. QED.

This is an address and operator identity: if a scalar factorization is L (C on r axes) R, then its f-fold tensor is L^(tensor f) (C on rf axes, identity on the remaining axes) R^(tensor f), after the displayed coordinate permutation. It does not follow by tensorizing a sum such as G-I; it uses the actual individual monomial factors. `clifford_exact.py:lower_child` explicitly compiles `right -> fourier(n, range(r)) -> left`, and `fourier` is a product of the C gates. Its rank-zero branch has no proper child. `bw_emit.py` emits paid `climb` and `center` requests; `bw_phases.py` lowers their directed local frame edges. These are the scalar producer sites to which the hypothesis must be bound.

## Whole job, factorwise execution, and the exact unclosed implication

An algebraic tensor product can be executed by f separate rank-r layers, each retaining the other columns as spectators. Each such layer still has the full packet volume V/B. Its invoice is f Gamma H_r rank-r jobs; it is not Gamma H_r rank-rf jobs. #4375 already proves that this factorwise invoice destroys the desired moment for f >= 2. Dropping the other columns to pretend that a rank-r layer has volume V/(fB), or dropping inactive address bits to pretend that a rank-rf child has volume 2^(rf-e)V/B, changes the problem.

COMPLEX.md asserts a whole job and the desired volume. The pinned producer files expose scalar ranks, frame labels, monomial factors and scalar emitted events. They do not supply a uniform node-level request list with an executable pack/unpack map carrying the complete rectangle and return labels. Consequently this package proves the unique volume normalization for the stated complete chart realization; it does not verify that actual tape execution realizes that hypothesis for every occurrence.

The remaining source-to-packet obligation is concrete: for every mixed local `climb`/`center` occurrence and every positive residual occurrence, at every g and f, identify the actual finite left/right recipe, its physical source-family label selected by the literal fill, the rf active axis list, all e-rf spectator axes, the R/B remaining row field, every compact/prefix/payload field and the return labels; verify a pack/unpack bijection and exactly one recursive call. The local emitted-program-to-native-recipe binding, residual global charts and gather/replace schedule must compose, including phases, translations and arbitrary dirty contents. A list of scalar histogram entries or a tensor identity alone is not that implementation.

N(i), the nonchild tape invoice for adapters, scans, monomials and parked ancestors, is a separate unresolved obligation assigned to the next task. Even a completed request adapter would not establish its cost. Precision, padding realization, the remaining all-size source hypotheses and the multiplication theorem are also outside this result. No kappa or selected record changes.
