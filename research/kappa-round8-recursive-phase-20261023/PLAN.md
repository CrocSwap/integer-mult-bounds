# Recursive phase interface design

intqwq; substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. Apache-2.0.

The task is to find a genuinely changed recursive producer/consumer, beyond
the frozen round7 fixed-frame screens and Cinna Davis's fixed-subspace
rank-debt argument. No parameter gain is assumed. All predecessor packages
stay frozen; no commits or external posts are made by this subtask.

The first mechanism tested will keep a coupled bank as one recursive job,
with a precisely declared entry/exit encoding. A job must accept arbitrary
input on every bank, restore every borrowed/owned field, and either return
the same encoding or pay its complete terminal conversion. A fixed number
of global Pauli phases may change only bounded rank; a gain proportional
to f requires a funded block family and an all-f residue rule.

The first acceptance gate is an explicit algebraic identity for arbitrary
operators, followed by the actual all-f split/merge invoice. A local scalar
identity or operator similarity is insufficient. The second gate is a
closed finite family of job types with complete paid/productive profiles
and a strict recurrence. Only a passing physical caller would justify a
new kappa calculation and scoped Lean verification.

Promising directions are a matrix-valued phase/query factor whose adjoint
or paired job closes at a shared bank boundary, and a recursively retained
coupled phase residue which cancels without a per-field query head. The
round5 S/T lift and round7 grouped movement primitive are available as
authenticated ingredients; their unclosed terminal bills remain payable.
