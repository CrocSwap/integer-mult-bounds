# Construction and scope of the new invoice

Let `F_U` denote the canonical nine-coordinate frame attached to an explicit
binary subspace U. A scalar contact is conjugated by the same frame at every
persistent role it touches. A paid frame change U<V contributes Fourier rank
`dim(V)-dim(U)`. A native warp K_G is free between U and V when the explicit
invertible binary map G transports U onto V; its exact phase is independently
lowered as part of admission. These are the retained PR4610 model contracts.

The frozen patch changes helper entrance frames and scalar contact frames
jointly with native maps. Resynthesis inserts every positive paid frame change
required before a scalar/warp contact and before every final endpoint. There is
no uncharged removal or merge. All scalar instructions and logical warp
identities remain in their old order. The exact all-column interpreters check
the new word, including all arbitrary helper inputs, owned storage, and main
endpoints. The old main operator interfaces remain unchanged.

A helper whose arbitrary input enters in frame U and leaves in frame V now
supplies the whole operator `F_U.adjoint().then(F_V)`. The new input chart is
part of that helper's interface. Strict U<V is checked for each of all 408
helpers; none is silently treated as initialized storage or duplicated credit.
Each changed helper operator is lowered with absolute phase in both orientations.
The inherited whole-job normalization/adapter contract makes these jobs usable
by the same mixed-target recursion; its hypotheses remain explicit premises.

The complete native profile comparison is

```
PR4610 paid P0 = {1:2201,2:412,3:506,4:135,5:36,8:8}
new paid  P1 = {1:2201,2:412,3:510,4:127,5:40,8:8}
helpers H0=H1 = {4:28,7:7,8:47,9:326}.
```

All paid jobs are included, in particular eight owned-center rank-eight jobs.
There are 3298 paid jobs and paid rank mass 5327 in either profile. This step
improves their concave moment without changing either total. For 0<t<1,
strict concavity of x↦x^t on positive reals gives

```
4^t > (3^t + 5^t)/2,
P0(t) - P1(t) = 4 * (2*4^t - 3^t - 5^t) > 0.
```

The productive moment is identical because the helper rank census is identical,
despite 26 distinct helper operators changing their input charts. The retained
p16 controller invokes the local supplier eighteen times. Thus its complete
paid moment decreases by 72 times the bracketed expression. Every retained
query-shear, idle, diagonal and completion bill is still required. The companion
round-three whole-profile package rebuilds those terms, tensor tail/completion,
the uniform compiler and outer inequalities at the stated new kappa.

Discovery uses a contact graph with scalar nodes and native-map edges. For the
twelve operators with extra prelude occurrences, a fixed line a must map to b
as well as the late input subspace A mapping into the next cap B. Feasibility
requires equal membership of a and b in the selected equal-dimensional source
and target subspaces. `constrained_map.py` constructs and checks the complete
invertible map on all 512 inputs. In the admitted witness all twelve prelude
operators retain their old G; the actual changes affect twenty other maps.

The mathematical certificate trusts neither solver optimality nor floating
objective values. `regenerate.py` authenticates the exact predecessor and
reconstructs the frozen word by guarded edits. `verify_candidate.py` checks its
native identities and full invoice through two retained interpreters.
`phase_check.py` checks exact Clifford lowering, including absolute phases of
all changed data. Lean verification and numerical/outer certification are stated
with their own scoped coverage in the companion package. None alone is a
formal proof of the full integer-multiplication theorem.
