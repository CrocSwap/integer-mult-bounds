# Attribution and unchanged dependencies

Gareth Davidson (GitHub bitplane) supplied the finite compensation construction
and source data in PR915 and the two-sided successor PR926. This package credits
those algorithms and certifies their pinned historical Boolean interface; it
does not claim to originate their optimization or adopt it into current main.

The PR590 target-translation interpreter and PR597 reversal proof are unchanged
prerequisites. The exact TargetTranslation source already exists on pinned main
at research/community-cold617-audit/pr612/lean/BitXorTargetTranslation.lean and
research/r420-scatter-lifecycle/BitXorTargetTranslation.lean. Its copy here is a
reproducibility dependency, not a new theorem claim. PR733 preparation lineage
and the prior generic/source-binding interfaces are retained.
