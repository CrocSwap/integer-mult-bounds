# p104-lowerbound-toolkit: kernel-checked lower-bound, bridge and caller lemmas for P104 (theory and tooling, no kappa claim)

**What it is.** 443 Lean modules on top of the earlier P104 Lean library: 261 modules carried unchanged (byte-identical to the library shipped with our CERTKIT package, plus the crossing statements; `Price7` imports `PriceLemma` instead of embedding it) and 182 new modules (about 3.7 MB of source). Every module is kernel-checked: no `sorry`, `native_decide`, `axiom` or `extern`; finite facts use `decide +kernel`. Every declaration printed by `lean/Check.lean` (361) depends only on `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` (`CHECK-axioms.txt`; the molecule certificates `PriceH.md4`..`md15` and `PriceHK.md16`..`md19` print `[propext]`). The package makes no kappa claim and proves no unconditional wall.

**Labels.** **[P]** proved in Lean from the stated hypotheses; **[C]** a finite computation checked by the kernel (`decide +kernel`).
**Wall56** is the tier-1 premise "every legal P104 word pays D >= 56 and R >= 56 per call" (aggregate form: Sum(D+R) >= 112 per call on average; **Wall56@Q96** its form on the 96-port sector). Nothing here proves it.
The package is split in two parts. **CLAIMS** are theorems whose only hypotheses are the definition of the class or branch they talk about (named in the Scope cell) plus verifier clauses of the word model; no row of this table uses Wall56 or any other unproved premise. **CONDITIONAL LEMMAS** are proved implications whose extra hypothesis is a statement that is NOT proved here; the exact extra hypothesis is named in the last column. In particular the rung conclusion `kappa < .0093454` is conditional (the aggregate premise D + R >= 112 per call: its D half is the open network statement NET-D56, its R half has no bridge), it is not a Wall56-only claim, and the package makes no kappa claim and no universal-caller ceiling.

## Not covered

- **Mid-k caller window.** HP(k) < 128.891 only for k >= 74 on Q = 0 (HP(72), HP(73) are above the bar, k = 72, 73 are K8) and the Q >= 1 law only for k >= 77 (the LP value at k = 76 is 129.0076, above the bar): callers with k = 5..73 (Q = 0) and k = 5..76 (Q >= 1) are not bounded below 128.891. `c-hat(.99) < 128.891` for all callers is open.
- **e >= 1 extra thins** (thin columns beyond T = Q, excess e = T + 2k - 2 - n >= 1): the laws of `CallerExtraThin` and the leaf channel hold, but no k-crossing is proved; direct sums show that without an indecomposability hypothesis there is none for e >= 2.
- **NET-D56 core**: D >= 56 for networks with more than 8 gathering or depositing nodes. K4 gives D >= 21 and K1 the 8/8 case. The level-cut route alone cannot give it (LEVEL-WALL is false: E8 with 96 single-port gatherers and 8 hubs has D = 104 and level sum 0).
- **R side**: R >= 56 per call is open. The R side is not bridgeable as a relay-blind cut (free edges and R-moves never point in opposite directions in either time direction). Needs ChainWall56@Q96 or a 2-adic tensor bound. K5 and K6 cover classes that do not cover all legal runs (CT-COVER false: a climb-sharing run has R = 2 < mass 3), and K6 assumes its three rank-8 facts.
- **General decoder wall**: non-linear half-integer decoders and decoders with denominator > 2 (C1, C2 are linear / Z/4-linear half-integer only; the general case is Wall56 itself). Wall56 and Wall56@Q96 themselves; every rung statement takes them as hypotheses.
- **Premises assumed, not derived**: `hbud` for every h other than 7, 8, 9; molecule certificates for h outside 4..19; the legal-word to banked-graph step for h other than 7, 8 and the #7113 chain; the J-schema, tightness, lambda != -1 and D >= 2 hypotheses of C10 for an actual legal caller (a Q = 0 word exists at k = 3 only; none is built in the window k = 5..73); tightness `n + 2 = |Iota|` and `D >= 1` of C14; `hlam` of `CallerQZero.c1_hp`.
- **No kappa claim and no universal-caller ceiling**: K7 is a conditional exclusion theorem; S_spec 865.58 is not a valid floor for the frontier, so no class-restricted ceiling may be cited as universal.
- **Data**: the choice of the 104 E8 lines is instance data (C2); the `Nodup` of the 759 frames is not proved.
- Caller-model gaps in Lean: the time-reversed (R-side) bridge, LIFT and PARITY (paper arguments), the triangle/e >= 1 excess analysis.

## Layout and checking

- `lean/Nov15/` 443 modules (build order in `lean/build-order.txt`, root `lean/All.lean`), `lean/Check.lean`
  (`#print axioms` of the 361 declarations the new modules print), `CHECK-axioms.txt` (result of loading the built closure).
- `tools/framecert/`, `data/frame/`: generator and inputs of `FrameCert.lean`; `python3 -I tools/framecert/asm.py OUT.lean`
  rebuilds it byte-identically (checked in `verify.py`).
- `verify.py`: package integrity (sha256 pins both ways, token bans outside comments, topological build order equal to
  the import closure, Check.lean / CHECK-axioms.txt consistency, toolchain and Mathlib pins, FrameCert regeneration).
  `verify.sh` runs it and then `lean/check.sh` (guarded sequential build, per-file RSS cap, then the axiom audit;
  needs elan with the pinned toolchain and `lake exe cache get`). Peak memory of the heaviest files is about 3 GB (`DataIdA`, `FrameCert`, `PriceHK*` pieces are split to stay below the 3.5 GB guard).
