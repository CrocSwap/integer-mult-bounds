# p104-lowerbound-toolkit: kernel-checked lower-bound and rung lemmas for P104 (theory and tooling, no kappa claim)

**What it is.** 104 new Lean modules (about 2.7 MB) on top of the earlier P104 Lean library, which is carried unchanged
(259 modules, byte-identical to the library shipped with our CERTKIT package, which is itself the one from #7796 plus the
crossing statements). Every module is kernel-checked: no `sorry`, `native_decide`, `axiom` or `extern`; finite facts use
`decide +kernel`. Every declaration printed by `lean/Check.lean` (270) depends only on `propext`, `Classical.choice`,
`Quot.sound` (`CHECK-axioms.txt`; the molecule certificates `PriceH.md4`..`md15` print `[propext]`). The package makes no
kappa claim and proves no unconditional wall.

Labels: **[P]** proved in Lean from the stated hypotheses; **[C]** a finite computation checked by the kernel
(`decide +kernel`); the premises of each row are listed in its Premise cell and are part of the statement.
**Wall56** is the tier-1 premise "every legal P104 word pays D >= 56 and R >= 56 per call" (and **Wall56@Q96** its form on
the 96-port Q96 sector). It is a premise wherever it appears below; nothing here proves it. The P104 label, frame, E8-Gram
and pattern data used by rows 2, 5, 8 are embedded as numerals (see "Not covered").

## Not covered

- Hot intake outside class CF; runs outside class F / P / carrier-forest: CT-COVER is false over every field (a climb-sharing run has R = 2 < mass 3), so these classes do not cover all legal runs; rendezvous K-jumps and public hot deliveries are also outside. R >= 56 per call is open; it needs ChainWall56@Q96 or a 2-adic tensor bound. Row 6 gives R >= 56 only under three assumed rank-8 hypotheses, for no legal P104 run.
- Premises assumed, not derived: the three rank-8 facts (`hF`, `hG`, `hM`) of row 6; `hbud` at h = 9 and for every h other than 7 and 8; molecule certificates for h outside 4..15; the legal word to BankedGraph step for h other than 7 and 8; the role-count, MAINS, SIGN1 and RC2 rows of row 12 for an actual legal caller.
- D >= 56 for networks with more than 8 gathering or depositing nodes (the NET-D56 core / network-coding gain). Rows 4 and 5 give D >= 21 in general and D >= 56 only with exactly 8 gathering and exactly 8 depositing nodes (any relay structure). The level-cut route alone cannot give it: LEVEL-WALL is false (E8 with 96 single-port gatherers and 8 hubs has D = 104 and level sum 0).
- Decoders with denominator > 2, and non-linear half-integer decoders (rows 1 and 2 are linear / Z/4-linear half-integer only; the general case is Wall56 itself).
- A universal caller bound. Row 12 is conditional (hypotheses above, Q = 0 branch, k = 72, 73 as named data). Open: `c-hat(.99) < 128.891` for all callers; callers with k <= 71 (row 12 proves only the bound HP(k) there; HP(k) < 128.891 is proved only for k >= 74, and k = 72, 73 rest on named data); the Q >= 1 branch; the piece-partition equality.
- No universal-caller kappa ceiling and no kappa claim: rows 10 and 11 are conditional exclusion theorems; S_spec 865.58 is not a valid floor for the frontier (ADDITIVE correction), so no class-restricted ceiling may be cited as universal.
- Data identity: the embedded P104 data (104 labels, 759 frames, E8 Gram mod 2, pattern Q) is not identified with the C120/E8 source inside Lean (regeneration and Python only).
- Wall56 and Wall56@Q96 themselves; every rung statement above takes them as hypotheses.

## Layout and checking

- `lean/Nov15/` 363 modules (build order in `lean/build-order.txt`, root `lean/All.lean`), `lean/Check.lean`
  (`#print axioms` of the 270 declarations the new modules print), `CHECK-axioms.txt` (result of loading the built closure).
- `tools/framecert/`, `data/frame/`: generator and inputs of `FrameCert.lean`; `python3 -I tools/framecert/asm.py OUT.lean`
  rebuilds it byte-identically (checked in `verify.py`).
- `verify.py`: package integrity (sha256 pins both ways, token bans outside comments, topological build order equal to
  the import closure, Check.lean / CHECK-axioms.txt consistency, toolchain and Mathlib pins, FrameCert regeneration).
  `verify.sh` runs it and then `lean/check.sh` (guarded sequential build, per-file RSS cap, then the axiom audit;
  needs elan with the pinned toolchain and `lake exe cache get`).
