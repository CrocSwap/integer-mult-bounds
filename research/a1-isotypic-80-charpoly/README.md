# Characteristic polynomial of one 80-dimensional isotypic summand

Prepared by unpaidmoon with substantial Cursor Grok assistance. Apache-2.0.

For the historical `c=1` centre block, the linear character
`ψ(r^k) = (-1)^k` and `ψ(r^k u) = (-1)^k` of the dihedral image generated by
the signed permutations `r` and `u` has an 80-dimensional isotypic summand
inside the trivial-character `(+1,+1)` joint space. On an explicit basis,

```text
chi_{A_1}(x) = x^10 (x-2)^3 (x+2)^3 (x-6)^2 (x+6)^2 f14(x) f14(-x) f16(x) f16(-x).
```

The polynomials `f14` and `f16` are the multiplicity-one degree-14 factor and
the degree-16 factor of the published degree-216 characteristic polynomial.
They are written in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md). The same basis splits the summand
into two `A_1`-invariant subspaces of dimension 40.

No new kappa. The selected conditional kappa stays `781451/250000000`.

## Result

Let `W` be the image of the projector for that character. Then `dim W = 80`.
The ascending coefficients of `chi_{A_1,W}` have SHA256

```text
bd72312859b05d7cd69c5cc94e4768998cd033869ab9f157fdf9d2042b6bc0d2
```

The polynomial is even. Algebraic multiplicity of eigenvalue 0 is 10. The
basis, the integer matrix of `B = 4 A_1`, and the coefficient list are in
[evidence/](evidence/).

PR #7023 computed the projector rank and did not list a basis or this
polynomial. The degree-216 factorisation does not by itself select which
factors lie on this summand.
