\subsection{A dimension-thirty continuation of controlled batching}
\label{sec:dimension30-batching}

The following continuation combines the ternary source geometry of
Zhihao Chen's PR~\#7, the controlled batching and complex depth guard of
icekylinx's PR~\#10, and the star resynthesis and matching extension of
Rohan Arun's PRs~\#9 and~\#11. It was prepared with substantial OpenAI
Codex assistance. These are conditional, unreviewed mathematical
extensions, not formal verification of the multiplication theorem.

\paragraph{The source family and finite producer.}
For the bit network only, take $h=30$, $F=\mathbb Q^{30}$ with form
$I-(2/25)J$, and the indicators $t_S$ of all five-subsets. Then
$\langle t_S,t_T\rangle=|S\cap T|-2$ and
$\langle t_S,t_S\rangle=3$. The scalar identity over $\mathbb F_3$ is
\[
 \binom j2-[j=2]=[j=5]\pmod3\qquad(0\le j\le5).
\]
The common-pair retained totals and exclusion outputs therefore have
the same interpretation as in the dimension-28 network. If $u$ lies
in a source span with common pair $C$, its two common coordinates equal
$a$ and its coordinate sum equals $5a$. Hence
\[
 u^{\mathsf T}(I-(2/25)J)u=\sum_{i\notin C}u_i^2>0\quad(u\ne0).
\]
Indeed vanishing outside $C$ forces $3a=0$. Every such source span is
nondegenerate, and a full retained-total span has dimension $h-2$.
The forward and complemented reverse frame schedules, arbitrary-scratch
cancellation, and negative-source endpoint correction are unchanged.

The paired degree-three producer uses 28 points outside each common
pair. Exact global support merging, pruning, and common-four-point
star resynthesis give
\[
 c=16089992,\qquad Q=1425495,\qquad R=c+Q=17515487.
\]
There are $\binom{30}{4}=27405$ stars and 456 canonical boundary templates.
All additions have disjoint input supports; every required boundary
mask is independently reconstructed and checked. Every replacement
remains in its original common-four-point star and therefore retains
the common-pair positivity argument. The actual compiled role-support
paths and all source, output, and dirty-scratch basis directions of
each selected template are checked in both orientations.

\paragraph{The bank permutation.}
The bipartite graph on two copies of the five-sets, with adjacency at
intersection two, is regular of positive degree
$\binom52\binom{25}{3}$. Counting edges incident to a left subset proves
Hall's condition and gives a neighbor bijection. An explicit bijection
can also retain the paired-point construction: replace the Euler tour
on the remaining pair indices by a cyclic ordering of the edges of a
complete graph with adjacent edges sharing one endpoint. Starting from
a triangle, insert the edges incident to a new vertex between two old
adjacent edges, with the first and last inserted edges meeting the old
endpoints. This inductively gives the required cycle at every order.
Keep PR~\#7's zero- and one-full-pair cases; in its two-full-pair case
flip the singleton and advance the pair of full-pair indices around
this cycle. Each case is bijective and has intersection exactly two.
All 142506 images are checked. Thus the first/third-stage join remains
nested, removes one role and $m$ rank units per join, and adds no loss.

Put $v=\binom{30}{5}=142506$, $m_{\rm b}=h^3=27000$, $N=v^3$, and
\[
 W=2v^2(v+R),\qquad L=3v^2\binom h2(h-2),\qquad s=Wm_{\rm b}-N+2L.
\]
In particular $\eta=(Wm_{\rm b}-s)/(Wm_{\rm b})=3857/52973979000$.

\paragraph{Why the controlled basis still exists.}
The proof in Section~\ref{sec:controlled-projector-basis} uses $H=h^2$,
the independence of $q$ from the $h$-space $U$, and freely chosen
rational $K,G_i$. It does not use the numerical value 28 or the parity
of $h/2$. At $h=30$, the required $2h$ first rows lie in the first
$H$ rows, the first $H+h-1$ rows comprise one whole multiplicity block
and $h-1$ rows of the next, and these first and last index sets are
disjoint since $2(H+h-1)<hH$. The same nonzero determinant witnesses
and finite-product argument therefore apply literally. Orthogonality
from the preceding bank permutation supplies $q\notin U$ and the
zero products of the two idempotents. The controlled parameter family
is closed under inverse transpose, as required in the dual argument.

Conjugate all gate and terminal matrices by the resulting single
rational basis. Endpoint differences remain $I$, scalar gates retain
common matrices, and all ranks remain unchanged. The three selected
classes have the following rank and contiguous-block data, where
$B=v^2R$:
\[
\begin{array}{c|r|r|l}
\text{copies}&\text{rank}&\text{singleton pivots}&\text{block widths}\\\hline
B&26940&60&26880\\
B&26100&0&25200,\ 900\\
2N&26071&929&25142
\end{array}
\]
The first and third use the large-projector Schur complement. The
second uses the diagonal corner supplied by the controlled basis.
The remaining singleton count is
\[
 S_1=s-B(26880+25200+900)-2N\cdot25142
    =373570603170925665960.
\]
Thus
\[
 \Psi(x)=\frac{S_1+B(26880^x+25200^x+900^x)+2N\,25142^x}
                 {W\,27000^x}.
\]
Using the positive rational logarithm series with sixteen terms,
rounding each upper enclosure upward to a multiple of $10^{-9}$,
and $e^z\le(1-z)^{-1}$, exact arithmetic gives
\[
 \Psi(1-253/10^9)<1,\qquad
 1-\text{certified upper bound}>9\cdot10^{-12}.
\]
Every child width is strictly below the parent width. The arbitrary
integer-width, row-reservation, and fixed-tape proof of
Section~\ref{sec:mixed-width-rows} applies with these new fixed constants.

\begin{lemma}[Dimension-thirty batched interchange]
\label{lem:dimension30-chunk-swap}
Lemma~\ref{lem:batched-chunk-swap} holds with $\tau=1-253/10^9$ in place
of $1-246/10^9$, using the dimension-thirty producer above. Its conclusion
includes arbitrary spectator gaps, integer widths, row padding and
removal, finite-alphabet preparation, and fixed-tape cleanup.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The scalar and source-span identities, retained-total loss, matching,
common conjugation, and strict moment comparison were established above.
Substitute these finite data into the proof of
Lemma~\ref{lem:batched-chunk-swap}. Its controlled-basis and row arguments
use no additional special property of dimension 28. The alphabet is
still $\mathbb F_3$, and the address prime is chosen after the finite
rational factorizations. The one-bit unequal-width extension is
unchanged.
\end{proof}

The complex network is deliberately left at $h_{\rm c}=28$,
$m_{\rm c}=21952$, with saving $7/10^7$. Its path-depth bound,
precision correction, and guard $C_1=11999/10000$ remain those of
PR~\#10. The bit and complex networks need not have equal finite
dimensions: the compact-control interface consumes the bit
interchange bound at arbitrary widths. The following stronger layer
interface changes only that consumed bit exponent.
