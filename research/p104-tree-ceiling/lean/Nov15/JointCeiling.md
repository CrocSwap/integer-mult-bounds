## Theorem as restated (paper)
For every rho = 2 nested two-arm caller (arms a,b >= 1, meeting only in hubs, host calls t != -1) with A ports at 4 QUERY sign units and B-form masks, composed with any native at the whole-floor per-call floor: kappa <= .0419448764, peak at 1|1 (k = 2). Conditions: [H-centre] (no line crosses the centre at h <= 4), [H-odd] (a B sign unit costs >= psi(1)), Theorem H + t = -1 closure (quoted), the R1 tail step (hypothesis, in NumCert), hN, and realisability of arms of 1 / k = 2 (no root built; Floor Lemma does not cover an innermost-only arm). Outside the class: trees, multi-centre, shared own roles. The .0681203279 figure (thin-3 masks) is only "scanned, conditional" for the true sign-floor class (audit); the Lean `ceiling3` certifies only the th3 B-form-A class under hB.
