# P104: node-partition refinement of the frontier-word ceiling, kappa <= .0116750 (Lean, no kappa)

Authors: DreamingOfClouds with Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), via Claude Code. **No kappa claim.** Everything here is a Lean statement with named hypotheses (the tree hypotheses are open: they have not been checked on any word); it builds on #7668 (`ceiling_native_J`, t0 = .0118103), whose modules are included unchanged. Lean proves nothing about the extracted data's fidelity, the word files, or the checker programs.

## The two new theorems
Both conclude `F(1 - t, invoice (caller 6 7) n 17) < 0` for `t` in `[t0, 1]`, i.e. kappa <= `t0` for any data meeting the hypotheses, on the 17-call invoice (`L = 17`) of the #7113 caller `caller 6 7` = C(8|9). Nothing shows that any word meets the tree hypotheses. On `[tJ0, 1]` they apply `ceiling_native_J` with its own hypotheses; on `[t0, tJ0]` they use the tree bound.

| theorem (module `CongestionTree`) | `t0` | what is new |
|---|---|---|
| `CongestionNative.ceiling_tree_CG` | .0117694 (`a0CG`) | node-partition tree bound: each source's I2 paths contain an out-tree of charts; a move is charged c2 / n(A) per source tree; minimum over source counts (\|J\| >= 4962, <= 48 partners per source, <= 104 sources); all sources use the shape list `cgR` with root `cgr0` |
| `CongestionNative.ceiling_tree_GD` | .0116750 (`a0GD`) | the same with `Bset ⊆ S`, `48 <= |Bset|`, `|S| <= 104`: sources in `Bset` use `gbR`/`gbr0` (the `E48` edge set), sources in `S \ Bset` use `gaR`/`gar0` |

The earlier (non-Lean) computations gave .0117604 and .0116675 using measured count profiles; the digits certified here are weaker by 9.0e-6 (.0117694 - .0117604) and 7.5e-6 (.0116750 - .0116675), because Lean takes only the stated counts (|J| >= 4962, <= 48 partners per source, <= 104 sources) and not the measured profiles. The certificate generator reported negative margins one digit lower (.0117693, .0116749); that is a generator output, not a Lean statement.

Scope. The statements are about abstract data. The shape tables (230 shapes `(dim, n, m)`) and `E48` were computed for one 104-port set; they are not claimed for other port sets. The five word files checked in `NATIVES.md` share that port set; `t7669` is our reproduction of the word of #7669, not that file.

What Lean proves: the soundness of 230-row DP tables (one `decide +kernel` per row, three tables), the tree cost bound over any `Tr` tree (`tree_cost_ge`), the double-counting reduction (`reduction`), the Lagrange line bound over the sources (`tree_lower`), a kernel certificate that the integer move weights are lower bounds of `c2` on the window (`W_lower`), and the per-piece numeric certificates (sigma, point enclosures, unit bounds, line checks, the derivative certificate for `hFbT_of`). All theorems print only propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound; no `sorry`, no `native_decide` (`CHECK.txt`).

Reading of the fields (status, as in `lean/Nov15/CongestionTree.md`):
- `J`, `S`, `hJS`, `hScard`, `hfib`, `hJcard`: junk cells, at most 104 sources, at most 48 partners per source, at least 4962 cells. Computed on the five word files (`NATIVES.md`); in Lean `T.J` is not tied to the `J` of `ceiling_native_J`.
- `R`, `x`, `hcell`, `hxk`: a source's cells are either transported (`x s`, each paying `capC`) or have a reader in `R s`. Open (I2 path and the own56 transport cap, as in `hcong`).
- `htreeA`, `htreeB` (`Tr`), `ms`, `Mv`, `hMv`: for each source the union of its cells' I2 paths contains an out-tree of charts whose nodes are shapes of the 230-shape DAG and whose edges are nested moves; readers of a node are covered by the readers of its children, and `|R| <= m(chart)`. Open (nested moves; path = chain of moves meeting at charts). Reader-set disjointness is not assumed.
- `hnd`, `hcap`, `nm`: tree moves are `Nodup` and every move is used by at most `nm e = n(A)` sources. Open (I2 capacity).
- `Mreg`, `Mun`, `hMsub`, `hdim`, `dA`, `dB`, and `hP1`, `hP2`: tree moves are register steps up to owned rises, `sum_{Mreg} c2 + capC * sum x <= sum over registers of c2 + Omega`, and owned-rise moves weigh at most `corrC`. Open (the same I2 charge as `hcong`, aggregated by moves).
- GD only: `Bset`, `hB`, `hBcard`, `htreeB` use `rsB`, the shape rows whose `mask` is E48, the edges realised by concrete nested charts for 48 ports. A computed fact on the one known port set (`NATIVES.md`, `e48.c`); the count behind it (the (3,4,12) and (5,8,6) charts cover only 1,344 of the 4,992 even cells) is neither in Lean nor script-checked for this package.
- `hcstMonoT`, `hcst0`, `hcst1`: monotonicity of cst on [a0, 1/10] (paper statement, as `hcstMono`) and two outward-rounded brackets (data, as `hcstTab`).
