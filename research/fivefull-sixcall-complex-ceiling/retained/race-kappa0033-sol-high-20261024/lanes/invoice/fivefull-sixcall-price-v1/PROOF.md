## Logarithm envelopes without evaluating a logarithm or fractional power

For integer rank r>=1 define

`L_r=(2/3)*floor(log2 r)`, `U_r=ceil(log2 r)`.

The atanh expansion ln2=2*(1/3+1/(3^3*3)+...) gives ln2>2/3. Also
ln2=integral_1^2 dx/x<1. Hence L_r<=ln r<=U_r. At C0=33/10000,
e^x>=1+x and e^-x>=1-x imply

```
r^(1-C0) <= r/(1+C0*L_r),
r^(1-C0) >= r*(1-C0*U_r).
```

The exact rational UPPER gate is therefore

`G=sum Tcap[r]*r/(1+C0*L_r) -sum P[r]*r*(1-C0*U_r)`.

## Why this one negative point excludes larger savings in this alphabet

Let D(C)=Tcap_(1-C)-P_(1-C). After combining equal ranks, its coefficients
are

```
{1:-11077,2:-2060,3:-2550,4:-495,5:-200,
 7:35,8:235,9:1590,17:224,25:224}.
```

For F(C)=D(C)/7^(1-C), every negative coefficient has base r/7<1 and every
positive coefficient has base r/7>=1. Increasing C decreases the exponent.
It therefore makes every negative term strictly smaller, every positive
term above rank7 strictly smaller, and the rank7 term constant. Thus F is
strictly decreasing throughout0<C<1. Since D(C0)<=G<0 and7^(1-C)>0,

`D(C)<0 for every C0<=C<1`.

The concavity cap holds throughout that interval, so the same negative
necessary moment applies to any four-bank degree allocation satisfying the
