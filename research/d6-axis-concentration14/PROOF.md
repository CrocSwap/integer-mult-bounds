# Sharp full D6 axis concentration at fourteen labels

Let R be the 120 E8 root lines, with leading-one binary labels
ell(r)=(1,c(r) mod 2) in the retained simple-root coordinates. For two
orthogonal root lines a,b, define the full axis

    F = {r in R : a dot r != 0, b dot r = 0}.

For every binary linear space V in F2^9 with dimension at most six,

    |{r in F : ell(r) in V}| >= 14
    implies {r in R : ell(r) in V} is contained in F.

The threshold fourteen is sharp. There is a six-space with thirteen axis
labels and seven additional root labels outside the axis. The theorem covers
arbitrary ambient spaces and arbitrary support subsets. In particular,
fourteen axis sites of any rank-at-most-six piece force its entire support
onto the axis. No coefficient, code-intersection or caller conclusion is
assumed. All geometric data are integral; the binary-label claim is independent
of a coefficient field and applies to supports over any field.

## The bulk seven-space

The full axis has 33 lines, consisting of the exceptional line a and a bulk
T of 32 lines. The exact model below follows the D6 model in PR3292, credited
there to PR3267 and the retained quadratic/root-label lineage.

The labels of T span a seven-space W. Its leading-one affine section H has
affine dimension six. The exceptional label ell(a) is outside W. Moreover,

    R intersect W = T.

Here and below intersections with binary spaces mean intersections of labels.
The norm quadratic form on the eight residue coordinates is

    q(x) = sum_i x_i + sum_(i<j) (s_i dot s_j)/4 * x_i*x_j mod 2.

Every full root label satisfies q=1. On H, the restriction f of q has polar
rank four, radical D of dimension two, and a nonzero derivative character
on D. T is exactly the locus f=1 in H. The primary replay verifies these
structural facts directly in one canonical ordered orthogonal pair. It also
checks the full-root closure equality, which must not be replaced by a
statement only about the 112 nonframe roots.

## Any rank-five bulk section has at most twelve labels

A binary space K of dimension at most five meets H in an affine space of
dimension at most four. If necessary, extend this section inside H to an
affine four-space with direction M. Its restricted polar rank is 0, 2 or 4.

If that rank is zero, the projection of M into the nondegenerate four-space
L/D is isotropic, hence has dimension at most two. Since dim M=4, M contains
the full two-dimensional radical D. The derivative of f on D is nonzero,
so translation by one radical vector pairs ones with zeros. The section
has eight ones.

If the restricted polar rank is two, a nonzero radical derivative again
balances the section. Otherwise the quadratic function descends to a
nondegenerate two-dimensional quotient. Its signed sum has absolute value
two, multiplied by the radical size four. The original section therefore
has four or twelve ones.

If the restricted polar rank is four, the signed sum has absolute value
four. The section has six or ten ones. Thus every case has at most twelve.
The standard signed-sum identity follows by squaring the sum and grouping
pairs by their difference. Only vectors in the polar radical survive;
a nonzero radical character cancels, and otherwise the magnitude is
2^(dimension - polar_rank/2). This uses no global E8 rank-five capacity
census or supplier premise.

For corroboration, the primary checker enumerates the 2,667 five-spaces
in W by their two-dimensional annihilators. Their bulk intersections have
sizes 0,4,6,8,10,12 with counts 63,120,512,1340,512,120.
The proof above explains the upper bound; enumeration is not its substitute.

## Concentration and sharpness

If V is contained in W, its full root closure is contained in T, hence F.
Otherwise dim(V intersect W)<=5. The preceding bound gives at most twelve
bulk labels, and the exceptional label adds at most one. Therefore

    V not contained in W implies |F intersect V| <= 13.

Fourteen axis labels force V to be contained in W and establish the theorem.
This argument does not assume that V is generated by its root intersection.
For an actual piece whose support has binary rank at most six, take V to
be its support span. All other support coordinates are then in the same axis.

The checker tests that the graph of its binary-label map has rank nine,
so the induced map is linear and invertible. Its orbit on ordered orthogonal

canonical theorem and sharpness witness transport to every ordered pair.
