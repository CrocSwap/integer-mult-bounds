# One internal gap in every stage misses total rank 130

This is a proof in characteristic two under the schedule hypothesis below.
The exponent comparison checks every stage, leaf, and power at `g=31`.
That domain is finite and the enumeration is complete. It is a check of
the monomial case split, not a census of matrices. Separately, the `q=1`
rank comparison is an exhaustive census of a finite set of `4` by `4`
matrices. The bound 403 does not use that census.

No new kappa is claimed. The selected frontier stays
`781451/250000000 = 0.003125804`.

## 1. Schedule

Use the paired-star contract in `research/coupled31-chronology/PROOF.md`.
The core and each of the `g` leaves have dimension `d`. This note uses two
equal halves, so `d = 2q`. Stage `j` has two complementary projectors on
the core and leaf `j`. In the eigenbasis of the half idempotent,

```text
P_+ = [[A+B, B], [B, A+B]],   R_+ = [[A, A], [A, A]],
P_- = [[A'+B, B], [B, A'+B]], R_- = [[A', A'], [A', A']],
```

with `A = diag(I_q, 0)`, `A' = I+A`, and `B = [[0, B12], [B21, 0]]`. The
off-diagonal blocks `B12` and `B21` stay arbitrary. A full call is
`(I+x P)(I+e R)` with `x = D+1` and `e^2 = 0`. A thin call is `I+e Z`.
The variables `D_j` are independent. The target is
`(I+e I) product_j (I+x_j E_j)`. The first-applied factor is the rightmost
factor. Matrix rows are outputs and columns are inputs.

**Hypothesis.** The stage blocks stand in the order `0, ..., g-1`. Inside
every stage, the two full calls occupy the two sides of one gap, either
half first, and that gap contains only thin maps. Every other thin map
stands in a gap before all stages, between two stage blocks, or after all
stages. No full call of another stage stands between the halves. Nested
intervals and crossed intervals are outside the hypothesis.

The core-to-leaf block is the core row and leaf column: the block that
sends a leaf into the core.

## 2. What a successful word must cancel

On one stage, with either half applied first, the pure product of the two
halves is `I+x E`, where `E` is the identity on the core and on that leaf.
The `e` coefficient of those two halves, with nothing between them, has
core-to-leaf block `D I_d` and leaf-to-core block `D I_d`. Both identities
are polynomial identities in the free algebra generated by the entries of
`B12` and `B21`, so they hold for every matrix size.

An internal thin map does not change this full-call contribution. Its pure
factor is `I`, and `e^2 = 0`, so the `R` terms are the same with or without
the map. The pure factor of every other stage remains `I+x E` for the same
reason. On the core, `I+x E_k` acts as multiplication by `D_k`. On leaf
`j` it acts as multiplication by `D_j` when `k = j` and as the identity
otherwise. Each of those actions is a single monomial.

Composing them, the full calls contribute exactly

```text
M_j = product_{k>=j} D_k
```

times `I_d` on the core-to-leaf-`j` block. The target's pure product is
block-diagonal in the core and the leaves, so its core-to-leaf blocks are
zero. In characteristic two the thin maps must contribute `M_j I_d` on
that block.

## 3. The squared monomial and the degree-1 block

Let `Z` be the sum of the thin maps in the internal gap of stage `t`, and
write `P_f`, `P_s` for the first and second halves. On the whole space

```text
U = I+x P_f = F + P_s + D_t P_f,
V = I+x P_s = F + P_f + D_t P_s,
```

with `F = I+E`. Then

```text
VZU = B0 + D_t B1 + D_t^2 B2,
B2 = P_s Z P_f,
B1 = (F+P_f) Z P_f + P_s Z (F+P_s).
```

`B2` is supported on the core and leaf `t`. The outside pure factors
multiply its four blocks by the monomials

```text
core <- leaf t:    product_{k>t} D_k,
leaf t <- leaf t:  1,
core <- core:      product_{k != t} D_k,
leaf t <- core:    product_{k<t} D_k.
```

Each product is a nonzero polynomial, and each resulting monomial has
`D_t` to the second power. The target and the full-call contribution are
square-free. A successful word therefore satisfies `P_s Z P_f = 0`.

On the stage leaf, the core block of `B1` is

```text
pi_core (P_f Z P_f + P_s Z P_s).
```

As a free-algebra identity, for either half order and with `B12`, `B21`,
and every entry of `Z` kept as noncommuting letters, this block equals

```text
pi_core (Z P_f + P_s Z)
```

on the leaf columns. It is the sum of two sandwiches. Each sandwich has
rank at most `rank(Z)`, so the surviving degree-1 core-to-leaf block has
rank at most `2 rank(Z)`. The outside factor on this block is the monomial
`product_{k>t} D_k`, which does not change the rank.

For a leaf `j` other than `t`, the same expansion gives

```text
pi_core VZU pi_j = pi_core P_f Z pi_j + D_t pi_core P_s Z pi_j.
```

There is no `D_t^2` term. Each of those two blocks is one sandwich, so
each has rank at most `rank(Z)`.

## 4. Which monomials meet `M_j`

Stages before and after `t` multiply the internal contribution by

```text
lambda(t,j) = (product_{k>t} D_k) * (D_j if j<t else 1).
```

`lambda` does not contain `D_t`. The equality
`lambda(t,j) D_t^a = M_j` holds for exactly these pairs:

- `a = 1` and `j = t`, with rank weight 2,
- `a = 1` and `j = t-1`, when `t >= 1`, with rank weight 1,
- `a = 0` and `j = t+1`, when `t+1 <= g-1`, with rank weight 1.

Power `a = 2` produces a square and never equals `M_j`. No leaf is met by
two powers. An endpoint stage therefore has weight sum 3, and an interior
stage has weight sum 4.

An external thin map in the gap after `gamma` completed stages is multiplied
by one monomial `mu(gamma,j)`. That monomial equals `M_j` exactly for
`j` in `{gamma-1, gamma}`, intersected with the leaf range. Each of those
blocks is the core-to-leaf block of the external map itself, so the rank
weight is 1 and the weight sum of one gap is at most 2.

The registered checker compares these exponents for every stage, every
leaf, and every power at `g=31`, and again at `g=4`.

## 5. Rank

Let `R` be the total thin rank, the sum of the ranks of every internal and
external thin map. On leaf `j` the thin contribution to `M_j` equals
`I_d`, which has rank `2q`. Rank is subadditive, and each contributing
block has rank at most its weight times the rank of its map. Summing over
the `g` leaves, the left side is `2 q g`. On the right, each thin map is
charged at most its weight sum. Every map in this schedule has weight sum
at most 4, so

```text
2 q g <= 4 R,    R >= q g / 2.
```

At `g=31` and `q=26` the bound is `26 * 31 / 2 = 403`. Five thin maps of
rank 26 have total rank `130`. Since `130 < 403`, no word in this schedule
meets the endpoint at total thin rank 130.

The factor 2 on the own-leaf block is the proved estimate. It is sharp for
the two-sandwich expression when the square is allowed to be nonzero: one
`q=1` matrix of rank 1 has a degree-1 block of rank 2. Under the square
condition the same finite domain tells a stricter story. Every `4` by `4`
matrix over `F_2`, every choice of `B12, B21` in `F_2`, and both half
orders were enumerated. Whenever `P_s Z P_f = 0`, the degree-1 block has
rank at most `rank(Z)`. That is an exhaustive census of those 4 by 4
matrices. It is not a proof for `q=26`, and the bound 403 does not use it.

## 6. Controls

A direct `g=3`, `q=1` word, for every stage, both half orders, and every
`B12, B21` in `F_2`, matches the predicted core-to-leaf polynomial of one
internal map. Two internal maps add, and the zero map does not change the
`e` coefficient. With `B = 0` and the leaf-to-core identity in every stage,
each core-to-leaf block cancels and no other core-to-leaf monomial remains.
That word has total rank `2 q g = 6` at `q=1`. It is a coefficient control.
It is not a word of total rank 130, and it is not claimed to meet the rest
of the target: the leaf-to-core blocks are still nonzero.

The same leaf-to-core identity at `q=26`, on one stage and with `B = 0`,
has rank `52`, square zero, and degree-1 core-to-leaf block `I_52`. One
stage can meet its own leaf. The obstruction is the sum over all 31 leaves.

## 7. What this does not prove

- It does not prove that every `g=31`, `q=26` word needs total thin rank
  403. A nested interval or a crossed interval is a different schedule.
- It does not prove the one-gap bound `q(g-3) = 728` of open PR #7025.
  That hypothesis has an internal gap in at most one stage. The bound
  proved here is the smaller number `q g / 2` for a larger schedule, and
  403 already separates 130 from this schedule.
- It does not prove the consecutive bound `q g = 806` of open PR #7005.
  That argument excludes the internal slot.
- The factor 2 is not claimed to be sharp for successful words. The `q=1`
  census says that, on that domain, the square condition removes the
  excess. Section 5 records the census as a census.
- No witness for `g=31`, `q=26` is constructed. The selected frontier is
  untouched.
