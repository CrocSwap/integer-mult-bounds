# Port windows are a fractional Haemers bound: w >= 53/6 on P104 (no new kappa)

**No new kappa.** No word is built and nothing is priced. This package proves that the window of a block mask pattern on the ports is the fractional Haemers bound of the odd graph on the ports, proves a floor of 53/6 for the window of P104 over every field, and records exact data at k = 2. Statements and proofs are in `THEOREM.md`.

Tags (as in `THEOREM.md`): **[C]** exact computation reproduced by `check.py` or `check_sat.py` (one PASS line per claim); **[P]** hand proof; **[P+C]** both; **[R]** a solver run recorded in `data/sat_results.json`, not repeated by the checks.

## Setting

Ports are vectors of F_2^9; two distinct ports are *adjacent* if their F_2 dot product is 1 and *orthogonal* if it is 0. P104 is the 104-port set in `data/ports_p104.json`; P120 is the 120 vectors of weight 3 mod 4; P112 is P104 plus one of the two odd 8-cliques that P120 deletes. A **k-block pattern** is a matrix B with k x k blocks, B_TT invertible and B_ST = B_TS = 0 on every orthogonal pair; the **window** is w = inf_k min rank(B)/k.

## Results

1. **Theorem 1 [P].** The window is the fractional Haemers bound H_f(G; F) of the odd graph G of Bukh and Cox (IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory 65 (2019), 3340-3348; arXiv:1802.00476), whose definition by d-representations is word for word the block pattern with identity diagonal blocks. The parameter is due to Blasiak's thesis (Cornell, 2013, as cited there). At k = 1 it is Haemers' bound (IEEE Trans. Inform. Theory 25 (1979), 231-232). The window of any port set with an orthogonal 8-set is at least 8.
2. **Theorem 3, Theorem 2 items 1 and 2 [P+C].** For an orthogonal 8-set K and weights on K, every k-block pattern of rank 8k + j has j/k >= (E - mu)/(mu + rho). On P104 (960 of its 1728 orthogonal 8-sets have E = 22 and degrees (5,5,5,5,6,6,6,6)) the weights 1/2 give **j/k >= 5/6, so w(P104; F) >= 53/6 over every field**, and rank >= 9k for k <= 5; the same theorem gives 1 on P112 and P120, and the Gram matrix has rank 9 in characteristic 2, so **w = 9 exactly on P112 and P120 in characteristic 2**.
3. **Theorem 4 [P+C].** In characteristic not 2, w >= 13 on P104 (14 on P112, 15 on P120): the Hadamard product with the Gram matrix of an isomorphic copy of the odd graph inside the E8 root lines is the identity.
4. **The 14-port pattern [C].** A pattern with k = 2 and rank 17 (< 18 = 9k) exists on a 14-port subset, exact over Q, F_2, F_3 and integral, with 150 forced-zero pairs and 0 violations. So "rank >= 9k" fails on subsets; the window need not be an integer multiple.
5. **The 104-port formula at k = 2, rank 17 over F_2 [R].** UNSAT with a DRAT proof (25.7 MB, `data/full_k2_rank17_f2.drat.xz`) that drat-trim verified (forward, 74 s). The formulas are regenerated byte for byte by `check_sat.py`. Theorem 3 gives the same fact over every field, so this is a cross-check of the encoding.
6. **Theorem 5 [P+C].** A tensor lift of width f has rank 9f on P104 (ratio 9), and on a tensor window the decoder price satisfies c_k >= k c_1 (column restriction).

## Scope and what is not claimed

No bound on kappa and no word. The window is defined by the block mask; that a physical construction realises a k-block window with exactly this mask for k > 1 is not derived. The value of w on P104 is only known to lie in [53/6, 9] in characteristic 2 (first undecided case k = 6, rank 53); odd characteristic has lower bounds only; the weights of Theorem 3 are from a grid and are not claimed optimal. No human has reviewed the proofs; Theorem 3 and the odd-characteristic argument were re-derived by a second model session (see `THEOREM.md`, section 8).

## Exact checks

`python3.12 -I -B research/p104-port-window-haemers/check.py` (about 15 s) and `check_sat.py` (a few seconds), or `verify.sh` from anywhere. Standard library only. The runs end with `PASS_P104_PORT_WINDOW_HAEMERS` and `PASS_P104_PORT_WINDOW_SAT`. `check.py` prints one PASS line per claim and fails on any missing marker, wrong count or failing control; the controls include planted violations that must be caught. The solver and proof-checker runs of item 5 are recorded, not repeated; to repeat them, emit a formula with `python3.12 -I -B checks/satenc.py OUT.cnf 17 full`, run kissat on it, and run drat-trim on the shipped proof (after `xz -d`).
