**Theorem.** Every such protocol pays total upward rank at least 4,750. The original word attains equality at each of the eight dimension thresholds. Therefore no replacement with total upward rank 4,749 or less can implement the operator. This includes replacing any single upward edge by one upward edge of strictly smaller grade with all other incidences held fixed, wherever the replacement is placed. It also includes an arbitrary redesign whose net upward loss is one grade. The statement permits changes to every field value and to the final local decoding.
