# Fixed cache paths and scalar merge trees

This appendix adopts two results from the unchanged
[chronological cache proof](frozen/lanes/cover-algebra/CHRONOLOGICAL-CACHE-PATHS.md).
It gives no new complete kappa bound.

For a fixed chronological path, a fixed read schedule, and supplied exact
native transitions, the accessible logical rows are exactly the module
generated by the available snapshot rows over the permitted coefficient
ring. The cache must start at zero in an owned field that may be cleared.
A rational solution need not be a dyadic solution. Final unchanged X and
restored-helper rows may be used for cleanup. All query cleanups must finish
before the first X repair if they use those endpoint rows.

Consider one scalar-content rooted tree whose edges move into containing
frames. Grant every snapshot time. An edge costs at least its frame dimension
increase. At a node v, let I_v be the intersection of its descendant leaf
frames. Codimension subadditivity and induction give

```text
cost below v >= dim(U_v) - dim(I_v).
```

The actual snapshot support law shows that every output row is supported
on ports perpendicular to I_root. The exact line minors show that a nonzero
centre query has a source-support annihilator of dimension at most one.
With FULL dimension nine, the tree therefore costs at least eight units of
native rank. Eight separately paid trees cost at least 64.

These bounds do not add across shared paid edges. They do not prove a global
64 bound for a shared multi-output network. Logical address changes, changed
core calls, and cuts inside native children are outside the tree model.
The general cut proof and stronger numerical network certificates in the
parent package remain separate results.

The [independent review](REVIEW.md) checks the proof. The appendix replay
checks the existing exact snapshot support and line-minor certificate. It
does not construct a native cache or prove a complete storage schedule.
