# Attribution and claim boundaries

The new sweep, exact experiment engines, balanced boundary program, cross-chart catalytic
restricted-ADD transport, observer-coset census, physical displacement action,
combined cost screens and synthesis report were prepared by zxv with substantial
OpenAI Codex assistance for Dillon Amburgey. No subagents were used in this sweep.
This is Apache-2.0 research code and prose; full papers were read temporarily and
are not redistributed.

The 25 papers and their authors are individually credited in PAPERS.json and
LITERATURE.md. An adaptation is not a claim of priority for its source technique,
a full-paper reproduction, or a transfer of the paper's asymptotic theorem to
integer multiplication. Access-limited entries explicitly say so.

Retain the complete native/source attribution in
`inputs/PROVIDER-ATTRIBUTION.md`. In particular, Cao Yifan / intqwq supplies the
native physical source; Dugongue / mdumitrean supplies the center-free code and

interfaces; Ryanson Jonathan supplies controller checks; Jacob Alan Sussman
supplies mixed-target recursion; Ali Romman's cached-center candidate is retained
in the provider lineage. The repository's coded-native-caller normalizers and
exact Clifford evaluator are reused, not newly authored here.
