\begin{abstract}
We give a checked finite composition of the balanced coarse-column sums
of PR69 with the anchored split-pair graph of PR71. A cost-ordered dependency
basis is used when reclaiming scratch carriers. At dimensions 23 and 25 the
result has 27,338 and 35,939 auxiliary roles. Under the inherited analytic,
all-size framed-word, residual-compiler, routing, exact-recovery and fixed
finite-alphabet multitape interfaces, it yields
\(T(n)=O(n(\log n)^{1-\kappa})\), where
\(\kappa=12886963972497/250000000000000000\).
This is a 0.2590891819\% improvement over the pinned PR71 witness.
The complete old child list is excluded at the new bit saving, while the new
list passes exact rational bounds. The finite tests and this conditional
argument are not a formalization or independent expert review of the full
multiplication theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Construction and inherited interfaces}
We retain the original-envelope geometry, simultaneous rational \(I+J\)
basis, two-stage data network and complex branch of the cited construction
chain. Address geometry is rational; the scalar payloads in the bit branch
are over \(\F\). The graph is Avi Eisenberg's interval-strip and core-aware
pair graph \cite{pr62}, with the split operation of Rohan Garg \cite{pr59}
and the anchored choices of Chafik Boukhalfa \cite{pr71}.

At each common point, start with the inherited alternating point order and
move the lowest intact global pair to the beginning, preserving the other
points' relative order. At the first three recursive levels use split vector
\([1,1,2]\): split the first pair at the first two levels and the middle
group at the third when it is a pair. Splitting is disabled when at most
three points remain. The groups have size one or two, partition the current
point set, and strictly reduce its size. Later levels retain ordinary pairing.
Region order is \texttt{cover-core} at \(h=23\) and
\texttt{reverse-node} at \(h=25\), as in PR71.

The new composition uses eumemic's balanced coarse-column identity
\cite{pr69} inside this split graph. For disjoint groups \(G_i,G_j\), set
\[
 c_{ij}=\sum_{b\in G_j}\left(\sum_{a\in G_i} e(a,b)\right).
 \tag{1}
\]
This equals the previous flat sum, with every edge appearing once. The two
column sums at a pair-pair interaction are interned by their actual supports
and can be shared with existing strip computations. Equation (1) also applies
when either group is a singleton. No producer output or data pair is removed.
Every realized support, scalar output and frame inclusion is checked again.

The chosen region compiler is descended from eumemic's reversible compiler
\cite{pr57}, with Chafik Boukhalfa's reclaimed carriers \cite{pr60}, Rohan
Arun's profile-cost oracle \cite{pr67}, and Dominik Scholz's compatible pending
controls \cite{pr68}. These are inherited contributions. Our finite witness
specifies their composition with (1), and orders eligible live and retired
basis candidates by their actual profile cost before selecting a clearing
dependency. This selection differs from considering retired candidates only
in descending frame rank. No optimal matching or global search theorem is used.

\section{Valid clearing with a different dependency basis}
Write \(F_s\) for a carrier's current frame and \(E\) for the frame of the
current region. Retired candidates satisfy \(F_s\subseteq E\). A pending live
control with scheduled next-use frame \(G_s\) is eligible only if
\[
 F_s\subseteq E\subseteq G_s.                         \tag{2}
\]
The temporary binary elimination basis contains current region anchors,
eligible live controls, and independent eligible retired candidates.
If a candidate's clean signal is dependent, elimination supplies a literal
expression \(v_s=\sum_{a\in A}v_a\), with \(s\notin A\).

\begin{proposition}
Changing the order of eligible basis candidates and selecting any resulting
dependency preserves the reversible dirty-scratch interface, provided every
selected operand raise is recorded and charged.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Searching and comparing dependencies changes no physical value or frame.
For the selected expression, emit the two-carrier XORs
\(s\leftarrow s+a\), for \(a\in A\), at frame \(E\).
Each is invertible, raises both operands through containing frames, and leaves
the control's scalar value unchanged. The clean signal on \(s\) becomes zero;
its arbitrary dirty part need not be zero. A live control retains its future
validity by (2). Removing \(s\) from the retired set and reusing it therefore
introduces neither a free copy nor a zero-initialized auxiliary assumption.
All frame incidences appear in the actual serialized word.
\end{proof}

For discovery only, let \(H(F,G)\) denote the sum of \(t\log t\) over the
actual fixed-basis blocks of the transition \(F\to G\), evaluated on a fixed
integer grid using rational logarithm enclosures. A live control is priced by
\[
 H(F_s,G_s)-H(F_s,E)-H(E,G_s),                         \tag{3}
\]
and a retired carrier is priced with the full terminal frame in place of
\(G_s\). Eligible controls and retired candidates are sorted by this cost;
the complete stored dependency is scored with all operands included.
The earliest candidate order is used only to construct a basis and its
expressions. Every dependent candidate is considered before selecting the
lowest scored expression. Equation (3) predicts local fragmentation and does
not assert the cost of unknown later uses. Only the final complete profile
is an acceptance criterion.

Let \(L\) be the resulting invertible mixer, \(V\) the source injection and
\(J\) the terminal scatter. The complete fresh-source check establishes
\(JLV=I\). For source \(x\), target \(y\) and arbitrary scratch \(d\), the
chronological word
\[
 L,\ J,\ L^{-1},\ V,\ L,\ J,\ L^{-1},\ V              \tag{4}
\]
adds \(JLd+JL(d+Vx)=x\) to the target and restores the scratch over \(\F\).
The reversed transposed word gives the other orientation. The literal
terminal ports, including all retained totals, are checked independently;
net cancellation does not excuse uncharged extra scatter gates.

\section{Complete physical accounting}
At \(h\in\{23,25\}\), let \(v_h=\binom h3\) and let \(R_h\) be the
auxiliary role count. Each local profile includes every actual operand
promotion, copied retained center, ordinary output growth and final cleanup.
Its rank mass satisfies
\[
 \sum_t t n_{h,t}=hR_h+h(h-1).                        \tag{5}
\]
Both the event log and transitions reconstructed from the literal XOR word
give the same profile input. The unchanged \(I+J\) profiler computes its
ordered contiguous blocks with the inherited bounded-minor and three-prime
CRT checks. There are no modular disagreements.

\section{Exact exponent and conditional assembly}
For saving \(a>0\), the characteristic of the complete bit profile is
\[
 F(a)=\frac{1}{mW}\sum_t n_t t
           \exp\!\left(a\log\frac mt\right).          \tag{7}
\]
The selected witness is
\[
 a=\frac{51550513208569}{10^{18}}.                    \tag{8}
\]
All logarithms are enclosed by a rational atanh series with explicit
remainder. For \(0\le u\le v<1\), use the degree-eight Taylor lower bound
at \(u\), and the upper bound
\[
 \sum_{j=0}^{8}\frac{v^j}{j!}
       +\frac{v^9}{9!\,(1-v/10)}                     \tag{9}
\]
at \(v\). The geometric remainder follows from the ratio of successive
omitted terms. Directed rounding widens the intervals. The exact accepted
upper moment is below one; the lower moment at \(a+10^{-18}\) is above one.
A second evaluation uses powers of \(3/2\) for logarithm range reduction,
60 atanh terms and a degree-twelve exponential bound, and confirms both signs.

The complete PR71 profile, with its own width, has a lower moment above one
at (8). Thus the new finite profile gives a strict structural improvement.
The comparison is to PR71 commit
\texttt{1bef94fd40a746452548c84a4a8f8834670a3113}.

\begin{theorem}[Conditional on the inherited interfaces]
Assume the base integer-multiplication theorem and the all-size ordered
residual compiler, framed-word transfer, fixed finite-alphabet multitape
layout, analytic and semantic reductions, routing, eligible-prime setup and
exact-recovery interfaces used by the cited construction chain. Then
\[
 T(n)=O\!\left(n(\log n)^{1-\kappa}\right),\qquad
 \kappa=\frac{12886963972497}{250000000000000000}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use (4)--(8) as the new fixed bit-network interface in the inherited balanced
assembly. The complex saving remains \(717/10^7\), \(\beta=1/20\), and the
semantic guard remains one. Set \(\eta=10^{-12}\),
\(q=a(1-2\eta)\), \(\epsilon=(1-\eta)/(1+q)\). The controlling margin is
\(G=\epsilon q\). The certificate recomputes all 47 strict constraints and
seven margins and verifies \(\kappa<G<\kappa+10^{-18}\).
The bit wire bound remains 28 bits and the bit halving degree remains nine:
\(2\cdot529^9<575^9\), whereas \(2\cdot529^8\ge575^8\).
The unchanged complex branch retains its literal scalar charge and halving
degree. The row-stock and semantic bounds are consequently valid with the
updated \(W\); their exact inequalities and sufficient arithmetic cutoffs
are reevaluated. The positive final slack permits the inherited logarithmic
absorption. Arithmetic cutoffs alone are not a complete operational threshold.
\end{proof}

\section{Reproduction and limits}
Run \texttt{make -C research/balanced-split-reclamation verify} to regenerate
both physical words byte-for-byte, replay every input and dirty basis column
in both orientations, rebuild the actual profiles and exact certificate, and
run failure-mode controls. Run the repository's \texttt{make -j1 verify}
for its broader retained checks. The twelve focused tests include malformed
indices, aliased sources, understated width, omitted paid children, wrong
terminal ports, canceling uncharged scatter additions and adjacent exponent
grid rejection. Source hashes are frozen explicitly; verification does not
refresh them.

These checks verify fixed finite objects and inequalities. They do not execute
the complete asymptotic multiplication machine, establish global optimality,
formalize the inherited interfaces, or demonstrate a practical speedup.
This work used substantial OpenAI Codex assistance; its additional checks are
not an independent human referee report. Contributor code, licenses and notices
remain preserved. The new result is the concrete composition and its checked
improvement, not authorship of the inherited ingredients.
