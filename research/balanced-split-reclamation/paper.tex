\begin{abstract}
We specify a finite refinement of the balanced, anchored split-frame
construction. It combines an offset coarse-cell rule, compatible carry
exchanges, and a cyclic coordinate flag at dimension 25. At dimensions 23
and 25 the selected words have 27,043 and 35,505 auxiliary roles. Under the
inherited all-size framed-word, residual-compiler, analytic, routing,
exact-recovery and fixed finite-alphabet multitape interfaces, the witness
gives \(T(n)=O(n(\log n)^{1-\kappa})\), with
\(\kappa=52062354654791/10^{18}\). This is 0.0568021906\% above the complete
pinned PR82 profile. Newer community submissions report larger values; no
latest-record or global-optimality claim is made. Exact finite checks and this
written conditional argument do not formalize the complete multiplication theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{The selected graph and compiler choices}
We retain the original-envelope geometry, rational \(I+J\) basis, complete
two-stage data network and complex branch of the cited construction chain.
Address geometry is rational; bit payloads are over \(\F\). The graph uses
Avi Eisenberg's interval strips and core-aware pair assembly \cite{pr62},
Rohan Garg's split operation \cite{pr59}, and Chafik Boukhalfa's anchored
\([1,1,2]\) choices \cite{pr71}. At every common point the lowest intact
global pair is moved to the start of the inherited alternating point order.
The first pair is split at the first two recursive levels and the middle
group at the third, when it is a pair. Splitting is disabled at at most
three remaining points. Thus the groups partition the points, have size one
or two, and strictly reduce the recursive size.

For groups \(G_i,G_j\), both evaluations
\[
 \sum_{b\in G_j}\left(\sum_{a\in G_i}e(a,b)\right)
 =\sum_{a\in G_i}\left(\sum_{b\in G_j}e(a,b)\right)       \tag{1}
\]
compute the same disjoint coarse edge sum. The balanced columns originate in
PR69 \cite{pr69}; the row and half-plane choices and future-compatible
completion are from PR79 \cite{pr79}. Our selected rule at \(h=23\) uses
columns for \(i+j<n_g+1\) and rows otherwise, where indices start at zero
and \(n_g\) is the current group count. This shifts the earlier boundary
\(i+j<n_g-1\) by two. At \(h=25\), every cell uses rows. Support interning
shares these intermediate sums with the strip computations. Every scalar
output, disjoint addition and envelope is rechecked after construction.

Thomas DiFiore's envelope ordering \cite{pr74} bijectively renumbers
non-source nodes, preserves order within each envelope and remaps every
argument, output and provenance reference. Derived support caches are cleared
after renumbering. Region order is \texttt{cover-core} at \(h=23\) and
\texttt{reverse-node} at \(h=25\). The dependency checks remain enabled.

The compiler descends from eumemic's reversible region synthesis
\cite{pr57}, Chafik Boukhalfa's reclamation \cite{pr60}, Rohan Arun's
profile-cost selection \cite{pr67}, and Dominik Scholz's compatible live
controls \cite{pr68}. At \(h=23\) we retain descending-rank retired candidates
and insertion-order live controls. At \(h=25\), both enter the temporary
basis in profile-cost order. After desired output and retained-input rows,
independent unit-completion rows are prioritized by their compatible future
uses, then clean-signal support size and input index, as in PR79.
These choices specify one finite algorithm; no optimal search is asserted.

\section{Reclamation, exchanges and arbitrary dirty data}
Let \(F_s\) be a carrier's current frame, \(E\) the current region and
\(G_s\) its scheduled next-use frame. Retired candidates satisfy
\(F_s\subseteq E\); a pending live control is eligible only if
\[
 F_s\subseteq E\subseteq G_s.                         \tag{2}
\]
The temporary binary basis contains region anchors, compatible live controls
and independent retired candidates. A dependent retired candidate supplies
a literal relation \(v_s=\sum_{a\in A}v_a\), with \(s\notin A\).
Emit \(s\leftarrow s+a\) for all \(a\in A\), raising both operands to
\(E\) and recording every incidence. This clears the clean signal, not the
arbitrary old dirty value. Each gate is invertible and each live control's
value is unchanged; (2) preserves its future eligibility.

For discovery, let \(H(F,G)\) be the sum of \(t\log t\) over the
fixed-basis blocks of \(F\to G\), evaluated on a rationally defined integer
grid. A live control is scored by
\[
 H(F_s,G_s)-H(F_s,E)-H(E,G_s),                         \tag{3}
\]
and a retired carrier uses the full endpoint instead of \(G_s\). All
operands of a proposed clearing expression are included. The score is a
heuristic: later operations can change its prediction, and only the final
complete physical profile is accepted.

The selected matching uses three passes of the improving single carry
exchanges of PR70/76 \cite{pr70,pr76}, supplemented by two-region cycles.
The two independence constraints are the direct sum of the regions' linear
matroids and the unique-use partition constraint.
\begin{proposition}
Suppose selected carries connect distinct regions \(g_1,g_2\) to distinct
uses \(u_1,u_2\). Replace them by existing eligible candidates
\((g_1,u_2)\) and \((g_2,u_1)\), provided each region's desired output rows
and remaining retained rows stay independent after its own replacement.
Then cardinality, unique uses, causal eligibility and local invertibility
are preserved.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Each region loses one row and gains one row; the two stated rank tests
are exactly its remaining linear constraint. The same two right uses remain
occupied once each, and every new edge already belongs to the checked
eligible set. Completing each independent row set to a square basis gives
an invertible local map. Its literal row-XOR synthesis therefore works on
arbitrary physical input values, including dirty components.
\end{proof}
The selected words use 113 two-region cycles at \(h=23\) and 86 at \(h=25\).
Open two-region reassignment paths were also screened; none was selected.
These are finite choices, not a theorem of weighted-matroid optimality.

Let \(L\) be the resulting invertible scratch mixer, \(V\) the source
injection and \(J\) the terminal scatter. The complete fresh-source map
satisfies \(JLV=I\). The chronological word
\[
 L,\ J,\ L^{-1},\ V,\ L,\ J,\ L^{-1},\ V             \tag{4}
\]
adds \(JLd+JL(d+Vx)=x\) to the target over \(\F\), restores arbitrary
scratch \(d\), and leaves the source unchanged. The reversed transposed
word gives the opposite orientation. Both identities are independently
replayed on every ordinary and dirty basis vector.

\section{Coordinate flags and their exact costs}
The coordinate-conjugacy method is inherited from PR74/78/82
\cite{pr74,pr82}. For a point permutation matrix \(P\),
\(PJ=JP\), so \(P\) commutes with \(I+J\) and preserves \(I-J/9\).
Consequently the frame projector obeys
\[
 P\,\Pi_{C,U}\,P^{-1}=\Pi_{PC,PU}.                   \tag{5}
\]
Ranks, nondegeneracy, inclusions and the inherited denominator bounds are
preserved. We relabel each source triple, output common point and triple,
frame mask and scatter target consistently. Slots and literal mixer XORs
are unchanged by this final relabeling. Thus the full source/target/dirty
identity is conjugated, not assumed from a rank histogram.

At \(h=23\), the retained PR82 flag is specified by the coordinate order
\[
 (1,3,2,4,5,\ldots,19,21,20,22,0),
\]
meaning that its \(j\)-th listed old coordinate maps to coordinate \(j\).
At \(h=25\), our selected flag is \(i\mapsto i+1\pmod {25}\).
The h23 discovery oracle retains the original coordinates; the h25 oracle
uses the final cyclic flag for both carry and reclamation pricing. Acceptance
in both cases uses the actual relabeled word and newly reconstructed profile.

Contiguous pivot blocks depend on coordinate order, so their costs need not
be invariant under (5). They are regenerated. In contrast, the data component
contains every pair of source triples. Independent point permutations induce
a bijection of this complete Cartesian product, preserving the aggregate
inherited data, endpoint and exterior classes, including all recovered
exceptional cases. This would not justify retaining an arbitrary sample's
profile.

Every scatter gate reads an auxiliary and writes a target, so the scatter
gates commute. Conjugation can change their lexical target order. The verifier
therefore checks the exact terminal-port multiset with multiplicities, while
rejecting even canceling extra gates. The inherited frame schedule can still
group retained-center reads and ordinary piece reads in its charged order.

\section{Exact exponent and conditional theorem}
For a bit saving \(a>0\), the complete characteristic is
\[
 F(a)=\frac1{mW}\sum_t n_t t\exp\!\left(a\log\frac mt\right).
                                                                  \tag{8}
\]
The strict witness is
\[
 a=\frac{26032532642421}{500000000000000000}.                       \tag{9}
\]
Rational atanh-series bounds enclose every logarithm. For
\(0\le u\le v<1\), a degree-eight Taylor lower bound at \(u\) and the
upper bound
\[
 \sum_{j=0}^{8}\frac{v^j}{j!}+\frac{v^9}{9!\,(1-v/10)}            \tag{10}
\]
are used with outward rounding. Exact arithmetic proves an upper moment
below one at (9) and a lower moment above one at \(a+10^{-18}\). A second
evaluation uses logarithm range reduction by \(3/2\), 60 atanh terms and
a degree-twelve exponential bound and confirms both signs. The complete
PR82 list, with its own width, has a lower moment above one at (9).

\begin{theorem}[Conditional on the inherited interfaces]
Assume the base multiplication theorem and the all-size ordered residual
compiler, framed-word transfer, fixed finite-alphabet multitape layout,
analytic/semantic reductions, routing, eligible-prime setup and exact-recovery
interfaces used by the cited chain. Then
\[
 T(n)=O\!\left(n(\log n)^{1-\kappa}\right),\qquad
 \kappa=\frac{52062354654791}{10^{18}}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use (4)--(9) as the fixed bit interface in the inherited balanced assembly.
The complex saving remains \(717/10^7\), \(\beta=1/20\), and the semantic
guard remains one. Set \(\eta=10^{-12}\), \(q=a(1-2\eta)\),
\(\epsilon=(1-\eta)/(1+q)\). The controlling margin is \(G=\epsilon q\).
All 47 strict constraints and seven margins are recomputed, and
\(\kappa<G<\kappa+10^{-18}\).

The actual width now requires 27 bits. The bit halving degree is nine,
since \(2\cdot529^9<575^9\) and \(2\cdot529^8\ge575^8\).
The unchanged complex branch has degree 20 and wire bound 30. Thus the
row coefficient is \(27\cdot9+30\cdot20=843\), and the inherited degree-2000
row stock has gap \(2000-(51/25)843=7007/25>0\), with suffix slope 8000.
The complex literal scalar charge is unchanged. The semantic, row-stock,
remaining strict inequalities and sufficient arithmetic cutoffs are checked
with these derived fields. Positive final slack gives the inherited logarithmic
absorption. Arithmetic cutoffs are not a complete operational threshold.
\end{proof}

The comparison is to PR82 commit
\href{https://github.com/rohanarun/integer-mult-bounds/tree/3410b940aa26e5876202152dfa7c4f66451ee22c}{\texttt{3410b940aa26}}.
The improvement is 0.0568021906\%. Later community submissions have larger
reported exponents; this is not a latest-record claim. Our earlier PR77
witness remains reproducible at
\href{https://github.com/huxint/integer-mult-bounds/tree/83298467291d3bd4b53f76faaef60dbf585be171}{\texttt{83298467291d}}.

\section{Reproduction, attribution and limits}
Run \texttt{make -C research/balanced-split-reclamation verify} for deterministic
word regeneration, independent full dirty-basis replay in both orientations,
actual transition/profile reconstruction, exact arithmetic and 17 focused
failure-mode tests. These include negative indices, aliased sources, omitted
paid children, wrong or canceling uncharged scatter ports, stale bridge fields,
adjacent exponent-grid rejection and disabled Python assertions. Run the
repository's \texttt{make -j1 verify} for its retained broad checks. Run receipts
record environmental interruptions and any sequential continuation separately.
Source freezes are explicit and are never refreshed by verification.

The finite checks do not execute or formalize the entire asymptotic machine,
prove global optimality, establish the inherited interfaces independently, or
show practical speedup. This package and its additional checks used substantial
OpenAI Codex assistance and are not independent human expert review. The new
contribution is the explicit selected refinement and checked comparison;
general carry exchanges, coordinate conjugacy and inherited graph/compiler
techniques retain their authorship and notices.
