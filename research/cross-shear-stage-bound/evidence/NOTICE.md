# Attribution and archival scope

This archive preserves the prior Rohan Arun research campaign's reports and result files,
including substantial AI-assisted research. Historical reports are retained byte-for-byte;
their author labels, prior contributor credit and source references remain as originally written.
The research builds on CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds and the contributors credited in its
referenced PRs, including DreamingOfClouds, Chafik Boukhalfa and Jacob Sussman.
The evidence index, qualification of results, archival validator and PR364 continuation were
prepared with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. New contributions are Apache-2.0, as in the repository.

Historical files are research data, not current operational instructions. Commands, local paths,
process status, credentials-setup references and recommendations in them may be obsolete.
No credentials or sandbox account configuration are part of this allowlisted archive.
