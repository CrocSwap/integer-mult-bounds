## What is new: helper splits and sinks
* **Splits, counted on the word** (`cover_check.py`, against the #6917 word): 69 new helper registers (labels 1362 to 1430), none of them a main, every one ending at rank 9; the 69 pieces are matched to 69 distinct parents among the old helpers (the matching is not unique; nothing below depends on which one is taken). Each parent keeps its path up to a frame F and its piece takes the path from F to the old final frame; **frames are strictly nested** on all 69 (initial of the parent, strictly below its new final frame, which is the initial frame of the piece, strictly below its final frame, which is the old final frame), as subspaces. By (initial rank of the parent, rank climbed by the parent, rank climbed by the piece): (0, 1, 8) × 4, (0, 3, 6) × 17, (0, 4, 5) × 12, (0, 5, 4) × 20, (0, 6, 3) × 13, (0, 7, 2) × 1, (0, 8, 1) × 1, (1, 4, 4) × 1.
* **One credit per helper.** All 1031 helpers follow one contiguous, strictly ascending, unowned chain of frames from the initial to the final frame, with chain mass equal to the rank gained; on each split the rank mass of the parent plus that of the piece equals the rank mass the register had in the #6917 word (the credit is cut in two, not doubled). In the invoice each helper is one field of its rank.
* **T change and P change** (per call, the invoice of this word against that of #6917, both by the same code): T gains one field per split (+69) and changes by +5, +1, +30, +41, +32, +30, +3, +11 at ranks 1 to 8 and by −84 at rank 9; P changes by −1, +2, +2, +2, −3, −2, −4, −1 at ranks 1 to 8; the rank mass of T and of P both falls by 46 per call, so S = 1520 is unchanged. The gain is in the scalar part of the kernel (a rank-9 field becomes two fields of smaller ranks), not in S.
* **Sinks (lift removals).** 16 registers of the #6917 word lose a hop: 15 lose their last hop to the full frame (7 end at rank 4, 7 at rank 8, 1 at rank 7) and 1 loses its first hop (it starts at rank 2). `cover_check.py` verifies that each has exactly one MOVE fewer with the old path minus its last (or first) hop, that no other register changes its initial or final frame, and that the MOVE count is 6374 = 6379 − 16 + 11, the 11 being the extra paid stops on hops of split registers. **The wording caveat applies: "lifts removed" is a plan-level statement.** The ADD lists of the word are fully rebuilt (ADD 65,902 against 49,461, SCALE 2,180 against 1,981 in the #6917 word); a plain deletion of the hop from the #6917 word is not legal, so the sinks come from re-scheduling the ADDs, and **correctness rests on the replay alone** (exact replay, streams, controls).
* **Failing controls** (`cover_check.py`, random-evaluation replay): on 12 sampled ADD events that read or write a split piece (of 11,054 such events) a wrong split coefficient (×2), a missing event and an event taken twice are all rejected; aliasing a piece onto its parent (every event of the piece rewritten to the parent label) is rejected on 8 of 8 sampled pieces; on 12 sampled ADD events that read or write a sink the same three mutations are rejected. The word is also replayed as streams over F_p[i] by `physical.py`.
* **Declared consequences.** The splits introduce coefficients up to 9027/2: two ADD events have |q| ≥ 2¹² (coefficient bits 16 / 9, against 13 / 10 in the #6917 word, where no ADD had |q| ≥ 2¹²), sum of log2(1+|q|) 92,303 against the 416,000 of the productive-controller text. In the core, 30 events couple a second main with a first main directly (14 across port types, 12 between two different two-chain ports, **4 between a second main and a first main of the same two-chain port**; 12, all across types, in #6917), and 458 helpers have ADD events against mains of both port types (352 in #6917); all are legal by the frame law and by the exact replay.

## Inherited from #6917: valves, deletions and flipped mirror pairs
* **Counted on the #6917 word against the #6911 word** (both are in `data/`; `cover_check.py`, definition: helper registers by initial and final rank, all other registers excluded): helpers of class (0, 9) 691 against 687 (+4: the four valve registers), class (5, 9) 91 against 99 (−8: the eight flipped helpers), class (1, 9) 15 against 16 (−1: one register dropped after the deletions); 962 helpers against 967. Core events: ADD 6639 against 6636, SCALE 739 (unchanged), MOVE 6379 against 6421; events before the core 25,686 (26,427), after it 18,378 (19,586). The individual valves, flips and deletions cannot be read off the word by identifier after renumbering, so their effect is what is counted and replayed.
* **The flip.** The word h += a·u*; h += c1·u; u += c2·h; u* += b·h has an inverse whose (h, u*) block is [[s + ab, −a], [−b, 1]] with s = 1 + c1·c2; with ab = s·c1·c2 the loop gain is s². The flips use SCALE gates of weight 1/2, so **a wrong scale must fail, and it does**: on 12 sampled core SCALE events of helpers (of the #6917 word) a doubled scale, a missing scale and a scale replaced by ±1/2 are all rejected by the exact replay, and on 12 sampled ADD events that touch the helpers of the changed classes a wrong weight, a missing event and an event taken twice are all rejected (`cover_check.py`).
* **R alone is not F⁻ᵀ.** Nothing here relies on a relation of the R block: correctness of the word rests on the exact replay of every event (`check.py`), on the second replay as 512-component streams (`physical.py`), and on the controls above. A reader should not infer the inverse-transpose property from the flip identity.
* **Solved around the gates.** Each of the eight flips alone saves about 5.55 R0 (the sum is additive), and the valve helpers are re-chosen on the flipped state, because the valve choice of the unflipped word leaks on it. Neither fact is used by the checks; they are the history of the word.

## The per-port root (inherited from #6911)
* **The root is a pair.** Type A (96 ports) is the root of #6902, as in #6911: two #6833 chain roots, n = 40, t = 0. Type B (8 ports) is the hosted root of #6889: n = 38, t = −1/2, pairs (−1, 1/2). Both have 18 stages with the same mask-rank profile, one stage variable per stage and M(D) = −diag(∏D^ε). A port instantiates only the roles of its own type; over the **78 roles** (type A 0-39, type B 40-77) the caller is **block diagonal**, with one stage value D_s per stage common to both types and **18 joint contacts**, each one native event serving both types (HOST2 at the type A ports, HOST at the type B ports). The cross-type blocks vanish (`check.py` replays the whole 78×78 matrix at the four central characters, 12 evaluations, 0 bad of 6,084 entries each; each type alone also replays).
* **Roots checked separately.** Type A by `root_check.py` (21/21 units of each chain regenerated from the closed form, cross Gram 0, SPEC-level identities, each stage variable once and affine); type B by `root_check_hosted.py`, the checker of the #6889 hosted root, every line passing. A wrong X entry in either root fails its checker; two swapped unit rows in the type A root fail.
* **Port types tied to the word.** The caller's type A ports equal the word's list of two-chain ports (96) and its type B ports the other 8; `check.py` asserts it and shows that exchanging the types of one port of each kind breaks the match. `cover_check.py` rejects, on a random-evaluation replay, a **wrong port type** (one two-chain port declared hosted) and a **swapped assignment** (a two-chain port and a hosted port exchanged), and also rejects a wrong weight, a missing event and an event taken twice on 16 sampled events that touch second mains or hosted-port mains.
* **The word.** Endpoint at the two-chain ports X = x, Y = x + y, X2 = x2 − y2, Y2 = y2; at the 8 hosted ports X = x − y, Y = (x + y)/2; helpers identity. **74,565 events, 1431 public registers (400 mains: 96·4 + 8·2, and 1031 helpers)**, 6374 MOVEs; the gauge SCALE 1/2 acts on exactly the 8 hosted-port mains X (the first SCALE of each of those registers; events 0 to 7). **Cross-type traffic, counted by definition** (an ADD or COPY whose destination and source are both mains and whose ports have different types): **218 events**, of which **123 involve a second main** (X2 or Y2 of a two-chain port with X or Y of a hosted port; 25 of those at a frame other than 127). They are legal (common chart, frame law clean), the endpoint rows of all 400 mains hold no input of a port of the other type, and transiently a main holds a foreign-type input after 3,135 events, which cancels. **458 helpers** have ADD events directly against mains of both types. Separately, of the 453 events that couple a second main with a first-plane main of any port, 423 lie in the two gate blocks and 30 inside the core (14 across port types, 12 between two different two-chain ports and 4 between a second main and a first main of the same two-chain port; the other definition, a subset in kind).
* **Invoice.** The root part is per port (type A for 96 ports, type B for 8), the native part is one call per stage: `check.py` recounts it from the emitted joint events and again from the two roots plus the word, and the two agree term for term. S = 1520. The same code reproduces the crossings of the #6917 word (0.0035301044238995680) and of the #6911 word (0.0035194870841667551) through the two roots, and rebuilds #6833; the comparison in the title is with #6916's published crossing 0.003570274998199567 (not recomputed here: its word is not in this package, and an independent review of #6916 recomputed 0.003570274998199567414531).

The κ rests on pricing LAMBDA as ordinary work, as in every caller of this line from #6726 on. **Reading, by an independent review, stated as the reading it is (not proved here, not on main):** Q = C^{⊗f} on (q,…,q) and C² = X (`upstream/build/sections/05-layers.tex:65`: "C^2=X, C^4=I"), so Q² = T, a pure translation, at every f; the reviews of #5599 and #6030 already use it ("Q^2 is the native translation with phase +1 at every width; scalar signs stay outside tensor powers", `docs/research/pr-intake-evidence/pr5599-completion/PROOF-REVIEW.md:59-60`; `pr6030-audit/PROOF-REVIEW.md:80-83`). Then LAMBDA = aI + bT with a, b = (1±i)/2 has row support 2 at every f (it is not the f-th tensor power), and compiles as `INIT t; COPY t = x; T(t); x := a x + b t` (numerator first); `CLEAR t`, with T the native translation: the macro of the thin query of the open #6900 (not on main) with its paid Q⁻¹ replaced by the ordinary translation. The lift is "substitution of the fixed coordinate, phase and scalar generators. It does not tensor a sum of matrix operators" (`research/higher-moment-strictification/vendor/p7-TENSOR.md:42-44`), native monomial maps and quadratic phases "lift column by column" (`research/kappa-rebuild/architecture/FINITE-PRECOMPILATION.md:243-248`), and finite compositions of permitted words are permitted (FP:45-46). Under this reading LAMBDA costs ordinary work and S does not go negative; I have not verified the tape-level implementation, and the κ is unchanged by it.
