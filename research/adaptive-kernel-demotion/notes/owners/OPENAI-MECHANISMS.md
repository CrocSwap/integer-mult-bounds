# Two sourced OpenAI mechanisms for owner-cycle replacement

## 1. Alternating overlays and reversible switches

Source: `D:/proof/external/openai-math/preprints/A-Fully-Polynomial-Randomized-Approximation-Scheme-for-Perfect-Matchings-in-General-Graphs-September-23-2026/build/main.tex`, lines 366-370 and 488-525 (Four-hole inequality, label `lem:four-holes`). The source overlays two matchings, retains parallel common edges, and switches an alternating path; the switch has an exact inverse and preserves the product weight. We use only that elementary combinatorial mechanism, not its FPRAS or counting conclusion.

Adaptation: overlay the old and proposed role-to-owner maps. Their symmetric difference decomposes into alternating directed cycles whenever each affected owner loses and gains one role. Cycle switching preserves the exact role count and every local helper occurrence. Unlike a two-owner swap or a three-role cycle with an equal-width neutral third role, three arbitrary widths can change three residuals simultaneously and bypass a busy owner family at an intermediate call.

Sufficient cycle replacement theorem: Let i_0,...,i_{k-1} be distinct literal roles with widths w_j and original distinct physical owner families A_j. Transfer i_j to A_{j+1}, indices modulo k. It is sufficient that (a) A_{j+1}'s inactivity window contains i_j's call time, (b) its family is unused in that call after all selected roles are removed, and (c) its new load u'_{j+1}=u_{j+1}-w_{j+1}+w_j is at most its rank capacity. Retain all other role assignments and order each owner's assigned axes chronologically, recomputing compact offsets. Then every role still occurs once, every per-call family remains unique, and each owner's coordinate blocks are disjoint. The old dirty/restoring local contract composes on the new disjoint axes. The exact remainder is r'_{j+1}=r_{j+1}+w_{j+1}-w_j, so the complete paid-profile difference is sum_j(H_{r'_j}-H_{r_j}), omitting H_0. Sum_j r'_j=sum_j r_j; local word, rank mass and deficit are unchanged. If sum_j (r'_j)^(1-b)<sum_j r_j^(1-b), this supplies a strictly lower recursive moment at that same admitted exponent. Independent chart and invoice checks remain mandatory.

Proof: removal followed by insertion is a finite bijective relabelling. Conditions (a)-(c) are exactly window, occupancy and coordinate-capacity checks. Compact chronological blocks have cumulative length u'_j, so are pairwise disjoint and fit. Apply the inherited arbitrary-dirty-input restoration contract at each chronological call; the pending child's saved state is recovered. The new unclaimed axes form its one paid remainder. Telescoping the width differences proves mass preservation. Multiplication by positive 73^b transfers the last inequality to the actual moment.

## Historical alternate source (not counted for this worker's two final mechanisms): species allocation

Source: `D:/proof/external/openai-math/lean/OAI/Computability/BinPacking/Inventory/SpeciesAllocation.lean`, lines13-29; theorem `OAI.BinPackingGap.SpeciesAllocation.exists_allocation` and `allocation_species`. For finite Slot and request:Slot->Species, exact stock counts yield a bijection e:Slot ~= Sigma_s Fin(stock s), with `(e b).1=request b` for every slot.

Adaptation: give every local helper role the species `(provider, orientation, helper index, width)`. Enumerate its exact call-labelled instances by the theorem's fibre bijection. Candidate owner changes operate only on these fixed literal instances; they never identify two equal-width helper occurrences or create a fake fractional helper. Cycles therefore keep the supplier inventory exactly, even when different calls host equal-width helpers. Within an eligibility class, a canonical representative can nominate a move; lifting still requires explicit family/window/capacity checks for every literal destination. The theorem licenses inventory correspondence, NOT arbitrary physical exchange. A width-only histogram is insufficient.

The checker should independently recompute each instance's local width, call order and family identity, rather than trusting a requested species tag. This second mechanism supplies exact reconstruction and lossless accounting; it is not counted as an independent numerical gain or claimed as a new OpenAI theorem. No Lean compilation, import, new axiom or kernel-certification claim is made in this round.

## 2. Disjoint first-hit projector partition (final second mechanism)

Exact source: `D:/proof/external/openai-math/preprints/Random-coordinate-frames-and-partial-permutation-laws-September-26-2026/build/sections/07a-disjoint-transfer-proof.tex`, paragraph “The common small-type cover,” equation `shear:eq:disjoint-projector-cover`. The source forms commuting orthogonal projections P_i and Q_i=P_i product_{j<i}(I-P_j), obtaining sum_i Q_i=I and Q_i Q_j=0. Its representation-theoretic conclusions are not imported here; the finite coordinate-indicator special case is the mechanism used.

For each exact live donor candidate, let P1 indicate “already one of the four selected helper roles and its pending remainder is one”; let P2 indicate “pending remainder is one”; and let P3 indicate “remainder is two or three.” On the vector space with literal donor occurrences as basis, these are diagonal orthogonal projectors and commute. Restrict to their covered eligible set. Q1=P1, Q2=P2(I-P1), Q3=P3(I-P1)(I-P2) partition each eligible occurrence once. The construction takes selected self-donors first, then external unit-gap donors, then at most one rank-two/rank-three donor. Self-donors remain in place; the remaining selected roles are bijectively swapped with the remaining external donor roles. Consequently no donor occurrence or physical family is charged twice, and already legal selected slack is not accidentally excluded.

Concrete effect: the first external-only construction realized 98 calls. The disjoint self/external partition construction plus the proved three-unit extension realizes 103 calls: 100 four-unit patterns, one [1,1,1,2] pattern and two [1,1,1,3] patterns. We do not attribute all five extra calls solely to the projector idea, because both changes were applied together. Its exact occurrence partition and reconstruction are present in `demote_gaps2.py`. Four different greedy orderings reproduce the same final paid profile. This is a sourced adaptation, not a new OpenAI proof or Lean import.
