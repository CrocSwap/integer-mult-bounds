The frame implementations use different data structures, forward/reverse
min-plus recurrences and different directed rational interval engines.
Controls cover exhaustive short paths with coupled endpoint credit, infeasible
descents, the sole zero public-state gap and source corruption. The BIT checker
propagates observations backwards, directly checks nine kernel vectors, and
replays their nine-bit values forwards. Its joint-stock control catches the
invalid inference that absence of individual basis inputs excludes every combination.

This is not a fresh full native, physical, caller, tape, precision, compiler,
outer or all-size replay. The #7613 word/invoice and selected BIT normalized
semantics are pinned premises; #7613's inherited CLEAR-content assumption is
neither accepted nor discharged here. The bound rejects the stated model even
if that candidate is provisionally granted its stated semantics. Separate Codex
agents supplied implementation-independent checks; no external human or
formal-kernel verification is claimed.
