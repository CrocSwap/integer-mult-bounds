## 1. Essential entry contract and a sharp depth-coupling control

The result starts with already supplied canonical coded w,A0,R0 packets, unary remaining-depth d, raw LEFT markers on payload tapes, BASE markers on global tapes, the prescribed body/head positions, and owned blank writable growth. Compile does not emit those initial raw markers. The excerpt's fixture explicitly writes them before constructing the Machine. Their storage belongs in K_entry/charged backing; their preparation and source/input production time is an external obligation and must not be inferred paid from K_entry, which is a space term. Runtime setup within the invocation—H production, logical markers, child delimiters, grants and retirement—is different and is counted below.

The global source coupling A0=T*(L*H)^d is stronger than validating only the first divisor. A concrete hand control is L=4,H=3,w=2,d=2,A0=24,R0=3. The first shape grant is valid and has child(A,R,V)=(2,9,72), exactly matching the selected parent bank volume72. At that child the unary depth still permits another CALL, but A=2 is not divisible by12; the literal shape loop reaches A's RIGHT during the H scan and rejects. Thus correct one-edge divisibility, packet volume and first grants do not establish all-depth entry legality. This is outside the source's stated A0=144*T domain, not a counterexample to its theorem, and was not executed.

Valid markers are likewise a promise, not a complete malformed-input recognition theorem. Raw origin searches continue left through non-marker symbols, including blank. Removing a required raw LEFT and leaving no such marker to its left can make that search continue indefinitely. The stated all-depth termination result therefore cannot be advertised for arbitrary corrupted tape states or as a successful admission check of all ownership/entry premises.

## 2. Finite control and codec

All field numbers, source-table ports, branches and continuations are compile-time constants. The two call sites are literal bank0/bank1 branches, not a runtime tape-index operation. The retained machine executes only branch/write/unit-move/goto/halt/reject and does not offer its stored heads, step counters or observer to the emitted program. The source imports/build loops are construction-time operations and remain an independently charged compilation boundary.

The raw renaming BL,Z,O,LEFT,BASE,SEP,END is bijective. Encoded logical symbols use three Z/O physical digits, so neither physical binary bit is physical blank and neither can mimic a raw delimiter. code_branch restores the code-origin head; code_write leaves it at that same origin. code_move(+1) materializes logical000 on an absent code cell before crossing it, and negative movement is exactly three unit steps. Thus erased logical BLANK may remain real000 backing until the explicit raw retirement; the proof does not treat it as deallocation.

The lowering preserves branches/defaults and source goto destinations via static labels; a source successful halt becomes a fixed jump to its continuation, while rejection reaches the core fail state. No implicit physical fallthrough bypasses a required continuation. A direct per-logical-instruction inspection supports the conservative32 bound: code_write uses9 steps, code_move(+1) at most13 including materialization, and the three-bit branch path is below32. Goto/halt redirection is a single goto. This is a hand bound, not execution or an independent20,897-instruction table count.

## 3. Generated H and role separation

For the multiplier's compiled carry c in0..b-1 and scanned bit x, floor((b*x+c)/2) stays in that same carry set. The emitted low bit and next carry therefore give ordinary binary multiplication. At the old RIGHT, the finite carry drain writes its remaining digits and a new RIGHT; positive input times b has a positive canonical result. A standard rewind returns its head. The width guide skips exactly its first UNIT, so it performs w-1 multiplications from H=1. The width header is preserved, work is logically erased and then its physical code backing is retired.

For k=max(16,L+11), router roles occupy0..L+6. Grants L+7,L+8, the extra guide L+9 and reserved return k-1 are distinct from them and each other. The nine global tapes begin at k and are distinct from all payloads. In particular shared H is outside the pushed local frames and is readonly during recursive nodes; current A/R remain frame-owned. H retirement happens only after the root returns. Fixed q,N and b are template parameters; table-size/build feasibility and their compiler cost are not established by the runtime carry proof.

## 4. Push/pop and protected return

On each payload, push records its current head offset as unary O bits while moving back to its nearest raw LEFT. It then scans the contiguous coded body to the first physical blank, installs a new child LEFT, and advances to blank child origin. The record's O/Z presence/offset fields, SEP, phase bit and END are disjoint from the frame-start LEFT, so pop can find the exact frame boundary without host pointers.

Before suspension, parent grants and all temporary coded work are physically retired, not cached for grandchildren. Fixed relays carry the selected entire bank and both actual descriptor packets. After child initialization those three relays are erased. CALL advances the readonly depth head once. A descendant therefore uses its own current A_j,R_j with the same generated H, rather than the root's old A/R or a host grant list.

At completion the entire child output is copied to its separate global output relay before all child payloads are retired. Pop then sees blank child bodies, erases child LEFT delimiters, finds each parked parent LEFT, consumes the saved offsets to restore exact heads, restores the one-bit phase, and erases the current control record. The previous END/BASE search crosses only the current erased record, not all ancestors. RETURN moves depth left once and verifies O. Only after pop does it install the protected output through the reserved parent return role, erase the relay, replace the selected owned bank, erase return/phase and continue with the statically correct next site.

The special reserved return role is blank before each CALL. Its shorter record does not alias a parent input. Original root source/header packets remain read-only; changing an owned copied bank is not a write to that borrowed source. The last root branch tests the actual previous control BASE, restores its head, retires generated H and halts. Under the initial marker/head promise these operations preserve parent bytes/heads and permit repeated sequential siblings without allocating a new permanent suffix.

## 5. Whole-high address and all-depth induction

The exact address identity is

```
a_parent=(a_child*L+l_child)*H+h_child,
a_parent*R+r_parent
 =(a_child*L+l_child)*(H*R)+h_child*R+r_parent.
```

It preserves high and earlier ancestor/body coordinates, not merely volume. The child volume is L*(A/(LH))*(HR)=AR=V/L. Returning its complete ordered output to the selected bank and applying the inherited inverse router preserves this address order. At depth zero the actual finite leaf complements bank0 and merges all banks; at positive depth the induction composes two such selected-child endpoints. The decreasing remaining unary length bounds CALL depth, and exactly two sites per node bound total nodes at every finite d. This proves the stated scoped recursive endpoint/termination under the primitive contracts, not a Gaussian/ordinary BIT leaf or a complete cover traversal for arbitrary final-template fanout.

## 6. Independent audit of the bill form

At internal nodes A>=LH implies HR<=V/L^2; A,R,H and every relevant positive descriptor/guide length are bounded by V. Encoded packet lengths are three times logical extents plus fixed markers. The local payload/stack/relay extents therefore fit the very loose100kV envelope. The raw helper costs are linear scans/rewinds: copy has bounded scans of its source/target, retire erases a contiguous coded extent, and push/pop scan payloads and their one control record. No raw helper scans across all parked ancestors.

For the stated fewer-than64 **aggregate** helper bound, K-wide child retirement and bank/scratch cleanup must be grouped by summing their per-tape extents, not charged K times the entire local-frame bound. This grouping is justified by the actual disjoint per-tape loops. Counting the call path gives fewer than64 fixed aggregate helpers per site; each needs at most four scans/rewinds of its participating extent. The stated512 passes over a100kV envelope at128 steps per position is consequently conservative, yielding6,553,600kV. The linked codec-table allowance70,016V and marker/control constants fit comfortably below10^8*(k+e_b+1)*V. This supports the coarse runtime constant; it is not an exact optimized instruction ledger.

H generation is once-only. At its jth multiplication the bit length is O(e_b*j), so the total fixed-state multiplier work is bounded by the source's(100+20e_b)*w^2 envelope, with conversion/retirement included. For b>=3, w^2<=2b^(w-1)=2H: check w=1,2 and use((w+1)/w)^2<3 thereafter. Since H<=R0<=V0, the stated extra C_time*V0 setup term is sufficient under the supplied-entry ABI. It does not fund producing that ABI or compiling the table.

The actual two-site runtime sum is V0*sum_(j=0..d)(2/L)^j. It cannot generally be replaced by a depth-independent O(V0) whole-tree bill. Sharp hand control: L=2,H=1,T=R_body=1 gives A0=2^d and each level's total volume exactly V0, hence sum=(d+1)V0. The source correctly retains that occurrence sum. A different final template with c sites needs its own c*T_(d-1)(V/L)+C*V recurrence, not the two-site bill.

Space uses an active chain, not the occurrence sum. At each fixed depth, descriptor lengths and coded packet extents depend on shape, not bit values; completed siblings erase and reuse the same suffix. Summing V0/L^j is strictly below2V0 for L>=2. Stack records, global relays and once-only width/depth/H backing fit the enlarged C_space coefficient and K_entry term. Clearing cells removes no visited-space charge, and supplied/retained backing preparation remains an external cost obligation.
