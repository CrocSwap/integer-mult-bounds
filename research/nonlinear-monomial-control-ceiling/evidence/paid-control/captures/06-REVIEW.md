## 1. Source and model discipline

The fixed address space is q+U+W, with one central and two disjoint eight-axis
blocks:17 axes, lifted as the same one-column map on every f column. The owner
matrix A has zero diagonal, distinct source port directions q_i=G_i e_q, and
nonempty rows/columns. The source polynomial
`A^2+12A-13I=0` gives the exact inverse `(A+12I)/13`, hence each physical code
`C_s=diag(K_s)^-1 A diag(K_s)` is invertible at every width. These are the
authenticated source premises/static source-check results; no new all-port
operator evaluation or source native replay was performed for this review.

The declared H is the projective group of **monomial Clifford** maps on those
17 one-column axes. It is finite projectively. Fixed finite scalar owner
programs, COPY/history and owned clear/reset yield matrices over its linear
span; coefficients may depend on width. Such a span is a mathematical
relaxation of explicit programs. Membership is necessary for a program in this
class; it is not by itself an executable tariff or an inverse program.

The proof does not assume an arbitrary nonsingular algebra element has a free
inverse. H, its conjugates and their intersection are groups and are inverse
closed, but that abstract fact supplies no physical implementation for a unit
of their matrix algebra. The forward membership obstruction already excludes
the handoff's invertible solution; proving inverse-program sufficiency is
unnecessary for this negative result.

## 2. Support and width separation are valid

For pairwise nonproportional nonzero operator vectors v_i, choose, for each
j!=i, a functional annihilating v_j but not v_i. Their tensor product isolates
v_i^(tensor(m-1)); extra factors nonzero on v_i extend the argument to larger
widths. Thus any fixed finite union of projective classes has independent
literal tensor lifts for every sufficiently large f. Equal projective classes
are grouped first and their scalar phases are raised to f. This handles
width-dependent owner coefficients without tensoring an owner-space sum.

The union must include the candidate control letters, their Q/D3 conjugates,
the terminal endpoint products and the letters of a proposed free endpoint
program. It is finite in this declared fixed-columnwise Clifford class. The
threshold is not claimed small or practical. No assertion is made at every
small width: a family supported only at finitely many small widths can evade
the nonzero-family wording, but cannot meet all-width invertibility.

The precise conclusion is pointwise at sufficiently large width: any t0_f
satisfying all four memberships must be zero there. It excludes every fixed
nonzero t0 and every all-width invertible family, including width-dependent
scalar coefficients. It does not assert that no exceptional small-width
identity exists.

## 3. D3 transport and the necessary quotient flag

The first three controls put surviving t0 support in

`H0=H intersect Q H Q^-1 intersect D3^-1 H D3`.

For t=D3 t0 D3^-1 the correct transported support is
`Ht=D3 H0 D3^-1`. It must not be identified with H0. In particular
`Q^-1 t Q=D3(Q^-1 t0 Q)D3^-1` is not automatically monomial. The revised
proof correctly retains this failure of the tempting closure inference.

On binary symplectic space Q and D3 are involutions, while their operator
squares remain nontrivial Pauli/phase maps. If h lies in H0, it stabilizes
L0,Lq,Lw. A transported k in Ht stabilizes L0,Lw,Lqw. Because k is monomial,
its X block R_k is invertible and preserves both W and span(e_q)+W. It fixes
the nonzero central class in the quotient by W; it need not fix e_q itself.
The allowed behavior e_q -> e_q+w is precisely why the stronger false
transport inference cannot be reused.

For **nonzero** v in span(e_q)+U and nonzero w in span(e_q)+W, if
k L(v)=L(w), with `L(v)={(v v^T z,z)}`, X projection gives R_k v=w.
The induced invertible quotient map has the central class as a fixed point,
so the preimage of its span is that span. Therefore v=e_q, while w may be
e_q plus a W vector. This proves exactly the one-sided implication needed;
it does not establish w=e_q.

## 4. The endpoint has no monomial support: cancellation does not rescue it

A terminal endpoint block has terms

`Q^-1 K_(3,i)^-1 K_(3,r) k K_(1,s)^-1 K_(1,j) Q^-1`,

with coefficient `A[i,r] b[r,s,k] A[s,j]` and k in Ht. For such a word to be
monomial, its central Gaussian chart must be carried to the central chart.
Using the actual orthogonal source normalizers, this requires k to carry

`L(G_(1,s)^-1 q_j)` to `L(G_(3,r)^-1 q_i)`.

The quotient lemma forces `G_(1,s)^-1 q_j=e_q`, hence s=j by distinct source
directions. Its coefficient then contains A[j,j]=0. Thus **every term with
nonzero coefficient is outside H**.

The corrected proof groups any equal nonmonomial projective classes. Internal
cancellation can remove such classes, but cannot create a class in H. Uniform
tensor separation makes their remaining span disjoint from the monomial span.
If the endpoint E also has a free representation in the declared class, it
must therefore be zero. But

`E=Q^-1 C3 t C1 Q^-1=0`

implies t=0 and then t0=0 because the source Q,C1,C3 are invertible. This closes
the wider-class argument without assuming unique intermediate products or a
free inverse for t0. Invertibility of the handoff's requested t0 makes the
contradiction immediate.

This direct-sum argument was the useful correction during review: an initial
draft left an unnecessary cancellation gap. The reviewed version resolves it
without reintroducing the invalid claim that Ht=H0 or R_k e_q=e_q.

## 5. Independent scalar-owner crosscheck

For t0=B tensor I, t=t0 and the first three controls are scalar owner maps.
Each endpoint word has an orthogonal binary map

`M=G_(3,i)^-1 G_(3,r) G_(1,s)^-1 G_(1,j)`.

Writing `Q^-1=aI+bX_q`, both a,b are nonzero. If M e_q differs from e_q,
the four translations0,e_q,M e_q,e_q+M e_q are distinct and have nonzero
coefficients; the word is nonmonomial. If the directions agree, the actual
Pauli sign yields either a single translation or a scalar times M. Thus the
stated monomial criterion is correct, including sign choices and the two
Q^-1 factors. Q is never treated as order two.

Returning the direction to e_q forces s=j and r=i by the disjoint stage
supports and distinct source q_i, annihilating the source diagonal factors.
For fixed i,j, equality of two remaining binary products would give

`G_(3,r')^-1 G_(3,r)=G_(1,s')^-1 G_(1,s)`.

Their stage-local intersection is the identity binary map: both sides fix U
and W, preserve the respective local spaces, and the only invertible map of
the single common binary bit fixes it. Distinct q_i then force r=r',s=s'.
So a nonzero B[r,s], together with a nonzero source entry in column r and row
s, gives a unique nonmonomial term. This independently confirms the scalar
subclass obstruction, with the same sufficiently-large-width qualification.

## 6. Scope, corrections and constructive continuation

The following draft issues were corrected before the reviewed hash was frozen:
the prematurely broad theorem claim while cancellation was open; an incorrect
sentence placing transported t support back in H0; missing nonzero-vector
quantifiers in the chart lemma; and the width-dependent coefficient wording.
The revised cancellation argument now legitimately proves the broader
**declared monomial-Clifford** class, rather than a universal FREE theorem.

This result does not import #5126's enlarged arbitrary finite monomial H as
an actual physical dictionary. It does not exclude non-Clifford monomial
controls, noncolumnwise/growing alphabets, extra paid corrections, a changed
controller or productive endpoint, extra axes, a different code, or reuse of
nonfree native information outside the four-free-controls template.

already available native information, or another explicit changed hypothesis.
