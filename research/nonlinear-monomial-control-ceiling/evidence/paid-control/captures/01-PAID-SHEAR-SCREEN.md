# Stop certificate for scalar-owner X-Gaussian shears

Local read/derive screen, requested after CONTRACT.md. No operator or
parameter search was run. This note is separate from the already reviewed
Clifford FREE proof and requires its own independent review.

## Decision and scope

The proposed affordable class cannot work: it requires at least 112 scalar
owner rank-one shears, whereas its optimistic complete query allowance
permits at most seven. This includes dense scalar owner vectors, not just
single E_(a,b) updates. A paid term creating one monomial endpoint component
does not eliminate the old nonmonomial identity component.

The result covers

    t0 = S_m ... S_1,
    S_k = I + u_k v_k^T tensor H_k,

where u_k,v_k are scalar 112-owner vectors and H_k belongs to the group
generated by the Pauli-X Gaussians C_v used in CONTRACT.md. Coefficients
are applied once on owners. For the explicit inverse program one assumes
v_k^T u_k=0, but the necessary lower bound does not need that assumption.

It does not cover operator-valued owner/address gather and scatter maps,
other intervening noncommuting control alphabets, retained native/query
observations, or a changed endpoint/controller. No universal fresh-query
lower bound is asserted.

## 1. The address group has a restrictive monomial intersection

Every C_v is a polynomial in the X-translation X_v, so every product H
commutes with every X-translation on the 17 address bits. If such an H is
monomial, write its action as a permutation p with nonzero multipliers c.
Commutation with all translations implies

    p(x+a)=p(x)+a,   c(x+a)=c(x).

Hence p(x)=x+b and c is constant: H is a scalar times X_b. This also
follows directly from the source Gaussian words. It is not an assertion
about all monomial Clifford operators.

The C_v group is finite up to phase because these are Clifford operators
on a fixed 17-axis space. The all-width lift and projective phase convention
are the same as in PROOF.md. Let B0 be the 112 by 112 scalar coefficient of
the projective identity address word in t0 after equal classes are grouped.

## 2. FREE terminal output forces B0=0

The endpoint is E=Q^-1 C3 t0 C1 Q^-1, since every H_k commutes with D3.
Fix an identity-word contribution at intermediate owner pair (r,s).
Before the fixed outer factors, its address word is

    K_(3,r) K_(1,s)^-1.

A contribution from any H at any other pair (r',s') could coincide with
this class only if

    H = phase * K_(3,r')^-1 K_(3,r) K_(1,s)^-1 K_(1,s').

The right side is a signed binary linear permutation with no translation.
By section 1 the left side, if monomial at all, is a scalar X-translation.
Therefore its translation must vanish, so H is projectively the identity.
The disjoint-stage binary-map argument in PROOF.md then gives r'=r and
s'=s. Thus the identity-word contribution at (r,s) cannot cancel against
any nonidentity H or any different owner pair.

For any nonzero B0[r,s], choose output i and input j with A[i,r] A[s,j]
nonzero, using the actual 52 nonzeros per source row/column. The associated
endpoint word

    Q^-1 K_(3,i)^-1 K_(3,r) K_(1,s)^-1 K_(1,j) Q^-1

is nonmonomial by the scalar theorem: it could be monomial only if r=i
and s=j, which would require zero source diagonal entries. Its coefficient
is nonzero and, by the preceding argument, cannot cancel against any
other contribution of t0. Uniform tensor-word separation therefore excludes
a fixed finite columnwise monomial implementation of E at sufficiently
large widths. Consequently a FREE E requires B0=0.

## 3. At least 112 paid shears are necessary

Let T_0=I and T_k=S_k T_(k-1). The exact telescoping identity is

    t0-I = sum_k u_k (v_k^T tensor H_k) T_(k-1).

Take the coefficient of the projective identity address word. Because each
u_k is scalar in owner space, this gives

    B0-I = sum_k u_k w_k^T

for some scalar row vectors w_k^T. In particular rank(B0-I)<=m. But section
2 requires B0=0, so

    112 = rank(I) <= m.

This statement holds at each sufficiently large width even if the scalar
coefficients vary with width; it excludes an all-width invertible solution
with fewer than 112 shears. It does not assert that 112 shears suffice.

## Invoice consequence and reopening boundary

The explicit middle/exit execution in CONTRACT.md costs 3m H2 in addition
to the released base profile. Since m>=112, even using H2>1 for C<1 gives
an added moment greater than 336, against the proven optimistic allowance
44.67695955358 at the comparison interval's right endpoint. The class is
decisively unaffordable before finite work, compiler or stock costs.

Stop this class; do not enumerate seven-shear words or tune coefficients.
A continuation must change a mathematical premise, for example literal
operator-valued owner/address gather/scatter with a paid mixed sandwich,
or a named already-paid native/query observation. Such a changed program
needs its own source access, ownership, phase, inverse and full cost proof.
The present rank argument cannot be transferred to it merely by calling a
whole operator-valued query a scalar rank-one shear.
