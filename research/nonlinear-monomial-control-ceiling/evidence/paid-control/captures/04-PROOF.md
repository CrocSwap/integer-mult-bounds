# An active local-query obstruction for the actual 112-port code

Prepared for Dillon, 2026-10-23, with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance.
This is a scoped exact local theorem with finite source checks, not a
complete-controller lower bound, a multiplication algorithm or an admission
claim. Apache-2.0.

## Statement

Use the actual physical native code from `research/coded-native-caller`:

\[
 C_{ij}=a_{ij}K_i^{-1}K_j,\qquad J=Q^{-1}CQ,
\]

where there are 112 owners, Q is the one-central-axis Gaussian, each K_i is
the retained signed orthogonal permutation normalizer, and the scalar matrix
a has zero diagonal and 52 nonzero off-diagonal entries in every column.
The 112 vectors q_i=G_i e_0 are distinct. The source factorization
C=I-14P has an eight-mode projector P.

Fix any finite projective group H of invertible one-column monomial maps on the
declared fixed ambient address space, containing the source normalizers and
all admitted free address maps. For example, H may be the full group of
one-column permutations with phases in a fixed finite root-of-unity group;
the free permutations need not be Clifford. Every address map is lifted as
the SAME one-column map h on all f columns, h^tensor(f). Independent choices
on individual columns are outside this grammar. Free operations are
arbitrary linear owner programs over the
monomial subgroup algebra, including scalar operations, every
actual C or C inverse, owned COPY/CLEAR, arbitrary clean buffers, saved
banks, and fanout. These are charged as ordinary finite work; "free" here
means no recursive Gaussian call. Allow every paid unary call to apply an
arbitrary fixed one-field linear operator, with chronological linear
feedback from all available fields. In particular its input may use all
earlier paid responses. All coefficients are data-independent, although
they may depend on width. Every counted field has the same address width as
one source owner; a wider packed channel is not counted as one field.

**Theorem.** A standalone exact implementation of J, or of J-I, on 112
arbitrary fresh owner fields requires at least 112 paid unary field calls.
The statement is valid for an identity at all positive tensor widths, and
allows the scalar owner coefficients to depend on width. The same conclusion
holds after adding any free owner operator to J.
More precisely, the bound holds at every sufficiently large tensor width,
so no all-width implementation uses fewer than 112 calls. It is not a bound
at every small width: at width one,
Q=((1+i)/2)I+((1-i)/2)X is in the scalar span of free monomials.

**Sharp one-sided corollary.** Put P=(I-C)/14. Each of QP and PQ, as a
112-input/112-output owner operator, requires at least 112 paid unary field
calls. Exactly 112 H1 calls suffice: apply P for free and then Q to every
output, or apply Q to every input and then P. Thus the eight scalar code
modes do not yield an eight-call one-sided Q repair. The statement also
holds with Q replaced by Q inverse.

**Canonical eight-mode corollary.** For the actual source mode matrix M,
let U_mode=diag(K_i^-1)M and V_mode=M^T diag(K_i)/56. Then
P=U_mode V_mode and V_mode U_mode=I_8. Each of Q U_mode (eight fresh inputs,
112 outputs) and V_mode Q (112 fresh inputs, eight outputs) also has exact
paid-field-call complexity 112, attained by 112 H1 calls. Thus the canonical
rectangular mode ABI does not avoid the one-sided obstruction.

It does not cover receiving Gaussian-dependent saved data, absorbing the
correction into the paid native FULLs or their prefixes, recycling an existing
paid IDLE, or a direct coded consumer with a different endpoint. Nonlinear
controls, noncolumnwise address operations, approximate endpoints, and
unequal-width packing are outside the stated model.

## 1. Phase-correct finite algebra and all-width evaluation

This uses the finite-phase algebra and sufficiently-large-width tensor
faithfulness already proved in `coded-port-adapter-ceiling/PROOF.md`, section
3, and clarified by the passive saved-query package's uniform-alphabet
addendum. Here only the FREE group H must be finite projectively. An ambient
group generated by H and Q need not be finite or Clifford.

Choose one invertible matrix representative for every projective element
of H. At width f its nonzero scalar multiplication cocycle is raised to
the f-th power; the scalar factors need not have modulus one.
Let B_f be the resulting finite-dimensional twisted group algebra over
k=C, with one basis element per projective element of H. Its evaluation
is the span of the literal tensor lifts h^tensor(f). Scalar factors are
retained through the cocycle; owner coefficients are applied only once.

Besides H itself, we will need only a fixed finite union of its projective
cosets: the sets g_ij H used for J, the analogous right cosets for PQ and
V_mode Q, and the analogous left cosets for QP and Q U_mode. Take S to be
their union with H, with repeated projective elements identified. This set
is finite because H and the source owner/mode sets are finite. Closure of
S under multiplication is not needed: all free products remain in H, and
every target-times-free expression used below stays in its declared coset
set. A different fixed finite number of source stages merely enlarges S.

For projectively distinct nonzero representatives v_1,...,v_N, individually
normalize each by its Hilbert-Schmidt norm. Their tensor-power
Gram matrix has diagonal one and off-diagonal entries
<v_i,v_j>^f, whose magnitudes are at most rho^f for some rho<1. For
(N-1)rho^f<1 the Gram matrix is strictly diagonally dominant and positive
definite. Apply this to the projective representatives in S. Their literal
tensor lifts are linearly independent for every sufficiently large f.
Thus B_f and all the required coset subspaces evaluate faithfully, with
distinct cosets in direct sum, at those widths. Scalar factors remain raised
to the f-th power; owner coefficients are never raised to that power.

We work at any one such width. The threshold is uniform over all free
coefficients, control choices, and control lengths because H and S are
fixed. Width-dependent scalar coefficients are allowed. A width-dependent
or noncolumnwise group of arbitrary payload maps is not allowed; neither
is an infinite projective phase grammar. No assumption that arbitrary
permutations together with Q generate a finite group is made.

## 2. Actual-source right-coset separation

Write g_ij=Q^-1 K_i^-1 K_j Q. For a fixed i,

\[
 g_{ij}^{-1}g_{ik}=Q^{-1}K_j^{-1}K_kQ.                 (1)
\]

For the signed orthogonal permutation M=K_j^-1 K_k, let G=G_j^-1 G_k be
its binary orthogonal coordinate matrix and e=e_0. Pauli signs affect only
tableau phases. The Z-to-X block of Q^-1 M Q is

\[
 B=G e e^T+e e^T G.                                  (2)
\]

If G e=e, orthogonality gives e^T G=e^T, and B=0. If G e differs from e,
then both pairs (G e,e) and (e,G^T e) are independent; the two outer products
in (2) have rank two. Thus Q^-1 M Q is monomial exactly when G e=e.
Equivalently, G_j e=G_k e, or q_j=q_k. The actual 112 source vectors are
distinct, so (1) belongs to H exactly when j=k.

Therefore the 112 g_ij lie in distinct right H-cosets for each fixed i.
The j=i coset is H itself, since g_ii=I. The scalar phase quotient does not
alter this test: multiplying by a scalar never changes monomiality.
The companion checker verifies all 112^2 ordered pairs by both formula
(2) and the retained exact tableau implementation. It separately checks
the actual code's zero diagonal and nonempty column supports.

## 3. No nonzero free input survives modulo a free output

Let F_0 be ANY 112-by-112 matrix over B_f. Suppose w in B_f^112 obeys

\[
 (J-F_0)w=0.                                         (3)
\]

Row i of Jw is sum_j a_ij g_ij w_j. Each summand lies in the right-coset
subspace g_ij B_f. These subspaces are in direct sum. Every component of
F_0 w lies in B_f, the H coset. The diagonal source coefficient is zero.
For each off-diagonal supported pair (i,j), projecting (3) onto g_ij B_f
therefore gives a_ij g_ij w_j=0. A nonzero scalar and a group unit are
invertible, so w_j=0. Each column j has a supported off-diagonal entry,
and hence w=0.

This is stronger than an eigenvector statement: J-F_0 has zero kernel on
B_f^112 for every free F_0. In particular the Q-conjugated eigenspaces of
C contain no nonzero free input embedding, despite the eight-mode
factorization of C-I. A cheap compression of J-F_0 through fewer than 112
owner fields is impossible.

### One-sided projector operators

Write p=(I-a)/14, so P_ij=p_ij K_i^-1 K_j and p_ii=1/14. For PQ, fix row
i. Its blocks are p_ij h_ij with h_ij=K_i^-1 K_j Q. Every h_ij has Fourier
grade one and lies outside H. Further,

    h_ij^-1 h_ik = Q^-1 K_j^-1 K_k Q.

Thus the h_ij occupy distinct right H-cosets as j varies. The same argument
proves ker_{B_f}(PQ-F_0)=0 for every free F_0; the nonzero diagonal of p
already supplies a supported entry in each column.

For QP fix column j instead, and put t_ij=Q K_i^-1 K_j. These grade-one
words lie in distinct LEFT H-cosets as i varies, because

    t_ij t_kj^-1 = Q K_i^-1 K_k Q^-1

is nonmonomial for i different from k. Consequently a free row w satisfying
w(QP-F_0)=0 must vanish: project each fixed output column onto the distinct
left-coset subspaces B_f t_ij, and use p_ii different from zero. This is the
left-kernel version of the same source-specific separation.

### Canonical rectangular eight-mode maps and free rebasing

The retained 112-by-8 scalar mode matrix M obeys M^T M=56I_8 and
a=I-MM^T/4; each row has four nonzero entries. The checker verifies every
entry of these identities from the pinned source receipt. Define

    U_mode = diag(K_i^-1) M,        shape 112 by 8,
    V_mode = M^T diag(K_i)/56,      shape 8 by 112.

These maps are free, with P=U_mode V_mode and V_mode U_mode=I_8.
For Q U_mode the fixed mode-column a has entries M_ia Q K_i^-1. The words
t_i=Q K_i^-1 lie in distinct left H-cosets, since
t_i t_k^-1=Q K_i^-1 K_k Q^-1 is nonmonomial when i differs from k.
Each t_i itself is nonmonomial. Every row of M has a nonzero entry, so
the same columnwise projection proves that

    w (Q U_mode-F_0)=0,  w in B_f^(1 by 112),

forces w=0 for every free 112-by-8 F_0. Thus the no-left-kernel conclusion
holds even though there are only eight fresh inputs.

Dually, V_mode Q has entries M_ja K_j Q/56. The words K_j Q lie in
distinct right H-cosets because their pairwise relative transports are
Q^-1 K_j^-1 K_k Q. Every source column is represented in at least one mode
row. Hence (V_mode Q-F_0)w=0, with w in B_f^112, forces w=0 for every free
8-by-112 F_0, even though there are only eight outputs.

These conclusions cover all free changes of retraction basis. If another
free pair U',V' has P=U'V' and V'U'=I_8, then

    W=V_mode U',        W^-1=V' U_mode,
    U'=U_mode W,        V'=W^-1 V_mode.

The two inverse identities follow by substituting U'V'=P and
U_mode V_mode=P. Thus Q U' and V'Q differ from the canonical operators by
invertible free input or output boundaries. No cheaper implementation is
obtained by any such free eight-mode basis choice.

## 4. Arbitrary chronological paid calls

Normalize any finite acyclic linear implementation by retaining all paid
responses y_1,...,y_r. COPY, saves, fanout, overwrite and CLEAR are included
in this normalization: enlarging owned storage can preserve the history
without introducing another recursive call. At paid call t its input is

\[
 b_t x+\sum_{s<t} e_{ts}y_s,\qquad
 y_t=T_t\left(b_t x+\sum_{s<t}e_{ts}y_s\right),        (4)
\]

where b_t is a 1-by-112 row over B_f, each e_ts is in B_f, and T_t is the
paid unary linear operator. The final output is

\[
 F_0 x+\sum_t l_t y_t                               (5)
\]

for free F_0 and free output columns l_t. Arbitrary preserved dirty
auxiliaries can be set to zero when restricting to the 112 fresh input
owners; this restriction does not weaken an all-input exact identity.

Stack the b_t into a matrix B:B_f^112 -> B_f^r. As a k-linear map its
domain has dimension 112 dim_k B_f and its codomain has dimension
r dim_k B_f. If r<112, choose a nonzero w in its kernel. Substitute the
formal input x=w z, with z one arbitrary field. Every direct term b_t w is
zero. Induction on t in (4) gives every paid response y_t=0, regardless of
T_t and regardless of the active feedback coefficients. Thus the claimed
endpoint J must satisfy Jw=F_0w. Section 3 contradicts this.

This proves r>=112. Replacing J by J-I or J+F_free merely changes the free
matrix F_0 and gives the same result.

The input-kernel proof applies verbatim to PQ. For QP use the dual output
cut: concatenate the free output columns in (5) into L in B_f^(112 by r).
If r<112, the k-linear map w -> wL from B_f^(1 by 112) to B_f^(1 by r)
has a nonzero kernel row w. Multiplying the endpoint by that row annihilates
every paid-response term, so w(QP-F_0)=0, contrary to the left-kernel result.
No assumption about the paid calls' chronology is needed beyond the
existence of the finite linear circuit normal form.

For Q U_mode the same output cut has 112 rows and r paid-response columns,
although F_0 has only eight input columns. It again gives r>=112. For
V_mode Q the same input cut has 112 input columns, although F_0 has only
eight output rows, and also gives r>=112. Both bounds are attained by
112 H1 calls: expand eight fields through U_mode for free and apply Q to
all 112 results; or apply Q to the 112 inputs and contract through V_mode
for free. Invertible free retraction basis changes preserve these counts.
The same arguments apply with Q inverse throughout.

## 5. Direct sums and local amortization

For any fixed positive m, consider m fresh physical code blocks, embedded
in one fixed ambient address space. Each block may use its own eight fresh
axes and physical normalizers, with the same 112 distinct-direction
property. Let N=112m. The same proof gives

* diag(J_1,...,J_m), or its free perturbation: at least N paid field calls;
* diag(P_1 Q,...,P_m Q): exactly N H1 calls are necessary and sufficient;
* diag(QP_1,...,QP_m): exactly N H1 calls are necessary and sufficient.
* diag(Q U_mode,1,...,Q U_mode,m), and its free retraction rebases:
  exactly N H1 calls are necessary and sufficient;
* diag(V_mode,1 Q,...,V_mode,m Q), and its free retraction rebases:
  exactly N H1 calls are necessary and sufficient.

Free controls may mix different blocks arbitrarily. To see why this does
not change the proof, write a candidate free kernel column or row in m
blocks. Project each target block's rows or columns onto its nontrivial
cosets. The free comparison operator still contributes only the H coset,
even when it mixes all N owners. The corresponding single-block argument
forces every block of the candidate kernel to vanish. Apply the input or
output dimension cut with B_f^N and r<N.

This closes amortization of these *standalone direct-sum local repairs*
through cross-block free coding and chronological linear feedback. It
does not close amortization into an already running native caller that
supplies correlated nonfree observations of the same input owners. The
displayed operator families are separate statements; no claim about
an arbitrary mixture of left- and right-sided endpoints is needed.

## Scope, overlap, and consequence

### Isolated restoration cannot recover the removed-center saving

For the physical right-sided backward FULL, write

    B_s = diag(D_s,D_s) U(-QC),
    U(T) = [[I,T],[0,I]],
    C = I-14P.

The standalone input shear T_s=U(-14QP) restores the original uncoded
interface exactly:

    B_s T_s = diag(D_s,D_s) U(-QC-14QP)
            = diag(D_s,D_s) U(-Q).

No commutation between Q and P is used. Any standalone implementation of
T_s on arbitrary fresh X,Y fields also implements -14QP on Y by setting
X=0 and selecting the first output. It therefore requires at least 112
paid unary same-width field calls. In the granted owned COPY/ADD/CLEAR
model, 112 H1 calls suffice for this local shear: form P Y for free, apply
Q to its 112 owner fields, add -14 times the result to X, and clear the
owned temporary fields. This is an endpoint and recursive-call statement,
not a claim that its ordinary precision/storage work has been admitted.

The actual center-free producer removed eight H8 calls per FULL. This bill
is inherited from the pinned #4929 producer; the coset checker does not
replay that native event-count certificate. For every
0<=c<1 under the recursive moment tariff w_c(H_j)=j^(1-c), their saved
price is 8*8^(1-c)<=64. Every nontrivial Gaussian query has integer rank
j>=1 and price at least one, so the local correction costs at least 112.
Its added recursive price exceeds the removed-center saving by at least
48 per FULL. The direct-sum theorem gives a gap of at least 48m for m such
standalone local repairs, even when their free controls mix all inputs.

Thus this isolated interface-restoration strategy cannot turn the removed
eight H8 calls into a recursive saving anywhere in 0<=c<1. Extra ordinary
work can only increase the cost. The conclusion does not price or rule out
absorption into correlated FULL, IDLE, or saved-query data in a complete
caller, nor a different direct coded endpoint.

### Relationship to predecessor and active caller work

The predecessor `coded-port-adapter-ceiling` (#4932) proves that one parallel
bank needs 112 grade-2 channels for its physical recovery adapter, using a
grade projection of full rank. It expressly excludes multiple chronological
banks. The argument here uses source-specific distinct right cosets and an
input-kernel cut to cover arbitrary chronological paid unary calls and
active feedback. Its conclusion is 112 calls of any grade; it does not
claim 112 grade-2 calls in this enlarged model.

The conclusion excludes an independently implemented 16-, 32-, or 42-H1
fresh-input correction of J, J-I, QP, or PQ through the native code's eight
modes. For J and J-I this is a lower bound, with the elementary 224-H1
conjugation implementation as an upper bound; 112-call attainability is
not asserted. For QP and PQ the 112-H1 bound is attained.
It does not prove that p2 needs four thin banks, nor that a complete active
three-bank caller is impossible. Such a caller can already hold nonfree
data from its FULLs, queries, and IDLE. A useful construction must exploit
that shared paid access, change the endpoint, or leave this local grammar.
The particular eight-mode ABIs Q U_mode and V_mode Q, including their free
retraction rebases, are covered above. An arbitrary different eight-mode
endpoint, the middle compression V_mode Q U_mode, and the two-sided
compression PQP remain outside the claims.

The actual #4932 entrance adapter is also covered by free equivalence. Its
physical monomial X wrapper is M_i=U_i K_i Q^-1. Therefore
U a U*=M (Q C Q^-1) M*, and Q C Q^-1=Q^2 J Q^-2. Since Q^2 and M are free,
and a^-1=(a+12I)/13, the physical U a^-1 U* differs from J by invertible
free boundaries, a nonzero scalar, and a free bypass. These operations
preserve the local paid-call lower bound. This correspondence uses the
retained exact physical wrapper identities; it does not replace U_i with
a bare Fourier along q_i.
