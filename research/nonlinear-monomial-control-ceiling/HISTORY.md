# Scope correction and preserved evidence

The first proof draft (frozen/FIRST-DRAFT.md inside the proof-review packet)
used a fixed-alphabet phrase too broadly. Independent review pointed out that
weighted monomial generators can have infinitely many projective products.
The final proof restricts the theorem to a fixed finite program or explicitly
fixed finite complete-product support independent of width. It permits only
outer scalar coefficients to vary with width. Both original drafts and the
independent review remain byte-exact; rejected scope wording is not silently
removed from the historical record.

The predecessor5160 proof is retained only as its exact source and tensor-
separation context. The new proof uses support partitions instead of a
Clifford/symplectic classification and preserves the transported-class warning.
The finite16x16 checker is a separate discriminator; its complete receipts
are not promoted into a uniform theorem or physical native-phase replay.

Source CRLF/LF bytes and all author receipts are preserved. No old package
is edited, no mathematical source is normalized, and private regenerated
outputs are compared to the immutable complete RESULT/MATRICES bytes.
