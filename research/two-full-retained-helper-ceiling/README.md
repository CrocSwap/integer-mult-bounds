# Retaining productive helpers does not complete two FULLs under separated controls

**Scoped written ceiling with exact native normalization checks; no new kappa.**
Two FULL main-owner calls cannot complete two independent nonmonomial main
jobs while retaining all their distinct simultaneous productive helper jobs,
when pre/inter/post owner controls are invertible over the stated separated
central-monomial/transverse-Clifford algebra. Arbitrary helper counts,
noncommuting query/helper words, restored B-valued spectators and width-dependent
scalar coefficients are included. Full central/fresh address mixing remains
outside this theorem.

This extends a premise explicitly excluded by the main-only theorem in
[PR4920](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/4920), exact head
`7eb4ef5251f7ba96c7e5d78c42fc98e66b7be4ba`: the helpers here are productive
operations emitted at the same time as the two paid FULLs and must be retained
as their original distinct jobs. The proof is self-contained; no4920 proof
files or fork-only history are imported. The separate4920 theorem with full
monomial routing has exactly two owners and does not combine with this result.

## Exact contract and ceiling

For two main owners, m1 non-B helper jobs in the first invocation, m2 distinct
non-B helper jobs in the second, and s B-valued jobs/spectators, write
`n=2+m1+m2+s`. The literal factors have their raw transverse and translation
terms in order; no d/Q commutation is assumed. For `M=T2 W T1`, select untouched
second-call rows U and columns C excluding first-call non-B helper inputs.
Their subgroup projection is an invertible B diagonal times `W[U,C]` times
another invertible B diagonal. As a finite vector-space map, its rank is at
least `(s+1)dim(B)`. The required target projects to rank `s dim(B)`.
Invertible external B controls preserve projected rank, giving a contradiction.
The argument tolerates zero divisors and arbitrary pure helper words outside B.

[The complete proof](science/PROOF.md) includes the literal four-owner case,
arbitrary m1/m2/s, the all-width central-character quotient/tensor separation,
and the reversible clean-work qualification. The frozen folder's original
name referred to an initial three-owner question; its corrected source contract
uses **four distinct owners** when m1=m2=1,s=0.

This is a theorem for **two main owners**, with additional helper/spectator
owners. The hypothetical `224H17+2H-2P` invoice is motivation for112 independent
replications of that controller. The result does not claim an arbitrary joint
224-main-owner controller ceiling. Its supplied optimistic margin

endpoint certificate or a new selected bound.
