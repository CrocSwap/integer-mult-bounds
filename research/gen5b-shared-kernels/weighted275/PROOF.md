## Why reused physical donor slots are legal here

The weighted word stores `(donor, recipient)` pairs. Its physical map identifies
the recipient with the donor slot. The kernel guard excludes recipient keys;
it does not exclude donor values. Every paired recipient is gauged at rank21.
It has no initial ZERO-frame compensation read. Hence the literal initial
response of a reused donor slot is the donor's response, not the XOR of the two
virtual responses. The later recipient compensation remains in the suffix.

We independently rebuild the signed adjoint and the physical initial prefix
from the pinned graph and word. This reproduces all 648,088 odd initial reads,
and all 1,720 existing kernel relations and cut-read triples. The new candidate
uses 105 reused physical donor slots. For each, the donor's last operation
strictly precedes the recipient gauge read, which precedes or coincides with
its first operation. Every complete physical frame chain is nested and ends at
FULL. The check includes 2,561 distinct exact rational containments, including
all 132 existing reused-donor examples.

before any source injection. Selected physical slots are disjoint from every
old kernel member and both descent endpoint sets. No selected slot is an old
early-restored helper or sunk helper. Thus its current entrance remains ZERO;
its first old required frame is explicitly recovered from the full alias path.

Write `Q` for the old initial compensation prefix and `Q'` for that prefix
with the selected pivot reads removed. Let `A` be the commuting shear layer
`d += p` for every listed pivot/donor incidence. Pivot and donor sets are
disjoint; shared donors are permitted. The literal parity relations are
`R_p = sum_d R_d`. They imply `A Q' = Q A` over F2.
If the inherited remaining word is `S` and its full decoder is `U = S Q`, then
`A^-1 S A Q' = A^-1 U A = U`, because the inherited decoder fixes all dirty
helper coordinates. This also covers reused slots and arbitrary independent
dirty inputs. The final inverse layer is appended after the complete old word,
when all selected slots are at FULL. Its listed reverse order is valid even
with shared donors, since the shear factors commute.

The checker evaluates this composition and its inverse on all 18,952 physical
pre-compaction coordinate columns. Removed sink slots are inert spectators.
This is a full-column check of the stated composition theorem, not a claimed
literal regeneration of the entire upstream scalar stream. Omitting a setup

## Discovery scope and a useful fixed-set limitation

The line screen uses exact F2 elimination with a fixed ascending-role basis.
Its maximum-closure instance selects pivot benefits while charging activated
donors once. Integer capacities come from a declared 10^9 rounding of the
entropy surrogate r*log(120/r); discovery is reproducible for this fixed screen,
but this surrogate is not the final certified objective.

For a fixed active set U, common entrance dimension e and response rank r,
changing only the donor basis cannot change the local histogram:

`sum_{s in U} ([d_s-e]-[d_s]) + r[e]`.

Here `[0]` is omitted. Each active old first climb is split once, and exactly r
donors pay the entrance; hence the rank decrease is e*(|U|-r). Basis changes can
still affect scalar count and scalar growth. Improving this histogram requires
changing the active set or the entrance/cut model. This elementary limitation
does not prove the chosen active set optimal.

The packaged selection replays exactly 276 lines, the three positive groups,
and two overlap-priority orders. The 166 witness is rederived as the retained
136+12 groups plus 18 secondary pivots, after reclosure and one deterministic
parity trim. Broader matching exchanges, donor bases, active sets and different
cut positions are outside this bounded experiment.
