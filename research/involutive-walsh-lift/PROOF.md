## A finite change of tensor width

Use two copies of each old coordinate. Write the new coordinates in
this order:

    (e_0,0),(e_0,1),(e_1,0),(e_1,1), ... .

Thus an old subspace V becomes `V tensor F_2^2`, an old matrix g becomes
`g tensor I_2`, and every dimension doubles. An old coordinate interval
`[a,a+r)` becomes exactly `[2a,2a+2r)` in this order.

This is a finite construction with new ambient dimension 146. Its
ordinary tensor parameter mu can be any positive integer. It uses the
old all-size native contract at `lambda_old=2mu`. A child that was
`F_(r lambda_old)` is exactly `F_((2r)mu)`. No padding rule, restriction on
the new parameter mu, or assumption about separable array inputs is
used. Equal native operators have equal tensor powers on the complete
array space.

The scalar word is not tensor-squared. Each scalar coefficient is
applied once to each entry of the enlarged array. Native frames, address
maps, and phase maps are tensor-squared. Common-frame scalar access and
the full arbitrary-input scalar proof therefore remain valid.

## Involutive Walsh frames

Let H_V be the exact scaled Walsh frame of old rank r from the reviewed
retarget. Its one-copy square is `i^r I`. Define the new base frame

    Gamma_V=i^-r (H_V tensor H_V).                 (1)

Then `Gamma_V^2=I`. On mu tensor copies the scalar in (1) is `i^(-r mu)`,
which is always a Gaussian unit. For a coordinate rank-r space, (1) is
the real normalized Walsh transform on 2r coordinates. Its entries are
dyadic rationals. General frames are its conjugates by invertible binary
address maps. Degenerate V causes no exception.

For old nested ranks a,b, the new transition is

    Gamma_B Gamma_A^-1
       =i^(-(b-a)) (H_B H_A^-1) tensor (H_B H_A^-1).

Its exact native rank is `2(b-a)`. The independent review checks its
literal monomial factors, coordinate gathers, and scalar phase. More
generally, every old transition rank doubles because every relevant
binary kernel and subspace intersection is a direct sum of two copies.

For an old source label p, the new port phase is

    Q_p=i^-1 (H_p tensor H_p),  Q_p^2=I.           (2)

It is a rank-two phase on the two independent p directions. It must not
be replaced by a rank-one phase on the single vector `(p,p)`.
Two is the least uniform number of coordinate copies that permits this
scalar normalization over the Gaussian rationals. An odd number of
copies would require a Gaussian rational c with `c^2=i` or `c^2=-i`
already at rank one and tensor parameter one. If `c=a+bi` with rational
a,b, this gives `a^2=b^2` and `2ab=1` or `2ab=-1`. It would require
`a^2=1/2`, which has no rational solution. This lower bound concerns
scalar normalization of the Walsh tensor powers. It does not exclude
a different interface or a replica construction.

At local ranks 0,1,8,9, equations (1) and (2) give the exact endpoint
pattern

    source entrance Q_p,  target entrance I,
    source exit Gamma_full,
    target exit Gamma_full Q_p^-1.

An old port route fixes p and is orthogonal. Its doubled route fixes
both p directions and commutes with Q_p. Thus the data phase Q commutes
with every actual diagonal port route D_s.

The full 146-axis output is

    Gamma_full=i^-73 D F_146 D,

where D is the product of the coordinate S phases. Apply D^-1 before
the complete job and `i^73 D^-1` after it to obtain the literal F_146
job. For mu tensor copies, take the corresponding tensor powers. These
are two monomial maps, with no new recursive child or irrational
coefficient.

## Monomial map count

Interior retargeting changes the two monomial factors of an existing
native child. If those factors were L and R, the new factors are of the
form `A_out L` and `R A_in^-1`. Their products are monomial, so these are
still the same two factor slots. Doubling the coordinate matrices and
including the Gaussian unit phase preserves that statement.

For each local invocation there are n=704 persistent registers, of
which R=480 are borrowed helpers. Allocate at most two extra monomial
maps to each persistent register to change its entry and exit
representatives. Allocate two more maps to each complete helper job if
that job is converted between its doubled Walsh representative and the
ordinary F_(2r) representative. The global history uses Gamma on every
completed owner interval. A literal F_(2r) occurs only inside its Gamma
adapters. The optional inverse conversion is not applied to a completed
owner in that history. Thus the number of extra local maps is

    2n+2R=2368 <= 4n=2816.

The four-per-register charge includes all these maps even when they
could be composed. Private centres and coefficient registers have
unchanged frame-zero endpoints. Their native transitions already use
the existing factor slots; they do not introduce a persistent helper
job. The helper conversion is exact on arbitrary incoming values.

For the full global word, allocate two maps per productive family for
the input and output conversion to F_146. Allocate two more maps per
physical family for terminal representative changes. Since W is at
most the physical count, this is at most four maps per physical family,
or 59304 maps. The invoice charges all of them in addition to the old
terminal and movement allowance.

An old helper wrapper contains at most `2(m-1)` elementary factors.

`2(2m-1)=290` is larger. The existing 69120 wrapper occurrences therefore
receive 20044800 charged elementary factors. These charges concern
