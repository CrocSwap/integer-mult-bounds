# Two FULL calls cannot complete two central Gaussian jobs in the main-only model

Prepared for Chris R / Clear1nsight with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance.
Apache-2.0. This is a written scoped proof with exact finite corroboration and
a separate agent review. It is not a proof-kernel verification or a new kappa.

## Actual question and boundaries

The prospective construction in Discussion4902 has two arbitrary 112-field
main banks, two independent 408-helper inventories, and the native supplier's
owned zero centres. Every helper output remains its autonomous, already
specified Gaussian job. Existing transformed helper values are not available
as extra central resources in the main-only controller studied here.

At frozen source `20bb93cd990d2ff683e4d0fc56b87a42cd5d3296`, each main port of
one FULL invocation, after its authenticated source boundaries, is exactly

    F_s = D_s [[1,0],[Q^-1,1]],
    B_s = D_s [[1,-Q],[0,1]].

There is one off-diagonal central Gaussian letter per invocation, with no
additional nonmonomial central diagonal factor. The source checks actual
routes and absolute phases, including the inverse/translation convention.
The source all-column implementation is retained as a written premise; this
package does not rerun the native 632-column verification.

Change the central function by actual columnwise monomial conjugation, for
example S=diag(1,i), acting on both main owners before/after a FULL call.
Different central functions need not commute. The free address grammar is
generated by a fixed finite group of one-column Clifford matrices, lifted
identically in every column. Arbitrary Gaussian-dyadic owner GL operations
and role-dependent admitted monomials are allowed. For the obstruction we
grant arbitrary complex scalar coefficients, even depending on width, and
arbitrary invertible matrices over the free address algebra. This grants
more free operations than a physical implementation supplies.

The two rank-eight transverse blocks are disjoint from the central axis.
Grant ALL transverse/fresh Clifford operations for free, and central
monomials for free, but retain their tensor-factor separation. This is the
subgroup H used below. Native D_s belong to H and can be absorbed into
adjacent free factors. This does not require D_s to commute with the chosen
S. In particular raw D_0=F_9 Q^-1 has a real translation correction to its
positive transverse-eight normalization; raw D_0 must not be silently
declared to commute with S. The independent source audit emits that exact
correction for all 112 actual ports.

A main target is a full rank-17 Gaussian up to the declared separated
central/transverse monomial boundaries. It lies outside H: an H element has central Fourier rank zero
and transverse Fourier rank at most sixteen. A central/fresh mixing free
address operation is outside this grammar. Adding arbitrary such mixing
while already granting all fresh Gaussians free could make the central
Gaussian free, invalidating the non-H hypothesis.

## Algebraic theorem

Let A be a finite-dimensional twisted group algebra over an infinite field
K, on a finite group Gamma. Its basis multiplication is group multiplication
times nonzero scalar cocycle factors. Let Lambda be a subgroup, and B its
twisted subgroup algebra. Let pi:A->B retain just basis terms in Lambda.
It is a B-bimodule map: left/right multiplication by Lambda preserves both
Lambda and its complement. No domain or positivity property is assumed.

Take n>=2 owners, including any additional dirty scratch owners returned
unchanged. Let L,W,R be arbitrary invertible matrices in Mat_n(B). Two paid
letters, with a,b supported on individual basis elements outside Lambda,
have the form

    M = L (I+e_i b e_j^T) W (I+e_k a e_l^T) R,
    i!=j, k!=l.                                      (1)

Signs, inverses, monomial pre/post factors, arbitrary orientations, and
different noncommuting central functions are permitted. Nonzero scalar
coefficients can be included in a,b. The physical F/B alphabet is a subset.

Let d=dim_K B. Then

    dim_K ker pi(M) <= d,                            (2)

where pi(M) acts by left multiplication on the right B-module B^n.
Consequently (1) cannot equal a target whose first two projected input
columns vanish; in particular it cannot equal diag(g1,g2,I,...,I) for
independent g1,g2 outside Lambda.

### Proof

Expand (1). The term containing no paid letter projects to LWR. Each
single-paid term vanishes under pi, because B a B and B b B have no
Lambda-supported term. The double-paid term has only the selected middle
entry W_jk between its two non-B letters. By the bimodule property,

    pi(M) = L [W + e_i c e_l^T] R,
    c = pi(b W_jk a).                                (3)

W is invertible on the K-vector space B^n of dimension nd. The perturbation
e_i c e_l^T factors through one copy of B and has image dimension at most
d. If x belongs to the kernel of W+e_i c e_l^T, then Wx lies in that image.
The map x->Wx is injective, so its kernel dimension is at most d. Left/right
multiplication by invertible L,R preserves that dimension, proving (2).

Two vanished projected input columns instead put two independent copies
of B in the kernel, of total dimension 2d. This contradicts (2). The
argument explicitly permits zero divisors and idempotents in B.

The same obstruction excludes zero or one paid letter: the projection is
then an invertible free matrix. Arbitrary identity scratch and arbitrary
outer owner GL operations remain in the theorem. Existing transformed
helper jobs are different: their non-B input states cannot be inserted
as identity scratch or absorbed into L,W,R over B.

## All positive widths, including width-dependent scalar choices

Let G be the finite group of exact one-column Clifford matrices needed for
the stated address grammar, including its absolute scalar phases. One may
take the full finite seventeen-axis Clifford group; H is the subgroup
retaining the central-monomial/transverse-Clifford separation. Let Z be the
subgroup of scalar matrices. H contains Z.

At width f, literal address words act as rho(g)^(tensor f). A product of
words lifts as the product of their tensor lifts. A sum of owner-operator
entries lifts by retaining its scalar coefficients; it is not replaced by
the tensor power of a sum. A phase word z=ζI acts as ζ^f, not scalar ζ.

Choose representatives v_t for the s=|G/Z| projective matrix classes.
For any t and u!=t, projective distinctness gives a linear functional
ell_tu on the one-column matrix space which vanishes on v_u and not on
v_t. The product over u!=t has degree s-1 and isolates v_t. For any
f>=s-1, multiply by a power of a functional nonzero on v_t. This is a
degree-f functional on tensor powers which annihilates all v_u^(tensor f)
except v_t^(tensor f). Thus these s tensor vectors are linearly independent
for every f>=s-1.

For each such f quotient K[G] by the central-character relations

    z = ζ_z^f.

The resulting A_f is the twisted group algebra on G/Z; its tensor
representation is injective by the preceding argument. The corresponding
B_f on H/Z has dimension |H/Z|. Projection pi_f onto H/Z is still a
B_f-bimodule map: phase cocycles change coefficients, not support membership.

Any exact operator identity at that width therefore gives an identity in
A_f. Apply (2) separately at this width. It fails for every sufficiently
large f, even if scalar coefficients and free word choices depend on f.
An alleged construction valid at every positive width is impossible.
This is an existence proof; no enumeration of a huge seventeen-axis group
or of physical tape words is needed.

For the reduced central one-axis model, the concrete generators used by
the checker have |G|=96, |Z|=4, |G/Z|=24, |H/Z|=8. The sufficient width
bound is f>=23. The full transverse/scratch relaxation uses its own finite
G and finite sufficient bound; 23 is not asserted for that enlarged group.

## Finite corroboration and discriminating controls

The standard-library checker constructs the exact 96-element central group
using Gaussian rational entries. Q^2 is the translation X, not identity.
It authenticates all 32 monomial elements, all 98,304 subgroup-support
bimodule checks, and 64 exact two-letter expansion fixtures with genuinely
nontrivial free L/W/R, both orientations, inverses and S conjugation.
These fixtures corroborate (3); they are not a complete circuit enumeration.

A separate modular Gram calculation proves exact independence of the
24 projective tensor vectors at widths 23,24,25,26: rank 24 over the field
F_1000003[i], i^2=-1, implies rank 24 in characteristic zero. It is a small
finite control of the written all-width argument, not a replacement for it.

The three-query word with opposite shears and a scalar signed swap returns
diag(Q^-1,Q) exactly in the group algebra. It is a positive control for
complete arbitrary-dirty targets and shows why the projection argument
stops at two letters. It supplies no profitable three-FULL invoice.

Two semantic fault controls expose the specific bad width-one shortcuts:
the matrix relation Q=((1+i)/2)I+((1-i)/2)X holds at one column but its
scalar sum fails at width two; and the word iI tensors to i^f I, not scalar
iI at every width. Enlarging the free subgroup to contain Q is explicitly
an invalidation of the theorem's hypothesis, not a new negative result.

No native scientific suite, whole caller/compiler/outer replay, numerical
root search, Lean build or physical tape benchmark is run here. Exact
source hashes and separate source/invoice audit evidence are retained.

## Addendum: full monomial routing, exactly two owners and no additional states

The preceding theorem grants arbitrary restored dirty scratch but restricts
the free address subgroup to separated central/transverse operations. The
following theorem removes that address separation for **exactly two main
owners**, without access to scratch or transformed helper states. These are
two distinct scopes; no theorem here simultaneously grants both extensions.

This direct-column argument was independently derived by the parent agent
and checked/refined by the separate gpt-6.1-sol/high reviewer. Its unchanged
review note and one literal controlled-phase check are under `grade/`.

Take G to be a fixed finite group of exact one-column seventeen-axis Clifford
matrices, including phases. Let H now be its FULL monomial Clifford subgroup,
including central/fresh axis mixing, and let B be its subgroup algebra. Let
grade(g) be the Gaussian Fourier rank of a Clifford word g. Multiplication
on either side by a monomial preserves grade. The projection pi_16 retaining
basis words of grade at most sixteen is therefore a B-bimodule map.

At a fixed sufficiently large column width use the same central-character
twisted quotient and faithful tensor representation proved above. Scalar
phases leave grade unchanged, so pi_16 remains well-defined and bimodular.
Scalar choices may depend on width, and all free matrices below may be any
invertible matrices over B.

Retain each actual native transverse factor instead of absorbing it. A FULL
main map is

    T_s = diag(D_sx,D_sy) E_s,

where each D factor has Fourier rank eight, and E_s is an elementary upper
or lower shear with one central letter. The two letters may differ and need
not commute. Role-dependent rank-eight D factors, signs, inverses and all
actual raw-D0 translations are allowed. Call-specific monomial boundaries
are absorbed into L,W,R. Suppose a successful word existed:

    L T_2 W T_1 R = diag(g1,g2),

with both g_j of Fourier rank seventeen and L,W,R invertible over B.
The target is pure grade seventeen. Bimodularity and invertibility give

    pi_16(T_2 W T_1)=0.                              (4)

Let c be the input column untouched by the first shear and r the output row
untouched by the second; let i denote the other row. Direct multiplication
in the original factor order gives

    (T_2 W T_1)_rc = D_2r W_rc D_1c.

Every basis word of W_rc is monomial. Fourier-rank subadditivity gives grade
at most 8+0+8=16 for every basis word in this entry. Thus the entry equals
its pi_16 projection and is zero by (4). The D factors are group units, so
W_rc=0. The other entry of the same input column then simplifies to

    (T_2 W T_1)_ic = D_2i W_ic D_1c,

because its possible second-shear contribution factors through W_rc.
It too is supported on grades at most sixteen, hence is zero. Therefore
W_ic=0 as well. An entire column of W is zero, contradicting invertibility.
Choosing untouched row/column directly handles every pair of shear
orientations; no commuting of D factors past changed central letters is used.

This proves that full monomial Clifford routing, including controlled phases
and arbitrary non-diagonal owner GL2 words, cannot supply the two independent
rank-seventeen jobs using two FULL calls on exactly two main owners. It does
not cover added owners or existing transformed helper resources. Arbitrary
width-specific address operations outside the finite columnwise alphabet
also remain outside both theorems.

costs one monomial pass per port and zero additional Gaussian calls. Its

Its nonzero M22 word has grade sixteen, so the full matrix fails (4).
The pure q/a coordinate swap is a discriminating control with only one
grade-seventeen off-diagonal word. Neither is a completed endpoint.
