## §1 Why avoiding Ξ-lines is not needed: the extra zeros enter only through frame coordinates
In every proof of P, A, S, Σ a matrix entry M_us (u ≠ s, u not⊥ s) is used (1) as a pivot coordinate: α_u(i)=0 ⇔ (u,f_i)∈Ξ, β_s(i)=0 ⇔ (f_i,s)∈Ξ, i.e. only if f_i ∈ X; (2) once, in the step Π(+)≠Π(−). All other uses are forced zeros M_us=0, u⊥s. (Audit item (a).) For S the s56 CNF contains only orthogonality incidences (s56z.py, read here), so it is sound for arbitrary Ξ provided rows/columns are points/lines of P² (P̂_u≠0, Q̂_s≠0, see §3).
**Step Π(+)≠Π(−) with budget.** If Π(+)=Π(−) then M_us=0 for every +row u∈Z of a tetrad T_i and every +column s of another T_j: the pair {+rows in Z}×{4 columns of one + class} has ≥ 4 non-orthogonal pairs (E8: classes have exactly 4 lines, no line missing), all would lie in Ξ: impossible for |Ξ| ≤ 3. PROVEN.

## §2 Lemma EX (robust exactness) — PROVEN, combinatorics CHECKED
Let F be a frame, I = {i : f_i ∈ X}, and assume (E1) |T∩I| ≤ 2 for every F-tetrad T (automatic when |I| ≤ 2). Then:
(R) for every u∈Z and k∈T(u): α_u(k)≠0 — and for every u with y-side analog: every column s∈Z′ likewise;
(C) for every column s (also s∈D′) and k∈T(s): β_s(k)≠0, provided (E2) for every tetrad T, k∈T there is c∈T∖{k}, c∉I, {k,c}≠{f1,f2}-pair indices (Theorem A situation; none needed for S/Σ), with a tetrad T′≠T through {k,c} not containing {1,2}.
*Proof (R).* Pick c∈T(u)∖{k}, c∉I (exists: |T∩I|≤2 and one more index removed leaves ≥1; if k∈I it is already removed). A tetrad T′≠T(u) through {k,c} contains a line s with ε_kc(s)≠ε_kc(u), so s⊥u, and c∈T(s). x_u=0 gives α_u(k)β_s(k)+α_u(c)β_s(c)=0. Now α_u(c)≠0 and β_s(c)≠0 because f_c∉X (so (u,f_c),(f_c,s)∉Ξ and non-orthogonal). Hence α_u(k)β_s(k)≠0. ∎
*Proof (C).* Pick c as in (E2) and a Z-row u in the class of T′ opposite to s (T′∌{1,2}, so its lines lie outside Λ12 ⊇ D, hence in Z): u⊥s, α_u(c)≠0 by (R)/f_c∉X, β_s(c)≠0 (f_c∉X). The relation α_u(k)β_s(k)+α_u(c)β_s(c)=0 forces α_u(c)β_s(c)=0 if β_s(k)=0, a contradiction, so β_s(k)≠0. ∎
**Consequence.** Z-rows and all columns are exact in every coordinate of their tetrad (the I-coordinates included), hence Ξ-pairs (f_k,s) with f_k∈F∩X cannot exist; only hidden off-frame zeros and entries in D×D′-type positions remain, and they are used only in §1(2).
**CHECKED lemex.py (exact, abstract on the 14 tetrads):** for every pair {a,b}, every I with |I|≤2: (E1) holds and (E2) holds for all (T,k), except exactly 168 cases = I is the partner pair {x,x′} of {a,b} (T={a,b,x,x′}, k∈{a,b}). So define a frame **good** for the pair {f1,f2} if |F∩X| ≤ 2 and F∩X is not the partner pair of {f1,f2} in F.
**CHECKED fchoice.py:** fix an orthogonal pair (W(E8) is transitive on orthogonal pairs; all orbits equal). For every X′ ⊆ the 30 lines orthogonal to the pair with |X′| ≤ 6−a (a=|X∩{f1,f2}|∈{0,1,2}; 974 580 configurations) one of the 15 frames through {f1,f2} is good: 0 failures. Since |X| ≤ 6, a good frame exists for every Ξ with |Ξ|≤3.
For S, Σ (index 1 only): frames through f1: 135, each line orthogonal to f1 lies in 15 of them (CHECKED hit.py), X∖{f1} ≤ 6 lines kill ≤ 90 < 135: there is F∋f1 with F∩X ⊆ {f1}; then **all coordinates off index 1 are exact for all rows and columns** and no Lemma EX is needed.
