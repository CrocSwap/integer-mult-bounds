# A characteristic-two symmetric fitting-rank obstruction

## Precise finite theorem

Let G be the 24-vertex graph specified by the integer coordinates in the
unchanged `research/turyn-profile-cnf/WITNESS.json`. An edge means that the product
of its three integer block inner products is nonzero. Let Gc retain all
21 anchor-clique edges and 79 anchor-to-variable edges, but only the 57
listed core variable-to-variable edges.

For every field K of characteristic two, the minimum rank of a **symmetric**
24-by-24 matrix M over K with Mvv = 1 and Mvw = 0 on graph edges is exactly
nine, for both G and Gc. The 57 core variable edges are inclusion-minimal
for this rank-nine obstruction with all anchor constraints fixed. This
is not a minimum edge-count claim and does not address nonsymmetric
fitting matrices, arbitrary global 25-dimensional Turyn labelings, or kappa.

## Reduction from symmetric rank at most eight

Suppose such a symmetric fitting M has rank at most eight. Put its seven
anchor vertices first. They form a clique, so their principal block is I7:

    M = [ I7  A ]
        [ Aᵀ  D ].

Congruence by elementary block operations gives

    rank(M) = 7 + rank(S),   S = D + Aᵀ A.

Consequently rank(S) is at most one. For each of the seventeen remaining
vertices put c_v = 1 + sum_k A_kv. Since M has diagonal one and K has
characteristic two,

    S_vv = 1 + sum_k A_kv² = c_v².

Every two-by-two minor of S vanishes. Since S is symmetric this gives

    S_vw² = S_vv S_ww = (c_v c_w)².

Squaring is injective in **every field** of characteristic two: if x²=y²,
then (x+y)²=0, hence x=y. Thus S_vw=c_v c_w, including cases with zero
diagonal entries. No perfectness or algebraic-closure assumption is used.
It follows that the vectors

    p(anchor k) = e_k,
    p(v) = (A_1v, ..., A_7v, c_v)

form an eight-dimensional standard-dot fitting of M. In particular,
A_kv=0 on all anchor-to-variable edges. These zero constraints leave
exactly 119-79=40 free scalar coordinates. The last coordinate of each
p(v) is forced to be one plus the sum of its first seven.

Conversely, an eight-dimensional standard-dot fitting gives a symmetric
fitting matrix of rank at most eight by taking its Gram matrix. Thus the
finite obstruction below is exactly the required rank obstruction, not
merely a restriction to arbitrary chosen labels.

## Ordinary-polynomial certificate

Use an indeterminate for each of the 40 allowed anchor coordinates.
For every core variable edge vw let

    f_vw = sum_{k=1}^7 A_kv A_kw
           + (1 + sum_k A_kv)(1 + sum_k A_kw).

All 57 f_vw must vanish in a fitting. They are polynomials in
F2[x_0,...,x_39]. The independently checked `ORDINARY-PC-PROOF.json` is a
straight-line derivation of the constant polynomial one from these
polynomials. It uses only:

1. An original edge polynomial
2. Addition of previously derived polynomials
3. Multiplication of a previously derived polynomial by one indeterminate

These rules preserve the ideal generated by the f_vw. If all f_vw vanished
at a point over K, every derived polynomial would vanish there, contrary
to the final value one. A polynomial identity with coefficients in F2
remains an identity over every characteristic-two field.

Crucially, **there is no rule x²=x, no finite-field relation, and no
multilinear reduction** in this certificate. The verifier represents a
monomial by a sorted tuple with repetitions retained: `(i,i,j)` is x_i²x_j,
not x_ix_j. Every ordinary intermediate polynomial has total degree at
most three. The proof has 3,121 steps: 57 edge axioms, 2,389 variable
multiplications, and 675 additions. Every source edge is used.

The all-field assertion follows from these ordinary polynomial rules;
no search over a finite extension field is used to infer it.

## Sharpness and minimality

The unchanged baseline supplies an explicit nine-dimensional fitting with
coordinates in F2 for all 171 edges of G. It remains a fitting over every
characteristic-two field. Therefore both G and its subgraph Gc have
symmetric fitting rank at most nine. The obstruction proves equality.

For each of the 57 core variable edges the baseline also supplies an
explicit F2 eight-dimensional fitting after that edge is deleted from
Gc. These assignments remain valid over every characteristic-two field.
They retain all anchor constraints, and each violates the omitted edge.
Thus the core is inclusion-minimal in the stated edge class over every
such field. The positive controls do not have to satisfy the fourteen
variable edges discarded from G before obtaining Gc.

## Degree boundary of this normalized equation system

The homogeneous quadratic parts of the 57 f_vw are linearly independent
already over F2. `QUADRATIC-RANK.json` supplies 57 quadratic monomial
columns on which the coefficient matrix has rank 57. The same nonzero
binary minor remains invertible over every characteristic-two field.
Hence no nonzero affine polynomial can be a constant-linear combination
of these 57 equations.

In ordinary polynomial calculus with just these edge axioms, a derivation
whose every polynomial has degree at most two can only take such linear
combinations: a nonzero quadratic multiplied by an indeterminate has
degree three; no nonzero affine polynomial has first been derived to
multiply instead. Induction on the derivation therefore excludes a
degree-two refutation. Combined with the checked degree-three refutation,
the minimum proof degree is exactly three **for this normalized equation
system and these proof rules**. Auxiliary equations, new variables,
Boolean axioms, division rules, or a different encoding are outside this
statement.

## Limits

This appendix is independent corroboration on the earlier, different
24-port graph. The main twenty-port parity lift gives an asymmetric
block result; that theorem is not being asserted for this 57-edge core.

This is a finite, exact, computer-assisted algebraic result. It is not a
Lean formalization or a formal proof of integer multiplication. It gives
no new kappa, no general global sum-of-profile dimension bound, no
nonsymmetric minrank claim, and no Hermitian-form claim. The inherited
physical/compiler, precision, tape, hosting and analytic assumptions of
the multiplication framework are untouched.
