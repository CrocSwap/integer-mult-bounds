# Attribution and scope

Prepared for George Lydakis with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance, including
coordinated independent research and review by Ody. New contributions are
Apache-2.0. No exclusive priority is claimed.

This package contributes the source-bound gen5 source-retiming selection, the
exact prefix-boundary search and target selection, their verified composition,
the gen5 bank/price adapters, independent circuit and invoice checks, immutable
replay and CI. The mechanisms build on prior contributions:

- DreamingOfClouds' PR285 supplies the new gen5 module circuits and immutable
  nine-stage package, prepared with Anthropic Claude assistance. Its full notice
  is preserved in NOTICE-PR285.md.
- Dugongue's PR283 supplies the compatibility exporter, native tools and exact
  pricers, prepared with OpenAI Codex assistance. Its full notice is preserved in
  NOTICE-PR283.md; retained native sources and the JSON header remain attributed.
- Rohan Arun's PR279 supplies source concave retiming and target-square work;
  PR281 supplies the target candidate sets and bases, with Anthropic Claude
  assistance disclosed. Our boundaries and source frames are re-derived on gen5.
- Eumemic supplies the target-prefix mechanism and source527 lineage; Chafik
  Boukhalfa, utcorvusvolat-dotcom and the full inherited contributor chain are
  credited in the preserved prerequisite notices.

All-size hypotheses remain conditional. Certificate success does not formally
verify the whole multiplication theorem.
