# The DEG2 mask model is a sound relaxation (proof)

Setting (imports, all from #3154 / its predecessors; not re-proved here). Row 579 = [7^8,4,4,4,3], d = 8: eight 7-flats L_a (sets of root lines of E8, a = 0..7), small flats F4,F4,F4,F3 with E their union, |E| <= 8+8+8+4 = 28.
Lemma F (SEVEN / KILL-A8, K2 via #3074): for every pair a != b there is a direction h_ab (a nonzero vector of R^8) with Off(h_ab) subset L_a u L_b u E, where Off(h) = {root lines t : <r_t,h> != 0}; the directions follow the **K8-graphic pattern**: h_ab is proportional to u_a - u_b for points u_a, so the matroid of {h_ab} is the graphic matroid of the simple graph K8 (no two pair directions parallel; the three pairs of a triangle span a plane, any two of them are independent).
DEG2 (C6): no seven a carries two non-root pair directions h_ab, h_ac (root = a vector with Off(h) of the root-line type, i.e. h proportional to a root).
Notation: Lo_xy = Off(h_xy) \ (L_x u L_y) is a subset of E.

**Claim.** If row 579 had sevens a,b,c with h_ab, h_ac non-root, then (with the other pairs of any six of the eight sevens) the following finite system P has a solution; P has none (DEG2A impl A, DEG2C impl C). Hence DEG2.

**S1 (finitely many masks).** Write h (up to W(E8) and positive scaling, sign is irrelevant to Off) in the closed dominant chamber, h = sum_{i in S} c_i omega_i with c_i > 0, S its support. For every positive root r, <r,omega_i> >= 0, so <r,h> != 0 iff <r,omega_i> != 0 for some i in S: Off(h) = U_{i in S} Off(omega_i) depends only on S. R579b section R1 (types.c; reproduced by `types.out`) lists min over (L_a,L_b) of |Off \ (L_a u L_b)| for |S| <= 2: root ({8}) 1, D7 ({1}) 14, E6A1 ({7}) 23, E6 ({7,8}) 24, A7 ({2}) 25, D6 ({1,8}) 26, all other S >= 31. Since Lo_xy subset E, |Lo_xy| <= 28, so only these six S can occur for |S| <= 2; every S with |S| >= 3 contains a pair {i,j} or a singleton, hence Off(h) contains Off(omega_i) u Off(omega_j), and for the pairs not in the list the minimum leftover is >= 31 (monotone: a larger Off cannot have a smaller leftover). The only three-element supports that could survive are supersets of {1,8} or {7,8}, which contain {1,7} (>= 33). So Off(h_xy) is one of the W-conjugates of the six masks (orbit sizes 120, 1080, 8640, 3360, 1120, 3780 as printed by the programs). This is exact, it does not depend on which h in a plane is chosen: the "continuum" of E6 (an A2-plane) and D6 (an A1A1-plane) directions is the same finite set of masks.
**S2 (equal masks).** For the types root, D7, A7, E6A1 the roots orthogonal to h span the 7-dimensional h-perp, so the mask determines h up to scalar. Two pairs carrying the same such mask would have parallel directions, impossible in a simple graphic matroid; so in P equal masks are allowed only for E6 and D6 (rank-6 perp: several directions in one plane have the same mask). Applied to all pairs of the system (impl C) / to all pairs at a given triangle and across (impl A: see its NOTES).
**S3 (triangle condition).** For a triangle x,y,z the three directions lie in a plane and, being pairwise non-parallel (S2) or in the equal-mask E6/D6 case, each is a linear combination of the other two. If a root line r were in exactly one of the three Off masks, say Off(h_xy), then <r,h_xz> = <r,h_yz> = 0 and h_xy = alpha h_xz + beta h_yz gives <r,h_xy> = 0, a contradiction. So no line lies in exactly one of the three masks (a necessary condition: dependencies of 4 or more directions and vector-realisability are not used: relaxation).
**S4 (L_x maximal).** Replacing L_x by a maximal 7-flat containing it only shrinks every Lo_xy; if the original U = u Lo_xy satisfies |U| <= 28 and U is covered by 3F4+F3 then so does the shrunken U (a subset of a covered set is covered; the cover oracle is monotone). So L_x ranges over the 43180 maximal 7-flats (codim-2 subspaces of F2^9; count reproduced by both implementations).
**S5 (size and cover).** U = u Lo_xy subset E, so |U| <= 28 and U lies in a union of three 4-flats and one 3-flat. The oracle (slot cover of the 8-bit labels, dims 4,4,4,3) is monotone in U, hence usable as a prune at every stage (the configuration of k sevens is a sub-configuration of that of k+1 sevens).
**S6 (symmetry).** The system is W(E8)-equivariant, so one La (impl A: 10 orbit reps of La under the stabiliser of the pair-mask type; impl C: h_01 = dominant rep of its type and L_0 over orbit reps of the stabiliser parabolic) may be fixed; everything else is enumerated in full (no further symmetry used).
**S7 (from 8 sevens to six).** Any 6 of the 8 sevens including a,b,c with the pair directions of row 579 satisfy S1-S5 (pair masks of the other pairs may be roots or any of the six masks), so a solution of P with 6 sevens is necessary. P with 6 sevens has no solution => DEG2. (Impl A: 4-seven survivors exist only for reps 1 and 8, 5-seven sets exist, 0 six-sets; impl C the same stage structure at the mask level.)
**Exactness caveats.** (i) Impl A additionally discards five-sets whose non-generic pair vectors are not exactly realisable (geomN.py, exact rationals); `deg2a_check.sh` also runs the six-set stage on the UNFILTERED five-sets, so the conclusion does not depend on that filter. (ii) The cover oracles of the two implementations are different code (impl A: `coverrec`/`buildmasks` in `quad.c`/`ext.c`; impl C: a slot-cover DFS re-implemented from R579 `b.c`, which had a bug, below). (iii) A positive count would only be an upper bound; a zero is a valid kill.

## Oracle bug found in impl C (fixed)
`d2.c` (first impl C) pruned empty slots with a single flag `seen_empty` instead of one per dimension (`seen_empty[dims[k]]`): once the first empty slot (of any dimension) had been tried, the empty slots of the other dimensions were skipped, so some coverable sets were declared uncoverable (the pre-fix quad/five-set counts did not match impl A; their effect is not analysed further and the pre-fix counts are not claimed). `d2fix.c` uses `seen_empty[8]` indexed by the slot dimension. The pre-fix outputs are not shipped and not claimed. The fixed rerun is the SLOT in PROOF_MAP.md.
