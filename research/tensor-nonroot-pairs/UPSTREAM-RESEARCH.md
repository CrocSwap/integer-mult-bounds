# Library-driven research record

Three lanes searched actual external Lean sources and the canonical typed DAG by operator structure. Two new mechanisms were admitted: response-kernel cancellation and nested-donor flags. The chronological lane implemented several genuinely different constructions but found no admissible improvement. It is not counted as a third successful technique.

1. Low-rank response/annihilator representation: treat a collection of immutable-source injections as a matrix update U V. The sufficient certificate U V=0 permits simultaneous deletion even when every individual injection is observable. The realized cross-helper packet improves the moment; ten additional duplicate packets only shorten the scalar program. See research-notes/response/PROOF.md.
2. Flags and matroid representations: replace the old one-entrance-per-donor restriction by explicit nested chains. Shared donors receive corrections in increasing subspace order; every logical shear remains identical. The complete residual packing and exact isometries are rebuilt. See research-notes/flags and the independent nested review.
3. Noncommuting transport, GL3 basis and chronological affine synthesis: exact commutator corrections and complete affine boundary maps provide sufficient rewrites. Native searches screened29,464 transport placements,62,999,748 GL3 matrix/window cases,252 legal chronological affine forms and cleanup resynthesis. None passed the paid-gain gate. These are bounded negative experiments, not impossibility theorems. See research-notes/chronological/RESEARCH.md and its portable C++ sources.

The source catalogue searches span all five registered catalogues in the algebra lane and structural symbol combinations in the typed DAG. Other lanes also read installed Mathlib, OpenAI, CSLib, Hadamard6 and project sources. Search limits, exact source reads and missing/stale verification status are recorded. This is broad retrieval, not a claim to have read every theorem in the full tree. No inference of mathematical correctness is made merely from a source-catalogue or DAG hit.

The new473 kernel was composed with our prefix and packet constructors and repriced, rather than adding reported gains. Nested-provider variants were first screened cheaply and only selected exact words were physically replayed. No unverified heterogeneous-provider portfolio or third-lane rewrite contributes to the headline bound. Previous affine-controller and heterogeneous-provider ideas remain in the historical notes with their gaps explicit.
