2. General rank-partition identity
For U,V with k rows, diagonal D, and coefficient-column partition
E=X disjoint-union Y,
 rank(U D V^t)<=rank(U_X)+rank(V_Y).
Indeed split the product into its X and Y summands, bounding the first
by its left factor and the second by its right factor. If the sum is
less than k, the k-square determinant vanishes for every diagonal D.
For an invertible selected prefix minor, the next Schur entry is the
augmented minor determinant divided by that prefix determinant. Thus
an exact rank-partition certificate proves an ordered zero identically
in all projector weights, not merely at the witness.

Every coordinate is nonzero and both normalization identities hold.

This proves all required prefix-minor functions are nonzero on the
actual rank-one projector family.

PR29 fixes compatible permutation completions M_beta but leaves L_a
and L_b free. Any actual rank-one projector in characteristic zero is
GL-conjugate to any normalized pair p,xi, and similarly in dimension b.
Thus for each physical data pair the displayed witness proves that
its new pivot conditions are nonzero rational functions of the same
free GL55 x GL53 factors used by the inherited local/auxiliary minors.
After clearing denominators, finitely many such nonzero conditions
have nonzero product on this irreducible family. A rational common
basis avoiding them all exists. No independent basis choice per gate
is made, and the rational witness need not itself satisfy every other
physical occurrence simultaneously. The zero identities already hold
for all weights, so they impose no additional closed specialization.
The address prime can avoid the finitely many denominators and minors
as in the inherited construction. The reverse invocation uses the
same positive residual and therefore the same profile.
