# Public one-relation cache price screen

This package screens every eligible public MOVE on the exact #7481 word for
its optimistic **per-target** price if one helper-only scalar relation could
make that target recoverable from strictly earlier same-span native
observations. It reconstructs the independent main-projection filter, the
paired exact-rational census of all 1,066 public candidates, and a direct
full-L replay of 16 deterministic witness cuts. The retained #7113 caller has
17 signed native calls; the exact current invoice is included and its complete
invoice verification is credited to joint package commit
[`509baf4514966e4202cfdde379d7adf3085b1cc7`](https://github.com/CubeJerry/integer-mult-bounds/commit/509baf4514966e4202cfdde379d7adf3085b1cc7).

The screen finds 376 positive, 437 exactly-zero and 253 negative gross
rank-price profiles at \(\kappa=0.0038\). Its best single-target allowance is
\(f(8)-f(1)\), for public event 40710, with exact interval

\[
[6.937034079270974725608699,\;6.937034079270974725608700].
\]

That number is before relation preparation, public zero/reframe operations,
ordinary operation counts, precision, storage, lifetime, or shared-target
debits. The exact full 17-call invoice requires a per-call net saving in

\[
[3.48481964025734707306705441176470588235294118,\;
 3.48481964025734707306997541176470588235294118]
\]

at the same \(\kappa\). This comparison is a price-only allowance for one
target. It is **not** a physical construction, a global simultaneous funding
bound, supplier admission, or a \(\kappa\) gain. The 1,066 individual prices
cannot be summed: their relation spaces and cheapest target debits may overlap.

## Source and scope

The bundled word is the exact unselected #7481 source, SHA256
`acee323696b18914bd863d55341f92112b48d3d1dc1443c276efb6c396fcc0a9`; its
canonical decompressed payload SHA256 is
`64fca675b91298868e12f09423b47e0c2327cba7095e35c8919c1953b4ed68b8`. The
bundled signed caller has SHA256
`255d89f38cd45e38e5770f559bc43dc217e989032359768cdc7e8cdfd64b47bc` and 17
native calls. The exact invoice has SHA256
`86f71ccbc923db5d7a6753aa971c3a9c8a572680534498ed17be072cfa2aae6b`.
Source paths, Git blobs, predecessor notices, and base provenance are recorded
in [SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json).

The first pass covers all 6,279 MOVE events, with 468 helper-only targets
reported separately. Among the nonzero-main targets, 1,080 hit the strict
earlier main-projection history (14 owned, 1,066 public) and 4,731 miss it
(33 owned, 4,698 public). A helper-only relation cannot repair a missing main
projection, so the 4,698 public misses are outside this mechanism. Every one
of the 1,066 public main-history hits is a full-history miss in the paired
source replay.

Rows at equal frame spans are pooled as normalized-history keys. This grants
more prior information when separate physical charts have equal spans; it
does not assert equal operator or phase semantics. Candidate credits remain
rank-price relaxations under the fixed source history. Changed producers,
cross-chart imports, and simultaneous relations are excluded.

## Reproduce

From the repository root, run both registered commands in
[VERIFY.json](VERIFY.json). They use Python's standard library, run in
isolated and optimized modes, and write no files. `verify.py` prints a compact
receipt, including the canonical SHA256 of the complete ordered 1,066-record
census:

`4db450ed8d88b2617f88b80609af56d4b255682a07420970973e6a8646d97844`.

The receipt also reports all 16 direct full-L checks and executed failure
controls. A complete per-target record log is deliberately not bundled; the
source-only replay regenerates it in memory and checks the complete census
hash.
