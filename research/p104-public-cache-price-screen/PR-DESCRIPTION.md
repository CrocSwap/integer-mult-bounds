opportunities unresolved. This package supplies a complete source-bound
per-target screen to identify the remaining price allowances and distinguish
them from physically realizable savings. It adds no compiler operation or
changed native word.

## Scope and lineage

prices or prove joint funding, physical implementation, source admission, a
new \(\kappa\), or a multiplication theorem. Same-span pooling is an
information overgrant, not physical operator equivalence. The conditional
A54 initial-access preparation check is separate and is not evidence in this
