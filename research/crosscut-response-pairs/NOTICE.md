# Attribution and scope

This package adds earlier-cut response-pair discovery, chronological donor
sharing, connected-block frame retiming, a source-bound composition and
independent audit. The connected-block search is new in this run; its exact
integer operand-span audit adapts PR263's retained method. Prepared for Dylan
Crow with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. No human peer-review claim is
made. Apache-2.0; all inherited licenses and attribution remain in place.

Dugongue (PR259/254) supplied the multi-cut kernel construction, native checkers
and exact bank machinery. Rohan Arun (PR263/265) supplied the retained 25-gate
descent, deterministic transcript ordering and fixed-prime composition.
sennemmi (PR261/230), Gabriele Nespoli (PR235), eumemic (PR249/244/210), hcg890
(PR234), Jacob Sussman, Chafik Boukhalfa (PR200), Avi Eisenberg (PR193), Evan
McKinney (PR197), and the complete inherited source/compiler lineage retain
their credits. The original NOTICE files, source archives and hashes are
unchanged. See the parent package notices and repository NOTICE for full lineage.

The majority of the new entrances come from broader early-cut pair discovery;
the gain must not be attributed exclusively to cross-cut donor sharing. The
retained all-size compiler, routing, row, precision/recovery, prime and analytic
interfaces remain assumptions. This package is not a practical multiplication
benchmark or a formal proof of the complete multiplication theorem.

At the initial overlap audit, Chafik Boukhalfa's PR266 had independently published
333 additional early-cut pairs (head a2b02af); it later grew to 365 families
(head b2b2a33). Dugongue's PR267 had published chronological
donor sharing. They overlap this run's search directions and deserve explicit
credit. This package was constructed from the pinned PR265 sources, not by
importing either newer witness; it claims the concrete 887-entrance composition
and connected-block retiming, not priority for early-cut pairs or donor sharing.
An exact comparison found 316 identical added pair witnesses in common with
the earlier PR266 head a2b02af. Its authors' public contribution is explicitly acknowledged.
