# A coordinate budget strengthens the P112 root-simplex obstruction

## Statement

Let K be a characteristic-zero field. Use the 112 live E8 root lines and
their binary labels ell(t) from the pinned P112 input. Put

```
C = {c_h : c_h(t) = <r_t,h>, h in K^8},
w(q) = rank_F2 {ell(t) : q(t) != 0}.
```

The root representatives r_t in the replay are doubled roots. Scaling the
representatives does not change supports. Ten linearly independent pieces
spanning T with dim(T intersect C) >= 5 cannot have weights bounded by any
of these five multisets:

```
2,2,4,5,6,6,6,6,6,6
2,2,5,5,5,6,6,6,6,6
2,3,4,4,6,6,6,6,6,6
2,3,4,5,5,6,6,6,6,6
2,4,4,4,5,6,6,6,6,6.
```

All five are actual survivors of the pinned TWOSIDE `(b,t)=(5,5)` BK/BK7
weight filters. This is a theorem about these explicit piece hypotheses.
Its application to the decoder reduction inherits that reduction's other
conditions. It does not settle C5, the decoder floor or a supplier.

## Geometry and finite dependencies

We use the following P112 facts, replayed rather than assumed from a receipt:

1. Supports of weight d=2,3,4,5,6 have cardinality at most 2,4,8,12,20.
2. Every nonzero element of C has support at least 53.
3. A nonzero element of C with support below 70 is a root direction.
4. The evaluation map h -> c_h is injective.

The first three dimensions follow from the labels' leading-one coordinate:
a binary d-flat meets that affine hyperplane in at most 2^(d-1) points.
For dimensions five and six, `geometry.py` enumerates every annihilator in
unique reverse row-echelon form: 3,309,747 and 788,035 subspaces. Their
kernel intersections with the 112 distinct labels have maxima 12 and 20.
A support of smaller rank extends to a flat of the stated dimension.

For facts 2 and 3, use the standard Weyl-chamber theorem for the real E8
root system. A real direction with support below 70 is perpendicular to at
least 43 live root lines. In a closed chamber the perpendicular root system
is generated by the simple roots that vanish on the direction. The replay
checks that all six-node parabolics have at most 36 root lines. The eight
seven-node parabolics have counts `42,28,22,14,16,23,37,63`; only the last
can contain 43 perpendicular lines, and its normal is a root direction.
Root directions have live supports 53 or 56, so the same argument also
rules out support below 53. The replay checks all 960 simple reflections,
simple-system positivity, parabolic counts and root supports. The chamber
theorem itself is a mathematical dependency, not a program-proved result.

These facts extend to K. Rational roots perpendicular to a nonzero
K-valued direction span a rational subspace of dimension at most seven.
If this dimension were at most six, choose a generic real normal to that
span. Its perpendicular roots are exactly the roots in that span; the
six-node parabolic bound allows at most 36 lines, contradicting 43.
Consequently, at support below 70 the perpendicular span has dimension
seven. Its rational kernel is one-dimensional, and the K-valued direction
is proportional to its rational generator. Apply the real argument to
that generator. Finally, the live-root Gram sum is 112 times the identity
in doubled coordinates, proving injectivity over K.

The geometry code is unchanged from Nick Petalas's PR3164. Its exact
source pin and the preceding E8/P112 lineage are in SOURCES.json.

## Four small pieces leave a root A5 simplex

Choose the four smallest pieces s_1,...,s_4. Write S for the union of their
supports and s=|S|. The remaining pieces are q_0,...,q_5; put
U=sum_i |supp(q_i)|. For every proposed shape, the support-cap sum for S
plus any one q_i is at most 48, strictly below 53.

Let D=T intersect C and lambda:T -> T/D. Since dim T=10 and dim D>=5,
the quotient has dimension at most five. The four small images together
with each q_i image are independent. A dependence would give a nonzero
codeword supported on fewer than 53 coordinates, contrary to fact 2.
Thus dim(T/D)=5, dim D=5, and the four small images are independent.

Modulo their span, the six q_i images are nonzero in one dimension.
Rescale each q_i to give it the same image there. The supports do not change.
For i=1,...,5 there is a unique small-piece combination a_i with

```
c_i = q_i - q_0 - a_i in D.
```

Set c_0=a_0=0. The five c_i are independent, since their coefficients on
q_1,...,q_5 in the original piece basis are distinct unit vectors. Every
edge c_i-c_j is nonzero and supported on S union supp(q_i) union supp(q_j).
The largest edge-support cap in the five applications is 68, below 70.
By facts 3 and 4, c_i=c_{h_i} for five independent directions, and every
difference among 0,h_1,...,h_5 is a scalar root direction.

Here is the root-simplex argument from PR3164 with its hypotheses retained.
Normalize roots to norm squared two. In the plane of two independent
rational E8 roots, a third root line exists only in an A2 subsystem, with
coefficient ratio +1 or -1. The replay verifies this for all 7,140 root-line
pairs and all 120 possible third roots. The same statement holds over K:
a rational vector in the K-span of two rational independent vectors is
already in their rational span, by an invertible rational coordinate minor.

Write h_i=b_i alpha_i for roots alpha_i. Every h_i-h_j is a third root line,
so b_i/b_j is +1 or -1. Absorb these signs to write h_i=b alpha_i with one
common b!=0. Then <alpha_i,alpha_j>=1 and alpha_i-alpha_j is a root. Thus
0,alpha_1,...,alpha_5 form a root A5 simplex.

Each live coordinate r evaluates on its vertices in six integer levels,
all pairwise differences in {-2,-1,0,1,2}. If a difference has absolute
value two, equality in Cauchy-Schwarz makes r the corresponding edge root.
The value multiplicities are 1,1,4, with nine unequal pairs. Otherwise the
levels occupy at most two consecutive integers, with at most 3*3=9 unequal
pairs. Therefore each coordinate lies in at most nine of the fifteen edge
supports. The common nonzero field scalar changes no zeros. This is a
universal proof; the replay's sharp A5 fixture is a calibration only.

## The new coordinate-budget inequality

At a coordinate outside S, the edge values are exactly q_i(t)-q_j(t).
Suppose k of the six endpoint values are nonzero. There are at most

```
k(6-k) + k(k-1)/2 = k(11-k)/2
```

unequal pairs. For every integer k=0,...,6, this is at most 4k+1, since
(k-1)(k-2)>=0. It is also at most 5k. This statement works for arbitrary
field-valued endpoints and needs no root-system assumption.

There are exactly 112-s coordinates outside S. Summing the new bound gives

```
outside edge incidences <= 4 U_outside + 112-s <= 4U + 112-s.
```

Using the nine-incidence bound inside S, and the distance 53 on all fifteen
edges, yields

```
795 = 15*53 <= 9s + 4U + 112-s = 112 + 8s + 4U.       (*)
```

The original endpoint count gives the additional bound `795 <= 9s+5U`.
Both right sides increase with s and U, so replacing them with their
support-cap sums is valid even when small supports overlap, when some
q_i support lies in S, or when the actual supports are smaller.

| Ten-piece weights | Four small weights | s cap | U cap | New upper | Margin | Old margin |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `2,2,4,5,6^6` | `2,2,4,5` | 24 | 120 | 784 | 11 | -21 |
| `2,2,5^3,6^5` | `2,2,5,5` | 28 | 112 | 784 | 11 | -17 |
| `2,3,4^2,6^6` | `2,3,4,4` | 22 | 120 | 768 | 27 | -3 |
| `2,3,4,5^2,6^5` | `2,3,4,5` | 26 | 112 | 768 | 27 | 1 |
| `2,4^3,5,6^5` | `2,4,4,4` | 26 | 112 | 768 | 27 | 1 |

Each row contradicts (*). The first three exclusions specifically require
the stronger coordinate budget; the original count cannot establish them.

## Bind the applications and distinguish earlier coverage

`beta.py` reproduces all 38 original `(5,5)` BK/BK7 survivors over all
weight multisets of lengths five through ten with weights two through seven.
The filters follow the two pinned TWOSIDE implementations. Their full
decoder-level derivation and generalized distances are inherited; the five
new exclusions themselves only need the explicit hypotheses stated above.
Replacing weights zero or one by two is conservative for support bounds.

For every proposed shape, the replay examines every support-certified
five-piece quotient basis and every weight-certified cover by two six-flats
plus individual deleted supports. It checks the earlier eight-cut span-three,
ten-cut span-four and twelve-cut rank-four-anchor theorems. A nonbasis
weight-seven piece is conservatively charged one extra independent direction.
Minimum cut allowances in the five rows are `12,16,12,14,16`, all above ten.
The first three rows admit no weight-four anchor in a support-certified
quotient cover. The last two need at least eighteen cuts with such an anchor.

This is an audit of those weight-certified rules. It does not exclude better
covers exploiting overlaps in actual supports, or prove that every other
research route was tried. A separate subset-cover oracle corroborates the
enumeration without its ternary-assignment implementation.

The superficially similar shape `3,3,3,4,6^6` is deliberately excluded from
the new-result count. PR3167 already excludes it: use quotient basis
`3,3,3,6,6`, support cap 52, and twelve common extra lines. The nonbasis
weight-four piece supplies the rank-four anchor. This overlap control is
checked on every replay. A nine-piece PR3164 exclusion does not directly
cover a ten-piece configuration: deleting one piece only guarantees an
intersection of dimension four, rather than the required five.

## Verification limits

The proof uses the standard Weyl-chamber theorem and exact finite label/root
facts. It is not proof-kernel verified. Internal mathematical review and
separate enumeration methods are not independent human review. Other beta
shapes, full C5, the C1-C7 reduction and supplier admission remain separate
obligations. The selected exponent is unchanged. No full supplier replay,
Lean build or historical long suite is required or claimed for this result.
