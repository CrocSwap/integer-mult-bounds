# Exterior rank and exact pairing for a fixed-paid 4+5 split

A cut t is the state immediately before native event t. For each strict
intermediate chart with dimension four or five, the census records the
initial cut and the cut after each event incident to that chart if a
public endpoint-cohort role is present. An event disjoint from the chart
cannot change the public bank, a current public bank role's normalized
prefix row, or its normalized suffix impulse column. A fixed-paid split
whose local basis uses only that bank has the same support equations
through such an interval. The receipt keys consist of the cut and chart.
Multiple eligible pivots in one bank share its exterior rank equations.

Let P be the normalized literal prefix to the bank and S the normalized
literal suffix from it. A split needs a local functional a and direction
b with `c = aP` and `d = Sb` supported on the retained endpoint cohort.
It also needs `ab = cd = 1`. For a public bank of size m, restrict the m
prefix rows to the exterior boundary coordinates. A nonzero m×m minor
proves the left kernel is zero. All literal scalar denominators are
units in the verifier's finite field. A modular nonzero minor therefore
certifies a rational nonzero minor. Modular rank drops may reflect
reduction, so they only select cases for exact work.

The first engine propagates sparse normalized rows through the literal
word and eliminates bank exterior rows using prime 65521. The second
propagates exterior-only rows, uses a different elimination pivot order
with prime 65537, and authenticates all 98 literal denominator values.
Both recover the exact public, owned and eligible role sets. Both replay
the original 24 selected banks as controls and agree on every exact bank
key and exterior rank. The independent engine also checks the signed
MOVE stock and fresh owned COPY behavior. The original selected 24 have
a separate rational replay and a transposed modular minor replay.

The six exact continuations use two independent chronological methods.
One propagates prefix rows forward and suffix columns backward. The
other propagates prefix covectors backward and suffix impulses forward.
Rational elimination gives bases for the pure prefix and suffix
spaces. Four keys have zero pure suffix dimension. For the other two,
let B represent the bilinear form cd on the pure bases and D represent
ab−cd. A pairing exists precisely when some x,y satisfy xBy≠0 and
xDy=0. For fixed x, this is equivalent to xB lying outside the span of
xD. The two engines check the wedge coefficients exactly. If all wedge
coefficients vanish, a solution requires a vector in the kernel of D
with nonzero B image. The independent check uses the transposed pairing
test and exact kernel. Both exclude the two remaining keys.

The structural census exhausted 4,207 representatives. The first

## Reverse suffix continuation

For each of the 2,128 remaining frozen banks, restrict the normalized
suffix matrix to public output coordinates outside the endpoint cohort.
A bank perturbation is pure only when it is in the kernel of this
exterior map. Both reverse engines propagate suffix influence from the
literal final boundary, where every exterior public output has its
coordinate indicator. In an ADD, the destination's suffix vector
contributes to the source with the literal coefficient. Fresh COPY
also drops the destination variable. SCALE rescales and INIT/CLEAR
erase the corresponding dependency. The exact signed MOVE changes the
chart and preserves the normalized scalar action.

The primary engine uses prime 65521 and row pivots. The independent
engine reconstructs live banks backwards and eliminates columns over
prime 65537. At full rank, the reported row and column order gives a
nonzero square minor of the original bank exterior matrix. The reverse
replay checks all 98 scalar denominator values are units modulo the
prime. Both fields give 663 rational exclusions and agree on the ranks
and banks of all 2,128 keys. The six earlier exact banks give four
full exterior suffix ranks and two drops as controls.

The 1,465 suffix drops enter a finite-field pairing prioritization
under a separate 30-second cap. It returns zero modular pairs. A

stage supplies no rational exclusion. The frozen fallback takes six

the unit pairing. Their prefix/suffix nullities are (1,2) at
(cut 36807, chart 288) and (1,1) at the other five keys. No materialized
basis, native candidate or changed caller replay was required.
