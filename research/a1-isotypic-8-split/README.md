# The 8-dimensional isotypic summand splits under A_1

Prepared by unpaidmoon with substantial Cursor Grok assistance. Apache-2.0.

For the historical `c=1` centre block, the multiplicity-8 isotypic component of
the dihedral action generated by the signed permutations `r` and `u` is an
8-dimensional `A_1`-invariant subspace of the trivial-character `(+1,+1)`
joint space. It is reducible. An explicit basis and the rational canonical
decomposition are in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md).

No new kappa. The selected conditional kappa stays `781451/250000000`.

## Result

Let `W` be the image of the projector for the linear character
`ψ(r^k) = (-1)^k` and `ψ(r^k u) = (-1)^{k+1}`. Then `dim W = 8`, and

```text
chi_{A_1,W}(x) = x^2 (x-2) (x+2) (x^2-2x-4) (x^2+2x-4).
```

As a `Q[A_1]`-module,

```text
W ≅ Q[x]/(x^2+2x-4) ⊕ Q[x]/(x^2-2x-4)
    ⊕ Q[x]/(x) ⊕ Q[x]/(x) ⊕ Q[x]/(x-2) ⊕ Q[x]/(x+2).
```

The two quadratic summands are irreducible. The two copies of `Q[x]/(x)` are
lines inside a plane on which `A_1` acts by zero. One explicit proper
invariant subspace is `span{w4 - w6}` in the basis of [PROOF.md](PROOF.md).

The character inner product only gave the dimension. This package builds the
projector image and the matrix of `B = 4 A_1` on it. A missing further
involution is not the argument.
