**A new coupled native interface; no higher kappa or independent-center credit.**
This changes Cao Yifan's PR4610 round-three word at its eight owned center
lifetimes. Replace each cleared/copied temporary by a distinct arbitrary input,
replace its copy by an addition, and retain its output. The 632 existing
inputs and the eight added inputs are independent. No field is erased or
overwritten. The exact recursive paid profile stays

```

```

Both the new forward word and its **actual raw inverse transpose** have
exact 640-column replay. The dual is constructed gate by gate in dual charts;
it is not the predecessor's backward word with a changed label.

After stripping the actual endpoint charts, the new map is

```
M = [[A, B], [R, I_8]],     A(X,Y,H) = (X,Y+X,H).
```

The recorded B and R both have rational rank eight, RB=0, and B is supported
only on the 112 Y outputs. R reads X and original helper inputs. If K=M-I,
then K^2 is nonzero and K^3=0. Its inverse is I-K+K^2. The explicit sparse
matrices are in ABI.json; RESULT.json is a regenerated receipt.

A second exact kernel accepts eight caller-supplied empty-chart fields and
removes all eight center transforms. Its complete operator is
`[[A-BR,B],[0,I_8]]`; the old native output is recovered exactly when the
caller supplies `c=Rx`. [CACHE.md](CACHE.md) specifies that contract, its raw
dual, the 106 cold helper contributions, and the full direct caller bill.
Direct acquisition loses at least 48 units of recursive moment. An exact p16
scalar-span recycling test also finds no free reconstruction at its 18 full
stages. These are scoped failed consumers, not admission or a universal
cache obstruction.

This is a constructive escape from **the pair-shear interface**, not a proof
that it escapes any complete controller or price obstruction. The added
centers are coupled outputs. Each one has 100--104 old-input terms, while
every old Y output receives incoming-center noise. Counting eight independent
rank-eight productive jobs now would be false.
