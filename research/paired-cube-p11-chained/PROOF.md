## 2. Invariant Telescoping Deficit

In any 3-stage completed-core cover, the deficit per vertex is defined by:
$$\text{deficit} = W \cdot m - \text{mass} = 2v - 3\ell$$

For $p = 11$:
$$\text{deficit}_C = 2(1320) - 3(440) = 1,320$$
For $h = 21$:
$$\text{deficit}_B = 2(1330) - 3(420) = 1,400$$

### Lemma (Step-Sum Equivalence)
*In the complex word DAG generated by `Graph(11).finish(...)`, every non-source physical role $s$ created at a multi-fanout copy operation has lifetime frame transitions whose step ranks sum to identically $h = 22$:*
$$\sum_{i} \Delta \operatorname{rank}_i(s) = h = 22$$

*Proof.* A non-source role $s$ is created with initial annihilator frame `full` (dimension $h$). Each subsequent operation $x$ touching $s$ refines its annihilator to $\operatorname{ann}[x]$ with step cost $\Delta = \operatorname{len}(\operatorname{prev}) - \operatorname{len}(\operatorname{ann}[x])$. At the termination of $s$, its residual frame has dimension $\operatorname{len}(\operatorname{roleann}[s])$. The sum of all step costs across the chain telescopes:
$$(h - r_1) + (r_1 - r_2) + \dots + (r_{k-1} - r_k) + r_k = h = 22$$
Each role contributes $3h = m = 66$ to the total child rank mass.

### Corollary (Deficit Invariance under Reuse)
*Recycling or eliminating $K$ dead non-source roles decreases $W$ by $K$ and decreases total rank mass by $K \cdot m$. Consequently:*
$$W_{\text{new}} \cdot m - \text{mass}_{\text{new}} = (W \cdot m - K \cdot m) - (\text{mass} - K \cdot m) = 2v - 3\ell = \text{deficit}$$
*The deficit is identically preserved.*
