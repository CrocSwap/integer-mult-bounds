For every commutative field $K$, with commuting indeterminates $x,y$, the
inherited statement is

$$
C=\begin{pmatrix}1+xy&x^2\\-y^2&1-xy\end{pmatrix}
\in \operatorname{SL}_2(K[x,y])\setminus E_2(K[x,y]).
$$

Here $E_2(R)$ is generated by $I+f e_{12}$ and $I+f e_{21}$ for $f\in R$.
Cohn identifies this group in §2, printed p. 7. His example states the
stronger exclusion from $\operatorname{GE}_2$, whose elementary generators
also include invertible diagonal matrices. The example uses total degree
on $K[x,y]$. Neither its field hypothesis nor Proposition 7.3 excludes
characteristic 2. Thus characteristic 2 is included in the general
statement, although Cohn gives no separate characteristic-2 corollary.
