# Faster exact preparation for observable connector repairs

Scoped algorithmic proof, exact finite conformance and local performance evidence.
The complete correction-mask planner from [Hanson Sun / PR1523](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/1523)
can replace its all-pairs transition scan with a weighted hypercube distance
transform. Path ranks and backpointers preserve every returned Bellman table,
output correction, terminal restoration and lexicographically chosen witness.

For m observation stages and N=2^d correction masks, the new algorithm uses
O((m+1)dN) arithmetic operations and label comparisons, with O((m+1)N) storage.
This replaces the dense O(mN^2) predecessor-candidate scan. The implementation
is deliberately bounded to 1<=d<=8, m<=256 and positive prices of at most
256 bits. The written algorithmic proof applies to every finite d>=1 and m>=0.
[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) states the invariants and cost model.

On the declared eight-coordinate, 64-stage changing-source preparation workload,
five alternating fresh-process pairs reduce median complete wall time from
**0.361382 s to 0.036996 s**, an **89.76% reduction**. The ranges are
0.360885--0.376054 s and 0.035862--0.038021 s. Both sides return the identical
58-price plan, all 16,640 table entries and the complete 616-gate program.
The timed region includes source authentication, demand construction, planning,
word emission, every observation check, the full inverse, invoice and output
digest. This is preparation performance on one explicit workload, not an online
supplier or multiplication speedup. [BENCHMARK.json](BENCHMARK.json) contains
the generated, source-bound measurements. [PROFILE.txt](PROFILE.txt) identifies
the baseline all-pairs scan, which occupies 99.48% of profiled preparation.
Other processes were active on the 18-logical-CPU machine; the alternating
runs have disjoint before/after ranges.

## Evidence and reproduction

[RESULT.json](RESULT.json) is generated by the verifier and bound to executable
file hashes. It records:

- 12,288 exhaustive two-coordinate transforms, checking all 49,152 output
  labels against direct minimization, including unreachable states and ties.
- Exact full-return agreement for all 6,226 successful planner calls reached
  by the unchanged PR1523 compatibility consumer, plus its three planner
  rejections. Its complete certificate and fourteen controls remain identical.
  These are compatibility checks for this implementation, not a new audit of
  PR1523's mathematical result.
- Twelve complete preparation comparisons through width eight and 64 stages,
  in connector-only and paid-output modes, with restored changing sources,
  arbitrary dirty inputs, all observable cuts and the entire reversed word.
- Eleven bounded-domain rejections, five additional semantic/integrity controls,
  and enumerated transition inventories. The benchmark workload needs
  4,129,024 dense predecessor candidates versus 131,072 directed edge slots in
  the new transform. Sorting, filtering and emission are additional work,
  included in the complexity proof and complete measurements.

From the repository root, with the standard library only:

```sh

/Users/nick/git/integer-mult-bounds/.venv/bin/python3.12 -I -W error -B research/observable-hamming-transform/verify.py

/Users/nick/git/integer-mult-bounds/.venv/bin/python3.12 -B scripts/check_research_commit.py --package research/observable-hamming-transform --check-source-history
```

Other machines can substitute their CPython 3.12 path. The expected scientific
marker is `PASS_OBSERVABLE_HAMMING_TRANSFORM`. Normal replay reads the committed
result and never rewrites it. `verify.py --emit` prints a new generated result;
`benchmark.py` prints five new alternating measurement pairs. Store authoring
outputs outside the checkout. Runtime thresholds are deliberately absent from CI.
