Exact operator convention

For q in F2^m, let X_q be translation by q and
  C_q = ((1+i) I + (1-i) X_q)/2.
Then C_q^2=X_q and C_q fixes the uniform vector. Write C_all for the product
over the coordinate vectors. An invertible binary matrix G acts by the
literal address permutation P_G. All products of C_q are commuting exact
Gaussian-dyadic operators. Their multiplier on Fourier character y is
i^(sum epsilon_q * parity(q.y)). Modulo 4, parity expands as its linear sum
minus twice its pair sum, so this multiplier is an exact Z4 quadratic form.
Equality of these forms proves equality including the scalar phase.

Its preimage Lagrangian is {(u,u+w): u in U, w in U^perp}. Thus a nested
transition has Fourier rank equal to the dimension difference. The supplied
