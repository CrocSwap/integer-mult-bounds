# Algebraic mechanisms for the native Gaussian controller

Prepared for Dugongue with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. This review reads 17 primary papers at explicitly recorded depths, applies their ideas in exact constructions, and tests the constructions against the current operator model. It supplies useful controller primitives and a sharper precision comparison. It does not exhibit five independent rank-three Gaussian jobs in fourteen paid calls, or establish a new multiplication kappa.

## The exact problem and evidence boundary

The frozen complex prefix uses C_j = ((1+i)/2)I + ((1-i)/2)X_j on C^8. At positive width f, G_f means the tensor power of each individual base letter G. The existing free grammar applies its same monomial letter to every digit; a paid call is one C_j,f. Arbitrary independent input banks must remain independent after decoding, all absolute phases and entrance/output charts must be correct, and every extra owner and native array pass is charged.

Write A_f=C_0,f, P=SUS, Q=SVS, and L_f=P_f+2Q_f, with U=CNOT(0 to 2), V=SWAP(0,1), and S e_x=i^popcount(x)e_x. The actual arbitrary-target two-call block is

    Port_f = [[A_f, 0], [A_f L_f A_f, A_f]].

Its inverse is [[A_f inverse,0],[-L_f,A_f inverse]]. The additional third call has the actual residual M0_f C_2,f + 2 K_f a_f. This review distinguishes the inherited residual monomial M0 from the new Hecke mixer M below. The sum P_f+2Q_f is formed after tensorizing P and Q separately.

## Applied result 1: an all-input Hecke coupling with a short inverse

Let h=SWAP(0,2) and g=SWAP(0,1). They generate a literal S_3 subgroup of the free address maps. At every f set

    e_f=(I+h_f)/2,
    T_f=e_f g_f e_f,
    M_f=(I-e_f)+T_f.

**Theorem.** At every positive width, M_f is an invertible free linear combination on the entire input space, with

    T_f^2=(e_f+T_f)/2,
    M_f^2=(M_f+I)/2,
    M_f inverse=2M_f-I=I-2e_f+2T_f.

Moreover ||M_f||_2 <= 1 and ||M_f inverse||_2 <= 2. Every input sector is retained.

**Proof.** In the six-element group algebra Q[S_3], e^2=e and T=e g e satisfies T^2=(e+T)/2 by exact coefficient collection. This is a literal group identity, so applying the representation generated by the individually tensorized coordinate swaps proves it at every f. Since eT=Te=T, multiplication proves the two displayed identities for M and its inverse. The swaps are real unitary involutions, so e and T are self-adjoint. On the e subspace, the polynomial for T has roots 1 and -1/2; on its orthogonal complement M is the identity. Therefore M has eigenvalues in {1,-1/2}, giving the norm bounds and an actual full-input inverse. Replacing M by T alone would discard the complementary space and would invalidate the full-input claim.

This is a concrete transfer-module use of the Hecke viewpoint, independently proved rather than inferred from an unread classical theorem. Mackey's [1949 paper](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.35.9.537) was accessible only at its introductory excerpt; its general theory supplies context, not proof evidence for this identity. [Cohn--Umans, Theorem 2.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0307321) and [Cohn--Kleinberg--Szegedy--Umans, Section 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0511460) motivate checking literal coefficients and cross-input collisions when several jobs are packed into a representation.

**Native implementation.** For N=8^f, these global coordinate permutations have orbits of size at most six. Enumerate addresses; process an orbit only at its least address; load every distinct orbit entry before storing any result. Add the coefficients of repeated images. The six permutations are computed with three periodic bit-lane masks and a fixed number of shifts and Boolean operations; the masks initialize in O(f). This implements M and its inverse in O(N) ordinary word operations with constant-entry temporary storage. Rational coefficients have denominator at most 4. No array is a unit-cost word and no Gaussian call or whole-array hidden copy is used.

**Actual hook comparison.** The unperturbed replacement L'=S_f M_f S_f has prefix rank 144 and target joint rank 224 in all eight actual width-five hook cuts. It is well-conditioned but not saturated. The explicit dyadic perturbation L'=S_f(M_f+U_f/16)S_f instead has rank 160 and joint rank 224 in all eight cuts. Here U_f is the same CNOT monomial used to define P. The norm bounds are at most 17/16 and inverse norm at most 16/7, by a singular-value perturbation estimate. This is an aligned, saturated width-five candidate coupling, not an all-width completed supplier. Its dirty-target block inverse still uses only -L' and the paid A_f inverses; no unexhibited short formula for (L') inverse is required by that schedule.

## Applied result 2: a matrix-valued native encoding that scalar search misses

The constructive rank-witness viewpoint comes from [Garg--Gurvits--Oliveira--Wigderson](https://arxiv.org/html/1511.03730), [Ivanyos--Qiao--Subrahmanyam](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1512.03531), and [Derksen--Makam's skew example and blow-up analysis](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1606.06701). Their results concern free skew fields and matrix spaces. They do not automatically impose the actual Clifford-group relations.

Let A and B be the sign and swap on **one designated native address bit**, acting as the identity on every other bit. Then A^2=B^2=I and AB=-BA at every array width. These are O(N) native sign/permutation passes. This grammar is broader than applying an identical single base letter to every digit.

**Theorem.** On three arbitrary independent banks, the free native map

    r=y+A z,
    s=-x+B z,
    t=-A x-B y

is invertible for every positive width. An explicit inverse is

    z=(AB t+B s+A r)/2,
    x=(-A t-s+BA r)/2,
    y=(r-AB s-B t)/2.

The forward coordinate norm is at most 2, the inverse coordinate norm is at most 3/2, the Euclidean forward norm is at most 2, and the Euclidean inverse norm is at most 1. In particular its Euclidean condition number is at most 2.

**Proof.** Eliminating x and y from the first two equations gives

    t-A s+B r=(BA-AB)z=2BA z.

Since AB is the inverse of BA, this gives z and then x and y. Expanding the formulas gives the stated three-term inverse and coordinate bound. If F is the three-by-three block matrix, then F is skew-Hermitian. Direct block multiplication using A^2=B^2=I and AB=-BA yields (F^2+I)(F^2+4I)=0. Thus -F^2 is positive definite with spectrum contained in {1,4}, proving the Euclidean norm and inverse bounds.

For a literal in-place implementation, load the six complex entries in an address pair from all three banks before any store. With bit value 0 or 1 the inverse becomes

    z0=(t1+s1+r0)/2,  z1=(-t0+s0-r1)/2,
    x0=z1-s0, x1=z0-s1,
    y0=r0-z0, y1=r1+z1.

A constant number of ADD/SUB and dyadic shift operations per pair suffices; real and imaginary components are treated separately. No output field is secretly assumed zero. Exact encoded grid data make the indicated halving exact. For arbitrary perturbed data, three-term half-sums have coordinate-error amplification at most 3/2, with any final rounding charged separately. The operation uses constant temporary words and O(N) native work, with no paid Gaussian call.

Every scalar specialization of the skew three-by-three pattern is singular, while this matrix-valued pattern is invertible. That demonstrates a real expressive difference for controller discovery. It does not itself compute K_f on any additional independent bank. In the narrower tensor-letter grammar A=Z_0 raised to tensor f and B=X_0 raised to tensor f, their commutation sign is (-1)^f. The construction then works for odd f and has an N-dimensional kernel at even f. test.py verifies both the anchored all-width repair and this phase regression. No narrow-engine supplier is admitted by silently substituting the anchored grammar.

For a larger search, [Derksen--Makam's determinant blow-up bound](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1512.03393) and [acyclic-quiver semi-invariants](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1603.00417) give bounded algebraic coordinates and witness sizes for a fixed finite module. An actual candidate must export the encoding matrices and inverse, not just an operator-scaling success flag. Unknown controller products, actual group relations and the native cost model remain explicit constraints.

## Applied result 3: a better-conditioned source pencil, with the decoder tradeoff paid

Put L_t=P_f+tQ_f, with t=1/16. Since R_f=P_f inverse Q_f has actual order four,

    L_t inverse=(I-t R_f+t^2 R_f^2-t^3 R_f^3) P_f inverse/(1-t^4).

Multiplication by P_f(I+tR_f) proves this formula at every width; sums are formed after tensorization. The singular values of L_t lie between 15/16 and 17/16, giving condition at most 17/15. This improves the prefix coupling's condition bound 3 at t=2. The two-call arbitrary-target block remains explicitly invertible without executing the optional odd-denominator coupling inverse.

The exact source residual becomes

    J_f W_f F_t,f = M0_f C_2,f + t K_f a_f.

Therefore the swap-sector decoder subtracts the same residual and divides by t. Changing t from 2 to 1/16 amplifies residual-path errors by an additional factor 32, requiring five extra bits for the same error on that path. Better prefix conditioning does not establish a better complete precision bill or a better kappa. If the optional inverse is executed, its fixed odd denominator and rounding must also be charged.

The optional hook checker tests t times each of 1,-1,i,-i. A successful four-point table, together with the predecessor's termwise target alignment, supports the same inherited extension to higher digit widths: diagonalize the order-four relative branch operator on the extra tensor factors. Each eigenspace sees the width-five pencil with the corresponding fourth-root coefficient. This is a refined parameter choice in the predecessor family, not a new mechanism or a finished five-in-fourteen continuation. The four-point conclusions are claimed only when the accompanying completed HOOK-EXPECTED.json records all four cases.

## Reading map and what each theory actually transfers

SOURCES.json gives all 17 primary URLs, dates, precise locators, models and reading depths; SEARCH.json records 19 queries and followed citation chains. Fourteen full-text entries were inspected at least at abstract/selected-statement depth. The Mackey item was introduction-excerpt only, and the two 1980 items were publisher-abstract only. No paywalled proof is claimed read and no copyrighted PDF is redistributed.

- [Bini 1980](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF02575865) distinguishes approximation degree from exact cost: its abstract states a factor 1+d in exactification. [Bini--Lotti--Romani 1980](https://doi.org/10.1137/0209053) explicitly incorporates numerical stability in approximate bilinear complexity. Neither paper gives this project a free border-to-exact compiler.
- [Demmel--Dumitriu--Holtz--Kleinberg, Theorems 3.1/3.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0603207) analyze fixed recursive bilinear families and polynomial normwise error growth. [Ballard et al., Theorem 3 and Proposition 9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.00687) separate coefficient growth from accumulation and input scaling. This motivates tracking actual coefficient heights and error propagation alongside ranks. An exponent infimum is not an explicit chosen multiplication circuit.
- [Dehaene--De Moor, Theorems 1-3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0304125) supply binary symplectic and quadratic phase formulas for Clifford conjugation; [Aaronson--Gottesman, Lemma 5 and Theorem 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0406196) supply tableau and canonical-form tools. Conjugation still identifies scalar multiples of the same operator. Our linear-array ABI additionally needs an absolute central scalar. test.py checks that I and -I have the same conjugation on Pauli generators while differing as actual tensor operators.
- [Grochow--Qiao, Theorems A/B](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.00309) and [Futorny--Grochow--Sergeichuk, Theorem 1.1 and Section 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1810.09219) encode linked role/basis constraints into tensor isomorphism problems. They suggest role-locking discovery coordinates. Their decision reductions do not preserve our exact Gaussian invoice or prove fast transform evaluation.
- [Landsberg--Manivel, Section 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0107032) describe D4 triality and exceptional Lie-algebra constructions. This is distinct from generic symplectic tensor padding. A useful triality route would need a literal map to our operators, actual scalar phases and native cost; no such speedup is asserted here.

## Exact quantitative admission and precision contract

For the hypothetical completed five-in-fourteen complex supplier, its productive moment is 5 times 3^(1-q) and its paid moment is 14. Integer arithmetic proves

    5^16 * 3^15 > 14^16,
    5^15 * 3^14 < 14^15.

Thus its strict moment root lies between 1/16 and 1/15. This checks a target invoice, not the existence of the supplier. With q_C=1/16 and target kappa=1/20, the existing native caller parameter choice has delta=1/676. The test checks every one of the nine caller rows and each layout, membership, key-volume, group, chirp and strict provider-loss guard with rational arithmetic. No BIT supplier or Turing-machine simulation is used by this arithmetic check. The native caller theorem and all original compiler hypotheses must still be bound to a real complete supplier before a multiplication claim follows. Credit the native assembly/provider lineage in [PR #4630](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/4630), [PR #4635](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/4635), and [PR #4713](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/4713), including Cao Yifan, Cinna Davis and their retained predecessors; this arithmetic check is a specialization of that framework.

A precision recurrence must declare its norm. If local encoded calls and error propagation have a fixed certified multiplier, an example scalar bound E_L <= 56 E_(L-1)+1 has multiplier sum_(j=0)^L 56^j and needs O(L) scalar guard bits. Coordinate roundoff on 3D entries first contributes a Euclidean factor sqrt(3D), so a sufficient guard is O(L+log D). For this digit model D=8^f, and recursive width f divided by 3 has depth L=O(log f); with f=O(log p), the extra guard is O(log p). If coefficient precision and input heights are O(p) with p=Theta(log n), these guards fit a fixed number of ordinary C log n-bit words. The test records the geometric bound and the dimensional guard separately. It does not infer a full compiler stability proof from a scalar recurrence.

A Laurent macro with coefficients depending on a small approximation parameter needs an additional global height analysis. Degree fixed per macro need not remain fixed under recursive substitution. A naive depth-L product of delta^(-D0) factors with delta=2^(-P) requires D0 P L extra bits. Conversely, a genuinely fixed complete-word degree can have an O(P) precision cost. A proposed border construction must prove which contract it satisfies. Interpolation work is paid: simply doubling fourteen calls to extract a degree-one coefficient gives 28 calls against productive moment at most 15, so that invoice supplies no positive saving. This observation is part of the admission audit; the positive results of this note are the explicit native constructions above.
