# Analytic A2 rank-six capacity and equality

Let R be the full120 E8 root-line labels `(1,c mod2)` in F2^9, using the
pinned simple-root coordinates. The leading coordinate is the low bit.
Let A be any27-line nonzero projective fiber of any A2 root plane, and
W=span_F2(A). For any ambient binary linear space V of dimension at most six,
put m=|R intersect V| and j=|A intersect V|.

**Theorem.** m+j<=32. If V is not contained in W, then m+j<=30.
Equality32 holds exactly when V is a six-space contained in W, its
leading-one affine five-section has zero signed quadratic sum, and V
avoids the unique root label p in `(R intersect W) minus A`.
For each A there are exactly27 equality spaces. The full127-hyperplane
inventory in W is derived below without enumerating support spaces.
The committed witness uses16 actual P112 labels and attains32 there too.

PR3262 already established the bound32 and its hyperplane table by finite
censuses. The new contribution is an analytic proof of that dependency and
its equality classification. PR3280 supplied the signed affine method for
rank five. These are pending predecessor claims, not selected science.
No shortening theorem, caller exclusion or kappa improvement is asserted.

## Finite binding of the exact configuration

For doubled simple roots s_i set G_ij=(s_i dot s_j)/4 and

```
q(x) = sum_i x_i + sum_(i<j) G_ij*x_i*x_j mod2.
B(x,y) = q(x+y)+q(x)+q(y).
```

The norm identity gives q(c mod2)=||sum c_i*s_i||^2/8 mod2.
Exact replay checks that all120 distinct root residues are precisely q=1,
B is nondegenerate, and the ambient signed sum is +16.

Replay constructs all1120 A2 planes from all7140 root-line pairs with
inner product +4 or -4. The third line is their appropriately signed sum.
The full evaluation-fiber sizes are36,27,27,27,1,1,1. This produces3360
distinct large fibers. For every A, replay checks that W has dimension
seven and its leading-one section H is an affine six-space. On H the
quadratic function f induced by q has nondegenerate polar form and signed
sum +8. Further, R intersect W=A union {p}, for a unique p with f(p)=1.
These are finite model-binding premises. No Weyl transitivity or arbitrary
normal-plane classification is needed by this binary lemma.

## Signed affine restriction lemma

Let f be an affine quadratic function on a binary affine n-space, with
nondegenerate polar form B and positive ambient signed sum G=2^(n/2).
Choose an origin and write q0(x)=f(x)+f(0). For a k-space L and translate
a+L, let T=L intersect L-perp have dimension t and
S=sum_(x in L)(-1)^f(a+x). Squaring and grouping pairs by their difference
h gives

```
S^2 = 2^k * sum_(h in T) (-1)^(q0(h)+B(a,h)).
```

The exponent is a linear character on T. Thus S is zero or has absolute
value2^((k+t)/2). Nondegeneracy gives t<=n-k; the polar rank k-t is even.
When k+t=n and the radical character is zero, L=T-perp. Every coset of T
outside L has zero signed sum for g(x)=q0(x)+B(a,x), while g vanishes on T.
Therefore the signed sum of g on L equals its ambient signed sum.
Translation by a and multiplication by (-1)^f(a) show S=+G.
In particular the negative extremum is impossible in this case.
The number of f=1 points is (2^k-S)/2.

For the ambient eight-space, take k=5. The possible radicals have t=1 or3.
At t=1, |S|=8 or S=0, giving at most20 ones. At t=3, S=0 or+16, giving
at most16 ones. Hence a rank-at-most-six label space has m<=20; smaller
nonempty affine sections extend to five-spaces.

Inside H of dimension six, an affine four-space has t=0 or2. At t=0,
|S|=4, giving at most10 ones. At t=2, S=0 or+8, giving at most8 ones.
Thus a rank-at-most-five binary space in W meets A in at most10 labels.
An affine five-space in H has t=1, so S=0 or+8 and has16 or12 ones.
The positive sign is necessary: the nondegenerate function
x0*x1+x2*x3+x4*x5+x4+x5 has signed sum -8 and20 ones on x0=0.
The behavioral controls retain this actual counterexample.

## Ambient split and equality

If V is not contained in W, dim(V intersect W)<=5. Its leading-one section
is empty or an affine space of dimension at most four inside H. Thus j<=10,
and the global bound m<=20 gives m+j<=30. This includes arbitrary support
spaces which are not generated by fiber labels.

If V is contained in W, its leading-one section extends to an affine
five-space in H, so m<=16 and j<=m. Thus m+j<=32.
If dim V<=5, the affine-four bound gives m+j<=20, so equality32 forces
six dimensions. In that case m=16 is equivalent to S=0. Since R intersect
W=A union {p}, j=m is equivalent to p not in V. These conditions are both
necessary and sufficient. For an actual support subset T spanning V,
equality in |T|+|T intersect A| additionally requires T=R intersect V.
No assumption that an arbitrary piece fills its binary closure was used.

## Derive all127 hyperplanes and all27 equality spaces

The hyperplane with leading coordinate zero has m=j=0. Every other
hyperplane of W cuts H in an affine hyperplane. There are63 nonzero normal
directions h in the six-dimensional direction space of H, and two cosets
per direction. Their radical character is f(a+h)+f(a), which differs on
the two cosets. Exactly one is balanced, S=0, and the other has S=+8.
There are63 hyperplanes with16 roots and63 with12 roots.

The balanced hyperplane in normal direction h contains p exactly when
f(p+h)+f(p)=1, or f(p+h)=0, because f(p)=1. Since H has36 zeros and28 ones,
there are36 such h; h=0 is not counted. Thus36 balanced hyperplanes contain
p and27 avoid it. A fixed point p lies in63 of the126 affine hyperplanes,
so27 of the nonbalanced ones contain p and36 avoid it. The full table is
therefore

```
(m,j)       (0,0)  (12,11)  (12,12)  (16,15)  (16,16)
count          1       27       36       36       27
```

This proves the same127-row inventory used by PR3262 analytically for
every large fiber, and proves exactly27 equality spaces for every A.
Canonical finite hyperplane replay is a corroborating check, not the proof.
The independent checker rebuilds roots/labels by a separate rational
elimination and checks all canonical127 hyperplanes without that proof.
