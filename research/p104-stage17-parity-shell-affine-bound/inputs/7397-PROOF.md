## Canonical tensor capacity at all widths m ≥ 8

$$
(E(q)-E_0(q))-(E(-q)-E_0(-q))=E(q)-E(-q).
$$

The right side has rank two because the two original bodies
and target rails are independent. Rank subadditivity gives a
sum of at least two displacement ranks for each opposite pair.

Write $C_j(m)=\sum_{k\equiv j\pmod 4}\binom{m}{k}$.
The smaller volume of each opposite pair supplies the
guaranteed common pairing. For one original field volume $V$,

$$
\frac{2(\min(C_0,C_2)+\min(C_1,C_3))}{2^m}
=1-\frac{|\operatorname{Re}(1+i)^m|+|\operatorname{Im}(1+i)^m|}{2^m}
=1-2^{-\lfloor m/2\rfloor}.
$$

The second equality uses the two alternating binomial sums.

For odd $m$ they have equal absolute value. Thus the required
fraction is at least $15/16$ for every $m\ge8$.
