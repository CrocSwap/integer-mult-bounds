\subsection{A contiguous block inside the retained A5 nullity corner}
Keep the PR18 dimensions $(a,b,c)=(25,23,57)$, $H=575$, the physical
inner order $i=23\alpha+\beta$, and all prescribed common-basis parameters.
In particular, with $I=(0,\ldots,24)$ and $\Sigma=(0,\ldots,6)$,
the first 82 row labels are $R=[I,\Sigma,I,I]$ and the last 82 inverse
column labels are $C=[I,I,\Sigma,I]$.

After absorbing the free factors $L_1,L_2$ into the line coordinates,
write the two rank-one inner projectors as $p\xi$ and $v\nu$, with
$\xi p=\nu v=1$. The inner A5 projector is
$(I-p\xi)\otimes(I-v\nu)$, of rank 528 and nullity 47. Its null
projector is $p\xi\otimes I+I\otimes v\nu-p\xi\otimes v\nu$.
Index its first-47/last-47 corner by $0\le i,j<47$, and put
\[
 \beta_i=i\bmod23,\quad b_j=(528+j)\bmod23,\quad
 r_i=R_i,\quad c_j=C_{35+j}.
\]
The prescribed rows of $M_{\beta_i}$ and inverse columns of $M_{b_j}$
give the exact corner formula
\[
 Q_{ij}=\mathbf1_{\beta_i=b_j}p_{r_i}\xi_{c_j}
       +\mathbf1_{r_i=c_j}v_{\beta_i}\nu_{b_j}
       -p_{r_i}\xi_{c_j}v_{\beta_i}\nu_{b_j}.
\]
The first term uses the identity on the second factor; the second uses
$M_{\beta_i}M_{b_j}^{-1}$ and hence the equality of the prescribed
coefficient labels. The actual A5 corner is $-Q$. Its additional outer
rank-one tensor factor only gives nonzero diagonal row and column
rescalings in the physical first-$H$/last-$H$ corner, which preserve
ordered pivot positions.

Assume the line and dual coordinates occurring below are nonzero, as
permitted by the same finite generic-coordinate conditions used in the
retained basis proof. For $25\le j\le46$, one has
$c_j=j-22$ and $b_j=j-24$. Thus the first ordered pivot is
\[
 Q_{0,46}=-p_0\xi_{24}v_0\nu_{22}\ne0.
\]
For $1\le i\le21$ and $25\le j\le45$, both $Q_{0j}$ and $Q_{i,46}$
come entirely from the last rank-one term. Eliminating the first pivot
therefore cancels that term exactly and leaves
\[
 \widetilde Q_{ij}
 =\mathbf1_{i=j-24}p_i\xi_{j-22}
  +\mathbf1_{i=j-22}v_i\nu_{j-24}.
\]
This is zero for $j>i+24$, while
$\widetilde Q_{i,i+24}=p_i\xi_{i+2}\ne0$.
Inductively, the earlier pivot rows have no entries strictly to the right
of their pivot columns, so eliminating them cannot change these entries.
The rightmost-pivot lower/lower elimination consequently has pivots
\[
 (i,i+24),\qquad 1\le i\le21,
\]
one increasing contiguous block of width 21. Diagonal pivot scalings are
absorbed into the retained triangular factors. No gathering or reversal
is introduced.

The existing primal and dual tree arguments still give full rank 47 for
the nullity corner; the remaining 26 pivots may conservatively remain
singleton children. The large-projector identity still gives the
unchanged middle block of width 481. Therefore every A5 macro of the
retained construction admits the profile
\[
 \boxed{26\text{ singleton children};\ 21,481}
\]
in place of 47 singleton children and 481. The rank sum is unchanged:
$26+21+481=528$. For the $2N$ physical copies, subtract $21(2N)$ from
the singleton multiplicity and add $2N$ width-21 children; the number of
recursive calls decreases by $20(2N)$. All other profiles, physical
roles, source frames, complex parameters and assumptions are unchanged.
The new nonzero requirements are individual coordinates on the same
rational parameter family, not additional structural restrictions; their
finite product can be imposed together with the retained nonzero minors.
The exact moment and final assembly with this profile are given in
Section~\ref{sec:a5-semantic-assembly}.
