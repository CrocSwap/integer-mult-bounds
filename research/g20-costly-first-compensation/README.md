# Costly-first G20 compensation: complete two-step closure

**No new kappa.** On the final word of
[PR #5708](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/5708), none
of the 5,114,417 second A2 rewrites after a costly first rewrite lowers the
complete local paid-query moment, for any real `0<t<1`: **5,114,412 strict
losses and five attained ties**. Every required frame change may use any
finite, separately charged path through all 52,624 native edges on the
645 fixed source frames. Correction frames remain fixed. The five ties
exactly repay a first-step debt of `2H2-H4`; they do not beat the source bill.

This answers the costly-first clause deliberately left open by
[PR #6324](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/6324).
Together, the two results cover all 5,164,282 two-rewrite paths on this
source: 5,163,804 strict losses and 478 attained ties. Those totals count
paths, not distinct resulting words. No all-depth conclusion follows.

The new ingredient is a stronger route floor. Thirty collapsed-distance
invoices appear favorable near exponent zero. Their actual demands include
three mandatory non-edge occurrences. Such a demand needs at least two
native edges, and its floor is `H1+H_(d-1)`, rather than merely `H_d`.
Replaying and charging those occurrences proves strict loss throughout
`(0,1)`. Raw exceptional invoices and multiplicities remain in the receipt.
The five ties have direct native paid attainers and give five literal
words absent from the neutral-first tie catalogue. Their complete third
neighborhood also has no saving: 11,363 paths, 11,236 strict losses and
127 attained ties. This is a five-host extension, not full depth-three coverage.

[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) states the exact scalar, route, target and ownership
contract. `Delta T=0` follows from the arbitrary-input A2 identity. Exact
per-role chronological invoices include zero rows and all final returns.
All signed inequalities use integer hinge witnesses; exponent sampling is
not part of the certificate. Full supplier and caller admission are outside
this negative result.
