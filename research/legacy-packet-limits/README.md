# Fixed packet-basis and delayed-injection limits

This package gives two exact negative results for historical packet interfaces. It gives no new supplier or multiplication kappa.

The joint-basis proof fixes the 16-helper packet factory, twelve second face copies, ordinary late pair deliveries, and the stated early-center carrier model. Every early query must lie in all final output hyperplanes of its connected output component. This common-cap condition is an explicit premise, not a consequence of arbitrary one-time restoration. Exhaustive rational enumeration gives a minimum of 36 helper slots per Hamming block, or 504 over fourteen blocks. The check covers 13,860 four-direction subsets, 8,820 independent bases, and 126 paired center cases. This result does not cover arbitrary rational query directions, a changed factory, or a new late operator.

The delayed-injection proof fixes the old eight center values, one held copy per center, and each existing B decoder with its actual target frames. It permits each center to choose its cut and delivery order. The minimum-spanning-tree lower bound gives private rank at least 64 and productive extra rank at least 128 for each of the zero-, four-, and eight-cold source words. Each word has 2,438 checked cuts: before B, after a scalar event, and after a full annotated TARGET-frame change before its scalar event. The theorem does not include injection inside an individual frame-transition implementation. The productive bound assumes one distinct productive carrier per center, a free exchange at its original rank-eight frame, and a required FULL9 exit. It subtracts only the old rank-one completion. The bound is an extra canonical rank cost in this favorable relaxation; it does not cover arbitrary productive encodings. The second checker uses reverse propagation, literal subspace point sets, and Kruskal's algorithm; the first uses forward propagation and Prim's algorithm. Split injections, shared carriers, a changed B word, and new source/target operators are outside this result.

The proofs are `source/packet-joint-basis/PROOF.md` and `source/productive-joint-research/HANDOFF.md`. The second-method claim is algorithmic independence, not independent human review. The source receipts are historical records. Their stored script hash differs from the preserved checker, so they do not bind the current checker. The driver regenerates all forward receipts and checks them with the reverse engine. Fresh script-bound receipts are in `evidence/`; all mathematical fields agree with the historical receipts. Historical unsuccessful R500/R502 constructions and old global budget comparisons are excluded.

## Reproduce

Use Python 3.10 or later with SymPy and assertions enabled. From this folder:

```sh
python3 -B verify.py --work /absolute/path/to/a-new-directory
```

The last line must be `PASS_LEGACY_PACKET_LIMITS checks=5`. The driver checks frozen input hashes and copies all inputs into the new directory. It runs the joint-basis primary and independent checks, two damaged-receipt rejection controls, three forward delayed-injection checks, and the reverse check. No historical source or receipt is changed.

The driver removes `PYTHONOPTIMIZE` from the child-process environment so that child checks and their descendants keep assertions enabled. `OPTIMIZATION-ENV-VALIDATION.json` records a fresh replay with `PYTHONOPTIMIZE=1` and an isolated parent interpreter.

## Open next questions

Can a joint late operator or a changed packet factory reduce the full paid cost? The first step is a literal scalar program with its complete frame and dirty-helper contract. Those objects are absent here. Can split center injections or shared carriers avoid the delayed-injection bound? A candidate must first define the shared state and pay each required target-frame visit. The present finite family does not settle that question.
