# Joint partial basis and center cut screen

Result: no helper count reduction in the stated finite family. The least
inventory is 36 helpers per Hamming block, or 504 for fourteen blocks.
No new scalar word, arbitrary-dirt check, owner plan or kappa is supplied.

## Contract

Keep the 16-helper packet factory and its twelve second face copies. Stop
after the first forty factory operations. Each tetrahedron has twelve
two-source partial directions in existing registers. Choose any four
independent directions for its own-output query basis Q. If O is the
four-by-four own-block response, the output basis is K = O Q^-1.
Thus K Q = O exactly. Row order and nonzero row scaling change no support
or component in this screen.

Restore each connected output component of K once. An early source query
for that component must lie in all final output hyperplanes of the component.
The screen allows early face functions to be recombined. It does not keep
the old individual early reads as a restriction. The twelve face keys have
disjoint source supports, so a combination of different face keys cannot
cancel an excluded source coefficient. The late delivery still uses the
current ordinary pair operator and its clone slots.

For centers, use only donors retired by face assemblies that do not touch
the eight protected own-output carriers. The remaining face operations are
disjoint. A missing independent center direction is assigned one new carrier
in the inventory relaxation. This is a favorable model for that restricted
early center interface. It is not a general lower bound for productive
source storage, a different factory or arbitrary center programs.

## Exact finite result

For each tetrahedron, all 495 four-direction subsets are checked over Q.
Exactly 315 are independent. Of these, 312 connect all four outputs. None
can receive an early cross-block packet. Every cross source has two nonzero
and two zero Gram entries against the four orthogonal roots. Its binary
port is therefore outside their common output cap. This is a clean-source
obstruction, which remains necessary when helpers have arbitrary dirt.

The other three bases have two components of size two. They are the three
coordinate partitions. Each permits four early face keys. All fourteen
literal Hamming blocks give the same basis counts.

Only these paired bases can permit more than four early keys in the union
of both tetrahedra. All nine combinations in each block are checked. A
different partition on the two tetrahedra leaves four ordinary clone slots
and four independent missing center directions. Its optimistic count is
16 + 12 + 4 + 4 = 36. An equal partition leaves eight ordinary clone slots.
The current first partition has no center deficit and count 36. The other
two equal partitions have a one-direction deficit and count 37. Every case
with a four-output component leaves at least eight ordinary clone slots
even when its center carriers are free, so its count is at least 36.

Coverage is fourteen blocks, 13,860 partial subsets, 8,820 independent bases
and 126 paired center cases. The primary checker uses Fraction arithmetic.
The second checker uses SymPy exact matrices, determinant tests and graph
closure. It checks every basis count and every paired center quotient rank.

This counts carrier slots in the declared template. It does not prove that
an arbitrary late packet implementation needs the old clone slots. It also
does not exclude a better paid profile with the same helper count.
