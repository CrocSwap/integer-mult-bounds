# Nilpotent rank-one couplings do not shorten shallow GL3 bit targets

**Scoped exact negative result; no new `kappa`.** In the two-role formal model over `F2[GL3(F2)]`, adding every rank-one nilpotent coupling to the six rank-one idempotent couplings does not reduce the direct CNOT bill for any ordered target pair whose two outputs are each at CNOT distance at most two. The complete domain contains 961 pairs. Every pair has exact minimum equal to its independent direct bill, which is at most four calls.

## Model and cost

The address group is `GL3(F2)`, generated by its six coordinate transvections `g_ij = I + E_ij`. A paid factor is

```text
D_N(g) = I + ([g] + [e]) N,
```

acting on two arbitrary dirty scalar roles, where `N = u v^T` ranges over all nine nonzero rank-one matrices in `M2(F2)`. Six are idempotent and three are nilpotent. If `g^2=e`, then `([g]+[e])^2=0` in characteristic two, so every factor is an involution even when `N^2=0`.

Each factor is charged one call, just as in the idempotent-only comparison. The two roles are the endpoint outputs; there is no clean helper, copied role, inter-call frame change, or restoration operation. This is the complete bill **inside the stated finite primitive model**. A native supplier implementation for nilpotent couplings is not established, and there is no selected-supplier mapping or multiplication-exponent claim.

## Exact result

For all `p,q` in the radius-two CNOT ball, compare `diag([p],[q])` with direct cost `d(p)+d(q)`. The packed search covers all 54 factors through length three: 160,435 raw words, reduced by exact transfer-state equality to 91,708 states over depths zero through three. It checks all 168 address-group coefficients in every transfer-matrix entry. No target appears below its direct cost. All 961 independent direct words are positive controls.

| Direct cost | Ordered target pairs | Shorter expanded-alphabet words |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1 | 0 |
| 1 | 12 | 0 |
| 2 | 84 | 0 |
| 3 | 288 | 0 |
| 4 | 576 | 0 |

Thus this bounded expansion of the register-coupling alphabet does not beat independent execution on shallow GL3 CNOT pairs. It says nothing about targets beyond CNOT radius two, more than two roles, other address groups, or other cost models.
