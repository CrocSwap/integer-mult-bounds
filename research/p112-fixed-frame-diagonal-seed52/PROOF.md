# Exact fixed-pattern support obstruction

## Statement

Fix the ten distinct frame-hit masks induced by the ten terms of the pinned PR #1347 `COVERING.json`. Consider every rank-six support triple `(left tag, right tag, cut)` whose rank-seven parent is in the complete P112 tagged-support model, whose cut is a nonzero column-code word, whose resulting row has rank six, and whose simultaneous frame-hit mask equals one of the fixed ten masks (each mask has 1,120 hits).

The union of the diagonal supports of all such triples omits ten live lines. Consequently, for any selection of one such triple per fixed mask, the sum of the ten rank-one terms has zero diagonal support on those lines. It cannot realize the required nonzero P112 diagonal there. This excludes only the fixed ten-mask support class.

## Complete domain

The pinned predecessor engine builds 120 E8 root lines, removes the eight punctured coordinate-frame lines, and enumerates the 1,260 live eight-frames. It constructs the 240 tagged supports used for the star and one-line-trimmed-star model. For every ordered support-tag pair `(a,b)`, the verifier retains exactly the pairs with parent rank 7. There are 7,616 such pairs. For each pair it forms the full nonzero binary code generated by the columns `n[j] & left` for the set bits `j` of the right support. Every such code has 127 nonzero words, for 967,232 parent/cut cases total.

For a word `cut`, the retained row support is `left XOR cut`. Its frame-hit mask is the intersection of the sets of live frames meeting the row and right supports. A candidate enters the census iff this mask is exactly one of the ten pinned masks and has 1,120 bits. Its diagonal support is

```text
(left XOR cut) AND right AND live-line-mask.
```

These operations use exact integer arithmetic. The verifier checks rank six for every retained row. It records every matching tagged triple (including distinct triples with the same diagonal mask), then computes the union of the 40 resulting masks. The union contains 102 live line IDs and misses `18, 47, 53, 62, 88, 92, 96, 100, 112, 117`; their exact root representatives are included in `RESULT.json`.

## Why the union proves the scoped negative

Every allowed one-per-mask choice is a subset of the complete 40-triple census. Its diagonal union is therefore a subset of the census union. Since the census union omits ten required live diagonal positions, no such choice can cover all 112 positions. This is a direct finite obstruction; no optimizer or heuristic search is involved.

The original ten terms are replayed as a positive control: the frame minimum, frame histogram, frame-count digest, diagonal minimum, and diagonal multiplicity histogram match the pinned PR #1347 receipts, and all ten original terms occur in the expanded census. Negative controls check that the zero word is rejected by parent-code membership, a representative cannot be assigned to another frame-mask class, and the ten omitted lines are absent from every candidate mask.

## Dependencies and limits

The standalone exact replay depends on the engine and `COVERING.json` copied from PR #1347 at `64fd5f9e91961dedc16118fcc7ad71ee82efe28f`; local hashes and Git blob IDs are in `SOURCES.json`. The geometric model and rank-six search descend from PR #1226 and PR #1323, which PR #1347 pins and credits. The base selected-result commit is `bce54fb3f895cbe5cd4c0eb477c3afeed100190f`.

No coefficient feasibility is established. The result does not cover another selection of frame masks, nonmaximum-hit triples, a broader support language, or a full physical decoder. It changes neither the selected certificate nor its κ.
