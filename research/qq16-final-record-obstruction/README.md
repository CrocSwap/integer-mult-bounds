# QQ16 final records still lose selected inputs

**Result:** for the actual routed QQ16 ordinary-only bridge/terminal replica,
the proposed **64 initial query coordinates per owner** still leave at least
`ceil(8|G|/7)` dimensions of pair 0's original selected inputs invisible.
This holds even when every final selected field, every completed-X descendant
and all five forward-query histories are supplied free. Here `G` is the finite
regular owner group. This is a scoped proof with finite source certificates;
**no new supplier or kappa is claimed**.

The contribution closes a precise negative branch of [PR2174's
handoff](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/2174): its correct
decoder requires a recorded post-B1 cut, which these final records cannot
replace. It upgrades [philippeVV's earlier finite-model
failure](https://github.com/philippeVV/integer-mult-bounds/tree/680d0997621a2cecfef2186ab143377658687825/research/joint-query-first-pair-catalogue/four-bank)
to an actual regular-cover theorem. That predecessor already supplied the
finite-model observation, the all-bank record choice, and an actual-route
moment census; none is presented here as a new discovery.

## Exact boundary

The local calls use pairs `0,0,0,1,1,2,2,3,3`. Bridges and the terminal act on
the 96 ordinary ports. No selected intervention is inserted before F2. The
initial read-only records are `(B0,B2)` on pair 0 and `(B3,B4)`, `(B5,B6)`,
`(B7,B8)` on pairs 1, 2, 3. These eight queries have eight coordinates each
per owner. The observation includes final Y ordinary fields (completed-X),
not the final X fields carrying former-Y values.

This is **not** a theorem about arbitrary early selected controllers or the
separate first-six-selected-swap program. Selected fields are nonzero after
B1 and become zero only after F2. Homogeneous selected-only linear operations
after that cut preserve this family. The theorem does not price the queries,
claim 64 paid native ranks, or contradict the selected conditional exponent
`0.001672660197076918`.

## Why the obstruction exists

[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) derives two constrained query-image spaces from the actual
routes. The credited exact moments `(24,28)|G|` give each image dimension at
least `ceil(32|G|/7)`. After the required invertible selected transport, their
intersection has dimension at least `ceil(8|G|/7)`. Rational sections provide
two ordinary inputs whose nonzero original selected data cancel through F2.
The complete 19-event trajectory then leaves every permitted observation zero.
At the post-B1 cut, however, `Y_A=d` is nonzero. That is the missing record.

All named observations and their access, storage and restoration are granted
zero additional cost in this screen. Paying more for the same read-only
records cannot repair its kernel; a viable successor must change the record
or early dynamics. No large owner group is enumerated and no compact witness
vector or coefficient-bitlength bound is supplied.

## Replay and evidence

Python 3.12, standard library, from the repository root:

```sh
python -I -B scripts/research_package.py --package research/qq16-final-record-obstruction
python -I -O -B research/qq16-final-record-obstruction/verify.py
```

Expected marker: `PASS_QQ16_FINAL_RECORD_OBSTRUCTION`.

- `verify_core.py` reconstructs R by forward propagation of the original
  scalar word, binds 1,008 actual owner routes to the native packet at their
  exact stage/port positions, checks six relative-route sets, and executes
  all 19 events in ordered symbolic algebra.
- `independent_moments.py` imports no research code. It reconstructs R by
  backward covectors and owner maps by swaps in a full orthonormal basis,
  then accumulates collision-aware group-algebra coefficients. Its matrix
  and route digests must agree with the primary checker.
- `independent_algebra.py` uses separately factored local maps on five exact
  rational models with noncommuting selected charts. It checks the full
  schedule and two sign controls. These fixtures check algebra only; the
  actual-cover existence argument is in the proof.
- `RESULT.json` stores the complete deterministic result. Default replay
  compares every field without writing. Semantic controls exercise wrong
  coefficients, route placement/collision, constraints, signs, schedule and
  observation types; adding the missing cut record is a detecting control.
- `test_integrity.py` runs a Git-free relocated copy, checks replay leaves
  its bytes unchanged, and requires altered receipts and sources to fail.

`python -I -B research/qq16-final-record-obstruction/verify.py --write` is the
explicit receipt generator. It is not needed for normal verification.

The source-bound baseline orientation passed on Windows/Python 3.12.2 using
an extended-length script path; ordinary Windows paths initially hid seven
long pinned inputs. No input was changed or pin weakened. The selected
`make frontier1094-verify` full cold replay was **not** run: this contribution
changes neither supplier nor selected record. Native compilation, physical
hosting, precision/tape, all-size admission and a full multiplication theorem
remain outside this package. The written argument establishes the regular-
cover statement; finite checker success is not formal theorem verification.
