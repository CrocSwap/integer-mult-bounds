## 2. Replacement clause

A native word may use ADD and COPY coefficients q in A with |q| + 1 < 2^16 (this bound is kept exactly as before), and SCALE by units of A (numerator and denominator products of 2, 3, 5), with frames as before, provided it declares S = 3^e 5^g such that:

- (i) for every prefix of the program, S times every row has entries in Z[1/2];
- (ii) for every ADD or COPY, S times the product q times the source row, formed before the addition, has entries in Z[1/2];
- (iii) every public output row of the complete word has entries in Z[1/2].

Execution. A coefficient a/b with b = 2^x 3^y 5^z is executed as: multiply by a, check divisibility by 3^y 5^z, divide. The inputs are multiplied by S numerator by numerator, and at the exit the division by S is exact. There are two forms, and they are not the same program:

- **Scale per call.** Every call multiplies its own inputs by S at entry and divides by S at exit, so a factor S is added along the unfinished contexts only. Its conversion is charged on every call (the per-call figures of section 5).
- **One scale at the outer boundary.** Multiply once by S^K, with K bounding the number of unfinished contexts, as #104 writes 21^K, and unscale at the outer return. There is no per-call conversion (the other figures of section 5).

Rows are formal (the vector of coefficients of the inputs), so (i) to (iii) are finite checks on the word, independent of the inputs and of the tensor width f. `verify_host3_odd.py` checks A-membership, the |q| bound, (i), (ii) and (iii) event by event; `controls.py` runs its rejections (S = 375, 225, 75, 45, 15, 25, 9 and 1; S = 7 x 1125; a coefficient 1/7; a SCALE by 1/7, 7 or 3/2; |q| + 1 >= 2^16; a non-dyadic public row; cancelling pairs of 1/7 and of 65536; and the dyadic #6931 word as the S = 1 control).

## 3. Theorem and proof

**Theorem (proved, conditional on exact children).** Suppose the word satisfies (i) to (iii), the root inputs are Gaussian dyadic, and every completed child returns the exact Gaussian-dyadic value of its operator. Then every stored value lies on the dyadic grid, every checked division is exact, and the word's output equals its exact action on every Gaussian-dyadic input, at every f, including dirty and correlated spectators.

Proof.

1. Work over R = A[i]. It is commutative and contains Z[i, 1/2]. The frames are matrices over Z[i, 1/2], and scalars from R are central, so the common-frame identity of `03-motifs.tex` (lines 168-175) holds with coefficients in R. The same holds for the Clifford representatives (`general-clifford-frames.tex` line 62).
2. The stored array on register r after any prefix is D_v applied to (S times the sum over inputs k of R_{r,k} x_k), where R_{r,k} are the row entries and D_v is the frame matrix. By (i), S R_{r,k} is in Z[1/2], and D_v has Gaussian dyadic entries, so the stored values are Gaussian dyadic. This is an identity in the formal inputs x.
3. Division by a/b: before the division the stored value is a times S q R_s x with S q R_s in Z[1/2] by (ii). Hence it is divisible componentwise by the odd part of b on the grid (#6901 lines 276-278), and the quotient is again on the grid.
4. By (iii) the exit division by S is exact, and the result is the logical output.
5. Scalars are not raised to tensor powers (FINITE-PRECOMPILATION lines 247-248), so S does not depend on f.
6. Composition. Each unfinished context multiplies numerators by at most S, so debts add along the active chain only. A completed child returns a Gaussian-dyadic row (by the premise), so it adds no odd factor at exit (the active-path argument of `ODD-GRID-COMPOSITION.md` lines 33-48).

The endpoint of the word is the same kind as the dyadic word's, so the caller, contact, host and controller identities are unchanged, and the word is one atom to its caller. Quantizers act on stored cuts, which lie on the grid; their identities need F and W recomputed, not their form. Magnitude contract: `05-layers.tex` line 875 binds only the outer proposition; the recursive child is in the common guarded format with no disk contract.

## 4. Width

The cost is width only, and it is per unfinished context, not per ordinary node. All numbers below are computed by `check.py` unless marked quoted.

- Live nesting is the number of unfinished raw contexts t = K_star * ell (#6901 PROOF lines 88 and 191), where K_star = v m (H+1)^2 (#6901 eq. (4a)), v = 1920 the chronological power and m = 153^1920 the carrier.
- Per unfinished context, on the stated word: the 2-adic denominator height is **24 bits** (the odd part of any denominator is at most 1,125, 10.1 bits) and the magnitude is **36.54 bits** (log2 of the largest l1 mass of S times a row or product, scale included) [computed]. The scale S itself is 10.1 of the 36.54 bits. #6932's word had 2-adic height 15 bits; the height is a separate per-context debt from the magnitude.
- Temporaries. A multiply-by-a, check-divisibility, divide-by-b step on a stored value x holds the product a x. The ADD coefficient numerators of this word reach **24 bits** (largest numerator 14,729,531, first at event 252; it recurs, for example at event 70779) and their denominators **19 bits** (393,216 = 3 x 2^17), and 864 ADD events have a numerator above 40,864 [computed]. The step therefore needs up to 24 bits above the width of x, where #6932's word needed 16. This is a constant additive term and is not multiplied by t [argued]; whether the numerator or the value governs the temporary needs a maintainer reading. The `W + 6` of #6901 is a #6030 figure and is not reused.
- Sufficiency (argued, following #6901 lines 296-316): W = p + h + 2D + 3 remains sufficient once c t < d. Two forms of c:
  - With the #6030 caller mass 10 as a placeholder (the form of #6932): 2 (log2 10 + 36.54) = 79.72 [computed], so **80 t < d** replaces the 82 t < d of #6932. It assumes the caller mass is recomputed.
  - With caller majorants. The quoted figures are a caller mass of at most 2^120.35 and a path-maximum height majorant of 157 bits, both computed on #6932's caller by a script that is not part of this package. Per context the height is then at most 24 + 157 = 181 bits and the magnitude at most 36.54 + 120.35 = 156.89 bits, so c = 2 x 181 = **362**, and 362 t < d follows from 363 K_star < 2^H as at #6901 lines 299-304. log2 K_star = 14,072.3 bits at the written H = 14,161,600,000,000,000,000, and the margin is about 1.4 x 10^19 bits [computed from the quoted majorants; `check.py` prints it]. The majorants were not recomputed on this package's caller, whose type A blocks are smaller (section 5); that a smaller block cannot raise a monotone mass measure is argued, not rerun.
  - The same computation for #6932's word, whose height was 15 bits, gives 15 + 157 = 172 bits and **344 t < d**, not 82 t < d (as corrected in #6934).
  - Status: the arithmetic is computed here; the hypotheses of #6901 at (4a) for the per-port root are not rechecked line by line; nobody has reviewed this.

The gain, kappa over kappa of #6934 minus one, is 0.00087175 (+0.0872%), and it is measured only against #6934.
- **Odd divisions.** The odd divisions are priced separately, as the reviewed text does (`CLIQUE-CODE-GRID.md` lines 9-11, `REVIEW.md` lines 10-11, `ODD-GRID` lines 122-123). A constant block is a maximal run of ADD, SCALE -1 and SWAP of one type between two nonconstant events or joint contacts; it is left multiplication of the role vector by one matrix of GL_40(Z), and any block with the same matrix gives the same operator. `check_caller_blocks.py` checks, for the 38 changed blocks, the 40 x 40 integer matrices, the whole 78 x 78 caller at eight points modulo a prime, and the type A stream against #6912's pinned source, and it rejects 12 failing controls. The premises of #6912 stay as it declares them: exact circuit equality is not rounded-state equality, and nothing here uses a freed allowance for a native change. #6912 is not merged; its blocks are taken as data (`data/caller_6912_before.json`, `data/caller_6912_after.json`, SHA-256 pinned in the script).
- **Raw counts**, recomputed by `check.py` with the new caller: literal 802,809,952; actual per-call work 815,970,652; reviewed upper 832,255,648. With the second LAMBDA count (12,560 jobs at 1,000 each) the upper is 844,815,648 in the form with one scale at the boundary and no per-call conversion. ## 6. Exactness premise

Exactness is conditional. The statement "odd debt resets at each word exit" holds only for completed children that return the exact Gaussian-dyadic value. This is a premise the odd word shares with the dyadic words, which also need exact children, and it is **declared**: it is the exact recursion of #6901 lines 92-97 and 290-292 and #6900 PROOF lines 56-58 (no child-return rounding), and the hypothesis named in `ODD-GRID-COMPOSITION.md` lines 55-56 and `REVIEW.md` lines 11-13.

Truncated children. `physical_exact.py` runs one column with generic commuting frames. At F = 33 fractional bits the word is exact (148,332 divisions, 70,752 by a non-unit, 0 bad of 1,461 public rows); F = 32 and F = 31 fail at event 61461; S = 375 fails at event 36997 and S = 225 at event 1103. A truncation of g = 13 low bits after every MOVE is not noticed at F = 33 (the dropped bits are zero there); g = 14 and g = 24 are rejected by the divisibility check at the first inexact odd division, and so is g = 44 at F = 60. So the check sees a truncated child only once enough bits are dropped, not for every truncation. The dyadic #6931 word under a truncation of g = 44 at F = 60 runs to the end and is wrong on 830 of 1,441 public rows, so neither word is exact on a truncating child.

A mode in which child returns are rounded, with no divisibility check, is outside this package and nothing here claims it; a separate note may treat it.

## 7. A6 and tensor width 2

`check_a6.py` ports the argument of #6914 (CubeJerry, with OpenAI Codex) to the 23 owned rises of this word, and adds the width-2 phase test of #6930 (unpaidmoon, with Cursor Grok) on the stored recipes. The owned MOVEs of this word equal those of #6934's word (and of #6932's) as a multiset, and the 23 rise chart pairs are those of #6902's word, whose rises #6914 treats. The peak of concurrent owned fields is **43** here (40 in #6934): the 54 lifetimes, the 51 owned fields, the 23 rises, their mass 56 and the 16 owned reads of mains are unchanged, and nine owned INITs are hoisted earlier, which is a retiming of exclusive accesses. The A6 declaration is therefore for 23 rises of mass 56, 51 owned fields and a peak of 43.

- **Computed.** An exact chronological scan of the owned lifetimes: 54 lifetimes, 53 dirty clears, 23 rises (10 of rank 1, 4 of rank 2, 1 of rank 3, 6 of rank 4, 1 of rank 5, 1 of rank 6), mass 56; the descending-only rule rejects exactly those 23 events. For each distinct rise map, all 512 exact Gaussian columns of T_b T_a^-1 equal right monomial, F_r, left monomial from the stored recipe; the adapter record lists realise the monomials at f = 1 on all addresses and at f = 2 and 3 on sampled addresses with spectators; the records stay under the allowance of 1,000 per rise. Eight failing controls (wrong phase, uncorrected adjoint child, wrong source frame, public and owned alias, omitted CLEAR, rank-zero MOVE, global phase not multiplied by f) are rejected.
- **Width 2.** A global phase i^p at width 1 becomes i^(p f) at width f; the stored phase p is a width-1 residue. The test executes the stored recipes jointly on two columns and compares with the exact product (M|x>) (x) (M|y>). The faithful phase i^(p f) gives 0 bad on every sampled pair of the 23 rise maps (rank 1 to 3: 576 pairs, rank 4 and 5: 64, rank 6: 8) and on 4,096 sampled input pairs of the first rank-1 MOVE of the word. Copying the stored integer p fails on every pair of each map with total stored phase not 0 mod 4 (18 of the 23) and passes the others; an off-by-one phase and a flipped Fourier sign fail on all pairs. At f = 1 the copied integer equals the faithful one, so a width-1 check cannot see the difference.
- **Not covered.** Joint width 3 or more (the records are exercised at f = 3 on sampled addresses only, not the joint run); spectator columns in the joint run; every map that is not an owned rise (a wider sample over all chart maps and main adapters of the word was run outside this package and is not shipped; in it, maps of rank 8 or more get one sampled pair each); the joint caller and any tape-level width-f execution; rounded child returns. Per-column execution with the global phase integer multiplied by f is the contract being assumed, as in #6930.
- **What is adoption, not replay.** That a completed positive rank-r child may take an owned field holding arbitrary contents (FINITE-PRECOMPILATION sections 1 and 6, #4416 review); that the child, adapters and role restoration complete before the parent reads or erases; the fresh finite-template constants; the width-f tariff. The check is not a tape replay, does not prove owned erasure and does not prove admission of the contracts. Three lifetimes read before their first rise and three have scalar contact between rises (census in `check_a6.py`); in such lifetimes the contents may carry odd denominators, and exactness there is the same conditional as everywhere (exact child return, the S certificate).
- The sentence at `native.py:139` ("frame induction at every positive tensor width") is replaced by what is run: width 1 for every listed map, and width 2 only through `check_a6.py`.
