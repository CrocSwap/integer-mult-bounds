## 1. Exact claim, sources and inherited interfaces

The frozen candidate is DreamingOfClouds' [#6934](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/6934),
head `4e13b7acdbea1c9f12aa88126435360eecea7401`: its 71,069-event native word
and actual 3,221-event joint caller. Its proof leaves the caller majorants
quoted from an earlier caller and the compiler-precision specialization
unreviewed. This package supplies an independently computed full prefix
envelope and its all-size numerical implication.

Use its **per-call scale-1125** implementation, then inline the retained
chronological compiler and ordinary recursion. Under the nonprecision
interfaces below, one common format suffices:
F=p+D fractional bits, W=p+h+2D+3 signed bits per real/imaginary component,
and W+26 temporary bits. Odd divisors require neither an odd-denominator
numeric field nor a numerical precision reset at child returns.

Odd-denominator clearing and depth-powered boundary scaling are credited to
icekylinx's [#104](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/104) and
its successors. The fixed-root Clifford bound and context recurrence belong
to [#6901](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/6901), with its
#4586/#5443/#6878 lineage. The candidate word, scale1125 and per-call wrapper
are #6934's, following #6932. None of these methods or endpoint identities is
claimed anew.

Retain precisely these interfaces:

1. Native and whole-job adapters have their stated arbitrary-input,
   absolute-phase Gaussian-dyadic Clifford actions with complete spectators. Owned INIT/CLEAR
   have ideal semantics. Distinct productive entrances are actual owners;
   copied correlated fields earn no new target credit.
2. Every occurrence and inverse return preserves the same complete D-bit
   selected root factor. Descendant remainder bits stay in that factor;
   only the true external ROW factor is split or selected.
3. Scalar/C operations and adapter returns finish before another operation
   consumes a partially written field. The four-instruction scalar SWAP is
   not interrupted by another swap, child or consumer.
4. Completed raw callers have the candidate's independent positive Gaussian
   endpoints and restored owners. Chronological product, completion,
   splitting and integer compiler use the retained scheduling, with the
   parameters in section5. Spectral strictification, complete native/caller
   admission and physical tariffs remain separate.

These interfaces do not assume a common grid, a numerical exact-child oracle
or physical precision closure. Define the finite ideal recursion by rank
descent, prove its invariant, then implement its arithmetic on that grid.
An ideal endpoint premise is not a claim that a rounded service already
returns that endpoint.

Start after a separately paid root boundary. Each original real/imaginary
component belongs to 2^-p Z and has absolute value strictly below2^h, with
p,h>=0 and D>=1. Finite external row counts, dirty public inputs, correlations
and spectators are arbitrary. Root preprocessing must supply p,h and layouts.

## 2. Exact finite source envelopes

The independent rational row interpreter treats MOVE as a chart change,
INIT as owned zero, CLEAR as lifetime completion, and ADD/COPY/SCALE as
literal row operations. It checks common-frame use and completed frames.
All stored rows and separate pre-ADD products, multiplied by S=1125, are
dyadic; completed public rows themselves are dyadic. The full scan visits
14,060,742 coefficients and obtains:

| Local property | Exact bound |
|---|---:|
| Dyadic denominator height after scaling | 24 |
| Maximum scaled row/product l1 mass | 1636447290138225/16384 <2^37 |
| Scalar numerator bit length | 24 |

Input scaling is included because S<2^37. These are finite source
certificates, not an independent native-map or all-size proof.

A separate caller recurrence majorizes expansions in **products of Clifford
operators**, without commuting the products or substituting numerical
characters. For ADD, use M_t+|q|M_s and
max(H_t,H_s+v2(den(q))). SCALE, SWAP and QUERY use their literal rules.
IDLE is a Clifford product. LAMBDA is expanded as
((1+s i)I+(1-s i)Q^2)/2 for s=+-1. With Gaussian coefficient mass
|Re|+|Im|, its safe update is M<-2M and H<-H+1.

At a joint event use the two actual type-A shear endpoints and type-B
(x-Qy,(Q^-1 x+y)/2), followed by the relevant Clifford. Their roles are
disjoint; both old B bounds are read before either is updated. The complete
current caller gives:

| Cut | Dyadic height | Coefficient mass |
|---|---:|---:|
| Any completed caller event | <=163 | <=16246698892210214036146399419475929893435/256 <2^126 |
| Entrance of any joint call | <=154 | <=2^124 |

Fresh productive helpers have mass1 and height0 relative to the current
raw-call entrance. A nested helper retains its actual entrance expansion and

including both caller-only and local-interior cuts, the raw caller has

A=max(163,154+24)=178 exterior denominator bits,

## 3. Per-call odd clearing and recursive reset

For formal input row R and current chart D_v, the scaled local word holds
D_v(S R x). The source certificate makes its exterior coefficients dyadic.
The same certificate covers a pre-ADD product. Thus the odd part of each
divisor divides the stored integer numerator on every sufficiently fine
binary grid. At completion the unscaled endpoint is dyadic, so final
division by S is exact too.

Each nested invocation scales its own actual entrances, runs the word and
unscales its completed outputs. Unfinished contexts accumulate mass and
binary denominator debt. A completed positive Gaussian endpoint resets the
**extra exterior debt relative to its entrance**, retaining all debt already
present at that entrance. Its Gaussian action joins the Clifford factor.
No numerical grid is reset.

One S is charged per unfinished local context. Completed siblings add no
persistent debt. Scaling once by S at the outer boundary is a different
program and cannot replace arbitrary nesting: the boundary-only program
needs S raised to a proved depth bound, as #104 prescribes. The verifier
includes a nested exact-division countercontrol to this mistaken shortcut.

This is a formal linear expansion in original complete root inputs, so no
independence of their values is needed. The expansion is proof state, not a
runtime data structure. Exterior scalars stay outside tensor powers.

## 4. Current compiler depth and the integer threshold

The candidate parameters are v=1920, carrier153, separated paid ceiling11^v,
productive floor12^v and m=153^v. In particular r_max<k_min and k_min>=2.
These apply to the separated rule, not the unpowered raw rank-one targets.

The retained compiler has complement n-s<(1-1/m)n and deepest-exit ratio
at most (r_max/m)(r_max/k_min)^G <=1-1/m. Its path has at most
m(1+floor(log2 n)) nonbase frames, each carrying at most
v(1+floor(log2 n)) raw contexts. Thus the credited recurrence specializes to

K(n) <= v m (1+floor(log2 n))^2.

Independently regenerate the written threshold from the source's exact
candidate/tested rationals and logarithm power2224:
H=1,339,210,000,000,000,000, N=2^H, and
K_star=1920*153^1920*(H+1)^2.
K_star has14066 bits; 357K_star has14075 bits, so 357K_star<2^H.
The threshold search has one crossing: with L=2224 and step=10^13,
(step*j+1)^L>2^j for j<=2L. For j>=2L the ratio of successive
((step*j+1)^L/2^j) values is strictly below
(1+1/j)^L/2 < 1/(2(1-L/j)) <=1. Thus checking the adjacent
integer indices justifies the regenerated threshold.
No floating logarithm or construction of N is used. Also153<2^8 and
8*1920<H, so N exceeds the literal carrier m=153^1920.

For an ordinary root with D selected axes, put ell=floor(log_N D) and
t=K_star ell. Each ordinary nesting divides selected width by N.
For ell>=1, N ell<=N^ell<=D; for ell=0, t=0. Therefore 356t<D.
This counts source-dependence depth, not banks, copies or the expanded call
count. It adds no storage-capacity theorem.

## 5. Ideal invariant and finite exact simulation

At a completed logical cut expand a row into a finite sum c U x, with each
x an original complete root input and U a Gaussian-dyadic Clifford on the
same D-bit root factor. Sections2-4 imply
c in 2^(-178t)Z[i] and sum|c| <=2*2^(161t).
Gaussian coefficient mass bounds ordinary complex modulus. The factor2
covers one unfinished SWAP; it does not repeat down the stack because a
SWAP cannot enclose a child.

The credited Clifford row theorem bounds its entry denominator by
2^ceil(D/2) and its Euclidean norm by one. Hence the sufficient fractional
and strict-magnitude bounds are

F_required=p+ceil(D/2)+178t,
J_required=h+ceil((D+1)/2)+161t+1.

Since356t<D, these satisfy F_required<=p+D and J_required<=h+D+2.
Raising the grid also raises the signed stored integer width. Thus
F=p+D and W=p+h+2D+3 suffice, per component.

A scalar numerator has at most24 bits. W+26 bits cover its signed product
and subsequent addition; the caller coefficients, S, LAMBDA, C numerators
and swaps require less. Odd divisions and binary shifts are exact because
the ideal quotient already lies on the grid. A nonzero remainder is a fault.
Complete C numerators are formed before division; no summand is rounded.

Induct on the finite ideal instruction tree, including child interiors.
Each instruction has exact grid arguments, a safe temporary and an ideal
result on the same grid. Its finite arithmetic agrees with the ideal
instruction. This derives numerical exact return and quantizer transparency
without a separate exact numerical child oracle.

A physical service using a different interior format or early rounding must
separately be related to this simulation. Partially written fields need
not be Clifford images, but each component is an old or new bounded
component and the no-consumer-before-return rule preserves the argument.
Active-coordinate projections are excluded and have a negative control.

## 6. Paid conversions, limits and remaining work

The per-call program scales and unscales all1452 public fields in each of
18 local calls:52,272 fixed-integer scaling operations per raw caller. This
counts arithmetic service applications, not individual physical tape passes.
Each application owes its full positive scan, staging, restoration and erasure
constant. The receipt field `per_caller_scale_scans` uses this service count. These add to
the source word, caller, native, movement, setup, restoration and owned
storage bills. Each fixed numerator/divisor is compiled source data; the
scan cost is linear in complete component records times W+26. No new
productive credit or free conversion is introduced.

#6934 already prices a per-call conversion form separately from its
boundary-only form. This package establishes numerical closure of the
per-call form. It does not independently admit the raw10^9 allowance,
native exponent, BIT dependency or outer nine-row ledger. Source access,
finite control preparation, retained backing and every physical child remain
charged through their own contracts.

The theorem starts after the paid root boundary. It does not reinstantiate
the separate full 05-layers/H0 preprocessing/output-normalization theorem
for this new caller, implement the complete dispatcher, accept arbitrary
rounded child returns or prove unconditional multiplication. All five
assumptions on the selected certificate remain unchanged.
