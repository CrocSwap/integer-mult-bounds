## 2. Statement and root boundary

Start the ordinary root **after reserved-axis preprocessing**. Let d>=1 be
all its selected binary coordinates, kept as one fixed factor throughout
recursion even when a descendant calls some of them spectators. Each original
productive real/imaginary component, on every complete external row, is on
the 2^-p grid and has absolute value strictly below2^h, p,h>=0 integers.
Establishing this p,h at the preprocessing boundary is separately paid.

An ordinary node of width e uses e=Nf+r, 0<=r<N, with width-f substitutions
at its rank-one leaves. Its unfinished ordinary depth is at most

    ell=floor(log_N d).

Earlier stopping reduces this number. Elementary base stages contribute no
unfinished raw-caller contexts. Define **symbolically**

    K_star=1024*145^1024*(H+1)^2, t=K_star*ell,
    F=p+ceil(d/2)+4t,
    W=p+h+d+18t+3.                                      (1)

**Theorem.** Under §1's nonprecision interfaces, every interpreted stored
component and exterior scalar product in the ideal recursively substituted
current uniform program belongs exactly to the 2^-F grid and fits a signed
W-bit integer component. W+6 temporary bits suffice for its literal scalar/C
numerators. Nearest rounding with upward ties and truncation toward zero on
this grid are identities. No child-return rounding or precision reset occurs.

The vector invariant below holds at completed logical field operations,
completed C stages and restored returns. A partially updated in-place field
need not have that invariant: its old/new coordinate mask can be a projector.
Its **component** bounds still hold because each stored entry is either old
or a completed-operation entry, with pair numerators covered separately.
Such a partial field is never consumed by another instruction. Exposed
numerical cuts inside a declared monomial adapter may also permute bounded
components or multiply them by fourth roots of unity; these preserve the
component grid/range. The whole restored adapter, rather than an arbitrary
intermediate address permutation, supplies the Clifford factor in (5).

## 3. Source-derived compiler context depth

### 3.1 Product, completion and integer clearing

`p7-TENSOR.md` §1 suspends the U copies at a common paid position, runs V on
their actual distinct owners, restores its matchers, then resumes U. A
contribution path has at most one unfinished raw unit per chronological
product level: a v-fold product adds at most v contexts. Simultaneous copies
and productive-field count do not multiply a per-field coefficient norm.
Completion runs after the product, on disjoint axes of that owner, under its
whole-job normalizers. Splitting replaces one positive job by consecutive
positive jobs on disjoint axes. These add Clifford jobs and complete adapters,
not exterior denominators. The v bound remains conservative during completion.
This is the actual orig/piece/completion rule of #5443 Lemma A.

Let m=max productive rank, k_min=min productive rank, r_max=max paid rank of
the separated rule. Here r_max<k_min and k_min>=2. At rank n>=m the finite
compiler has classes 0,...,G where

    G=max{|a|:m*product(k^a_k)<=n}.

The minimum type realizes m*k_min^G<=n, so G+1<=1+floor(log2 n).
Every source rank satisfies1<=s<=n. A deepest exit is a product of G+1 paid
ranks and therefore

    e<=r_max^(G+1)<k_min^(G+1)<=m*k_min^G<=n.             (2)

Thus e<n, including G=0; nonzero complements n-s<n, and zero complements
are skipped. This proves well-founded **ideal** compilation without assuming
its precision or physical closure.

FINITE §§3–4 and unchanged `Compiler.plan`, `uniform`, `run_class` execute
all complements before flow; suspend a class at one round while its entire
child class finishes; and finish each exit before the next group/parent round.
Simultaneous class units use distinct fields. Batch clearing chooses a copies
on a*S distinct arrays. It does not multiply payloads by a,1/S,1/B_e or
normalized flow weights: such fractions occur in accounting identities only.

### 3.2 A conservative closed bound

Let K(n) be the maximum unfinished raw contexts on one contribution path in
the fully inlined rank-n program. K=0 for the ordinary elementary base and
rank0. A complement has no enclosing flow class in this program; an exit
has at most G+1 suspended classes, each with at most v raw contexts. Hence

    K(n)<=max(max_s K(n-s),
              max_(compatible path,exit e)[v*|path|+K(e)]).       (3)

A cut before an exit uses the same expression with K(0)=0. This counts
numerical dependence depth, **not** owned banks: bank cardinality still
uses #5443's different live-inventory recurrence. Replication creates disjoint
copies; their coefficients do not add into a field merely by existing.

Strong induction on n gives

    K(n)<=v*n*(1+floor(log2 n)).                         (4)

For L_n=1+floor(log2 n), any nested positive rank is <=n-1 by (2), so its
bound is <=v*(n-1)*L_n; the path contributes <=v*L_n. Complements are smaller
and base cases are zero. A stronger bound uses the fractional rank descent already proved in #4586,
not a new contraction lemma. Actual sources satisfy s>n/m, so
n-s<rho*n for rho=(m-1)/m. Since m*k_min^G<=n, equation(2) gives

    e/n <= (r_max/m)*(r_max/k_min)^G <= rho.

Thus every nested nonbase compiler frame decreases rank by this fixed factor.
Bernoulli gives rho^(-m)=(1+1/(m-1))^m >=1+m/(m-1)>2. After m descents
rank is less than half. A path has at most m*(1+floor(log2 n)) nonbase
compiler frames, including its root; every frame contributes at most
v*(1+floor(log2 n)) raw contexts. Consequently

    K(n)<=v*m*(1+floor(log2 n))^2.                       (4a)

Either (4) or (4a), or their minimum, is valid. With the current
m=145^1024,v=1024,N=2^H, (4a) gives K_star in (1). K_star has exactly7455
bits and2244 decimal digits, so the coefficient is encoded and checked
without constructing N. It is strictly smaller than (4)'s coefficient:
m<2^8192, H+1<2^46 and8192+46<H imply m*(H+1)<2^H. The fractional
contraction and O(log n) compiler-frame depth retain #4586's credit;
the current numerical context application is the present contribution.

Across ell unfinished ordinary nodes at most K_star*ell contexts survive
without an endpoint reset. This counts source dependence depth, not the
total expanded unit count U or the number of live banks.

## 4. Fixed-root provenance and entrance-relative reset

At a completed logical cut, for each external row r write the interpreted
field as

    y_r=sum_a c_a(r)*U_a(r)*x_(origin_a(r)).             (5)

Each original x is an arbitrary complete root selected-coordinate vector.
The origin selects an original field/external row or the zero sentinel;
it never inspects a selected input coordinate. Each U is a Gaussian-dyadic
Clifford on the **same d-bit root factor**. Coefficients, U and origins may
depend on r and static role history. There need not be one U tensor I_ROW.

Initially the expansion has coefficient1, or is empty for owned zero.
COPY copies the expansion into an owned zero. A child acts on that exact
expansion, preserving correlated input provenance. CLEAR deletes it under
the separate ideal reset premise. At the ordinary root, additional source slots are original inputs of mass1.
At nested compiler or ordinary entries they are distinct actual entrance
fields/fibers carrying the inherited root mass and debt. They are never
reinitialized as fresh mass1 inputs. Original padded INPUTS use the zero
origin sentinel; a current padded field can acquire nonzero terms during
unfinished mixing and is discarded only after the completed endpoint.

The actual ordinary split in 05-layers and batched-complex-rows is u=b*g+w.
It changes only external row/origin labels, retaining complete root fibers.
No power-of-two g is required. #6878 embeds every occurrence with a full
ambient coordinate permutation, not a restriction to its contact support.
Its role maps act on complete fields and are undone. Hence every emplaced
native gate/restored endpoint composes U on the original root axes, including
axes a descendant currently calls spectators.

Relative to arbitrary entrance rows, the raw caller has coefficients in
(1/16)Z and l1 norm<=8500, for stored rows and separate scalar products.
Substitution of entrance expansions of common mass cap M therefore gives
mass<=8500M and at most four additional exterior denominator bits. This is
the triangle inequality, even for repeated origins and correlated centers.
It does not require independence of values or tensor-pure inputs.

The completed raw endpoint acts independently on each productive entrance
row by one positive Gaussian target and clears owned outputs. Thus its
**temporary** scalar debt resets to its entrance expansion, not to globally
zero debt. This endpoint property propagates through chronological products,
completion, integer source/exit substitution and the uniform compiler by
rank induction. Ordinary recursion propagates it by selected-width induction.
These ideal equalities are established before choosing numerical formats.
Only unfinished contexts can therefore remain in (5).

A role swap takes (x,y) through (x+y,y), (x+y,-x), (y,-x), (y,x). Its maximum
mass is twice the entrance cap, with zero denominator debt. A complete swap
is just a permutation. Since no other swap or child interrupts it, only
one such factor2 can be unfinished; it does not multiply once per depth.
With t=K_star*ell we consequently have

    c_a in 2^(-4t) Z[i], sum_a |c_a|<=2*8500^t.         (6)

The actual exterior coefficients are rational; allowing Gaussian integers
is harmless. Unit phases can be retained inside U. No operator sum is
incorrectly tensor-powered.

## 5. Grid, magnitude and noncircular exact simulation

Use the credited Gaussian-dyadic Clifford lemma reproduced in C4 §4. A
Clifford basis row has affine support of size2^k, k<=d, with equal nonzero
magnitude2^(-k/2). Every literal gate, including its absolute phase, has
Gaussian-dyadic entries. Write a reduced entry as (a+ib)/2^m with at least
one of a,b odd. Its norm gives a²+b²=2^(2m-k). If exactly one is odd the
right exponent is0; if both are odd it is1, by reduction modulo4. Hence
m=ceil(k/2)<=ceil(d/2). Products stay in this exact Clifford gate group.
No denominator is charged anew at each completed native child.

Combining this entry denominator, (6), and the original p-grid proves F in
(1). A unitary row has l1 norm<=2^(d/2). Original complex inputs have modulus
strictly below sqrt(2)*2^h. Every real/imaginary component is strictly below

    2*8500^t*sqrt(2)*2^(h+d/2).

Since8500<2^14, J=h+ceil((d+1)/2)+14t+1 strict magnitude bits suffice.
A signed F-grid integer needs F+J+1 bits. The identity
ceil(d/2)+ceil((d+1)/2)=d+1 gives exactly W in (1).

First prove the well-founded **ideal** invariant; then induct on that finite
execution to simulate on the fixed grid. For each scalar a/b, multiply the
stored integer by a, then check divisibility by b before division. The
separately bounded ideal scalar product proves that division exact, including
before ADD. For a C pair, retain both old complex entries, form complete
four-term integer numerators, and divide by2 exactly. Literal scalar
numerators have magnitude<=16 and denominators<=16; compiler swaps add
only signs and sums. W+6 safely covers products, complete sums and signs
before division, as in C4's actual arithmetic. Both declared Q_F quantizers
return grid values unchanged. Arbitrary nonreachable W-bit inputs are not
admitted by this argument.

At a partially completed in-place scan, each stored component is from the
old state or the completed ideal operation. This proves component bounds
without a false vector Clifford claim about the masked intermediate state.
No later instruction consumes it until the scan returns. Approximate child
returns, termwise-rounded C summands or exposed partial fields are different
programs and do not receive this conclusion.

    F_required=p+ceil(d/2)+4t <= p+d = F_simple,

## 6. Discriminators and why the typing matters

Let P=|0><0| on an active selected bit, and let C have diagonal(1+i)/2.
Then (P C P)^j has (1+i)^j/2^j in its00 entry, of reduced denominator
2^ceil(j/2). At j=3 it exceeds the one-bit Clifford cap2, with no exterior
scalar division. P is an active-coordinate projector, not a complete-row
selector. This exact countercontrol explains why ancestor remainder axes
stay in the root factor and only the true external ROW can be split.

Nested interrupted ADD-swaps can accumulate their factor2; the admitted
whole swap finishes before recursion. The verifier checks its intermediate
mass and reset and rejects an interrupting schedule. It also checks C4's
actual denominator16 coefficient witness against a proposed three-bit
coefficient certificate, rejects an odd exterior division, distinguishes
disjoint replication from increased nesting, and checks zero padding/origin
preservation. These are discriminators, not a universal finite replay.
