# Causal frame ascent and descent

The scalar word, source mixing, gauges, gauge-read chronology, roots and persistent stock are fixed. Each scalar operation is assigned one nondegenerate rational subspace for the address form `G = I - J/9`. The finite obligations are that its conservative value support lies in that subspace and that both participating physical role chains remain nested.

The original logical node frame is a sufficient operation frame, but it is not an upper bound imposed by these obligations. An enlarged operation frame is legal when both physical roles can enter it and subsequently reach their prescribed frames. The independent checker therefore verifies these actual obligations for every final operation, rather than requiring all replacements to descend from their original logical node frames.

For a proposed downward move, the search takes the full same-frame ancestor closure of one operation. It spans every incoming frame and every conservative source vector used by the component. For an upward move, it takes the same-frame successor closure and intersects every outgoing frame. Singular candidates are discarded unless a tested intermediate nondegenerate frame exists. Every accepted move is checked for exact containment in its incoming and outgoing neighbours. Duplicate edges are retained because two physical roles create two separately charged transitions.

Let the old boundary rank jumps be `old`, the new jumps be `new`, and let `a=1/1600`. The paid profile has an ideal contribution proportional to `sum r^(1-a)` and a fallback contribution proportional to the number of positive-width children. Every local role transition gives three children. After cancelling the positive common factor `3*m^a/(W*m)`, the improvement is

```
sum(old r^(1-a)) - sum(new r^(1-a))
  + (32*m^2 / 10^16) * (positive_old - positive_new).
```

`search.py` accepts a move only when an exact rational lower enclosure of this expression is strictly positive. Interior equal-frame edges have zero rank on both sides. All other profile terms and `W` are unchanged, so this is the complete changed paid objective, including its fallback bill. It is an optimization at one fixed trial saving; the final exact paid supplier is separately recomputed from the entire recounted profile.

`verify.py` does not trust the search history. It validates the exported plan's parameter, index ordering and types, integer coordinates and basis independence; verifies every final frame's nondegeneracy and source support; recounts every nested physical chain and all target/source charges; and checks the preserved telescoping deficit. It then replays every source, target and arbitrary dirty-register column over F2 and over the defining integer decoder. Negative controls must reject malformed plans, illegal frames, omitted compensation and broken partner delivery.

For every new integer basis `B`, `prime_check.py` computes the exact determinant of `9*B*B^T - (B*1)*(B*1)^T`. Each nonzero determinant is divided by the displayed small-prime powers, leaving a positive remainder below `2^80`. Thus every prime factor is below the retained prime lower bound. The denominator of the actual Gram determinant is a power of 9, so every retained prime greater than `2^80` sees a nondegenerate frame. The unchanged source-frame exclusions remain inherited.

The paid moment includes `32*m^2` fallback children on a fraction `10^-16` of every child edge. Rational interval arithmetic proves adjacent accepted/rejected points on the `10^-18` coarse-saving grid, then pays the least strict atom toll on the `10^-24` grid. The construction remains conditional on the inherited all-size compiler, tensor, internally borrowed/restored row and analytic interfaces. This finite module makes no final multiplication-bound, global-optimality or practical-runtime claim.
