## The move, in plain language
* **What 1/3 and 1/5 are.** Until now every coefficient of a native word is a power of two or a ratio of powers of two. Here a coefficient may also have a denominator 3 or 5 (and products up to 1125), and a SCALE gate may be by such a unit. The word has **4,125 such events** (60 SCALE, 4,065 ADD), **8,250 scans per call**, priced separately from the reviewed scalar count.
* **The 2^k ± 1 arithmetic.** 3 = 2² − 1 and 5 = 2² + 1, so multiplying by either is one shift and one add or subtract. Exact division by an odd d is one pass over the bits of the stored integer from the least significant bit with a carry below d; a nonzero remainder is flagged at the end of the pass (`division_pass.py`). To run a coefficient a/b with b = 2^x 3^y 5^z: multiply by a, check divisibility by 3^y 5^z, divide.
* **Why nothing is rounded.** Every root input is multiplied by S = 1125 = 3² 5³. S times every prefix row and every product q·row (formed before an ADD) then has dyadic entries, so every checked division is exact; at the exit the division by S is exact because every public row is dyadic. 1125 is the least certificate among its divisors by 3 and by 5, and the 3-adic and 5-adic heights are 2 and 3 with no other prime. Two execution forms exist (S per call, or S^K once at the boundary) and `PROOF.md` section 2 states both.

## What is declared as a framework change (details in `PROOF.md`)

* **Penalty:** none in S; T and P change as counted. Width only (above).
* **Premise:** exactness is **conditional on exact children** (shared with the dyadic words). A truncated child return is noticed by the divisibility check only once enough bits are dropped: at F = 33 a truncation of 13 low bits per MOVE is not seen, 14 and 24 are rejected (`physical_exact.py`); the dyadic #6931 word under a truncation of 44 bits at F = 60 is wrong on 830 of 1,441 rows. * **Event bound:** this package declares its own bound of 71,069 events; the 26,000-event cap of retained verifiers of other packages is not applied.
* **Odd-grid precedent:** the note of #104 is pinned in `certificates/selected-result.json` under `proof_supplements`; the same method with divisor 3 is in `paired-cube-assembly.tex:87-92`. Every precedent is a caller-level centre with an exact child; no admitted native word uses odd scalars. The odd-grid reviews in merged archives are contributor notes, not maintainer validation.

## Disclosures
* **The word is a full rebuild.** Per-move attribution is not isolated; the gain is measured on the whole word against #6932's word.
* **Seven ADD mutants are not caught.** Of 60,979 ADD doublings (and sign flips) 60,972 are caught by the second engine; the other seven, events 32482, 32993, 37692, 40002, 40003, 40025 and 42613, leave the exact endpoint unchanged under both the doubling and the negation: six are dead stores into owned registers cleared before any read, and event 42613 is a temporary pair that cancels downstream (`mutants.py` shows each by exact replay). They are equivalent mutants, not holes.
* **The truncated-child control is weak below 14 bits.** At F = 33 it rejects g = 14 and g = 24 and passes g = 13 (the dropped bits are zero there); it does not catch every truncation.
* **Width-2 coverage is partial** (above and `PROOF.md` section 7); the integer run is one column with generic commuting frames, not true Clifford frames and not tensor width 2.
* **Caller majorants are quoted.** The 362 t < d form uses a caller mass of at most 2^120.35 and a path height of 157 bits computed on #6932's caller by a script that is not part of this package; they were not recomputed on this package's caller.
* **Adjustments to retained checkers, exactly:** `native.py` lines 65-67 (power-of-two denominator and numerator tests replaced by 2,3,5-smooth tests, and a helper `_sm` at lines 15-20) and the `proof` string at line 139; `verify_relaxed.py` and `verify_strict.py` lines 36-37 (same relaxation, helper at lines 7-12); `verify_host3_odd.py` against `verify_host3_dyadic.py` (docstring; helper lines; the SCALE clause; the ADD and COPY clause with the S certificate and the pre-ADD product test; the public-row test). The strict verifier still rejects the word at exactly the 23 owned-ascent events and nothing else. The physical streams (`physical.py`, `caller_physical.py`) are unmodified.
* **Not claimed:** maintainer acceptance of the clause, of A6, of the per-port root, of LAMBDA priced as ordinary work, or of the BIT admission; the type B caller rewrite; a rounded child return; no adjoint physical replay; no streams at tensor width 2 or more; no human has reviewed the word or this package.
