## What the price plane must represent

For a source-bound candidate X, let T and P be its **actual complete** productive and paid rank histograms. At complex saving c, use

`M_X(c) = sum_r T[r] * r^(1-c) - sum_r P[r] * r^(1-c)`.

This is a necessary recursive-price screen and one premise of admission. It is not the entire invoice. Track six coupled planes:

1. **Paid and productive:** full controller occurrences, main targets, independent helper targets, thin queries, idle jobs, sacrificed outputs, aliases, completion, splitting, padding and rounding. A surrendered or copied helper is not productive credit. Histograms carry word/controller/source hashes and a realizability label.
2. **Ordinary operations:** all preparation, scalar arithmetic, COPY/CLEAR, address work, chart changes, routing, movement, boundaries, parking and eventual restoration. Fixed overhead may be absorbed only by the source's demonstrated finite-template argument. Do not insert a raw scalar count into a rank moment without a justified conversion, or silently set it to zero.
3. **Stock and ownership:** simultaneously live independent payloads, disjoint owned banks, suspended children, ordinary BIT and complex stocks with their required product, fresh axes, spectator support, legal erasures and zero returns. Same rank is not the same physical job.
4. **Precision and tape:** fresh expanded `W,P,O,R,G,N`, magnitude/denominator growth, rounding/child errors, fixed tapes, setup degrees and a newly instantiated eventual threshold. A raw local cap is not an expanded cap. Reuse of generic source lemmas requires an explicit applicability argument.
5. **Compiler and reserves:** tested saving, paid ratio, original and completion tails, product power, cutoff/floor, rounding, shifted exit, complement capacity, uniform gap, finite height and adjacent integer-grid tests. A raw root is never an attained endpoint.
6. **Ordinary BIT and outer transfer:** authenticated stronger BIT admission, actual conversion and stock domains, all applicable 54 strict plus four functional predicates, and the same five assumptions. Re-evaluate a changed interface's predicate inventory instead of hard-coding 58 as a universal theorem.

Each cost is `proved`, `constructively_upper_bounded`, `optimistically_relaxed`, or `unknown`; every entry names evidence and invalidation conditions. Unknown is never zero. Maintain separate labels `MODEL_ONLY`, `WITNESS_READY`, `PHYSICAL_CHECKED`, `COMPLETE_BILL_READY`, `FUNDED_CONDITIONAL`, `PUBLIC_REVIEWABLE`, and `SELECTED` (read only). A model can be rejected by a favorable upper bound; a passing model cannot be promoted without its witness and complete bill.

Price the existing source family with its own certified formulas: `alpha=P(t)/T(t)`, `gamma=P(t+u)/(cutoff^u*T(t))`, and `delta=target_count*floor/(cutoff^c*T(t))`, then its tail/rounding, exit and compiler conditions. Parameter generalization is allowed only over the domain covered by those source lemmas. For a changed law, expose a new model ID and proof obligation rather than forcing the old calculator to accept it.
