# P112 endpoint dimension and linear-lift obstruction

**Scoped negative result. No new kappa.** Any nondegenerate two-plane
representation of the selected P112 interaction graph needs ambient dimension
at least 18. This bound is sharp. For arbitrary coupled linear endpoint lifts,
the graph forces a tensor form in every fibre dimension. In the retained
supplier and controller, these lifts cannot improve the complex moment exponent.

The proof permits arbitrary native factorizations of each individual retained
role-stream edge, rank-zero adapters, and any number of extra coordinates.
Extra frames can be slanted and nonmonotone. The scalar-gate boundaries and
physical live stream paths remain fixed. Joint children that encode several
arrays or combine different frozen edges are outside the theorem.

This closes a concrete coverage-law proposal: replacing each old unpaid line
by a plane does not give two free coordinates at the old geometric cost.
Even arbitrary nonlinear plane labels need twice the old local dimension.
For linear lifts, the complete rank cost grows with the unpaid dimension.
The broader question of a new word on nonlinear dimension-18 labels stays open.

## Results and full cost

[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) gives three arguments.

1. A 32-vertex subgraph forces ambient dimension at least 18 for arbitrary
   nondegenerate two-plane labels. The form can be any nondegenerate symmetric
   binary form. Alternating planes are allowed. The tensor construction attains 18.
2. The 5,824 ordered graph-edge tensors span dimension 80 of the 81-dimensional
   bilinear-form space. Thus a linear lift with fibre dimension r has pullback
   form `I9 tensor D`, where D is nondegenerate. It needs dimension at least 9r.
3. Let M(p) be the complete reference moment. With z extra coordinates and
   `lambda=73r/(73r+z)`, every lift in the declared class satisfies
   `M_lift(p) >= lambda M(p) + 1-lambda` for every `0<p<=1`.
   The proof uses exact Fourier rank, rank subadditivity, concavity, and a
   signed sum along the 14,336 persistent live paths. Private centre descents
   cannot cancel that sum. No private-frame return condition is imposed.

For r=2 and z=0, the optimistic complete width is 146 and each reference
native rank doubles. The normalized moment is exactly the old moment. This
includes source access, all 480 helper roles per call, the eight copied
centres, restoration, all 144 complete calls, and every idle child. Additional
scalar, chart, movement, precision and finite-invoice costs are nonnegative.
No part of the decoder, centre bill, or cleanup is removed from the comparison.

## Investigation and overlap

Before the experiment, three routes were considered: enlarge the old lines,
choose arbitrary graph plane labels, or use nonprojection labels. The first
proof target was the full coupled linear class, with a separate attempt at
arbitrary graph plane labels. The linear class was declared before computation.

The broader attempt used eight mutually orthogonal anchor planes. A small
one-thread constraint experiment found no dimension-16 assignment for the
24 edge vertices with standard anchor planes. That restricted solver result
was not enough. The rank-sum proof now excludes dimensions 16 and 17 for all
allowed anchor forms and all plane assignments; no solver premise remains.
The dimension-18 tensor example stops any stronger dimension-only exclusion.
A cost theorem for every nonlinear dimension-18 assignment would require new
control of its internal frames or a new scalar-word bound. This is the concrete
remaining obstacle, not an inference from a failed search.

The deterministic final certificate has 80 edge-tensor basis rows and a
32-vertex graph witness. Its scope is complete for those finite premises.
There is no random seed and no timed search in the decisive command. The
working experiment stopped when the sharp dimension proof and the all-size
linear-lift cost proof were complete.

## Open next questions

Can nonlinear dimension-18 plane labels support a lower-cost complete word?
They meet the sharp dimension bound, so a dimension argument alone cannot
answer this. A first step is to classify their internal frame intersections
on the 32-vertex witness and then price a scalar identity on all 112 ports.
The missing step is a construction or a cost theorem for such assignments.

Can a new controller combine different retained edges or arrays while keeping
complete source, volume, phase, hosting and restoration accounting? Such a
construction changes this theorem's boundary contract. No compatible saving
from the other sprint lanes is assumed.
