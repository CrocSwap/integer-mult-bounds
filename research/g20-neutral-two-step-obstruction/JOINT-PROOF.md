Fix the final #5708 scalar word, its public/owned boundary and all 2,369
retained directed primitive routes. Begin with any of the fourteen neutral

conjugation, including zero-contact commuting cancellation. Keep every

For every `0<t<1`, the minimum complete local paid-query moment change is

|---|---:|

Route closure admits 280 paths rejected by the direct table. Every one has

## Why the path relaxation is sufficient

The [retained-route proof](MULTIHOP-PROOF.md) establishes an integer potential
`L` on the 636 route-graph frames. For every directed edge of rank `r`,
`r=L(target)-L(source)>0`. The graph therefore has no directed cycle. Its
exact transitive closure contains 23,560 positive reachable pairs.

For a path whose positive ranks are `r_1,...,r_k`, strict subadditivity gives

`sum_i r_i^t >= (sum_i r_i)^t = (L(target)-L(source))^t`.

Thus charging one fictitious query at the endpoint rank difference is a
lower bound for **every** permitted realization. It is an optimistic bill,
not a newly supplied direct native operator. A request outside the exact
closure has no permitted path, irrespective of a search cutoff.

The normalized scalar row associated with a role is independent of its
chosen route under the inherited invertible frame/telescoping contract.
Every nonzero requested transition is therefore paid at least its collapsed
rank power, while zero rows remain zero and have no paid query. They still
require a reachable route. Zero-source ADDs omit both frame visits exactly
as in the source emitter. Public final returns and owned teardown are
included. Ordinary work on a zero row is not claimed free.

The source program uses direct routes, so its reference histogram is exact.
For each rewritten program, subtract that histogram from the optimistic
collapsed histogram. Every nonzero resulting shape is either termwise
positive or a positive rank partition minus its summed rank. The same strict
subadditivity argument makes each shape positive for all `0<t<1`. Therefore
the actual path bill cannot improve the reference bill.

All 297 zero lower bounds request direct retained edges on every nonzero
role transition. Choosing those edges attains zero paid change. The joint
checker also verifies that these are exactly the previously certified
direct-neutral paths. Consequently their per-role ordered paid traces remain
the source traces, as separately checked by `trace_audit.py`. This proves
sharpness; it does not identify all minimum-cost scalar implementations.

## Scope, attribution and next questions

No new κ. This is a fixed-source paid-query obstruction, not a universal
BIT-supplier barrier. It does not optimize ordinary scalar work or establish
the complete native/caller/compiler/precision/tape/outer bill. All five
selected framework assumptions remain. Native phase correctness and the
route-normalization interface are inherited, not proved from graph ranks.

New primitive routes, moved corrections, other first neutral moves and three
or more rewrites remain open. A new primitive edge requires its exact native
action and complete cost, not merely a connection in this graph. All-depth
neutral exploration requires a justified finite quotient or stopping rule;
increasing word lengths do not establish finite closure.
