## Critical implications checked

The full right action follows by expanding `(g0+qX)S`. Its carry cocycle
follows by inserting and subtracting the intermediate low representative.
With the inverse matcher, both the low representative and the complete
high matrix return. Every row of the high matrix participates; restriction
to a small high submatrix would not establish this result.

In pushforward convention, ingress of role i gives
`(R_i x)(h)=x(h S_i^-1)`. A completed block from i to j therefore reads
`x_i(g S_j S_i^-1)` after return to parent row g in role j. The right action
of GL on itself is free. A nonzero block and arbitrary independent payload
support can distinguish this row from g unless the two reduced matchers
agree. This proves the stated same-row criterion. Diagonal completed
endpoints permit different routes because their foreign dependencies have
already cancelled, regardless of internal role mixing.

The precise weight condition is commutation, rather than a general ban on
fresh dependence. The two orders of scaling and routing see the arrays
at h and `h S_i^-1`, respectively. Equality of these arrays is sufficient;
it is necessary because distinct diagonal units act differently on the
complete address module. Invariance under S_i also gives inverse invariance.
The determinant-based positive control satisfies this condition and is
nonconstant. The actual seven-swap port determinant is -1, so squared
determinant is invariant under those ports; this does not extend to arbitrary
subsequent matchers, as the explicit Z/9 control demonstrates.

The split-projector interchange identities are identities over the address
ring and tensor with the identity on all columns and spectators. Their
induced permutations act on binary payloads; the cancellation uses XOR
there, without confusing payload addition with odd-ring address addition.
The mixed-column reversal is included in the physical slot identification.

The complete-cylinder row map retains the previous high coordinates and
unused stock and admits an inverse by Euclidean division. Its volume divisor
is L while its stock factor is LH. This correctness statement consumes the
separately stated allocation, fresh-cylinder, suspension and route tariffs.
The finite ideal-job identity and subsequent strict-width implementation
induction are distinct; neither is a proof of the other by finite tests.
