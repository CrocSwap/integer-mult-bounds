## Sufficient theorem

Fix an admitted framed linear reversible word, source injections, and an observation after a specified gate. Write its scalar observation as P(X)+sum_r d_r a_r, where a_r is the actual entry value of logical scratch role r. Entries may be arbitrarily correlated with each other, source fields, and other workspace. Suppose every r with d_r != 0 has an available genuine initial physical head at frame zero. Internal aliased recipient births must have coefficient zero. Any terminal role folded into a receiver must instead be restored to a genuine zero-head role. Suppose the observation is at a nondegenerate frame F, and F is contained in each intended receiver cap used for delivery.

Allocate one new arbitrary dirty role c at frame zero. At zero execute c <- c - sum_r d_r a_r. Execute the unchanged old word. Move c monotonically from zero to F and, immediately after the observation gate, add its old observation row to c. Because both incident registers use the identical representative at F, their old dirty terms cancel exactly. The new row is the fresh query P(X) plus the transported independent initial c. Its only remaining scratch channel therefore has gauge zero.

For a delivery at cap T containing F, move this copy to T and use the ordinary same-frame read. Its exact initial-c adjoint correction is executed while the receiver remains at frame zero. Multiple singleton copies may be used at different receiver caps. The reads can be inserted at literal existing receiver cuts, so target entropy does not increase. After reads, complete each role to full frame and reverse the combined old and new scalar gate tape in reverse chronological order. In particular, inverse old gates after an observation are performed before reversing its inserted copy, and the initial negative dirty reads are undone only after the old word has restored its entry fields. Full-frame inverse gates restore all old and new scratch.

The identity is -(sum d_r a_r)+[P(X)+sum d_r a_r]=P(X), with the independent c handled by its own zero-frame adjoint. It holds over arbitrary coefficient rings and for arbitrary entire arrays. It uses linearity and identical physical representatives; no rank-one spatial support or clean scratch is presumed. The initial negative reads are ordinary same-frame scalar shears. They have zero frame increment, but their fixed implementation/routing cost still belongs to the inherited selector toll.

This is an exact finite-word observability construction: gauge eligibility is tested against the requested observation response, not only against the old output response. The control-theory analogue is passage to an observable quotient; no numerical truncation or approximate low-rank assumption is used.
