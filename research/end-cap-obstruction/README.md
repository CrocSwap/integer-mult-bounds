# The end-cap wall (no κ claim): why copies are forced in the E8 skeleton's verifier model

> **What is claimed, and what is not.**
> **Scope.** The k8 / #1018-type local word of the E8 complex unit: 112 targets with ports u_t ∈ F₂⁹ (112 distinct odd-weight vectors with u·u = 1, in 14 blocks of 8), frames are subspaces of F₂⁹, the verifier rules R1 (common frame at an ADD), R3 (monotone frames) and E (every target register ends in perp(u_t), `gk_word.py:116`, `:102`, `:117`; `audit_local.py:137-138`, `:122`, `:115`, `:142`), fixed ports, fixed decoder and fixed targets. The deletion experiments are run on the **gauge-forward word** of that skeleton (the stage that precedes the port, cold44 and layer stages of the chain), regenerated by `verify.sh` from vendored inputs; the census, bound and window rows are run on the #1018 word itself.
> **Result.** (T, argued from the cited rule lines; instances checked) A direct read `y_t += s` at time τ forces the final frame of y_t to contain D_s(τ), the join of every frame register s has had before τ. Since y_t must end inside perp(u_t), the read is legal only if D_s(τ) ⊆ perp(u_t); a word that breaks this has to pay one of four prices: a version of s that is held or reconstructed (a), a reordering that puts the offending operations on s after τ (b), a descent or FULL end on the target, which costs a rank unit per call (c), or a descent on s itself (d). (M) On the skeleton, deleting any of the 24 legal single S3 packet copies, the 16 hub copies, or any of five larger families (up to 80 copies) makes between 4 and 64 targets end FULL, one dimension outside perp(u_t); every one of the 30 distinct deleted-copy words regenerated here passes the generator's own frame check and fails the verifier's end rule E (`gk_word.profile` is run on two of them; the end-cap replay on all). (P) One unit costs −2.819% κ per call, linear to 32 units, and the exact certificate disappears at 35, against a helper worth +0.15% (all 112 S4 copies together: ≤ +3.5152%), so a copy is about 19 times cheaper than a unit and the copies are economically forced whenever the obstruction fires. (E) The escapes we could build are closed in their stated models: one-accumulator version reconstruction (0 orders in an exhaustive search of the 20-21 operation cluster, four absorption variants), the snapshot hoist and slide (0 of 64, 0 of 86), rematerialising the 161 never-read helpers (0 of 65), coded target pairs and cold-slot holds (0 of 224), the frame-conflict bound (111 helpers forced, 113 attained by the greedy cover; it does not close the rest; #1140 proves a stronger bound on the same word, see E4).
> **Not claimed.** No κ gain, no word, no proof that the 268 S3/S4 copies are necessary for every skeleton or every op multiset. The theorem is a necessary-condition and pricing statement for the verifier model above. It does not exclude version reconstruction with **two accumulators** (+1 helper per group, which B253 estimates eats most of the credit; not searched), a **new h = 8 family** with a different pairing (C.6 shows none exists at E8 window ≤ 9 with the current decoder), **joint deletion and reordering** at scale (single hoists and slides only), asymmetric pairings that unpin Lemma R, non-projection rational labels, or helpers (they have arbitrary ends via `helper_final`; the wall applies to registers that feed targets). The deletion rows are at the gauge-forward stage: no deletion word passes the chain, so no κ is computed for one and the later stages are not run on them.

- **ARGUED**: a proof or file:line argument in this README; every quantity it uses is itself checked where stated;

- **S4 Prices** use the package's own `price_profile.py` (two exact moment engines, outer 47, PR589 bit supplier) on the frozen #1018 invoice (72 calls, idle profile of `PLAN.json`); the 2560 deficit pin is replaced by the honest deficit, so added rank lowers the deficit (card B258; nothing else of the package is edited). - **S5 Convention bookkeeping.** "y_t ends in perp(u_t)" is three rules stacked (card B258): the swap contract (a complement hyperplane, SPEC), the choice w_t = u_t (convention, pinned by Lemma R), and no descent edge (convention, priced). Relaxing the second is vacuous on every measured word (row T4); the third is the unit price of row P1.

## Theorem and lemma

**Notation.** For a register s and a time τ, D_s(τ) is the join of the start frame of s and of the frames of all operations that touch s (as destination or as source, since R1 climbs the read operand too) strictly before τ. Under R1 and R3 the frame of s at τ equals D_s(τ). cap_t = perp(u_t).

**Lemma (read drag; ARGUED from R1, R3; anchors CHECKED).** If y_t reads s directly at τ (an ADD `y_t ±= c·s` with declared frame F), then F ⊇ D_s(τ) and the end frame of y_t contains F. This holds whether s was written or only read before τ, because a read climbs s as well.
*Proof.* R1 climbs both operands to F and R3 asserts `cur[s] ⊆ F`, so F ⊇ cur[s] = D_s(τ); monotonicity gives end(y_t) ⊇ F. ∎

**Theorem (end-cap obstruction; ARGUED, instances CHECKED).** In the verifier model of S2, let y_t read s directly at τ. Then
1. *(necessity)* the word is legal (E as the assertion of `gk_word.py:102`, `:117`, unpriced) only if D_s(τ) ⊆ cap_t; when D_s(τ) ⊄ cap_t the final climb of y_t to cap_t fails. Prices (c) and (d) below are the priced departures from E and R3.
2. *(the four prices)* If D_s(τ) ⊄ cap_t, a word that still delivers the value of s at τ to y_t must pay at least one of
   - **(a) a version:** a register r ≠ s that holds the value of s at τ (or is reconstructed from the operands of the writes since a copy) and is read by y_t at a time with D_r ⊆ cap_t. A copy taken before the first offending operation needs no write to s in between; otherwise the copy must receive the later writes (it is then a second register, the existing S3 copy) or the value must be rebuilt by forward substitution (extra ADDs);
   - **(b) a reorder:** all offending operations on s are moved after τ. This is legal only for operations that commute with the read (other reads of s, not writes);
   - **(c) a unit on the target:** a FULL sink with M_in(X_t) = 0 or a descent FULL → cap_t of d = 1: at least one rank unit per call (that the minimum is one unit, rank-intrinsic, is from cards B29/B35 of the swarm, not shipped: RECORDED), −2.819% κ each (row P1);
   - **(d) a descent on s itself:** d ≥ 1 per call (R3 is priced, not forbidden); reader descents were registered dead in earlier swarm rounds (R39-H, R40-C, J24-C1, about 2 rank units per call; not shipped: RECORDED).
3. *(complement sinks do not rescue)* The set of admissible sinks is {perp(w) : w·u_t = 1} (S2). A final frame that contains u_t lies in none of them. On every violating target of every measured word the final frame is FULL (dimension 9) and contains u_t (row T4).

*Proof of 2.* By the Lemma end(y_t) ⊇ D_s(τ) ⊄ cap_t. Every legal word delivers the value of s at τ to y_t through a last hop, a register r read directly by y_t at some τ′; by part 1, D_r(τ′) ⊆ cap_t. If r = s then D_s(τ′) ⊆ cap_t while D_s(τ) ⊄ cap_t, so τ′ ≠ τ and the offending operations are after τ′ (b); if r ≠ s, r holds or rebuilds the value of s (a). Otherwise the word ends y_t outside cap_t, which is (c) or (d). ∎

**Corollary (versions; ARGUED).** If s is written after the offending operation and before τ, a copy taken before the drag carries the wrong value. Then (a) needs a *version*, not a snapshot: the copy also receives the later writes, or the value is reconstructed from the operands of those writes. Reconstruction is value-exact with no per-operand frame floor (row E1), but it needs the operands to be joined in an accumulator, which is where the cross-pair join of row E1 bites.

**What this says about the skeleton (rows M, P, G).** The ≈ 268 S3/S4 packet copies of the k8 word (cards B172, B255; `cards/B260` finds 248 helper-from-register events with readers on both sides) are the version register of (a) for the readers whose targets cap the packet. Deleting a copy lets the packet serve its readers directly: its frame then grows by the late ops of the other readers (the drag), and four targets per copy end one dimension out. Price (c) is about 19 times the value of a copy (row P3; 20 to 70 times under the other conventions of the cards), so (a) is paid, and the copies are forced.

## The deletion experiment, in detail (rows M1-M5, T4)

`cards/B252/cgx_y.py` is the #1005 gauge-forward generator with switches (`STAG` stagger of the hub blocks, `GLAZY` lazy widening of explicit-frame ops, `CF` the S3 copies to delete (16 copies of the hub packets of blocks (0,1,2,3) and (4,5,6,7)), `RO` and `WRC` the read-only and write-read copy roles of the larger families). `cards/B253/caps.py` replays the declared frames and lists the target registers whose final frame is not inside perp(u_t) with the first operation that breaks it; `prof.py` runs `gk_word.profile` (the native verifier model: all of `gk_word.py:100-117`); `cards/B258/endcap.py` classifies the violators (dimension, dimension of the intersection with perp, whether u_t is in the frame). The word names of `verify.sh`: `base` (switches off), `cf0` (copy 0, STAG = 0), `all16` (16 hub copies, STAG = 1), `rr`, `wr`, `rrss`, `rrwr`, `all80` (the larger families), `nogl` (GLAZY = 0), and the 32 single-copy words `sw_STAG_CF`.

Why the pre-cap results of earlier cards (+1.3% to +2.0% for the hub copies) are void: the generator's check only looks at the explicit frames of the working ops against hyper(P_t); the final climb to perp(u_t) is what `gk_word.profile` and `audit_local` enforce. The CF = 0 deletion makes packet 283 serve its readers directly; `add 124 -= 283/2` moves from B12 (283 at dim 2) to B245 (dim 4); 283 then grows 5 → 7 → 8 through its other readers, helper 591 += 283 enters at dim 7 (the base word has 592 at dim 5) and `170 -= 591/2` needs F ⊇ cur(170) + cur(591), dimension 9. The deleted copy was exactly what absorbed that growth (`trace283`, `trace170` in the logs).

## Open problems and escapes

The escapes are ranked as in the card notes. None is closed in general.

1. **Two accumulators.** Version reconstruction with a second accumulator register (pair A, pair B), so that terms join only inside their pair and y170 reads both: avoids the cross-pair join of E1, costs +1 helper per group (a width-inventory change and an owner re-plan; B253 estimates that this eats most of the +1.3% .. +2.0% credit). The rescue check is to add a second accumulator (`ops b* → 598b`) to `cards/B256/dfs.py`; it was not run.
2. **A new h = 8 family** (puncture: merge two of the 9 source lines, so ports differing by e_i + e_j coincide). For a reader triple one merge fixes at most one pair, the triple stays dim 7 against the final frame 9; it changes h = 9 → 8, so ports collide and Lemma R and distinctness are untested; `format-limits-corrected` C.6 shows no asymmetric pairing at window ≤ 9 for the current decoder, so this needs a different decoder. (B259, RECORDED.)
3. **Joint deletion and reordering.** Singles (E2) and the single-copy cluster (E1) are closed; a joint delete + reorder over many copies (cap-ascending drag ordering, B256/B257) has not been run. The hoist and slide results do not transfer: they fix the multiset and move one op family.
4. **Snapshot hoist on the final word / join-frame relay.** Moving the read of s to a time where the frame of x is nested in the frame of s, or copying at the Y-partner frame (a relay at the join frame): the join relay adds mass at least equal to the copy it removes (B249).
5. **Reader-set-aware decoder.** Choosing the u_t so that the readers of a hub are collinear or of low rank (pairs of weight 2 are only 776 of 5,824 cross-block pairs; reader sets are triples). This changes the u_t, outside the scope of Lemma R.
6. **A decoder / motif redesign** that changes the sink interface, or a descent that is priced cheaper than a unit (the one-unit minimum is rank-intrinsic for the current format by cards B29/B35, RECORDED).
7. The frame-conflict bound (E4) leaves ~369 helpers unexplained by frames (about 256 beyond the 224 of #1140). Closing them needs the value or algebra side (rotation lock, rematerialisation cascade) or an ILP over incarnation merging with the real rewrite-op frames; not run.

The 144 S3 copies by consumer role (SS16, rr32, Wr32, WW32, WS8, SW8, rS8, Sr8) were tried under the generator switches (rows M3, M4): WW32 needs the copy for its value, WS/SW/rS/Sr stop at the `second` cap or at block-order cycles that need an event-level order (card B252, RECORDED).
