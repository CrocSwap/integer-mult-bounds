# From repeated matrix units to a clean canonical readout

## Inputs, lift and target

In the four-qubit dictionary of width 13, use the normalized columns $(\operatorname{vec}(W_pU)/4)^{\otimes13}$, where $W_p=X^xZ^z$. An observation is explicitly supplied as an $m$-record sum
$$y=\sum_{\nu=1}^m i^{t_\nu}\bigl(\operatorname{vec} |r_\nu\rangle\langle c_\nu|\bigr)^{\otimes13}.$$
All rows, columns, Gaussian-unit weights and target fields are runtime inputs. Records may repeat and cancel. The output is the real and imaginary coefficient of $A^\dagger y$ for the queried label, each rounded to the nearest multiple of $2^{-p}$ with ties resolved to even. The circuit family is determined only by $m\ge1$ and $p\ge0$.

A monomial Clifford field $M$ maps $|v\rangle$ to $i^{q_M(v)}|Av+u\rangle$ on four-bit vectors. The phase polynomial is
$$q_M(v)=\sum_{a=0}^3 l_a v_a+2\sum_{a<b}d_{ab}v_av_b\pmod4.$$
Its binary linear matrix is supplied as four row masks, with $A\in\mathrm{GL}(4,2)$. The query lift is $U=i^gM_L^*H_b^\sharp M_R$, where $H_b^\sharp$ applies $(1+i)\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)/2$ exactly to the mask bits of $b$. Its support-rank shell is $j=|b|$ because both monomial maps preserve the diagonal symplectic Lagrangian. The Gaussian phase convention matters even though the symplectic map only identifies a fibre. All four-qubit Clifford classes admit such factors. The optional compiler produces factors from a supplied binary symplectic map and can match an additionally supplied literal Gaussian-dyadic lift. Factoring is a separate precomputation when the caller starts with that other representation. Source input validation is a promise of well-formed factors, including nonsingular $A$. The CLI checks their dimensions and admissibility before executing the generated circuit. [Factorization and phase matching](BRUHAT.md)

## Five scales and the sharp denominator

PR #7821 gives the exact canonical coefficient of a single repeated matrix unit as $h_j\overline{\langle r|W_pU|c\rangle}$, with real rational shell weights $h_j$. Put $r'=r+x$, $l=A_Lr'+u_L$, and $v=A_Rc+u_R$ over the binary field. A record is active exactly when $l$ and $v$ agree away from $b$. For an active record the matrix entry is
$$
 i^{g+q_R(c)-q_L(r')+2(z\cdot r'+l\cdot (b\wedge v))}
 ((1+i)/2)^j .
$$
This follows by inserting the two monomial basis images on either side of the partial Hadamard. Conjugating the entry and multiplying by $i^t$ gives an eighth-root direction with angle index
$$
 e=2\bigl(t-g-q_R(c)+q_L(r')+2(z\cdot r'+l\cdot(b\wedge v))\bigr)-j\pmod8 .
$$
Its nonzero real or imaginary components have magnitude $2^{-\lceil j/2\rceil}$. Odd $j$ gives both components and even $j$ gives one. When $h_j<0$ the producer adds four to $e$ before extracting signs.

Let $H=4,116,898,131,384,729,600=7,852,360,022,325\cdot2^{19}$. Putting $a_j=Hh_j/2^{\lceil j/2\rceil}$ gives the following signed integers in order from $j=0$ to $j=4$.
$$
 (71470953728,\quad3372907072,\quad299589952,\quad-12441328,\quad-14688133).
$$
The retained #7772 spectrum and the tridiagonal five-shell projector of #7821 reconstruct the exact rational $h_j$ and certify each integer. The absolute signed-component numerators of every contribution are therefore zero or $|a_j|$. Rank four alone forces the full denominator: take all four Hadamards, both monomials the identity, $p$ the zero Pauli label, $g=t=0$ and $r=c=0$. The canonical value is $14688133/H$, already in lowest terms. Thus $H$ is the least positive constant clearing *both components on the whole promised family*, even for one record. Its odd part is the exact common odd divisor required for this scaled-numerator interface. An unrestricted circuit could choose a different arithmetic representation. [Exact scale reconstruction](check_arithmetic.py) · [Canonical shell theorem](https://github.com/apoursh/integer-mult-bounds/blob/eead545a4ba6c50010fcac0919a9fdc6b14bd673/research/clifford-compact-adjoint-fibres/CANONICAL.md)

For a fixed target in shell $j$, allow arbitrarily many unit records. The attainable coefficient lattice is $(|a_j|/H)(1+i)^{j\bmod2}\mathbb Z[i]$. Choose any nonzero matrix entry and vary $t$ to realize every direction of that parity. For even $j$ the available scaled vectors are the four coordinate unit vectors, so the minimum number of unit records to make $(u,v)$ is $|u|+|v|$. For odd $j$ they are the four diagonals. An attainable pair has $u\equiv v\pmod2$ and needs at least $\max(|u|,|v|)$ steps. At exactly that length, choose each coordinate's signs independently to reach the prescribed value. These statements give the minimal number with arbitrary record coordinates, permitting the empty sum for zero. They distinguish the two parity lattices even when the supplied input format allows null records.

## Producer and signed accumulation

The runtime target logic counts the four mask bits, selects the five integer scales bitwise, and evaluates both affine addresses and quadratic phases for every record. A test on the unaffected bits suppresses excluded records. A three-bit angle selects the real and imaginary positive and negative indicators. For each component the accumulator adds the selected signed magnitude. The carry circuit uses $a+b+c\bmod2=a\oplus b\oplus c$ and outgoing carry $ab\oplus c(a\oplus b)$. Negative addends use the complemented magnitude plus one. With $B=\operatorname{bitlength}(m\max_j|a_j|)$, all partial sums fit signed two's-complement words of $B+1$ bits, including sums that change sign. The final conditional complement and increment produce the two absolute numerator magnitudes with their signs. Only after this exact accumulation does rounding begin. ## Circuit, tape and physical prices

All initially occupied input wires are the fields of the request. Every other site bit starts zero. The constants $a_j$ and the common odd modulus are part of the uniform gate program; a runtime mask selects the scale in logic. The modulus bits are explicitly written with NOT gates and erased at the end. No table of answers or undocumented register content is supplied. The two output lanes and their sign bits are initially zero and occupy named distinct wires. All logical XOR and AND operations in fresh zero wires compile to CNOT and Toffoli. A single wire for one is toggled at the beginning and cleared at the end. Work is retained for reversal rather than silently discarded.

Eight local bits of each site hold logical data and two additional bits are clean routing tracks. The tape head accesses one site or a pair of adjacent sites. Gate cost counts NOT, CNOT and Toffoli separately and the head cost is the sum of absolute displacements between lower site indices of successive gates, including return to site zero. If a logical gate is already adjacent it is emitted directly. Otherwise each remote control copies its bit to a distinct routing track at its home and propagates it by CNOT one adjacent site at a time to the target. After the central local gate, every propagation is undone in reverse. Other occupied logical wires are untouched. For control distance $d>0$ in such a route the two sweeps use exactly $2(d+1)$ CNOT gates, in addition to the central gate. The two tracks accommodate the maximum two controls. Head prices include travel between the two routes and between separate gates. The literal replay meters every elementary gate. A second metering method uses just the first and last anchors on each monotone sweep and agrees exactly. [Local lowering](physical.py)

The conversion block uses the adjacent-site bit program from #7820, embedded at the end of the tape. For each lane, load the magnitude into the appropriate divisor input positions, run nearest-even division of the signed magnitude by $H$ to the requested precision, copy the rounded magnitude to the outputs, and reverse the divider and the load. Compute the output sign as the exact sign gated by a nonzero *rounded* magnitude and clean its work. After both lanes, erase the modulus and reverse the entire numerator program. Each step is a reversible gate sequence. Hence zero-initialized work is restored exactly, including all division remainders, sign flags and input copies, while the inputs and two signed outputs remain. The external CLI prints the stored magnitudes and signs as integers. No rational, nonlinear or random-access arithmetic operation is counted as a single physical gate. For an exact per-size receipt the generator uses $s=\max(19-p,0)$, $n=\max(B,s+1)$, quotient length $q=n+p-19+1$ and $k=\operatorname{bitlength}(7852360022325)=43$. If $A$ logical bit addresses have been allocated in the producer, output and sign work together, the physical tape has $\lceil A/8\rceil+q+k+\max(s,1)+2$ sites. In four fixed qubits, the coordinate and phase circuits per record are bounded, each signed accumulator uses $O(B)$ gates per record, and the output sign calculation uses $O(q)$ gates and wires. Hence the sites $S$ are $O(m(1+\log m)+p+1)$. Local lowering needs at most $O(S)$ elementary gates and head moves per nonlocal gate. Together with four local divider traversals this gives $O(S^2)$ elementary gates and head moves. These are conservative upper bounds for this explicit layout. Runtime values never change its schedule; every input with the same $m,p$ has the same receipt.

At $m=3,p=48$, the supplied nonidentity example has two active terms and one zero. The active terms are $(a,-a)$ and $(-a,-a)$ with $a=777583/257306133211545600$. Real parts cancel and the imaginary parts sum to $-777583/128653066605772800$. The exact final rounded integer is $-1701$ in the imaginary lane, while separate per-record rounding would give $-1702$ (check the signed values with the replay). The complete example uses 458 ten-track sites, 97,574 NOT, 849,046 CNOT and 171,478 Toffoli gates and 1,353,992 head moves. All inputs and every other bit are checked at the endpoint.
