# Finite coverage of every odd-order character counting cut

## Model and inherited necessary inequality

Let H be the subgroup generated by finitely many nonzero rational paid address
matrices A_1,...,A_n. It is free abelian. In F2[H], a paid factor is

`P_j = I + ([A_j] - 1) N_j`,

where N_j is a binary rank-one scalar coupling. Idempotent couplings are
included. Require the full formal endpoint
`diag([C_1],...,[C_w],1,...,1)`, with C_i in H. All additional scalar roles
start dirty and are restored. Free address maps and clean-only endpoint
contracts are outside this model.

For any character chi:H -> mu_odd, where mu_odd is the group of all odd-order
roots of unity in the algebraic closure of F2, specialize `[A] -> chi(A)`.
The finitely generated image is a finite cyclic group of odd order. Every
specialized paid increment has rank at most one and is zero when chi(A_j)=1.
Telescoping the product minus identity over that field proves

`#{j: chi(A_j) != 1} >= #{i: chi(C_i) != 1}`.                 (1)

Repeated targets count with multiplicity. The inequality is independent of
factor order, scalar-role count and restored dirty workspace. This is the
retained projection/telescoping method, including its ternary specialization
in PR2949. The contribution below is a finite complete decision procedure for
all such odd-order cuts. No new rank inequality is claimed.

## Target avoidance in a quotient

Fix a positional subset S of paid calls to kill and let
`L = sum_(j in S) Z A_j`, a possibly unsaturated subgroup of H.
For a subset U of target occurrences put

`d_L(U) = [(L + sum_(i in U) Z C_i) : L]`.

An infinite index is permitted. Define

- `b_L(U) = 0` when d_L(U) is infinite;
- `b_L(U) = 1 / oddpart(d_L(U))` otherwise.

For a target subset T define the exact rational number

`alpha_L(T) = sum_(U subset T) (-1)^|U| b_L(U)`.             (2)

**Avoidance lemma.** There is an odd-order character killing L and nontrivial
on every target occurrence in T if and only if alpha_L(T)>0.
In particular alpha_L(T) lies in [0,1].

Proof. Write the finitely generated quotient as
`H/L = Z^f direct_sum P_2 direct_sum P_odd`, where the last two groups are
finite 2-primary and odd-order groups. Every odd character kills P_2. Call a
target torsion when its free component is zero.

When U contains a nontorsion target, d_L(U) is infinite and its term in (2)
is zero. Otherwise its generated finite subgroup projects onto a subgroup of
P_odd of order oddpart(d_L(U)). A uniform character of P_odd kills that
subgroup with probability its reciprocal. The finite character group has the
same order as P_odd. Inclusion-exclusion therefore identifies alpha_L(T)
with the fraction of characters of P_odd nontrivial on all torsion targets
in T. This establishes the range and the necessary implication.

For sufficiency, fix one such finite-component character. Let D be the
exponent of P_odd. Choose an odd prime p larger than |T|, coprime to D and
not dividing at least one nonzero coordinate of each nontorsion target's
free vector. Only finitely many primes are excluded. Choose a character
`Z^f -> mu_p`. A nontorsion target whose fixed P_odd value is not 1 cannot
become trivial, because mu_D and mu_p intersect only at 1. For any remaining
nontorsion target, triviality excludes one hyperplane in F_p^f. At most |T|
such hyperplanes cannot cover F_p^f since p>|T|. A choice avoiding them exists.
Together with the fixed finite-component character this has cyclic odd image
of order dividing Dp and avoids all of T. When f=0 there are no nontorsion
targets to handle. This proves the lemma without any modulus cutoff.

The same argument explains the term "avoidance density" used by the checker.
It is a fraction of characters on the finite odd torsion component. It need
not equal the fraction at an arbitrarily chosen finite modulus when free
components are present. The certificate is a rational existence certificate,
not an emitted explicit character or a bound on its order.

## Finite complete screening

For every positional paid subset S set `t = n-|S|`. If t>=w, skip it.
Otherwise test every positional target subset T of size t+1 using (2).

If alpha_L(T)>0, the avoidance lemma gives a character killing all calls in
S while retaining at least t+1 targets. At most t paid calls survive, so (1)
fails. The checker reports S, T, t and alpha_L(T).

Conversely, if a character violates (1), take S to be exactly the paid
occurrences it kills. It retains t=n-|S| paid calls and at least t+1 target
occurrences. Any t+1 of these are a tested T with positive alpha_L(T).
Thus the procedure rejects if and only if some odd-order character violates
(1). Positional subsets preserve repeated paid and target occurrences.
There are finitely many tests, at most 2^n times 2^w; their mathematical
coverage has no bound on character order, rational frame coefficients or
number of restored dirty roles.

Passing only decides this necessary screen. It does not produce scalar
couplings, a formal endpoint identity, determinant equality or a supplier.
Neither dyadic unipotent cuts nor matrix-valued representations are included.

## Exact subgroup indices without saturating the lattice

The implementation clears one common denominator from all input matrices.
This is an injective scaling of the actual additive group; it does not replace
H by the ambient integer lattice or by its rational saturation. Flatten the
scaled matrices into integer row vectors.

A target outside the subgroup generated by all paid rows cannot occur in the
formal product support. It is rejected separately. To calculate d_L(U),
first compare the rational ranks of the base and combined row matrices.
A rank increase means infinite index.

Otherwise choose r pivot columns of the base row matrix, where r is its
rational rank. Projection onto these columns is injective on the common
rational span. The projected groups are full-rank sublattices of Z^r. The
standard integer-lattice index formula gives

`[Z^r : lattice(rows)] = gcd of all r-by-r row minors`.

For example, this follows by integer row and column operations in Smith
normal form; each unimodular operation preserves both the index and the gcd,
and the diagonal form makes them equal. No Smith form implementation or
floating-point decision is imported here. The ratio of the base and combined
gcds is exactly d_L(U). The rank-zero empty minor is 1. Bareiss elimination
computes each determinant and checks every exact division. Stripping only
powers of two gives oddpart. In particular, using rational span alone or the
full torsion index in (2) would be wrong.

## Why prime-order checks alone do not suffice

In the one-dimensional address model take paid labels 15 and 1 and target
labels 6 and 10. Both paid labels have rational rank one and the determinant
condition holds: 15+1=6+10.

Every prime-order counting cut passes. At p=3 the surviving paid and target
counts are both one; at p=5 they are also both one. At every other prime,
a nonzero character retains both paid labels, so it cannot fail with two
targets. The trivial character retains neither. This includes p=2 as a
counting comparison, although the field-character theorem concerns odd order.
All rational projection cuts pass too.

At order 15, the character sending 1 to a primitive fifteenth root kills the
paid 15 but retains 1, 6 and 10. Hence one paid call would have to serve two
targets. For L=15Z, formula (2) is

`alpha_L({6,10}) = 1 - 1/5 - 1/3 + 1/15 = 8/15 > 0`.

This small control demonstrates why the checker must include composite odd
orders, even if every prime order had been tested. It is a rank-one-address
diagnostic, not an eight-call full-rank dimension-three candidate.

## Verification boundary

The avoidance and coverage lemmas are written algebraic proofs. The direct
finite-character oracles, retained ternary example, exact direct-word control
and actual implementation mutants corroborate the code, not the universal
quantifiers. There is no proof-assistant formalization, complete predecessor
replay, scalar-word search, native compilation, hosting, precision/tape,
all-size supplier or multiplication-theorem verification here.
