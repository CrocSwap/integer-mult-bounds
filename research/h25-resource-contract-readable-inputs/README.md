This contribution exposes six exact existing source/review records needed to reconcile a complete H25 resource bill. It resolves a concrete source-access limitation in the Pro consultation after complete source assembly was accepted in [PR #6752](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/6752). It adds readable inputs, not a completed numerical bill or an execution result.

The original investigation is [PR #6321](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/6321); the fixed benchmark is [PR #6029](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/6029). Direct predecessors are the adopted graph [#6715](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/6715), readable full callers [#6744](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/6744), ordinary caller/preparation [#6588](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/6588), and accepted retention source [#6736](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/6736).

## Contents and meaning

- `control-union-census-index.json`: historical bounded feasibility index. Its counts and unresolved-name observations belong to that snapshot; they are not a new complete-control denominator or a claim that later identity repairs are absent.
- `model-source-fit-review.json` and `model-fit-dispositions.json`: exact prior source-fit review and conditional bounds, with their unresolved journal, graph, memory and elapsed-cost obligations preserved.
- `bill-and-resource.json`: the existing C01-C15 cost decomposition. It is input to reconciliation, not proof of complete current workload fit.
- `native-packet.py.txt` and `data-adapters.py.txt`: the exact adopted inner bodies. They are inert source text and are not imported or executed by this contribution.

`PROVENANCE.json` binds every retained input to its original byte count, SHA-256 and Git blob. The final two bodies and the bill also have published archive locators in the acquisition map of #6752. The review records' identities were already referenced by the published original criteria; this contribution supplies their readable bodies and the associated fit dispositions. Original files and all earlier negative evidence remain unchanged.

## Validation and reproduction

All six input bodies were read as opaque bytes, matched against their independently retained expected SHA-256 values and copied without decoding archives, importing target modules, compiling, creating runtime inputs, running controls or replaying science. Read the supplied records against their pinned predecessors and run `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS` for package byte integrity. There is no scientific replay hook. No complete-fit, performance, theorem or frontier claim follows from this export.
