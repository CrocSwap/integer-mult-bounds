## Exact source census and rank descent

The ambient flow agrees with the retained selected proof: a unit at paid
prefix product rho and target product c is admitted by A*rho*c<=n. A parent
job at rank r matches a target-k child whose ambient extent is multiplied by
r/k<1. Reconstructing the parent from the paid site, first target letter and
remaining word proves injection. Hence the signed aggregate census counts
actual unmatched source slots; it does not obtain nonnegativity merely from
a runtime assertion. Equal-product words still contribute their multiplicity.

A root source has degree G+1 and integer rank at least k_min^(G+1)>floor(n/A),
so it is strictly above n/A. A deeper fresh source has an absent potential
parent, giving rank strictly above r*n/A>=n/A. Fit bounds the rank above by n.
This uses A, not the maximum productive rank m. The code checks the valid
stronger upper bound A*s<=m*n, inherited directly from s=k*rho*c.

Every deepest paid path exists because r_max^G<=k_min^G<=floor(n/A), and its
empty target word has multiplicity one. The G+1-site exit census is therefore
exactly the paid-product table at that degree. Source complements are strictly
below (A-1)n/A, exits are at most (A-1)n/A, and both are positive when used.
No rank-zero child is constructed. These statements hold without a native
realization or moment-root saving, as the scope says.

The gcd factor for a complement is necessary and sufficient for its grouped
source count. The exit constraint uses B_e for **each individual cut**; its
multiplicity belongs in the expanded cost, never as a gcd divisor of the
required replication. Equation (3) is consequently the least positive valid
replication. The paid-leaf and executed-unit recurrences include every whole
child copy and the local a_n*U_n term. They are not peak-space recurrences.

## All-prime divisor and the exposed ambient constant

For fixed A, the census changes only when floor(n/A) crosses a product of
productive/paid rank generators, including the height thresholds. Counting
all semigroup plateaus may introduce redundant cut points, which only weakens
the divisor bound. If gamma_next follows gamma, k_min*gamma is another
semigroup member, so gamma_next<=k_min*gamma.

Set alpha=(A-1)/A. The elementary inequality
(1+1/(A-1))^A>2 gives alpha^A<1/2. With
`tau=A*ceil(log2 k_min)`, alpha^tau<1/k_min. A chain beginning in
[A*gamma,A*gamma_next) is below A*gamma after tau edges. Its visits to the
plateau are contiguous, so there are at most tau of them, including the first
vertex. This checks the off-by-one convention in the visit bound.

For each prime separately, the exact LCM recurrence gives
v_p(B_n)<=v_p(S_n)+max_child v_p(B_child). Following a maximizing child is
legitimate even though that child chain can differ with p. The uniform visit
bound controls every chain, hence every prime, and yields the displayed
integer divisor Q_N. No common maximizing chain or restriction to rank primes
is needed. Source counts can introduce new prime factors; the independent
fixture with only rank generator 2 and target multiplicity 3 exercises this.

There are at most (H+1)^b possible bounded exponent vectors for semigroup
products <=C. With S_n<=W*(G+1)*max(W,P)^G, taking the logarithm of Q_N proves
beta. This counts plateau locations, not the prime support of widths. Replacing
A by m in tau is invalid; the exact lower-bound family provides a witness.

## Exact output obstruction and quantifier boundary

For fixed targets 2 and 3 with unit multiplicities and one paid rank-one site,
A<=n<2A has G=0, two fresh sources and one exit. At A>=4 both complements
are positive, with source count one. The recurrence is therefore exactly
B_n=2*lcm(B_(n-2),B_(n-3)), with preceding base widths one. Its binary exponent
is floor((n-A)/2)+1, by a direct two-predecessor induction, including t=0,1.

The endpoint bit length is ceil(A/2)+1. Summing the actual width lengths,
including their leading one bit, gives floor((A-1)^2/4)+2A. With N=2A-1,
these required output bits alone force Omega(N^2) sequential writes. This is
a lower bound for explicit all-rank evaluated output, not a claim about a
compressed symbolic formula, arbitrary representations, or all polynomial-N
algorithms. The fixed histogram is a formal metadata family; it need not be
an admitted native supplier to refute the advertised uniform metadata bound.

The varying-A family cannot contradict the retained multiplier's order of
quantifiers: its template and ambient are fixed before p varies. The README
correctly makes this distinction and says the selected A=153^1920 only lies
in the parameter domain. Its huge-A fixture evaluates toy bounds/lengths and
materializes no selected rows. The theorem does not transfer default-A bounds
or claim to have generated the selected native manifest.

## Separately written exact corroboration

It imports no author verifier, literal oracle, predecessor library generator
or compressed product engine to construct expected answers. The expected
census uses a different positive formulation: enumerate paid-site paths and
productive multiplicity lattice points, then count a slot iff its potential
parent fails the actual ambient test. It never subtracts consumed demands.
Multinomial weights use direct factorial identities, without enumerating all
productive words or matching slot lists.

Expected replication is found by trying consecutive positive integers against
every literal divisibility constraint, with an explicit 50,000-trial cap per
rank. All declared cases finish within that cap; no failed rank is dropped.
This avoids both the production gcd/LCM update and the author's prime-valuation
oracle. Expanded counts then sum the exact integral whole-child copy totals.

Eight declared domains cover 269 complete rows, 57,744 literal replication
trials and 1,697 represented target slots. All source, exit and local-unit
censuses and all four row values match. A bounded integer-division membership
DP constructs semigroup plateau locations; exact Q divisibility is checked
for every row. Longest-path DPs on the independent dependency graph check
chain depth and plateau visits. Bounds on combined output bits, ordinary
scalar fields and reported implementation-operation counters also hold.
