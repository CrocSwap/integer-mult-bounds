# Cyclic 2-power roots cannot save dirty diagonal calls

For every k>=1, a diagonal target over F2[C_(2^k)] with t nonidentity
entries requires at least t rank-one increment factors. This permits any
number of restored dirty helpers. If every nonidentity cyclic power is an
allowed unit-cost label, t coordinate calls attain the bound exactly.

The written [proof](PROOF.md) closes the explicit C8 handoff in Cinna Davis's
[PR2187](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/2187), whose
four-role C4 augmentation argument gave a minimum of four. This is a uniform
chain-ring argument covering arbitrary role count and mixed powers, rather
than another enlarged word search. The predecessor README is retained at its
exact head in [PREDECESSOR.json](PREDECESSOR.json), with original attribution.
The result is a side negative theorem, not a new kappa or native supplier.

## Model and invariant

All register inputs are arbitrary and may be correlated. Work over the full
formal group algebra R=F2[C_q], q=2^k. A paid factor is
I+(g^s-1)xy^T with 0<s<q and binary x,y satisfying y^T x=1. Each factor
costs one; there are no other free transformations. The required endpoint is
diag(g^{s_1},...,g^{s_n}); s_i=0 marks an unchanged dirty role. Every cross
term must vanish and every such helper must be restored. Native power tariffs
and initially clean helpers are outside this exact-minimum statement.

Identify R with F2[z]/(z^q). For an R-module M, its socle here is the vector
space of elements killed by z. Elementary valuation pivoting proves that
the image of an n by L matrix has socle dimension at most L. Telescoping
the product expresses P-I=UV through L columns. The t nonzero diagonal
ideals in P-I have t independent socle directions, forcing t<=L. This lower
bound even permits arbitrary ring-valued outer-product increments; the
direct upper bound uses only the specified original alphabet.

For four C8 involution outputs, ordinary binary regular rank gives only
ceil(16/7)=3 calls. The new invariant gives four. It also covers all higher
cyclic 2-powers and any restored dirty helper count. The chain-ring premise
is essential to this proof: over F2[x,y]/(x^2,y^2), U=(x,y)^T has one
column but its image has two-dimensional socle. That counterexample rejects
the invariant's extrapolation; it is not a shorter circuit.

## Evidence and reproduction

The universal theorem is the elementary written proof. It is not formally
verified in a proof assistant. The standard-library verifier supplies
independent exact diagnostics: valuation elimination versus the nullity of
multiplication by z on the binary image. It checks all 4,096 two-by-three
matrices at q=2 and all 65,536 two-by-two matrices at q=4, plus 768
deterministic rectangular samples at q=3,8,16. Four mixed-power seven-role
direct words and 24 telescoping instances pass. Five semantic controls
reject wrong rank, identity-role counting, rank-two single-call charging,
the weak C8 rank screen and the non-chain-ring extrapolation.
