# Private cleanup stock and literal work

The borrowed-word cleanup really allocates one independent full physical array for every label in `CANONICAL-WORD.json.gz.zero_buffers`. These are private zero arrays, in addition to arbitrary dirty public helper inputs. A rank-zero allocation does not make the array occupy zero physical space.

The controller reads the actual sorted labels of every selected provider and sets `Z` to their maximum count. It reserves the disjoint interval `[DATA+8+R, DATA+8+R+Z)`, between the reserved helper tableau and the two coefficient arrays. Each provider's j-th sorted label maps to the j-th family of this interval. The generated `private_zero_buffer_lowering` defines this map and its FULL frame, with an actual CLEAR on allocation and on release. Calls execute completely before the next local call starts; the bank is therefore reused only after all its local buffers have returned to zero and been cleared. No additional recursive child acts on this bank. The independently replayed scalar word establishes the zero-return endpoint. The fresh invoice checks its literal allocation/free sequence, its actual maximum live count, and that no child or centre event occurs while cleanup buffers are live.

The physical identity is

`physical = DATA + R + 8 + Z + 2`.

For the original 437-helper, 76-buffer derivative this is `66304+437+8+76+2=66827`. The pure additional alias removes one public helper while retaining all 76 cleanup buffers. Its 436-helper candidate therefore uses `66304+436+8+76+2=66826`. Both are below the retained parent stock 112178, so the parent's conservative initialization and nonlocal compiler allowance remains an overcharge. The `65*(physical-parent_physical)+(live-parent_live)` rebase is recomputed using the corrected physical stock; the invoice retains the larger complete parent allowance.

Each allocation and release lowers to two real-component CLEAR primitives. A provider using Z buffers consequently receives 2Z additional scalar macros and 4Z additional real primitives per complete invocation. These counts are added to, rather than substituted for, the hash-bound literal-program counts. Every literal rank-zero warp also receives its own complete monomial routing reserve in the adapter bound; rank zero does not make its physical permutation circuit free. Its exact all-column phase/permutation recipe preserves the magnitude and dyadic-grid bounds. The adapter bound further includes every public column, all Z private columns, eight centres and two coefficient columns. `assemble.py` independently checks both additions and the resulting exact stock identity. The paid recursive profile and rank mass are unaffected by zero-buffer CLEARs.
