# Sharp total image at every no-edge pattern dimension

For any binary rank-one idempotent collection with nonzero patterns and a
k-dimensional pattern span that contains no matrix unit, the joint image
has dimension at most `floor(3k/2)`. The bound is sharp for every positive k.
If the action on a nonzero joint image is irreducible, its dimension is at
most `floor((3k-1)/2)`.

In particular, pattern dimension five has maximum total image seven. The
existing seven-dimensional irreducible example attains the irreducible
maximum as well. This closes the upper-bound question left open in
[PR #7281](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/7281).
The coordinate classification of all attaining pools remains open.

Read [PROOF.md](PROOF.md), [RESULT.json](RESULT.json), and
[TASK.json](TASK.json). The proof selects any independent image subcollection
and shows that its diagonal relations are generated by disjoint circuits of
at least three members. It does not require an irreducible basis or a finite
classification step. Incomplete collections are included.
