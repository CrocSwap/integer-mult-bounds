# Paired bit outputs, physical frame descent and compensated reuse

This package supplies finite conditional witnesses within the paired-cube
and completed-core interfaces of PR144/130. Its complex scalar construction
is the pinned PR163 fused word, built from PR161's modules. Its bit word is
a new output-pair composition of PR161's p12 word. The certified exponent
and exact margins are generated by `parameters.py`; numerical search scores
are not proof inputs.

## Bit output merging

For each target T, replace the two singleton reads `face1` and `edge01`
by a read of their XOR, and similarly replace `face2` and `edge02`.
The original producer supplies positive support-disjoint sums. Within each
merged pair, the first channel uses source cubes sharing one specified pair
with T's cube, while the second shares two. Their original source supports
are disjoint. The generator checks this condition before every added sum
and checks the complete mod-two decoder again after merging.

Every original support label lies in T's rational target cap. Its span,
and hence the span of each merged support, remains in that cap. The merged
values are read at singleton caps; their repeated use is compiled into an
actual carrier word rather than treated as free broadcasting. Copied-star
centers and the original source-register partner mixer are retained.
The edge12 two-target root still anchors each partner-pair delivery.

`bit_merge.py` regenerates the graph, exact rational frames, carrier arcs,
gauge schedule, partner chronology and full ledger. The independent
PR157/161 checker verifies the decoder, all frame inclusions, target chains,
nondegeneracy for G = I - J/9, and the baseline ledger. The additional fresh
support audit binds every actual phased XOR to the graph node naming its
frame and checks every root value. Thus a scalar identity alone does not
authorize a physical frame.

The merged virtual bit word has h = 24, v = 1,760 and R = 21,608.
Its complete carrier and readout order is part of the regenerated witness.

## Physical frame changes

Each selected common operation frame contains the full conservative value
span of its operation and lies between its participating roles' adjacent
frames. All role chains are checked after every proposed change. The bit
checks use exact integer descriptions of rational subspaces and exact Gram
rank tests. The complex checks use the retained binary arbitrary-subspace
Clifford frames, including the permitted degenerate subspaces.

Moving several connected operations at the same frame can improve a chain
when moving a single operation cannot. The search also uses exact
histogram-neutral moves to expose further feasible changes. Only the final
explicit frames are mathematical inputs: their containment and complete
positive-rank recount are checked independently of search weights.

## Compensated physical slot reuse

The selected bit plan pairs 1,760 ungauged, non-root donors with 1,760
rank-21 deferred terminal recipients. Donors and recipients are disjoint,
every recipient has no earlier operation, and each donor's last operation
precedes its recipient's first-operation deadline. If E is the donor's last
frame and sigma the recipient's entrance gauge, the checker requires
E contained in sigma. It splices the two actual role chains and checks all
aliased gate ports remain distinct.

At the deadline, read the donor slot's current value with the recipient's
exact future response coefficients before using that slot as the recipient.
This is PR124's compensated-birth identity. If a newly interpreted virtual
register has initial value g and future output response Dg, the explicit
old-value correction removes Dg. This remains true when g contains earlier
source contributions or correlated dirty values. No reset to zero or
deletion of live scalar data is assumed. Reversing the actual aliased gates
and then undoing source injections restores every original physical slot.

For a donor exit dimension e and gauge dimension s, one reuse replaces
three local exit children of width h-e and one shared exterior child of
width 3s by three handoff children of width s-e. Rank-zero handoffs remain
actual scalar/frame adapters. The removed rank mass is exactly 3h, and
one persistent auxiliary role is removed. The recipient's later transitions
remain paid. This local splice formula and a separate full recount both
preserve the telescoping deficit; the deficit identity is not used to
invent a histogram.

Retimed old-value reads are ordered by their actual first-operation
deadlines. `bit.py` rebuilds every affected target chain in that order,
then checks side-root and original partner deliveries. The selected bit
profile has physical R = 19,848, W = 23,368, m = 72, rank mass 1,680,560,
deficit 1,936 and maximum child 60. Source mixing remains the inherited
two-dimensional partner mix and full-frame inverse schedule.

## Exact scalar checks and the complex supplier

The bit replay uses independent formal variables for all source and
physical dirty coordinates. It executes initial corrections, source
injections, phase one, copied centers, unpaired and deadline compensation
reads, aliased remaining gates, root and partner deliveries, and the true
inverse chronology. Every output coefficient equals the identity shear and
every source/dirty coefficient is restored. Target coordinates are only
written additively, so their original identity coefficients are preserved.
Omitted compensation, a missing XOR and a read before donor death are
rejected. Frame controls also reject a missing value span, retreat,
degeneracy, duplicate alias and a still-live donor.

The complex side regenerates PR163's signed fused graph from the pinned
PR161 modules and replays its carrier word and full backward intersections.
The new selected physical frames retain its 2,640 compensated pairs.
The independent signed checker verifies all source/target coefficients of
H + K + B = I. An exact integer adjoint audit checks every recipient's
response, absence of center dependence, legal birth chronology and the
polynomial inverse of each signed gate. The inherited aliased modular
replay is an additional control; it does not replace this arbitrary-dirty
algebraic argument.

The complex scalar inventory is c = 19,385, q = 3,157, 6,861 carrier arcs,
R_scalar = 15,681 and M = 33,746 mixer operations. Its persistent inventory
is R_physical = 13,041 and W = 15,681 per cover vertex. With m = 66, the
rank mass is 1,033,626, deficit 1,320 and maximum child 20.

## Paid moments, finite bridge and scope

All three completed local cores, source and target fronts, copied centers,
surviving exterior gauges and the two width-two finishing banks are paid.
The bit moment includes the entire 32m^2-child fallback on fraction 10^-16
of every positive ideal edge. Both its contaminated moment and rank mass
contract. The fixed prime is chosen after the finite rational frames are
specified, larger than 2^80 and avoiding their denominators and rank
witnesses, as in the retained uniform compiler. Exact rational
nondegeneracy supplies only a finite exclusion set; no new prime-distribution
assumption is introduced.

The complex bridge retains the full group V = |O(66,2)|. Full W, rank,
routing and scalar charges include V even though it cancels in the
normalized moment. The scalar bill uses R_scalar and M of the regenerated
word. Common-grid precision, fixed odd divisor three, all row reserves and
the positive degree-70,000 reserve gap remain charged.

For bit coarse saving a0 and ordinary leaf saving a_old, the stopped
supplier is A_B = (1-theta)a0 + theta a_old. `parameters.py` chooses a
positive rational theta strictly satisfying A_B < theta < 1-A_B. It also
keeps positive complex stopping and assembly backoffs. Independent outward
intervals certify each supplier and reject its next grid point. The full
retained 47-constraint assembly checks seven strict margins and rejects
the successor of its selected kappa. These are fixed-profile, fixed-envelope
grid statements, not global optimality claims.

This submission uses the retained paired-cube assembly, not PR163's
additional balanced-layout improvement. Its finite results remain
conditional on the inherited all-size Clifford/shared-core, weighted
local-ring, complete-spectator and restored-row, analytic, recovery,
precision and fixed-tape contracts. It asserts no unconditional integer
multiplication theorem or practical runtime threshold.
