# Attribution and retained provenance

Apache-2.0; preserve the repository LICENSE and all source headers. Prepared
for James Chang with substantial OpenAI assistance. Independent source and
terminal review here means separate AI review, not independent human review.

The source-bound PR1094 selected profile and ce7ad1b6 scientific inputs retain
their original contributor notices. PR585 provides exact finite rational
checkpoint and aggregation modules; PR605 provides generic real exponential,
logarithm and rounding enclosures, with the repaired PR799 lineage retained.
The historical values in those unchanged sources are not relabeled as the
current selected κ. Their exact accepted bytes are reused.

The new normalized-cost assembly composes NormalizedCostEnvelope/Descent,
RoundedRecurrence and its existential-budget successor, ProfilePotentialTransfer
and the selected PR1094 characteristic/κ bridge. It does not claim the general
supremum or strong-induction ideas are novel. PR1379 by commdata2338 supplies
relevant parking/normalization interface analysis. PR1641 by Dugongue, retaining
rfu08 PR64, gives complementary concrete multitape wrapper results; it is not
silently imported into this Lean4.21 package. PR1430 by Hanson Sun/bbMisk gives a
separate conditional precision evaluator and is not adopted as a physical-cost
supplier. Existing exact source reviews of those interfaces are reused.

Mathlib, Lean and Batteries community attribution and licenses remain in their
pinned upstream repositories. SOURCE_MANIFEST.json identifies every borrowed
or reused source by immutable content, original path and source commit.
DUPLICATION_SCREEN.json records the bounded comparison and its limits.
