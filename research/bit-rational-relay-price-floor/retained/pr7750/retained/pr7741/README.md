bundles of direct nested deliveries from the original strictly earlier chart
libraries. The result covers both orientations, all independent inputs, free
retention and destination XOR, and every rank tariff s^t with 0<t<=1.

This closes the nine higher-arity exceptions left by Nick Petalas's PR7739.
