# Grouped phase-recursive interface checkpoint

Cao Yifan; substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. Apache-2.0.

This package turns the frozen global-Pauli adapter into a precisely charged
grouped interface. It establishes reusable address and phase algebra,
identifies a fixed-global-adapter rank limitation, and supplies an all-f
affine-width job contract for the actual-frame census. **No kappa improvement
or complete new recursive supplier is claimed here.** The baseline is
PR4610's conditional `kappa=0.0030217758`.

[PLAN.md](PLAN.md) records the design. The full written physical lowering,
guards, counter costs, restoration and job types are in
[PHYSICAL-INTERFACE.md](PHYSICAL-INTERFACE.md). Its key conclusions are:

* One `S(P_q^tensor f)` is a single rank-one symplectic transvection. The
  tensor of f column phases has defect rank f. A word of k global phases,
  even conjugated by arbitrary invertible owner maps, changes the relevant
  matrix rank by at most k. The generic Lean word theorem places the defect
  in a span generated by its k update vectors; the dimension/block-rank
  corollary is the accompanying finite-dimensional argument, not a Lean
  Clifford semantics theorem.
* A grouped product with r blocks is fundable as **one Gaussian rank-r job**
  plus `O(V((f log p)^tau+1))` movement. It is not a free tensor gauge. The
  exact split has a controlled interaction which must remain in the bill.
* A new parallel selected-position primitive supports interleaving source
  and target bits at the same rho in distinct K-bit chunks. It retains the twelve dirty-field swaps, ten rotations,
  exceptional repair and full compact exponent. Six such layers gather
  selected axes through two cycle-reversal involutions. This avoids an
  implicit growing transpose.
* Actual frame realification must use its canonical Clifford operator,
  including quadratic phases. The native warp may cease to be monomial;
  that entire new Fourier rank is paid. Scalar shared nodes must have one
  common encoding. Main, independent helper, adapter and owned endpoints
  retain their actual complete contracts.
* With `f=ell*r+t`, use the new frame on full blocks and the original one
  on the t leftover columns. A lowered one-block rank d' and old rank d give
  the exact child width `d' r+d t`. This avoids constant-factor padding at
  every recursive call. A positive ledger still needs a concrete cutoff
  paying bounded offsets and the inherited precision/stock/tape obligations.

The actual-frame census and any binary gauge-choice optimization are separate
coordinated work. Their results must be attached before a complete new paid
or productive profile can be asserted. The unconditional replacement is not
declared profitable by this package.

The finite route replay checks 93312 complete dirty-load/unload cases with
interleaving selected source/target positions, 46233 permutation
decompositions, and 5454 nonzero Pauli transvections. These supplement the
written uniform construction rather than certify an all-size machine bound.
The parent's overlapping-window counterexample is rejected by the model's
explicit same-rho admission check; arbitrary adjacent positions are excluded.

The active-chart recipe replay checks 1023 rank/kernel cases and eighteen
actual first/last registered canonical frames, including thirty-two exact
unit-direction cancellations with absolute Clifford phases. The concrete
palette is old, first active unit, last active unit, and full active mask.
It supplies exact operators for a later census, rather than a claimed gain.

The Lean replay freshly compiles four modules with fifteen exported axiom
reports: arbitrary-index parallel control, generic rank-one word span and
gauge algebra, exact split sectors, fourth-root rephasing, and the
characteristic-two active-mask kernel identities. Runtime,
auditor, source/log/object and frozen-input hashes are bound in the receipt;
only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` are allowed. No custom
axiom, `sorry`, `native_decide` or kernel bypass is accepted. The proofs do
not formalize the native Clifford amplitude evaluator, full tape execution,
new actual paid profile, or complete multiplication transfer.

The final command is a read-only fast coherence check. It verifies the
manifest, all four local compiled objects, source/log hashes, actual fifteen
axiom reports, runtime/dependency pins, frozen inputs and finite-receipt
bindings. Five altered in-memory receipts must be rejected. Compiled objects
are ignored local outputs and must first be generated by the fresh Lean
replay. The checker does not replace compilation or rerun the finite controls.
After intentional source changes and fresh replays, explicitly refresh the
manifest and its receipt with `verify_bundle.py --write-manifest`.
