## Exact three-product identity

Use the classical Gauss/Karatsuba three-product identity with sign choices to bound operand widths. Choose \(s,t\in\{-1,1\}\) so that the two sums \(a+sb\) and \(c+td\) cancel magnitudes within each operand. Each is bounded in magnitude by the larger original component on its side. Set \(P=(a+sb)(c+td)\). If \(s=t\), also compute \(A=ac\) and \(B=bd\). The required components are \((A-B,s(P-A-B))\). If \(s=-t\), compute \(A=ad\) and \(B=bc\). The components are \((P-tA-sB,A+B)\). These identities also apply to the signed packed integers in one ring row.

The two outputs before truncation or centered limb recovery are equal as integers. For any signed incoming carry \(k\) and any positive normalization divisor \(D\), equality of those integers gives the same quotient and remainder of \(z+k\) modulo \(D\), hence the same next carry and signed truncation. Identical packed sums give identical centered extraction, fold and divisional normalization. Identical field values then give the same radius admission, normalized opposite transform, recovered coefficients, inverse CRT and carry. This uses the original numerical proof and does not require a wider grid or a larger radius. [Recovery scaling and admission](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/4f0c67d5bb41d4af5501dcd40b3b388b35e0ff02/research/fine-prime-gaussian-product/PROOF.md#L45-L61) · [Error bounds](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/4f0c67d5bb41d4af5501dcd40b3b388b35e0ff02/research/fine-prime-gaussian-product/ACCURACY.md#L67-L85)

The two retained products form one of the two matchings between component pairs. If the mixed product crosses the physical cutoff, its wide side has an original wide component, so at least one discarded product crossed it too. The number of physical dispatches weakly decreases. It strictly decreases when all four original calls cross the cutoff. There are also inputs where the physical count ties. The four components \(a=b=c=d=2^{8191}+1\) fit the pointwise disk at grid precision 8,195. All original calls have 8,192-bit operands, while an unconditional \((a+b)(c+d)\) has 8,193-bit operands and launches one physical child. The cancellation rule keeps these inputs below the physical cutoff.

At component width \(L\), the signs, mixture operands and reconstruction can use a fixed number of scratch words with \(O(L)\) read, write, rewind and erasure work. All intermediate reconstruction sums fit within \(2L+O(1)\) bits. At the packed site this is \(O(rp)\) per ring row. The original field scans, home positions, decoders and releases are retained. The public word interface copies signed operands, consumes the exact child return and restores its word roles. A reduction in recorded dispatches leaves the net finite bit transition difference dependent on the extra signed scans.
