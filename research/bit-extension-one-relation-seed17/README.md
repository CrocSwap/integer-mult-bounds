# Two extension-field roles cannot save paid rank with one frame relation

**Scoped negative result; no new kappa.** Allowing arbitrary characteristic-two
scalar coefficients does not let **two** roles reduce the sum-of-target-ranks
bill when the rational paid frames have at most one independent relation.
[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) supplies the written universal argument; the finite
receipt is corroboration, not a proof by enumeration or a proof-assistant result.

The binary predecessors' cyclic-pattern uniqueness step does not extend
unchanged: over F4, `[[w,1],[1,w+1]]` and its complement are distinct rank-one
idempotents with the same off-diagonal entries (`w^2=w+1`). The new argument
classifies that complementary pair, prices both decompositions of its common
address shift, and rules out an independent coordinate gate inside the block.
This limits a proposed extension of the lemma; the predecessors' explicitly
binary statements are unaffected.

For `K=F_(2^e)`, an extension-field paid call uses `e` rank-one binary calls
in the componentwise restriction-of-scalars simulation. Even granting free
scalar mixing, access, frame changes and restoration, two rank-m targets cost
at least `2em` counted binary calls **under this grouped-call pricing contract**,
the direct bill. Joint binary resynthesis across field calls is outside that
cost screen. Actual additional physical costs cannot improve its optimistic
bill. No supplier implementation is proposed; no general recursive moment,
all-size compiler, hosting, precision, tape or analytic obligation is discharged.
More roles over K, higher relation nullity and nonabelian labels remain outside scope.

## Open next questions

relations? That would cross the theorem's principal-kernel boundary. First
specify a small exact frame family and complete dirty endpoint, and screen the
binary restriction-of-scalars cost before synthesis. No such candidate or full
physical supplier contract is supplied here. More roles over K also require
a new pattern/interleaving argument; the binary arbitrary-role theorem alone
does not settle that extension. These are scope boundaries, not a recommendation
