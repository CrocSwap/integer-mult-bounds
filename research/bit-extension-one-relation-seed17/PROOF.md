# Two extension-field roles still cannot amortize one frame relation

This is a written, scoped algebraic proof. The accompanying finite checks
corroborate its new complementary-projector case; they do not prove the
unbounded statement by enumeration or formalize integer multiplication.

## Model and claim

Let K be any field of characteristic two. Use two arbitrary dirty scalar
roles over the formal group algebra K[M_m(Q)] of the **additive** group of
rational m-by-m matrices. Distinct rational matrices are distinct monomials,
and [A][B]=[A+B]. A normalized paid call is

    D_N([A]) = I + ([A]-1)N,
    N in M_2(K), rank_K(N)=1, N^2=N,
    A nonzero, rank_Q(A)=1.

Each call costs one extension-field invocation. Arbitrary GL_2(K) scalar
mixing is optimistically free. There are no other free address maps, resets,
clean allocations, discarded outputs, or uncharged paid calls. Require the
complete arbitrary-input endpoint

    product_(j=1)^k D_(N_j)([A_j]) = diag([C_0],[C_1]).       (1)

Zero C_i restores that dirty role. Equality means every formal coefficient,
including off-diagonal couplings. Products below use their displayed algebraic
order; a chronological column-vector execution reverses that order.

**Theorem.** If dim_Q span{A_j} >= k-1, then

    rank_Q(C_0) + rank_Q(C_1) <= k.                        (2)

In particular, two rank-m targets need at least 2m paid calls in this class,
even with extension-field scalar mixing. For K=F_(2^e), the componentwise
binary simulation below charges e rank-one binary calls per extension-field
call. Under that pricing contract this route cannot save counted calls
against the direct 2em construction.

The binary one-relation predecessors prove stronger arbitrary-role statements
over F2. This note changes the scalar field, restricts to **two** roles, and
resolves an additional case that those binary proofs do not address. It does
not assert their arbitrary-role theorem over K.

## 1. Inherited Laurent reduction

Free scalar changes can be pushed to one end by conjugating the N_j.
Augmentation [A]->1 sends both every paid call and the endpoint to I, so the
remaining scalar map is I. All conjugates remain rank-one idempotents.

The finite subgroup H generated by the A_j is free abelian; K[H] is a Laurent
polynomial domain. Every C_i belongs to H, because its monomial occurs in the
product's support. Introduce independent invertible t_j and set

    P(t) = product_j (I+(t_j-1)N_j).

If the A_j are rationally independent, all subset sums are distinct. Hence
every off-diagonal coefficient of P vanishes. Differentiate at t=1: each N_j
is diagonal, hence a coordinate projector. The targets are disjoint sums of
their assigned frames, which proves (2) by rank subadditivity.

Otherwise the saturated integer relation kernel has primitive generator r.
If some |r_j|>=2, no two subset sums collide: their difference lies in
{-1,0,1}^k and cannot be a nonzero integer multiple of r. The preceding
argument applies again.

It remains that r_j is in {-1,0,1}. Put S={j:r_j!=0}, T=S-complement. The
Laurent substitution kernel is generated by

    g = product_(r_j=1) t_j - product_(r_j=-1) t_j.

A primitive lattice vector extends to an integer basis, in which this map
sets one Laurent variable to one, proving the kernel assertion. Every
off-diagonal entry of P is divisible by g. Since g(1)=0, differentiation
at one says that all N_j on S have **the same off-diagonal pair** (b,c),
and all N_j on T are coordinate projectors. Here both +1 and -1 map to
one in K. These are the inherited principal-kernel and derivative steps,
not a new finite-search inference.

## 2. The new case is a complementary pair

A two-by-two rank-one idempotent over K has trace one and determinant zero.
It therefore has the form

    N(a) = [[a,b],[c,1+a]],        a(1+a)=bc.              (3)

Conversely (3) implies N^2=N and rank one. If bc=0, the diagonal entry a
is zero or one. Every matrix with this fixed pair (b,c) is triangular in
the same coordinate order (including b=c=0). Each diagonal has a single
one. Product diagonals therefore allocate all frames disjointly and prove (2).

If bc!=0, the polynomial X^2+X+bc has at most two roots. Once it has root
a, its roots are exactly a and a+1, because their difference factors as
(X+a)(X+a+1). Thus every S factor uses one of

    N = N(a),                 N(a+1)=I+N.                 (4)

These projectors are complementary: N(I+N)=(I+N)N=0. This is a real change
from F2: over F4 with w^2=w+1, the two projectors

    [[w,1],[1,w+1]],         [[w+1,1],[1,w]]

have the same cyclic off-diagonal pattern. Reusing the binary assertion
that a cyclic pattern determines one projector would be incorrect.

Split S into S_0 and S_1 according to the choice in (4), and let
B_i=sum_(j in S_i) A_j. Set all T monomials to one in (1). The S product is

    [B_0]N + [B_1](I+N).

Its off-diagonal entry is b([B_0]+[B_1]). Since b!=0 and distinct monomials
are linearly independent, B_0=B_1=:B. Consequently the S product is [B]I,
and sigma_j=+1 on S_0, -1 on S_1 is an integer relation supported on all S.
Its entries have gcd one. Uniqueness of the primitive relation gives
r=+/-sigma, including when one S_i is empty. Moreover

    2 rank(B) <= 2 min(|S_0|,|S_1|) <= |S|.              (5)

The common shift uses two separate decompositions of B; both are charged.

## 3. Outside gates cannot cut the complementary block

The sole relation is supported on S, so H=H_S direct-sum Z^T. Keep one
outside factor I+(t-1)E_aa, setting all other T monomials to one. If the
S prefix before that factor has sums b_0 and b_1, its product is

    V = [b_0]N + [b_1](I+N) = [b_1]D_N(z),
    z=[b_0-b_1].

All S factors commute. Their whole product is [B]I, so the specialized
word is

    [B] (I+(t-1)Q),      Q=D_N(z) E_aa D_N(z)^(-1).       (6)

The specialized endpoint is diagonal. The Laurent domain has t-1!=0,
so Q is diagonal. It is an idempotent, being conjugate to E_aa. Diagonal
idempotents over a domain have entries zero or one; augmentation sends Q
to E_aa, hence Q=E_aa. Thus D_N(z) commutes with E_aa. Since b,c are both
nonzero, N does not commute with either coordinate projector, forcing
z=1. The address group is torsion-free and formal, so b_0-b_1=0.

The signed indicator of this prefix is an integer relation: its j-th
entry is sigma_j when j is in the prefix and zero otherwise. As it is
an integer multiple of the primitive sigma, that multiple is zero or one.
The prefix therefore contains none or all of S. Every outside gate is
before all S factors or after all of them; no inside placement is possible.

The S block is consequently consecutive in the original normalized word.
It is [B]I and commutes with every outside gate. Write F_i for the sum
of outside frames assigned to coordinate i. Then C_i=B+F_i, and

    rank(C_0)+rank(C_1)
    <= 2 rank(B)+rank(F_0)+rank(F_1)
    <= |S|+|T|=k,

using (5) and the disjoint outside assignment. This completes the proof.
The empty word is immediate.

## 4. Complete optimistic cost screen and limits

For K=F_(2^e), represent each field symbol by its e binary coordinates.
A rank-one K-projector is conjugate over K to diag(1,0). The binary
representation of that change of basis is invertible on 2e dirty binary
roles. In this basis one extension-field call consists of e copies of the
same rank-one address call, one on each binary component; conjugating back
restores the scalar basis. This is an exact algebraic simulation, with no
clean helper or target discard. The checker explicitly confirms it for F4.

Price every extension-field invocation by those e componentwise binary
children. If U_1 denotes the cost of one such rank-one binary recursive child,
the optimistic completed local bill under this contract is

    e k U_1 + C_scalar + C_access + C_frames + C_restore,

where the extra terms include every required scalar basis change, source
access, physical frame adapter, copied field and restoration. We even
relax all these nonnegative extra costs to zero. For two rank-m targets,
(2) still gives e k U_1 >= 2em U_1, the direct counted child bill. There
is no favorable paid-call reduction to implement in this family. Unknown
physical costs are not asserted to vanish in an actual supplier. A different
binary implementation that jointly resynthesizes across field calls need
not preserve this grouping and is not bounded by the displayed simulation
bill. The universal algebraic conclusion is (2), measured in K-calls.

This is a stopping rule for **two roles and at most one rational relation**,
not a moment bound for arbitrary recursive ranks. It does not cover more
roles over K, two or more relations, nonabelian labels, nonlinear gates,
nonformal finite-address aliases, different endpoints or uncharged
address maps. No all-size native construction, supplier admission, new
kappa or lower bound for general multiplication follows.
