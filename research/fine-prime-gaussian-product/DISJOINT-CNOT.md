# Simultaneous disjoint CNOT with the ordinary physical transpose

The input contains C complete rows of 2^n signed complex words, with C any positive integer. A word has 2w bits. Write V=C·2^n·2w. The input pairs (a_i,b_i) have distinct endpoints, with both indices in [0,n). The operation sends the record at address x to the address whose b_i equals x[b_i] XOR x[a_i]. It keeps all other bits, the complete outer row and the two signed word components. No data bit has a prescribed initial value.

`ScatteredCNOT` uses the compact dirty-control gadget of Vlado Vojdanovski, the encoded sparse XOR construction of Jose Manuel and the subsequent physical support-router layout. The original support router assembles three such shear stages for a swap matching. The present consumer needs one shear stage with the original direction on every pair. `scattered_cnot.py` runs that stage directly and binds ordinary interchanges of actual complete rectangles. The older sparse interpreter checks a fixed ordinary class and profile at entry. Its numerical rate cannot be changed by supplying it a different object with the same method names.

## Exposing auxiliary fields

Let L=ceil(log₂ p), ell=L², G=4L+6, K=64G and H=rG for r input pairs. The empty request just returns its owner. The controller seeks a consecutive interval of ell unused bits disjoint from the true low ell address positions. It tests n−ell>=10H. When either test fails, `literal` applies each requested CNOT by a four-bucket scan. These scans also supply a finite total implementation when no spare fields exist.

On acceptance, exchange the unused interval with the physical low ell positions and conjugate every pair by that interchange. The map fixes the new low bits pointwise. The raw word therefore treats all 2^ell complex coefficients in one such suffix as a single complete record. The code keeps them in their actual order on the tape. Set N=n−ell for the remaining prefix. In most-significant order reserve three disjoint regions of 3H bits at its start. Assign each pair to the first region that avoids its two endpoints. At least one exists. A region is placed in temporary positions u,t at the front and b at the back by at most three width-H interchanges. After applying these precise exchanges to each endpoint, every pair lies in a middle field y of N−3H bits. Only then color the valid targets by their coordinate modulo K. For tails with no K-bit target guard, execute the exact literal CNOT on its actual full-field positions. There are fewer than K such targets per cohort. The inverse placement restores all auxiliary bits before another cohort starts. Undo the low suffix interchange after the last cohort.

Every width-h interchange on disjoint intervals [i,i+h),[j,j+h) with i+h<=j is one actual B1 call with

    width = h
    prefixes = C·2^(n−j−h)
    gap = 2^(j−i−h)
    suffix_records = 2^i
    final payload width = 2w·suffix_records bits.

This applies to suffix preparation, cohort placement, every internal data shuttle and all inverse placements. There is no short-record B1 assumption during preparation. Prefixes and the gap may have extent one. No sparse mask or fixed spectator value enters a B1 call.

## The raw word and exact correction

Write B=2^G and index the active pairs (s_i,j_i) of a single color in increasing target order. The fields in most-significant order are u,t,y,b, with u,t,b of width H. For digit i of u let z_i=u_i modulo 2. Let E(y)=sum_i y[j_i] B^i and P(y)=sum_i y[s_i] B^i. The four principal updates of F are

    y += sum_i 2 z_i t_i 2^j_i
    t += E(y)
    y += sum_i z_i(1−2t_i) 2^j_i
    t -= sum_i (y[j_i] XOR z_i) B^i.

Apply F, add P(y) to u, apply F again and subtract P(y) from u. All sums are modulo the full corresponding field, including inactive high digits. Updates of u or t first exchange that field with b, apply a rotation controlled only by earlier current fields, and exchange it back. Each four-update F uses four width-H interchanges. Each load or unload uses two. Thus the actual word has twelve B1 calls and ten rotations. At a rotation on a target field of width s and lower suffix of v bits, the code seeks the split, copies the suffix of that row, then copies its prefix. The split is the negative forward offset modulo 2^s. It depends only on more-significant current fields. Each row costs a constant times its full physical extent, including both seeks.

An address is good if u_i,t_i<B−1 and every target's K−1 higher guard bits encode an integer from 2B through 2^(K−1)−2B−1. At z_i=1 and initial target bit e, the target updates add 2t_i and 1−2(t_i+e). Their sum is 1−2e. At z_i=0 both additions vanish. The guarded disjoint blocks contain each intermediate carry, and t returns. The central load changes the control parity by y[s_i] and unload restores u. A source inside a different target's guard is valid because the completed F restores every guard bit. Thus the raw word S equals the requested ideal shear T on the good set. T preserves the good set, and both S and T are bijections. It follows that S also preserves the exceptional set.

The code copies the good records in order. For each exceptional record at current output q it computes T(S^-1(q)), reversing the actual word one instruction at a time with its then-current controls. All N prefix bits remain in this key, including inactive controls and the complete back field. Stable least-significant-digit passes sort the keys and whole exceptional records over the full N bits. On output each exceptional key must equal its current hole. The good stream fills the remaining slots. The map on the exceptional set is a permutation, so this test succeeds exactly once per hole on every input. A key/payload tape pair and a fixed number of sort buckets suffice. The implementation releases its source, temporary rotation tapes, both sorts and their scratch, and all good streams. The terminal inverse swaps restore the original address interpretation. The caller owns the returned field with its source width and extent. All mathematical statements here concern the successful-input contract.

## Complete time and backing

Suppose that the supplied ordinary call takes O(V(1+h^(1−a)) polylog(p)) bit operations and O(V) backing on the complete spectator extents above, including both ordinary boundary copies and ordinary cleanup. During sparse processing let V' be the volume of one actual caller invocation. The standard guards give B>=64p^4. For each active target, at most a 2/B fraction of u,t digit pairs touches a forbidden top digit. At most 8B/2^K of the possible y guards are too close to an edge. Hence the exceptional fraction is at most r(2/B+8B/2^K)=O(p^-3) when r=O(p). No independent-randomness hypothesis between the guards or between different batches is used.

The setup, placement, reindexing and coloring operate on O(r log n) public bits. Even linear tape scans for each access have fixed polynomial cost in p when n=Theta(p). A per-grouped-record offset, inverse, target key or good predicate likewise has fixed polynomial bit cost in p. There are at most V'/(2w·2^ell) such records. For every fixed j, p^j/2^ell <= 2^(j²/4), so this work has an O(V') charge per batch. A source row may hold an arbitrarily long record. Reading, writing, clearing and rewinding it is charged at its actual width. The sparse radix sort has at most N passes over the exceptional fraction, with at most O(p) key bits per grouped record. Its cost is O(V') since N=O(p), the exceptional density is O(p^-3), and a key fits within the charge for a grouped record. The actual full-field scans, merges, cleanup, elementary tails and rotations each cost O(V'). An empty or one-record exceptional list needs no sorting passes.

There are at most two suffix B1 calls, eighteen placement/restoration B1 calls, 3K nonempty sparse batches, thirty K rotations and 3K literal tail CNOT calls. All internal B1 calls in a batch have width H, **the full reserved width**, even if the color has one active pair. It follows that

    time = O(V' (r+1)^(1−a) polylog p),
    space = O(V') plus the fixed ordinary compilation.

Good, exceptional, keyed, bucket, output and outer input streams have total length O(V') by a constant number of live payload tapes. The repair handles one actual outer row at a time. The public plan is polynomial in p and has O(V') room. There is a constant number of live ordinary children. A mandatory startup cost polynomial in p can be paid by the complete 2^Theta(p)-record row. The geometry has C at most 2^O(p) and width w=Theta(p). Even an O(p)-bit public address update at each scalar record costs O(V'). The physical child may treat the final field as one opaque record with bit width up to 2^O(p). Its length, gap and arbitrary prefix count are encoded and their actual binary lengths are O(p).

## Domain of the recursive C caller

The original full tensor has n=log₂(T)=Theta(p₀) address bits and d=Theta(p₀^.997) axes. An active synthetic layer or CRT batch has h<=d. At root entry and exit a support extension moves at most 2h address bits, so each disjoint matching contributes at most h pairs to this routine. Its row-count reservoir has u=ceil(log₂(W^floor(log_N h)))=O(log p₀) bits for the fixed admitted C word. Inside the recursion, the physical address has n'=n−u=Theta(p₀) bits on **every** active register. The enclosing number of complete rows decreases by W at each step, remains a positive integer, and need not be a power of two. It is never used as a source of free fixed bits. Each internal column CNOT supplies r=f<=h pairs in that same n'-bit space.

The public precision is p=Theta(p₀), both on the initial layout and after the C caller adds at most a fixed constant times h private binary places. The signed component width w is Theta(p₀). There are at most 2r touched positions. The union includes no Cartesian product with temporary row labels. Eventually n'−ell>=10rG and there is an untouched ell-bit interval disjoint from the true low suffix: cut the n'−ell positions into at most 2r+1 gaps at the touched positions and use (2r+2)ell+2r=o(n'). These facts hold uniformly for every r<=d, including the fastest-growing allowed support. All geometries for which the literal policy could lose the exponent therefore occur within a fixed finite initial range of p₀. The actual C-node subset, signs and payload values do not affect this conclusion.

At the ordinary boundary the largest auxiliary width is H=r(4ceil(log₂p)+6)=O(p₀^.997 log p₀)=o(p₀). The actual suffix may be as wide as 2^O(p₀) bits, and the ordinary call receives its complete real width. Using the compact ordinary exponent requires its genuine multi-prefix, gap, full-record and restore contract over this range. The signed Gaussian producer, the arithmetic of the recursive C word, the finite funded-program existence argument, and the ordinary numerical rate come from their respective constructions.

## Evidence and source credit

`verify_shears.py` compares every payload record with its independently calculated XOR destination. The standard total policy is tested with scattered directions and three outer rows. The direct small-geometry exercise reaches one sparse batch, ten rotations, sixteen charged equal-width interchanges and 2,688 exceptional grouped records. Additional complete streams exercise an active bit in the moved suffix, the third reservoir after the first two are occupied, and the unguarded-tail branch. Independent enumeration checks all 2,048 raw addresses of a second geometry and both the ideal and raw inverses. A seeded 5,000-address probe checks two simultaneous unequal-gap targets whose sources lie in target guards, with 913 good cases and 4,087 exceptional cases. The independent checker first supplies a slow exact B1 for the same finite calls. A separate pass binds the actual compact ordinary adapter and checks all 6,144 records again. The recursive C-word test then uses this shear provider on real positive outer-row counts 1, 2 and 6, compares all Walsh outputs with independent scalar sums and checks that all native tapes are retired. These small ordinary calls use the adapter’s bounded elementary branch. The proof uses the growing ordinary binding for its rate.
