# Open construction obligations

The local data and reader words are complete within their stated types.
The following steps remain open for a complete multiplication supplier:

- Supply the recursive child helpers at their exact event times and frames.
  Unchanged arbitrary top-level fields do not prove a recursive host.
- Bind a closed routed caller with the required entrance, intermediate,
  and exit types. A selected-field role partition does not price a bank
  permutation or identify readers from different stages.
- Price each finite owner and monomial map implementation. The map counts
  in this package are upper bounds on calls, not finite arithmetic costs.
- Give a terminal decoder and any required odd-denominator representation.
  The route32 rational inverse contains factors of one third; its dyadic
  capture codes do not remove those factors from the inverse response.
- Prove precision through scalar primitive interiors, recursive children,
  and the complete caller. The recorded row norms apply only at emitted
  ideal-content boundaries.
- Recalculate a complete supplier at the intended selected rate after the
  construction obligations are met. The historical budget tests do not
  perform this calculation.

The route32 common-W lift and whole-W reflection decoder remain outside
the adopted scope. This package makes no claim that a proposed decoder is
necessary, that a search found a global optimum, or that native mass alone
determines recursive cost.
