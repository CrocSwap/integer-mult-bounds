# Source credit, license, and extraction scope

This package extracts completed work from the October 2026 centre-encoding
campaign. SOURCE-MANIFEST.json records each original path, byte hash, size,
and decompressed hash where needed. The original proofs, scripts, freezes,
and receipts in frozen/ are unchanged. They describe historical local
inputs, not the current selected supplier.

## Campaign contributions

The controller-followthrough lane supplied the row reader, source code,
row-involution completion, literal scalar words, and completion limits.
The new-core-transfer lane supplied productive capture, direct FULL,
line-entry reuse, clean exchange, clean dual, the native row involutions,
route32 model and native words, and the direct clean pair.

The bridge-access lane supplied the actual D25 views, forward involution
reader, and complete inverse-adjoint splice. The bridge-review lane
supplied the independent matrix, owner-route, forward-service, and late
query checks. The new-native-recipes lane supplied separate complete
word, physical phase, direct-clean, and complete-dual checks. The
independent-review lane supplied the cyclic flags and separate
row-involution and route32 model checks.

The coordinating researcher supplied the route split and reflection
formula. The budget-geometry lane supplied conditional native budgets.
The bridge-review lane supplied the independent invoice reader, third
rational interval engine, and strict mixed-rank consequences. Those
numerical checks remain conditional on the stated profiles and do not
admit their proposed caller or decoder.

The new extraction adds a portable exact input closure, two explicit
budget I/O adapters, an adopted-claim index, unified scope and cost notes,
and fresh cold replays. The independent publication review is a separate
agent calculation. It is not a maintainer acceptance or a formal proof of
the full multiplication theorem.
