The new work binds the remaining actual residual labels, gives causal symbolic
field references and completed leaf/header bases, and composes the weighted
caller by strong induction. We derive the resulting ordinary-interchange
saving with the elementary base. We claim neither a new generic weighted
factorization nor a new generic parking lemma.

The retained `physical_stream.py` contains an explicit weighted-child test
oracle and `qadic_streams.py` contains an ordinary test oracle. We retain
those files unchanged for their sequential primitives and arithmetic. The
new completed leaf/header paths never call those oracles; the replay replaces
them with rejecting functions. Their presence does not implement recursive
children. PROOF.md supplies that separate construction.

The pending predecessor PRs remain subject to review. We have not merged or
closed them. The repository's selected result remains unchanged.
