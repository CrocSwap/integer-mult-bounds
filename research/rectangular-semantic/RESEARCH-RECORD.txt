Geometric theorem and hypotheses
--------------------------------
Let H=ab,m=Hk,s0=k-2a,n=a+k. Require0<s0<a,2n<=H,H>2k,
2(H+k-1)<=m, nonzero residues a mod b and k mod b, and positive macro
middle widths. Exclude the inherited singular h=9 ambient form.
Retain the prescribed inner row/inverse-column labels

  R=[I_a,S_s0,I_a,I_a], C=[I_a,I_a,S_s0,I_a].

At each middle index beta, all prescribed rows and inverse columns must
consistently complete to an invertible matrix. The supplied explicit
completions are permutation matrices. Both A5 restriction graphs must be
trees. The first/last n outer multiplicity intervals are disjoint. Generic
outer line conditions include: each prescribed first row ell has ell(t)
nonzero and is not proportional to phi; each prescribed last inverse
column z has phi(z) nonzero and is not proportional to t. The opposite
functional is free subject to the actual duality constraint ell(z)=0.

For these hypotheses, the written A1/A3 proof supplies a nonempty rational
open set in the actual common prescribed family. Ordinary local producer
blocks, translated exits, A1/A3/A5 minors and line conditions are finitely
many nonzero rational functions on the same irreducible family. Their
product is nonzero, giving one simultaneous rational basis; finite tests
alone are not substituted for this argument.

A1: the actual corner separates by coefficient label. Eliminate its
first a-block and s0-block; remaining2x2 factors have determinants
containing distinct-position minors with shifts k mod b and a mod b.
The third a-pivot is a nonzero polynomial, and full rank gives the fourth.
Profile: a,s0,a,a,m-2(a+k).

A3: the top-H/right-H corner is a diagonal term minus a rank-(k-1)
perturbation. First/last k prescriptions expose an ordinary rank-(k-1)
corner. Its singleton plus k-2 block exhaust that perturbation, leaving
exact diagonal on indices[k-1,H-k], of width H-2k+2. Full null-corner
rank is proved independently: first H restrictions eliminate the inner
coordinates; the next k-1 restrictions can be chosen as a basis of the
annihilator of the outer line by genuinely free second rows. The dual
uses free penultimate inverse columns on the disjoint last interval.
Profile:2k-1 singletons; k-2,H-2k+2,m-2H-2k+2.

A5: both tree restrictions have full rank a+b-1. In the b=a-1 family
used here, the first corner pivot removes the rank-one term. The next
b-2 rightmost ordered pivots form a lower-bidiagonal block with nonzero
diagonal p_i*xi_(i+1). Count only that block, regardless of additional
runs observed in finite controls. Profile:a+1 singletons; b-2,
H-2(a+b-1). The middle block is disjoint and counted once.
