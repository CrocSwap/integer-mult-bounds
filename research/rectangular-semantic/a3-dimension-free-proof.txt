A3 in the actual prescribed outer family: dimension-free proof

Let H=ab, k>=3, r=H+k-1 and m=Hk. Assume H>2k, 2r<=m, and
the first/last n=a+k multiplicity intervals are disjoint. Let K be
any retained invertible inner basis and P_q=q xi a rank-one inner
projector in that basis, with all q_i and xi_i nonzero. Let P_t=t phi
be an outer rank-one projector, phi*t=1. The untransformed A3 projector
is (I_k-P_t) tensor (I_H-P_q). Its null projector Q has image
(F_k tensor q)+(t tensor F_H) and rank r. Conjugate by the actual
controlled family T, acting as G_i independently at multiplicity i.

The top-H/rightmost-H physical corner (outer row0, outer column k-1)
has the exact form D - diag(q) U V diag(xi), where
 D_i=-(G_i t)_0 (phi G_i^-1)_(k-1),
 U_i=e_0^t G_i (I-P_t), V_j=G_j^-1 e_(k-1).
Indeed for i!=j the entry is -q_i xi_j e0^t G_i(I-P_t)G_j^-1 e_last;
for i=j the additional identity-inner term is exactly D_i. The
rank-(k-1) term is global across this entire H-square corner.

For i=0,...,k-1, the prescribed first outer row of G_i is the i-th
row of L0. For j=H-k,...,H-1, the prescribed last inverse column is
the corresponding column of L0^-1. These two multiplicity intervals
are disjoint, so D is absent on their crossing. The crossing block is
-diag(q_i)(I-L0 P_t L0^-1)diag(xi_j). The ordinary rank-(k-1) lemma
therefore gives a first singleton pivot and k-2 further contiguous
pivots: used rows0,...,k-2 and used columns H-k+1,...,H-1. Its
(k-1)-minor is invertible on a nonempty open condition on L0.

These pivots exhaust the entire rank-(k-1) perturbation. Consider the
untouched matching index interval I={k-1,...,H-k}. For rows and columns
in I, all crossings to a selected row or column have no contribution
from D (their index sets are disjoint). The Schur complement of the
rank perturbation is therefore zero, leaving exactly diagonal D_I.
Every D_i is a nonzero rational function of the admissible bases: one
prescribed outer row or inverse column imposes a nonzero linear line
coordinate. The opposite inverse column or row remains free subject to
annihilating the prescribed one (because row0 and column k-1 differ).
Avoid the proper conditions that phi is proportional to a prescribed
row or t is proportional to a prescribed inverse column; then the free
functional can have nonzero pairing with the relevant line. The first
and last prescription intervals are disjoint. Their finite product is
nonzero. Thus this matching interval supplies one contiguous block of
size H-2k+2. Entries against remaining boundary columns are to its left;
no unselected column to its right can obstruct these ordered pivots.

It remains to justify full rank of the complete first-r/last-r nullity
corner in this same family; the subblock calculation alone is insufficient.
A vector in image(Q) before T is (w tensor q)+(t tensor z), with the
one-dimensional redundancy (w,z)=(lambda*t,-lambda*q). Its coordinates
after T are q_i(G_i w)_gamma+(G_i t)_gamma z_i.
Restrict to the first H physical rows, gamma0. Since (G_i t)_0!=0,
these equations determine z_i=-q_i(G_i w)_0/(G_i t)_0. The next k-1
physical rows have gamma1 and i=0,...,k-2. Their restrictions are
 q_i [e1^tG_i - ((G_it)_1/(G_it)_0)e0^tG_i] w.
Each of these k-1 matrices G_i has only its first row prescribed;
no inverse-column prescription acts there, by disjointness. Choose its
second row to be the corresponding member of a basis of Ann(t). It
is independent of the prescribed first row because that first row has
nonzero value on t. Complete each G_i invertibly. The k-1 displayed
functionals then form a basis of Ann(t), forcing w to be a multiple
of t and yielding only the known redundancy. The first-r restriction
of image(Q) therefore has full rank r at an admissible point, so its
minor is a nonzero rational function in the prescribed family.

Apply the dual argument at the other boundary to the last-r columns:
the last inverse column is prescribed and the preceding inverse column
is free, on the last k-1 multiplicity positions. These positions are
disjoint from the first ones. Taking the dual line/covector and completing
the inverse matrices gives a nonzero right-restriction minor. By the
irreducibility of the product of invertible completion families, their
product with the earlier pivot minors and line coordinates is nonzero.

Factor Q=XY, YX=I_r. Since first-r and last-r coordinates are disjoint,
the full A3 corner is -X_left Y_right and is invertible on that open
set. After the first k-1 pivots and the H-2k+2 matching pivots there
remain exactly 2k-2 independent corner pivots, retained as singletons.
The standard large-projector identity then supplies the separate middle
block of width m-2r=m-2H-2k+2. Hence the final allowed A3 profile is
 (2k-1) singleton pivots and blocks
 [k-2, H-2k+2, m-2H-2k+2].

Every step uses the actual prescribed family and dimension inequalities,
not a numerical substitution or an arbitrary generic matrix. This gives
individual nonzero minor functions for A3; combine their finite product
with the separately proved A1, A5, translated-exit and local-profile
conditions to select one common rational basis for the finite family.
The argument is supplied for independent mathematical review.
