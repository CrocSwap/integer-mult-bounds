Rectangular PR18 boundary family: independent A1/A3/A5 audit

Status and scope
----------------
This is a general compatibility argument for the prescribed family, not
an exponent or producer certificate. The intended checked instances are
(a,b,k)=(25,24,57) and (28,27,57). Their new producer counts, complete
physical histograms, mixed-width moments, semantic scalar bridge, joint
row stock and final assembly remain separate required checks.

Put s=k-2a, n=a+k, H=ab and m=Hk. Retain

  R=[I_a,S_s,I_a,I_a], C=[I_a,I_a,S_s,I_a],
  outer rows=[I_k,I_a], outer inverse columns=[I_a,I_k].

Required conditions: 0<s<a, 2n<=H, H>2k; all inner row/inverse-column
prescriptions consistently complete to invertible matrices; both A5
bipartite restriction graphs are trees; k mod b and a mod b are nonzero.
The two proposed instances satisfy these finite structural conditions.
The h=9 singular ambient form is excluded, as in the inherited producer.

The prescribed matrix family is nonempty and irreducible: inner fixed
rows/columns are consistent partial permutations and leave an affine
completion space; outer prescribed first rows and last inverse columns
occur on disjoint inner-index sets. Invertibility removes a nonempty
closed zero set. The free bases L0,L1,L2 are general linear factors.

A1: structural blocks and explicit nonvanishing conditions
----------------------------------------------------------
Write p,xi for the rank-one line pair in dimension a, t,phi for the pair
in dimension k, and u,eta,v,nu for two orthogonal rank-one projectors in
dimension b. Thus xi p=phi t=eta u=nu v=1 and eta v=nu u=0.
The actual null corner has entries

  delta(outer_row_i,outer_col_j) p_Ri xi_Cj v_beta nu_gamma
  + delta(R_i,C_j) t_outer_row_i phi_outer_col_j u_beta eta_gamma,

where beta=i mod b, gamma=(H-n+j) mod b. This is precisely the retained
factorized corner, not an arbitrary sparse-matrix model.

Partition rows into lengths a,s,a,a and columns into a,a,s,a. Call the
row groups A,B,C,D and column groups X,Y,Z,T, in physical order. The
A-by-T corner is diagonal from the second summand; the first summand
vanishes because its outer indices lie in disjoint sets. Its diagonal
entries are generically nonzero, giving the first ordered a-block.

After eliminating it, the B-by-Z corner is diagonal from the first
summand. The rank-a second summand cancels, and B-by-X and B-by-Y are
zero. Thus the next s pivots form an ordered s-block. Eliminating this
block does not alter the remaining X/Y entries on rows C,D.

The remaining 2a-square corner is a direct sum, by inner coefficient
label i=0,...,a-1, of two-by-two blocks. The following calculation proves
the necessary nonvanishing rather than inferring it from a sampled rank.
Write the rank-a contribution on a given label as f_row*g_column, where

  f_row=t_outer_row*u_beta, g_column=phi_outer_col*eta_gamma,

and denote a nonzero rank-k contribution by q_row,column. Then after
the first block elimination,

  C_X=-(f_C/f_A)q_AX-(q_CT/g_T)g_X-q_CT*q_AX/(f_A*g_T),
  C_Y=-(f_C/f_A)q_AY-(q_CT/g_T)g_Y-q_CT*q_AY/(f_A*g_T),
  D_X=q_DX-(f_D/f_A)q_AX,
  D_Y=q_DY-(f_D/f_A)q_AY.

The D row is a nonzero multiple of (nu_gammaX,nu_gammaY) whenever

  v_betaD*u_betaA-u_betaD*v_betaA != 0.

The two beta indices differ by k modulo b. The determinant of this
two-by-two block is, up to already nonzero scalar factors,

  (v_betaD*u_betaA-u_betaD*v_betaA)
  * (eta_gammaX*nu_gammaY-eta_gammaY*nu_gammaX).

The two gamma indices differ by a modulo b. Both displayed minors are
nonzero rational functions of the free GL_b basis because their two
coordinate positions are distinct. The constraints making u,v and
eta,nu dual do not force either such minor to vanish. A rational pair
of orthogonal rank-one projectors is simultaneously conjugate to the
first two coordinate projectors, so this assertion applies to every
actual paired motif, not only to an abstract line example.

The top-right pivot C_Y is also a nonzero rational function. Clear its
denominator f_A*g_T. Its numerator has the term -q_CT*q_AY, independent
of the two affected outer line-coordinate products. Setting the two
other products to zero, while supplying phi t=1 on a different outer
coordinate, leaves this term nonzero. This is a nonvanishing witness
for the numerator; it need not also satisfy all the other open tests.
Their finite product is nonzero on the same irreducible family.

Thus the remaining ordered profiles are a,a, and the complete A1
corner profile is a,s,a,a. The full null corner is nonsingular. Its
large-projector Schur middle remains m-2(a+k). This proves the retained
A1 argument for both proposed rectangles: the relevant shifts are
(9,1) for b=24 and (3,1) for b=27.

A3: the middle block and the full null corner
--------------------------------------------
The data-entrance projector has nullspace

  span(t) tensor F_H + F_k tensor span(q),

of dimension H+k-1; the two summands share one line. Under the existing
controlled outer basis, write its complementary projector as a diagonal
term in the inner index minus a rank-(k-1) term. In the first-k rows
against the last-k columns within the H-coordinate corner, the inner
index intervals are disjoint, so the diagonal term vanishes. The fixed
outer row/inverse-column prescriptions give a diagonal rescaling of
an ordinary rank-(k-1) projector in dimension k.

Its ordinary large-projector corner supplies one singleton and a
contiguous block of k-2 pivots. These exhaust that rank-(k-1) term.
Their row indices use the first k-1 inner positions and their column
indices use the last k-1 inner positions. On the untouched matching
interval [k-1,H-k], both couplings from the original diagonal term to
those pivot intervals are zero. The Schur update therefore cancels
the rank-(k-1) perturbation exactly and leaves a diagonal of length

  H-2k+2.

Its entries are -[G_i t]_0 [phi G_i^{-1}]_(k-1), up to the fixed
nonzero line-coordinate scalings. They are generically nonzero even
on indices with a prescribed first row or last inverse column: these
prescriptions occur on disjoint G_i, leaving the other factor free.
The corresponding first and last inner-index intervals stay disjoint.
This is a genuine contiguous matching block, not a rank-mass estimate.

For completeness, the remaining full null-corner determinant is not
merely assumed. A null vector can be parameterized as t tensor x plus
z tensor q, with the one-dimensional overlap factored out. Its first H
transformed coordinates have entries

  [G_i t]_0 [Kx]_i + [G_i z]_0 [Kq]_i.

Choose all [G_i t]_0 and [Kq]_i nonzero, then eliminate x using these H
coordinates. The next k-1 coordinates leave, on z modulo span(t),

  [Kq]_i * (row_1(G_i)
    - ([G_i t]_1/[G_i t]_0) row_0(G_i)) z,  i=0,...,k-2.

The first rows here are prescribed, but the second rows are free.
Choose those residual row functionals to be a basis of t's annihilator.
Each chosen second row is independent of its first row, so it completes
to an invertible G_i within the prescribed family. This proves the
primal restriction determinant is a nonzero rational function.

The dual restriction has the identical argument in inverse-transpose
coordinates at the last H+k-1 positions: the last inverse columns are
prescribed, and the penultimate inverse columns of the last k-1
multiplicity matrices are free. They give a basis of the corresponding
dual annihilator. No same G_i receives both first-row and last-column
prescriptions because 2n<=H. These two nonvanishing witnesses need not
be the same specialization; finite-product nonvanishing supplies one
simultaneous rational choice with all other conditions.

After the first k-1 pivots and the H-2k+2 matching block, exactly 2k-2
null-corner pivots remain. Treat them as singletons. Including the first
ordinary singleton, A3 therefore has

  2k-1 singletons; blocks k-2, H-2k+2, m-2H-2k+2.

A5: full restriction and the conservative b-2 block when b=a-1
-------------------------------------------------------------
The nullspace of (I-P1) tensor (I-P2) has dimension a+b-1. Rescaling
its prescribed first and last restrictions converts each into a weighted
bipartite incidence matrix. The checked primal and dual graphs are trees
on a+b vertices. Tree rank therefore proves both restrictions nonsingular
over Q whenever the line coordinates used as scalings are nonzero.

For both new instances b=a-1, n5=a+b-1=2b and H-n5 is divisible by b.
The rightmost a boundary labels are 0,...,a-1. The top-right nullcorner
pivot is -p_0*xi_b*v_0*nu_(b-1). For rows i=1,...,b-2 and columns
j=b+1,...,2b-2, eliminating it leaves

  delta(i,j-b) p_i xi_(j-b+1)
  + delta(i,j-b+1) v_i nu_(j-b).

The rightmost entry is at j=i+b, equals p_i*xi_(i+1), and is nonzero.
Earlier pivot elimination cannot change any entry to its right. Thus
these b-2 pivots form one ordered contiguous block. The actual projector
corner is the negative of the null corner, with the same pivot positions.
Keep the other a+1 corner pivots singleton and preserve the old middle
block H-2(a+b-1), disjoint from this new corner block.
