\subsection{A sharper certificate for the same three complex classes}
\label{sec:source-corner-complex}

Retain exactly the dimension-28 complex producer, three whole-residual
classes, and dependency-path guard of
Section~\ref{sec:source-frame-complex}. No source-frame modification is
made to the complex network. Its weights, ratios and displayed rational
logarithm upper bounds are unchanged. Evaluating the same exact bound at
\[
 a_{\rm c}=18179/10^{10},\qquad \sigma=1-a_{\rm c},
\]
gives a strict gap greater than $2.1196\times10^{-11}$. This changes only
the selected exponent, not the complex construction or its path budget.
In particular $C_1=11999/10000$ remains certified.

For the combined bit saving $a_{\rm b}=1816/10^9$ and $\beta=1/1000$,
the leaf saving is
\[
 (1-\beta)a_{\rm c}=18160821/10^{13}>1816/10^9.
\]
Consequently both $\sigma<\tau$ and
$\sigma+\beta(1-\sigma)<\tau$. This latter condition is essential:
comparing the bit and complex savings alone would miss the leaf cost.
Use $\lambda=\tau+10^{-16}$ and $\lambda'=\tau+2\cdot10^{-16}$ in the
following active layer interface. All twenty-nine assembly inequalities
and all seven margins are strictly positive in the exact certificate.
