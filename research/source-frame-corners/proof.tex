\subsection{Source frames with dimension-thirty data corners}
\label{sec:source-frame-corner-combination}

We combine eumemic's auxiliary source frames
(Section~\ref{sec:scratch-source-frames}, PR~\#13) with the dimension-thirty
producer of Section~\ref{sec:dimension30-batching} and the two data-edge
refinements of Section~\ref{sec:controlled-corner-refinement}.
This combination and the additional data-corner analysis were prepared by
Rohan Arun with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. The source-frame
construction and third complex class are credited to eumemic, the bulk
recursion to icekylinx, and the ternary producer to Zhihao Chen.

\paragraph{Compatible changes and an unchanged rank deficit.}
Set $h=30$, $H=900$, $m=27000$, and retain exactly the producer, matching,
$R=17515487$, $v=142506$, $N=v^3$, $B=v^2R$, $W$, and $s$ of the
dimension-thirty construction. The proof of
Lemma~\ref{lem:scratch-source-frames} is dimension independent.
For every stage-two auxiliary role use source $P_{D_0}$ and sink
$I+P_{D_0}$. Its entrance becomes zero, its internal edges are unchanged,
and its exit is the rank-$(m-h)$ projector
\[
 E=I-\Pi_t\otimes\Pi_X,\qquad \operatorname{rank}\Pi_X=h.
\]
This replaces the old entrance and sink profiles; none of their old
width-840, width-900, or width-25200 blocks is counted. Instead the exit
has $h=30$ singleton pivots and one width-$(m-2h)=26940$ block.
The edge-rank sum, deficit, scalar circuit, and common gate labels do not
change. In particular the source-frame modification affects no data edge.

On each of the $2N$ stage-three data entrances, keep the width-25142
middle block and use the refined first corner of
Section~\ref{sec:controlled-corner-refinement}: 87 singleton pivots and
one width-842 block. On each of the $2N$ stage-two data entrances, use
59 singleton pivots and a width-782 block. The first/third-stage auxiliary
joins keep 60 singleton pivots and a width-26880 block, with $B$ copies.

\paragraph{One controlled basis still suffices.}
The source-frame exit requires a nonsingular first-$h$-row,
last-$h$-column corner. Lemma~\ref{lem:source-frame-basis} proves this is a
nonzero rational-function condition in the same free variables $K,G_i$.
Its proof applies at $h=30$: the first and last $h$ coordinates are
disjoint, and a rank-$h$ subspace of $V$ can be sent to any independent
$h$-tuple by $K$. The first/third join conditions are unchanged.

Impose the additional data conditions proved in
Section~\ref{sec:controlled-corner-refinement}: nonzero $a_i b_i$, the
nonsingular large stage-three corner, its nonsingular first $H$ corner
and off-diagonal $(h-1)$ corner, and the stage-two data corner of size
$2h-1$. The nonzero $a_i b_i$ may still be imposed even though the old
auxiliary sink edges no longer occur; they are conditions on the basis,
not additional circuit edges or costs. Every condition individually has
a nonzero numerator polynomial, and the parameter space is the same
product of general linear groups. Their finite product is nonzero.
Thus one rational tuple satisfies every exit, join and data condition,
including the finite collection of dual projectors. Enumeration of
rational tuples with the stated determinant tests terminates.

All selected blocks have matching physical order under lower/lower
elimination. The two data proofs already track their Schur complements;
adding nonvanishing conditions for the new exits changes neither those
identities nor their ordering. This proves compatibility, rather than
assuming that separately optimized bases can be used on different edges.

\paragraph{The combined moment.}
The complete list of batched widths and multiplicities is
\[
 (26880,B),\quad(26940,B),\quad(25142,2N),\quad(842,2N),\quad(782,2N).
\]
Treat every remaining rank unit as a singleton. Then
\[
 S=s-B(26880+26940)-2N(25142+842+782)
   =65379670780393117512,
\]
\[
 \Psi(x)=\frac{S+B(26880^x+26940^x)
                 +2N(25142^x+842^x+782^x)}{W\,27000^x}.
\]
At $x=1$ the normalized mass is exactly $1-3857/52973979000$.
For each child ratio $r$, use the sixteen-term positive logarithm series,
extracting powers of two and rounding its upper endpoint upward to a
multiple of $10^{-10}$. With $e^z\le(1-z)^{-1}$, exact rational arithmetic
certifies
\[
 \Psi(1-1816/10^9)<1,\qquad
 1-\text{certified upper bound}>1.9154\times10^{-10}.
\]
All child widths are strictly smaller than 27000. The retained mixed-width
row construction and recurrence therefore apply with these fixed constants.

\begin{lemma}[Source-frame and data-corner interchange]
\label{lem:source-corner-chunk-swap}
The arbitrary-width finite-tape interchange interface of
Lemma~\ref{lem:batched-chunk-swap} holds with
$1-\tau=1816/10^9$ for the dimension-thirty construction above.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The source-frame endpoint identity, common controlled basis, ordered
recursive blocks and strict moment have just been established. Apply
Section~\ref{sec:mixed-width-rows}, retaining its bounded remainder
handling, row reservation, fixed alphabet and spectator fields.
\end{proof}
