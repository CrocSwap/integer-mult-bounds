# Assumptions and resource boundaries

The B3 proof and heterogeneous-bank theorem are separate conditional implications. In particular, the latter's rank-eight/rank-nine helpers cannot instantiate the former's common-W local premise unchanged. The concrete `1` versus `-i` example obstructs that substitution; it is not a counterexample to a different paid, normalized, heterogeneous interface.

The B3 local premise is an exact full-array `Inv`/`GInv` route, in both orientations, with the claimed scratch behavior and exact paid-block price. A scalar matrix identity alone does not supply it. The external seven-stage geometry is proved from that premise. Every live slot remains distinct physical storage; arbitrary correlations of contents are allowed. Designated erased scratch is not live helper stock.

The bank theorem assumes complete local macros have the prescribed data action, independent helper blocks, no completed data/helper coupling, no undeclared live effects and restored private workspace. Its proof permits serial complete macros, not arbitrary interleaving of their interiors. Both actual signed adapters and paid missing-axis children are required.

The abstract block model prices recursive blocks; rational pointwise maps and address translations have zero **paid block** cost. Their bit-operation, router and workspace costs are not zero and are not established by this packet. PR390's full pre/main/post chronology, signed local children, private centres, coefficient lowering, nine-stage data phases, compiler bounds, precision/error analysis and exponent remain separate obligations.

Exact replay uses binary matrices of dimension at most 73 and Gaussian integer-pair vectors of length 512. Each literal adapter column uses at most sixteen C factors, with a common denominator bounded by `2^16` and numerator component magnitude bounded by `2^16`. Both adapters are checked on all 512 basis columns. Binary matrix and phase arithmetic is exact. No floating point or parameter search is used.

At h=9 the B3 checks execute instances covering all 255 allowed ports under two owners, including a nonsymmetric owner: 12,240 boundary projector equalities, 3,060 phase-polynomial identities, 510 terminal identities and 14 helper prefixes. Schedules are checked for h=2 through 16. The general proof supplies universal owner and parameter quantifiers; two checked owners do not by themselves prove them. These are repeated instances of a few set and orthonormal-family identities, not thousands of independent obligations. The signed-completion theorem also passes all 16,384 direction multiplicity choices modulo four in dimension three.

Comparing the degree-two modulo-four phase polynomial checks every character in the tested dimension-63 case without enumerating `2^63` characters. Generic matrix operations admit an O(m^3) bit-operation bound with O(m^2) storage; all-column state simulation is exponential in local dimension, O(r*2^(2h)) Gaussian operations. Here h=9 is fixed. Python integer bit complexity is included in these small explicit operand bounds, not treated as an oracle for unbounded arithmetic.

The abstract B3 construction uses `|O(7h,2)|(6v+R)` live roles, with `|O(m,2)| <= 2^(m^2)`. One-column arrays require `2^m` scalar addresses each; f-column arrays require `2^(mf)`. The checker does not materialize that network or enumerate the cover. No all-size algorithmic efficiency claim follows merely from finiteness.

`StructuralCore.lean` is a partial standalone formalization compiled with installed Lean 4.33.0. It proves the joint-state induction, correlated substitution, initial-frame cancellation, right-owner bijections, concrete character arithmetic and positive, strict idle-rank bounds. The concrete list theorems establish orthogonality to q and phase arithmetic; Lean does not prove those lists orthonormal/spanning or connect their phases to C-operator semantics. The rank bounds are arithmetic lemmas, not a formal connection to the schedule. It does not formalize the complete Clifford geometry or certify a pinned repository build. It contains no sorry or new axiom. The printed dependencies are ordinary Lean foundational axioms only; the finite obstruction and owner proofs use none.
