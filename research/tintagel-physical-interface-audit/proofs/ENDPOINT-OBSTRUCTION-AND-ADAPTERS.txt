Heterogeneous completion theorem and exact common-endpoint obstruction

For integer signed multiplicities epsilon_l and directions v_l, put
  F=product C_vl^epsilon_l,
  n_i=sum_l epsilon_l (v_l)_i,
  K=sum_l epsilon_l v_l v_l^T over F2.
Then F=X_delta C_full if and only if K=I, with unique
  delta_i=(n_i-1)/2 mod2.
Indeed its character exponent modulo four is
  sum_i n_i z_i - 2 sum_(i<j) (sum_l epsilon_l v_li v_lj) z_i z_j.
Terms of degree >=3 vanish mod4. Equality on e_i determines the linear
coefficients and requires n_i odd. Equality on e_i+e_j forces the mixed sums
even. These are exactly K=I. They also suffice on every character, which is
a basis. Both operators fix the constant character, so no global phase remains.
For an orthogonal g this yields
  product_i C_(g e_i)=X_delta(g) C_full,
  delta(g)_i=(weight(row_i(g))-1)/2 mod2.
Applying X_delta(g) again removes it. Orthogonal geometry alone does not do so.

the coordinate blocks of each bank together with paid padding partition all

need not commute or stay in the eventual small coordinate block.

For fixed bank owner g, role j visits it uniquely at d=g A_j. Its directions
therefore become exactly g e_b. The factors commute, so once-only coverage
and the preceding criterion complete each bank to X_delta(g) C_full. Correct
it to C_full. Every invocation identity holds on all simultaneous current

No helper-state matrix inverse and no probabilistic independence is used.
Within an invocation, equal owner factors imply equal A; distinct bank labels
separate roles even if their A agree. Conversely, a repeated bank/route pair
