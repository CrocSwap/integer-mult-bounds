Work over V=F2^m with m=7h and h>=2. Let ports t have vectors u_t in F2^h
of odd weight other than the all-ones vector. Let v be the number of ports and
R the number of distinct shared helper slots. Assume a local physical package
I having the pinned Inv contract, or the GInv contract with the gwinB windows
below, in BOTH orientations F:(x,y)->(x,y+x) and B:(x,y)->(x-y,y).
Assume its advertised cost C_inv is the actual exact paid block price.

The route premise is not just a scalar matrix identity. With legal window Phi,
Phi(0)N0=1 and Phi(I)=W Phi(0), it carries bank frames
  (Phi(u_t u_t^T), Phi(0)) -> (Phi(I), Phi(I+u_t u_t^T)),
and every helper frame Z_s -> W Z_s, for every simultaneous family Z_s.
It must act on the entire joint live state with no additional completed cross
terms, implement the specified scratch scalar action, and affect no other
physical roles. The pinned construction may erase designated copies/scratch;
such roles are not arbitrary live helpers. All embeddings must be injective.

Then a finite proper block-route network on
  |O(m,2)| (6v+R)
live roles has completed live action equal to a direct sum of K_m, with price
  |O(m,2)| [7 C_inv(phi) + 2v (
    2b(h-1)+b(2h-2)+b(4h-4)+b(4h+2)+b(2h+4)+b(6))],
where b(0)=0 and b(r)=phi(r) for r>0. Scratch is excluded from this live count.
The statement is valid on all live arrays simultaneously, hence on arbitrarily
correlated banks/helpers, without a clean-helper assumption.

Proof of phases. Let W be the Walsh matrix and
  F(P)=W diag_z(i^wt(Pz)) W^-1,
  C_a=((1+i)/2)I+((1-i)/2)X_a.
On character chi_z, C_a has eigenvalue i^(a dot z). Binary addition gives
  wt(x+y)=wt(x)+wt(y)-2|supp(x) intersect supp(y)|,
so F(P+Q)=F(P)F(Q) whenever P^T Q=0. In particular, for orthonormal columns
b_i and coordinate set S,
  F(sum_{i in S} b_i b_i^T)=product_{i in S} C_{b_i}^{wt(b_i)}.
Odd wt(b_i)=1 or 3 modulo four gives respectively C_b or C_b^-1=X_b C_b.
Thus a nested climb is one routed positive-rank block followed by translation
X_c, c=sum_{i in increment, wt(b_i)=3 mod4} b_i. This keeps every sign.
The full address-router inverse is B^T; it cannot be replaced by B in general.

Proof of owners/windows. Choose a zero coordinate j of u_t, w=u_t+e_j,
and A_t=(I+ww^T) swap(0,j). Since w dot w=0, A_t is orthogonal and A_t e_0=u_t.
Let D_t=diag(A_t,I,...,I), with blocks W_0,...,W_6 each of width h.
Let pi_k exchange the NONPIVOT coordinates of blocks 0 and k, keeping pivots
fixed; let tau_k exchange the WHOLE two blocks. Define rho_kt=D_t pi_k D_t^T.
For bank owner g, stage k invocation owner is d=g rho_kt. Invocation d uses
helper owner gamma=d tau_k. These right multiplications are bijections since
rho_kt and tau_k are involutions. Set them to identity at stage zero.
Do not accumulate their products across stages. In a fixed stage, every
physical bank/port and every helper set is used exactly once. Different ports
and pairs remain different physical roles even when they contain equal arrays.

Put Q_k=sum_{j=1}^k P_(W_j minus pivot), with Q_0=0, and
  Phi_kd(P)=F(d (P direct-sum Q_k) d^T).
The window is W_kd=F(d P_W0 d^T) and its base inverse is F(d Q_k d^T)^-1.
The base/window projectors are orthogonal, proving both required window
equations. This is the source gwinB at extra-block type Fin 6; h<7h supplies
its strict ambient-size hypothesis. The port hypotheses supply its adapters.

In the fixed owner basis B=gD_t set
  S_k^X=W_0 union W_1^- union ... union W_k^-,
  S_k^Y=S_k^X minus the initial pivot.
For k>=1, (g rho_kt)D_t=B pi_k. Acting by pi_k sends the four local coordinate
sets {pivot} union Q_k, Q_k, W_0 union Q_k, W_0^- union Q_k precisely to
S_(k-1)^X, S_(k-1)^Y, S_k^X, S_k^Y. Hence the complete projector matrices
match at every invocation boundary. F of equal matrices is exactly equal,
not equal only up to an unspecified phase. Stage zero starts at line/zero.

Proof for helpers. Fixed helper owner gamma is used at d=gamma tau_k, so
its multiplier at stage k is F(gamma P_Wk gamma^T). Orthogonality of the seven
blocks gives, after stage k,
  Z^(k)=F(gamma P_(W_0 union ... union W_k) gamma^T) D,
for its initial matrix D. The arbitrary right factor D is never commuted or
inverted. At the end the left factor is F(I)=K_m. For an actual fixed physical
network choose initial helper frame I, then the route is valid for all helper
contents. Universal quantification of the interface over Z does not require
one uniform implementation independent of every possible bookkeeping frame.

Proof of schedule and legal twin gates. Use the literal zero-indexed stages
  F1, B1, F1, B2, F2, B3, F3.
After stages 1/2 align pair 2 to pair 1's current X/Y frames; after stages 3/4
align pair 3 to pair 2's. At the odd cuts use X_old+=X_new, Y_new-=Y_old;
at the even cuts use Y_new+=Y_old, X_old-=X_new. Complete all tails, then for
new=3,2 use Y_old-=Y_new and X_new+=X_old. Every twin addition is between equal
complete frames. The idle increments, on EACH side of each pair, are
  pair 1: [4h+2]
  pair 2: [2h-2, h-1, 2h+4]
  pair 3: [4h-4, h-1, 6].
They are nested differences in one basis; every nonzero rank is <7h for h>=2.
The scalar word gives X_p=-y_p,Y_p=x_p. This follows by elimination or the
borrowing identity A;F_old;B;B_new;F_new;C = F_old direct-sum quarter_new,
where quarter(x,y)=(-y,x). Apply it first to pair 2/3 and then pair 1/2,
leaving FBF on pair 1. The independent checker additionally verifies every
integer input column of the literal six-role word.

Proof of terminal correction. Write u=g(u_t,0,...,0), L=F(uu^T), J=F(I+uu^T).
Initially the pair stores (Lx,y); after the word/tails it stores (-K_m y,Jx).
The identities L^2=X_u and K_m=LJ imply X_u J=K_m L. Translate Y by u and
simultaneously exchange (X,Y)<-(Y,-X). The output is (K_m Lx,K_m y), which
is K_m applied to the original physical pair. Helpers already have K_m.
All initial live frames are invertible; cancelling the initial frame proves
the claimed full live operator. Omitting this translation leaves X_u on the
completed X output; u=e_0,z=e_0 gives an exact -1 character defect.

Only finite tensor products, additions, bijective relabeling and exact matrix
equalities were used. All claims extend to f address columns by applying each
frame as its f-fold tensor product. Arbitrary correlations follow by substituting
any joint function of common parameters into the full-state equality. Sharing
an address array by value is allowed; aliasing two distinct roles is not.

This K=3 write-up instantiates existing generic block/window machinery.
The scalar check(k,h) already includes k=3 in the pinned bridged.py. The
PR377/PR381 lineage already includes nine-stage B4 geometry (as credited by
the bundled PR404 notice, without independently inspecting PR377 here).
The premise is non-vacuous: pinned Bridge/Inv.lean's XCircuit.inv constructs
homogeneous Inv instances from block circuits. This does not supply the
particular optimized local package or make PR390's heterogeneous one common-W.

The sum of idle ranks per side is 14h+4. Define sigma as the excess of the
local paid rank mass over 2v(h-1)+Rh; it is an accounting parameter, not a
proved bound for PR390. If local rank mass is
2v(h-1)+Rh+sigma, total B3 mass is (6v+R)7h-6v+7sigma. Its deficit is therefore
6v-7sigma. This is accounting, not an exponent proof. B2 has two pairs, five
stages, dimension 5h and deficit 4v-5sigma; its theorem does not instantiate B3.
