## 2. Chronological product preserves the stronger native contract

For units U,V with targets k_i,h_j and paid ranks r_l,s_a, run a distinct U
copy at column count h_j for each productive role j of V. At a fixed paid
position l, pause those U copies and invoke V at column count r_l on their
current owner fields. Those owners are distinct across U copies. They may be
correlated and may include owned centers; arbitrary-input correctness permits
that, but does not permit aliasing two physical slots.

Use the inherited whole-job adapters to match each requested operator, finish
V, restore the adapters, and only then resume U. Separate owned centers remain
allocated until erased. Different chronological positions of the same U copy
are never batched as if they were independent simultaneous inputs.

The expanded word is finite and acyclic. It gives target ranks k_i h_j and
paid ranks r_l s_a, so its moments multiply. This is a substitution of native
programs, not the invalid identity (A+B) tensor-powered = A tensor-powered
plus B tensor-powered. An ambient m_U m_V space suffices, including all
spectators; every rank product fits. Iterating gives T_t^v and P_t^v.

Normalization is a whole-job operation. General native factorizations use
both their recorded input and output monomial factors; these need not be an
inverse pair. Canonicalize with those factors before using the product
contract. Once a job is canonical, relocation to specified axes is ordinary
coordinate conjugation. This is the precise reading of the completion proof:
there is no assertion that every raw Clifford endpoint is conjugate to the
positive kernel by one monomial, and no output-only normalization shortcut.
All such adapters belong to the counted finite program.
