Helper restrictions can make previously unavailable histories informative,
and zero deletions and caches can share target debits. Separate negative
results do not automatically compose. Fresh reconstruction gives567 rows of
rank525 and a direct sum of418 groups. A determinant argument allocates target
funding across those groups without double spending it. Eleven groups contain
caches; the rest inherit source-checked zero-row funding. The proof handles
arbitrarily many constraints and arbitrary block operators, including
noncommuting operators, rather than a finite search over proposed relations.

The fine calculation permits any deterministic processing of the granted
original history on the linear input domain. It does not assume the provider
was written as a linear formula. A common-tangent argument also covers C1

proof preserves all finitely many zero/cache dependencies at one point and
