All 89 .lean modules in lean/Nov15 (the sources as staged, comments scrubbed of internal references) were compiled in this final form.

METHOD
 * Every module was compiled ALONE, in dependency order, one lean process at a time: `lean -j1 -o out/Nov15/X.olean Nov15/X.lean` from lean/, under a 3.5 GB (3,500,000 KB) RSS watcher. Fresh output directory first on LEAN_PATH (so every module of this package was compiled here), then the pinned Mathlib packages.
 * CongestionI3Cfg was killed by the watcher once (peak 3.43 GB) and passed on the re-run under the same guard (75 s, 3.34 GB); CongestionI3 failed only because its import was missing at that moment and passed after. WordDuality and PlaneLaws were built in a second pass, after the main pass.
 * Axioms: the `#print axioms` lines inside each file (count and union below). Union over everything: {propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound}; NO Lean.ofReduceBool, no sorryAx, no `declaration uses sorry` warning in any log. Data-only files print nothing.
 * Warnings in the logs are linter notes; no errors in any module.
 * The `lake build` route was not run (it would download Mathlib).
