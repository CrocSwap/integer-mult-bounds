Kernel-only (`decide +kernel`, `ring`, `linear_combination`), 0 sorry, 23 s / 3.2 GB (`Nov15/Spec0K0Core.lean` must be built first). Axioms [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound] (reduction, cover, distinct, Gl_closed, step_T checked).
- Data: `Gl` = the 8 letter permutations <(AB),(CD),(AC)(BD)>, `reps` = RB5/reps0.txt (90 orders), orders = permutations of the six letters, g.o = o.map g.
- `Gl_closed`, `Gl_inv`, `Gl_id`: Gl is closed under composition and inverses (a group of order 8 acting on orders).
- `cover` (via `coverC`, canonical form = least base-6 code in the orbit, 720 orders over `List.permutations'`): every order is the image of a rep. `distinct` (via `distC`): no two reps are in one orbit, `reps_nodup`, `reps_length = 90`. Hence exactly 90 orbits.
- Invariance of the kill condition (model level): `Rel'`/`Kill'` use word products of letter matrices rho : Fin 6 -> M4 (A..D jobs, E/F flags). For every g in Gl: `step_F` (g fixes the pairs {A,B},{C,D}: rho'' = rho o g) or `step_T` (g exchanges them: rho'' = conj(rho o g), conj = conjugation by the (2 3)-swap of the middle 2x2 block, which keeps jobs/flags in the model: conj_MJ, jobOK_conj, epsOK_conj; swaps units 1<->2, 5<->6 and their diagonal signs, conj_unit/conj_omega/conj_LLm/conj_KU). Both give: rho model, Rel'_{g.r}(rho) => Rel'_r(rho'') and Kill'_r(rho'') => Kill'_{g.r}(rho).
- `reduction`: if Kill holds for the 90 reps over all model points satisfying Rel, it holds for all 720 orders.
