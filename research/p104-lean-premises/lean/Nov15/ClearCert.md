# ClearCert.lean: CLEAR certificates / CLEARFORM on the TokenCancel row model
Kernel-only, no sorry, no native_decide, 13 `#print axioms` within [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]. 8-9 s, 1.9 GB. Imports Nov15.TokenCancel (St, Ev, step, run, LegalEv, Legal). Source: the CLEARFORM argument and `verify_certificates` of the #7630 checker (read as data).

## Model
Rows are in initial-row coordinates (`Reg -> K`). `rowSpan s` = span of all rows (zero rows add nothing, a cleared register keeps a zero row). `Lossless s i` = row i is in the span of the other rows. Input space U = span of the public inputs; `FullEnd U s es` = the final rows span U. `Good` = what check.py enforces besides legality: no birth in the tail, SCALE unit, ADD between distinct registers, COPY/INIT into a zero row (check.py:119-120, :112). `DeadZero` = never-born registers have zero rows (preserved under legality).

## Theorems
1. RANK LEMMA. `span_step_le`: any non-birth event only shrinks the span. `span_step_ge`: any good non-CLEAR event preserves it exactly. `span_clear_eq`/`lossless_iff`: CLEAR of i leaves span(others), so lossless iff span unchanged. `finrank_clear_le`: a CLEAR lowers the rank by <= 1. `every_clear_lossless`: if the initial rows lie in U and the final rows span U (endpoint exact), then EVERY CLEAR is lossless. No Legal hypothesis is needed.
2. CHECKER. `Certified s es cs` = every CLEAR at position n has a finitely supported certificate, keys live and != the cleared field, erased row = exact combination (CertOK, mirrors check.py:672-678; `cs` = the JSON `clear_event_index`). `certOK_lossless`: a passing certificate means lossless. `certified_span_ge` + `checker_sound`: a passing checker on a good, birth-free word started from full span gives a full-span endpoint AND every CLEAR lossless (so the check certifies information preservation, not just the single clear). `checker_complete`: an endpoint-exact legal word has certificates at all CLEARs, so the checker rejects nothing it should accept.
3. CLEARFORM. `legal_congr`: legality (chronology/field identity, clause E3) reads only `loc`, never rows. `clearform`: let `ErasureOK Sk owned s es := Sk(owned INIT/MOVE/CLEAR events) /\ AllLossless`. Two endpoint-exact words with the same owned events are both AllLossless, and satisfy ErasureOK together or not at all, whatever they erase. `Sk` is an ARBITRARY predicate of the owned-event skeleton: it stands for E1-E3 (allocation, chronology, volume) and is assumed, not proved.
4. INSTANCES. `toy_certified`/`toy_end`: a 4-register word in the real certificate shape (2-term cert {0:-1,1:1}, like event 127621 {500:-1,1072:1}), checked in the kernel and fed through `checker_sound`. The 180 real certificates are NOT replayed: that needs the 208,738-event word (too big for the kernel, no native_decide). They enter as the hypothesis `Certified W0 W certs` with W, certs = the #7630 word file and its clear-certificate file; the checker `certificates` mode prints PASS_CERTIFICATES_ON_7623 for it. Fidelity of `Certified` to check.py is by reading, not proved.

## Paper statement corrected (said at once)
The CLEARFORM note, section 1(a): "COPY into a zero owned row ... rho unchanged" is right only for a ZERO target. `copy_nonzero_loses_rank` (Lean, over Q): in two public registers, COPY 0 <- 1 drops e_0 from the span. check.py enforces the zero-target condition, so the words are fine, but "every event except CLEAR keeps the rank" is false as stated without it. The headline lemma survives (the span never grows; `span_step_le` needs only non-birth). Also "lossless is implied by correctness" needs endpoint exactness at the span level over U, not over all registers: with owned coordinates in the row space the target is U, not the whole space.

## What is still ASSUMED (and how much of `owned_erasure` this narrows)
- Narrowed: the "dirty content" sub-condition of the clear-form gap (Q1). Content is not a premise: losslessness follows from endpoint exactness, and the certificate check is sound and complete for it. Sk-equal words are interchangeable.
- Still assumed: the erasure SERVICE (E1-E3 allocation/chronology/volume as `Sk`, E4 linear cost, E5 paid non-reversible erase, E6 dirty residuals counted via earlier ADD reads). E4-E6 are not even expressible in the row model. That is roughly the cost/permission half; the Lean work covers the content half only.
- Model scope: INIT after CLEAR of the same name is renamed to a fresh register (TokenCancel has no loc reset on clear); MOVEs and charts are only used through TokenCancel legality (the extra z/V move conditions only strengthen `Legal`). Scalar projection of the arbitrary-array statement is the compiler-proof reduction (assumed). Rows over Q (checker uses exact Fractions).
- Not done: replay of the 180 real certificates, and the cost-invariance (E2/E4) side of CLEARFORM, which would need the cost model.
