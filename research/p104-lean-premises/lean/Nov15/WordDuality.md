## Correction to DU1 wording (stated at once)
DU1: "COPY becomes ADD". As matrices this is false: `copy_not_add` (COPY 0←1 has matrix [[0,1],[0,1]], whose transpose [[0,0],[1,1]] is not 1 + q·e_ab for any a ≠ b, ADD matrices have diagonal 1). The dual of a COPY is an ADD only on a clean target (the usual INIT-then-COPY use) and must carry the dual of the target's INIT (a CLEAR). `dualEv` sends COPY ↦ ADD for legality only; matrix semantics (`Mrec_dual`) is restricted to MOVE/ADD/SCALE.
Not formalised: the P104 chart/physical check (DU1 caveat 2), the 271 missing perp frames, and the palindromic construction W = D(A).A.
