### ClearCert.lean (imports TokenCancel)

- `span_step_le`: any non-birth event only shrinks the row span. `span_step_ge`: any Good non-CLEAR event preserves it. `lossless_iff`, `finrank_clear_le` (a CLEAR lowers the rank by at most 1).

## C. SWAP-NULL (SwapNull)
4-op swap gadget at a shared chart (`add h1 h2 +1; add h2 h1 −1; add h1 h2 +1; scale h2 −1`) swaps two helper rows. `undo_one_iff`: when the final matrix is the identity on all registers (F = 1), the swap is hub-undoable iff both helpers end in V or both are born in G0. `stays_multiset`, `delta_eq`: the stay multiset is unchanged and the cost change is f(e2−b1)+f(e1−b2)−f(e1−b1)−f(e2−b2), zero whenever the two ends or the two births agree (`delta_zero_end`, `delta_zero_birth`), hub-undoable or not; `swap_null`: every hub-undoable swap is cost-null (given V-enders end at one dim and G0 members are born at 0). The converse (zero change ⇒ undoable) is not claimed. `nonzero_not_undoable`, `converse_witness`: a swap with nonzero cost change cannot be undone by the hubs.

## D. B-form optimality (BFormOpt)
Pointwise in s, for all arm lengths a, b and parameters h, e: `bill_lb` (any feasible schedule), `CB_closed` (arms of 1 included), `own_abel`, `hub_nonpos`, `bform_optimal` (F ≤ F(B form) on B ports), `port_total` (96 A + 8 B ports reproduce the caller cost). `hB_discharge` / `ceiling_of_ports` discharge JointCeiling's hB for B ports, with A ports of the built type. `A_drop_*`: on A ports the B form is NOT optimal (dropping a last-stage own role gains 1 − ψ(1)), which is why A ports are fixed to the built type. Still hypotheses: H-odd, the native floor, NumCert.

## E. Fibre structure, first pieces (FibreK)
`K_word`, `K_invoice`, `kappa_depends_only`: with the cost table P0 as an explicit parameter, the invoice F(t) depends only on the ends E, the timed helper step multiset S and the climb multiset Π = {e_h − b_h}. `tail_pairs` / `head_pairs` (proved directly), `tail_F_null`, `head_F_null`: with helpers ending as identity, σ fixing every non-helper, the V-enders being exactly the helpers ending at one chart d, G0 members born at 0, and hub-undoability in the shifted gauge (`Undo (V ∘ σ) G0`), the relabel preserves the (birth, end) multiset, hence Π and F(t). `c3_not_undoable`. NOT covered: relabel combined with an interior gauge, loops, mixed head+tail relabels, and any "native is optimal over its fibre" statement (open).
