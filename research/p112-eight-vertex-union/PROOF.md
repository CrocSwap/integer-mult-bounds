# An eight-vertex support-union exclusion

Let `K` have characteristic zero. On the pinned 112 live E8 root lines define
`C={c_h:c_h(t)=<r_t,h>, h in K^8}` and let `w(q)` be the binary rank of
the nine-bit labels on `supp(q)`. The labels are a leading one followed by
simple-root coordinates modulo two. Eight removed root lines form an
orthogonal frame. The executable uses doubled roots of squared norm eight.

**Theorem.** Eleven independent pieces spanning `T`, with
`dim(T intersect C)>=7`, cannot have weight bounds `4,5,5,6^8`.

This excludes one actual TWOSIDE `(7,4)` profile in DreamingOfClouds's PR3154.
It does not settle that cell, full C5 or the decoder floor.

## Geometry and field of definition

The retained PR3176 geometry, following PR3164/3175, checks these facts:

- The 120 projective roots are closed under 960 simple reflections; their
  integral simple coordinates have one sign.
- Six-node parabolics have at most 36 root lines. Seven-node faces have
  `42,28,22,14,16,23,37,63` lines. The 42-line face has a D7 normal; the
  63-line face has a root normal.
- Root directions have live support 53 or 56. The live Gram sum is `112 I`,
  so `h -> c_h` is injective over `K`.
- Three root lines in a plane form A2; in norm-two normalization the third
  has coefficient ratio `+1` or `-1`. All 7,140 root-line pairs are checked.
- Full-root binary four- and five-flats contain at most eight and twelve
  roots. Live six-flats contain at most twenty. Complete annihilator censuses
  check 3,309,747 flats in each of dimensions four and five, and 788,035 six-flats.
  Smaller supports extend to such flats.

We use the standard real Weyl-chamber theorem: a real direction moves to a
closed chamber, and its perpendicular roots are generated by vanishing simple
roots. This theorem is an explicit dependency, not proved by the replay.
It and the counts give minimum code support 53.

A codeword of support at most 72 has at least forty perpendicular rational
roots. Their span has rank seven. Rank at most six would, by taking a generic
real normal with exactly the same perpendicular roots, contradict the 36-line
bound. Rank at most seven follows from its nonzero normal. Its rational normal
space is one-dimensional, so even over `K` its direction is a scalar multiple
of a rational generator. Applying the chamber theorem to that generator leaves
only root and D7 directions.

The retained norm-four inventory consists of sums and differences of orthogonal
doubled roots. It has 1,080 projective lines, contains the D7 ray and is Weyl
closed. Its live support histogram is `70:128,73:896,76:56`. Thus the complete
support-72 alphabet has 120 root and 128 D7 lines. The latter have one entry
`+1` or `-1` in each coordinate pair. Even signed permutations preserving
the deleted frame move each to `a=(1,0,1,0,1,0,1,0)`. All 128 transformations
are checked on every root and frame line.

## Quotient reduction

Let `B` be the three small pieces and `S` their support union, with `|S|<=32`.
Put `D=T intersect C` and `lambda:T -> T/D`. The quotient dimension is at
most four. The pieces in `B` and any large piece have support union at most
`32+20=52<53`. Their quotient images are independent: a dependence gives a
nonzero codeword on fewer than 53 coordinates. Independence of the original
pieces makes such a combination nonzero.

The quotient dimension is therefore exactly four. Modulo the three small
images, rescale the eight large pieces `q_0,...,q_7` to have the same nonzero
image. Suitable combinations `s_i` of `B` give

```
c_0=0,    c_i=q_i-q_0-s_i in D, i=1,...,7.
```

The seven `c_i` are independent by their distinct coefficients on `q_1,...,q_7`.
Injectivity gives eight affinely independent vertices in `K^8`. Each edge is
supported on at most `32+20+20=72` lines. Outside `S`, the endpoint values
differ from vertex evaluations by a common shift at each coordinate.
For eight values every common shift leaves at least
`cost(t)=8-max_z #{i:vertex_i(t)=z}` nonzero values. Shifting a largest equality
class to zero attains this relaxed bound. The eight pieces have total support
at most 160. We prove that support outside `S` alone totals at least 162.

## Mixed edges

If an edge is non-root, translate and normalize it to endpoints `0,a` by a
frame-preserving symmetry and common scale. Every further vertex is `x=s*u`
with `a-x=t*v` for distinct allowed lines and nonzero coefficients. Solve
`a=s*u+t*v` on a nonzero rational two-by-two minor and check all coordinates.
The unique solution is rational even over `K`. This gives 58 additional
half-integral vertices. Join two when their difference is an allowed line.
The graph has 595 edges and 9,737 six-cliques. Their ranks after adjoining `a`
are `4:105,5:2520,6:5096,7:2016`.

For each rank-seven configuration, delete its 32 largest coordinate costs.
This relaxes every possible `S`. Remaining support sums have histogram
`162:784,163:112,170:784,175:336`, so the mixed branch needs at least 162.
A minimizing witness has spectrum `1:15,2:48,3:18,4:31`. It leaves 162 after
32 arbitrary cuts and 159 after 33. This is a witness in the arbitrary-cut
relaxation, not a realization of the small pieces.

A separate checker uses Gram intersections, a three-clique/three-clique join,
integer elimination and explicit equality counting. It reproduces the graph,
rank histogram and support histogram.

## The two root A7 branches

If every edge is a root direction, write independent vertices `b_i alpha_i`.
The root-plane fact forces every ratio `b_i/b_j` to be `+1` or `-1`. Orient
the roots to absorb signs. There is one common scale and norm-two roots with
pairwise inner product one. The rational plane argument extends over `K` by
an invertible rational minor. A common scalar preserves all equality classes.

Extend each ordered root-star prefix by every root with the required inner
products. Reflections perpendicular to the prefix fix it pointwise. Retaining
every orbit of that subgroup covers all stars, even if it is smaller than the
full stabilizer. Extension orbit sizes are `240;56;27;16;10;6;(2,1)`.
The two branches have full-root cost spectra `1:8,2:56,3:56` and
`0:1,2:84,4:35`. For the first, relax the frame and `S` to forty arbitrary
cuts. The lower bound is `8+56*2+16*3=168>160`. The same relaxation leaves
only 158 in the second branch, so the actual support ranks are needed.

## Frame orbits and weighted unions

Moving the star also moves the deleted frame. Complete increasing orthogonal
eight-clique enumeration gives 2,025 frames, visiting 27,565 states.
Reflections in the second star's 28 edge-root lines and its perpendicular
root line preserve costs. The replay checks that and partitions all frames
into five orbits of sizes `30,420,1260,105,210`.

Weyl moves preserve binary support ranks. An integral invertible Cartan action
on simple-root coordinates is invertible modulo two; adjoining the unchanged
leading-one coordinate gives an invertible nine-bit map. Signs disappear modulo
two. The replay verifies the label maps for every generator used.

Let `W(A)=sum_{t in A} cost(t)`. For each frame representative enlarge the
three actual supports to live intersections of binary flats of dimensions
four, five and five. Enlargement cannot decrease `W`. Complete enumeration gives:

| Frame orbit | Live total | Maximum four-flat weight | Maximum five-flat weight |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 30 | 280 | 28 | 40 |
| 420 | 284 | 32 | 48 |
| 1260 | 288 | 32 | 48 |
| 105 | 288 | 32 | 48 |
| 210 | 292 | 32 | 48 |

The first and last rows leave at least 172 and 164 by summing individual
maxima. The middle rows need the union rather than that sum.

In the 420-frame orbit, a union weight at least `284-160=124` requires
four-flat weight at least 28 and each five-flat at least 44, since individual
maxima sum to 128. The candidate counts are 101 and 150. All 1,143,825 triples
of one four-flat and an unordered pair of five-flats with repetition have
maximum union weight 104.

In the 1260- and 105-frame orbits, union weight at least 128 requires exact
individual maxima `32,48,48`. Candidate counts are `61,86` and `45,54`.
All 228,201 and 66,825 triples have maximum union weights 108 and 108.

This filtered search is complete for a hypothetical failure. It does not
claim its maxima optimize all unions. Since all costs are even, any discarded
triple has union weight at most 122,126,126. Combining discarded and enumerated
bounds gives global union caps 122,126,126, leaving at least 162 in every middle
row. `RESULT.json` retains flat histograms, candidate counts and maximizing
triples. A separate set-based calculation reproduces the filtered maxima
from the complete candidate inventories.

Thus both root branches, and the mixed branch, require at least 162 endpoint
incidences outside `S`. This contradicts allowance 160 and proves the theorem.

## Application and limits

The unchanged PR3154 `bk2b.py` prints all 24 original cell counts. A separate
transcription reproduces the 154 `(7,4)` survivors and includes this profile,
whose raw-excess deficit is two. The decoder reduction itself is inherited.

PR3176 needs twelve pieces and code intersection dimension eight. There is no
guaranteed extension of the present eleven pieces by another independent
weight-six piece. PR3164/3169/3173/3175's explicit profiles do not directly
cover this one by deletion and componentwise weight comparison. These are
applicability checks, not exclusion of every consequence of their arguments.

The final overlap refresh found Nick Petalas's concurrent claim at
[comment19000159](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/discussions/3156#discussioncomment-19000159),
posted after the initial snapshot. Its proposed root branch relaxes the rank-four
support to eight arbitrary coordinates. The present argument retains all three
binary support ranks and supplies a separate finite union certificate. Parallel
profile work is credited without asserting exclusive priority or validating its
unpublished proof.

Source heads, hashes and attribution are in `SOURCES.json`. Evidence is
author-checked. Separate algorithms and internal read-only review are not
independent human or proof-kernel review. Other profiles, full C5,
`C(P112)>=64`, physical suppliers and a better kappa remain open.
