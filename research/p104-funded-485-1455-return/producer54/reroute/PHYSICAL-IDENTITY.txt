Why the identity lifts through every tensor chart

Let C_f be the exact invertible physical chart map for frame f, including
its absolute Gaussian Clifford phase. A field currently in frame f is
written physically as C_f v, where v is its native row value. A MOVE a->b
must realise the exact map C_b C_a^{-1}. It therefore leaves v unchanged.
For coframed operands, ADD with a central rational coefficient c obeys

  C_f v + c C_f w = C_f(v+c w).

This equality is exact on arbitrary vectors, not just on basis labels or
Pauli conjugacy classes. Tensoring with any untouched tensor factors
preserves it. The complete physical operator is obtained by conjugating
the native row operator by the diagonal input and output chart maps.
Because the reroute preserves the complete native row operator and all
input/output charts, its complete physical operator equals the source's.

All chart composition identities telescope exactly, for example

  (C_904 C_724^{-1})(C_724 C_721^{-1}) = C_904 C_721^{-1}.

An unrecorded phase factor would invalidate this inference when a stream
is subsequently added to another stream. Therefore projective tableau
agreement alone is insufficient. The canonical chart maps and their
lowered recipes must agree in absolute phase, or equivalently on complete
Gaussian matrix columns. The new or changed paid routes are
3->724 of rank 6, 721->724 of rank 2, and 724->904 of rank 1. The separate finite
