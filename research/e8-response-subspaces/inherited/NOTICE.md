# Provenance and credits

This package retains upstream code, data, licenses and notices. The new contribution is the target-prefix recovery construction and explicit dirty preconditioning for heterogeneous helper endpoints, together with the changed composition and checks.

Direct sources in CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds:

- PR376: exact helper reuse and its reconstruction selections.
- PR377: compressed nine-stage complex E8 supplier, bank padding, Clifford/cover framework.
- PR378: the far-retimed complex word on which the new prefix construction operates.
- PR381: nine-stage unsplit bit assembly, inherited exact moment and outer inequality machinery.

Essential ancestry and directly used ideas include PR352's E8 local identity and original sources, PR363 and PR373's stage constructions, PR370's bit E8 supplier, PR375's unsplit degree-two halving, PR315's compiler/dirty response machinery, PR266's exact checkers, and PR234's geometry machinery. Their bundled authorship and licenses remain authoritative. PR365's coupled complex moves are part of the retiming lineage. PR371's rounding correction and PR379's signed-compensation discussion inform the explicit conditional scope.

PR382 is the frozen stronger benchmark that invalidated our initial composition-only novelty claim; PR383 is the subsequent low-read composition reviewed in the same sweep. Neither is presented as our new mechanism. See research-notes/PR-SWEEP.md for every new PR surveyed in this round.

All PR numbers refer to https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/<number>. No publication, push or external message is performed by the supplied scripts.
