# Dense-pairing floors for overcomplete root-code decoders (E6 36, D5 20, every m) and for every rank-T realisation (E8 72, E7 49, D6 30, D4 12, P104 56), with a correction to Conjecture ID (a) (no κ claim)

> **Scope, stated first.** This package is about the **algebraic cost** `Σ_j rank_F2 B[R_j, A_j]` of exact decoders of root units (and punctured root units) in the model of #446 / #595 / #1590 / #2111 / #2125 / #2228. It proves lower bounds **in stated classes** and one **correction** to a conjecture of ours. It contains **no κ claim, no window closed, no new supplier, no copy / hosting / restoration cost**, and it says nothing about decoders whose factors leave the column space of the realisation, about realisations of rank above T, or (except where a SAT statement says so) about pairings B other than the dense one. Every statement carries one of the labels below; every scope is spelled out in §2.
>
> **Labels.** **PROVEN** (full proof in this README); **CHECKED** (recomputed by `verify.sh`, exact integer / `Fraction` / bit arithmetic, or SAT with a DRAT proof that `drat-trim` verifies); **RECORDED** (a number of another PR or of the author's working notes, or a solver verdict whose proof is not shipped, not re-run here; for SAT items, "RECORDED: DRAT-verified at generation" means `verify.sh` regenerates the CNFs and checks their sha256 hashes and the recorded verdicts, while the DRAT proofs themselves are regenerable with `sat/run.py solve` but are not shipped); **OPEN**.
>
> **Correction (read this first).** Conjecture ID of #2228 §6, branch (a), says that for an *odd geometry* (an integral normalisation with unit diagonal exists) the optimal cost is `v`. **That branch is false when the number of terms is unbounded**: the 120-line unit "all lines pairwise orthogonal" with `M = I` has an exact decoder of cost **72 < 120** (§3, **PROVEN + CHECKED**). The values of Conjecture ID for the nine even root units are untouched, and #2228's Theorem 1 and Corollary 2.2 are not contradicted (their hypothesis is that all lines survive); but "odd geometry ⇒ cost* = v" and the framing of "geometry" as an invariant of the cost are wrong in the any-number-of-terms model. §3 gives the repaired statement `cost*(U) = min over admissible B of c(B)`.

## 1. Setting and notation

* A **unit** `U` is a list of `v` pairwise non-proportional vectors `r_t ∈ Q^n` ("lines"); `t ⊥ s` means `r_t · r_s = 0`. `V := span{r_t}`, `T := dim V`. All units here are root systems or punctures of E8: `D4, D5, D6, E6, E7, E8` and `P112 = E8 minus an 8-frame`, `P104 = E8 minus two 8-frames`, `E7-frame = E7 minus a 7-frame`; `lib/roots.py` builds them (E8 in doubled integer coordinates, E7 / E6 as orthogonal complements of one resp. two roots, D_n from `±e_i ± e_j`).
* A **realisation** of `U` over a field `K ⊇ Q` is a matrix `M ∈ K^{v×v}` with `M_tt ≠ 0` and `M_ts = 0` whenever `t ⊥ s` (other entries arbitrary, possibly 0). A **decoder** is `M = Σ_{j=1..m} p_j q_jᵀ` (`p_j, q_j ∈ K^v`) together with an **admissible pairing** `B ∈ F2^{v×v}`: `B_tt = 1`, and `B_ts = 1` (`t ≠ s`) only if `t ⊥ s`. Then `B̃ ∘ M = diag(M_tt)` holds automatically. `R_j = supp p_j`, `A_j = supp q_j`, and
  **cost = Σ_j rank_{F2} B[R_j, A_j]**.
  The factors are *unrestricted* unless a theorem says otherwise. Terms with `p_j = 0` or `q_j = 0` contribute nothing and are dropped.
* The **dense pairing** is `B_d := J + Ā`, i.e. `B_ts = 1` iff `t = s` or `t ⊥ s`; it depends on the orthogonality pattern only. `h := rank_F2 B_d` (D4 3, D5 5, D6 5, E6 7, E7 7, E8 9, P104 9, P112 9, E7-frame 7). It is symmetric.
* **Label classes.** Let `C := col(B_d) ⊆ F2^v` (dimension `h`). A **class** is the support of a nonzero vector `w ∈ C` (`2^h − 1` of them, counted by vector). For `R ⊆ [v]` put `Def(R) := {w ∈ C : supp w ∩ R = ∅}` (a subspace of `C`) and `δ(R) := dim Def(R)`. In the notation of #2228, the classes are the sets `O_z = {t : Ω(u_t, z) = 1}`.
* **Root-code decoders** (the class of #2228 Theorem 4). For a unit with line matrix `R ∈ Q^{v×n}`: `p_j = D₁ R x_j`, `q_j = D₂ R y_j` with `x_j, y_j ∈ V_K` and `D₁, D₂` invertible diagonal. Then `R(x) := supp(D₁Rx) = {t : r_t · x ≠ 0}`.
* **Frames.** A **T-frame** is a set of `T` pairwise orthogonal lines; `α_⊥` is the size of a largest orthogonal set. If `U` has a `T`-frame `F`, every realisation `M` has `M[F,F]` diagonal and invertible, so **every realisation has rank ≥ α_⊥** (PROVEN, one line).

## 3. A correction to Conjecture ID (a), and the shape of cost*

Free terms are the point (the "free rectangle" of #1331 / #1375). A term whose block `B[R_j, A_j]` is zero costs nothing, and a single-entry term `c e_t e_sᵀ` is free whenever `B_ts = 0`.

**Proposition 3.1 (monotonicity) — PROVEN.** If `orth(U') ⊆ orth(U)` on the same `v` lines (patterns of required zeros, possibly directed) then `cost*(U) ≤ cost*(U')`.
*Proof.* Take an optimal decoder `(M', P', Q', B')` of `U'`. Its `B'` is admissible for `U` (its off-diagonal ones lie in `orth(U') ⊆ orth(U)`). For every `(t,s) ∈ orth(U) ∖ orth(U')` add the term `−M'_ts e_t e_sᵀ`; its block is `B'_ts = 0`, so it is free. The new `M` vanishes on `orth(U)`, its diagonal is unchanged. ∎

**Proposition 3.2 (reformulation) — PROVEN.** `cost*(U) = min over B admissible for U of c(B)`, where `c(B)` is the optimal cost, with the fixed pairing `B`, of decoders whose realisation `M` has `M_tt ≠ 0` and `M_ts = 0` exactly where required by the pattern `S(B) := {(t,s) : t ≠ s, B_ts = 1}` (a directed pattern if `B` is not symmetric; nothing else is asked of `M`).
*Proof.* "≤": Proposition 3.1 with `U'` the `B`-unit. "≥": take a decoder of `U` with pairing `B`. Every line has `M_tt ≠ 0`, so some term covers `(t,t)`, and its block contains `B_tt = 1`; hence free terms never touch a diagonal entry. The paid terms (nonzero block) sum to a matrix `N` with the same diagonal as `M`, and `N_ts = 0` wherever `B_ts = 1`, `t ≠ s` (there every free term contributes 0 and `B̃ ∘ M = Δ`). So the paid part is a decoder of the `B`-unit of the same cost. ∎

**Proposition 3.3 (the 120-line frame unit costs at most 72) — PROVEN + CHECKED (`c2`).** Let `F_120` be the unit of 120 lines, all pairs orthogonal; it admits every `B` with unit diagonal, and `M = I` (unit diagonal, "odd geometry") is a realisation. Take the E8 lines `r_t` (norm 2), `B = J + Ā_{E8}` (dense pairing of E8), and the terms
 * `j = 1..8`: `p_j(t) = r_t[j]/2`, `q_j(s) = r_s[j]` (coordinate `j` of the E8 lines in the standard coordinates), whose sum is `½ G` (`G` the E8 Gram matrix);
 * one **free term** `p = −(G_ts/2) e_t`, `q = e_s` for each of the 6,720 ordered non-orthogonal E8 pairs `(t,s)`.
Then `Σ p q^T = ½G − ½(G − 2I) = I` exactly (`G_tt = 2`, the free terms cancel the off-diagonal part), `B̃ ∘ I = I`, the 8 coordinate terms have `F2`-rank 9 each (the 78 lines with a nonzero coordinate `j`; computed), and every free term has block `B_ts = 0` (the pair is non-orthogonal in E8), i.e. rank 0. Total cost **72 < 120 = v**. `c2` recomputes the sum `M = I` over 6,728 exact `Fraction` terms and the 8 + 6,720 ranks. ∎

*Consequences, stated plainly.* (i) Conjecture ID (a) ("odd geometry ⇒ cost* = v") fails for `F_120`; the "odd geometry" property (an integral normalisation with unit diagonal) is not an invariant of cost* in the model where the number of terms is unbounded. F_120 (and F_104) are degenerate (`T = v`); no counterexample to (a) with `T < v` is known, and the only non-degenerate odd-geometry row of #2228 (Eisenstein, 40) still satisfies (a) by #2017. (ii) The correct way to read the frame unit is Proposition 3.2: the unit allows every `B`, and `c(B_dense(E8)) ≤ 72`. In this reading the search for good units is a search for **admissible pairings `B` with small `c(B)/v`**. (iii) #2228's Theorem 1 is *not* contradicted: it bounds the cost below by the number of lines whose leading 2-adic terms survive, and #2228 Corollary 2.2 derives "cost ≥ v" only for decoders in which all lines survive for some prime and shift; the decoder above has cost 72 < 120, so by Theorem 1 not all lines survive in it. (We have not recomputed the count of surviving lines for this decoder.) (iv) The theorems of §§5–7 are *not* affected: they assume `col P ⊆ col M`, `col Q ⊆ row M`, and a single-entry term `e_t e_sᵀ` lies outside `col M` as soon as `rank M < v`. (v) Scope: the decoder needs more than `T` terms; with the number of terms capped at the rank of the realisation, free terms are not available. The same move gives `cost*(F_104) ≤ 56` from the known P104 decoder; we did not re-run that and do not claim it.

## 4. Lemma OC (overcomplete coupling)

*Relation to #1224.* For E8 with `R` the matrix of live roots, #1224 Theorem A contains the identity `A Bᵀ = 2I` for the root-code parts; Lemma OC below is the same identity written as a statement about the cross matrix `K = YᵀX`, which (i) is basis-free, (ii) holds for any unit and any field (it is used here for E6 and D5, where a root basis does not have a dual basis of roots), and (iii) gives the idempotent / rank-one-off-diagonal structure used in §7. It is elementary and we claim nothing deeper.

**Lemma OC — PROVEN.** Let `V = 𝕜^T` (`𝕜` a field of characteristic 0, or any field), `x_1..x_m ∈ V`, `y_1..y_m ∈ V*`, and `Σ_j x_j ⊗ y_j = id_V` (absorb coefficients into `y_j`). Put `K_ij := ⟨y_i, x_j⟩` (an `m × m` matrix). Then **`K` is idempotent of rank `T`**, so `Π := I_m − K` is idempotent of rank `m − T` and `trace Π = m − T`. Conversely, if `rank(x_j) = rank(y_j) = T` and `K² = K`, then `Σ_j x_j ⊗ y_j = id_V`.
*Proof.* With `X = [x_j]`, `Y = [y_j]` (`T × m`): `XYᵀ = I_T`, so `K = YᵀX` satisfies `K² = Yᵀ(XYᵀ)X = K`, and `rank K = rank X = T` (`Yᵀ` is injective since `XYᵀ = I`). Conversely `K² = K` reads `Yᵀ(XYᵀ − I)X = 0`; `Yᵀ` is injective (rank `T`) and `X` surjective (rank `T`). ∎
**Lean:** `lean/Overcomplete.lean` proves `oc_idempotent` (`(Yᵀ X)² = Yᵀ X`), `oc_trace` (`tr(Yᵀ X) = T`), `oc_complement_idempotent`, `oc_complement_trace` (`tr(1 − YᵀX) = m − T`) and `oc_square` (`m = T` ⇒ `Yᵀ X = 1`), with `#print axioms` showing only `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`, no `sorry`. The rank statements, the converse and the consequences below are written proofs (not formalised).

**Consequences — PROVEN.**
 (a) `m = T`: `Π = 0`, so `⟨y_i, x_j⟩ = δ_ij` (biorthogonality; this is also `oc_square`).
 (b) `m = T + 1`: `Π = u wᵀ` with `wᵀ u = 1` (rank-one idempotent), so `⟨y_i, x_j⟩ = −u_i w_j` for `i ≠ j`: **the cross matrix agrees off the diagonal with the rank-one matrix `−u wᵀ`**, and `⟨y_i, x_i⟩ = 1 − u_i w_i`.
 (c) A term with `⟨y_i, x_i⟩ = 0` has `Π_ii = 1`.
 (d) (**criterion for prescribed directions**) Let `m = T + 1 ≥ 3`, prescribed lines `x̂_j, ŷ_j` with `rank[x̂_j] = rank[ŷ_j] = T` and `Λ_ij := ⟨ŷ_i, x̂_j⟩ ≠ 0` for `i ≠ j`. There are scalars with `Σ_j (s_j x̂_j) ⊗ (t_j ŷ_j) = id` **iff** `Λ = D + a bᵀ` with `D` diagonal and invertible.
 *Proof of (d).* "⇒": by the Lemma, `K = diag(t) Λ diag(s) = I − u wᵀ` for some `u, w` (the scalars `s_j, t_i` multiply `x̂_j, ŷ_i`; and `Π := I − K` is idempotent of rank `m − T = 1`, hence `Π = u wᵀ`), so `Λ = diag(1/t) (I − u wᵀ) diag(1/s)` has the form `D + a bᵀ` with `D = diag(1/(t_i s_i))` invertible. "⇐": given `Λ = D + a bᵀ`, choose `s_i t_i = 1/D_ii`, `u_i = −t_i a_i`, `w_j = s_j b_j`. Then `K := diag(t) Λ diag(s) = I − u wᵀ`. Since `rank Λ ≤ T < m`, `det Λ = 0`, i.e. `det(I + D⁻¹ a bᵀ) = 0`, i.e. `1 + Σ a_i b_i / D_ii = 0`, i.e. `wᵀ u = −Σ s_i t_i a_i b_i = 1`. So `Π = uwᵀ` is idempotent, `K² = K`, and the converse of the Lemma gives `Σ = id`. ∎
 (e) A term-count consequence: at least `T` terms have `x_j ≠ 0 ≠ y_j`.

## 5. Theorem 4′ and the frame-intersection certificate (realisation-free floors)

Fix a unit with a `T`-frame and a realisation `M` of rank exactly `T` (so `T = α_⊥` is the minimum possible rank). Write `M = P₀ N Q₀ᵀ` with `P₀, Q₀ ∈ K^{v×T}` bases of `col M`, `col Mᵀ` and `N ∈ GL_T(K)`; `π(t)` (resp. `κ(s)`) is row `t` of `P₀` (row `s` of `Q₀`). Consider decoders with `p_j = P₀ x_j`, `q_j = Q₀ y_j` (`col P ⊆ col M`, `col Q ⊆ row M`); **every square decoder (`m = T`) is of this form** (`rank M = T ≤ rank P ≤ m`). Then `N = Σ_j x_j y_jᵀ` (as `P₀, Q₀` are injective), so at least `T` terms are nonzero.

**Lemma 5.1 (support-only bound) — PROVEN.** For any `R, A ⊆ [v]`: `rank_F2 B_d[R, A] ≥ h − δ(R) − δ(A)`, and `rank B_d[R,A]` depends only on the two subspaces `Def(R), Def(A) ⊆ C`.
*Proof.* Choose a rank factorisation `B_d = X Yᵀ` with `X, Y ∈ F2^{v×h}` of full column rank; `col X = col Y = C` (`B_d` is symmetric). `ker X_R = {z : X_R z = 0} ≅ {w ∈ C : w|_R = 0} = Def(R)` (via `w = Xz`), so `rank X_R = h − δ(R)`; likewise `rank Y_A = h − δ(A)`. Sylvester: `rank(X_R Y_Aᵀ) ≥ rank X_R + rank Y_A − h`. If `X_R` and `X_{R'}` have the same kernel they have the same row space `W`, and `X_R = S W` with `S` of full column rank, so `rank(X_R Y_Aᵀ) = rank(W Y_Aᵀ)`; the same on the other side. ∎

**Theorem 4′ — PROVEN.** In the setting above and with the dense pairing `B_d`,
  **cost ≥ Σ_{j : x_j,y_j ≠ 0} ( h − δ(R(x_j)) − δ(A(y_j)) ),** where `R(x) = {t : π(t)·x ≠ 0}`, `A(y) = {s : κ(s)·y ≠ 0}`.
Call a class `O` **π-deficient** if `π(O)` lies in a hyperplane of `K^T` (**κ-deficient** analogously). If `w ∈ Def(R(x))` then `supp w ∩ R(x) = ∅`, i.e. `π(supp w) ⊆ x^⊥`: the class `supp w` is π-deficient. **If no class is π-deficient or κ-deficient, every term costs ≥ h and cost ≥ T·h.**
*Proof.* Lemma 5.1 and the count of nonzero terms (§5, last sentence of the setting). ∎
This is #2228 Theorem 4 (which already allows `M = D₁ R N Rᵀ D₂` with arbitrary `N`, for factors in the scaled root code) extended to rank-`T` realisations whose column space is not a scaled root code (`col M ≠ D₁·RV`); in the Gram case `π = κ = ` coordinates of the line.

**Lemma 5.2 (frame-intersection certificate) — PROVEN.** Let `M` be a rank-`T` realisation and `O ⊆ [v]` with `π(O) ⊆ ker c` for a nonzero functional `c`. Then
 (a) `O` contains no `T`-frame;
 (b) for every `T`-frame `F`, `c ∈ span σ(F ∖ O)`, where `σ(s) := N κ(s)ᵀ`;
 (c) if a `T`-frame `E` contains the sets `F_1∖O, …, F_k∖O` of `T`-frames `F_i` whose intersection `∩_i (F_i ∖ O)` is empty, then `c = 0`, a contradiction.
Hence **a class that contains a `T`-frame, or satisfies (c), is not π-deficient in any rank-`T` realisation.** Replacing `M` by `Mᵀ` (a realisation of the same pattern) gives the same for κ.
*Proof.* For a `T`-frame `F`, `M[F,F] = P₀[F] N Q₀[F]ᵀ` is diagonal (all pairs orthogonal) with nonzero diagonal, hence invertible, so `{π(f)}_{f∈F}` is a basis of `K^T`, and `π(f)·σ(f') = 0` for `f ≠ f'`, `≠ 0` for `f = f'`: `{σ(f)}` is the dual basis up to scalars, and **σ(f) depends on the line `f` only, not on the frame**. (a): `F ⊆ O` would give `c ⟂ π(F)`, a basis. (b): expand `c = Σ_{f∈F} λ_f σ(f)`; then `π(f')·c = λ_{f'} π(f')·σ(f')`, which is 0 for `f' ∈ F ∩ O`. (c): by (b), `c ∈ ∩_i span σ(F_i∖O)`; all `F_i ∖ O ⊆ E` and `σ(E)` is a basis, so these spans are coordinate subspaces and their intersection is `span σ(∩_i F_i∖O) = 0`. ∎

## 6. The cap on what is proven: realisations of rank above T

If `rank M = ρ > T`, a `T`-frame no longer spans `K^ρ` and Lemma 5.2 fails. Theorem 4′ still gives `cost ≥ Σ_j (h − δ_j − δ'_j)` with at least `ρ` terms, but `δ` is no longer controlled by frames. This is **OPEN** (§8).

## 7. E6 and D5 for every number of terms (scaled-Gram realisations)

**The class.** `p_j = D₁ R x_j`, `q_j = D₂ R y_j`, `x_j, y_j ∈ V_K`, any `m`, dense `B`. Then `M = D₁ R N Rᵀ D₂` with `N = Σ x_j y_jᵀ ∈ V ⊗ V`.

**Lemma 7.1 — CHECKED (`c4.nspace`, `c1.d5nspace`).** For E6 and for D5 the space `𝒩 = {N ∈ V ⊗ V : r_tᵀ N r_s = 0 for all orthogonal t, s}` is **one-dimensional**, spanned by the metric (identity in orthonormal coordinates). (Exact rational rank of the constraint system on the `T²` unknowns; #2137 computes the same kernel for E6 in its coordinates.) Hence `N = c·Id_V`, `c ≠ 0` (the diagonal of `M` is nonzero), and after absorbing `c`: **`Σ_j x_j ⊗ y_j = id_V`**, so Lemma OC applies with `T = 6` (E6) resp. `5` (D5), and `m ≥ T`.

**Lemma 7.2 — CHECKED (`c4.dirs`, `c4.pairranks`, `c4.cross`, `c1.d5dirs`, `c1.d5ranks`).** Over `Q`, the classes of E6 (127) with `rank π(O) < 6` are exactly 27, each with a one-dimensional annihilator; the 27 annihilators are 27 distinct lines (the minuscule weights). Call a direction `x ∈ V_K` **deficient** if it lies on one of them; for a deficient `x`, `δ(R(x)) = 1`, for every other `x`, `δ(R(x)) = 0` (a class sits inside `x^⊥` iff `x` spans its annihilator, and the map class ↦ direction is injective). The same holds on the column side with the same 27 lines (`B_d` is symmetric). Dense-pairing ranks of terms (Lemma 5.1 and the table): both deficient: **5** (432 ordered pairs) or **6** (297 pairs); exactly one deficient: 6; none: 7. **All 27 × 27 cross products `⟨y, x⟩` of deficient directions are nonzero.** For D5 the deficient directions are the five axes `e_1..e_5`; both deficient: rank **4** if matched (`e_a, e_a`), **3** if `a ≠ b` (20 pairs); one deficient: 4; none: 5.

**Theorem 7.3 (E6 ≥ 36 for every `m`) — PROVEN from CHECKED data (Lemmas 7.1, 7.2 and `c4.transitive`, `c4.nodes`, `c4.noleaf`).** Every root-code decoder of E6 with dense `B` has cost `≥ 36`.
*Proof.* By Lemma 7.1 `Σ x_j y_jᵀ = id`. Suppose a decoder of cost `c₀` exists and take one of cost `≤ c₀` with the fewest terms. Two terms with proportional `x` and proportional `y` have the same supports and can be merged into one term of the same rank (or both vanish), contradicting minimality; so the ordered pairs of lines `([x_j],[y_j])` are pairwise distinct (and terms with a zero factor are absent). Every term costs `≥ 5` (Lemma 7.2).
 *`m ≥ 8`*: cost `≥ 40`.
 *`m = 6`* (square, `m = T`): by OC(a), `⟨y_i, x_j⟩ = δ_ij`. Let `k` (resp. `l`) be the number of deficient `x_j` (resp. `y_j`). If `y_j` is deficient, every `x_i`, `i ≠ j`, is non-deficient — otherwise `⟨y_j, x_i⟩ = 0` for two deficient directions, contradicting Lemma 7.2. Hence `l ≥ 1` forces `k ≤ 1`, and `k ≥ 2` forces `l = 0`. By Lemma 5.1 each term costs `≥ 7 − [x_j def] − [y_j def]`, so cost `≥ 42 − k − l`. If `k ≥ 2` then `l = 0`, cost `≥ 42 − 6 = 36`; symmetrically for `l ≥ 2`; if `k, l ≤ 1`, cost `≥ 40`.
 *`m = 7`*: cost `≤ 35` forces all seven terms to have rank 5 (`7 · 5 = 35`), i.e. seven distinct pairs from the 432 rank-5 pairs. By OC(b) the off-diagonal of `Λ_ij = ⟨y_i, x_j⟩` has rank one (rescaling rows and columns by the nonzero scalars `s_j, t_i` does not change this), and `rank[x_j] = rank[y_j] = 6`. W(E6) is transitive on the 432 rank-5 pairs (`c4.transitive`: the orbit of one pair under the 36 reflections has 432 elements), and it permutes the lines and so preserves everything, so one pair may be fixed. `c4` then enumerates **all** 7-sets containing that pair whose `Λ` has rank-one off-diagonal (exact integer cross-multiplication, no floating point): there are 27,250 triples, 206,275 / 898,460 / 2,502,409 / **5,504,660** sets of sizes 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 (`c4.nodes`; these numbers reproduce the node counts of an independent floating-point search of the working notes, which used `numpy`), and **none** of the 5,504,660 has both its row directions and its column directions spanning `V` — in fact none has `≥ 6` distinct row directions and `≥ 6` distinct column directions (`c4.noleaf`). So no 7-term decomposition exists.
 Since `m = 6, 7` and `m ≥ 8` exhaust the possibilities (`m ≥ 6` is needed for `Σ x_j y_jᵀ = id`), cost `≥ 36` in all cases. ∎
 *Remarks.* The value 36 is attained by the trivial decoder (`c5.E6`, `M = I`, a different realisation). (RECORDED, not re-run: the working notes' floating-point search found 5,208 exact 7-term decompositions with deficient `x_j, y_j`, the best of cost 40, consistent with the above; #2240 / #2247 report minimum 40 / 37 for their finite alphabets.)

**Theorem 7.4 (D5 ≥ 20 for every `m`) — PROVEN from CHECKED data (Lemmas 7.1, 7.2 for D5).** Every root-code decoder of D5 with dense `B` costs `≥ 20`.
*Proof.* Each term has rank `≥ 3`, with equality iff `x_j = e_a`, `y_j = e_b` with `a ≠ b` (both deficient, unmatched); all other terms have rank `≥ 4`. Let `n₃` be the number of rank-3 terms. Cost `≤ 19` needs `3 n₃ + 4(m − n₃) ≤ 19`, i.e. `n₃ ≥ 4m − 19`; with `n₃ ≤ m` this gives `m ≤ 6`, and `m ≥ 5` is forced. *`m = 5`* (square): `n₃ ≥ 1` is impossible, since a rank-3 term has `⟨y_i, x_i⟩ = ⟨e_b, e_a⟩ = 0`, contradicting `⟨y_i, x_i⟩ = 1`. *`m = 6`*: `n₃ ≥ 5`. By OC(b), `Π = u wᵀ`; a rank-3 term `i` has `⟨y_i,x_i⟩ = 0`, so `u_i w_i = 1` (OC(c)), and for two rank-3 terms `i ≠ j`, `⟨y_i, x_j⟩ = −u_i w_j ≠ 0`, i.e. `b_i = a_j`. Three rank-3 terms `i, j, k` then give `a_j = b_i = a_k` and `a_i = b_j = a_k`, so `a_i = b_i`, a contradiction (`c1.d5core` checks that no three pairs `(a_i, b_i)`, `a_i ≠ b_i`, satisfy `b_i = a_j` for all `i ≠ j`); so `n₃ ≤ 2 < 5`. ∎ The value 20 is attained by the trivial decoder (`c5.D5`). An independent counting proof for D5 is in the working notes (RECORDED).

### 9.2 SAT-2: B-independent shadows cannot certify E6 or E7 — E7 CHECKED (shipped DRAT), E6 RECORDED (DRAT-verified at generation), consequence PROVEN
**Shadow Lemma — PROVEN** (this is Lemma CR2 / `hadamard_diag_rank_sum` of #2162, `lean/DecoderDuality.lean`; rectangular Lean form in #2267; cited, not shipped). For any field `L ⊇ F2`, `x_j, y_j ∈ L^v` with `supp x_j ⊆ R_j`, `supp y_j ⊆ A_j`, and `N := Σ_j x_j y_jᵀ`: for every admissible `B`, **cost ≥ rank_L(B ∘ N)**. *Proof:* `B ∘ N = Σ_j D(x_j) B D(y_j)` and each summand factors through `B[R_j, A_j]`, so has rank `≤ rank_F2 B[R_j,A_j]` (rank is invariant under field extension); rank is subadditive. If moreover `N` vanishes on every orthogonal pair, `B ∘ N` is diagonal (a non-orthogonal pair has `B_ts = 0`), so **cost ≥ #{t : N_tt ≠ 0} for every B**.
A shadow `N` certifying all `v` lines with `m` terms is a matrix of rank `≤ m` over `L` with nonzero diagonal vanishing on orthogonal pairs. Over `L = F2` this is a matrix of rank `≤ m` with unit diagonal and zeros on orthogonal pairs: its minimum rank is `mr₂(Γ)`. **E6 RECORDED** (DRAT-verified at generation; the two ~90 MB proofs are not shipped, `verify.sh` checks the CNF hashes and verdicts) **and E7 CHECKED:** `gamma_E6_r6_c{0,1}` UNSAT (so `mr₂(Γ_E6) ≥ 7 > 6 = T`) and `gamma_E7_r7_c{0,1}` UNSAT (`mr₂(Γ_E7) ≥ 8 > 7 = T`); symmetry-breaking cliques of pairwise orthogonal lines (E6 has `α_⊥ = 4`). **PROVEN consequence:** for square decoders (`m = T`) no **F2-valued, B-independent** Shadow-Lemma certificate certifies all lines of E6 or of E7. Larger fields of characteristic 2 are not covered (OPEN, §8.5). The E-type floors for arbitrary `B`, if true, need an argument using that one `B` is shared.
