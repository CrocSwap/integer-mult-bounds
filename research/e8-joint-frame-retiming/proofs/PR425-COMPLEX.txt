Hamming packets with mutable source and target bases
Conditional finite extension of the complex E8 supplier

1. What is new

The old r783 word keeps source values fixed and does not read target registers.
Those are sufficient restrictions on a scalar word, not necessary conditions
for a common-frame linear program. The new word uses 680 helper registers:

  * source registers themselves carry one branch of a simultaneous signed
    packet computation;
  * retired packet registers retain a full source basis, from which the eight
    central coordinate sums are reconstructed;
  * 56 pairs of targets carry a first two-output butterfly, with its input
    basis explicitly compensated at frame zero and in the center decoder;
  * an exact inverse on all source/helper registers is executed at their
    common full frame after the last recursive climb.

No recursive work is deleted from the ledger. A helper is counted for its
entire lifetime, including unused final climbs. All 120 sources and targets
retain their original line/full and zero/hyperplane endpoint ranks.

The new local histogram is
  {1:2248, 2:896, 3:168, 4:336, 5:392, 8:24, 9:8}.
Its rank mass is 9*680 + 240*8 + 72 = 8112. There are 4072 local children.
There are more local children than in the old r783 word, but substantially
less helper rank mass; the complete moment, not a count proxy, decides gain.

2. Exact Hamming geometry and signed packet algebra

Use the pinned PR370 table of 120 E8 root lines in Construction-A coordinates:
eight coordinate lines 2e_i and 112 sign lines on the 14 weight-four Hamming
blocks. For integer representatives r_s, let G_st = r_s dot r_t / 2.
The matrix C_st = 1 + G_st mod 2 has binary rank nine. The construction gives
an explicit orthonormal realization p_s in F_2^9 with

  p_s dot p_t = C_st,  and  p_s dot p_s = 1.

Consequently the nonzero off-diagonal entries of G/2 occur only where a source
line lies in the target hyperplane. The rank-eight central factorization is

  (G/2)_ts = sum_i r_ti r_si / 4.

For a source Hamming block d, a coordinate pair {i,j} in d, and epsilon=+-1,
define the signed face packet

  P(d,ij,epsilon) = sum_{s in d, r_si*r_sj=epsilon} r_si x_s.

Each packet has four terms and its binary port span has rank three. Split
the eight sign lines of a block into its two parity tetrads. Each tetrad pair
occurs in two face packets with independent signed coefficient rows. The two
rows are formed simultaneously by two determinant-one shears, rather than
forming independent one-output sums. The packet factory uses its eight source
registers and sixteen additional helpers. Its twelve retired registers can
be oriented to retain a full rank-eight source basis. Exact elimination on
that stock produces four coordinate sums without allocating more helpers.

This comparison of an individual factory is not a claim that the whole old
supplier used 48 helpers per block. The relevant complete counts are 783 and
680, both charged with their full actual profiles.

For two target/source blocks whose intersection is {i,j}, the contribution
to target t is -r_ti P(d,ij,r_ti*r_tj)/2. The remaining within-block and
coordinate-line contributions are explicit four-output signed transforms.
Every one of the 14400 rational decoder entries is rechecked. Adding the
eight coordinate centers leaves precisely the identity matrix on sources.

The binary transvection x -> x+(x dot 43)*43 is orthogonal because 43 has
even weight. It maps the constructed port set exactly to the old fixed E8
port set. All frames and register indices are transported, so the old nine-
window data routes and their global phase correction apply to exactly the
same ordered ports, without introducing an unproved new covering geometry.

3. General scalar-interface lemma

Write z=(x,s) for the source and arbitrary dirty helper streams, and y for
the targets. Permit scalar additions and invertible dyadic scalings between
registers at the same current label. No target may feed a z register. The
raw A/center/B word therefore has block form

  z' = U z,
  y' = V y + J x + K s,

where U and V are invertible, and the retained-center operations are included
in J and K. The actual word has J=I after its adjusted center scatter. The
checks compute every coefficient over Q, rather than inferring it from a
desired sparsity pattern.

At frame zero, before entry adapters or recursive children, execute

  y <- V^{-1} y - V^{-1} K s.

Both y and s have label zero there. The code emits the literal inverse of
the 56 disjoint two-output target bases and a direct, charged dyadic old-
response matrix with 13216 additions. There is no uncharged oracle for K.
It is computed from the explicit scalar word and serialized. All coefficient
work is cleared immediately after its use.

After the last main gate, finish every z register's climb to the full frame,
then execute U^{-1} by reversing and inverting the non-target gates. These
gates are legal at one common full frame, including helper-to-source gates.
The result is exactly

  (x,s,y) -> (x,s,y+x).

The second orientation is the inverse transpose: negate and transpose every
addition and invert every scale in the SAME event order. Each retained
center is implemented by the transposed gather/child/scatter stream. The
result is (x,s,y) -> (x-y,s,y). Both orientations are replayed on all 920
independent live columns, including all 680 arbitrary helper columns.
Missing compensation, missing output preconditioning, missing cleanup and
the wrong inverse-transpose order are rejected.

Why the scalar argument lifts to exact complex labels: for each binary
subspace F use the exact canonical representative L(F). A climb from A to B
acts as L(B)L(A)^{-1}. If two registers share F, a scalar addition commutes
with their common L(F), as does a scalar scaling. Induction through the
actual stream therefore gives the block-diagonal conjugation of its scalar
matrix by the current labels. Center copies explicitly move from full to
zero, or zero to full in the other orientation. The preconditioner uses
only label-zero registers, and the z cleanup uses only full-label registers.
Thus the proof never moves a scalar operation across unequal labels or
identifies two frames merely because their ranks agree.

Every used canonical frame, nested transition, center direction and endpoint
adapter is independently lowered by the exact Gaussian Clifford compiler.
Equalities include global phase. The old restricted gx gate-shape checker
is not used to admit this new interface; the stronger explicit matrix and
common-frame checks replace its source-immutability/target-read restrictions.
This is a written finite algebraic extension, not a new Lean theorem.

4. Complete programs and work lifetimes

Each orientation emits 27537 events, including startup, entry/exit adapters,
all recursive climbs, eight sequential center children, scalar operations
and final cleanup. Temporary centers are initialized, used and erased one
at a time. No center or coefficient work stream survives a live child.

All added coefficients are dyadic. To apply a/2^j, compute |a| times the
source by binary Horner additions on a cleared private stream, halve j
times, accumulate with its sign, and clear the private stream. In-place
scales are signed powers of two. Every such primitive is charged. This
introduces no new denominator prime. Independent replay bounds the actual
cancellation-free prefix norms, including coefficient work; a 192-bit local
payload allowance is retained conservatively.

5. Nine-window composition and finite bill

Use the previously verified m=73 compressed nine-window geometry, four data
pairs, eight replicas, and the original signed bridges, idle children,
terminal exchange, terminal translations and full-cover phase correction.
The source scalar identity and its negative inverse-transpose are exactly
the two local interfaces consumed by that unchanged geometry.

There are 680 whole-block banks per stage, each containing eight rank-nine
helper blocks plus one paid rank-one padding child. The live width is

  W = 240*4*8 + 9*680 = 13800.

The conservative physical width is W+680+9+2=14491. The old-response matrix
uses no persistent tableau, but the old-size reserve is still charged.
The emitted owner table has 48960 distinct stage/replica/helper assignments.
The new global profile is

  {1:167976, 2:64512, 3:12096, 4:24192, 5:28224,
   8:7488, 9:576, 16:3840, 32:3840, 48:3840}.

Its rank deficit is 73*13800 - sum(r*H_r) = 2496. The maximum child is 48,
and 73^2 > 2*48^2 supplies the same charged two-level halving argument.

The finite bill is recomputed from the new scalar programs, bank owners,
work stock and adapter counts. It is not asserted unchanged. The local
primitive bound fits 2^48; the tape router fits 2^30000; the literal envelope
fits 2^100000; the precision envelope fits 2^210000. The physical row
coefficient is 15445, below the explicitly inherited external allowance
20161. Full-cover corrections apply to all 13800 live families.

6. Conditional numerical conclusion and retained assumptions

Two independent rational moment engines, with the inherited 10^-16 bad-
branch envelope, accept

  b = 1049104978040580 / 10^18 = 0.001049104978040580

and reject the next 10^-18 grid point. With the unchanged bit parameter
c=0.001057833103534317, three bootstrap steps and all 47 strict outer
conditions give the prospective finite-assembly value

  kappa = 1048005508028206 / 10^18
        = 0.001048005508028206, approximately 2^-9.898137985359.

The finite statement is conditional on the same all-size weighted compiler,
common fine grid, restored-row/base allowance, tape/prime/precision and
analytic transfer interfaces as the retained nine-stage result. The new
scalar-interface lemma above and its serialized program are additional
reviewable material, not an appeal to gx accepting a program outside its
grammar. No unconditional integer-multiplication theorem is claimed.
The result is approximately +4.97% over the locally verified retimed bound,
and remains well short of 2^-9. A fresh extracted-package replay is required
by this research workflow before publication or final promotion.
