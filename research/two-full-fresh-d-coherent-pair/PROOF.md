## Exact paired defect

Keep the original two-full controller and independent fresh axes. Since D_1,D_2,Q commute and R is scalar on its two main slots,

    Sε D_2 R U(Q) D_1 R L(-Q^-1) B Sε^-1
      = D_2 D_1 [[t(t-1)+tQ, -t(t-1)/ε],
                 [ε(1-Q^-1), Q^-1]],

where B=[[1,-1],[1,0]] and Sε=diag(1,ε). Direct multiplication proves the identity; the upper-right defect is independent of Q. For initial main input (0,Y), its additional X output is -t(t-1)D_2D_1Y/ε. Since the ideal D factors are unitary, this component has norm t|t-1| ||Y||/ε. This holds with arbitrary helper inputs; setting them to zero gives a unit-input witness without deleting their endpoints.

This tests the source binding, while the operator formula above retains arbitrary Q. ## Scope and next obligations

The actual-inverse recipe remains an inverse of this entire deformed word. It does not turn the inverse into a new fresh-D call with the required diagonal endpoints. The countermodel refutes sufficiency of additive potentials + paid holonomy + unchanged warps + same-realization inverses for the retained caller. It does not prove that a corrected paired caller, stronger coherent family, calibrated boundary, or a different controller is impossible.

An implementation could attempt to enforce λ_9=λ_1λ_8 (and every helper's required endpoint), or explicitly compensate the endpoint defects. Such compensation must retain all 632 endpoints and charge its scalars, exact inverses, precision, storage, routing and recursive work. This package supplies neither that construction nor a new precision lower bound or κ. In particular, the abstract paid profile is not a full invoice for a new numerical supplier.
