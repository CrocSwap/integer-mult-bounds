## Why the lower bound is restricted

Write `A_i=q^-1 Kp_i^-1`. Ordinary left multiplication gives classes `G A_i`;
these sets are right cosets in conventional group terminology. The physical
alias is explicitly `A_i=L_i A_rep`, so the side of multiplication is fixed.
For each class take the rational rank of its scalar M rows. Their sum C is 112
for this retained source.

Over `R=Q(i)[G]`, each paid output adds at most one history-module generator.
The all-width tensor-family argument distinguishes formal group elements,
including per-column phase, from exterior scalars. After the common right Km
normalization, disjoint source classes require C free copies of R beyond the
ordinary initial span. Finite dimension yields `m>=C`. This accommodates finite
feedback, overwrite/CLEAR and zero divisors; it does not assume a division ring
or cancel an ordinary nonunit.

The conclusion is a count of total calls at the same capacity. Allowing higher-
rank Gaussian calls does not turn it into a weighted recursive invoice. Width-
one identities, projective phases, coefficients that depend on width, and
arbitrary subsequences of widths do not meet the proof's hypotheses. Nor does
the proof price a caller that begins with already paid nonordinary history.
