Let H be the subgroup of GL(V,K) generated by G and paid operators/inverses.
The formal group algebra K[H] distinguishes group elements from exterior
coefficients. In particular `[iI]` evaluates as `i^f I`, whereas `i[I]`
evaluates as `i I`.

For finitely many distinct nonzero matrices h_1,...,h_t, choose a K-linear
functional ell with pairwise distinct nonzero values lambda_j=ell(h_j). Such a

faithful on the formal group algebra.

The same argument works on **one consecutive tail** f>=F: use F,...,F+t-1;
the determinant is a nonzero Vandermonde determinant multiplied by

permission for a width-dependent program or an arbitrary subsequence. For
