# Find rank-compatible BIT grade exchanges and prove their address-mixing limits

Same paid count and equal grade sum, old grades 1–6, new grades 1–8, and two through four fields. Full target-trial price applies only to the neutral abstract difference model with unchanged other charges.

Exactly three triples and thirty-two quadruples pass abstract all-width rank and signed price. An explicit forward/adjoint construction passes for the strongest triple and the strongest parity-surviving grade-seven quadruple. The unordered-axis-pair rank excludes all 35 under coordinate-permutation mixing. Determinant sign excludes two triples and 16 quadruples even with finite rational-linear mixing. Two actual paid grade-two and grade-three native maps have product traces 10 then 8, forcing a negative cycle count and excluding simultaneous coordinate permutations.

The source archive contains the exact pins, proof, licenses, commands and control outcomes.
