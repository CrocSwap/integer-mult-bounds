# Seven replicas with whole-residual complex hosting

The numerical screen improves the complex supplier of PR454 by removing an
integer packing remainder. The local complex word, all 120 ports, eleven-stage
recipe in dimension89, local Clifford operators and data/terminal phases are
unchanged. PR454's bit supplier is unchanged. This construction never divides a
helper residual across physical families.

## What was rejected

PR451's exact trace obstruction was replayed independently from its pinned source.
A product of two coordinate factors on one family is not the direct sum of those
factors on two families. Their traces differ. PR453 and the prior pooled56 screen
used that disputed PR448 rule; their scores are not established bounds. The new
construction retains the PR442 whole-residual interface and must not import the
rejected cross-family derivation as a lemma.

## Whole-family reassignment

For a fixed cover matrix, let the seven replicas be indexed by t. Each physical
data family is identified by (t, pair, side, port). All replicas use the same
stage schedule; each complete stage sweep on all replicas finishes before the
next schedule event. An idle child has the same untouched window and the same
pending rank in every replica.

A helper at (stage, invocation replica, helper) is assigned a single interval of
nine orthogonal pending axes of one family (owner replica, pair, side, port).
The owner pair must differ from the active pair and the stage must belong to the
owner child's untouched window. The assignment contains no two-piece record.
The native rank9 residual is routed onto that interval by a coordinate basis
permutation followed by the owner's pending-axis basis. The replica index selects
a whole family; it does not turn a product into a direct sum.

Under the inherited PR442 complete-invocation correlated dirty-helper contract,
the hosted helper contributes exactly the rank9 factor on that family, including
the complex phase, independently of its contents. Replica independence is not
used. The intervening recipe does not touch this owner pair on any replica, so
that whole factor can be moved to its idle child. On the same family, factors on
disjoint orthogonal pending axes commute. Multiplying all hosted factors and the
remainder gives that family's original child, with its original rank-zero phase
correction retained. This equality is applied separately to every owner family.
The resulting data/bridge/terminal operator is therefore the parent's under the
same complete-call contract, now allowing a whole owner in another replica.

This is an explicit extension of the permitted owner index. The finite auditor
checks its hypotheses; it does not prove the all-size complete-call contract.
A future objection to correlated whole-residual hosting would affect this
construction as it affects PR442/454.

## Finite checks and invoice

The independent auditor checks all 43,120 whole helpers, unique occurrences,
coordinate disjointness, window chronology, physical owner identities (including
owner replica), all idle remainders, both orientations, terminal translations and
cover correction. The local programs remain byte-identical to the parent. It
recomputes the full 1,913,989-event physical instruction digest, 8,520 exact phase
equalities, 1,320 orthogonal routes and all 24 omitted-bridge controls. Nineteen
inherited mutation controls include an inconsistent owner-replica mutation.

There are 8,400 live and 8,978 physical families, deficit2,856. Each residual is
rank9, the largest remainder is64, and 2*64^3 < 89^3 gives three-level halving.
The finite invoice must be recomputed using seven replicas and an additional
whole-family selector factor for every helper. The per-vertex instruction count
sums every invocation and selector; it does not reuse a maximum over independent
replicas. The complete orthogonal-cover stock and row bound must be checked with
the actual physical count. Publication must distinguish component reuse from a
fresh full supplier-chain replay and retain the external shell-path adapter if
it was used. No unconditional theorem or Lean proof is claimed.
