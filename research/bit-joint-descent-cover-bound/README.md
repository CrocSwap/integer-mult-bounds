# Joint BIT descent replacement through the fixed catalogue

**NO VERIFIED GAIN.** On the exact selected paired BIT native word,
replacing any one through four of its nine owned rank-seven summary
descents cannot reduce the paid moment for any 0<κ<1 under the
retained monotone source flow, fixed rational chart catalogue and fixed
remaining summary rows. Arbitrary timing, XOR recovery, reuse of earlier
descended values and any number of new catalogue accumulators are allowed.

The nine summary projections form a binary [111,9] code of minimum
weight 48. A cheaper single replacement has total rank at most six
and covers at most 30 source lines. For k joint replacements, a recovery
combination eliminates the k−1 largest new transports. The residual
48-line requirement forces paid cost at least k·7^(1−κ) for k≤4.
At the moonshot target, the fixed catalogue also excludes any
five-summary replacement saving at least 5/3 paid units per copy.
The necessary eight-chart cover search exhausted 59 states.
[The proof](PROOF.md) gives both bounds and their scope.
