Explicit GL-cover transfer and its retained all-size contract
Prepared by OpenAI Codex from the pinned PR473 construction and PR465 proof.

The PR473 bit emitter declares an orthogonal cover. PR465's polynomial
minor argument counts the general linear group, so it is not an estimate
on that orthogonal cover. This package makes a declared construction
change: for a fixed odd prime q>2^128, use G_w=GL_78(Z/q^w Z), for every
atom width w>=1. The complex supplier keeps its original orthogonal
cover. wrapper/gl_cover.py first preserves the fresh original bit
template, then changes only its cover declaration and variant metadata.
The independent invoice compares the decoded source template with the
frozen source hash and verifies that those are the only template changes.
All scalar words, actual owner matrices, frame ranks, paid profiles,
instruction streams, and physical families are preserved.

Why the finite identities survive
--------------------------------
For a split idempotent P, the doubled interchange D_P is an involution.
The nested commuting-projector identities are polynomial identities;
they do not require the conjugated projector to be symmetric. For
G=g tensor I_f, Q_g=diag(G,C_n G C_n) commutes with the full interchange
F and conjugates D_P to D_(G P G^-1). Thus conjugating each complete
local word by Q_g has the same complete physical endpoint for every
g in G_w. All selected fixed route matrices are orthogonal, hence
invertible and elements of GL. The whole-owner argument in
WHOLE-OWNER-BRIDGE.txt uses group cancellation and right multiplication,
so it also applies to this cover. It never splits one helper across
different physical families.

The fresh bit audit checks all 963 fixed frame Gram determinants and
finds only the prime factor 2. Their inverses give dyadic projectors;
the checked eleven-stage geometry and owner routes are also dyadic.
Consequently every fixed denominator is invertible for the chosen q.
Reduction preserves idempotence and its trace r. Since 0<=r<=78<q,
the rank over F_q is exactly r; idempotents have only eigenvalues 0,1.
An idempotent over the local ring has free image and kernel, so it
remains split over Z/q^w Z. No numerical primality oracle or unverified
chosen prime is used: the statement permits any fixed prime q>2^128.

Generic classes and the exact bad-class charge
---------------------------------------------
For each rank-r projector P and each 1<=k<=r, clear det(g)^k from the
leading k-minor of g P g^-1. Each entry's numerator has degree m, so
the cleared minor has degree at most m*k, where m=78. Similarity to
diag(I_r,0) supplies a nonzero witness over F_q. The sum of these
degrees is at most m*r*(r+1)/2 <= m^3. The polynomial zero estimate
therefore bounds the bad fraction among all matrices by m^3/q.

For q>=3, |GL_m(F_q)|/q^(m^2)=product_(i=1)^m(1-q^-i)>1/2:
the product is at least 1-sum_(i=1)^m q^-i > 1-1/(q-1).
Conditioning on GL costs a factor less than two. Each individual edge
thus has bad fraction at most 2*m^3/q < 2*m^3/2^128. The moment sums
average each edge separately; they need no union bound over edge types.
Every class lift has the same q^(m^2*(w-1)) multiplicity. The complete
fallback is added without subtracting the displaced good-class cost.

The pinned PR465 local-ring fallback performs unit-pivot elimination
on the full invertible N-by-N residual, N=2*m=156. Charging every
addition as upper gives 6*N*(N-1) children, row swaps give 3*N, and
paired diagonal scalings give 6*(N-1). The exact conservative count is
146478. Each child has at most one coarse field's width, hence ratio
at most 1/m. Nonunits are not inverted or silently treated as zero.
The proof, including the reversed calls and diagonal weights, is in
invoice/pr465/three-stage-cover-bit.tex. Its numerical three-stage
example is historical; only its stated general GL compiler/fallback
contract is used by the present eleven-stage construction.

Rows, routing, and the symbolic constant B_GL
--------------------------------------------
The GL cover cannot receive the orthogonal group's row discount.
Its low group has cardinality less than q^(m^2), and its high matrix
chart contributes m^2*(w-1) digits. Because 9609<q, physical families
cost at most one additional exponent. At depth three the complete
bit row coefficient is
  3*(78^2+1)+9909+252 = 28416.
invoice/gl_rows.py explicitly rejects the old cap 20161. The outer
assembly pays max(28416,20161)=28416 and verifies the resulting strict
row margin. The historical pinned bit/code/row_bound.py is retained
as source provenance and is not called by this package's assembly.

Fix q before the asymptotic width grows. Write g=g0+q*G1 with g0 in
GL_m(F_q) and m^2 high-digit coordinates. Right multiplication by a
fixed dyadic sigma permutes the finite low-type set and acts affinely
on G1, with carry determined by g0. The retained ordered-affine and
weighted GL-routing contract supplies a uniform-in-w implementation
for these finitely many fixed routes, batching all high matrices of
one low type. Their high cardinality cancels in the normalized moment
but remains charged in row stock. This is an inherited all-size
compiler statement, not a consequence of the finite regression toys.

The finite normalized local invoice rounds its two-level coefficient
to 2432 bits. That number is not asserted to bound every q-dependent
low-type dispatch or GL routing constant. Let B_GL be the arbitrary
finite nonnegative integer bit allowance supplied by the inherited
fixed-q routing contract. The actual six bootstrap cutoffs use, for
each positive gap Delta,
  max(ceil(36/Delta^2), ceil(2*(4+2432+B_GL)/Delta)).
The assembly records each affine offset and slope exactly; the
independent invoice checks their identities for every finite B_GL.
The field normalized_local_cutoff_log2 is only the normalized B_GL=0
component. No numeric cap for B_GL is claimed. Increasing this finite
constant changes the finite cutoffs, not the six exponents or kappa.
The outer analytic reduction, weighted leaf supplier, and uniform
compiler assumptions remain inherited. This package supplies the
new explicit finite construction and conditional sharp assembly;
it does not claim a new unconditional all-size theorem.
