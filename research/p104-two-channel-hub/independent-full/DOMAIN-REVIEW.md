# Independent physical-domain coverage

Prepared for @CubeJerry with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. The lead
derived the event-grid sweep separately from the original lifetime/critical-cut
enumerator. The original hub worker adversarially reviewed its argument and
mutation controls. This is team review, not human or formal-kernel acceptance.

`verify.py` first runs the full independent contact/rank verifier. It then
passes the **freshly reconstructed** nonzero prefix contact times directly to
`physical_domain.py`. A free-form or stale contact map is not an input to the
registered coverage check. The primary word and cut receipt remain byte-pinned.

## Coverage argument

The sweep maintains actual helper charts directly through the scalar program,
including owned initialization, clearing and physical MOVE endpoints. It does
not import the primary trace or use its helper lifetime intervals. It inspects
every event boundary and, for each MOVE, grants its moving helper simultaneous
availability at every stored strict intermediate frame. This is a relaxation:
the helper can choose one such frame during a real move, while the test grants
all choices. Every resident pivot set in the certificate is matched exactly.

For each additional eligible group/frame/grid-cut, the preceding certified
cut has a superset of its pivots and there is no new nonzero collection contact
between the two times. Thus the earlier collection space is unchanged and the
later delivery space can only shrink. Its pairing rank cannot exceed that of
the preceding certified cut. The sweep checks 22,079 global times and 689,190
eligible group/frame/cuts: 4,296 certified and 684,894 additional dominated cuts.
All 77 helper boundary groups are classified directly from source labels.

Scalar events are atomic. During one serial MOVE the scalar prefix/suffix
vectors do not change; all within-MOVE visits can therefore be collapsed to
the canonical odd midpoint used by the relaxation. In the remaining gaps,
collection histories stay fixed while delivery histories can only shrink.
This extends the grid check to the allowed chronology. The original residency
lemma still reduces an arbitrary intermediate physical frame to one with an
already stationary old pivot. No retiming, extra relocation, substituted
buffer pivot or changed scalar contact is admitted.

Frames 1091 and 1092 encode the same physical span with different binary bases.
The source never names 1092 in an original chart, event or endpoint. It has
only subdivision contacts, all of which are also granted at 1091; that
representative additionally includes the original stationary contacts and
pivots. The verifier explicitly authenticates this unused-alias property.
The alias therefore cannot hide a meeting excluded at the representative.

## Controls and limits

Synthetic exact fixtures contain a pivot meeting available only at a MOVE
midpoint and two intercritical cuts with a constant collection history.
Actual altered inputs are rejected for an omitted required midpoint, a new
uncovered prefix contact and an invalid pivot label. A rank-two positive
control, dirty-axis cancellation controls and 2,600 nullspace fixtures run in
the complementary full-history checker.

The full-history checker takes candidate cuts from the primary certificate;
the separate sweep establishes their physical coverage. Neither supplies a
native-phase, all-width, compiler, caller or multiplication theorem. The
frozen scalar program, common-boundary old-helper pivots, stationary buffers
and inherited five framework assumptions remain material. No new κ.
