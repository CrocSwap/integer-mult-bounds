# Literal operand recognition and product delivery

The inputs to the special branch are ordinary little-endian binary tapes. A cell contains the digit zero or one, including a leading zero supplied by the caller. No coefficient array, state descriptor or measurement is part of the input. The output is a canonical little-endian binary tape, with one zero cell for zero.

## Recognise the affine support and quadratic on it

For a nonnegative site count m and x in the binary m-cube, put d(x) = Σᵢ xᵢ 3ⁱ. The positive sparse family has digit one at d(x) precisely when x belongs to an affine binary subspace A and q(x) = 1, for a Boolean polynomial q of degree at most two on that subspace. Every other digit is zero. Read short inputs with virtual trailing zero digits. The complete controller chooses the least m with 3ᵐ ≥ n, where n is the larger literal length. For n ≥ 2, n ≤ 3ᵐ < 3n. The largest potential nonzero sparse address is (3ᵐ−1)/2. Larger positions are checked as holes. The separate periodic-template producer can provide a residual to this same positive recognizer. The full ternary scan fits the linear input budget, including on a failed admission.

First scan the raw tape in order and separate the potential nonzero positions. The complete ternary address tree has three children per node, in the order 0,1,2. A prefix stays valid exactly when it contains no digit 2. A depth-first traversal writes one bit on the compact work tape at each valid leaf, and checks zero at every other raw position. The valid leaves appear in the increasing binary order of x. Each traversal frame needs the number of its next child and one validity bit. Prewrite m+1 marked frame cells, including the bottom and terminal marks. Pushing, resuming and popping each child takes a fixed number of tape-head steps. At a physical input end marker, supply virtual zero bits until traversal ends. The total number of edges in this tree is less than 3·3ᵐ. There is no binary address division or comparison over m bits per raw digit. Input length can be counted during an initial scan with a binary ripple counter whose total number of carry movements is linear. Calculating m takes poly(m) additional steps.

On the compact tape let S be the sites whose digit is one. The zero operand has an empty set and gives the full cube with q identically zero. Otherwise choose the first occupied x₀. Form the binary row-reduced basis vₕ for span{x⊕x₀ : x in S}. Its pivot h is an actual original coordinate bit. For each nonpivot bit j, the affine equation is

    xⱼ ⊕ Σₕ (vₕ)ⱼ xₕ = (x₀)ⱼ ⊕ Σₕ (vₕ)ⱼ (x₀)ₕ.

These equations cut out exactly the affine hull. On that hull the coefficient of vₕ is tₕ = xₕ ⊕ (x₀)ₕ. Read the table at x₀, at x₀⊕vₕ for each h, and at x₀⊕vₕ⊕vₖ for h < k. The single finite differences give all linear coefficients and the second differences give all coefficients of tₕtₖ. Substitute tₕ = xₕ ⊕ (x₀)ₕ to produce an ambient square-free quadratic on the *original* site bits. Check the predicted value at every one of the 2ᵐ compact positions, including zeros. Return the affine equations, Boolean constant, linear mask and original-index pairs only on equality.

The affine hull is sufficient for unrestricted recognition in this family. If a larger unknown affine set A and quadratic q on A gave the tape, the hull of S is contained in A. The restriction of q to that hull still has degree at most two. The sampled finite differences must recover this restriction. The compact and hole checks then succeed. Conversely, a returned descriptor describes every raw digit. The cost of all Gaussian elimination, interpolation and verification is 2ᵐ poly(m) = o(3ᵐ). The executable visits all requested interpolation positions during one complete scan of the compact tape, and keeps only those O(m²) sampled bits and their masks. On fixed tapes even checking their keys by a sequential pass per compact entry fits this bound. The compact table occupies 2ᵐ bits. All masks, counters, coefficient arrays and elimination state take poly(m) further bits. Clear these regions after their last use and restore all input heads by a return scan. This gives O(n) time and O(n) backing on arbitrary success and failure paths. Calling the pre-existing multiplication algorithm after rejection adds O(n) to its existing running-time bound. ## Consume the four actual output streams

Each character-pair traversal emits a stream from the alphabet `>`, `<`, `+`, `-`. The first two moves advance or retreat by exactly one physical output bit. A sign means add one signed power of two **at the current head cell**. All exponent shifts and returns must be emitted as actual moves by the producer. A pass begins at cell zero. After the last event the consumer returns to cell zero before the next pass. The consumer takes four such streams. It neither computes the coefficient formula nor requests an unreported long-distance jump. The identity used by the producer is 2·1_A q = 1_A (1−(−1)^q). Applying this independently to both operands gives the division by four after all four signed streams have been combined.

Use separate positive and negative binary tracks on one marked physical interval, initially zero. At a sign, mark the caller's current head. On just the selected track, replace its consecutive one bits by zeros, moving right. Set its first zero bit to one. Move left to the mark, clear it, and resume the producer. Extend the marked right boundary whenever the head moves past it. At a left move never cross the origin. Initially the total number of ones in both tracks is zero. Each sign contributes exactly one new one and clears one one for each extra carry move. Thus the total number of carry moves is at most the total number of signs, for arbitrary signs and arbitrary visit order. The mark-and-return walk doubles that cost at most. Separating the two signs prevents an adversarial sequence from repeatedly borrowing through and reconstituting the same long prefix.

At completion, subtract the negative track from the positive track from low to high with a single borrow bit. Check the low two bits vanish and the final borrow is zero. Write the other bits to the output. Canonicalize trailing zeros by a reverse scan, leaving one zero digit for zero. Erase both accumulator tracks through their actual historical right bound and return their heads. The program takes O(number of emitted moves and signs + highest visited position + output length) tape steps, including the four rewinds. The fixed number of tracks and the output use O(highest visited position + 1) backing. For the certified character traversals this is O(n). A malformed negative walk, missing or excess pass, inexact division, or negative final magnitude produces no successful product. The Python code uses a list index to simulate a physical head and reports the number of conventionally counted movements for independent stress tests. The associated arithmetic and carry tariff are kernel-checked. `linear_ripple` bounds all propagated carries by the number of emissions, for any sign and address order. `signed_numeral` proves that the two tape numerals differ by the full sum of the emitted signed powers. The formal event addresses describe the cells reached by the stream's explicit moves. The signed recurrence also permits zero-cell extension when a carry increases the high-water mark. ## Independent checks and scope

The normal and optimized checker enumerates every Boolean function for m through three. Its separate oracle generates all intersections of affine hyperplanes and all degree-two truth tables and takes their masked products. It checks the exact returned truth table as well as admission. It checks 278 Boolean tables of which 214 are accepted, and all 511 raw tapes of length zero through eight. Additional literal tests cover missing upper digits, mixed holes, translated quadratics and cubic rejection. A separate brute character oracle enumerates source pairs at small m and feeds their signed powers to the actual delivery entry point. It checks 256 positive sparse raw-input products, all four character signs for each, long carry ripples, cancellation, backward moves, origin restoration and malformed events. These finite controls do not generate the growing construction. Its character traversal and its movement estimate are separate parts of the caller proof. The physical data path in this note makes no complete hidden tensor table and requires no pre-supplied Clifford measurement. [Independent tests](check_io.py)
