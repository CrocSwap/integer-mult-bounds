# Every optimal width-two odd-cycle fit has one local normal form

For every field and every odd n>=5, every rank-n fitting matrix on the cycle
C_n with identity two by two diagonal blocks is block-diagonally similar to
the canonical one-coordinate-per-edge construction. Thus every optimum obeys
`M^2=2M`. In characteristic two its Jordan form is exactly n nilpotent blocks
of size two. In other characteristics it is diagonalizable with eigenvalues
zero and two, each n times.

Claim type: scoped mathematical proof with exact finite corroboration. This
contribution was produced by Nick Petalas. Independent human and formal
proof-kernel review are unclaimed.

JoyboyBrian's [PR5023](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/5023)
already proves the rank value n as part of its unequal-payload theorem. This
package classifies **all attaining matrices**. It uses that rank value as a
cited premise and does not replay the predecessor's capacity computations.
The [proof](PROOF.md) extends Nick Petalas's older
[PR3248](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/3248) C5 edge-line
argument to all longer odd cycles, then obtains the local similarity and the
spectral identity from a dual edge basis. It permits arbitrary directed edge
blocks over any field, without symmetry, positivity or commuting-block
assumptions.

## Concrete contract and consequence

Write the two ports at vertex i as left and right. The canonical matrix D
has diagonal I_2 and one coefficient in each direction between right at i
and left at i+1. All other off-diagonal coefficients are zero. The theorem is

    rank(M)=n  implies  M=L D L^-1,  L=diag(L_0,...,L_(n-1)), L_i invertible.

A port permutation makes D a direct sum of n all-ones two by two matrices.
Every ordered edge block of an optimum has rank one; the two edge return maps
at each vertex are complementary rank-one projectors, and every directed
product along two distinct consecutive edges vanishes. These conditions can
reject a proposed optimum before a decoder construction is priced.

The width-two and odd-length hypotheses remain explicit. Even-cycle optima
with alternating rank-two and zero edge blocks defeat the normal form.
Rank-(n+1) odd-cycle fits need not obey the quadratic identity. Both boundaries
are executable controls.

Any CPython 3.12 interpreter can replace that local path. Each direct verifier

The bounded replay checks:

- 200 exact fixtures over Q, GF(2), GF(3), GF(5) and GF(101), at cycle lengths
  5, 7, 9, 11 and 13, including rational nonintegral basis changes.
- All 7,776 local GL2(GF(2)) basis choices on C5. Their matrices are distinct;
  direct reconstruction finds the canonical form and independent column-bit
  elimination checks their rank.
- Every binary assignment to two ordered edge blocks of the canonical C5 or
  C7, for every choice of the two slots. Among 11,520 assignments on C5,
  55 have rank five; among 23,296 assignments on C7, 105 have rank seven.
  Every such optimum passes reconstruction and the local/spectral identities.
  The counts enumerate assignments, including repeated matrices. They are
  exhaustive only for this specified two-block completion domain.
- 22 rejection controls, covering rank slack in five fields, even-cycle
  boundaries, forbidden and nonidentity blocks, malformed coefficients,
  matrix capacities and an unsupported modulus.

The all-field, all-odd-length result rests on the written proof. The finite
checks corroborate it and exercise the constructive normalizer; they do not
form an exhaustive census of all fitting matrices.

## Open next questions

Can an explicit minimum-rank unequal-width odd-cycle fit fail to be locally
equivalent to an integral edge/private-channel construction? Start with the
smallest width vectors for which the half-total bound has slack, inspect the
neighbor-intersection dimensions, and compare local similarity invariants.

Do not assume that finding a normal form pays for its physical realization.

## Verification implementation limits

The executable is intentionally bounded to matrices of at most 26 by 26 and
the five stated coefficient fields; the proof has no such finite restriction.
Input coefficients have numerator and denominator bit-length at most 256.
Python exact arithmetic allocates dynamically, a power-of-ten rule 3
deviation. Live matrices have fixed bounded dimensions; the only retained
census set has 7,776 encodings of 100 bytes. The three source inputs are
fixed hash-bound documents. No recursion, solver, subprocess or background
work is used. Negative-control callbacks have three fixed call targets.
Normal and optimized registered runs retain every check.

The isolated script adds only its fixed package directory before importing
its sibling module. The single E402 annotation documents that required import
order; the remaining selected Ruff diagnostics are enabled without exclusions.
