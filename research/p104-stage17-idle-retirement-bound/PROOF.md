# Stage-17 sign capacity of the original two-role host

On the pinned 4,783-event #7113 tape, the only Type B operations depending on D17 are the rank-eight IDLE_ONEs at 4646 and 4660 and the native J17 at 4691. The two idles act on global role 36. J17 acts on global roles 36 and 37 in Type B. All other events and all productive endpoints are fixed. Grant an arbitrary two-role replacement of the Type B part of J17 at its original clock. The proof allows its two-by-two matrix to be different in the two body signs.

Use central q=+1, all other bodies +1 and D17=+1 or −1. These sectors are physically present. The literal binary Gaussian gate has Walsh eigenvalues 1 and i. Its eight stage-17 axes occupy positions 137 through 144, disjoint from the central axes and all other stage bodies. At every positive integral width, zero or two minus Walsh factors on the stage-17 bits give the two signs, with all other factors plus. Arbitrary independent role amplitudes can be chosen on either sector.

At these signs the exact old Type B endpoint is diagonal. The local roles 0,1,16,17 carry D17 exactly once and the other 32 roles do not carry it. The difference between the two endpoints has rank four. Both replays verify the literal product on all 36 basis vectors at each sign.

If one idle is removed, the difference of the two products can be telescoped across the remaining idle and J17 replacement. Each telescoping term is a local difference multiplied by other gates on its left and right, so its rank is at most that local difference rank. The retained idle contributes at most one and the replacement at most two. With both idles removed only the replacement can contribute and the bound is two. Thus each permitted retirement has capacity less than four. This proof needs no signed relation between the two proposed host matrices.

## Forced map and coordinate support

The common prefix and suffix are invertible at the two physical signs. Let P_old and P_deleted denote the retained interval just before J17, from clock 4646 up to 4690. Cancellation forces the replacement to be J_old P_old P_deleted^−1. At the plus sign the deleted idles are already identities and the forced replacement is J_old. At the minus sign reverse covectors give the following exact outside-host entries of the forced replacement minus identity, using local role numbers.

| Deleted clocks | Outside-host entries `(row,column,value)` |
| --- | --- |
| 4646 | `(16,17,−6)`, `(17,17,−2)` |
| 4660 | `(16,16,−2)`, `(16,17,6)` |
| 4646 and 4660 | `(16,16,−2)`, `(17,17,−2)` |

In all three cases the forced host block on local roles 0 and 1 is the old negative-sign block with rows `(−1,1)` and `(−1/2,−1/2)`. Thus the required coordinate support contains all four global roles 36,37,52,53. The forced product preserves the original Walsh sectors because all the common factors and target do. Allowing a more general linear action within the two original role fields still cannot change the outside coordinates of this uniquely forced map.

## Optimistic price

For n retired slots the original paid histogram drops by 8n copies of rank eight, one per retained Type B port. Productive stock is fixed. With H(r)=r^(1−κ), the optimistic gross before host changes is 8nH(8). The exact directed incumbent quotes in both receipts agree and place the single-slot gross between 63.51 and 63.52 raw. The double-slot quote is exactly twice the single-slot interval. There is no valid replacement in the fixed host class even before charging setup, routing, ownership, restoration, precision or tape.
