Exact scope and algebra
=======================

1. Full-shell geometry and the binary representation

The generator uses the Golay matrix displayed in the official SageMath
documentation and the three minimal-vector shapes in Bannai--Sloane,
Uniqueness of Certain Spherical Codes, Canadian J. Math.33 (1981),
pp.437--449, especially p.443:
https://doc.sagemath.org/html/en/reference/coding/sage/coding/golay_code.html
https://neilsloane.com/doc/Me71.pdf

Integer coordinates x have squared norm32; v=x/sqrt(8) has squared
norm4. All196560 vectors are generated, tested for the lattice congruences,
uniqueness, norm, and antipodal closure. Selecting first nonzero coordinate
positive gives98280 lines. The all-pairs inner-product spectrum is a
literature fact: the finite generator independently checks whole-shell
distributions at66 specified rows, not all98280 squared pairs.

Choose24 shell vectors whose integral Gram matrix G is nonsingular
modulo2. The explicit binary inverse and isometry are recorded. As G
has odd determinant, reduction of its rational inverse is well defined
modulo2. Every shell vector has integral pairings with this real basis,
so the real basis identity proves the binary pairing for all pairs:

    p(v).p(w) = 1 + <v,w> mod2,   p(v).p(v)=1.

The24-dimensional alternating part is embedded in the even subspace
of F2^25 and the all-ones vector is added. No floating rank computation
or sampled identity is used to extend the proof to all pairs.

K(t)=(t^3-4t)/48 has diagonal K(4)=1. At distinct lines the possible
t are0,+/-1,+/-2, so nonzero K occurs only at t=+/-1, precisely where
p(v).p(w)=0. It is therefore a legal scalar/binary fitting pair.

The real rank is2600. The Leech shell is an11-design; hence evaluation
on it is injective on the degree1 plus degree3 harmonic spaces, whose
dimensions are24 and2576. K has nonzero components in both spaces.
This rank statement alone does not price a supplier.

2. A dyadic2600-center factorization

For raw coordinates x define

    f_i(x)=(x_i^3-x_i)/3,  a_i(x)=f_i(x)-10x_i,
    S_i(x)=sum_{j!=i} x_i*x_j^2.

The integer norm identity gives x_i=S_i+3a_i and f_i=10S_i+31a_i.
Use the following2600 encoder/decoder pairs:

    a_i(x)            ; 32*y_i^3-256*y_i                   (24)
    x_i*x_j^2        ; y_i*y_j^2+10*y_i^3+f_i(y)-85*y_i   (552)
    x_i*x_j*x_k      ; 2*y_i*y_j*y_k, i<j<k              (2024).

Their scalar product divided by8192 equals K(<x,y>/8). Expand the
cube and substitute the two identities to verify this equality.
Every displayed feature value is an integer. The divisions by3 define
precomputed integer constants; the scalar decoder uses only a power
of2 denominator. Thus there is no hidden run-time division by3.

On the coordinate alphabet0,+/-1,+/-2,+/-3,+/-4, a_i(x) vanishes
exactly when x_i does. Each encoder has the same support as its cubic
monomial. The common odd-coordinate orbit gives rank24 in every
support; each recorded additional even-coordinate witness increases
that rank to25. All2600 source supports therefore have rank25.
The center bill is the sum of actual source-support dimensions, namely
65000. It is neither2600 nor2600*24.

This gives a scalar factorization, not a complete paid side circuit.
The existing center-copy-to-zero interface permits an arbitrary dense
decoder at frame zero; it does not require source and decoder supports
to share the encoder frame.

3. Norm-six encoder basis

Let a be a Leech vector of squared norm6 obtained as a sum or difference
of two minimal vectors with appropriate inner product. For a minimal v,
integrality and minimum lattice norm imply |<a,v>|<=3. Consequently
f_a(v)=<a,v>^3-4<a,v> is supported exactly where <a,v> is odd.
Its binary source labels lie in an explicit proper hyperplane of F2^25.

The generator finds2600 independent such homogeneous cubic functions.
An exact integer elimination modulo251 proves independence over Q.
Every one has24 explicit independent source-label witnesses, proving
support dimension24 rather than merely an upper bound. This nominates
center bill62400, but rational basis inversion need not yield a decoder
over Z[1/2]. No dyadic decoder, common-frame helper word, or finite
certificate is inferred from the modular basis calculation.

4. Turyn and what a basis change actually accomplishes

TURYN.txt supplies the polarization and tensor-product arguments. The
code independently builds two Hamming codes H,H' meeting only in
{0,allones}; their Golay code is

    {(a+x,b+x,a+b+x): a,b in H, x in H'}.

All4096 words and their weight distribution are checked before building
the corresponding Leech shell. Block norms split into360 single-block,
5760 two-block, and92160 three-block antipodal lines. Quotienting the
four block-sign variants of each three-block line gives23040 ports.

The product of the three block inner products has diagonal2 and real
rank512. Exact whole-pair checks verify its off-diagonal raw products
and the proposed fits. The initial fitting rule1+4K mod2 has rank505
on the full set. A parity-based completion lowers this to53; neither
number is a lower bound for arbitrary fitting matrices.

A better construction uses one low-norm E8 block in each fixed norm
profile, giving9-bit labels per profile. Within each profile, all64
ports at a fixed selected low root have the same label. With raw
remaining-block tensors as rows, their64x64 matrix Z_g satisfies

    Z_g Z_g^T = 128 I.

Fix one group0 and set R_g=Z_g Z_0^T/128. Then R_g is dyadic,
R_g^T Z_g=Z_0, and R_g^T R_g=I. Applying these changes within the
identically framed groups transforms the normalized product Gram into
the ordinary120-line E8 Gram tensored with I_64. The inverse changes
restore the original coordinates. This is an exact identity, checked
for all120 groups in each of the three profiles.

The full120-port E8 geometry has center bill72: each coordinate support
spans9 bits. Restricting to the112 non-coordinate ports restores bill64.
The corresponding Turyn restriction scales both ports and cost by64;
the normalized moment therefore cannot improve merely by this change
of basis. Implementation choices for helpers can still differ.

For three fixed9-bit within-profile fits, cross-profile pairings have
the form p^T X q with81 unknown entries in X. For each pair of profiles,
81 explicitly recorded active-edge outer products are independent.
Legality forces X=0. Linear embeddings of these three fixed rank9 label
maps therefore cannot reduce their total dimension below27. This does
not cover representations with extra isotropic radical components inside
a profile that are invisible to its own Gram but pair with other profiles.

5. Remaining construction obligations

Neither a rank nomination nor a moment relaxation establishes a new
integer-multiplication exponent. A changed candidate still needs a
dyadic scalar word with arbitrary helper dirt restored, phase checks,
actual frame paths, helper widths, physical hosting, finite invoice,
and the outer certificate under the repository's conditional interfaces.
Different binary completions of the Turyn product graph, other kernels,
or selective packet transformations remain open in this package.
