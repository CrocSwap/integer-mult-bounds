# Nine calls for three parity-uniform targets with dirty workspace

Three different invertible rational 3-by-3 targets still require at least nine
rank-one paid calls when their classes in the actual generated address lattice
modulo two are equal. The common class may be zero. Any finite number of dirty
scalar roles may be used, provided the full endpoint restores them.

This extends the identical-target result to a particular mixed-target domain.
It does not settle all mixed targets. The hypothesis concerns the lattice of
the proposed word, not entrywise parity of rational matrices. Nine calls attain
the bound for the explicit nonidentical, noncommuting, nonflagged example below.
No supplier, physical storage saving or new kappa is claimed.

## Statement and dependencies

Work in the group algebra over F2 of additive rational matrices. A paid factor is

```text
D_N([A]) = I + ([A]-1)N,
rank_Q A = 1, N in M_R(F2), rank N = 1, N^2 = N.
```

Let H be the subgroup generated over Z by the finitely many paid frames A_j.
Assume equality of every formal coefficient at the endpoint

```text
diag([C_1], [C_2], [C_3], 1, ..., 1),
```

where each C_i is invertible of address dimension three, and
`C_i - C_1 in 2H` for i=2,3. Then there are at least nine factors.
Free scalar mixing is absorbed in the projectors; arbitrary free address maps
between paid calls and clean ancillas are outside the model.

The proof uses the following retained written results, not new replays of their
complete computer-assisted dependencies:

- [Mixed-target compression](../bit-mixed-target-register-compression/README.md)
  gives `dim span(N_j) <= k-d+q`, using H/2H, and invariant image restriction.
- [The identical-target proof](../bit-identical-three-targets-dirty/PROOF.md),
  sections 3 and 6, supplies the span-four normalization and the rank-three
  projector construction. The mixed-label endpoint argument is supplied below.
- David Govea's [PR720 proof](../three-register-no-target-flag/README.md),
  sections 5 and 7, gives the six-call bound for arbitrary invertible 2-by-2
  targets on exactly three scalar roles, and the nine-call bound for arbitrary
  invertible 3-by-3 targets on exactly three scalar roles.
- The [dirty-auxiliary theorem](../bit-dirty-auxiliary-registers/README.md),
  sections 2 through 4, gives the six-call bound for two arbitrary invertible
  3-by-3 targets with arbitrary dirty workspace and the low-span lemma.

[SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json) pins these exact files at the base commit. Their
original attribution and scope remain unchanged.

## Proof

### 1. Parity is preserved by address projections

Every target is an exponent in the expanded formal product, hence belongs to H.
An additive rational map phi sends H onto the subgroup H' generated by the
nonzero projected paid frames. If `C_i-C_1 = 2h`, then
`phi(C_i)-phi(C_1) = 2 phi(h) in 2H'`. Thus equal classes stay equal, although
a nonzero common class may become zero. Deleting identity projected factors
does not change H'. Common rational left and right changes of address basis
also preserve this property.

Write `d = rank_Z H` and let t be the common target class in H/2H. First-order
augmentation of a successful word gives

```text
sum_j a_j tensor N_j = t tensor P,
P = diag(1,1,1,0,...,0).
```

The paid classes a_j span H/2H. If t=0, the scalar span S of the N_j has
dimension at most k-d. If t is nonzero, P belongs to S and
`dim S <= k-d+1`. This is the retained mixed-target tensor argument with all
three target classes equal, not an entrywise reduction modulo two.

The joint image U of the paid projectors has dimension at most dim S and
contains all three active coordinate axes. To see the latter for different
or repeated target labels, the coefficient at each distinct nonzero C is the
coordinate projector onto the roles with that label. Each such image lies in U.
Use a basis of U consisting of the active axes followed by a basis of U
intersected with the dirty-coordinate space. Restriction preserves the diagonal
endpoint, rank-one idempotence and every formal coefficient. In particular,
`dim S <= 3` reduces to exactly three scalar roles.

### 2. Two-dimensional address blocks need six calls

Consider three parity-uniform invertible 2-by-2 targets with arbitrary restored
dirty roles. Suppose there are k<=5 paid calls. If their rational frame span
has dimension at most two, the retained low-span lemma requires six calls.
If d>=3, section 1 gives `dim S <= k-d+1 <= 3`, including when t=0.
The three nonzero target roles lie in U, so restriction gives exactly three
scalar roles. PR720 section 5 requires six calls there, a contradiction.

No common target flag is assumed in this argument. The lattice is that of the
projected word itself, not a lattice chosen before projection.

### 3. Paid-frame span at most four needs nine calls

Spans at most three are excluded by the retained low-span lemma. At dimension
four, the retained determinant normalization chooses three paid rank-one basis
frames with independent left and right factors. Common rational basis changes
put the entire span in the form `D + t uv^T`, with D diagonal.

If every coordinate of u and v is nonzero, a 2-by-2 minor on rows i,k and columns
i,l, with i,k,l distinct, equals `D_ii t u_k v_l`. Consequently every paid
rank-one frame lies on one of the four independent lines E11,E22,E33,uv^T.
Projection to each line requires at least as many calls on that line as there
are targets using it. Every invertible target uses at least three lines by
rational rank subadditivity. Summing gives at least nine calls. This branch
does not need the parity hypothesis.

Otherwise u or v has a zero coordinate. The retained support-graph argument
puts the span in block upper-triangular form with diagonal address block sizes
at most two. A rank-one block upper-triangular matrix has at most one nonzero
diagonal block, so calls assign disjointly to these blocks. All three targets
have invertible diagonal blocks. Projection to each block preserves their
common lattice parity by section 1. A one-dimensional block requires three
calls by the rank of target-minus-identity; a two-dimensional block requires
six by section 2. The block dimensions sum to three, so at least nine calls
are required. This is the new step needed to handle unflagged mixed targets
rather than silently reuse an identical-target normalization.

### 4. Higher span reduces or splits the scalar roles

Now suppose k<=8 and d>=5. If t=0, `dim S <= 3` and restriction contradicts
PR720's three-role nine-call theorem. If t is nonzero, P belongs to S and
`dim S <= 4`. Dimension at most three has the same contradiction.

At dimension four choose four actual rank-one idempotents as a basis of S.
Their binary traces equal one; P has trace one and rank three. Its expansion
therefore uses exactly three basis members, say `P=N_1+N_2+N_3`. Factoring
`P=XY^T` and using P^2=P shows `Y^T X=I3`. Thus these three projectors are
mutually orthogonal and give active coordinate axes after a scalar basis
change inside im P. Their complement is ker P.

Write the remaining basis member Q=xy^T. All paid images lie in
`im P + span{x}`, of dimension at most four. Restrict there. If the dimension
is three, PR720 applies. Otherwise use coordinates with P=diag(1,1,1,0) and

```text
x=(a,1), y^T=(b^T,c), a dot b + c = 1.
```

The rational target endpoint need not remain diagonal in these new active
coordinates. This does not invalidate the split: its restriction on im P is
scalar-conjugate to the original three diagonal monomials, and it preserves
all subspaces preserved by every paid projector. To keep the target split
valid without assuming diagonality in this basis, use its formal coefficients.
Each coefficient is the spectral coordinate projector for one distinct target
label. Any common invariant subspace of the paid projectors is invariant under
these coefficient projectors. It therefore splits, within each equal-label
space, into active and complementary parts. An adapted basis within each
label space makes the restriction and quotient endpoints diagonal again.
The number of active monomial roles is their dimension, independent of their
labels, and all nonzero target frames remain invertible.

If c=0, the fourth coordinate line is killed by all four basis projectors.
Quotienting it gives three active target roles. Every induced projector retains
rank one and idempotence: if its image were contained in the killed line, its
square would be zero, contradicting nonzero idempotence. PR720 excludes this
three-role word.

If c=1, `a dot b=0`. Some active i has `a_i b_i=0`, since there are three
active coordinates. If b_i=0 the line through e_i is invariant; if a_i=0 its
complementary coordinate hyperplane is invariant. These subspaces are inside
im P or contain ker P, respectively, and split the endpoint into one active
role and two active roles plus one dirty identity role. The coefficient
argument above identifies their target labels even if the active basis mixed
different labels. Each rank-one idempotent preserving this subspace induces a
rank-one idempotent on exactly one of restriction and quotient, and zero on the
other. The original calls partition disjointly. One invertible 3-by-3 target
needs three calls; two with dirty workspace need six. Their sum exceeds eight.

This covers every span and proves the lower bound directly for all k<=8.
No padding or claim that splitting preserves a chosen nonzero class is needed.

### 5. Sharpness beyond identical or flagged targets

Let C0=I3, C1=I3+4E21 and C2=I3+4E12. The following triples of rank-one
frames, applied on scalar roles 0,1,2 respectively, are a nine-call word:

```text
E11,                 E22,                 E33;
E11+2E21,            E22+2E21,            E33;
E11+2E12,            E22+2E12,            E33.
```

Each row sums to its stated invertible target. Their generated lattice has
basis `E11,E22,E33,2E12,2E21`. All targets have class `(1,1,1,0,0)` modulo
two. The targets are different and do not commute. They have no common full
rational flag: E12 and E21 generate the full matrix algebra on the first two
coordinates, which has no invariant line there; the only common invariant
line in three dimensions is the third axis, and its quotient has no invariant
line. Since C0=I3, simultaneous left/right triangularization would give such
a flag for the ratios C0^-1 C1 and C0^-1 C2. This example is strictly outside
the identical-target theorem and the common-target-flag domain.

## Evidence and reproduction

The universal result above is a written proof using the named predecessor
theorems. The finite replay checks the generated-lattice hypothesis and exact
formal endpoints; it does not enumerate all rational words or rerun PR468's
complete computer-assisted search.

```sh
python3.12 -I -B scripts/research_package.py --package research/bit-parity-uniform-targets
python3 -I -O -B research/bit-parity-uniform-targets/verify.py
```

[RESULT.json](RESULT.json) records five fixtures: the nine-call word at integer,
half-integral, all-even and negative-third scales, and its eighteen-call split
with zero common class. The all-even fixture has a nonzero lattice class,
exposing the error in entrywise parity. Exact formal replay includes two dirty
roles, a noncommuting dirty prefix restored before the word, and omitted-call
and omitted-restoration controls. Every displayed paid frame has rational rank
one, and every displayed target is invertible.

An independent oracle enumerates literal integer combinations for 49 ordered
generator pairs in a fixed two-coordinate domain and checks all 81 targets per
pair, totaling 3,969 cases. Its pool consists of `(1,0)`, `(0,1)`, `(1,1)`,
`(2,0)`, `(0,2)`, `(1,-1)` and `(2,2)`. The coefficient box `[-16,16]^2` covers the tested target
box `[-4,4]^2`: for independent pairs, Cramer's numerators have absolute value
at most 16; for dependent pairs, their primitive direction has coordinates of
absolute value at most one and the line multipliers are one or two. A separate
6/10/15 fixture checks a lattice whose unit needs three generators. The lattice
routine uses unimodular integer row operations, never rational saturation.

The scalar control checks all 64 rank-one idempotents whose image has a dirty
component in four coordinates, all 168 active bases and all five equality
partitions of three target labels. Of 53,760 cases, 42,528 preserve the selected
common paid-invariant subspace and 11,232 do not. Among the compatible cases,
27,936 have nondiagonal target coefficients in the paid-projector basis. Each
compatible case reconstructs the invariant subspace from its spectral and
dirty parts and has the claimed active split. The incompatible cases explain
why section 4 must use spectral invariance rather than assume the new active
basis keeps the endpoint diagonal. This is a finite check of the new mixed-label
step, not another universal rational-frame census.

Seven behavioral test groups exercise lattice membership, class collapse under
projection, capacity failures, a literal formal endpoint, relocated normal and
optimized execution, and read-only behavior. Three actual implementation
mutants fail before receipt comparison: saturating the lattice, skipping target
spectral invariance, and changing one paid frame. Corrupting the receipt also
fails. No verifier check depends on Python assertions or floating point.
Normal execution is read-only. `--write` regenerates only RESULT.json.

Build the Lever led to the rerunnable lattice and formal-endpoint checker.
Prove It Works led to actual implementation mutants, not just a stored count.
Test Behavior, Not Implementation led to literal coordinate and endpoint
expectations. Laziness Protocol kept the result in one package without a new
search framework.

## Open next questions

Can a three-target eight-call word with dirty workspace have unequal target
classes in H/2H? First specify a candidate passing the complete rational
rank-cut and determinant screens, then compute its actual target classes
before searching scalar couplings in the retained compression bound. This
result removes the parity-uniform case only. Unequal classes, full formal
identity, native realization and the complete cost remain blockers. It does
not justify a larger unchanged search on its own.

## Implementation limits

60 physical lines. There is no recursion or code generation. Internal matrix
sizes are fixed at most six scalar roles and nine address coordinates. The
lattice has at most eighteen generators, rational numerators and denominators
at most 65,535, and a common denominator at most 128 bits. Integer rows are
capped at 4,096 bits; Euclid has at most 8,193 iterations per call. Formal
support is capped at 4,096 terms after each of at most eighteen calls, with at
most twice that many temporary terms. The closed fixtures need much less.
