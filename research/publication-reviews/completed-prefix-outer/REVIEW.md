## Separate review of the fixed-past supplement

The frozen campaign has a producer normalization check but no distinct
final independent review of this complete closure note. This publication
review adds a bounded independent proof check and an exact algebra check.
It does not relabel the earlier producer check as independent evidence.

The conversion `h=S C S=(1+i)H0` gives `h^2=iI` and `h^8=I`. Only the
active coordinates receive the usual `b` normalization. On one fixed
completed space P, every later active exact round commutes with `h_P`.
The complete endpoint is therefore `h_P^n T`, and one final inverse
decodes it. The final long-only opposite round has the required disjoint
support. An even inverse power is a phase; an odd inverse power needs one
past Walsh tensor and a phase. This gives the stated `O(Vd)` decoder cost.

For odd powers, each row has `2^|P|` entries of magnitude
`2^(-|P|/2)`. Even powers are phases times the identity. Thus
`||h_P^j||_infinity<=2^(|P|/2)` for every integer j. After final decoding,
an error injected after round j has past factor `h_P^-j`, followed by
an active contraction. The sum is at most `n 2^(|P|/2) eta`. The claimed
`O(d)` precision and magnitude allowance follows when `|P|=O(d)` and
`d=o(p)`. The supplier must still prove its own approximation and prefix
bound on inputs of that guarded magnitude. This is a material premise.

The independent standard-library checker uses exact rational pairs for
Gaussian numbers and imports no producer. It checks 114 tensor-power
cases at widths zero through five and exponents from -9 through 9, and
30 decoded error propagators with varying active rounds. Its controls
reject normalization of the past to H0, movement of a current-bit code
through its twiddle, omission of the final decoder, and treatment of an
odd past power as a maximum-norm contraction. The result is in
[FIXED-PAST-INDEPENDENT.json](FIXED-PAST-INDEPENDENT.json). The general
claim rests on the written tensor and commutation proof, not on these
finite examples.

The selected historical cost margins admit the separate `O(Vd)` startup
and decode terms and the optional `O(V ell)` row-axis term. The latter
uses a different completed axis. A fully priced native endpoint on every
