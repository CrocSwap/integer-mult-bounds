# Independent review of the general-completion annex

The annex passes for its stated algebraic claims. This is a separate
review of the added theorem. The earlier storage proof and acceptance
remain unchanged. No material finding remains open. This review used a
separate OpenAI Codex agent. It does not claim independent human review.

The [proof map](../../borrowed-centre-storage/annex/general-completion/PROOF.md)
and [frozen proof](../../borrowed-centre-storage/annex/general-completion/frozen/work/centre-encoding-campaign-20261014/lanes/schur-selection/unit-query-review-20261015/GENERAL-COMPLETION.md)
state the accepted result. A unit selected minor gives the two-shear unit
completion for arbitrary selected K. It retains every unselected row
and stores the full query in the selected outputs. Both inverse orders
follow from the support identities. The repair is a split projection
only with the stated selected-K compatibility condition. The simplified
inverse with coefficient 2-g needs that condition and RK=gI.

The onto-query converse is valid for the finite free modules over the
stated commutative ring. An onto query forces the square readout to be a
unit. The block row operation then forces the selected minor to be a
unit. The two retained controls explain why the onto premise and the
full-query output contract cannot be removed.

When RK=gI and a unit d-field minor exists, the minimum changed-field
count is zero if 1-g is a unit, and d otherwise. The lower bound permits
any linear unit repair. Reduction at a maximal ideal gives a dN
dimensional kernel. A unit repair must be injective on that kernel, but
c changed complete fields have only cN output coordinates. The explicit
completion gives the matching upper bound. This proves a count of output
fields, not a minimum native-child count.

For the actual Gaussian-dyadic matrix, the two reductions to F13 give an
exact minimum of eight changed complete fields. The realified argument
uses both sectors and covers dyadic real-linear unit maps. The complete
cover and payload module is required. On ker(R), or after admitting
division by 13, the stated obstruction does not apply. The nonlinear
extension requires a well-defined surjective residue map. Its unchanged
field set is U_c, with n-c fields. The new proof map states this correction
to the historical proof notation.
