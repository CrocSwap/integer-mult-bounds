## Accepted claim

`rho(U T^-1) = d(L(T), L(U))`, where `L(T)=S(T)^-1 Z` and
`d(L,K)=n-dim(L intersect K)`. The relative operator order is correct.
The intersection inequality in the middle space gives the triangle
inequality. A left monomial Clifford preserves Z. A common right gauge
applies one invertible symplectic map to both endpoint spaces. These facts
give the rank lower bound for each retained productive path.

The theorem permits arbitrary Clifford frame gauges and exact adjacent
native fusion. It retains productive lifetime paths, legal common-frame
scalar accesses, and the stated boundary frames. It prices each native
event once, including events that had rank zero. It permits no free
productive CLEAR, frame reset, or lifetime transfer into private zero
workspace. It gives no free native operator-valued scalar coefficient.

If N productive paths start at I and end at full F in binary dimension n,
up to left monomial wrappers, their rank sum is at least `n N`. The tensor
argument scales both dimensions and native ranks by the allowed tensor
power. Cover permutations transport path labels. The proof sums over the
whole cover before division by its size. It does not equate independent
payload values in different cover classes or grant shared native children
across productive paths.

The global application also assumes the identity joint content endpoint.
It is a distinct class from the primary package's original virtual-Q
boundaries and 511-line translation gauges. The original virtual-Q
supplier does not satisfy the new common-I/full-F premise. The annex is
therefore not an extension of the original-boundary result to all
Clifford gauges, or a general circuit or encoding lower bound.
