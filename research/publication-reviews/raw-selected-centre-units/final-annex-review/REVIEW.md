## Proof review

The reviewer read the actual proofs, original independent reviews, six
decisive checkers, and the imported reverse interpreters and line-kernel
engine. The review checked the following points.

1. **Complete minors.** The inverse formulas follow from
   `G_U^2=(d-k)lambda G_U` and `GPG=(d-2k)lambda G`. The principal
   blocks of the inverses force the two power-of-two denominators.
   This proves the criterion for unions of the specified complete
   minors; it does not classify irregular selected sets. In the actual
   six-minor instance, all 64 unions and all 20 balanced splits are
   checked. The one-minor kernel and modulo-five query bound have the
   stated algebraic scope. The serial gauges use one owned zero field,
   and their literal words return it to zero after each query.

2. **Positive diagonal metrics.** The exact witness checks all 112
   coefficient polynomials for the specified 42-dimensional symmetric
   family. The two-variable positive block has determinant `5/144`.
   Positivity of every diagonal entry forces coefficients 32 through
   41 to vanish. The remaining response has rank at most 64. This does
   not restrict a larger response space, nonsymmetric reciprocal
   responses, or nondiagonal interface gauges.

3. **Signed principal pivots.** The two outer block factors are shears;
   their middle factors are dyadic units under the stated minor
   hypotheses. The six-step word uses arbitrary ordinary data as its
   centre coordinates and does not erase that data. The general data
   interface and its displayed inverse multiply to the identity in
   both orders without an operator commutation assumption. The actual
   graph has six eight-cycles and three pairs of four-cycles. One
   parity condition per cycle gives exactly 960 choices in this fixed
   construction. Only the displayed choice has emitted and priced
   gauge words. Jacobi's identity gives the sharp selection-size bound
   of eight within the component-sign family. It is not a native
   capture-rank bound.

4. **Raw and signed local words.** The raw selected exchange keeps
   the port operators in their written order. In B0, selected Y is
   `F Q_A^-1 Q_A a=F a`; the inverse formula proves information
   preservation, not a free decoder. B8 and B16 retain the correction
   terms that follow from `BD=13I`. The signed-unit and oblique words
   use the separate response `S=I-KR-Pi` and its exact dyadic inverse.
   They are not the component-sign response of the 960-choice result.
   The two families have different owned-output contracts. The annex
   states those contracts and the full local invoices separately.

5. **Direct live-field suffix.** The identity
   `Q_v=P_G Q_e0 P_G^-1` holds with the displayed zero-phase
   permutation wrappers. Translation commutation gives `Q_v F=F Q_v`
   with the full global phase. The subtraction reads current physical
   `Y_S=q d` and `X_U=k`, for arbitrary d. Its targets are not its
   sources, so the scaled subtraction has the same map and saves 288
   scalar primitives. All eight added H1 children and sixteen wrappers
   remain explicit. No caller hosting or descriptor cost is inferred
   from the local identity.

The stated native invoices are literal local invoices on pinned
historical providers. They are not admissions into the current selected
supplier. Routed access, exterior phases, decoder operations, owner
