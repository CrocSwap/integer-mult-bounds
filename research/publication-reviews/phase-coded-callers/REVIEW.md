## Scope and proof review

I read the actual proofs, their direct reviews, the relevant checker
implementations, the primary stream/layout sections, and the final
wrapper. [`PROOF-READ.json`](PROOF-READ.json) records the files and hashes.
The all-width conclusions use the written arguments below. They are
not inferred from the finite replay alone.

The phase-coded local word has the stated ordered forward and backward
maps. The physical entry additions occur before the adapters. They keep
Q or q attached to its original source. The local replay checks both
providers, all three orientations, all 640 independent inputs, and all
416 arbitrary helper returns. Native kernels and endpoint adapters
retain their separate exact source contracts.

The D25 caller applies three scalar shears at the stated FULL cuts.
The complete middle cover sweep and helper returns must finish first.
The endpoint is `(-F25 q Y0, F25 q X0)` for Q entry and arbitrary inputs.
The D73 proof preserves that same q through three corrected triples.
Its endpoint is `(F73 q Y0, -F73 q X0)`. The proof keeps the operator
order and each literal affine translation. It does not commute q
through a reader or read an ordinary hole as FULL.

The local scalar price is 4632. The direct D25 and D73 scalar subtotals
are 20040 and 60120, before the stated terminal and map charges.
The prospective 2912-scalar word is not a premise. The two D25 paid
profiles have rank mass 5600. The D73 paid profile has mass 16352.
These equal `224*25` and `224*73`. Every child rank is below its
ambient dimension. The stated strict native moment inequality therefore
fails for these profiles. The terminal line counts are not lower bounds
for all possible decoders. A recursive common-q consumer remains open.

The section result has a different interface. The caller uses 16/96
ports with `D=-H*`. The section uses 8/104 ports with `D=B*` and
`BD=13I`. The section inverse does not decode the caller's q endpoint.

Its oblique projector has norm 13. The complement is `im(QD)` plus
`ker(BQ)`. The positive- and inverse-line images intersect.
The odd-power obstruction uses the rank-seven width-five witness and
extends by zero characters. It applies at width at least five, including
nonlinear sections constrained to that image. It states no minimum width.

The translation words have the stated exact local endpoint, arbitrary

The one-bank proof uses the free orbits of nine independent translations

## Corrections retained in the accepted wrapper

The historical map correction is mandatory. The 144 simultaneous
translations retain their packed-layout cost. The selected bits are
interleaved at general K, so the withdrawn contiguous-complement shortcut
does not prove a linear whole-body bound. The marked-counter proof for
conditional scalar scans remains valid at arbitrary selected positions.

This review also found a missing record-count factor in the historical
two-bank service review. Its stated odd-scan bound omitted n. The pinned
A8 proof gives `64*n*(w_g+1)` arithmetic-head steps for n complex entries
per field, plus stream control. The producer added this qualification
to README, PROOF, VALIDATION, and result metadata. No frozen historical
file or mathematical checker changed. The scalar invoices are unaffected.

The appendix publication note also contained a COMMIT placeholder link.
The coordinating review found it, and the appendix owner replaced it
with a relative proof link. Only that publication note and its manifest
references changed. All new wrapper links resolve in the target layout.
