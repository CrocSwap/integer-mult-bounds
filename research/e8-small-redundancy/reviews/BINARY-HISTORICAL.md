# Independent audit of the eleven-centre obstruction

Reviewed proof: `theory/ELEVEN-PROOF.md`.
SHA-256: `2bc8e531ce6d3d683f760a4208e9be2a061fe15b54bca0034de59c292c17f65e`.

Result: the proof is valid for its stated fixed binary E8 matrix and scalar
source-reader factorization model. Every factorization with at most eleven
terms has rational source-reader pairing-rank sum at least 72. This is a
computer-assisted proof. It is not a formal Lean verification.

## Independent finite checks

The primary checker uses the Hamming coordinates from the pinned word. The
independent checks generate the 120 standard E8 root lines separately and
use a different simple root basis.

- `independent_structure.py` verifies all eight root-hyperplane cases, all
  511 nonzero codeword support ranks, all required outside support spans,
  and the E7 common-neighbor and neighbor-rank facts.
- `independent_eleven_facts.py` verifies the full 512-word outside-weight
  histogram, the root inner products, all seven E7 maximal proper flats,
  their rank-seven complements, and the maximum proper-flat size of 36.
- `independent_coordinate_map.py` constructs a rational similarity between
  the two coordinate systems and checks the image of every one of the 120
  root lines. Thus the independent standard-coordinate checks concern the
  same pinned matrix, up to a simultaneous coordinate permutation.

All three scripts passed. Results are in the corresponding JSON files.
For rank-eight support decisions, modular rank eight is a valid lower bound
on rational rank, hence certifies equality. Lower ranks and required
rank-seven support spans in the structural checker use Fraction arithmetic.
The E7 complement checks establish rank at least seven modulo 101 and rank
at most seven from containment in the E7 hyperplane.

## Argument review

The arbitrary-flat reduction is complete: a root span extends to a root
hyperplane; a Weyl transformation moves its normal to the closed dominant
chamber; the roots perpendicular to that normal are generated by exactly
seven simple roots. The corresponding E7 argument covers every proper E7
root span. Reflection closure is checked by the primary implementation.

The coset star classification includes centres of rank eight. The proof
therefore does not silently assume that a centre itself is a selected
low-rank vector. Its defect identity also permits omitted centres.

For independent centres, the small-error lemma uses the full outside-weight
histogram. The earlier D_H classification alone would not suffice here.
The rank-seven sum forces all residual supports into the same E7 hyperplane.
An omitted rank-eight centre contains its normal in the span of its removed
orthogonal roots. Unless that normal is one of those roots, exactly four
roots have nonzero coefficients. Root parity would require four shared
satellite roots, but basis independence permits at most one shared root for
each pair of groups. This gives the stated contradiction. The remaining
normal-root cases give explicit binary dependencies among selected vectors.

For dependent centres, the three stated lifted-root dual vectors pair only
with their own root and have nonzero self-pairing. They prove the bound on
the number of distinct selected satellite roots. The duplicate count covers
all three pair types. Its only possible defect-17 case forces a rank-one
centre and two rank-eight centres. The repeated root is then isolated in a
rank-eight centre, so its removal has rank seven. This is inconsistent with
the other centre having rank eight.

The final factor-rank reduction is complete. After zero terms are removed,
Sylvester's inequality leaves only the factor-rank pairs listed in the
proof. Repeated columns can be merged without increasing pairing-rank cost.
For factors of rank (10,10), the ten-dimensional coset bound gives the
termwise pairing-rank argument. For (9,11), the rank-nine side has full
rational source span in every term, and the other side is a basis of an
eleven-dimensional space containing C. The symmetric case is identical.

## Scope

This proof does not exclude twelve or more terms. It does not exclude a
change to the matrix, the allowed operations, or the scalar retained-total
model. No positive bound improvement is claimed.
