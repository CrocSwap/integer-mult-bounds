For each fixed sum s of the other coordinates and any nondecreasing convex f, the chord inequality gives f(s+y)≤(1−y/2)f(s)+(y/2)f(s+2). Integration and the mean bound give an upper bound from replacing Y with twice a Bernoulli(3/8) variable. Independence allows this replacement one coordinate at a time, taking a new Bernoulli independent of all other coordinates. **Clipping and complete paid denominator.** Apply that convex bound to the untruncated power.
