# Independent review of the short-axis witness and short-word arithmetic

## AX — source-matched sieve

I checked the staged axis proof, Git blob `90932cb4e9deeded51e9bf63b03f54cbe66d661d`, against the source's width, descending gcd test, pairwise-coprime box and density contracts. The written argument establishes its claimed witness at every prescribed search, with its stated cutoff.

The half-open band (sqrt(u),u] lies in the dyadic shells from a through 2a+1, inclusive, for a=floor(log₂ sqrt(u)). These shells also cover either endpoint falling on a power of two. The binomial divisibility bound gives reciprocal mass at most 2/a + 2 ln((2a+1)/a) ≤ 2 ln 2 + 3/a < 3/2 whenever a≥32. The band survival is at least exp(−(32/31)·3/2)>1/5. The residual interval of primes can depend on z; it always lies below 2^64, which gives both the claimed absolute primorial upper bound and survival lower bound. There is no prime at a missing band endpoint.

For the divisibility indicators at any integer, even Bonferroni gives 0≤h_j≤U_j, and the following odd term E_j bounds U_j−h_j from above. Telescoping the product difference uses these nonnegative h_j and U_j; each omitted difference is at most E_j times the product of the other upper polynomials. The lower Bonferroni polynomial itself may be negative, which causes no sign reversal. Under independent prime trials, the correction divided by the product of the expectations is bounded by the stated 5Σ_j (3/2)^(5+2j)/(5+2j)!, which is <0.34 using the ratio bound 9/168. Multiplication by the residual coprimality indicator is valid before and after taking expectations.

The true interval moment for a squarefree prime subset with product d differs from Y/d by at most one. Unique factorization bounds the number of monomials in each product by their maximum d. The exponent 14 in the staged proof is safe. In fact Σ_j(4+2j)/2^j=12, and a single correction adds at most 1 to that exponent. The source proof's extra factor J+1 for the discrepancy is also safe.

With t=log₂k≥2^100, each of the three normalized error terms is strictly below 2^(-70)/t^3, one quarter of the stated main lower bound. For the first this requires 72+4 log₂t+log₂ D₀<6t/5; for the second it requires 71+3 log₂t<t/5. Both follow from 2^70≤t/1000, log₂(2t)+100≤t/1000 and log₂D₀≤2^70. The third is smaller still. The positive count in Y consecutive integers **above** X−Y supplies a witness in the source's closed window of Y+1 integers. Its accumulator is positive and at most 2^(k+1), and the appended binary axis remains coprime to all the odd full axes. The source's short chirp and CRT accept these composite or prime odd lengths. AX leaves the deterministic search and dense volume intact. It replaces the written dependency on the interval-of-primes or quadratic Jacobsthal theorem for this task. The released Lean module still imports its original number-theoretic proof.

## A2 — where large integer products occur

I audited F2, Git blob `3b65f6cd0de0ef7b247211dedb4e6a1518191a71`, the S003 scalar and address schedules, and the published integer and permutation callers. F2 confines its internal rational scalar action to a long word and operands of B=m^O(1) bits. Even widths use dyadic four-point leaves and even remainders. Its odd butterfly is an integer add/subtract, and the squared midpoint comparison is performed in a single output scan. For each global Walsh layer this requires at most a constant number of general O(k)-bit products per record, plus one polynomial(k) preparation. This review checks the A2 arithmetic substitution. F2's rational matrix and denominator-support proofs have a separate algebraic review.

For each global phase layer, the source extends prefix coefficients by complete local block tables. Each successive stream is at least twice as long. Fewer than 2N general products build the full coefficient stream, and N apply it; a complex product uses a fixed number of integer products. The two forwards and the inverse have O(log(k+2)) layers in all. O(N) additional products suffice for the pointwise product and final multiplication by the approximation to sqrt(S). All chirp tables, square roots, e.g. bisection for the odd Walsh normalization, and initial axis data have polynomial(k) setup. Reuse the marked scratch regions between calls.

Some expensive computations in controlled permutations do recur with the data. Their multiplication costs are covered differently. For each guarded prefix, offset/control evaluation costs k^O(1), while the target fibre has at least 2^G complete records. Each exceptional record may require k^O(1) work to invert the eight shifts, recover its rank and sort it, but their fraction is O(k 2^(−G)). Choosing G=C ceil(log₂(2k)) after fixing these polynomial degrees covers both expenditures. The full-volume scan only evaluates the guard mask in O(k) per record. The bound on B2's geometric shears likewise pays long modular-offset preparation with entire complete physical fibres; short field/grade arithmetic and finite local lists obey their original polynomial-m invoices. No appeal to a general k-bit multiplier per numerical child is needed.

For completeness the fixed Toom-3 word multiplier evaluates at 0,1,−1,2 and infinity. If w₀,…,w₄ are the product coefficients and v_t are evaluation products, set w₀=v₀, w₄=v_∞, w₂=(v₁+v₋₁)/2−w₀−w₄, T=(v₁−v₋₁)/2 and Q=(v₂−w₀−4w₂−16w₄)/2. Then w₃=(Q−T)/3 and w₁=T−w₃. The divisions by 2 and 3 are exact. A fixed number of signed scans, fixed-small-divisor quotients, shifts and carries implements interpolation and recombination in O(k). Each input splits into three limbs of at most ceil(k/3) bits. Evaluation increases operand magnitude width by at most three. Consequently T(k)≤5T(ceil(k/3)+3)+O(k). For example shifting the size parameter by 6 gives ceil(k/3)+3−6≤(k−6)/3; the constant base covers small widths. Level summation yields O(k^α) with α=log₃5. A depth-first scratch stack uses O(k) cells, as child widths are at most half for large k and siblings reuse the same fixed banks. Rewinds, signs and fixed interpolation coefficients require no machine parameter depending on k.

The total general-word multiplication expense is O(N k^α (1+log k)) plus polynomial(k) setup. Since 5²<3³, α<3/2 and this is o(N k^(3/2)). It fits the claimed envelope and absolute space bound.

There is a backup option for the predecessor rounded rational Walsh machine. For a signed input mantissa u and an exact reduced scalar A/b, with numerator and positive denominator of at most B=m^O(1) bits, multiply by A using at most B long shifts/additions. Divide the O(k+B)-bit magnitude by b one bit at a time with a B-bit running remainder and round once to the original grid by comparing twice the final remainder to b. This takes O((k+B)B+B^O(1)) steps. All local arities satisfy B≤k beyond the same global guards. The new per-operation fault is at most one half-unit in the last place of the exact scalar action, already covered by NW's larger allowance for coefficient approximation plus rounding. Thus a direct short-rational action can replace the old multiplication by a full-precision coefficient approximation. A uniform substitution of a slower general word multiplier at **every** old recursive large product would have required a different local-time proof.
