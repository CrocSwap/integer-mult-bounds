# Numerical tape bounds for the adaptive interchange data stream

The direct radix-three fallback of [AI](foundations/adaptive-interchange.md) has a transition count for all native widths. The fixed rule table generated by **build_direct** in [node-tapes.py](../checks/node-tapes.py) sends arbitrary bits at address $(i,x,g,y,s)$ to $(i,y,g,x,s)$ on one-dimensional tapes with one head each. For positive $P,G,S$, width $e\ge0$ and $V=PGS9^e$, it takes at most

$$
             128(e+1)V \text{ transitions},\qquad
             51V \text{ distinct tape cells}.
$$

It preserves the three canonical, most-significant-bit-first binary descriptors $G,S,e$, returns all heads to their origins, leaves the data bank with the exact result, and clears every work tape. The prefix count $P$ is detected by the end of the data stream. A stated direct cutoff $E$ now has local cost $128(E+1)V$.

## Machine and counts

A transition branches on currently scanned symbols, writes at the heads and moves each by at most one. The 169-state direct machine uses 17 tapes. Each bank begins at absolute position zero on a two-sided tape. Position $-1$ is blank and temporarily holds its home marker. At exit it is blank again. The data alphabet consists of 0 and 1, both distinct from the blank. Array extents end at the first blank. The joint alphabet also contains a unary mark and a comparison cursor. Loop labels in the builder compile before loading any tape. Numerical inputs cannot change the number of heads, alphabet, states, or instruction table. The [published machine model](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/lean/ComparatorChallenges/NearSquareRootMultiplication.lean) permits these simultaneous head actions. Opening and closing the group of temporary left markers costs four transitions altogether. The [handoff recount](recount-handoff.md) accounts separately for the caller with a marked right endpoint.

For unary length $z$, a rewind from the first blank takes $z+2$ transitions. A productive run takes $2z+3$, including the terminating test. An outer unary loop makes its $z$ body invocations and takes $3z+3$ additional transitions. Converting canonical binary $z$ to unary from empty scratch costs

$$
 C(z)=11z-6\,\mathrm{popcount}(z)+12\,\mathrm{bitlength}(z)+3,\qquad
 \mathrm{bitlength}(0)=1 .
$$

One input-bit dispatch calls the elementary scaling $a\mapsto ra+b$, $b\in\{0,1\}$, here with $r=2$. This scans the old unary bank, emits $ra+b$ letters to a temporary bank, rewinds both heads, transfers back while clearing the temporary, and rewinds both again. Its cost is $(5r+1)a+5b+10$. Add one transition per bit, the final test and descriptor rewind, and sum the binary prefix values using $\sum_{i\ge1}\lfloor z/2^i\rfloor=z-\mathrm{popcount}(z)$. In particular $C(z)\le24z+15$. An exact division $z\mapsto z/3$ on unary triples takes $2z+4z/3+10$. Work regions are reused on every call.

At digit $j$ from the left, set

$$
 p=P3^j,\quad g=G3^{e-1},\quad s=S3^{e-1-j},\qquad V=9pgs .
$$

The mixed-radix stream is exactly $[p][3][g][3][s]$ at these fixed address positions, regardless of the contents delivered by earlier passes. For each of the nine values $(a,b)$ of the selected trits, copy the bits to a dedicated lane in their existing $(p,g,s)$ order. The second sweep reads the nine lanes back in the order $(p,b,g,a,s)$ while clearing them and overwriting the original bank. Each sweep takes $p(18gs+36g+10)+1$ transitions. The two sets of resets, one before writing back and one afterwards, each rewind the data bank and all nine lanes for $2V+20$ transitions. The **exact** cost of the pass is

$$
              8V+72pg+20p+42\ \le\ 23V .
$$

Here $g,s,p\ge1$ implies $V\ge9$. Lane lengths sum to $V$. Both sweeps stop at a complete prefix boundary.

For $e=0$ the program costs five transitions. For $e>0$, materialize unary $e,G,S$. Triple the latter two values $e-1$ times. The sum of their entering lengths is less than $(g+S3^{e-1})/2$. The triplings and their outer tests cost $16(G+S)3^i+22$ at iteration $i$ starting at zero. Rewind $e$. Apply one pass at each $j$. Between consecutive passes divide just the suffix length by three. The sum of entering suffix lengths is below $3S3^{e-1}/2$. Advance and test the width head twice per digit, rewind it at the end, then erase and rewind the unary banks. Their final erase costs $2(e+g+S)+9$. An exact whole clock for $e>0$ is

$$
\begin{aligned}
5+C(e)+C(G)+C(S)+1+
 \sum_{i=0}^{e-2}[16(G+S)3^i+22]+e+3
 &+\sum_{j=0}^{e-1}[8V+72(P3^j)g+20P3^j+44]\\
 &+\sum_{j=0}^{e-2}(10S3^{e-1-j}/3+10)
 +(e+2)+2(e+g+S)+9 .
\end{aligned}
$$

Empty sums vanish. Since $e,G,S,g,S3^{e-1}\le V/9$ for $e\ge1$, these terms give the stated $128(e+1)V$. Each tape visits a subset of $[-1,V]$, including descriptor scans and retired scratch maxima. Thus the total is at most $17(V+2)\le51V$. All counts permit arbitrary independently occupied spectator fibres.

## Coarse roles, leading padding, coefficient packing and comparison tags

Each row below is a separate finite rule table in the same source file. The bounds include conversion of the stated positive dimensions from canonical binary, both end-marker transitions, all run resets, final tape rewinds and work-tape erasure. Zero indices are encoded by the single binary digit 0. Two-input operations preserve their read-only banks. An output-only bank starts blank.

| Port | Actual input and result | Time | Cells ever visited |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| **build_role** | Copy or XOR source role $i<J$ at $[p][J][s]$ into role $d<K$ at $[p][K][s]$. Both banks can be independently occupied. $W=ps(J+K)$. | $103W$ | $30W$ |
| **build_padding** | From binary $W,U$, form $B=\min\{3^\ell:3^\ell\ge W\}$, $C=W\lceil B/W\rceil$. Map $[p][B][U]$ to $[p][C][U]$ with zeros in new slots, or restrict the converse array. $Z=pU(B+C)$. | $256Z$ | $27Z$ |
| **build_pack** | Interchange positional order $[p][2^k][T]\leftrightarrow[p][T][2^k]$ on the whole $V=p2^kT$ bits, given $k,T$. | $128(k+1)V$ | $27V$ |
| **build_tag** | For each width-$H$ record of $[p][t][g][t][s]$, append two bits coding the comparison of the $t$-indices. $V=pt^2gsH$. Accept either trit sign. | $512V$ | $44V$ |

**Role transfer.** Unary $i,J-i-1,d,K-d-1$ describe the four skipped sections. Compute each complement by walking $i$ cells on the source extent, skipping the selected cell, appending the remaining unary marks and rewinding. Within a prefix, scan the two leading skips, copy/XOR the entire selected run in one aligned pass, and then scan the two trailing skips. Repeat until the source ends. The independently reconstructed exact clock is

$$
 \sum_{x\in(J,K,i,d,s)} C(x)
 +p\{s[3(J+K)-2]+6(J+K)+4\}
 +7(J+K)+2s+38 .
$$

The exact clock and its $103W$ bound appear in the [independent recount](recount-node.md). This operation covers the prefix-major selection in [AQ-P](foundations/aq-p.md), including empty skip sections, independent source/destination payloads and $p>1$.

**Leading padding.** Grow a unary power of three from 1 until its length reaches $W$, comparing lengths by a simultaneous scan and rewinding. Each unsuccessful comparison at $z$ costs at most $4z+8$ and its tripling $16z+10$. The sum of entering $z$ is less than $B/2$. The final comparison uses at most $4W+5$. To obtain the padding, advance the unary $W$ head cyclically while scanning $B$ marks. Rewind a full $W$ only when another mark of $B$ remains. At the end the $W$ head sits either on its blank, for no padding, or at the beginning of the missing suffix. Copy that suffix to its own unary bank. This construction costs at most $8B+4W+10$. Per outer prefix the transfer traverses $B$ real and $C-B$ padding blocks. The productive inner run costs $2U+3$ and its containing loop at most three additional transitions per block plus three for a completed loop. The two outer data resets cost $Z+4$. Binary setup costs $C(W)+C(U)$, and final cleanup at most $2(W+U+B+C-B)+12$. Combining these charges proves the table, including $C=B$ and positive $p$. This gives the [PB](foundations/packed-batch-with-small-radix.md) pad before the main word and restrict after its completed identity. The caller supplies the batch width and the physical suffix length.

**Coefficient packing.** Move one binary coordinate digit across $T$ address positions while retaining $d$ trailing coordinate bits as a block, $z=Td$. Gather $z$ bits at a time alternately to two tapes. Merge their $d$-bit runs alternately to the canonical data bank. The inverse interchanges the two run lengths. For raw volume $V$ the exact per-digit cost including both sets of three bank rewinds is

$$
 8V+7V/(2z)+7V/(2d)+14\ \le\ 22V.
$$

The two lanes have identical prefix order. Taking $d=1,2,\ldots,2^{k-1}$ preserves the previously moved trailing coordinates and gives [FP](foundations/full-rate-field-packing.md). Initialize unary $d=1,z=T$ and obtain new lengths by doubling. For the inverse, grow to the last lengths then divide by two while returning. Complete entering $d$ and $z$ sums in each sweep are at most $V$ each. A doubling costs $11z+10$ and an exact halving $4z+10$. Together with $C(T)+C(k)$ and the final erasures this gives the displayed bound. For $k=0$, only the five identity transitions execute.

**Physical spectral key.** Retain a length-$t$ unary template with exactly one marked position at the current earlier coordinate $x$. Each sweep over the later coordinate $y$ begins in the $y<x$ state, sees the marker at equality, and then uses the $y>x$ state. Copy $H$ bits and append the two respective bits for each of the $s$ records. Repeat the sweep for all $g$ intervening fibres. Advance the mark by one position with a scan and rewind. After the last $x$, put it back at zero before the next prefix. The output trits are 2 before the mark, 0 at the mark and 1 after it for the forward physical order in [SR](foundations/compact-spectral-repair-executor.md). The inverse negates these values modulo three. If $Q=pt^2gs$, binary initialization costs at most $96V+60$. Each individual record incurs $2H+3$ copied-bit steps, two tag writes and at most three additional suffix-loop steps. Each $y$ costs at most ten additional loop/rewind steps, each intervening fibre at most six, and each $x$ at most $2t+10$ to locate, advance and restore its threshold (including wrap and prefix tests). The total of these costs is at most $64V$. Further resets, cleanup and boundary markers cost at most $16V+32$, giving the table also at $t=s=H=1$. Only the output can exceed $V$ cells in extent, and its data length is at most $3V$. The other ten tapes each have extent at most $V$; the 22 boundary cells give at most $44V$ distinct cells. On the $j$th tag pass $H=h_K+2(j-1)$. Selecting a single role among $32B$ before growing tags keeps even the largest tagged array below its raw repair parent volume, since $B\ge2m+1$ and $h_K\ge2$.
