# Raw product for the bounded initial range

The input consists of two equal positive-length, most-significant-first strings separated by one symbol on a single tape. The output is otherwise blank and its positions starting at zero hold the full $2n$ bits. These are the [original conventions](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/3344da59991ed94f9ba711217b29c1d760badfa6/lean/OAI/Computability/NearSquareRoot/Specification.lean). [schoolbook.py](../checks/schoolbook.py) generates the 19-state five-tape fallback using the fixed transition interpreter in [node-tapes.py](../checks/node-tapes.py).

Copy the operands to two delimited banks. For each input digit write one zero on the accumulator, allocating exactly $2n$ digits. Its marked digit initially denotes offset zero. At multiplier bit $j$, if the bit is one, add the multiplicand starting at the mark and propagate its carry. The new accumulator contains the multiplicand times the processed multiplier digits with their correct binary weights. A partial product is at most $(2^n-1)^2<2^{2n}$; carry cannot leave the bank. Restore the multiplicand head to the low bit and the accumulator head to the mark. Advance the mark right one place. Finally read the accumulator backwards and write only raw bits at output positions 0 through $2n-1$.

The intervals give at most $8n+9$ visited cells, including all five initial head positions. One iteration reads the multiplier, takes at most $2n+1$ bit-add transitions, propagates through at most $2n$ carry positions, seeks the mark in at most $2n+1$, restores the multiplicand in $n+2$ and shifts the mark in two. Copying, initialization and readout use at most $10(n+1)$ additional steps. Thus $64(n+1)^2$ bounds the raw machine. Exhaustive inputs through five bits and 50 extra pairs up to 128 bits gave 1,414 exact products.

In the final program the first length test is $n\le2^{48}$. Count the raw first word by a little-endian counter, restore the input head and compare its binary value with an ordinary 49-bit constant. Over $n$ increments at most $2n+1$ digits toggle and the return tours take at most the same number of steps. Boundary handling, comparison and restoring the input head cost at most $256(n+1)+512$ steps. The direct branch including dispatch costs at most $4096n^2$ transitions for every positive length in its range. Fewer than 32 test tapes each visit at most $n+50$ cells. The fewer than 8192 dormant fast heads at their origins also fit the common memory bound. The [final dispatcher](../CONSTRUCTION.md) accounts for the preparatory work on longer inputs.
