# A numerical coefficient bound for the finite repair

This evaluates the exact on-demand matrix in [SR3–SR5](foundations/compact-spectral-repair-executor.md) on the [short-word tape controller](compiler-controller.md). The bounded domain is `m>=4096`, `B=3^r` the least power with `(B−1)/2>=m`, `s0=floor(B/32)`, and `j=|J|<=s0`. The prior pairwise-independent concentration proof supplies the last inequality. The field is `K=F4[X]/f` with `e` the least power of two at least `ceil(log_4 B)`. An entry of the genuine finite repair, including the exception set from the actual signed comparison key, takes at most **`m^44` physical transitions per record** after setup. Setup takes at most **`m^48` transitions**. The entire controller uses `m^34` visited cells, alongside the caller's current tagged role. All record costs are per invocation; no frequency space of size `3^m` is built.

## Field arithmetic and shift data

`2m+1<=B<6m+3<=7m` and `Q=4^e<=16 B^2<=784m^2<=m^3`. Store an element as the base-four string of its coefficients packed into one `b`-bit controller word. Polynomial inputs, the monic leading bit and a product through degree `2e` fit in `2 log2 Q+3<=b` bits. Coefficientwise addition is XOR. For base F4 multiply the low two-bit polynomial representatives modulo `Z^2+Z+1`, using four terms or a fixed sixteen-entry finite control. To multiply extension elements form their convolution in `e^2` pairs, XORing the two-bit coefficient into the packed word by masks and shifts. Descending by degree from `2e−2` to `e`, eliminate the leading coefficient with one scaled shifted copy of the monic modulus. This takes at most `128(e+1)^2` word instructions, including addressing, loop control and clearing. Polynomial remainder modulo a smaller monic trial divisor is the same division loop of at most `(e+1)^2` coefficient visits.

Enumerate all `Q` monic degree-`e` candidates in coordinate order. For each, enumerate every monic divisor through `floor(e/2)`: there are at most `2 sqrt(Q)` candidates total in those degrees. Select the first with no divisor. Exactly the reducible polynomials have such a divisor. Existence of a degree-`e` irreducible is proved in [the field constructor](foundations/power-two-degree-predictor.md). Fill the entire pair table `mult[xQ+y]` by the above convolution and reduction for every `x,y<Q`. If the product is one, store the inverse of `x`. This includes all nonzero inverses without exponentiation; inverse zero is never read. Include `H_[c,t]=alpha_t^c` by repeated multiplication down each column, with `alpha_t=t` the actual distinct node words in the canonical field encoding. One field product now takes at most five word instructions, counting the indexed lookup; an inverse takes at most two. One fully initialized `Q^2` table occupies at most `m^6` slots.

For the shift table enumerate the `B` ternary vectors, insert the `r` unit vectors first and then lexicographic normalized nonunit representatives whose first nonzero coordinate is one, stopping at `m` vectors. This implements the required basis-containing nonproportional choice. Compute each `beta_j(t)` by a length-`r` dot product modulo three, recovering trits by repeated `divmod 3` or shifts through a generated trit table. The latter costs at most `64B(r+1)` instructions and stores at most `(r+1)B` trits. Scanning trial roots `1,2,...` through `m` and successive dyadic powers fixes the prescribed window, the bit count, and the rounded field degree in fewer than `64(m+1)^2` further instructions. The complete dot-product table, row sums, and phase constants use at most `128 m B (r+1)` additional instructions. These loops, the modulus search, the field tables, and their initialization need at most `m^10` word instructions: use `Q<=m^3`, `e+1<=m`, `Q*2 sqrt(Q)<=2m^(9/2)`, and bound field loops by `128(e+1)^2`. All retained arrays fit `M=m^32` words. The physical setup bound follows from the word-machine theorem.

## A single inverse-minor entry from its interpolation polynomials

Given the `m` trits `k_j` for the actual address and its query direction, compute `z=omega^(sum_j k_j)`. For every shift index `t<B`, compute `c_t=sum_j ((k_j+beta_j(t)) mod 3)`, append `t` to `J` exactly when `c_t` is outside the prescribed interval `[u,u+D]`, and compute `g_t=omega^(u−sum_j beta_j(t)+((c_t−u) mod (D+1)))−z` there. Signed residues use the unique nonnegative remainder, including when `c_t<u`. Exponents of `omega` reduce modulo three. A signed intermediate here is between `−4m` and `4m+2`; add a known multiple of the modulus before unsigned remainder as needed. Forming the complete exceptional list costs at most `128B(m+1)` word instructions, including key reads. Some `g_t` in this containing list may be zero.

For ordered positions `(a,b)` in the check matrix, return zero if `a>=j` or `b>=j`. Otherwise, for each `s in J` let

    P_s(Z) = product_{k in J, k != s}(Z+alpha_k)
    D_s = product_{k in J, k != s}(alpha_s+alpha_k).

`D_s` is nonzero. The coefficient vector of `P_s/D_s` evaluates to `1` at `alpha_s` and to `0` at every other `J` node. For the actual Vandermonde matrix `V_[p,s]=alpha_s^p`, row `s` of `V^{-1}` is the coefficient vector of this polynomial. Hence the required entry is exactly

    M_[a,b] = sum_{s in J} g_s^2 [Z^a]P_s [Z^b]P_s / D_s^2.

The formula includes `j=0`, zero entries of `g`, and independent inverse requests. For each `s`, initialize a coefficient array with constant one. For every `k!=s`, update in decreasing degree `p` by `Cnew[p] = Cold[p−1] + alpha_k Cold[p]`, taking absent coefficients zero, and update `D_s`. Multiply, add the displayed summand using the inverse lookup, and reuse the array for the next `s`. There are at most `j(j−1)j` inner coefficient updates, each needing at most 32 word instructions including indexed input/output, a field product, XOR, and loop control. Allow `128(B+1)^3` operations including clearing arrays, denominators, readout and other controls. Adding exception generation, loading the small key and input scalar, multiplying/accumulating the result at that same record and returning `z` times the second family is below `m^6` word instructions. This gives `m^44` transitions including the input/output record boundary. A literal key of `2m` bits can be read into short trit words by at most `8m` stream operations in addition. No matrix of independent module values is transposed here.

## Repeats at the parent

There are exactly `32B` coarse input roles per parent block, including the 32 independent duplication indices. Padded one-family checks have `B` coarse roles. An input/output row pair of matching duplicate type is a single aligned entire-prefix/entire-module traversal after grouping. Using a wasteful loop over **all** pairs bounds the count as well. One check, transpose lift, signed `U−zV0` combine and two output families can be implemented with at most `64(32B+1)^2` pair passes: each of the four width-changing linear maps needs at most `(32B+1)^2` pairs, both families and their additions a further factor below 16. An absent/padded contribution is zero. Role tagging happens only inside these passes at the SR position between the two completed converters. Tags and the per-record scratch are cleared before spatial or finite converter calls. The full bank/grouping/physical trit generation scans remain the data mover's separately counted responsibility.

The general result is a written tape-count lemma for the actual insertion point in the algorithm.
