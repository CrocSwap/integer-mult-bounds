# A numerical clock for an adaptive ternary interchange node

Use the native operation of [AQ-P](foundations/aq-p.md), with two width-$e$ ternary address fields and independently occupied, positive prefix, gap and suffix ranges. The data bank contains exactly $V=PGS9^e$ bits. The shape counts and $e$ are canonical binary words. Fix $R=240$, $E=2^{R(2^{17}+2)}$. At $e<E$ use the direct digit machine. Otherwise let $m$ be the greatest odd integer with $m^R\le e$ and follow S003's adaptive program. The following bounds cover **the node's own transitions**, including issuing and accepting every child. Each child's internal execution is charged separately.

| Domain | Maximum local transitions |
|---|---:|
| $0\le e<E$, empty child list | $256(e+1)V$ |
| $e\ge E$, actual grouped children and full reserves | $2m^{120}V$ by the [refined local count](node-root-parameter.md) |

The program uses one absolute number of finite-alphabet one-head tapes. A raw port preserves its source unless specified as in-place. All ordinary operations finish at canonical bank boundaries before an adaptive call. The result is the mathematical interchange $I\otimes Q$ of [AI](foundations/adaptive-interchange.md). The [child-mass ledger](child-volume.md) prices the *volumes and widths* of repeated children. The unweighted counts below pay only for this caller's own data and control work.

## Big shape words

Small label, formula and scalar indices use the [finite controller](compiler-controller.md). A second fixed set of descriptor tapes handles the unbounded physical prefix, gap, suffix, module width and field extents. This work is additive per shape or complete pass. It is never run once per bit of a stream.

Set $u=1+\lceil\log_2(2V+1)\rceil$. On a nonbase request use binary word width

$$
 b_{\rm long}=64(16+e+|P|+|G|+|S|),
$$

where $|z|$ is canonical bit length. Write that many unary marks by converting $e$ to unary once, walking each of the three binary strings once and emitting 64 marks per counted cell, followed by the constant prefix. This costs at most $2^{12}(b_{\rm long}+1)$ transitions including source and work-head resets. The clock and register terminators are reusable, and $b_{\rm long}+2\le2^{11}u$. Products for grouped shapes do not exceed $4V$. Allocate a double-width temporary for products before comparison or truncation. Computing $3^f$ by left-to-right binary exponentiation never exceeds its final extent when using square-and-conditional-multiply by 3. The same construction gives $3^e$ and powers for contiguous grade coordinates. In the integer $R$th root a product may be capped at $e+1$ as soon as it exceeds $e$.

Use up to 32 double-width registers on separate tapes, low-order bit first, and one memory tape with $m^{16}$ slots of $b_{\rm long}$ bits plus one delimiter each. Locate a slot by copying its index to a short binary counter and skipping that many complete delimited words, testing/decrementing the short counter once per word. Read or overwrite one full slot and return to the origin. This takes at most $64(m^{16}+1)(b_{\rm long}+b+3)$ single transitions apart from preparing the index, where $b=128\,\mathrm{bitlength}(m)$. Preparing a bounded index with up to 32 small-controller operations costs at most $32m^{37}$, including general indexed memory. A long register operation uses at most $2^{10}(b_{\rm long}+2)^3$ transitions. For a product take at most $b_{\rm long}$ rounds, inspect one multiplier bit, add if requested and shift on the double-length accumulator. Restoring heads and locating each new bit by a fresh sweep costs at most $64(2b_{\rm long}+4)^2$ per round. Long division uses the same rounds, shifts, compares and conditionally subtracts the partial remainder, and emits one quotient bit. Copy, compare, shifts and row-end counters cost less. This allowance also covers reversing a canonical binary input into a marked little-endian word and emitting a most-significant-bit-first word at a physical port. For zero, emit the one digit 0. Seek the high nonzero bit once before emitting a nonzero word.

A layout is an ordered list of positive extents. The native list has $P,G,S$, at most $2(m+2a)$ ternary width-$f$ factors, the two leading and two remainder pieces, and the separated packed batch. Inside a finite call the list includes the coarse roles, module grades and binary scalar coordinate. An endpoint operation changes data through the coordinate map and keeps the mixed-radix extents of the selected slots. A subset, rank or finite check array replaces only its stated coarse role dimensions, as in [UP](foundations/uniform-payload-shared-pencil.md) and [SR](foundations/compact-spectral-repair-executor.md). The module has at most $m^8$ grade factors and $\log_2 w\le m^9$ by [the formula construction](compiler-factor.md). Save each extent as a finite index, a power-of-two width, a binary count or one of the stated ternary powers. No catalogue has an entry per module row or physical position. Fewer than $m^{10}$ extents suffice for each stream. Even 128 separate local descriptions fit $m^{12}$ slots. The $m^{16}$ allocation also holds cursors, work specifications and newly generated shapes.

For a chosen position or pair, multiply its preceding, intervening and trailing factors in their current order, using three running products. Include the coordinate and record-width factors precisely when counting bits. For an address comparison distinguish suffix *records* from the separate record-width word. A child gets the full product of the factors before its first field, between its fields, and after its second field, plus $f$. Clear unused slots on release. A slot identified by active coordinate name can be found by one scan, or use the [emitted permutation](compiler-geometry.md) on the finitely many named geometric slots. No shape operation arbitrarily permutes the module grades. Its only internal movement is a single named cyclic grade shift with all other grades kept in order. Allow $m^{12}(b_{\rm long}+2)^2$ long or short-indexed operations per full shape delivery: fewer than $m^{10}$ factors, up to $8(b_{\rm long}+2)$ register steps per binary powering, three products, two copies, and the bounded number of shape changes for the individual port. For the canonical small-field grouping, inner and outer products can be recomputed independently for each delivered role pair. Produce one physical layout at a time and reuse the same scratch.

A combined wide/indexed operation with register arithmetic, one scan of long slots and 32 small-controller preparations costs at most $m^{39}(b_{\rm long}+2)^3$. This allowance covers initializing a fresh long-slot tape by sweeping $m^{16}$ slots with a short countdown and writing their markers and cells. At a nonbase, $m\ge2^{2^{17}}$, $3^{2e}\le V$, so $\smash{(b_{\rm long}+2)^5}\le(2^{11}u)^5\le V$. For example $55+5\log_2 u\le u-4$ for $u\ge1024$ and $V\ge2^{u-4}$. Therefore a complete shape delivery costs at most $m^{52}V$ transitions. The small index controller touched per scalar uses its separate count. No factor of $b_{\rm long}$ is paid per scalar.

## Node dispatcher and data-bearing calls

1. From the first digit of $e$, compare the length of its canonical word with $R(2^{17}+2)$. The adaptive branch is exactly $|e|>R(2^{17}+2)$, so $e=E$ enters it. A finite-state chain of this fixed length may count until the threshold. Return to the descriptor origin. This requires at most $2|e|+3$ transitions. If $e<E$, open the marked port, run the 17-tape [direct fallback](node-streams.md), and close the port. Opening/closing the data costs at most $4V+7$ by the [adapter count](recount-node.md). Marker translation on the three used parameters costs at most $4(|G|+|S|+|e|)+24$. Preserve $P$ at home. These charges and the fallback give $256(e+1)V$ even at $e=0$, including a call/return jump.
2. At $e\ge E$ find $m$ by binary search between $1$ and $e$. Compare a tested power with $e$ by $R$ successive capped long multiplications and decrease the last root by one if even. This loop uses only fixed long registers before $m$ is known. There are at most $R(|e|+2)$ multiplications, and $R=240\le m$. Compute $u_0=\lceil\log_2m\rceil$, $v=\lceil\log_2(u_0+2)\rceil$, the greatest dyadic $d$ with $d^3v\le u_0$, and least $t$ with $2^{td^2}\ge2^{2d^2-2}m$. For odd $m>1$, $u_0$ is its bit length. Repeated doubling, small products and integer ceiling division implement these tests. Form $N,D_g,a$, repair depth and shift parameters by the [child schedule](child-volume.md). Generate the finite directories and scalar field. Read the grade-width sum $z$, write $w=2^z$ in binary as a one and $z$ zeros, and multiply the batch factors $h_K16^{h_r}N32B w$. Grow a power of three until it reaches the result, counting $\ell$ triplings. Calculate $f=\lfloor(e-\ell)/(m+2a)\rfloor$ and its remainder by long division. The [rounded guard](child-volume.md) gives $f\ge1$ and $\ell<e$. Its proof also admits the weaker $\ell\le m^{10}$ at $R=240$. The $m^{10}$ batch triplings and $R(|e|+2)$ root multiplications fit $m^{54}V$ along with long-word preparation. Finite-table construction costs at most $3m^{60}$ separately.
3. Deliver the trailing extent after the earlier leading range. Pad to a complete multiple of the binary batch with **build_padding**. This order exposes batch and row. Factor the row as $(16^{h_r},N,B,32,w,h_K)$ and move the $h_K$ coordinate into its final position with **build_pack**. All original bits are independently populated. The initial padding is below $2V$. Perform the three prescribed order-three transforms and diagonals in the reverse textual order of $F\Delta F^{-1}\Delta^{-1}F\Delta$ in [O3-4](foundations/characteristic-two-order-three-interchange.md). Each direct and inverse finite converter orders its two $F$ calls and pointwise diagonals as in O3-3. Produce the spectral key only between the completed converters.
4. At each finite internal transform apply its two predictors, checks, four calls at one-sixteenth of its row volume, table correction and lifts in the order of [SR3–SR5](foundations/compact-spectral-repair-executor.md). At a leaf apply each $C_j$ or $C_j^{-1}$ to its rows. These are one copy of the spatial swap plus the lower or upper comparison of the *old* input, in characteristic two. Keep the old bank until addition finishes. Execute the [two-pass pencil](foundations/uniform-payload-shared-pencil.md): prepare one rectangle at a time, accumulate each negative delivery and its **unscaled** rank gathers over queries, undo prepared negative destinations, apply the selected inverse minor and coefficient at rank indices, then deliver the positive terms and undo their destinations. Iterate supporting indices for recursive exchanges. Rejected candidate incidences may take ordinary control time. They issue no child. Give each [emitted elementary operation](compiler-geometry.md) its named pair and physical shape. Use the counted [fixed-tape address implementation](address-dyadic.md) for a lower shear or a diagonal. An upper shear issues its stated recursive swap, lower shear on the exchanged slots, then swap back. A straight field exchange issues its one child.
5. For a contracted call collect all independent rows sharing its selected endpoint or rank into the neutral bank. A direct leaf issues its whole array as a call. Deliver the real prefix/gap/suffix products and $f$. Park parent survivors and descriptors, transfer neutral to active and issue the call. On return move active to neutral first, restore the parent, and scatter/accumulate the result at its original prefix-major positions. The [contracted wrapper](recount-handoff.md) costs $\sum(4u_i+8-2d_i)+4g+9$ for prepared bank lengths $u_i$, displacements $d_i$ and child length $g$. Arguments may depend on earlier returns. At the end unpack coordinates, then unpad after the rowwise identity has completed. Release all main payload except the sole canonical result. The $2a$ reserve swaps take that common bank in succession. Each [full wrapper](recount-handoff.md) parks only control. Finally exchange the $\ell$ leading and fewer than $m+2a$ leftover trit pairs. For each pair prepare its gap/suffix and use the single 23-volume digit pass. Rewinds and clears apply to the current extent.

These loops issue exactly the charged spatial calls. The argument and return shapes are those of the [physical correctness proof](foundations/aq-p.md). Every value crossing a child boundary is the parent's actual independent binary coordinate data in its selected group.

## Counting the ordinary data work

The [event inventory](compiler-events.md) counts fewer than $m^{38}$ ordinary grouped events across the three finite trees, including endpoint repetitions, rank additions, coefficient slices and spectral repair. It bounds separately by $m^{88}V$ the finite small-word controller work and the actual scalar coefficients at all visited record positions. Record supplies below complete the loop boundaries for that count.

- **Subsets and roles.** Take a common arbitrary prefix, a coarse role ID, and its entire positive suffix. A small arbitrary subset, finite regrouping or rank-coarse map is allowed up to *all* ordered source/destination role pairs, using **build_role**. Before the $w$ module, complete coarse counts are at most $16m\cdot m^5\cdot7m\cdot32<m^8$ on the active domain. The combined $mr$ rank or query coordinates have $mr\le N$, so do not enlarge this upper bound. Local split/join repair blocks have at most 16 extra roles and at most two check families; use the looser $m^9$ on each endpoint. Group an arbitrary prefix across these roles in one call for the *same* geometric map. A temporary deposit or extraction into an entire subset can always use at most $m^{18}$ ordinary role-pair transfers. Grouped recursive calls remain outside this ordinary-copy loop. It writes the complete neutral request before the one child is issued. Empty subsets deposit explicit zeros when required and issue no spurious child.
- **Coefficient rows.** Before each mask, initialize an odometer on the actual grade radices, all in the small-word controller. A unary suffix clock marks each complete physical row; advance or wrap the odometer only after such a row. It takes at most $64(S_{\rm form}+1)$ small operations to update once and $m^{48}$ transitions per mask/field scalar as in [the factor compiler](compiler-factor.md). Read the contiguous $h_K$ bits into its short scalar word and return exactly $h_K$ bits. Moving a short word to/from streamed cells and returning its register head costs fewer than $8(b+2)^2$ transitions per scalar even by repeated seeks. Tag or de-tag at a fixed row width. A field addition is coordinatewise XOR. For a masked token perform its grade rotations only *after* multiplying by its scalar, using up to $m$ role copies into a fresh bank per single grade. Add the resulting stream into the accumulator and recycle the bank. An entire grade tuple is never read from a physical-address counter.
- **Physical comparisons.** Select the current coarse role first. Apply **build_tag** for each required pair; at pass $j$ its records have width $h_K+2(j-1)$ including earlier trits. The old and new tagged arrays occupy at most one repair-parent raw volume each, since the role is one of $32B$, $B\ge2m+1$, $h_K\ge2$. The same computation can select a single original-layout projection, or a pointwise diagonal by multiplying the short field element and deleting that pass's tag. For the spectral table, copy a single source role to a stream before forming its key, retain only the one current stream and the destination accumulator, and strip tags before any converter. The algorithm for the true exception set and interpolated entry charges $m^{44}$ transitions per scalar in [the repair controller](compiler-spectral.md). Reading a width-$2m$ key one trit at a time and reading/writing one $h_K$-bit coordinate word is within the per-record controller allowance. These computations do not alter the child word or its width.
- **Physical mask or associated output at different role counts.** Use the source selection into a separate one-role stream with **build_role**, perform the scalar operation in the aligned one-role layout, then deposit or XOR the one-role result by a second **build_role**. A unary $h_K$ clock scans a scalar word; a unary physical-suffix clock ends a grade row. Each may be constructed once for a pass at the shape charge above. Resetting the former costs at most $h_K+2$ per record. Reset the latter only when its entire suffix of records has elapsed. Arbitrary outer prefixes and occupied destination slots are preserved.
- **Other stream operations.** A full-bank copy, clear, zero-fill, join or field-coordinate addition is a forward tour and rewind. It costs at most $16(L+2)$ if its marked extent and the extent to be initialized total $L$. Counts such as $P$ or $\Omega$ are delivered once in binary or by a generated unary range for a pass. Open/close a raw stream against the marked-port convention with at most $4L+7$ transitions per data or descriptor bank. The signed geometric map costs at most $2^{15}(1+\lceil\log_2m\rceil)$ per input bit per emitted operation (including its up-to-two signed diagonal components), plus separately counted recursive service when required. The $2a$ reserve and at most $\ell+m+2a$ leftover/leading trit moves outside the finite body introduce fewer than $m^{12}$ further events including their copies and child issue/reply.

For a uniform tally, permit $m^{20}$ primitive transfers and full shape deliveries for **one** of the $m^{38}+m^{12}$ source events. This covers all $m^{18}$ candidate coarse pairs at both extraction and deposition even when the event inventory already split them. It covers finite-source selection, grade/suffix streaming, clearing, geometry-list instructions, raw/marked adapters and return normalization. A whole shape costs $m^{52}V$. Each delivered stream has length at most $4V$. The bound $m^{54}V$ per delivered transfer pays for its individual address/role/scan cost, both returned argument-shape words, and the handoff of the participating banks. The finite scalar-controller total $m^{88}V$ can be added separately. Repeating construction of the short tables on every return, if used instead of keeping them, costs at most another $3m^{60}$ per return. Even charging this last cost for *each* expanded transfer and using $m^{62}V$ in place of $m^{54}V$ gives

$$
  m^{88}V+3m^{60}+m^{54}V+(m^{38}+m^{12})\,m^{20}m^{62}V < m^{123}V .
$$

The stated $m^{256}V$ leaves room for the finite descriptor stack/control issuances and the fixed wrappers detailed below. In particular its degree was fixed before choosing the width root degree. It is a deliberately loose certified upper bound on this one implementation.

## Arena, stack and visited cells

A fixed assignment with 128 ordinary payload banks suffices. During one native frame reserve eight for the input/result, padding, canonical and neutral bank. During one finite repair reserve 16 more for the input, two predictor values, the two check families, their output, transpose lifts, two correction values and pointwise/sum scratch; separately suspended finite ancestors live on an end-access stack. One pencil uses at most 16 more for input, output, both complete rank families, the current rectangle's source and destination subsets, the unscaled rectangle sum, its scaled copy, a singleton, a rank intermediate and their scatter/copy banks. Only one rectangle, one scalar token or one geometry list runs at once. Reserve 32 more banks for tagged or action-specific outputs, rotation, the nine trit lanes, two packing lanes, affine-operation buffers, unary run clocks and transfer aligns. The implemented ports may instead use disjoint private heads; since there are finitely many ports, dedicate up to 128 private ordinary *work* heads separately. A suspended frame parks only its live data words at marked boundaries, never its cleared port-private scratch. The coefficient tables, odometer and remaining long controls have their own tapes.

Every live ordinary payload bank has at most $4U$ bits at a finite node of incoming volume $U$. An internal finite phase at $U/16$ keeps its finite parent's survivors on a stack, with no active pencil or port across that phase. All 128 possible banks can therefore be charged at $512U$ bits at the stay-behind stage. Along one main repair path their total is at most $512(16/15)\beta V$, where $\beta<2$. At a contracted spatial issue, its current finite ancestors may already be on this same stack; leave their frames where they are and append the active parent's current survivors. Include at most one additional native $512V$ allocation for the root-frame banks parked outside the padded finite word. Thus use $2^{12}V$ retained payload/frame marks at any single contracted spatial parent, with room for the $128$ delimiters at each finite level, since each nonempty level has $U\ge1$. Finite and spatial stack heads always push and pop at their current ends. A full reserve has cleared its entire finite tree, releases all other parent data, and parks zero payload. The geometric contracted-spatial sum is at most $2V_{\rm root}$ along any path, regardless of intervening full reserves. The stack therefore has a fixed absolute bound on the union of cells it ever visits. If a path stops inside a finite tree, the last live main word adds at most $2^{12}V_{\rm root}$ to the same allowance.

The large finite-state threshold chain runs only on entry and issues no child. A suspended frame saves a bounded call-site mode (direct finite leaf, endpoint exchange, finite correction phase or full reserve), along with the ordinary current short/long loop indices and explicit return phase. On resumption a common dispatcher reads this representation. No full finite-state program counter is encoded as a single tape letter. When the affine port expects an empty private bank with a left marker followed by a blank, erase the usual right marker before its call and restore it afterwards. Taking its head to the adjacent cell and back each time costs four transitions per such bank. These fixed conversions are included in the raw/marked adapter allowance.

Finite controller/metadata can also be saved as marked canonical binary words. For the finite short machine its $m^{34}$ cells may be encoded by a fixed 8-bit byte per used symbol. The $m^{16}$ long slots, 32 register banks and 128 layout descriptors use at most $2^{12}m^{34}(b_{\rm long}+2)^2$ binary control cells per spatial or finite frame, including end markers, fixed loop states, short indices and the stack tag. One can discard and regenerate all finite tables at a return as counted above. Allowing the larger cost of parking them, each *individual* nonbase frame is at most $V$ bits on the active domain. In fact $m^R\le e\le u$ and $b_{\rm long}+2\le2^{11}u$, so its allocation is at most $2^{34}u^3$, far below $V$. There are at most $e_{\rm root}+1\le u+1$ spatial ancestors because widths strictly decrease and at most $h_r+1\le u+1$ finite ancestors per spatial level. Charging all frames by the root $u$ bounds cumulative control high-water by $2^{36}(u+1)^5\le V_{\rm root}$ on a nonbase root ($u\ge2^{17}$). Control frames are not copied repeatedly onto fresh tapes for sibling calls. A base-only root uses the direct clock and bank bound, including raw/marked port adapters.

In particular the transfer sum at a call has at most $1024V$ active parent payload bits, at most $V$ control bits, at most 1024 participating banks and $g\le2V$. Serializing the fixed control alphabet as 8-bit strings and reversing the encoding is at most $32$ steps per encoded cell and bank boundary by forward/return tours. Valid empty-slot scans were included in the $m^{34}$ control allocation. Together with the handoff formula and raw adapters, $2^{18}V$ transitions pays a complete issue/return outside the child's own service, and the $2a$ full calls each pay only their actual control and transfers. Finite calls use the same local ownership protocol on their shrinking arrays. This charge is below the per-transfer allowance above. Each of the at most 1024 fixed ordinary/control private heads visits one interval of length at most $8V_{\rm root}+4$ over the whole run; the single large control memory also fits there by the explicit nonbase guard. The two end stacks together take less than $3\cdot2^{12}V_{\rm root}+2V_{\rm root}$ including origins. Allowing the original data descriptors and root input copy, **$2^{18}V_{\rm root}$ distinct cells** bounds the entire native traversal. The base-only root satisfies the same bound. This counts ever-visited cells, including erased banks and positions occupied by intermediate returns.
