# Construction note: signed shared-donor lifting

## Contribution and provenance

This package constructs a new finite, source-bound bit supplier by discovering larger signed response dependencies among cold helpers sharing an admissible cap. The final selected integer lift has 171 pivots and 283 distinct donors across 48 role-disjoint cohorts. Its 560 nonzero signed terms include 16 terms of magnitude two. Every term is implemented as literal unit shears: coefficient +2 is two +1 ADDs, and coefficient -2 is two -1 ADDs. The 576 setup ADDs and corresponding restore ADDs are charged explicitly; their repeated same-frame use creates no additional child movement.

The underlying kernel setup/restore algebra and compiler frame interface are inherited from prior public kernel engines. Terminal-sink algebra, early cache interfaces, and changed-source correction algebra are likewise prior mechanisms. This contribution is the new signed/cohort witness extraction, bounded integer lift, and its fully source-compatible physical composition; it is not claimed to invent all those interfaces or to prove a universal warm-donor compression theorem.

The important finite extension over unit-only/parity synthesis is real: sixteen selected even terms are invisible to F2 term-omission checks, while exact integer target-response checks detect their semantic contribution. The independent signed equation audit checks all 164160 selected input-target entries and checks omission/sign reversal of every nonzero signed term.

## Sufficient mathematics

A selected pivot p and its donors d_j are cold at their common setup cut: their clean-source values are zero; each has completed its initial zero-frame target READs; none has undergone its first non-READ use. A cap E is contained in all first-use frames. D_p denotes the removed READ contribution transported through the actual suffix target shears, not an untransported raw sum. T_d is a donor's complete signed target response to its initial dirty value. The checked equation is

    D_p = sum_j alpha_j (T_dj - D_dj).

Install p initially at E, remove its zero-frame READs, and insert d_j += alpha_j*p at E after all participating READs. The extra target response cancels precisely the deleted column. At the final full frame insert d_j -= alpha_j*p. General integer alpha is expanded into abs(alpha) unit ADDs in both places. The scalar proof is linear and does not assume a projected read or a free oracle.

For these selected finite families, every donor has T_d=0. Thus the delivered relation is also an exact signed READ-kernel relation. Transported suffix-response extraction is the correct general framework, but this successful witness does not establish a novel warm-donor family with nonzero full donor response.

The donor's first jump is split into rank(E) and its remaining difference; a pivot loses rank(E) from its first jump. Donor splitting is charged once per shared entrance. Rank mass alone is not the objective: the exact paid histogram, fixed-stock toll, residual work, and bank invoice must be composed before pricing. The final price is not a sum of stand-alone gains.
