# H6 compensation network extension

The verified 2×2 word has been extended to a complete compressed compensation
network on all 30,960 ports. Its ordinary helpers have legal monotone frames,
both orientations restore every arbitrary dirt column, and its K interface
is an explicit 816-center word. The address-bucket construction reduces
compensation helpers from 480,337,920 to 227,312,640. Its complete nominal
invoice still fails the pinned selected target by a large margin.

This is a finished finite construction/accounting contribution toward a
supplier. It does not admit a new multiplication theorem or κ. The full
multi-billion-event word is defined by its schema and proved phasewise; it
has not been materialized and executed. Emitted finite words, full factor
binding, complete incidence counts and the stated physical recipe family
have separate reproducible checks.

## Complete legal chronology

Write `A=I-K`, `P_s=span(p_s)` and `Q_t=p_t^⊥`, using the actual 17-bit
odd-norm port labels. Every nonzero entry has odd real pairing±1 or±3,
hence `p_s·p_t=0` and `P_s⊂Q_t`. Its coefficient is±1/16 or±5/16.
The complete edge census is 480,337,920, including 3,363,840 exceptional
absolute-pairing3 entries. Source and target registers are disjoint even
when their port indices coincide.

For each supported edge `(t,s)`, the fallback allocates dirty ordinary
helper `h`, initially logical value `d` at frame zero:

1. At zero, `y_t -= A_ts h`.
2. Climb `h` to `P_s`, then `h += x_s` while the source is still at `P_s`.
3. Run the explicit K word. It gives `y+=Kx`, restores its ordinary
   centers and preserves logical sources, placing sources at FULL and
   targets at their caps.
4. Climb `h` to `Q_t`, then `y_t += A_ts h`.
5. Climb `h` to FULL, then `h -= x_s`.

Thus targets receive `-Ad+Kx+A(d+x)=x` and helpers return to `d`.
In the same-order inverse-transpose orientation, the scalar gates become
`h+=A_ts y_t`, `x_s-=h`, K dual, `h-=A_ts y_t`, `x_s+=h`, yielding
`x-=y` and restoring every helper. Ordinary frames stay in the same
chronological order. Correlations among initial dirt values cause no issue:
the proof checks every independent input column exactly.

The helper path is `[1,15,1]`; data source and target paths remain `[16]`.
Every scalar incidence uses the same exact current representative on both
registers. K private streams are disjoint, begin cleared, and end erased
before subsequent ordinary phases. See `contract/THEOREM.md` and
`construction/README.md` for the full compositional proof.

One bucket helper can serve all targets with the same cap label for a fixed
source. Its prefix and scatter retain every separate weighted coefficient;
its gather and cleanup occur once. This gives 227,312,640 helpers, with the
same legal paths. The complete cap-span census proves that this lane count
cannot be reduced by chaining distinct target caps within the specified
per-source mask architecture: each is a different rank 16 cap and they are
incomparable. This is not a lower bound on general mixed arithmetic networks.

## K is an explicit word

The 816 integer features satisfy

`A_feature^T diag(w) A_feature =384D−96UU^T`,

where `D` is the cubic multinomial diagonal and `U` lifts the squared norm.
The actual feature values equal `A_feature*monomials/12`. With squared
doubled-coordinate norm 16 and scale 8192, this gives the required
`K_ts=(t^3−4t)/48`. All 665,856 polynomial coefficients and all 25,263,360
actual feature evaluations are independently checked. A sampled evaluator
binding is retained only as a diagnostic.

At target zero, K precompensates center dirt. Sources then climb to FULL.
The 816 ordinary dirty centers climb `[17]` and gather their integer
features. Each center has one cleared private FULL→zero copy that scatters
its weighted feature and is erased. FULL ungathers restore centers, then
targets climb to their caps. The dual private word is explicitly emitted:
gather targets at zero, ascend to FULL, subtract from its center, erase.
It is not justified by transposing a noninvertible erase.

K's ordinary centers and private work are both paid:816 helpers plus
`C=816×17=13,872`. The full K coefficient census has6,440,208 incidences
on each side and25,760,832 weighted additions. Its maximum orientation
uses247,219,488 scalar primitives before real-component doubling.
`coefficient_horner.py` emits and validates cleared same-frame streams for
all 34 signed dyadic coefficients. It pays beginning/clearing and each
doubling, bit addition, halving and accumulation. No Fourier child runs
inside these coefficient temporaries.

## Exact physical frame family

Use `C_p=((1+i)I+(1−i)X_p)/2`, `F=product_j C_ej`, line representative
`C_p` and cap representative `F C_t^-1`. Every actual label has odd norm
and differs from the characteristic vector `(1,...,1)`.

For any orthogonal binary matrix G, with
`b_i=(wt(row_i G)−1)/2 mod2`, the exact identity is

`P_G F P_G^-1 = X_b F`.

A deterministic orthonormal completion extending one actual label, or an
orthogonal source/target pair, therefore lowers each transition to one
minimal-rank positive coordinate child between at most two affine address
movements. All phases, including the global phase, are fixed by the identity;
there are zero unit-phase multiplication factors. The inverse orientation
has the same rank and movement bound. The helper transitions telescope to
exact F, with inverse residual correction `F²=X_all`.

The theorem covers every declared incidence. Verification includes all 14,670
endpoint completions,512 actual pair property checks, and 17 representative
recipes compared with the pinned lowerer through all 34 signed Pauli images
and the complete 131,072-entry exact Gaussian-dyadic state, including inverse
and global-phase controls. The theorem supplies universal pair coverage;
the512 samples do not. See `physical_compiler/UNIVERSAL-THEOREM.md`.

## Finite precision and remaining admission

With exact17-bit children, a rank-r child increases a Gaussian-dyadic
denominator exponent by at most r; affine movements preserve it. K gathers
are integral and its scatter denominators require at most 10 bits. Repeated
ADDs within a phase draw from fixed source roles, so their count does not
accumulate denominator precision. The forward upper bound is
`17+17+10+16=60` extra fractional bits; the dual bound is 27. Explicit
8/32-port edge and bucket words verify this ledger. Cleared Horner temporaries
fit the same bound. For the edge fallback only, its separately audited range
bound also gives at most 92 extra numerator bits. This is finite exact-child
grid closure, not accumulated approximate-child error admission.

Remaining work is to compile the family and coefficient streams with the
bank/controller/pad placement into one immutable physical tape, integrate
approximate child errors and the all-size common-grid/precision contract,
then establish bit-supplier and analytic transfer. The complete word's
nominal failure already follows without those additional positive costs.
No new selected κ, frontier move or all-size supplier claim is made.
