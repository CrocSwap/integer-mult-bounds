This package composes #147's terminal elimination, #150's delayed dirty-read
register reuse, #146's gauge subset, and #148's atom exponent. It retains

Source heads and attribution are pinned in SOURCE.json and NOTICE.

## Complete operation and composition

Start from the pinned PR97 word and the #144 local frame contract. Delete
the 1,549 terminal accumulators in #147's plan; each has no subsequent use
as a control, so redirecting every forward update to its unique target
replaces its initial-dirt cancellation and final read without changing any
other signal. Keep #150's 3,338 donor/recipient pairs and actual inverse
chronology. At each birth, the donor's final value is the recipient's
arbitrary initial value. The recipient's actual delayed read cancels that
value's future contribution. Every workspace and source update is then
undone in exact reverse chronological order. Targets are never controls.
See the preserved #147/#150 arguments for the scalar derivation.

The #146 subset contains all 9,543 original #144 selected slots and 187
additional slots. Delete the eliminated slots and recycled recipients
from its entrance-gauge set; recipients retain their read at birth. The
same timetable constraints are solved again, including donor death before
recipient read, untouched recipient at read, source-leaf order, target
read order, and redirected-write deadlines. This is not an addition of
the parents' numerical gains.

The actual regenerated composed ledger has exactly the same set of 4,878
new frame-pair obligations as #150: 3,338 handoffs and 1,540 target-chain
pairs. Every such inclusion is checked over Q from reconstructed integer
bases, and both endpoint frames are nondegenerate. Remaining transitions
are ordered subsequences of the original certified chains. The optional
`--all` additionally rechecks every distinct inherited inclusion.

Each scalar gate is executed at a common incident frame. The complete
word is evaluated on independent formal variables for all 27,521 data,
target and physical scratch inputs, both over F2 and over Z. Every scratch
register returns its initial variable; every target receives its initial
variable plus its prescribed source expression. Over F2 that expression
is exactly its corresponding source. Negative controls use a stale birth
value, a premature birth read, and the old incorrect inverse order.

## Reproduction and limits

Run `python3 research/recycled-bit-integration/verify.py --all` to check the
committed certificate, all frame inclusions, complete scalar identities
and conditional assembly. `--write` regenerates the certificate after the
same checks. Optimized Python is rejected explicitly before verification.

The analytic, recovery, fixed-tape, uniform weighted compiler, admissible
prime, ordinary-leaf and inherited frame interfaces remain conditional.
Finite exact checks do not instantiate a full all-size tape multiplier or
establish a practical runtime improvement.
