# Common-flag obstruction for rational-frame XOR circuits

**Scoped negative result; no new kappa.** In the all-role finite-bit
interface, rational frames that preserve one common complete flag cannot
give a positive rank deficit. This includes arbitrary commuting rational
frames, even with nonrational eigenvalues or Jordan blocks. It also includes
noncommuting upper-triangular frames. Nonprojection labels alone therefore
do not escape this obstruction.

The result is a written theorem. The executable supplies exact examples,
finite consistency checks and rejection controls; it is not a formal proof
by enumeration or a proof-assistant formalization.

## Statement and cost consequence

Use the fixed-width XOR model of
[`finite_bit_contract.py`](../../scripts/finite_bit_contract.py) and the
[upstream finite shear contract](../../upstream/build/sections/04-swap.tex),
lines 103–204. There are W independently initialized roles, including every
scratch role, and a rational m by m matrix at each source, sink and gate.
Every port of a gate uses that gate's one common matrix. The complete
pointwise XOR circuit has scalar permutation rho, and

    M_out(rho(w)) - M_in(w) = I_m            for every role w.
    Delta_e = M_head(e) - M_tail(e)
    s = sum_e rank_Q(Delta_e).

Every physical edge is included, including terminal edges and untouched
roles. Suppose there are a single common matrix A and an invertible B over
a characteristic-zero extension K of Q such that

    B^-1 (M_v - A) B

is upper triangular for every assigned vertex v. Then **s >= Wm**.
There is no restriction on scalar circuit length, topology, rho, signs,
denominators, idempotence or the entries above the diagonal.

This is already a complete optimistic rejection of the proposed mechanism
within this interface. Even granting zero additional fixed overhead, its
rank-r child moment, for any 0 < q < 1, satisfies

    (1/W) sum_e (rank(Delta_e)/m)^q
        >= s/(Wm) >= 1.

The inequality uses x^q >= x on [0,1]. Splitting a child into smaller ranks
cannot help. All source access, internal and terminal frame changes,
restoration paths and scratch roles are included in the edge sum; adding
other nonnegative expenses cannot produce contraction. There is consequently
no candidate bit supplier to compose with a complex supplier or outer
assembly. This conclusion does not price a different copied-stream interface.

## Proof

Write T_v = B^-1(M_v-A)B. For each i in {1,...,m}, let lambda_i(M_v-A)
be the i-th diagonal entry of T_v. This is an additive functional; it takes
I_m to 1. For an upper-triangular matrix D,

    rank_K(D) >= number of nonzero diagonal entries of D.       (1)

Indeed, the principal submatrix on those entries is upper triangular with
nonzero determinant. Field extension and conjugation preserve rank, so (1)
applies to every Delta_e with its original rational rank.

Fix i. Let Gamma be the additive subgroup of K generated by 1 and the
finitely many lambda_i(M_v-A). It is finitely generated and torsion free,
so it is a free abelian group. The group algebra F2[Gamma] is a Laurent
polynomial domain. Work in its fraction field L. Denote its monomial with
exponent a by z^a. In particular z^1 != 1.

The **coefficients** are in F2, but the **exponents** stay in Gamma in
characteristic zero. No rational or algebraic matrix entry is reduced
modulo two. This distinction permits even denominators and nonsplit spectra.

Replace each edge on role w by the W by W diagonal operator that multiplies
that role by z^(lambda_i(Delta_e)). Retain each scalar XOR gate as its
ordinary constant matrix over F2. At a gate, every participating role has
the same monomial frame. Scalar mixing therefore commutes with that common
frame. The same common-frame invariant as in the upstream transfer gives

    U_i = D_out P_rho D_in^-1 = z^1 P_rho.                   (2)

Here D_in and D_out contain the monomials of the corresponding terminal
frames; the second equality uses the endpoint condition for **every** input
role. Setting every edge operator to identity instead gives P_rho.

For products of matrices of equal size, telescoping one factor at a time
and using rank subadditivity bounds the rank of their difference by the
sum of the ranks of the differing factors. Each edge operator differs
from identity in rank zero if lambda_i(Delta_e)=0 and rank one otherwise.
The scalar factors are unchanged. By (2),

    W = rank_L((z^1-1) P_rho)
      <= #{e : lambda_i(Delta_e) != 0}.                      (3)

Summing (3) over all m diagonal positions and then applying (1) proves

    Wm <= sum_e #{i : lambda_i(Delta_e) != 0}
       <= sum_e rank_Q(Delta_e) = s.

No edge-disjoint routing or path-homogeneity assumption enters this proof.
The bound is sharp: zero source/gate frames and identity sink frames attain
s=Wm for any valid scalar permutation circuit.

### Commuting rational frames

A finite commuting family over an algebraic closure of Q has a common
eigenvector: take an eigenspace of one matrix, restrict the other commuting
matrices to that invariant space, and repeat. Pass to the quotient by the
resulting invariant line and induct on dimension. This gives a common
complete flag, without assuming diagonalizability. Apply the theorem with
A=0. The same conclusion holds when subtracting one common A makes the
assigned matrices commute. A field extension changes none of their ranks.

## Exact finite evidence

Run from the repository root, using Python 3.11 or later and the standard
library (tested on CPython 3.12.14 and 3.14.7):

```sh
python3 -I -B research/common-flag-frame-obstruction/verify.py
```

The command checks four local mathematical-source pins, replays all formal
input columns over F2 Laurent polynomials with exact Fraction exponents,
checks the frozen
`EXPECTED.json`, and runs the rejection controls. It ends with:

```text
PASS_COMMON_FLAG_OBSTRUCTION cases=14 tests=13 bound=s>=W*m kappa=unchanged
```

`--emit /new/path.json` regenerates the receipt after the same checks and
refuses to overwrite an existing file. JSON payload equality, rather than
container metadata, is the acceptance criterion.

Run `python3 -I -B research/common-flag-frame-obstruction/validate.py` for
the isolated relocated replay, source/certificate tampering controls,
no-overwrite control and byte-identical regeneration check. It ends with
`PASS_COMMON_FLAG_CLI_CONTROLS` followed by the control names.

The completed finite domain is:

- Nine deterministic examples, m in {2,3,4} and W in {4,5,6}; seed
  `42 + 10*m + W`. Each has 15 XOR gates. Upper-triangular entries are drawn
  from {-2/3,-1/2,0,1/2,1,2}.
- A seed-42, W=5, m=3 example explicitly containing E12 and E23. These are
  nonzero nilpotents and do not commute: their commutator is E13. A dense
  rational conjugate plus a common additive gauge has exactly the same
  edge costs and character counts.
- A five-port grouped gate containing all 15 XOR updates, testing the
  single-frame condition with more than two participating roles.
- A sharp six-rank-unit example containing three untouched arbitrary-input
  roles, and a commuting m=2 example with frames aI+bJ, J^2=-I. The latter's
  two characters are a+bi and a-bi, stored as pairs of Fractions. A frame
  with b nonzero has no rational eigenline.
- All 729 ordered differences of 2 by 2 upper-triangular matrices whose
  three entries are in {-1,0,1}. A separate determinant formula checks
  their exact ranks against elimination.

These are consistency cases with a fixed stopping rule: complete this
finite list. No optimization search, timeout, or impossibility inference
from failed search is involved. The exact per-edge rank and active-character
masks are retained in `EXPECTED.json`.

Controls reject a damaged endpoint, wrong scalar permutation, omitted XOR gate,
unlisted gate source, singular basis, non-preserved supplied flag, and
different per-port gate frames. Direct replay controls reject incorrect
characters, and the nonsplit example checks its eigenvector equations and
all pairwise commutators. Further controls check fractional exponent
addition and characteristic-zero rank. The dense matrix [[1,1],[1,1]] has
rank one but two nonzero diagonal entries: it explicitly invalidates step
(1) if the triangular hypothesis is discarded. Rejecting a supplied flag
does not certify that no other flag exists.

## Relation to previous work and limits

Douglas Colkitt's historical
[commuting-projection obstruction](../../docs/research/small-block-control.md)
bounds a particular triple-core label family by dimension counting. This
contribution establishes a related exclusion in a different model: the
complete fixed-W XOR interface with arbitrary nonprojection rational frames,
nonsplit spectra and noncommuting common-flag families. It neither derives
that predecessor's label-count inequality nor extends its numerical ceiling.

The [routing obstruction](../../docs/research/cyclic-topology-audit.md)
excludes a topology for every rational frame assignment when the specified
permutation has edge-disjoint paths. This result instead restricts frame
geometry for any topology. The finite examples here are controls, not new
examples of nonroutable circuits.

DreamingOfClouds' [#717](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/717),
section S2, explicitly leaves nonprojection rational labels open. The
common-flag subclass is excluded here; the unrestricted question remains.
@Curtis-D's [#791](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/791)
prices paths between idempotents. Ivan's
[#705](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/705) and
@jeremydiviney's [#800](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/800)
concern Clifford path valuations. Those are distinct models or hypotheses;
none of their executable code is imported. Exact inspected heads are in
`SOURCES.json`. @davidgovea's
[#720](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/720) removes a
flag restriction on three endpoint targets; the present restriction is on
every assigned circuit frame. No literature-priority claim is made for the
elementary rank telescope or simultaneous triangularization facts.

The theorem requires a common full flag, linear XOR gates, one common
frame per gate and the endpoint identity on all W independent roles.
Arbitrary nontriangular frames, nonlinear gates, clean-input restrictions,
dependent copy streams counted as extra volume, changed-width circuits,
and the complex Clifford interface are outside scope. Common flags are
supplied in the finite examples; this package is not a general flag-finding
algorithm. It does not audit the selected supplier's physical realization.
