# Odd direct sums of the P112 line geometry

This is a scalar and binary geometry construction. It is not a native
supplier or a new integer-multiplication bound. All vector spaces in the
geometry argument are over F2. The fitting matrices are over Q.

## 1. Exact base data

Use the 112 vectors q_i and the 112-by-8 matrix M in the pinned
`NORMALIZERS.json`. Put C=M M^T/4. The verifier checks

```
q_i dot q_i = 1; all q_i are distinct;
span(q_i) has dimension 9;
span(q_i+q_0) has dimension 8;
M^T M = 56 I_8; C_ii = 1;
C_ij = 0 if i != j and q_i dot q_j = 1.
```

Thus C has eigenvalues 14 and zero, with multiplicities 8 and 104.
The 12,544 entry checks establish the literal base mask. They do not assert
that rank eight is the minimum for every matrix with that mask.

For each of the eight columns of M, its 56 nonzero positions select binary
vectors which span a nondegenerate 8-space V. Their affine differences span
the even subspace D of V. The verifier checks dim(D)=7 and rank(D)=6. Its
radical is one line, generated by z with z dot p=1 for every vector p in
the selected support. These eight checked contracts will suffice below.

## 2. The port space

Let k=2s+1 be positive and odd. For an ordered tuple of base ports, use

```
q_(i1,...,ik) = (q_i1,...,q_ik) in F2^(9k).
```

The 112^k labels are distinct and have norm one. Their span U consists of
the vectors whose k block parities agree. The affine-difference contract
in section 1 gives dim(U)=8k+1.

Write chi_i for the all-ones vector in block i. The orthogonal complement
U-perp is the span of chi_i+chi_k for i<k. Its vectors have the form
sum c_i chi_i with sum c_i=0. If such a vector also belongs to U, all c_i
are equal. Since k is odd, they must all be zero. Hence U is nondegenerate.

Its characteristic vector is chi=sum chi_i. This vector has norm one,
and chi-perp inside U is a nondegenerate alternating space. A symplectic
basis therefore identifies U with I_1 plus 4k hyperbolic planes. This form
is isometric to the standard dot product in dimension 8k+1. For the basic
step, the vectors (1,1,1), (1,1,0), (1,0,1) in I_3 have Gram matrix
I_1 plus a hyperbolic plane. Apply this step repeatedly. Thus these labels
give rank-one ports in the standard binary address space of dimension 8k+1.

## 3. The fitting matrix

Let C_i act as C on factor i and as identity on the other factors. Define

```
t = s+1
a_j = (-1)^(j-t) * binomial(j-1,t-1) / 2^(j-1)
F_k = sum_(j=t)^k a_j * sum_(|J|=j) product_(i in J) C_i.
```

Every coefficient is dyadic. This polynomial is twice the multilinear
extension of Boolean majority, evaluated at C_i/2. To see the coefficient
formula, expand

```
2 * sum_(|J|>=t) product_(i in J)(C_i/2)
                    * product_(i not in J)(I-C_i/2).
```

The coefficient of a fixed degree-j term is
2^(1-j) times sum_(l=t)^j (-1)^(j-l) binomial(j,l), which is the stated a_j.

Put A_i=I-C_i. The polynomial I-F_k is the multilinear majority function
on signs A_i. Majority on an odd number of signs changes sign when all
signs change. Its multilinear expansion therefore has only odd-degree
monomials in the A_i. Each A_i has zero diagonal, and each nonzero entry
joins distinct orthogonal base labels.

Consider a nonzero entry of one such monomial. The two port tuples differ
in an odd number of coordinates, with dot product zero in each changed
coordinate. They agree in the remaining even number of coordinates, with
dot product one in each. Their total dot product is zero. Also every such
monomial has zero diagonal. It follows that F_k has diagonal one and zero
at every forbidden off-diagonal position. This argument proves the full
mask without materializing a 112^k-square matrix.

For k=3, the formula is particularly small:

```
F_3 = (C_1 C_2 + C_1 C_3 + C_2 C_3 - C_1 C_2 C_3)/2.
```

## 4. Rank of this matrix

On a tensor eigenspace with exactly l active rank-eight factors, the
eigenvalue is

```
lambda_l = sum_(j=t)^l a_j * binomial(l,j) * 14^j.
```

It is zero for l<t. It is nonzero for l>=t. Here is a direct proof of the
second statement. Let

```
B_(l,t)(x) = sum_(j=t)^l binomial(l,j) x^j (1-x)^(l-j).
```

Then lambda_l=2 B_(l,t)(7), and differentiation gives

```
B_(l,t)(x) = t*binomial(l,t)
            * integral_0^x u^(t-1) (1-u)^(l-t) du.
```

If l-t is even, the integral is positive. If l-t is odd, its positive
part on [0,1] is at most 1/t. Its negative part already has absolute value
at least 2^(t-1) on [2,3], and is larger on [1,7]. Thus it is negative.
Consequently

```
rank(F_k) = sum_(l=t)^k binomial(k,l) 8^l 104^(k-l).
```

For k=3, the nonzero eigenvalues are 98 and -1078. Their multiplicities
are 19,968 and 512. The rank is 20,480. This is the rank of the displayed
matrix, not a minimum-rank theorem for the graph.

## 5. An explicit decoder and its pairing tariff

Expand each C as M M^T/4 in the formula for F_k. A degree-j term gives
8^j * 112^(k-j) rank-one factors. A reader column uses j columns of M and
a coordinate unit vector on each remaining factor. Its source row is
the transpose of that column, multiplied by a_j/4^j. This specifies every
coefficient and has no odd denominator.

The binary support of such a column has span T of dimension 7j+1. Indeed,
its differences are the direct sum of j spaces D_i of dimension seven,
and any one support vector p supplies one further direction. On the
differences, the form has rank 6j and radical spanned by z_1,...,z_j.
Each z_i pairs to one with p. The combinations sum c_i z_i with
sum c_i=0 form the radical of T, of dimension j-1. One remaining radical
direction pairs with p and supplies a nondegenerate two-dimensional
block. Thus

```
dim(T) = 7j+1
rank(dot product on T) = 6j+2.
```

The second number is the separate-total source/reader pairing tariff.
It does not assert that a physical COPY operation at that price has been
implemented. The two numbers must not be interchanged.

The total factor count and pairing tariff of this explicit factorization
are respectively

```
R_k   = sum_(j=t)^k binomial(k,j) 8^j 112^(k-j)
ell_k = sum_(j=t)^k binomial(k,j) 8^j 112^(k-j) (6j+2).
```

For k=3 there are 21,504 factors of pairing rank 14 and 512 of pairing
rank 20. Hence ell_3=311,296, or 76/343 per port. The verifier directly
checks all 7,168 feature-pair/fixed-label supports and all 512 feature
triples; permutations of the three orthogonal blocks give the other
two degree-two families.

For general odd k, ell_k/112^k is at most
(6k+2)*2^k/14^((k+1)/2), and thus tends to zero. This is a geometry and
decoder statement. Native production costs need not tend to zero.

## 6. A local scalar core

The rows of M split into 14 groups according to their four nonzero
coordinates. Each group contains all eight relative sign patterns.
Within one group, let C_b be its Gram matrix divided by four. The literal
root data give C_b^2=2 C_b. Thus K_b=I-C_b is an involution.

After each root is oriented by its first nonzero coordinate, label the
three remaining relative signs by epsilon in F2^3. The scaled core 2 K_b
has entry zero on the diagonal and wt(epsilon+eta)-2 elsewhere. It has
nonzero entries only between opposite selector parities. The verifier
checks the actual binary labels: each parity class spans dimension three,
and the two spans pair to zero. It also checks every literal cube Gram
entry, including the original root orientation signs.

On each three-cube product, set

```
K = (K_1 + K_2 + K_3 - K_1 K_2 K_3)/2.
```

The K_i commute and square to identity, so K^2=I. Each term reverses total
selector parity. Both parity classes have 256 ports and span dimension
nine. The two spans pair to zero. The verifier checks these spans for
all 2,744 product cubes. Thus both 256-by-256 blocks of K are invertible
dyadic scalar maps between orthogonal source and target classes.

Let F_block be F_3 formed from the block-diagonal C_b operators. Then
K=I-F_block. The remaining side map is H=F_block-F_3, and

```
K + H + F_3 = I.
```

The off-diagonal support of H is orthogonal, since both fitting matrices
have that property and their diagonals agree. A chronological native
producer for H is not supplied. The K identity alone does not provide it.

## 7. Exact allowance for the missing work

For a proposed three-stage caller, grant the source children [8,15,1],
the target children [15,1,1,1,1,1,1,1,1,1], and physical copies at the
pairing tariff above. These are planning assumptions. In particular,
no H readout order compatible with the target flags is certified here.
The resulting granted inventory, with v=112^3, is

```
paid:    H1:30v, H8:3v, H15:6v, H14:64512, H20:1536
targets: H73:2v.
```

At a=31/10000, the signed moment of this inventory lies strictly between
3v/10 and v/3. The verifier encloses each r^(1-a) with rational numbers.
Decimal arithmetic only proposes an endpoint; the integer check

```
b^10000 <= r^31 * 10^240000 < (b+1)^10000
```

proves every bracket. The printed rational intervals are therefore exact.

The omitted work must fit the remaining signed-moment allowance, which is
less than 1/9 per port per local invocation. It includes complete helper
lifetimes and their productive targets, physical copies, old-value reads,
source restoration and any changed caller costs. No omitted item is zero
by assumption of the result. The favorable incomplete moment is not a
supplier exponent, an outer exponent or an admission certificate. No
balanced-interface a/(1+a) conversion is applied.

## 8. A gate with full source-dimension copies

The positive allowance does not require the smaller pairing tariff. Replace
one rank-14 copy by rank 15 for every feature pair, and one rank-20 copy by
rank 22 for every feature triple. The copy mass becomes

```
15*21504 + 22*512 = 333824 = (163/686)*v.
paid:    H1:30v, H8:3v, H15:6v+64512, H22:1536
targets: H73:2v.
```

At the same exponent 31/10000, the exact remaining moment lies between
v/4 and 3v/10. Its value per port is about 0.2707505542. Thus less than
1/10 per port per local invocation remains for all omitted work.

This is a second planning gate. It does not assert that these copies have
been scheduled. A producer could use it without implementing the smaller
pairing-rank service. It would still need to supply the source totals at
their actual frames, compatible target reads, compensation for arbitrary
old values, and every helper return. All other assumptions of section 7
remain in force.
