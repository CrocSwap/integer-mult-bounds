ALL COPY COUNTS AND ALL ROOT THIN-QUERY COUNTS FOR THE SMALL HELPER PROFILE
2 November 2026

This proof closes the apparent amortization window for the unchanged
children (1,2,2,1), stage ranks (2,3,3,2), and two consumed child qD outputs.
It permits arbitrary global repacking of the simultaneous stage supports.
It uses no conjectured bound m2 <= 2 delta or m2 <= 3 delta.

1. COMPLETE RESOURCE PROFILE

Take ell positive copies of that resource profile. There are 6ell root
fields, 4ell full nilpotent query factors, 2ell consumed helper reflections,
and delta root thin-query factors. Splitting the mixed full/helper factors
into adjacent commuting rank-one pieces gives 6ell+delta factors on 6ell
dimensions. The retained central endpoint is -I. All body variables remain
independent, and every full stage is supported by its own idempotent in one
simultaneous commuting stage basis.

The full-stage body sizes are 8,16,16,8 per block; their projector ranks are
2,3,3,2. Thus total root body mass is

  ell [2*8 + 3*16 + 3*16 + 2*8] = 128ell.

There are 6ell complete native source invocations, and 2ell complete binary
child nodes. Both consumed H17 targets per block are removed. The root
queries cost delta H1=delta. If L is the complete paid-minus-helper source
moment per port, the remaining cost per block is

  N = 6L + 2(2H16+1-2H17) + 2H17
    = 6L + 4H16 + 2 - 2H17.

This retains the whole native/helper invoice and both binary child nodes.
Leaving ordinary and compiler work out only enlarges the possible margin.

2. GRAM DEFECT FORCES A SMALL COVER OF ALL FULL STAGES

For the chronological rank-one factor list I-2u_i v_i, put G=VU,
K=I+2 strict_lower(G), and Z=K-G. The exact product identity from the
higher_roots proof gives UK^-1 V=I and hence rank Z=delta. At a nilpotent
full-query site Z_ii=1; off-diagonal entries are signed entries of G.

In the simultaneous stage basis, full-stage projector i has a coordinate
support A_i of size two or three. Both u_i and v_i are supported in A_i.
If a set of these supports is pairwise disjoint, all its cross-Gram entries
vanish, and its principal Z submatrix is an identity matrix. Consequently
every disjoint-support matching has at most delta members.

Choose any maximal such matching. Its union C meets every full-stage
support, since otherwise the matching could be extended. Therefore

  |C| <= 3delta,
  body mass on C >= sum_i 8s_i = 48ell.

No intersection property is assumed after deleting occurrences or lowering
projector ranks. These are the actual supports of the proposed construction.
The argument works at every defect delta. Delta=0 is impossible because a
full nilpotent site gives a nonzero diagonal entry of Z.

3. PRICE THE FORCED LOAD CONCENTRATION

Write rho=delta/ell. First consider 0<rho<=1/3. Pad C, if necessary, to
exactly 3delta coordinates. This is possible because 3delta<=ell<6ell and
does not reduce its body mass. Let X be that core's body mass. Concavity
gives a productive root moment at most

  3delta H(1+X/(3delta))
   +(6ell-3delta) H(1+(128ell-X)/(6ell-3delta)).

The uniform allocation would put 64delta body units in this core, whereas
X>=48ell and rho<=1/3 imply 48ell>64delta. Above the uniform allocation,
the displayed expression decreases with X. Substituting X=48ell is thus
an upper bound. Divide by ell to obtain

  B(rho)=3rho H(1+16/rho)
         +(6-3rho) H(1+80/(6-3rho)).

The full margin per block per port is at most f(rho)=B(rho)-N-rho.
Each term of B is a perspective of the concave tariff H, so f is concave
on 0<rho<2. Its tangent at rho0=1/6 lies above it. The two arguments there
are 97 and 171/11, and

  f'(rho0) = 3H97 [kappa+(1-kappa)/97]
             -3H(171/11)[kappa+(1-kappa)/(171/11)] -1.

Outward rational intervals bound f(rho0), f'(rho0), and both endpoint
values of this affine tangent on [0,1/3]. If both endpoint values are
negative, every actual positive rho in this interval has negative margin.
The endpoint rho=0 is used only for the affine bound, not as a proposed
zero-defect construction.

For rho>=1/3, ordinary Jensen without a core suffices:

  margin/(ell*ports) <= 6H(67/3)-N-rho
                    <= 6H(67/3)-N-1/3.

The script checks these three rational signs at both targets. The resulting
upper margins per block per port are

  kappa 0.0042:        below -0.0251816831713406029526496;
  kappa 0.00424927762:  below -0.0576062843336408717589753.

The result closes every integer ell>=1 and every root thin-query count
delta, with arbitrary support repacking, at both goals. It is a price
obstruction, not a query-word search limit.

PAID RANK-THREE PROMOTIONS. The same bound permits any original rank-two
stage to be promoted to rank three by adding a paid whole H8 idle. Choose
the hitting core from the actual supports; their sizes are still at most
three, so the matching argument is unchanged. For each promoted stage,
delete one of its three body occurrences and the corresponding H8 charge.
Choose the deleted coordinate so that the two remaining coordinates retain
at least one intersection with the core. This is always possible. Removing
a productive body occurrence of size8 loses at most H8 by concavity, so
the resulting margin is an upper bound. It has total body mass128ell and
core mass at least48ell again. The trimmed supports need not be projectors;
no algebra or pairwise intersections are assumed for them. This explicitly
avoids the invalid inference that arbitrary trimming preserves geometry.

4. SCOPE AND CONSEQUENCE

The positive four-copy invoice was real accounting but omitted the geometric
concentration forced by the global query identity. At defect one, the
separate rank-two-projector theorem already rejects it. This covering proof
also handles every larger defect in the unchanged small-helper profile.

It does not exclude other child sizes or child contracts, ranks above three
or other stage profiles, new independently productive helpers, changed sources, noncommuting
stage descriptions, or operator-dependent bases. In particular it does not
establish a general sparse-transvection inequality. Those are different
construction problems and must carry their own complete native invoices.
