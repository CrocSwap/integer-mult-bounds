## The binary construction

The public mixed-projector construction supplies the six-field/four-child
architecture and binary-child recursion. Its complex normalization and
dyadic bases are not reduced modulo two. Instead this package constructs
binary projectors and verifies the operator identity directly.

Let q be the retained central BIT involution and put t=q+1. In
characteristic two, t squared is zero. On six fields, let J be the all-ones
matrix and use the canonical seven rank-one square-zero matrices

`R_i = e_i (1^T + e_i^T)` for i=0,...,5, and `R_6=J`.

Their sum is the identity. Consequently

`product_i (I + t R_i) = I + t sum_i R_i = q I`.

All mixed products disappear because t squared is zero. This argument
does not use the complex two-character decomposition or division by two.
It remains valid for the all-width q supplied by the retained BIT source.

Four sites, 0,1,5,6, receive full binary children. The other three sites
receive paid thin queries. The stage projectors have ranks 2,3,3,2. They
are idempotent, commute pairwise, contain their site's rank-one query, and
commute with that site's query prefix. Exact enumeration finds six binary
roots with the desired joint eigenspaces. The stored projectors and bases
are explicit binary matrices.

Every full child supplies `D U(q)` on its first two arbitrary public
fields. One ordinary addition converts this to `D U(q+1)`. A rank-three
projector also requires one separately charged whole D job. All scalar
basis changes use only ADD and SWAP; there is no complex or dyadic
normalizer and no additional owned field.

With child sizes 7,2,2,7, the six root outputs are:

| AB | 64 | 1 |

| B | 15 | 1 |

The four binary children contribute another 28 independent main outputs
per port. The 18 native helper families remain disjoint, arbitrary and
fully productive under the unchanged native endpoint.

## A stopping rule for further local projector searches

All 35 choices of four full sites among the seven canonical queries were
tested with rank-two/rank-three projectors. Sites 2 and 4 have no eligible
projector of those ranks. Only sites 0,1,5,6 admit commuting solutions:
270 exact cliques give 30 output-mask profiles, or 16 profiles up to stage
permutation.

The complete child-size screen uses rational outward power bounds rounded
in safe directions to an integer grid. It prices 47,447,400 child tuples
through 80 total native leaves. At 0.00424927762 the best margin upper
bound is -0.534299379874 per port. A rational concavity/Jensen bound is
negative and decreasing from 80 onward, so no larger binary tree is
omitted from this target exclusion.

There is a stronger economic relaxation. For each charged extra stage
placement, delete that placement from one output. The inequality
`H(x+r)-H(r) <= H(x)` means this can only increase the margin. Every
remaining stage then appears on exactly two of the six output fields.
This removes all projector algebra constraints, including their ranks.
There are 132 formal incidence profiles, or 21 stage-permutation orbits.
All are priced exactly over 33,213,180 child tuples, with the same tail.

Even this optimistic family fails:

| Target | Best formal upper margin per port |
|---|---:|
| 0.0042 | -0.043546231878 |
| 0.00424927762 | -0.107964213526 |

The consequence is specific: changing projector bases, sites or ranks
inside this six-field, four-binary-child architecture cannot alone reach
the target with the current native source. Additional helper consumption,
a changed child constructor, a different root width or a cheaper source
falls outside this ceiling. Those are the useful next directions.

## Review and fixed constants

`ADMISSION-REVIEW.json` records a semantic review of the independent audit
and finite/ordinary admission. It checks source binding, the order of the
native triangular operation, pure q outputs, arbitrary helper inputs,
nonzero owned erasure, and ordinary/stock funding.

One bookkeeping omission was corrected during that review. If
`D=11640^768-W` complete targets are specialized to owned zero, the fixed
compiler constants now explicitly include

`O = 2798802 U_raw + D`

and

`G = 10^12 U_raw + 8 R + 2*11640^768`.

This charges the specialized fields and their allocation/reset records
without relying on spare reserve. The corrected admission was rerun. The
native-all-columns, owned-erasure, compiler, precision/tape and analytic
recovery assumptions remain inherited; no unconditional theorem or
materialized enormous uniform word is claimed.
