# Prove four downstream stock separators after a BIT chart-388 recode

Fixes sender banks, timing, six input slots and the four first outgoing payloads at the next boundary. The proof allows any same-bank XOR and optimistic free histories. A changed more distant exterior or paid new information can escape.

When chart 283 supplies x43, exactly exits 2021,2022,2025,2026 change. In each first receiving bank, the original first departure lies outside the entire three-sender stock union; the ranks rise 26→27, 28→29, 28→29 and 26→27. Free recoding of the four exits and those recipient computations cannot restore the boundary.

Observed validation. Literal scans, separated GF(2) parity checks, original 680-column forward/adjoint and 38-column caller controls, and independent outward prices are retained. The known paid correction and corrupted-transfer tests calibrate the rank and accounting gates.

The source archive contains the exact pins, proof, licenses, commands and control outcomes.
