## Static connection and all-depth endpoint

Let K=max(16,L+11). The old core uses K+9 roles. New T and R_body are the two new indices K+9,K+10, outside all old frames/globals. For L4 the natural-root table has27 roles; no runtime-dependent tape selection appears.

The old H-generation work runs first and preserves width. The old post-H jump is redirected to the appended prefix. The prefix raw-copies T to the owned root-A role, then explicitly restores both T and A data heads. It copies immutable H to the previously empty reserved return role and applies the finite constant-L multiplier; this dummy becomes g=LH. H's own body head is restored.

For every `product(source,target)`, the nine producer ports remain distinct. Dummy g is input0, immutable H input1, the actual source input2, quotient and product outputs are separate, and four scratch ports remain distinct. At the initial R product the source is new R_body and target is old root-R. During the depth loop the source is current owned A and the target is grant-R. Thus producer input0 is always g, its quotient is exactly1, and its product is H times the source. Arbitrary positive T does not need to be divisible by g.

After each product, quotient and all four guide/work bodies are raw-retired, including coded logical blanks. The initial product delivers root R0=H R_body. Inductively, if current A=A_k=Tg^k, the loop produces H A_k in a different role, retires old A, copies that product back, clears the temporary and multiplies by fixed L. Hence the next A is A_(k+1). One depth O is consumed per iteration. On the terminating blank the depth origin scan restores its exact original raw head; d=0 works because the first backward step finds the existing BASE immediately. Dummy g is then retired before old node entry. Every temporary required blank by that core is physically blank again.

Relocation offsets every appended branch/default/goto target and sends the successful prefix HALT to the old node entry. The old final HALT instead goes to appended A/R retirement; cleanup's successful HALT remains the final HALT. Rejects retain rejection semantics, not fallthrough. The source constructs the old rows first and permits only its named post-H jump and unique final HALT to differ. No old node/grant/stack/return instruction is otherwise changed. ## Geometric preparation invoice

The source producer's linear logical bound is retained as a primitive/source premise, rather than inferred from finite runs. With dummy first input g and other source X it is

`80g+58H+48X+24HX+46`.

Its form is consistent with the actual converter/quotient/product/guide loops: three converters cost at most44 times their unary extents; the remaining scans and amortized increments are charged to g,H,X,HX. Codec factor32 and raw retirement/copy add fixed linear passes through the real encoded extents. No variable multiplication is made free: every product is the original emitted producer, followed by fixed-L carry code and explicit cleanup.

For d>=1,

`sum_(k<d) A_k = T*(g^d-1)/(g-1) <= A_d/(g-1)`;

`H*sum_(k<d) A_k <= A_d`, since H<=LH-1;

`d*g <= g^d <= A_d`, since d<=g^(d-1) for g>=2.

Thus all repeated dummy-g and H conversions, actual A conversions and H*A products have total charge O_template(A_d), including per-iteration constant work and depth motion. The fixed-L multiplication scans lengths bounded by positive input values, and its finite carry cost depends on the fixed template; summing those costs is likewise linear in A_d. Billing each iteration by the final volume would introduce an unnecessary d factor; omitting repeated dummy conversions would instead undercharge real work.

The root-R product costs O_template(g+H+R_body+H R_body). Initial T/H copies, final A/R cleanup and head restoration cost their actual extents. For all d>=0, `g,H,T,R_body,A_d,R0<=V0`. Empty d=0 has no loop, so none of the d>=1 inequalities is needed. At w=1,H=1 all statements remain valid and the old H prefix skips its multiplication loop. For arbitrary w, inherited H generation costs O((ell(b)+1)w²), and w²<=2b^(w-1)=2H pays its unary width guide, real backing and cleanup. Unary depth has length d<=A_d when d>=1; binary borrowed lengths are bounded by their positive values.

Only a fixed number of descriptors/guides/products coexist. Their extents are bounded by a template multiple of g+H+A_d+R0, plus charged input/prefix backing. Successive loop iterations retire and reuse the same physical suffixes; the maximum visited footprint is bounded by the largest extents, not by deleting visits after erasure. The intentionally coarse C_root/S_root envelopes are compatible with these explicit fixed-pass bounds, conditional on the named primitive-resource and exact encoded-entry premises. This review is not an independent measured microinstruction/table census or recertification of each predecessor's exact constant.

The full invocation still adds the core occurrence-time sum, not just preparation O(V0). At two sites, `sum_nodes V_node=V0 sum_(j=0..d)(2/L)^j`; L=2 retains d+1. Space instead adds the old active-chain geometric sum. Root preparation does not remove those charges, and a different final template needs its actual call multiplicity.

## Explicit hand controls — all unexecuted

1. **Do not require T divisible by g.** L4,H1,d0,T2,R_body3 is a valid domain point with A0=2,R0=3,V0=24. Replacing dummy g by current A=T in the initial producer would wrongly reject because2 is not divisible by4. Actual code uses dummy g, so this is a discriminating mutant, not a source counterexample.
2. **Do not leave depth at the end.** L4,H1,d1,T=R_body=1 gives A0=4,V0=16. Without the depth rewind, old node entry sees blank and treats the root as a leaf. On an all-zero packet the correct depth-one result flips root positions0,1, whereas that wrong leaf flips0,4,8,12; the raw final depth head also fails restoration. This exposes both endpoint and head consequences without a run.
3. **Immutable H is not current R.** With w1,H1,R_body2,d1, replacing H by root R0=2 during A production changes the generated A factor even though width is valid. Cases restricted to R_body1 can hide that substitution. The actual mapping uses H.
4. **w1 alone cannot validate the H factor.** If loop multiplication by H were omitted, w1 would hide the fault. A w2,H3,d1 case distinguishes T*L*H from T*L. Likewise omitting the fixed-L multiplier is exposed even at w1,d1.
5. **Owned cleanup is not optional.** Leaving dummy g at handoff violates the return-role blank contract and can block later output installation; d0 can already expose a final owned residue. Omitting final A/R cleanup preserves the semantic output while violating the new outer ownership endpoint. Erasing original T/R_body instead corrupts borrowed inputs.
6. **Payload promise and markers remain hypotheses.** A source word of the wrong promised length can fail the router's exact-end condition after prior writes; missing outer BASE/LEFT can make an origin scan leave its intended segment. No arbitrary-store rejection, rollback or free marker/payload producer is proved.
