`H=b^(w-1), g=L*H, A_k=T*g^k, R0=H*R_body, V0=L*A_d*R0`.

## 3. Port separation, dummy reuse and the product helper

Let K=max(16,L+11). Existing router roles end at L+6. Grants are L+7,L+8; extra guide L+9; reserved return role K-1 is at least L+10. The core globals occupy K..K+8. The prefix borrows the empty reserved return role only for dummy g. It is distinct from H, both original inputs, both root descriptor targets, the grant outputs and all four guide/work roles. In `product` lines49-55, source-body use maps the producer's(A,H,R) to(dummy,H,R_body); repeated-A use maps to(dummy,H,current root A). Outputs are quotient grant_A and respectively root R or grant_R. Work roles are router repeats/block, extra r_guide and binary_work, all distinct. All input/output/work ports of each producer invocation are therefore injective and have the required blank/head state.

Initially copy T to owned root A. Raw copy restores source and target to their physical origins, and the following body operations restore both to the encoded logical body head. Thus T's original head is preserved. Copy generated H to the dummy and restore H/dummy body heads. The one-port constant-L routine produces g=L*H in place, preserving a positive canonical header and returning its logical body head.

The retained shape producer requires its **first** input to be divisible by L*H. It always receives dummy g, so its quotient is exactly one. Its third input may be any positive x. ## 4. Root R and the depth-loop invariant

The old H prefix is reached before the modified entry link. Static inspection of old `compile` shows that this portion reads width and uses only high/repeats/binary_work; it does not require root A/R to be populated yet. It generates H, restores width, physically clears those scratch roles and reaches the redirected jump. Thus intercepting that jump with blank A/R is justified by the actual code, rather than applying the old full-program precondition prematurely.

First call `product(R_body,root_R)`. It yields R0=H*R_body, preserves original R_body and its logical body head, leaves root R canonical at its logical body head, and clears its quotient/work. Root A already contains T=A_0. H and dummy g remain canonical and unchanged.

At loop entry after k marks, 0<=k<=d, use the invariant:

- root A contains A_k=T*g^k at its logical body head;
- root R contains R0, and H/dummy contain H/g at theirs;
- depth head is k positions after its supplied unary origin;
- quotient, grant_R and all four work bodies are physically empty at their required raw origins;
- original T/R_body/w/payload bytes are unchanged with their prescribed heads.

If k<d the scanned symbol is O. The actual product helper computes H*A_k in grant_R. Only after that result exists does the code retire old root A, copy grant_R into it, restore its body head and retire grant_R. The constant-L routine then changes H*A_k into L*H*A_k=A_(k+1). Moving the depth head right once reestablishes the invariant. There is no overwritten source before its output is protected, no alias between the input and target of the product helper, and no surviving grant cache between iterations.

If k=d the scanned symbol is physical BLANK. `origin(depth,BASE)` returns to the unary start; its initial left move also works when d=0. Dummy g is then physically retired. Both node descriptors and H remain canonical at the exact heads expected by old node entry; all prefix-only grant/work/return roles are blank, relays/phase/control are in their original empty state, and depth is restored. The prefix's linked halt jumps directly to that original entry. The prefix terminates for every finite d by consuming one mark per iteration.

At d=0 the loop is absent, but the implementation still copies T, builds dummy g, generates root R, rewinds empty depth and retires dummy. Then A0=T. In particular T need not be divisible by g. At w=1, H=1 and g=L>=2; width generation makes zero multiplications after its first guide mark, while all prefix/product/divisibility arguments remain valid. No zero-length or free-product shortcut is used. These cases must be included in the bill below.

## 6. Independent geometric root-prefix charge

This section supports the deliberately coarse source envelope without claiming a new optimized tariff or an independent proof of every original primitive. Retain the original converter bound c(x)<=44x, complete producer bound and codec/scan premises. In particular we do not independently reprove its entire1000V linked bill. For the producer's actual first input g and third input x, its stated logical bound becomes

`P(x)<=80g+58H+48x+24Hx+46 <=200*(g+Hx)`.

The last inequality uses H<=g/2, x<=Hx and g>=2; it includes fixed setup/terminal/halt charges. Codec factor32 gives at most6400(g+Hx) physical transitions. The five subsequently retired regions have logical extents bounded by quotient-one, unary g/H/x and max binary conversion length, plus markers/materialized logical-BLANK triples. Their total physical extent is conservatively below40(g+Hx). Even allowing four scans at128 transitions per participating position adds at most20,480(g+Hx). Thus a safe wrapper allowance is30,000(g+Hx) per product, not an uncharged multiplication primitive. These constants are handwritten overestimates, not a table census.

Write S=sum_(k=0..d-1)A_k, with S=0 for d=0. For d>=1,

`S=T*(g^d-1)/(g-1) <=A_d/(g-1)`,
`H*S<=A_d`,
`d*g<=g^d<=A_d`, and `d<=A_d`.

Here H/(L*H-1)<=1, and d<=g^(d-1) follows inductively for g>=2. Therefore all repeated product-wrapper charges sum to at most60,000*A_d. The one root-R product costs at most30,000(g+R0). Repeated work on the same dummy g/H is charged by d*g, **not** by d copies of V0.

After each repeated product there are a fixed number of raw source/target copies, retirements and head returns on packets of sizes bounded by H*A_k and constants. A deliberately enlarged allowance120,000*H*A_k covers these scans, including marker/000 backing and the intermediate grant; summing gives at most120,000*A_d. This is not inferred from treating erased dictionary entries as unvisited cells.

For completeness the actual fixed-L multiplier admits a direct logical path bound. If its input has m bits and the final carry has s<=e_L=bitlength(L) bits, it uses an initial goto, four transitions per input digit, the RIGHT branch, two per carry digit, final RIGHT/goto, complete rewind and the final continuation. This is at most `7m+5e_L+8 <=20*(m+e_L+1)` logical transitions. It is dominated by the source's looser displayed bound. For repeated inputs H*A_k, sum m<=H*S<=A_d, while `d*(e_L+1)<=2*d*g<=2*A_d` since e_L+1<=2L<=2g. Thus all repeated multipliers cost at most1920*A_d after factor32. There is no hidden e_L-squared or d*V0 term. The initial H-to-dummy multiplication is similarly O(g), because its bitlength plus e_L+1 is bounded by a fixed multiple of H+L<=2g.

Initial T/H copies, root-R retention, final A/R retirement and unary-depth restoration use only fixed many scans of T,H,g,A_d,R0 and d. All are bounded by V0, including at d=0: `g=L*H<=L*T*H*R_body=V0`, T<=A_d<=V0 and R_body<=R0<=V0. Constants from markers/empty depth are covered because V0>=2. Root input packet lengths are bounded by their positive values; their preparation is still an external premise, not paid here.

The inherited H-from-w portion also has a direct width charge. Its finite carry is below b; one multiplication scans at most e_b*w+1 input bits, and summing w-1 products plus the <=44w converter/cleanup is O((e_b+1)w^2). The elementary inequality `w^2<=2*b^(w-1)=2H` holds at w=1,2 and propagates since ((w+1)/w)^2<=9/4<3 for w>=2. Codec and raw cleanup therefore give O((e_b+1)V0) once. The listed product/copy/multiplier/restoration allowances are far below `10^8*(K+e_b+e_L+1)*V0`; the large coefficient has ample conditional slack for the finite endpoint checks and head/marker returns. Hence the source choice

## 7. Retained backing, including repeated erasure

At any prefix iteration the only live new arithmetic objects are fixed-role T/R_body/w inputs, H, dummy g, current A_k, root R0, quotient one, one H*A_k product, four producer work regions, unary depth and their markers. Producer unary guides have lengths bounded by g,H,x, with x=R_body once or A_k thereafter. The conversion work is bounded by the corresponding maximum binary length. Coded physical spans are at most a fixed multiple of these positive values plus markers; raw retirement includes000 triples.

Crucially all repeated calls start these same work roles at the same prepared origins. A_k grows monotonically; H*A_k<=A_d during the repeated loop, while the one R_body use is bounded by R0. Thus even the union of visited extents is bounded by the per-role maxima, not by a new backing block per iteration. Copying after erasure reuses that physical suffix. Old erasures do not reduce the retained-space bill. Depth costs d+constant<=A_d+constant, width/unary work is O((e_b+1)H), and markers contribute only a fixed amount per fixed tape.

A direct broad bound by a few hundred times `(K+e_b+e_L+1)*V0` covers these fixed many encoded spans, including the original complete payload if it is not already counted in K_entry. Hence the source's

`S_root=1000*(K+e_b+e_L+1)`

has margin under the stated blank-growth/marker ABI. Initial inaccessible backing remains K_entry. The core then uses its separately charged active-chain envelope; the two new readonly input tapes are outside its frames, so they do not create a per-depth copy. Sequential core siblings retain the old restored-suffix reuse proof.

Combining terms safely gives

`time <= C_root*V0 + C_core*(V0+sum_actual_nodes V_node)`,
`backing <= K_entry+S_root*V0+S_core*sum_(j=0..d)V0/L^j`.

The old C_core term may double-charge its width setup; this is a safe overestimate. For two sites the actual node-volume sum is `V0*sum_(j=0..d)(2/L)^j`. At L=2 it has a depth factor; natural-root generation being linear does not make the entire tree linear or assign94V0 to it. A different final template must keep its own number of recursive occurrences and full bill.

## 9. Additional written boundary controls (unexecuted)

1. **Do not require T divisible by g.** L4,H1,d0,T2,R_body3 is a valid domain point with A0=2,R0=3,V0=24. Replacing dummy g by current A=T in the initial producer would wrongly reject because2 is not divisible by4. Actual code uses dummy g, so this is a discriminating mutant, not a source counterexample.
2. **Do not leave depth at the end.** L4,H1,d1,T=R_body=1 gives A0=4,V0=16. Without the depth rewind, old node entry sees blank and treats the root as a leaf. On an all-zero packet the correct depth-one result flips root positions0,1, whereas that wrong leaf flips0,4,8,12; the raw final depth head also fails restoration. This exposes both endpoint and head consequences without a run.
3. **Immutable H is not current R.** With L4,w1,H1,R_body2,d1, replacing H by root R0=2 during A production makes dummy g=4 fail the producer's exact-divisibility requirement L*R0=8. Thus this wrong wiring rejects a valid root input; it need not reach a changed output. Cases restricted to R_body1 can hide that substitution. The actual mapping uses H.
4. **w1 alone cannot validate the H factor.** If loop multiplication by H were omitted, w1 would hide the fault. A w2,H3,d1 case distinguishes T*L*H from T*L. Likewise omitting the fixed-L multiplier is exposed even at w1,d1.
5. **Owned cleanup is not optional.** Leaving dummy g at handoff violates the return-role blank contract and can block later output installation; d0 can already expose a final owned residue. Omitting final A/R cleanup preserves the semantic output while violating the new outer ownership endpoint. Erasing original T/R_body instead corrupts borrowed inputs.
6. **Payload promise and markers remain hypotheses.** A source word of the wrong promised length can fail the router's exact-end condition after prior writes; missing outer BASE/LEFT can make an origin scan leave its intended segment. No arbitrary-store rejection, rollback or free marker/payload producer is proved.
