## Result

For all 3453 actual raw selected MOVE occurrences, this package joins the
exact right monomial, positive rank-r child and left monomial on a protected
complete current coded field. Both monomials use the verified serial
one-column route. The positive child uses its separate rank-scaled view.
On the stated direct domain, **no ordinary numerical runtime call occurs**.
Real closed ordinary constructors and all their otherwise unused tapes remain
in the finite setup and backing bill.

The original cutoff is retained: **r*f<=cutoff**, with exact intermediate
Gaussian halves and every serialized signed phase representable. The useful
sufficient envelope requires input components divisible by 2^(r*f) and strict
absolute component bound 2^(h-r*f-1). The exact accepted prefix domain is
larger; final-output fit alone does not prove admission. The original batched
and serialized monomial success domains are not equated.

[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) gives all-column/spectator and absolute-phase correctness,
complete success and named valid-return failure atomicity, geometry and the
full invoice. The inventory is `479+Q_R+Q_L`, including 129 separate descriptor
ports and the protected stage. The explicit q2/capacity64 constructor witness
in [check_move.py](check_move.py) gives 4235 private ports without supplying
an ordinary numerical implementation. Capacity65 instead gives 4247.

The complete bill is both repeated one-column costs, both descriptor loops,
the rank-r positive leaf, its header work, all copies/restoration/retirement,
F_template, and 22V+35 protected-stage actions. The named valid returned
failure overhead is 13V+21 plus all executed factor work. The inherited
`M1*A1^6` and polynomial header terms remain unless the explicit long-record
and control-absorption conditions apply. Only with those added conditions
does the route simplify to `F_template+O(fV)`. This proves no faster nonleaf
tariff or complete multiplication exponent improvement.
