# Sharp bilateral Gaussian lattice rounding: proof-role package

An explicit bijection of Z^4 approximates H4/2 with error0 on even inputs
and error1 on odd inputs. It preserves parity and changes every radius by
strictly less than1/2. Forward and inverse outward suprema both equal1/2.
No error-one total integer-output map can have smaller absolute radial
supremum, so the bilateral optimum is sharp, without a parity, period,
conserved-sum or equivariance premise on competitors.

The construction pairs half-integer coordinates in two staggered phases.
Toggling the coordinate with least partner-invariant(score,index) is a
global involution. Two exact orthogonal coset maps turn it into the lattice
bijection and its explicit inverse. PROOF.md proves all coset, radial,
nearest-corner and directional statements for unbounded integer inputs.

This is a constructive forward upper1/2 and a universal bilateral lower1/2.
It does not prove universal forward-only optimality. The known R5 forward
lower sqrt21-sqrt19 remains a different bound. Gaussian-pair transfer does
not inherit exact complex sum, phase covariance or fourth-power identity.
Its actual inverse is S F_H^-1 L; conjugating the positive nonlinear map
is a different provider and is not its inverse.
