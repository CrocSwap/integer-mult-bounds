# An odd minus on u keeps the trace -48 on (+1, +1)

This note answers the second open question of the sector-3 payload-involution
certificate: whether one other cross-coset pair, with a different odd sign,
moves the trace `-48` of `X_3` onto another `(u, v)` eigenvalue. The selected
conditional kappa stays `781451/250000000`. Nothing here is a characteristic
polynomial, a basis, or a census of all 3072 cross-coset pairs.

The claim is a **proof**. The joint trace formula is an identity for commuting
involutions. The 512 addresses, the 16 odd signs, and the 16 characters below
are finite sets, and the verifier evaluates the identities on every element of
each set. That evaluation is exhaustive on those sets. It is not a heuristic
search, and it is not a proof that an arbitrary change of mask keeps the trace.

## The pair

The input is the pinned 8-by-112 query, SHA256
`5c6c51fbffc07a683e922f17532fd90262ae9e23cc3560a696002073dd234ef3`.
Bell block `c=3` and the payload involution `X_3`, translation of the address
by 3, are the operators of the degree-881 certificate. The sign group `H` is
the order-16 group of diagonal signs supported on the odd coordinates
`{1, 3, 5, 7}` and positive on the even coordinates. Its characters are the
16 homomorphisms `F_2^4 → {±1}`, indexed by coordinates `(1, 3, 5, 7)`.

The explicit pair from the c=3 sign-split certificate is

| name | permutation | minus signs |
|---|---|---|
| `u` | `(0 2)(4 6)` | coordinates 0 and 2 |
| `v` | `(0 4)(2 6)` | coordinates 0 and 4 |
| `uv` | `(0 6)(2 4)` | coordinates 0 and 6 |

The mask of `u` is 5 and the mask of `v` is 17. These even signs are one legal
opposite-cycle pattern: each 2-cycle is uniformly signed, and the two cycles
carry opposite signs. The odd signs of this pair are all positive. On the
trivial character, `X_3` has joint traces `-48, 16, 16, 16` in the eigenvalue
order `((+1,+1), (+1,-1), (-1,+1), (-1,-1))`. The verifier recomputes that row,
and the other published weight rows, before any sign is changed.

## One odd minus

Let `D` be the diagonal sign that is `-1` only on coordinate 1. Set
`u' = u ∘ D`, with `D` applied first. The permutation of `u'` is still
`(0 2)(4 6)`. Its minus signs are coordinates 0, 1, and 2, so the mask is 7.
The product `u' v` is still `(0 6)(2 4)`, now with mask 67. Both operators are
involutions, they commute, they commute with every element of `H`, they fix
sector 3, and `X_3` commutes with every element of the group they generate.
The group is the same set of 64 signed permutations as the group generated by
the unvaried pair and `H`. In particular `u'` lies in the original coset `uH`.

The joint trace on the character `χ` and the eigenvalues `(ε, δ)` is

    tr(X_3 P)
      = (1/64) sum_{h in H} χ(h)
        [tr(X_3 h) + ε tr(X_3 u' h) + δ tr(X_3 v h) + εδ tr(X_3 u' v h)].

As `h` runs through `H`, so does `Dh`. For the trivial character every
coefficient `χ(h)` equals `+1`, and `{u' h : h in H}` equals `{u h : h in H}`
as sets of 16 operators. The same holds for the product coset, because `D`
commutes with `v`. The four unweighted sums are therefore the sums already
computed for `(u, v)`. The trivial-character traces stay

| `(u', v)` eigenvalue | dimension | trace of `X_3` |
|---|---:|---:|
| `(+1, +1)` | 216 | -48 |
| `(+1, -1)` | 216 | 16 |
| `(-1, +1)` | 216 | 16 |
| `(-1, -1)` | 216 | 16 |

The value `-48` remains the trace on the eigenvalue `(+1, +1)`. The
`+1` eigenspace of `X_3` on that 216-dimensional space still has dimension
`(216 + (-48))/2 = 84`, and the `-1` eigenspace still has dimension 132.
Those dimensions are the same arithmetic as in the unvaried pair. This package
does not list a basis of either space.

The sign change is visible on another character. On the weight-1 character
that is `-1` on coordinate 1 and `+1` on coordinates 3, 5, and 7, the same
reindexing multiplies the `u`-eigenvalue by `χ(D) = -1`. The traces move from
`(36, -12, -12, -12)` to `(-12, -12, 36, -12)`, and the dimension 180 moves
from `(+1, +1)` to `(-1, +1)`. The value `-48` does not occur among those four
traces. The weight-1 character that is `-1` only on coordinate 3 is `+1` on
`D`, and its traces stay `(36, -12, -12, -12)`.

The coordinate trace of `u'` is 2, while the coordinate trace of `u` is 4, so
`u'` is not the same operator as `u` on the whole block. The agreement on the
trivial character is the character average, not an unchanged matrix.

## Every odd sign on this u

The same identity applies to every diagonal sign supported on
`{1, 3, 5, 7}`. There are `2^4 = 16` such signs: one for each subset. The
empty subset is the unvaried pair. The other 15 are the odd-sign patterns on
this `u`, with `v` held at mask 17 and with the even minus signs held on
coordinates 0 and 2. Each pattern preserves the permutation `(0 2)(4 6)`,
keeps the product permutation `(0 6)(2 4)`, and uses the same 64 operators.
On the trivial character, all 16 patterns have `X_3` traces
`-48, 16, 16, 16` in the same eigenvalue order. For every character `χ`, the
traces and dimensions equal the unvaried traces and dimensions at eigenvalue
`(ε χ(D), δ)`.

That is the complete list of odd signs that can be added to this `u` without
changing its even signs. The verifier checks the signed-permutation identity,
the fresh address trace of every element, and the joint formula, for every
pattern and every character.

## What is not claimed

The argument uses this query, Bell block 3, the operator `X_3`, the group
`H`, the permutations of the explicit pair, and odd signs added to `u` only.
It does not change the mask of `v`. It does not evaluate the other legal
even-cycle pattern on the same permutation, the pattern with minus signs on
coordinates 4 and 6. Flipping the sign on the single even coordinate 0 is a
different operation: that signed permutation still preserves the query, and
it sends sector 3 to 470, so it is not an endomorphism of this block.

The degree-16 polynomial of a weight-4 summand, and the question of whether a
nontrivial odd-sign pattern splits that polynomial, are untouched. The
characteristic polynomial of the 84-dimensional `X_3 = +1` subspace is
untouched. No trace of `Z_508` was recomputed. A trace that stays equal to
`-48` on one joint space does not say that every cross-coset pair has this
trace, and it does not say that no other mask can move it. The selected
conditional kappa is unchanged.

The rejection controls are part of the verifier, not part of the theorem. The
identity trace on the same 216-space is 216. Dropping one sign whose
contribution to the `(+1, +1)` numerator is nonzero does not reproduce `-48`.
Translation by 1 does not commute with the mask-7 representative. The
coordinate transposition `(0 1)` is not a symmetry of the query.
