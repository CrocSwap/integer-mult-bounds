# Finite ordinary backend replacement on the productive BIT supplier

Scope. This is a conditional composition of the completed ordinary BIT
contracts retained by PR4760 and its source. The raw local/global WORD, its
11400 independent originals, all helper jobs, projector routes and raw mixed
moment are unchanged. The new claim is ordinary BIT b=1737729/500000000.
The complex supplier and selected whole multiplication kappa are unchanged.

Attribution. Finite acyclic ordinary-leaf bootstrapping and its nested row
restoration argument are prior work: rohanarun's PR185, followed by PR199 and
PR204. Their exact historical heads are identified in SOURCES.json and linked in README.md.
They are not claimed as merged or as a new theorem here. We rederive the
composition on the current productive/delayed-cover contract instead of
reusing their historical numerical constants, tapes or acceptance arithmetic.

1. Fixed uniform program and paid cover.
On the unchanged PR4760 native invoice, new exact tail verification at tested
saving0.00347545806 and alpha_gap=2^-39 supplies C0=0.00347545805,
H0=377228190883840, H1=262208150916063859466241 and N=2^H1, under its existing
finite compiler and implementation premises. Set d=2^-36+10^-20 and C1=C0-d.
Choose one fixed odd q>2^45*N^5 avoiding all denominators, determinants and
rank witnesses of this completed finite program. Since 2^45*d>256, its full
bad-class multiplier is below 1+d/4, whereas N^d>=1+d/2. Thus the cover
retains the claimed strict moment. Regenerate all q-dependent route, stock,
precision, semantic-height and machine constants. They may be enormous.
No old 21-bit/raw1e9/tape threshold is reused as a bound for this new program.

2. Backend interface and order of quantifiers.
A_0 is the already completed ordinary BIT supplier of PR4760 with saving
b0=0.0034597. It operates on arbitrary widths and complete chunks, keeps all
preceding/intervening complete spectators, and internally borrows and returns
its row stock. These completed-interface conclusions retain the same source
analytic/native/compiler/tape assumptions; a scalar exponent alone is not
enough. Weighted leaves use four unweighted ordinary atom interchanges, so
this completed A_0 has the required type. Fixed radix ranges are numerically
padded to binary ranges and unpadded at completed endpoints, with volume
factor below four. The backend's internal prime need not equal the outer q.

For j=1,2,3 define a distinct finite algorithm A_j. Use the fixed uniform
supplier above, w=ceil(e^(1/100)), and only the already frozen A_(j-1) for
ordinary atom, routing and high-part exchanges. Freeze its new constants and
eventual threshold before varying e. Coarse recursive calls decrease atom
count; backend calls decrease j. There is no forward/self-reference and no
depth tending to infinity with the input size.

3. Costs and separators.
Put rho=1/100. The three retained root costs of A_j are
  O(V[e^(1-rho)
      + e^((1-rho)*(1-C1)+rho*(1-b_(j-1)))
      + e^rho*log(e)]).
Thus a_j=(1-rho)*C1+rho*b_(j-1). On the1e-10 grid choose
  b1=0.0034753004,
  b2=0.0034754564,
  b3=0.0034754580.
Exact rational checks give, at each j,
  b_(j-1)<b_j<a_j<C1<rho<1-a_j.
The positive rho-a_j and1-rho-a_j gaps pay the linear adapter and stock
logarithm; a_j-b_j pays the declared relaxation. All finite constants are
absorbed at a new sufficiently large threshold, with bounded smaller widths
handled by the completed backend. No physical elapsed-time speedup is claimed.

4. Nested ROW and complete spectators.
The outer stock is W(w)^D*S_old,
  W(w)=B*|GL_N(F_q)|*q^(N^2*(w-1)),
and uses O_(N,B,q)(w log e) borrowed digits. An inner completed backend borrows
from its own argument chunks while keeping the entire outer row/spectator
coordinate fixed, and restores its borrow before returning. Its internal stock
is not charged as externally supplied empty space. The high-part width is
O(w log e); its completed exchange is conservatively O(V*w log e), included
in the third root cost. At each of the three fixed levels, tape counts,
padding factors and constants multiply only finitely. The source completed
ordinary endpoint, not a raw reversed body, is used at every call boundary.

inputs for publication. Independent Fraction checks validate all three
separators, strict gaps and full-prime allowance. Separate source reading

machine-prove those retained implementation assumptions. There is no new
