## Finite-reflection dependency

The finite-group proof inherits a chamber theorem. Nathan Reading, [Lattice theory of the poset of regions](https://nreadin.math.ncsu.edu/papers/regions.pdf), Theorem 10-2.1, states simpliciality for every essential Coxeter arrangement. Proposition 10-2.2 states transitivity of the finite reflection group on its chambers. Theorem 10-2.5 gives the stronger chamber action result. The statements are on printed pages 493–495.

Use this source for the converse chamber step in `source/native-finite-proof/PROOF.md`. The Casselman notes already cited there support the remaining Cartan, rank-three, and classification deductions. The search-indexed primary text was checked on 2026-10-22; the direct PDF fetch timed out. No local proof of the inherited theorem is claimed.
