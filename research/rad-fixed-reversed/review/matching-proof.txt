Original-envelope weighted carrier matching: schedule and copied-center audit
2026-10-08. Scoped construction theorem, not an optimality claim.

Sources and credit
The original event-slot graph and rank-histogram delta are icekylinx's
scripts/partial_swap/match_exported_dag.cpp and the Carrier matching subsection
of notes/partial-swap-construction.tex, inherited from PR18 and subsequent
retained-total producers. The reversible fanout construction is also visible
in scripts/exclusion_circuit.py:compile. PR36 supplies copied retained centers.
This audit permits changing the selected matching inside that SAME original
envelope graph; it does not claim positive-label propagation remains unchanged
after arbitrary changes to its propagation arcs.

1. Precise allowed choices
For an active addition u=a+b choose at most one later use e of one of its
operands v. The use must be the exact gate operand slot (consumer,side), or
the exact designated output index. No use may receive two selected arcs.
Order events by (original-envelope rank, original node index), with an output
index offset past all nodes in the second coordinate. Require donor strictly
before receiver, and E_u subset E_target(e). Also require the unchanged
producer dependencies E_v subset E_u. For the retained envelopes, inclusion
is certified by core_target subset core_u and cover_u subset cover_target.
Sources keep their original triple-line labels, not positive enlargements.
The two operands of an addition have disjoint nonempty scalar support.

The exact source order and source/terminal identity assignments remain fixed.
The partial matching need not have maximum cardinality for validity. Having
the same cardinality as the selected maximum matching is what preserves R.
Merely preserving cardinality, projected donor/target-node pairs, or rank
inequalities is NOT sufficient.

2. Carrier allocation for any such matching
Make one vertex for each use of each scalar value. A selected donor u preserving
v joins the v-use at u to its selected receiver use of v. Every vertex has
indegree at most one; every gate has at most one outgoing selected arc, so at
most one of its operand-use vertices has an outgoing arc. Event order rules
out cycles. For each value, its use vertices split into disjoint directed
paths with at least one head. No value loses its first use.

Allocate one delivery carrier per path head. At an addition, preserve the
operand having the outgoing selected arc, if any, and overwrite the other
operand (the pivot) by their sum. If there is no selected outgoing arc, choose
either pivot. Fan this sum into fresh roles for every other head of the result
value; its first head uses the pivot role. Follow each selected path in order
on its unchanged operand carrier. No two simultaneous gate inputs alias:
the only identifications are between chronologically disjoint uses on these
paths. The addition never overwrites its continuing nonpivot operand.

Let k_x be the number of heads for value x. Sources cost k_x roles, additions
cost k_x-1 fresh roles. Since the original use count is 2c+q and each selected
arc removes one head, total roles are
    sum_x k_x - c = (2c+q-|M|)-c = c+q-|M|.
This construction visits all original gates and all designated uses. It does
not replace a source-to-output path by a direct bypass or change its support.

The schedule_audit.py implementation reconstructs these paths and physical
slots independently of the matching optimizer. It checks all source
coefficient vectors exactly as bitsets, not just numerical scalar samples.
Its dirty-wrapper replay is an additional finite regression, not the proof
of arbitrary-scratch restoration.

3. Arbitrary dirty scratch and both orientations
The resulting scalar schedule L is a composition of invertible elementary
XOR additions, including fanout into initially arbitrary destination roles.
Fresh-zero coefficient correctness is the unchanged intended map JLV.
For arbitrary scratch z, the schedule L,J,L^-1,V,L,J,L^-1,V adds
    JLz + JL(z+Vx) = JLVx
to the destination and returns scratch to z. Thus fresh-zero correctness
does not assume that actual scratch starts clean. Every selected continuation
changes frame by D_(P_target-P_donor), since projectors are nested and
D_P is involutive. All incident scalar roles share the gate's frame. Reversing
the complete schedule uses the inverse scalar gates and complementary frames;
the same nested difference and the same fixed-basis child profile are charged.
The full inherited dirty wrapper and paid endpoint corrections remain in use.

4. Exact matrix-profile delta
Write B(A) for the complete charged ordered block profile of matrix A in the
single fixed I+J basis (B(0)=0). Each legal selected arc u -> e preserving v
replaces the donor's retirement and one independent fresh delivery by one
continuation. Its additive delta is exactly
    B(P_t-P_u) - B(I-P_u) - B(P_v) - B(P_t-P_v).
The three subtractions have distinct event provenance. Matrix profiles must
be certified for every new residual; their ranks alone do not determine cost.
The rank delta is
    (r_t-r_u)-(h-r_u)-r_v-(r_t-r_v) = -h.
The simultaneous changes sum because each donor retirement and each receiver
delivery-head use is removed at most once. The path-head construction proves
the final transition multiset has a nonnegative event realization, even where
different physical events aggregate into the same matrix-key counter.

5. Copied retained-center terminals survive every allowed choice
A designated output use is a terminal vertex, not an addition donor. It has
no outgoing selected arc, even when its scalar root also has ordinary later
consumers. Those consumers have distinct use vertices/carriers. A matched arc
may enter a center output use; it cannot leave it or skip its observation.
The terminal role's last ordinary frame is the output root frame (after the
incoming continuation), and its scalar value is still that root's value.

In both h=23 and h=25 retained producers, the h kind=1 outputs have cores
{i}, full covers, and original envelope U_i of rank h-1. Independently tag
their paid terminal transform P_Ui and old full-identity cleanup before any
profile aggregation. The copied-center schedule keeps the former on a paid
copy and replaces the latter by I-P_Ui, rank one. In either orientation this
is the PR36 local schedule on an arbitrary incoming role state and arbitrary
fixed background frame, so a different incoming carrier changes none of it.

For an arc into a designated center, the term -B(P_v) is a removed fresh
delivery head. It is NOT permission to delete the separately tagged terminal
copy transform, although both can have the same aggregated matrix key. The
per-value bound number_of_incoming_arcs <= uses-1 ensures a head remains;
at additions there are enough ordinary fanout initializations to remove.
Sources have one initialization per head and also retain at least one head.
All h center copy transforms and h rank-one cleanups therefore remain paid.
The rank saving from copying is exactly h(h-1), independently of M.
Thus the copied original-envelope local rank sum remains hR+h(h-1).

6. Proof gates and counterexamples
Required receipt: exact DAG/source pin; unique donor and receiver slots;
same shared operand; strict event order; exact envelope inclusions; full
physical allocation with no simultaneous aliasing; every terminal protected;
all-source output coefficients; all h tagged center copy/cleanup occurrences;
full actual residual profiles certified using the reviewed rank-four CRT bound;
nonnegative final block histogram and unchanged rank/role counts. A complete
characteristic and assembly recalculation is still required for an exponent.

Invalid relaxations include two arcs from one donor (both inputs would have
to remain nonpivots while producing their sum), two arcs into one use (role
aliasing), a backward arc/cycle (no initial path head), an arc preserving the
wrong operand (wrong scalar value), or an output-as-donor arc (overwrites a
protected terminal). Matching only dimension inequalities does not establish
projector nesting. Reusing positive labels computed from different matching
arcs requires a fresh inclusion/propagation audit and is outside this theorem.

The original envelope labels, fixed bases and tensor contraction formulas do
not change under a legal exchange. Genericity is not silently reintroduced:
the new nested residuals must be profiled at the same fixed bases. The old
rank-four low-rank envelope representation applies to any such nested pair,
but its exact actual denominators must still be used by the CRT profiler.
