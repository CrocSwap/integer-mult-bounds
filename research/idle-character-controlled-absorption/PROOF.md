# Character-controlled charts cannot absorb the concrete idle-axis defect

This is a scoped obstruction to a proposed recursive-cost change. It does not
exclude encoders coupling different Fourier characters or a joint rewrite.

## 1. Construction and favorable cost screen

Fix the retained PR3148 T740 allocation, owner replica zero and port zero.
The eight stream inputs are arbitrary complex arrays on `F2^73`, including
arbitrary entering dirt. Move axis `16` from residual child `10` to child `12`.
Their residual ranks become `47,49` instead of `48,48`. Every hosted child,
other residual, interaction, separate translation and exact target stays fixed.

For `p=1-a` with `0<a<1`, strict concavity gives

```
(47/73)^p + (49/73)^p < 2(48/73)^p.
```

Thus, conditional on retaining the rest of the complete recursive invoice and
its stock/normalization, this is a favorable direction in recursive-child cost.
The changed word is not the target operator. PR3220 excludes separately added
whole-child repairs under its fixed-mass tariff. Here the proposed repair
replaces one existing completed phase child, avoiding an additional child call.
PR3229's independent direct-sum sharing has different premises.

At any one of the twenty residual phase sites, grant a replacement child on
one scalar stream with *any* nonzero Fourier eigenvalue function `z(x)`.
Grant an arbitrary invertible stream chart `L(x)` before/after that child with
its exact inverse, independently chosen at every Fourier character `x`:

```
R(x) = L(x) [I + (z(x)-1) E_s] L(x)^-1.
```

Here `E_s` projects onto the selected scalar stream. The chart acts within
one Fourier fiber. It can depend on the address character, but cannot couple
distinct characters. All chart/inverse, movement, source-access, helper,
precision, restoration and stock costs are granted free for this first
algebraic screen. Such free implementations are not asserted to exist.

The reciprocal requirement is part of this contract. Two arbitrary unrelated
maps `L(x),K(x)` could implement the entire repair themselves: for a chosen
invertible child `D(x)`, take `K(x)=D(x)^-1 L(x)^-1 R(x)`. A negative result
about reciprocal charts is therefore not a claim about unrestricted unpaid
nonreciprocal adapters. A paid nonreciprocal construction needs a new bill.

## 2. Necessary pointwise rank condition

Subtracting identity in the displayed contract gives

```
R(x)-I = (z(x)-1) L(x) E_s L(x)^-1,
rank(R(x)-I) <= 1.
```

This holds at each character separately; no common projector across
characters is required. In particular, accepting different rank-one
projector directions at two different characters is consistent with this
contract. A nonzero two-by-two minor at even one character excludes it.

Arbitrary dirty restored spectator streams cannot help. For any number of
such streams the required endpoint is `R(x) direct-sum I`, whose defect has
the same rank. Conjugating one single-stream phase on the extended fiber
still gives rank at most one. Clean ancillas, garbage outputs, an additional
phase use or encoders coupling different Fourier characters are other models.
The argument applies to arbitrary input columns, not only a clean subspace.

## 3. Required replacement at the literal cut

Write the altered chronological word at a candidate site as
`B(x) D_site(x) A(x)`, with earlier factors on the right. Omit only that site's
residual phase, retaining its separately executed translation, the other
child at the same event and every hosted phase. Its required replacement is
unique:

```
T(x) = i^popcount(x) I_8,
R_site(x) = B(x)^-1 T(x) A(x)^-1.
```

The exact check reconstructs `B D_site A` and checks it against the altered
word; it checks `B R_site A=T` on all eight independent input columns. It also
checks the original unchanged word equals `T` with exact global phase.
These equalities include independent arbitrary stream dirt.

This is the retained eight-stream projection at completed-child boundaries.
Helper-only bank padding and family translations are outside that projection,
as in the pinned physical interpreter; their implementations are inherited,
not freshly verified here. The spectator rank argument is a separate endpoint
statement and does not claim a replay of every underlying helper circuit.

PR3254 excluded fixed scalar stream encoders. Its exceptional receiver site
12 has the following two required defects, using zero-based indices:

```
R_12(16)-I       = (i-1)(E_(6,4)+E_(6,6)),
R_12(33554432)-I = (i-1)E_(0,0).
```

Each is rank one. Their different projector directions exclude a fixed
chart, but do not exclude the character-controlled contract in section 1.
This distinction is the reason for the present changed-premise test.

At the combined character `x*=16 XOR 33554432=33554448`, the new exact
source-bound reconstruction gives

```
R_12(x*)-I = (i-1)(E_(0,0)+E_(6,4)+E_(6,6)).
```

Its rows `(0,6)` and columns `(0,4)` have minor `(i-1)^2=-2i`, which is
nonzero. The receiver therefore fails even though the chart can change at
every character. The formula at the combined character is established by
literal reconstruction; it is not inferred by adding the earlier samples.

The same character gives a nonzero two-by-two minor at **every one of the
twenty sites**. `RESULT.json` records the full required matrices, selected
rows/columns and exact Gaussian-rational determinants. The checker covers
exactly four characters `{0,16,33554432,33554448}`, twenty cuts and all eight
input columns. Each witness refutes a necessary full-domain identity; a
passing finite sample would not establish a construction.

## 4. Conclusion and remaining boundary

No one-site replacement in this reciprocal, character-preserving chart
contract realizes the favorable profile change. The conclusion quantifies
over arbitrary invertible character-dependent stream charts and arbitrary
nonzero phase functions, regardless of the rank or axes proposed for the
replacement child. It remains true with arbitrary dirty restored spectator
streams. It concerns this transfer, owner, port and fixed surrounding word.

Mixing different Fourier characters, paying for nonreciprocal adapters,
replacing at least two sites, changing interactions or using other transfers
remains outside scope. Those routes require a concrete arbitrary-dirt
endpoint and their complete recursive/source/frame/routing/volume/helper/
precision/restoration/stock costs. Unknown costs are not zero. This result
neither closes those mechanisms nor establishes a supplier or new kappa.
