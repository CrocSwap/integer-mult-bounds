## 1. What has actually been instantiated

The p3 complex unit is a complete native word, not just a histogram. The
independent audits verify its serialized scalar controller, 3,136 physical
main-contact maps and their absolute phases, 672 complete main endpoints,
2,856 actual local helper roles, causal idle windows, every paid completion,
and the final order of whole-field normalizers. Its productive targets are

```
{6:192, 7:56, 8:93, 9:1229, 25:448, 57:224}.
```

There are 2,242 independent productive fields, one explicitly owned center,
and paid children

```
{1:17623, 2:5065, 3:1738, 4:714, 5:254}.
```

All productive inputs are arbitrary. The owned center is initialized and
cleared; it is never an additional credited output. Every helper endpoint
is autonomous at the completed local boundary. Independent normalizers
therefore surround an entire local call, without changing the frames of
internal ADD instructions.

The exact integer precompiler in `AUDIT.md` turns this unit into a finite
uniform word with parameters

* ambient rank `N >= 2`;
* `W` productive arrays, each receiving the **full** rank-`N` transform;
* `P` rank-one paid children;
* `U` copies of the mixed unit in the completely expanded finite word;
* at most `O=U` owned centers, conservatively one per mixed-unit instance.

For every rational `z` strictly above the mixed root and below one, the
constructive count argument provides an `N` with `P < W*N^z`.
“Finite” here means fixed before the eventual multiplication input grows.
It can be astronomically large. Neither this note nor the receipts claim
to have materialized the winning near-threshold instance. The later
`../controller/EXPLICIT-TEMPLATE-BOUNDS.md` strengthens the existence
argument to the specified complex rank `N=2^276540184361`, with uniform
saving `0.002304`; no search for a successful rank is left unperformed.
The integer recursion defines its exact finite `W,P,U`, and the constants
below are computable functions of that finite result.

The fully materialized, depth-zero `N=57` example has `W=2242`, `P=127570`,
and `U=1`. It is a useful independent check that complementary source jobs
are really paid: `57*2242-127570=224`. This small example is not claimed to
attain the limiting mixed exponent.

## 2. Every column count and every complete spectator field

The local logical identities were replayed in a free noncommuting operator
word algebra. Apart from rational scalar arithmetic, the checker uses only
composition and cancellation of an operator with its recorded inverse.
For any positive integer `f`, substitute the `f`-column physical operator
for each such generator. This extends to an algebra homomorphism on the
free word algebra and preserves the checked identities.

This is not the false assertion that tensor power is additive on arbitrary
matrix sums. The role-level scalar word is unchanged. Each physical address
generator is replaced by its column lift, while its formal products and
linear combinations are evaluated on the same independent role arrays.

A rank-zero native monomial has the form

```
|x> -> i^(a+q(x)) |M*x+b>,
```

with invertible binary `M` and a permitted quadratic phase `q`. Its column
lift has blockwise address map `M`, translation `b` in every column, and
phase `a*f + sum_j q(x_j)` modulo four. This explicitly retains the absolute
phase. A fixed coordinate matcher in the precompiler becomes the same
fixed permutation of `f`-axis blocks. All untouched axes remain complete
spectator fields. A rank-one leaf becomes the standard rank-`f` child.

The retained exact Clifford normal form supplies the monomial factors
around each child. The independent p3 audit checks the whole main boundary,
including the output translations required by negative powers of `C_q`.
The fixed THREE/base selection is valid for every column count: THREE/base
and THREE/extra have identical entrance and exit operators; their difference
is an internal frame and its associated warps. No layer-dependent selector
is required for the endpoint contract.

For any integer input width `e=N*f+r`, first apply the `r<N` individual
kernels and preserve these axes as completed spectators. Then apply the
lifted finite word on the consecutive `N*f` active axes. Its final endpoint
is the full selected-axis tensor, so the separately completed remainder
commutes with it. This gives the ordinary arbitrary-width interface and
strict child ratio at most `1/N`.

## 3. An actual new coefficient charge

`precision_closure.py` reads the authenticated serialized forward and
backward native words. For every scalar macro it independently checks that
the coefficient is dyadic and that the elementary allowance dominates its
logarithmic row-norm and denominator-height increment. For an ADD with
coefficient `a`, a sufficient height increment is

```
ceil(log2(1+abs(a))) + log2(denominator(a)).
```

For a dyadic SCALE replace `1+abs(a)` by `max(1,abs(a))`. COPY and CLEAR
have zero height increment and still retain their implementation cost.
The audit reproduces the native real primitive allowances 158,120 forward
and 158,168 backward, and the complete p3 arithmetic charge 1,147,304.
In particular, the six boundary swaps contribute their six additional
negations; they have not been priced as signed swaps.

Address permutations and fourth-root unit phases do not increase magnitude
or denominator height. We nevertheless add an explicit conservative charge
of eight for every non-scalar native record and every listed whole-field
normalization. The respective counts are:

| Kind | Count |
| --- | ---: |
| Non-scalar records in the seven native calls | 36,260 |
| Complete helper normalizations | 2,856 |
| Main contact monomials | 3,136 |
| Complete main endpoint normalizations | 672 |
| Paid completion normalizations | 460 |

This gives the complete elementary coefficient/unit-phase envelope

```
G_unit = 1,147,304 + 8*(36,260+2,856+3,136+672+460)
       = 1,494,376.
```

Eight bounds the two monomial factors around a native child, each of which
needs at most a sign and an `i` phase. Some records are just annotations, so
this deliberately overcounts. The pointwise phase descriptor may have
larger fixed geometry, but each resulting coefficient is still a unit.

Whole-job coordinate matchers in the finite compiler have zero coefficient
height. The canonical positive tensor jobs can be placed on disjoint axes
by binary coordinate permutations; there is no new scalar mixing at these
boundaries. The final scalar/phase charge is therefore bounded by

```
G = 1,494,376 * U.
```

This charge is not a promise that an arbitrary binary address map costs
eight tape steps. Every actual matrix has its separately paid native
row-addition decomposition and compact movement implementation. The finite
number of those operations is another fixed constant of the expanded word.

## 4. Explicit linear semantic guard

Put `Z=W+O` and replace the old constants by

```
E  = 64*(Z+N+P+G+1)^3,
B  = P+E,
C0 = 32*N*B^2,
C1 = 1.
```

In particular

```
2*G*Z^2 + 8*P + 4*Z + 4 + 32*N < E.
```

The receipt verifies this exact inequality. It is deliberately much larger
than necessary: it pays the full elementary word, unit phases, role and
owned-field passes, and fewer than `N` remainder kernels. The inclusion of
`P` in the fresh cubic bound avoids relying on any old relation between the
paid-child count and the number of physical fields.

Let `A(e)` bound both temporary magnitude exponent and binary denominator
excess relative to the incoming common fine grid. A **completed** forward
or inverse `u`-axis child has coefficients in `2^-u Z[i]` and absolute row
sum at most `2^u`; the unit sign wrappers preserve those bounds. Thus its
returned stream costs at most `u` in each semantic measure. Its larger
internal temporary values do not become unfinished parent operands.

At a node of width `e`, set `f=floor(e/N)`. At most `P` completed child calls
have preceded any active child; their total selected width is at most
`P*f`. Their input contents may be arbitrary, including data on the owned
centers. All scalar growth and remainder kernels fit in `E`. Consequently

```
A(e) <= A(f) + P*f + E.
```

For an elementary leaf, `A(e)<=8e`. Since `N>=2`, `B=P+E`, and `E>=64`,

```
2*B*(N-1) >= P+E,
2*B*f+P*f+E <= 2*B*N*f <= 2*B*e.
```

Induction gives `A(e)<=2B e`. Completed disjoint selected-axis pieces have
total width at most `d`. The retained prefix kernels, outer phases and
normalization add at most `18d`, giving

```
A_layer <= (2*B+18)*d < C0*d.
```

Every calculation remains on one fixed fine grid. A child is never rounded,
compressed, or re-encoded at return. The change is to the proof's bound on a
completed value, not to its stored representation. Other fields remain
parked while the child executes. This is why the linear semantic bound
survives a huge scalar word and why the older pathwise `C1>1` estimate is
unnecessary.

The stopping rule can be any fixed rational `0<beta<1`. Above the eventual
threshold `d^beta>=2N`, every internal call has the required positive `f`;
the completed-leaf estimate remains `8e` at the stopping leaves. No old
numerical cutoff is carried over.

### An effective threshold, including enormous constants

Suppose `d<=c*p^epsilon`, with fixed `c>=1` and rational
`epsilon=a/b` in `(0,1)`. Let `L` be the binary bit length of
`4*C0*ceil(c)` and choose

```
t = max(4, ceil(b*L/(b-a))),       p0 = 2^t.
```

For all `p>=p0`, `p^(1-epsilon)>4*C0*c`, so the integer guard
`Delta=C0*d` is at most `p/4`. Signed components with `p+Delta` fractional
bits and `Delta+3` magnitude/sign bits have width

```
p+2*Delta+3 <= 2p.
```

The audit emits `p0` as a compressed power of two. It does not try to
allocate that many bits. This construction proves the eventual `O(p)` word
bound for the new finite template, including the current outer choice
`epsilon=99999/100000`. Huge fixed constants change `p0`, not `C1` or the
power in the final exponent inequalities.

## 5. Productive stock, owned storage, and native tape closure

For the ordinary uniform complex word, let

```
D(e)=0 if e<N;  D(e)=1+D(floor(e/N)) otherwise,
q0=ceil(log2 W)*D(d).
```

Use the original compact front and back fields separately. The first `q0`
reserved complete chunks provide a physically preceding row field. Their
individual selected-axis kernels need not be completed beforehand: the
active invocation acts as identity on these coordinates for arbitrary
incoming contents. In the support-local outer wrapper their kernels must
be deferred to later phases, as explained in `OUTER-TRANSFER-AUDIT.md`.
The row field's cardinality is
`R=2^(q0*K)>=W^D(d)` for `K>=1`. Pad complete rows once to the next multiple
of `W^D(d)`. The padding factor is below two. Every descendant then has
exactly `1/W` of its parent's productive volume and enough divisibility for
its remaining depth, with complete active, control and spectator fields.
All-row endpoint correctness restores appended zero rows to zero.

Owned storage contributes no factor to productive output credit. A safe
choice is one full center field per finite mixed-unit instance, namely
`O=U`. At an ordinary recursion node each such field has the same shape as
one productive role stream, of volume `V/W`. Initialize, retain and clear
it as its actual word prescribes. If simultaneously suspended units keep
live centers, they receive distinct banks. Reusing one bank per recursion
depth would not suffice. Owned-space scans and parking cost fixed multiples
of volume; paid calls on those banks are already in `P`.

The integer audit checks row divisibility and one-time padding at several
floor-depth boundaries. It also gives an explicit sufficient threshold
for `q0<d/4`. In general `q0=O(log d)` with a possibly huge fixed
coefficient. This does not change the compact-field reservation exponents.
If an inherited implementation uses separate simultaneous BIT stock, its
factor must also be retained in the row product; taking only the maximum
of two stocks would be invalid. The delayed BIT cover has its own updated
row-reserve argument and must not be charged to old constants.

Each fixed binary matrix factors into finitely many row additions and
coordinate swaps. `notes/compact-control-movement.tex` implements these on
complete packed rectangles, with the actual compact cost and polynomial
descriptor preparation. The finite compiler's matrices have fixed rank
`N`; their `f`-column lifts are therefore fixed block recipes, not dynamic
RAM gathers. They preserve the retained native operation alphabet.

The depth-first fixed-tape execution parks unused role streams, their
owned banks and descriptors while a child runs. The numbers of roles,
tapes, routers and instruction types are fixed constants of this one
template. The ordinary tree has at most `P^D(d)=d^O(1)` nodes. Polynomial
description/setup costs still have a fixed degree and are absorbed by the
retained superpolynomial record fiber when `epsilon<1`. That absorption
needs a new eventual threshold if the degree grows; it does not authorize
an unpriced RAM-to-tape conversion.

## 6. Consequence for the new outer calculation

After the finite precompilation and the native phase/ownership audits,
the complex supplier fits the retained ordinary uniform interface with
moment `(P/W)*N^-sigma<1`. All real primitive, movement, center and row
constants are replaced as above. Its precise stopping and internal-cost
exponents are still the retained weighted-tree bounds; only their constant
multipliers and eventual cutoffs change. The nearly critical outer ledger
can therefore use `C1=1` with the new complex saving.

This closes the **complex finite-template** precision and stock obligation
under the inherited native movement and outer hypotheses. A complete
conditional multiplication claim additionally needs the independently
bound BIT uniform interface, the strict mixed moments, and the actual outer
parameter inequalities. Those are separate receipts rather than implicit
consequences of the favorable complex histogram.
