# Signed recursive-invoice preflight and a current exchange-family ceiling

**Reusable arithmetic tool, scoped budget proof and independent formal-row audit;
no new supplier or selected exponent.** Canonical base:
`1ec1119972c9aefe42525172d07b332fcff81cb4`, selected conditional κ=.0030217758.

The practical result is a cheap check before extending a construction:
compare its complete **paid and target** histograms. The new shared CLI gives an
exact certificate of improvement, equality, worsening or inconclusive concave
order, covering every power r^(1-C), 0<C<1. It needs no root search, native word
replay or compiler. See the [guide](../../docs/research/recursive-invoice-preflight.md).
The mathematics is classical and credited; the new work is the portable signed
interface, its independent tests and its current source-bound application.
Concurrent [#5391](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/5391) supplies
the later receipt-compatibility/funding oracle. This preflight does not duplicate
its evidence join or claim to fund a candidate from declared histograms.

## Why this is timely

Recent contributions give a sharp contrast: #5350's
largest scalar deletion worsens its recursive bill, #5383 improves it by owning
relations while surrendering helper output credit, and #5387/#5390 tighten the
compiler with that same bill. A scalar count, a paid-only histogram or another decimal
root does not distinguish these cases.

The newest centre-paired relation method also has a small, provable remaining
budget. Optimistically grant **all 144** original exchanges
`−H1−H8 paid / −H9 targets`; its complete raw crossing is below
**.00302728824994**. The 128-exchange #5383/#5387 bill crosses in
**(.00302667476807,.00302667476808)**. Thus this exact lever has less than
**.00000061348187** raw saving left, and even its optimistic maximum misses .0034.
The latest #5390 claimed endpoint is within 5.6×10^-15 of this fixed-profile raw
upper bracket. All basis choices or schedules with this same invoice obey that budget.

This gives an immediate stopping rule for a .0034-oriented continuation of this
exchange family: change the paid ranks, target debits or caller before building
another endpoint. It does not retire other ownership or relation methods.
The [proof](PROOF.md) and [directed receipt](RESULT.json) retain that distinction.

## Independent audit of the fixed eight relations

An independent interpreter checks every one of the source's 3,298 paid events
in each orientation. After the exact eight backward relations, it finds exactly
the eight known zero climbs and eight known proportional same-frame centre
snapshots. It finds no additional such opportunity. The unchanged forward
contract has neither. All historical snapshots are favorably available; each
actual nonzero match is unique with factor +1.

This is exhaustion **in the retained formal free-word grammar**. New native
identities, scalar combinations, relation spans, preparation programs and event
graphs are outside the census. It does not establish physical non-equality.

[Ali Romman's live claim](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/discussions/5125#discussioncomment-19154639)

and applies to the proposed
[forward extension](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/discussions/5125#discussioncomment-19154367)

## Reproduce

Python 3.12, standard library, from the repository root:

```sh
python3 -B tests/test_compare_recursive_invoices.py
python3 -B scripts/research_package.py --package research/owned-relation-exhaustion
python3 -B scripts/compare_recursive_invoices.py \
  research/owned-relation-exhaustion/examples/source.json \
  research/owned-relation-exhaustion/examples/exchange-128.json
```

The registered replay executes normal and optimized interpreters, reconstructs
the full source invoice, checks exact root brackets and a rational interval
nonfunding cover, reruns the independent formal-row census, rejects five budget/
census defects and compares the complete deterministic receipt. The CLI tests
include 625 exhaustive signed small profiles, direct discrete-slope tariffs,
unequal masses, changed contracts and malformed-input controls.

All scientific inputs are self-contained committed exports with exact hashes in
[SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json), including #5383's unchanged witness JSON. The replay
does not require that pending PR's Git objects or a network fetch. The shared CLI
is an explicit replay input. Normal/O success corroborates the written proof;
it is not independent human or proof-kernel acceptance.
Executed environments and the shallow-history limitation are recorded in
[VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md).

Full physical/native phase replay of the source word, compiler and BIT supplier,
physical tape/precision execution, analytic decomposition and the full selected
cold replay are not rerun. No supplier
admission, practical speedup or selected-record update is claimed.
