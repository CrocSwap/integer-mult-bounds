# An actual involutive transfer on the current 632-field source

This ports the eight supported line-repair mechanism of PR #1931 to the current Cao Yifan round-three source, through PR #4929's helper-clean center-free word. It proves a forward local producer with its actual paid profile. It does not establish a new κ or a physical raw-dual implementation.

Let R be the checked 8 × 112 E8 observation matrix, K = Rᵀ/4, and A = I − KR. The verifier binds these matrices to the actual deleted-center error on all 632 independent input fields: E_X = −KR. It checks RK = 14 I.

Choose the eight ports 0, 7, 20, 23, 86, 93, 108 and 111. Let P equal K on these eight rows and zero elsewhere. The exact check RP = I certifies a supported right inverse. Put U = K − 12P and F = I − UR = A + 12PR. Then RU = 2 I, so

    F² = I − 2UR + U(RU)R = I.

Both U and R have rank 8 because RU = 2 I. Hence F − I has rank 8, its negative eigenspace has dimension 8 and its positive eigenspace has dimension 104. This is an involution, not a claim of orthogonality or unit condition number. All coefficients are fixed rationals; fixed-word multiplication/division must use the project's admitted scalar tariff.

## The actual forward word

First execute the source-bound center-free forward word with the signed ground helper precompensation from PR #4929. Its exact normalized endpoints are X unchanged, Y ← Y + AX, and every arbitrary helper unchanged. Physically, every X exit is the common full frame, whereas Y_i exits at its rank 8 hyperplane F_i.

For each of the eight selected rows i:

1. Clear the already owned C0 at the common full frame.
2. Gather C0 = 12 Σ_j (PR)_ij X_j there, using ordinary ADDs between equal frames.
3. Apply the exact full-to-F_i Gaussian transport to C0. Its child rank is 1.
4. Add C0 into Y_i at F_i and clear C0.

The repair affects only those eight Y fields. Consequently the complete endpoint is X unchanged and Y ← Y + FX; all 408 borrowed helpers retain their arbitrary inputs and original exit charts, all 632 persistent fields retain their original physical ABI, and C0 is zero. No borrowed field is erased or overwritten.

The complete paid profile is 2,209 rank 1, 412 rank 2, 510 rank 3, 127 rank 4 and 40 rank 5 children. In particular, the eight original rank 8 center children remain absent and eight rank 1 repairs have been added. The full literal word, its frame IDs, repairs and finite-code lowering circuits are retained in INVOLUTIVE-WORD.json.gz.

The compiler separately constructs each lowering operator as C(F_i) C(FULL)⁻¹ and factors it into two native monomial maps and one rank 1 Gaussian child. Explicit checks compare both the complete Clifford tableau and the exact zero-input state; together these check the operator including its global phase. The kernel identity is fixed on the nine-axis native block and extends by its admitted native tensor interpretation; it is not an empirical numerical comparison. The semantic replay checks every arbitrary persistent input column, not only zero helpers. Sign mutation and omission of the paid lowering are rejected. The replay is not a Lean kernel certificate.

## Precision and scope

This is a fixed finite word with fixed coefficients, monomial maps and unitary Gaussian children. In the maximum over fields of the fiber Euclidean norm, each ADD by c has operator norm at most 1 + |c|, COPY and CLEAR have norm at most 1 under their owned-field contracts, and the native unitary maps have norm 1. The product of these finitely many factors bounds every contiguous suffix independently of the fiber width. Thus a standard per-operation rounding allocation uses O(log n) guard precision for n-bit inputs under the source's precision convention. An entrywise norm conversion additionally includes the square root of the fiber volume; its logarithm is linear in the fiber width. The recorded prefix bound 457 is a state bound, not a claimed bound for every rounding suffix.

The abstract inverse-transpose of the normalized forward shear is X ← X − FᵀY, Y unchanged. Although (Fᵀ)² = I, that fact alone does not supply the correctly typed physical backward word. This release does not claim one. Distinct routed stages have different normalizers, so F² = I does not by itself close a recursive controller. No new κ is claimed.

## Source-mutating unit-transfer corollary

The separate UNIT-TRANSFER-WORD.json.gz appends eight serial source updates I - u_k r_k at the common full X exit frame. Since r_l u_k = 2 delta_lk, the cross terms between distinct updates vanish, and their product is F. Its exact endpoint is X_new = FX, Y_new = Y + X_new.
