# Bounded routes screened in this session

The user requested a substantial kappa increase, with frequent commits and
PRs. No increase was established. These screens preceded the all-p result
in PROOF.md and do not supply additional global obstructions.

1. A logical ADD commutation/combination pass on the exact PR4470 native
   words combined 70 scalar updates in each orientation, reducing each
   emitted word from 21,461 to 21,363 events. The complete paid histogram
   remained `{1:2237,2:388,3:494,4:147,5:36,8:8}`. This scalar reduction
   has no claimed kappa gain and is not promoted as a changed supplier.
2. A local discovery screen allowed adjacent commuting persistent ADDs
   sharing one role to exchange order, with their two frames jointly
   selected from the existing 1,224-frame catalogue under all neighboring
   monotone constraints. No strict improving move was found. This is a
   finite floating screen, not a barrier for longer words or new frames.
3. Exact Laurent screens deleted the initial full F stage, final full F
   stage, or both from the balanced-cut controller, for p=1 through 8.
   All 24 changed words failed the constant-basis monomial factor test;
   all eight unchanged positive controls passed. No losing word is priced
   as a supplier. This does not exclude other replacements for those stages.
4. Independent per-stage idle choices were tested at small p. All 32
   individual stage subsets at p=6 were admissible as one-variable
   projectors, so that screen alone did not reject the model. Joint
   commutation supplied the missing constraint. The resulting interval
   argument, not the finite optimizer's failure to improve, proves the
   general normal form and cost domination in PROOF.md.

The published package contains the exact source-bound controls supporting
the theorem. The discarded construction screens are discovery observations,
not dependencies or claimed native/phase/outer validation. The new result
specifically says to stop expanding independent idle choices in this fixed
path. It does not justify stopping work on a changed native word, query
