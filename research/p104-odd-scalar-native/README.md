## The move, in plain language
* **What 1/3 and 1/5 are.** Until now every coefficient of a native word is a power of two or a ratio of powers of two. Here a coefficient may also have a denominator 3 or 5 (and products: 9, 15, 25, 45, 75, 225), and a SCALE gate may be by such a unit. The word has **3,001 such events** (59 SCALE, 2,942 ADD), **6,002 scans per call** (one multiply-and-check and one division each), priced separately from the reviewed scalar count.
* **What they buy.** Ten more helper splits (−55.24 per call in the kernel term) and 39 stops merged into rank-2 jobs (−54.07 per call). Twenty of the 39 stops are made by a legal reordering of commuting events (`reorder_check.py`); the other 19 are present in the word before it (data/stage_before_stops.json.gz). With the dyadic rule the verifier rejects the word at event 50 (`controls.py`).
* **The 2^k ± 1 arithmetic.** 3 = 2² − 1 and 5 = 2² + 1, so multiplying by either is one shift and one add or subtract. Exact division by an odd d (3, 5 and the products above) is one pass over the bits of the stored integer from the least significant bit with a carry below d; a nonzero remainder is flagged at the end of the pass (`division_pass.py`: widths 1 to 12, 65,520 cases, all 59,020 non-divisible inputs flagged). To run a coefficient a/b with b = 2^x 3^y 5^z: multiply by a, check divisibility by 3^y 5^z, divide.
* **Why nothing is rounded.** Every root input is multiplied once by S = 225 = 3² 5² at the root. S times every prefix row and every product q·row (formed before an ADD) then has dyadic entries, so every checked division is exact; at the exit the division by S is exact because every public row is dyadic. S = 225 is the least certificate (75, 45, 15, 25, 9, 1 fail). The 3-adic and 5-adic heights are 2 and 2 and no other prime occurs.

## What is declared as a framework change (details in `PROOF.md`)
* **Changed clauses:** the dyadic scalar alphabet (`local.py:146,149`, the verifier copies, `native.py:65`); the Gaussian dyadic text of `03-motifs.tex:57,556` for the internal arrays of this parent word only; the #6030-specific scalar constants of #6900 and #6901; one added operation (multiply, check divisibility, divide) at `05-layers.tex:510-513`. The clause "the first 8 SCALEs are the hosted-port gauge" of the dyadic words' joint check is not used (it is false for this word; the gauge is covered by the exact endpoint); `check.py` prints it as information.
* **Replacement:** coefficients and SCALE units in A = Z[1/2, 1/3, 1/5], |q|+1 < 2^16 kept, a declared S = 3^e 5^g certifying every prefix row, every pre-ADD product and (dyadically) every public row; rational operations stay local to the parent word and completed endpoints stay Gaussian dyadic on every public role.
* **Penalty:** none in S; T and P change as counted above. Width only: **15 bits of 2-adic denominator and 37.48 bits of magnitude per unfinished context** (S itself is 7.8 of them), absorbed in W = p + h + 2D + 3 by the written inequality 82 t < d, **which assumes K_star and the caller mass are recomputed for this word; they are not**. Odd scans priced separately; root conversion charged to entry and exit.
* **Premise:** exactness is **conditional on exact children** (a premise the dyadic words share). If a child return is truncated, this word is rejected by its own divisibility check, at event 26,563 for g = 44 dropped bits and at event 24,158 for g = 50 (`physical_exact.py`); the dyadic baseline word under the same truncation runs to the end and is wrong on 830 of 1,441 public rows. Fallback: rounded division, with a measured exit-error gain over the stored truncation unit of 2^14.65 (g = 44) and 2^14.64 (g = 50) against 2^14.76 and 2^16.18 for the dyadic parent, measured on the earlier stage word on one input and not re-measured on this word; the rounded-return error budget is open for both.
* **Unverified here:** K_star and the caller mass; a rounded child return; the actual P_r, the physical CLEAR and the LAMBDA pricing (as for dyadic words). The integer run is one column with generic commuting frames, not true Clifford frames and not tensor width 2.

## Disclosures
* **Five ADD mutants are not caught.** Of 57,023 ADD doublings (and sign flips) 57,018 are caught by the second engine; the other five (events 35583, 35627, 35628, 36299, 41169) leave the exact endpoint unchanged: four are dead stores into owned register 1459 (never read before its CLEAR; the same four ADDs occur in the stage word), one is a coefficient-neutral temporary. `mutants.py` shows each by exact replay. They are equivalent mutants, not holes, and removing the four dead stores would be a free micro-gain not taken here.
* **Validated by replay only:** 120 events rewritten by the retiming and 76 events removed by the final same-frame rewrite (`reorder_check.py`; the commutation test covers the other 66,895 matched events: no pair of events sharing a register with a writer changes order).
* **Adjustments to retained checkers, exactly:** `native.py` lines 65-67 (power-of-two denominator and numerator tests replaced by 2,3,5-smooth tests, and a helper `_sm` at lines 15-20); `verify_relaxed.py` and `verify_strict.py` lines 36-37 (same relaxation, helper at lines 7-12); `verify_host3_odd.py` against `verify_host3_dyadic.py` (docstring; helper lines; the SCALE clause; the ADD and COPY clause with the S certificate and the pre-ADD product test; the public-row test). The strict verifier still rejects the word at exactly the 23 owned-ascent events and nothing else. The physical streams (`physical.py`, `caller_physical.py`) are unmodified: they work mod p, so they do not test divisibility; divisibility is covered by `physical_exact.py` and the S certificate.
* **Not claimed:** maintainer acceptance of the clause, of A6, of the per-port root, of LAMBDA priced as ordinary work, or of the BIT admission; no adjoint physical replay; no streams at tensor width 2 or more; the new word was not reviewed by a second reader beyond the checks listed.
