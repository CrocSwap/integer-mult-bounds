# Three-total root-unit decoders (partial, no κ claim): reductions, the sharp example tied_min and the lift route

> **What is claimed, and what is not.** This package is deliberately partial. It concerns exact decoders with **T = 3 totals** under the cost formula of Theorem C of #446 (closed, not merged; the model is carried in the merged #595, FL C.6), in the setting of the merged #1590 (`research/root-unit-decoder-lemmas`: Lemma A for T ≤ 2, the lift identity Lemma B, ERR1, the D5 CNF); the DRAT certificate of that D5 CNF is the subject of the pending #2031 and is not duplicated here.
> **Claimed.**
> (1) PROVEN, every configuration: two reductions (Lemma SCC, Lemma Peel), monotonicity under deleting lines, and the structure of a minimal counterexample to (A) (§2).
> (2) PROVEN, partial theorems: Theorem M1 (cost ≥ v/2 when no point has full support, any field), Theorem U (unit world: certificate ≥ |G| + |I|/2 ≥ v/2), and the F2S rowspace identity cost = 3ρ − Σ w_j ≥ 2ρ (§3).
> (3) PROVEN with one computer-assisted step, CHECKED exactly: tied_min, a 12-line configuration whose cost is exactly v = 12 (exchange proof, all 217 optima, all of them lift to Q) (§4).
> (4) CHECKED: every order, label, multi-copy and per-scale 2-adic certificate stops at v − 1 = 11 on tied_min, so no certificate of that kind proves (A); the multi-copy certificate does reach 20 ≥ v on the 19-line cex_min_1 where the one-copy certificate stops at 18 (§5).
> (5) PROVEN and CHECKED, the lift route: Lemma W′ (a TU signing gives cost ≥ v), Theorem 9.3 (joint signability is one linear system over F2), the two failure modes (one block not signable; a purely three-block Čech relation), and exact counterexamples: whole-matrix TU fails at v = 9, non-regular minimisers, K22-freeness does not give TU-signability, the peeling induction (Conjecture PP) is false (§6).
> (6) The two experiments, X1 (joint signing) and X2 (Hesse configuration over Q(ω)), are reported in §7 and §8:
> **X1 = OPEN (G1′).** The statement "some cost-minimiser admits one signing with all three blocks TU" (G1-joint) is false: exact counterexamples at v = 7 and v = 10, and 14 instances in all, with (A) checked to hold on every one. What survives is the rank-preserving lift G1′ (a same-support rational lift with rank_Q = rank_F2 on each block), which is open, as is its parent (Q ≤ F2) (§7, §10).
> **X2 = NONE FOUND.** No tested rank-3 decoder built on the Hesse configuration over Q(ω) costs less than v (counts in §8): (A) also holds on all tested Hesse geometry, so the lack of a field order is not supported as the mechanism. Recorded evidence, not a proof.
>
> **Not claimed.** No κ gain, no word. **(A) — cost ≥ v for every exact three-total decoder — is not proved and not refuted over Q.** No new window is closed: M1 and U give v/2 only, and only for restricted types. Nothing here says the E8 frontier cannot be improved.

## 0. Reading this package

Every result has one of three statuses:

- **PROVEN**: a proof is in this README or in the cited section of `evidence/` (a verbatim snapshot of our working cards; the proof is the claim, the scripts only sanity-check it on instances);
- **CHECKED**: recomputed by `verify.sh` (exact integer / rational / F2 arithmetic, exhaustive enumeration, or a small CNF) — the check group is named. **CHECKED (recorded)** marks a machine result of our own working cards (CP-SAT or SA run, long enumeration) that `verify.sh` does **not** repeat; the card, script and log line are named, and the stored certificate is verified wherever one exists;
- **OPEN**: a precisely stated problem, or an argument that is not a proof.

## 1. Model (as in #1590 and #446 Theorem C)

A configuration is a list of lines t = 1…v, each with a point p(t) ∈ Q³ and a line q(t) ∈ Q³ with p(t)·q(t) ≠ 0. A **pairing** B is a 0/1 matrix over F2 with unit diagonal and B_ts = 1 (s ≠ t) only where p(t)·q(s) = 0 (*admissible*). An **exact decoder** with T = 3 totals is M = Σ_{j≤3} p_j q_jᵀ over Q (p_j the j-th coordinate vector over the lines, so M_ts = p(t)·q(s)) with M_tt ≠ 0 and M_ts = 0 wherever B_ts = 1. Write R_j = supp p_j, A_j = supp q_j, D_j = R_j ∩ A_j, r_j = rank_F2 B[R_j, A_j], and π(t) = {j : p_j(t) ≠ 0}, σ(t) = {j : q_j(t) ≠ 0}, D(t) = π(t) ∩ σ(t) ≠ ∅. Theorem C prices the decoder at Σ_j max(w, r_j) ≥ **cost := Σ_j r_j**; we drop the floor w, so a lower bound on Σ_j r_j is the stronger statement. The incidence t → s (t ≠ s) means p(t)·q(s) = 0; only entries inside some block R_j × A_j matter.

Facts used from the earlier work (all PROVEN; proofs in `evidence/T3-PROOF.md` §§2–3 and in #1590, whose lemmas are cited, not reproved):

- **Lemma A** (#1590 §2): (A) holds for T ≤ 2, any admissible B.
- **L5**: an incidence t → s forces |π(t) ∩ σ(s)| ≠ 1 (a single nonzero product cannot vanish); so every off-diagonal entry lies in ≥ 2 blocks. **L6** (K22): no t ≠ t′, s ≠ s′ with B_ts = B_ts′ = B_t′s = B_t′s′ = 1 unless the two points or the two lines coincide. **L3/L4**: no transitive 4-tournament; a full clique B[S,S] = J with |S| = 3 meets every D_j.
- **Lemma B = L7** (#1590 §3): for every rational B′ with supp B′ ⊆ supp B and B′_tt ≠ 0, **v ≤ Σ_j rank_Q B′[R_j, A_j]**.
- **L8**: for F2 vectors a(t) ⊆ π(t), b(t) ⊆ σ(t), cost ≥ rank_F2(B ∘ K), K_ts = |a(t) ∩ b(s)| mod 2; so a set S with K_tt = 1 on S and an order in which every incidence with K_ts = 1 points forward certifies cost ≥ |S| for every admissible B. Theorem P (2-adic reduction with one-sided repairs, cost ≥ |G| + |F|) is the main source of such certificates.

**Target (A).** At T = 3, **cost ≥ v for every exact decoder over Q and every admissible B.** (A) is the strong form: it would close every T = 3 window of #1590's table, since N(h) ≤ ⌈2v/3⌉ ≤ v there. It is known for T ≤ 2 (Lemma A), for decoders over a field of characteristic 2, and fails for T ≥ 7 (E7/E8 coordinates, cost 49 < 63 and 72 < 120; #1590 ERR1, Fano-type F2 cancellation). Whether it holds at T = 3 is **OPEN** (§10).

## 2. Proven reductions (every configuration)

**Lemma SCC (strong components) — PROVEN.** Let H be the digraph t → s (t ≠ s) with p(t)·q(s) = 0 and π(t) ∩ σ(s) ≠ ∅ — exactly the incidences that land inside some block. Order the strong components of H topologically. Then every block B[R_j, A_j] is block upper triangular in that order for every admissible B, so **cost(config) ≥ Σ_C cost(C)** over the strong components C. *Proof.* An entry (t, s) of block j that can be nonzero has t = s or t → s in H, and then comp(t) ≤ comp(s). A block upper-triangular matrix has rank ≥ the sum of the ranks of its diagonal blocks, and the diagonal block of C in block j is block j of the sub-configuration C (with B restricted to C, admissible there). ∎ So (A) need only be proved for configurations whose H is strongly connected. CHECKED: G4.SCC (triangularity on 300 random configurations; tied_min and cex_min_1 are single components of sizes 12 and 19) [`scc.py`]; G1.structure (tied_min strongly connected).

**Lemma Peel (unit rows) — PROVEN.** If |π(t)| = 1 (or |σ(t)| = 1), then **cost(config) ≥ cost(config − t) + 1**. *Proof.* Say π(t) = {j}. Then t lies only in R_j and, since M_tt ≠ 0, in A_j. For s ∈ A_j, p(t)·q(s) = p_j(t) q_j(s) ≠ 0, so row t of block j is the unit vector e_t and rank B_j = 1 + rank(B_j without row t and column t). Deleting column t from the other blocks does not raise their ranks. (The case |σ(t)| = 1 is the transpose.) ∎ So (A) also reduces to configurations where every point and every line has ≥ 2 nonzero coordinates. CHECKED: G4.Peel (36 exact enumerations over all admissible B) [fprime §13].

**Monotonicity — PROVEN.** Restricting an admissible B to a subset S of the lines gives an admissible B for S whose blocks are submatrices, so cost(S) ≤ cost(config). CHECKED: G4.Peel (36 instances, exact).

**Structure of a minimal counterexample — PROVEN** [fprime §13.4]. Let config be a counterexample to (A) (cost < v) with v minimal and B a minimiser. Then:
1. cost(config) = v − 1 and cost(config − t) = v − 1 for every line t: by minimality cost(config − t) ≥ v − 1, and by monotonicity cost(config − t) ≤ cost(config) ≤ v − 1. Every one-line deletion is *tight*.
2. No line is rank-critical for B: if deleting row t and column t lowered some block rank, B restricted to config − t would have cost ≤ v − 2 < v − 1.
3. H is strongly connected (otherwise cost ≥ Σ cost(C) ≥ Σ |C| = v by Lemma SCC and minimality) and every point and every line has ≥ 2 nonzero coordinates (Lemma Peel, same reason).
4. For every group X of lines (same point, or same line), cost(config − X) ≤ v − 1, so a counterexample is ruled out as soon as one group X satisfies cost(config) ≥ cost(config − X) + |X| (the induction scheme **PP**; it is false in general, §6).

Tight configurations (cost = v) are therefore the building blocks of any counterexample; tied_min (§4) is a 12-line tight configuration in which no group of lines can be peeled off at the price of its size (it has tight sub-configurations of every size ≤ 10, and every one-line deletion costs 12 again). CHECKED: G1.subconfigurations (all 12 one-line deletions of tied_min cost 12; a tight 10-line subconfiguration is found) [`s2b.py`, S2 §4].

## 3. Partial theorems

**Theorem M1 (no point of full support; any field) — PROVEN** [fprime §7]. If every point p(t) has a zero coordinate (|π(t)| ≤ 2), then
 **cost ≥ Σ_j r_j ≥ Σ_j MAS(G[D_j]) ≥ Σ_t c(t) ≥ v/2**, with c(t) = |D(t)| / C(|π(t)| + |σ(t)| − 2, |π(t)| − 1),
where MAS is the largest induced acyclic subgraph of the incidence digraph (an acyclic X ⊆ D_j makes B[X,X] unit triangular, so r_j ≥ |X|). The same holds if every line has a zero coordinate (reverse all edges). *Proof (summary).* Fix j and the chart x_k(t) = p_k(t)/p_j(t), y_k(t) = q_k(t)/q_j(t). Give t the vertices ξ_k(t) = (k, −1/x_k(t)) for k ∈ π(t) − {j} and η_k(t) = (k, y_k(t)) for k ∈ σ(t) − {j}. Since |π(t)| ≤ 2, an incidence t → s inside D_j is a vertex coincidence ξ_k(t) = η_k(s). Order the vertices uniformly at random; X_j = {t : every η-vertex of t precedes every ξ-vertex of t} is acyclic (the earliest ξ-vertex strictly increases along edges) and t ∈ X_j with probability 1/C(m_ξ + m_η, m_ξ). Summing the expectations over j and taking the best order for each j gives Σ_t c(t), which is ≥ 1/2 for every line. ∎ v/2 is sharp for the MAS-sum (the private digon has MAS-sum 1 of v = 2 while its cost is 2). What blocks the general case is the *wild* edge: a full-support point on a full-support line, where 1 + x₂y₂ + x₃y₃ = 0 has no vertex form (fprime §7 end, §8: value-order potentials give coverage 0 on some sign types). CHECKED: G4.M1 (80 random configurations with a zero coordinate at every point, v 6–14, exact MAS by subset DP: no violation; the digon: Σ c(t) = 1 = MAS-sum, cost 2) [`thmM1.py`, `mas.c`].
 Related lemmas, **PROVEN** [fprime §6.2–6.3]: for the private lines P_j = {D(t) = {j}}, MAS(G[P_j]) ≥ #sources + #sinks + ⌈#(types III, IV)/2⌉, hence cost ≥ |P|/2 over any field; two-shared classes give 1/4 per class. **CHECKED (recorded)**: 400 random private-only configurations, no violation, 34 tight [`private.py`, fprime §6.2].

**Theorem U (unit world) — PROVEN** [fprime §2]. Call a configuration *unit* if, after row and column scalings, every nonzero entry of all p(t), q(t) is a 2-adic unit (e.g. entries in {−1, 0, 1}, or all odd). Let G = {t : |D(t)| odd} and let I be the tied lines (|D(t)| = 2). In the unit world an incidence forces |π(t) ∩ σ(s)| even (Lemma 1.1), and for a tied class C_ij = {D(t) = {i, j}} with α(t) = p_i/p_j, β(t) = −q_j/q_i (α ≠ β) an incidence inside C_ij forces α(t) = β(s). Split C_ij into the halves H^< = {α < β} and H^> = {α > β}: each half is acyclic. Then the labels G: a = π, b = σ; one deletion of a coordinate for each chosen half (the order A_12, H_02, G, H_01, B_12 of fprime §2) give an L8 certificate of size **|G| + max-half(C_01) + max-half(C_02) + max-half(C_12) ≥ |G| + |I|/2 ≥ v/2.** CHECKED: G4.U (explicit labels and order, every conflict verified forward, for cex_min_1: 15 of 19, the 121-line pool of all primitive pairs in {−1,0,1}³: 106 of 121, and 250 random unit-world configurations of 4–60 lines with forced incidences: all valid, smallest best-certificate/v = 0.706) [`theoremU.py`]. **CHECKED (recorded)** [fprime §2 end, §11.2]: by column generation over *all* group-level one-deletion schemes (and, with copies, §11.2) the value 1/2 is exact, so no scheme depending only on (π, σ, half) can prove more than v/2 in the unit world. It does not cover all rational decoders: the third product p_k(t)q_k(s) can be even but nonzero, and the three tied lines p = (3,−1,−6), (−1,7,−4), (1,1,0) (with suitable q) form a complete digraph on three vertices that no splitting into two acyclic halves can handle [fprime §6.1, **CHECKED (recorded)** `k3class.py`].

**F2S rowspace identity — PROVEN** [fprime §13]. Let F2S be the class of configurations in which every point has full support and every line has exactly two nonzero coordinates (tied_min is its sharp member). Then R_j = all lines, and with W = rowspace(B), ρ = dim W, C_j = {columns with q_j = 0} and w_j = dim(W ∩ F2^{C_j}):
 **r_j = ρ − w_j, so cost = 3ρ − Σ_j w_j,** and Σ_j w_j ≤ ρ because the subspaces W ∩ F2^{C_j} have disjoint supports (they sit in different column classes), hence **cost ≥ 2ρ**; in particular (A) holds in F2S for every B with rank_F2 B ≥ v/2. *Proof.* B[:, A_j] is the restriction of the row space W to the columns outside C_j; the restriction map W → F2^{A_j} has kernel W ∩ F2^{C_j}, so its rank is ρ − w_j. ∎ Equivalently cost = 2 Σ w_j + 3(ρ − Σ w_j). On tied_min: the 216 class-block-diagonal optima have ρ = 6, w = (2,2,2); the special optimum B0 has ρ = 4, w = (0,0,0). CHECKED: G1.f2s-identity (all 217 optima; w_j computed by counting elements of W), G5.identity (179 random (F2S configuration, B) pairs). Natural strengthenings are false by exactly 1 (§5, per-class rowspace lemmas).

## 4. The sharp example tied_min: cost = v = 12

**Definition.** Four points a ∈ {(1, ±1, ±1) with a fixed product sign pattern}, the vertices of a K4, and the three perfect matchings M_k (k = 0,1,2) of K4. Class k consists of the lines whose q has q_k = 0: q ∈ {(0,1,±1)}, {(1,±1,0)}, {(1,0,±1)}. Line (a, k) = point a with the class-k line q that misses a. So v = 12 lines, all with |D| = 2 (*tied*), 72 incidences, one strong component (`data/tied_min.json`; it is the complete quadrangle against its own diagonal triangle). Every point has full support, so R_j = all 12 rows, and A_j = the 8 columns of the two classes k ≠ j. Entry (row (x,c), column (b,k)) is free iff x lies in the pair of M_k not containing b. CHECKED: G1.structure [fprime §12.1, `quads.py`].

**Theorem T (cost(tied_min) = 12) — PROVEN, computer-assisted in step (b) only** [fprime §12.3].
(a) Every block has rank ≥ 3: MAS(G[D_j]) = 3 gives a unit-triangular 3×3 principal minor. CHECKED: G1.step-a.
(b) A block of rank ≤ 3 has a **unique** filling of its 8 columns (exhaustive enumeration of all B with rank_F2 of the 12×8 block ≤ 3, by CNF with blocking clauses: exactly 1 solution for each j = 0,1,2). In it dim S_k = dim S_l = dim(S_k + S_l) = 3 (S_k the column span of the class-k columns), so S_k = S_l =: U, and every class-j row is 0 on those columns. CHECKED: G1.step-b [`rank3.py`].
(c) *Exchange.* If block j has rank 3, each of the other two blocks contains the class-j columns and one of the classes k, l; say it is S_j + U. U vanishes on the class-j rows T_j; the projection of S_j onto T_j is B[T_j, C_j] = [[I₂, ∗],[∗, I₂]], whose rank is ≥ 2 for every filling. So dim(S_j + U) ≥ rank B[T_j, C_j] + dim U ≥ 2 + 3 = 5. CHECKED: G1.step-c (all 2⁸ fillings of the 4×4 block have rank ≥ 2).
(d) So either all three blocks have rank ≥ 4 (cost ≥ 12) or some block has rank 3 and the other two have rank ≥ 5 (cost ≥ 3 + 5 + 5 = 13). Hence cost ≥ 12, and 12 is attained. ∎
 Independent confirmation: one CNF "cost ≤ K" (per-block factorisations plus a cardinality bound): K = 11 UNSAT, K = 12 SAT with block ranks recomputed by elimination (4,4,4) — CHECKED: G1.cnf [`costsat2.py`; the card also cross-checked with a CP-SAT model and a split-by-split CNF, **CHECKED (recorded)**, fprime §11.5].

**The 217 optima — CHECKED** [fprime §12.2, `enumW.py`, `anaB.py`]. Enumerating to exhaustion all B with block ranks ≤ (4,4,4) gives exactly **217**, all of cost 12 with ranks (4,4,4) (G1.optima). 216 = 6³ are class-block-diagonal (only same-class entries; each 4×4 class block [[I,U],[V,I]] has rank 2; dim S_k = 2, ρ = 6, w = (2,2,2)); one, B0, uses 24 cross-class entries and has ρ = 4, w = (0,0,0), S_0 = S_1 = S_2 = W of dimension 4. G1.f2s-identity confirms cost = 3ρ − Σ w_j for all 217.

**All 217 lift — CHECKED.** For each optimum the Camion linear system of the three blocks (one shared sign per entry, `jsign.c`) is consistent, and the resulting ±1 signing of supp B has exact Q-ranks (4,4,4) = the F2 ranks (217 of 217); the whole 12×12 B is even TU-signable (217 of 217) (G1.lifts) [`liftall.py`, S1 addendum A]. By Lemma B the lift gives cost ≥ v on tied_min independently of (a)–(d).

**Sub-configurations — CHECKED / CHECKED (recorded).** Every one-line deletion costs 12 (G1.subconfigurations: the CNF "cost ≤ 11" is UNSAT for all 12). S2 computed the exact cost of **all 4095** sub-configurations: cost ≥ v for every one, with tight ones (cost = v) of every size ≤ 10 and none of size 11 [`s2b.py`, S2 §4; **CHECKED (recorded)**: the table is in `evidence/fleet-S2.md`; verify.sh recomputes the size-11 and one size-10 case only].

## 5. Certificate limits: every order / label / multi-copy / 2-adic certificate stops at v − 1 on tied_min

**Lemma MC (multi-copy certificate, MCC) — PROVEN** [fprime §9]. Give each line t any number of *copies* (t, c) with labels a(t,c) ⊆ π(t), b(t,c) ⊆ σ(t), |a ∩ b| odd, and an order on the copies such that every pair (t,c) ≠ (s,c′) with |a(t,c) ∩ b(s,c′)| odd and (t → s an incidence, or t = s) is forward. Then **cost ≥ Σ_i r_i ≥ #copies.** *Proof.* With U_i[(t,c), t] = [i ∈ a(t,c)] and V_i[s, (s,c′)] = [i ∈ b(s,c′)], N := Σ_i U_i B[R_i, A_i] V_i has entries B_ts · |a(t,c) ∩ b(s,c′)| mod 2, rank N ≤ Σ_i r_i, and N is unit upper triangular in the given order. ∎ L8 is the one-copy case; MAS-sum is the case of copies (t, j), j ∈ D(t), with a = b = {j}; Theorems P, U and M1 are MCC bounds. CHECKED: G3.lemma-MC (N unit upper triangular, Σ r_j ≥ 20, on 60 admissible B for cex_min_1).

**On the 19-line cex_min_1 (entries in {−1, 0, 1}; `data/cex_min_1.json`)** [T3 "Conjecture F"; fprime §0, §9]:
- the maximum one-copy L8 certificate is **18 < v = 19**, so T3's Conjecture F (a certificate of size v always exists) is **false** — **CHECKED (recorded)** (CP-SAT, two independent encodings, `certlib.py`, `verify.py`; T3 Conjecture F; `evidence/cex-README.txt`);
- the **multi-copy certificate reaches 20 ≥ v = 19** (CP-SAT optimal with up to 3 copies per line). Here the stored certificate (20 copies on 17 lines) is *verified*, not solved: labels inside supports, odd diagonals, every odd entry on an incidence or same-line pair forward — **CHECKED**: G3.mcc-certificate. A stored B of cost 22 (ranks 10, 6, 6) gives 20 ≤ cost(cex_min_1) ≤ 22 — CHECKED: G3.lemma-MC. So cex_min_1 refutes the *certificate*, not (A);
- parity structure: every incidence has |π(t) ∩ σ(s)| ∈ {0, 2} (Lemma 1.1, an odd number of ±1 cannot vanish), tied lines with the same D and the same sign class never meet (Lemma 1.2) — CHECKED: G3.parity-lemmas (all 114 incidences of cex_min_1 and of the whole 121-line pool); G3.cex-structure: D-types {0,2}:5, {0}:4, {1,2}:3, {0,1}:3, {2}:2, {0,1,2}:1, {1}:1, eight lines of odd |D|.

**On tied_min** [fprime §11.5, §10, §12; all **CHECKED (recorded)**, CP-SAT OPTIMAL, stored solutions re-verified on the card; the solves are not repeated here]: L8 certificate = **11**; multi-copy certificate = **11** with 1, 2, 4 and 6 copies per line; the per-scale 2-adic copies with one-sided repairs (the restricted family of §10) stay below the unrestricted MCC. The true cost is 12 (§4). Consequently **no certificate of the multi-copy kind — in particular none built from Theorem P, Theorem U, M1, MAS-sum, L8 or per-scale 2-adic reductions, and no cross-scale ordering lemma for them — can prove (A) at T = 3** (a proof must see genuine F2 rank, not one triangular minor of Σ_j U_j B_j V_j with copy-type U_j, V_j). Caveat: "up to 6 copies per line" is what was solved; the number of distinct label types per line is larger, and the claim for arbitrarily many copies is not proved. Also **CHECKED (recorded)** [fprime §11.2]: even allowing copies, every group-level unit-world scheme is capped at 1/2.

**Per-class rowspace lemmas are false by exactly 1 — CHECKED (recorded)** [fprime §13.2; `lemmaN.py`, `lemmaNsum.py`, `lemmaN2.py`, adversarial annealing with exact F2 ranks on F2S configurations of 4–7 lines where cost ≥ v still holds]: N (a_j + w_j ≥ n_j), N-sum (Σ_j (a_j + w_j) ≥ v) and N′ (n_j ≤ 2w_j + ρ′) fail; and the polymatroid bound 2·cost ≥ 3ρ + Σ_k dim S_k gives only 9 on tied_min [S2 §3]. In F2S the slack must move between classes: any proof needs a joint argument across the three classes, as in step (c).

**The mod-4 lift framework — a framework, not a proof tool** [fprime §12.4]. For any F2 rank factorisation B_j = UVᵀ and integer lifts U + 2X, V + 2Y, N = Σ_j D(p_j)(U + 2X)(V + 2Y)ᵀ D(q_j) has rank_Q N ≤ Σ_j r_j; on tied unit lines N = Δ + 2W and the obstruction is a linking form; on the 217 optima of tied_min the linking forms are mostly nonzero. For a *fixed* B, "some lift has rank v" is equivalent to Σ r_j ≥ v, so the framework needs a B-independent argument on the coset family (**OPEN**).

## 6. The lift route

**Lemma W′ — PROVEN** [fprime §13.3, S1 addendum A]. If B has a **TU signing** (a ±1 signing of supp B under which B is totally unimodular — equivalently the binary matroid M[I | B] is regular, Camion/Tutte), then every block of the signed matrix B′ is TU, so rank_Q = rank_F2 on every block, and by Lemma B **cost(B) ≥ v**. (Regularity of the column matroid M[B] alone is *not* enough: a full-rank B has M[B] free; A = [[1,1,0,1],[1,0,1,1],[0,1,1,1]] has M[A] graphic but M[I|A] = F7.) The weaker hypothesis Lemma B actually uses is any rational B′ supported on incidences plus the diagonal, with nonzero diagonal, and Σ_j rank_Q B′[R_j, A_j] ≤ cost: the **open core is (Q ≤ F2)**, "the minimum Q-cost over such B′ is ≤ the minimum F2-cost", and (A) = (Q ≤ F2) + Lemma B (g1 §14). A *same-support* rational lift of a minimiser with **rank_Q = rank_F2 on the three blocks** (**G1′, lift**) is a convenient witness for (Q ≤ F2) — it implies (A) for the configuration — and probably not the final form (§10.2). Since F7* blocks occur for non-optimal admissible B (below), a statement about same-support lifts must be about a minimiser, not about every admissible B.

**Camion, chordless cycles, and joint signability — PROVEN** [g1 §9]. Let G be the bipartite support graph of B (every 1, diagonal included); block j is the *induced* subgraph G_j = G[V_j], V_j = R_j + A_j. Signs are written additively, x ∈ F2^{E(G)} (x_e = 1 meaning −1).
- **Lemma 9.1 (chordless holonomy).** If a signing of a 0/1 matrix is TU, every chordless cycle γ of its support graph (length 2m) has Σ_{e∈γ} x_e = m mod 2. *Proof.* γ spans a square submatrix with support exactly γ, a 2m-cycle matrix of determinant ±(Π₁ + (−1)^{m−1}Π₂), which must lie in {0, ±2} and so be 0, i.e. Π₁Π₂ = (−1)^m. ∎
- **Lemma 9.2 (uniqueness up to scaling).** Two TU signings of M differ by a coboundary x′ = x + (r_t + c_s): chordless cycles span the cycle space Z(G), so x and x′ agree on all of it; coboundaries (row/column scalings) preserve TU. ∎
- **Theorem 9.3.** B admits one signing with all three blocks TU **iff** (i) each block B[R_j, A_j] is TU-signable and (ii) the F2 system {Σ_{e∈γ} x_e = |γ|/2 : γ a chordless cycle of some G_j} is consistent. *Proof.* (⇒) Lemma 9.1 in each block. (⇐) Take a solution x and a TU signing s_j of block j: on every chordless cycle of G_j they have the same sum, hence on Z(G_j), so x|G_j = s_j + coboundary, which is TU. ∎ Equivalently, with h_j the holonomy functional on Z(G_j): h₀(g₀) + h₁(g₁) + h₂(g₂) = 0 whenever g₀ + g₁ + g₂ = 0, g_j ∈ Z(G_j).
- **Lemma G (gluing; S1 §3).** Pairwise relations g ∈ Z(G_j) ∩ Z(G_k) = Z(G[V_j ∩ V_k]) never obstruct, because a chordless cycle of the induced graph G[V_j ∩ V_k] is chordless in both G_j and G_k. So an obstruction to (ii) is a genuinely **three-block (Čech) relation**.
 CHECKED: G2.theorem-9.3 — the Camion system (`jsign.c`, which builds exactly the system of 9.3) agrees with an *independent* oracle (`brutetu.c`: every ±1 signing of the entries off a spanning forest, every square minor computed exactly) on 53 instances (all minimisers of the v = 7, v = 8, v = 9 examples below and 40 random small B), covering the outcome classes J = 1; J = 0 with one, two or three blocks signable; and the Čech class (all blocks signable, no joint signing); the pure-Python Camion solver (`jointsign.py` method) agrees with `jsign.c` on every minimiser of the instances below.

**Two failure modes of G1-joint, both occur — CHECKED** [g1 §9–10]. (F1) one block is not TU-signable: the basic obstruction is an induced *odd theta* — branch vertices row 0 and column 6, three paths of length 3 — e.g. the 4×4 submatrix [1110 / 0011 / 0101 / 1001], which uses diagonal entries, so non-degenerate geometry does not exclude it (G2.obstructions: not TU-signable; F7 = [110,011,101,111] likewise, J2 and the odd 3-cycle are signable). (F2) all blocks TU-signable but no joint signing: `cech_v8.json`, v = 8, f = 11, one of 320 minimisers (G2.failure-modes).

**Exact counterexamples to nearby statements — all CHECKED:**
1. *Whole-matrix TU fails* (`cex_wholeTS_v9.json`, g1 §6): v = 9, f = 13; exhaustive minimum cost 18, 16 minimisers, block ranks (7,4,7) for all; **none** of the 16 is TU-signable as a whole 9×9 matrix (`tusign.c` and the Python Camion solver agree), yet every block of every minimiser is TU-signable, one joint signing exists for each, and its exact Q block ranks equal the F2 ranks (so the lift Lemma B needs exists and (A) holds: 18 ≥ 9). The unique fewest-ones minimiser (8 off-diagonal ones) is support-minimal and not whole-TS, so "support-minimal ⇒ whole-TS" and "fewest-ones ⇒ whole-TS" are false. The example is degenerate (lines 1 = 2, lines 4 = 6 equal, lines 7, 8 on one projective line, points 4, 5 coincide); whether whole-TS fails also for non-degenerate configurations is open. G2.w9-instance, G2.w9-no-whole-TS, G2.w9-lift.
2. *Support-minimal ⇏ jointly signable* (`supmin_fail_v8.json`, v = 8, cost 14, 2 minimisers, both with 9 ones, both support-minimal, ranks (4,5,5)): one is whole-TS, the other has block 0 non-signable; they differ by the swap (3,0) ↔ (1,6). Any selection rule must see signability. G2.failure-modes.
3. *Non-regular minimisers* (S1's F7* gadget, `gad.json`, v = 10, f = 29): the recorded exhaustive run over all 2²⁹ matrices gives minimum cost 19 and 23552 minimisers, 3052 of them with a non-TU-signable block [g1 §1; **CHECKED (recorded)**; the 2²⁹ run is not repeated]. CHECKED: G2.gadget — a stored minimiser (11 off-diagonal ones) has block ranks (6,6,7), sum 19, blocks 1 and 2 not TU-signable. So "every optimum is regular" is false.
4. *K22-free ⇏ TU-signable* (S1's aff14, n = 14): an admissible B with 14 distinct points and 14 distinct lines, off-diagonal K22-free (L6 holds), ranks (9,11,11), one block equal to the whole matrix, **not TU-signable** — so non-degeneracy plus L6 does not give a Lemma W′ signing; the property must come from optimality (this B has cost 31 vs an SA minimum of 24 on that configuration, **CHECKED (recorded)** S1 addendum B). G6.aff14.
5. *Peeling induction PP is false* (S2): there are 272 of 4095 subsets of tied_min with no group X satisfying cost(S) ≥ cost(S − X) + |X|; the smallest failure is v = 2, since a lone line costs |D(t)| = 2 and the pair {0, 3} (distinct points, distinct lines) costs 2. CHECKED: G4.PP; the 272 of 4095 is **CHECKED (recorded)** [`pp.py`].
6. *Single-block regularity of the exchange*: single-entry deletion does not restore signability (32 minimisers of the gadget have no single deletion that stays a minimiser; 2–3 entries are needed) [g1 §1, **CHECKED (recorded)**]; any exchange proof must be a multi-entry move.
 The packing structure — "every fewest-ones minimiser is a vertex-disjoint union of directed cycles and K3 cliques" — is true on 450 random configurations but fails on three configurations (v = 7, 7, 10) [g1 §2, **CHECKED (recorded)**], so G1 cannot be reduced to cycle and clique packings.

**Where the route stands.** Whole-B TU-signable ⇒ joint block-TU signable ⇒ G1′ (lift) ⇒ (Q ≤ F2) ⇒ (A) for that configuration. The first implication is false in general (item 1) and so is the second (§7, X1). G1′ and (Q ≤ F2) are OPEN (§10).

## 7. Outcome X1: does some cost-minimiser always admit one signing with all three blocks TU? ("G1-joint")



**G1-joint is false at T = 3 — CHECKED, exact; G1′ (lift) is OPEN.** Two counterexamples are described in full (the other twelve instances of the same kind are checked in G2.nojoint14) (`data/cex_joint_v7.json`, `data/nojoint_21_1.json`; g1 §10–11 and the jclimb2 search of the same card).
- **v = 7** (f = 11): P = (2,0,−2) (−32,−32,−48) (−4,−4,−4) (0,1,1) (−2,0,−1) (0,−1,1) (−2,−2,0), Q = (2,−12,−4) (1,−2,0) (−2,2,−1) (−1,2,−1) (−2,0,2) (−4,4,0) (32,64,−64). Exhaustive over all 2¹¹ admissible B: minimum cost **12**, exactly **four** minimisers (7, 8, 9, 10 off-diagonal ones), all with block ranks (4,4,4). In **all four, block 2 (rows 0–5, columns 0,2,3,4,6) is not TU-signable**, so no minimiser has a signing with all three blocks TU (G2.j7-no-joint: `tusign.c`, `jsign.c`, the Python Camion solver and the exhaustive oracle agree). Obstruction: the 4×4 submatrix on rows 0,2,3,4 × columns 0,3,4,6 — an induced odd theta (branch vertices row 4 and column 3, paths r4–c0–r0–c3, r4–c4–r2–c3, r4–c6–r3–c3) using the diagonal entries (0,0), (3,3), (4,4) — is not TU-signable in any of the four. **(A) holds here: 12 ≥ 7.**
- **v = 10** (f = 19): minimum cost 14, two minimisers, ranks (5,4,5); blocks 0 and 1 are TU-signable but block 2 is not, in both, so there is no joint signing; (A) holds: 14 ≥ 10 (G2.j10-no-joint).
- **14 instances in all** (`data/nojoint_21_1…14.json`, v = 7–10, found by the climb `jclimb2.py`): none has a jointly signable minimiser, and (A) holds on each (cost ≥ v; e.g. 15 ≥ 7, 14 ≥ 9, 16 ≥ 9); G2.nojoint14.
- **The lift that Lemma B needs exists on the v = 7 example.** The same-support rational matrix [[−1,0,0,−2,0,0,0],[0,3,0,0,0,0,0],[0,0,1,−2,1,0,0],[0,0,0,−2,0,0,−1],[−2,0,0,0,−2,0,2],[0,0,0,0,0,2,0],[0,0,2,0,0,0,2]] has the support of the 7-ones minimiser and exact Q block ranks (4,4,4) = the F2 ranks (G2.j7-lift). Reason: block 2 has only 4 nonzero rows and F2 rank 4, so any lift with the same support whose 4 nonzero rows stay linearly independent over Q has Q-rank exactly 4 — TU is irrelevant for a full-row-rank block.
- **Exact lifts for every minimiser tested — CHECKED (G2.nojoint-lifts).** An exact computation in the manner of the card's `exactlift.py` (row/column scalings fix a spanning forest to 1; the lift conditions are the vanishing of all (r_j + 1)-minors of each block, with all support entries nonzero; a lex Gröbner basis decides the system over C on the card, and `scripts/make_lifts.py` finds explicit points) gives an explicit same-support rational lift with exact block ranks equal to the F2 ranks for: all 4 minimisers of cex_joint_v7; the first ≤ 12 minimisers (by ones count) of each of nojoint_21_1…14; 8 sampled non-TS minimisers of the F7* gadget — 102 lifts in all, mostly with ±1 entries in a unique gauge class, stored in `data/lifts_nojoint.json`; `verify.sh` re-checks, for each, that the matrix is a minimiser's support with nonzero diagonal and exact Fraction block ranks equal to the F2 ranks. These are ±1 (or small integer) signings that preserve every block rank without being TU. Earlier random-weighting searches that found no lift on some of these were inconclusive (the lift is a structured solution of the minor equations). The tested minimisers are not all minimisers of nojoint_21_*, and no failure of G1′ is known.

**Hierarchy, exact at T = 3.** whole-B TU-signable (false: v = 9, §6.1) ⇒ one signing with all three blocks TU (false: v = 7 and v = 10) ⇒ G1′, a rank-preserving same-support rational lift on the three blocks (OPEN; holds on every minimiser tested, 102 exact lifts) ⇒ (Q ≤ F2) ⇒ (A). So TU-signability is too strong an invariant: **the Lemma W′ route cannot prove (A) as stated.** G1′ needs the (r_j + 1)-minors of each block to vanish — an algebraic, not a linear, condition — and only where a block is rank-deficient. The TU-signability test is a *linear* system; its failure says nothing yet about (A).



## 8. Outcome X2: rank-3 decoders on the Hesse configuration over Q(ω)

**Setting.** The Hesse configuration is the 9 flexes of a cubic, 9 points and 12 lines with 3 points on every line and 4 lines through every point; it is realised over Z[ω], ω² + ω + 1 = 0, and over no real or rational field (no ordinary line exists, so Sylvester–Gallai fails). `lib/hesse.py` builds it exactly (G7.hesse: 9 points, 12 lines, 3 points per line, 4 lines per point, 21 intersection points, every pair of the 9 points on one of the 12 lines). The experiment draws configurations of v = 6–12 lines (p, q) with p among the 9 Hesse points (or the 21 intersection points of the 12 lines) and q among the 12 Hesse lines (or the lines through pairs of the 21 points), after a random projective change of coordinates so that the coordinate triangle is generic; the pairs (p, q) with p·q ≠ 0 are biased towards incidences; the exact minimum of cost over **all** 2^f admissible B is computed (f ≤ 22) with the same C enumerator as G2. Lemma B (and the lift route) hold verbatim over Q(ω); the ordering arguments of Lemma A, Theorem U and M1 do not (Q(ω) has no field order). The question: *does any rank-3 decoder built this way cost less than v?*

**Smoke check in `verify.sh` (G7, CHECKED).** 16 random configurations with fixed seed, evaluated exhaustively: smallest cost − v = 0, cost = v in 1, cost < v in 0. It checks the harness; it is not the experiment.



**No cost < v found — CHECKED (recorded), evidence only** (scripts `g1/hesse.py`, `g1/hesse_hc.py`; logs `g1/hesse_runs/`; the harness of `lib/hesse.py` is a port, G7). Snapshot 2026-10-14, 14:20: four random-sampler runs (seeds 1–4, 400 configurations each, cap f ≤ 22, every one evaluated exhaustively over all admissible B): 1600 configurations, smallest cost − v = 0, 61 with cost = v, none with cost < v. Three hill-climbs over the configuration (seeds 11, 12, 13; 30 restarts of 200 steps each, objective (cost − v)·100 − f, cap f ≤ 22): 90 restarts, 6 reach cost = v, none goes below v. So **(A) also holds on all tested Hesse geometry over Q(ω)**: the lack of a field order, or the absence of an ordinary line, is **not** supported as the mechanism of any cost < v. This is evidence only: the tested configurations are small (v ≤ 12, f ≤ 22) and the search is stochastic.

## 10. Open problems, stated precisely

1. **(A) at T = 3 over Q.** For every configuration of lines (p(t), q(t)) ∈ Q³ × Q³ with p(t)·q(t) ≠ 0 and every admissible B, Σ_{j=1}^{3} rank_F2 B[R_j, A_j] ≥ v. Known: T ≤ 2 (Lemma A); fails in char 2 for no T (cost ≥ v there) and for T ≥ 7 over Q; tied_min is tight; no counterexample in any search (§9).
2. **(Q ≤ F2), the open core.** For every such configuration, is min over rational B′ supported on incidences plus the diagonal (nonzero diagonal) of Σ_j rank_Q B′[R_j, A_j] ≤ min over admissible B over F2 of Σ_j rank_F2 B[R_j, A_j]? Then (A) = (Q ≤ F2) + Lemma B. It fails at T ≥ 7. *Witness form.* **G1′ (lift):** some cost-minimiser B has a same-support rational B′ (B′_tt ≠ 0) with rank_Q B′[R_j, A_j] = rank_F2 B[R_j, A_j] for j = 1, 2, 3. It holds on every minimiser tested (102 exact lifts, §7) and is a convenient witness, but probably not the final form: with a_i collinear on a line L, all Y-lines equal to L, all Y-points equal to one point c off L and X-lines through c, the admissible pattern contains B = [[I_k, F],[G, I_m]] with F and G arbitrary 0/1 matrices (g1 §14, **CHECKED (recorded)** for k = 3, m = 4), so any F2/Q minrank gap of a 0/1 pattern embeds in a same-support lift question; there sub-support lifts still reach the F2 optimum, so (Q ≤ F2) is unaffected, and a proof must allow the support to change (an exchange to a liftable minimiser, or a direct construction of a cheap rational B′).
3. **A uniform F2 certificate beyond multi-copy.** Does there exist, for every configuration, a family of F2 matrices U_j (m × |R_j|), V_j (|A_j| × m) — arbitrary row and column combinations inside each block, not only copy-type labels — such that Σ_j U_j B[R_j, A_j] V_j has rank ≥ v **for every admissible B**? (It would give (A) with cost ≥ rank ≥ v.) On tied_min the optimum has all three block ranks 4 and each of its kinds is tight in a different way (block-diagonal: ρ = 6; B0: ρ = 4), so such a family must be B-dependent in structure or rank-based.
4. **F2S.** Is Σ_j w_j ≤ 3ρ − v, i.e. is (A) true in F2S? Equivalent to (A) there; the observed frontier of (ρ, rank pattern) on tied_min is (4; 4,4,4), (5; 2,3,3), (ρ ≥ 6; 2,2,2). A general lower bound Σ_{i≠j} dim(S_i ∩ K_j) ≥ v − 2 Σ_j mr_j (fprime §12.5) would suffice and is not proved.
5. **M1 without the hypothesis.** Does cost ≥ v/2 (or Σ_j MAS(G[D_j]) ≥ v/2, Conjecture M) hold when some point has full support? The wild edges (full-support point on full-support line) need a block-rank argument; fprime §9 gives a heuristic warning that MAS-sum alone may be o(v) on Szemerédi–Trotter-extremal full-support configurations, so a proof must use the F2 label part.
6. **Theorem U beyond the unit world.** A rational "digit lemma" for tied classes whose third product p_k(t)q_k(s) is even but nonzero, plus an e-selection (multi-scale) argument; a lower bound of 2/3 needs labels beyond one deletion even in the unit world (minimax of one-deletion schemes is exactly 1/2).
7. **Whole-B TU-signability for non-degenerate configurations.** The v = 9 counterexample is degenerate; is a non-degenerate one possible?
8. **Lemma SCC/Peel completeness.** Are there further universal reductions beyond strong components and unit rows (a rank-critical-line lemma fails: 850 of 1117 low-cost B on tied_min have no rank-critical line [fprime §12.5, CHECKED (recorded), `critical.py`])?
9. **T ≥ 4.** All lemmas but SCC's three-block structure and the specific tied_min exchange carry over verbatim; a T = 4 statement needs the analogous theory in PG(3,2).
