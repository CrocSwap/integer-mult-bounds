## Universal existence and uniqueness, without a numerical root

For every real t put x_r=log r and

    A(t)=sum_r P_r exp(t x_r),
    pi_r(t)=P_r exp(t x_r)/A(t),
    m(t)=sum_r pi_r(t)x_r.

There are finitely many terms, all strictly positive, so A(t)>0 for every
finite real t. All these functions are smooth; finite differentiation gives
A'=sum P_r exp(t x_r)x_r and A''=sum P_r exp(t x_r)x_r^2. Consequently

    m'(t)=A''/A-(A'/A)^2
         =sum_r pi_r(t)(x_r-m(t))^2
         =(1/2)sum_{r,s}pi_r(t)pi_s(t)(x_r-x_s)^2.

The ordered pairs (r,s)=(1,2),(2,1) alone contribute
pi_1(t)pi_2(t)(log2)^2>0. Thus m'(t)>0 for every real t, with no sign or
range restriction on t. In particular m is continuous and strictly increasing
on the whole real line. This is variance about the actual m(t).

As t tends to minus infinity, every r^t with r>1 tends to0. The denominator
tends to P_1>0 and the mean numerator tends to0; hence m(t) tends to0.
As t tends to plus infinity, divide numerator and denominator by48^t. Every
(r/48)^t with r<48 tends to0, leaving m(t) tending to log48, since P_48>0.
The strict inequalities 0=log1<log5<log48 follow from monotonicity of log.
There therefore exist finite t_- and t_+ with
m(t_-)<log5<m(t_+). The intermediate value theorem gives a real alpha_star
with m(alpha_star)=log5; strict monotonicity makes it unique. No endpoint
certificate or approximate root is needed for this universal conclusion.

The same argument applies to any finite positive law with at least two
distinct support points and target strictly between its extreme log ranks.
Here every one of those premises is supplied by the literal law.

   variance0: uniqueness fails. The positive rank1/rank2 pair is substantive.
2. If all ranks greater than5 are removed while positive mass below5 remains,
   then m(t)<log5 for every finite t. Convergence to log5 at infinity gives
   no finite root. The target must be strictly inside the support interval.
3. Signed counts are not allowed. For counts1 and-1 on ranks1 and2,
   A(t)=1-2^t vanishes at0; even away from0 the variance/positivity argument
   is invalid. Rejecting signed or malformed law data is substantive.
4. A rational bracket alone proves nothing about this root. Reversed endpoints,
   a wrong interval or zero-containing endpoint enclosures cannot pass the
   two strict sign gates. Rounded positive/negative display values are not
   substitutes for full signed rational bounds.

   as variance or claiming E(X_t-log5)=0 throughout the bracket is false.
   S5's actual-expectation centering is what makes its all-bracket result
   transfer correctly at the one exact root.
6. Positivity of m' does not imply positivity of F'; the actual-law limiting
   countercalculation above rules out that proof shortcut.
