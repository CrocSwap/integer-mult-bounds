## Analytic assessment

The stated pool theorem is sound under its explicit hypotheses: the entire
noncoordinate rank-one-idempotent pool is included; the binary pattern space
has dimension three; and it contains exactly two, or exactly three, matrix
units. No word-length or ambient-width bound is being inferred from enumeration.

For the quotient observation, equality of off-diagonal patterns outside a
coordinate block U gives `x_out y_U^T = u_out v_U^T`. Independent image classes
force both inside covectors to vanish. Hence both inside blocks vanish and
the full off-diagonal patterns agree. The inherited binary fiber lemma then
forces the distinct projectors to be the two lifts of one edge, with both
endpoints outside U. This is a complete arbitrary-width argument.

For two edges, their complete fibers ensure the endpoint coordinate space E
lies in the joint image. Projectors from the zero coset have image in E.
The preceding observation prevents two independent nonzero images modulo E
in the other coset, since that would create a third edge. Over F2, every
nonzero quotient image is therefore the same vector. Thus `dim(V/E)<=1`,
including the case when some coset projector has zero quotient image. This
proves `dim V<=dim E+1<=5` without a restriction on ambient width.

For three edges, the matrix units form a basis of the pattern space. A
noncoordinate rank-one idempotent has intersecting x/y supports, and any
intersection coordinate connects every other support coordinate in the
undirected off-diagonal graph. Thus the entire support lies in one connected
component of the three-edge graph. Such a component has at most four vertices.
This proves the component statement; the explicit six-dimensional total image
for three disjoint edges correctly prevents an incorrect global bound five.

For the irreducible five-dimensional case, E has four distinct endpoints and
one can choose the fifth basis vector entirely outside E by subtracting its E
coordinates. The two edge shears diagonalize the four edge lifts and fix this
fifth vector. All other projectors have common off-block vectors. If either
vanishes there is a proper invariant subspace. If both are nonzero, rank one
over F2 fixes the entire restriction as `(a,1)(b,1)^T`. Irreducibility forces
each entry of a and b to be one, giving J5 uniquely. The displayed coordinate-
projection argument proves irreducibility of this normal form. This conclusion
concerns the set of restrictions, as the theorem correctly states.

The explicit pool is complete: its third-pattern coset has fixed nonzero
row-4 and column-4 off-diagonal data. Those force both fourth-coordinate
factors to be one and determine every other factor entry, yielding only B.
The two disjoint-edge sum cannot be rank-one: its required entries force two
additional cross entries. The four edge lifts and B are therefore the whole
pool, not merely a selected subfamily.

Three small clarity corrections were sent to the author: explicitly invoke
that disjoint-edge-sum exclusion in the uniqueness proof; remove the word
"four" from the unrelated free coordinate-call generators in Section 7; and
describe reducible reductions as upper-triangular invariant subquotient blocks,
rather than a direct-sum decomposition. These do not invalidate the pool theorem.
The exact word/rank-saving problem remains open as stated.

## Independent bounded checks

`bit_three_relation_review.py` is a separate standard-library implementation.
Normal and optimized Python produced identical `BIT-THREE-REVIEW.json` data:

- All 496 width-five rank-one idempotents give exactly the five advertised
  projectors in the explicit pool. The stated shear conjugates their complete
  set to four coordinate projectors and J5.
- All 372 nonzero proper subspaces fail invariance. An independently generated
  orbit closure also confirms irreducibility; deleting the fifth projector
  produces a reducible negative control.
- The quotient/fiber observation is checked on every applicable projector pair
  and every coordinate subspace at widths three, four and five (12, 48 and 160
  nonvacuous independent-image cases respectively).
- Two-edge pattern spaces generated by the fixed edge pair and one realized
  third pattern are checked for all five directed edge-incidence types at
  widths five and six. Every image bound holds. The disjoint-edge case has
  409 such spaces at width five and 1,872 at width six; respectively one and
  six have irreducible five-dimensional image, and every restriction has the
  claimed unique normal form. These are generated-space checks, not a census
  of all ambient zero-diagonal three-planes.
- All 4,060 triples of directed edges on six coordinates satisfy the component
  claim, with 27,960 projector-support checks. The maximal total image is six.
