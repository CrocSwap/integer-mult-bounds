# Completing a partial signed-Pauli native contract

Prepared for JoyboyBrian with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance; Apache-2.0.
This is a written proof with finite exact corroboration, not proof-kernel review.

Use bare Paulis P=i^p X^x Z^z on n binary axes. Labels v=(x,z) lie in
V=F_2^(2n), with omega(v,w)=x·w_z+z·w_x. A Pauli is Hermitian exactly
when p≡x·z (mod2). We allow dependent equations and ±I constraints.
Given Hermitian equations U P_i U^-1=Q_i, put W=span(v_i), k=dim W,
rank(omega|W)=2r, and t=k−2r. Count Cliffords modulo arbitrary scalar phase;
this is the finite projective Clifford group, not a count of physical headers.

## The complete compatibility theorem

The equations have a Clifford solution if and only if:

1. The ordered source and target labels have exactly the same binary relations,
   so v_i↦w_i defines an injective linear map h:W→V.
2. omega(v_i,v_j)=omega(w_i,w_j) for every pair.
3. Every central product relation has the same actual i-phase. Equivalently,
   choose any independent source sublist. For each remaining equation, express
   its label as a sum of this basis; multiply the corresponding Hermitian
   generators in that fixed order, and compare the central scalar between
   this product and the equation's Pauli. The scalar must agree on both sides.

These conditions preserve all Pauli multiplication relations, not only signs
on a chosen independent basis. They are necessary under conjugation. For the
equivalence in item3, the presentation of the Pauli group generated by the
chosen basis consists of its central i, generator squares, and pairwise
commutators. Squares are fixed because every supplied generator is Hermitian;
item2 fixes commutators. Every other source generator is a uniquely specified
central multiple of the fixed ordered product. Thus checking these finitely
many relations suffices for every word relation. No exponential enumeration
of generator relations is needed.

## Constructive symplectic extension

Here is a direct binary proof of the required special case of Witt extension.
Process independent source labels v_1,…,v_k in order, maintaining a symplectic
map S which is already correct on earlier labels. Let v=S(v_j), w=h(v_j),
and let P be the span of earlier target labels. Then v,w are outside P and
omega(v,p)=omega(w,p) for every p in P. If v=w nothing is required.
Otherwise v+w∈P-perp. If omega(v,w)=1, the transvection

    T_(v+w)(a)=a+omega(a,v+w)(v+w)

fixes P pointwise and sends v to w. If omega(v,w)=0, choose u satisfying

    omega(u,p)=omega(v,p) for p in P,
    omega(u,v)=omega(u,w)=1.

This binary linear system is consistent. A relation among a basis of P,v,w
cannot involve just one of v,w, since each is outside P. The only possible
new relation has v+w=p in P; the prescribed right-hand side for p is
omega(v,v+w)=omega(v,w)=0, equal to 1+1. Nondegeneracy of omega then makes
every consistent functional representable by a vector u. The two axes v+u
and u+w belong to P-perp. Their transvections fix P and send v→u→w.
Thus at most 2k explicit transvections construct a full symplectic extension.
Gaussian elimination solves the displayed systems; no ambient vector search
or unproved isometry oracle is used.

For a nonzero axis a=(a_x,a_z), let H_a=i^(popcount(a_x&a_z)) X^a_x Z^a_z.
It is Hermitian and squares to I. The literal dyadic Clifford

    C_a=(I−i H_a)/(1−i)

implements T_a and is exactly the source compiler's positive `turn` gate.
If P anticommutes with H_a, C_a P C_a^-1=−i H_a P; otherwise P is fixed.
This proves the phase update used by the implementation, including Y axes.
The product of these literal gates gives a chosen Clifford U0 extending h
on labels. Its Hermitian images may still have wrong signs. By conditions2–3,
the difference between desired and actual signs is a binary linear functional
on h(W). Nondegeneracy gives a Pauli K whose conjugation supplies precisely
this character. Solve omega(K,w_j)=delta_j, then append literal X and Z
gates. Their global phase is part of the emitted word; it is not discarded.
This constructs a solution of every signed equation and proves sufficiency.

## Exact projective completion count

First count symplectic extensions of the fixed h. Choose a nondegenerate
2r-dimensional summand H of W; W=H direct-sum R with R=rad(W), dim R=t.
The pointwise stabilizer of H acts as Sp_(2N)(2) on H-perp, N=n−r. Fixing W
further means fixing an ordered basis of the isotropic t-space R. There are

    I(N,t)=product_(j=0)^(t−1)(2^(2N−j)−2^j)

ordered independent isotropic t-tuples: after j choices the next vector is in
their orthogonal complement of dimension2N−j but outside their 2^j-element
span. The constructive extension argument above implies transitivity on such
tuples. Since |Sp_(2N)(2)|=2^(N^2) product_(j=1)^N(2^(2j)−1), orbit-stabilizer
gives |Sp_(2N)(2)|/I(N,t) symplectic extensions of h. The same group-order
formula follows by choosing a first nonzero symplectic basis vector, a partner
of pairing one, and recursing on their orthogonal complement.

Over each full symplectic map, projective Clifford lifts form a torsor for
the 2^(2n) Pauli conjugations. This can be seen without counting matrices:
two lifts differ by signs on the 2n standard Hermitian generators, every such
sign character is implemented by a unique Pauli label, and a map fixing all
signed generators commutes with X and Z and hence is scalar. The k independent
specified signs impose k independent linear equations. Thus the number is

    2^(2n−k) |Sp_(2(n−r))(2)| / I(n−r,t).                (1)

All compatible choices have this same count; incompatible input has count zero.
The formula includes k=0, the full signed tableau k=2n, and n=0. No unused
workspace or discarded input is introduced by the construction.

## A real phase obstruction and the absolute-phase boundary

For one qubit, X→X, Z→Z, Y→−Y preserves all label relations and pairwise
commutators, but X Z Y=−iI would become+iI. It has zero completions. X fixed
alone has four projective completions; X and Z fixed have one.

Partial conjugation equations, even a full signed tableau, never specify the
global scalar of U. In particular I and −I satisfy exactly the same equations.
The API emits an actual chosen literal dyadic word. It does NOT assert that
this word equals an independently supplied physical target. Such equality
still requires the source compiler's exact nonzero amplitude anchor. Both this
distinction and the i^(phase*f) law at tensor width f are explicit controls.

Every emitted base word is tensorized letter by letter. Functoriality proves
U^tensor(f) P_i^tensor(f) (U^-1)^tensor(f)=Q_i^tensor(f) for all positive f.
Source global phases tensorize too. Tensorizing an entire sum or dropping an
anchor phase is not authorized by this argument.

## Actual caller and complete obligation ledger

The pinned native Clifford compiler (#4841, Dugongue and its retained source
lineage) accepts a full literal gate word or an unsigned Lagrangian and emits
phase-exact free adapters around one rank-d Gaussian call. This package adds
the missing entry contract from incomplete signed operator constraints. Its
emitted literal words are passed to the actual source CLI, retaining full
small-input column checks and nine-bit tableau/anchor replay. The existing
compiler is a declared source dependency, not a theorem independently audited
in full by this contribution. Classical Witt and Clifford structure retain
their attribution; no mathematical or synthesis-priority claim is made.
