# Search record

The search began with twenty GPT-6-sol agents at medium reasoning effort,
then continued through separate construction and adversarial audit rounds.
The following are distinct tested directions, not twenty names for a parameter
sweep. Negative results apply to the stated models; they are not impossibility
theorems for every construction in the family.

| Direction tested | Result and limiting charge |
|---|---|
| Fuse retirement cleanup into the exterior | Rejected: the proposed merge crosses a required late payload gate. |
| Replace the complex kernel with rounded partial Walsh transforms | Exact local identities pass, but the rank-one copied-output correction is essential. Removing it fails a basis input. |
| Compile complex Clifford transforms into bit routing | Exact identities pass; recursive basis alignment exceeds the tested residual budget. |
| Compress tensor addresses to an affine space of dimension `2h-1` | Exact isometry passes; the resulting boundary frame changes consume the saving. |
| Use a third shear to eliminate the final copy | The tested monotone frame word charges the removed rank-one cost back. |

| Use odd-field projective incidence geometry | The incidence involution passes; isotropic labels and correction costs obstruct the tested networks. |
| Build a fast finite projective Radon transform | Dirty-scratch scalar replay passes; no compatible paid frame word was found. |
| Replace triples with five-subset intersection mixers | Correction-channel rank bounds and charged larger labels miss the target. |
| Lift a ternary five-set construction to characteristic zero | The natural degree-three correction already exceeds the center budget. |
| Give each occurrence its future-root kernel frame | Optimistic auxiliary moments worsen; complete data geometry remains unproved. |
| Enlarge thousands of bit readout frames together | No new legal full-cap witness in the frozen model. Its relaxed bound is specific to the old fragmented compiler. |

| Delay direct signed-adjoint scratch reads to partial frames | Produces the selected valid complex improvement after complete source, target, endpoint and cost checks. |
| Combine the delayed reads with PR108 dual-suffix pair stars | A stronger producer can be combined, with predecessor attribution and fresh matching, adjoint and physical-path audits. |
| Combine partial frames with PR111/113 cyclic strips | The selected witness uses their exact credited producer and a new 9,356-role subframe schedule; all physical paths, Gram residuals, adjoint supports and full costs are rechecked. |

An important audit correction was the split-event schedule. Its scalar replay
passed, but a scan of every role's physical frame path found 2,948 descending
transitions. The apparent saving from that order was discarded. The repaired
closure preserves every original role's event order; only its separately
audited histogram can enter the selected certificate. This is also why a
scalar replay or a successful moment calculation alone is insufficient.

The selected proof and source reconstruction are reproduced by this package.
The exploratory negative screens are summarized here to describe the search;
they are not additional premises of its exponent certificate. No rejected
retirement, missing-copy, free-clean-workspace, or uncharged split schedule is
part of the submitted bound.
