# Universal resource-signature substitution and strict moment transport

This is a sufficient theorem for replacing complete supplier invocations. It is not a theorem that the retained all-size multiplication interfaces are unconditional, and it makes no claim of global novelty. Its application below uses a new bit word; it does not merely reprice an unchanged public word.

## Typed interface and theorem

Fix a finite outer controller C. Each call carries a physical owner map, entrance/final frames, input/output data conventions, orientation, and a complete-call footprint. A provider signature consists of these interfaces together with its actual residual widths and normalized endpoint. The endpoint on a helper family is Q on its assigned pending residual axes and identity on spectators. Scalar-body arbitrary-dirt restoration is a separate property; the physical helper endpoint is not asserted to be identity on the pending axes.

Suppose A and B have the same signature, each has a finite certificate of its scalar endpoint in both orientations, actual frame incidence, complete wrapper/adapter endpoint, and source-bound emitted paid profile. Assume C's literal owner assignments are legal for this signature. Replace any finite subset J of its complete A calls by B, preserving every boundary and owner assignment, and regenerate the source-bound stream and finite invoice.

Then (i) the outer operator, dirty-work endpoint, owner admission, and pending remainder inventory are unchanged; (ii) the paid histogram is exactly H_C + sum_{j in J}(H_B-H_A), with any orientation-dependent histogram taken in the call's actual orientation; (iii) rank mass and number of calls are preserved whenever those two quantities agree for A and B; and (iv) any pointwise paid-moment inequality between A and B transports to C with the same inequality, multiplied by the number of replacements.

Proof. At a replaced atomic boundary the two complete normalized linear maps agree on every datum and arbitrary dirty helper. Composition with the unchanged prefix and suffix therefore gives the same operator. Induction over the ordered calls proves (i), including cross-replica contexts: nothing is commuted through an incomplete call. The existing physical chart is instantiated with B's source-bound internal frames, whose entrance, exit and whole residual agree with A; hence the same pending intervals and full-owner assignments satisfy the admission premise. Recursive children are counted inside exactly one complete invocation or in the fixed controller remainder stream. Disjoint union of these ledgers proves (ii), and applying the linear mass/count functionals proves (iii). For fixed 0<a<1 the moment functional sum_r H(r)(r/m)^a/W is additive in H, proving (iv). A rank-one bad-class envelope proportional to the number of children is unchanged when the count is preserved. New scalar coefficient and prefix costs must still be checked; they are not inferred from profile equality. QED.

The theorem is also closed under substitution of already certified contexts: signatures compose at their exact boundaries, endpoint equality composes, and the histogram differences add. Thus a local quantitative improvement can be reused in any future finite controller satisfying the same signature, rather than being tied to a particular optimizer or stage count.

## Quantitative strict version used here

If one replacement changes a histogram by e_1 - 2e_2 + e_3, both total rank and child count remain fixed. For f(x)=x^a, 0<a<1,

    2 f(2) - f(1) - f(3)
      = a(1-a) integral_{x=1}^{2} integral_{t=0}^{1} (x+t)^(a-2) dt dx
      >= a(1-a) 3^(a-2) > 0.

Consequently n such replacements lower the normalized moment by at least

    n a(1-a) 3^(a-2) / (W m^a).

This is a uniform strict inequality for every admissible recurrence exponent, not a first-order entropy approximation or a fit at one floating-point value. The recurrence moment is strictly increasing as saving b=1-a increases, so its unique positive root strictly increases. The theorem does not authorize adding numeric kappa gains from separate constructions.

The unchanged literal selection hosts every one of 138,240 residual occurrences and uses no banks. The changed source word moves 60 source additions to admissible early rank-one frames.
