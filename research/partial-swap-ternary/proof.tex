\subsection{Partial swaps on the dimension-thirty ternary network}
\label{sec:partial-swap-ternary}
This composition is by Rohan Arun with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance.
The partial-swap address frame and its exact triangular conjugation are
due to icekylinx (PR~\#18). We apply them to the six-block ternary
composition of PR~\#17: eumemic's physical producer (PR~\#15) and
auxiliary source relocation (PR~\#13), the data corners of Rohan Arun
(PR~\#14, independently present in PR~\#16), and Zhihao Chen's nested
controlled basis (PR~\#16). The mixed-width tape construction of
icekylinx (PR~\#10), the earlier five-subset construction, compact
controls, and the inherited multiplication interfaces remain dependencies.
The contribution here is the compatibility argument and its parameter
certificate, not priority for those ingredients.

\paragraph{Address permutations and arbitrary scalar payloads.}
For a rational idempotent $P$, define the address map
\[
 D_P=\begin{pmatrix}I-P&P\\ P&I-P\end{pmatrix}
 \quad\hbox{on }(H,D).
\]
It satisfies $D_P^2=I$. After choosing a fixed good address prime $q$,
the same identity holds over the residue rings $\mathbb Z/q^f\mathbb Z$ for
every positive integer $f$. We use the same symbol for its induced
permutation of array entries. The rational address matrices do not act
on the scalar payload field, which remains $\mathbb F_3$.

The common-frame identity holds for arbitrary address permutations.
Assign one invertible address permutation $F_v$ to every gate, common
to all of its incidences, and apply $F_vF_u^{-1}$ along each edge
$u\to v$. For physical input $f_w$, define the logical input as
$g_w=F_{\mathrm{source}(w)}^{-1}f_w$. This is an interpretation, not a
preprocessing pass. A common permutation of addresses commutes with
every pointwise scalar gate. Induction along the circuit therefore gives,
when its scalar action is the role permutation $\rho$,
\[
 f'_{\rho(w)}=
 F_{\mathrm{sink}(\rho(w))}
 F_{\mathrm{source}(w)}^{-1}f_w.
\]
This statement includes all auxiliary inputs and requires no zero scratch.

The PR~\#15 producer retains the transparent ternary wrapper: with
source copy $V_0$, invertible mixer $L_0$, retained scatter $R_0$ and
side injection $J_0$, its chronological schedule is
\[
 L_0,-R_0,-J_0,L_0^{-1},V_0,L_0,R_0,J_0,L_0^{-1},-V_0.
\]
It subtracts $(R_0+J_0)L_0z$, adds
$(R_0+J_0)L_0(z+V_0x)$, restores arbitrary $z$, and adds $x$ to the
target. The inverse wrapper supplies the middle tensor invocation.
The three invocations send $(X,Y)$ to $(-Y,X)$; their retained final
pointwise negation on $X$ gives the exact exchange. Negation commutes
with every address permutation and remains at its existing full frame,
so it adds no edge operation. Thus replacing the address frames does
not change the scalar circuit or its arbitrary-scratch guarantee.

\paragraph{Nested edges and all-role endpoints.}
For nondegenerate nested labels $U\subset V$, orthogonal projectors
satisfy $P_UP_V=P_VP_U=P_U$. Hence
\[
 D_{P_V}D_{P_U}=D_{P_V-P_U}.
\]
The inverse transition is the same involution. Its positive residual
projector is $P_V-P_U$ in either orientation, and its pivot count is
$\dim V-\dim U$. A decreasing edge must consequently be compiled
using this positive residual, not the signed difference of its head
and tail matrices. All ordinary edges of the retained physical producer
join nested nondegenerate frames, including continued physical registers.
The only exceptional additive-shear source edges are handled next.

For a physical $X$ input with initial line projector $P$, assign source
frame $D_P$ and routed sink frame $D_{I-P}$. Their product is
\[
 D_{I-P}D_P=D_I,
\]
the full interchange of the two address chunks. Its first gate already
has frame $D_P$, so its first edge is the identity. In the additive-shear
construction this same edge had matrix $2P$ and required one singleton
call. Thus precisely one call disappears per $X$ input, for a total of
$N$ calls. The $Y$ inputs have source frame $D_0=I$ and routed sink
frame $D_I$, as do the ordinary auxiliary roles. Their endpoint product
is again $D_I$.

For each relocated stage-two auxiliary role, write $P_0=P_{D_0}$ and
$P_1=P_{D_1}$ for its first and last gate projectors, with
$P_0P_1=P_1P_0=P_0$. Here the subscripts $D_0,D_1$ denote the retained
subspaces, rather than partial-swap operators. Assign source frame
$D_{P_0}$ and sink frame $D_{I-P_0}$. The endpoint product is still
$D_I$. The entrance is the identity and the exit is
\[
 D_{I-P_0}D_{P_1}=D_{I+P_0-P_1}.
\]
Its projector $I+P_0-P_1$ has rank $m-h$ and nullspace
$D_1\cap D_0^\perp$. It is exactly the projector used for the nested
exit profile in PR~\#17. Source relocation therefore preserves that
profile and does not reintroduce an auxiliary entrance block.
The shared stage-one/stage-three roles retain zero source and full
sink frames and their unchanged nested join. This proves the required
endpoint interchange on every role.

\paragraph{The same pivot blocks are direct interchanges.}
Choose the single simultaneous rational basis of
Lemma~\ref{lem:nested-source-basis} for the entire finite projector
family and its duals, as in Section~\ref{sec:stream-nested-composition}.
Conjugate every label projector by this one basis. Nestedness,
idempotence, common gate frames and the endpoint products survive.
No separate coordinate choice is made for an individual edge.

For any resulting residual projector, write its lower triangular
factorization as $P=L\Pi R$, with $L,R$ invertible and $\Pi$ a normalized
partial permutation. Set
\[
 C=RL,\quad Q_X=\Pi\Pi^{\mathsf T},\quad
 Q_Y=\Pi^{\mathsf T}\Pi,\quad K=Q_YC-CQ_X,
 \qquad
 G=\begin{pmatrix}L&0\\0&R^{-1}\end{pmatrix}
   \begin{pmatrix}I&0\\K&I\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Idempotence implies $\Pi C\Pi=\Pi$. Direct multiplication gives
\[
 G^{-1}D_PG=
 \begin{pmatrix}I-Q_X&\Pi\\\Pi^{\mathsf T}&I-Q_Y\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The right side swaps exactly the coordinate pairs specified by $\Pi$
and fixes all other coordinates. Both $G$ and $G^{-1}$ are lower
triangular in the physical order with the $H$ fields before the $D$
fields. The retained ordered-affine primitive implements each in
$O(V)$ work, with arbitrary intervening spectators. First apply
$G^{-1}$, then these swaps, then $G$. An aligned contiguous block of
$t$ pivots uses one interchange of width $tf$; unselected pivots use
singleton width-$f$ interchanges. No gathering of separated pivots,
reversal, or extra recursive call is implicit in this factorization.
If elimination instead stores $L_1PR_1=\Pi$, the factors in this
display are $L=L_1^{-1}$ and $R=R_1^{-1}$.

Choose $q$ after the finite rational basis and all factors have been
fixed, avoiding their denominators and the nonzero diagonal numerators
of the triangular factors. The identities then hold for every field
width. This uses precisely the finite preparation already allowed by
the tape model.

\paragraph{Rank mass and the mixed-width moment.}
Retain the PR~\#17 constants
\[
 h=30,\quad m=27000,\quad v=142506,\quad R=13056812,
 \quad B=v^2R=265157216293565232,\quad
 N=v^3=2894006152890216,
\]
\[
 W=2v^2(v+R)=536102444892910896,\qquad
 L=3v^2\binom h2(h-2)=742052859715440.
\]
The six block widths and their multiplicities remain
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrr}
 t_i&26880&30&26940&782&842&25142\\\hline
 B_i&B&B&B&2N&2N&2N.
\end{array}
\]
Only the $N$ exceptional source calls disappear. Accordingly,
\[
 s_{\rm ps}=Wm-2N+2L,\qquad
 S_{\rm ps}=s_{\rm ps}-\sum_{i=1}^6B_it_i
            =41126567423153946552-N.
\]
All other edges retain their former pivot counts, including the
positive residuals of decreasing edges. The physical width, producer
and downward loss are unchanged.

Put $r_0=1/m$, $w_0=S_{\rm ps}/(Wm)$, and
$r_i=t_i/m$, $w_i=B_it_i/(Wm)$. Then
\[
 \Psi(1-a)=\sum_{i=0}^6 w_i\exp(a\log(1/r_i)),\qquad
 \sum_{i=0}^6w_i=\frac{s_{\rm ps}}{Wm}<1.
\]
The accompanying exact arithmetic certificate uses rational upper
enclosures $\ell_i>\log(1/r_i)$ and verifies, at
\[
 a_{\rm b}=\frac{4187}{10^9},\qquad \tau=1-a_{\rm b},
\]
that $0<a_{\rm b}\ell_i<1$ and
$\sum_iw_i/(1-a_{\rm b}\ell_i)<1$. The positive logarithm series and
$e^x\le(1-x)^{-1}$ for $0\le x<1$ then prove $\Psi(\tau)<1$.
No floating-point root estimate is used as the strict certificate.

\begin{lemma}[Partial-swap ternary arbitrary-width interchange]
\label{lem:partial-swap-ternary-chunk-swap}
There is one fixed finite-alphabet multitape procedure which, for every
$P,G,B_0\ge1$ and $u\ge1$, interchanges the two equal chunks in an
arbitrary bit array on
\[
 [P]\times[2^u]\times[G]\times[2^u]\times[B_0]
\]
by $(p,x,g,y,z)\mapsto(p,y,g,x,z)$ in $O(Vu^\tau)$ steps, where
$V=PGB_0\,2^{2u}$ and $\tau=1-4187/10^9$. The constant is independent
of all field lengths and payload values. Shape processing, complete-row
padding and removal, address-radix padding and removal, and fixed-tape
workspace cleanup are included. Chunks differing by one bit cost
$O(V(1+u^\tau))$, where $u$ is the smaller width, with the same arbitrary
spectator gap.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Split complete rows into the $W$ retained role streams and execute the
unchanged pointwise ternary circuit with the partial-swap edge compiler
above. Merge output role $\rho(w)$ back into input row position $w$.
The all-role endpoint products show that this circuit directly performs
the main interchange. In particular, omit the two outer ordered-affine
passes that formerly converted the circuit's address shear into a swap:
they would be incorrect here. In pseudocode, the main operation is
\texttt{MAIN\_INTERCHANGE := CIRCUIT\_PARTIAL\_SWAP}.

At width $e$, the children have widths $\lfloor e/m\rfloor$ with
multiplicity $S_{\rm ps}$ and $t_i\lfloor e/m\rfloor$ with
multiplicity $B_i$, each on volume $V/W$. Every $t_i<m$, so all
children are smaller. The strict moment just established supplies the
same strong-induction bound as Section~\ref{sec:mixed-width-rows}.
The row-depth reservation, bounded digit remainders, high-digit row
field, and one-time complete-row padding depend only on that child list
and the already proved interchange interface. They therefore apply
unchanged. The payload alphabet is the fixed $\mathbb F_3$ alphabet,
while address-radix conversion remains the retained ordered numerical
padding procedure; these are different operations. Arbitrary auxiliary
payloads are restored by the scalar wrapper. The original two layouts
for the one-bit unequal-width extension use this equal-width routine,
and the retained depth-first workspace cleanup proves the stated tape
bound.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Complex side and conditional assembly.}
The complex network remains the dimension-28, 34-class all-residual
construction of Section~\ref{sec:nested-complex}, with the stopping
guard of Section~\ref{sec:nested-complex-guard}. We use its stronger
certified moment at
\[
 a_{\rm c}=\frac{4191487}{10^{12}},\qquad \sigma=1-a_{\rm c},
\]
rather than PR~\#17's conservative $4/10^6$. The exact certificate is
recomputed by \texttt{scripts/experiments/complex\_all\_residuals.py};
neither the complex producer nor its rank histogram changes. The guard
depends on those unchanged ranks and path budgets, not on the bit
address-frame choice. Thus with $\beta=1/1000$ and $\zeta=1/10000$,
\[
 C_1=\frac32-\frac\beta2+\zeta=\frac{3749}{2500},\qquad
 (1-\beta)a_{\rm c}
 =\frac{999}{1000}\frac{4191487}{10^{12}}
 >\frac{4187}{10^9}=a_{\rm b}.
\]
The final assembly uses these two interfaces and the exact parameter
certificate for $\kappa=2093495/10^{12}$. Every assembly constraint and
margin must be checked with these parameters; a bit recurrence alone
does not establish the multiplication claim. The resulting statement
retains the original fixed-tape, analytic and precision assumptions,
and the general finite-family basis and transfer arguments remain
subject to independent mathematical review.
