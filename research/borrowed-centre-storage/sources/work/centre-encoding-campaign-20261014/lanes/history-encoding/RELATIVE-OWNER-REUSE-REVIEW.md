## Representation and restoration

Let the old physical owner value at capture be a. Let T be its complete
native map through the interval. The source query has physical values
q0 and q1 at the two cuts. If the known cover permutation M satisfies
T M q0 = M q1, the capture, old jobs, and erase have the exact effect

```text
a -> a + M q0 -> T a + T M q0 -> T a.
```

This identity holds for every a. It also holds when a or q0 depends on
other input fields. The complete helper contract acts by the stated
native map on every helper input. Its other complete outputs do not
depend on that helper input. Thus a complete helper job can process
the dirty cache without a separate assumption about data independence.

The query alias has relative map P_g F_E P_g^-1 at source cover class
g. The actual donor interval has owner-chart map
T0 = product C_v, where the eight v are distinct columns of its actual
orthonormal chart. The certificate supplies an orthogonal map U with
U(E) equal to those eight columns. Thus

```text
T0 = P_U F_E P_U^-1.
```

For donor class h, use source class g = h U. The resulting cover
permutation is (M q)(h) = q(h U). The physical array address does not
change. It follows that T M = M T_query. No equality of absolute entry
frames is used. In fact, 499 of the 512 selected donor entry ranks
differ from their query entry ranks.

This proof uses the exact positive original-C factors. For a binary
vector v, the Fourier phase exponent of C_v is parity(v dot z).
Modulo four, this function has only linear and quadratic terms:

```text
parity(v dot z) = sum_i v_i z_i - 2 sum_(j<i) v_i v_j z_i z_j.
```

The checker compares every coefficient of the full 73-variable phase
polynomial for the actual helper path and the conjugated fresh block.
It also checks zero scalar phase and zero translation. This proves
equality on all characters, including the spectator coordinates.
Tensor products give the same identity at every positive tensor size.

## Cost and scope

The dirty transport adds no native child and no field. The original
live and physical stocks remain 14,336 and 14,826. No new productive
rank credit is taken.

The normalized capture and erase counts are 27,136 and 512 complex
ADDs. Their real primitive subtotal is 274,432. This is not a complete
finite invoice. Cover reindexing, source gathers, address control,
precision, and the local source logger require their separate bills.

The result does not make the cache clean. A later use must cancel its
unknown old value. It does not permit an alias to a local C register,
restore the source code, complete the data controller, or improve
kappa by itself. The earlier equal-entry-frame bounds remain valid
for their own interfaces.
