# Independent review of the two-cut donor allocation

The frozen allocation passes this independent source review. The 128 old
productive donor fields are distinct. Each has one cover map that gives
the required native advance at both cuts. All three adapters have the
stated exact phases, including the adapter that returns the arbitrary
donor baseline to its old scheduled output.

The added donor profile is 200 rank-one children and seven rank-two
children, with rank mass 214. There are 414 native wrappers, 107 nonzero
translations within those wrappers, no separate translation map, and
768 record cover maps. At most 48 records are live at one time. No new
productive or clean record family is used.

This review accepts the fixed allocation and its conditional native
transport. The local dirty repair, whole-W data parking, and delayed
cleanup are separate accepted premises. The review does not prove the
new recursive child hosting, physical stream invoice, precision, other
data outputs, or a complete supplier. There is no kappa claim.

## Independent source reconstruction

The checker reads the selected archive directly. It does not import or
execute the donor producer, its loader, or the prior consumer checker.
It reconstructs all 482 events, 144 local owner sets, 69,120 helper jobs,
7,526 idle suffixes, and 35,840 complete pending-child partitions.
Every helper width, axis slice, route, owner, strict release, and deadline
agrees with the archive and the canonical local frame word.

It also reconstructs all active-data, bridge, and terminal barriers.
The added full-W cleanup writes both pair-1 data banks at the sixteen
source-event-5 idle cuts. These new writes are excluded as well. The
checker reproduces the full bounded candidate histogram for each of the
sixteen replica intervals. It checks the fixed allocation; it does not
run a matching search or claim a globally minimum allocation cost.

For each replica the three boundaries are after its complete F0 sweep,
after its complete B1 sweep, and after its pair-1 idle that follows F2.
For replica 0 these are execution positions 0, 4, and 19. Every selected
donor has no active-data or scalar barrier between its capture and its
return. The primary allocation uses 262 complete old helper jobs of
total rank 2091. It uses no partial helper cut. The helper width counts
are 12 of width 4, 59 of width 7, 89 of width 8, and 102 of width 9.

All 128 family IDs are distinct, which is stronger than interval-only
reuse. Cover reindexing is a bijection within each family, so it creates
no alias between simultaneous records. The checker finds the maximum

## One exact isometry at both cuts

Let K1 and K2 be the two old donor interval operators. Each is a product
of intrinsic C lines and a recorded translation. The checker rebuilds
these products from the actual jobs. Their line sets are orthonormal and
disjoint. The two desired sets D1 and D2 each have eight axes. One
orthogonal U maps E1 to D1 and E2 to D2. Both inverse orders of U are
checked on all 73 coordinate vectors.

The source-to-donor convention is

    (M z)(g) = z(g U).

Thus a source at g is added to the donor at g U inverse. For Vj equal
to the C product on Dj, covariance gives

    V1 M = M F_E1,       V2 M = M F_E2.

These are exact physical identities. At donor cover label g, both sides
use the same lines g U e_i. No equality of different cover values or
unproved common payload frame is used. The same M is used at capture,
repair, and erase. A match of only the total sixteen-axis advance would
not prove the first consumption cut.

The three adapters are

    A1 = V1 K1 inverse,
    A2 = V2 K2 inverse,
    A3 = K2 K1 (V2 V1) inverse.

The checker uses explicit linear coefficients and quadratic adjacency
rows for the full 73-variable Fourier phase. This representation is
separate from the producer's packed bit planes. It proves A1 K1=V1,
A2 K2=V2, and A3 V2 V1=K2 K1. Each negative line includes the required
translation from C_v inverse = X_v C_v. Each nonempty adapter has one
rank-r child and two exact inverse orthogonal wrappers. The translation
is part of its left affine wrapper. All map orders and native axes agree
with the serialized recipes.

The tensor identity holds at every positive multiplicity: the checked
one-layer operator is applied in each tensor layer. This statement does
not prove the recursive implementation or its work stock.

## Arbitrary baselines and complete old helper jobs

Let a be the donor's arbitrary physical value at capture. Define
h = F_L inverse M inverse a. This is a coordinate definition, not a zero
input condition. After capture, the first old path, and A1, the donor is
M F_U1(h+u_A). The accepted dirty repair acts on this common-frame value.
It leaves data z+E h and donor M F_U1(h+G z).

The accepted full-W consumer parks the entire data pair through F2.
The second old path and A2 give donor M F_U2(h+G z). The accepted cleanup
returns the data to F_U2 z and the donor to M F_U2 h. A3 then returns the
donor to K2 K1 a, exactly the old scheduled output. The donor is never
cleared. Both arbitrary data and arbitrary donor input are retained.

Old complete helper jobs remain valid when a donor carries these values.
Their physical output is the fixed native job applied to their arbitrary
input. Their completed active outputs have zero dependence on that input.
This is the pinned old local contract; it is not inferred from a matching
frame name. The added capture, repair, and cleanup words reference only
data, record, and private-C roles. They contain no old helper-role read
outside a complete core. Thus a donor can be an old helper during a core
without an additional boundary alias.

The checker reads the literal eight-record boundary word. Its eight
leading record E1 children are exactly the children replaced by these
old paths and adapters. Its remaining 112 positive Y lines are retained.
It also reads the accepted one-C cleanup word: it has no new native child
and no borrowed CLEAR. The local algebra and the all-column cleanup
replay are pinned, not rerun by this owner review.

## Counts, child hosting, and limits

The per-replica adapter masses are

    16,16,16,10,16,6,14,16,28,8,16,6,16,0,14,16.

The sum is 214. The separately reported pair-2/3-only allocation also

With 112 retained Y lines and 112 endpoint X lines per replica, the
conditional complete added profile is {1:3784, 2:7}, of mass 3798.
This count does not remove the raw QQ16 core costs or any old helper,
idle, or tail job. It gives no productive-width credit for the donors.

The 207 new donor children have explicit rank-one or rank-two axes and
correct native wrappers. This is not a full recursive hosting proof.
Before this component can enter a supplier invoice, a caller must bind
their compact and spectator ranges, row divisibility, recursive private
stock, and child workspaces to the retained compiler. It must also bind
the remaining repair children and the final decoder lines. The scalar
boundary words use private C only after old cores or idle sweeps return;
no persistent donor is a private C field. This separation does not by
itself price recursive scratch or the new physical map streams.

The exact rank and native transport result therefore survives. The
complete precision and supplier claims remain open. In particular, the
selected-16-only parking consumer cannot replace the full-W premise.
