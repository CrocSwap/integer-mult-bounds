# Two-cut transport in 128 old borrowed fields

The pinned 482-event schedule has 128 distinct old fields that can carry
the eight dirty A records in each of its sixteen replicas. One fixed cover
map per field aligns the two required native advances. Three short native
adapters, including full donor restoration, have total profile

    200 rank-one children, seven rank-two children; rank mass 214.

The word needs 414 native wrappers. Of their left maps, 107 have a recorded
translation. The certificate also records whether a translation needs a
separate map. Capture, repair, and erase use three pairs of inverse cover
maps on each field, or 768 cover maps in total. No new record family is
introduced. The assigned 128 old fields are all distinct, even when their
use intervals do not overlap.

This is a native transport and restoration result. It applies to the
delayed full-W consumer in which all 224 recovered data fields are parked
through F2. It does not prove the delayed query read, the remaining data
outputs, new recursive child hosting, or the complete finite and precision
invoice. The selected-16-only consumer does not inherit the full-W data
alias. There is no supplier or kappa claim.

## The actual cuts and exclusions

For each replica, the record interval starts after its whole F0 sweep.
Its first internal cut is after the whole B1 sweep. Its last cut is after
the old pair-1 idle that follows F2, before the second bridge. In replica

The checker reconstructs all 69,120 complete helper jobs and all 7,526

the field has an active, bridge, or terminal use inside the interval. It
also excludes every new full-W erase write to the parked pair-1 bank.
Thus no unrecorded scalar use of a chosen donor is assumed to be harmless.

All retained helper jobs have the complete two-sided arbitrary-input

outputs and other helpers have zero dependence on that input. The proof

The source loader and complete helper reconstruction are reused from the

## Two native advances with one cover map

Write C_v for the original intrinsic line on an odd unit vector v. For
orthogonal vectors these operators commute, and

    C_v squared = X_v,       C_v inverse = X_v C_v.

Here X_v is the exact address translation. Both identities include phase.
Let B1 and B2 be the two disjoint sets of native axes used by one donor
between the three cuts. Let t1 and t2 be the two total translations. Its
old interval operators are

    K1 = X_t1 product_(v in B1) C_v,
    K2 = X_t2 product_(v in B2) C_v.

The actual axes belong to one recorded orthonormal basis of dimension 73.
Choose two disjoint eight-axis sets D1 and D2 from this basis. One
orthogonal matrix U sends E1 to D1 and E2 to D2. Complete its other columns
with the unused basis vectors. The same U is used at all three cuts.

Let V1 and V2 be the products of C_v on D1 and D2. Apply

    A1 = V1 K1 inverse             after B1,
    A2 = V2 K2 inverse             at the erase cut,
    A3 = K2 K1 (V2 V1) inverse     after the erase and data correction.

All terms commute. Thus A1 K1=V1, A2 K2=V2, and A3 V2 V1=K2 K1.
Each adapter is a product on the symmetric difference of two axis sets,
with an explicit translation for every negative line. The checker proves
these three phase identities as exact quadratic polynomials modulo four
on all 73 character coordinates. It also emits an orthogonal wrapper and
its inverse for every nonempty adapter. A rank-r adapter is one original
rank-r recursive native child, on r tensor axes, with these two wrappers.
The tensor identity holds for every positive multiplicity.

For a source field indexed by cover element g, use the donor at g U inverse.
Equivalently, (M z)(h)=z(h U). This is a permutation of independent cover
classes. It does not identify their data. Orthogonal conjugation gives

    V1 M = M F_E1,       V2 M = M F_E2.

Therefore one fixed M gives the required first advance and the required
second advance. Matching only their product would not suffice.

## Arbitrary donor baseline and its exact return

Let a be the donor's old arbitrary physical value at capture. Let the
saved query be F_L u_A. Capture adds M F_L u_A to a. Put

    h = F_L inverse M inverse a.

This is a coordinate definition for an arbitrary value. It is not a clean
input premise. After the first retained native interval and A1, the donor
is M F_U1(h+u_A). Undo M for the dirty repair, then restore M. The local
word in [EIGHT-RECORDS.md](EIGHT-RECORDS.md) leaves the data z+E h and donor
M F_U1(h+G z), where G E=0.

The full-W consumer parks this entire data pair through F2. Complete old
helper jobs and the old idle advance it to F_U2(z+E h). At the last donor
cut, A2 makes the donor M F_U2(h+G z). A legal full-U2 cleanup must subtract
G from the data, then subtract E of the restored donors. If that cleanup
is supplied, its output is F_U2 z and M F_U2 h.

Finally apply A3 to the donor. Its value becomes exactly K2 K1 a. This is
the value required by the old schedule at that point. It can differ from
a because the old schedule had native work to perform. No donor CLEAR
or clean-baseline assumption is used.

## Construction and cost

For one path let r1 and r2 be the two old advance ranks. Take up to eight
axes from each advance for its matching desired set. Fill a deficit first
from unused axes of the other advance, then from unused basis axes. Define

    e = max(r1-8,0)+max(r2-8,0),
    d = max(8-r1,0)+max(8-r2,0).

The three adapters then have total rank 2 max(e,d). This is the minimum
for this fixed-basis assignment class. An axis used in its matching cut
costs zero. An unused old axis, a new axis, or an axis moved to the other
cut costs two. Pairing an excess axis with a deficit gives the stated
bound. This is not an optimality claim over other native representations.

The selected allocation uses each of 128 old fields once. Its per-replica
adapter masses are

    16,16,16,10,16,6,14,16,28,8,16,6,16,0,14,16.

Their sum is 214. For example, role 0 uses owner family 9313. Its first
path has nine axes; its second path has seven. Move the extra first axis
to the second desired set. The first adapter is one negative line, the
second is its positive line, and the final restoration is identity.
The record has the exact required frame at both cuts.

The packet also supplies a simpler allocation restricted to old pairs 2
and 3. It costs 256 rank-one and 60 rank-two children, of mass 376. Neither
allocation is claimed to have minimum total cost among all assignments.

In the full-W consumer, the eight owned E1 record children per replica
are replaced by the retained donor paths and these adapters. The remaining
new repair children are 112 positive Y lines per replica. The endpoint
still needs 112 positive X lines. Thus the conditional complete added
native profile of this repair and consumer is

    {1:3784, 2:7},       mass 3798,

which includes every new donor adapter and its restoration. The raw QQ16
calls and all original helper, idle, and tail children are separate retained
costs. The final query read and scalar erase need their own literal invoice.
No rank credit is added for the borrowed fields. The new child workspaces,
the wrapper streams, precision, and the complete remaining data map are
still open obligations.
