## Masked current-query unit

Work over a ring with free coordinate modules. Let R and K have
compatible sizes. Choose coordinates A for which R_A is invertible,
and let the A part of K be `P_A=iota_A R_A^-1`. Write

```text
L_A = R_A pi_A,   R_offA = R - L_A,
K_offA = K - P_A, J = I - K R,
T = (I - K_offA L_A)(I + P_A R_offA).
```

The two factors of T are unit shears. Their inverses have opposite signs
and reverse order. Direct multiplication gives `L_A T=R` and
`pi_offA T=pi_offA J`.

Let U be the retained coordinates, and let V0 be their complement. Put
`R_U=R pi_U` and `L_V0=R pi_V0`. Suppose there is a section P_U with

```text
R_U P_U = I,   pi_V0 P_U = 0.
```

Then `L_V0 P_U=0`, and

```text
B = I - P_U L_V0,  B^-1 = I + P_U L_V0,
R B = R_U.
```

For any prescribed source query D, set

```text
H_D = J + P_A(D - L_A J).
```

Only its A output rows differ from J, and `L_A H_D=D`. The complete map

```text
X_out = X,
Y_out = H_D X + T B Y
```

is a unit, with inverse

```text
X = X_out,
Y = B^-1 T^-1 (Y_out - H_D X_out).
```

Its selected query is `D X + R_U Y`. All products keep their given order.
No inverse of J or commutation assumption is used.

The section condition is also necessary for a unit C with `L_A C=R_U`.
A section supported on U is `pi_U C^-1 P_A`, since
`R_U pi_U C^-1 P_A=R_U C^-1 P_A=L_A P_A=I`.
The projection pi_U is required for the support claim. This corrects the
support detail in the frozen necessity paragraph without changing that
historical file.
