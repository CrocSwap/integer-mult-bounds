## Nondegenerate extension

Let B be a nondegenerate readout subspace in the admissible cap C, the intersection of its slot's start frame and all subsequent target frames. Orthogonal decomposition gives C = B ⊕ (C∩B⊥). In characteristic zero, a nonzero symmetric residual form has an anisotropic vector: either a basis vector has nonzero norm or the sum of two basis vectors with nonzero pairing does. Adjoining such vectors preserves nondegeneracy and reaches the Gram rank of C. The primitive rational representatives are independently checked; a degenerate full cap is never accepted merely because its dimension is larger.

PR99 performs the extension backwards through the readout order. Enlarging a later frame to contain its former value preserves the admissibility of all earlier original frames. Intersecting with the actual new subsequent frames ensures the newly chosen frame is contained in each successor. A dimension sort preserves each target chain: comparable equal-dimensional subspaces are equal. The submitted exact review checks all 119 affected target edges and every changed start-frame inclusion. The inherited complete rational checker also checks every target/data chain, nondegeneracy and both conjugations of all required new high-rank partial swaps at the common integer point. These finite nonvanishing witnesses support the inherited generic common-basis argument; they do not formalize that all-size argument.
