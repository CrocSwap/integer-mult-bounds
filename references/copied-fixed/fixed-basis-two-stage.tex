\subsection{Full data corner without a generic-basis assumption}
The null projector of $(I-p\xi)\otimes(I-v\nu)$ has image
\[
 \{u\otimes v+p\otimes w:u\in\mathbb Q^a,w\in\mathbb Q^b\},
\]
whose only parameter redundancy is $(u,w)=(cp,-cv)$. At a physical row
with coefficient label $r$ and residue $\beta$, divide its evaluation by
$p_rv_\beta\ne0$. The resulting functional is $u_r/p_r+w_\beta/v_\beta$.
The first $d=a+b-1=91$ restrictions are therefore the unsigned incidence
matrix of the bipartite graph with edges $(R_i,\beta_i)$, where
\[
 R=[0,\ldots,46;1,\ldots,44],\quad \beta_i=i\bmod45.
\]
This is a tree on 92 vertices. The dual last 91 restrictions have edges
$(C_j,\gamma_j)$ with
$C=[2,\ldots,45;0,\ldots,46]$, $\gamma_j=(j+44)\bmod45$, also a tree.
The supplied finite check reconstructs both actual graphs and verifies
91 edges and connectivity. Leaf elimination proves incidence rank 91.
Thus both nullspace restriction matrices are nonsingular for \emph{every}
nonzero primal and dual coordinate choice, including these fixed bases.
Their product is the full null corner; no modular sample is used here.

On the first $b-1$ rows and last $b$ columns the diagonally scaled null
corner is
\[
 Z_{ij}=y_i[i=j]+x_i[i=j+2]-1,\qquad
 x_i=(p_i\xi_i)^{-1},\ y_i=(v_i\nu_i)^{-1}.

row0 from rows $1\le i\le b-2$ leaves
$y_i[i=j]+x_i[i=j+2]-y_0[j=0]$. This is zero for $j>i$ and nonzero at $j=i$.

\section{Exact local profiles and all transition cases}
