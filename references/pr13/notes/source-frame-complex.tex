\subsection{A third whole-residual complex class}
\label{sec:source-frame-complex}

The whole-residual construction of Section~\ref{sec:bulk-complex-child}
applies to any edge residual. We additionally select the stage-three data
entrance edges. At stage three the incoming physical $X$ and $Y$ labels
have dimensions $h^2$ and $h^2-1$, and their first relevant frames have
dimensions $(h^2-1)h+1$ and $(h^2-1)h$. Both entrance residuals are
$B\otimes t^\perp$ with $\dim B=h^2-1$, of rank
\[
 d_3=(h^2-1)(h-1)=21141.
\]
There are $2v_{\rm c}^3=70317217152$ such edges. Each has an orthonormal
residual basis by the retained binary-frame lemma. It is therefore applied
as one child on $21141f$ selected axes, with the same sign wrappers.

\paragraph{The dependency-path guard.}
The path budget $q=m+6h=22120$ of
Section~\ref{sec:pathwise-guard} is unchanged. Since $2d_3>q$, a dependency
path still crosses at most one of the three selected classes. For the new
class put $t=(m-d_3)/m=811/21952$. With the same bound for $(1-t)^{6/5}$,
\[
 \frac{q-d_3}{m^{6/5}}+\left(\frac{d_3}m\right)^{6/5}
 <\frac{979}{160000}+1-\frac65t+\frac3{25}\frac{t^2}{1-t}
 =\frac{372026142559}{386739360000}<\frac{999}{1000}.
\]
Consequently the guard of Section~\ref{sec:bulk-complex-guard} holds with
three classes, with the same $\rho=6/5$, $\overline\theta=999/1000$,
$C_{\rm dep}$, $C_0$ and $C_1=6/5-\beta/5+\zeta$. The mixed-width layer of
Section~\ref{sec:mixed-complex-layer} has child widths $f$, $21896f$,
$21168f$ and $21141f$.
