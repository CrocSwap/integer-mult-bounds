\begin{abstract}
We extend the rational-frame interchange transfer to a fixed finite scalar
alphabet and construct a ternary five-subset network with shared common-pair
side computations. Every auxiliary role carries arbitrary data and is
restored. The finite circuit supports bit saving $467/10^{11}$. Combined
with the retained complex network, compact-control movement and precision
proofs, it gives the conditional exponent $\kappa=2^{-30}$.
The upstream algorithmic claims remain assumptions.
\end{abstract}
