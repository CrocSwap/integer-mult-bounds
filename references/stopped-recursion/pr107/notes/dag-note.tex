\begin{abstract}
A cancellation-free computation graph replaces the separate rectangle mixers
in our preceding finite bit network. Reusing roles along computation paths
needs one auxiliary role per output plus one per binary addition. Source
supports and reachable-target supports supply nondegenerate frames for the
forward and reversed stages, without extra rank loss. The resulting conditional
integer-multiplication saving is $\kappa=2^{-63}$, a factor 16 above the prior
$2^{-67}$ witness. No practical runtime improvement or optimality is claimed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Conditional exponent and checks}
The logarithm enclosure used above follows from
\[
 2\sum_{j=0}^{23}\frac{z^{2j+1}}{2j+1}\le\log x\le
 2\sum_{j=0}^{23}\frac{z^{2j+1}}{2j+1}
 +\frac{2z^{49}}{49(1-z^2)},\quad z=\frac{x-1}{x+1},\quad 1\le x\le2,
\]
applied to $2$ and $m/2^{16}$ with exact rational arithmetic.
With $a=a_{\rm b}$, take
\begin{gather*}
 \epsilon=1/21,\quad\beta=9/10,\quad c=9a/10,\quad
 \lambda=1-19a^2/20,\quad\lambda'=1-9a^2/10,\\
 \delta=1/16,\qquad C_1=20.
\end{gather*}
Then $\tau(1+c/\beta)=1-a^2<\lambda<\lambda'<1$; the separate complex
conditions follow from $19a^2/20<a_{\rm c}$ and $9a^2<a_{\rm c}$. The exact
minimum of the seven exponent margins is
\[
 G=\frac{3a^2}{70}=\frac{104907}{700000000000000000000000}>2^{-63}.
\]
The remaining margins are $g_1>1/2$, $g_4=20a/21$, $g_5=13/112$,
$g_6=299/336$, and $g_7=1/21$. The strict gap absorbs fixed logarithmic
factors, giving $O(n(\log n)^{1-2^{-63}})$ under the retained assumptions.
The previous dimension, stopping, and guard audits still apply: the guard
is $p^{20/21}=o(p)$ and $K=\Theta(p^{3a/70})=o(\ell)$ for
