\section{Removing the redundant complex center}
\label{sec:dyadic-central-factor}
Retain the disjoint tensor circuit and all phase frames of
Section~\ref{sec:disjoint-tensor-compression}. Replace only its central scalar
factor, retaining a common gate on all data and center incidences for each
gather or scatter, even when a coefficient is zero. The ground size $h\ge8$
is even, and $v=\binom h3$.

Fix $T_0=\{0,1,2\}$. Define $v\times h$ and $h\times v$ integral matrices
$U,V$ by
\[
 U_{S,0}=|S\cap T_0|-1,\qquad
 U_{S,i}=\mathbf1_{i\in S}-\mathbf1_{0\in S}\quad(1\le i<h),
\]
\[
 V_{0,T}=1,\qquad
 V_{i,T}=\mathbf1_{i\in T}-\mathbf1_{i\in T_0}\quad(1\le i<h).
\]
Writing $t_T$ for the indicator vector, its difference from $t_{T_0}$
has coordinate sum zero, so
\[
 t_T-t_{T_0}=\sum_{i=1}^{h-1}(V_{i,T})(e_i-e_0).
\]
Taking its scalar product with $t_S$ proves
$(UV)_{S,T}=|S\cap T|-1$. Thus gather $V$ and scatter $U/2$ realize
the same central map using $h$ arbitrary center roles. Entries of $V$ are
$0,\pm1$ and entries of $U$ are $-1,0,1,2$; only additions, subtractions,
and halvings are required. No division by an odd integer is introduced.

Writing $V_{\rm side}$ for the side copy map, the twelve signed operations
still give $JLV_{\rm side}+(U/2)V=I$. More explicitly, the side part
adds the unchanged correction and restores every arbitrary side input;
the central commutator adds $(U/2)Vx$ and restores every arbitrary central
input. The sum is $x$. The inverse invocation subtracts $x$ and also restores
all auxiliaries. Grouping all incidences at the retained common frames
makes this scalar change independent of the phase assignment.

Each central role has the same frame history as an old center, with precisely
one downward change $D_1\to D_0$ of residual dimension $h$ per invocation.
Its residual is the standard nonalternating $h$-space, tensored with the
retained norm-one lines. No side or data transition changes. First/third-stage
reuse preserves local center indices and has exactly the previous endpoint
frames. Consequently, with $R$ the fully counted side roles,
\[
 m=h^3,\qquad W=2v^3+2v^2(R+h),\qquad L=3v^2h^2,
 \qquad s=Wm-2v^3+2L.
\]
Relative to the $h+1$-center construction, $W$ decreases by $2v^2$ and
the deficit increases by $6v^2h$. The signed bank exchange, all-role endpoint
corrections, and phase corrections remain those already proved.

Let $G$ be the number of nonzero entries in $V$. Charge one addition for
each $U_{S,i}/2=1$, and one halving plus one addition for coefficients
$\pm1/2$; let $H$ be the sum of these charges. If the side mixer uses $M$
additions, $C$ copies and $J$ weighted injections, the complete scalar count
is bounded by
\[
 3v^2(4M+2C+4J+2G+2H).
\]
The checker expands the factor at $h=26$, verifies all $v^2$ coefficients,
and checks the full node charge against $E=64(W+m+1)^3$ as well as $s<m^5$.
Exact logarithm bounds give $1-\log_m(s/W)>3.6\cdot10^{-8}$.
This is separate complex-interface headroom. It does not change the retained
bit interface or assert a new multiplication exponent.
