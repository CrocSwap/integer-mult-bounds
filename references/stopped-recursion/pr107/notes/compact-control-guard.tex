\subsection{A generalized stopped-depth guard}
\label{sec:compact-stopped-guard}
Use the complex network's own constants $m,W,s$, independently of the bit
network used by swaps. Assume $m\ge3$ and $2\le s<m^5$, as checked below.
For rational $0<\beta<1$ and $\zeta>0$, put
\[
 E=64(W+m+1)^3,\quad B=s+E,\quad C_1=5-4\beta+\zeta,
\]
\[
 C_0=\left\lceil\max\{128mB^2,18mB^2(1+1/\zeta)\}\right\rceil,
 \qquad \Delta=\lceil C_0d^{C_1}\rceil.
\]
As in the retained exact-arithmetic guard proof, a dependency-depth upper
bound is
\[
 A(e)\le sA(e/m)+E\quad\hbox{internally},\qquad A(e)\le8e
 \quad(e<d^\beta).
\]
The fixed node charge $E$ remains additive. All new compact-control,
padding and repair operations only permute complete coefficient encodings
and introduce no coefficient arithmetic.

For a root $e\le d$, the number of internal levels is at most
$(1-\beta)\log_m d+1$. Hence
\[
 s^j\le s d^{5(1-\beta)},\qquad
 A(e)\le s(8+E)d^{5-4\beta}\le9B^2d^{5-4\beta}.
\]
A root that is already a leaf also satisfies this bound. There are at most
$m(1+1/\zeta)d^\zeta$ base-$m$ pieces, using
$\log d\le d^\zeta/\zeta$ and $\log m>1$. Individually processed
row, front and back axes are disjoint and number at most $d$, so their
depth is at most $8d$. The outer phases and $b^D$ conversion add at most
$\lfloor D/2\rfloor+9$ units, including denominator shifts. Consequently
\[
 A_{\rm layer}\le9mB^2(1+1/\zeta)d^{C_1}+18d
              \le C_0d^{C_1}.
\]
The exact integer comparison for the final inequality is included in the
certificate. The usual induction gives denominator exponent at most
$p+\Delta$ and magnitude at most $2^\Delta$. Thus $p+\Delta$ fractional
bits and $\Delta+3$ integer/sign bits suffice per component. The condition
$\epsilon C_1<1$ makes these widths $O(p)$, uniformly over the retained
parameter bands. Integer power comparisons implement the rational guard
ceilings in polynomial descriptor time.
