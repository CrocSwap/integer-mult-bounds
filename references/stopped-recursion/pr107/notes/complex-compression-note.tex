\begin{abstract}
We give two reusable methods for compressing weighted side computations
while retaining the binary phase-frame contract in OpenAI's integer
multiplication manuscript. Coordinate enclosures support disjoint-triple
rectangles; an explicit orthonormal family supports shared intersection-two
sums. Signed transparent computation restores arbitrary scratch, and an
even-ground-size matching reuses complete first/third-stage auxiliary banks.
At $h=26$ the construction supports complex saving $5\cdot10^{-9}$,
exceeding the retained bit saving. An independent combined upstream patch
integrates this construction and the retained compact-control arguments,
supporting the conditional bound $\kappa=2^{-31}$. The previously published
$2^{-34}$ artifacts are unchanged.
\end{abstract}
The retained manuscript is pinned at

Its algorithmic interfaces remain assumptions. This local research note
concerns the same fixed finite-alphabet, fixed-tape model and makes no
practical runtime or unrestricted optimality claim.
