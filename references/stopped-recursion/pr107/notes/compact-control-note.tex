\begin{abstract}
We replace the full-slot permutations in the simultaneous-layer routine by
swaps of compact dirty-control fields. Complete temporary ranges are reserved
from existing address coordinates and survive every recursive row split.
The row-addition overhead becomes $O(V((f\log p)^\tau+1))$, with no spacing
factor $K^\tau$, and deterministic exceptional repair is included. Together
with an independently sized original complex network at $h=25$, the retained
paired bit network and downstream refinements give the conditional witness
$\kappa=83/10^{12}>2^{-34}$. The underlying manuscript's algorithmic and
analytic interfaces remain assumptions. This is a research draft awaiting
independent review.
\end{abstract}

arithmetic; the general claims rest on the written arguments, not on simulation
or formal verification.

\section{Parameters and complete assembly}
Put $a=296/10^{11}$ and $a_{\rm c}=418/10^{12}$. Choose
\begin{gather*}
 \tau=1-a,\quad\sigma=1-a_{\rm c},\quad
 \epsilon=\frac{1999}{10000},\quad c=\frac15,\quad
 \beta=\frac1{1000},\quad\zeta=\frac1{10000},\quad\delta=\frac1{10^6},\\
 C_1=\frac{49961}{10000},\quad
 \lambda=1-\frac{1671}{4\cdot10^{12}},\quad
 \lambda'=1-\frac{167}{4\cdot10^{11}},\quad
 \kappa=\frac{83}{10^{12}}.
\end{gather*}
The layer exponents are
\[
 \chi=\tau+(1-\beta)(\sigma-\tau),\qquad
 \chi_{\rm leaf}=\sigma+\beta(1-\sigma),\qquad
 \chi_{\rm reserve}=1-c=\frac45.
\]
Exact rational comparisons show
$\max\{\tau,\sigma,\chi\}<\lambda<\lambda'<1$ and
$\max\{\chi_{\rm leaf},\chi_{\rm reserve}\}<\lambda'$.
Also $\epsilon C_1=99872039/10^8<1$. This supplies the same completed
normalized-butterfly contract as the original layer theorem, with exact
evaluation before truncation and the same disk and error guarantees.

The retained Gaussian choice satisfies
$\alpha^4\theta_i>8b>p=6b$. It gives
\[
 \alpha=\Theta(p^{11999/40000}),\qquad
 \gamma=O(p^{15997/20000})=o(p).
\]
Since $\epsilon<1/5$, $b\ge2^{40}$ implies
$46b^{(1+3\epsilon)/2}\le b/4$: indeed $184<2^8$.
Prime selection still has $1-2\epsilon=3001/5000>0$.
The polynomial record length remains superpolynomial, while
$\epsilon(1+c)<1$ ensures $K=o(\ell)$ and the required address-size bounds.
The new reserved-axis count is $O(\log d+d^{1-c}\log p)=o(d)$.
For small $D$ the individual-kernel fallback was already charged.

The seven normalized assembly margins are unchanged in form:
\begin{align*}
 g_1&=1-\epsilon(1+c),&g_2&=\epsilon c(1-\tau),\\
 g_3&=\epsilon(1-\lambda'),&g_4&=(1-\tau)(1-\epsilon),\\
 g_5&=1/4-\delta-5\epsilon/4,&g_6&=1-\delta-\epsilon,\qquad g_7=\epsilon.
\end{align*}
All reservation, compact movement and repair costs have been included in the
layer bound, so require no extra assembly term. Exact arithmetic gives
\[
 G_*:=\min_i g_i=g_3=\frac{333833}{4\cdot10^{15}},\qquad
 G_*-\kappa=\frac{1833}{4\cdot10^{15}}>0.
\]
This strict gap absorbs all remaining fixed powers of $\log p$. Hence the
retained multiplication assembly yields
\[
 T(n)=O\bigl(n(\log n)^{1-83/10^{12}}\bigr),\qquad
 2^{-34}<\frac{83}{10^{12}}<2^{-33},
\]
conditional on the identified upstream and extension proofs. The simpler
dyadic statement with $\kappa=2^{-34}$ follows immediately.

\paragraph{Exceptional streams must be charged locally.}
The original manuscript separately bounded the global sorting work using
an exponentially small $2^{-K}$ bad fraction. The compact gadget instead
has an $O(p^{-3})$ fraction. We do not reuse that global $o(V)$ claim.
For each call, key sorting costs $O(V_{\rm call}p^{-2})$ and extraction and
reinsertion cost $O(V_{\rm call})$. Those costs were included in every
node of the volume-weighted recurrence. Summing that recurrence, including
all children and base-$m$ pieces, already proves the stated layer cost.
Polynomial setup per record is independently absorbed by the superpolynomial
record suffix, as in the retained proof. Thus there is no uncharged factor
equal to the number of recursion nodes.

\paragraph{Earlier targets and the next ceiling.}
The exact checker also verifies the previously hypothetical $2^{-39}$
parameters using the original $h=50$ complex saving, and the $2^{-34}$
parameters using $h=25$, now with the compact cost and reservation term.
For the fixed $h=25$ complex motif, its actual saving is at most an exact
enclosure of approximately $4.1847990372\cdot10^{-10}$. The retained
Gaussian margin forces $\epsilon<1/5$, while the leaf margin forces
$1-\lambda'<(1-\beta)(1-\sigma)<1-\sigma$. Consequently this motif and
these assembly inequalities have the strict ceiling
\[
 \kappa<\frac{a_{\rm c}^{\rm actual}}5
       <8.369598075\cdot10^{-11}<2^{-33}.
\]
The displayed witness exceeds 99\% of this upper enclosure. This is not an
optimality theorem for other complex networks, stronger leaf routines, or
different multiplication algorithms. A next dyadic improvement within this
strategy requires improving one of those ingredients.
