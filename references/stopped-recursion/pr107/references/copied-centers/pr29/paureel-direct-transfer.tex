\subsection{A direct partial-swap transfer}
\label{sec:direct-swap-transfer}
The preceding shear transfer is retained as a historical construction. The
new bit network instead uses direct partial-swap frames. For a nondegenerate
rational subspace $U\subset\mathbb Q^m$, let $p_U$ be its orthogonal
projector and define the address matrix
\[
 S_U=\begin{pmatrix}I-p_U&p_U\\p_U&I-p_U\end{pmatrix}
 \quad\text{on }(H,D).
\]
It is an involution. Orthogonal summands give commuting factors:
$S_US_V=S_{U\perp V}$ when $U\perp V$. In particular
$S_{U^\perp}S_U=S_{\mathcal F}$, the full interchange.
These rational address matrices induce permutations of bit arrays; the
pointwise scalar circuit still operates over $\mathbb F_2$.

\begin{lemma}[One interchange for a partial-swap line]
\label{lem:direct-swap-line}
For a fixed nonisotropic rational vector $v$, the address permutation
$S_{\langle v\rangle}$ uses one interchange of an earlier and a later
equal-width field and $O(V)$ additional work on the retained fixed tapes.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $d=v^{\mathsf T}Gv\ne0$ and
\[
 u=(v,-v)^{\mathsf T},\qquad
 \phi=(v^{\mathsf T}G,-v^{\mathsf T}G)/d.
\]
Then $S_{\langle v\rangle}=I-u\phi$ and $\phi u=2$.
Let $i$ be the first nonzero coordinate of $u$ and $j$ the last nonzero
coordinate of $\phi$. The first is in $H$ and the second in $D$, so $i<j$.
A lower triangular change first sends $u$ to $e_i$. Its diagonal entries
are nonzero; elimination below $i$ does not annihilate the last coefficient
of $\phi$. Now $\phi_i=2$, $\phi_j\ne0$, and no coefficients follow $j$.
Lower triangular row additions from coordinates before $i$ to coordinate
$i$ clear the coefficients of $\phi$ before $i$ while preserving $e_i$.
Row additions from coordinates $i<k<j$ to coordinate $j$ clear the remaining
coefficients between $i,j$, again preserving $e_i$. Thus a lower triangular
conjugation reduces the reflection to
\[
 (x,y)\longmapsto(-x-ty,y),\qquad t\ne0,
\]
on positions $i,j$, with every other coordinate fixed. It is implemented by
\[
 y\gets x+ty,\quad (x,y)\gets(y,x),\quad
 y\gets(x-y)/t,\quad x\gets-x.
\]
All nonswap updates use earlier controls for later targets, or a unit
scaling, and are covered by the ordered-affine streaming lemma.
Compile the finitely many triangular matrices, their inverses and pivots
into the machine. Choose one odd prime $q$ avoiding all their nonzero
numerators and denominators as in the retained rational shear proof.
The identities and invertibility hold over every $\mathbb Z/q^b\mathbb Z$.
No change of coordinate order is assumed free.
\end{proof}

Every nondegenerate rational residual has an orthogonal basis of
nonisotropic vectors: a nonzero symmetric form over a characteristic-zero
field has a nonzero quadratic value, and orthogonal splitting gives
induction. For nested labels, their partial-swap frames consequently differ
by exactly one line reflection per residual dimension. An edge of absolute
dimension change $r$ costs $r$ recursive interchanges.

\begin{proposition}[Direct power-width interchange]
\label{prop:two-stage-power-interchange}
For the certified two-stage bit constants, two disjoint width-$e=m^k$
radix-$q$ address chunks can be interchanged in $O(Ve^\tau)$ time on fixed
tapes, if a preceding row field has length divisible by $W^k$. All other
fields are arbitrary spectators and are preserved. The constants are
uniform in their lengths and values.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For the two-stage network, let $P=S_{U_a}$, $E=S_{U_a^\perp}$ and
$F=S_{\mathcal F}$. Its common-frame identity gives
\[
 A=Fy,\qquad B=Fx+Ey.
\]
Copy $A$ to a temporary tape, apply $P$ to the copy, and XOR it into $B$.
Because $PF=E$, the result is $(Fy,Fx)$. The one-dimensional $U_a$ costs
one child interchange. Retain the original $A$: transforming and then
recursively restoring it would cost a second child and remove the saving.
The copy is a duplicate of one role stream, of logical volume $V/W$,
with the same rows and address ranges. It is not an extra routed role or
an independent address coordinate. Copy, XOR and erasure cost $O(V/W)$.
Park the original stream with the other inactive parent streams while
processing the copy. The retained depth-first parking-stack proof applies
with this one additional fixed stream; push/pop never scans parked ancestors.
It charges $O(V)$ per parent node on the same fixed number of tapes.

After undoing the data-bank exchange at row merge, every role, including
arbitrary scratch, receives the full interchange. With $s$ counting edges
\emph{and} these corrections, the recurrence is
$F_k\le(s/W)F_{k-1}+O(1)$, exactly as in the retained power-width proof.
The base case is the retained single-digit operation. The geometric bound
and descriptor accounting are the ones proved above. Every recursive call
acts on $V/W$ with rows divisible by $W^{k-1}$; all original address ranges
remain complete, including the spectators outside its two subfields.
\end{proof}
The arbitrary-width padding, high-digit row construction and radix conversion
consume only this power-width contract and its exponent. They apply to this
replacement without alteration. We use the rational
$\tau=1-47/10^8$ from the two-stage certificate, rather than the preceding
historical shear exponent.
