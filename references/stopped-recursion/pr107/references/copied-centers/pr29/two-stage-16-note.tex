\begin{abstract}
We combine Paureel's two-stage partial-swap topology, including its paid
rank-one copy correction, with icekylinx's retained-total producers and
controlled inner basis. At factor dimensions $(55,53)$, one rational basis
supports both auxiliary small corners and all local projector blocks.
The resulting bit saving is $15537/10^9$. Keeping the PR21 complex circuit
and PR23 semantic/bulk transfer gives the conditional final saving
$\kappa=15536/10^9>2^{-16}$. The construction uses actual regenerated
finite producers, exact rational containment checks and an exact modular
nonvanishing witness for the common-basis argument. It retains the
predecessors' analytic and fixed-tape hypotheses; it is not a formal
verification or a practical multiplication implementation.
\end{abstract}

\[

\section{Two stages, endpoints and the copy correction}
Put $a=55$, $b=53$, $m=ab=2915$, $N=v_av_b$ and
$\mathcal F=F_a\otimes F_b$. Write $P,Q$ for the two triple-line
projectors and $U=\operatorname{im}(P\otimes Q)$. For any rational
projector $A$, the partial-swap address frame is
\[
 D_A=\begin{pmatrix}I-A&A\\A&I-A\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Nested nondegenerate labels give $D_AD_B=D_{B-A}$ when $A\le B$;
the same positive residual executes either orientation.

Use the first forward scalar shear, followed by the second inverse shear.
Their binary scalar map is $(x,y)\mapsto(y,x+y)$, with distinct restored
auxiliary banks. The data source frames are $(D_U,I)$ and terminal frames
$(D_{\mathcal F},D_{U^\perp})$. The tensor-stage boundary labels are
the first two stages of the retained construction: stage one grows
$X:U\to F_a\otimes\operatorname{im}Q$ and
$Y:0\to\operatorname{im}(I-P)\otimes\operatorname{im}Q$;
stage two ends at $(\mathcal F,U^\perp)$.

Stage-one auxiliary source and sink are $0,\mathcal F$. Its final
outer residual is $I_a\otimes(I_b-Q)$, rank $m-a$.
In stage two use auxiliary source
$D_0=(I_a-P)\otimes I_b$ and complementary sink $I-D_0$.
Raise the physical data source to $D_0$ before its initial inverse copy,
as in PR21/24. Every subsequent local label contains $D_0$, so the
auxiliary entrance vanishes. Cleanup ends at $I$, giving exit $D_{D_0}$,
rank $m-b$. Arbitrary-scratch restoration makes the total auxiliary
endpoint $D_{I-D_0}D_{D_0}=D_I$; no physical preparation or free
reordering is assumed. The two stage banks are not shared.

Let $F=D_I$, $E=D_{I-P\otimes Q}$ and $T=D_{P\otimes Q}$. The framed
data outputs are
\[
 A=Fy,\qquad B=Fx+Ey.
\]
Retain $A$, copy it to a temporary stream, apply $T$ to that copy, and
XOR into $B$. Since $TF=E$, the result is $(Fy,Fx)$. This is exactly
Paureel's correction, charged as one rank-one child per pair. Its volume
is the existing role volume $V/W$. Copy, XOR and erasure cost $O(V/W)$;
the original is parked without recursively restoring it. Processing these
copies sequentially adds a fixed stream to the retained parking stack,
not a new row-index family. After undoing the data row-bank exchange,
every role, including arbitrary scratch, receives full interchange.

\section{One common basis for both axes}
Use the PR24 inner family with physical index $i=b\alpha+\beta$,
\[
 K=T_2(I_a\otimes L_b),\qquad
 T_2(x\otimes e_\beta)=M_\beta L_a x\otimes e_\beta.
\]
Prescribe first-$2a$ row coefficient labels
$R=[0,\ldots,a-1,1,\ldots,a-1,0]$ and last-$2a$ inverse-column labels
$C=[a-1,0,\ldots,a-2,0,\ldots,a-1]$.
Their effective residues are $\{0,1,3\}$ and
$\{b-5,b-3,b-2\}$ modulo $b$. They are disjoint at $b=53$;
the finite certificate constructs all 53 permutation completions.
Fix these $M_\beta$, leaving $L_a,L_b$ free.

Absorb $L_a,L_b$ into local matrices and denote the transformed line
projectors by $p\xi,v\nu$, with $\xi p=\nu v=1$. If boundary row $i$
and column $j$ have labels $r_i,c_j$ and second-factor indices
$\beta_i,\delta_j$, the two local corner formulas are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 (A\otimes v\nu)_{ij}&=v_{\beta_i}\nu_{\delta_j}A_{r_i,c_j},\\
 (p\xi\otimes B)_{ij}&=p_{r_i}\xi_{c_j}B_{\beta_i,\delta_j}.
\end{aligned}
\]
These are direct contractions of the prescribed rows and inverse columns.
On first/last $a$ coordinates, $r_i=c_i=i$. Thus the corner of
$I_a\otimes v\nu$ is diagonal and invertible, and any local $A$ is
represented up to nonzero diagonal row and column scalings. On first/last
$b$ coordinates, $\beta_i=\delta_i=i$, $r_i=i$, $c_i=i+2$.
Thus $p\xi\otimes I_b$ also has an invertible diagonal corner, and every
local $B$ is represented up to such scalings. Consequently the two
auxiliary profiles are $(a,m-2a)$ and $(b,m-2b)$ simultaneously.

For a local rank-$r$ projector in dimension $h$, the retained generic
projector identity gives $h-r$ singleton pivots and a block $2r-h$ if
$2r>h$; otherwise retain $r$ singletons. At $r=h$ use a whole $h$-block.
The first/last local corner formulas just proved transfer all these
profiles to the ambient physical order. Each needed ordinary minor is
nonzero as a rational function of the corresponding free $L_h$ by
similarity of rank-$r$ projectors and the usual rank-one pairing witness.

The remaining data macro is $(I-P)\otimes(I-Q)$, with nullity
$d=a+b-1=107$. Its null-corner has entries
\[
 Z_{ij}=[\beta_i=\delta_j]p_{r_i}\xi_{c_j}
       +[r_i=c_j]v_{\beta_i}\nu_{\delta_j}
       -p_{r_i}\xi_{c_j}v_{\beta_i}\nu_{\delta_j}.
\]
The exact certificate supplies normalized line coordinates modulo the
prime 1,000,003 and proves this $107$-square matrix has full rank.
This is a nonvanishing witness, not a numerical inference: lift the
coordinates to integers and normalize the two dual vectors over
$\mathbb Q$. The normalization denominators are $1$ modulo this prime,
so the lifted rational determinant is nonzero. Any fixed pair of rank-one
projectors can be conjugated over $\mathbb Q$ to these lifted projectors.
Hence this determinant is a nonzero rational function on the free
$\mathrm{GL}_a\times\mathrm{GL}_b$ family for each physical data pair.
The large-projector identity then gives $107$ singleton pivots and a
block $m-214=2701$.

There are finitely many physical local projectors and data pairs.
The ordinary minors, line coordinates and displayed data determinants
are individually nonzero rational functions on this same irreducible
parameter space. Clearing denominators, their finite product is nonzero.
Rational enumeration therefore constructs one common basis satisfying all
requirements. Choose the address prime after this choice, avoiding its
finitely many bad denominators and minors. The witness prime above is
only a certificate device and need not be this address prime.

The retained lower-triangular conjugation for $D_A$ turns every contiguous
identity-pivot block into one child interchange. Nonzero diagonal scales
are absorbed in the triangular factors. Thus there is no uncharged
gather, reversal or independent per-edge choice of basis.
