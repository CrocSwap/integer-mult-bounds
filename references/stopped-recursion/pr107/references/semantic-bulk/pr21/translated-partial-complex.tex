\subsection{An operator identity for arbitrary dirty auxiliary inputs}
\label{sec:translated-complex-source}
Let $F=\mathbb F_2^{28}$ with its standard dot product. For a nondegenerate
binary space $U$, let $P_U$ be its orthogonal projector and $C_U$ its
retained phase operator. In common Hadamard coordinates, $C_U$ is diagonal
with entries $i^{\operatorname{wt}(P_Ux)}$. These operators commute and
are invertible over the Gaussian dyadic ring. If $U\perp V$, then
$C_{U\mathbin\perp V}=C_UC_V$: the correction in
$\operatorname{wt}(u+v)=\operatorname{wt}(u)+\operatorname{wt}(v)
-2|\operatorname{supp}(u)\cap\operatorname{supp}(v)|$ is divisible by
four when $u\cdot v=0$ over $\mathbb F_2$.

For a stage-two invocation let $P,Q$ be its norm-one triple lines. Put
\[
 D_0=P^\perp\otimes F\otimes Q,\qquad D_1=F\otimes F\otimes Q,
 \qquad E=P\otimes F\otimes Q.
\]
Then $D_1=D_0\mathbin\perp E$, with dimensions $756,784,28$ respectively.
Each stage-two auxiliary role, including all 29 centers, has first common
gate frame $C_{D_0}$ and last common gate frame $C_{D_1}$.
Give it source operator $C_{D_0}$ and sink operator $C_I C_{D_0}$,
leaving every common gate unchanged. For arbitrary physical input $f$,
interpret the logical input as $g=C_{D_0}^{-1}f$. The scalar identity
restores every auxiliary $g$, so its physical output remains
$C_I C_{D_0}g=C_If$. This is a proof invariant, not executed source
preprocessing: formal denominators of $g$ are never stored. Scalar
cancellation holds for arbitrary arrays over the Gaussian dyadic ring.
Data endpoints, signed scalar exchange, and their corrections are unchanged.

The first edge is identity; the final edge is exactly
\begin{equation}
 (C_I C_{D_0})C_{D_1}^{-1}=C_I C_E^{-1}=C_{E^\perp}.
 \label{eq:translated-complex-exit}
\end{equation}
This is multiplicative operator cancellation. Replacing $C_I C_{D_0}$
by a binary projector expression would incorrectly discard repeated phases.
The common-frame invariant and arbitrary-input scratch restoration permit
this independent endpoint choice on each auxiliary role.

\subsection{Residual basis and a phase certificate on all addresses}
\label{sec:translated-complex-phases}
Coordinate permutations in the first and third factors reduce every
actual pair $P,Q$ to $t=e_0+e_1+e_2$. They preserve inner products and
integer Hamming weights. An explicit orthonormal basis of $t^\perp$ is
\[
 e_3+e_0+e_1,\ e_3+e_1+e_2,\ e_3+e_0+e_2,\ e_4,\ldots,e_{27}.
\]
An orthonormal basis of $E^\perp$ is the disjoint union
\[
 (\mathcal B_{P^\perp}\otimes\mathcal E_F\otimes\mathcal E_F)
 \ \cup\ (t_P\otimes\mathcal E_F\otimes\mathcal B_{Q^\perp}).
\]
The two orthogonal parts have dimensions 21168 and 756. Their total
$21924=m-28$ is proper and nonalternating. Of the basis vectors, 18900
have weight $1\bmod4$ and 3024 have weight $3\bmod4$. The whole-residual
identity uses one width-$21924f$ child, two parity-sign passes, and
constant phase $(-i)^{3024f}=1$. The sign passes remain necessary.
Inverse orientations use the inverse signed wrappers; all other residual
bases and both orientations retain their previous complete checks.

The control additionally proves the phase equality on every binary address.
Set
\[
 \delta(x)=\operatorname{wt}(x)+\operatorname{wt}(P_{D_0}x)
 -\operatorname{wt}(P_{D_1}x)-\operatorname{wt}(P_{E^\perp}x)\pmod4.
\]
Its polarization is
$2x^t(I+P_{D_0}+P_{D_1}+P_{E^\perp})y=0\pmod4$, by the symmetric
idempotent projector identities. Hence $\delta$ is additive.
On the 21952 coordinate vectors the weight triples are $(0,0,1)$,
$(3,3,1)$, $(6,3,8)$, with counts 19600, 2100, 252. The script checks
every coordinate and obtains zero in each case. Additivity proves
$\delta=0$ on all $2^{21952}$ addresses. Tensor Gram identities and the
coordinate-permutation reduction cover every actual invocation.

\subsection{A precision guard across endpoints and stage boundaries}
\label{sec:translated-complex-guard}
Keep the original common-gate dimension potential. Give a changed source
potential $756$ and its sink the formal potential $m+756=22708$.
The latter is bookkeeping, not a binary subspace: its difference from the
last actual gate dimension $784$ is precisely the new rank $21924$.
Every physical edge rank is the absolute change in potential; a scalar
gate permits a path to change role without changing its common potential.
The only decreasing edges are the retained central returns, losing $h$
each. Invocations in one stage are disjoint, so a path meets at most one
return per stage. Telescoping across the entire physical path gives
\[
 \sum_i r_i\le q'=m+(h^2-h)+6h=22876.
\]
This includes paths entering stage-two auxiliaries from data. The old
$m+6h$ cannot be reused, because such a path may reach the enlarged exit
without traversing the removed auxiliary entrance.

The finite audit conservatively connects every incident input at each
gate to every incident output. At each tensor-stage data boundary it uses
the maximum over the entire incoming data bank, covering all coordinate
permutations. For the shared bank it preserves the exact per-role depths
from stage one into stage three: the local programs and zero initial
depth are identical, and the bank matching preserves local role indices.
It bounds the maximum rank sum by 22820 and the maximum squared-rank sum
by 481197866. These are conservative envelopes, not claimed realizable
maximizing paths.

Every recursive child stays within its parent role. Frame basis changes,
row splitting, and parking permute addresses without creating cross-role
arithmetic dependencies. Therefore physical coefficient paths project to
scalar paths with $\sum r_i\le q'$ and $r_i\le M=m-h=21924$.
Since $M<q'<2M$, convexity gives the simpler universal guard
\[
 \sum_i(r_i/m)^2\le\frac{M^2+(q'-M)^2}{m^2}
 =\frac{614245}{614656}<\theta:=\frac{9999}{10000}<1.
\]
Child-width floors only improve this estimate. Set
\[
 \rho=2,\qquad E_0=64(W+m+1)^3,\qquad
 C_{\rm dep}=10000(E_0+16m+1).
\]
The exact certificate checks
\[
 36W^3+4s+4W+8m+4<E_0,\quad
 (1-\theta)C_{\rm dep}\ge E_0,\quad C_{\rm dep}\ge16m.
\]
There are at most $s$ nonzero edges, so $4s$ pays for every mixed-sign
wrapper. Fewer than $m$ remainder axes are processed individually and
charged to local overhead, including their at most $8m$ depth contribution.

For large $d$, stop below $d^\beta\ge2m$. A child leaf of an internal
node has width at least $d^\beta/(2m)$, so $8e\le16m d^{-\beta}e^2$.
Strong induction using the strict path moment proves
$A(e)\le C_{\rm dep}d^{-\beta}e^2$. The retained concatenation and
endpoint allowances then give
\[
 \beta=1/1000,\quad\zeta=1/10000,\qquad
 C_1=2-\beta+\zeta=19991/10000,
\]
\[
 C_0=\left\lceil128m(1+1/\zeta)C_{\rm dep}\right\rceil,
 \qquad\Delta=\lceil C_0d^{C_1}\rceil.
\]
The completed layer uses $p+\Delta$ fractional bits and $\Delta+3$
integer/sign bits. The final assembly must have $\epsilon C_1<1$.
Logical source reinterpretation adds no physical operation or rounding term.
