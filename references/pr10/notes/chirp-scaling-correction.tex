\label{sec:chirp-scaling-correction}

The retained fast Gaussian construction generates
$2^{-B}e^{\pi\alpha^2\beta_j^2}$ at precision $P$, where
$B=\lceil1.14\alpha^2\rceil$, then shifts by $B$ and multiplies by
the disk-grid input $u_j$. The exponential interface gives absolute
error at most $2^{1-P}$ before the shift. Thus the error after shifting
is controlled by $2^{B+1-P}$, not by a constant multiple of
$2^{-P}e^{\pi\alpha^2/4}$ uniformly in $\alpha$.

Set $F=2^B$ in the retained chirped-correlation block lemma. Since
$|\beta_j|\le1/2$ and $\pi/(4\log 2)<1.14$, this bounds the exact
input magnitude. Including the product and rounding, it also gives
the required input accuracy $|\widetilde f_j-f_j|\le2^{4-P}F$.
The distinction matters: $2^B/e^{\pi\alpha^2/4}$ is unbounded, so
the smaller exact-magnitude bound alone does not justify this accuracy.

No additional asymptotic precision is needed. For the inherited integer
parameters $p>100$ and $2\le\alpha<\sqrt p$, let
$L_A=3\lceil\sqrt p/(2\alpha)\rceil+1$ and $\Gamma=29p$.
Then $L_A\le p$, $\lceil\log_2 L_A\rceil\le p-1$ and
$B\le1.14p+1$. The block lemma requires
\[
 P\ge p+\Gamma+\lceil\log_2(FL_A)\rceil+11.
\]
Its right side is at most $32.14p+11<34p$, so the retained
$P=34p$ satisfies the corrected error budget. The packing widths
remain $O(p)$, and the target errors, tape-time exponents and final
rounding guarantee are unchanged. The combined manuscript patch
includes this correction directly in the retained Gaussian proof.
