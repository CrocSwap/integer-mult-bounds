Physical cost and why the change is legitimate
1. review-balanced-transform.md:3-17,20-36,50-108 constructs the balanced
   common named low-bit groups, of widths K_j in [K,2K), with the same K_j
   on every participating axis. Only one extra top position per long axis is
   processed individually. Its O(T p d) cost has saving 1-epsilon. There is
   no low-bit prefix of length K on every axis. Whole source, control,
   spectator and row fields remain complete; named FFT level/twiddle order
   and forward/inverse alignment are retained. Geometric feasibility
   epsilon(1+c)<1 remains necessary, although that small positive gap is
   no longer required to exceed kappa.
2. That report ALONE is insufficient: lines38-48 and145-151 pay old
   sequential group transpositions, leaving positional margin epsilon*a*c.
   The claimed improved construction must actually replace those moves.
3. review-arbitrary-routing.md:46-78 supplies the replacement on a known
   complete coordinate permutation: paid original interchanges place and
   restore reservoirs, matching swaps use three directional shears, and the
   two-involution decomposition completes the map. Supplied-map preparation
   is an explicit additive polynomial term. Short records borrow and restore
   an untouched complete spectator using original paid placements; for the
   small-n fallback epsilon>a suffices. Applying this once to the full
   axis-major/group-major positional permutation and once to its inverse
   costs O(T p p^(1-a) polylog(p)) plus absorbed polynomial setup, rather
   than charging it separately for every group. This gives margin a.
4. review-bulk-resampling.md:158-199 separately pays a constant number of
   global gathers/inverses and local passes. Its displayed total is
   O(Tp[p^(1-a)polylog(p)+d polylog(p)+d p^delta+w^2 p^delta
        +d w^2 p^delta/L])+polynomial setup.
   Consequently exposure margin a and Gaussian margins 1-epsilon-delta,
   r-delta remain. This relies on the full multi-axis tensor for catalogue
   absorption; it is not an isolated short-record theorem.
5. downstream-semantic-bulk-assembly.md:30-59 explicitly COMBINES these
   constructions with linear semantic C1=1 and states the seven margins
   [1-epsilon,a,epsilon*q,a,min(1-epsilon-delta,r-delta),
    1-epsilon-delta,epsilon]. Lines63-76 state the exact balanced family
   q=a(1-2h), c=q+h/4, epsilon=(1-h)/(1+q),
   G=epsilon*q, r=(G+1-epsilon)/2, delta=h/8.

Parameter and interface audit
Algebra gives 1-epsilon-G=h, 1-epsilon-r=h/2,
1-epsilon(1+c)=h-epsilon*h/4>3h/4, and c-q=h/4>0.
Thus K=o(ell), normalization epsilon+r<1, and reservation still hold even
though the original-prefix saving is far below kappa. Since epsilon*c>0,
K is eventually superlogarithmic; the tiny constants change the threshold,
not the asymptotic implication. The extra top phase contributes only d
additional guard bits and O(p) precision because d=O(p).

The downstream source's worked beta=1/2 example assumes a<b/2; it must NOT
be imported literally for PR34. The general stopped-leaf formula in
notes/semantic-bulk-17-note.tex:144-164,206-215 permits each fixed beta>0.
Here beta=1/20 gives (1-beta)b=17.1e-6>a, hence also >q, while sigma<tau.
C1=1 depends on the completed Gaussian maps, not the bit adapter exponent.
PR34 ASSEMBLY_PROOF.md:70-90 retains those maps and exact-recovery interface;
its lines92-109 pay the product row stock and existing rank-one correction.
Changing the positional layout does not delete the N paid corrections or
license incomplete row/control/spectator fields.
