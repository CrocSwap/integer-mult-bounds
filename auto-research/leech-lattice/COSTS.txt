Scope and accounting for the Leech/Turyn screens
================================================

These are exact comparisons of explicitly stated relaxed cost models.
They do not construct a local scalar supplier or certify a new kappa.
The source frontier is PR588, exact head
6d0e53b66c29abd17981881a09eaff41ac862d85, conditional kappa
0.001602278921983212. Its title/body and actual SPEC.json were inspected;
its verifier was not replayed here. sources/PR588.json is the preserved
host response and FRONTIER-SOURCES.json records its hash and source head.

Center support is an encoder condition
-------------------------------------
The native emitter in direct-delivery504-global/code/emit_local.py copies
a retained center at its actual frame cf, descends that copy to frame
zero, scatters into targets at frame zero, and clears the copy. The
independent local auditor checks those literal common-frame incidences.
Therefore cf must contain the encoder support; it need NOT contain the
union of encoder and decoder supports. Dense decoding is allowed at zero.
Nonzero target entrance gauges add nested-level requirements, which are
absent from this default schedule. The reverse word is the inverse
transpose, not a second same-direction encoder.

Thus 2600 encoding rows supported in rank-24 frames would give a center
bill C=62400 despite dense decoding. Full-rank-25 centers instead give
C=65000. These remain scenarios until a dyadic factorization with the
claimed supports is constructed. A low-rank rational factorization alone
does not establish the necessary dyadic coefficients or frame supports.

The inherited paired controller
-------------------------------
With K pairs, L=2K+1 stages, h=25 and v=98280 ports, set
  m=24*L+1, W=2*K*v, D=2*K*v-L*C.
Replica count scales every mass equally, so the screen sets it to one.
The complete paid rank mass must be m*W-D. Additional banks add m per
bank to both stock mass and paid mass; they do not create deficit.
For paid child histogram H, the normalized moment is
  F(a)=sum_r r*H[r]*(m/r)^a/(m*W).

The numerical root is converted only nominally by kappa=a/(1+a). Actual
outer assembly includes additional margins. Failing this nominal target
is sufficient for rejection in the screened model; passing it is not
an outer certificate.

The first baseline scenario omits helpers, maximally coalesces every
local data path into rank24, retains the supplied individual center
children, and retains the inherited idle paths. Long idle paths are
split into the cheapest pieces satisfying the two-level cap
cap=floor(m/sqrt(2)). This is hypothetical, not an executable word.

The stronger relaxation keeps only local data-path obligations, whose
mass is A=2*v*L*24. All other paid mass B=m*W-D-A can be arbitrarily
concentrated into fractional children at cap. Its moment numerator is
  A*(m/24)^a + B*(m/cap)^a.
For r<=cap, (m/r)^a >= (m/cap)^a, proving this relaxation optimistic
within the stated obligations. Splitting a mandatory path increases
its moment, since r^(1-a) is concave. An additional bank contributes
the positive excess m*((m/cap)^a-1), so no banks is also optimistic.

For K=1..50 and C65000, every stronger-relaxation comparison fails the
nominal frontier exactly. C62400 retains ideal headroom at K=2,3,4.
None of this is a universal Leech bound: different controller, endpoint,
factorization, center cost or halving-depth contract can escape it.

Turyn block-sign Fourier interfaces
----------------------------------
The block norm census gives 360 one-block ports, 5760 two-block ports,
and 92160 three-block ports. After identifying global sign, their
independent block-sign fibers have sizes1,2,4. For a fiber with k active
blocks, its distinct odd binary port labels span a k-dimensional U.
Their Gram entries are all1, so U has restricted Gram rank1 and radical
dimension k-1. It is not a nondegenerate rank-k replacement port.

For the four-point case, the script uses representative labels
1,7,25,31 in F2^25. Their common frame U has rank3. The exact embedded
Clifford-frame distance is d(A,B)=dim A+dim B-2dim(A intersection B).

Mutating the original sources by a complete Walsh transform at U can
stay on the monotone path
  line -> U -> full, ranks (k-1)+(25-k)=24.
The corresponding target inverse mixing can use
  zero -> U-perp -> original hyperplane, ranks (25-k)+(k-1)=24.
This adds no rank MASS. However it splits the child payments, increasing
the moment. The resulting intermediate values remain at U and U-perp;
calling them independent rank-one ports would discard a real obligation.

The two-sided mandatory data profile per local call is
  rank1:11520, rank2:184320, rank22:184320,
  rank23:11520, rank24:720.
Keeping C62400 and relaxing all other mass to cap still fails the
nominal frontier for every K=1..50. Its best floating nominal estimate
is about0.00157136279432 at K=3 (seven stages). C65000 gives about
0.00138471362621. A one-sided source transform retains ideal headroom:
the C62400 scenario peaks near0.00164975807555 before real helper costs.
The script preserves both scenarios rather than excluding one-sided work.

If a transformed source is forced back to a rank-one line Q inside U,
its path line -> U -> Q -> full costs 24+2*(k-1). Applying the analogous
target detour costs the same excess. Across all fibers, both detours add
760320 rank per local call; this is not a free change of port labels.

Cloning avoids mutation but does not avoid payment. Keeping each original
source path unchanged and gathering one copy per source into U adds
  5760*1 + 92160*2 = 190080
rank per local call, already exceeding v. Even with C=0, the inherited
deficit (L-1)*v-L*190080 is negative for every positive L. This excludes
that specific all-fiber source-copy strategy, not selective copying.

Common-frame legality is a separate obligation
---------------------------------------------
Every Walsh output row has all fiber sources in its clean support, so
it must be represented at U in a monotone common-frame factory. A read
from this row into an original target's hyperplane is legal only if
every source label in the fiber is perpendicular to that target.
Invertible Fourier mixing does not annihilate a nonzero side-response
row supported on a proper subset of the fiber. Partial packet production
before the full mix can behave differently and is outside these bounds.

Turyn organizes the E8 pieces and their glue; it does not itself supply
that missing scalar word. Likewise, splitting the unchanged cubic kernel
over R8+R8+R8 retains the feature channels
  3*dim Sym^3(R8) + 6*dim Sym^2(R8)*8 + 8^3
  = 360+1728+512 = 2600.
This dimensional decomposition is not a lower bound for every possible
new fitting kernel. A new block-dependent kernel or a cheaper compatible
factory remains a distinct research direction.

Later rank-nine profile candidate
---------------------------------
A separate Turyn tensor-profile candidate has N7680,h9 and512 real
features. Its literal coordinate-center support ranks are9, so its center
bill is4608, not4096. The analogous full E8 total T_i contains both its
coordinate port and its56 code ports: the latter span a rank8 hyperplane,
while the coordinate port lies outside it. This is why full E8 costs
8*9=72 rather than64. It is a support calculation, not an automatic
extra toll for repeated labels or for diagonal entries.

At corrected costs, N7680,C4608 equals64 times full E8 N120,C72. A
punctured N7168,C4096 candidate similarly equals64 times N112,C64.
For the corresponding repeated words, every paid histogram coefficient,
stock denominator and deficit scale by64. Their normalized moments and
resulting exponents are therefore exactly unchanged. Large absolute
port count or deficit alone does not establish a gain.

The three fixed rank9 profile embeddings also admit no nonzero binary
cross-profile Gram block: in each pair,81 independent active-pair
constraints exhaust all81 entries of the possible9-by9 block. This is
a separate finite geometry certificate produced in the research run;
screen.py does not verify that certificate. Its scope fixes each chosen
rank9 embedding and keeps each profile's span rank9. It does not exclude
new profile labels with extra radical components invisible to their own
Gram, different labelings, or a different fitting kernel. The overall geometry evidence should be
consulted before treating this observation as a package-level result.

Primary references used for the Turyn interpretation:
G. Nebe, A generalisation of Turyn's construction of self-dual codes,
section3: https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Gabriele.Nebe/papers/Turyn.pdf
V. Corlay et al., A Turyn-based neural Leech decoder, section2:
https://www.lebesgue.fr/sites/default/files/proceedings_WCC/WCC_2019_paper_60.pdf
These establish the polarized-E8 gluing interpretation. The cost and
frame deductions above are our own analysis of the repository interface.

Reproduction
------------
python3 -I code/screen.py --verify
The script uses only the standard library. Every comparison at the
frontier uses exact Fraction intervals:40 terms of the atanh log series
and20 exponential Taylor terms, each with an explicit tail bound. Stored
integer intervals enclose F(a)-1 at scale10^18. Floating root estimates
are rounded to12 significant digits and are not the rejection evidence.
No elapsed timing or machine-specific paths occur in SCREEN.json.
