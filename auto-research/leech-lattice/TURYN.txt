Turyn product-kernel nomination (2026-10-12)
==========================================

Status: a concrete algebraic supplier candidate, NOT a kappa certificate.
A profile-dependent construction below gives an explicit binary fitting
representation in dimension 27. No helper schedule or native word is supplied
by this note. A numerical exponent still requires full cost accounting.

Setup
-----
Use L=E8/sqrt(2), so minimum squared norm(L)=1. Choose a polarization
L=M+N, M intersect N=2L, with M,N copies of the norm-2 E8 lattice.
Write bars for cosets modulo 2L. Both Mbar,Nbar have dimension 4 over F2.
The Leech lattice is

  Lambda = {(m+n1,m+n2,m+n3): m in M, ni in N,
                                      n1+n2+n3 in 2L}.

On L/2L the quadratic form is q(x)=||x||^2 mod 2 and its polar form is
beta(x,y)=2<x,y> mod 2. Mbar and Nbar are complementary totally singular
4-spaces, and beta pairs them nondegenerately.

The minimum vectors have squared-norm profiles (4,0,0), (2,2,0), (2,1,1),
including permutations. Standard finite E8 coset facts used in the argument:

* Each nonsingular coset of L/2L contains one norm-1 line, i.e. two roots.
* Each nonzero singular coset contains eight norm-2 lines, i.e. sixteen
  vectors, mutually orthogonal except for antipodal pairs.

The second orthogonality assertion follows directly from minimum norm(2L)=4:
if u,v have squared norm 2 in one coset, then both u-v and u+v lie in 2L;
unless v=+/-u this forces <u,v>=0. The finite counts follow by enumerating
the 240 norm-1 and 2160 norm-2 vectors of L.

Counts
------
The three norm profiles have respectively 720,11520,184320 vectors.
For (2,1,1), mbar cannot be zero. For each of the 15 nonzero mbar,
there are eight choices of nbar for a norm-1 component, since beta(m,n)=1.
Choose the two norm-1 component cosets independently (8^2 choices).
The norm-2 component coset is then fixed, nonzero and singular. It contains
eight lines. Thus each fixed position of the norm-2 component gives

  15 * 8^2 * 8 = 7680 triples of local lines.

There are 23040 triples across the three positions. Select one sign for
each of the three local lines, by a fixed deterministic convention. This
is legitimate: changing one block sign changes it by an element of 2L
and hence preserves the Turyn gluing constraint.

This retains one of the four global line representatives from each block-line
triple. The candidate therefore has 23040 ports, NOT 92160 ports.

Product kernel
--------------
For canonical triples v=(v1,v2,v3), w=(w1,w2,w3), set

  K(v,w) = <v1,w1> <v2,w2> <v3,w3>.

This is an ordinary real Gram kernel of the tensor feature
v1 tensor v2 tensor v3, so its real rank is at most 8^3=512.
In fact its rank is exactly 512. Fix any nonzero mbar and any one
position for the norm-2 block. There are 8^2*8=512 canonical triples
with these choices. The parity-000 argument below shows that their
tensor feature vectors are mutually orthogonal, each with squared
norm 2. Thus they already form a basis of the 512-dimensional space.
Its diagonal is 2. The normalized diagonal-one kernel is K/2.

Claim: for distinct canonical triples, either K=0 or 4K is an odd integer.
Consequently

  B(v,w) = 1 + (4K(v,w) mod 2)

has diagonal 1 and B(v,w)=0 at every nonzero off-diagonal of K. This is
exactly the graph-compatibility requirement for a binary fitting matrix.
The exact finite computation gives rank_F2(B)=505. This is the rank of
this completion; the later profile construction gives a smaller fit.

Proof of the claim
------------------
Write ti=<vi,wi> and si=2ti, so si are integers. Since Lambda is integral,
s1+s2+s3 is even. Thus their parities have weight zero or two.
Assume K is nonzero.

First suppose the two triples have the same norm profile. In parity
pattern 000, both low-low inner products are nonzero integers between
norm-1 vectors. They therefore equal +/-1, and both pairs of local lines
are identical. In particular the common mbar labels coincide and the
two low cosets coincide. The high cosets then coincide as well. Within
that norm-2 coset the eight lines are orthogonal, so nonzero high inner
product forces the same high line. The triples would be identical.
Thus pattern 000 is impossible for distinct canonical triples.

Next suppose the triples have different norm profiles. There is one
low-low block. In pattern 000 its nonzero integer inner product again
forces the same local line and hence the same common mbar. For the two
high-low blocks, equality of mbar and opposite q-parities imply
beta(vi,wi)=1, contradicting pattern 000.

We are left with parity pattern 110, in some order. The even block has
a nonzero integer inner product. For a low-low or high-low pair it must
be +/-1, by Cauchy-Schwarz and the norms. For a high-high pair it could
also be +/-2, but that would mean identical local lines and hence the
same mbar. With equal mbar and equal norm profiles, all three beta
pairings would be even, a contradiction. Thus the even block always
has ti=+/-1. The other two ti are odd half-integers. Their product times
the even-block ti is an odd integer divided by four, proving the claim.

There is a further restriction: for equal profiles the even block must be
the high-high block. An even low-low block with nonzero product would
force equal local root lines, and therefore equal mbar. With equal mbar
and equal norm profiles, all three pairings are even, a contradiction.
Thus the odd blocks are both low-low, and both have inner product +/-1/2.
For different profiles every odd block is low-low or high-low and likewise
has inner product +/-1/2. Consequently the exact off-diagonal values are
K in {0,+/-1/4}; the superficially possible +/-3/4 values do not occur.

Profile-dependent binary representation in dimension 27
-------------------------------------------------------
Fix a position k for the norm-2 block and choose one of the other positions
j(k), which always has norm 1. Map the chosen low block v_j into
E8= sqrt(2)*L. It is an E8 root of squared norm 2. Modulo 2E8 the E8
bilinear form is a nondegenerate alternating form beta in dimension 8.
Appending a constant coordinate yields the nonalternating 9-dimensional
form 1+beta, equivalent to the standard dot product over F2.

Thus there are 9-bit odd vectors p_k(v) satisfying

  p_k(v) dot p_k(w) = 1 + 2<v_j,w_j> mod 2.

For equal profiles and a nonzero off-diagonal K, both low-block pairings
are odd half-integers by the preceding proof. Hence the dot product is
zero, as required. For distinct profiles, put these 9-bit vectors in
orthogonal summands of F2^27. Their dot product is then automatically
zero. This produces a legal h=27 fitting representation for all 23040
ports. Each individual fixed profile has h=9, N=7680 and real rank512.

This 27-dimensional representation is stronger than the generic
B=J+(4K mod2) nomination. It does not prove that 27 is optimal.

The h=9 fixed-profile supplier is 64 copies of E8 after a free basis change
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Fix one profile and its selected low-block position j. There are 120
possible selected low root lines u. The 64 ports sharing u have features

  u tensor z,  z=v_high tensor v_otherlow.

Their 64 vectors z form an orthogonal basis of R^64, each of squared
norm2. To prove this, the common glue mbar is fixed by u. The other low
block has eight possible nonsingular cosets; different such cosets give
orthogonal roots (the bilinear pairing is even, and distinct norm1 lines
can have a nonzero even pairing only if they are the same line). For a
fixed other-low coset, the high block has eight mutually orthogonal lines.
Thus there are8*8=64 pairwise orthogonal z vectors of norm squared2.

Let Z_u be the64x64 matrix with those z as rows, in fixed real tensor
coordinates, so Z_u Z_u^T=2I. Fix any reference matrix Z_0 and define

  A_u=Z_0 Z_u^T/2.

Then A_u Z_u=Z_0, and A_u is orthogonal. Applying the block diagonal
matrix with blocks A_u to the port rows takes the scalar kernel to

  K_new=2 Gram(u) tensor I_64,
  (K/2)_new=Gram(u) tensor I_64.

The u are norm1 E8 roots in L, so the normalized kernel is exactly64
copies of the normalized120-port E8 Gram kernel. The matrices A_u have
dyadic entries: the original E8 block inner products are half-integral,
their pairwise products are quarter-integral, and the final division is2.

Under the existing model, these are free in-frame scalar changes because
all64 ports in the group have the same binary label p_k(u). The inverse
uses A_u^T and has the same property. This establishes an actual
transform/operate/inverse-transform identity. It does not establish a
new exponent: the fixed-profile construction is geometrically equivalent
to64 copies of the existing full E8 supplier.

Combining profiles still shares the real512-dimensional tensor space,
and the inter-profile scalar blocks remain relevant. The equivalence
above does not prove that every multi-profile construction reduces to
independent E8 copies, and it does not bound other binary fitting maps.

A second glue-only fitting matrix is

  B2(v,w)=(1+alpha1)(1+alpha2), alpha_i=2<vi,wi> mod2.

The relation alpha1+alpha2+alpha3=0 and the parity-000 argument prove
legality. The finite experiment gives rank_F2(B2)=53; that is the
rank of this matrix, not a lower bound for other fitting matrices.

Bounded compression experiment for the 27-dimensional representation
-------------------------------------------------------------------
For each pair of distinct profiles, solve the linear homogeneous system

  p_k(v)^T G_kl p_l(w)=0

for the 81 entries of G_kl, using only pairs with nonzero K. Diagonal
9x9 blocks stay fixed at the standard dot form. The exact computation finds81 independent edge outer products for each
profile pair, so every cross block is zero. This only excludes linear
overlaps of the fixed rank9 label maps, with each profile span remaining
nine-dimensional. Extra isotropic radical components that pair with other
profiles are outside this result. The witness edges are serialized in
evidence/TURYN-CROSS-CONSTRAINTS.json.

How this differs from merely re-coordinatizing Leech
---------------------------------------------------
The invariant cubic t(t^2-4) of the full 98280-line shell has real rank
2600 in every coordinate system. This candidate instead changes BOTH
the port set and the real scalar kernel, then derives a new fitting
matrix. A small rank of B would therefore be a real opportunity.

Potential next transform
------------------------
The four global block-sign variants of a (2,1,1) triple carry four odd
characters of the block-sign group: 100,010,001,111. The full cubic
feature space splits into dimensions 696,696,696,512 respectively:
696=dim Sym^3(R^8)+2*dim Sym^2(R^8)*8=120+576.
The product kernel is precisely the 111 sector.
Using all four sectors after a Walsh transform would require paying
for the changes to a common frame and the inverse changes. It is not
valid to count those four sign variants as free extra product ports.

Scope
-----
This is a candidate and a compatibility lemma, not an improvement claim.
Completed: exact generation, whole-pair parity checks, explicit binary
labels, source-support ranks, E8 equivalence, and the bounded cross-map
constraint certificate. An improved supplier would still need a legal
scalar word, helper inventory and hosting, and a moment/outer certificate.

Basic cost threshold
--------------------
If B has standard-dot frame dimension h and every center can use width
h-1, then the center bill is 512(h-1). A necessary condition for any
positive deficit in the repeated rank-one supplier model is h<=45,
since N=23040=45*512. At h=46 the center bill already equals N.
This is only a nomination threshold: h<=45 by itself does not certify
a competitive exponent, and literal supports may require width h.

Color-only labels do not save rank mass
-------------------------------------
Color the ports by (mbar, position of the norm-2 block), giving 45 colors.
Within each color the 512 tensor features form an orthogonal real basis,
so the 45 one-hot binary labels form a legal fitting representation.
However, every nonzero linear feature on the 512-dimensional real tensor
space is nonzero on at least one vector from each of those 45 bases.
Its label support therefore spans all 45 binary coordinate vectors.
Thus every nonzero encoder feature costs width at least 45 under these
specific labels. Any factorization of the rank-512 tensor kernel needs
at least 512 nonzero encoder features, so the center bill is at least
45*512=23040=N. This rules out positive rank deficit for this color-only
label choice, including changes of real feature basis. It does not rule
out the profile-dependent h=27 labels or other labels varying within
the 45 colors.
