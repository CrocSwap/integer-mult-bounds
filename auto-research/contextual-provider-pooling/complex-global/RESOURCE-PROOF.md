# Five-clone remainder pooling

Fix a complete arbitrary-dirt local wrapper with physical helper endpoint Q on its assigned residual coordinates and identity on spectators. Start from a checked controller with eight replicas, 73 ambient axes, and 72 complete invocations. Make five copies of its data-family set. Interleave the five corresponding complete invocations at each original invocation time. The local ambient dimension remains 73; this construction does not widen a local call to 40 sets of residual axes.

Relabel every data family in clone c by adding 7168 c and every invocation/owner replica by adding 8 c. Each cloned owner's original idle window contains all five copies of every formerly admitted invocation time: the source event projection on that owner's replica is unchanged, and extra intervening events belong to other replicas. Borrowed spectators are restored at each complete invocation. Private centers, tableau, and coefficient workspaces may therefore be reused between sequential complete invocations.

In the source certificate, owner replica 1, child 10, port 107 (family 1787) contains exactly one width-9 helper. It is helper 376 in stage 2, invocation replica 2, source time 12. Its pending child has rank 48. For clone c=0,...,4, redirect just this helper to clone zero's family 1787, on pending offsets 9c. These five invocations occur at times 60,...,64. The destination family is idle throughout, is not an active data family, and occurs only once in each complete call. The five assigned coordinate intervals are disjoint. Source-bound full 73-column charts implement their actual residual coordinates; no helper is split between families.

The clone-zero pending child now uses 45 axes and pays one rank-3 remainder. The other four formerly partial children pay rank 48. Every other local child, bridge, and remainder is unchanged. Hence the complete recursive profile changes from five copies of the original by

    -5 children of rank 39 +4 children of rank 48 +1 child of rank 3.

Both total rank mass and live stock scale by five: live 35840, deficit 12800. For every 0<a<1, strict concavity and 39=(4*48+3)/5 give

    4*48^a +3^a < 5*39^a.

Thus the recurrence moment strictly improves, without a fractional child or fractional owner. The exact finite invoice, larger prefix charge, and all 47 outer inequalities must also pass; the inequality alone is not a numerical kappa certificate.

The literal emitter and independently structured controller auditor check 181440 helper roles, all 360 complete calls, source widths, active-data exclusion, one family per helper, exact owner idle intervals including bridges, pairwise disjoint pending axes, 73-column charts, all remainders and source hashes. Seven genuine negative controls test aliasing, active/private owners, residual width, spectator coordinates, omitted remainder, and understated physical reserve.

That floating optimization nominated the construction; the explicit five-clone witness and strict concavity prove it. No exact global integer-optimality claim is made.
