\subsection{Mixed centers with dyadic scatter}
The complex payload is Gaussian dyadic; its address labels are binary.
For an even axis dimension $h$ define
\[
 T=\sum_U X_U,\qquad G_i=\sum_{U\ni i}X_U,\qquad D_i=T-G_i.
\]
Choose a point set $\mathcal D$ of size $d_0$ with $|3-d_0|$ a
nonzero power of two. Retain $D_i$ for $i\in\mathcal D$ and
$B_i=2G_i$ for $i\notin\mathcal D$. Since $\sum_iG_i=3T$,
\[
 T=\frac{\sum_{i\notin\mathcal D}B_i-2\sum_{i\in\mathcal D}D_i}
          {2(3-d_0)}.
\]
The required central correction at triple $S$ is
\[
 \frac{\sum_{i\in S}G_i-T}{2}
 =\sum_{i\in S\setminus\mathcal D}\frac{B_i}{4}
  -\sum_{i\in S\cap\mathcal D}\frac{D_i}{2}
  +\frac{|S\cap\mathcal D|-1}{2}T.
\]
Substitute the expression for $T$ to obtain one fixed dyadic scatter
on exactly $h$ retained centers. Adding half the disjoint sum and
subtracting half the intersection-two sum gives coefficient one on
$X_S$ and zero on all other source triples. There is no global-total
role. The transparent wrapper subtracts the old scatter and adds the
new scatter after source copying, then restores the auxiliaries.
Only the fresh contribution must satisfy the identity; arbitrary old
auxiliary values are permitted.

Label $B_i$ by $E_i=\{x:\sum_{j\ne i}x_j=0\}$ and $D_i$ by the
coordinate space omitting $i$. Both are nondegenerate of dimension
$h-1$: the normal of $E_i$ has odd norm because $h$ is even.
Every contributing triple line lies in its assigned frame.
Hence center loss is $h(h-1)$. Use $d_0=19$ when $h=30$, and
$d_0=35$ when $h=40$, choosing the first $d_0$ points in the fixed
producer order. The expanded scatter denominators divide 128.

The base-two paired exclusion producer retains complete disjoint sums,
full pair-star sums, and their leave-one-out side sums, sharing by
actual source support. A support with at least two common points has
its orthonormal common-pair-star span; otherwise use the coordinate
space of its union. The central $B_i$ additions use $E_i$. These
labels are nested and nondegenerate, so the inherited deterministic
carrier compiler applies. Its selected counts are
\[

They exclude all external macros and physical-data frontiers.

\subsection{General residuals and translated endpoints}
The general normal form of \path{notes/endpoint-gauge-complex.tex}
remains applicable, including alternating residuals. For clarity, if
$V$ is a binary residual basis, $G=V^tV$ nonsingular, and
$q(z)=\operatorname{wt}(VG^{-1}z)\pmod4$, then
\[
 K(x,y)=2^{-r}S_q i^{-q(Gx)}(-1)^{x^tGy}i^{-q(Gy)},\qquad
 S_q=\sum_z i^{q(z)}.
\]
This follows by completing the square in the Fourier kernel; the polar
form is $2G^{-1}$. With $b_0=(1-i)/2$ and $S_0=\operatorname{diag}(1,i)$,
\[
 \tfrac12\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix}=b_0 S_0CS_0.
\]
The Gauss sums of odd one-dimensional blocks are $1\pm i$ and those
of alternating two-dimensional blocks are $\pm2$, so $S_qb_0^r$ is
a fourth root of unity. Therefore one rank-$r$ child, binary address
permutations and unit fourth-root phases implement the residual.
Mixed signs retain the same nonsingular polar form. Tensoring over
$f$ parallel columns gives one width-$rf$ child. No orthonormal basis
of the whole residual or new non-dyadic coefficient primitive is used.
