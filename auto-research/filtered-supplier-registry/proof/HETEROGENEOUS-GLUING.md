# Theorem: contextual gluing of a finite registry of normalized suppliers

## Interface, not one privileged implementation

Fix a finite sequence of complete controller invocations, with data owner maps,
orientations and interleaved external operations. Fix a common local data space
D, dirty helper space H, and external normalized data endpoint A_j for invocation
j. A finite supplier registry contains explicitly constructed reversible words.
For every admitted variant v and orientation j, its fully realized endpoint on
arbitrary local state is

    (d,(h_k)_k) -> (A_j d,(Q_(v,j,k) h_k)_k).

Here Q_(v,j,k) is the explicitly normalized residual operator on that
helper's assigned pending axes. It is the identity on the borrowed spectator
coordinates, not on the entire helper state. Scalar-body dirty restoration
and this physical residual endpoint are distinct assertions.

Temporary scalar/center/spectator storage is restored to its prescribed state.
Each variant carries its exact entry frames, finite phase recipes, paid local
child histogram L_(v,j), actual scalar envelope and concrete wrapper construction.
These are certificates of actual programs, not an assumed unknown oracle.

For each invocation choose a variant v(j), a full injective physical owner map,
and actual orthogonal charts. All helper owners must be idle for the complete
invocation; no active data owner is used as its helper. Residual coordinates
assigned within a pending idle child are disjoint. Spectator wrappers satisfy
the same arbitrary-state endpoint and are explicitly charged. Every positive
unassigned residual dimension is executed as a recursive remainder child.

Then replacing the uniform supplier by this heterogeneous finite registry
preserves the full external controller endpoint for every global initial state.
Its exact recursive histogram is

    H = sum_j L_(v(j),j) + sum_pending_bins [remaining dimension].

Any other unchanged controller children are included on both sides explicitly.
No averaging of exponents or mixing of separate claimed gains is permitted.

## Proof of semantic contextuality

Conjugating a local supplier by its actual owner charts embeds its normalized
endpoint into the global state space. The complete invocation acts as A_j on
its data families and as Q_(v,j,k) on each distinct helper family, restoring
all spectator coordinates. This holds for arbitrary initial helper dirt.

First split each original pending idle operator, in its actual orthogonal
chart, into the product of its assigned residual factors and the explicitly
executed remainder. The factors act on disjoint coordinate supports; they
commute and their product is exactly the original pending operator.

Move each assigned residual factor from the original idle operation to its
nominated complete-invocation boundary. Its entire route lies within the
owner's checked idle interval, which contains no intervening data or bridge
touch of that family. Other complete invocations that use this family have
only their explicitly assigned residual endpoint there. Those residuals have
disjoint supports in the same pending chart and commute. Invocations not
using the family act on other factors. Complete spectator restoration is
essential: no unresolved within-call interaction is moved across a boundary.
Thus every move is an equality of full operators. Injective owner maps exclude
any simultaneous data/helper or helper/helper alias within an invocation.

At each nominated boundary the data endpoint and assigned helper residuals
act on distinct families. Their product is precisely the admitted supplier's
complete endpoint, so replace that product by its actual finite implementation.
Doing this for every boundary realizes the original controller with its
original pending operators. This common pending-operator normal form proves
contextual composition even when variants have different residual widths or
assignments. It does not assert that such variants have identical physical
helper endpoints call by call.

In the final finite witness, filtered and observationally retargeted variants
have identical entrance frames, widths and actual owner charts. Consequently
they have the SAME Q_(v,j,k) at every fixed call slot. For these two variants,
direct call-by-call substitution is valid, because their entire data-plus-
residual endpoints are identical; neither endpoint is helper identity.

Counting the emitted local calls and each remaining idle child proves the
histogram identity. Scalar work and precision bounds use the actual selected
program for each call, or an explicit conservative maximum over the finite
registry. A finite registry has a common finite maximum for all inherited
compiler constants/cutoffs; it introduces no input-dependent unproved advice.
The existing all-size realization hypotheses remain explicit for the admitted
words. The theorem proves composition, not those external hypotheses anew.

## A conserved resource quantity

For fixed ambient rank m, live stock W and pending-bin ranks q_b, write w_(j,h)
for each actual helper residual width. The rank mass of the full profile is

    mass(H) = sum_j mass(L_(v(j),j)) + sum_b q_b - sum_(j,h) w_(j,h).

Hence changing a provider preserves the global deficit m*W-mass(H) whenever
its local rank-mass change equals its total helper-width change, call by call.
This identity follows by expanding each remainder q_b minus occupied widths;
it does not require the width distribution or pivot count to remain fixed.
The identity is a conservation check, while the explicit charts and capacity
checks above provide sufficiency. The invariant alone does not construct a map.

## Optimization consequence

Uniform suppliers are the constant selections v(j)=v. They form a subset of the
finite heterogeneous construction space, so retaining all feasible uniform
witnesses ensures that exact search over the larger space cannot worsen the
best admitted result. A concrete nonuniform gain still requires a witness.
Different pending-bin remainder sizes impose different marginal moment costs,
so the same source rewrite need not be profitable in every invocation.

At a fixed candidate exponent b, compare complete histograms using the actual
moment sum over r of H_r*(r/m)^(1-b), including the finite fallback toll. A strictly
smaller exact moment at the old endpoint yields a larger admissible neighborhood
by continuity; the numerical bound is certified separately by rational engines.
This is why per-call variant selection can expose gains hidden by a single
global provider choice. The final finite construction below is checked separately.

## Status and attribution

This is a mathematical sufficient composition theorem, with a finite concrete
application to be recorded separately. It is not a Lean theorem or a claim of
global originality for contextual equivalence. The viewpoint is inspired by
program refinement, full-state dirty-workspace restoration in the local TI
implementation, and the explicit complete-bank endpoint interface discussed in
PR528. That PR's actual physical realization hypotheses are not erased here.
The genuinely useful proposed extension is a supplier registry with per-call
choice and a common exact resource ledger; all numerical contribution remains
subject to the unchanged full admission checks.
