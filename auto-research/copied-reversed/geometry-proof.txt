1. Actual prescribed permutations and contractions

Put a=23,b=25,m=575,d=47 and I=(0,...,22). Prescribe first-d row labels
R=[I,22,I] and last-d inverse-column labels C=[I,0,I]. A physical index i is
25*alpha+beta. Both prescriptions impose entries of the same permutation
M_beta; all25 completions are explicitly constructed and verified in the
companion JSON. The local factor bases remain arbitrary in GL23 x GL25.
The completed matrices are fixed once for the entire finite circuit.

With the factor bases absorbed into projectors, the actual tensor contraction
on arbitrary physical indices is
 (T_M(X tensor Y)T_M^-1)_(i,j)
 = X_(M_beta[alpha],M_delta[gamma]) Y_(beta,delta).
The companion checker evaluates this through the completed permutations for
arbitrary nonsymmetric rational X,Y and independently normalized line pairs.

On first23/last23 indices, A tensor(v nu) is
 diag(v_i) A diag(nu_(j+2)), i,j=0,...,22.
Thus arbitrary local first-factor projectors transfer up to nonzero diagonal
scales, and A=I gives a nonzero diagonal auxiliary23-corner. On first25/last25
indices, (p xi) tensor B is
 diag(p_(r_i)) B diag(xi_(c_j)),
 r=(0,...,22,22,0), c=(22,0,0,...,22).
Matched second-factor residues transfer arbitrary B and give the auxiliary25
corner at B=I. Repeated first-factor labels are allowed; they are not grounds
for requiring equality of r and c. All scale coordinates are nonzero on a
nonempty open subset of the same generic factor bases.

2. Exact full data corner

The companion checker evaluates this directly from the completed permutations,
not only from a copied symbolic formula. The actual large-projector corner

After nonzero row/column scaling this is

4. Copied-center complements in the same generic basis

PR36 keeps every old rank-(h-1) center residual as a paid transform of the
copied stream, and replaces each original full-rank transition by the
rank-one projector onto U_i^perp. Since U_i is a nondegenerate codimension-one
retained center space, I-P_U_i is an idempotent of rank one. It need not be a
triple-line projector or positive in the ambient indefinite form. Generic
ordinary-profile existence only needs its rank and nondegeneracy, not its
identity as a source line: any rank-one idempotent is GL-conjugate to a
normalized p xi with all required primal and dual coordinates nonzero.

The local contraction identities in Section1 hold for ANY rational matrix,
so they also transfer each new complement. Retained copied residuals use
their unchanged generic ordinary profiles. For each physical ordinary or
complement projector, its generic profile conditions are nonzero rational
functions of GL23 or GL25. Every pair of fixed source-line projectors can be
sent to the exact data witness by factorwise conjugation. Thus all required
ordinary, new complement, auxiliary, line-coordinate and data-prefix minor
conditions are nonzero functions on the same irreducible GL23 x GL25 family.
Their finite product is nonzero, giving one rational basis simultaneously
for the entire finite circuit. Pick the address prime afterwards, avoiding
all finitely many denominators and nonzero minors. No basis choice per gate
or per retained center is needed.

5. Orientation and accounting scope

The written copied-center schedule is stated in either invocation orientation
and in a local factor of arbitrary dimension h, with background projectors
D0 and D1 and rank-one other factor. Exchanging the chosen dimensions23 and25
does not change those hypotheses. The selected h23/h25 scalar producers,
original carrier matchings and compatible positive labels can be reused.
The H'_h update is unchanged: add h to its rank-one count and subtract h from
its full-rank count, preserving the old rank-(h-1) copied transforms.
Independent audit of the full dirty-scratch schedule is a separate task;
this local proof does not infer that schedule from rank accounting alone.

The two auxiliary bank sizes exchange stage names. Their complete child
multiset, the copied local histograms replicated N/v_h times, both data
fronts and the N paid endpoint corrections are symmetric in the factor
names. Consequently W=188181929, N=4073300, L=2226400 and rank
s=W*575-N+L=108202762275 remain unchanged. The sole new data delta is
 -4N singleton calls -2N width15 calls +2N width17 calls.
Its rank change is -4N-30N+34N=0. The two bank profiles are(23,529) and(25,525),
and maxchild remains529. A complete exact characteristic and assembly check
