## Construction and operator proof

Take five disjoint copies of every data family in the old controller. A family is tagged (block,old_family), with block in {0,...,4}. Keep m=73, all73-column charts, the local112-port scalar word, the old orientation choices, and each old per-replica recipe unchanged. Rename all literal family references injectively. Private work may be separately allocated per block; sharing private work instead requires its already-checked erasure at each complete call. A conservative construction can retain all five private namespaces.

Before pooling, this is the literal direct sum of five programs. Renaming preserves each elementary operator, and operations on disjoint families commute, so the endpoint is the direct sum of the five original data endpoints and restored private work. This establishes every copied data identity without changing the73-axis cover or inventing a larger local operator.

Now choose a new finite whole-owner allocation among pending idle children of any block. Retain all of the following checks:

1. Each complete local cover call is indivisible and retains the original local program, exact phases and both entrance wrappers. Its data roles lie in its own controller block.
2. Every helper occupies a different whole family from every other helper and active data role of that call. Its residual axes are an explicitly charted subset of one pending child of its owner family; the rest of that same full family supplies the normalized spectators.
3. The actual global serialized order places the entire call strictly inside that owner's pending window, including all bridge touches and remainder deadlines. Checking only a stage label is insufficient.
4. Across all calls, no pending axis is consumed twice. All unused axes execute as a literal remaining child before the owner's next conflicting operation. Every terminal translation is performed exactly once per final live family.
5. The new complete profile, global namespace and actual73-column maps are independently recounted; no fractional role or split-family invocation is permitted.

By the retained normalized-call theorem, arbitrary helper dirt does not survive as a coupling into the call's data output. At the complete endpoint, the call has its prescribed data shear and the prescribed residual transform on each selected whole helper family, with its spectators restored. Therefore a helper may belong to another controller block: temporary cross-block scalar interactions have disappeared at the complete boundary, and the only remaining action on the owner is the intended piece of its pending operator.

On each owner, the assigned residual axes and the remaining axes partition the original pending child. Their transforms compose to that child within its no-touch window. Replacing each such partition by the original pending operator gives the disjoint five-controller program already proved above. This proves the pooled data endpoint. It does not assume the endpoint is identity on helper families; the residual operator is essential to the argument.

For arbitrary sizes admitted by the retained compiler, the block tag is a finite family-name tag, not a new vector-space coordinate or a change in the cover group. Every existing local chart and complete-call identity is reused at the same m and same size, and the above finite composition is uniform in that size. The only enlarged quantities are the finite number of families, calls, elementary operations and recursive children, all explicitly represented in the new program and invoice. Thus this uses the existing uniform primitive/compiler contract, rather than requiring an unproved conversion between different m values. The inherited all-size/compiler and analytic assumptions remain conditional exactly as before.
