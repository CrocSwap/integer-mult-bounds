Scoped Turyn moment comparisons
==============================

Reference and limits
--------------------
The comparison target is PR592's declared conditional kappa
0.001612719427075359 at head 9092320f7932dbe61a78f15b6c5fd6a0cbd41307.
It was read, not freshly replayed by this package. PR595, at head
39112c2577452fa278489fb9fd77a9aecb2cdc24, supplies structural accounting
context, especially its B_K slot relaxation and explicit scope assumptions.
Its claimed E8 format bound is not transferred to the Turyn geometry.

Every number below is a nominal zero-helper moment calculation in a
specified model. There is no full-cost Turyn word, helper factory, owner
map, or outer-inequality certificate behind these values. In particular,
the 0.00142213 full-port result is not a universal dimension-27 ceiling.

Equations
---------
For N ports, label dimension h, center width c, and odd stage count L,
the inherited paired controller uses

    d = h-1,  K = (L-1)/2,  m = dL+1,
    W = (L-1)N,  C = 512c,  D = W-LC.

The required active data mass is 2NLd at child rank d. The center mass
is LC at rank c in the literal model. Every idle slot of rank r carries
mass 2Nr. The slot ranks are the initial pair's tail 2(K-1)d, and for
p = 1,...,K-1 the pre-stretch 2pd, bridge d, and positive tail
2(K-p-1)d. The exact identity total rank mass = mW-D is checked.

For mass M at child rank r, define pressure

    g(M,r,a) = M*((m/r)^a-1).

The nominal root satisfies sum g = D, and kappa = a/(1+a). All reported
roots have rational lower and upper bounds using positive series with
explicit remainders. Floating arithmetic only nominates the root and
stage. Every other odd L from 3 through 101 is certified inferior to the
selected root's lower bracket. No stage maximum outside this range is
claimed. Exact brackets and target slacks are in evidence/COSTS.json.

Four models
-----------
literal_twolevel splits every idle slot into as many children of rank
floor(m/sqrt(2)) as fit, then one remainder. It uses center child rank c.
This fixes a particular zero-helper residual profile.

hosting_aware_slots instead allows fractional packing of each idle mass
at rank max(r,h), and center mass at rank h. It follows PR595's necessary
slot accounting under its compressed-window, star-copy and whole-residual
hosting assumptions, applied conditionally to these Turyn parameters.
The relaxed ranks account optimistically for possible helper hosting;
they do not construct helpers or prove a sufficient recurrence.

no_bridge_split additionally merges the idle pre-stretch and bridge into
rank (2p+1)d. This drops a controller convention. idle_free removes all
idle pressure. Both are optimistic tests of changed controllers, not
implementations and not deductions about the inherited controller.

Relative to PR592, the full h=27 literal value is 11.82% lower; the
punctured h=27 profile has only +0.74% literal or +1.06% hosting-aware headroom.
The hypothetical h=25 profile has +27.20% before helper and outer costs.
Even at h=27, a changed controller has room in these relaxations: removing
the bridge split raises the full-port nominal value to +2.69%. This is
why binary dimension alone does not settle the gain question.

A concrete budget for a future full-port 25-bit fit
-------------------------------------------------
For N=23040, h=c=25, L=3, m=73, the stock is W=46080 and deficit D=7680.
At three target kappas, the unused pressure D-sum g is approximately

    PR592 value          1646.444530
    PR592 plus 10%       1041.419313
    PR592 plus 20%        436.089641

This L=3 schedule has no idle hosting slots. Helpers that require new
banks must be paid; the table cannot be read as a count of free helpers.
If a proposed circuit loses e units of deficit (D_new = D-e) and changes
pressure by DeltaP after subtracting removed idle children, the necessary
budget is

    e + DeltaP < the recorded unused pressure.

For a new paid bank of rank mass mb, even ideal cap packing costs at
least mb*((m/floor(m/sqrt(2)))^a-1) in pressure; fragmentation costs more.
The full cost must include both this pressure and any lost deficit.
At L=5 the inherited literal profile already fails the +20% target
without helpers (slack about -820.94), despite its extra hosting slots.

The unclosed levers are thus structural: a nonlinear full-port map with
controlled encoder supports, redundant real factorizations that split
support economically, or a changed controller/helper factory that earns
back its own cost. An algebraic rank reduction by itself is insufficient.
