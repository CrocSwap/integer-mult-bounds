\paragraph{Setting.} The two-stage bit motif with partial-swap frames, $m=h^2$, $v=\binom h3$, $N=v^2$, with
every auxiliary role's selected edge compiled by the partial-swap batching lemma
(\texttt{partial-swap-batching.tex}). Write $P_a$ for the $G$-orthogonal projector onto $\langle t_a\rangle$ and
$Q_a=I-P_a$.

\begin{lemma}[Data entrances]
For every data pair, the stage-two entrance edges of the $X$ and $Y$ roles both have idempotent difference
$Q_1\otimes Q_2$, of rank $(h-1)^2=m-2h+1$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Since $t_1^\perp\otimes F\perp t_1\otimes t_2$ under $G\otimes G$, the $X$ entrance is
$\operatorname{proj}(D_0+t_1\otimes t_2)-\operatorname{proj}(F\otimes t_2)=Q_1\otimes I+P_1\otimes P_2-I\otimes P_2=Q_1\otimes Q_2$.
The $Y$ entrance is $\operatorname{proj}(D_0)-\operatorname{proj}(t_1^\perp\otimes t_2)=Q_1\otimes I-Q_1\otimes P_2=Q_1\otimes Q_2$.
\end{proof}

For $h\ge4$, $(h-1)^2>m/2$, so the partial-swap batching lemma applies with $k=2h-1$ corner transpositions and
one aligned block of width $t=m-4h+2$. The common basis is the one already used for the auxiliary edges: each
data corner adds one nonzero polynomial condition $\det(S^{-1}U)_L\det(VS)_R$ on $S$, nonzero on the big cell
$S=L\,U$ (unit lower times unit upper), so finitely many conditions have a common integral unimodular solution.

\paragraph{Accounting.} Per data role the ranks are $(h-1)+(m-2h+1)+(h-1)=m-1$; with one copy-correction child
of width $1$ per pair this reproduces $s=Wm-N+2L$, and $Wm-s=v(v-4h^2)>0$ for $h\ge28$.

\paragraph{Saving.} With the side-role counts of our generator (valid for $28\le h\le50$), the batched moment
certifies $a_b=10033/10^9$ at $h=47$ ($R=418678$), against $10027/10^9$ at $h=46$ and $4431/10^9$ at $h=32$
without data batching.

\paragraph{Checks.} Corners invertible under one common $S_0$ for every auxiliary edge and every data pair at
$h=6,7,8$ (all $3136$ pairs at $h=8$); exact Bruhat profiles match; the closed form is checked in rationals.
