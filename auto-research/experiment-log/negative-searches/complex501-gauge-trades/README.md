## New composition nomination

The [heterogeneous model](complex501-heterogeneous-words/LP.json) considers143 distinct legal profile/width classes across the72 invocations, with a3179-variable135-row allocation relaxation. The [integer nomination](complex501-heterogeneous-words/MILP.json) uses the baseline at eight stages and helper540's rank2 entrance at the middle stage only, all eight replicas together. It uses no banks and gives profile {1:172064,2:19000,3:42024,4:6056,5:22496,6:6624,7:1800,8:4120}.

This is a useful exception to the homogeneous failures: stage-specific demands can fit existing capacity even though the changed word loses when used at every stage. Subsequent exact arithmetic and explicit coloring are published as an early [draft candidate in PR473](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/473), computed conditional kappa0.001343523016252057. Physical and finite-invoice verification was still running when this archive entry was prepared; consult the candidate's current receipts before treating it as admitted. The aggregate mixed-word model alone does not prove a physical assignment. Never promote floating optimizer output directly into a bound.

Retry guidance: do not discard a locally legal losing word solely because its homogeneous price is lower. Retain its full width and paid profile for stage-dependent composition. For general mixtures within a stage, matching colors must also respect the chosen word for each invocation; unconstrained aggregate edge coloring does not automatically do that. The present nomination avoids that ambiguity by choosing one word per complete stage.
