# Architectures And Stages

Historical campaign entries 16–30; original statuses and scope are preserved. This is a topic index, not a claim that all proposals ran. Baselines vary and must be read in the linked reports.

## 16. Map κ(p)

Status: `bounded_no_gain`. Generic p=8 through 14 and restricted annealed modules were evaluated. None beat the then-current p=10 word; module quality confounds any claim about the optimal size.

Evidence: [reports/A-RESULT.md](https://github.com/rohanarun/integer-mult-bounds/blob/fa8ab946df57ebadae1a470b637c2a590a0a5317/research/campaign-100-results/reports/A-RESULT.md)
