D16 spinor continuation: exact local results and implementation scopes
====================================================================

No kappa improvement or complete improved supplier is claimed. These files preserve the local research, now packaged for reproducible replay;
the computations do not establish a new supplier.

Conventions
-----------
For x in F2^14, the rational address line is represented by

 v(x) = (1, (-1)^x1, ..., (-1)^x14, (-1)^sum(x))/2.

There are N=16384 lines in dimension16. Their squared norms are4, and
v(x).v(y)=0 exactly when wt(x+y) is7 or8. The scalar fitting kernel over
F2 is C(x)=1 at x=0 and at weights7,8, and zero elsewhere. Its previously
certified binary rank is576, giving full-width center bill9216 and nominal
surplus N-9216=7168 before further paid costs.

H consists of the16 evaluations of linear functions on F2^4, with the
zero coordinate and last parity coordinate handled by the above convention:

 H(a)_(j-1) = parity(a & j),  j=1,...,14.

The H-cosets are1024 orthogonal16-port Hadamard bases. J_H denotes the
convolution kernel that is1 on H. Its rank is1024. The residual
D=C+J_H has rank1268, certified in
workbench/bit-routes/{clique_residual.py,CLIQUE-RESIDUAL.json}.

1. A basis-independent bound for the rank-minimal residual-copy route
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The truth function C has Boolean algebraic normal form

 C=e0+e1+e2+e3+e4+e5+e6,

where er is the elementary square-free polynomial in the14 index bits.
The equality follows by checking the15 possible Hamming weights. Thus
all translations of C and all vectors in im(C) have Boolean degree<=6.

Let w be any nonzero vector in im(D). For h in H,

 Delta_h(w)=Delta_h(Cg)

for some g, because J_H*g is H-invariant. A translation derivative of a
degree<=6 Boolean polynomial has degree<=5. Every nonzero degree<=5
Boolean polynomial on14 variables has at least2^(14-5)=512 nonzero
values. This elementary Reed--Muller distance bound follows by induction:
split in the last variable; a nonzero derivative of degree d-1 accounts
for at least2^(n-d) nonzero positions across the two slices, and if that
derivative vanishes the identical slices double the n-1 variable bound.

Suppose the spinor lines in supp(w) span a real subspace of dimension k<=8.
A k-dimensional real subspace intersects the sign cube in at most2^k
vertices: choose k coordinate projections injective on that subspace.
Antipodal pairing leaves at most2^(k-1)<=128 of our canonical lines.
Consequently every Delta_h(w) has support size<=256, so it must vanish.
Then w is H-invariant. A nonempty H-invariant support contains a whole
H-coset, hence16 mutually orthogonal lines spanning the entire16-space.
This contradicts k<=8.

Therefore EVERY nonzero column in im(D) has rational support span>=9.
For a rank-minimal factorization D=AB with1268 columns in A, im(A)=im(D).
A FULL16 aggregate that is privately copied into the common target cap
for one such decoder column must descend by at least9 ranks. The total
is therefore at least

 1268*9 =11412 >7168.

This rejects every rank-minimal1268-column full-aggregate/private-copy
implementation of this residual, not just its natural columns. It does
NOT reject redundant factorizations, mutable-source schedules, partial
or fused transforms, intermediate target descents, or all circuits.

frame_bounds.py checks the complete truth table by weight and the exact
16x16 Hadamard Gram matrix. FRAME-BOUNDS.json records these finite premises.
The rank1268 is an input from the separate full convolution-rank receipt,
not recomputed by frame_bounds.py.

2. Matching additions cannot lower the Hadamard baseline rank
-----------------------------------------------------------
In the F2 group algebra, set zi=1+gi for a group basis extending H. Then
zi^2=0 and J_H=z1*z2*z3*z4. A complementary8D packet-matching layer has
kernel g_a*J_W for a7-dimensional translation subgroup W. Its augmentation
filtration degree is at least7. Any XOR sum of those layers therefore
leaves the degree4 initial term of J_H unchanged.

Multiplication by the initial term has rank2^(14-4)=1024. Ordering domain
and codomain by degree makes its independent pivots a triangular minor
in the perturbed multiplication matrix. Hence the perturbed kernel has
rank>=1024. This is a bound for this Hadamard-baseline plus affine7-subgroup
matching family; it says nothing universal about different baselines.

3. Exact width of the useful one-matching fused construction
----------------------------------------------------------
The budget agent constructed a complete clique16 adjacency schedule with
34 dirty helpers per clique, and a compatible matching can be fused at
no additional paid profile. This paragraph audits its scalar kernel,
not the separate word replay.

In each complementary8D packet pair U,U-perp, the Hadamard cliques cut
into16 halves on each side, each half consisting of8 orthogonal lines
spanning that side. Collapsing constant values on those halves gives

 T = [[I16, A], [A, I16]],  A=I16+J16.

Since A^2=I16, im(T)={(z,Az)}. Every nonzero z has Az nonzero. Therefore
every nonzero image column uses at least one entire8-port half in U and
at least one entire8-port half in U-perp. Its rational support spans16.
An active packet-pair component already forces this span even if the
column also uses other components.

Thus every column of EVERY rank-minimal1024-column factorization of the
fused kernel has full16-dimensional address support. Its rank-minimal
center bill is exactly1024*16=N. The matching fusion is a legitimate
schedule improvement but produces no positive full-width center surplus.
The statement does not lower-bound every redundant scalar factorization.

frame_bounds.py independently checks A^2=I, rank(T)=16, and the image
identity. The physical half-incidence and complete arbitrary-dirt word
belong to the budget agent's separate receipt.

4. Full D16 sparse--spinor cross block
------------------------------------
The14,576 sparse norm-four lines against the16,384 spinor lines have
orthogonality incidence matrix X of exact binary rank470.

cross_rank.py regenerates all its rows from the regenerated doubled integer
coordinates workbench/bit-routes/d16-ports.npz, computes X by exact
integer dot products, and records470 independent natural sparse rows in
CROSS-RANK.json. cross_factor.py expresses ALL14,576 rows in those470
original rows and independently replays every resulting equality.

For this explicitly chosen lexicographic natural-row factorization,
each of its470 decoder supports spans the full16-dimensional rational
space. This is established by finite-field101 rank16, which exhibits
nonzero integer minors and therefore proves rational rank16. Such decoder
columns have common target cap0. Direct FULL16 private copies would cost
16*470 per orientation, or15040 across both orientations. This figure is
not a complete word or a viable total price.

CROSS-FACTOR.json records the verified statement; CROSS-FACTOR.npz stores
the witness rows and coefficient matrix. This width result is about this
one factorization, not all470-rank factorizations.

5. Bounded alternate-baseline search
-----------------------------------
An odd sum of Hadamard J_H kernels is a valid fitting kernel. There is no
universal obstruction to cancelling their degree4 initial forms:
clique_leading.py finds an exact433-clique odd relation among910 sampled
cliques, whose initial4-form span has rank909. The resulting kernel has
2720 nonzero entries in its convolution row, lowest augmentation degree5,
and exact binary rank2942. Its full-width center cost is worse than N.
CLIQUE-LEADING.json contains all selected cliques and the resulting kernel;
clique_rank.py performs the complete packed convolution elimination.

The bounded structured searches clique_small.py and clique_half.py find
no odd leading-term relation in the tested small coordinate-permutation
orbits. This is a finite search outcome, not a theorem for all cliques.
These searches do not produce an improved supplier or a cheap word.
