Scoped spinor frame bounds
=========================
Derived by Codex, with kernel/schedule interfaces supplied by the parent
and budget tracks. These are bounds for specified implementations, not
universal lower bounds for spinor circuits.

Conventions. G=F2^14 indexes lines
v(x)=(1,(-1)^x1,...,(-1)^x14,(-1)^sum(x))/2 in Q^16.
C is the convolution kernel 1 at0 and at Hamming weights7,8. H is the
4-dimensional linear-evaluation subgroup; each H-coset consists of16
mutually orthogonal lines. Let J_H be its indicator and D=C+J_H. Previously
certified ranks are rank(C)=576, rank(J_H)=1024, rank(D)=1268.

A. Hadamard baseline plus matching layers
----------------------------------------
Identify F2[G] with F2[z1,...,z14]/(zi^2), using zi=1+gi for a group basis
whose first4 generators span H. Then J_H=z1z2z3z4. A matching layer on a
coset of a7-dimensional translation subgroup W has kernel g_a J_W and
augmentation-filtration degree>=7. Any sum of such layers leaves J_H's
degree4 initial term unchanged.

Multiplication by z1z2z3z4 has rank2^10=1024: the1024 monomials disjoint
from {1,2,3,4} map to distinct monomials. Order their columns and images
by degree. The same rows/columns in the perturbed multiplication matrix
have an identity diagonal block at each degree; all additional entries
have strictly higher output degree. This is a triangular1024-square
minor with determinant1. Hence the new kernel's rank is at least1024.

Scope: Hadamard-baseline kernels plus these affine7-subgroup matching
kernels. Different baselines are not covered.

B. Every nonzero vector in im(D) has address-support span>=9
----------------------------------------------------------
As a Boolean truth function,

 C=e0+e1+e2+e3+e4+e5+e6,

where er is the elementary square-free polynomial on14 bits. The identity
is verified by the15 possible Hamming weights. Thus im(C) consists of
Boolean functions of degree<=6. For w=Dg and h in H,

 Delta_h w = Delta_h(Cg),

since J_H g is H-invariant. Translation differentiation lowers Boolean
degree by at least1, so a nonzero Delta_h w has degree<=5 and Hamming
weight>=2^(14-5)=512. This Boolean-polynomial weight bound follows by
induction on the number of variables, splitting into two slices in the
last variable: either the slices agree, or their nonzero difference has
degree one lower and supplies that many nonzero entries across the slices.

A k-dimensional real subspace contains at most2^k sign-cube vertices:
choose k coordinate projections injective on the subspace. Pairing
antipodal vertices therefore leaves at most2^(k-1) canonical spinor lines.
If supp(w) spanned dimension<=8, its size would be<=128. Each Delta_h w
would then have weight<=256<512 and would vanish. Hence w would be
H-invariant. A nonempty H-invariant support contains an entire H-coset,
which spans16 dimensions. This is a contradiction.

For any rank-minimal factorization D=AB with1268 decoder columns in A,
im(A)=im(D), so each nonzero decoder column has target-support span>=9.
Its common target cap has codimension>=9. A private copy from FULL16 into
that cap must descend by at least9 ranks. Total descent is therefore
at least1268*9=11412, exceeding spinor full-center surplus
16384-576*16=7168.

Scope: rank-minimal1268-column factorizations implemented as full-frame
aggregates and private copies into common target caps. Nonminimal
factorizations, other bases with additional columns, fused or partial
schedules, mutable sources, and additional target motion are not ruled
out by this proof.

C. Fused Hadamard-clique / one-matching kernel
--------------------------------------------
For the compatible half-incidence used by the budget track's fused word,
each complementary8D packet pair U,U-perp contains16 clique halves on
each side. Each half comprises8 orthogonal lines spanning its side.
After collapsing the constant values on each half, the scalar block is

 T=[[I16,A],[A,I16]],  A=I16+J16.

A^2=I16 because J16^2=0 overF2. Thus im(T)={(z,Az)}, and a nonzero z gives
a nonzero Az. Every nonzero block image column contains an entire half
on each side. Its address support contains U and U-perp and spans16.
If a global column has multiple packet components, any nonzero component
already supplies that full span. Every nonzero global image column thus
has support span16.

The global kernel has rank1024. In any rank-minimal factorization, every
nonzero decoder column lies in its image, so all1024 center factors need
width16. The rank-minimal center bill is1024*16=16384, exactly N. This
kernel and the matching fusion therefore have no positive full-width
center surplus.

Scope: the specified fused kernel and rank-minimal scalar factorizations.
The compatible physical half-incidence and arbitrary-dirt replay are
established by the separate budget-track word. No lower bound for all
redundant factorizations or all possible circuits is asserted here.

Finite premises and checks
--------------------------
frame_bounds.py / FRAME-BOUNDS.json check the degree-six identity, exact
Hadamard Gram matrix, A^2=I, rank(T)=16, and im(T)={(z,Az)}. Normal Python
and -I -O both pass. The1268 convolution rank comes from the earlier
workbench/bit-routes/clique_residual.py receipt.
