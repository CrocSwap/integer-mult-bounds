Search scope and retained negative results

6,000 order proposals; 4,150 evaluated order mutations; 108 structured modes;
508 point-order proposals/settings; 300 finite-moment refinement proposals.
Additional exact role-count screens covered output parenthesizations, single
and multiple pair splits, shifted singleton/pair compositions, longer prefix
skips, balanced/chunked strip layouts and 512 hybrid strip masks.

The selected method keeps skip-prefix strips and changes recursive partitions
and pinned summand orders, followed by paid whole-chain clones. Longer skips,
balanced/chunked strip layouts and tested hybrids did not improve the baseline.
Exact-moment matching instead of the t*log(t) objective and direct profiling
of rank-one/two transitions did not improve PR53's complete histograms.
Five neighboring dimension pairs failed the exploratory screen against (23,25).
Alternative bases had tiny uncertified signals and were not incorporated.

No global optimality claim follows from this bounded search. Scalar graph and
physical certificate reconstruction, rather than the heuristic search, is the
acceptance criterion for the selected finite witness.
