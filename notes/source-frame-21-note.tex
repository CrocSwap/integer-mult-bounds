\begin{abstract}
The batched recursion of PR~\#10 makes a contiguous block of $t$ address
fields cost one recursive call of width $t$, so a role's rank is cheaper
when it is concentrated in one large edge. Auxiliary roles may start in any
fixed frame, provided they end in that frame plus the identity. Starting
every stage-two auxiliary role of the interchange network in the frame of
its first gate removes its rank-$(h^2-h)$ entrance edge and turns its exit
into one projector of rank $m-h$. The rank sum and deficit are unchanged,
but the interchange saving rises from $246/10^9$ to $154/10^8$. A third
whole-residual class in the complex network raises its saving to
$18/10^7$, so it does not bind. The conclusion is conditional on the
pinned upstream multiplication theorem and the retained interfaces of
PR~\#10 and its predecessors.
\end{abstract}

one-dimensional tapes. This note changes two finite choices in the
construction of PR~\#10 by icekylinx \cite{pr10}, pinned at commit

complex producer of Zhihao Chen's PR~\#7 \cite{pr7} unchanged. Sections 2
to 5 reproduce the batched framework of PR~\#10 verbatim. Section 6 records
its exact complex exponent and its $2^{-23}$ assembly, which the present
note supersedes. Sections 7 to 9 contain the new arguments. No scalar
circuit, label, producer or analytic interface changes. Only two choices
change: the source and sink matrices of the stage-two auxiliary roles, and
the set of complex residuals applied as whole children.

\section{Further levers}
Two further batchable classes are not used here. The stage-two data
entrances have rank $(h-1)^2=729>H/2$ in the fast factor $V$. Their
pivots, after the controlled basis, lie in one row block and one column
block, and they would give about $3.5\%$ more interchange saving. In the
complex network the same source frames apply, but they are not needed
because $\sigma<\tau$. The interchange denominator is now dominated by
the singleton climbs of the stage-one and stage-three auxiliary roles,
which are set by the side producer and its gate frames.
