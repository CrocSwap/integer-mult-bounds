$\dim A=h$, $\dim B=\dim C=h-1$, and $m=3h-2$.

Choose $d\notin S$. The orthogonal involution

its images of the other coordinate vectors give an explicit orthonormal

\[

For a standard nonalternating binary space of even dimension $2n$,
its characteristic vector $\Omega$ is isotropic and fixed by every
orthogonal map. Choose $e$ with $e\cdot\Omega=1$ and let
$U=\langle\Omega,e\rangle^\perp$; $U$ is a nondegenerate alternating
space of dimension $2n-2$. The induced quotient action is symplectic.
For each $S\in\operatorname{Sp}(U)$, all lifts are parametrized by
$u\in U,c\in\mathbb F_2$ through
\[
 \Omega\mapsto\Omega,\quad e\mapsto e+u+c\Omega,\quad
 v\mapsto Sv+(u\cdot Sv)\Omega\quad(v\in U).
\]
Checking the pairings verifies these are exactly the orthogonal lifts.
Counting symplectic bases then gives
\[
 |O(2n,2)|=2^{2n-1}|\operatorname{Sp}(2n-2,2)|,\qquad
 |G|=2^{69+1156}\prod_{i=1}^{34}(2^{2i}-1).
\]

$d$ satisfying $70^d>2\,68^d$ is 24, so the complex row coefficient is
$24\cdot2431=58344$. These full finite role counts are retained in the
scalar guard and row stock. Group elements and involutions are specified
