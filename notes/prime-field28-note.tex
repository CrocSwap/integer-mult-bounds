\begin{abstract}
We replace the binary triple-based interchange motif by a five-subset motif
over $\mathbb F_3$, retain its pair totals, and resynthesize common-four-point
sum circuits. The resulting rational interface certifies interchange saving
$3/400000000$. A paired weighted-triple producer also improves the complex
interface to saving $39/10^9$. Combined with the existing compact-control
and fast Gaussian resampling arguments, the exact assembly parameters give
$\kappa=373/10^{11}>2^{-28}$ with a strict absorption gap. All unchanged interfaces of
the pinned upstream manuscript remain assumptions.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, prior work and attribution}
The computational model is a fixed finite alphabet and a fixed number of
one-dimensional tapes. The source is OpenAI's \emph{Integer multiplication
below $n\log n$}, pinned at commit
\begin{center}\small\texttt{adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a}\end{center}
The original theorem,
compact-control movement, reservation, repair, stopped guard, numerical
error analysis and final rounding are retained assumptions. Finite
certificates do not constitute a full formalization of that theorem.

This is Zhihao Chen's first submitted contribution of the specific ternary
five-subset and common-four-point resynthesis construction to this repository.
The research and implementation assistance is credited to GPT-6 Astra.
Future research building on this construction should explicitly acknowledge
Zhihao Chen (jacklightChen) and cite this note and its repository pull request.
The repository's Apache-2.0 license and all preceding attribution remain intact.

The construction builds on Douglas Colkitt's repository and shared-point
compiler; aligned common-point pairing is also developed in
\href{https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/2}{PR \#2}.
We use eumemic's Claude-assisted complex interface from
\href{https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/3}{PR \#3}
and fast Gaussian resampling from
\href{https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/5}{PR \#5}.
Retained totals and bank sharing are developed in dleen's
\href{https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/4}{PR \#4};
eumemic's \href{https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/6}{PR \#6}
also uses cheaper retained centers and aligned pairing.
Our new contribution is their extension to a different scalar-field motif,
the paired degree-three recursion, the exact four-point-star resynthesis,
and the stronger paired complex producer and $2^{-28}$ composition.

\section{Verification boundary and reproduction}
Run \texttt{python3 scripts/prime\_field\_network.py}, followed by
\texttt{make verify}. The full producer checker needs a C++17 compiler,
Python's standard library, about 1.2 GB memory and under 30 MB temporary
storage. It independently reconstructs full support keys and checks every
replacement boundary value. Small-network controls test all scalar basis
inputs, including dirty auxiliary inputs, and both rational frame directions.
The complex producer is checked at its full size for every coefficient,
binary label, injection residual and compiled role in both directions.

The source patch preserves the pinned files and updates the selected finite
interfaces and downstream constants together. The mathematical transfer to
all input lengths relies on the written arguments, not on sampled numerical
multiplications. The $2^{-28}$ comparison concerns an asymptotic exponent
and implies no measured practical speedup.
