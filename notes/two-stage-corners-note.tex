\begin{abstract}
We refine the data-corner profile in Zhihao Chen's fixed $(55,53)$ two-stage construction, PR29. Exact rank-partition identities certify2265 augmented-minor zeros for contiguous blocks of widths51 and45. An exact normalized nonzero-pivot witness and the retained common-basis argument establish their simultaneous availability. The resulting conditional bound is
\[
T(n)=O\bigl(n(\log n)^{1-\kappa}\bigr),\qquad
\kappa=\frac{15878574}{10^{12}}=1.5878574\times10^{-5}>2^{-16}.
\]
This improves PR29's reported saving by about2.205\%. All topology, producers, frame endpoints, paid copy corrections and analytic mechanisms are inherited unchanged. Exact identity certificates supplement the written proof; the full inherited mathematical theorem is not formally verified.
\end{abstract}

\section{Exact polynomial-identity certificates}
For arbitrary $e$-row matrices $U,V$, diagonal$D$, and coefficient partition $E=X\sqcup Y$,
\[
\operatorname{rank}(UDV^T)\leq\operatorname{rank}(U_X)+\operatorname{rank}(V_Y).
\]
Split the product into its $X$ and$Y$ summands and bound the first by its left factor and the second by its right factor. If this sum is below$e$, its determinant vanishes for all diagonal weights. For an invertible selected prefix minor$P$, each next Schur entry is the corresponding augmented determinant divided by$\det P$, with row and column order retained.

The claimed rightmost-pivot sequence is
\[
\begin{gathered}
(0,106);\quad(i,i+54),\quad1\leq i\leq51;\\
(52,54),(53,53),(54,52),(55,51),(56,50),(57,49);\\
(i,i-54),\quad58\leq i\leq102;\\
(103,3),(104,2),(105,1),(106,0).
\end{gathered}
\]
Outside the two long runs each pivot uses the largest available column. For every still available column to the right of a claimed long-run pivot, the supplied certificate gives a partition with rank sum strictly below the augmented minor size. There are $51\cdot50/2+45\cdot44/2=2265$ such minors. Bits0--54 encode first-factor vertices, bits55--107 second-factor vertices and bit108 the constant. The prefix rows and columns follow the displayed order.

The search uses matroid intersection and an incidence-forest rank oracle to find partitions. The mathematical implication does not depend on that search: all4530 rank values are checked directly by integer elimination with gcd reduction. A separate verifier independently reconstructs the required minor set and verifies its exact coverage, both ranks and the strict deficiency. Thus these are finite exact polynomial-identity certificates for the fixed geometry, not numerical sampling of a conjectured zero pattern.

The search oracle itself has a simple proof. For a vertex-column subset$S$ of a forest incidence matrix, rank is $|S|$ minus the number of components wholly contained in$S$, including isolated vertices. Adding the constant column increases rank exactly when some component has omitted vertices on both bipartition sides. On a connected tree, solutions to $z_R+z_B=1$ have $z_R=t,z_B=1-t$; the omitted coordinates can all be zero precisely when they do not occur on both sides. The direct integer checks independently verify every oracle value used in a final certificate.
