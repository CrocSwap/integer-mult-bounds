\subsection{The selected paired-cube identity}
Let $h=2p$ and partition the binary address coordinates into $p$ pairs.
A port $S$ selects one coordinate from each of three distinct pairs;
thus $v=8\binom p3$. Its address $q_S=\chi_S$ has norm one.
Ports with the same three pair labels form an eight-port cube.
Define the rational scalar matrix
\[
 B_{T,S}=\frac{|T\cap S|-1}{2},\qquad
 B_{\rm block}=\bigoplus_{\text{cubes }I}B|_I,\qquad
 K=I-B_{\rm block},\quad H=B_{\rm block}-B.
\]
Consequently $K+H+B=I$. If distinct addresses are not orthogonal,
their intersection has size one, and $B_{T,S}=0$; also $B_{S,S}=1$.
Thus the side terms $K,H$ use only orthogonal source--target pairs.

Within a cube let $P$ be antipodal permutation and $A$ distance-one
adjacency. Directly from intersection sizes, $K=(P-A)/2$.
Its Walsh eigenvalue in degree $d$ is
$((-1)^d-(3-2d))/2$, namely $-1,-1,1,1$ for $d=0,1,2,3$.
Hence $K^2=I$; its two opposite-parity $4\times4$ blocks are signed
Hadamard matrices divided by two.

\subsection{A scalar producer with actual geometric supports}
For an input cube $I$ with selector $\epsilon$, form its total $F_I$,
its six half-cube sums $A_{I;i,a}$, and its twelve two-input edge sums
$E_{I;i,j;a,b}$. Use six signed channels
\[
 G_{I;i,j;0}=E_{I;i,j;0,0}-E_{I;i,j;1,1},\qquad
 G_{I;i,j;1}=E_{I;i,j;0,1}-E_{I;i,j;1,0}.
\]
These require 25 binary additions per cube before sharing: twelve for
$E$, six for $A$, six for $G$, and one for $F$.
For a distinct target cube $J$ with selector $\eta$, the contribution of
$I$ to $H$ is respectively
\[
 \frac12F_I\quad(|I\cap J|=0),\qquad
 \frac12A_{I;i,1-\eta_i}\quad(I\cap J=\{i\}),\qquad
 \frac{2\eta_i-1}{2}G_{I;i,j;\eta_i\oplus\eta_j}
 \quad(I\cap J=\{i,j\},\ i<j).
\]
Indeed the last case assigns $-1/2$ to two matching shared selectors,
$+1/2$ to two opposite selectors, and zero to mixed selectors.
The equal-cube contribution is zero. These formulas specify one
disjoint-triple module on $F$, $2p$ disjoint-pair modules on $A$, and
$2\binom p2$ all-but-one modules on $G$. The selected coarse modules
are restrictions of the retained PR117 circuits.

Every aggregate's conservative support consists of original source
ports orthogonal to every target in its broadcast group. Its physical
frame contains the span of that support and is contained in the common
target cap. Signed cancellations do not remove visited address directions.
Within each target cube use root groups in the order
\[
 F;\quad\text{face }0;\quad\text{face }1;\quad\text{edge }01;
 \quad\text{face }2;\quad\text{edge }02;\quad\text{edge }12.
\]
Their counts are $1,2,4,4,8,8,8$: a literal 35-call schedule.
The first group contains eight targets; the next fixes selector 0;
the next two fix selectors 0 and 1; the rest are singletons.
The target spans have dimensions $4,3,2,1$, since cube vectors have
form $q_{000}+\epsilon_0d_0+\epsilon_1d_1+\epsilon_2d_2$.
Their orthogonal caps form ascending chains, so every target pays
children $[h-4,1,1,1]$.

\subsection{Coordinate-star copied centers}
For a coordinate $i$ with partner $\bar i$, put
$S_i=\sum_{S\ni i}x_S$. Its binary source span is exactly
\[
 U_i=\{u:u_{\bar i}=0,\ \sum_{j\notin\{i,\bar i\}}u_j=0\},
 \qquad \dim U_i=h-2.
\]
To prove equality, the contributing vectors are $e_i+e_a+e_b$,
where $a,b$ belong to distinct other pairs. The graph of such outside
edges is connected, and for $p\ge4$ contains a triangle.
Summing its three generators gives $e_i$. Subtracting $e_i$ then
gives all edge vectors, whose span is the full outside even-parity space.
Each star is assembled from its half-cube channels, at frames contained
in $U_i$. The copied-center interface therefore charges
$\ell=h(h-2)$, including degenerate $U_i$.
Its exact scatter is
\[
 (Bx)_T=\frac12\sum_{i\in T}S_i-\frac16\sum_iS_i,
\]
because each source occurs in precisely three stars. The scatter and
copied-center branches retain their physical readouts and cleanup;
the scalar identity alone does not make their address frames free.

\subsection{The original-source implementation of $K$}
The four cube directions of either selector parity span a binary
three-space $U$: their sum is zero, and any three are independent.
Opposite-parity cube directions have even intersection size, so
$U\subseteq q_T^\perp$ for every opposite-parity target $T$.
Perform every $H$ and star source injection while the original sources
retain their rank-one entrance frames. Those injected carriers now
contain all source information needed by the forward producer.

Raise the four original sources of a parity class to the identical exact
representative $T_U$, paying rank two each. Apply the appropriate
Hadamard/2 block pointwise and assign its four outputs to their actual
opposite-parity target rows. After the $H+B$ readouts have completed,
raise each parked source to its assigned $q_T^\perp$, paying $h-4$,
and add it to that target at the identical representative. The target
now contains $(H+B+K)x=x$.
Raise every original source to the full active frame, paying rank one,
then invert both scalar blocks at that common full frame.
The sources recover their original logical values before the full-frame
inverse producer and source-subtraction cleanup. Their physical endpoint
is the retained $F_hT_{q_S}^{-1}$ endpoint. Thus each source pays precisely
$[2,h-4,1]$, and $K$ needs no additional auxiliary bank.
Scalar mixing is used only at actual common frames; it does not replace
the completed identity shear by a shear with matrix $K$.

\subsection{Arbitrary dirty contents and the frozen witness}
For the signed reversible carrier word $M$, injection $V$, decoder $J$,
and arbitrary dirty vector $z$, the literal sequence
\[
 y\gets y-JMz,\quad z\gets z+Vx,\quad z\gets Mz,\quad
 y\gets y+Jz,\quad z\gets M^{-1}z,\quad z\gets z-Vx
\]
restores $z$ and adds $JMVx$. The retained copied-center and deferred
old-value interface supplies its chronological realization.
A gate $a\gets a+\varepsilon b$ is undone by
$a\gets a-\varepsilon b$, in reverse gate order. More generally,
$a\gets c_a a+c_b b$ with $c_a,c_b\in\{1,-1\}$ has inverse
$a\gets c_a a-c_a c_b b$. Conservative dirty supports and exact
coefficients are retained separately.
Selected old-value reads follow an ascending subspace chain on every
target and occur while the corresponding role still has its original
dirty coordinate. All later inverses occur after the original sources
have been restored. Exact frame representatives and rank-zero phase
adapters are those of the arbitrary-subspace Clifford interface.

For the selected $p=12$ graph, 24,767 active additions and 7,724 root
uses, with 6,074 actual monotone carrier links, give
\[
 h=24,\quad v=1760,\quad R=26417,\quad\ell=528.
\]
The frozen chronological gauge witness selects 4,840 roles, each of
dimension 20. Its graph, matching/frame witness and gauge-selection file,
not an abstract circuit-rank bound, determine the paid internal histogram.
The selected source histogram is $[2,20,1]$ per port; the target histogram
is $[20,1,1,1]$ per port. Zero-width adapters remain paid scalar work.
