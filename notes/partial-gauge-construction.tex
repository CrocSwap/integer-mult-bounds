\subsection{Interval and pair-first cube producer}

$I(a,1)=v_a$ and $I(a,l)=I(a,l-1)+v_{a+l-1\bmod k}$ for $2\le l<k$.
Then $I(j+1,k-1)$ is the all-but-$j$ sum; add the omitted term once for
a total. Singleton and empty cases use the corresponding direct values.
Every addition has disjoint formal source supports.

For two binary choices $u,v\in\{0,1\}$, write the desired four outputs as
$F+A_u+B_v+D_{uv}$. Form $P_u=F+A_u$ and $Q_v=F+B_v$, then
\[
 Y_1=Q_0+A_0,\quad Y_2=P_0+B_1,\quad
 Y_3=P_1+D_{10},\quad Y_4=Q_1+D_{11},
\]

compatible retained-carrier links, with compatibility witnessed by the
