\subsection{The dimension-thirty-two bit network}
\label{sec:nested-bit-producer}

This construction retains the ternary five-subset geometry and transparent
scalar wrapper of Zhihao Chen's PR~\#7, the controlled and arbitrary-width
batching interfaces of icekylinx's PR~\#10, and the support resynthesis and
dimension checks of Rohan Arun's PRs~\#9 and~\#11. PR~\#12 records the
preceding dimension-thirty transfer of these batching interfaces. We use
PR~\#13's auxiliary source-frame translation. The additional entrance and
corner blocks and the nested simultaneous basis below are new refinements;
none of the preceding constructions is claimed as new here.

For the bit network take
\[
 h=32,\quad v=\binom{32}{5}=201376,\quad H=h^2=1024,
 \quad m_{\rm b}=h^3=32768.
\]
Give $\mathbb Q^h$ the nondegenerate form $I-(2/25)J$ and use the indicators
$t_S$ of all five-subsets. Then
$\langle t_S,t_T\rangle=|S\cap T|-2$ and
$\langle t_S,t_S\rangle=3$. The scalar identity
\[
 \binom j2-[j=2]=[j=5]\pmod3\qquad(0\le j\le5)
\]
provides the same retained-total and exclusion-output factorization as in
dimension 28. If a source-span vector has common pair $C$, its two common
coordinates equal $a$ and its coordinate sum is $5a$. Consequently
\[
 u^{\mathsf T}(I-(2/25)J)u=\sum_{i\notin C}u_i^2>0\qquad(u\ne0).
\]
If the outside coordinates vanish, the sum relation gives $3a=0$, proving
the strict assertion. Such source spans are nondegenerate, and every full
retained-total span has dimension $h-2=30$. The forward and complemented
reverse frame schedules, the restoration of arbitrary dirty auxiliaries,
and the negative-source correction therefore have the same proofs.

The complete support producer at $h=32$, including common-four-point star
resynthesis, has the checked counts
\[
 c=23210704,\qquad Q_{\rm out}=2014256,
 \qquad R=c+Q_{\rm out}=25224960.
\]
There are $35960$ stars and $561$ canonical boundary templates. Exact support
merging, pruning, disjoint additions, and independent boundary-sum checks
are supplied with the retained dimension checker. Every resynthesized
boundary stays within its original common-four-point star and hence within
its required common-pair source space. The selected templates preserve
the complete scalar maps and dirty-scratch restoration in both orientations.

The intersection-two neighbor bijection is checked on all $201376$
five-subsets. At this dimension the paired-point construction may use the
Euler tour on the complete graph on the fifteen remaining pair indices;
every degree is fourteen. The retained general matching implementation gives
the same required properties. Thus each shared stage-one/stage-three bank
join has nested labels, removes one role and exactly $m_{\rm b}$ rank units,
and adds no decreasing-frame loss. Put $N=v^3$ and
\[
 W=2v^2(v+R)=2062192473897500672,
 \qquad L=3v^2\binom h2(h-2)=1810254376304640,
\]
\[
 s=Wm_{\rm b}-N+2L=67573918438923423744000,
 \qquad \eta=\frac{Wm_{\rm b}-s}{Wm_{\rm b}}
             =\frac{3503}{52073136128}.
\]
Padding, if necessary, uses identity gates at the ordinary low and high
frames, so these upper role counts give literal finite networks.

\paragraph{Source-frame translation applies at this dimension.}
An auxiliary role is fixed by the scalar permutation. Assigning a rational
matrix $M$ to its source and $I+M$ to its sink preserves the endpoint
difference $I$. For an arbitrary physical stream $f$, interpret its logical
input as $\Phi_{-M}f$. Since the unchanged scalar network restores arbitrary
auxiliary values, its physical output is
$\Phi_{I+M}\Phi_{-M}f=\Phi_I f$. This interpretation requires no tape
operation. Common gate matrices and every interior edge remain unchanged.
This is precisely the source-frame freedom of PR~\#13, whose proof does
not depend on $h=28$.

At stage two take the source matrix $P_{D_0}$ and sink matrix $I+P_{D_0}$,
where $D_0=(P^\perp\otimes F)\otimes Q$ and
$D_1=(F\otimes F)\otimes Q$. The entrance residual is zero, while the
exit is $I+P_{D_0}-P_{D_1}=I-\Pi_{P\otimes F\otimes Q}$, of rank
$m_{\rm b}-h$. The old ranks $(h^2-h)+(m_{\rm b}-h^2)$ have the same sum.
In particular, no rank deficit or scalar improvement is inferred merely
from this change of source frames.

\subsection{A contiguous block at each stage-two data entrance}
\label{sec:nested-data-entrance}

Temporarily allow the outer controlled family
$S=T_3(I_{F_3}\otimes K)$ with arbitrary $K$ on
$V=F_1\otimes F_2$, $\dim V=H$. Coordinates are $(\gamma,i)$ with $i$
fastest and $T_3$ acts by independent $G_i\in\mathrm{GL}_h(\mathbb Q)$.
The subsequent nested-basis lemma proves compatibility when $K$ is restricted
further.

\begin{lemma}[Controlled data-entrance pivots]
\label{lem:nested-data-entrance-pivots}
Let $A=\Pi_t\otimes R_0$, where $\Pi_t=t\varphi$ is a rank-one rational
idempotent and $R_0$ is a rational idempotent of rank $H-r$, with
$0<r<H/2$. Suppose all $a_i=e_1^{\mathsf T}G_it$ and
$b_i=\varphi G_i^{-1}e_h$ are nonzero and the first-$r$ by last-$r$ corner
of $KR_0K^{-1}$ is nonsingular. Then $SAS^{-1}$ has a lower-lower pivot
profile with $r$ singletons and one contiguous block of $H-2r$ fields.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The first-$H$-row, last-$H$-column block is
$\operatorname{diag}(a_i)KR_0K^{-1}\operatorname{diag}(b_i)$.
Factor $I-KR_0K^{-1}=UV'$ with $V'U=I_r$. Its corner is $-U_LV'_{R}$,
where $L,R$ are the first and last $r$ indices, so both square factors are
invertible. The middle Schur complement is the identity on the middle
$H-2r$ multiplicity coordinates, with nonzero diagonal scalings $a_ib_i$.
The rightmost-pivot rule first uses the $r$ corner pivots. Its next pivots
have physical positions
\[
 (\text{row }i,\ \text{column }m_{\rm b}-H+i),
 \qquad r+1\le i\le H-r.
\]
The two intervals are contiguous and have the same order. Earlier alpha
blocks cannot intervene because the final alpha block still contains the
entire remaining rank. The displayed $H-r$ pivots exhaust the matrix rank.
Fixed diagonal normalizations give the asserted identity block and its
single recursive interchange.
\end{proof}

For the actual data wires, the incoming source frame
$(B\perp P)\otimes\langle t\rangle$ grows to
$(B\otimes F_2)\perp(P\otimes\langle t\rangle)$, or the incoming target
frame $B\otimes\langle t\rangle$ grows to $B\otimes F_2$; both include the
future line in $F_3$. The retained inverse middle-stage schedule has these
same low source-copy and target-injection boundaries. Both residuals have
form
\[
 \Pi_Q\otimes((I-\Pi_P)\otimes(I-\Pi_t)),
\]
with $r=2h-1$. Thus each of the $2N$ rank-$(h-1)^2$ edges admits
$2h-1$ singleton pivots and a block of $H-4h+2$ fields when the lemma's
conditions hold. Stage-two auxiliary entrances are zero after translation
and are not counted again here.

\subsection{A second contiguous block on the stage-three data entrance}
\label{sec:nested-a3-corner}

Retain the common controlled coordinate family
\(S=T(I_F\otimes K)\), where \(\dim F=h\), \(\dim V=H=h^2\),
\(m=hH\), and \(T\) acts by \(G_i\in\mathrm{GL}(F,\mathbb Q)\)
on multiplicity coordinate \(i\). Coordinates \((\alpha,i)\) are ordered
with \(i\) fastest. Assume \(h\ge3\). For the finite family of stage-three
data entrance projectors
\[
 A=(I_F-\Pi_t)\otimes(I_V-\Pi_q),\qquad
 \operatorname{rank}\Pi_t=\operatorname{rank}\Pi_q=1,
\]
put \(r=h-1\), \(R=H+r\), and \(\widetilde A=SAS^{-1}\).
The existing controlled-basis lemma supplies the nonsingularity of the
first-\(R\)-row, last-\(R\)-column corner. The following additional
conditions can be imposed on the very same parameters:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the first-\(H\)-row, last-\(H\)-column block \(B\) is nonsingular;
\item the first-\(r\)-row, last-\(r\)-column corner of \(B\) is nonsingular;
\item all diagonal factors specified below are nonzero.
\end{enumerate}
They are compatible also with any finitely many of the existing
nonzero-polynomial requirements for other edge classes, including the
controlled entrance conditions. No edge receives its own coordinate system.

\paragraph{Structure of the corner.}
Write \(\Pi_t=t\varphi\), \(\varphi(t)=1\),
\(K\Pi_qK^{-1}=q\psi\), \(\psi(q)=1\), and \(P=I_F-\Pi_t\).
Let
\[
 \ell_i=e_1^{\mathsf T}G_i,\quad v_i=G_i^{-1}e_h,\quad
 a_i=\ell_i(t),\quad b_i=\varphi(v_i).
\]
Since the first and last \(\alpha\) blocks are disjoint,
\[
 B_{ij}=(\delta_{ij}-q_i\psi_j)\ell_iPv_j
       =-\delta_{ij}a_i b_i-q_i\psi_j\ell_iPv_j.
\]
The second term has rank at most \(r=\dim\operatorname{im}P\).
Consequently \(B=D+UV\), where
\(D=-\operatorname{diag}(a_i b_i)\) and \(U,V\) have inner dimension \(r\).
The existing controlled stage-two sink conditions give nonzero
\(a_i b_i\) for their finite set of \(t\); any additional \(t\) can be
included by the same nonzero rational-function argument.

\paragraph{A rational witness for the additional determinants.}
For each one projector, choose coordinates in \(F\) with
\(t=e_0\), \(\varphi=e_0^{\mathsf T}\), and choose \(K\) so that
\(q=\mathbf1\), \(\psi=\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}/H\).
These choices are possible by rational similarity of rank-one idempotents.
Color the \(H\) multiplicity indices by \(c(i)\in\{1,\ldots,r\}\),
with the first \(r\) and last \(r\) indices each containing every color
once. Fill the middle indices arbitrarily. Every color then occurs at
least twice, and, because \(r\ge2\), strictly fewer than \(H\) times.
Choose \(G_i\) so that
\[
 \ell_i=e_0^{\mathsf T}-e_{c(i)}^{\mathsf T},\qquad
 v_i=e_0+e_{c(i)}.
\]
This is possible: use the displayed \(\ell_i\) as first row, the
coordinate rows \(e_j^{\mathsf T}\) with \(j\ne0,c(i)\) as middle
rows, and \(e_{c(i)}^{\mathsf T}\) as last row. These rows are independent
and send \(v_i\) to \(e_h\). Thus \(a_i=b_i=1\), and
\[
 B=-I_H+\frac1H\bigl[\mathbf1_{c(i)=c(j)}\bigr]_{i,j}.
\]
On a color class of size \(n_c<H\), its eigenvalues are \(-1\) and
\(-1+n_c/H\), hence it is nonsingular over \(\mathbb Q\).
The first-\(r\)-row, last-\(r\)-column corner is \(1/H\) times a
permutation matrix and is also nonsingular.

This witness shows that both determinant rational functions are not
identically zero in the common parameters \(K,G_i\). Clear inverse
matrix denominators and multiply their numerators over the finite family,
together with the existing determinants and all matrix invertibility
conditions. The product is a nonzero rational polynomial. Some rational
parameter tuple satisfies every requirement simultaneously. In particular,
the witness above need not itself satisfy the previously imposed corner
conditions. Effective enumeration of rational tuples terminates as in the
original simultaneous-basis argument.

\paragraph{The pivot profile inside \(B\).}
The following elementary fact does not require \(B\) to be a projector.
If \(B=D+UV\) is nonsingular, \(D\) is invertible diagonal, and its
first-\(r\)-row, last-\(r\)-column corner is nonsingular, split its
coordinates into \(L,M,R'\) of sizes \(r,H-2r,r\).
Then \(B_{L,R'}=U_LV_{R'}\) is nonsingular, so both factors are
invertible. Its middle Schur complement is exactly
\[
 B_{M,M}-B_{M,R'}B_{L,R'}^{-1}B_{L,M}=D_M.
\]
Lower-lower elimination in physical order therefore uses \(r\) corner
pivots, then \(H-2r\) diagonal pivots in matching consecutive row and
column intervals. The remaining \(r\)-square block is nonsingular
because \(B\) was nonsingular, and needs at most \(r\) singleton pivots.
Fixed diagonal normalizations make the middle block the identity.

\paragraph{Embedding the profile in the full edge.}
For the first \(H\) rows of \(\widetilde A\), all columns outside the
last \(\alpha\) block precede those of \(B\). Since \(B\) is nonsingular,
the rightmost-pivot rule consumes exactly the pivots just described inside
that last block. In particular, the middle \(H-2r\) pivots map
\[
 (\alpha=1,\ i=r+1,\ldots,H-r)
 \quad\hbox{to}\quad
 (\alpha=h,\ i=r+1,\ldots,H-r)
\]
in the same order. These are two contiguous physical intervals.
The existing nonsingularity of the first-\(R\)-row, last-\(R\)-column
corner then supplies the next \(r\) singleton pivots. All these
\(R\) corner pivots lie within the original large-projector corner.
The original idempotent Schur-complement lemma subsequently supplies
its unchanged contiguous middle block of size \(m-2R\).
The total pivot rank is
\[
 3r+(H-2r)+(m-2R)=m-R=(h-1)(H-1),
\]
so no further pivots remain. The two identity blocks use one child each,
with widths \((H-2r)b\) and \((m-2R)b\), and all \(3r\) other pivots
use width \(b\). Triangular factors and diagonal normalizations have
only the already allowed fixed linear-scan cost.

At $h=32$, the resulting profile is $93$ singleton children and
contiguous blocks of $962$ and $30658$ fields. Scalar multiples and
transposed projectors are included in the common finite family; no
symmetry hypothesis is needed.

\subsection{A nested controlled basis for the reframed exit corners}
\label{sec:nested-source-basis}

This refinement uses the auxiliary source-frame choice of PR~\#13 and
retains the dimension-thirty-two producer just specified and every interior
gate frame. In particular,
a stage-two auxiliary source has matrix $P_{D_0}$, its sink has matrix
$I+P_{D_0}$, its entrance edge is zero, and its exit is
\[
 A_4=I-\Pi_{P\otimes F_2\otimes Q},
 \qquad \dim P=\dim Q=1.
\]
The rank of $A_4$ is $m-h$, where $m=h^3$. The endpoint difference is
still $I$, so this choice preserves the transfer identity on arbitrary
dirty auxiliary streams. The new contribution below changes only the
simultaneous rational basis and the pivot grouping of these exit edges.

Put $F_1,F_2,F_3=\mathbb Q^h$, $V=F_1\otimes F_2$, and $H=h^2$.
Assume $h\ge4$. Order the full coordinates as $(\gamma,\alpha,\beta)$,
with $\beta$ fastest. Thus $F_3$ is the outer factor and $V$ the inner
multiplicity space. Restrict the former controlled family to
\[
 K=T_2(I_{F_1}\otimes K_0),\qquad
 S=T_3(I_{F_3}\otimes K),
\]
where $K_0\in\mathrm{GL}(F_2,\mathbb Q)$,
$T_2(x\otimes e_\beta)=J_\beta x\otimes e_\beta$ with independent
$J_\beta\in\mathrm{GL}(F_1,\mathbb Q)$, and $T_3$ acts by independent
$G_i\in\mathrm{GL}(F_3,\mathbb Q)$ at every inner coordinate
$i=(\alpha,\beta)$. All projectors and both orientations use this one
fixed $S$; there is no edge-dependent rational coordinate change.

\begin{lemma}[Simultaneous nested controlled basis]
\label{lem:nested-source-basis}
For the finite tensor-projector family of the retained motif, one rational
choice of $K_0,J_\beta,G_i$ has all the following properties.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The first-$2h$-row, last-$2h$-column corner of every shared-bank
      projector $A_1$ is nonsingular after conjugation by $S$.
\item The first-$(H+h-1)$-row, last-$(H+h-1)$-column corner of every
      stage-three data entrance $A_3$ is nonsingular.
\item Every reframed stage-two auxiliary exit $A_4$ has a nonsingular
      \emph{diagonal} first-$h$-row, last-$h$-column corner.
\item Each stage-two data entrance retains the controlled-entrance
      profile with $2h-1$ corner pivots and a consecutive middle block
      of size $H-4h+2$.
\item Every $A_3$ additionally has the corner refinement with
      $3(h-1)$ singleton pivots and consecutive blocks of sizes
      $H-2h+2$ and $m-2(H+h-1)$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
All required nonzero conditions will be rational functions on the one
parameter domain displayed above. We prove individually that their
numerators are nonzero. Their witnesses are not required to coincide.
At the end a finite product supplies one simultaneous rational choice.

\paragraph{The structural diagonal corner of $A_4$.}
Write $\Pi_Q=t\varphi$ on $F_3$ and $\Pi_P=p\xi$ on $F_1$.
The factor $I\otimes K_0$ commutes with $\Pi_P\otimes I_{F_2}$, so
\[
 K(\Pi_P\otimes I)K^{-1}
\]
is block diagonal in the multiplicity coordinate $\beta$, with block
$J_\beta\Pi_PJ_\beta^{-1}$. Its first-$h$ by last-$h$ corner is
therefore diagonal, with entry
$[J_\beta\Pi_PJ_\beta^{-1}]_{1,h}$ at position $\beta$.
The corresponding corner of the full nullspace projector after $S$ is
this diagonal block multiplied on the left and right by diagonal factors
\[
 (e_1^{\mathsf T}G_{(1,\beta)}t),\qquad
 (\varphi G_{(h,\beta)}^{-1}e_h).
\]
Every one of these factors is a nonzero rational function of its matrix
parameter. Likewise,
$[J_\beta\Pi_PJ_\beta^{-1}]_{1,h}
 =(e_1^{\mathsf T}J_\beta p)(\xi J_\beta^{-1}e_h)$
is not identically zero. Requiring the product entries nonzero makes
this corner nonsingular. The corner of $A_4$ is its negative, since the
first and last coordinate sets are disjoint. The diagonal structure holds
identically throughout the restricted parameter family.

\paragraph{The shared-bank class $A_1$.}
Its nullspace is
\[
 (F_3\otimes\langle q\rangle)\mathbin\oplus
   (\langle t\rangle\otimes U),
 \qquad q=p\otimes v,\quad U=F_1\otimes\langle u\rangle,
\]
where $p,t$ are nonzero and $u,v$ are independent. The retained bank
matching gives orthogonal distinct nondegenerate second-factor lines,
which in particular supplies this independence.
We first show that $K(U+\langle q\rangle)$ can restrict with rank $h+1$
to its first $2h$ coordinates within the nested family.
Choose $K_0u=(1,\ldots,1)$ and $K_0v=(1,2,\ldots,h)$; an invertible
rational $K_0$ can do so because $u,v$ are independent. Choose a functional
$\ell_0$ with $\ell_0(p)=1$ and a basis
$e_1^*,\ldots,e_{h-1}^*$ of the annihilator of $p$.
The first $2h$ coordinates consist of the first two rows
$\ell_\beta,f_\beta$ of each $J_\beta$. On a vector with parameters
$(x,c)\in F_1\oplus\mathbb Q$ their values are
\[
 \ell_\beta(x+\beta c p),\qquad f_\beta(x+\beta c p).
\]
For $\beta=1,2$ set $(\ell_\beta,f_\beta)=(\ell_0,e_1^*)$;
for $3\le\beta\le h$ set
$(\ell_\beta,f_\beta)=(e_{\beta-1}^*,\ell_0)$.
Each prescribed pair is independent and extends to an invertible
$J_\beta$. If all evaluations vanish, the first rows for $\beta=1,2$
give $\ell_0(x)+c=\ell_0(x)+2c=0$, hence $c=\ell_0(x)=0$.
The remaining displayed rows give every annihilator coordinate of $x$
equal to zero, so $x=0$. This proves the claimed restriction rank.

For each of the same $2h$ coordinates, the requirement $(Kq)_i\ne0$
is separately a nonzero rational-function condition: the appropriate
row of $J_\beta$ can have nonzero value on $p$, and
$(K_0v)_\beta$ can be nonzero. The rank minor just constructed and these
coordinate conditions therefore hold simultaneously on a nonempty
rational open set. Choose an $(h+1)$-row subset $J$ of these $2h$ rows
on which $[Kq\ \ KB_U]$ is nonsingular, where $B_U$ is any basis matrix
of $U$. Its complement $I$ has $h-1$ rows and $(Kq)_i\ne0$ on $I$.
For $i\in I$ give the first row of $G_i$ the successive members of a
basis of the annihilator of $t$. They determine the outer $F_3$ variable
modulo $\langle t\rangle$. For $i\in J$ use one fixed row functional
with value one on $t$. After the quotient coordinates are eliminated,
these rows give precisely the nonsingular $(h+1)$-matrix on $J$.
Every prescribed row is nonzero and extends to $G_i$. Thus the
first-$2h$ restriction of the transformed nullspace is nonsingular.

The dual-last restriction follows within the same parameter family:
\[
 K^{-\mathsf T}=T_2^{-\mathsf T}(I\otimes K_0^{-\mathsf T}),\qquad
 S^{-\mathsf T}=T_3^{-\mathsf T}(I\otimes K^{-\mathsf T}).
\]
The form-dual spaces still have product-line and full-space-tensor-line
shape, with independent second-factor lines. Prescribing last rows in
place of first rows repeats the argument. Both square restrictions can
therefore be required nonsingular. Factoring the rank-$2h$ nullspace
projector as $UV'$ shows that its first/last corner is their product,
so the corresponding corner of $A_1$ is nonsingular.

\paragraph{The main $A_3$ corner.}
The original controlled restriction proof for
$A_3=(I-\Pi_t)\otimes(I-\Pi_q)$ uses only nonzero specified coordinates
of $q'=Kq$, besides freely chosen rows of the outer $G_i$.
Each specified coordinate remains a nonzero rational function in the
nested family, since $q$ is a product line and the corresponding rows of
$K_0,J_\beta$ can be chosen nonvanishing. Thus the same first and dual-last
restriction proof remains valid. It supplies the first-$(H+h-1)$ by
last-$(H+h-1)$ corner condition.

\paragraph{Stage-two data entrances.}
Their inner residual is
\[
 R=(I_{F_1}-\Pi_p)\otimes(I_{F_2}-\Pi_v),
 \qquad \operatorname{rank}R=(h-1)^2=H-(2h-1).
\]
At this smaller dimension the same controlled $A_3$ restriction argument
uses $K=T_2(I\otimes K_0)$, with multiplicity dimension $h$ and extra
nullspace dimension $h-1$. It requires only nonzero specified coordinates
of $K_0v$ and independently chosen rows of $J_\beta$. It therefore
supplies a nonsingular first-$(2h-1)$ by last-$(2h-1)$ corner of $KRK^{-1}$.
The two sets are disjoint because $h\ge4$. Nonzero outer diagonal factors
then give the controlled-entrance profile, with $2h-1$ singleton pivots
and $H-2(2h-1)=H-4h+2$ matching consecutive pivots.

\paragraph{The additional $A_3$ corner block.}
Let $r=h-1$. The first-$H$ by last-$H$ block of the outer $A_3$ is
$B=D+UV'$ with $D$ an invertible diagonal matrix and
$\operatorname{rank}(UV')\le r$. The earlier corner-refinement proof
requires $B$ and its first-$r$ by last-$r$ corner nonsingular.
Its rational color-block witness remains available in the nested family.
Indeed the actual $q$ is a product line. Choose $K_0$ to send its
second-factor rank-one projector to $\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}/h$,
and choose every $J_\beta$ to send its first-factor rank-one projector
to $\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}/h$. Then
$K\Pi_qK^{-1}=\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}/H$ exactly.
The independent outer $G_i$ may now be chosen as in that witness, giving
\[
 B=-I_H+\frac1H[\mathbf1_{c(i)=c(j)}]_{i,j},
\]
with each of the first and last $r$ indices containing every color once.
Each color occupies strictly fewer than $H$ positions, so $B$ is
nonsingular; the small corner is a permutation matrix divided by $H$.
Consequently both additional determinants are nonzero rational functions
in this restricted family as well. Their lower-lower profile is
$3r$ singleton pivots plus contiguous blocks of sizes $H-2r$ and
$m-2(H+r)$, as previously proved.

\paragraph{Simultaneous choice.}
Include every actual projector and its required signed or dual orientation
in the finite list. Transposition preserves the displayed product shapes,
and inverse transpose preserves the nested parameter family. Clear
matrix-inverse denominators in all the nonzero rational functions above,
and include determinants of $K_0,J_\beta,G_i$. Their finite product is
nonzero over $\mathbb Q$, hence some rational tuple makes every factor
nonzero. Enumeration of rational tuples with exact finite determinant
tests terminates. The address prime is chosen only after all rational
constants and triangular factorizations. No small-coefficient or efficient
search assertion is required by the fixed-machine preparation interface.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The improved reframed-exit compiler.}
For every $A_4$ the $h$ corner pivots now lie in the same order in two
contiguous intervals: the first $h$ rows and last $h$ columns. Fixed
nonzero diagonal normalizations turn them into one identity block.
The large-projector Schur-complement lemma leaves the unchanged middle
identity block of size $m-2h$. The rank is $h+(m-2h)=m-h$, so these are
all pivots. One width-$hb$ child and one width-$(m-2h)b$ child replace
$h$ width-$b$ singleton children and the old middle child. Both are strict
contractions. All triangular operations and wrappers retain fixed linear
scan cost, with no gathering or digit reversal.

All endpoint identities, common gate matrices, exact scalar maps, dirty
auxiliary restoration and physical edge ranks remain those of the
source-frame network. Only the proven recursive child list changes.
The exact bit moment, independent complex interface, path precision guard
and final multiplication inequalities must still be certified separately.
