\subsection{Exact lower-triangular conjugation}
For a rational projector $P$, define
\[
 D_P=\begin{pmatrix}I-P&P\\P&I-P\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Then $D_P^2=I$ and $\operatorname{rank}(D_P-I)=\operatorname{rank}P$.
For nondegenerate $U\subseteq V$, their orthogonal projectors satisfy
$P_UP_V=P_VP_U=P_U$, whence
\[
 D_{P_V}D_{P_U}=D_{P_V-P_U}.
\]
Thus either orientation of a nested frame edge costs its absolute dimension
change. A scalar gate whose incident roles share one frame commutes with
that frame's common address permutation.

Write the ordered lower-lower elimination as $P=L\Pi R$, where $L,R$ are
invertible lower triangular and $\Pi$ is a partial permutation. Put
\[
 C=RL,\quad Q_X=\Pi\Pi^t,\quad Q_Y=\Pi^t\Pi,\quad
 K=Q_YC-CQ_X,\qquad
 G=\begin{pmatrix}L&0\\0&R^{-1}\end{pmatrix}
   \begin{pmatrix}I&0\\K&I\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Idempotence gives $\Pi C\Pi=\Pi$. Multiplication, using
$\Pi Q_Y=\Pi=Q_X\Pi$, yields the exact identity
\[
 G^{-1}D_PG=
 \begin{pmatrix}I-Q_X&\Pi\\\Pi^t&I-Q_Y\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Both $G$ and $G^{-1}$ are lower triangular in the physical order of the
two banks. The displayed matrix is exactly the collection of cross-bank
swaps specified by $\Pi$. A contiguous identity block of $t$ pivots is
therefore one width-$t$ interchange, surrounded by the retained $O(V)$
ordered-affine passes. No reversal or gathering of separated pivots is
assumed. Choose the address prime only after fixing all rational bases
and factorizations, excluding their finitely many bad denominators and
nonzero minors.

\subsection{Scalar map and dirty-scratch wrapper}
For $h\in\{25,23,57\}$ write $v_h=\binom h3$. There is one input $X_T$
for each triple. In common-point context $i$ put $y_{ab}=X_{\{i,a,b\}}$
and compute
\[
 A_i=\sum_{a<b,\ a,b\ne i}y_{ab},\qquad
 D_{i,\{a,b\}}=\sum_{u<v,\ u,v\notin\{i,a,b\}}y_{uv}.
\]
The $q_h=3v_h+h$ designated outputs retain all totals. The local
cancellation-free producer pairs consecutive vertices, sums coarse edges
between pairs, and puts internal edges into the corresponding coarse
vertex weights. Recurse on these weighted vertices down to at most two
vertices; leave-one and leave-two group sums, together with the surviving
strips, partition each desired exclusion sum. This proves correctness by
induction. The order in context $i$ lists intact global pairs first, then
the unlisted vertices other than $i$. Intern equal supports; pair-star
supports shared across contexts are identified by their actual common
mask and union mask. Retain total ancestors and prune unused nodes.

Let $V$ copy physical inputs into source-use roles, $L$ be the reversible
addition/fanout circuit, and $J$ inject the designated outputs. For target
$T$, inject the three side outputs indexed by $i\in T$ and the three
totals $A_i$, $i\in T$. If a source triple is $S$, the first coefficient
is $[|S\cap T|=1]$, and the second is $|S\cap T|\bmod2$.
Their sum in $\mathbb F_2$ equals $[S=T]$. Hence $JLV=I$.
For arbitrary initial auxiliary vector $z$, the chronological schedule
\[
 L,\ J,\ L^{-1},\ V,\ L,\ J,\ L^{-1},\ V
\]
injects $JLz+JL(z+Vx)=x$ and restores $z$. This description treats $J,V$
as elementary copy/injection sequences, which are self-inverse over
$\mathbb F_2$. Three alternating data shears exchange the two data banks;
use the inverse schedule for the middle invocation. Scratch is restored
for every initial value, not only zero.

\subsection{Positive labels preserving carrier dependencies}
Use $H_h=I-J/9$ on $\mathbb Q^h$ and triple indicator $t_T$. Then
\[
 \langle t_S,t_T\rangle=|S\cap T|-1,\qquad
 U_i=\{x:\textstyle\sum_jx_j=3x_i\},\qquad
 x^tH_hx=\sum_{j\ne i}x_j^2\quad(x\in U_i).
\]
Thus $U_i$ is positive definite, each triple line has norm $2$, and
intersection-one triple lines are orthogonal. A side output receives
$U_i\cap\{x_a+x_b=0\}$, of dimension $h-2$; a total receives $U_i$,
of dimension $h-1$.

For an original cancellation-free node with common set $C$ and union
$M$, its envelope is
\[
 E(C,M)=\{x:\operatorname{supp}x\subseteq M,\ x_i=t\ (i\in C),\
                    \textstyle\sum_jx_j=3t\}.
\]
Identify addition nodes having equal envelopes for label propagation,
keeping sources separate. Find the initial carrier matching using these
envelopes and add its continuation arcs as label-only dependencies.
They respect envelope inclusion and strict event order. Backward
intersection of all reachable output constraints gives
\[
 W_g=\{x:\textstyle\sum_jx_j=3x_a\ (a\in F_g),\
                    x_b+x_c=0\ (\{b,c\}\in E(G_g))\}.
\]
Here $F_g$ lists reachable common points and $G_g$ their excluded-pair
graph. Sources keep their triple lines. We have $E_g\subseteq W_g$ and
$W_g\subseteq W_{g'}$ along every augmented arc. Every retained node
reaches an output, so $W_g$ lies in a positive $U_a$. Nondegeneracy,
nesting, retained matches, and side-output orthogonality follow.

For addition nodes $|F_g|\in\{1,2\}$ and $G_g$ avoids $F_g$.
A nonbipartite component forces zero; each bipartite component, including
isolated vertices, supplies a signed parameter $z_j$. If its color
classes are $A_j,B_j$, the remaining relation is
\[
 (3-|F_g|)t=\sum_j(|A_j|-|B_j|)z_j.
\]
Consequently $\dim W_g$ is the number of bipartite components on the
coordinates outside $F_g$. Signed component relations also give an exact
rational containment test. Orthogonal complements and tensor products
of these nondegenerate spaces are nondegenerate; ambient $H_h$ is
nondegenerate since $h\ne9$. Reversed invocations use orthogonal
complement frames, so reverse dependencies remain nested.

\subsection{Carrier matching and its rank histogram}

use or designated output use. A donor can preserve either operand as
its nonpivot and continue that operand's carrier to a later use $w$
when $W_u\subseteq W_w$. A matching joins carrier paths without cycles
or simultaneous identification of occupied roles. Every value retains
an unmatched first use, which supplies its carrier path. Thus
\[
 R_h=c_h+q_h-|\mathcal M_h|.
\]
Use events ordered by new dimension, old envelope dimension, and original
node/output number. Deterministic Hopcroft--Karp orders donors by node
number, first operand before second, and uses by consumer then output
order, in both breadth-first and shortest augmenting-path searches.
Initial matching arcs were propagated before enlargement; they remain
valid with this event order.

\subsection{Endpoint and asymmetric bank accounting}
Use source frame $D_{P_U}$ and destination frame $D_{P_{U^\perp}}$.
Their product is $D_I$, the full address interchange. Together with the
scalar bank exchange and arbitrary scratch restoration, this is the
retained all-role endpoint identity. In particular the extra source
rank charge of additive-shear framing is absent.

Put $N=v_{25}v_{23}v_{57}$ and
\[
 B_1=N R_{25}/v_{25},\quad B_2=N R_{23}/v_{23},\quad
 B_3=N R_{57}/v_{57},\quad E=B_3-B_1,\quad
 W=2N+B_2+B_3.
\]
Match all real stage-one roles to a subset of stage-three roles using
an intersection-one neighbor permutation of middle-axis triples.
The intersection-one graph has a regular bipartite double cover of
positive degree; edge counting proves Hall's condition, hence such a
permutation exists. Orthogonality makes each stage-one terminal
25-space a subspace of the matched stage-three entry $(m-57)$-space.

stage-three roles enters directly with rank $m-57$. No extra scalar
role is introduced. With $m=25\cdot23\cdot57=32775$,

 L=N\left(\frac6{23}+\frac6{21}+\frac6{55}\right),\qquad
 s=Wm-2N+2L=190768577823254200,
