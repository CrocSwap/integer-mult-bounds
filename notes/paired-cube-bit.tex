\subsection{Selected inherited bit gauges}
The bit supplier uses a hash-pinned word at $h=21$, with $v=1330$ data
ports and $R=21526$ dirty roles, in PR97's frozen format and read by PR97's
unchanged reader.  Its compressed witness, literal-ledger receipt, producer
and gauge selection are under \texttt{references/paired-cube/fib-bit-h21/}.
The selection retains $7575$
of the $8910$ entrance gauges, in their original readout order,
and omits $1335$.  This selects actual inherited frames, rather than
optimizing an unattached multiset of ranks.

For an omitted role with entrance frame $\sigma_s$ of dimension $f_s$,
perform its old-value correction at the common initial zero frame,
before any positive-rank operation.  Its exact scalar coefficients are
the coefficients of the original word's adjoint, including copied-center
readouts.  Both ends of each moved dirty read have frame zero, so arbitrary
dirty inputs are allowed.  Its subsequent first-use frame is unchanged.
All ordinary scalar operations, positive-rank $V$ injections, source-data
paths, copied centers and later role frames retain their pinned values.
Retained dirty readouts form the original subsequence.  Deleting members
of a certified nested target chain preserves actual subspace nesting;
dimension monotonicity alone would not establish that assertion.

If the original first-use dimension of an omitted role is $r_s$, its
first auxiliary transition changes from $r_s-f_s$ to $r_s$, and its
shared-core exterior child of width $3f_s$ disappears.  Target chains
retain precisely the positive integer adjoint supports of the chosen
readouts.  This is the inherited conservative support convention; an even
coefficient would merely give a zero bit read with a retained frame.
The source histogram and all $21$ copied-center transitions of width $20$
are unchanged.  The selected gauge dimensions have multiplicities
\[

\]

auxiliary, source, target and copied-center transition, one exterior child

\[

\]
Its largest child is $60$. The strict supplier moment below also charges
