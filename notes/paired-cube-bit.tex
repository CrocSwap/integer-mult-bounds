\subsection{Paired-cube bit word}
The bit supplier is a bit-side analog of the paired-cube decoder at $p=12$
($h=24$, $v=1760$, $R=20052$), in \path{research/paired-cube-bit}. Its
pair-disjoint module (442 additions) is annealed, its all-but-one module
is the nested prefix of the complex word at $n=10$, and every cube merges its
face-1 and edge-01 outputs into $u(b_0,b_1)$ and its face-2 and edge-02
outputs into $w(b_0,b_2)$, each read by two single roots (variant u). Its
local channels use the L1 association of the complex word: the $G$ sums with
keys $(0,1,0)$, $(0,1,1)$ and $(1,2,1)$ add the two long face diagonals, and
the other sums add two edges as in PR144. Ports are
transversal triples of coordinate pairs; the mod-2 decoder is
\[
 x_T=\sum_{c\in T}\mathrm{star}(c)+\sum_{|S\cap T|=1}x_S ,
\]
with point stars of span $h-2$ (centre loss $\ell=h(h-2)=528$), the face and
edge channels of PR144's pair and all-but-one modules, and partner-pair sums
mixed on the source registers. \path{research/paired-cube-bit/LEMMA.md} proves
the mixing step: the carrier and passive chains $[1,h-4,2]$ and $[1,h-2]$
are nested, every XOR happens at one common frame, the delivery happens at the
receivers' level-2 cap, and the mix completes inside its core, so it is
sequential rather than lockstep. The word is compiled with PR144's ledger on
rational frames, with 3960 chronological gauges: 2200 of dimension 20 and
1760 of dimension 21.

The physical word (\path{references/paired-cube/bit-physical}) adds late
copies, 3,762 moved operation frames and 1,760 compensated late reads: each
recipient is a gauged role in a dead donor's slot, read immediately before
its first operation (PR124, PR143). \path{scripts/paired_cube_bit_physical.py}
checks register contents, frames, spliced role chains, pair chronology,
read-order target chains and the partner chronology, recounts the ledger and
replays the aliased word over F2 with three controls.
In the cross-stage orthogonal sharing architecture, $m=3h=72$ and
$W=2v+R-1760=21812$ per cover vertex, and
\[
 \sum_r r C_r=1568528=Wm-1936,\qquad 1936=2v-3\ell.
\]
Its largest child is $60$. The strict supplier moment below also charges the
retained rare-class fallback and stopped atom wrapper.

The standalone checker \path{research/paired-cube-bit/check_paired_cube_bit.py},
run by \path{scripts/paired_cube_network.py}, recomputes the exact frames, the
decoder identity, every role and target chain, the partner-pair chronology,
$G$-nondegeneracy, a literal F2 replay with arbitrary dirty scratch and the
ledger. The generator regenerates every output byte for byte, and five
mutation controls are rejected.
