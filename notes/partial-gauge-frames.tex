\subsection{Backward enlargement with nondegenerate repair}
Work in $H=\mathbb F_2^{24}$ with its standard bilinear form.
Include scalar dependencies and retained-carrier edges in the acyclic
forward dependency graph. Impose the side output hyperplanes $t_T^\perp$
and the retained-center coordinate hyperplanes as output constraints.
For a vertex with old nondegenerate frame $E$, intersect these constraints
with its already chosen successor frames to obtain $L$. The original
containments and backward induction give $E\subseteq L$.

\begin{lemma}
There is a maximal nondegenerate subspace $\widehat E\subseteq L$
containing $E$. Choosing such spaces in reverse topological order preserves
all dependency containments.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Nondegeneracy of $E$ gives
$L=E\mathbin{\perp}(L\cap E^\perp)$ by orthogonal projection.
On the second summand repeatedly split off a vector of norm one, or, if
all norms vanish, a pair $u,v$ with $\langle u,v\rangle=1$.
The corresponding Gram blocks are $(1)$ and
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$.
After orthogonal elimination the remainder is radical. Discarding it
leaves a nondegenerate space of the maximum possible dimension, containing
$E$. Each chosen frame lies in the already chosen successor frames by its
definition. This proves nesting, including the carrier edges.
\end{proof}
The supplied old bases are orthonormal, so projection is explicitly
$x\mapsto x+\sum_{u\in\mathcal B_E}\langle x,u\rangle u$.
New alternating blocks are permitted: the retained general quadratic
Gauss compiler implements every nondegenerate residual of rank $r$ by one
$C^{\otimes r}$ child, fixed input/output binary maps and unit phases.
No orthonormal-basis claim is made for the enlarged frames.

\subsection{Dirty readouts and partial first-frame gauges}
Let $M$ be the literal invertible scalar addition word on the $R$ roles,
$V$ the source injection, $J$ the rational designated decoder, and $C=JM$.
For arbitrary dirty contents $z$, the sequence
\[
 y\leftarrow y-Cz,\quad z\leftarrow z+Vx,\quad z\leftarrow Mz,
 \quad y\leftarrow y+Jz,\quad z\leftarrow M^{-1}z,
 \quad z\leftarrow z-Vx
\]
adds $JMVx$ to $y$ and restores $z$. These are signed operations over
$\mathbb Q(i)$. Take the predecessor closure, under per-slot operation
precedence, of the final operations producing retained centers. Execute
this phase first, then their positive copied readouts. A role outside both
the injected sources and the phase's touched slots still holds its
original dirty coordinate, so its negative column readout can be delayed.

Propagating unions of designated target sets backwards through $M$ gives
a superset of each column's support in $JM$. An eligible untouched role
cannot influence a retained-center output: such an influence would place
its first operation in the center predecessor closure. Thus every target
in its propagated support is a side output. Dependency nesting implies
that its first frame $A_s$ is contained in every such target hyperplane.
This justifies initializing a target's latest constraint to all of $H$;
intersecting with $A_s$ already enforces its output constraint.

Select roles in descending $\dim A_s$, breaking ties by support size and
role index. Intersect $A_s$ with the latest selected frame of each incident
target, and retain a maximal nondegenerate summand $\sigma_s$ by the lemma's
block elimination with $E=0$. If its dimension is positive, select it and
replace every incident target's latest frame by $\sigma_s$.
For each target, successive selections therefore form a descending chain
of actual subspaces. Execute the selected reads in \emph{reverse selection
order}. Each target then has an ascending chain from zero, ending in its
side hyperplane; equal-dimensional members coincide. Complete the remaining
$M$, the positive side reads, and the inverse $M,V$ at the full frame.
The phase closure allows this reordering because omitted predecessors
never touch the slots of the phase operations.

For a selected role set $r=\dim A_s$, $a=\dim\sigma_s$.
The first transition becomes the nondegenerate orthogonal difference
$A_s\cap\sigma_s^\perp$, of rank $r-a$. Choose input gauge $C_{\sigma_s}$
and output gauge $C_HC_{\sigma_s}$, retaining the full-frame inverse
cleanup. The auxiliary exterior width changes from $m-h$ to $m-h+a$;
the first transition is replaced, not removed unless $a=r$.
The 6,341 selected roles and their order are regenerated by
\path{scripts/partial_gauge_producer.py}; with \texttt{--work-dir DIR},
\texttt{selection.json} retains the selected list and \texttt{word.json}
retains \texttt{ops}, \texttt{sources}, \texttt{rootroles}, and
\texttt{phase1}. The construction and independent checker are in
\path{scripts/partial_gauge/}. The remaining internal and both data-chain
histograms are recorded in
\path{certificates/partial-gauge-complex-input.json}.

Boolean support is used only for safe containment tests; readout
coefficients are those of $JM$ over $\mathbb Q$. At the first copy into a
fresh role choose any source present in the copied nonempty value. Its
coefficient dominates that role's original-dirty coefficient thereafter,
since subsequent additions are positive. Each raw designated output is
a disjoint source sum, so the dirty coefficient is at most one.
Injected source roles use their own source as witness. Consequently
$|(JM)_{t,s}|\le\sum_j|J_{t,j}|\le h+4$, with denominator dividing
$2(h-3)$. Partial direct-readout sums are polynomially bounded in $R,h$.
