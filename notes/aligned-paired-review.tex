\textbf{Finite claim F.} For formal inputs $x_S$ indexed by three-element
subsets of $\{0,\ldots,49\}$, every three-element target $T$ and $i\in T$ has
\[
 y_{i,T}=\sum_{S\cap T=\{i\}}x_S.
\]
The graph uses 435,450 binary additions with disjoint supports and 58,800 partial
outputs: 14,944 fewer additions than the preceding paired graph. Summing the
three outputs for $T$ gives the intersection-one map. These finite identities
and counts are independently reproducible.

\textbf{Conditional consequence C.} Assume the retained embedding and motif
argument realize the stated finite shear contract, the power-width and
arbitrary-width interchange procedures have their claimed uniform fixed-tape
costs, and the retained layer, stopped-guard, resampling, transform, precision
and assembly interfaces apply. Then
\[
 T(n)=O\bigl(n(\log n)^{1-17\cdot2^{-63}}\bigr).
\]
The machine has a fixed finite alphabet and a fixed finite number of
one-dimensional tapes. The network is fixed independently of $n$.
No worldwide priority or practical speedup is established here.

\textbf{Construction and local frames.} For common point $i$, order the other
points increasingly except for $i\mathbin{\oplus}1$, which goes last. Relabel
inputs and outputs of the unchanged weighted paired circuit (9,813 additions,
1,176 outputs). Complete root pairs agree across groups. Intern equal fixed-pair
star supports, retain the first decomposition, prune and recompile physical roles.

For node support $A$, let $U_A$ span its source indicators in the rational form
$B=I-J/9$ on $\mathbb Q^{50}$. The sources share a point $i$, so
\[
 \sum_j u_j=3u_i,\qquad u^TBu=\sum_{j\ne i}u_j^2\quad(u\in U_A).
\]
Zero forces $u=0$. Thus $U_A$ is positive definite, and $U_A^\perp$ is
nondegenerate because the ambient form is nondegenerate ($50\ne9$).
At an output $1_S^TB1_T=|S\cap T|-1=0$. Support inclusion nests forward spans;
orthogonal complements reverse inclusions for the backward graph. These local
premises do not by themselves prove the entire tensor/motif transfer.

Gather/fanout compilation has $2C+Q$ outgoing uses and reuses one incoming role
per addition, giving $R=C+Q=494250$ side roles. The retained schedule cancels
$JLz$ and $JL(z+Vx)$ for arbitrary initial scratch $z$, leaving $JLVx$ and
restoring scratch. Here $L,V,J$ denote mixers, source copy and output injection.
\textbf{R1:} check the full forward/reverse schedule, tensor frames,

\end{gathered}
\]

\begin{center}{\large R2: circuit to fixed-tape recurrence}\end{center}
The required contract routes every scalar input by a permutation $\rho$ of
all $W$ roles, including arbitrary scratch. Each gate has one common rational
$m\times m$ matrix $M$; its edge matrices satisfy
\[
\begin{gathered}
 M_{\rm out(\rho(w))}-M_{\rm in(w)}=I_m\quad(1\le w\le W),\\
 s=\sum_e\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}
 (M_{\rm head(e)}-M_{\rm tail(e)})<Wm.
\end{gathered}
\]
This is \src{eq:finite-shear-contract} in \src{04-swap.tex}. A correct sum
circuit alone does not imply the contract.

\textbf{Logical volume.} In \src{prop:power-interchange}, width $e=m^k$ has a
preceding row count divisible by $W^k$. Cyclic splitting creates $W$ role
streams of the same remaining shape, each with logical volume $V_{\rm log}/W$
and row count divisible by $W^{k-1}$. By \src{lem:matrix-shear}, each edge shear
uses exactly its rational rank in recursive pivot interchanges. Every spectator
range remains present. One fixed prime avoids the finite rational table's
denominators and invertible-factor diagonal numerators.

\textbf{Uniform node bound to audit.} There must be exactly $s$ child calls
on volume $V_{\rm log}/W$, with at most $C_0V_{\rm log}$ other tape steps.
$C_0$ may depend on the fixed network and prime, but not on depth, field lengths
or data. The existing proof reserves $W$ role tapes, fixed I/O/work tapes, and
parking and descriptor stacks. It parks inactive streams, copies the active
stream to child I/O, erases current parent work and pushes its descriptor. On
return, streams are restored from the stack top without traversing ancestors.
Charge every copy, erasure and head movement. A fixed tape count can be huge.

\textbf{Descriptors and one-bit payloads.} The pinned proof includes both a
parking schedule and a descriptor argument. At depth $j$ of root width
$e_0=m^{k_0}$, all original chunk ranges remain as targets or spectators:
\[
 V_j=V_0/W^j\ge q^{2e_0},\qquad
 j\log W\le(\log_m e_0)\log W=O(e_0),\qquad
 \log V_0=O(\log(2V_j)).
\]
The child keeps its two targets and collapses consecutive spectators into a
bounded number of intervals. Its descriptor processing has fixed polynomial
cost in $\log(2V_0)$, absorbable in $O(V_j)$. Check every allowed shape and that
cleanup stays within the current call's extent. The question is the validity
of this existing argument, not the absence of a schedule.

\textbf{Conditional recurrence.} Under that uniform node bound, time divided
by logical volume satisfies
\[
 F_0=O(1),\qquad F_k\le(s/W)F_{k-1}+C_0.
\]
The exact enclosure gives $\log m<11737/1000$ and $\eta>a(11737/1000)$ with
$a=305/10^{11}$. Therefore
\[
 1-\eta<\exp(-a\log m),\qquad s/W<m^{1-a},
\]
and the geometric recurrence gives $O(V_{\rm log}e^{1-a})$.
\src{lem:chunk-swap} must preserve this exponent when removing row/width
restrictions, including padding and cleanup. A fixed number $s$ of parking
operations fits in $C_0$; using total workspace as the child's logical volume
loses the claimed coefficient $s/W$.

\textbf{Requested assessment.} An expert uninvolved in the original
construction or audit should examine this uniform bound, complete address
ranges, spectator compression, stack discipline and primitive stream costs.
Finite coefficient and dirty-scratch tests do not prove the machine time bound.
