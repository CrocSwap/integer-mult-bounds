\begin{abstract}
The prescribed common basis of icekylinx's PR18 already contains a
width-21 contiguous block inside an A5 corner previously charged as
singleton pivots. We prove that ordered pivot pattern and combine it
with Zhihao Chen's PR21 finite construction and PR23 semantic/bulk
transfer of RaD's PR20 interfaces. The conditional multiplication
saving is $\kappa=11447067/10^{12}=1.1447067\times10^{-5}>2^{-17}$.
No physical source frame, scalar producer, complex interface or maximum
recursive width changes. Exact rational controls supplement the written
argument; they are not formal verification of the inherited theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Compatibility with semantic precision and joint row stock}
The regrouping changes the bit child multiset only. Each of the $2N$
A5 copies replaces 21 singleton children by one width-21 child. Thus the
complete bit rank sum is conserved and the recursive call count falls by
$40N$. In particular
\[
 m_b=32775,\quad W_b=5820554077800,\quad
 W_bm_b-s_b=82076640800,\quad M_b=32729
\]
remain unchanged. The other children and physical streams are identical.
The retained arbitrary-width interchange primitive implements the new
contiguous block, and its triangular surrounding passes are already
covered by the existing ordered-affine accounting.

The complex network is entirely unchanged. PR23's literal scalar charge,
semantic induction, $C_0$ and $C_1=1$ therefore apply verbatim. Its bit and
complex halving degrees depend only on $(m_b,M_b)$ and $(m_c,M_c)$; its
wire-stock bounds depend only on $W_b,W_c$. All these quantities are
unchanged. Consequently its degree-89000 joint row stock and restored
nested bit factors remain valid. The new grouping requires no additional
row family, padding operation, source translation or address reversal.
This is the reason the PR23 bridge can be reused, rather than an inference
from the numerical exponent alone.

\section{Exact assembly}\label{sec:a5-semantic-assembly}
\begin{theorem}
Under the retained interfaces just specified,
\[
 T(n)=O\!\left(n(\log n)^{1-\kappa}\right),\qquad
 \kappa=\frac{11447067}{10^{12}}>2^{-17}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The new exact bit child multiset gives
\[
 a=\frac{1144733}{10^{11}},\qquad
 \frac{\sum_t c_t t^{1-a}}{W_bm_b^{1-a}}<1-\frac3{10^{14}}.
\]
The certificate uses the retained rational logarithm enclosures and
$\exp(u)\le1+u+u^2/[2(1-u/3)]$ in its stated range. The next
$10^{-12}$ grid point does not pass this upper-enclosure certificate;
this is not a lower bound on the actual moment. Omitting the new A5
block fails to certify the displayed $a$.
The unchanged complex saving is $b=18/10^6$.

Use the PR23 parameter formulas with
\[
\begin{gathered}
 h=10^{-8},\quad\beta=1/4,\quad\delta=h/8,\quad
 \tau=1-a,\quad\sigma=1-b,\quad q=a(1-2h),\\
 \lambda'=1-q,\quad\lambda=(\tau+\lambda')/2,\quad
 c=q(1+h),\quad\epsilon=\frac{1-h}{1+c+q},\\
 G=\epsilon q,\quad r=(G+1-\epsilon)/2,\quad C_1=1.
\end{gathered}
\]
The complex leaf saving $(1-\beta)b=13.5\times10^{-6}$ remains larger
than $a$. All 47 strict assembly constraints pass exact rational
arithmetic, including the literal scalar charge and joint row-stock gap.
The seven margins are
\[
\begin{aligned}
 g_1&=1-\epsilon(1+c),&g_2&=a,&g_3&=G,&g_4&=a,\\
 g_5&=\min(1-\epsilon-\delta,r-\delta),&
 g_6&=1-\epsilon-\delta,&g_7&=\epsilon.
\end{aligned}
\]
Their minimum is
\[
 G=\frac{520333166208186467703}{45455586120893480913532297},
 \qquad G-\kappa>\frac5{10^{13}}>0.
\]
The positive slack supplies the retained strict logarithmic absorption.
The PR23 eventual-cutoff calculation, reevaluated at these parameters,
remains valid with the same fixed interfaces and row-stock degree.
\end{proof}
