\begin{abstract}
We combine the partial-swap network of icekylinx's PR18 with a new
auxiliary source-frame translation and its compatible contiguous corner.
An operator-correct phase-frame translation strengthens the independent
complex interface. Under the retained analytic and fixed-tape hypotheses,
the exact witness is $\kappa=5499/10^9=5.499\times10^{-6}>2^{-18}$ in
$T(n)=O(n(\log n)^{1-\kappa})$. All original scalar maps and physical
allocations are retained. New finite controls and exact certificates
support the written construction; they are not formal verification of
the complete multiplication theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and attribution}
The computational model is a fixed finite-alphabet multitape Turing machine.
Every finite network, basis, prime, and tape count is fixed independently of
the input length. The bit payload field is $\mathbb F_2$; its address
geometry is rational and is subsequently reduced at a suitable odd prime.
These two fields have different purposes. The complex payload consists of
Gaussian dyadics and uses the retained binary address-label interface.

The retained framework is Douglas Colkitt's compact-control construction
\cite{main}, with Bortlesboat's alignment \cite{pr2}, eumemic's complex
producer and Gaussian resampling \cite{pr3,pr5,pr6}, dleen's retained totals
and stage sharing \cite{pr4}, and Zhihao Chen's finite-network contribution
\cite{pr7}, pinned at
\texttt{6725c6a17b17871a35353fd29157f4ed851bc114}.
The upstream multiplication manuscript is pinned at
\texttt{adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a} \cite{upstream}.
The partial-swap and selected retained-triple development is by icekylinx,
with substantial OpenAI GPT-6 Astra assistance; review and integration used
OpenAI Codex assistance. Prior contributions retain their attribution and
licenses in \texttt{NOTICE} and \texttt{SOURCES.json}.
This submission extends icekylinx's bulk-recursion PR~\#10 \cite{pr10},
pinned at \texttt{62691e395a0458ce089a1c7b5d89e74291e95e29}; its
parameterized mixed-width and corrected Gaussian arguments are retained
below with the new construction and constants.
