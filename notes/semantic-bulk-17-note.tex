\begin{abstract}
The unchanged finite bit and complex interfaces of PR21 compose with the
semantic precision, arbitrary-coordinate routing and bulk resampling
interfaces exposed by the RaD contribution in PR20. We supply the
circuit-specific scalar charge, the larger-child precision induction,
the simultaneous nested row stock and a strict rational assembly.
The resulting conditional exponent saving is
$\kappa=1099/10^8=1.099\cdot10^{-5}>2^{-17}$.
The original FFT prefix layout suffices. This is a composition of attributed
constructions with written proofs and executable checks, not formal
verification or completed external mathematical peer review.
\end{abstract}

\section{Statement, provenance and proof boundary}
\begin{theorem}
Under the retained multiplication framework and its fixed finite-alphabet
multitape interfaces, with the attributed phase-cell inverse, routing and
bulk resampling arguments identified below, integer multiplication has
\[
 T(n)=O\bigl(n(\log n)^{1-\kappa}\bigr),\qquad
 \boxed{\kappa=\frac{1099}{10^8}>2^{-17}}.
\]
All finite graphs, bases, native tables and tape counts are fixed
independently of input length. Their constructive setup and the original
analytic and exact-recovery thresholds remain eventual conditions.
\end{theorem}

Its parameters are

 m_b=32775,\quad W_b=5820554077800,\quad r_b=32729.

The retained common rational basis supplies actual contiguous recursive

The independent complex network has

 m_c=21952,\quad W_c=2085111546336,\quad
 s_c=45772350635112192,\quad r_c=21924.
\]
Its complete physical child histogram has weighted rank sum $s_c$ and
strict homogeneous characteristic at exponent $\sigma$. All its physical

Thus each completed recursive call is exactly the tensor phase operation

The signed residual implementation is
$(-i)^{f n_-}Z_- C^{\otimes rf}Z_-$ with the paid binary adapters.
It never substitutes a characteristic-zero address projection for a binary

\section{Linear semantic precision for this actual circuit}
Every completed forward or inverse child on $u$ selected axes has Gaussian
coefficients in $2^{-u}\mathbb Z[i]$ and absolute row sum at most $2^u$.
Its unit sign wrappers do not increase that bound. With incoming magnitude
$2^t$ and denominator exponent $p+t$, a returned stream therefore has
magnitude at most $2^{t+u}$ and denominator exponent at most $p+t+u$.
Internal intermediate values may be larger. They are never returned as
unfinished operands to the parent.

All computation uses the same fixed fine grid, with exact internal halves.
The argument changes a bound on a returned value, not the stored encoding.
No child is rounded, compressed or re-encoded at its return. Other parent
operands remain parked on the fixed stack tapes until the child completes.
The scalar schedule restores arbitrary dirty auxiliaries, so this statement
holds on every physical role, rather than on a selected zero-scratch output.

Here the local PairedComplex28 circuit has 64298 active nodes, 3276 data
triples, and 93838 side roles. Each of its three tensor stages uses four
mixer sweeps, two copies, two injections and four grouped central gates.
Counting input nodes as mixer gates only increases the bound. Consequently
\[
 G=3\binom{28}{3}^{\!2}
       \bigl(4\cdot64298+4\cdot3276+4\bigr)
\]
bounds its grouped scalar gates. Put
\[
 E=64(W_c+m_c+1)^3,\qquad B=s_c+E,\qquad C_0=32m_cB^2.
\]
Exact integer arithmetic verifies
\[
 2GW_c^2+8s_c+4W_c+4+32m_c<E.
\]
This conservatively pays scalar evaluation, all unit phase wrappers,
role corrections, and the fewer than $m_c$ tail kernels. In particular a
smaller physical register count is not substituted for a gate count.

Let $A(e)$ bound temporary magnitude and denominator excess for an
$e$-axis call. A leaf has $A(e)\le8e$. Before any active child, the total
selected widths of already completed calls are at most
$s_c\lfloor e/m_c\rfloor$. The active call has at most
$r_c\lfloor e/m_c\rfloor$ axes. The correct recurrence is therefore
\[
 A(e)\le A(r_c\lfloor e/m_c\rfloor)
              +s_c\lfloor e/m_c\rfloor+E.
\]
Writing $f=\lfloor e/m_c\rfloor\ge1$, induction gives $A(e)\le2Be$,
since
\[
 2B(m_c-r_c)\ge s_c+E,
 \qquad 2Br_cf+s_cf+E\le2Bm_cf\le2Be.
\]
This argument explicitly accommodates a child almost as wide as its parent.
It holds for any fixed stopping exponent. Completed disjoint selected-axis
pieces have total width at most $d$; prefix kernels, outer phases and final
normalization contribute the retained $18d$. Hence
\[
 A_{\rm layer}\le(2B+18)d<C_0d,\qquad C_1=1.
\]
The condition $\epsilon<1$ makes this guard eventually $O(p)$ for
$d=\lfloor b_{\rm in}^{\epsilon}\rfloor$, $p=6b_{\rm in}$.
The old PR21 path guard remains a valid older estimate but cannot be
used with the new near-one choice of $\epsilon$.

\section{Complete simultaneous rows and the stopped tree}
Exact integer powers establish the least halving depths
\[
 m_b^{494}>2r_b^{494},\qquad m_c^{544}>2r_c^{544}.
\]
Also $W_b<2^{43}$ and $W_c<2^{41}$. At a top-level call, reserve the
product $Q=W_c^{D_c}W_b^{D_b}$, with
$D_c=544\lceil\log_2 e\rceil$ and
$D_b=494\lceil\log_2 e\rceil$, using a conservative global width bound.
The bit factor is consumed temporarily by an adapter and restored before
another complex child proceeds. Using the maximum of the two stocks
would not establish this nested contract.

There is a physically preceding row prefix: apply exact individual $C$
kernels once to the first $q_0=\lceil\log_2 Q\rceil$ selected positions,
retaining their full chunks. If fewer positions exist, this is the whole
$O(V\log p)$ elementary fallback. Otherwise these transformed chunks
supply a leading row field of cardinality $R\ge Q$. Pad complete rows
once to $Q\lceil R/Q\rceil<2R$. Descendants inherit the same stock,
with complete active, control and spectator fields in every role.
Their exact all-role return restores appended zero rows to zero, so they
can be removed at the completed boundary. The prefix is neither moved
from a suffix for free nor transformed again inside descendants.

For eventual $e\le Cp$, $p\ge C$, $\log_2p\ge25$,
\[
 \log_2 Q\le(43\cdot494+41\cdot544)\frac{51}{25}\log_2p
 <89000\log_2p.
\]
Thus the safe combined stock is $p^{89000}$; the supplementary suffix
condition is
$b_{\rm in}^{1-\epsilon}>356000(\log_2 b_{\rm in}+8)$.
Its compressed integer certificate is supplied, rather than borrowing
PR20's stock for a different graph.

For the stopped complex tree, give each node potential equal to its
relative logical volume times $e^\sigma$. The strict homogeneous
characteristic contracts the sum of child potentials. Complete leaves
at $e<d^\beta$ have total cost exponent
$\sigma+\beta(1-\sigma)$. Since $\tau>\sigma$, the paid bit-adapter
cost sums geometrically with exponent $\tau$. Compact reservation costs
have exponent $\max(1-c,0)$. These conclusions permit unequal child
depths, retain every binary adapter and tail scan, and yield the three
exponents used in the final certificate.

\section{Attributed movement, inverse and bulk interfaces}
The following are the RaD analytic and tape constructions, not new
finite-graph claims. Their full written arguments and discriminating
controls are pinned in \texttt{references/rad20}.

\paragraph{Coordinate routing.}
The active-masked compact gadget implements disjoint-source XORs, with
arbitrary dirty reservoirs. A completed four-update identity restores
guards and temporary digits, so a source in an inactive slot or guard is
read consistently by the load and unload. Actual modular rotations are
bijections. Correct bad addresses by $T S^{-1}$ evaluated at the current
address, using stable radix reinsertion into the bad holes. The density
bound is local to each invocation; every extraction and reinsertion scan
is paid. Three untouched reservoirs partition a matching, and two
matchings implement a coordinate permutation. For short coefficient
records, a separate untouched spectator field of width
$\ell=\Theta(p^{1-\epsilon})$ enlarges records without enlarging volume.
Original chunk swaps place and restore that spectator; the proof is not
circular. The small-width fallback requires $\epsilon>a$. The resulting
cost is $O(Vp^\tau\operatorname{polylog}p+\operatorname{poly}p)$ for
known coordinate permutations on complete rectangles.

\paragraph{Phase-cell inverse.}
RaD's rounded structured global matrix $H$ has exact diagonal one,
row gap at least $1/(8p)$ and inverse row norm at most $8p$.
Its phase cells preserve the physical period cut. Truncated Toeplitz
interiors, Schur complements and rounded banded boundary solves operate
with $O(p)$-bit online coefficients; exact rational setup is reused once
per axis and is $n^{o(1)}$. The rounded similarity weights are downward
approximations with a four-ulp allowance, not assumed exact irrational
roundings. With $\alpha^2=\Theta(p^r)$, the band satisfies
$w^2=O(p^{1-r})$. The normalized inverse has norm below $1/8$;
the logarithmic normalization term is retained in
$\gamma=d(2\alpha^2+\lceil\log_2(32p)\rceil+1)$.
Thus $\epsilon+r<1$ is essential.

\paragraph{Bulk locality and tape movement.}
Take target core length $L=2^{\lceil8\log_2p\rceil}$ and halo
$A=4096p^3$. Every principal window restricts the same global rounded $H$.
With $J=512p^2$ and artificial-boundary distance at least $Jw$, the two
Neumann tails give row error at most $32p\,2^{-J/(16p)}<2^{-16p}$.
The complementary period is greater than $w$, and the original phase cut
is never merged after lifting a wrapped window. Local normalized maps
retain norm headroom; tensor errors add in row norm.

Near-one source padding fills each source halo into exactly $L$ entries,
using $L\ge64dA$. Target halos instead have persistent length $L+2A$;
their total tensor volume is $T(1+2A/L)^{d-1}<2T$. Only the currently
moved short field is temporarily padded to binary length. Simultaneous
binary padding of all halo fields is invalid and explicitly rejected.
Two alternating overlap tapes emit monotone windows without shifting a
FIFO or rereading an entire line. Factor pages are scanned sequentially
in their original coarse-axis order and reused over complete spectator
fields. An axis is cropped only after its own inverse completes; remaining
axes retain their halos. The final ordered cores restore the original
prime-box row order and normalization.

A constant number of full coordinate routes and the short-field passes
therefore give the complete bound
\[
 O\!\left(Tp\,[p^\tau\operatorname{polylog}p+
 d\operatorname{polylog}p+d p^\delta+w^2p^\delta+
 d w^2p^\delta/L]\right)+n^{o(1)}.
\]
All the displayed terms occur in our assembly. The phase-cell proof uses
an unconditional conventional multiplier for its small convolutions, so
there is no circular invocation of the new multiplication theorem.

\paragraph{Prime supply.}
The old restriction $1-2\epsilon>0$ is replaced, not silently dropped.
Baker--Harman--Pintz, Theorem~1, supplies a prime in
$[x-x^{21/40},x]$ for sufficiently large $x$.
Place $d$ disjoint such intervals in the required window of length
$x/(4d)$. The sufficient inequality $12d^2<x^{19/40}$ holds eventually
when $x=2^{\Theta(p^{1-\epsilon})}$ and $\epsilon<1$.
The required distinct odd primes, near-one ratios and deterministic
$n^{o(1)}$ search remain available. Their theorem's eventual threshold
is retained separately from our explicit power tests.

\section{Exact strict assembly}
Choose
\[
 h=10^{-8},\quad\beta=\tfrac14,\quad q=a(1-2h),\quad
 \lambda'=1-q,\quad\lambda=\tfrac12(\tau+\lambda'),
\]
\[
 c=q(1+h),\quad\epsilon=\frac{1-h}{1+c+q},\quad
 G=\epsilon q,\quad r=\frac{G+1-\epsilon}{2},\quad\delta=h/8.
\]
Here $\epsilon\simeq0.9999779904845393$ and
$r\simeq1.6504636568\cdot10^{-5}$. The retained original prefix is
sufficient; no changed balanced-layout estimate is needed. The seven
margins are
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{operation}&\text{saving}\\\hline
\text{original prefix}&1-\epsilon(1+c)\\
\text{coordinate movement}&a\\
\text{compact phase layer}&\epsilon q\\
\text{bulk exposure}&a\\
\text{Gaussian arithmetic}&\min(1-\epsilon-\delta,r-\delta)\\
\text{scalar work}&1-\epsilon-\delta\\
\text{dimension}&\epsilon
\end{array}
\]
The exact minimum is $G$, with
\[
 G-\frac{1099}{10^8}>9\cdot10^{-9}>0.
\]
The program recomputes 47 positive constraints, including these seven
strict absorption gaps, all exponent orderings, $(1-\beta)b>a$,
$c>q$, the linear guard, compact geometry, cell/band separation,
short-record fallback, artificial microbox boundaries and row-stock
allowance. These replace the old separate-exposure/old guard constraints
using the written constructions above. Negative controls show that the
old guard, old separate exposures, and claimed $2^{-16}$ saving each fail.
The strict gaps absorb fixed logarithms and prove the theorem.

The explicit numeric cutoffs use rational arithmetic and integer bit
lengths. A test $j\ge\operatorname{bitlength}(N)$ proves $2^j>N$
without allocating an enormous power. Six checkpoints verify both the
joint stock and stopping condition; monotonicity beyond the supplied
threshold follows from the retained real-log comparison. Native
constants, prime existence, catalogue domination and strict asymptotic
absorption retain additional eventual thresholds. None of these numbers
is a practical running-time estimate.

\section{Reproduction and limits}
Run \texttt{python3 research/semantic-bulk/verify.py}. It checks all
recorded PR21 theorem/code hashes and the pinned RaD source manifest,
reconstructs the actual complex scalar charge and new stock, and produces
\texttt{research/semantic-bulk/certificate.json}. The complete previous
finite producers are reused by hash, without repeating their unchanged
large computations. New routing, phase-inverse, bulk and semantic controls
are retained separately under \texttt{research/semantic-bulk/controls}.
Their finite checks discriminate incorrect endpoints, masking, rounding,
period handling and row-stock reuse; the all-size claims also require
the written proofs identified above.

The present declared finite bit saving bounds this composed witness below
$2^{-16}$. This does not exclude a better finite network or compiler.
Future work at that threshold must improve the actual bit characteristic
and recheck the complex leaf headroom; an optimistic role budget is not
a multiplication bound.
