\begin{abstract}
Three signed shears on a regular Cayley cover align all interstage data
frames. Arbitrary-subspace Clifford frames support the selected complex
word; weighted reversed interchanges on a batched finite local-ring cover
support the bit word. Exact rational moments and the retained semantic/bulk
assembly give the conditional saving $\kappa=3.146011\times10^{-4}$.
\end{abstract}
