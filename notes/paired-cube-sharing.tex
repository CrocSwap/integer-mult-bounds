\subsection{Sharing completed cores rather than incomplete producers}
Write $\mathcal G=O(3h,2)$ and $m=3h$. Embed the retained three-stage
data incidence cover in a fixed nondegenerate $(3h-2)$-space $E_0$;
extend its exchange generators by the identity on $E_0^\perp$.
Use invocation vertices from the full ambient group $\mathcal G$.
The retained three shears still compose to $(X,Y)\mapsto(-Y,X)$,
with the inherited exact source Pauli, sign and bank normalizations.
Every data port has one invocation in each stage. After stage three,
each of its two data banks pays one rank-two complement child, producing
the full ambient transform.

Independently partition the ambient address space into orthogonal
$h$-blocks $P_1,P_2,P_3$. Fix orthogonal maps $H_j$ taking the local
active space $A$ to $P_j$. Label the single physical auxiliary bank by
$(g,r)$, $g\in\mathcal G$, $1\le r\le R$; in stage $j$ route this role
to role $r$ of vertex $gH_j$. For each stage this is a bijection, and
the role's active block is $gP_j$. The data cover and auxiliary routing
are separate permutations of actual role slots. Execute complete
stage-one cores, then complete stage-two cores, then complete stage-three
cores. No role is used simultaneously by two invocations.

\subsection{Exact raw operator contract and the final gauge child}
Here a completed core includes all signed old-value corrections,
copied centers and full-frame scalar inverse cleanup, but excludes the
old independent-bank exterior tail. This distinction matters.
If its raw dirty entrance is $z$, interpret it as
$T_\sigma\widehat z=z$ at its prescribed entrance gauge.
Its scalar contract restores $\widehat z$ for every input, so its raw
exit is $F_A\widehat z$ and its exact operator is
\[
 U=F_AT_\sigma^{-1}.
\]
The complemented complete time reverse has $U^{-1}=T_\sigma F_A^{-1}$,
including the actual rank-zero endpoint reconciliation. It is not merely
the inverse of the positive DAG. A later invocation may reinterpret its
arbitrary raw dirty entrance using its own gauge; it requires no retained
copy of a previous logical interpretation.

Common inactive offsets cancel from these relative actions. The three
relative actions of a physical role operate in $gP_1,gP_2,gP_3$;
they tensor exactly, including their phases. In a forward block the
correction needed to obtain its full $F_A$ action is
\[
 F_AU^{-1}=F_AT_\sigma F_A^{-1},
\]
of Fourier rank $\dim\sigma$. In the reversed block it is
\[
 F_A(U^{-1})^{-1}=F_A^2T_\sigma^{-1},
\]
of the same rank, since $F_A^2$ is an exact rank-zero Pauli.
The arbitrary-subspace Clifford theorem proves these ranks even for
degenerate $\sigma$; no orthogonal-projector interpretation is used.
The tensor correction is a single child of width $3\dim\sigma$.
After transport by $g$, the tensor endpoint is its conjugate of the
coordinate full transform. Its binary symplectic action agrees with the
canonical ambient $F$; their exact ratio is a fixed Pauli and phase.
Apply this actual rank-zero endpoint adapter, charged in the router guard.
Zero gauges need only actual rank-zero adapters. No commutativity of
different partial frames within one block is assumed.

\subsection{The complete paid ledger}
Let $s_a$ count entrance gauges of dimension $a$, and let $H_r,D_r,T_r$
be respectively the remaining local internal, source-data and target-data
child histograms. Per group vertex the role count and child multiset are
\[
 W_0=2v+R,\qquad
 n_r=3(H_r+D_r+T_r)+2v\,\mathbf1_{r=2}
       +\sum_{a>0}s_a\,\mathbf1_{r=3a}.
\]
Thus all three copies of the actual local word remain paid. In the local
auxiliary ledger the entrance gauges subtract $\sum_a as_a$ from the
forward/complement core rank, and copied centers add $\ell$;
the two data banks contribute $2v(h-1)$. Consequently
\begin{align*}
 \sum_r rn_r
 &=3\bigl(Rh-\sum_a as_a+\ell+2v(h-1)\bigr)
       +4v+3\sum_a as_a\\
 &=mW_0-2v+3\ell.
\end{align*}
For the selected complex word,
\[
 m=66,\quad W_0=13894,\quad
 \sum_r rn_r=915684,\quad mW_0-\sum_r rn_r=1320.
\]
Physical frame descent (\path{scripts/paired_cube_physical.py}) moves
13,595 common operation frames between the span of their previous frames
and the intersection of their next frames; connected operations that share
a frame move together. Each of the 2,310 gauged
roles then occupies the physical slot of a dead donor whose last frame
lies in its gauge. Its old-value read moves to the deadline immediately
before its first operation, and the slot keeps the donor's zero start.
So no exterior correction remains: every local internal child has width
at most 20, and final data children have width two. Hence the largest
child is 20. Finally, 42 all-eight side roots are terminal sinks
(\path{research/terminal-sinks}; the terminal-sink lemma of PR166, checked
by its own gate): such a root's register starts at frame 0, is only ever a
destination outside phase one, and has a pivot target $p$ with no deferred
read of $y_p$ during its writes. The register is deleted; at the phase cut
$y_t\gets y_t-y_p$ for the other targets $t$, every write goes to $y_p$ at its
frame, and after the last write $y_t\gets y_t+y_p$ at the root frame $U$.
Each sink removes one physical register and one pair of children $[\operatorname{rank}U]$
and $[h-\operatorname{rank}U]$, and the deficit is unchanged. This leaves
11,254 physical roles. Full stock is $W=|\mathcal G|W_0$;
the group factor cancels from the normalized moment, not from routing,
scalar work, row stock or the finite semantic guard.
