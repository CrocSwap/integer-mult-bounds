\section{Endpoint-gauge bit substitution and stronger assembly}
\label{sec:endpoint-semantic-composition}
This extension is contributed by Dominik Scholz with substantial OpenAI
Codex assistance. It uses icekylinx's endpoint-gauge bit construction
(PR24, commit \texttt{ed90fd940279c336ebc45968631bdebfb087b505})
and Zhihao Chen's semantic/bulk composition (PR23, commit
\texttt{661ebabc1076c9f525d7ce4967c876b203fa9827}). The latter attributes
its routing, precision, inverse and bulk arguments to RaD/hipotures.
All their mathematical and tape hypotheses remain dependencies.

\begin{theorem}[Conditional endpoint/semantic composition]
Under the hypotheses and interfaces of the preceding semantic/bulk
construction, with the endpoint-gauge bit interface of PR24,
\[
 T(n)=O\!\left(n(\log n)^{1-\kappa}\right),\qquad
 \kappa=\frac{119720853}{10^{13}}=1.19720853\cdot10^{-5}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the complete PR24 bit characteristic and prescribed common rational
basis at dimensions $(32,30,40)$. Its arbitrary-width, spectator and
scratch-restoration contract is the same interface consumed by the
coordinate router and the complex binary adapters. Its exact certificate
gives
\[
 a=\frac{2993093}{250000000000},\quad m_b=38400,\quad
 W_b=9854325632000,\quad r_b=38340,
\]
and a strictly contracting homogeneous moment at exponent $1-a$.
No child profile is inferred merely from its rank, and no new gathering
or edge-specific basis change is assumed.

Retain the PR21 complex circuit used above, with $b=18/10^6$,
$m_c=21952$, $W_c=2085111546336$ and $r_c=21924$.
PR24's different complex network is not substituted. Consequently the
actual complex scalar charge, $E$, $B$, $C_0=32m_cB^2$, and the semantic
induction with $C_1=1$ are exactly those already proved above. Changing
the bit address router does not alter any Gaussian-dyadic coefficient or
selected complex axis. The two finite rational setup families admit a
shared sufficiently large eligible prime by excluding the union of their
finite exceptional primes. Their distinct finite dimensions are allowed
by the arbitrary-width interfaces; they need not be equated.

Recompute the simultaneous product stock for the actual new bit circuit:
\[
 m_b^{444}>2r_b^{444},\qquad m_c^{544}>2r_c^{544},\qquad
 W_b<2^{44},\quad W_c<2^{41}.
\]
The preceding powers fail, so these are the least halving depths.
The coefficient is $44\cdot444+41\cdot544=41840$, and
\[
 41840\,\frac{51}{25}<86000.
\]
Thus $p^{86000}$ bounds the \emph{product} stock, with sufficient suffix
condition $b_{\rm in}^{1-\epsilon}>344000(\log_2 b_{\rm in}+8)$.
The preceding leading-prefix, single-padding and restored nested-bit
arguments apply with these constants. No maximum-of-stocks shortcut is used.

Choose the same parameterized semantic/bulk assembly, now with
\[
 h=10^{-12},\quad\beta=\tfrac14,\quad q=a(1-2h),\quad
 c=q(1+h),\quad\epsilon=\frac{1-h}{1+c+q},
\]
\[
 \tau=1-a,\quad\sigma=1-b,\quad
 \lambda'=1-q,\quad\lambda=(\tau+\lambda')/2,\quad
 G=\epsilon q,\quad r=(G+1-\epsilon)/2,\quad\delta=h/8.
\]
In particular $(1-\beta)b=13.5\cdot10^{-6}>a$, preserving the
stopped complex leaf headroom. The seven margins remain
\[
 1-\epsilon(1+c),\quad a,\quad G,\quad a,\quad
 \min(1-\epsilon-\delta,r-\delta),\quad
 1-\epsilon-\delta,\quad\epsilon.
\]
Exact rational arithmetic checks all 47 strict conditions and proves
that $G$ is their minimum assembly margin, with
\[
 G-\kappa>\frac{3}{10^{14}}>0.
\]
The symbolic identity $1-\epsilon(1+c)-G=h$ still holds. All geometry,
guard, inverse, routing, phase-boundary, prime-packing, reservation and
exposure conditions remain strict. The positive gaps absorb fixed
logarithmic factors. The same corrected Gaussian and exact-recovery
arguments then establish the displayed conditional bound.
\end{proof}

The certificate also recomputes the explicit compressed power tests with
the new parameters and product stock. Its numerical cutoff is
$\log_2(\text{input bit length})\ge446523314177$; the preceding additional
eventual setup, prime-existence, catalogue and absorption thresholds still
apply. This is not a practical runtime claim or formal verification.

The derivative assembly is checked for exact equality with PR23's assembly
when given its original $a$ and $h$. Both pinned predecessor certificates
reproduce, including their source hashes. The old quadratic guard, old
separate exposures, and a proposed $2^{-16}$ saving are rejected as
negative controls. No stronger conclusion about the optimum possible
network or multiplication exponent follows from these tests.
