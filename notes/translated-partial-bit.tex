\subsection{Dirty auxiliary sources for partial-swap frames}
\label{sec:partial-swap-source-extension}

For an idempotent $P$ on the rational address space, write
\[
 D_P=\begin{pmatrix}I-P&P\\P&I-P\end{pmatrix}.
\]
If $P,Q$ commute, direct block multiplication gives
\[
 D_PD_Q=D_{P+Q-2PQ},\qquad D_P^{-1}=D_P,
 \qquad D_{I-P}D_P=D_I.
\]
These are multiplicative frame identities; an additive-shear source
identity is not being substituted into the partial-swap construction.

\begin{lemma}[Free auxiliary source frame]
Suppose a scalar auxiliary role is restored for every possible initial
value.  Its source frame may be any fixed invertible address permutation
$F$, provided its sink frame is $D_IF$.  All common gate frames and all
interior edges can remain unchanged.  Its physical endpoint map is $D_I$,
as required by the retained all-role interchange interface.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For physical initial stream $f$, define its logical interpretation to
be $g=F^{-1}f$.  This is a choice of coordinates, requiring no preliminary
tape operation.  Because $F$ is invertible and $f$ arbitrary, $g$ is also
arbitrary.  The scalar dirty-scratch identity returns the same logical
$g$, so the physical sink holds $(D_IF)g=D_If$.

Every gate still has one common frame on all its incident roles; hence
the retained conjugation argument implements the same scalar gate.
Only the transition from the source to the first common gate frame,
and from the last common gate frame to the sink, change.  No value of
the initial auxiliary stream is assumed, and no new register is added.
\end{proof}

Apply the lemma separately to the dedicated stage-two auxiliary bank.
Keep the PR18 tensor order $(F_3,F_1,F_2)$ with
\[
 \dim F_1=25,\quad \dim F_2=23,\quad\dim F_3=57,
 \quad H=575,\quad m=32775.
\]
For its fixed first-factor line $p$ and future third-factor line $t$,
the first and last common gate subspaces are
\[
 U_0=\langle t\rangle\otimes p^\perp\otimes F_2,
 \qquad U_1=\langle t\rangle\otimes F_1\otimes F_2.
\]
Their orthogonal projectors $P_0,P_1$ are commuting and satisfy
$P_0P_1=P_0$.  Both subspaces are nondegenerate.  These are the same
common frames used in PR18 after moving its initial inverse-stage data
enlargement before the first copy; no gate schedule is altered here.

Assign the auxiliary source frame $D_{P_0}$ and sink frame $D_{I-P_0}$.
The first transition is the identity.  The last transition is
\[
 D_{I-P_0}D_{P_1}=D_A,\qquad
 A=I-P_1+P_0
   =I-\Pi_t\otimes\Pi_p\otimes I_{23}.
\]
The omitted subspace has dimension $23$, so $A^2=A$ and
$\operatorname{rank}A=m-23$.  The former entrance and exit ranks were
$H-23$ and $m-H$, respectively; their sum remains $m-23$.
Every stage-two physical auxiliary role, including a central role,
has these endpoint gate frames.  The dedicated bank is separate from
the paired stage-one/stage-three roles, so the latter's endpoint
identities and paired-join compiler are unchanged.  Both scalar
orientations retain arbitrary scratch restoration.

\subsection{Compatibility with the PR18 prescribed common basis}
\label{sec:partial-swap-source-basis-extension}

Use exactly the PR18 parameter family
\[
 S=T_3(I_{57}\otimes K),\qquad
 K=T_2(I_{25}\otimes L_2),\qquad J_\beta=M_\beta L_1,
\]
including its prescribed rows and inverse columns of $M_\beta$ and
$G_i$.  Write $\Pi_p=p\xi$, $\Pi_t=t\varphi$, with
$\xi p=\varphi t=1$.  The $L_2$ factors cancel on the identity factor.
For the null projector $Q=I-A$, its conjugate has entries
\[
 (SQS^{-1})_{(\gamma,\alpha,\beta),(\delta,a,b)}
 =\mathbf1_{\beta=b}
 (G_{(\alpha,\beta)}t)_\gamma
 (J_\beta p)_\alpha
 (\xi J_b^{-1})_a
 (\varphi G_{(a,b)}^{-1})_\delta.
\]
Consequently the first-23-row/last-23-column corner is diagonal.
Its $\beta$th entry is
\[
 d_\beta=(G_{(0,\beta)}t)_0(J_\beta p)_0
 (\xi J_\beta^{-1})_{24}
 (\varphi G_{(24,\beta)}^{-1})_{56},\qquad0\le\beta<23.
\]
The PR18 boundary prescriptions specialize its factors to coordinates
of $L_0t$, $L_1p$, $\xi L_1^{-1}$, and $\varphi L_0^{-1}$.
More explicitly they are, in that order,
\[
 (L_0t)_\beta,\quad (L_1p)_\beta,\quad
 (\xi L_1^{-1})_{\beta+2},\quad
 (\varphi L_0^{-1})_{\beta+34}.
\]
All indices lie in their stated factors.  For any fixed nonzero line
vector and dual functional, each specified coordinate is a nonzero
rational function on the appropriate general linear group.  The bases
$L_0,L_1$ remain free in PR18's nonempty irreducible parameter family.
Thus the finitely many conditions $d_\beta\ne0$, for every actual
stage-two invocation and both orientations, can be multiplied with
all PR18 invertibility and minor conditions.  They impose no new
structural restriction and admit one simultaneous rational choice.

The corresponding corner of $SAS^{-1}$ is $-\operatorname{diag}(d_\beta)$.
It supplies 23 equally ordered contiguous pivots.  Since its size is
the full nullity of $A$, the ordinary large-projector Schur-complement
identity leaves the matching middle identity of width $m-46=32729$.
The physical lower-lower profile is therefore
\[
 \boxed{23,32729},
\]
with no singleton children.  The retained exact partial-swap triangular
conjugation applies to $A$, so these are genuine contiguous recursive
interchanges, with no gathering or reversal of fields.  The sole
compiler change replaces, on each of the $B_2$ stage-two roles,
\[
 (575,31625,23,529)\quad\hbox{by}\quad(23,32729).
\]
Their equal rank sums are $32752$.  Hence $W$, $m$, $L$, the complete
rank sum and its deficit are unchanged.  All other local and macro
profiles remain the PR18 profiles.  A new exact moment and final
assembly are required before claiming a new multiplication bound.
