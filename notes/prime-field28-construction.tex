\subsection{A five-subset interchange motif over $\mathbb F_3$}
Let $h=28$, $\mathcal T=\binom{[h]}5$, $v=98280$, $m=h^3=21952$,
and $N=v^3$. Scalars in the interchange network lie in $\mathbb F_3$.
Use the rational address-label space
\[
 F=\mathbb Q^h,\qquad H=I-\frac2{25}J.
\]
Its exceptional eigenvalue is $1-2h/25=-31/25$, so it is nondegenerate.
For the indicator $t_T$ of a five-set,
\[
 \langle t_S,t_T\rangle=|S\cap T|-2,\qquad
 \langle t_T,t_T\rangle=3.
\]
Neighbors have intersection two. For every pair $C\in\binom{[h]}2$, retain
\[
 A_C=\sum_{T\supset C}x_T,\qquad
 D_{C,E}=\sum_{T=C\cup U,\ U\cap E=\varnothing}x_T
 \quad\left(E\in\binom{[h]\setminus C}3\right).
\]
Scatter $\sum_{C\subset S}A_C$ to $y_S$ and inject
$-\sum_{C\subset S}D_{C,S\setminus C}$. A source and target with intersection
$k$ have coefficient $\binom{k}{2}-[k=2]$ modulo three. For $0\le k\le5$
this is $[k=5]$. Thus their combined map is the identity.

\paragraph{Cancellation-free triple exclusion.}
The local producer works with a polynomial of degree at most three whose
monomials have disjoint formal source supports. Pair its point indices.
Aggregate a monomial according to the set of pairs it touches, and recurse
on those pair indices. To exclude a set $E$ of at most three points, let $G$
be its touched pair indices. Begin with the coarsened sum excluding $G$.
For every nonempty subset $K$ of the surviving partners in the pairs $G$,
add the contraction that selects exactly $K$ there and avoids the remaining
pairs of $G$. These cases partition the permitted monomials.
A one-point contraction has degree at most two and uses the repository's
weighted paired-exclusion recursion. A two-point contraction is a weighted
vector exclusion, and a three-point contraction is one original coefficient.
At at most four points direct summation supplies the base case. The exported
producer checks all $\binom{26}3=2600$ output coefficient vectors exactly.
Retaining the total adds twelve ancestors to its 41427 active additions.

For each common pair, order the intact global pairs first and place the
partners of its removed points last. Identify equal source supports across
these local producers, retaining an existing decomposition, then remove
unused ancestors. Source-support cardinality strictly increases at every
addition, so this identification cannot create a cycle. There are 11240978
addition nodes and $q=10v+\binom h2=983178$ designated output uses before
the next compression.

An addition shared between different common pairs has actual source
intersection of size three or four. In the first case its support is specified
exactly by the common three-set and a bit vector of remaining pairs; in the
second by the common four-set and a bit vector of remaining points.
These are exact keys, with full equality comparison after hashing. Nodes
with common intersection of size two belong to a unique local context.
Consequently the global enumeration does not rely on probabilistic hashes.

\paragraph{Resynthesizing four-point stars.}
For each four-set $B$, extract every sum of $x_{B\cup\{u\}}$ used by a node
outside that star or by a designated output. Replace the entire star
subgraph by a cancellation-free circuit for precisely those sums. A
reproducible greedy common-subexpression rule combines the pair of current
terms that occurs in most requested sums; ties are broken by increasing
support cardinality, union mask, and operand masks. An already constructed
union is reused. Every maintained expression is a partition of its requested
support. Thus each replacement addition is disjoint and each boundary
coefficient vector is unchanged.

Canonical ordering puts intact global pairs before the remaining partners.
The 20475 stars require 365 distinct templates. The independent C++ checker
verifies both operands and their disjointness at every template gate and
checks every required boundary sum against the independently reconstructed
global DAG. This replaces 3006276 additions by at most 2623060. Hence the
complete producer has at most
\[
 c=10857762,\qquad q=983178,\qquad R=c+q=11840940
\]
reversible auxiliary roles. The bound remains valid if a replacement creates
unused nodes: delete them and after stage sharing, pad by untouched auxiliary roles with source
matrix zero and sink matrix $I_m$ when exact width is wanted.

\paragraph{Nondegenerate source spans.}
Label every producer node by the rational span $U$ of its source indicators.
Each retained node, including every replacement star node, has a common
pair $\{a,b\}$. Every vector $u\in U$ satisfies
$u_a=u_b=t$ and $\sum_i u_i=5t$, so
\[
 u^{\mathsf T}Hu=\sum_{i\notin\{a,b\}}u_i^2.
\]
If the right side vanishes, $\sum_{i\notin\{a,b\}}u_i=3t$ gives $t=0$.
Thus $U$ is positive definite. Along every addition edge source supports
and their spans are nested. Every side-output source meets its target in
exactly two points, so $U\subset t_S^\perp$. Orthogonal complements supply
the increasing labels in the reversed computation as in the shared-point
construction. No binary orthonormal-basis condition is imposed on these
rational labels.

The retained total $A_C$ has source span of dimension $h-2$. Indeed, its
coordinates outside $C$ are the indicators of all triples on $h-2$ points,
which span the whole rational coordinate space: differences of triples give
all coordinate differences, and any triple has nonzero coordinate sum.
The two coordinates on $C$ are both one third of the outside coordinate sum.

\paragraph{Transparent retained-total schedule.}
The retained-total wrapper follows PRs~\#4 and~\#6; the paired input ordering
follows the aligned common-point idea in PR~\#2. Use the outgoing-use/pivot
compiler on this addition DAG. Its invertible
mixer $L$ uses one role per addition plus one per output use. Write $V$ for
source copy, $R_0$ for retained-total scatter, and $J$ for the negative side
injection. In chronological order apply
\[
 L,-R_0,-J,L^{-1},V,L,R_0,J,L^{-1},-V.
\]
For arbitrary initial auxiliary vector $z$, this subtracts $(R_0+J)Lz$,
adds $(R_0+J)L(z+Vx)$, restores $z$, and changes the target by $x$.
Its inverse gives the inverse shear. The three tensor stages send
$(X,Y)$ to $(-Y,X)$; a final pointwise negation on $X$, at its existing full
frame, produces the exact exchange and adds zero edge rank.

Use the original prefix decomposition $A=B\perp P$ and future line $Q$.
Set $D_0=(B\otimes F)\otimes Q$, $D_1=(A\otimes F)\otimes Q$, and
$D_U=D_0\perp(P\otimes U\otimes Q)$. Early mixers and scatters have frame
$D_0$, the source copy its source-line frame, the middle mixer $D_U$, the
retained scatter $D_0$, the side injection its target-complement frame,
and cleanup $D_1$. Only the retained output roles decrease, from $D_U$ to
$D_0$, by $h-2$ each. In the inverse schedule the retained scatter is at
$D_1$ and the following inverse mixer uses $D_{U^\perp}$, giving the same
loss. The source and target data boundaries are the original tensor-stage
boundaries. Thus total decreasing dimension is
\[
 L_{\rm b}=3v^2\binom h2(h-2).
\]

\paragraph{Sharing first- and third-stage banks.}
An explicit neighbor permutation $\pi$ of the five-sets is obtained by
pairing the ground points. For a set containing no full pair, keep the two
singletons in its least indexed pairs and flip its other three singletons.
For one full pair, keep it and flip the three singletons. For two full pairs,
flip the singleton, and move the unordered pair of full-pair indices to the
next edge of a fixed Euler circuit of the complete graph on the other
thirteen pair indices. That graph has even degree twelve. Its Euler edges
are distinct, consecutive edges meet in exactly one vertex, and their cyclic
successor is a bijection. The three cases preserve their classes and all
have $|T\cap\pi(T)|=2$.

Match the stage-1 bank with fixed coordinates $(A,B)$ to the stage-3 bank
with fixed coordinates $(B,\pi(A))$, keeping local role indices. Each
invocation restores arbitrary auxiliary inputs. The joining labels are
\[
 E=F\otimes\langle t_A\rangle\otimes\langle t_B\rangle,
 \qquad H=(\langle t_B\otimes t_{\pi(A)}\rangle)^\perp\otimes F.
\]
Orthogonality in the middle factor gives $E\subset H$. Their dimensions
are $h$ and $m-h$. Replacing the separate endpoint edges by this join
removes one role and exactly $m$ rank units, preserving the absolute deficit.
All roles retain the required source/sink matrices. Therefore
\[
 W_{\rm b}=2v^2(v+R)=230640858616896000,
 \quad s_{\rm b}=W_{\rm b}m-N+2L_{\rm b}
 =5063027748645128371200.
\]
The extra $N$ source ranks come from the unchanged negative source projections.
Consequently
\[
 \eta_{\rm b}=\frac{W_{\rm b}m-s_{\rm b}}{W_{\rm b}m}
 =\frac{39}{520019360}>
 \frac3{400000000}\frac{9997}{1000},
 \qquad \log(21952)<\frac{9997}{1000}.
\]
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:prime-field-bit-interface}
The fixed $\mathbb F_3$ circuit exchanges the two data banks, restores every
auxiliary input, and satisfies the all-role rational endpoint identity with
width $W_{\rm b}$ and rank sum $s_{\rm b}$ above. Its interchange exponent is
$\tau=1-3/400000000$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The scalar schedule, nested rational frames, bank matching and negative
source projections just established give the interface. The strict
rank-ratio comparison and the finite-alphabet argument below give the exponent.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Finite-alphabet interchange.}
The address radix in the rational-shear construction is still the fixed
prime chosen to avoid the rational matrix denominators; it is unrelated to
the payload field $\mathbb F_3$. Represent original bits as payload symbols
zero and one. Recursive role streams may contain symbol two. Address
permutations commute with pointwise $\mathbb F_3$ gates exactly as they do
with XOR gates, and all local gates are fixed finite tables. At completion
the corrected scalar permutation restores the original payload symbols.
Thus the all-role endpoint identity and the recurrence
$F_k\le(s_{\rm b}/W_{\rm b})F_{k-1}+O(1)$ hold over this fixed alphabet.
There is no conversion between binary and ternary numerical addresses and
no multiplicative per-level encoding cost. Existing record streams may be
processed bit-position by bit-position with this fixed payload alphabet.
