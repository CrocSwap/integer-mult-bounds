\subsection{Three geometric rank classes in the retained ternary network}
\label{sec:batched-bit-rank-accounting}

The five-subset producer of PR~\#7 is retained. Put
\[
 h=28,\quad v=\binom{28}{5}=98280,\quad m=h^3=21952,
 \quad R=11840940,\quad N=v^3,
\]
\[
 W=2v^2(v+R),\qquad
 L=3v^2\binom h2(h-2),\qquad
 s=Wm-N+2L.
\]
This subsection needs the producer's proved width upper bound and frame
schedule; it does not enumerate its internal addition graph again.
If the producer uses fewer than $R$ roles, insert dummy auxiliary roles
with identity gates at the ordinary low and high frames of every invocation.
Do this before identifying the first- and third-stage banks. Every dummy
role restores its arbitrary scalar, has no decreasing edge, and contributes
exactly $m$ to the final rank sum. Thus the stated width and the rank classes
below are realized exactly, including any padding.

Write $a=h^{j-1}$ at tensor stage $j$. With the notation of the retained
frame proof, the early auxiliary frame is
\[
 D_0=(B\otimes F)\otimes Q,\qquad \dim D_0=(a-1)h,
\]
and the final auxiliary frame is
\[
 D_1=(A\otimes F)\otimes Q,\qquad \dim D_1=ah.
\]
The two auxiliary banks give the following edges.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Each role joining stages one and three passes from a frame of
      dimension $h$ to a containing frame of dimension $m-h$.
      This is a projector edge of rank $m-2h$.
      There are $B=v^2R$ such roles.
\item Each stage-two auxiliary role passes from its final frame of
      dimension $h^2$ to the full sink frame of dimension $m$.
      Its rank is $m-h^2$, again with $B=v^2R$ copies.
\end{enumerate}

There is one further large class on the data wires. The incoming physical
$X$ and $Y$ dimensions at stage $j$ are $a$ and $a-1$. Their first relevant
frames have dimensions $(a-1)h+1$ and $(a-1)h$, respectively. Hence both
entrance edges have rank
\[
 d_j=(a-1)(h-1).
\]
The remaining growth of each data role in that stage is $h-1$.
These dimensions hold for the reverse middle-stage schedule as well:
its low source copy and its target-line injection give precisely the same
physical frames. At stage three,
\[
 d_3=(h^2-1)(h-1)=21141>m/2,
\]
and there are $2N$ copies. The special negative source matrix contributes
one rank-one edge on each physical $X$ source, as in PR~\#7.
Consequently the complete data rank sum is
\[
 2N\sum_{j=1}^3\bigl(d_j+h-1\bigr)+N=2Nm-N.
\]
All remaining auxiliary changes inside an invocation have rank at most
$h$, and a stage-two auxiliary entrance has rank $h^2-h$. Thus the three
classes listed above exhaust the ranks exceeding $m/2$, although exhaustion
is not needed for the certificate: leaving any other edge unbatched can
only weaken the claimed upper bound.

Apply the large-projector batching lemma to these classes. A rank-$a_i$
projector with $a_i>m/2$ uses $m-a_i$ singleton pivot interchanges and
one contiguous interchange of $(2a_i-m)$ subfields. The resulting table is
\[
\begin{array}{c|r|r|r|r}
 \text{class}&\text{copies }B_i&\text{rank }a_i&
          \text{singleton pivots}&\text{batched width }k_i\\\hline
 \text{stage 1--3 join}&v^2R&21896&56&21840\\
 \text{stage 2 sink}&v^2R&21168&784&20384\\
 \text{stage 3 data entrance}&2v^3&21141&811&20330
\end{array}
\]
Treat every rank unit outside these three middle blocks as a singleton
interchange. The number of singleton calls is therefore exactly
\[
 S=s-\sum_{i=1}^3B_i k_i.
\]
At a parent width $e$, the child widths are $e/m$ with multiplicity $S$
and $k_i e/m$ with multiplicity $B_i$; all children operate on streams of
volume $V/W$. The finite mixed-width recurrence consequently has normalized
moment
\[
 \Psi(\tau)=\frac{S}{Wm^\tau}
   +\sum_{i=1}^3\frac{B_i}{W}\left(\frac{k_i}{m}\right)^\tau.
\]
At $\tau=1$ this is $s/(Wm)=1-\eta$, where
$\eta=39/520019360$.

\paragraph{The intermediate middle-block certificate.}
This paragraph records the first batching step. The controlled basis in
the following subsection increases its saving from $177/10^9$ to
$246/10^9$, which is the value used in the final assembly.
Set
\[
 a_{\rm b}=\frac{177}{10^9},\qquad \tau=1-a_{\rm b}.
\]
Let $r_0=1/m$, $r_i=k_i/m$, and
\[
 w_0=\frac S{Wm},\qquad w_i=\frac{B_i k_i}{Wm}.
\]
Then $\sum_iw_i=1-\eta$ and
$\Psi(1-a_{\rm b})=\sum_iw_i\exp(a_{\rm b}\log(1/r_i))$.
Use the rational logarithm upper bounds
\[
 \log m<\frac{9997}{1000},\quad
 \log\frac{196}{195}<\frac{512}{100000},\quad
 \log\frac{14}{13}<\frac{7411}{100000},\quad
 \log\frac{21952}{20330}<\frac{768}{10000}.
\]
They follow from the positive series
\[
 \log x=2\sum_{j\ge0}\frac{z^{2j+1}}{2j+1},\qquad
 z=\frac{x-1}{x+1},
\]
using sixteen terms and remainder at most
$2z^{33}/(33(1-z^2))$; for $m$, first extract its power of two.
For $0\le t<1$, $e^t\le(1-t)^{-1}$. Calling the four displayed upper
bounds $\ell_i$, exact rational arithmetic gives
\[
 \Psi(1-a_{\rm b})
 <\sum_{i=0}^3\frac{w_i}{1-a_{\rm b}\ell_i}<1.
\]
The strict gap in this last bound is greater than $1.78\times10^{-10}$.
All integers, rational logarithm enclosures, weights and the exact gap are
supplied by \texttt{scripts/batched\_bit\_rank\_moment.py} and its JSON
certificate. Under the accompanying arbitrary-width recurrence lemma,
this proves the interchange saving $a_{\rm b}=177/10^9$ for the unchanged
finite scalar producer. A smaller role upper bound can be inserted directly
into the same three-class formula.
