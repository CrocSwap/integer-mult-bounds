\begin{abstract}
The compact-control witness $\kappa=83/10^{12}$ was limited by the original
complex network, which spends one side wire per ordered neighbor pair. We
compute the same side correction with shared sums: $108195$ side roles per
invocation instead of $3693800$. Binary coordinate and pair-star labels give
every frame transition an orthonormal residual basis, after one necessary
change: no injected piece of a disjoint sum may cover every point outside its
target, because that residual is an alternating hyperbolic plane. The
complex saving rises from $418/10^{12}$ to $14/10^9$, and the retained paired
bit network becomes the only binding motif. With the compact-control
recurrence this gives the conditional witness
$\kappa=59/10^{11}>2^{-31}$, a factor $590/83\approx7.11$ above the
preceding witness. The underlying manuscript's interfaces remain assumptions.
\end{abstract}

\section{Parameters and assembly}
Put $a=296/10^{11}$ and $a_{\rm c}=14/10^9$. Choose
\begin{gather*}
 \tau=1-a,\quad\sigma=1-a_{\rm c},\quad
 \epsilon=\frac{19999}{100000},\quad c=\frac{999}{1000},\quad
 \beta=\frac1{1000},\quad\zeta=\frac1{10000},\quad\delta=\frac1{10^6},\\
 C_1=5-4\beta+\zeta=\frac{49961}{10000},\quad
 \lambda=1-\frac{2958}{10^{12}},\quad
 \lambda'=1-\frac{2956}{10^{12}},\quad
 \kappa=\frac{59}{10^{11}}.
\end{gather*}
Now $\sigma<\tau$, so the compact-control internal exponent is
$\chi=\tau+(1-\beta)\max\{\sigma-\tau,0\}=\tau$. Exact comparisons give
\[
 \max\{\tau,\sigma,\chi\}<\lambda<\lambda'<1,\qquad
 \max\{\sigma+\beta(1-\sigma),\,1-c\}<\lambda',\qquad
 \epsilon C_1=\frac{999170039}{10^9}<1.
\]
The compact-control layer theorem therefore supplies the completed
normalized-butterfly contract at cost $O(Vd^{\lambda'})$, using the complex
constants of Proposition~\ref{prop:compressed-complex-interface} and the
generalized guard with $B=s_{\rm c}+64(W_{\rm c}+m+1)^3$.

The spacing exponent enters only through $\epsilon(1+c)<1$, which gives
$K=\Theta(p^{\epsilon c})=o(\ell)$, through $K/\log p\to\infty$, and through
the reserved-axis count $O(\log d+d^{1-c}\log p)=o(d)$. All three hold. The
retained Gaussian setup needs $\epsilon<1/5$ for the cutoff
$46b^{(1+3\epsilon)/2}\le b/4$ at $b\ge2^{40}$, and gives
$\alpha=\Theta(p^{119999/400000})$ and $\gamma=O(p^{159997/200000})=o(p)$.
Prime selection has $1-2\epsilon=30001/50000>0$.

The seven assembly margins keep their form:
\begin{align*}
 g_1&=1-\epsilon(1+c),&g_2&=\epsilon c(1-\tau),\\
 g_3&=\epsilon(1-\lambda'),&g_4&=(1-\tau)(1-\epsilon),\\
 g_5&=1/4-\delta-5\epsilon/4,&g_6&=1-\delta-\epsilon,\qquad g_7=\epsilon.
\end{align*}
The former choice $c=1/5$ would now make $g_2$ binding; with $c=999/1000$,
exact arithmetic gives
\[
 G_*=\min_ig_i=g_3=\frac{14779261}{25\cdot10^{15}},\qquad
 G_*-\kappa=\frac{29261}{25\cdot10^{15}}>0.
\]
This strict gap absorbs all remaining fixed powers of $\log p$, and the
retained assembly yields
\[
 T(n)=O\bigl(n(\log n)^{1-59/10^{11}}\bigr),\qquad
 2^{-31}<\frac{59}{10^{11}}<2^{-30},
\]
conditional on the identified upstream and extension proofs.

\paragraph{The next ceiling.}
With $\sigma<\tau$ the layer needs $\lambda>\tau$, so
$g_3<\epsilon(1-\tau)=\epsilon a$, and the Gaussian margin forces
$\epsilon<1/5$. For the published bit network and these assembly inequalities,
\[
 \kappa<\frac a5=\frac{296}{5\cdot10^{11}}<2^{-30}.
\]
The witness is above $99.6\%$ of this bound. The complex motif now has
spare saving: $a_{\rm c}$ exceeds $a$ by a factor $4.7$. Further progress in
this strategy therefore needs a stronger bit network, or a Gaussian step
permitting larger $\epsilon$. This is a scoped bound, not a ceiling for other
networks or algorithms.

\begin{itemize}

\item \path{scripts/complex_network.py}, with certificate

\item \path{tests/test_complex_network.py}: label algebra against

The full check compares all $3693800$ nonzero side coefficients and all
zeros, checks all $82895$ labels and $188790$ inclusions with their residuals,
and follows all $108195$ compiled roles in both directions. These checks
support the written arguments; they are not a formal verification of the
upstream theorem, and the new construction has not received independent
review.
