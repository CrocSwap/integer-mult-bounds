\section{Exact assembly beyond $2^{-18}$}
\label{sec:translated-partial-assembly}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional multiplication saving]
Assume the pinned upstream multiplication theorem and the retained analytic,
ordered-affine, compact-control, Gaussian-map and fixed-tape interfaces
specified in the opening section. The translated partial-swap bit interface
and translated complex phase interface proved here give
\[
 T(n)=O\!\left(n(\log n)^{1-\kappa}\right),\qquad
 \boxed{\kappa=\frac{5499}{10^9}=5.499\times10^{-6}>2^{-18}}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The bit construction keeps all three PR18 scalar producers and their exact
physical histograms. Only the dedicated middle auxiliary bank endpoints
change. If $c_t$ is the prior complete child list and $B_2=2609547248000$,
replace on each such role the list $(575,31625,23,529)$ by $(23,32729)$.
The rank sum is unchanged because both lists sum to $32752=m-23$.
The dimensions, total width and rank deficit remain
\[
 m_b=32775,\quad W_b=5820554077800,\quad
 W_bm_b-s_b=82076640800.
\]
There are no zero or full-width recursive children. The proof above
establishes actual contiguous blocks in the one inherited rational basis.
With the rational logarithm and exponential enclosures of the retained
bit certificate, exact arithmetic gives
\[
 a_b=\frac{11}{10^6},\qquad
 \frac{\sum_t c_t t^{1-a_b}}{W_bm_b^{1-a_b}}
 <1-\frac{159}{10^{10}}.
\]
The retained arbitrary integer-width row construction therefore applies,
including padding, arbitrary spectator gaps and workspace cleanup.

The independent complex phase construction uses the PR7 finite circuit
with its unchanged internal frames and the translated middle auxiliary
sources. Its certificate gives $a_c=18/10^6$ and a strict moment gap
greater than $666/10^{11}$. Its new path bound is $q=22876$, maximum
child rank $21924$, and path exponent $\rho=2$. In particular the old
untranslated guard is not being used. The bit and complex dimensions are
allowed to differ by the retained arbitrary-width interface.

Take
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \tau&=1-\frac{11}{10^6},&\sigma&=1-\frac{18}{10^6},\\
 \epsilon&=\frac1{2+a_b}=\frac{1000000}{2000011},&c&=1,\\
 \beta&=\frac1{1000},&\delta&=\frac1{10^{16}},\\
 \lambda&=\tau+\frac1{10^{20}},&\lambda'&=\tau+\frac2{10^{20}},\\
 \zeta&=\frac1{10000},&C_1&=2-\beta+\zeta=\frac{19991}{10000}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The compact-control internal exponent is $\tau$ because $\sigma<\tau$.
The complex leaf saving $(1-\beta)a_c=17982/10^9$ is greater than
$a_b$. Thus the internal, leaf and reservation exponents are below
$\lambda'$. The strict gaps absorb fixed logarithmic factors. Also
$1-\epsilon C_1>0$, so the new precision guard remains admissible.

For the seven retained assembly margins,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 g_1&=1-2\epsilon,&g_2&=\epsilon a_b,&
 g_3&=\epsilon(a_b-2/10^{20}),\\
 g_4&=(1-\epsilon)a_b,&g_5&=1-\delta-2\epsilon,&
 g_6&=1-\delta-\epsilon,&g_7&=\epsilon,
\end{aligned}
\]
the minimum is $g_5=a_b/(2+a_b)-\delta$. Exact subtraction yields
\[
 \min_i g_i-\kappa
 =\frac{11}{2000011}-\frac1{10^{16}}-\frac{5499}{10^9}
 >\frac9{10^{10}}>0.
\]
All 29 strict interface constraints are evaluated exactly by the same
certificate. The positive $1-2\epsilon$ and $1-\epsilon(1+c)$ supply
the eventual Gaussian and spacing conditions. Use the retained corrected
chirp bound $F=2^{\lceil1.14\alpha^2\rceil}$ at precision $P=34p$.
No analytic or rounding hypothesis is removed. The positive absorption
margin proves the displayed conditional bound.
\end{proof}

The headline is the final multiplication saving $\kappa$, rather than
the bit-component saving $a_b$. It exceeds both $2^{-19}$ and $2^{-18}$,
and is below $2^{-17}$. These are asymptotic exponent comparisons, not
claims of practical running-time speedups or linear-time multiplication.
