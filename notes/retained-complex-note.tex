\begin{abstract}
A complex producer combines disjoint rectangles with retained pair roots and doubled point totals,
then scatters them at one common low/full frame. At $h=24$ this supports
$1-\sigma=750/10^{11}$. With the existing compact-control construction and
paired-bit producer, the exact conditional witness is
$\kappa=591/10^{12}>2^{-31}$, a factor $591/83\simeq7.12$ above the preceding
compact-control witness. The original fixed-tape and analytic interfaces
remain assumptions; this is not a full formal verification or runtime result.
\end{abstract}
The source is OpenAI's \emph{Integer multiplication below $n\log n$}, pinned at
\begin{center}\small\texttt{adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a}.\end{center}
We retain Douglas Colkitt's compact-control movement, layout and generalized
guard arguments, paired-bit producer, Gaussian setup, and the original
resampling, synthetic-transform, error and rounding interfaces.
