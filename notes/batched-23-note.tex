\begin{abstract}
We batch rational projector blocks and whole complex residuals in the
retained finite networks. A controlled common basis yields contiguous
recursive calls; mixed-width moment bounds and a dependency-path precision
guard give a saving beyond $2^{-23}$. The construction includes integer
remainders, bounded row padding, and a corrected Gaussian input error
bound. The conclusion is conditional on the pinned upstream multiplication
theorem and the retained finite-network and analytic interfaces.
\end{abstract}

\section{Result and retained framework}
The model is a fixed finite-alphabet Turing machine with a fixed number
of one-dimensional tapes. Section~\ref{sec:batched-final-assembly} gives
the rational parameters and strict assembly margins.
All constants defining the finite networks, their rational bases,
the address radix, and the number of tapes are fixed independently
of $n$. The scalar domains and the address-label fields are distinct.
The interchange network carries fixed $\mathbb F_3$ payloads and uses
rational address matrices. The complex network carries Gaussian dyadic
values and uses binary subspace labels.

The finite constructions are those of Zhihao Chen's PR~\#7
\cite{pr7}, at commit
\begin{center}
\texttt{6725c6a17b17871a35353fd29157f4ed851bc114}.
\end{center}
That contribution supplies the ternary five-subset network, the paired
complex producer, their frame schedules, and their finite counts.
It builds on Douglas Colkitt's compact-control framework
\cite{main}, Bortlesboat's alignment \cite{pr2}, eumemic's complex
producer and Gaussian resampling \cite{pr3,pr5,pr6}, and dleen's
retained totals and stage sharing \cite{pr4}. The upstream manuscript
is pinned at OpenAI commit
\texttt{adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a}
\cite{upstream}.
