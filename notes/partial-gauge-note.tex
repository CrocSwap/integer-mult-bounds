\begin{abstract}
A cancellation-free interval and cube producer, backward nondegenerate
frame enlargement, and chronologically ordered partial source gauges give
a strict complex saving $92651/10^9$. Applying the stopped opposite-bank
compiler to a literal deferred bit word supplies the bit interface.
Under the retained analytic, semantic-precision and fixed-tape interfaces,
the exact assembly yields $T(n)=O(n(\log n)^{1-\kappa})$ with
$\kappa=7237/78125000=9.26336\times10^{-5}$.
\end{abstract}
