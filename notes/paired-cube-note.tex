\begin{abstract}
A paired-coordinate triple motif decomposes the identity into signed
coarse queries, coordinate-star centers and an involution implemented
on the original source registers. Complete arbitrary-dirty cores share
one auxiliary bank across three orthogonal address blocks. The exact
selected ledgers, globally optimal bit gauge subset and retained semantic
assembly yield $T(n)=O(n(\log n)^{1-0.000461028508707})$ under the inherited
interfaces.
\end{abstract}
