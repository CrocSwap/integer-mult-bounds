\begin{abstract}
PR29 (Zhihao Chen) composes Paureel's two-stage partial-swap topology,
including its paid rank-one copy correction, with icekylinx's
retained-total producers and controlled inner basis at factor dimensions
$(55,53)$. Every finite object in that construction is parametrized by the
factor dimensions $(a,b)$ with $a=b+2$. We re-run the same construction at
$(a,b)=(47,45)$: both producers are rebuilt from source with the exact
label audit, the common-basis prescriptions and modular nonvanishing
witnesses are regenerated, and the complete physical list is recomputed.
The bit saving becomes $16039/10^9$. Keeping the PR21 complex circuit and
PR23 semantic/bulk transfer gives the conditional final saving
$\kappa=16038/10^9>2^{-16}$, compared with PR29's $15536/10^9$. No new
analytic assumption is introduced; this is not a formal verification or a
practical multiplication implementation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Why the construction is dimension-generic}
Each step of PR29's argument is stated for general $h$ or general
$(a,b)$ with $a=b+2$, apart from finitely many concrete checks, which we
redo at the new dimensions.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Scalar interfaces.} For any $h$, $v_h=\binom h3$, the metric
$I-J/9$, the identity $[|S\cap T|=1]+|S\cap T|\equiv[S=T]\pmod 2$, hence
$JLV=I$ and the chronological word $L,J,L^{-1},V,L,J,L^{-1},V$, hold verbatim.
\item \emph{Producers and labels.} The cancellation-free paired exclusion
producer, equal-support sharing, positive labels and oriented carrier
matching are the unchanged PR24/18 algorithms; the exact integer label audit
of PR29 is rerun at $h=45,47$ (Section~\ref{sec:producers}).
\item \emph{Two stages, endpoints and correction.} Section~3 of PR29 uses
only projector identities $D_AD_B=D_{B-A}$ ($A\le B$), $TF=E$ and rank
counts $m-a$, $m-b$; it is dimension-free.
\item \emph{Common basis.} The row labels
$R=[0,\ldots,a-1,1,\ldots,a-1,0]$ and inverse-column labels
$C=[a-1,0,\ldots,a-2,0,\ldots,a-1]$ have effective residues $\{0,1,3\}$ and
$\{b-5,b-3,b-2\}$ modulo $b$; at $b=45$ these are $\{0,1,3\}$ and
$\{40,42,43\}$, disjoint. The certificate constructs all $45$ permutation
completions $M_\beta$ and checks both incidence trees of size $a$.
The direct-contraction corner formulas then give diagonal invertible
auxiliary corners of sizes $a=47$ and $b=45$ for every local $A,B$.
\item \emph{Data null-corner.} The macro $(I-P)\otimes(I-Q)$ has nullity
$d=a+b-1=91$. The certificate supplies normalized line coordinates modulo
$1{,}000{,}003$ (three independent seeds) for which the $91$-square
null-corner $Z$ has full rank. As in PR29, lifting to integers with
normalization denominators $\equiv1$ modulo this prime gives a nonzero
rational determinant at one point of the irreducible
$\mathrm{GL}_a\times\mathrm{GL}_b$ family, hence a nonzero rational function.
\item \emph{Finite product.} Finitely many physical local projectors and data
pairs give finitely many nonzero rational functions; their product is
nonzero, so one common rational basis satisfies all ordered-pivot
requirements, after which the address prime is chosen.
\end{enumerate}

With $a=a_b$, $b=a_c$, $h=10^{-8}$, $\beta=1/10$ and PR23's definitions
\[

The minimum margin is $G$, and $G-16038/10^9>4.8\times10^{-10}$, while
$16039/10^9$ is rejected.

\begin{theorem}
Under the retained analytic and fixed-tape interfaces of PR23/PR29,
\[
 T(n)=O\!\left(n(\log n)^{1-\kappa}\right),\qquad
 \kappa=\frac{16038}{10^9}=1.6038\times10^{-5}>2^{-16}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Sections 2--4 establish the bit interface exactly as PR29 does, with the
new finite evidence. The complex interface and semantic bound are retained
by source hash; the product stock supplies both recursions; the exact
margins instantiate the PR23 assembly.
\end{proof}
