It certifies the sign across a bracket of the **reference root**, not at an
arbitrary nominal exponent. This identity prices a declared profile change;
it does not establish that the profile or transport is legal.

## What the pending two-transport census actually covers

PR1392's stated domain starts from the frozen PR589 word. A move is an
elementary ADD transport within its A or B section, crossing only commuting
operations and preserving frame nesting and retained COPY frames. Its frame
choices are the original **636-frame catalogue plus the current slot's own
rational lower-bound closure**. The catalogue does not accumulate unrelated
earlier closures, and both intermediate words must be legal.

The producer reports 1,452,893 legal single transports, 59,998 distinct
overlapping ordered operation pairs, and 1,379,084 final pair representatives,
with no better or unresolved result. These count different objects; they are
not interchangeable literal path counts. Its proof separately reduces repeated
operation pairs to singles and register-disjoint pairs to baseline single
deltas. This map has **not rerun either full census** and does not replace the
pending review with the producer's report.

Even after the stated replay obligation is met, that result would concern this
frozen class. It would not cover three or more moves, growing catalogues,
arbitrary subspaces, simultaneous changes through illegal intermediates, global
circuit optimality, native hosting, or a new selected multiplication exponent.
PR933's grouped moves and global frame reoptimization form another domain;
the retained PR1562 witness explains why its first-position quotient cannot
be transferred on contact equivalence alone.
