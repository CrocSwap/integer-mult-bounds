## Construction and accounting

Jeremy Kothe's #622 replaces twelve copied face packets with direct reads of
unformed leavers in four K-triangles. It reduces each provider from 504 to 492
helpers. The schedule keeps the target frames inside their legal hyperplanes;
exact scalar identities and the complete dirty-input restoration are checked.
The porting step uses a heuristic to transfer the older optimization stages.
Acceptance comes from the subsequent exact all-column, native-frame, wrapper,
phase and global audits, not from that heuristic.

The generalized accounting distinguishes the actual provider count R=492 from
the fixed parent template R0=504. There are 716 columns per native word,
35,424 hosted helper roles, 7,168 live rows and 7,670 physical rows. The parent
stock remains 11,704 live / 12,218 physical. Width-8 spot checks follow the
constructor's renumbering map; they have not been removed. The rank mass remains
520,704 and the deficit remains 2,560. Gains come from the child-width and
hosting profiles, not a larger deficit.

The new frame-regroup steps preserve the scalar operations and check their
sequential profile deltas, omissions and over-promotions. The cold-end guards
from #617 remain relevant: exact scalar endpoints alone would not catch a
compensation term that illicitly reads an early-ended helper. Those independent
wrapper/compensation checks remain in the replay.

The contributor's PROOF-NOTE-R describes 32 additional scratch revert controls.
Their source and original reports were not submitted; this review does not
claim to have reproduced that particular suite. The submitted strict replay's
construction, local, generated-identity, controller and admission controls are
recorded individually in the maintainer receipts.

## Research contributions

**#619, Chafik Boukhalfa:** an exact ceiling for the fixed #615 supplier family
and a finer final rounding grid. The proof relaxes chronology, uses integer
width-pattern bounds and checks 92 capacity/remainder cases. It does not bound
#617 or the K-triangle providers. Its fine-grid κ is a historical-family
refinement, not a new selected frontier. Independent enumeration covers all
915 multisets; explicit rejection tests complement the strict replay.

**#621, Ali Romman:** a uniform analytic inverse bound for prime-power cyclic
polynomials. The written proof uses reduction modulo a prime ideal, a nonzero
resultant, a unit-circle lower bound and an absolutely convergent Fourier
inverse. The two exact arithmetic engines agree on overlapping examples; the
second also checks 36 finite cyclic inverse identities. These examples support
the reviewed proof but do not prove the uniform theorem by enumeration. No paid
tape implementation, efficient preparation, rounding/restoration theorem or new
multiplication saving follows from this submission alone.

**#620, Joel Pulikkan / GamingPuzzled:** retained as preliminary research,
partially reviewed. The E8 decoder, extension-ring and cold-end reports clearly
separate successful instances, unfinished searches and numerical screens.
Several SAT infeasibility claims have no proof logs, and scripts rely on
unshipped author-local directories. The aggregate LP lead is not a literal
owner certificate. These claims are not promoted to independently verified
barriers. A portable reproduction package and exact certificates or independently
replayed scoped solver runs remain needed before closing this draft.

## Reproduction and limits

Original source bytes and notices are preserved. To avoid overwriting earlier
packages, #618 lives at `research/complex-571-cold14-consolidated618` and #624 at
`research/complex-571-cold14-k492-repacked624`; their historical instructions name
the original paths. Use these relocated paths for their own scripts. The complete
changed-file mapping is in `research/community-k492-audit/CONTENT-COVERAGE.json`.
Some inherited README boilerplate calls verifier code unchanged from #604; the
new K-triangle and generalized-R code is explicitly reviewed above and in the
package's newer proof notes, rather than relying on that historical wording.

The new #622 baseline and both suppliers are replayed fresh. Allocation variants
reuse these exact provider outputs after checking their source definitions and
semantic identities, while rebuilding their typed construction chains and
replaying their own global accounting. #618/#619 reuse separately checked prior
native providers and the unchanged fresh #617 baseline. The evidence inventory
records reuse explicitly. Contributor-reported Linux runs are distinguished
from this maintainer's macOS execution and from remote CI.

The selected composition is bound to the unchanged, previously replayed #543
bit source and its consumed receipts. `make frontier626-verify` rebuilds both
suppliers from scratch, checks the finite helper interface, the independent
owner check and all 47 strict assembly inequalities.

These are conditional finite constructions. All-size compilation, common-label
and mutable-basis realization, pooled whole-call hosting, normalized residuals,
precision/tape/exact recovery and analytic-transfer assumptions remain. Inherited
Lean literals and exact finite Clifford checks are not a complete Lean proof of
integer multiplication. No practical runtime speedup or global optimality is claimed.
