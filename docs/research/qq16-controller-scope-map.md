# Which QQ16 observation contract has been checked?

Use this map before changing a QQ16 first pair or adding a query. The
[retained local words](../../research/joint-query/README.md) do not by
themselves close a controller. Later evidence separates three questions:
whether a read detects one witness, whether it recovers the original selected
inputs given an intermediate cut, and whether the final records supply that
cut. A positive answer to the first or second question does not answer the next.

## Common notation and the observation cut

The selected sets `A,T` have eight ports each; `U` has 96. Write
`B_s = R_s iota_U` for the ordinary-source query in chart `s`. Each query
returns eight coordinates per owner. These are logical observations, not a
paid recursive-child rank or a native implementation.

The fixed first pair is `F0` followed by `B_(1,2)` on bank pair 0. The retained
local-call pairs, at stages 0 through 8, are `0,0,0,1,1,2,2,3,3`.
An ordinary bridge acts on `U` by
`X_dst += c X_src; Y_src -= c Y_dst`. The terminal is
`(X_U,Y_U) -> (Y_U,-X_U)`. Thus the **named final Y** is the completed-X
descendant; named final X carries the former-Y field. Its name alone does not
make it an allowed completed-X observation.

The distinction between the selected state **immediately after B1** and the
selected state **at the end** is essential. If a construction promises only
the latter, a decoder using the former needs a separate retention or
reconstruction argument, with its physical cost.

## How the positive and negative results fit together

PR2174 reconstructs the initial ordinary Y input through the ordered identity
`Y_(0,U)^initial = Y_(0,U)^final - K_(2,U) f2`. It then uses `q0,q2` and
`p_A,p_T,d_A` at B1 to recover the original selected inputs. The B1 record
`d_A` is an explicit input to that decoder, not a consequence of the two
initial reads.

PR2346 supplies a family where every allowed final record is zero but the
post-B1 `d_A` is nonzero. Its proof obtains compatible ordinary inputs from an
intersection of two constrained query images. Each image has dimension at
least `ceil(32|G|/7)` in an `8|G|`-dimensional space; after the required
invertible selected transport, their intersection has dimension at least
`ceil(8|G|/7)`. The written proof supplies the regular-cover existence
argument; its finite fixtures alone do not establish that quantifier.

There is therefore no conflict between the two results. Adding records while
removing a previously granted observation cut is not a monotone enlargement
of the observation set. Likewise, PR2079's detection of one family and
PR2093's different invisible family can both hold.

## Open next questions

For one proposed successor, first state its first maps, every ordinary read,
the exact time each record is taken, subsequent selected interventions, and
which native endpoint types are observable. Then compare its complete
selected-input kernel with the required output, rather than testing only a
known witness. This is a task-definition step, not a claim that the resulting
question is unclaimed.

* **A changed first pair:** identify a hypothesis outside the 252-pair
  catalogue, such as a changed logger or response. This avoids repeating a
  covered route choice. The smallest test is the ordered first-pair map with
  its compensated witness; a native boundary and full cost remain blockers.
* **A final-only successor to PR2174:** show that its actual available records
  recover `p_A,p_T,d_A`, or specify paid retention of those values. Compare the
  early dynamics with PR2346 before using its obstruction. Full recovery and
  legal native access are separate requirements; detecting that one family
  is only the first test.

Only after recovery survives should a candidate price all reads, retention,
frame/cover maps, restoration, hosting, address work and precision. None of
the six entries supplies a completed QQ16 supplier or changes the selected
exponent. The related shear, tap, native-line and transcript-minimum work is
outside this small index; consult the live board before choosing a mechanism.
