## What changed

The complex construction uses the same coordinate partition in both target
tetrahedra. An invertible basis of eight retired source rows finishes the
centers earlier. Own-block contributions can then enter targets directly,
and selected cross-block contributions arrive through two existing face
copies. This removes 56 assembled packet sums and reduces helpers from 560
to 504. The exact source-frontier check reconstructs all fourteen bases;
each determinant is 1/4, so the required inverse is dyadic.

Seventy-two line gauges and eighty shared-donor pivots reduce selected helper
entrances. A further 248 frame promotions coalesce actual delivery climbs.
Their legality requires predecessor containment, successor containment and
the fixed center/target barriers. The scalar word, chronology and boundary
frames remain fixed during that pass. Fresh scalar and phase checks cover
the resulting word, rather than pricing the old word with a new histogram.

Every one of 36,288 helper uses receives one whole idle physical family for
its complete invocation. The audit checks 728 distinct active owners per
call, actual bridge-aware idle windows, 2,352 orthogonal charts, spectator
coordinates and all 7,968 residual children. Physical stock is 7,682;
moment normalization uses 7,168 live data families. They are not substituted
for one another in the finite invoice. Global rank mass is 520,704 at
width 73, leaving deficit 2,560. The changed distribution of child ranks
improves the moment while retaining that deficit.

The bit construction uses 489 helpers and allows sources to carry partial
sums. This requires a structural check beyond the older checker's rule that
sources are never written. No target may feed a source/helper; compensation
occurs at frame zero; the source/helper transformation is invertible; its
literal inverse runs at the common full frame. Exact replay separately
checks the required data shear and restoration of every arbitrary input.
Two scalar-neutral mutations violating the no-feedback condition are rejected
by the structural checker even though scalar replay alone accepts them.

## Scope and research notes

This is a **reviewed conditional finite construction**, not an unconditional
integer-multiplication theorem or a new Lean formalization. In particular,
the following remain explicit interface assumptions: all-size bit/Gaussian
compilation; common-label and mutable-basis realization; normalized residual
wrappers as uniformly priced operations; whole-call correlated-helper hosting;
compressed windows and restored rows; common grids, tape and precision;
and the analytic transfer to multiplication. Exact finite wrapper identities
do not on their own establish their uniform tape implementation.

The controller's retained data-phase derivation remains inherited. This review
checks the port restriction and exact dependency identities, rather than
claiming a new independent derivation of every upstream phase polynomial.

Research notes, earlier negative experiments, discovery scripts and the
fixed-word ceiling are included with their original scope and attribution.
The fixed-word ceiling and ancillary family-optimality discussions were **not
independently certified** in this integration and are not needed for the
positive witness. Cited alternative complex rates in #493 are not selected
here; the disputed split-residual construction is not used. Dedicated Linux
workflows are retained; these maintainer execution receipts are local runs.

## Reproduce

Install NumPy and SymPy and provide a C++17 compiler and Boost headers, then:

```sh
python3 -m pip install -r research/e8-bit-carrier-skeleton/requirements.txt
make frontier531-verify
```

Set `BOOST_INCLUDE` or `CXX` if they are outside the system defaults. The runner
rebuilds all three finite packages, runs the additional controls and joins the
fresh dependencies. `make selected-record-check` only checks the frozen source,
certificate and proof bindings; it does not replace that reconstruction.
