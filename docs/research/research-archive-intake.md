# Research archive intake and verification status

Research packages listed here are accepted for preservation and collaboration. **Archival acceptance is not mathematical approval, an independent replay, or a selected-frontier update.** Original source and contributor attribution are retained. The central [PR ledger](pr-ledger.md) keeps these submissions `partially_reviewed`, including after GitHub marks them merged. Their remaining obligations are recorded below.

## Review lanes

- **Archive intake:** new isolated research paths only; authenticate exact author Git blobs/modes, full changed-file inventory, syntax and available local manifest pins. No execution of submitted code is implied. Historical author evidence stays attributed as such.
- **Validated research:** targeted mechanism/proof review and required source-bound checks completed within a stated scope.
- **Shared tooling:** review changed behavior and affected consumers before integrating. Archive intake cannot approve production changes.
- **Selected frontier:** complete applicable supplier/outer replay and mathematical review with inherited assumptions explicit. Only `certificates/selected-result.json` selects the result.

Known counterexamples, misleading scope and unresolved changes to shared code stay out of this archive lane. A failed search is not an impossibility theorem. An archive claim may be wrong; verify it before relying on it. Prior expensive-check timeouts remain open obligations, not negative mathematical results.

## Efficient queue procedure

Use the [delta-only intake planner](pr-intake.md) to reconcile the whole queue
and select up to 100 **actionable** heads. Unchanged explicit holds remain dormant;
unfinished review remains visible. Preserve exact-source evidence by reference
and record changed decisions only, rather than producing another full audit
package per nominal batch. Group related construction chains, preserve contributor
credit and check live heads before integration. Archival publication and scientific
validation remain separate.

No author workload was run for this intake. Local expensive verification remains serial under the sampled 1 GiB descendant-process watchdog, with explicit time limits and no unchanged automatic retries after resource stops. No Lean or Docker was run. Remote CI, if triggered by publication, is separate evidence and is not represented as a local pass.
