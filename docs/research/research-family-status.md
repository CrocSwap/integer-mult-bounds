## Contribution acceptance and family expansion are different gates

**A contributor may finish one useful subproblem.** A checked local identity,
counterexample, interface lemma, cost bound or independent audit can merit a
focused PR without a complete supplier or a new kappa. State the exact result,
dependencies and remaining obligations. No additional standalone session report
is required. A handoff without a PR is also useful.

**Expanding a family requires an explicit research decision.** This is an
evidence checkpoint, not an approval queue. A solo or looping agent can make
and record the decision in its existing task/thread; no new permission or
separate director agent is required. Before another large search,
implementation expansion or round of downstream optimization, update the
canonical investigation thread or this record with:

1. The exact source heads, declared target and current evidence status:
   reviewed, independently reproduced, author-checked, proposed or local-only.
2. A boundary specification: inputs, outputs, frames, owned/borrowed state,
   required reads, dirty compensation and eventual restoration.
3. A complete obligation ledger. Mark each cost **proved**, **constructively
   upper-bounded**, or **unknown**. Unknowns are not zero. A favorable optimistic
   relaxation can keep an idea open but cannot admit a supplier. An unfavorable
   relaxation may reject its stated model.
4. Why the next subtask can change the decision: what work disappears, what
   replaces it and why it does not return in the consumer or cleanup. Explain
   the cost allowance for a missing module before optimizing that module.
5. Continue, narrow, expand, pause or replace; a bounded next test; a stop rule;
   and the evidence that would reopen a paused route. Compare a different
   mechanism before continuing solely because existing code is easy to extend.

Design exploration is allowed before a constructive upper bound exists. Its
deliverable is a contract, discriminating example or budget decision, explicitly
labelled exploratory. It should not silently grow into an implementation campaign.
For an audit, identify the particular acceptance gap and downstream consequence;
do not demand an invented kappa gain from verification work.

## Distributing many agents

Keep the broad track allocation and prefer live unclaimed handoffs. Refine a
task by **mechanism, changed contract, exact obligation and bounded domain**.
Separate alternative constructions from deliberately independent checks.
Different random seeds, larger censuses and renamed helper types are not by
themselves distinct mechanisms. Useful small improvements remain welcome.

Use one existing canonical thread per family/question, with its latest result,
source pins, remaining costs, active scoped tasks and next review trigger near
the top. Do not require every contributor to maintain a second status file.
When all immediately useful tasks in a family are claimed, choose a distinct
obligation or a different family rather than manufacturing a peripheral PR.

Keep negative proofs tied to decisions. A scoped closure merits preservation;
its ever-narrower extensions do not automatically merit more assigned effort.
Corrections, independent verification and a genuinely changed premise can reopen
it. No finite failed search establishes a universal limitation.

Maintainers should refresh these entry points after a reviewed completion,
decisive scoped obstruction, changed supplier or substantial queue review.
Preserve historical pins and attributions; update the task catalogue, guide and
priority log together when assignments change. Current weights are allocation
choices, not estimates of success probabilities or proof that the portfolio is
optimal.
