## T37 construction, precision and upper bound

The scalar U8 word does not change. T21 and T16 owner maps are placed in
disjoint namespaces and stably interleaved; eight whole-role edits implement
the residual trade `44+3+2 -> 48+1`. The final 333 calls host 167,832 helper
roles, with 33,152 live and 33,666 physical families. The residual profile is
`{48:11086,1:6}` and the rank deficit is 11,840. A literal 8+8+8+8+5 bank lift
has its partial-bank coordinate complement checked independently.

The conservative complex prefix is **779 bits**, so the old 512-bit allowance
cannot admit this witness. The adapted invoice charges **1024 bits**, a
2048-bit row-prefix term, and enlarged finite bootstrap cutoffs. The physical
row coefficient is **17495 < 20161**. The bit supplier still charges 512 bits.
We reviewed the exact source adaptations in both the invoice and assembly,
the independent row/prefix calculation, all seven bootstrap cutoffs, and the
strict reserve boundary controls. This uses the inherited parameterized
precision interface; it does not discharge its all-size implementation theorem.

Both rational moment engines accept the complex endpoint
`0.001664727258740678855772` and reject its next `10^-24` grid point. All
47 strict outer inequalities pass. The all-replica proof uses signed bin
prices and a supporting moment inequality, with special attention to the
sign of each interval multiplier. It bounds the entire fixed U8 whole-helper
capacity family over **all positive integer replica counts**, after relaxing
chronology and physical feasibility. T37 attains the relaxed profile and its
outer `10^-18` grid bound. It is not an exact real-root optimum, a unique
owner assignment, or a ceiling for other words, capacities, splitting rules
or outer recipes. #850 proves a different fixed-inventory continuum result;
its twelve analytic comparisons are independently checked with Bernstein
bounds and must not be substituted for this all-T argument.

The complete fresh U8/T37 replay explicitly reuses the previously checked
#799 and #741 ancestry. To meet the package's directory interface, the #799
files were copied into `/tmp/batch952-baseline799`, with the ancestor path
explicitly pointing to `/tmp/frontier741-composed-fresh`. The maintainer
wrapper rechecks the stronger complete-evidence and bridge-identity contract
before reuse and after the new replay. An initial link-only adapter was
rejected by its containment check. The initial raw replay also correctly
rejected a maintainer workflow edit at final source binding; the immutable
workflow was restored and the complete replay restarted in a fresh directory.
No failed receipt was edited into a pass.

The final replay, package and binding controls cover source closure, actual
owner geometry, emitted charts/controller, complete cost invoices, all inherited
native stages and adversarial mutations. The frozen package is unchanged.
`make frontier942-verify` invokes the maintainer coverage wrapper; the original
source-pinned Linux workflow remains intact. Published receipts distinguish
explicit baseline reuse from a cold ancestry rebuild and local macOS execution
from subsequent hosted Linux jobs.

## Circuit review and scope

The four-, five- and six-wire prefix series is explicitly cumulative:
**24,305 -> 24,051 -> 23,711 -> 23,673 ADDs**. The six-wire step allows changed
positive integer transfers while preserving the complete Boolean map and
bounding both positive orientations. Every common-frame substitution is
checked at its boundary; its literal reverse cleanup is charged. All 661
arbitrary input columns, unchanged frame/COPY/ERASE obligations and native
5/9/11-stage checks pass. These operations leave the recursive rank histogram
unchanged and therefore do not improve κ on their own.

The helper-matching candidate is **separate**. It retains the #915 target basis
and proves the exact disjoint-pair floor with 439 vertex prices and 19 odd-set
prices. Summing edge inequalities over a matching proves the bound even for
overlapping odd sets. All 601 budgeted words have their positive bounds priced.
The 11,949-ADD witness uses about 87.43% of the nine-stage allowance; three
nominated 11,948-ADD words fail that allowance. This does not exclude every
arithmetic optimum from satisfying it. Its arbitrary-dirt boundary is a valid
finite semantic D substitution; the old gate-local H2 and literal K extractor
remain false. Full hosted emission, invoices and assembly are still needed
before changing the selected bit supplier. Its count is not added to the
prefix-series savings in this review.

The single-block results preserve distinct bounded search classes. #925's
backward positive-matrix DAG and independent breadth-first search establish
12 as the minimum for its exact positive transfer, not for every XOR circuit.
#880/#908/#914 finish each destination once within fixed factors. #873 uses a
separate backward Bellman implementation for its fixed single-write class;
its submission provides local column/record checks, not a native multistage
harness. #857 and #906 require full prefix accounting because reverse positive
bounds can grow. #857 receives parallel credit even though #891 already has a
shorter frame523 candidate. #923 improves on Gautam Gupta's #844 and retains
that explicit attribution. The single-block candidates are not silently
composed with one another or with the helper-matching word.

## Corrections, bounds and verification tooling

Six historical source manifests (#857/#906/#908/#914/#923/#925) now address the
byte-identical immutable #799 checkpoint instead of the moving selected pointer;
the original expected digest is unchanged. #923's native environment receipt now
supports standalone Boost.Multiprecision without `boost/version.hpp`, explicitly
records that header's absence and hashes the available Boost headers. Its full
native replay succeeds. Original submitted bytes and adaptation descriptions
are retained. No mathematical assertion was removed to make a replay pass.

#845's quantified obstruction follows from an annihilator-dimension argument,
not from searching coefficient magnitudes. The full eight-coordinate case needs
its explicit incompatible-port check: rank 63 of the relaxed system alone is
insufficient. It covers real scalars and fixed coordinate readers; changed
readers, complex scalars, masked identities or several new totals remain open.
#848's D obstruction assumes the original syntactic H2. The later semantic-D
constructions deliberately escape that premise, so this is a scoped historical
negative result, not a prohibition of the improvements accepted in this batch.

#950 packs signed coefficients into unbounded integers with a radix larger than
the maximum possible coefficient difference. A zero packed difference then
forces every digit difference to vanish. The bound is computed from the supplied
factor, including invalid large entries; it is not assumed from the generator.
The complete historical audit and 1,806 corrupt-factor controls pass. Timing
numbers in the submission are the author's local measurements, not a new
maintainer benchmark or acceleration of the current supplier replay.

#946 addresses real encoding and newline issues, but its frozen-file detector
misses package-local pins. Its **165** changed files include eight inputs to
#950's source-bound audit. The first GitHub `files` response was capped at 100;
paginated REST and the Git diff recover the complete inventory. Applying its
126 script edits and 27 certificate digest changes without migrating the
historical consumers would invalidate their evidence. The patch is not imported
or closed; the pin conflicts and remaining replay obligation are recorded.
