## BIT working geometry: a repair that preserves the saving

existing conservative working-cap construction therefore gives
**8 + 18·7 = 134**, including native interiors and whole-job adapters.
No source-specific 127-axis interior construction has been established by
this review. This does not prove that such a smaller construction is impossible.

authorize substituting an independently rounded scalar service or partially
written child return into that induction.
