## Bind the ordinary producer correctly

The selected complex supplier has `m=73` and binds the shared saving, but the
ordinary stopped producer in these four proposals is **PR589 BIT `m=64`**.
Its maximum child rank is 42, with 28,416 live roles and **28,425 physical
roles**, including nine work roles. Complex rank-48 depth work is a different
producer. The fourteen selected arithmetic headrooms do not identify fourteen
implemented reserve envelopes.

For a fixed eligible odd prime `q>2^80` and integer `w>=1`, the BIT stock
proof charges a complete level divisor

```text
W_q(w) = 28425 * |GL_64(F_q)| * q^(4096*(w-1)),
log_q W_q(w) < 4097*w,
S(n,w) = W_q(w)^D(n).
```

Here `W_q(w)` is an actual row divisor, not the time moment's live-role
denominator. High-matrix fibers may cancel in normalized time but not in
stock. Siblings and the two ordinary-wrapper calls reuse a restored quotient;
they do not multiply it. The zero external leaf factor requires an internally
completed ordinary leaf, not a claim that the leaf has no internal workspace.
For borrowing, #2120 chooses stock from original width e **before** choosing
borrowed width k and the resulting body width.

The numerical connections are cheap to check without a supplier replay:

```sh
python3 -I -B - <<'PY'
from fractions import Fraction

assert 2**17 * 42**28 < 64**28
assert 4097 * Fraction(28, 17) == 6748
assert Fraction(6748, 8194) == Fraction(14, 17)
for sigma, ell in ((8194, 17), (6748, 16)):
    assert 2**(ell-1) < 8*sigma <= 2**ell
assert max(401501, 2*6748) == 401501
print("PASS_SELECTED_BOOTSTRAP_MAP_ARITHMETIC")
PY
```

Thus substituting the conditional `sigma=6748` into #2087's displayed
first-stage formula leaves `K=401501*C`. This is arithmetic, **not** a
measurement of C or a composed implementation certificate. At the recorded
native first-stage cutoff `L=4785575287428`, both stock proposals check the
sufficient guard. Neither result lowers that cutoff or prices the selected
supplier again.
