## Construction changes

Jeremy Kothe's #601 replaces the constructor's first-cleanup-gate rule with
a sufficient commutation test. The eight additional helpers have a preceding
gate that only reads their cleanup partner. The two additions commute, so the
helper's cleanup can move earlier. All intervening partner climbs remain paid.
This increases the count of hyperplane-ended helpers from 38 to 46 per word.
higherbeing's #608 independently implements this mechanism with a different
allocation. Its parallel contribution is retained even though its bound is lower.

DreamingOfClouds' #604 makes fourteen additional scratch helpers finish at
four-dimensional frames. A premix runs after the wrappers, a multi-gate tail
restores each early-ended helper, and a refactored dyadic remainder restores
the other arbitrary inputs. The remainder may not touch a target or an
early-ended helper. Both scalar orientations, the frame histories and the
paid profiles are checked on the resulting word. Scalar additions are still
charged in the finite primitive and precision invoice, even at rank-zero frames.

Jeremy's #615 adds two entering helpers with the previously proved third
entrance/terminal pair. #617 adds a second cold endpoint in each factory block,
for 28 cold endpoints per word. Its new operations are additions to a dead
port at the operands' existing common frame, and additions from cold helpers
to targets at frame zero. Exact scalar replay alone is insufficient to admit
the target writes: the recomputed compensation must not touch an early-ended
or entering helper before its wrapper. The transport and event checks enforce
that ordering, and mutation controls exercise it. The full-frame remainder
restores the modified dead ports.

These changes improve the paid rank distribution and allocation. They do not
establish a larger rank deficit or discharge the inherited uniform-realization
and multiplication-transfer assumptions.

explicit factorization, scalar, label and physical hypotheses. The three
entering-pair identities are the exact literals from #593. A cold helper's
new factorization is checked by the finite phase-aware wrapper verifier; the
abstract `exact_cold` theorem is not a new literal Lean proof of every pair.

separate review obligations. No complete Lean multiplication theorem is claimed.

#595 format analysis and uses the lower interval endpoint when comparing
competing stages. The headline 0.0051595 upper bound is unchanged. The
corrected package is kept at `research/format-limits-corrected`, preserving
the historical #595 bytes. Its ceilings retain the stated S2 assumptions and
are not universal barriers or achieved multiplication savings.

Dominik Scholz's #606 records Niemeier factors, transformed scalar operations,
paid compensation experiments and scoped obstructions. A saving within a
compensation subword is not a completed supplier or a new κ. Exact geometry,
