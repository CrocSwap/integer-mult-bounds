# Two-FULL controller contracts

This is navigation across scoped results, checked against canonical main
`b91956876e5b4122fd89179f1b57557ff8378f15` on October 23, 2026. It neither
replays nor scientifically accepts the cited proofs. The selected conditional
κ remains [60403/20000000](../../certificates/selected-result.json). The
[source receipt](two-full-controller-contract-map.json) pins the exact inputs
and assignment context; later PR status does not change those frozen sources.

A FULL is a complete native invocation, including its main and productive
helper outputs. A controller joins such invocations to produce independent
target jobs. An attractive formal invoice still needs its declared endpoint,
actual source frames, independent input owners and a complete recursive bill.

## Contract checks before a new experiment

First name the actual native source and the main input/output owners. The
central-obstruction and retained-helper packages bind the #4610 native source
at `20bb93cd990d2ff683e4d0fc56b87a42cd5d3296`. The precision and inverse
proofs instead consume the round-three `ACTUAL-WORD.json.gz`, whose compressed
SHA256 is
`7679daa4ec6a40f56fc7f0202379a6f454310e94945aba4f48beaaaafb838d05`.
Do not transfer an invoice or a source-normalizer identity between them
without checking the actual version and interface.

Then state which helpers may be touched, whether their original outputs are
retained, and what scratch must be returned. Extra helper owners change the
exactly-two-owner contract. Allowing address mixing changes the separated
algebra contract. Two source relaxations proved in different theorems cannot
simply be joined into one stronger exclusion.

For a Laurent approximation, distinguish its formal exact algebraic identity,
its error model, and the leading precision tariff. Deterministic inverses can
correlate errors without restoring Gaussian/frame identities. A coherent
potential proposal must address the actual scalar gates as well as paid and
warp transitions. Changing the scalar gates, using nonlinear potentials, or
allowing a quantitative scalar defect requires its own caller and bill.

Finally price all target, helper, center, correction, return and precision
work. A passing optimistic moment is a reason to continue the endpoint test;
an impossible endpoint cannot be repaired by a more accurate evaluation of
that same invoice. Conversely a scoped negative moment does not establish
the failure of a different cost model.

## Open changed contracts

The retained-helper source explicitly leaves literal central/fresh monomial
routing that preserves both distinct helper jobs open. Begin with the actual
forward/backward owner frames: the rank-one X entrance and empty Y/helper
entrance differ. Price every conversion and cleanup before a routing search.
The full-routing main-only addendum does not already close that helper access.

The inverse-interface source leaves a Gaussian-compatible paired caller with
both independent fresh D factors open. An additive coherent realization must
satisfy its paid and warp relations with a complete precision/storage bill.
A nonlinear realization could instead change its scalar circuit or bound and
pay scalar defects; no such implementation follows from invertibility alone.

These are source-stated remaining obligations, not recommendations that a
particular mechanism will improve κ. Recheck live discussions and PR heads
before claiming or expanding either route.
