complete trees, 5678 distinct blobs, every original changed path, author and predecessor notices.

identify exact reused packages; a title or shared ancestry alone does not establish coverage.

[Fresh execution](../../research/community-reconciliation-third100-audit/EXECUTION.json) and
[per-PR coverage](../../research/community-reconciliation-third100-audit/EXECUTION-COVERAGE.json)
are separate from historical Linux CI. CI is bound to its actual checkout, not just the PR head:
all contributed package bytes match except PR318's unrelated historical job, which is excluded.
PR311/313/314/316 have seven disclosed root-wrapper differences. Their package bytes match.
Inherited green workflows in PR350/362/364 do not test those new packages.

PR323's dependency-path failure is repaired by PR324 without scientific source changes. PR332's
final gzip-container mismatch is repaired by PR333 through complete expanded-byte checks and
adverse controls. PR391 repairs PR388's missing-log manifest and incomplete replay; the four
source archives and emitted programs are byte-identical, and the fresh complete PR391 run covers
that candidate. Fresh-copy packaging adapters separately document absent metadata/log entries
in PR350/355/360 and 104 restored, exactly pinned files in PR390. Original submissions remain intact.
The first #309 replay stopped on local disk capacity; that failure is retained separately from
its retry. Filesystem compression of completed temporary outputs checks byte equality and SHA256;
it changes storage allocation, not the submitted sources or mathematical checks. The retry reaches
a distinct retiming51 predecessor-pin mismatch. The word matches, four JSON pins are explained
by CRLF, and the remaining state-file mismatch is retained for correction; #309 remains open.

The E8 branches use different contracts. PR404/408 and successors use native chronological,
phase/bank and paid-dyadic checks; legacy gx E4/E5 acceptance is not implied. PR407/412/419 provide
checker-compatible alternatives but explicitly retain reopened phase, owner, invoice and
sink-isolation obligations. Passing either local checker is not proof of the other interface or
the complete asymptotic multiplication theorem.

PR304/327/363/417 strengthen individual suppliers. PR377's larger headline is a projected
composition; its verifier explicitly reports no new final kappa at the retained fixed bit rate.
Local Lean evidence is not a formalization of the global layouts or multiplication assembly.
PR368's 100-idea archive retains failed, partial, analysis-only and unevidenced proposals as such.
