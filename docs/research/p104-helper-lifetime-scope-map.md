## Common object and units

The retained [PR1991 native channel](../../research/p104-native-channel/README.md)
realizes `Y += (G/2) X` on 104 ports, with 72 arbitrary dirty helpers and eight
owned private-copy lifetimes. The original PR1972 word has 4,058 events. The
missing correction is `C = I - G/2`. With doubled root-coordinate matrix
`R` of size 8 by 104, `G = R^T R/4`, `R R^T = 104 I`, and C is nonsingular.
The original local rank mass is 2,368: 2,312 boundary progress plus COPY56.

Keep these units separate:

| Quantity | What it counts | What it does not supply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rational dimension | Independent coefficient rows in an information space | A chronological acquisition, storage or transport schedule |
| Whole-array crossing | One communicated array of dimension d; its information capacity is at most d | The native child's rank: a rank-seven descent still carries one array |
| Native transport rank | Dimension gap of a nested frame transition in the stated model | The net recursive excess after coverage and hosting |
| Helper lifetime | One initialization followed by a permitted frame path; reinitialization starts another lifetime | Peak simultaneous physical storage or number of final target writers |

In particular, a ZERO-to-FULL monotone lifetime telescopes to nine boundary
rank units under dimension-gap billing. Crediting those units against idle
work requires a realized owner/coverage construction; charging them all as
excess without that analysis is also unjustified.

The two appearances of 96 have different proofs. PR2251 projects pooled
coefficient rows into an eight-dimensional root code and constructs a
complement. PR2127 factors source-to-target communication through eight
original array crossings plus the added crossings. The numerical agreement
does not identify their witnesses or their costs.

The 208 proof counts **first entrances**, not eventual writers. Two entering
helpers may mix at a target frame and use only one writer. PR2353 includes
that explicit cancellation example. An individual increasing lifetime cannot
visit two distinct eight-dimensional target frames, which is why those
entrance requirements can be summed.

## Three comparisons that change the next experiment

**Private-descended values are already covered in a stronger relaxation.**
The private descent at original event 2463 carries X8 with coefficient -2 into
a line excluding its label, so PR2353's ordinary support invariant fails if
that value is admitted. PR2383 checks that all eight private source rows lie
in K and span K, and that `K intersect U(H_t) = {0}` at every target endpoint.
Free read-only access to those values therefore does not escape the extended
208 floor. Feeding new information through an original descent, or reading a
source row outside K, is a different premise; it needs its own argument.

**Acquisition bounds do not add independently across targets.** Y0's
48-lifetime theorem is compatible with just two final arrivals. For Y0/Y1,
PR2445 shares the 18 common acquisitions and attains `48 + 48 - 18 = 78`.
Its comparison word uses one helper per nonzero correction entry: sharing
saves 18 lifetimes and 162 boundary rank units, but raises scalar ADDs from
384 to 440. The 384-ADD comparison is not two copies of PR2393's 238-ADD word.
Neither acquisition count nor ADD count alone orders the complete moments.

Do not extrapolate the first-expansion escape test from Y0/Y1 to Y2.
[PR2521's three-target construction][2521] retains a late-acquisition component
from PR2504: one helper reads X6 at cut 0, X1 at cut 669 and X0 at cut 842.
It uses 90 lifetimes for Y0/Y1/Y2 after cross-group sharing. This is a concrete
reason to inspect each actual source path rather than assume all useful
acquisitions happen on distinct initial lines. The 90 is a feasible count,
not an unrestricted minimum.

**A transport floor needs a separate excess-cost premise.** PR2127's
`M(0) >= 7659/7592 > 1` corollary assumes the old idle requirement and **no
new creditable persistent endpoint progress**. Its own `0 -> line -> H -> FULL`
example has ranks 1,7,1, all boundary progress. PR2393/2445 restore logical
dirty values at FULL after entering at ZERO; physical arrays at those different
charts are not asserted identical. Neither their changed boundary nor the
208 lifetime floor licenses reuse of the unchanged-idle corollary.

There is also a stronger obstruction for a particular expensive word:
[PR2594's relay-v2 audit][2594] checks a complete 104-target logical correction
with 1,031 lifetimes and rank mass 9,279. In the stated fixed `m=73`, `W=832`,
nine-stage template, **local calls alone** give
`9*(2368+9279)/(73*832) = 104823/60736 > 1`, before idle and other costs.
That rejects this word in that template without assuming its new helper
progress has zero value. It does not reject all monotone relay designs.

An assumed per-lifetime histogram is a further premise. [PR2604][2604]
constructs a dirty-restored arrival with dimensions `0 -> 1 -> 8 -> 9`, hence
rank steps 1,7,1, in the literal relay grammar. It refutes the excluded-shape
argument used for a uniform 463-lifetime screen; it does **not** exhibit a
budget-feasible design above 463 or disprove that numerical limit. The weaker
conditional mass requirement is `9L <= 4344`, hence `L <= 482`; this is
necessary only, not a positive-saving or hosting certificate. A stronger
profile bound needs a joint constraint justified by actual contacts.

## Logical, native and complete admission

The six core results stop at different interfaces. In particular, the
native proof for the original G/2 channel does not automatically compile
new correction transports. [PR2458][2458] separately covers PR2445's 236 new
occurrences using 161 distinct frame pairs and checks the complete finite
forward boundary. Its edge-adjoint checks do not certify a complete
inverse-transpose program with COPY/discard. This distinction matters even
when another successor addresses that next interface: apply a certificate
only to its actual word and orientation.

Similarly, PR2594's row-growth bounds are for the correction with the original
channel omitted. They are not bounds on the combined program or accumulated
physical rounding errors. A logical word, native forward binding and a
complete hosted supplier are separate claims. Scalar/address work, owners,
idle and residual children, precision, tape and all-size hypotheses must be
resolved for the proposed complete construction.

## Open next questions

Before proposing another bound, state which of the six contracts applies and
which counted quantity the new statement concerns. If allowing original-helper
reads, identify their actual source rows and test whether they leave K; if
sharing acquisitions, test the named source/target paths and collector ranks.
These finite checks decide whether a proposed hypothesis change is real.

For construction work, start from a literal candidate's complete word and
budget. The all-target relay-v2 example makes reducing its charged bill a
concrete question, while its current fixed-template failure makes native
lowering alone insufficient to rescue that bill. Check the live thread before
claiming a modification; this map does not declare the linked follow-ups
unclaimed. A favorable completed budget, chronological hosting and the
remaining physical interfaces are the blockers to supplier admission.
